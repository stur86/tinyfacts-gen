A young boy lived in a town. He had little stuff. He was not a man of good goods. By some way, he found a strange light making thing. Inside this light making thing was a big god man. The god man could give him many things. The boy used these things to make his life good. He met a girl, a girl from a big house. He loved her. A bad man wanted the light making thing for his own use. He tried to take it from the boy. But the boy used his mind to beat the bad man. The good boy saved the girl and found a way to live with her. They were happy and free.
