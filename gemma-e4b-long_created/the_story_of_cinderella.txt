A girl has a hard life. The woman who is not her mother and her other sisters are bad to her. They hurt her in their house. She must do all the work. She is sad. One day, a man of power holds a big party for all the young people. The girl wants to go, but the women of the house stop her. A good power comes to her and helps her. The power makes her a fine dress and gives her a small glass shoe. She goes to the party and meets a good boy. They dance all night. When the time comes, she must run away, and she drops her shoe. The boy looks for the shoe and looks for the girl who fits it. He looks at all the girls, but only she fits the shoe. They marry, and the girl lives a good life. This story shows how good is better than bad.
