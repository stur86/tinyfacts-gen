The story is about a family with five young girls. The dad has many daughters. In that time, it was very hard for a girl to find a good life. A good match was key to a good life. One girl is kind. Her sister has a quick mind. They meet a man. He is a great man with a great deal of money. He thinks the family is not good enough for him, and this shows he thinks too high of himself. The girl sees fault in him because of his looks and his family. This is her wrong thought before seeing him. They fight. They say words against each other much. He does bad things at first. She feels anger. Later, both learn they were wrong. He acts kind and tries to lower his too-high thinking of himself. She sees that he is not as bad as she thought. They start to like one another. The story shows how people can be wrong in their first look. They learn that love is more than just looks or money. They find a way to be happy.
