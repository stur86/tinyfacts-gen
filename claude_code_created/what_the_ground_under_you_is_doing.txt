What The Ground Under You Is Doing

The ground is the one thing you never think about. It is what everything else moves on. When people want to say that something will not change, they say it is set in rock.

The ground is moving right now, under your feet, and it has never stopped.

Deep down, the world is hot. So hot that the rock down there is not really hard any more. It is thick and slow, but it moves, the way the thick stuff at the bottom of a hot cup will turn over if you wait long enough. Hot rock comes up, cools, and goes back down, round and round, over more years than anyone can hold in their head.

The cold hard part we live on is not one piece. It is broken, into a small number of great pieces, and those pieces are carried about on the slow turning under them. They move about as fast as the hair on your head grows. That sounds like nothing. But run it for many hundreds of hundreds of years and it moves land clear across the world.

This is why the sides of the great wide water look like they would fit together. They did fit together. All of it was one land once, and it broke up and the pieces went their own ways, and they are still going.

Everything else follows from what happens where two of these pieces meet.

Where they pull away from each other, hot rock comes up between them and makes new ground. Where they push into each other, one has to give. If one goes down under the other, it takes water down with it, and that water comes back up through the rock above as fire and grey stuff thrown into the sky. If they both hold, the ground between them has no way to go but up, and you get the tall ground. Every high place you have ever seen is two pieces pushing.

And where they only rub past each other, they do not move a little at a time. They hold. They hold for a hundred years while the push builds, and then they let go all at once. The ground moves feet in a moment, and everything people have built on top of it comes down.

We have known all this for less than a hundred years. When it was first put forward, the older heads laughed at the man who said it, and he died before it was shown to be true.
