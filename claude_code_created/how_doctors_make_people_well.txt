How Doctors Make People Well

For most of the story of people, almost nothing a doctor did was any help. They would open you up and let the blood out, or hand you something to drink that was worse than what you had. If you got better, it was your own body that did it.

Two ideas changed everything, and both are less than two hundred years old.

The first was learning that much of being sick is the work of living things far too small to see. They get in through what you eat, through the water, through an open cut. They eat, they have young, and there are soon so many of them that your body is in trouble. Once people knew this, they knew what to do about it. Clean your hands. Clean everything you are going to cut with. Keep what is dead away from what is not. Nothing else has saved so many people as those few very easy ideas.

The second was finding stuff that kills those tiny living things without killing you. That is harder than it sounds, because you are made of much the same stuff they are. But they are not quite the same as you, and if you can find something that goes only for what makes them different, you can put it into a person and it will run through them killing only the ones that should not be there. Before this, a cut on the hand could take a strong young person in a week. Now it is nothing.

Then there is the best idea of all. Your body already knows how to fight. It just needs to have seen the thing before. So we show it a dead one, or a broken bit of one, and the body learns the face of it without ever being hurt. Years later the real one gets in, and the body knows it at once and takes it down before you even feel it. This is why some of the very worst are now gone from the world completely, and no child will ever meet them.

The rest is not so different from what people always did. Set the broken part straight and hold it still while the body puts it back together. Cut out what has gone wrong. Take away the pain. In the end the doctor is not the one making you well. The doctor is getting things out of the way so the body can.
