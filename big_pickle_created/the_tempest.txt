A man who knew things other people did not know about the world lived on a small piece of land surrounded by water. Many years before, he had been the top person in a big city, but other men had taken his position and sent him away on a big thing that moves on water. He had been going on the water for a long time with his young daughter, and they had come to this piece of land and made it their home.

On this piece of land, he had learned to use the power of the world around him. He could make the wind move fast, call up great times of very bad wind and rain, and talk to the air. He had also found a being that was not like a person who had been stuck in a tree for many years, and he had set it free. In return, this being did what he told it to do.

One day, the man who had taken his position many years before was going on the water not far from the piece of land. The man who lived there used his power to make a great time of very bad wind and rain come up and break the big thing on water into many pieces. The people on it were thrown into the water and pushed onto the land, each one in a different place.

The man who lived there used this chance to do what he had wanted to do for many years. He brought the people together one by one, playing things to confuse them and making them confused. He brought his own young daughter together with the son of the man who had taken his position, and they fell in love.

In the end, the man who had taken his position said he was sorry for what he had done. The man who lived there said he would not hurt him, gave up his power over the world, and went back to the city to be the top person once more.
