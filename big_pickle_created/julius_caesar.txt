In the big city where many people lived and decided what was right, one man was loved by almost everyone. He had fought for the people, come out on top of great fights, and was seen as a good and kind person. But some of the men who decided what was right for the city were afraid that he wanted to be the top person for life, with no one able to tell him what to do.

One of these men, who had been a good friend of the loved man for many years, put together a group of men who felt the same way. They decided that the loved man had to be stopped. They told him they were going to put the thing on his head that means he is the top person, and when he came to get it, they surrounded him.

The loved man saw what was happening and said, "Even you, my friend?" But it was too late. The group took his life, right there in the place where they decided what was right. The man who had been his good friend was the first to use his pointed thing on him.

After the loved man was dead, the group thought the people would thank them. But when one of them spoke to the people to explain why they had done it, the people became very angry. They had loved the man who was now dead, and they wanted the ones who had taken his life to pay for it.

A great fight broke out in the city. The group of men who had taken the life of the loved man fought against the people who were angry about what had happened. In the end, almost all of the group were killed. The man who had been the good friend of the one who was loved took his own life, and the one who had spoken to the people was killed in a fight in a place far away. The city was left in the hands of those who had fought to pay back what had been done to the loved man.
