Deep in space, there are places where the pull toward the ground is so strong that not even light can get away. These very dark places are some of the most strange and strong things in everything that is.

A very dark place starts when a very big star runs out of the stuff that gives off light. For many hundreds of hundreds of years, the star has been pushing out from the inside while the pull toward the ground pulls in from the outside. When the stuff that gives off light runs out, there is nothing left to push back, and the star falls in. The pull becomes so strong that the star gets smaller and smaller, until it is very small but still has all the same stuff pulling together.

The pull of a very dark place is like nothing else in space. If you were close to one, you would need to go faster than light to get away, and nothing can go that fast. Even light, which is the fastest thing there is, gets pulled in if it gets too close. This is why these places look like nothing - they do not give off any light of their own, and they take in the light that comes near them.

Around the outside of a very dark place, there is a place called the point of no return. Once you cross this line, you can never come back out, no matter how fast you go. Everything that crosses this line - light, air, stars, even other very dark places - falls in and is pulled toward the middle, where it gets very small.

Very dark places can be very small or very big. Some are as small as a single ball, but with the pull of many hundreds of stars. Others are in the middle of big groups of stars and have the stuff of many hundreds of many hundreds of stars pulled into them.

Even though very dark places take in light, they are not like a thing that eats everything around them without doing anything. When stuff falls toward one, it goes around and around, getting hotter and brighter as it gets closer. This makes a bright ring of light around the very dark place that we can see from far away. By looking at this ring, people who study the world can find where these places are, even though the very dark place on its own is dark.

Very dark places change how everything around them works. The pull of one can move whole groups of stars, making them go faster or slower or change direction. Some people who study the world think that in the middle of every big group of stars, there is a very big very dark place. These big ones have been here for a very long time, and they help hold the group of stars together.
