A long time ago, a fighting man came home from a great fight. On his way, he and the man who fought next to him met three women who said strange things. They told him that one day he would be the top person in the land, the one who sits in the big chair and has the thing on his head that means he is the one who leads.

When he told his wife what they said, she wanted it to happen right away. She told him he should kill the person who was the top person now, while he was sleeping in their home. At first he did not want to do this, but she kept telling him it was the right thing.

That night, he went into the room where the top person was sleeping and took his life. The next morning, everyone was shocked. The top person had been kind to everyone, and now he was dead. The man who had done it became the new top person, but he was not happy. He kept seeing the dead man in his mind, even when no one else could see him.

His wife told him to stop thinking about it, but it was too late. He was so afraid of losing his new position that he started to kill anyone he thought might stand in his way. He had the good friend who had fought next to him killed, and he took the lives of the family of the man who had been the top person before him.

The people of the land turned against him. A group of fighting men, led by the son of the old top person who had run away, came to take back what had been taken from them. They fought him and were the ones who were the best. The man who had wanted power so much lost everything, even his life, and the son of the old top person took his place.
