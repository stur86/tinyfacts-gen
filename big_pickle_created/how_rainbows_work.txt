After the rain stops and the sun comes out, you can sometimes see a bright band of colors in the sky. This is one of the most beautiful things we can see. But what is it, and how does it happen?

This happens when sun light moves through tiny drops of water that are still in the air after rain. Each drop of water is like a very small ball of clear stuff. When light from the sun goes into one of these drops, something interesting happens.

The light moves from air into water, and when it does, it changes direction. Think of it like a car turning when it goes from one kind of road onto another - how fast it goes changes, and so the direction changes too. But light is made up of many different colors joined together, and each color turns a little more or a little less than the others. This is what makes the colors come away from each other.

After the light turns going in, it hits the back of the water drop and comes back. Then it turns again as it comes back out of the drop into the air. By this time, the colors have come out enough that you can see them on their own.

The red light turns the least, so it comes out at the top of the band. The blue light turns more, so it comes out at the bottom. Between them are all the other colors, one after another: red, then the color like the skin of a sweet thing from a tree, then the color of the sun, then green, then blue, then the color like the night sky.

This is why you see the bright band of colors in a round form in the sky. It is set by where you are standing and where the sun is behind you. If you move, the bright band of colors moves too - it is not a thing that sits in one place. It is made by the light, the water drops, and your eyes all working together.

Every person who looks at this sees their own. The person next to you sees a slightly different one, because the light is coming to their eyes from different drops than the ones sending light to you. Two people never see the same bright band of colors.
