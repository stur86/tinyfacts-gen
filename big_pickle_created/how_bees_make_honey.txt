Many small animals with soft stuff around their bodies and things they use to fly live together in large groups. They work as a team to make sweet food that can keep them going through the cold months when there are no bright parts on growing things outside. This sweet food is what many people call the sweet stuff that small flying animals make, and the way they make it is one of the most amazing things in the world.

The work starts outside the home. The animals that fly go out and land on bright parts of growing things. They have tiny parts on their heads that they push into the sweet wet stuff inside the bright parts. They take this wet stuff and hold it in a bag inside their bodies.

After they have taken the sweet wet stuff from many bright parts, they go back to the home. Inside the home, they give the wet stuff to the ones who stay and work there. These ones take the wet stuff into their own mouths and work it with their body parts, changing it in important ways. What comes out is a different kind of wet stuff - more thick and more sweet.

The ones making the food then put this changed wet stuff into rooms made of a hard white stuff they make from their own bodies. They move air over it with the things they use to fly to push out the wet air, and over time the stuff becomes thick and sweet enough to keep for a long time. When the rooms are full, they cover them with a soft top so the stuff stays good.

The home stores this food for the cold time of year. When it is too cold to go outside and find bright parts, the group eats from what they have stored. Each one takes what it needs from the rooms, passing the food from mouth to mouth until it reaches the ones deep inside who do not go out.

A single group of these animals can make many rooms of sweet food in one year. They visit many hundreds of bright parts to get enough wet stuff. The work they do helps the growing things too - as they go from one bright part to another, they carry the small parts that make new growing things, helping them go and grow in new places.
