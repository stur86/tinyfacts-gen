Inside your chest, behind your front wall of hard stuff inside your body, there is a thing that works every moment of your life without you telling it to. It is about how big your closed hand is, and it pushes the red wet stuff through your body more than one time every second.

This thing has four rooms inside it. Two rooms are on the top, and two are on the bottom. The top rooms take in the red wet stuff from the body after it has gone all through and given away the good air that the body needs. This stuff is dark red and has already done its work.

From the top rooms, the stuff goes down to the bottom rooms. Then the bottom rooms push it out - one room pushes the dark red stuff to the place where it gets new good air, and the other room pushes the bright red stuff, now full of good air, out to the whole body.

Every time the thing in your chest beats, it does two things at once. Both top rooms take in stuff at the same time, and both bottom rooms push stuff out at the same time. The sound you hear when you put your hand on your chest is this beating - the walls of the thing closing and opening, over and over, keeping you going.

The red wet stuff moves through the body in long lines that go all through. Some lines are big and thick, and some are so small that the red wet stuff can only go through them one piece at a time. The big lines carry the stuff away from the thing in your chest, and other big lines bring it back.

The thing in your chest has its own way of starting each beat. It does not need your brain to tell it when to go. Deep inside the thing, there is a tiny group of cells that sends out a call, and this call moves through the walls of the thing, telling them when to close and when to open. Your brain can make it go faster when you run or slower when you rest, but even without the brain, it keeps going.

From the time you are very small, before you come into the world, this thing works without stopping. It beats more than one time every second, every minute, every hour, every day. In a long life, it may beat more than two hundred times without ever stopping once.
