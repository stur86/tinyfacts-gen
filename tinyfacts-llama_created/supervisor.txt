To be a manager means you are in a place where many people work together. The person who is in this place is the one who tells them what to do. They do not do everything themselves. They help other people and give them the right to do their jobs.

Imagine you are the one who runs a store. You tell the people who work there what they are supposed to do. You also help them if they ask. This is your job. But when you are in a job that has many people, you need someone else to help you. This person is your manager.

The manager is the one who makes sure the work is done right. They do not let things go. They make sure the work is done before the next day starts. They also help people who are not doing their jobs. They are the ones who can say,  This is not done yet. The person who does not do the job has to go and do it.

A manager can be in many different jobs. Some work in offices. Some work in schools. Some work in hospitals. They all do the same job. They are the ones who help others do their jobs.

Being a manager is hard work. You have to know how to make things work well. You have to know how many people are there and how much money is left. You have to know what is wrong with the job and how to fix it. You also have to know how to deal with people who are not happy about what they are doing.
