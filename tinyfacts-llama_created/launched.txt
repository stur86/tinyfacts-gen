To start something is to move it from one place to another. Imagine you have a ball. You throw the ball. It goes up in the air and then comes back down to the ground. This is starting something.

You can also start something by moving a car. If you push the car, it starts to move. Then it goes very fast and then it stops. It is now moving from one place to another.

Sometimes we use this word when we talk about a thing that is flying. A person or a group of people builds a thing that can fly. They put it in the air and then the thing starts to move. This is starting a new trip for the flying thing.

You can also use this word to talk about a person who starts a new thing. If you are a teacher, you start a new class. You tell the students what to do and then they learn.

This word can also mean to leave something behind when you move away. If you live in a city and you move to the city where you grew up, you are leaving the city behind. This is starting a new life in a different place.
