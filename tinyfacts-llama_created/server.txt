A computer is a thing that can do many things. It can do work, play games, show pictures, and talk to other computers. A computer has many parts. It has a brain, a hard round thing inside that makes it work, a box to hold the brain, and many other parts that help it do its work.

One of these parts is a computer that tells the other parts what to do. This computer is called a user. The user tells the computer what to do. The computer follows the computer.

Another computer is the one that makes the words. It is like the person who talks. When you type on a computer, the computer writes the words. It does not speak like a person does. It only uses numbers to write the words.

There are many computers in a big room. Each computer does a little part of the work. The computers talk to each other. They send the work they do to the person who makes the words. The person who makes the words reads the work they get and makes a new word.

There is also a computer in your home. It is the one that helps you play games. It knows how to do games. It is like a very good friend that you can talk to.

When you ask a computer to do something, it is like asking a person to do something. The computer does not understand how to say yes or no. It only does what you tell it to do.
