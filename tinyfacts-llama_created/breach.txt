To break something means to make a small opening in a hard thing. You can do this with a rock, a piece of wood, or even a piece of paper. When you break a thing, the pieces fall out and you have a few pieces left.

In a home, the door is a thing that you use to enter and leave. If you open the door and you do not close it again, you are breaking the door. This is bad because it lets people or animals into your home without your help.

In a game, you might break a small thing. If you hit a ball with your hand, you break the ball. The ball can still be a good game if you use it later, but now it is broken.

A person can also break something because they are angry. If you yell and hit a person, you are breaking their face or their heart. They may feel hurt and want to get away.

When a person is very good at their job, they can break things too. They might use a car to drive away from a place they are not allowed to be. This is a bad thing to do because it is not safe and it is against the city or school.

Sometimes, a person breaks their word. They promise to do something, but they do not follow through. This can make the other person angry and they might say bad things about the person.
