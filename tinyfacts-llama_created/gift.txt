A thing you give to someone to show you care is called a present. You can give it to a friend, a family person, or a person you like. This is a nice way to share your money or your time with another person.

Imagine you have a lot of money. You could buy something you really want. But instead, you decide to give it to someone. This is a kind thing to do. You are showing that you are happy to be your friend.

You can also give a small thing that is not money. A book, a song, or a small box filled with sweet stuff are all presents. People like to give these to each other because they are fun and make the other person happy.

Sometimes, you give a present because you know the person will like it. You might give a thing that is from your home. This helps the person feel like they are a part of your life.

If you give a present, you show you care about the other person. You are not just giving things. You are showing you are kind and that you want to be near them.
