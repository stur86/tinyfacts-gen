A person who works in a food place is called a head. This person is the top one. They make sure everyone follows the right way. They plan the food for the day. They decide what to make and how to make it. They also decide how much money each person will get and how the food will be set out.

Some people work in a small house. They make food for one person. They are very good at this job. Some people work in a big house with many people. They work together to make food for many people at once.

In some places, the head is a man. In other places, the head is a woman. This is because there are more women than men in many places. Some places also have a person who is both the head and the one who makes food. This is very strange, but it is true.

The head wears a white shirt and a hated thing on their head. This hated thing is very important. It is a sign of their job. The head also wears a pair of shoes that are very nice. These shoes help them walk while they are standing very high.
