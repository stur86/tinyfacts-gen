The word that we use to talk about how things go up is called a word that goes up. When you say this word, you are telling the person next to you that something is happening. If you are talking about a chair, you might say,  The chair is going up. 

When you say this, the word is telling the person you are talking to that the chair is moving. The chair is not falling down. It is going up into the air. You are also telling the person that you are looking at the chair and that it is going up. 

This word can also mean something that starts in the ground and goes up into the sky. You can see trees that are growing. The tree starts in the ground and goes up into the sky. As the tree grows, the ground becomes smaller and the sky becomes bigger. The word is telling you that the tree is moving from the ground into the sky.
