An owner is a person who owns a house, a car, or a dog. They are the ones who pay money to keep the thing for them.

Imagine you have a box where you keep your favorite books. You are the one who keeps the box. If you want to move the box to a different room, you need to tell the person who owns the box. They have to let the box go. You did not give them the right to touch it. They must follow the way you say.

A person who owns a house is a very important person. They pay money every month to keep the house safe and clean. They also pay for food and other things the house needs to stay standing. This person is the one who makes sure the house is safe for everyone who lives there.

A person who owns a car is also important. They pay money to buy the car. They pay money every month to keep the car safe and clean. They also pay for gasped when the car needs to go far.

A person who owns a dog is even more important. They pay money to buy the dog. They pay money every month to keep the dog safe and clean. They also pay for food and other things the dog needs to stay happy.

When a person owns something, they are making a promise to keep it for them. They are saying, I am here to help you. I am here to keep you safe.
