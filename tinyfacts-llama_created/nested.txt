To be inside something else

Imagine a tree with many big leaves. Each big leave is like a small house. The big leaves are inside the small house. Inside the small house, there are even more big leaves. They are all inside each other like a house inside a house.

When we talk about something, we are saying it has many parts. Each part is inside the other part. This is how a house is inside a room. The big house is inside the room. The room is inside the street. The street is inside the city.

We can also say this with things that are not living. A small part of a thing can be inside another small part. This makes the thing bigger. Think of a ball. The white part is inside the red part. The white part is inside the red part because everything inside is inside everything else.

You can use this word to talk about anything that has many parts. It is a good word to use when you want to show how a thing is made up of many pieces.
