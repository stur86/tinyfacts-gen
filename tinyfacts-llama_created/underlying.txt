The word under is used when you talk about what is not on top. It is about what is not on the top of a thing. Think of a table. The top part is where people sit. Under the table is the floor. The floor is what is not on top of the table.

Imagine you are in a room with a big table in the middle. The room is made of many small rooms. Under the table, there is more room. The room under the table has walls and a floor. It is like a big room that is not on top of the table.

Sometimes, you use this word to talk about people. If you say someone is under someone, it means they are not the top person. They are the one who is not in the group or the one who is not as strong as the top person.

It can also mean something is hidden. If you are under a strong thing, it is under a high wall or a thick rock. This means the thing is not on top of the ground anymore. It is now hidden under the top part.

So, the word under is a way to talk about what is not on top. It is about what is behind or under something.
