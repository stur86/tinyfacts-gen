To share something means to give it to others. You might share a book with a friend. You tell them about the story. They might like it too. You also share food with your family. You eat from a big box and you give some to your sister.

Sometimes, you share your money. You tell a friend you need money to buy a new game. Your friend gives you some. This makes you feel happy because you have more money.

Sharing is also about being kind. If you see a person who is sad, you can share a smile. You put a kind word in their ear. This makes the person feel better.

Sharing things helps everyone. When you share a game, you are part of a group. You feel happy when you are with many other people who like the same game. Sharing is a way to be friends with others.
