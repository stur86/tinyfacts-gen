To do something is to act on a thing. You do not just stand there. You move. You say a word. You do not just think. You are the one doing it. You are the one in the place.

Think about a song. You hear the music. You move your body. You are doing it. You are the one. You are not just listening. You are the one doing it.

This is what you do when you do something new. You try it. You act on it. You see how it works. You are the one. You are the one doing it.

You can also do something for others. You give them a thing. You show them a way. You help them. You are doing it for them. You are not just doing it for yourself.

Sometimes people do this with words. They speak to each other. They talk about things. They say things that help. They do it so people can understand each other.

You can also do something for yourself. You try to find a thing. You search. You act. You see what you need. You are doing it for your own life.

So, to do something means you act on a thing. You move. You say a word. You are the one. You are the one in the place.
