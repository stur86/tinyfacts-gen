A long time ago, people made things from wood and stuff. They put many small pieces of wood together and made a big thing. This was a very hard thing to do. Today, we make many things from small pieces of stuff. We put many of these small pieces together many times and make a very strong thing that is easy to work with.

When we do this, we change the way the small pieces work. Each small piece is like a tiny thing that can be broken down and made again. When we put many of these tiny things together, we make something that is strong and can hold a lot of things. This is why we use this word when we talk about things that are made from many small pieces.

Think of a line of hair. The hair is made from many small pieces of hair. Each hair piece is like a tiny thing that can be broken down and made again. When we put many of these tiny hair pieces together, we make a strong line of hair. The hair can hold many things in it and feel like a strong thing when you touch it.

The word is also used for things that are made from long lines of many small pieces. These are things that are very strong and can hold many things. You can use this word to talk about a car, a book, a chair, or anything else that is made from many small pieces.

In a different way, when we say a thing is made from many small parts, we are talking about how many small pieces are in it. Think of a book. A book is made from many hundreds of pieces of paper. Each piece of paper is like a tiny thing that can be broken down and made again. When we put all these tiny pieces together, we make a book that can carry many words.
