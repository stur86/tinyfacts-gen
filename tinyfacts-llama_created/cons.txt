To consider something is to think about it for a long time. You might think about a problem, a person, or a thing for a long time. You might try to figure out what is best for you or what you can do.

It means you are making a plan. You might think, why I should do this? Or why I should not do that? You are using your mind to decide. Sometimes you might feel afraid or angry. You are using your body to act on what you decided.

If you consider a person you love, you are thinking about what to do next. You are trying to decide how to be with them. You might feel happy or sad.

You can also consider a thing. You might think, should I buy this? Or should I not buy this? You are using your mind to decide what to do with a thing.

Sometimes you consider a problem that is very hard. You are trying to figure out what to do. You are thinking about the good and the bad. You are trying to find a way out.

To consider is to think and try to understand. It is to use your mind and body to make a plan.
