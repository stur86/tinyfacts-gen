When someone is moved, they are made to act. They do not just do something because they say they have to. They are moved by a feeling.

You might feel this when you are happy. You might want to do something because you are glad. You might want to go to a party or play with a friend. In this case, you are moving because you want to have fun.

Sometimes, people are moved by a story. They hear about a person who did something great and they want to do the same thing. This can make them want to work hard and try to be the person they want to be.

You can also be moved by what you see. If you see a beautiful picture of a place, you might feel a happy feeling. You might want to go there one day. This is because you are moved by the picture.

Being moved is a good thing. It shows you care about something. It means you are not just doing something because you have to. You are doing it because you want to.
