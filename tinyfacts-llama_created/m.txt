The word makes a lot of things. You can use it to talk about people or animals. If a person is moving, you say they are moving. If a dog is running, it is moving. You can also use it to talk about things that are moving too fast.

When you are talking about a child, you might say,  This is a boy. Or,  This is a girl. You are talking about a person.

You can also use the word to talk about food. If you eat a thing, you are eating it. If you drink a drink, you are drinking it. You can say,  I am drinking a cold drink right now.

Sometimes people use moneys to buy things. If you have money, you can buy food, clothes, or a new book. You are using money to buy things you want.

You can also say,  I am making a thing. This means you are doing work so a new thing can be made. If you are making a bed, you are using your hands to put the soft stuff on the ground.

The word is important because it shows how people talk and what they mean. It helps us understand each other.
