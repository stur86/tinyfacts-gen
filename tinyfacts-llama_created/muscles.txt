Your body has many parts that help you move around. These parts are made of strong stuff that can change when you want to lift your hands or run. You have parts inside your chest that help you breathe. You also have parts on your legs that help you walk.

The parts you see on your arms and legs are the ones that help you move. They are made of hard, strong stuff that can get very hot. When you want to lift a heavy box, your arms go up and your hands pull. This is because your arms have a long part inside them that gets stronger and can push the box. Your legs also have these parts that help you walk. When you walk, your legs keep moving in a long way. This makes your feet touch the ground many times.

You also have parts inside your head that help you think. Your brain is the part of your body that keeps everything going. It is like a very big computer that makes you think, feel, and remember things.

The parts you see on your face are the ones that help you look. Your eyes are the parts that see. They help you see the world. Your nose is the part that smells things. Your mouth is the part that talks. Your hair is a long part of your body that helps keep you warm. Your skin is the part that keeps you safe and helps you feel.

All these parts work together so you can move around, breathe, think, look, and feel. Without them, you would not be able to do any of these things.
