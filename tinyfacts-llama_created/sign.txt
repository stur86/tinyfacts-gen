A sign is a thing that shows you something. It can be a word, a picture, a word with numbers, or even a thing you hold up with your hands.

You can sign a paper to say you were there. You might sign your name on the paper, so that everyone knows you were the person who did it. You can also sign a word to tell others you are talking about it. If you are talking about a story, you might sign the word to show that you agree with what the person said.

You can also use a sign to show that something is true. If you see a sign on a road that says, you must stop, you must stop. If a sign says, do not enter, you should not enter.

Sometimes, a sign is a picture of something. If a picture of a cat shows a cat, the picture is a sign. You can see the picture and know what the word means.

In a store, you might see a sign that says, buy this now, and the person who writes it is the person who is in control. This sign lets everyone know what they can do.
