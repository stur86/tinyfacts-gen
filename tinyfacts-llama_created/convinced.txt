To be sure that something is true

You might feel this way when you are sure about something. You do not just think it is true. You are sure.

If you are sure you are tired, you are not afraid. You are not worried. You feel calm because you know what is happening.

You can also feel this way when you are sure you are right about a person or a place. You believe they are doing the right thing. You trust them.

If you are sure you can do something, you are more ready to try. You are not afraid of the hard work. You are sure you will be okay.

Sometimes, you feel this way when you are sure you are in the right place. You know where you are and where you have to go. You feel safe because you know the way.

The word is also about how people act around each other. If you are sure that another person is doing the right thing, you might feel happy to help them. You want them to be okay.

You can also be sure about how you look. You feel good about your hair. You feel good about your clothes. You feel sure that you are the right person for a job.
