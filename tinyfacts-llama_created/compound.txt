When you think of things that are made, you often think about how they are made from other things. If you have a ball, and you cut it into pieces, you still have the same ball. You just broke it into smaller pieces. In the same way, when you make a new thing from many things, you still keep all those things inside it.

A group of many small things is called a group. A group is like a little set inside a big set. Think of a group of numbers. You have a group of ten numbers. You can add two of them together and get another number. You can use this group many times and always get the same number.

A group of many things that are not numbers is called a group. It is like a group of friends. You are all the same people. You all do the same things. You all like the same things. You are a group because you are all one group.

When you use this word in a story, you might say that a group of animals lives in a big city. Or you might say that a group of friends works together at a school. It is a way to tell about many people or many things that are all the same.

You can also use this word with small things. If you have many small things in a box, you call it a group. You are not just one small thing. You are many small things put together in one box.
