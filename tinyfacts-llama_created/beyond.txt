To go past something means you move away from it. If you are in a room with your friends, you can go to the other side of the room to find a chair. You are going past the chairs and the walls. You are moving forward.

When you talk about going past a time, you are going to the next time. If you go to bed at  night and you go to sleep at  night, you are going past the time. You are moving into another day.

You can also go past a place. If you are in a city and you walk out of the city, you are going past the city. This is a new place you are visiting. It is not the same place as your old home.

Sometimes, going past something means you are afraid. If you see a big dog, you might feel afraid to go near it. You are going past the dog because you are afraid. You are moving away from the thing that makes you feel scared.

You can also go past a thing. If you have a book and you put it down, you are going past it. You are not looking at it anymore. You are moving on to another thing.

Going past is also about change. If you move to a new school, you are going past the school you knew. You are moving into a new life. You are changing what you know and who you are.

So, going past is about moving away, changing, or going into a new time or place. It is a way to move forward and become something new.
