A well is a deep opening in the ground. It is usually made by a person. They go down deep into the ground and use hard stuff to break the rock. Then they put a big round thing in the ground. This round thing holds water or air inside. When the ground is ready, the person can open the well and take water or air out.

The word well comes from a big tree. The tree had a long way down into the ground. The people who lived near the tree thought it was a well. They would go down the tree and drink the water from the well. Later, they built their own wells.

A person who works with wells is called a well dragger. They use a big round thing that goes down into the ground to open the well. They also use a small round thing to take water out of the well. They have to be carefully close to the ground to do this, so they do not fall in.

There is also a well in a story. The story is about a man who lives in a town. He loves to go out and look at the world. One day, he goes to a place where water comes out of the ground. He drinks from it and feels very good. The story ends with him going home, happy and tired.

Well is also used to talk about something that is very strong. A well can break under heavy water. It can also break under heavy ground. If something is very strong, it is called a well.
