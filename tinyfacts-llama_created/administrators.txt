An office is a place where people work. One of these people is an important person who helps the other people. They are the ones who make sure everything runs well. They are the ones who pay the money, who buy things for the office, who pick the food, and who keep the office clean.

The person who is the top one is called the head. They make the big plans and decide what to do. They talk to everyone and make sure everyone knows what is going on. They are like the leader of the group.

Sometimes, the head does not work. They might leave the job. In this case, the people who work there have to decide who is the new head. They pick the person who they think will do a good job. This is a way to make sure that the office stays running well.

The person who is in this job is not the one who made the plans. They do not decide what to buy or who gets the money. They just follow what the head tells them. They are the ones who have to follow the plans.

People like these are important because they keep the office going. Without them, the office would be a place where people do nothing. They are like the ones who keep the car moving so we can get from one place to another.

Sometimes, there are more of these people. They work together to make sure everything is okay. They share the work and make sure that no one person does all the work. This way, everyone helps and the work is done well.
