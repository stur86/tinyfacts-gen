To make something means to take tiny pieces of a thing and put them together very fast. You can do this with your hands. You start with one small thing. You add a little bit of another. You add a third. Before you know it, you have a whole new thing.

Sometimes, people make things that need to be held. They cut a piece of wood. They put the piece together with a small thing that makes it stay. The person does this many times, and the wood becomes a very strong chair. You can hold it and sit on it.

Another kind of making is when you use computers to do a job. A computer has many tiny parts that work very fast. The computer takes a set of numbers and makes something new from them. It can make a picture, play music, or write a story. This is called a computer job.

You can also make things that are not made of wood. You can make a box with many pieces. You put all the pieces into a box. You close the box and the box becomes a new thing. It is something you can keep in your home.

To make something is to change a thing into a new one. It is a way to bring new life into things that are old. This is why you can say a baby is made. A baby is a new person who grows from a tiny thing in a woman.
