To set a number for something means to tell it is not bigger or smaller than that number. If you say you can only eat so many pieces of food, you are setting a number for how many you can have.

Imagine you want to go to a big park with many tall trees. But you do not want to walk too many steps. You tell the park people you can only walk a few hundred feet. They write it down so they know what to do. This is like setting a number. It helps keep you safe and on the right track.

You can also set a number for how much money you have. If you do not have enough money, you might not be able to buy what you need. This is like setting a number for how many things you can buy.

Sometimes, you might try to do something that is not safe. If you drive a car and you have a lot of money, you should not drive at night. You have to follow the number of people who are allowed to drive at that time. This number helps people stay safe.
