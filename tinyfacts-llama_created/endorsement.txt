To end something means to bring it to a close. You might use this word to talk about a story. If a person tells you how to do something, they are often said to have an end in mind. They think that the story is the way to go, and they want you to follow it.

Sometimes, when you see a person who is very good at something, you say they have an end. You think they have found the right way to do it, and you want them to be the one to share their way with others. This is a way of saying that the person is the one who has the right idea.

You can also use this word for many other things. If a food is the best, you might say it has an end. If a game is the best way to play, you might say it is the end of the game. Even if you are the best person in your class, you might say you are the end of the year.

In this sense, an end is the finally part of something. It is the last step before you can move on to the next part of life. It is the point where you stop doing one thing and start doing another.
