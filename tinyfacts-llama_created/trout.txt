A small animal that lives in water. It is often green or brown when it is small. When it is bigger, it is usually red or white. It has a round body that is long and strong. It has four legs and can jump a lot. It likes to run in the water and climb out on rocks.

Trails are long lines of ground that people walk on. Some are made of wood. Some are made of rock. Some are made of cold stuff. People walk these lines to see the world. They go through fields, over small pieces of water, and past tall trees.

Trains are big things that carry many people at once. They go very fast. You can sit in a seat next to someone and look out the window at the ground going by. You can also sit in a seat far away from the other people and read a book or watch a movie. Some trains have tables where you can eat and drink. Some trains go very far away from where you started. Others go closer to your city.

The word comes from the sound of running water. You can hear it when you are near a big body of water. It is a sound that makes you want to move.
