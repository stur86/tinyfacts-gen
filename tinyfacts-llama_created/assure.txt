To be sure something is true means to show that it is real. You can be sure that the sun will come up tomorrow. You can also be sure that a friend is there. If you say you are sure something, you are saying it is real and you trust it.

You can also be sure of a fact. If you learn something new, you might be sure that it is true. This is because you have seen it with your own eyes. Being sure is important when you are trying to act. If you are sure you will do the right thing, you are more ready to act.

Being sure can be hard. Sometimes, you might wonder if you are right. You might feel afraid. But if you can be sure, you can feel more safe. Being sure is like having a rock under your feet. It helps you feel ready to face what is happening.
