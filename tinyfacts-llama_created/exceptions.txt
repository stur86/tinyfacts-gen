An eventually happens. But if you do something wrong, the person telling you can still say, you did it. This means you are the one who made the wrong move. You are not the one who made the other person do something they did not want to do.

Think of a room. You are in the room. You are not the one who made the door open. You did not tell the person in the room to open the door. You just walked in. The room is happening because of what happened before you got there. You did not make it happen. You just followed what had already happened.
