To use something to make a picture or a picture of something is to use it to make a new one. This is how you make lots of pictures at once.

You need something that can cut and hold things together. You fill it with the picture you want. Then you press it on paper. The paper has lines in it that go around the picture. When the paper is pressed, the lines cut the paper into tiny pieces. Each piece is the picture you want.

The person who does this job is called a picture maker. They make many pictures at once. They can make a picture of a person, a dog, a car, a house, and many other things. They can also make a picture of a group of people. This way, you have many pictures all at once.

It is very fast. You can make a picture in a few minutes. This is why people use it to send pictures to friends. It is also good for people who have a lot of work. If you make a picture of a group of people, you do not have to write all of them down. You can send one picture with all of them on it.

Sometimes, people use the word this way to mean something is very good. If a picture is made by a person who is very good at this job, you can say it is this way.
