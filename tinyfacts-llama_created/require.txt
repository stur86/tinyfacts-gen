To need something means you want it very much. You do not have it, but you want it. If you are very cold, you need a warm thing to keep you safe.

If you have a thing, you need it to do your work. You do not do your work if you do not have the thing. If you have a book, you can read it. If you do not have the book, you can not read it.

When you need something, you must tell others. You say, I need a drink. The person gives it to you. If you did not need it, the person would not give it to you.

Sometimes, you need a thing because you are afraid. You do not want to go to a place that makes you feel bad. You need to go there. You say, I need to go to the doctor.

You can also need something because you want to do something. You might want to go to a game with your friends. If you do not have money, you need to get money. You tell your mom, I need money for the game.
