A group of people who help decide how things work. They listen to many different voices and then make a plan. They try to keep everyone happy.

These people are usually picked by the city, town, or state. They do not get to decide on their own. They are only here to help everyone. They help fix things that are broken, pay people who have done wrong, and make sure everyone follows the same way.

They have a leader. This person is very important. They help the group make good plans and keep everyone in line. They also help pick a new leader for the group if they decide they want one.

Sometimes, these people help fix a big problem. They talk with many people, read what they have to say, and then decide what to do. They try to fix things that are very wrong.

In some places, the group is made by the people who live there. They get to decide on their own how things work. But in many places, the group is made by the city, state, or town. They do not get to decide on their own.
