To put something together means you take small pieces of something and put them into one place. You might use this word to talk about how you do your work.

Imagine you make a picture with paint. You start with a small piece of paper. Then you add more paint to it. Next, you add a small piece of paper on top of the first one. This is like putting something together. You are taking small parts and putting them into one place so they look nice and strong.

You can also use this word to talk about how people feel. When you are happy, you might feel a lot of warm inside. You might put a small piece of paper around your chest. This helps you feel happy. It is like putting something inside you.

Sometimes, you put things together to make something new. You take old paper and you cut it into small pieces. Then you put these pieces into a small box. Inside the box, you can put many small pieces of paper. When you open the box, you have many small papers inside. You made them all together using the old paper.
