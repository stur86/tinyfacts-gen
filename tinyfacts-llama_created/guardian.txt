A person who looks after something is called a guard. They keep the door closed so no one can enter. They may also watch the street at night so no bad people come.

Another kind of guard is one who looks after a house. They make sure the front door is open. They see who comes and who goes. They might also check the windows so no one is in the house.

A very important kind of guard is one who is very strong. They might be a police man. They help keep people safe. They also help if someone is hurt. They are a good thing to have.

There is also a person who is a guard of ideas. They keep the things that are right. They help keep the world in order. They tell people what to do and what not to do. They are very important for keeping the world going.
