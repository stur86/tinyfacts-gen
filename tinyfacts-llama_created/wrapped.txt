To wrap something is to cover it with a piece of something. This can be a long thing, a small thing, or even a small ball of air. The thing you wrap is usually a thing that you want to keep warm or keep safe.

Imagine you have a small ball of air. You can put the ball in a box to keep it safe. You put the box over the ball. Then you can put a long piece of paper over the box. The paper is wrapped around the box. Now the box and the paper are one thing. You can hold the box and the paper together so the air inside does not come out.

You can also wrap a thing that is too heavy to hold up. You wrap the thing with a piece of clothes or paper. The clothes or paper hold the thing up so it does not fall down. This helps keep the thing safe and makes it look nice.

Sometimes you wrap a thing that is too big to put in a box. You put the thing in a long bag made of hard stuff. The bag is wrapped with many long pieces of clothes. The bag is so big that it can hold many bags inside it. This way you can keep many of the same things together in one place.

A person can also wrap their hands around a thing to keep it safe. If a person is afraid of a thing, they can put their hands around it. This helps the person feel safe and less afraid.
