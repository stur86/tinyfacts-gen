Your lips are the parts on the top of your face. They are the round things that you use to speak and to show how you feel.

Your lips are very important. When you are happy, your lips are up and your face is bright. When you are sad, your lips are down and your face looks like you are about to cry. This is because your body is sending a sign that you are feeling a strong feeling.

Your lips are also the parts you use to eat food. You put food in your mouth, and then you use your lips to move it around. Your lips are very good at this. They are like tiny little hands that you can use to do many different things.

People use their lips to say many different words. When you say a word, your lips move. Your lips move up and down, side to side, and round. This is how you make sounds that we can hear.
