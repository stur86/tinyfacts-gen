Explain The Word Expects

When you expect something, you think it will happen right now. You might expect a phone call from a friend. You feel happy and a little nervous. You are ready to talk.

A person might expect to eat a good food for dinner. They have been looking forward to it all day. They might feel a little bit angry if they do not get what they want. This is an important word because it shows how much you care about a thing.

Expects can also mean you are waiting for something to happen. You might wait for a friend to arrive. You are waiting for the morning. You are waiting for the food to be ready.

Sometimes, you might expect a surprise. You do not know what it is until it happens. You are surprised and happy. This is a good thing because it makes life exciting.

You can also expect to get a good deal. You look at the papers. You see that the store is offering a good buy. You feel happy because you got a good deal. You are glad that you were able to get what you want.

In some ways, the word expects is also about how you feel. When you expect something, you are using your brain and your heart. You are making plans for what is to come. This is an important word because it helps us understand how we act.

To expects something is to be ready and to have hope. It is about being strong and about being sure that what happens will be good.
