To flip something means you change its position. Imagine you have a small thing that hangs from a line. If you push it gently, it moves to the other side. If you push it harder, it goes all the way around and lands on the other side.

A person who does this is called a flipper. They use their arms to push and pull the thing. It helps them move it from one place to another without using their hands.

You can also flip a thing that is on the ground. If you push it, it goes up in the air. It falls down again and lands in another spot. This is how animals move when they jump. Even small things like a ball can be flipped by a person.

In a kitchen, you can flip food. You might put a round food thing in a hot pants. When it starts to brown, you use a long thing to turn it over. This makes it look nice and makes it ready to eat.
