To build something with small, hard pieces is to use adder. You put these pieces into a wet ground. The wet ground makes the pieces hard. Then, you put more wet ground over the hard pieces. When this is done many times, the ground is strong and holds a lot. It makes a hard, brown rock. This rock can hold water and keep it inside. It is very good for making houses and walls. People also use it for making things that are for the fire. It does not burn easily like the wood from trees. It keeps the fire going for a long time.
