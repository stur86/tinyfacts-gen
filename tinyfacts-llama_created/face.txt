To face something means you look at it. You do not turn away. You are looking at it straight.

Imagine you are on a big field. You want to see what is behind you. You stand up straight and look at what is behind. You do not hide your eyes. You see everything that is in front of you. This is facing.

You can also face a person. If you see a friend, you might face them. You are looking at them. You are talking to them and they are talking to you. You do not run away. You stay where you are and listen to what they say.

Sometimes, you might face a hard thing. This is when something is in front of you that you do not want to see. You might be afraid of it. But you still look at it. You look at the wall or at the ground. You do not turn away. You keep your eyes on the thing until it goes away.

In some places, you have to face the sun. The sun is up in the sky. You stand still and look at it. You feel the warm light on your skin. This is facing the sun.
