To be in a state of change can be very hard to do. You might start with one thing and then you change into another thing. The world is like this too. Everything is always moving.

Think of a ball. At first, it is sitting still on the floor. But then, it starts to roll. It goes faster and faster. It keeps going in a straight line until it hits the ground. After it hits the ground, it starts to roll again, but in a different direction. It keeps going, always moving.

This is what happens with people, with animals, and with things that are very big. They all change over time. You can see this happening in your own life. One day you are in school and you learn something. The next day you are playing a game with your friends. The next day you are at home with your family.

Things can also be in different states at the same time. A light can be bright and hot, but it can also be dark and cold. This is because the light is changing from one state to another. This is how the world works.

Sometimes we try to keep things in one place. We want to stay in one moment in time. But life is not like that. Things move and change all the time. It is better to learn to let go of our need to stay in one place. When we learn to do this, we can feel more at home.
