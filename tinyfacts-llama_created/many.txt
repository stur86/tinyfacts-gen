Many is a word that tells how many things are there.

Imagine you have a lot of books. You have many books in your room, in your car, and even in your bag. This means there are many books.

You also have many friends. You are friends with many people. You are friends with your family, your school friends, and your friends from work.

If you have a very big number of things, you can say you have many of them. This number is often very high. If you have one hundred many things, then you have one hundred many things!

You can also use many to talk about things that are not so many. If you are feeling many sad, you are feeling a lot of sad. This means you are very sad and it is hard to stop.

In some cases, many can mean too many. If you have many people and you do not know how to handle them, it might be a problem. You need to find a way to make sure everyone is safe and happy.
