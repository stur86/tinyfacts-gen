To month something means to take a long time to do it. You can think of it like taking a few weeks, but for a long time.

When you are a baby, you are in a room with many other babies. You are not alone. There are other babies who are very young too. They are all in the same room. You are all in the same house. You are all in the same time. This is a month.

A month is a big number. It is much bigger than a week, but much smaller than a year. If you have a month, you have one whole time that has passed. You can tell how many days are in it. You can counters each day and see how the days go.

Months can be very different. In the summer, the days are warm. In the cold months, the days are dark. In the months with the most days, there are many days. There are many weeks. In the months with the fewest days, there are only a few days. You might feel happy in the warm months and sad in the cold months.

Some months are very important. The first month is called the first month. We start this month when we begin a new year. We use the first month to remember the first day of the year. Other months are important too. You might use one of them to plan a trip, to buy food, or to do work.

People often move from one month to another. In the month with the warm days, people might go to the city to play. In the month with the cold days, people might stay home to rest. They might do things they enjoy before the month ends.
