An office is a place that many people work in. It is often where big groups of people make sure everyone follows the same way. If you are a student, your school is an office. You work there to do your work and to follow the school plans. You do this job by following the plans that the teachers write.

The word we use for these places is a bit different. When we talk about a group of people who work in an office, we say they are in an office. If someone works in an office, it can mean they are the ones who do the work. If you are the one who says what to do, it is also an office.

If there is a person who is the top person in the group, we call that office a high office. There is also a very high office, like a head of a big city. This person is the one who makes sure everyone follows the city plans.

Sometimes, an office can be a place where people are not always in their usual jobs. They might be there to talk or to help someone. In these cases, the word is used to show that the person is there for a reason, even if they are not doing their work job.

In some places, the word is used for a man who works in a big building. This person might be the one who cleans the office. They are the ones who keep everything clean so the office does not become a place for bad things to happen.

When people talk about offices, they often use words that mean they are at a high place. They are very important. They help keep everything moving.
