This is a word that we use when we talk about things that are right in front of us. It tells us where something is. If you look at your hand, you are looking at this. If you look at a book, you are looking at this. This word is also used to talk about time. You might say, this hour is very late. Or you might say, this is the time when we eat dinner.

Sometimes, this word can be a bit confusing. Imagine you are walking down the street. You see a big car. You might feel a bit scared. If you say, this is a car, you are talking about the car. But if you say, this is a building, you are talking about the building. Your mind is doing both things at the same time.

This word is very important because it helps us talk about the world around us. It makes sure we know where things are and when things happen.
