To break something means to use it until it is no longer safe to use anymore. When you break a thing, you are making it go very wrong. You might drop it and it falls on the floor. You might hit it with your hand and the wood or glass breaks into many small pieces.

If you are a person, you can break your own heart. If you love someone very much, you might feel sad because you want to keep them close. But when you are too sad, you get angry and you want to hit them. You are using your body to try to fix the problem. You are trying to make things right.

You can also break money. If you spend too much money, you can run out of money. This is a bad thing because you do not have enough money to buy what you need.

Sometimes, you might break a promise. If you say you will do something, and then you do not do it, you broke your word. You did not follow what you said, and this is not okay.

To break something is to let it go. You let go of the thing that was hurting you or the money that you needed. You are letting the problem go away and you can move on to a new thing.
