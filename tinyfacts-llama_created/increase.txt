To show an idea is bigger or more than what you already have is called to be bigger. You are in a room and there are many people. If you sit in a corner, you are smaller. If you walk to the other end of the room, you are bigger. You are doing something that makes you larger.

You can also use this word to talk about things that go up. When you look at the sky, you are looking at the top. If the top is higher than the bottom, the top is bigger. The sky is bigger than your house.

Sometimes, you might feel this way. If you are sad, your heart feels bigger. You feel more than you can handle. If you are happy, your face feels smaller. You feel like everything is right.

You can also say that something is going up. Imagine you have a ball and you throw it very far. The ball goes higher and higher. It is going up. You are moving your body in a way that makes the ball go bigger into the air.
