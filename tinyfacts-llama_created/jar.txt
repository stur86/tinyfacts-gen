A thing that holds food and drinks is called a bottle. You can put food inside it. When you open it, the food comes out. You can also put wet stuff in it and it holds it. You can drink water from it.

Another kind of bottle is made from a hard stuff. You can put things like food and drinks inside it. When you open it, the air comes out. This bottle is good for very hot days because the air goes out so you do not get too hot.

There is also a thing that holds things. You can put books, papers, and other things in it. It keeps them safe and you can find them when you need them.
