A long time ago, people used to walk across a wide water to get to a far away place. They would pay to cross the water. This was a hard way to go because the water was very strong and the water was very deep. People who had money could pay a little bit of money each time they crossed the water. This money was like a small piece of paper that said they paid for the right to cross.

When you are on a road that goes to a far away place, you often pay a small feeling. This is like a small piece of paper that the person who owns the road pays to let you cross. This is why you say you have to pay a feeling to cross a road.
