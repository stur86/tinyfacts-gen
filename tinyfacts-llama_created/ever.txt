The word ever is a word that has many uses.

When we talk about time, ever means something that happens all the time. This is true for days, weeks, months, and years. Every day after the first day is another day. Every week after the first week is another week. Every month after the first month is another month. Every year after the first year is another year. This word helps us understand how very long something is.

You can also use ever to talk about things that stay the same over time. If you wear the same clothes every day for many years, you are wearing the same clothes ever. The way things look is always the same for you. You stay the same person even if you change your hair, your clothes, or your face.

Sometimes we use ever to talk about things that happen many times. If you pick a piece of food many times, you are eating ever food. You are always eating something. This word can help you remember things you have done many times.

Another way we use ever is in the word you ever. When you say you ever do this, you mean you do this very often. You do it so many times that it feels like you are always doing it.

So, ever is a word that helps us understand how things change over time and how many times something happens.
