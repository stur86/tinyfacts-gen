To be a person is not the same as to have a job. Having a job means you do things for money. You go to a place and you do work. You are not in a class with other people, and you do not get to sit with them. You work alone.

To be a person is also about what you do. You might be a doctor. You help people who are sick. You give them things that make their bodies feel better. You do this job every day and for many years. You learn how to do it and you become very good at it.

Some people are in a job for many years. They see many people. They know every face. They know every story. They know how each person feels. They can help people when they need it.

Other people do not work in a place. They do work. They go to a school. They do a job. They write things that people read. They tell stories. They make things that people buy. They do not have to go to a place every day. They do not have to sit with other people. They are alone with their work.

You can be a person and you can be a job. You can be a person who works in a school and you can also be a person who works in a hospital. Both are important. Both help people.
