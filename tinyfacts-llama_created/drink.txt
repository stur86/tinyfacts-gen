To drink something is to take a big mouth full of it. You put the drink into your mouth and close your lips around it. Your body then takes in the water, the white stuff from the food you eat, or the sweet stuff from a drink.

When you drink, you feel something. You might feel a bit full in your tight side. You might feel a bit warm. You might feel a bit like you are going to laugh. Sometimes, when you drink, you want to drink more. You want to have more of the drink inside you.

People drink for many reasons. Some drink to feel happy. Some drink to feel calm. Some drink to feel strong. Some drink to be quiet. Some people drink when they are tired. Other people drink when they are sad. Some people drink when they are with friends.

You can drink water, cold drink, or sweet drink. You can drink many drinks in one hour. But some drinks are not good to drink. Some drinks can make your body feel bad. You should drink what is safe for you.
