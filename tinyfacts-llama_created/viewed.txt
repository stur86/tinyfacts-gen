To see something is when you look at it with your eyes. You use your eyes to understand what is in front of you. This is how you see your family, your friends, your home, or a beautiful place.

When you look at a picture, you are seeing it. You see the lines, the colors, and the pictures of people. If you look at a book, you are seeing the words. You read the words and try to understand what they say.

You can also see things that are not in front of you. If you look at the wall in your room, you are seeing the wall. If you look at the sky, you are seeing the big blue above you. If you look at the stars, you are seeing the tiny lights in the sky.
