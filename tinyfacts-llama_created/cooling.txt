To cool something means to make it not hot anymore. When the sun is very strong, the air around us gets very hot. If you walk outside in this hot air, your skin will feel very bad. The ground and the walls of your house get very hot too. This makes the air around you feel heavy and makes you feel tired.

When something is hot, it has a lot of power inside it. But when you put water or ice on it, the power inside it goes away. This makes the hot thing feel less strong. It is like the hot thing is giving up its power and becoming smaller.

Water is a good way to cool things. You can put a cup of water on your hand when you are hot. The water will make your hand feel better. You can also put ice in a cup and drink it. The cold water will make your hand feel nice and calm.

The ground and the walls of a house can also cool things down. When it gets hot, the ground gets hot too. If you put a box over the hot ground, the hot ground will feel less strong and the air next to it will not feel so heavy. This makes the area feel nicer to stay in.

A car also cools the air inside it. When the car is moving, it makes the air inside the car move faster. This makes the air feel cooler because the hot air gets pushed out of the car and new air comes in. This helps keep the car not too hot for the people inside.
