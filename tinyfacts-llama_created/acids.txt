An across is a thing that does two different things at once. When you cut a piece of wood, the wood is an across. The cut part is hard and strong. The part that is left is soft and broken.

An across can also be a word for things that are bad for you. When you breathe in bad air, you are breathing in an across. The bad air can make your body feel sick. You might feel hot and your head hurts.

Some things are across too. An across can mean you are in two places at once. If you are in a room, you can be an across and still be inside. You are not in one place, but in both at the same time.
