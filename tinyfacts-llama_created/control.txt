To control something means you are in a place where you can decide what happens. You have power over a thing that can move, a thing that can say, a thing that can make things happen.

Imagine you are playing a game with a ball. If you throw the ball to your friend, you are controlling it. Your friend is doing what you say. You are the one who decides where the ball goes and who gets it.

In a school, a teacher might control a group of people. The teacher is the one who decides what to do, who says what to say, and who does what. The teacher is the one who keeps everyone safe and makes sure the work is done.

Sometimes, you might have a car. The car is a thing that moves around on the ground. You are the one who controls the car. You decide where to go, how fast to go, and when to stop. You are the one who gets to decide how the car works.

Being in a place of control is important. It helps you feel safe and in control of what happens.
