Head lines are the very first words of a big story in a paper or a computer thing that everyone sees at the top of the paper. They tell you the most important thing about what is happening.

Most of the time, the first line of a story is the one that the person writing wants to tell the most. They might say a few words that are very important, or they might write a few words that are a little funny, or they might write words that are very sad. This is the part of the story that everyone wants to know.

The person writing the story often uses big words to show they are important. They write words like big words, words that are very strong. They also use words that are easy to understand, so everyone can read it.

The head line is usually the one that is most important. If you read the story, you might see the head line first. If you see the head line, you know that the rest of the story is about this one thing very much.

People also write head lines to make the story seem bigger than it really is. If a story is about something small, the person writing it might make it seem like it was a very big eventually. They might add words like, the biggest thing ever happened, the biggest fight, or the biggest fight ever. This makes the story seem more exciting and important.

Head lines can also be the start of a story about a person. If you read about someone who did something very strange, the head line might say, the man who did that. This shows you are about to learn about a person.

Finally, head lines can be about something that is happening right now. If you see a story about a new movie coming out or a new game coming out, the head line might say, today a new game is coming out. This lets you know what is happening in the world right now.
