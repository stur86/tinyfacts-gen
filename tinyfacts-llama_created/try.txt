To try something means you do it to see if it works. You do the thing before you ask if it is right. If it does not work, you might try again.

Think of a child who wants to fly a small thing. They do not fly it right the first time. They try again and again. They move the thing faster and faster. They change the way they push it. They watch how it moves. They are trying to see if the thing flies. If it does not fly, they try again. This is how you try things in life too.

You can also try to do something you are afraid of. You do not go into a dark room because you are afraid of what might happen. You stand in the room and look at it. Then you decide to go in. You take a deep breath and walk into the room. You try to be strong. You try to not be afraid. You are trying to see if you can do it.
