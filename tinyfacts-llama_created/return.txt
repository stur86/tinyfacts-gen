To return something means you give it back to the person who gave it to you. You might do this when you are playing with a ball. You throw it to a friend. The friend catches the ball. Then you can start playing with the friend.

You can also return something when you pay for it. Imagine you buy a book. You pay for it with your money. Later, you need the book to read. So, you go back to the store and give the money back. The store gives you the book.

Sometimes, when you are very tired, you might close your eyes. When you wake up, you feel better. This is a return to your body and mind.

You can also return something if you are not happy with it. If you buy a thing you think is not good, you might return it. The store gives you the money back. This is a way to fix a problem so you can be happy.

In life, you can return to a place you loved when you are old. You might go back to your home to see your family. You can also return to a job you loved when you are older.
