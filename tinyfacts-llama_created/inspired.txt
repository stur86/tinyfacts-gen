When we say a thing was made with love, we mean it was made by a person who cared about it. This person did not just throw things together like a person who wants to make money. They took a piece of wood or a line of paper and put something good in it. They put their heart into it. This makes the thing feel real and important.

We can feel this when we see a painting made by a person who loves to draw. We feel the love in the way the colors are put together and the way the lines are cut. We feel like the painting is speaking to us. We are not just looking at the picture. We are hearing the story it tells.

Sometimes, people are made with love. A child might be made from wood. A dog might be made from animal hair. A person might be made from the blood of a father and mother. This is why we call them living things. They are not just things that do not stop. They are living things that feel and grow.

We can also be made with love by being friends with someone. When you like a person, you feel happy inside. You want to be with them. You want to hear their voice and see their smile. This is a good thing. It is a sign that you care about them and that they care about you.

Sometimes, we are made with love by being part of something. A fire burns because it needs air to stay going. A heart beats because it needs love to stay strong. A work of music is made by many people, but the music stays the same because it is made with love.
