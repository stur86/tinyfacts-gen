To be sure means you are very sure that something is happening or that a job is done. You have no doubts left in your mind.

Imagine you are in a room with a friend. You are sure the door is closed, but you hear a sound coming from outside. You are sure it is not the door. You are sure you are safe. You can feel safe now.

If you are sure something is true, you do not have any questions left. You know it is so. You are very sure.

You can also be sure about your body. If you are very sure of your body, you trust your body. You do not worry about a thing.

You can also be sure about a plan. If you know what you are going to do next, you are sure you will do it. You are sure you will be safe. If you are sure about a plan, you feel strong and sure.

Sometimes, we are sure of the past. We are sure who people were, what happened, and how things are. When we are sure of the past, we can feel calm.

But being sure is not always easy. Sometimes we are sure, but then we see something that makes us question. We might see a bad thing that makes us feel very afraid. Or we see a friend we love, and we feel sad. When we see that, we must be sure again. We must question what we thought before.
