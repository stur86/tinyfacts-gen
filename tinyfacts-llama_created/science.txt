To study something is to look at it. When people say a thing is a thing, they are not talking about how it looks. They are talking about how it is made. How it works. How it grows.

You can study the air. You can look at how the white stuff in the sky changes. You can look at the ground. You can look at the water. You are learning about the world.

You can also study animals. You look at how they move. You look at how they eat. You look at how they live. This is important. It helps us know how to take care of the world.

Sometimes you study people. You look at how they think. You look at how they feel. You look at how they act. This helps us understand how we are and how we can change.

We also study the world around us. We look at the sun. We look at the stars. We look at the world in space. This is important because it helps us understand how the world works and how we can live with it.

To study something is to ask questions. It is to want to know more. It is to want to see how it works. It is to want to learn. When we study, we are not just looking at something. We are learning about the world and about how we fit into it.
