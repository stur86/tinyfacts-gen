To guard something means to keep it safe from bad people. You might use a box to keep your food safe. You close the box and make sure it is very hard to open. If someone tries to break in, you will be the one to stop them.

When you are in a place where you do not want to be, you might feel scared. You might want to run away or hide. But you do not let the bad people get you. You stand up and say, I am not going to let you do this! You are the one who is safe now.

You can also say you are guarding your family. You help them when they are afraid. You keep them safe from things that might hurt them.

If you are the one who is afraid, you can also use the word to mean you are not afraid of something. You are ready to fight if you need to. You are very strong and sure that you can do it.

Sometimes, you might feel like the world is against you. But if you stand up and say, I am here, I am not going to let you hurt me, then you are the one who is strong.
