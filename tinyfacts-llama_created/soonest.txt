The word soon is a word for time.

When you say a thing will happen, you are talking about the time after it happens. You say it is soon. This means you are looking forward to when it will happen.

Think of a tree. A tree grows every year. If you look at a picture of a tree, you see how old it is. If you want to know how old the tree is now, you can ask how old it is. If you know it is going to be a month, then you know it is going to be soon.

You can use this word with people too. If you say you will meet your friend soon, you mean you will see them soon. If you say you will be home soon, you mean you will come home soon.

But you also use it with things. If you say a thing is soon, you mean it will be ready soon. If your book is soon to be read, it means the book is going to be read soon.
