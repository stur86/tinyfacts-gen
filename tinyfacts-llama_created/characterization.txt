To give an animal a name is easy. You look at its face, and you know it is a cat, a dog, or a horse. But a name is not just a word. It is a picture of what the animal is like inside. It shows how the animal acts, what it does, and how it feels.

Think about a small child. You name him after his father. You did not just name him because he looked like his father. You did it because he acts like his father. He is kind. He smiles. He looks at his face. This is the name you gave him. This is how you see him.

In stories, the person who tells the story gives a name to the people. A person who is very good might be named a good man. A person who is very bad might be named a bad man. This helps us understand who is doing what. We see the world through their eyes. We see the good, the bad, and everything in between.

When you write a story, you also use names. You use names to show who is who. You name the people in the story so that the reader can understand them. You name the food they eat. You name the places they live. You name the things they do. This makes the story more real. It makes the reader feel like they are there.

A movie is a different thing. It is not just a set of pictures. It is a story with a beginning, a middle, and an end. The people in the movie act like the people in your story. They do things that are the same, but they do not act like the people in your story. They act like the people in your story. They act like they are you. This makes you feel like you are in the story.

In this way, names are not just words. They are pictures of how we see the world. They show us who we are. They help us understand the world and how it works.
