A small red or green thing from the ground is called a tiny round thing. We eat it. It is sweet and makes our faces happy when we see it.

It comes from a tree. The tree makes many of these tiny round things. The tree grows on a long thing that is hung from a tall place in the ground. The red part of the tiny round thing is the top of the tree. The green part is the middle, and the bottom is the bottom of the tree. The top part is the top of the tree. The middle part is the green middle of the tree. The bottom part is the bottom of the tree.

These tiny round things grow on the tree. When the tree is old and the green parts are gone, the tiny round things start to grow. They turn red and then green and then red again. When they are red, they are ready to eat. We pick them, cut them, and put them in a bag to eat later.

People like to eat these tiny round things with other food. They are a good food. They are easy to eat and they give us a little bit of the tree.
