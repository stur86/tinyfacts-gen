To search means you look for something. You might look in a box, a room, a street, or even the sky. You want to find what you need. You might not know where it is at first, but you keep looking until you see it.

Sometimes you want to find a word. You have a question and you want to know the answer. You look at books, talk to people, and read the words. You are looking for the word.

You can also search for a person. If you are looking for your friend, you look in the phone book, the school, and the streets. You ask the people you know if they know where she is. You keep looking until you find her.

To search is to go through many things. You start with a small thing. As you go, you see more things. You learn about the world and about yourself. It is a way to learn and to grow.

You can also search for a feeling. If you are sad, you might go to a park. You look at the trees, the ground, and the sky. You want to feel better. You are trying to find a way to make your heart feel happy again.
