To make something more confusing is to add more things to it. When you do this, the thing becomes harder to understand. It is a very big word, but it is easy to use.

To start, a thing can be very clear. It is just what it is. If you say a dog is a dog, you are done. This word is short and easy to remember.

But then, you add more things. You tell it is a dog with a long face. You tell it is a dog with a point on its nose. You tell it is a dog that can run fast. This makes the word much bigger. Now, you have many more questions. What does it mean to have a long face? What is a point on the nose? What is fast?

The word is like this. You start with a clear word. You add many more words. Each word has its own meaning. And then, you have many more words. You are left with many questions.

It is like trying to remember a lot of numbers. You start with one number. You add two numbers. Then, you add three. Then, you add four. Each number has its own meaning. And then, you have many more numbers. You are left with many questions.

A very long time ago, people did this with words. They were very good at it. They added words that were very hard to understand. They made words that were very confusing. Then, people learned to stop. They said, now we know how to be clear. We do not add more words. We only take what is already there and put it on paper.

Today, the word is still used. But the meaning has changed. We talk about things that are very hard to understand. We talk about things that are very sad. We talk about things that are very big. The word is still there, but we do not add more words as we did in the past.
