To make something move

To make something move is to give it power so it can go. You can use this word for many things. You might see a car or a dog. If a car is made to move, it is called a car. If a dog is moved from one place to another, it is moved.

You can also talk about something that makes things move. A small thing with many lines inside it can move. This is why a group of people can walk together. They are all moving on the same line. When many people move together, they are making a big line.

In a computer, a small part can make a thing move. This part is inside the computer and helps the computer think and do things. If you open a picture, the computer is making the picture move so you can see it.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about how things change. If a thing is made to move, it changes. It becomes something new.
