The word disappears is the same as the word disappear. But, in a different way. When you see something, it is there. But if you want to see it no more, it is gone.

You can use the word for a person. If you are afraid of a dog, you are afraid. If you are afraid of a big thing, you can say you are afraid of it. This word is about how you feel.

You can also use it with things. If a thing is gone, it is gone. You can not see it anymore. If a game is over, it is over. You do not have to wait to see what happens next.

Sometimes, the word is used with numbers. If you counters something, you are taking it away from the thing that comes next. You are not letting it stay. This word is about taking away what is already there.

In the end, the word disappears is about how things go away from us.
