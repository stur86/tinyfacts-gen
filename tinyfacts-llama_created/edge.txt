The word edge is something that is at the end of something. It is the part that is next to a wall, a floor, a table, or a car. It is the part of a thing that is not part of the middle or the back.

Imagine a line. The line is the middle part of the paper. The edge is the part of the paper that is next to the line. It is the part that is not part of the line.

If you look at a picture, the edge is the part that is not part of the picture. It is the line that goes around the picture and makes it bigger.

It can also mean a very small part of something. A small edge is a tiny edge on a thing that is not very big. It is like a tiny line on a paper.

Sometimes, an edge is used to talk about people. If someone is on the edge of something, they are right on the edge. If something is on the edge of being safe, it means it is very close to falling in.
