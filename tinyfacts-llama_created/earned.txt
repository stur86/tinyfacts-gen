To have something is a way to show that you are ready. If you work hard, you can get money. You work because you want to be able to buy what you need. If you do not work, you are not ready, and you have nothing.

Sometimes, people get something from others. If you are nice, the person might give you a small thing. This can be a book, a game, or even a funny story. When you are nice, you make others happy, and you feel happy too. You have something that you did not have before.

You can also have something because you have done well. If you are a great player, the team gives you a big number on the back of your shirt. You are the best, and you feel very happy. You have something that shows you are the best at what you do.

Sometimes, you get something because you wait. If you wait for a long time, the day comes. You have been waiting for a very long time, and now the day is here. You feel very happy because you were ready.

To have something is always better than to have nothing. If you have nothing, you are lost. But if you have something, you feel ready and strong.
