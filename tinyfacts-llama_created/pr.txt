The word practically means you do things that are real. When you learn how to run, you start to run. You do it every day. Your legs move under you. You feel the ground. You also learn how to eat food. You know what food is good for your body. You might drink water or eat a full breakfast.

This word is also used when you do things that make you feel good. You might feel happy when you play with a friend. You laugh and run around. You feel like you are safe. You are not afraid of anything.

It is not just about doing things. It is also about how you act. If you are a teacher, you act like you care about your students. You listen and help them understand. You are not afraid to show your face if you are sad. You want people to know that you are there for them.

In short, the word practically means you act like you are living your life.
