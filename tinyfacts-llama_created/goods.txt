Goods are things that people buy and keep for their own use. They are things you need to live or work. They are not things that change your life like a person does.

Think about food. You need food to stay living. If you do not eat, you die. Food is a good. You buy food to eat. You keep food in your house.

You also buy clothes and shoes. These are good because you wear them every day. You keep your clothes clean and look nice. They help you feel well.

Sometimes you buy things you like to play with. You play with a ball or a book. You keep the ball in your hand or the book on your table. These are good because they make you happy.

You can also buy things that help you do your work. A computer is a good. It helps you write papers and do your jobs. You buy computers to work.

A good is something that helps you live or work. It is not a thing that changes your life like a person does. It is a thing that you keep in your life to help you feel good.
