A thing is made up of many parts. Each part does its own job. When we talk about things, we use words to say how they are made.

A thing can be many different kinds. You can be a person. You can be a car. You can be a tree. You can be a house. You can be a book. You can be a picture. Each of these things has a different job.

When a thing is very old, the parts that make it up change over time. The wood in a tree is different from the wood in a chair. The hard stuff in a rock is different from the soft stuff in a soft thing. This changes the way everything looks and works.

Sometimes, things can have many many many more parts than one. A huge building has many floors. A big animal has many legs. A group of people is many many many more than one person. This is why we use words like many and a lot.

Things also change when they get older. As you get older, you might feel a bit different. Your legs might not feel as strong. Your hair might become shorter. But everything that happened before you were a baby stays with you.
