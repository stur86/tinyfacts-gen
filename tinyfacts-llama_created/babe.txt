To be a baby is a very big change. When you are a baby, you are very small. You are not able to walk or talk. You are only able to feel. Your arms and legs are very soft. You have little hard parts on your head and body.

When you are one year old, you are still a baby. You are not a big kid yet. But you start to walk and talk. You can say many words. You start to understand what people say. You also start to use things you did not know how to use before.

Babies are very full of love. They love to be near the people they love. They love to be held and kissed. They also love to play. They like to move their arms and legs. They like to hear music. They like to look at pictures.

Babies need to eat many times every day. They need to drink a lot of water. They need to rest a lot. They are very quiet. They do not talk like other people. They do not use words like they do when they are older.

Some babies are very small. Some babies are very big. Some babies are in between. Some babies are very strong. Some babies are very slow. Every baby is different.

Babies grow very fast. In just one year, you go from being a baby to being a big kid. It is a very big change.
