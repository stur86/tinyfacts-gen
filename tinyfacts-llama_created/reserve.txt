To save something is to put it away so you can use it later. When you save money for a thing you want, you are putting your money into a place. You do not spend it right away. Instead, you keep it until you need it.

You can also save things for other reasons. Maybe you want to buy a new book. You might put your money into a bag and keep it until the book is ready. This way, you will not have to spend your money before you can buy the book.

Saving is an important thing because it helps you be ready for things that are coming up. If you are worried about something, you can save your money so you are not afraid. This way, you will feel more calm when it happens.

You can also save things that are not money. You might save food you did not eat. You might keep a small piece of paper that says you did something good. These things help you feel safe and make you feel better.
