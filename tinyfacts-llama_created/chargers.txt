To start something with power can be very important. Think about your car. When the car starts moving, it needs a lot of power to move. You put the key in the car and it begins to go. The car needs to get enough power from the power lines before it starts moving. These power lines carry power from a place where many cars live to the car.

A computer is another thing that needs power to work. It needs to get power from the power lines before it starts to do its job. It is like the car, but instead of moving, it does work for you. It makes you things, lets you talk to other computers, and helps you remember things.

Some things need power to stay on. A light in your home needs power from the power lines. A small box in a store also needs power. When the power lines go out, the box is not able to work anymore. If the power goes out for a very long time, things like computers and phones can stop working too.
