To say something in a short way is to explain it quickly. It usually means you do not tell the whole story. You might just say a few words or a short story.

Imagine you are telling a friend how you did something. You might say,  I did a thing, and then you go on to tell them everything. But if you are in a hurry, you could say,  I did a thing. This is a short way to say the whole story.

Sometimes, you say something in a few words because you are afraid of talking for too long. It is like telling a small story quickly when you are not sure if the person wants to hear it.

This word is often used when you want to stop talking so a person can understand what you are saying. You might say,  Can you understand this?  It means you want them to listen.

We also use this word when we talk about things that happen quickly. If something happens in a few minutes, we might say,  It happened quickly. It means the thing happened fast.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If you are angry with someone, you might say,  They are bad. This means they are very bad. It is not the whole story, but it gives you an idea of what happened.
