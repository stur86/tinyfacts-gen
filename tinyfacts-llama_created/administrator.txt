An office manager is a person who keeps things running in a place. They work with many people. They make sure that work goes on without a problem.

Imagine you have a store. You want to keep everything in order. The manager does this. They pick who does what job. They tell the person who does the work where things are. They also tell them if something is wrong.

The manager does not do all the work. They let the people they work with do it. They are like a middle person. They help the people who work with them and the people who work for the store all at once.

They also know about money. They make sure that money is being used well. They might talk to people who are in trouble about money. They might help if someone is going to make a bad job.

Sometimes, they have to make hard calls. They have to decide if a person should get a job. If they say yes, the person starts doing their work. If they say no, the person does not get the job.

The word comes from old times. In old houses, the person who kept things was the one who made sure everything stayed in order. Now, the word is used for many kinds of places. It is about keeping a group running well.
