To be in an office is to be doing things that you agreed to do. It is when you are not watching a person, but you are still part of the group. You have your own desk, but you are not alone. There are other people around you, and they all have their own jobs.

Imagine you are the one who buys the food for the office. You do not just pick food from a store. You go to a store, pick out the food, and then you bring it back to the office. You do this every day. You do not just sit at a table. You go to a store, buy food, and then you go back to the office. You are the one who makes sure that everyone has food to eat.

If you work in a big city, you might be the one who sends money to people who live far away. You use a computer to write the money. You are not the one who actually hands the money. You are the one who sends it, and then the money goes to the people who need it.

An office is also a place where people talk and share ideas. They sit at their desks and use their computers to look at papers and talk to each other. They use words to tell each other how they feel. If someone says something bad, the other people might get angry and talk to them. This is how they work together to make the office run.

Sometimes, people say that something is in an office. It means that it is happening there. You might say, I am in an office. This means you are doing a job, and you are not watching anyone else.
