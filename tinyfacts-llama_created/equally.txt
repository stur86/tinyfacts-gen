When you say something is the same for everyone, you mean it does not favorites one person over another. You can say the word this way if you want to tell a story. Imagine you are talking to a friend. You say,  this is how it happened. Now you are telling everyone the same thing. 

You can also use this word in other ways. If you have many books and you want to know which one to read next, you might say, this one is the most interesting. Here, the word is used because the book is the best and everyone agrees. 

But, if you have a group of friends and they all love the same thing, you might say, this is the favorite game. In this case, the word is not saying one is better than the others. It is just saying one thing is true. It is like saying, this is the way I saw it.
