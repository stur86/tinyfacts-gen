A power line is a long line that carries power from one place to another. It is usually green because it is made of green stuff. You can see it on your table or on the wall in your house.

The power line goes through many big cities and towns. It helps make your home and your school work. It also keeps the lights on in your house, the lights in your park, and the lights on your bus. It is very important for the world.

In the old days, people would carry power in bags. You would carry a bag with power inside it and put it in a box. This box was like a big bag. The power was kept inside the box and the box was held by a person. This was a lot of work.

Then, people came up with a better way. They put the power inside a small box that you carry with you. This box is small and strong. It can carry a lot of power. You can carry it in your pocket or in your bag.

Now, most people have these small boxes. They are like a tiny power line in your pocket. You can carry them with you all day. You can use them to power your phone or your computer. It is a very easy way to have power when you need it.

The word Raising comes from a word that means to make something bigger. When you raise a power line, you make it go higher. This helps the power reach more places.
