To be ready and act like you are going to do it, even if you do not have to.

When you say you will do something, you might promise to do it. But if you do not follow through, you have not actually done it. You might say you will walk to school, but then you do not leave your house.

It is the same for people and things. If you say you will follow a way, but you go on another road, you have not followed. You are not doing what you said.

It is also about what you do when the time comes. If you say you are ready to do a job, but you do not do it, you have not been ready. You did not wait for the right time. You acted like you should be doing it at any time.

You can also say you will do something if you say you want to. You might say, I want to go to the movies. You do not have to go at once. You can wait until the time is right. But if you do not follow through, you have not actually wanted to do it.

Sometimes you are asked to follow a set of things. You might say you are ready to follow the orders of a teacher. You follow what they say, even if you do not like it. You are not the one who decides, but you are ready to do what they want you to do.
