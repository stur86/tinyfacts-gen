To be able to do something means to have the power or the right to do it. When you are able, you can do many things. You can play games, watch movies, read books, or talk to your friends.

To be able to stop something is to take away the power for it to happen. If you do not want something, you can tell the person who made it that it should not work anymore. You can say, I do not want you to make this thing, and this will make it stop.

If you are able to do something, you can also be able to not do it. If you are able to eat food, you should not eat too much. If you are able to go to school, you should stay at home and rest. This helps your body stay safe.

Sometimes, you might feel able to do something, but you are not actually able. You might feel like you should do it, but you are afraid. This is like being able to walk, but you are afraid to move your legs. You might be able to say, I want to go, but you are afraid.

You can also be able to not do something. If you are able to sleep at night, but you are not happy, you are not able to be happy. You might feel sad or angry, but you still have the power to stop those feeling.
