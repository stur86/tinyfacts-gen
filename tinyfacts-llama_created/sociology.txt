To study something is to look at how people act and how they make their world. It is about how we live together and how we use the world we have.

A long time ago, people studied how the big animals lived. They saw how a big cat works and how it keeps its home. This is how they started to study how people live.

Now, people study how we make our world. They look at how we share food, how we play with each other, and how we build our homes. They try to understand how we feel and how we act when we are happy and when we are sad.

The word comes from the word for people. It is about how we are and how we are with each other. It is not just about one group of people. It is about all of us working together.

To study something is to ask many questions. You might ask, What makes a person happy? What is the most important thing to them? What do they do every day? What are their dreams?

This kind of study can help us be a better friend and family. It can help us understand why we act the way we do. It can help us build a world where everyone feels safe and happy.
