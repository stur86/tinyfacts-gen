To up or up is to go higher. When you walk, you go down. You move down to the ground. But if you want to go up, you push yourself up. You use your arms and your legs to go up.

When you use this word, you might think about things that have happened. Maybe you did something good. You felt happy because you did it. You feel like you are doing a great job. You could say, you are up because you did something nice. Or you could say, you feel up because you are feeling strong.

Sometimes, people use this word for things that are not quite right. You might buy a thing that is not good for you. You might buy a food that is not good to eat. You might feel like you should not have bought it. In this case, you are up against the thing. You are against what you bought.

You can also use this word for things that are hard to do. If you have to work hard to learn something, you might feel up. You feel like you are going against the hard thing. When you are up, you are doing something that is very hard.

So, to up means to go higher, to feel good, to do a great job, or to be against something that is not right.
