The word how is used to talk about things that are very small. It can also mean how you feel about something.

To how someone you love you are showing you care. You might do this by being kind to them. You might listen to what they say. You might also be there for them when they are sad. This shows you are there for them.

To how a story is to show the order of what happens. You start with something that happens first. Then you move to what happened next. This keeps going until you reach the end.

People use the word how to talk about their lives. They might say, how I feel about this. This shows how they really feel.

We also use how to talk about things that are very old. How something started is like how how you feel. It tells you what was happening before something happened.

There are also hows with numbers. How many people are in a group can show you how many people are there. This helps you plan things for the day.

The word how can also mean how things work. Think of a car. The car is how a person goes from one place to another. The car has a way of moving. It has a body and a power thing inside it. The body makes the car go. The power thing makes the car move.

Sometimes the word how is used with animals. If you see an animal, you might ask how it is. This is like asking, how are you? The animal might say it is fine. It might say it is scared. It might say it is happy. This shows you care about the animal.

In some places, people also use the word how. They might say, how old are you? This is like asking, how tall are you? It helps us know about a person.
