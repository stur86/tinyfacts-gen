When we say a person is very good at something, we use the word they are good at. This word is about how well a person can do something.

If you are very good at playing a game, you can do it fast and very well. You might even beat another person. But if you are only good at one game, you might not be as good at anything else.

If you are very good at reading, you can understand many books very quickly. You can also read many books very fast. This makes you a very strong reader.

Being very good at something does not mean you are the best. Many people are very good at many things. You might have a very good voice, a very good way to draw, or a very good way to tell stories.

You can be very good at many things. You can be a doctor, a teacher, a writer, a player, or a very good friend. These are all jobs where you use your brain and your body to do your job.
