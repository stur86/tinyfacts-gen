To build something means to start from the beginning and make it from nothing. You start with a plan. You think about how you want it to look and how it will work. Then you pick the parts that will make it happen.

One part is the body. It is the hard stuff that holds everything together. It is made of many small pieces that are all working together. It is like a big box that keeps everything safe and strong.

Another part is the brain. It is the part inside the box that tells the body what to do. It is like the mind of the box. The brain makes sure everything works right and that you can move and think.

A third part is the power box. It is the box that makes the brain work. It is like a big heart that makes everything happen. It takes power from the sun or a power line and sends it to the body.

You also need parts that can hold things. These are the boxes that keep your computer or phone safe. They keep the power inside the box and keep it clean so it does not break.

You also need parts that can tell you things. These are the boxes that help you know if you need to do something. They show you if your body is okay and if you should move or stop.

All of these parts work together to make things that we use every day. They are the building blocks of how we live and work.
