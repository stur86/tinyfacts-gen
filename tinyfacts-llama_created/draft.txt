To draw something means to make it on paper before it is done. You can draw a picture of a cat, a dog, or even a house. You might use a piece of paper, a ball of red stuff, a small thing you make from wood, or a small thing you find in the ground.

When you draw something, you first think about what you want. You decide if it is a big thing or a small one. Then you start to make the lines. Your hand moves from one end to the other. The lines go across the paper. You add more lines. You make the thing start to look like what you wanted.

Sometimes, you do this for a person. You draw a face for a friend or a family person. You might draw their eyes, their hair, their nose, and their mouth. This helps people know who they are.

A group of people can also draw something. They might decide to make a picture of their city, their school, or their town. They use many lines of paper. One person might draw the city and another person might draw the road that goes around it. Many people work together to make the picture look nice and clear.

People use the word to talk about things that are about to happen. If a team is going to play a game, they can say, they are going to draw a plan. They have a plan to do well. If they do not follow the plan, they might not do well at all.

In some places, you might hear people say, they are going to draw a word. They are going to write a word on a piece of paper. They write the word over and over until they get it right. They might use a computer to help them.
