To name something is to tell its name. Every living thing has a name. You name a person, a dog, a tree, a house. When you use this word, you are telling others who the thing is.

A baby is named when its mother gives it a name. She might say, I am going to call this baby Baby. Then, the baby is also called Baby. Later, people might call the baby by another name. The baby might be called Jacketed because the mother is wearing a funny old dress.

In some places, people use these names for days too. They call the day after the sun is up a new day. They might name the day after a story, a game, or a funny thing that happened. This is a way to remember the day and to keep it in their minds.

Sometimes, we name things that are not living. We might name a thing that is very heavy. We might say, This thing is heavy. Or, we might name a thing that is very old. We might say, This old book is a book. These names help us understand what the thing is and how it is named.

Naming can be fun. When you hear a name, you know who the person is. It helps you remember and understand.
