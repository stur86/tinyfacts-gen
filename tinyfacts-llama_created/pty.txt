To form a group is to join many people together to do something. This is how a business, a school, a team in a game, or a group of friends can work together.

Imagine you are a person who loves to read. You want to start a group where many people can read books. You can ask other people to join. They can agree to do this work together. They can decide who does what job and who is in each group. This way, all of them are working toward the same thing.

This can also happen with people in a group of friends. You are part of a group of friends. Together, you do things. You share stories, laugh, and have fun. You are all doing the same things. The word here is about many people coming together.

Another way this happens is when a person starts a group for people with a problem. They need help. They form a group to work on this problem. All the people in the group are doing the same work. They are all working together to find a way.

When you put people together to do something, you are forming a group. This is a way to work together.
