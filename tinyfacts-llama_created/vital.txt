To be important. This word is often used when something is very needed for a person, a place, a thing, or a group of people.

Think about water. Water is very important for your body. You need it to stay warm and to move through your body. Without water, you would not be able to live. This is why the word is so important.

Water is also important for the world. It helps the ground stay in place. It helps keep the air clean. And it is what makes the world look nice. Imagine a big green field with blue water in it. It is a very important thing.

When you talk about something, you are talking about how it is. If something is very important, it is considered a high thing. If something is not very important, it is a low thing. This helps us understand the world.

You can also use this word with people. A very important person might be a teacher, a doctor, or a leader. If someone is important, they are doing a job that is very important for the whole city or the world.

So, being important is about how much something can help or be needed. It is about being a part of something big.
