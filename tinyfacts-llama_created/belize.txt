The land has many different kinds of animals. There are big animals with long hair and small animals with hard skin that you can not kill. You can go to the land and see them. They are very beautiful to watch.

Becomes a place where people go to live is also true. Many people from all over the world come to this land to start a new life. They leave their old homes and families.
