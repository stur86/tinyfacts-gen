A person who is not the one in the top job, but still helps the top person do their job. They might help with problems or answer questions. They can also help keep things going while the top person is away.

Imagine you are in a school. There is a teacher. If a problem comes up, a second person who is very good at it might come and help. They do not have to be the teacher, but they still do very important work.

In the city, there might be a police person. If the police person is not there, another person who is very good at it might come and help. This person helps to keep the people safe until the police person can be there.

In a place where money is not all in one hand, there are people who help each other. A person might be the one who helps if they need money. Another person might be the one who helps if they need food. This is how we help each other when one person is not able to do everything on their own.

When the top person says, you can do it, the people who are not in the top job say, okay, we will do it. They trust the top person to tell them what to do, and the top person tells them. This is how the top person can know that everything is going well.
