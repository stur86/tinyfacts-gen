To somewhere means you are moving from one place to another. You might be walking through a big park, or you are driving a car down a long road. You are going in a direction, and you are not sure where you will end up.

If you say you are going to a place, you are going to that place. You are not saying you are going somewhere, you are telling what you are going to do. You are planning to be there later.
