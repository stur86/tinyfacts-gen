The word is for a number. It means the biggest one. Imagine you have many things to pick from. If you pick the biggest one, you have the best thing. This word helps us talk about things that are very strong.

If you have a lot of money, you could say you have a big money. If you have a lot of time, you could say you have a long time. If you have a big house, you could say you have a very big house. This word is about the most of something.
