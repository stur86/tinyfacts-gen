To complete something means to finish it so it is ready for the next step. When you start to do something, you often start with a few steps. Then you do more steps and more steps. After you finish all the steps, you can stop and say you are done.

Imagine you build a house. You start with the ground floor. Then you build the middle floor. After you finish the middle floor, you move to the top floor. When you reach the top floor, you are done. You did not leave anything behind.

Sometimes you are trying to do a big job. You start with one part. After you do the first part, you go to the second part. You finish the second part. You are done with the first part. Then you can move on to the next part.

To complete something can also mean to fix a thing that is broken. If a chair is broken, you can fix it. You take the broken pieces and you put them together again. You do this until the chair is as good as new. You have fixed the chair and now it is ready to use.

You can also complete a story. If a story starts with a beginning, then comes a middle, and then ends, it is complete. You know everything that happened before, after, and around the story. The story is finished and now you can look back and see how it all came together.

In your life, you might want to complete a long plan. You start with a plan that seems big and hard. You work hard for a long time. You do many steps and many hard work. After many months, you reach the end. You have done everything you set out to do. You have completed your plan.
