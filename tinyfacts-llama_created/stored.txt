To store means to keep something safe in a place. You can store things in your home, a school, a store, or even a box in your room. You put them there because you want to keep them for later.

Imagine you have a bag of food that you did not eat. You put the food away in your home so you can eat it later. You are storing the food for now.

You can also store things that are not food. You might store books, a computer, or a phone. When you store these things, you keep them safe so you can find them when you need them.

Sometimes, you store money. You save your money in a box or a safe place so you do not lose it. You keep it safe until you want to buy something you want.

To store is to keep something for later. It is to be ready when you need it. It helps you plan and stay safe.
