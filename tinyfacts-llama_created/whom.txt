To know someone is to understand who they are. This is when you learn about a person. You might hear about them by people you know. You see them in the street. You see them on the computer. You watch a movie about them.

To know someone also means you see their face. You can see their eyes, their hair, and their clothes. You see how they look. This is important. If you know someone, you can feel safe with them. You know they mean what they say.

Sometimes, we know someone without even knowing their name. We see how they act. We see how they smile when they are happy. We see how they laugh. We can tell who they are just by looking at their face. This is how we know people.

You can also know someone by what they say. If someone tells you they love you, you know they love you. If someone tells you they are happy, you know they are happy. This is how you know if someone is true.

Knowing someone can be very important. It helps us build a relationship. It helps us understand each other. It helps us make sure we are okay.
