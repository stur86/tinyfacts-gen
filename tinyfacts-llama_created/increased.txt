To make something bigger or better when you have more of it.

Imagine you have a small ball in your hand. You throw it up into the air. As it goes up, it gets heavier. It is also bigger. This happens because the air that holds it up is pushing on it from all sides. This makes the ball heavier and the air pushes harder, making the ball go up higher.

When you do this many times, you get a ball that is much heavier and bigger than the first one. You have made it bigger and heavier. You are also getting more of it, because you are throwing it up into the air many times.

You can do this with any thing that can go up. You can throw a thing into the air, make a thing go up into the sky, or even make a thing go up into a tall building. It is always about making something bigger and better when you have more of it.
