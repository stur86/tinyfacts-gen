The word brings is about how things move from one place to another. When something brings a person to a new place, it happens because of the person.

Imagine you have a bag. You put your things inside the bag. Then, you walk to a new town. The bag stays in your hands. You do not have to walk all the way to the town. The bag is carried by someone else. The person carries the bag so you do not have to. This is what brings.

A car brings people. A car takes many people to work, school, or to go to a movie. It carries them from one place to another. It helps people get where they need to go.

A baby brings life. A baby is the beginning of a new family. When a mother gives her baby to the world, the baby starts a new life. The baby is the start of something new.

Sometimes, you bring something to a room. You bring a book or a picture. You carry the thing with you. You show it to others so everyone can see.

You can also bring something to a place. You bring food to a friend. You give them something to eat. This shows you care.

In our world, we use the word brings all the time. We use it to talk about how people move and how things happen.
