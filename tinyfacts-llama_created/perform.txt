To do something means you act on a plan. You do not just sit and watch. You are the one who takes the first step.

If you want to eat, you do not just look at the food. You open the box and take a bite. You use your hands to eat the food. You do this every day until you are full.

If you want to go to a place, you do not just look at a picture. You leave your home and go to the place. You walk to the door and you open it. You take a deep breath and you step into the new place.

If you want to play a game, you do not just listen to a story. You take a piece of paper and you start to write. You write the words of the game. You write the moves that you will make. You write the way you want to play the game.

When you do something, you are acting. You are not just thinking about it. You are doing it with your body and your mind. Your heart is beating and your brain is working. You are living the moment.

If you are afraid, you are not just thinking about the thing that scares you. You are doing something to get away from it. You are taking a step back and you are moving away. You are not just sitting there looking at the wall.

When you do something, you make your life better. You show the world that you are a person who is doing. You are not just a name or a number. You are a person who is living and who is changing the world around you.
