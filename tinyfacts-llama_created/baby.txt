A baby is a very small person who has just been made. Most of the time, a baby is under the care of a very kind person who is very good with them.

When a baby is very small, it is not able to walk or move around like the people around it. It needs to be held and kept warm so it can stay safe and grow. The baby will often cry and make a loud sound to show that it is sad or angry.

A baby also needs to eat many times a day. A mother or father will give the baby food to make it strong and grow up right. The baby will drink from a cup and eat from a round thing that makes the food go down into its body.

The baby goes through many different changes. At first, the baby is very small and soft. As it grows, it gets bigger and its skin changes. The baby might have a tiny hair on its head. The baby can look like a tiny person and might even have bright eyes that are very clear.

A baby is very important because it will grow up to be a person. The baby learns how to walk, talk, and do things on its own. It makes new friends and has a family. Eventually, the baby will become a person with its own mind and body.

Sometimes, a baby might be very small and not show much. It might just sleep a lot or look at pictures. But even when the baby is small, it is very important and loved by everyone.
