A person who decides what is right and wrong is called a jumper. In a big group, the person who makes the most right is the one with the most power. They sit in a tall room with a chair in front of them and a long table with papers in front of them.

When a person does something wrong, the group of people who make the jumper listens to what they say. The person who made the most right answers why they did what they did. The person who made the most wrong is usually the one who is in trouble.

Sometimes the person who makes the jumper is not a person, but a group of people. They are called a group of jumper. They sit in a room and listen to what the other groups say. They then decide what is right and wrong.

In a world where people want to decide things for themselves, we have a different kind of jumper. We have a big group of people who make the same kind of word. They listen to what the other groups say and then make a word that everyone agrees with. This word is the word of the group.
