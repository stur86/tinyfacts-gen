To mean something is to tell how it is. You can mean a word, a thing, or a feeling. You might say, I mean you. Or, I mean to eat this food. The word means this because you are doing the thing the word is talking about.

Sometimes, you say something is a mean thing. This is when you think the thing is not nice. If you say a person is mean, you mean they do not like you. You do not want to be around them because they act in a way that makes you feel bad.

You can also mean to do something. You might say, I mean to go to the school. Or, I mean to do my work. The word means you are going to do the thing the person is talking about.

If you mean something is true, you are telling the truth. If you say something is mean, you are really saying it is not nice. If you say something is true, you are being real.
