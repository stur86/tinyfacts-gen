To be told what to do means you are given a set of things to follow. This is what a doctor does when you go to the hospital. They tell you to stop taking the thing you are taking because it is making you very sick. They also tell you to drink water and eat food to help your body get better.

If you are a person, a teacher might tell you what to do on a day. They might say you have to be quiet and listen to the other person. They want you to stay in the room until the end of the class.

Sometimes, a person tells a dog what to do. They might say, if the dog goes near a fire, you have to stop it. The dog follows the words because it knows what the person means.

In a different way, if you are a person who makes things, you might be told to follow a plan. A plan is a set of steps you follow to make something. If you do not follow the steps, the thing might not get done right.

You can also be told what to do by someone you trust. This is when a friend tells you to stay away from something that makes you sad. They want you to feel better so you do not need to act on it.
