To keep something is to hide it from others. If you keep a phone number, you do not tell your friends. You are keeping it for yourself. Often, people keep things that are important for their job or for their family. They keep these things so that only they know them.

You might have a friend who is very good at running. You trust your friend and you do not tell anyone how they do it. You keep this because it is not your job to tell them how to run. Keeping something is like being in a safe place.

Sometimes, you keep something that you did not do. You might take a book without asking. You keep the book because you want to read it. You do not tell anyone about it. You keep it hidden until you are sure you are not in trouble.

Keeping something is also about being kind. If you see someone in trouble, you might keep them safe. You do not tell the police. You keep them safe because you care about them.
