To have an attack is to feel a kind of power that makes you want to act. It is a feeling in your heart and mind when you are near someone or something you love.

Imagine you love a game very much. When you see the game on the box, you feel a great feeling. You want to watch it and play it. When you are sitting on the couch, your eyes are on the box. You might feel a bit of a pull inside. This pull is an attack. You feel like you should be there. You want to be close to the game. It is a feeling of needing to be near the thing you love.

Sometimes, this feeling is not good. If you have an attack, you might get very angry. You might shout at the box or your computer. This is because you want to act. You want to tell the box what to do. But the box is a thing you do not control. It is a thing that happens to you. You can not control how it works.

But other times, the attack can be a good thing. If you love a food, you might feel a happy feeling. You want to eat it. You might feel a bit of a pull to the food. This is a good kind of attack. You feel like you should be near the food. You want to be close to the thing you love.

An attack can happen with people too. If you are in a group with friends, you might feel like you are a part of the group. You want to be close to the group. You want to follow them. But if someone is mean, they might make you feel an attack. They might say bad things or push you. They want to act against you. This is not a good kind of attack.

An attack is also about how you act. When you feel an attack, you might feel like you should act. You might want to do something. You might want to get up and go. You might want to talk to the person who made you feel this way. You might want to tell them how you feel. This is a way to act in the face of an attack.
