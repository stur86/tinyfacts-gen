A small group of numbers and words that goes over the lines to another place is called a piece. When a computer sends a piece to another computer, it is like sending a small bag of money. The bag has all the words the first computer needs to do a job. The bag is small and can not hold many bags. So, the computer breaks the bag into many small pieces. Each piece is like a small bag of money that can go to another computer.

When you send a piece, the computer sends it to the right place. If you send a piece to a computer in a far city, the computer in your city must get the piece before it can do the job. If the computer in your city gets the piece, it can do the job and send the answer back. This way, computers can talk to each other even if they are very far apartments.

People also use this word to talk about how something is put into a bag. You can put a small thing into a bag with many other small things. The bag can not hold too many small things. If the bag is full, you must take out some of the small things to make room.
