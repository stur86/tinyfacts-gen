The word is used to talk about what is inside a person or a thing. It is about what is inside your mind or body.

When you are sad or angry, it is because something is wrong inside you. You might feel a strong feeling that you do not want to show. This is because your body is sending a sign to your brain, saying, you are sad or angry. Your brain then uses this sign to change how you feel.

Imagine you have a small room inside your head. Everything in that room is inside your body. This room is like the inside of your heart. When you are happy, the room is full of good things. When you are afraid, the room is full of bad things. This is what the word means.

You can also use it to talk about your own mind. If you are thinking about something, you are in your mind. You are not in the world outside. This can help you feel more calm.

Sometimes, we need to think about what is inside us before we act. We might need to think about our own body before we move. We might think about how we feel before we do something that will happen in the next few minutes. This helps us be ready for what is coming.
