To be able to stay in the air is to be a ball of water that is not on the ground. When you hold a drink in your hand, it goes up and stays there. If you throw the drink very high, it goes over your head and comes back down again. This happens because the pull of the ground is pulling the drink down, and the pull of the air is pulling it up.

When a thing goes up, it is moving away from the ground. It is not going in a straight line. It turns and moves around in a way that takes it higher and higher. This is why you can see a big piece of paper that you made from a piece of paper go up into the sky. It is not going to the ground. It is going away from the ground.

The thing you hold in your hand can also be a thing that is moving very fast. If you throw a ball, it goes from one hand to the other. It keeps going and keeps going, never falling to the ground. This is why you can see it flying through the air.

Sometimes, when the sun is very high in the sky, a ball of water can be very high, too. If you look at the sky for a very long time, you can see many of these balls of water, all going up and away from the ground.
