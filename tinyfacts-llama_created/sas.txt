To say something means to tell others what you think. When you say a word, you are making a sound. You are using your voice. You are not always thinking, you are just doing it. When you say something, you are taking a thing and turning it into words.

Sometimes, to say something is not the same as to do it. To say a thing is something you have done. You can say, for an hour, and then you have done it. You have taken the time. You have put the work in. If you say you are going to do something, you are not doing it. You are just talking about it.

To say a story is to tell a story with words. You tell the story over and over again. People listen because they want to hear it. You tell it so they can understand. You tell it so they can remember. You tell it so they can be glad they heard it.

To say something is to show you think. You show people you care. You show them you care about them. You show them you are not alone. You show them you are a friend. You are saying, here is what I think, here is how I feel.

A person who says a lot might be a writer. They write books. They write stories. They write what they think. They write to share with others. They write to help people understand. They write to make the world a better place.

A person who only says a few words might be a doctor. They fix people. They fix the hurt. They fix the pain. They fix the body. They fix the mind. They fix the heart.
