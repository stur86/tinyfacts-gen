To be free means you are not held back by anything. You can do what you want when you want. You are not afraid to try things, even if they are not good for you. You are not stuck to one thing.

Imagine you are a dog. You can run as fast as you want. You can jump over walls. You can go where you want. You do not need anyone else to tell you what to do. You are your own person.

In our world, people are free when they have money. They can buy food, clothes, and a home. They can do what they like with their money. They are not held back by how much money they have.

But being free does not mean you are happy all the time. Sometimes you feel sad. You might want to be alone. You might want to stop doing something. Being free does not mean you can do anything you want. You still have to follow the same set of things that everyone else follows.

There are also bad kinds of free. You might not pay for something. You might not do what you are told. You might be mean to others. You are still not free. You are still under the control of others.

So, being free is about having the power to do what you want, but also about not being held back. It is about being your own person.
