A long time ago, people used a thing that was made of many small parts. This thing was very big, and it did many jobs for people. It could make water run through a house, and it could even make a power line so that a car could go very fast.

Later, a man came along. He did not use many small parts. He just had one big piece. This big piece did the same jobs as the old things. People liked the big piece better because it was easier to fix and it was less heavy.

Today, the big pieces are made by other people. They make them many ways. Some are made of a strong hard stuff. Some are made of a soft stuff that is easy to cut. Some are made of a stuff that is very hard on the outside and soft on the inside. Some are made of a stuff that is very good at carrying a lot of power.

The word we use today is from this man. It is the word for a person or thing that does one job very well. We also use it to mean a thing that is very strong, or a thing that helps a lot.
