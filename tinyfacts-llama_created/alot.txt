A lot of things can mean the same thing. Think about how you use the word.

If you have a lot of food, you eat a lot. You might eat many pieces of food, or many different kinds of food. This can be good because you have enough to eat. But if you have a lot of money, you might feel like you are in a lot of trouble. You do not know how to spend it.

Sometimes, you might say you have a lot to do. This means you have a lot of work. You have many things to do and you do not have time to do them all. This can be very hard.

There is also a word that is very big. It is called a lot of money. This is money that is not enough for anyone. It is like a huge number in your head that does not go away. Many people have a lot of money, but it is still only a small number. The word is strong because it shows how many people you are not able to help.
