A thing that does a job is a thing that helps other things. Think of a door. A door opens and closes so you can go in and out of a room. It is a thing that does a job. It does not change. It just does what it was made to do.

Think of a car. A car does a job. It moves from one place to another. It makes the sound of moving. It keeps you safe and gives you a way to get where you need to go. The car is a thing that does a job.

A computer does a job too. It works with numbers. It makes things happen. It does not change. It just does what it was given to do. It is a thing that does a job.

You can also use this word for a thing that makes a sound. A bell is a thing that does a job. It rings. It tells you something. It does not change. It just does what it was made to do.

Sometimes, a thing that does a job is a person. A person can do a job. They work with other people. They do what they need to do to make money or to help others. The person is a thing that does a job.

In a group, you might have many jobs. One person might do the work of the group. One person might help others with their jobs. One person might keep everyone safe. The person who does all the work is like a thing that does a job.
