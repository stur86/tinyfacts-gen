To think is when you use your brain to figure out things. When you think, you are not just sitting there. You are doing something. You are doing it for a reason.

You think when you want to know a word. You might ask a friend, a teacher, or a computer. If you do not know the word, you think about it. You go over it in your head. You try to remember what it looks like and how it sounds.

You also think when you want to be strong. If you are afraid, you might think about how you can stay safe. You might think about what to do if a bad thing happens. Your brain is working very hard to help you.

Sometimes you think while you are doing other things. You might be watching a movie, listening to music, or playing a game. Your brain is working behind the eyes, thinking about what is happening.

You can also think while you are asleep. Your brain is still working, even though you are not in a big group. You might dream about things that did not happen. Your brain is just doing its job, even when you are not paying attention.

Thinking is an important part of how we live. It helps us to understand the world. It helps us to know how to act. It helps us to know what is right and wrong. It is a way of being with your own mind.
