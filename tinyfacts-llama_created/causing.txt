When you cause something, you make it happen. You do not just let it happen. You are the one who makes it start.

Think of a small animal. If you move the animal in a way that it jumps over a small wall, you are causing it to jump. You did not just let it go. You did something to make it happen.

This happens with many things in our world. You can cause a car to move by pushing it. You can cause a tree to grow by giving it water. You can even cause a fire if you use a thing that makes it burn.

Sometimes, people cause problems. They do something that makes a lot of people hurt. They might drive fast when they are not supposed to, or they might cut a street. These things are not good for anyone.

But other times, you cause something good to happen. You might help a friend who is sick, or you might help a family by fixing a broken thing. In these cases, you make something better because you did something to make it happen.

So, to cause is to make something happen. You can cause problems, but you can also cause things that make life better.
