A line is a way to tell where two things start and where they end. It is like a big piece of paper that you can stand on. You can walk on one side of the line, but you can never cross it into the other side.

Think of a place. Imagine a park. The park has a line around it. The line is like a big wall that keeps people from going in. No one can cross the line into the park. If you try to go into the park, the people who run it will stop you.

Things that are not a line can also have a line of power. This means there is a person or a place that has a lot of power over the other things. The line of power can be very strong. If you cross the line, you might be in trouble. It can mean you are no longer safe.

Sometimes, there are lines between two groups of people. One group has a lot of power, and the other group has less power. This line can be very important. If you cross the line, you might change where you live or where you go to school.

In the world of money, there are lines too. Some lines help you buy things. Other lines help you save money. These lines are like a set of steps that tell you which way to go.

A line can also mean a long time. If you walk a long line, you are moving far. Or if you are waiting for a long time, you are standing in one place.
