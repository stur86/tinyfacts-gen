To get something is to add to a thing that is already there. If you buy a book, you get something new to read. You do not just keep the same book and look at it.

When you work, you do work. This work is the thing you do to get money. You do the job and then you are paid. If you work hard, you can get more money. You are getting something you need.

If you eat a food that gives you power, you get something for your body. This power helps you to run, to think, and to stay strong. You are getting something that helps you live.

Sometimes, you might want something that is not there yet. You might want a new car or a pretty dress. You might be happy to get it because it is something you have never had before.

If you are happy, you are getting something good. You are getting a feeling that you like. This feeling can make your heart feel warm and your face feel happy.
