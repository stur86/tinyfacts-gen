To need something means you want it. You might need food to eat, a bed to sleep, or water to drink. A person might need money to buy a car, a house, or a job. If you have something, you need it. You are not happy until you have it.

Sometimes you need something to be okay. If you are sad, you might need to talk to someone. If you are angry, you might need to yell. If you are afraid, you might need to be close to a person you trust.

To be needed also means you are important. A doctor is a person who helps people get better. A parents is a person who gives you life. A friend is a person you like to talk to. All these people need you to be happy.

To need something can be hard. If you want to go to a place, you need money to pay for the bus. If you want to see a movie, you need money to buy a seat. But if you have a job and you like what you do, you are not needed. You are important because you do it.

We all need things in our lives. We need food to eat, water to drink, air to breathe, and a warm place to sleep. If we do not have these, we will be very sad.
