To explain is to tell someone why something happens. It means you are telling them how it works.

Think about a story. If you tell someone about a very bad day, that is explaining. You are making the story clear so they can understand. You are helping them see what happened.

You might explain something that is hard to understand. If you are trying to tell them about the world, you are explaining it. You are breaking it down so it is easy to see.

Sometimes, explaining is about letting someone know something. If a child asks why they are tired, you can explain. You might say, My body needs to rest because it is busy. This helps the child feel better.

You can also explain in your own life. If you want to tell a friend why you are late, you are explaining. This lets them know what happened.

To explain is important because it helps people learn. When you explain, you are showing them how something works. This helps them remember and understand things better.

Sometimes, you explain when you are trying to stop a bad thing. If a friend is being mean, you might explain to them why their words are wrong. This helps them see that they are being bad. It helps them change their way.

To explain is to share your idea with others. You are telling them why you think something is true. You are showing them how your mind works.
