When something happens to many people at once, you say it is happening all over the world. This can happen when a big city is built, or when a new food is made that everyone wants to eat, or when a new game is played that everyone wants to play.

You can also say that something is happening to many people. This means that the people are in many places and they are all doing the same thing. Sometimes, this happens because a person is very good at what they do. The people might be watching them and trying to do what they do too.

This word is important because it helps us understand how big something is. If something is happening to many people, it is a big thing. If something happens to just one person, it is a small thing.
