A computer is a thing that can do many things. It uses many parts to work. One part does the hard work. Another part keeps it safe. A third part shows what to do.

Most computers have a small part on the front. This part has numbers and letters. When you type on it, the computer reads the words. It then follows what you ask.

Some computers are very fast. They can do many things very quickly. They can play music, watch movies, and show pictures. They can also help you write letters to people far away.

You can buy one of these computers when you are young. When you are older, you might buy another one. It is like a new friend that you can use for many years.

You can also use a computer to talk with people. You can send words from your computer to another computer. It is like sending a letter, but faster. You can use these computers to work, to learn, and to play games. They are very important for our world.
