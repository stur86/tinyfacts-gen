To round something means to make it look like a number. It is a way to change a number so it is easier to understand.

Imagine you have a number from a game. The number is twenty five. To round this number, you look at it. If the number is less than ten, you round down. If the number is ten or more, you round up.

If you round down, you take away the part after the ten. So, if you round down twenty five, you have seven. If you round up, you add one to the number. So, if you round up twenty five, you have seven. 

Now, let us look at a number. If twenty five is a number that is not easy to write, you might round it. You would put a small number in front of the ten. So, twenty five becomes two hundred and five. This makes the number easier to write.

Rounding is used in many ways. A doctor might round the number of your blood when you take it. If the number is very big, the doctor might round it down. This is because the number is too big to write.

It is also used in money. If you pay money, you might round the number of the money. This helps you find out how much you have.

In some games, people round numbers when they are trying to get a high number. This is because high numbers are often easier to work with than low numbers.
