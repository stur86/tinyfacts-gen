Evening is the time when the sun goes down and the stars start to come out. It is the time of day after the day. When the sun is high in the sky, the day is full and bright. When the sun goes down, the day ends.

When the sun goes down, the light from the sun is not as bright. The light from the sun is made of many colors and it becomes more red and less white. The stars start to come out. They are very bright in the sky at this time. The world feels quieter because the day is ending.

Evening is a time for things to happen. People go to their homes or to work. Some people watch a movie or a game. Others read a book or talk with a friend. This time is for rest. It is a time to relax before the night begins.
