When you see a thing that is in front of you, but you do not see it because it is behind you, you have missed it. This is called a missed moment. It can happen in many ways.

Imagine you are walking down a street and you see your friend. You run to meet them. You are so happy to see them that you do not see the person who is standing next to you. They are the one who is walking in the other direction. You have missed them. You could have been the one who was walking in the other direction.

Sometimes you might miss a ball. You are watching a game and you see the ball being thrown. You are so excited to see it go into the air. Then the ball goes past your head and you feel you have missed it. You still hear the sound of the ball going through the air.

You can also miss a chance to do something. You are in a room full of people. You are the only one who wants to talk to the person in front of you. You want to say hello, but you are too afraid. You do not see the person. You have missed the chance to talk to them.

Sometimes people miss things that are very important. They might have a big meeting at work or a very important date. They are waiting for someone to call them, but the person does not answer. They are left waiting for a long time. This is a missed moment because they were supposed to be together.

Missing things can be very sad. Imagine you are in a room with your family and you hear a door close. You look around, but you do not see anyone. You think someone else is in the room with you, but when you go to get them, you find out they are not there. You have missed the person you love.
