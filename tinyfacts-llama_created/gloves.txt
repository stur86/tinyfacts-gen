A long time ago, people used to wear their hands in a bag when they went out. This was because of a bad thing that happened. The rain and the cold made the hands wet and heavy, and they could not walk. The people who lived near the water did this because it was a safer way to get to the other side.

Later, people started making a bag with a small opening at the top. They put a soft thing inside the bag, and the bag could hold the hands. This was safer because the rain and the cold would not come into the bag. The bag could keep the hands warm and dry.

Today, we still use these bags for this reason. We put a soft thing inside the bag, and it keeps our hands warm. Some people also wear them when they go out to play or when they are afraid of animals because they think they might bite.

A person who works with their hands is also called a hand worker. They use their hands to build things, to fix things, or to make food. They may wear a bag that helps keep their hands warm and safe.
