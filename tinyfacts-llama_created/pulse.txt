A thing that goes is called a beat. You can hear it when you walk fast. The beat is the moving of your heart. Your heart is inside your chest. It is the part that makes you feel strong.

When you are happy, your heart beats fast. When you are afraid, your heart beats slow. This beat is like a short sound. It is very fast. It goes very quickly from one beat to the next.

The beat is also in your body. It is in your fingers and your feet. It is in your arms and your legs. It is even in your head. You can feel it when you are excited or when you are afraid.

Sometimes, a beat happens alone. You might hear it in your head. You might feel it in your chest. It is like a tiny sound that is always there. This beat can help you stay safe.

The word can also mean a group of things moving together. When many animals move at once, it is called a group. This group can help them find food or run away from a bad thing.
