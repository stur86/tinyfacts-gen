To change something means you do not keep it the same. If you buy a book, you can read it. Later, you might want to watch a movie instead. The book is still the same book. But the movie is different. You have changed it.

If you change your clothes, you are changing the way you look. Your clothes may be red today and blue tomorrow. The same clothes are still the same clothes. You are just changing how you look.

Sometimes, you might change your mind. You might decide you like a different food. You might decide to go to a different school. In these cases, you are changing what you already have. Your mind is different. Your body is still the same body.

We also change things in our lives. If you love a person, you might change how you act around them. You might be kind to them more. Or, you might become angry with them. You are changing how you act.

If you look at a picture, the picture changes. The person in the picture is different. The clothes, the hair, and the face are all different. The picture is a new picture.

To change can be hard. Sometimes, we want to stay the same. We like how we are now. But sometimes, it is good to change. It can help us grow and learn more.
