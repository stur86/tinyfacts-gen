To understand something means you know it. When you hear a story, you understand it if you see what happened, feel the words, and know why. You try to know what is true.

You can understand a word, a name, or a story. You can see it in your mind. You see a picture, a face, or a word, and you know what it is. You can understand a problem. If you see what is wrong, you can fix it.

You can also understand how people feel. If you see a sad face, you feel sad too. You can understand a joke. When someone laughs, you feel happy.

To understand also means you learn. When you learn a new thing, you do not just remember it. You understand why. You understand what it means and how it works. This is a big part of how we grow and learn.

You can understand a song. You can hear the words and the sound, but you do not just hear it. You understand why the words are important. You understand the story or the feeling the song makes.

Sometimes, you understand something that is hard. You see how it works, and you know why it is like that. This is a big thing. It means you are not just a person, you are also a person who can learn and grow.

To understand is to be with the other person. When you listen to a friend, you are understanding. You are not just talking, you are thinking about what they say. You are trying to know what they are feeling.

Understanding is also about being safe. If you understand a thing, you feel no fear. You do not worry about it. You know it is okay.

So, understanding is when you know or see the world, people, and things. It is when you learn and grow, and you feel safe and happy.
