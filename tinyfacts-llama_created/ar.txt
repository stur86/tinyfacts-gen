The word arrives from the old days. It comes from the word before. Before time, people did things. They would go to the city. They would buy things they needed. They would eat food. Then they would go home. They would sit on the floor. They would talk.

Later, people started to move. They wanted to live in the city. They wanted a job. They wanted to buy food and clothes. They used a thing to carry things. This thing had a round part. The round part goes around and round. It never stops. It goes very fast.

When people have children, they give them the word. The child learns to say it. The word is easy. It is short. It is hard to write. People use it every day.

Sometimes, people say the word wrong. They might say it is a word for a dog. A dog does not go on a ride like a car. It does not carry a round part. It does not go very fast.

The word arrives can also mean something that happens very fast. When the sun goes up, it is very bright. When a car goes very fast, the sound is loud.
