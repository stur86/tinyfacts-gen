The word toward is about how you move toward something. When you want to go somewhere, you are moving toward that place. You are moving in a line that is toward the end you want.

Imagine you are very angry and you want to get to a place where your friend has been working. You are moving toward that place because you are going toward what you want. You are not running away from it. You are going toward it, even if it is hard. When you move toward something, you are moving toward a better day, a better time, or a better person.

You can also use this word with people. If a person is toward you, it means they are happy to see you. They want to talk and be nice. If you are toward them, you are being kind and showing them you care. If you are not toward them, you might be feeling angry or sad.

Sometimes, you are toward a thing you do not like. You might be walking toward a dark place because you are afraid. You are moving toward the fear. This can help you get ready for what is to come. You are taking a step forward.

So, the word toward is about going toward what is important, or toward what you want, or toward people you care about. It is about moving toward a better time or a better life.
