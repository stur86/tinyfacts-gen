To have something means you have it. You do not need to ask anyone or wait for it. You already have it in your hands.

Imagine you want a book. You go to a store. The store has the book. You can see the book. You can hold it. You can read it. You have the book right now.

If you do not have a book, you must get it from someone else. This is very different from having it. If you have something, it is already your thing. If you do not have it, you must find it.

You can have many things in your life. You have a friend. You have a job. You have a house. You have money. You have a car. You have food. You have clothes. You have a bed. You have everything you need to live.

You can also have a feeling. You have the feeling of being happy. You have the feeling of being sad. You have the feeling of being angry. You have the feeling of being scared. You have the feeling of being excited.

Having something is a big thing. It is a part of who you are. It is what makes life easy. It is what makes life fun. It is what makes life real.
