An office is a big room in a big building where people work. They sit at a desk, use a computer, and talk to people on the phone. Some people work in this room, and some do not. They all do the same job, but they do it in different ways.

You use a computer to make the pictures, and you talk to people who need pictures. This is your job. You are the person who does this job.

You can also have a job that is not in a big building. You might work in a place where people make food. You might work in a place where people fix things. You might work on a big road. You do not sit at a desk, but you still do important work. Your job is the thing you do every day to help people.
