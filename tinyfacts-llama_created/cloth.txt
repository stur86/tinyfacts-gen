There are many kinds of clothes. You have clothes for your body, clothes for your hair, and clothes for your face. You also have clothes for the room you are in. You might wear a shirt with a funny picture on it. You might wear a dress when you go to a party.

Sometimes, people clothes a thing for them. They put a thing on a dog or a cat so the dog does not get hurt. They clothes a thing for a person to keep them safe. They clothes a thing for a person to wear so they look nice.
