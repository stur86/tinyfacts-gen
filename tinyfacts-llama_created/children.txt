To children means they are young. They are not yet old. They do not know many things yet. They are often very happy and full of life. They like to play, learn, and make new friends. They are still learning how to use words and how to act around other people.

Children need love and care from their grown people. They need to be safe and to have things they want. They need a place to stay and food to eat. They also need a teacher or a person who can help them learn new things.

Sometimes children act like they are much older than they really are. They might say they can do things that are hard for grown people. Or they might want to do things that are not safe for them. This can be because they are still learning and trying to figure out what is right and wrong.

Children also need to play. They need to run around, jump, and climb. They need to make noise and have fun. This helps them grow and learn. Playing with other children is one of the best ways to learn about the world.

When children grow older, they become more like grown people. They might start to think about what they want to do with their lives. They might start to learn how to work and pay money. They might start to have their own families and homes. They are becoming more like the people they will become.
