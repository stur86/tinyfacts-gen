A group of people who go to a place to listen to a person who talks about their life. They often go there to find answers to why they are here, or to feel a little better. Inside the place, they sit in a way that feels calm. Some are big with a lot of people. Others are small with only a few. They all have the same idea: to be near this person who talks and to feel their love.

Sometimes these groups have their own ways. They might say the same words every day to the same music. Other times, they change them to fit their own stories. Some have a long story. Some have a short story. Some do both. They all share one thing in mind.

People go to these places because they want to know more about the person who talks. They want to understand why they talk so much. They want to see if they can feel what they say. They want to know that there are other people who feel the same way.
