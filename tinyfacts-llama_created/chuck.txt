To chuckle is to laugh. When you chuckle you make a soft sound and your face feels happy. You might laugh at a funny joke, at a picture, or at a small thing that makes you smile.

A person might chuckle when they are happy or when they are surprised. They might also chuckle when they are laughing at someone else. It is a way to show you are not mad.

If someone is not happy, they might try to stop laughing. This is because they do not want to feel their face get warm. They might try to say they are not laughing because they are afraid.

To chuckle can be hard. If you are laughing very hard, you might feel like you will cry. But you still laugh because you are happy.

Sometimes, you can chuckle at your own doing. When you do something funny, you might laugh at yourself. This is a good kind of laughing because you are not afraid.
