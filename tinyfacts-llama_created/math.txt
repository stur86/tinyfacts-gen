To do with numbers

One way to think about this is to look at the numbers you can find in the world. You can find numbers on the floor, in the air, in the water, and in the ground. You can see numbers in the stars in the night sky. You can also see numbers in the words of the story you are reading.

Every time you use these numbers, you are doing something with them. You are using them to plan what you are going to do next. You are using them to figure out how much money you have so you can buy the things you need. You are using them to make sure you are safe and to make your life go well.

Another way to think about this is to look at the numbers that are in your body. Your heart beats when you are happy or afraid. Your body uses numbers to send out small pieces of your body that tell your brain what to do. Your brain then follows those numbers with what to do.

You can also use numbers to build things. If you know how many pieces of wood you need to make a chair, you can build it. You can use numbers to make a car so it moves. You can use numbers to build a house where people can live.

In school, you are often asked to use numbers to do things. You might have to add or take numbers from a problem. You might have to find out how many times a number goes into another number. You might have to figure out what is missing when there are many numbers that add up to a number.

To do with numbers is to use them to help your life. It is to use numbers to plan your day, to plan your life, and to feel safe and happy.
