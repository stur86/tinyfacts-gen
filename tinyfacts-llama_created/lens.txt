A window is a thing you use to let light into a room. It lets the bright light from the sun come in and makes the room feel warm. You can open the window to let the wind through it, so you do not feel too cold.

Inside your body, there is a clear part that lets light reach your eyes. This is how you see the world. Light from far away goes through the clear part and into your eyes, and then your brain makes you see things.

But when you want to take a picture, you use something else. This thing is like a window that lets light go in, but it keeps the dark out. It has a tiny opening on top. You put something with a long, hard part in front of the opening, and the light from the world goes through the small opening. The picture is then taken, so you can see the things you want.

Sometimes, a small piece of glass or a hard part is put in front of a window. This helps keep the light from going in. It keeps the bright light from hitting your eyes, so you do not get a big, bright light in your eyes. You can use this piece of glass to look at things that are very far away, without looking at the bright light.

You also have a small, round piece in your eye. This piece lets light into your eye, so you can see. It is like a window in your head, but instead of letting light in, it lets the light from the world into your brain. You do not need a window for your eyes because the light comes in through the clear part inside your head.
