To teller something means to tell it again. You might say you had a great time at the party because you had to tell it again many times to the people who were there. You wanted to make sure everyone remembered how much fun you had.

A teller is a person who likes to tell stories. They often write books or make movies. They sit down and think about what happened and then they write it down. This helps others understand what happened.

Sometimes a teller is a person who helps children. They might read to them before bed. They show them pictures and stories so the child can understand better. This is very important for a child to learn.

You can also use this word when you talk about a story. If a person has a good story to tell, you might ask them to teller it. This means you want them to tell it again, so everyone can hear it.

In some places, it means to show something again. If you told a story many times, you might get tired. But if you tell it one more time, you are teller the story.
