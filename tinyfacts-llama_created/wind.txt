Wind is air that moves from one place to another. It is the way air moves around the world. When the air in a high place pushes against the air in a low place, the air gets moving. This moving air can go across the ground and over trees.

Imagine you are in a room with a window. If you open the window, the air in the room moves out. The air that moves out is the wind. You can also feel the wind when you walk outside. It is the moving air that touches your face and your hair.

There are different kinds of wind. There is the wind that comes from the open area to the high area. There is the wind that comes from the high area to the low area. There is the wind that comes from the land to the water. And there is the wind that comes from the water to the land. Each kind of wind has its own way and its own time.

Wind is important for many things. It helps grow the green on the ground. It helps move water from one place to another. It helps make the air warm for you to breathe. It also helps make the sky look different when the sun is high in the sky. Without wind, it would be very cold and very quiet.
