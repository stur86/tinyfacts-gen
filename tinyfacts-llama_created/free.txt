To be free means you do not have to do anything. You can do what you want, when you want. You are not tied to one place or one thing. You can go where you want, say who you want, and do what you want.

When you are free, you are not afraid of people. You do not feel like you have to hide. You can talk to anyone. You can say what you think, even if it is wrong. You are not afraid to be yourself.

Sometimes, being free means you can do things that make you happy. You might want to run, play music, or draw. You might want to play with your friends or go to the movies. Being free allows you to enjoy life.

Being free does not mean you are not scared. There are still bad things in the world. If you are free, you might be afraid. You might be afraid of the dark, afraid of animals, or afraid of people you do not know. But you are still safe because you are not tied to one place or one thing.

To be free is to be the person you want to be. It is to be yourself. It is to have the power to do what you want, when you want. It is the best feeling in the world.
