To miss something means you do not get it when you want it. It is a word you use to talk about this feeling.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You want to throw a ball to them. You throw the ball, but you did not reach them. You did not get the ball. You missed it.

You feel sad. You wanted the ball. You did not get it. You were the one who should have it. Because you did not get it, you feel you have missed it.

This feeling is also in your heart. If you want to see a movie, but the movie is not there, you feel you have missed it. You were looking forward to the movie.

Sometimes you might miss a person you love. You might feel like you are not there with them. You might feel sad and want to be near them. This is also a feeling of missing something you wanted.

We can also miss things outside our home. We might miss the sun in the morning. We might miss the sound of a song we love. These are small things we miss when we are not in our place.

To miss something can make us feel alone. It is a feeling that we are missing out on something important.
