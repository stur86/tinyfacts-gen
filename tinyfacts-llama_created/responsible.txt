To be in a job or a position that means you have to act for the people you work for. This is because you are not the one who made the thing happen. Instead, you do what the people gave you to do. You tell them if you think something is right or wrong, or if you did it in a way that is safe. They pay you for this work, and you are the one who has to do it.

This is important because it means you are not alone in this. Everyone else is also doing their part. If you are not doing your job, the whole thing falls down. You are the one who has to clean up the problem.

A person who is in this kind of job is called a person who is in a position. It is a word that we use to talk about the people who work for other people.
