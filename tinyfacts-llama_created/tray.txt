A thing that holds food. It is a small box with a round bottom. You put food on it, and it keeps the food safe.

Imagine you are eating breakfast. You put your cup of coffee or your glass of water in the thing. The thing keeps the drink away from your face. The thing also keeps your food clean. If you put a lot of food in the thing, it might get heavy. But if you put one small thing in the thing, it stays light and easy to carry.

People use these things in many ways. You use them to hold your books. You use them to hold your papers. You use them to hold your food. You use them to hold your drink. They are very important things in our homes.
