To Interrupt

To interrupt is to stop what is going on so that something new comes up. It means you stop a group of people talking or a group of animals moving so they can say something else.

You might interrupt your friend while they are talking about a movie. You might ask them what they think or you might just say something to stop them. This is a way of telling someone you want to know more.

You can also interrupt a song or a story. This can be very confusing. The music stops and then someone else starts.

Sometimes, when you interrupt, you are being loud or you are doing something wrong. It is like a sudden sound that moves your body. It can be very sudden, or it can be slow. It can make people feel angry or surprised.

A doctor might interrupt a person who is telling a story. The doctor might say, I need to check if you are okay. This is important because it helps keep the person safe.

In a big group, you can interrupt someone who is speaking. This is a way of making sure everyone hears what is important. It is like a voice in the middle of many people.

If you interrupt someone, they might get angry. They might say, Stop! You are doing that! They are trying to finish what they are saying.
