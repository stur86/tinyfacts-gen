The word edges is about the parts of things around us.

Think of a line. A line is a long, straight part of a thing. If you draw a line on paper, it has two ends. The ends are the edges of the line.

One edge is the part that is on the outside of a thing. It is the part that is farthest away from you. If you look at a picture, the edges are the parts of the picture that are not in the middle. They are the parts that are around the whole picture.

Another edge is the part of a thing that is next to another thing. If you have a book and a chair, the edges of the book are the parts that are next to the chair. They are the parts of the book that touch the floor.

Edges can be hard. If you cut something and there is a hard edge, it can hurt your hand. Edges can also be soft. If you hold a soft thing, like a ball, the edges are not hard and can feel nice in your hands.

Edges also show where a thing begins and ends. Think about a house. The front door of the house has an edge. It is a hard edge that marks where the house starts. After the house ends, there is another edge. It is the edge of the street.

In the world of people, edges can mean different things. If you say, a person is on the edge of something, it means they are not quite in. They are just about there, but they do not quite reach the other side. It can mean they are a little bit different from everyone else.

So, the word edges is about the parts of things around us. It is about where things start and end, and how they are different from one another.
