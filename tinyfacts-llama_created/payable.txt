To be pay is to give money to someone. This happens when you do work for a job. You might work in a school, a hospital, or a store. When you do this work, the person you work for gives you money. You pay them because they gave you the money.

You pay with money from your pocket or with money from a job. You give the money to the person and they give you a small thing. This thing is usually a box with numbers on it. The numbers show how much money you paid and how much more money you need to pay.

If you pay for a thing you bought, like a food or a game, you pay the person who made it. You give them the money and they give you the thing you want. In this way, you give money to the person who made the thing so you can enjoy it later.

You also pay for things you did not do. If you go to a movie and the movie is longer than you wanted, you might say you are paying for the time. If you eat a lot in a room, you might say you are paying for your food. This means you are paying the room for the time you spent there.

In some places, you must pay to go into a room. If you want to go into a room where people talk, you must pay before you can enter. This is like paying for a seat at a big room where many people are talking.
