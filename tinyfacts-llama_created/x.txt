A number is something that you use to show how many of something else you have. Think of a house. If you have  five books, you have five of them. You can say, five. This is a number.

When you say the number five, you are not saying you have five pieces of food. You are not saying you have five cats. You are saying you have five things. You are using the number to help your brain understand how many you have.

There are many numbers. You can use one, two, three, or four. You can use five, six, seven, or ten. Each number is different. Each number can help you do something different.

You can also use numbers to keep track of time. You can counters how many minutes, hours, or days you have left in a day. When you finish a job, you know how many hours you worked. You can use numbers to help you remember.
