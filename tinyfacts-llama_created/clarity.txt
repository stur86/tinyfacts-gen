To have clear means you see things well. You can see what is right in front of you, and you can see what is not there.

Imagine you are looking at a dark room. You can not see anything, but you hear sounds. The sounds are very quiet, but they are there. The sounds are clear. You can hear a person in another room, a dog, and even a low human voice. You know what they are even if you can not see them.

If you want something clear, you try to see it well. You look at a picture. You see the bright colors and the lines. You see who is in the picture. You know who is there. You are clear about what is happening.

You can also have clear about your own mind. When you know what you think, you have clear thinking. You can decide what to do next. You are clear in your head.

Being clear can be important. If you are clear about what you want, you can do it. If you are clear about your body, you know if you have a cut or a pain. You do not feel the pain anymore.

Sometimes, people have clear words. When they say something, they mean what they say. They do not try to hide the truth. They tell the truth, and everyone knows that.

In a big city, you might hear many sounds. Cars, people, and animals are all there. The sounds are many, but you can hear some words too. The words are clear. The words mean something. You can understand them without looking at a paper.

To be clear is to be sure. It is to have a clear mind and a clear body. It is to know what is going on around you.
