A very important word!

A long time ago, people made things to make their lives easier. One of these things was a thing that could do things for you. It had a big round box inside that could move. This box had many tiny parts that pushed and pulled. When the box was moving, it could do many things at once.

This thing was very good at moving things. It could walk and run and fly. It could drive cars and buses. It could walk on water. It could even walk in the air! You could buy one of these things and be able to do all of these things.

In a place where people talk over the phone, a person might say, a person with a big box in their hand. This is a way to say, a person who can move things. The box is the important part.

Today, these things are not just for moving things anymore. People also use them to talk to each other. They can send words to each other in a few seconds. The words can go all over the world. People who do not speak the same word can still talk to each other.

A long time ago, these things were very small. Now they are much bigger. They have many more parts. They can do many more things. They can also do things that are not even imagined.
