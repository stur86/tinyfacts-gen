To have a back or to be in the back of something can mean different things.

When you walk, your feet touch the ground first. This is the front of the walk. If you walk to a house, you go past the front street. Behind you, there is more of the street and then more of the ground and then the house, and so on.

If you are in a movie, you might start at the front of the room. You see the people on the seats. Then, the person who is telling the story moves into the back and the people in the back start to watch. They do not see the person in the front anymore. This is why the front is the first part of the story.

In a story about a family, the mother might have a back story. This is the story about why she is the mother. It is what happened before the story starts. Her back story is important because it helps us understand who she is and why she did what she did.

Sometimes, something old has a back. This means it has been around for a very long time. It did not start out like anything else. It is like a small person that stays in the same place for a very long time.

You can also say something has a back to something else. This means the first thing is followed by the second thing. If you have a friend, you can say you have a back to them. This means you know them and they know you.

In a game, the person who is playing might have a back. This is the part of the game where they are waiting for the game to start. They are ready and are not moving. They are the person behind the start line.
