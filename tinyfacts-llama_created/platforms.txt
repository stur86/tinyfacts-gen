To build a place where many people meet is to build a big area with many different small areas. Each small area has its own group of people. They might all be doing the same thing, like playing a game or watching a show. They stand on top of the big area. They can see what is happening far away. They can also see what people are doing in other small areas.

Sometimes, people build a place that is not on the ground. It is high up in the air. It is not under the ground, and there is no water under it. People walk to it from many different places. They can see the ground far away and the sky above them. It is a place where many different groups of people can meet.

You can also build a place that is not on the ground. It is under the water. Many people go there to work or to play. Some people live there. They build homes and schools. Some people go there to see the world from far away.
