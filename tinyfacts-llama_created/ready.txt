Ready is a word that means you are ready to do something. It is a word you use when you are almost ready to start a job or a game.

Imagine you are waiting for a bus. You are standing near the stop, and you can see the bus coming. You have put on your shoes and your clothes. You have your bag with you. You are ready to go. You can do nothing else. You are ready to be on the bus.

You can also use this word when you are ready to do a thing. If you are ready to eat, you are ready to eat your food. If you are ready to play a game, you are ready to start the game.

It is like a light is turned on. When a light is ready, it means the room is ready for you to enter. It is ready for people to see you.

Sometimes, you may be ready to leave. You are ready to go out of your home, out of your school, or out of a store. You are ready to be alone with yourself.

Ready is also a word when you are in a state that is ready to give up. If you are ready to say good night, you are ready to stop talking. If you are ready to stop a fight, you are ready to stop.
