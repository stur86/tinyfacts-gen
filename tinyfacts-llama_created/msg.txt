To send a small word or piece of writing through the air is to send a short note. You might send a friend a funny picture to tell them they look great. You could also send a short word to tell a friend you are at a different place. Sometimes people send a short word to ask how someone is doing.

You can send a short word by typing it on a computer. You can type it in a group of words, so it is easy to read. You can also send a short word by using a phone. You tell the phone to send the word to a friend, and the phone takes it from you.

Sometimes, people send a short word to a computer. They type the word and then press a big round key. The computer reads the word and does what the person wants it to do. It can also send the word to another computer. This way, many people can talk to each other in a short time.

A short word is usually very small. It has only a few letters. It can be one word or many words put together. When you send a short word, you are telling a computer where to find the word.
