A word you might see in a big building or a car.

When things push or pull on each other, they need something that lets them do that without breaking. This is why you need a door that opens and a door that closes. If you push the door, it opens. If the door closes, it stays closed.

A big building has a thing inside that makes water go up and down. This is the power that makes the things move. It is like a big box that gets very hot and very cold, and when it gets hot, it pushes the water up.

When you turn a key in a car, you are doing this. You turn the key and the big round thing inside the car starts to turn. It makes the water go up and down in the car, and the car moves.

You can also say a thing is under this kind of power. If a power line is broken, the thing it is under can fall down and hurt people.

The word can also mean something that is like a power. If you have a great idea, you are using this kind of power inside you. If you have a great job, you are using your power to help other people.

This word is important because it shows us how the world works. It tells us about the power of the things that go around us every day.
