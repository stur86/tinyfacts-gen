To work together is to help each other. When you work with others, you share your ideas and your time. You do not do everything alone. You use your parts to make something better.

Imagine you are building a house. One person makes the wood. Another person makes the walls. Another person makes the floor. You all work together. You tell each other which wood is the right kind. You use the right kind of wood. You put it together. You build a strong house.

People also work together to make new things. A group of people makes a movie. Each person does a job. One person writes the story. Another person makes the pictures. Another person makes the music. When they all work together, they make a movie that tells a story.

Colleges are places where people from different parts of the world go to learn. They study how to be good friends and how to work together. They share their ideas and their work. They help each other grow.

When you work together, you can do more than you could alone. You can do what you want and what you need. You can make things that make life better for many people.
