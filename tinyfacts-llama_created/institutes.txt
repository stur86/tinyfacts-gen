An answer is a place where people go to learn from one person to another. It can be a school, a hospital, a city, a town, or a place with many people who help each other.

You can also have an answer to a problem. If you have a question, you can look for an answer. A person might write the answer on a paper or show you what they think. This helps you understand what to do.

Sometimes, an answer has many words. It is like a long story with many parts. You might read the story to learn about a place or a thing that happens in the world. Or you might look at the story and understand the words on your own.
