A big word for how we make things that help people.

When you take a small drink or a quick thing that makes you feel better, you are taking a thing that is made to help you. These things are made from things that grow in the ground or from animals that have tiny parts that can fix things in your body. When you take these things, they go into your body and do their work.

They can be for many reasons. Sometimes they help when you are sick. Sometimes they help when you have a bad day. Some are for people who are very old. Some are for people who have a problem with their heart. Some are for people who want to feel very happy. There are over ices that can help with different kinds of pain.

The word has nothing to do with how a person does a job. It has nothing to do with how you look or how you act. It is just about the tiny parts you take that help your body work well.
