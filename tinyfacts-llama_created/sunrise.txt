To see the sun come up can be very different for everyone. For one person it is a bright and warm light that stays in the sky. The sun can go down before the day is over and still be in the sky. For another person it is a dark and cold light that leaves the sky before they wake up.

One way to look at it is that the day starts in the sky. The sun is the middle part of the sky, and everything else is on the ground. When the sun starts to go down, the sky starts to get dark and the ground gets cold. The light from the sun goes down and gets less strong.

Another way to look at it is that the sun is the start of a new day. When the sun comes up, the old day is gone and a new day starts. The sun is like a door that opens the old day and closes the new day. When the sun is up, the old day is gone and the new day is here.

Sometimes people just say it is a morning. It is the light in the sky that comes before the day gets very dark. This can happen in the morning before the sun is high in the sky. It can happen in the morning when the sky is very bright and the sun is low in the sky.

People also say it is a good morning. This means that everything feels okay. The sun is up and the world is bright. You can see the people in the street and the houses. The air is calm and the sky is clear. It is a morning when you wake up feeling good and ready to start the day.
