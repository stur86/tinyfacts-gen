The word we use today is a bit different from the one from the past. In the past, we used the word to talk about how things grow. We would say something is in the ground, or something is on top, or something is in the sky. It was a way to show how something was there.

Now, the word means how we keep the world around us safe. The air, the water, the ground, the sky, and the land all need to be kept in good order. If we throw a rock into the water, it goes down, down, down into the deep. If we keep the water clean, no rock can go in and make more rocks come out.

The ground is like a big table. We walk on it, and we breathe air that comes from the ground. The ground keeps us safe from things that want to hurt us. The ground also keeps the water clean. It holds water in its sides, and when it gets old, the water comes out and goes into another place. This keeps the air clean too.

The sky is like a big box around us. It keeps us safe from the big things that fall from the sky. The sky also keeps us warm. When it is hot on one side of the sky, it is cold on the other side.

We also need the land. The land gives us food, and it gives us jobs. We grow food, and we make things to eat from it. We make things to drink water from it, and we make things to build houses and cars. The land is like a big family. We take care of it, and it takes care of us.

All of this is called the world. The world is our home. We are a part of it, and it is a part of us. We need to keep the world safe so that we can stay there for a long time.
