To make something easier means to make it less hard. When you can do something in a short time, it is easier. Imagine you have to walk a long way. If you walk quickly and go over many small rocks, you are easier than if you walk slowly and go over one big rock over many long steps.

Think about your friends. If you are busy and have many things to do, it can be hard. But if you have a friend who is always ready to talk and help, you do not feel so hard. You feel more calm because your friend is there to help you.

You can also say that something is easier if it is less hard to do. If you have to do a long work, and you do it one day, it is easier than if you do it many days or many weeks. This is because you are not working for as long.

Sometimes, you might say you are having an easier day. This means that things are going well for you. You do not have to worry about many problems right now. You can feel happy because everything is going the way you want it to be.
