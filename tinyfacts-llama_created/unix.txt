A computer is a thing that you can use to do many different jobs. You can use it to send pictures to each other, to make things move, to talk to people far away, and many other things.

A computer is made of many parts. There is the part that tells the computer what to do. This part is often called the brain. There is also the part that keeps the computer running and that stores the things the computer already did. This part is often called the hard drive.

You can not see the parts inside, but you can see the computer when it works. You see lines of numbers and words. You see the pictures on a big box. You can also see the numbers and words that the computer is using to do its work.

A computer also has a way to talk to other computers. This way is like a long line of words. You can send a word to another computer and it will send the word back to you. This way is very important for the world because it lets people share ideas and work together.

There are also different kinds of computers. Some computers are very fast. They can do a lot of work very quickly. Other computers are very slow. They do one thing very well, but they do not do as many things as fast computers do.

There are also different kinds of computers for different jobs. A computer that does work for a business might be very fast. A computer that does work for a school might be slower. A computer that does work for a game might be very fast.
