To pull something means to move it using a long thing that pulls. When you pull a piece of wood, you are moving it away from where it started. This is the same with the thing you are pulling. It is like you are pulling on a line that is holding a thing.

Imagine you have a small ball and you want to move it across a floor. If you stand behind the ball and use your hand to pull it, you are moving the ball. The hand is the long thing that pulls. You are using your hand to move the ball.

You can also pull a door. If you want to go inside, you need to push the door. But if you want to go out, you need to pull it. When you pull the door, you are moving it away from the door you want to go through.

Sometimes, you pull a thing that is stuck to the floor. This is when you use your hand to pull. It is hard work and it needs a lot of power.
