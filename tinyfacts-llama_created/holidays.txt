Holds are things you do to show you care. You do them because you want to make someone happy.

Think about a time you went to a place with lots of people. They were all happy and laughing. You want to join them. You might give them a small thing, like a small rock or a paper thing, and say, Thank you. This shows you are happy for them.

You also hold things for your family. You might go to a place with the same family, and you all eat the same food. You play games with your kids and tell stories. This makes the time feel nice because you are with the people you love.

In the summer, many people hold things. They go to the same places, eat the same food, and play games. It is a time to forget about work and school. People feel happy because they are doing something together.

Sometimes, people hold things for their friends. They might go to a nice place, eat food they like, and have fun. This is a way to show they care about them.

You can also hold things for a person who is sick. You bring them food and drink. You sit next to them and talk. You want to make them feel better. This is a kind way to help the person.

A day to hold is usually on a month. People buy paper that says they are going to hold something. They write their names and the day on the paper. They then put the paper in a box with the other papers.

Everyone holds things. It is a way to show love and care for others and for the time you have together.
