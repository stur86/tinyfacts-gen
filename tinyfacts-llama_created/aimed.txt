To Aired Something Means You Watched It

When you watch a movie or a show, you sit in a room and look at the people in front of you. You see what happens. Sometimes you laugh or feel sad. You might want to get up and walk away, but you stay and watch. If you are really interested, you might watch for a very long time. You watch something that is not on the ground, and you do not leave your seat until the end.

When you Aired something, you put it into the air. This means you send it out to many people at once. You might use a box to send the sound and pictures into the air. You can also use a computer to put the picture into the air. This is like putting a small box into a big box that sends the picture to many rooms. The pictures are sent to a big group of people, and then the group sends the pictures to many more people.

In this way, many people watch the same thing at the same time. This can happen with a movie, a game, or a show about something you like. It is like a big group of people watching something together.
