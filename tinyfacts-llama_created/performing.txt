To do something is to give your body power. You are doing something when you move your hands, your legs, your arms, or your head. You can be doing something you love. You might play a game with your friends. You are having fun. You might be doing something you think is hard. You are trying to learn.

To do something is also to show how well you do it. If you are a doctor, you are doing something that helps people feel better. You are giving them power to stay well. You are doing a good job.

Sometimes you do something by your own power. You decide to do it. You might want to go to a new place. You decide to go. You are doing something on your own. You are the one who is doing it.

You can also do something by someone else. You give them power. You let them do it. You are giving them the power to act. They are doing it for you.

If you are doing something, you are not alone. There are many people around you. If you are feeling sad, your friends are with you. If you are feeling happy, your family is happy too. Everyone is being a part of the act.
