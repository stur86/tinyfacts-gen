When you say something is fixed, you mean it will not change. It stays the same. This is important for many things.

Imagine a line on a wall. If you move the line, the wall moves too. The line is not fixed because it is changing. It is not fixed because it is not staying the same.

Think about a chair. You sit in the chair, and the chair does not move. If you get up and walk away, the chair stays in the same place. The chair is fixed because it does not change.

You can also say something is fixed when it is set for a long time. A person might be fixed to a job they love. They stay in that job and do not change. They do not want to leave because they like it too much.

Sometimes, you see people who are fixed in their ideas. They do not want to change, even if things are different around them. They like what they are doing and do not want to change.

In some places, you might see people who are fixed to a person. They love that person very much, but they do not want to leave. They stay with the person because they feel safe and happy.

Being fixed is not always easy. Sometimes, it is hard to change because of fear or because of what is wrong with you. But when you stay the same, you do not have to worry about the world changing around you.
