When you forget something, you do not remember it. You look at what you want to remember, but your brain does not know it.

Think about a time when you were playing with a friend. You had a ball. You threw the ball. The ball went away to a far place. After that, you did not see the ball. You looked at the ground. You saw the ground, but you did not see the ball. You forgot the ball.

You also forgot to do things you planned to do. You said you would go to school, but you forgot. You went to a movie instead. You went to a game. You forgot your phone. You looked at your computer, but the computer was not there.

Sometimes, forgetting is a good thing. If you forget a word, you can ask a friend to help you. If you forget where you are, you can ask someone else. You can also forget things that hurt you. You might forget how you feel. You might forget what you did last night.

But sometimes, forgetting is bad. If you forget a piece of your life, you feel like you are losing time. You might feel sad. You might feel angry. You might feel afraid. You might forget your name. You might forget what you say. This can make things hard.

We all forget things. We forget names of people, names of places, names of things. We forget to do things. We forget to be kind to others. But we can always try to remember. We can look back. We can use our brains. We can try again.
