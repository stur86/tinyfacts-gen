A group of people works together to decide who can run for the office. They pick the person who thinks they can do the job well. In some places, people can run for office and many can be in the group. In other places, only one person can run.

Sometimes the office has a chair person. The chair person helps with the work and makes sure everything is done right. They also tell the group who should run next. The chair person helps keep everyone happy and makes sure no one gets too angry.

A group can be made up of many people. They might all have the same job but they decide who is the best person to run. The group works together to help the office run well.
