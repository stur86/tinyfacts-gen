To put something means you move it to a different place. You do this when you carry things from one room to another. You might carry a book, a small box, or a bag of food. You put them on a table in a room you are using for a short time.

You can also put a person in a place where they are safe. A doctor puts a person in a room where they can get better. A police car puts people in a car that drives fast to catch a person who has broken the big red sign.

When you put a thing, you make it go in the right direction. You do not hold it anymore. It is now in its new place.
