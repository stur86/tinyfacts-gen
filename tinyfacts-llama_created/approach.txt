To approach something means you are moving toward it. You are getting closer. This is often when you are about to see a person or a place you do not know.

Imagine you are walking to a school. You are moving toward the building. You do not turn back. You keep going until you reach the door. You are not afraid because you know you are getting close. You are almost there!

You can also approach a big problem. You might not know how to do it, but you want to learn. You go to the teacher or the person who knows. You ask for help. You ask for their ideas. You listen to what they say. Then you try to understand. This is the first step.

Some people use this word with animals. They are approaching a very big animal. They are moving toward it because they know it is not going to hurt them. This shows they are calm and ready.

You can also approach a place when you want to go there. You have a plan. You know you have to get there. You start moving toward the place. You walk quickly or drive. You want to be there before someone else does. This is important because it helps you reach your place before the time runs out.
