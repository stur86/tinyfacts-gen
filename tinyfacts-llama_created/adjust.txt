To change something means you change how it works. You might change how you walk or how you speak. You can also change a thing that is old. Imagine you have a box. One day the box is very heavy. You want it to be lighter. You do not want to carry it around. So you use a thing you found to turn it. You turn it around. This makes the box move around in a different way. You have made it smaller or bigger. This is what you do with a word.
