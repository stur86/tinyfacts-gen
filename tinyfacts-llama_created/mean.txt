The word mean is one of our most important words. It tells you how we use a word. You can use it in many ways.

First, you can mean something. You can mean something you see. You can mean a tree. You can mean a dog. You can mean a house. You can even mean a feeling. You can feel happy, angry, or sad.

Next, you can mean what something means. If you say something is mean, you mean it is not nice. You might say a person is mean if they yell at you, hit you, or call you a bad word.

Sometimes, you can mean what you mean. You can mean you are going. You are going to the store. You are going to see a friend. You can mean you are angry. You are angry at your brother.

When we say, you are mean, it means you are not nice. We do not like how you act. We do not want to be around you.

But we also say, you mean something. You have a good idea. You love to read books. You love to learn. You have a lot of power inside you.

The word mean is very important. It helps us know how to talk to each other. It helps us understand each other.
