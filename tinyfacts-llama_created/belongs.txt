To be a part of something means you are not alone. Every thing has a name and a place. If you are part of a group, you are with them. 

If you have a dog, the dog is part of your family. The dog is not alone. The dog is with you every day. You give the dog food and a home. You say good morning and good night. The dog loves you. 

You also have a house and a bed. You are part of your home. The house is not alone. It has walls and a door. The door opens and closes. You live in the house with your family. You go to bed at night and wake up in the morning. The house is your home.

If you have a book, the book is part of your life. You read it every day. You learn new things. You feel happy when you read something good. You are not alone when you read. The book is with you. 

Sometimes, you have a friend. You do things together. You play games. You laugh and tell jokes. Your friend is part of your life. You are not alone. Your friend is with you.

Being a part of something is important. It shows you care about the people or things around you. It makes you feel a way inside your heart.
