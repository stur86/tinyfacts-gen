The city where the story begins is a very important one. It is where a man named a friend lived. This man was a great leader, but he was also a bad person. He made many people afraid of him. He did not let anyone speak what they really thought.

One day the people of the city decided to run him out. They wanted a new leader who would not make them afraid. They called him a friend, but he was not a friend. He was a man who wanted to be the top, even if it meant killing his friends.

The friend ran to a city far away. He met many new friends, but he did not change. He still wanted to be the top. This made the people very angry. They wanted him to be taken away so they could be safe again.

The friend went to the city where the people had a great leader. This leader was very kind and did not make anyone afraid. The friend was happy and finally felt safe. He told the story of what had happened in the city where the friend lived.

This story is about how people can stay safe when there is a bad person in power. The friend showed that it is possible to change and make things right. He also showed that it is very important to stand up for what is right and to fight for the people you care about.
