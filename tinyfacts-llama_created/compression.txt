To make something smaller is to cut it down. You can do this with your hands. You can press your arms together so your chest is smaller. You can also use a box that can close. This makes the air inside go out and the room gets quieter.

Imagine you have a big box with many things inside. The box is full, and you can not put more things in it. If you make the box smaller, the air inside goes out. The things that were inside the box go out with the air. This is what happens when you use the word to mean making something smaller.

Things can also be made smaller without using a box. You can use your hands to put things into a smaller space. You can put a small ball into a small box. This makes the ball smaller. When you do this, you are making the space smaller.

Making things smaller is often good for people. When things get too big, they can cause a problem. They might fall over. They might make it hard to move. So, people use the word to say that something is smaller. This helps us understand what is happening.
