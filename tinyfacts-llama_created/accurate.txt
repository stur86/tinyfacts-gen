To be very sure about something is to know it is true. If you are not sure, you can be wrong. An answer is always an attempt to figure out the truth.

If you say something is the same as something else, it is not very sure. The word is used to show what is happening. If you say something is a big dog, you are not sure if it is really a dog.

If you are sure about something, you feel like you know it. You feel this way because you know the facts are the same. You do not jump to a wrong idea.

When you are very sure, your words are clear. You use the right words. You do not use words that are wrong or that you did not know. This makes your answers more strong.

In this way, being very sure is important. It helps you do your work well and keep your friends and family happy. If you are not sure, you might act in a way that is not right.
