A small body of water that is much smaller than a big body of water. These are usually found near the ground where the land meets the water. They can be very still or they can have lots of water in them.

Water from the sky falls on these bodies of water. The water then comes down to the ground around them. On the top of these bodies of water, the water can stay for many years without falling down. This is why they stay standing and do not go down into the ground like a big body of water.

Some of these bodies of water are very deep. The water can be many hundred feet deep. Others are only a few feet deep. They can be very cold, even in the warm months. Some of these bodies of water are home to many different kinds of animals. People use these bodies of water for drinking water and for making things like food and paper.

Lives are often found in these bodies of water. Many animals that live in the water, like big animals and tiny animals, live in them. They also live in the water if they can not find any other home. These bodies of water are very important for many people and many animals.
