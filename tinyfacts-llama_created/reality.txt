To see something is to know it is there. To believe something is true is to think it is real. A man who sees a tree does not believe it is made of wood. It is just a thing you see. When you think something is real, you are seeing it in your mind.

Real is what you can touch. A piece of wood is real. A chair is real. Real is also what you can hear. When you hear a sound, you know it is there. Real is what happens when you look at a picture of a cat. You do not really see the cat, but the picture shows you the cat.

Real is also what happens when you feel it. When you are happy, you feel real. When you are afraid, you feel real. Real is what you do when you act.

Think about time. Time is what goes by. When you are here, you are real. When you are gone, you are not real anymore. Time is also what you leave behind. When you are dead, no one can see you anymore.

Sometimes we try to see something that is not there. This can be very bad. If you believe something is real when it is not, you can be scared or hurt. To see what is real is to know how things are.
