To carry something means to hold it for a while. It is a way to move things from one place to another. When you carry a ball, you hold it in your hands. You can walk or run to get it to your room. You do not drop it. Your hands stay around it.

Sometimes, people carry books. They have many books in their bag. They carry them for days so they can read them when they are at school or work. This is a good way to learn new words and stories.

We also carry money. When you go to a store or a house, you need to bring the money. You carry it with you so you can buy what you need. If you lose your money, you must tell a grown person so they know where it is.

If you carry your phone, you carry it with you all the time. You look at it when you want to see a picture. You can also use it to talk to friends. This is a way to stay in touch with people.

Some people carry food. When they are going to a place far away, they carry food to eat while they walk. This helps them stay strong and not feel very tired.
