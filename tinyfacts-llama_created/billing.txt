To pay for something is to give money to the person who owns it. When you buy a thing you need, you have to give money. If you buy a book, you must give the money to the store. The store gives you the money so you can read the book.

You also pay for things you use every day. If you drink a glass of water, you pay for it. If you eat food, you pay for it. When you use a phone, you pay for it each month. The phone makes you the money so you can talk to people.

Sometimes, you pay for things you do not want. If you watch a movie, you pay money to see it. If you buy a game, you pay money to play it. This makes sure the person who made the movie or game can make more money.

You also pay for things that hurt you. If you get hurt in a fight, you pay money to the doctor. You pay money to the police so they can keep you safe. You pay money to a person who helps you feel better.

The word also means you are being asked to pay for something. If you ask a friend to help you move, you are asking them to pay for it. If you ask a teacher to help you do your work, you are asking them to pay for it.
