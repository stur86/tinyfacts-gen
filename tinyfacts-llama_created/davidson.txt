A person who works in a place with lots of people is a worker. They do many things for a big group. This can be a store, a school, a hospital, a police building, or a park. They might work with people who do the same work or with people who do different jobs.

A person who builds something is called a builder. They use power from a box to make a house, a car, a table, a computer, or other things that people need to live. They follow a plan, which is like a set of steps they must follow to make the thing. They use many different parts. They are very good at making things that people need.

A person who helps animals is called a dog person. They work with the dogs of a city, a school, or a park. They give the dogs food and make sure they stay safe. They might also help the dogs if a child is afraid of them. They are kind to the animals.

A person who writes words for people to read is called a writer. They use their brain to make words that tell stories, make people laugh, or make them feel happy. They might write a book, a story, or a letter. They must think about what they want to say before they start writing.

A person who works with a computer is called a computer person. They use the computer to do many jobs. They can look at pictures, make games, or help a doctor. They must know how to use the computer so they can work well.
