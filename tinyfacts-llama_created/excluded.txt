When you say something is not allowed, you usually mean that a person or group is not allowed to do it. But if you say someone is not allowed, you might also mean they are not allowed to be in a place where they should not be.

Imagine you have a big room with many chairs. You make a sign that says, do not sit in chairs number three. If a person sits in chair number three, they are not allowed to be there. This is like a person is being left out.

A person might also be left out if a group does not let them join. Imagine a group of kids. They are planning a game. If one kid wants to play, but the group says no, the kid is not allowed to join the game.

Sometimes, when you say someone is left out, it means they do not get to do something they wanted. If a person wants to play a game, but a group says no, the person might feel left out.

It can also mean you are not allowed to speak to someone. If a teacher says you are not allowed to talk to a student, you might feel left out because you wanted to ask them a question.

So, when you say a person is left out, it means they are not allowed to be there or do something. It is an important word to use when you are trying to tell others that something is not okay.
