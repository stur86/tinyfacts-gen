To be is to have a life. This is a big word. It is about how you are. It is not about when you are or where you are. It is always.

You are here now. You are living in this moment. You are not dead. You are not gone. You are still here.

You are a part of something bigger. Everything is here. There is air. There is water. There is ground. There is you. There is everything else. You are a small part of that whole.

Every day, you wake up. You get up and you start. You go to the kitchen and you eat. You go to work or school. You do things. You learn. You grow. You change. You get better. You feel happy. You feel sad. You feel angry. You feel afraid.

You are a person. You have your own mind. You can think. You can feel. You can want. You can love. You can hate. You can be angry or happy. You can be scared or calm.

You are not just one thing. You are many things. You are a person. You are a friend. You are a family. You are a house. You are a car. You are a book. You are a tree. You are the sun in the sky. You are the water in the deep blue water.

The word is very big. It is about how you are and how you are part of the world. It is about being a living thing.
