To handle something means you act with care. You use your hands to do it. Sometimes you hold a thing for a long time. Other times you move it from one place to another. You can handle a ball during a game or a book if you want to read it.

When you handle something, you keep it safe. You do not drop it or let it fall. You keep it in your hand or on a table. You do not let it go until you are sure it is safe.

If you are a person, you can also handle things. You might handle money or a job. You decide how to spend it or how to make it work. You are the one in control.

In a group, when people handle things, they work together. One person might handle the food. Another person might handle the money. Everyone must follow the same way so the work goes well.

It is also to handle the past. If you are afraid, you may handle your fears. You think about them and try to calm yourself. You might talk to a friend or write in a book to remember what happened.

You can also handle things that are not safe. If a dog bites you, you might handle the hurt. You might sit on the floor and tell the person who hurt you that they should not touch you. You are showing you are strong and in control.
