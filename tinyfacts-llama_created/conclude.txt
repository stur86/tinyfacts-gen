To begin with, an end is the very end of something. This end is the last part of a story, a book, a song, or a big eventually happening thing. It is the point at which you reach the end of a line of things that happened before.

To say that something has ended is to say that it is done. You can use this word to talk about how a game is over or how a long story is finished. It can also mean the end of a time in a city or a town. When a city is full of people, and all of them go home, the city is said to be finished.

Sometimes, to end something is to say that it is not going to happen anymore. If you have been waiting for something to happen, and it does not happen, you can say, I have ended it. This word can also mean the end of a fight.

You can also use it to talk about the end of a plan. If you wanted to take a car to a far place, you could begin with the plan. Then, you started moving. After some time, you reached the place. To say that the plan has ended is to say that it is done.

It is very important to use this word. It is the word that people use when they are finished with a story, a game, a plan, or when they tell you that something has ended.
