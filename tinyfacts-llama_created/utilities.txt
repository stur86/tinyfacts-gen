To use something means to make it work for you. In a big house, you might have a phone. If you call a friend, the phone makes the line and lets them hear you. You are the one who uses the phone.

But a person who works for the power can also use a thing. If they turn on a light, they are using the power. They are making the thing work so you can see. They are the one who uses the power.

In a city, people use many things. Some might use a computer to do their jobs. Some might use a car to go to work or to school. Some might use a phone to talk to friends. Some might use a water thing to drink water or to clean the house.

When we say a person uses a thing, we mean they are working with it. They are making it do what they want. This is how we use many things every day.
