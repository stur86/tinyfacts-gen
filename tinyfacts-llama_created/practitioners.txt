Practically means to do things. It is often used for people who are not a teacher. They do not have to read from a book. Instead, they learn by doing. They might watch a teacher do a thing. They then try it themselves. They are learning as they go.

Some people are very good at a job. They can do it very well. They might be doctors, teachers, or people who help with cars. They are always doing the job until they feel they have learned enough. They are very good at what they do.

There are also people who only do one thing. They are very good at that one thing. They might be a very good writer, a very good music person, or a very good person who is kind and happy. They know their own good things.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about something that is not a real thing. If you say, a doctor is a good person, you mean they are good at their job. It is not like a dog is good at doing things. A dog can only do one thing, but a person can do many.
