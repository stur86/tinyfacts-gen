To draw is to make a picture with your hands. You use a small thing with a point to move across a piece of paper. You use the point to make lines and make the lines go in different directions.

You can draw a person. You might start with a head and arms. Then you add a body, legs, and feet. You can also draw animals. Some animals have lots of hair and other parts that go around their body. You can also draw things that are only in your mind, like a bright light or a tiny thing that is too small to see.

You can also draw pictures of things you see. If you look at a tree, you can draw the big green parts. If you look at a face, you can draw the eyes and the mouth. This helps us remember what we saw.

Sometimes people draw to be quiet. If you do not speak for a long time, you might start to draw. You might draw a picture or a story. This can help you relax.

You can also draw things that are not real. You can draw a picture of something that does not actually happen. This is like making up a story. You use your hands to make lines and colors. It can be fun to see what you can do.
