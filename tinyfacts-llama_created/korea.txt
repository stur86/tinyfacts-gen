Kinds is a word that tells us about people and things. When we talk about kinds, we are talking about how things are different from one another. We can say there are many kinds of dogs. Some dogs are very small. Some dogs are very big. Some dogs are very fast. Other dogs are very quiet. Each kind of dog is a different way of acting or looking.

We can also say there are many kinds of food. You might like sweet food. You might like food with a lot of cold stuff inside. You might like food that is very hot. Every kind of food is different. There is no one kind of food that is better than all the others.

In a big group of people, there are many kinds. There are kinds of how they look. There are kinds of how they speak. There are kinds of how they act. There are kinds of how they live. People are all different kinds of things.
