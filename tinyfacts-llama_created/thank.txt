To thank someone means you want them to be happy. You might say you are happy because they did something nice for you. You feel glad because they showed you they care.

If you give someone a book, you thank them. You say thank you so they know you are glad they gave you the book. This shows you love the book and you want to keep it.

You can thank someone in many ways. You can thank them for helping you. You might say, thank you for fixing my car. You thank them for being kind to you. You thank them for being there when you needed them.

Sometimes, you thank a person when you are afraid. You might say thank you for not yelling at you. You are afraid you will get in trouble. By saying thank you, you show you are sorry for what happened.

When you thank someone, you show them you care. You use words that mean you are glad and that you love them. This helps build a happy feeling between you and the person you thank.
