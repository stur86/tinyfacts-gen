Minutes are short time pieces. We use them to help us keep track of what is happening.

Imagine you are waiting to see someone. You might look at a small piece of paper that says a time. This time piece is like a minute. One minute is one hundred seconds. You can use this time piece to see if you are late.

When you talk about minutes, we usually talk about how many minutes have passed. If you talk about a long time, you might say how many minutes that is. You could say,  ten minutes, twenty minutes, or one hundred minutes.

There are many different ways to tell time using minutes. Some people use their hands on a face. They move the hand around in a round way. This way they can see how many minutes have passed and how much time is left. Others use a small paper with numbers on it. They write down how many minutes are left after they know how many minutes are already passed.

Minutes are also important for people who work. They help us keep track of how much time has passed so we can know when we should stop working. When you work, you might have a set time. If you work for ten minutes, you can stop and get a break. This helps you stay focused and make sure you do not forget what you are doing.
