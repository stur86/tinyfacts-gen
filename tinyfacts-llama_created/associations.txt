To form an idea or a feeling from many small things is called an idea. An idea is like a word that you know but can not quite remember. It might be something you see or hear, or something you feel. When you hear a word, you might think about a time when you were in a place where that word was used.

We form many of these ideas from things that happen in our lives. A time when you are afraid might be an idea. This idea can help you remember what happened. It can also help you feel how you feel about things.

We can also form ideas from what other people say. If someone tells you a story, you might form an idea about what it was like. You might think about the people and the way they act.

Sometimes, we form an idea from things we see. A picture or a picture of a house might help you remember where you live. This idea is like a word that you know, but it is a picture.

Making an idea is also how we learn. When you learn something new, you form an idea. You remember what you heard, saw, or felt. This idea can help you remember later if you need to do something.

We can also form ideas from things that are not real. You might form an idea about a story that is not true. You might think the story is interesting or exciting. This can help you feel a way about the story.
