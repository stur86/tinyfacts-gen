In a big group of people, some of them are in the room all the time. They watch and listen to what happens. They also get to decide on some things. Each one is like a person with a job.

Some of these people are very old. They have seen many years go by. Others are very young. They are in this job for a long time.

The job is to help the people who live near them. The group is called the Upping House. The word comes from the word for a house. It is a place where many people go to work.

Some of these people do not even live in the city where the group is. They live far away. They still watch and listen when they can. They are important because they help make sure everyone is safe.

There are two kinds of jobs for these people. One is to sit in the big room. The other is to go home and do other jobs. They both are important because they help keep the group working.
