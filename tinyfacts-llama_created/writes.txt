To write something means you use your mind to make words. You sit down and think about what you want to say. You use your fingers to press the small round thing on your hand. This small round thing holds the words.

When you write, you are telling the story, making the words your own. You can write about a person, a place, a thing, or a feeling. You might write a story, a letter, or a picture.

Writing is a way to show what you mean. If you have a problem, you write it down. If you are happy, you write about it. If you are sad, you might write about how you feel.

People write every day. Some write for fun. Some write for work. Some write for school. Some write for their friends. Writing is a way to expressions.

You can write in many ways. You can write with your hand. You can use a computer. You can write with a ball. You can write with a stick. The most important thing is to use your words.
