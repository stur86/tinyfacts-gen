The word is about what happens under the ground. It is about the parts of a house, a street, or a big area that are not seen by people. These places are deep in the ground. They can be very hot or very cold, and they can have water inside them.

In a house, there are many of these parts. The big room you live in is under the ground. There are other rooms that are under the rooms, and there are rooms under the rooms of those rooms. This is like a big line of houses. You walk through the line and go down, then you come up and walk up again.

Cars use these parts to get around. They drive through the ground, and the ground gets under them. The cars go very fast because the ground is strong and the cars are very well made.

Some places have more of these parts than others. Some places have many rooms under many other rooms. Some places have only one room under many other rooms. These are different kinds of houses.

The ground can be broken. There are big pieces of ground that are under other pieces. These big pieces are like very strong boxes. When a box breaks, the pieces under it can fall down. This can make the ground shake and make people feel strange. But the ground is very strong and it usually holds together.
