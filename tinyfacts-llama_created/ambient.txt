To make something feel calm and quiet is to put it in a soft place. An air of a place can make you feel like you are there. Imagine you are in a quiet room with a soft light. The light is not bright enough to hurt your eyes, but it makes you feel calm.

In a place with many people, the air can feel different. The sound of the voices and the sound of a busy street can be very loud and keep you moving. If you sit in a quiet room, the air feels more calm. You might feel like you are in a different world.

Sometimes, a small place can feel like a big one. A room with a small window can feel like a big room with a huge window. The big room is bigger, but the small one still feels like a part of it.

Things that are in the air can also change how we feel. If the air is very hot, you might feel tired. If the air is very cold, you might feel cold. The air can make us feel happy, sad, angry, or scared.

In music, a soft sound can make a song feel calm. A deep sound can make you feel strong. A light sound can make you feel happy. The sound of a soft air can make everything feel softer.
