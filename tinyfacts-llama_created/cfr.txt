To Carry For Someone is a way to talk about how much someone cares for you or something.

Imagine you love a book. You read it a lot. If someone sees how much you care about the book, they might say you carry for it. This means you are very happy for it. You want it to stay with you.

You can carry for a person too. You might carry their smile. You see a happy face every day and you carry it. You feel good when they smile at you. You feel happy inside.

You can also carry for a thing. You carry money for your family. You carry a bag for your things when you go out. You carry your phone when you need to talk to a friend.

When you carry for someone, you show that you care. You are happy for them and want them to be happy too. It is a nice feeling to have someone carry for you.
