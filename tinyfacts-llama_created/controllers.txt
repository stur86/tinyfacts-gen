A controller is something you use to act on things. You can use them to move things you do not hold. They help you manage things when you are not there.

Imagine a car. If you are in the car, you do not touch the car. You let the car move by pressing a key. The car does what you told it to do. If you are not in the car, someone else uses the key. They press the key and the car moves. The controller is what you use to press the key. It makes the car go.

There are many types of controls. You might use a computer to send orders to a box that does a job. You tell the computer what to do and it does it. You do not touch the computer. The computer does not know you are there. You use the control to act on what the computer is doing.

You can also use a control to act on things that are not a car. A person might use a control to move a table or a chair. They press the control and the table moves. They can also use a control to stop a car. If the car is going too fast, the person can press a key and the car stops.

Sometimes, a controller is a thing that helps people think. You can use a computer to find things on a big book. You tell the computer what you are looking for and it shows you the answers. The computer does not know you are there. You use the control to act on what the computer is doing.

In some places, a controller is a person who acts for a group. They help the group make big things happen. They use a control to send orders to the people who make the things. They act on what the people are doing. This is how people work together to make big things happen.
