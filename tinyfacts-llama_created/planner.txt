To plan something is to think about it before you start doing it. You use your brain to figure out what you need to do, where you will do it, and how you will do it.

When you plan, you think about the day or the week. You look at the week or the day and decide which things you want to do. You might decide to go to the store, watch a movie, or work on your computer. You plan the time you will spend on each thing.

You also plan where you will do your work. You might sit at a desk, or you might work on your computer. You decide which chair you will use and where you will stand.

Planning is important because it helps you stay on track. If you are not sure what you need to do, you might feel very worried. But when you plan, you feel more calm. You know what to expect and you can start doing things right away.

Some people plan their lives. They make a listening plan for the day, week, or month. They write down what they need to do, when they need to do it, and how they will do it. This helps them feel more in control.

You can also plan for a big thing. You might plan for a big trip. You look at the road you will take, the places you will visit, and the money you will need. You make a plan so that you know what to expect and can feel safe.

Planning is important for many people. It helps them feel ready for what is coming next. It helps them make the most of their time and money.
