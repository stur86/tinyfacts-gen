To greet something means to say hello to it. It is when you talk to a person, a thing, or an animal. You use words to tell them you are there and you want to talk.

When you greet a person, you are saying they are not alone. You are making them feel not alone because you are there. You are also showing you care. You might say a few words, like, hello, how are you, or thank you.

Sometimes you greet a place too. You might say hello to a street, a park, or a house. You are saying, this is a place where people go to live or work.

You can also greet a thing. You might say hello to a tree, a dog, or a book. You are saying, this is a thing that is here and it is not alone.

When you greet something, you are starting a new conversation. You are opening the door to a new time. You are showing that you are ready to talk and share your words.

To greet is to open your heart and your mind. It means you are ready to meet someone or something new. It is a way to say, I am here, I am ready, and I want to talk.
