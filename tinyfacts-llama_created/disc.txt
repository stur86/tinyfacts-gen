To make something round is to give it a wide top. Imagine a ball. It is round so it can go where you want. You can put a ball in a game if it is round. You can also make a round thing from paper. It is a nice way to keep things safe.

A thing that is round is often green or blue. You can see these colors in the sky on a bright day. Animals can also be round. They might sit on the ground and not move. A round thing is not a round thing. It is something you can hold in your hand.
