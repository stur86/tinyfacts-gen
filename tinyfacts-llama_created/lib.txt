A book is something you read. You sit down in a quiet room and look at many words that tell a story. You read the words to learn about a person, a city, a time far in the past, or a world far in the farthest place.

A book is usually made of paper or hard stuff that holds many words. You open the book and start to read the first words. The words tell you what happened before. Then, you read about what happened next. You might read about a story, a book about a person, or a book about a place.

You can have many books. One book might be about a person. Another book might be about a place. You can even have books about many different things. You can have books about animals, or about food, or about money, or about how to live.

Some books are very short. You can read one book in one night. But other books are many hundreds of words. You need to read them slowly to understand all the words.

People like to read books. They like the words, the stories, and the ideas. Reading books can help you learn new things. It can help you feel better when you are sad. It can help you understand how the world works.

You can also read books on a computer or a phone. This is how some people learn new things. They can look at pictures and words from many books all at once. This is very fast and can go very far in a short time.
