An area of work or study where people are picked to see if they are right. They are asked many questions and try to understand what is happening. A person might be asked to tell the truth or to show that they understand a word.

Sometimes, a group of people are picked to do this work. They sit around and listen to each other. They ask questions to see who is the right person. This helps to decide which person to trust.

It can also mean a long look at a thing before it is done. Imagine you want to know if something is very good. You might look at it many times to see how it looks and feel. You might also ask people about it.

In some places, people have to do this work to get money. They have to answer many questions and show that they know the right answers. It is a way to make sure that the work is done right.
