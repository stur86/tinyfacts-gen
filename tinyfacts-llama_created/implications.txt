Important means important. It is about what happens when you do something. 

Imagine you buy a new car. The car makes your life easier. You can go faster and feel happy. This is an important thing. People talk about the car because it is very good.

When you do something, you have to think about what will happen later. This is called an idea. If you act, the idea might change. If you are kind, people might smile at you. If you are mean, they might not want to talk to you.

Think about a school. If a teacher says a thing that is wrong, it can cause trouble. If a student acts well, the teacher is happy. They might give the student a good job. This is important because it helps keep everyone safe.

You can also see things that happen far away. If a big fire breaks out, people are afraid. The fire can hurt many people and make the area look very bad. This is an important thing to watch.

Even the sun is important. It gives us light and makes our days go around the world. Without the sun, we would not have a day to live on.

So, an idea is a thing you think about before you act. It helps you make good or bad moves. It is very important in our lives and in the world we live in.
