To see in a new way means to understand something in a different way. It is to look at a thing and not just see it as it is. When you first see a thing, you see it as it is. But then, you start to notice its parts, its color, its feel, and its sound. You might see a cat and think it is a small animal. Later, you might see the cat and think it is a living being that can move and make sounds.

This can happen with people too. You might see a friend and think they are happy. Later, you learn that they are sad because something happened. You understand their story and their feeling.

This is a good thing. It helps us learn and grow. It makes us new people because we see the world in a different way. It is like having a new eye that sees the world in a different way.
