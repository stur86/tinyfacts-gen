To use something means you use it for your own needs. You might use a chair to sit or a book to read. This word is very important because it tells us what we do with things.

Imagine you are a child. You might use a ball to play a game. You throw the ball to your friend. You use the ball to have fun. If you do not use the ball, it stays in the box. This shows that the ball is not for you anymore.

We also use words to talk. If you say, you are going to the store, you are using the word. It means you are going to do something. This word is very important in our lives. It helps us know what to do.

Sometimes, to use something is to show that you are ready. If you are in school and you are about to do your work, you are using the time. You are ready to learn.

In a house, you might use the phone to talk to a friend. You use the phone to stay in touch. This is a way to use something for your own needs.
