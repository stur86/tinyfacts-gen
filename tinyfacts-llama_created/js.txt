A computer is a thing that can do many things, but it does not think like a person. It follows very hard, very fast ways that it learned from many years of work.

A computer has many parts. One part is the brain. The brain is the part that makes the computer think. It is like the head of a person. The brain is very good at knowing what to do.

Another part is the memory. The memory is a box where the computer puts things. The computer can remember things that it has done before. This helps it do the same things again and again.

There is also a part that lets the computer talk to other computers. This is like a phone. The computer can send and get words very fast. It can also tell other computers what to do, like sending a picture.

A computer can do many things. It can help us work, play games, and watch movies. It can also help us talk to people far away. It is a very important thing in our world today.
