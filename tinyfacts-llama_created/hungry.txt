To be hung up is to be in a state where you do not have enough money to buy what you need. It is like being in a room with many doors but you can not open them all because you do not have enough money.

Imagine you have a bag with food and water in it. You eat all the food and you have nothing left. Then you look at your bag and you say, I am hung up. Now you do not have any money to buy more food.

Sometimes you feel this way when you do not have money for a long time. You feel sad and you want to buy food and drinks so you can feel better. You might go to a store to buy things. You walk and walk until you reach the store. When you get there, you look at the things and you decide which ones to buy. You might buy food to eat. You might buy drinks to drink. You might even buy clothes to wear.

If you are hung up, you must find a way to get more money. This can be very hard. But if you work, you can get money. You can work many hours to get enough money to buy what you need. You can also ask friends to help you. Sometimes, you have to tell your family that you need money. They might give you money if you say you need it.

Being hung up is a big word that means you are in a hard place with no way out. But you can get out of this. You can work, ask for help, or tell the right people.
