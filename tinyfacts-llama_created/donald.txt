A person is a living thing. They are a human. A person has a name. They have a face. They can say words. They can act like a person. They do things every day.

You also have a name. You are a person too. You have a face, a name, and you can say words. You act like a person. You do things every day. People like you. They know you. They know what you do.

Sometimes people are very happy. They are very nice. They like to play. They like to eat food. They like to sit on a chair. They like to watch a show. They like to hear music. They like to see a movie.

Sometimes people are very sad. They feel bad inside. They want to cry. They may be angry. They may be scared. They may want to hide. They may want to do something else.

People have a story. They tell others about what happened. They tell others how they felt. They tell others what they did. They tell others why they do things. This helps people know each other.

People also have friends. Friends are people you like. You play with them. You have fun together. You talk and laugh. You share your ideas. You help each other. Friends are important.
