To be always means you do not change. If you walk to school every day, you walk to school every day. You do not leave your house. You do not go to work. You just go to your house. You do the same thing every day.

If you love someone, you want them always. You do not like it when they leave you. You want them to be with you all the time. You want to see them every day.

This is true for many things in life. If you play a game for many hours, you are always playing. If you work hard for many days, you are always working. If you write a story every week, you write a story every week.

Being always can make you feel very strong. It makes you feel like you are the top person in the world. It makes you feel like you can do anything. But it can also make you feel very alone. You might not see anyone else. You might feel like you are the only one in the world.

Sometimes, being always is better if it is bad. If you walk to school every day, you get to school before many other kids. You can do other things before everyone else starts. You can eat breakfast. You can play with your friends. You can have fun before the day starts.

But if you walk to school every day and you are very tired, you might not be able to do anything else. You might feel very sad. You might feel like you want to give up. You might feel like you want to hide.

So, being always is a big word. It means you stay the same every time. It means you do not change. But it is also a word that can make you feel very strong or very alone. It is up to you to decide if it is a good thing or a bad thing.
