When you say you are going to do something, you are saying you are going to happen. You are going to be there. You are going to be the one doing it. This is when you start to do the thing you said you would do.

Think about a baby. A baby wants to eat. A person who has a job is the one who does the eating. They are the one who does the work. The baby is not doing it. The person is the one. This is what we mean by an act.

Sometimes, a group of people want to do something. They say they will do it. They make plans. Then they do it. The plans are like a set of words. The words tell them what to do. They tell them how to do it. If they do not follow the words, they did not do it.

Imagine a group of people trying to build a house. They say they will build it. They make plans. They pick the wood. They pick the money. They work on it every day. After many days, the house is built. They did it. They followed their plans. The plans were the words. The words told them what to do.

You can also say this when you are going to do something. You say you are going to go to a place. You are going to do something. You are going to be there. This is when you start to do the thing you said you would do.

In a work place, you work. You are the one doing the work. Your job is to do it. You follow the words of your job. If you do not follow, you do not do it. Your job is like a set of words. The words tell you what to do. If you do not follow, you do not do it.
