To feel the warm air around you is to be in a warm place. When you put your hand in the warm water from the shower, you are feeling the warm air. The warm air makes your skin feel soft. You might even laugh because the warm air makes your face feel happy.

You can also feel the warm air when a fire is burning. The warm air from the fire goes up into the sky. This makes the sky look bright and warm. Some people sit near the fire to feel the warm air and enjoy the warm feeling.

Sometimes, you feel the warm air when you run. As you move your legs, the warm air from your body goes down your legs and into your feet. This makes your feet feel warm and your legs feel good. You might even feel a little happy because your body is moving and you are warm.

You can also feel the warm air when you sit in a room that is very hot. The warm air makes your face feel warm and your hands feel warm. This is why you might want to sit in a room with a window so you can feel the warm air coming out.
