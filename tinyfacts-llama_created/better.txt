To better something means to make it better. You can do this by doing things in a different way. You might change how you walk. You move your feet in a way that makes you feel less tired. Or you learn to say things in a way that people understand better.

You can also better a group of people. If you are part of a team, you work to make sure everyone does their job. If the work is not being done well, you help the team find a better way to do it. You want everyone to be happy and safe.

Sometimes, you want to better your own life. You might want to learn a new game. You watch movies and listen to music. You try to do better at the game, so you become a good player. You also try to do better in your work. You learn new things and try new ways to do your job.

You can also better a place. If a town has a big problem, you work with the people to fix it. You talk with the people who live there and listen to their ideas. You find a way that everyone can be happy. This makes the town a better place to live.

In a small way, you can better yourself too. When you are afraid, you try to be stronger. You take deep breaths and look at the thing you are afraid of. This makes you feel less scared. You become a better person in that moment.
