To be safe means to keep yourself from getting hurt. If you wear a jacket to school, you are safe. You do not feel a thing that could hurt you.

To be a good friend is also to keep others safe. If you tell your friend that someone is doing a bad thing, you are helping them stay safe. You are not the one who has to do it.

When you are safe, you feel calm. You can breathe without having to hold your air. You are not worried that something bad is going to happen to you. You are ready for what is ahead.

Sometimes you need to be safe for a reason. If you are in a place that is not safe, you need to find a way to stay safe. You might go to a different room or find a place to hide. You are keeping yourself safe so you do not get hurt.

Being safe is important. It helps you feel happy and safe in your home. It also helps you feel safe in school or at work.
