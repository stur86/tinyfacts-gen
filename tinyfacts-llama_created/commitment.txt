A person gives their word to do something. They say they will be there. This is a strong word because it means they are not going to be easy to leave.

To be a person with a word is to be a person who keeps their promise. You can see this in your family. Your mom and dad are people with a word. They say they will be home at five. When it is five, they are there. If you say you will be home at five, and you are not, you have broken the word.

You can also have a word for a job. If you say you will work for a week, but you do not, you did not keep your word. You broke the word of the job. This is why it is a word that people use when they do not follow what they said they would do.
