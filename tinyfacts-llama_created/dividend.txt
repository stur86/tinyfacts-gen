A thing that gives you money is called a thing that gives money. This money is often from a group of people who own a big place. When the group makes more money, they decide to share some of it with the people who work for them.

Imagine you are a person who helps a big store buy food and clothes. You work very hard to get the money the store needs. After a few months, the store says, thanks for all your hard work, you get a small piece of money. This small piece of money is a thing that gives money. This money is not from the store but from the store because you helped them make money.

This is how people who are in a job can get money. They get a small piece of money from the person who owns the place where they work. This is one way to share the money that people make.
