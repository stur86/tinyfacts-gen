To turn means to change direction. Imagine you are on a moving car. You want to go to a place. If you turn the car to the right, you move forward. You are now on the right side of the road.

If you want to go back, you need to turn the car to the left. This is important because it keeps you in the same line as the road. If you turn too quickly, you might hit something.

You can also turn your body. If you want to look at something behind you, you turn your head. If you are walking and you see a tree, you turn your body to face it. This helps you stay safe.

In the sky, we use turns to change direction. Imagine you are flying a small thing that can go around. If you want to go up, you turn the thing to the top. This is like a big turn in the air. It helps the thing stay on the right line.

To turn something also means to make it move in a new way. If you put a ball on a table, it might start to go around. This is a turn. You did it by pushing the ball with your hand.

Sometimes, we use turns to change our way of thinking. If you are feeling sad, a turn can help. You might look at a happy picture or listen to music. This can help you feel better.

In life, you might turn a corner. This means you are moving from one place to another. You might be in a room and then you turn the corner. You are now in a new room.
