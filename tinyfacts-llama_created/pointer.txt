A tiny stick is called a point. It is very small. When you point to something, you are giving it your attention. You are telling the world that this thing is important to you. It is like a finger.

Imagine you are in a big room. You are looking at a picture on a wall. You need to find a person. You look at the picture. You see the face of the person. You point at the face. The person looks up. They see you. You are saying, I am looking at you. You are pointing to yourself.

A computer has a point too. It is a tiny stick that you can move. It tells the computer what to do. You move the point around on the computer. It is like a little hand. It helps you type words or draw pictures.

Sometimes, a point is not just a small stick. It can be a number. In a game, you might have to hit a ball with a point. It is a number that tells you where to hit the ball. The number helps you plan your shot.

In another way, a point can mean a small thing in a story. If you are looking for a book, you might look for a point. The point is the word in the story. It tells you where to find the book. It is a small word that helps you understand the story.

So, a point is a small thing that helps you show or tell. It can be a tiny stick or a number, but it is always a small thing that makes things happen.
