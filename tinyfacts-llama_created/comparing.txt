To talk about something means to use words so you can understand each other. When you say something is better than something else, you are saying it is a better way.

You can talk about people. If you say your friend is better than your sister, you are saying your friend is a better person. You are not saying your sister is not a good person. You are just saying how they are different.

You can also talk about things. If a car is better than another car, you are saying it has more power or it is more safe. You are not saying the other car is not good. You are just saying which one you like more.

Sometimes you talk about how you feel. If you are happy and your friend is sad, you can say you are happy because your friend is sad. You are not saying your friend is happy. You are just saying how you feel.

To talk about something can be easy or hard. It can be easy to say something is better because you have a lot of power. But it is hard to really know if something is better. You must really think about it and not just say it because you feel like it.

You can also talk about how things change. If you love a place, you might say you love it more after you go there a few times. You are not saying the place is not good at all. You are just saying how you feel about it now. You are not saying the place is not good anymore.

When you talk about something, you are trying to help each other understand. You are trying to share your ideas so you can see what each other thinks.
