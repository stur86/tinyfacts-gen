A cup is a thing you use to hold food or drink. It is usually round and has a top and a bottom. You can put your hands inside it. Some cups are for hot things and some are for cold things.

You can use a cup to drink from. You can also use it to put things into it. If you fill it with water and then you drink it, the cup stays full. If you put a tiny bit of something on top, like a piece of food, and then you drink it, the cup gets a bit full too.

People use cups every day. You might use one to hold your coffee or team. You might use one to carry a glass of water. Some people use cups to eat food from. A cup is a very easy thing to carry around.

You can also use a cup for fun. You can put a ball inside it and shake it. This makes the air inside the cup go very fast. Then the ball comes out very fast too. This is a fun game to play with a cup.
