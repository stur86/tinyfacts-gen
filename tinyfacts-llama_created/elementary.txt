The word you asked about is very important. It is a word we use to talk about what we learn when we are young.

When you are little, you go to a school with other kids. The teacher gives you a book with many words. The teacher also gives you a small thing to help you write. You learn how to read and write. This is the first big group of kids you meet.

After you go to that school, you go to another group. You learn to do things with your hands. You learn how to build things, how to use a computer, and how to make food. This group helps you become a strong person.

Then, you go to a group where you learn how to live with other people. You learn how to share, how to be kind, and how to say sorry if you do something wrong. This group helps you become a good person.

The word you asked about is the first group. It is the group where kids start to learn how to read, write, and do things with their hands. It is the group where kids become strong and good.

We call the first group of kids the first year of school. After that, we go to the second year, and then the third year, and so on. Each group is like a big step. After many groups, you become a big person with lots of ideas and a life of your own.
