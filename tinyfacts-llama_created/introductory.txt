To start something is to begin. When you first start, you are not sure what will happen. You might feel a bit nervous. You might want to know if it will work.

If you are talking to someone, the first words you say are called the beginning. You might say, you are here, or I am happy to see you. These words show you are ready to talk.

If you are doing a job, the first step is usually the most hard. You must learn how to do it. It is like learning a new game. You might feel like you are going to make a big problem.

When you are doing something new, you often start with small steps. You start by doing one thing. Then you try another thing. This helps you get ready for the next step.

Sometimes, you start with a plan. You know what you want to do. You write down your steps. You follow the plan until you reach the end. Then, you can see if you did it well.
