A person who writes stories about what happens is called a runner. But this person does not just write about things that happen. They also watch people and places. They take their words and put them into a form that is easy for many people to read.

Imagine you are very angry. You want to tell the whole world. You go to a place where many people live. You talk to many people there. You write down what you say. You write about the things you saw and the things you heard. You write this so that other people can see what you felt.

Many people do this job. They watch what happens in a city, a town, or a place with a lot of people. They watch how people act. They watch how people speak. They write this down so that we can learn about how people live.

The word you are looking for is a person who does this job. They are the ones who tell us what is happening in a world that is far away. They are the ones who make us know what to do when we are in a place with many people.

In some places, this job is very important. People who run these stories help us understand how the world works. They help us know about the people who live there. They help us know about the things that make a place feel like home.
