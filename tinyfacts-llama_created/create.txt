To be something new is to make it from nothing. You can do this when you write a story. You start with nothing and build it into a story.

You can also do this with your mind. You can think of a new idea and make it into a plan. You use your brain to come up with a new way to do something.

Sometimes, you use a thing to make more of something else. You take a piece of wood and use it to make a chair. The chair is a new thing that you made from the wood.

You can also be something new. You can change the way you act. If you are afraid, you become afraid. If you are happy, you become happy. This is because your mind has changed.

Sometimes, a new thing comes from the past. A person who is old might tell you stories about their life. These stories are new because they come from the past. They are a way to remember what happened before.

When you are something new, you are different from what you used to be. You are a new person with new ideas and new ways of thinking.
