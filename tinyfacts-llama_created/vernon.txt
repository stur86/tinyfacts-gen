The word very is used to show how true a thing is.

When you say a thing is very good, it means it is very good. You might say,  This food is very good. This is because it is very good to eat.

When you say something is very, you are using this word to show how much something is. If you say something is very small, it means it is not very big at all. If you say something is very big, it means it is a lot bigger than what you can see.

Very can also mean safe. If you say a place is very safe, you mean it is a safe place where you can go. This means you are not afraid of anything there.

The word very is often used in stories and books. It helps us understand how a person feels. When you see a person who is very happy, you might smile. This is because the word makes you think they feel very good.

You can also use this word when you talk about time. If you say a time is very late, it means it is very late. This is because time moves very fast. If you say a time is very early, it means it is very early.

So, when you hear the word very, you know it is about how true a thing is, how big something is, or how safe a place is.
