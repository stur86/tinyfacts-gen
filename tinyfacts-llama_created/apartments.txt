To live in an apartment is to have your own house. This is different from living in a big house with many people. When you live in an apartment, you pay money to live there. You get to have a room with a bed, a table, and sometimes a window.

Most apartments are for people who move into one place. You are not allowed to move to another apartment in the same building. If you want to move, you must talk to the person who lives in the apartment you want to leave and get their okay. This is so that the new person can also find a room.

In some apartments, you pay more money for the room. These are more like houses. They have more rooms and a bigger living room. In these apartments, you pay more money each month to live there.

There are also apartments that are not for people. These are usually for people who are not allowed to live in a big house. They are usually in a building with many different floors. You live in a tiny room and share the floor with many other people. This is not like having your own house.

If you have a job, you can live in an apartment with your family. This is a good way to live because you do not have to drive a car to go to work. You can walk or take a bus to get where you need to go.
