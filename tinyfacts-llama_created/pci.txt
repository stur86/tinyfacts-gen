The third kind is the one that helps the computer remember what it has learned. This part is like a very good mind. It stores many bits of your words and pictures. It can find what you are looking for very fast.

When you build a computer with these parts, you must follow a set of steps. You put the first kind of part inside. You put the second kind of part on top of the first kind. You put the third kind of part inside the computer. Then you turn it on and it starts doing things.
