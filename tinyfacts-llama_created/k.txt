The word kisser is a word used to talk about a person who is the leader of a group. When a group of people want to do something, they might ask the leader if they can do it. If the leader says yes, the group follows what the leader says. This is because the leader is like a top person in the group.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a thing that is strong. A strong thing can make a lot of power. If a thing is very strong, it can break anything that tries to push it.

Also, if a person is very good at a game, the game is said to be kisser. This means the game is very fun and the person who is doing it is the best. When a game is kisser, everyone wants to play with them.

In a group of animals, a kisser is the person who is the one that everyone follows. The animal follows the kisser and does what they say. This is how animals stay together and do what they want.
