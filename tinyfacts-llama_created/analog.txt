An old word for things you do with your hands.

You have a long piece of wood. You can make it many things by doing the same things over and over. You can make a picture, a box, a chair, or a thing you use to write. When you do the same thing many times, the wood gets very strong. It does not break as easily.

The word is for things you do with your hands. It is not for how a computer works. It is for how you make things by hand.

You can also use it for other things. If you have a small thing that helps you see, you can call it an old word. But it is not the same as the computer word. The computer word is for how a computer works.

This word is very old. It comes from a time before people had computers. A person could make something by doing the same thing over and over. Now we have many things that can do things for us. But we still use this word because it is about doing things by hand.
