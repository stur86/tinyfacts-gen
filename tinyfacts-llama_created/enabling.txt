To allow something is to give it the power to happen. It is like giving a child the keys to a car. Without the keys, they can not move. But with them, they can drive and go where they want.

Think about money. You have money to buy food and clothes. But if you do not have money, you have no way to buy what you need. You are not able to do anything. But once you get money, you can buy food, clothes, and other things you need. You are able to live.

You can also allow a person to do something they want. If you are angry, you might say, I am mad. This shows you are not happy. But if you say, I am afraid, you are showing that you feel something else. This helps you talk about what you are feeling.

Sometimes, something can be a problem. This is when something stops working. But if you can allow something, you can change the situation. You might allow a friend to stay in your home. This means you are letting them be there. You are not stopping them, but you are not making them leave. This can help your friend feel safe and happy.

To change something is to make it different. If you are in a room and you hear a noise, you might decide to leave. You could say, I am going to go and find another room. This change happens because you are moving your body and your mind.
