A small round thing that makes a lot of power. When you turn the handle, it moves around inside the thing, which makes the power go out of it and into the world.

Imagine you have a big box that you use to carry heavy things. The box has a round thing inside that turns when you push it. This round thing turns the box around, and the box goes very fast. The box is like a tiny, living thing that works for you.

There are many kinds of these round things. One is in a car. It makes the car move. Another is in a room. It makes a door open and close. Some are in big power lines. They carry power from a high place down to houses and schools.

When these round things work, they make the world easier to live in. They power many things we use every day. They help us move around, make food, and keep our homes and schools running.

Sometimes, these round things are very strong and can do a lot of work at once. They are like little power animals that help us do our jobs. They are also very good at moving around in the water and on land.
