To run an animal through the water is an easy thing to do. You put a big animal in a small box and you let it go. You watch it and you know what it does. But there are many things you have to try before you are sure. You try to let a small animal go into the water. You put it in a box with water on top. You close the box so the air is out. You wait. You watch. You try again and again.

Most of the time the animal will go in the water. This is good. It shows you that the water will not hurt it. But sometimes the animal does not want to go. It tries to get out. This is bad. You have to stop the box before the animal can go back over the side. You do this by putting a cover over the top. You keep the water in the box and the box is safe.

This is what you do with a small animal. You try to see what it can do. You try to see if it will do the things you want it to do. This is important for learning about how living things work.
