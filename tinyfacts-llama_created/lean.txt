To lean is to be on your side. You can lean to the left or to the right. When you lean to the right, your body is turned toward the side where you are sitting or standing. This is good if you are watching a movie. You can lean your head to the side and see what is happening.

You can also lean on something. If you have a chair, you can lean on it. This makes you feel safe. If you are feeling sad, you might lean your head on your hand. This is a way to hold a feeling inside.

If something is leaning, it means it is moving toward the side. Imagine a tree leaning toward the ground. The tree is leaning because of the wind. It is leaning so it does not fall over.

It is a word we use every day. It helps us talk about how things are.
