Think about a person who wants to be faster. They try to run more times. They eat food that helps them run. They also train their body. Their body becomes better at running. They can run much faster than before. They can do this because they are using the word to change the way they feel.

Another way to use this word is when you want to do something that is very good. If you work hard on a computer, you can do many more things quickly. Your computer becomes better at doing work. You can do more work in less time. Your work is more important because you are doing it well.
