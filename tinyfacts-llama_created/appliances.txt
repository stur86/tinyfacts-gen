An old word for things that do work.

You see a box that makes food. Inside the box, tiny things move. They move round and round and push air through small walls. The air goes out of the box and into your kitchen. When you put food inside, the box works. It turns the food into a kind of food that you can eat. The box is very good at this job.

You also see a thing that makes water. This thing is very small. It turns water from one kind into another kind. This is why you can have hot water and cold water in your room.

Some things are for cleaning. They make the floor and the table clean. They take away the yells and the stuff that makes you want to cry.

You can also see things that clean your hair. They go round and round and pull the hair. They make your hair feel good to touch.

Most of these things you do not think about. You just turn them on and they do their job. You do not need to be a doctor to know how they work.

Some old words tell us that things can be very strong. They can make the air hard to breathe. They can make the water cold. These things are not good to be around.
