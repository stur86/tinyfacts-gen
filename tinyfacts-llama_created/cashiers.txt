A person who works in a store to give you money when you buy things is called a shop person. They are usually in a place with many small boxes. Inside each box, the person takes money from you, and then they give you the money and the things you want.

Imagine you have money and you want to buy a thing you like. When you say, you want to buy a drink, you go to the person who works in the store. You tell them how much money you have, and they take it from you. Then they show you the drink and say, here is the money you gave me. You pay for the drink, and you get the drink.

Some people work as a shop person because they like being with many people all day. They must be very calm and not get angry if a person is very angry. They also must remember the money you gave them for each time you buy something.

A shop person can also work in a place that is only for computers. They are the ones who take money from your phone or from a computer, and then they give you the money in a different way. They do this all day, every day.

In some places, a person who works at the front of the store is also called a shop person. They are usually the one who answers the phone, and they help you when you have a question. They are very important because they can help you when you are confused.

People use these words to talk about how the stores are run. You can see people with these words in stores all around the world.
