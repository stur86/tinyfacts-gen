To control something means you are in a position to decide what happens. You are the one who decides what you want, what you do, and how you act.

Imagine you are in a room with people who do not want you to leave. You try to get out, but they do not let you. You are trying to get free, but they are holding you back. You are the one with the power to decide what happens.

You can control a thing that is not your own. You control a car, a dog, or a computer. You tell it what to do. You decide if it goes fast, slow, or stops. You decide if it turns right or left.

You can also control people. You are the one who tells them what to do. You decide if they should talk or if they should be quiet. You decide if they should follow you or do their own thing.

Sometimes, you control your own body. You decide to breathe or to stop your heart. You are in control of your own life. You are the one who decides what happens.

To control someone is to be in a position of power. You are the one who gives them orders. You tell them what to do and how to act. You are the one with the power.

To control something is not always easy. Sometimes, you have to be very carefully. You have to make sure you do not hurt anyone. You have to follow the right way. You have to stay safe.

But when you control something or someone, you are in a good place. You have the power and the power to decide what happens.
