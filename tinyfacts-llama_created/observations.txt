An eye is a thing that sees. When you look at something, your eyes look at it. Your eyes are important because they help you know what is happening around you.

We can not see the world without an eye. We do not see the sky, or the water, or the ground. We do not see the stars at night. But we see the people around us, the animals, and the things on the ground. Our eyes help us understand the world.

Sometimes you might watch something happen. You do not see it at first. Then your eyes see what is happening. You might see a dog running or a ball flying through the air. You might see a person talking or a car moving.

We also have many eyes on our bodies. Our face has three eyes. One eye looks at the side. The other eye looks at the front. The third eye looks at the back. This is how we see the world. Our eyes are very good at seeing.

An eye can be hurt. If something is hot, your eyes might feel burning. If something is bright, your eyes might feel like they are being opened wide. You might feel a bit of pain. But your eyes are strong. They can keep going even when you hurt them.

We also have eyes that are small on our faces. These eyes are often used for looking at people. We use them to see what they say. We look at their faces to understand how they feel. These small eyes help us talk to others.

In some places, people have eyes that are not like these. They have very bright eyes. These eyes can see light that is very far away. They can see things that are very small. These eyes help them see things that are hard to see with our eyes.

All of these eyes help us understand the world. They help us know about the people around us, the things around us, and the world around us.
