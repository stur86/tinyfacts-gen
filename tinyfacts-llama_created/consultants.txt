A person who works for a group of people, but is not a part of that group, is called a business person. They help the group make the right big picture for their work. They talk to many people, listen to what they say, and then they write a plan. This plan shows how the group will do things.

They might work with a person who wants to start a new business. They might work with a person who wants to buy a house. They might work with a person who wants to start a new food business. They do this work because it is better for everyone. They are not part of the group, but they help the group make the right move.

Sometimes, people who are not part of the group need help. They might need to figure out how to do something new. They might need to learn about a new idea. They need the help of the group so they can learn and get ready.

The word comes from the word to follow. People who work for a group of people follow the plan. They follow what the group is doing. They follow how they are doing it. They follow what they say they will do.

A business person can be very good at this job. They know how to make a plan that works. They know how to listen to many different ideas. They know how to make sure everyone is on the same side. They know how to stay calm in a big room with many people.
