To make a money is to get more money than you give. This makes you better. This is a good thing.

Think of a small store. You buy food and other things for your shop. You give the money to the person who runs the store. In this case, you give more money than you get back. The money stays in the store.

If you are a person who works, you might get paid more money for doing more work. This is also a good thing. You have more money. You can buy more food, clothes, and other things you need.

If you are a person who makes things, you might make more money from making more things. You have more money to spend. You can buy more houses, cars, or other things you want.

When a group of people work together to make things or run a store, they all get some money in return. They share the money. This is how everyone is better.

To get a money from something is to take it from you. You do this so you can do more work. You can then get more money, so you are better.
