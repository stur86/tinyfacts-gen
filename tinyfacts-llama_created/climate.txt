A warm place has a big warm air. The warm air goes up and comes back down many times. This goes on for a long time. The warm air makes the ground very warm. It makes trees grow very fast and makes the green things grow strong.

A cold place has a small cold air. The cold air goes up and stays up high. The cold air keeps things very still. It makes the ground very hard and cold. The cold air can make it hard to breathe.

A place with a lot of rain has a wet air. The rain falls from the sky many times. The air gets wet and then it goes back down again. This keeps happening many times. The ground stays wet but the ground does not get warm.

A place with a very little rain has a dry air. The air is very still. There is not much water in it. The ground can be hard and dry.

A big wide area of the world has a very good air. The warm air from the warm places goes into the big area. The cold air from the cold places goes into the big area too. This keeps the big area warm and green. The warm and cold air go around the big area many times. This keeps the world warm and green.

The word can also mean how things are. If you are in a warm place, you are happy. If you are in a cold place, you are sad. If you are in a place with a lot of rain, you might get wet. If you are in a place with a dry air, you might feel hot because your skin is dry.
