To put a thing out is to give it to the world. When a person or a group of people put a notice up, it tells everyone that something is happening. This is often done to make other people know about something.

Imagine you have a small business. You want to tell the world that you are open for business. You write a small piece on a piece of paper and put it on a street corner. The paper says you are open from morning to night and you are looking for people who want to buy things.

This is like a small word that lets everyone know what you are doing. It is a way to share what you have to offer with the world.
