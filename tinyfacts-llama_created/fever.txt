A high body feeling is what we call a cold, but it is more than that. You might feel hot and tired, and your face is red and you might be a little bit angry. It is like your body is saying, I need to make some more warm water.

When you get a cold, your body starts to make more red stuff. This red stuff goes out through your skin and into your hair and your clothes. It makes your face red and your eyes feel funny. It makes you want to stay in bed so you do not get worse.

Sometimes a cold can make you feel very bad. You might not want to eat or drink anything. You might feel like you want to cry. You might have to drink a lot of water or take a drink that has very few or no tiny bits.

Your body also makes more white stuff. This white stuff can be like a white cover on your face. It helps keep the white red stuff from getting into your brain. It is like a soft white cover. When you get a cold, you might feel like you have this soft white cover all over your body.
