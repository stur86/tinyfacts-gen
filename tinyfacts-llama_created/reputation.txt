A person has a good or a bad name. You know them, and you know how they act. If you are a doctor, people trust you when you say you can help them. If you are a friend, they know you will be there for them. Everyone has a name that people use when they talk about them.

When you do something good, people tell others about it. It is like a word that goes around. When you do something wrong, people talk about it too. This is how you get people to think about you. If you are kind, your name becomes good. If you do something you are afraid of, your name becomes bad.

You can also have a bad name if you do something wrong many times. This is called a bad story. You might have to change your name because of the bad name you have. It is like starting over.

A person can also have a good name if they do something very nice. People might call them a good person. This name is good because it shows how happy they are when they do good things.

Everyone has a name that is not just about who they are. It is about how they act, what they do, and how people think about them.
