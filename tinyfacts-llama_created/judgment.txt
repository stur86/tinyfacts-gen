To make a feeling about something

A person who makes a feeling about something is a just person. They look at what is happening and decide what is right or wrong. This is an important job.

When you act in a way that is not right, you can get a feeling. This feeling can be good, like when you are happy. Or it can be bad, like when you are afraid. This feeling is what we use to decide what to do.

A person can give a feeling to a group of people. They might say, in front of many people, that something is wrong. They might also give a feeling to one person. A person who does this can help the group understand what is happening and what to do.

Sometimes people need to make a feeling about something that is not clear. They might have to look at many things and then decide if one thing is right or one thing is wrong. They might ask other people for their help.
