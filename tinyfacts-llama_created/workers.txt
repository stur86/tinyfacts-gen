To work is to do things for a person or a group. A person works to get food, money, or clothes. They may go to a place where food is made or where clothes are made. They use their hands and their brain to do their job.

Some people work in a big office. They type on a computer and talk to other people who are far away. They do this job when no one else is there. They sit at a desk and work for a long time, but they are not always alone.

Other people work in a small house. They make things that people want to buy. They use their hands and their brain to make the things they want. They work hard to make sure everything is done right.

When you work, you do things for another person. You give them food, clothes, and money. You make them happy. You help them do their job.

There are also people who work on the ground. They walk around a city. They help people who are sick. They give them water and food. They are on the street until the day is over.

Being a worker can be hard. Some people work very long hours. They may work very little money. They may have to work when they are sick. But they are doing important work.

You can also work in a school. A teacher gives kids what they need to learn. A parents work with a child. They help the child do their work. They are helping the child grow.

If you want to work, you have to do your job. You have to follow the orders of your leader. You have to follow the way the work is done. You have to work hard and be safe.
