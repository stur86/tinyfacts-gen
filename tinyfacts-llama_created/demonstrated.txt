To show is to show something. When you want someone to see a thing, you say it is there. You do this by moving your hands or by using your voice. This makes the person understand that the thing is real.

Think about a small animal. You hold a picture of the animal and show it to another person. The person sees the animal and feels it in their heart. They know the animal is real. They did not just look at a picture.

When you say something is done, it means you did it. You did not just tell someone that you did it. You actually showed them. You were the one who did the work. Your words are like a picture. They show what happened.

Imagine you want to tell a friend that you love them. You use your words. You say, I love you. You have shown your friend how you feel. Your words are like a light in your heart. They show your true self to the world.

To show is important. It helps us understand each other. When you show a picture, you are telling a story. You are showing how a thing looks and feels. This is how we learn new things and remember old ones.
