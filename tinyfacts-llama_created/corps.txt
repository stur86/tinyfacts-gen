A group of people who work together for a reason is called a group. When many people work together, they form a team. They can be a group of friends or a group of people who do the same job. They have a leader and follow the leader. They also have their own way of doing things.

A group can be big. Imagine a group of hundreds of people. They do one thing together. They all work to get a job done. One person is the leader and does all the work. They all follow him.

A group can also be small. Imagine a group of three or four people. They all go to a party. They talk and laugh together. They are all friends.

A group can be made of many different kinds. There are groups of people who like the same kind of music. There are groups of people who like to run. There are groups of people who like to read books. There are groups of people who like to play games. There are even groups of people who like to eat food together.

People also form groups when they join a team. They are not alone anymore. They are part of a team with many other people. They work together to do a big job. They learn how to work with each other. They learn how to help each other.

The word can also mean something that is made of many parts. Imagine a group of different things that are all together. Each part has a job. When they all work together, they make a new thing. This new thing is strong and can do many things.

Finally, a group can be a place where people live. Imagine a group of houses on a street. The houses are all in one place. People live there and go to the same school. They all know each other.

In short, a group is a set of people who work together for the same reason. They can be big or small, made of different kinds of people, or made of many different things.
