A big water thing that moves across the land. It comes from a place with a lot of water. When it gets very strong, it goes over the high ground and the land gets covered in water.

The water from the big water thing goes over the city and the towns. People must move to other places because the water comes in and goes out. Many people lose their homes and their jobs. Some people move to other parts of the world.

The water can also make the roads and streets very bad. It can fill the old streets and make them hard to walk on. It can also make the water under the ground move, which can cause more trouble later.

Sometimes, the water is very high. This can hurt the people who live near the water. The water can also make the air hard to breathe. The water can also make the ground soft and fall apartments.

In the end, the water goes away, but the places where it hit are changed. The land is broken and the ground is still soft from the water. The water can come back again some years later, but it will look very different from before.

The big water thing is very strong and can do many things at once. It is a very big part of our world.
