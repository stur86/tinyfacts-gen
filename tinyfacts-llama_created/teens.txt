Teeth are hard things that grow from your mouth. You start to get them when you are very young. They are white and help you eat food. As you grow older, your teeth change and become smaller. You can only eat food that is soft or hard and small.

Most people are not very old yet when they start to get teeth. The first ones are often very small and very hard. Later, the small ones turn into the ones you can hold in your hand. By the time you are ten years old, you have almost all of your first set of teeth.

Teeth are important for eating. If you do not clean them, food can get stuck and cause hurt. You must brush your teeth every morning and before you go to bed. This helps keep your mouth clean and your teeth strong.

Some people are afraid of the hard, white parts on their teeth. They might try to bite them or pull them out with their fingers. This is not good. It can cause pain and make your mouth hurt. It is better to talk to a doctor if you feel this way.

You should also be carefully about the food you eat. Some foods can break down your teeth. You should only eat foods that are hard and small. Foods that are very soft or run can get stuck in your teeth and cause trouble.

Teeth are also important for your face. When you are happy, your face feels better. When you are sad or very cold, your face feels worse. Your teeth help show how you are feeling.
