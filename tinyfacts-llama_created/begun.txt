To begin something is to start it. It means you start a new thing. You can begin a new day. You can begin a new game. You can begin a new story. You start by doing one thing first.

Imagine you have a box with many things inside. You open the box and find a small thing. This is the first thing you do. You did not know what was inside. You did not expect it. You were surprised. This is what you call the beginning.

You can also begin something that is very old. A tree is old. It has been standing for many years. You begin to walk around the tree. You start to move. You step on the ground. You walk past the leaves. The tree is still there. You did not make it start. It just kept going for many years.

When you begin something, you can start it in a few ways. You can start with one thing. You might start with a word. You might start with a sound. You might start with a picture. You might start with a story. Each person starts something in a different way.

The word begun is used in many ways. You might say, I began to feel sad. This means you started to feel sad. You did not know how to feel before. You started to think about it. You started to cry. You began to understand why you felt this way.

You can also begin a new thing that will last a long time. You might start a new job. You start to work. You learn about the job. You start to do your work. You begin to feel like you are a part of the job. You are not just a person. You are a part of a team. You begin to feel like you are doing something important.

In the end, to begin something is to start a new story. It is the first step in a new place. It is the start of a new day. It is the start of a new life.
