To write something with a soft wet stuff is to use the word. This wet stuff goes on a piece of paper. When you press the paper against your hand, the wet stuff makes lines. You can write words, short lines, or many lines that tell a story.

You use a small thing that makes the wet stuff. This thing can be made of hard stuff or of a very wet and strong stuff. The small thing goes into a box filled with the wet stuff. You fill the box with the right kind of wet stuff and close the box.

The wet stuff is what makes the word real. If you do not use it, nothing is there to write. The wet stuff is also what makes the paper soft and can leave tiny marks if it gets wet again.

People use this word for many things. You can write a note to a friend. You can write a story for a book. You can write a sign for a store. You can write on the wall of a room so people know you are there.

The word can also mean to leave something behind when you go away. If you are afraid, you might use this word. You might say, I am afraid. This means I am afraid of something. You are afraid to go there because it is dark or because it is very loud.

To write something is to bring a story, a picture, or a feeling into words. It is a way to share your ideas with other people.
