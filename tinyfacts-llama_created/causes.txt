When something happens, it is because of many different things. Each of these things is a cause. Think of a tree and a rock. The rock is the cause. It is the one that makes the tree grow. The rock is the cause because without it, there would be no tree.

But how does the rock become the cause? The rock was already there before the tree started growing. Before the tree started growing, there was no rock. So, the rock is not the cause of the tree. It is a cause of the tree because it makes the tree grow. And the tree grows because of the rock. This is why the rock is the cause.

This is true for many things. A fire is a cause of a room burning down. The fire is the cause. It is the one that starts the burning. Without the fire, there would be no burning. The fire is the cause because it makes the room burn. The room is the cause because it gets hot and starts to break down. So, the fire is the cause of the room and of the burning.

You can also see this with your body. Your heart is the cause of your life. It is the one that makes your body move and your brain think. Your brain is the cause because it tells your body what to do. Your brain is the cause because it is the one that decides what to do. So, the heart is the cause of your life because it makes everything happen.

This word is important because it helps us understand how things work. It helps us see how one thing is the cause of another thing. Without a cause, there is nothing. But with a cause, there is something that starts to happen.
