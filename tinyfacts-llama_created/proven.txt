When something is known to be true, we say it has been shown to be right. This is a way to make a fact clear.

Think of a small thing you see every day. If you are sure that the floor is on the ground and you look at it, you have been shown. You have seen it with your eyes. You are not guessing.

In school, you learn facts by reading books or listening to people talk. When you find out something is true, you know you have been shown. This can also mean you have seen how it works.

A person might show you how to do a thing to help you. If a teacher tells you how to do a problem, you have been shown. You know how to do it now.

When you show you have learned something, you feel strong. You feel like you know the truth. You can trust what you know.

Sometimes, we show things to show we are right. If you want to say a thing is true, you can show it to others. This is a way to make sure everyone knows the same thing.
