To feel something means you are in it. When you feel a pain, you know it is there. You might see red or feel cold. It is a way you know what is happening.

You can also feel happy. When you love someone, you feel a happy feeling. You might smile and laugh. This is a good thing. It helps you know you are safe and happy.

Sometimes you feel sad. This is when you are very worried or angry. Your body might feel heavy. Your eyes might feel dry. You might want to cry. This is a way you show you are feeling a strong way.

You can also feel strange. This is when something is different from what you know. You might feel a strange feeling inside. You might feel like you are going to fall down. This can be a big thing to feel.

You can feel many things at once. Sometimes you feel happy and afraid at the same time. This is a lot to handle.
