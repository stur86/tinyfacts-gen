When you are afraid, you feel very bad inside. It is when you think something is going to happen that makes you want to stay where you are or go away. You might be afraid of being alone or of things that are very big or very loud. Sometimes, you feel afraid of people or animals.

Your body can tell if you are afraid. If you are afraid, your heart might beat faster. You might breathe very fast. You might also have a tight feeling in your chest. You might feel a cold feeling on your skin. You might want to hide or run away.

If you are afraid of a thing that is in front of you, it can be very hard to stay still. You might want to look at the thing or try to touch it. But if you are afraid of something that is inside your own body, you might feel even more afraid. You might feel sick or your body feels tight.

Being afraid can make you feel sad or worried. You might want to talk to a friend or a grown up about how you feel. They can help you feel better.
