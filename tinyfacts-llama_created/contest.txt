To do something is to act on a thing. When you say you want to run, you are doing it. You are moving your legs. You are not just thinking about it. You are acting on the word.

If you want to be the best, you do not just think about it. You do it. You train. You run. You eat well. You watch other people run. You see how they move. You try to do the same. You are doing something.

You can do this with people. You can have a game with friends. You tell each other to run. The person who runs the farthest gets a point. If a lot of people do it, the person who does the best gets the most points. The person with the most points is the best. They are the one who did it the best.

You can also do this with things. If you want to be the one who can do it, you do it. You try. You learn. You do it again and again. You become better. You are the one who can do it.

So, to do something is to act on a thing. It is about moving your body, your mind, or your heart. It is about being in the present. When you do it, you are living and you are happy.
