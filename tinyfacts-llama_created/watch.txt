To watch something happens when you follow it so you can see what is happening. You keep your eyes on it for a very long time. You might sit next to it or stand far away. This way you can know what is happening before someone else does.

You can watch the world around you. You might watch a dog run across the street. You might watch a ball fly through the air. You might watch a person walk down a street. Or you might watch a movie on a computer.

Sometimes you watch things that are moving. You might watch a car drive or a person run. You might watch a ball being thrown or a book being opened. You see how things move and how they are doing things.

You can also watch things that are not moving. You might watch a story on a computer. You see the words and the pictures. You learn about a person or a place. You see how people act and how things happen.

Sometimes you watch things that happen in your life. You watch when a friend is happy. You watch when a family is smiling. You see how they feel. You might feel happy too.

Watching is important because it helps you learn and understand what is going on around you. It helps you know how to act and how to be safe. It is a way to stay safe and to know what is happening in the world.
