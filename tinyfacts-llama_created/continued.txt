To continue something means you keep doing it after you started doing it. Think about a story. If you start a story, you have already done it. You have shared it with the people you care about. Now you want to tell it again. You might talk about the same things you already said. You keep the story going because you are happy about it. You are still in the middle of it.

When you continue something, you keep the same way. You do not start over. You do not change. You just keep doing what you did before. This is how you keep things going. You do not stop. You just keep going.
