To give up something you have because of another person is to let them take control. This word is used to talk about what happens when one thing is not enough for another person.

Think about a game where you are the best. You do everything and you get to keep the ball. But then you do not want to play anymore. You tell the other people you are too tired. You tell them you do not want to play. They are happy because you are giving up. They are happy because they have the ball.

This word is also used for people. If a person is tired of their job, they say they are giving up. They are not happy with what they do anymore. They want to do something else. They want to be happy.

But this word can also mean something that gives you something you do not want. If you eat a food you do not like, you give up. You stop eating it because it is not good. You do not want to touch it anymore.

To give up can be hard. Sometimes you want to keep something. But if the thing is not good for you, you might have to let go. This is why people say, you have to give up your things when you decide to change your life or move away. You have to give up the place you know, the job you love, or the things you are used to.
