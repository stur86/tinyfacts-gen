To notice means to see or hear something. You do not need to be right next to it. You can be in a room or in a house far away and still notice something. If you see a man walking down the street, you are noticing him.

If you are in a place and you hear a sound, you are also noticing it. You are paying attention. You are not just going through your day without paying any mind. You are paying attention to the world around you.

To notice can be a good thing. If you notice a person who is hurt, you can help them. If you notice a fire, you can call the police. It means you are seeing or hearing something and you are taking care of it.

Sometimes, when you are busy, you might not notice something. But if you are still there when it happens, you are not just doing nothing. You are paying attention. You are watching what is happening.
