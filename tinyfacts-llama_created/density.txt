To have a high or low number in a group or group of things means you have many of those things in one place.

Imagine you have a lot of small things, like tiny pieces of paper. If many of those pieces are very small, the group is very heavy. That is because the tiny pieces are heavy. This makes the group more hard to lift or move.

On the other hand, if many of those small pieces are very small, but you have many more pieces of a different kind, the group is light. The tiny pieces are not as heavy as the pieces of the different kind.

You can also talk about things that are heavy. A heavy chair is one that you can feel under your body. A heavy book is one that feels very much.

In the world of numbers, we use this idea. If you have many people, the group is very large. This is what we mean when we say something has a high number. If you have fewer people, the group is small, and you have a low number.

In the world of things, too, this idea helps. Imagine a huge room filled with many people. The room is very heavy. It is heavy because all those people are there. On the other hand, if the room is empty, it is very light. The room is light because there are fewer people in it.

We also use this word when talking about animals. If an animal has a high number of babies, it is very many. This is what we mean when we say something has a high number. If an animal has fewer babies, the number is low.

So, to have a high or low number is to have many of those things in one place. It helps us understand how many of something there are.
