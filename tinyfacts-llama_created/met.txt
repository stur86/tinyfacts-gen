To met someone means to see them. You are standing on a street and you look up. You see a friend or a family person. You want to talk to them. You stop and say, okay. Then you walk to their house.

You can also say you met a dog. If you see a dog in a park, you might say you met him. This means you saw him and he saw you.

When you meet a new person, you may want to know more about them. You might ask them their name, what they do, and where they live. This is called talking to them. You are using your words to learn about them.

Sometimes, you might meet a person who is not a person at all. Maybe they are a thing that makes things move. Or they might be a thing that makes sound. You see them and you do not know what they are or what they do. This is a strange kind of meeting.
