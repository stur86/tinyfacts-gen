To have something done is a way to show that something is real. When you tell someone you are going to do something, you are saying you have it ready. If you do not follow through, the word is used to say what happened.

When a person has done something, they have been done with it. This is why we use the word. If you tell a child you have done a job, they know that is the end of it. Now they do not have to tell you again. This is why we use words like this in our own lives too.

Sometimes, when something is done by a person you do not know, you might use the word. This is not always a good thing. If a bad person does something and you follow them, you are following them. It is not your job to follow them. You should say no if you do not want to follow them.

It is also important to know when to say you have done something. You might say this when you have finished a job at work. You are done, and now the day is over. Or you might say it when you have eaten food. You are done eating, and now you can do other things.

To be done with something is also a way to say you have been tired. If you have been running for a long time, you are done. You need to stop. You do not need to keep going any more.
