To make a movie is a big job. You need to write a story, make the pictures, and put them together on a computer. It is like making a very long song with a lot of words and pictures.

The computer has a big box inside. Inside the box, there are many tiny parts that go around very fast. The tiny parts go around the box many, many times. Each time, the tiny parts move the picture a little bit. After the box goes around many times, the picture is done. The picture is now a movie.

To make a movie, you need a lot of people. There are the ones who write the story, the ones who make the pictures, and the ones who put them together. There are also people who clean the computer, people who fix the tiny parts, and people who make sure everything is safe.

Sometimes, the people who make the movie are very tired. They work for many days and nights. They are always looking at the computer and thinking about what they need to do next. They are very good at their jobs. When they are done, they feel very happy.

Movies can tell stories about things that happened in the past. They can also show us how to feel. They can show us happy times, sad times, and times that are very hard to understand. People all over the world watch them to relax and to learn.

You can also make a movie with pictures and words. You use a computer to put the pictures and words together. You can make movies about animals, about people, or about places. You can make movies that are very short or very long.

In some places, people make movies by hand. They draw the pictures and write the words on a piece of paper. They show the movie to other people. This is a good way to share stories and to teachers.
