To stay means to be here in a place for a long time. You do not go away. You live and you stay.

Imagine you are at a big house with your family. You have a bed and a table to eat from. You have a chair to sit in. You do not go to another town. You stay in this house and you do not leave.

Sometimes you stay in a room when you are not at home. You might stay in a school when you are not at school. You stay in a room so you can rest when you are tired.

If you stay in a place for too long, you might feel sad. You might miss your friends and your family. You might feel like you are stuck there. But if you stay and you learn about the place and the people, you can become a good person.

You can also stay in a place when you are waiting. If you are waiting for a phone call, you might stay in your room. You stay and you think about what you want to do next. You stay and you wait.

To stay is to be here. It is to live in one place for a long time.
