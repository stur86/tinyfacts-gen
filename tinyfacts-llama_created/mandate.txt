To make a thing do something is to give it a power that tells it what to do. This is what a word like this does. A word is not a thing, but it can tell us how to act.

Think about a teacher. The teacher has a job and a set of things they must follow. They must say a few words before the bell rings. They must not talk to the other students during the time the teacher is there. They must not touch the things in the room. They must listen to what the student writes. If they do not follow these things, they might get in trouble.

The word we use today is from this idea. It means to tell someone what they must do. A group of people might make a word that says everyone has to wear a shirt on a day. Everyone who does not wear the shirt is not allowed to be there. This is like a power that tells everyone what to do.

Sometimes, people use the word to mean something is expected of them. If you are supposed to be home at tonight, you must be there. If you do not follow this, you might get in trouble with your family. The word helps us stay in line.

In a place where people live, there is often a word that tells everyone what they must do. This word might say that everyone must help with the work or pay their money on time. It is a way of keeping everyone in one place.
