To make a thing is to take something and turn it into something else. This happens when you use your hands and your mouth.

You start with a piece of hard stuff. This is the ground that makes your feet feel good when you walk. This is the ground under your car when you drive. This is the ground under your bed. It is the ground that is all around you.

You start with this ground. You pick it up. You put it into a big box. The box holds the ground. Now the ground is inside the box.

The box is very small. It is like a tiny room. Inside the box, the ground is not changed. It stays the same. But outside the box, the ground is changed. It is moving. It is falling. It is moving around.

If you open the box, the ground will come out. You can walk on it. You can move it with your feet. You can use it to make a road. You can use it to make a floor for a house. You can use it to make a top for a building. You can use it to make a floor for a car.

You can also make the ground from other things. You can take some of the ground and add it to a box. Then the box has more ground inside it. You can use this new ground for the same reasons as before. You can use it to make a road, a floor for a house, or a top for a building.

This is how you make new ground. You start with something old and turn it into something new. You are always making new ground.
