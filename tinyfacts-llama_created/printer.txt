A computer is a thing that does many things at once. It is like a very big box with a lot of numbers and a bright box that shows the words you want. You can tell the computer what to do with those numbers and words.

When you want to write a big word, you type it into the computer. The computer reads the words from your hand and then it makes them into the bright box. The bright box shows the words in a way that looks like the words you see on paper. You can see the words right away.

Some computers can also make pictures. You can tell the computer to make a picture of a face. It looks like the face you saw before. It is like a very good friend that always does what you want.

There are many different kinds of computers. Some are very small and can fit in your hand. Some are very big and have many parts inside them. Some have many hundreds of lines of numbers and many bright boxes. Some are used to work with numbers, while others are used to make pictures.

Computers do not need air to live. They use power from the wall. You can turn them on and off as you want. When you turn them on, the power starts and the computer starts to work. When you turn them off, the power stops and the computer goes quiet.

Computers are very important. They help us write books, talk to people over long lines, and show pictures to our eyes. They are the things we use every day to do many different jobs.
