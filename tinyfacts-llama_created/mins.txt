A minute is a small time that is one hundred seconds. You might hear a person say, for one minute, to tell you how long something took.

Think of a minute like a small group of seconds. If you are waiting for a drink, one minute will pass very quickly. You can listen to a song, look at a picture, or read a few lines of a book before you know it is one minute gone.

You can have one minute, two minutes, three minutes, or more of this time. A minute is a tiny bit of time, like a tiny drop of water.
