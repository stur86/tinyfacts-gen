To be in a place at a time means you are there. When you say a book is on a table, you mean it is in a room, not far from you.

Imagine you are in a school. You have a class to be in. If the class starts right now, you will be there. If the class starts later, you will not be in the room. You are in a place at a time. You do not have to wait for something to happen before you can do it.

You can be in a place with many people. Maybe your friend is there. You are with them. You have fun together. Or you can be in a place alone. You might be in a room and no one is around. You can do what you want and you are alone.

You can also be in a place when you are not there. You might be watching a movie. The people are in another room, but you are in your room. You are in a different place, but you are still there. You are not really in one place, but you are in one way.

Being in a place can be hard. Imagine you want to go to a far land, but you do not have a way to get there. You are in a place, but you are not in the place you want to be. This is when you feel like you are not where you should be.
