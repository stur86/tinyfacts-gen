To stop something means you stop what you are doing. You can stop a car, a dog, a group of people, or even the air.

Imagine you are walking down a long road. You come to a big stop sign. You do not go any more. The sign says: stop, and you must not move. You must stand still until the light turns green again. If you go any faster, the police will come and take you away.

You can also stop a story. If you are very angry, you might stop the story. You stop telling what happened next. You stop the people who are listening. You stop the story from going any further.

Sometimes, you stop to think. If you see a big tree, you might stop to look. You look at the tree, the sky, and the ground. You do not move until you decide if you want to move on.

You can also stop a feeling. If you feel very sad, you might stop your tears. You stop smiling and stop feeling happy. You just stand there, feeling the pain.

Finally, you can stop a fight. If two people are fighting, you can stop them. You use your words to calm them down. You say, I do not like it either. We can stop the fighting and start talking again.

So, to stop something is to stop doing it. It is to change your plan or your mind. It is to stop making noise or making a lot of air.
