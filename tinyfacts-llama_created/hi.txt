To say hi is to say that you see someone. When you say hi, you are letting the other person know that you are there.

You can say hi in many ways. You can say hi when you meet a friend. You might say hi when you see a family person. Or you might say hi when you see a person you do not know.

If you are in a room and you see a person, you might say hi. This can help the person know that you are there and that you are not angry. It is like a small way to say, I am here.

You can also say hi when you are leaving a place. You might say hi as you are going out the door. This can help the people in that place know that you are going to another place.

Sometimes, if you say hi to a person, but you do not know them, you might feel a little strange. You are not really sure what to do with the person. But if you say hi to a person and then you talk with them, you are showing that you are interested in them.

To say hi is important. It helps people know each other. It is a small way to start a conversation. It shows that you are kind and that you care about the other person.
