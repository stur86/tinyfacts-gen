A bests reader is something that many people like to buy. These books are often very good. They have many good words and are easy to read.

A person might write a book about a story. If many other people like this story, then the book becomes a bests reader. The people who buy these books like to read them again and again. They feel happy to have found a book that they like.

Some books are bests for children. These books are easy to understand and have fun stories. Children might read these books alone or with a friend. They learn new things and have fun.

Other books are bests for grown people. These books have hard words and ideas. People who read these books like to learn more about the world. They read them to understand why things happen.

A bests reader can be a book, a story, or a picture. It is something that many people enjoy.
