An air line is a way to move many people and things from one place to another in the sky. These lines use big, strong pieces of air that go up and down in the sky, and people fly on them. The people on these lines are often called the air line people.

These lines have many cars in them. The cars are very big and strong. They carry many people at the same time. The people work very hard to get from one place to another. They have to know where they are going and how they get there.

Some air lines fly very fast. They can go from one city to another in just a few hours. Some go so fast that you can see the ground while you fly! Some go so slow that you have to sit and watch the ground go by for a long time.

Some air lines carry animals. They have big cars with rooms where the animals can sit. The animals are often happy to fly. Some people pay a lot of money to fly to a far away place with these animals.

There are many different kinds of air lines. Some are very small. They only carry a few people. Others are very big. They carry many people at once. Some air lines go to many different places. Some go only to one or two places. The lines can go very high into the sky or very low down to the ground.

Sometimes people fly on these lines for work. They go to different cities to talk to people. Some people fly on these lines for fun. They might go to a far away place to see a big city or to go to a far away place to see the water.
