Three is a number that comes after two and before four. It is a very important number in the world.

When you have three things, you can do many things. You can hold three books, three pieces of food, or three friends. This is a big number because it helps us understand how many things are in front of you.

Imagine you have three legs. You can stand on each leg. You are standing on three legs. This is the same with numbers. If you have one, two, or three, you are three in number.

Three is also a number of days in a week. We have three days of the week. We start with one day and end with three days. This helps us keep our days and weeks going.

Sometimes, when people talk about three, they mean three people. This is a group of three people who are friends. If you have three friends, you are happy. If you have three money, you can buy more things.

Three is a number that helps us see how many things are around us. It is a number that we use every day in our lives.
