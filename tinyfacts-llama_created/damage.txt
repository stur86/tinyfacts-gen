To cause a problem can be a very bad thing. Think of a small thing that you use to write. If you push the paper too hard, it breaks. You have caused a problem.

You can also cause problems for other people. If you tell a lie, you have caused trouble for the other person. If you make someone sad because you are angry, you have caused a problem.

When you break something, you are the one who causes the problem. You are the one who makes it worse. If you are the one who breaks something, you are the one who has the problem.

You can also cause a problem in your own life. Maybe you feel angry because someone did something wrong. This can make you feel sad or angry too. The problem is that you are the one who is causing it.

In a big group, problems can happen many times. If one person does something wrong, the others might get angry. They might fight or yell. This can cause a problem for everyone.
