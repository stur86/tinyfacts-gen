A friend is a person you know and like. They are people you talk to every day. You might go to school with them. You might play games together or watch movies. You share your hopes and fears with them. You trust them and they do the same for you.

You can have a friend from your town. You might live next to them. Or you might have a friend in a different city. You can talk to them on the phone or send a short note. You can also meet them at a park or a place you both like.

A friend is not a person you know from school. They are a person who cares about you. They might help you when you are sad. They might tell you funny stories. They might give you a big hug. They are people you feel safe with.

You can also be a friend to animals. You might take them to a park. You might let them run around in a field. You show them you care. This is a kind thing to do. It makes everyone feel happy.

Sometimes, a friend is a person you do not know very well. You meet them at a party. You talk for a while. You do not know much about them yet. But you feel a little bit happy. You might feel a little bit excited. This is okay. You can still be a good friend to them. You can be a friend who listens.

A friend is a person who is kind to you. They do not laugh at you. They do not make you feel bad. They just want to be near you. They want to help you. They want you to feel safe.
