To go means you move from one place to another. You can walk or run. You can also use a car, a bus, a train, or a small flying thing that can carry people.

When you go, you leave your home. You might go to a city to see people, or you might go to a far away place to see a big city or a beautiful area with green trees and big water. You might go to the store to buy food or clothes, or you might go to the movies to see a movie.

Going can be a safe thing. You might go to school or work. You might go to a place where you live to pick up your things. If you are afraid, you might go to a place that has a strong light or a quiet room. Going can also be a fun thing. You can go to a park to run, or you can go to a place with a big water to play in the water.

Sometimes, going means to change your life. If you work very hard for a long time, you might go to a new job. If you learn a new game or a new way of doing things, you might go to a place to learn more. Going can help you grow and learn new things.
