A person or thing you buy from when you want something new. You tell a store what you want, and they send it to you. If you are a person, you buy things to help you live. If you are a computer, you buy parts so it can do more work.

When you buy something, the person does not care if you are happy or not. They just want you to pay. But if you are a friend, you might be happy to help them. You might buy them food or a drink. This is not about paying money, but about helping each other.

Sometimes, the word can mean a person who works at a store. They help you find what you need. They also answer your questions when you are lost. They are important because they help you get what you need.

We also use this word to talk about things that are not people. A car is a thing you drive to get from one place to another. A phone is a thing that lets you talk to your friend. A computer is a thing that does many hard jobs for you. A house is a place where people live. A food is something you eat. A car is also a thing that goes very fast.
