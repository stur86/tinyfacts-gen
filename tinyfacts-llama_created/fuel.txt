To give someone or something something to do is to give them power. It happens when you put something inside a thing that needs to move. You put the power into the thing, and it starts to go. This power can be from the sun, from the ground, from animals, or from burning things.

Imagine you have a car. You put some power inside the car. The car starts moving. It takes you to a place, or it helps you get home after a walk. If you give the power to a dog, the dog will run and play. The power is what makes it move.

You also give power to people. If you give money to a teacher, the teacher can buy food for the children or buy books for the school. If you give a small animal food, it will eat and grow. If you give a person a job, they can do work and pay money for doing it.

In a fire, the power is what makes the fire go. When you give a small piece of wood to a fire, it starts to burn. The wood gives the power to the fire, and the fire keeps burning until it reaches the wood.

To need something is to want it. If you need a drink, you want to drink water. If you need money, you want to buy something you need. When you need power, you want to move your body or do work.
