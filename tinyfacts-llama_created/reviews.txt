To write a story about a movie is a big job. The writer has to tell what happens. The writer tells this story to many people. This is how we learn about the story.

If you watch a movie and you like it, you can tell others. You might write a few words. You might say, this movie is the best. You do not have to be a writer. You tell what you think. Your words can help others decide if they want to watch the movie.

If you did not watch the movie, you can also write a word. You might say it is a good movie. This is also how others learn. They read your words. If you write many words, people start to know the movie is good.

Sometimes, people make their own movies. They write a story and make a movie with their own words. This can be a fun way to tell a story. They make it happen all by themselves.

Everyone gets to see a movie. They watch it and they say what they think. You can read these words. You can learn about the movie. It is a way for many people to share their ideas with each other.
