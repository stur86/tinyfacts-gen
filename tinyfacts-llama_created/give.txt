To give something is to share it with others. You can give your food to a friend when you are full. You can give your book to a child to read. You can also give your time to help a person who is in need. This helps people who are sad or hurt.

Imagine you have a box of bright colors. You want to share them with your friends. You open the box and give them each a color. Now everyone has a color, and they can use it to make their own things.

Sometimes you give money to someone who needs it. You do this because you care about them. You want them to have the things they need to live a good life.

You can also give a word to someone. If you are very happy, you might say, I am so happy! You are telling others that you are very happy. This helps them feel happy too.

Giving is a way to show love and care. It is a way to make the world a better place.
