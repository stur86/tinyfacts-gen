The word caller is a man who drives a car. He goes from one place to another. The car moves very fast. He uses a round thing on the front of the car to make the sound people hear. The round thing is made of small hard pieces that turn round and round. When the car moves, the round thing makes the sound.

A caller can drive in the dark too. The car has lights on the front and back. The lights help the driver see at night. The driver can also hear the sounds from other cars. This helps the driver stay safe.

Some caller cars are very fast. They can go very many hundreds of feet perfect second. The driver must stay very still and follow the road carefully. They also must follow the signs on the road.

You can call a caller a car driver. This is because they drive the car. They are in control of the car. They decide how to go and where to go. They use their brain to make sure they are safe.
