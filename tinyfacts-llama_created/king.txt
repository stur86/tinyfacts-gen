To be a kind person is to act in a way that makes everyone feel safe. A kind person does not fight. They do not yell. They do not make the other person angry. They let everyone talk.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You want to have fun. If you take a piece of food from their bag, they feel sad. A kind person would say, please give me a piece of your food. They do not want to fight.

A kind person also helps those who are hurt. They give money to those who are very sick. They tell the doctor, the police, or the teacher if something is wrong. They want everyone to feel better.

In a group of animals, a kind person is the one that keeps everyone safe. They find a safe place in the dark and keep everyone there. They do not run away when they hear a loud sound. They stay and help the others.

Being kind is important for everyone. It makes everyone feel happy. It helps us feel safe and happy.
