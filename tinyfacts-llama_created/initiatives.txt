An idea is a plan for doing something. It is a thing you want to happen. Sometimes people have an idea and they try to make it happen. They look at what is already there and think about how to change it. They might ask others to help them. They might also talk to people who work in different places to see if they can do things together.

When people start a new idea, they often call it an attempt. This is because they are taking a step forward. They are trying to see if their idea is the right one. They might face many problems along the way, but they do not give up.

Some people try to start many different ideas. They want to see if one idea is better than another. They look at how different people act and how different things work. They try to understand what is wrong with each idea and what is right.

It is also important to remember that an idea is not always set. Ideas can change as people learn more or as they see how things work. Sometimes an idea is not the best and another idea becomes the one that is better.
