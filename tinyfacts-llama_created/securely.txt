To be in a safe place means you are not afraid. You know that the thing is not going to hurt you. You feel calm inside.

Think of a box. If you put a heavy thing inside the box, it will stay in the box. The box is in a safe place. You can close the box and feel safe.

You can also be in a safe place in your own mind. If you are not afraid of a big thing, you can stay calm. You do not feel a strong feeling of fear.

You can feel safe when you are with a person you trust. If a friend is near you, you feel safe. You do not feel like you are going to get hurt.

In life, being in a safe place is important. You can be in a safe place at home, in a school, or in a place that is far away. It means you are not in a place that could hurt you.

Being in a safe place does not mean you are not doing anything. You can still work, learn, or play. You are just in a place where you feel safe.
