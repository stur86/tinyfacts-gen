When we say a race, we are talking about people who move quickly. They go over a long line of ground. This line is often marked with lines and signs. The first person to cross the line gets a small thing.

Most races are about running. A long time ago, people did this for a long time. They wanted to see who was the fastest. They would run in front of each other, and then they would all meet at the end. The person who crossed the line first was the best.

Some races are about other things. There is a race for cars. Cars go very fast over a long line of ground. There is also a race for people who fly. These people fly high in the sky and cross lines before they reach the end.

A race can also be about animals. A group of animals might go over a long line of ground. They are trying to get to a place that is their favorite. Sometimes the animal that gets there first gets a small thing.

Sometimes, people hold races for fun. They might run around a big area. They might jump over a wall. They might do other things to see who can do them the best.
