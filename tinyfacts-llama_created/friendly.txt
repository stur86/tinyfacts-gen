To be a good friend is to have nice ways. A person who is a good friend is kind. They do not yell at you or make you feel bad. They listen when you talk. They are the same as you. They make you feel safe. They do not take your things without asking. They do not say bad things about your family or your house.

If a person is a bad friend, they might do things that hurt you. They might say mean things. They might tell lies. They might not want to talk anymore. They might not want to see you. They do not care about you anymore.

A good friend is someone who is always there for you. They help you when you need it. They tell you the truth, even if it is not what you want to hear. They help you feel better when you are sad. They make you feel happy when you are happy. They show you that you are loved.

You can also be a good friend if you are a kind person. You do nice things for others. You help a person who is hurt. You listen to a person who is afraid. You are a good friend because you care about the other person.
