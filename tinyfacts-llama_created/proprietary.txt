A thing that is owned by one person and kept safe is called a thing that is owned by one. This is the way you are with your money, your car, or your phone. When you have this, you are the one who has it.

A thing that is owned by one person and given to another is called a thing that is given. This is how you are with your books at school or your computer at work. When you give something to another person, you are giving it to them. You are not keeping it anymore.

Something that is owned by one person but not given to anyone else is called a thing that is kept. This is how you are with your money, your car, or your phone. You keep it and you do not give it away. You are the one who keeps it.

When you own something, you have the right to do what you want with it. You can use it, keep it, give it to someone else, or you can throw it away. If you do not follow these steps, it is not your thing anymore. The thing becomes something else, and someone else has it.
