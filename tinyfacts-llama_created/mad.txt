To be mad is to feel very angry. It is when you are so mad that you want to shout. You might want to hit someone or things. This is because your brain is too hot inside your head.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You are happy. You love the feeling. But then you see a dog. You see the dog. You want to fight. You want to hit the dog. You are mad. You are angry because of the dog.

You can also be mad because you are afraid. Maybe you are afraid of a big animal. You are afraid to go near it. You want to hide. This is also a kind of being mad. Your brain is too hot, but you are afraid.

Sometimes, we just feel mad for a few minutes. We might feel angry because someone did something bad. We might shout and cry. This is okay. We can be mad and still be okay.

But sometimes, being mad can be very bad. If you are mad for too long, your heart gets very heavy. You might feel like you want to kill someone. This is a big problem. People who are mad often hurt others. They might say things they do not mean.

We need to learn how to be mad in a good way. If we are mad, we can shout and cry. This helps us. It shows everyone that we are very angry. This can help us stay safe. We can also try to calm down. We can breathe and think about what we are doing. This helps us feel better.

Sometimes, we are mad because we love someone very much. We love them so much that we get very angry when they do not want to be with us. This is a good kind of mad. It shows how much we care.
