A person who is old is often called a man or a woman. They have lived for a long time and see many things. They know many stories and things that people do not know yet.

A person who has a lot of good ideas is often called a man of many words. They write many books and give talks. They try to help people understand the world.

A person who is kind and has a quiet way of thinking is often called a man of calm things. They do not get angry or shout. They stay calm and are happy. They help other people when they need it.

A person who loves animals is often called a man of animals. They like to look at the animals and understand how they live. They might help animals in a place where there are no homes for them.

A person who is very good at numbers is often called a man of numbers. They work with numbers every day. They build things and make money.

A person who is very good at talking is often called a man of words. They can tell people things quickly. They might speak with a lot of words so that everyone can understand.

A person who has a lot of love for a place is often called a man of places. They like to go to the same places. They feel happy when they are there.

A person who is very good at things is often called a man of things. They can do many things well. They might be a man of music, a man of books, or a man of games.

A person who is very good at making things is often called a man of things. They make things that help people. They might make a chair for a room or a car for a road.

All these words are about people. They tell us who they are and what they do.
