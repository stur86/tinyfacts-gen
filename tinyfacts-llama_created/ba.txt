Back

A back is a part of a body. It is the part behind you. On your back you have a round area at the top. This round area is your head. You can lift your head up to look around.

You also have long lines on your back. These lines help you walk. They help you step from one foot to the next. When you walk, your back moves from side to side.

Sometimes people use the word back to talk about the past. If you say, I have a bad back, it means you have a problem with your body that makes you feel tired or hurt.

You can also use this word to talk about a story. A back story tells us about a person before they became who they are today. It helps us understand their past.
