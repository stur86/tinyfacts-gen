This head can also send people to the head of another head. They tell the new head about the first head. This way, all the heads work together and everyone is safe.

If the head is very good at making sure everyone is safe and happy, the people might give the head a lot of money so the head can buy more things to keep everyone safe. If the head does not keep everyone safe, the people might get very angry and the head might lose their power.
