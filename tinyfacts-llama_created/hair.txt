Hair is the long stuff that grows from your head. It is very important to you because it makes you look nice. You can have one type of hair or many different kinds.

Hair comes from tiny things inside your body. These tiny things grow and move along the ends of your hair. When the hair grows, it pushes the tiny things out through the top. This makes the hair look like a line going out from your head.

You can have hair that is short and strong, or hair that is long and soft. Some people have hair that is very black and dark, while others have hair that is light brown and bright. The color of your hair can change as you grow. When you are very young, your hair is usually a light color. As you get older, your hair might become darker.

Hair can be very thick or very thinking. Thick hair can cover a lot of your head and make you look round. Thinking hair is very fine and can make your face look round too. Some people have hair that is very strong and can fall out easily. Other people have hair that is very easy to grow back.

Hair is also very important for your body. It helps keep your head warm in cold days. It helps keep your head clean by catching tiny bits of food. It can also help you feel good when you touch it. Many people like to play with their hair because it is fun to run through or pull.

Sometimes, hair can get hurt. If you pull on it too much or if you get a cut, your hair can fall out. This can be very sad because your hair is a part of you. But most of the time, hair grows back.
