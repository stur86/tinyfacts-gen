An excuse is a thing you say to get out of a problem. You might say you were tired when you were late to a meeting. You might say you forgot something. You might say you were scared.

When you are late, you might say, I was running late because I was sick. When you have a fight with a friend, you might say you were sad and you did not want to talk. You use the word to get away from the thing that happened.

You do not have to be the one who started the trouble. The person you are sorry for is the one who did the thing that made you angry. But you still have to tell the truth.

Sometimes, an excuse is not very good. If you say you were afraid to go to the doctor because you did not want to see the doctor, you did not really want to go. You were just afraid to say you had a problem. This is a bad way to act. A good way to act is to tell the truth, even if it is hard.

You can also use an excuse to hide something. If you eat a bad thing, you might say you were sick. If you lie, you are not an excuse. You are a lie.
