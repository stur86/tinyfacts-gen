An idea is a thing you think about. It is a thought or a plan that you want to try to make real. You can have an idea that makes you want to act, or an idea that makes you want to think about things in a different way.

Think about a time when you saw something new and it made you wonder. You might think about how it would happen or what it would look like. You have an idea in your head. You can not always say what you want to say, but you have an idea that is there in your mind.

Sometimes an idea is something big. It is a plan for how the world will change. Sometimes it is something small. It is a way to do something or a way to feel.

You can have many ideas at once. You might have one idea and another idea at the same time. You might be working on an idea for a long time and then you get another idea that makes you want to change what you are doing. You can have a lot of ideas and change them as you learn.

Ideas help us. They help us understand the world and what we are doing. They help us decide what to do or how to act. They help us be better than we are today.
