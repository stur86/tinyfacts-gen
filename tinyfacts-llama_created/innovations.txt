An idea that changes how things work is called an idea. An idea can help make life better. It might tell us how to grow food more quickly. It might help people live in warmer places with more air. Ideas can come from many different places. They can come from people who think about how things work, or from people who see how things are used every day.

Some ideas are small. They might change one thing at a time. Other ideas are bigger. They change many things at once. They might change how we walk, how we fly, or how we live in our homes.

Some ideas are good for people who are very old. They help them do things they can't do anymore. Other ideas are good for people who are very young. They help them learn new things and make new friends. Even though some ideas are old, we still use them today because they work well.

Everyone gets to decide what ideas are good. Some people might say that some ideas are better than others. This is because people have different ways of thinking.
