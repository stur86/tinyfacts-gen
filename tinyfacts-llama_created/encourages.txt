When you want something to happen, you can use the word to help. It tells us to make sure that people or things are happy or that a good thing happens.

If you want a friend to be your friend, you can say,  This is what you mean. This means you want them to be nice to you and to you alone. It is like a way of telling another person to like you.

If you want a game to work well, you can use this word. You want the game to be fun and to keep everyone happy. It is important to say this so everyone knows what you mean.

We also use this word to talk about the sun. When the sun is in the sky, it is very bright. If you want the sun to stay in the sky, you say it. You are telling everyone that the sun should stay there.

Sometimes, when you are talking, you might use this word with a person. You might say,  You are a good person. This means you are a person who does good things and is kind.
