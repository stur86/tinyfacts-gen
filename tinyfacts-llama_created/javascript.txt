Jacket

A jacket is a thing you wear to keep warm when it is cold. It is usually made of soft stuff and has a front that opens so you can move around easily. Some jackets are very long and cover your whole body. Others are short and only cover your arms and legs.

People wear jackets to work, to go to school, or to run around. They can also wear them when they go to a place that is far away, like a city or a big town. Sometimes, a jacket is very pretty. It might have a nice color, a fun cut, or a cool way it looks around the neck.

You can also wear a jacket to feel safe. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might wear a jacket that makes you feel safe. It can be one with a hard hard part on it, or one with a soft cover that makes you feel like you are in a safe place.

In some places, people use the word jacket to talk about a small thing that is on their person. When someone asks you what you are wearing, they might ask if you are wearing a jacket. This is because a jacket is a kind of thing that you wear on your body, and it helps keep you warm.
