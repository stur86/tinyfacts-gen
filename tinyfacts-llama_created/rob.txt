To take something that is not yourself

You can take money from someone, or you can take a thing that is not yourself. This is when you take something that is not yourself. You do not have to say why, and you do not have to do anything to get it. You just take it. If you take money, you might feel good. If you take something that is not yourself, you might feel bad. It is a way of getting what you want, without being good or right.

You can also use this word with animals. If you are a dog and you find a ball, you might want to have it. Then you can play with it. If you are a cat and you see a ball, you might want to have it too. You do not have to say why. You just want it.

Sometimes, you might be afraid of taking something. You might feel nervous or worried. You might want to leave it alone. But you still do it anyway. You take the thing. This is when you are doing something that you do not want to do.
