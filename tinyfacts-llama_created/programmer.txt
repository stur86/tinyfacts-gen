A person who makes things for computers and other computers is called a computer person. They write what the computer should do. They tell the computer how to use things like a door, a chair, or a car.

Some computer people only work with the computer part. They do not touch the parts inside the computer. They write what the computer will do. Other computer people touch the parts. They make sure the computer works well. They fix things when the computer makes a wrong move.

There are many different kinds of computer people. Some make games for children. Some make things for doctors to help them do their jobs better. Some make things for schools so that teachers can show pictures and stories to their students. Some make things for people to carry in their bags. Some make things to help people move from one place to another.

A computer person must learn a lot about how the computer works. They must know what the computer can and what it can not do. They must also know how to follow the steps the computer does.

Some computer people work alone. They sit in a room with a computer in front of them. They tell the computer what to do. Other computer people work in teams. They all sit together and talk about what they want to build. They use what everyone has learned from each other to make a better computer person or a better computer.

A computer person can work for many years. They can build many different things. They can work for a small group of people or for a very big group. They can make things that are not found in stores. They can make things for people to use every day.
