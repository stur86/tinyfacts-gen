To put someone to sleep is to make them very quiet and still. This happens when a person is very tired or feels sad. They might be lying down and their body starts to feel heavy. Their face becomes soft and their eyes drop.

Imagine you are in a quiet room with many people. If you start to feel very tired, you might close your eyes and wait for everything to be quiet. When this happens, you are not listening anymore. You are not thinking about anything. Your body is not moving, and you are not doing anything.

If you put someone to sleep, you might not wake them up at first. They might stay there for a while, maybe even a few minutes, before they start to feel their body wake up. When they wake up, they might be confused and look around. They might feel a bit scared or worried if they remember that someone was in their room.

Sometimes, people put someone to sleep to make them feel better. If a person is very sad, they might lie down and close their eyes. A kind person might sit next to them and talk softly. This can help the person feel less alone and more calm. But putting someone to sleep is not always safe. It can hurt their body or make them feel very cold.
