To go before something means you come before it. If you go to school before the day starts, you have done this. You are the first one in the room.

You can also go before something that happened. If you were the first person to go to the movie, you went before everyone else. You were the one who started the story.

Think about the days of the week. Each day comes before the next one. You start with the first day of the week. It is the day before.
