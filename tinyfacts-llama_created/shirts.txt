A shirt is a piece of wear you put on your body. It is a short piece of clothes made of soft stuff. You can wear it over your other clothes or on its own.

You can wear a shirt for many reasons. You might wear one to stay warm in the cold. Or you might wear one because it looks nice. Some shirts are very long and cover your whole body. Others are short and only cover your chest and arms.

Shirts can have many different parts. One part is the front. This is where you put your face and your hair. You might wear a shirt with a big front so you can show your chest. Another part is the back. This is where the shirt covers your back and sometimes your arms.

You also wear shirts for fun. You might wear one with funny words or pictures on it. You might wear one with a very tall neck so you look like a tall person. Some shirts are for work. You might wear one to go to a school or a job.

In some places, you do not wear a shirt at all. This is okay if you are very hot or very cold. You might wear a top or a dress instead. But if you go to a party or a fun place, it is a good idea to wear a shirt.
