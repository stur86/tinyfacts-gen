To build something means you use your hands or a big thing to make it from the ground. You start with a tiny piece. You take the piece and you add more and more pieces to it. The pieces go down and the thing gets bigger.

You can build a house, a car, a table, or a chair. You use wood, glass, or other hard things. You might use a big box and fill it with the pieces, or you might use a person and many hands to put the pieces together.

Sometimes people build a house for a family or a group of friends. They want a home where everyone can live together. Other times, people build a thing for fun. They build a game or a thing to play with. You can also build a thing to help a person who is sick.

To build something is to start from nothing. You take nothing and you turn it into something new and strong. It is an important job.
