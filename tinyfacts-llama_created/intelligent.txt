An animal that is very good at thinking is called an animal. Animals have many parts inside their bodies. They also have a brain. The brain is the part of the body that does the thinking.

Imagine you are a dog. You see a ball. You want to catch it. You use your eyes to look at the ball. You use your ears to hear the sound of the ball. You use your nose to smell the air. Your brain is working very fast. You are thinking about what to do next.

Animals are not always the same. Some are very good at remembering things. They can remember where they put food. Some animals are very good at telling other animals things. They can say how far away something is. Some animals are very good at moving very fast. They can go very, very fast.

Animals also have a way of talking to each other. They make sounds. Some sounds are like a voice. Other sounds are like a loud noise. They use these sounds to talk to each other when they want to share something.

People are also very good at thinking. We can learn many things from books and from our teachers. We can also think about our own life. We can plan what we want to do tomorrow.
