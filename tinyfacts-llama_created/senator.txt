A person in the big building in the city is a kind of group leader. They help decide what the city should do. They listen to many different people and try to find a way that works for everyone. They talk with the city people, the police, the teachers, and other people who help the city.

The person gets this job because they are very good at listening and thinking. They want to help the city and its people. They also want to be sure that everyone has a chance to say what they think. Sometimes, a group of these people with many different jobs together make a better group than one person.

Some people are only a little bit in the group. They might only live in one part of the city. They still help, but they do not get to speak for the whole group. Other people are only a little bit in the group. They live in a far away town. They still help, but they do not get to speak for the whole city.

The person who is a group leader is called a person of the people. They are not the one who gets to decide what happens in the city. They are the ones who listen and try to find a way that works for everyone.
