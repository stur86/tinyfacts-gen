A listening is when you hear. You listen to a person, a sound, or a word. You do not look away. You pay attention to what the person is saying. You try to understand what they mean. This is important because it shows you care about what is happening.

Imagine you are in a room. You see a friend. You want to know what they are doing. You ask them to tell you. You use your words to ask them questions. This is a way to listen.

When you listen, you are not the one talking. You are the one hearing. The person speaking is the one doing the talking.

Sometimes, you might listen to a story. You hear how a person felt when they were sad or happy. This helps you understand how they lived. You can feel what they went through.

You can also listen to music. When you hear a song, you feel the beat in your body. You might want to move your feet or your arms. This is because the music makes you want to act.

In a school, a teacher might ask you to listen. They want you to pay attention to what they say. They might give you a lot of words to read. You need to understand what they are saying so you can do what they tell you to do.

A listening is important because it helps you understand and remember things. It is a way to stay safe and be kind to others.
