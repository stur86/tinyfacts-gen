A person who is in a high place is called a manager. They help many people at a big place. They are usually very good at making sure everyone is safe.

Think of a city. A city has many many people. There are many jobs, but the city is run by one person. This person is the city manager. They make sure the city is clean, the roads are good, and the police are strong.

A person who is a city manager is like a top leader. They decide what the city should do. They talk to many people and listen to their ideas. They also pay the people who work there and make sure they get enough money.

Sometimes, a city manager is like a teacher. They help the kids learn. They show them how to do their jobs and what is important.

A city manager can also be a person who works for a big group of people. They are the top person in that group. They make sure everyone works together and does their jobs.

One thing to know is that a city manager does not make all the money. They are only one person. They help the people who run the city, the police, and the people who work at the city. They help make sure the city is good for everyone.
