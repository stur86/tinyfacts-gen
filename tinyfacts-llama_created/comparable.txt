To be like someone means you are very close to them. It is about having the same things as another person or being in the same situation.

Imagine you love a book. You like the story, the pictures, and the words. If you meet another person who also loves the same book, you feel the same way. You can talk about the book, read the same words, and even show the same picture. You are like them.

You can also be like a game. If a game is easy, you are easy to beat. You do not have to try as hard to do something. This is like being like someone.

When you say something is like a person, you mean you think they are the same. You might feel happy when you hear about them. You feel you are a part of them.

Sometimes, you might not be like someone. You might like a different kind of food, a different kind of game, or a different kind of music. You are not the same person, but you are still a person.
