To learned

When you learn something, you are trying to understand it. You might watch a movie. You see a person do a thing. You try to follow what they do. If you do it right, you learn. You know how to do it.

You can also learn from a book. You read a story. You see what happens. You learn about a person or a place. This is important for your life. You can learn about your family. You can learn about a city. You can learn about animals. Every time you learn something, you are growing.

When you learn, you become stronger. Your brain gets more power. You can do more things. You can remember things that happened before. You can remember words. You can remember numbers. You can remember your name. You can remember where you are.
