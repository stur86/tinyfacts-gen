To have a brother is to have a person who is your brother. You are two people who are very close. You grew up together with your family. You played with the same friends when you were young. You share a room in the same house.

Most of the time, a brother does not talk to everyone. You talk to him, but you do not talk to the other people in your life. You are only one person to him. You care about him very much because he is your brother.

Sometimes, you have a little bit of trouble. You might fight or be very angry. But because you are brothers, you can talk to him and make things right. You can laugh together and be happy. You have a big family with many other brothers and sisters. Being a brother is a very important thing.
