A big person who is the head of a huge group of people. They are the ones who make the big plans. They decide what the group should do. They also make sure everyone follows those plans.

Imagine you are the head of a school. You make the plans for the whole year. You decide what games to play, what days to have school, and what the dress is for. Your job is to make sure everyone follows those plans.

This person does the same thing with a big group of people who work with money. They decide how much money to spend, what jobs to do, and how to make money. They make sure the group is doing well.

The person with this job is very important. They are like the top leader of the group. They help decide what happens every day. They also help the group stay safe and make sure everyone is happy.
