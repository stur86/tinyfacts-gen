To be outside means you are not in your home. You are in a place where you can see the ground and the sky. This is far away from your house.

When you are outside, you can hear many sounds. You can hear the sound of a dog, a car, or a tree. You might hear the sound of a fly or a fly that is flying around your head. This is very interesting!

Being outside can be a good thing. It helps you feel strong. It also helps you learn new things. You can see how animals live and what they eat. You can see how trees grow and what they do. You can even learn about the world around you.
