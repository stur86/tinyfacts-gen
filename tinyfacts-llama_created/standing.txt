To stand means you are not on the ground. You are up and you do not fall down. You are standing with your feet together or you might be standing one foot in front of the other.

When you stand, you look around. You see what is in front of you. You can see people, cars, houses, and trees. You can see the sky too. The sun is up and the light is strong.

You can also stand while you are waiting. If you are waiting for a friend who is going away, you can stand and watch. You might be sitting on a chair or a table. You can look at the wall or at the people around you.

Standing is important because it helps you feel safe. When you stand, you feel like you are the top of a tall thing. You are in control and you are not afraid.

Sometimes, you stand for a long time. You might stand while you are waiting for a bus or a train. You might stand while you are waiting for your favorite person to call you. Standing can make you feel strong and happy.
