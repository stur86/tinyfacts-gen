To cross a line can mean different things. One way is to reach a far place. If you live in a city, you may cross the city to reach a place with more land or where you live. This is called going across the water or a big body of water. It is a way to get from one place to another, even if the places are very far away.

Another way is to mark a line. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You both have a small piece of paper that says where you stand. If you cross the line between you, you are now on the other side. This is how people keep their homes and their places. It is also how we tell other people where to stay.

Sometimes, when we talk about a place, we use a word for the line that goes through it. We call that place the land area, or the area where the people live. We also call the line the edge of the world. This is because the world is a big round thing, and the edge of the world is where the land meets water.

We use this word to talk about many things. If you cross the street, you are moving from one place to another. If you cross a line at work, you are doing something new. If you cross a line to be with your family, you are leaving your old home to be with your loved ones.
