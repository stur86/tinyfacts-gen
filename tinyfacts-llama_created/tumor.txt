A part of the body that does not need to be there can become a kind of thing that grows. This happens when the body does not want the thing to be there anymore.

Imagine you have a small area on your skin that does not feel right. You might see a small white thing under your skin. This white thing is like a tiny thing that is growing. Over time, the white thing can become much bigger. It can push the skin around it, making it hard to move or breathe.

Sometimes, the white thing can break through the skin. This can happen in the stomach, the chest, or the brain. If it grows in these places, it can cause pain and make it hard to eat or drink.

We do not know why the white thing starts to grow. It might be because of something inside the body that is making it grow. It might be because of what someone eats or how they live. Sometimes, we do not know why at first, and it takes a long time to figure out the reason.

Most of the time, the white thing can be stopped with a doctor. They can give you a kind of drink that kills the tiny thing. They can also give you a thing to put under your skin that kills the white thing. In some cases, the doctor has to cut a little bit of the skin to get the white thing out.
