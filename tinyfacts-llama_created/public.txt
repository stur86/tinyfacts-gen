A word you see every day.

You see a sign on a street. It says, for everyone, come here. The sign is made by a group of people. They did not have to ask anyone else to make it. They just did it because it is a thing that should be there.

The word is about people. When you talk about a word, you are talking about what it means. This word means that many people can see it and understand it.

Imagine you are in a room with your friends. You are all talking. You do not need to say the word. You all know what it means. A word is like a group of friends who talk to each other in a way that only they understand.

People use this word in their lives. They say, the park is open. Or, the store is closed. They tell this to other people who might not know what to do.

Sometimes, a word can change how we act. When we hear the word, we act that way. If a word says, be kind, we act kind. If a word says, be mean, we act mean.

So, a word is a way to talk to each other. It is a way to share things we think or feel.
