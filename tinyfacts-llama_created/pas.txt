To pass something means to let it go so you can move on. Imagine you have a bag of food. You want to eat it, but a friend comes and takes the bag from you without asking. You feel angry. You want to say, but you can't. You just let go and walk away. You have passed the bag.

You can pass a story. If you tell a friend a story, they listen and then they tell you a story too. You are passing the story from one person to another until everyone has heard it.

You can also pass a game. If a team plays a game and they do not have enough people, someone else can join. This person is passing the game to the team. They are helping the team by joining.

Sometimes, you pass money. You take money from one place and give it to another. You are passing the money so everyone can buy what they need.

In life, you can pass on something. If you know how to do something and you tell others how to do it, they can learn from you. This is passing on the way to learn.

To pass something can be different for each person. You might pass a word that means you agree or a word that means you don't. You might pass a job so someone else can do it. You are letting go of the job so another person can take over.
