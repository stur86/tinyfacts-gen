An idea for a party or a movie can be sent to many people. This is called an idea. Sometimes, you might want to share this idea with your friends. You might say, I want to go to a big house for a night. You are giving them a chance to come.

You can give this idea to a person by saying, I want you to come. Or you can put it on a computer so many people see it. This way, many people know you are planning something. They might decide to come.

In this way, you are letting people know that you want them to come. This can be very nice. It can make them feel happy and excited. It is like a sign that you are ready for them to be there.

Sometimes, you might give this idea to a movie. If you say, watch the movie this night, many people will come. This is like an idea for a night. You are telling them to come so you can share the story.

You can also give this idea to a place. If you say, come see my house, many people might come. They will see the place and decide if they want to go in.

We use this word when we want to share a plan with many people. It helps us tell them what is happening and what time it is.
