When you sort something, you put it in a way that the things that are smaller are before the things that are bigger. You put the things that are the same first.

Imagine you have a long line of papers. Some papers are very small, and some papers are very big. If you were to put the papers in order by how big they are, the small papers would go first. Then, the big papers would go next, and then the small papers again at the very end.

You can also sort things by color. If you have many red papers, red books, and red animals, you can put them together. Red is the color that the papers and books and animals all have in the same place.

Sometimes you sort things so that the things that are on the left are the ones that are first. This is like a big line. If you are in a line and you see your name, you know you are first. If you are in a line and your friend is first, you are second, then third, and so on.

You can also sort things so that the things that are the same are together. If you have many books that are about animals, and you put them all in one place, they are together. This helps you find the book you want quickly.

People use this word to talk about many things. If you sort a box of food, you are putting them in order. If you sort people, you are putting them in a way that the people who are the same are next to each other.
