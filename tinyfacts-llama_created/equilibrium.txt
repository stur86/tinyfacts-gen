An easy place to stand and a hard one to hold. Think of your body. When you walk, you stand. When you sit, you stand. This is your body being in its right place. But if you move too fast or start to run, you can fall down. This is not a place you want to be.

Things in the world also have this. A ball in the air is in the right place. If you throw it, it will stay up. But if you pull it back, it falls down. This is the right place for it. We use this word for many things. To have an answer means to find the right place for what is happening.

A person can also have an answer. If you are sad, you might cry. This is your body telling you that you are in a hard place. But if you smile, you are in a good place. You have found a way to feel better.
