To do something together can be very nice. If you and your friend want to go to a park, you both want to be there. You can sit and talk while you walk. They can be glad you came. If you do not like what you are doing, you can just say, I do not want to. Your friend can still say, I do not want to. They are doing nothing. They are not really doing anything at all.

Sometimes you do something for someone else. You give them a book you read. They might be happy. You do not need to say, thank you. The person feels good because you helped them. You are both happy.

You can also do something for yourself. You might watch a movie or read a book. You feel good. You have time to think. You can relax. You are alone in a way that feels nice.

A group of people can work together to do a job. They all want to do the same thing. They talk and plan. They work as a team. When everyone does their part, the job gets done. They are all doing something together.

When you are with your family, you are doing something together. You play games. You watch a movie. You talk. You laugh. You are all happy because you are together.
