Decides

To decide something

When you want to do something, you need to think about it. You want to know if it is a good idea. If you are not sure, you might want to wait and think again. This is called deciding.

A word is an easy word to know. It is the word you use when you say you do something. You use it to tell others or to write a story. A word is not a number. It is a sound that you make. You can also use words to talk about things. You can use words to say how you feel. If you are happy, you might say, I am happy. If you are sad, you might say, I am sad.

A group of people can also decide. If many people want to do something, the group decides. They talk and then do it. They follow the idea that the group wants. Sometimes, a group decides a lot of things. They decide what to eat for breakfast, what to wear, or where to go next.

In a world with many numbers, you can also use a word to show how many people agree. If a lot of people think something is bad, you use the word many. If a few people think it is good, you use the word few. You can use this word to show how much you agree or how much you do not agree.

Sometimes, you might need to decide about something that is hard. You might see a thing that looks bad, but you are not sure if it is really bad. You must think about it and decide if it is the right thing to do. This is a big job for a person.
