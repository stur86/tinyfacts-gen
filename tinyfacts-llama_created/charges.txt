When you are talking about a person, you might say they have a lot of power. This is because they have a lot of money or a lot of things that give them the right to act in a way that is not allowed. They can tell people to do what they want, even if it is against the word of the people. This is why they are called a power.

The word can also mean something that is left behind. You might leave a book on a table. When the morning comes, you might find it there. You did not put it down, but it was there waiting for you. This is how we know things even if we are not there.

If you have a small thing, you can give it a number. This number tells you how many times it has happened. You might have five little dogs. Each dog is like a number: two, three, four, five, and six. This number shows you how many times each dog has done its job.

Sometimes, you might feel angry. Your heart is like a heavy thing that makes you want to shout. You want to tell the world that someone did something wrong. You might use your words to say what is wrong. This is how we talk when we are angry.

In a game, you might give a number to a person. This number is like a word that tells you how to act. If the number is high, the person might do more. If the number is low, they might do less. This is how the game is made so that everyone knows what is going on.
