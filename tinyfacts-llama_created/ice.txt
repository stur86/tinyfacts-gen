Ice is a white stuff that forms when water gets very cold. It is made of tiny, hard pieces that stick together. When warm water gets very cold, it turns into this white stuff. This is what happens when you put a glass of cold water in the cold part of your kitchen.

The white stuff has a very hard bottom. This makes it very heavy. If you put it in a glass cup, it will stay there. It does not go down into the cup like water does. This is why ice keeps your drinks cold for a long time.

Ice is very clear. You can see it from far away. It looks like a very thinking stuff. It is usually white, but sometimes it is clear or blue. Some ice is very bright and looks like glass.

Ice is very good at keeping things cold. You can put ice in your drink, on your face, or even in your car to keep it from getting too hot. This keeps people and animals safe. It also helps keep food cold so it does not get broken.

You can make ice at home. You can put water in a box and put the box in the cold part of your kitchen. The water will turn into ice. It is also possible to buy ice in stores. People buy it when they do not have time to go to the cold part of their kitchen.

Ice can be very hard. It is very strong and does not break easily. This is why it stays in one place when it falls from the sky. If it falls on the ground, it becomes soft and breaks into tiny pieces.

Ice can also be very soft. This is when the water is very cold but not quite as cold as ice. It can be very slow to change from water to ice. When it gets warm, it changes quickly.
