To be in a different place means you are not in the same spot. This happens when something moves or moves around. Imagine you are sitting in a room. If you move to the other side of the room, you are in a different place. You are not in the same spot as you were before.

Things that are in the same place are in the same spot. They are not moving around. This is important for our world. If you were to move into another room, you would not be the same person anymore. You would be in a different place. You would have a different life.

Think about the number line on a piece of paper. Each number is a place. If you move to the right, you are in a different place. If you move to the left, you are still in the same place. The number is the same, but you are in a different spot.

Also, think about time. Time is a way to show when things happen. If you are in the past, you were before the time that is now. If you are in the present, you are right now. If you are in the next time, you will be after that time. This is how we understand the past and the present and the next time.

Everything is made of many places. A rock is made of places that are very far away. A person is made of many people they love and people they do not love. A tree is made of many years. The sky is made of many places that are very high. The ground is made of many places that are very low. Every place is a different place.
