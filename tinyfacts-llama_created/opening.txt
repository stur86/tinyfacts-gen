An opening is a big part in a box or a room. It is the part that you can see and use. When you open a box, you are letting the things inside come out. You can also open a door, a window, or a bag.

Think about a door. When a door is closed, it is like a small cover. It keeps the inside of the house or the room inside. But when you push the door open, you are letting the light and the air in. This makes the room feel bigger and more warm.

You can open a book or a picture. When you open a book, you are starting to read. You can also open a picture and look at the colors. An opening is the beginning of a story or a new day. It is the start of a new chance.

Sometimes, an opening can mean something that is very small. A small opening in the ground can let air and water come out. This is important for the life of the ground and the water that lives in it.

An opening can also mean a break in a situation. If you are feeling angry, you might open your face and show your face. This is a way to let go of the feeling and start to feel better.

So, an opening is a part that lets in light, air, or water. It is the beginning of something new or the start of a break in a situation.
