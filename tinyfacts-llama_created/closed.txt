A closed thing is something that is not open anymore. It is stopped or turned away so you can not go inside. When a door is closed, you can not get out. When a window is closed, the light is kept inside.

This is important when you are in a room and you do not want people to see what you are doing. You can close the door so no one can hear you. You can also close a book so you do not read what is inside. This keeps things safe and quiet.

Sometimes, a closed thing is also about time. If you close your eyes, you are not seeing anymore. You are only thinking about something else. You are closed in your mind.

In some places, you might see a closed sign. This sign says that something is not allowed to happen. It is closed because it is not safe for people to go there.

In a very big way, a closed thing is when a plan or a plan for a big thing happens. Maybe a man wanted to build a tall thing. He started with a plan. When he finished, the plan was closed. The tall thing was built and there was no more plan to close it.
