To be in a good place with someone means you do not fight or make things wrong. When two people are in the same place, they can talk and share their ideas. This makes it easier to understand each other.

If two things are not in the same place, it can cause trouble. Think about a room with many people. If one person is in the corner and the others are in the middle, they might not see each other. Or if a person is in a big room and another person is in a small room, it can be hard to talk to them.

People like being in a good place with others. They like it when everyone is happy and can talk. This is why it is good to be in a group with people you like or with friends.
