A group of people who live in the same place can have a different way of doing things. They may eat different food, talk the same way, and even look different. This is what we mean by a group of people.

Imagine you go to a city with many different houses. Each house is a different family. They all have a door, a window, and a floor. But they are all in the same city. If you go to a different city, you would see different houses. People from different cities have different ways of living.

People also have different stories. A story is a way of telling how something happened. If you hear a story about how a tree grew, you can remember it for later. When you hear a story from a different place, you learn about a different time.

A group of people who share a way of doing things is called a group. They are the people who live in the same city or the same land. They might have the same food, the same clothes, and the same way of talking. They work together and help each other.

A group can also have different ways of being strong. Some groups are strong because they work together and help each other. They are like a big team. They do things together and make the world a better place. Other groups are strong because they are only about themselves. They do not help each other.

People also have different ways of feeling. They might feel happy when they are playing with a friend. They might feel sad when they lose a game. They might feel angry when someone does something wrong. Each group has its own way of feeling.

Couples are also a group of people. Two people love each other and want to stay together. They work together and help each other. They share their life, their money, and their time.

A group of people can also change over time. People grow and learn. They find new ways to do things. They try new things. They get better at doing things. This is what happens when a group grows and becomes stronger.

In the end, a group of people is any group of people who live together. They might have the same way of doing things, the same story, or the same feeling. But they are all one group because they are all people.
