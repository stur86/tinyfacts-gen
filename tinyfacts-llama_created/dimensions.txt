To have a sense of a number or space can be important. These are the things you use to tell what is there around you. Imagine you are standing in a room with a wall on one side and a wall on the other. If you can see the wall you are in front of, you have a sense of the side of the room. You know that one side is shorter than the other side. This is your first sense of space.

But space is also much bigger than just the sides of a room. Imagine you are looking at a big picture with many pictures in a line. Each picture is a small piece of space. If you can see all the small pieces, you have a sense of the whole room. This is your second sense of space. You know how many pictures are there and how they are lined up.

Sometimes, you might see a picture that is very far away. You can only see a tiny small part of it, but you know it is much bigger than your own room. This is your third sense of space. You are not standing in the same room as the picture. You are seeing it from a very far way.

Sometimes, we use these words to talk about how we feel about things. If you are happy, you might say you have a good sense of space. You feel like everything is right. If you are sad, you might say you have a small sense of space. You feel like one side of your room is too high or too low.

We also use these words to talk about things that are bigger than our own bodies. Think about the sky. You see a small picture of the sky, but you can only see a tiny part of it. The sky is much bigger than that small picture. You have a small sense of the whole sky.

Sometimes, we use these words to talk about things that are very far away. If you are looking at the sun, you need to use a big set of numbers to tell how far away it is. The sun is so far that if you were to walk very fast, it would take a very long time. The numbers help you keep the right sense of space when looking at it.
