An ear is a thing that helps you hear. It is on your head. You put your ear to a small piece of wood or a soft thing to hear sounds.

When you hear a sound, it goes into your ear. The sound is very quiet. Your ear is like a quiet room. Inside your ear, there are three parts. The front part is where you hear sounds. The middle part is where your brain listens to the sounds. The back part is where the sound goes into your body so you can feel it.

You can hear many different sounds. You hear your mother when she says your name. You hear the sound of a dog when it bites. You hear the sound of a car when it moves. You can even hear your own voice when you talk.

An ear also has a part to keep air out. If air gets in, the sound will not come out. So you must close your ear with your hand. You can also use a small thing to help you hear better. These things are like a long, small stick that you put in your ear.

Sometimes, you might hear a strange sound. This can be a noise that is not there at all. It might be a sound from another room or a sound from a far away place. If you are very still, the sound will stop. If you move your head or your body, the sound goes away.

An ear is very important. It helps you understand what people say. It helps you see the faces of the people you talk to. It helps you feel when a door closes or when a car starts moving. It is a very important part of your body.
