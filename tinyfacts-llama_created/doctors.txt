Doctors are people who help us when we are sick or hurt. They are the ones who tell us what to do so our bodies can get better.

A doctor starts by listening to what the person says. They ask questions and try to find out why the person is not well. They look at the person and see if there is anything wrong. They also look at the body with a light under the skin. This can help them find the problem.

A doctor can do many things. They can give you a drink to make you feel better. They can give you a small cut to close so the blood does not come out. They can give you a small piece of paper with numbers and words to show you how to take care of yourself at home. They can also give you a new name so you do not have to worry that you are the same person all the time.

Doctors also talk to us about our lives. They ask about how you feel, what you did the day before, and how you feel after sleeping. They also talk to your parents and teachers about you. This is important because it helps them know what to do.

Sometimes doctors do things that hurt a little. They give you a tiny cut to put in your arm or hand so you can get a small piece of paper. They also give you a small drink. They do this because it helps the body get better faster.

A doctor is very important. They help us feel better when we are sick, hurt, or afraid. They are like the people who fix things when things are broken.
