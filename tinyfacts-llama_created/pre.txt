To present something means to show it before other people. It is about putting things together so that everyone can see them.

Imagine you are waiting for a friend. You put your drink down and your phone. Then you look up and say, I am here! This is how you present yourself to others.

You can also present things in a group. When you are speaking, you are presenting an idea. You are telling everyone what you think and why. This helps others understand your point of mind.

Sometimes, a person presents a story. They start and stop at the right times. This helps the story feel more like a real story. They use words that make it easy to understand.

You can present people too. If you want to make someone feel better, you can present them a smile. A smile can make a room feel warm and happy.
