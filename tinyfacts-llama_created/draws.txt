To draw something means you use your hands to make a picture. You start with a line. Then you add more lines to the line. You might move your hands in different directions to make a moving line. You can also draw a round thing. You start with a small part at the top and then you add more parts all around it.

Sometimes you want to draw a person. You might start with the head and arms. You add the face, the hands, and the feet. You can make the face look happy or sad. You can also make the hands look strong or soft. You can draw hair on a person. You might use lines and tiny bits of color to make the hair look like it is growing from the head.

You can also draw things that are not people. You might draw a house, a car, or a tree. These are things you see in your world. You can use your hands to make these things look good.

To draw something is to use your hands and your brain. Your brain tells you what to do. Your hands follow the brain. This is why it is so important to learn how to draw. The more you draw, the better you get at it.
