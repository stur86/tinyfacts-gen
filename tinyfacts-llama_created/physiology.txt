To have a body is to be living. Inside you is where you get the power to move. You have things that help you breathe. You have parts that keep you warm. You have parts that help you see. You have parts that help you hear. Your brain tells your body what to do.

Your body is made up of many parts. There are parts that are very small inside you. There are parts that are very big. There are parts that are hard and parts that are soft. You need all these parts working together so you can live. Without any of these parts, you would not be able to breathe, you would not be able to see, and you would not be able to hear.

The body also has parts that make you different. If you have a baby, the baby gets some of the body parts from the mom. This helps the baby learn how to live on its own.

Sometimes you get hurt. Your body sends a sign to your brain. This sign tells your brain to make the body hurt or to make it feel better. Your brain tells your body what to do. Your body acts on the sign. If you are hurt, your body tries to fix the hurt. If the hurt is too bad, your body might die.

The word is very important. You use it to talk about how your body works. You might say, someone is very strong. Or, a person is very tall. You might say, someone is very happy. These are all about how well the body works.
