He tried to kill the son, but the son was able to die without being hurt.

After this, the son went away and left his world. He went to a place where no one would hurt him. There, he lived with other people who loved him. He told them to love one another too. When the son came back, his father was very sad. The son said that he had been gone for a long time and that he could not come back. But then, the son told his father that he was ready to go back. The father said yes and the son went back to his world. When the son came back, people were afraid. They did not know if he was the real son or just a friend. They asked him if he was the one who had told them to love one another. The son said yes and the people believed him.

The story of the child of God is important because it shows us how we should live. It tells us to love one another and to care for the world. It shows us how to live a life of love and care.
