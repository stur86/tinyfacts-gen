A power thing is a way to give or get work. You use it to light a light, to power a computer, or to make things move on a car. The work it does is what we want.

One thing to know is that the word has two different uses.

First, a power thing gives power. Think of a power line that carries power to homes and businesses. The power is the work being done. The power line carries the power to the house through many long lines. People and things need this power to work, to live, and to move around.

Second, a power thing takes power. Think of a car that needs to keep moving. When the car runs out of power, it stops. When a person or an animal needs power, they use something to take the power from the power thing. This is why you might need to use a table or a chair to hold something heavy, or why you might use a piece of paper to write.

The word is named after a man. The man gave the world a way to tell how much work a power thing is doing. He said, if you have one power thing doing one job, you have a hundred power things doing one job. This number can change if the power thing gets more or less work.

Today, we use many different kinds of power things. Some make light. Some make music. Some make food. Some make air. Some make water. Some make money. Each power thing is like a different kind of work.
