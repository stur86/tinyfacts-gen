You use your ears to hear things. When a sound goes in, the tiny things in your ear move. This makes a tiny wave go around your head. Your brain then reads what the wave tells you. This is how you hear words, music, and other sounds.

Your ears also help you feel. You can hear how hard things are. You can hear if something is near or far. You can even hear the beat of your own heart. This is important for keeping yourself safe.
