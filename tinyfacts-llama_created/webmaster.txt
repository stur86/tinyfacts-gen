A person who makes the parts of a computer work is called a computer person. But there is a different kind of person who makes things for the world wide. They are called a computer person.

These computer people build things from many small parts. They use a computer to tell them what to do. They write the words that the computer must follow. They make sure the computer is strong and can do many things.

Some of these people are only on the computer. They do not move. They work in a room with many other computer people. They talk to each other using words and pictures. They plan how the computer will work.

Sometimes these people work from home. They do not have to go to the office every day. They work from a room that is far away. They can use their computer from many places. This is good because they do not have to go to the same office every day.

Some computer people work for a big group of people. They are part of a team. They work together with many other computer people. They share their ideas and do not have to do everything alone.

Some computer people are very good at using computers. They can write very fast and make the computer do many things quickly. They can make the computer find things that were not there before. They can make the computer understand what a word means.

These computer people are important. They help us. They help us buy things we want. They help us talk to people. They help us know where we are.
