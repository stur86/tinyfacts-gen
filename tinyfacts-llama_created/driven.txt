To be driven means you are not happy with what you are doing. You are also not afraid to do something. You might feel a big power inside you, and you want to go. This power can be from inside or from outside.

Imagine you are walking in the rain. You do not want to get wet. But you walk anyway. You push through the rain. You might feel a bit cold. You might feel a bit scared. But you keep going. You are still in the rain, but you are not afraid because you are doing something.

You can also feel this power when you want to do something very much. You are not just going to do it. You are going to do it with all your heart and brain. You feel a big push inside you.

Sometimes, you are driven by money. You want to get a lot of money so you can buy the things you want. You work very hard to get it. You feel a big push in your chest. You feel a big push in your head.

You can also be driven by a big idea. You want to change the world. You want to make sure everyone is safe. You want to help people. You feel a big power inside you. You want to take a stand.

Being driven is about taking control. It is about not letting other people make you do what you want. It is about being the one in the room.
