To imagine is to put your mind into a picture. You use your words and your body to make it happen. You think about what you see, hear, feel, and smell. You put those things into your brain and your heart.

Imagine a very bright light. You see it and you feel it. You want to touch it. You want to be near it. You want to be safe under it. You can only imagine if you close your eyes and use your brain.

You can also imagine a story. You start with a small thing. Then you add more things. You add a friend, a car, a house, and many other things. You see the whole story in your mind. You can imagine a story even if you never saw the real thing.

Sometimes you imagine a very hard thing. You put your body and your brain into it. You feel the pain, the cold, and the hard. You feel the hard thing is real. You feel the hard thing in your body. You feel it deep inside.

Imagine is also about people. You can imagine a friend. You see their face, their hair, and their words. You know they are there. You know they care about you. You can imagine a child. You see their smile, their eyes, and their tiny hands. You know they are happy.

To imagine is a way to use your brain and your heart. It is a way to have a picture in your mind. It is a way to feel something without seeing it. It is a way to dream and to be happy.
