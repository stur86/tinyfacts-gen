To see means you can look at something. You are looking at a room, a tree, a person, or a thing. You use your eyes to understand what is in front of you.

When you are able to see, you can see many things. You can see the color of a bright blue sky. You can see the bright white of a hard, round thing. You can see a face and a body. You can also see things that are not there. Your brain helps you understand what you see.

Sometimes, you see a far away thing that is not in your room. You might see a house far away. You might see a big city with many tall houses. You see the roads and the streets. You see the people and cars moving. Your brain helps you understand the story.

To have a good dream is also to see. When you dream, your brain makes up a story while you are still asleep. You might dream about a fun day or a very sad day. You might dream about a place you have never been. Your brain uses your eyes and your brain to make the story happen.

Seeing is important for us. It lets us know about the world. It lets us understand the people around us. It helps us understand the world.
