To have a lot of something means to have a large number of things. This is especially true when you talk about body. If you are very heavy, you are very much. This is because your body is made of many strong parts that help you move around.

Think about a small piece of paper that is very heavy. You can not carry it in your hand. It is too heavy for you to hold. This is because the paper is full of stuff. The paper is making your hand feel heavy.

A heavy thing does not always fall down. If you are standing on a soft ground, you will stay up. You are not going to fall. This is because the ground is not strong enough to push you down. The ground and the thing you stand on are both heavy, and they hold you up.

Sometimes, we use this word in other ways. When you have a lot of money, you have a lot of power. You can buy many things. You can buy food, clothes, and a house. You have a lot of money that can help you live a good life.

We also say a heavy person is a person who is very strong. This is because their body is built to carry a lot of stuff. They can lift a heavy box or run fast. This is important because it helps them do their job or play a game.
