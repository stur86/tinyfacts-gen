To look forward is to plan for the days that are yet to come. You think about the things that will happen next week, next month, next year. You can see that your life is moving forward and you are excited about what is ahead.

Imagine you are a parents. You are looking at a picture of your child when they are very young. You are thinking, soon they will learn how to walk, soon they will start school, soon they will have a dog and a cat. You are looking forward to all these happy moments.

You can also look forward to things that are not yet here. Maybe you are waiting for a new phone that you want. Maybe you are looking forward to a trip you have planned. This is when you are excited about what is coming later.

Sometimes, you look forward to things that might not happen at all. You might look forward to a day of fun with your friends. You might look forward to a quiet morning when you can relax. This is when you are looking at the good times ahead.

To look forward to something means to hope that it will happen. It means you are ready for what is to come. It is a way to feel that your life is moving in the right direction.
