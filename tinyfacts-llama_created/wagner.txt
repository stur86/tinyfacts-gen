A man who writes music is called a music writer. He makes music that people like to listen to. He writes the sounds of a story, the sounds of a fight, the sounds of a big room. He can use only a few numbers and some sounds to make the music. He can make the music go up and down, the music go round, or the music stay one way.

A person who works at a big house, a place where many people live and work, is called a manager. They are in a high place and get to decide who does what job. They make sure everyone does their job right and that the house runs well.

A man who works with a big team of people to build a new house is called an builder. He makes sure all the pieces are in the right place so the house can stand. He also makes sure that the walls are strong and that the doors are safe.

A person who works at a big place that makes food is called a food writer. They write about the food they make and the people who eat it. They tell the story of how the food is made and what makes the food good.

A person who writes about a story with a man and a girl is called a writer. They write the words that tell the story. They make sure the story is good and that the reader can imagine what is happening.

A man who writes a story about a man who wants to fly is called a writer of stories. He makes the story go up and around. He uses his words to help people feel what the man feels.

A group of people who write stories together is called a group. They all write the same kind of story and they try to make it better than the last story.
