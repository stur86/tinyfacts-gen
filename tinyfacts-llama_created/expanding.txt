When something is growing, it starts out small and then gets bigger. It can happen in many ways. You can imagine a tiny ball. After a while, the ball gets bigger and the air inside it gets more. The ball is now much bigger than it was before.

You can also talk about a person. If a person has a big smile, it means they are happy and their face is very full. Their eyes are bright and their nose is small. If a person has a hard day, they might get angry. Their face might be red and their body might feel heavy.

In a house, if the walls get taller, the room becomes bigger. The same is true if you put more books on a high book. The books make the top part of the book bigger.

In life, you can also get bigger. You can learn new things. You might see things that make you feel strange. You might meet new people. You might try new things. As you get older, you might feel your body change. Your hair might grow longer. Your skin might get softer.

When something is growing, it is changing. It is not always the same. It can be bigger, smaller, or change direction. This is how the world works.
