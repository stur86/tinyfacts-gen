To learned something means you get to know it. When you learn a new thing, you do not just remember it, you also understand it. You can see how it works and why it happens.

Think of learning a new word. You did not just hear the word. You saw the word in a book. You saw how the word looks and how it sounds. You also saw how the word is used by other people. This helps you remember the word more easily.

You can learn many things in your life. You learn to read, to write, to speak, to play a game, or to drive a car. When you learn, you become better at doing the thing.

Sometimes, you learn from your friends and family. You listen to what they say and try to do it yourself. This is a good way to learn because it comes from people you trust.

You can also learn from books, movies, and shows. These are ways to get new ideas and understand how things work. When you watch a movie, you see how people act and how they do things. This can help you understand how to act yourself.

Learning is always happening. We learn every day. We learn to be kind, to share, and to care about others. When we learn, we grow as people.
