When you change, something old becomes new. This happens when you do something different from what you did before. It can happen in many ways. If you have a book and you read all the words, then you change it. You take the words and you make them new. You change how the book looks.

Sometimes, changes happen in your own life. You might feel sad. You might want to cry, but you are too strong. You take a deep breath. You let go of the feeling. This is a change. You are changing how you feel.

You can also change your mind. If you think something is wrong, but then you learn something new, you might change your mind. This is a good thing. You learn and grow.

In the world, people change too. A person might be happy one day, but then they are very sad. They might have to move to a different place. This is a change. They are leaving one life behind and starting another.

Sometimes, changes are very big. A city might be built on top of an old one. The old city is gone. The new city is new. People move there because of work or school. This is a change. The people and the places are different.

Changes are important. They help us learn and grow. They help us become the people we are today.
