The word worst is used when we are talking about the things that are very bad.

When you feel very angry, you might say, this is the worst. It means that something is the very top of the listened to things. It is the one you do not want to happen again.

If you have a bad day, you might feel sad. In this case, the word worst is used. It is the one thing you wish would not happen.

Sometimes, people say this is the worst thing you ever did. It is the one thing that makes you feel very bad inside.

You can also use this word when you talk about people. If a friend is doing something very bad, you might say, this is the worst thing about them. It shows that you care about them because you do not like what they are doing.

It is also used when things are very broken. If a car has a bad part, you say it is the worst part. It means that part is the worst of all.

So, the word worst is about what is very bad or the one thing you do not like.
