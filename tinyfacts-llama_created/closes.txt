To close something means to stop it from moving anymore. When you close a door, you shut the door and make it not open again. You might also close your eyes to stop a loud sound.

Think about a small animal in a house. The animal might be afraid of a loud noise. It can close its eyes and hide. This helps the animal feel safe.

You can also close a book. If you finish reading a book, you close the cover. This helps the book stay closed until you open it again.

In life, you close a door when you decide to leave a place. You close the door and walk away. This is the end of a time when you were there. You might close your heart to a person or a time. This means you stop thinking about them.

Sometimes, you close a door to your mind. You might feel afraid or sad. This can make you want to hide and not think about the problem anymore. But then you open your mind and start to think.
