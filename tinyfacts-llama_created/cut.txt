To cut something means to make a line across it. This line can go through a piece of wood, or it can go across a piece of paper. When you cut, you are making a new piece from the old one.

Imagine you have a box with many small pieces. You want to get one piece out. You can hold the box and cut it open. The cut is like a line that goes around the box. This line makes it easy to pull out the piece you want.

Sometimes we cut things that are hard. We use a thing with a hard point to break the hard thing. This makes it smaller and makes it easier to carry. We can also cut a piece of food so it is easy to eat. This makes it easier to hold and to take to your body.
