To complete something means you finish it. If you build a house, you complete it. If you write a book, you complete it. You have done all the work you were going to do. You can stop doing it now.

Sometimes, we complete a job at work. If you finish your work at the office, you are done. You did the work. You did not leave it for someone else to do.

You can also complete a problem. If you have a big problem and you work on it for a long time, you have finished it. You have found a way to fix it. You are done.

When you complete something, you feel happy. You feel like you did a good job. It is like you are the one who did it.
