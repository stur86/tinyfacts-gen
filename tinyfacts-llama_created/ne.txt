The word needing goes back a very long way. It is about something that you need to stay safe. If you are a baby, you need food to live. You need to drink from a bottle or a cup. When you are very young, your body does not know how to make its own food, so you need to eat from a bottle or a cup.

The word also means you need to do things. If you want to go to a school, you need to be there. If you are a friend, you need to do things to help your friend. You need to be kind and not say bad things.

Sometimes, when you are very old, you may need things to help your body. You might need to take a drink or eat food that makes you feel better. This is because your body is getting older and it needs help.

In some places, people use the word to talk about things that are important. They might say, a friend needs a friend, or a family needs to work together. This word helps us understand what is important when we are making plans or talking about people.
