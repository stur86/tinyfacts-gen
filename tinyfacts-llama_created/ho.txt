To houses people

When you go to a big room, you might say, I need to go to the houses now. This means you need to go there because it is the right place for you. You do this because you are the one who needs to go there.

If you do not want to go to the houses, you can say you do not want to go. You are not going because you do not need to go there.

You also use this word to talk about things that are ready to go. If a car is ready to go, you say it is housing. This is because it is getting ready to move.

Sometimes, you can also say you are housing yourself. This means you are feeling very calm and you are happy. You might feel this way because you are doing nothing or because you are feeling safe.

A house can also be a word for a place where people live. You might say, I am housing in my home tonight. This means you are staying in your own house.
