To be the one who holds something is to be the person who keeps it. This happens when a group of people come together to make a place work. Sometimes, a person is the one who is in the middle of this group. They are the one who answers the questions and makes sure that everyone is doing what they are supposed to do.

Imagine you are at a big party. There are many people there, and they all want to talk to the person who is making food. The one who is in control of the food is the one who holds it. They have to make sure the food is right, and they have to let everyone know when they need to eat. This is important because it helps everyone stay safe and happy.

Sometimes, a group of people does not need one person to hold them. They can just work together and help each other. In this case, everyone is the one who holds the work. They share their ideas and work, and they all do their part to make things happen.

Even if you are not in a group of many people, you can still hold things. For a person, holding a piece of paper means they have it. For a store, holding money means they have it to pay for things later. For a city, holding a place means they are the ones who keep the streets clean and make sure the power goes on.
