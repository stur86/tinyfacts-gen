Corners are places where three lines meet. On a wall, a corner is where a wall ends and another wall starts. It is the point where two lines cross. This makes the space in the corner very small.

Imagine a room with many walls. If you walk around the room, the walls are like long lines. Between any two walls, there is a small space. This space is called a corner.

A corner can also be a place where people meet. When many people are in one room, the corner is the spot where they all stand. This is where the room feels like one big group.

A building has many corners. There are corners on the walls, on the floor, and on the top. Each corner is a place where lines meet. They are important because they hold things together and make the room look nice.

Sometimes, a corner is broken. If a wall is broken, the corner is the point where the wall ends. This can happen if the wall falls down or if a person falls on it. In this case, the space in the corner is open and there is a big area in the middle.

People use words like this to talk about places and things. When someone says, I am in the corner of the room, they mean they are right in the middle of the room.
