To have a back up means you have a plan or a thing ready to help when things get bad. Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You think you can beat them, but then something happens. You fall down and your friend helps you up. You did not have a plan at first, but you had a back up. You had a friend ready to help you.

In computers, a back up is a set of things that the computer keeps ready. If something bad happens to the computer, the back up can be used to fix it. It is like having a plan for when you get hurt in a game.

Sometimes, a back up is also about keeping money. If you spend all your money, you have a back up. You can use the money you saved to pay for things later.

In a way, having a back up is about being ready for the worst. It is about knowing you have a plan and things will get better.
