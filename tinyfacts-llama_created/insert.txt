To put something in a place is to add it. If you put a book into a box, the book is now in the box. The book is not still lying on the floor. It has moved into a new place.

Imagine you have a bag with food. You want to eat some of it. You open the bag and take out a piece. Now the bag is empty, but the food is still there. You can put the food in your mouth.

To put something in a place means you are adding it to something else. You do this so the new thing is part of a bigger group. Think of a city. The city has many streets. One street is the road that comes from your home. If you put a new street into the city, it is now a part of the city.

You can also put a word in a story. If you want to add a new idea to a story, you might put the word there. This helps the story grow and become more interesting.

Sometimes, you put things you do not need in a place. If you have too many things, you might put them in a box. This keeps your home clean and makes it easier to move from one place to another.
