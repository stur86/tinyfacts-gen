To have something means to have it now. You can be happy because you have a friend. You can be sad because you lost your favorite book. You can also be tired because you did too much work.

When you have something, you do not need it anymore. You can keep it for a long time. You can give it to someone else. You do not have to be afraid that it will go away.

You can also have a job. A job is a job you do for money. You work every day and get paid. You might feel good because you are doing something important.

The word also means you are there. If you are in a room, you have that room. You are in a place with other people. You are not alone.

It can also mean you are doing something. If you have a plan, you are following it. If you are learning a new thing, you are getting better. If you are afraid, you are worried.

You can say you have something because you are in a place. Or you can say you have a feeling because you are doing something.
