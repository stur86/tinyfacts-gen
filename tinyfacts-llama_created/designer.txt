A person who makes things. They plan how to make them. They look at what other people use. They decide how to make it look and what it will do.

Imagine you want a new book. You look at books from many stores. You might pick a book with a nice cover. You pick a book with words you like. You look at the back of the book. You see the name of the person who made it. You see how the book is made. You did all this so you have a book that is like the one you wanted.

They also make clothes and shoes. A person who makes clothes is a good one. They take some pieces of old clothes and make them new. They add parts that are different. They fix things that are broken. They make clothes that are easy to wear.

Sometimes, a person makes things for people who do not want their own things. They make things that are fun to play with. They make games for kids. They make things that help people walk. They make things that help people breathe.

A person who makes things is a very important person. They help us live our lives. They make us want to play, to write, to build, or to fly. They make us feel happy.
