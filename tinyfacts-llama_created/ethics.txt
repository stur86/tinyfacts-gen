To do what is right is to act with a good heart. It is about following a set of things that everyone agrees on. This is how we make sure everyone is safe and happy.

Imagine you are playing with your friends in a big field. You are supposed to stay in the green area where the trees are. If you go into the red area where the houses are, you might hurt someone. Or if you take something that is not yourself, you would be doing something wrong.

People use this word to talk about their lives. They might say something is right or wrong. It helps them decide what to do when they face a situation. It is a way to show we care about how the world works.

Some people follow a set of good things and say they are doing what is right. Other people follow their own way and say they are doing what is right. But everyone agrees that doing what is right is the best way.
