To make something smaller is to make it harder to see. Imagine you are holding a big ball. If you push the ball down into your hand, it becomes smaller. Now you can not see the ball at all. This makes it much easier to carry around.

Sometimes you do this with air. You can fill a bag with air and then use it to make things. You can fill a bag with air quickly, so you can carry many things in one bag. This is how you can carry books and papers.

You can also make things smaller by pushing on them. If you push a small thing, it becomes much harder to move. This is why you can not push a small ball very far. A car is a small thing that you drive on the ground. It is made to be small so you can drive it quickly and easily.

We also use this word to talk about how things feel. When you feel angry, your heart is beating very fast. This makes your face red. Your body is hard. You might want to hit someone. In this case, the word is used to show how you are feeling.

There is a word for making something bigger. If you make something bigger, it becomes harder to fit into a small place. You might try to put a big thing into a small room, but it does not fit.
