An idea is something you think about. It is a way you feel about a thing. You might think an idea is a good one, or you might think it is a bad one.

When you have an idea, you are thinking about how things can help each other. You are trying to find what works and what does not. You want to find the best way to make life better for everyone.

This word is also used to talk about people. A person with many good ideas is often called a great idea man. They can come up with new ways to do things. They help people and make things better.
