A little is a word you use to say how small a thing is. It is a small number.

To be little means you are small. You are not as big as someone else. A little dog is small. You can hold it in one hand. A little girl is also little. She is a small child.

You can also use this word to say how much money is. If you have a little money, it is not much. But if you have a lot of money, it is a lot.

Sometimes, you use this word when you talk about things that are small in your life. If you are going to a party, you might say you are going to a little party. If you do something you love, you might say you did a little thing.

We also use this word to talk about how things look. If a picture of a cat is a little picture, it is small. If a big book is a little book, it is also small.

The word is important because it helps us know if something is small or not. It helps us to understand the world around us.
