The knee is a part of your body. It is the part in the front of your leg where you stand and walk. Your knee is a small area where your leg meets your body. It is made of three long pieces of hard stuff that come together.

Inside your knee, there is a round part. This round part is soft and has a soft cover. When you move your leg, this round part helps your leg go up and down. It helps you stand up straight, sit down, and walk.

The round part also helps you push your leg forward. When you push forward, your leg moves in a way that is easy to see. Your knee helps you do this.

Your knee also has a line that goes across it. This line is strong and helps keep your leg in one place. It is like a stay that holds your leg in position.

Sometimes, people hurt their knee. This happens when the round part gets hurt or the line gets torn. You might feel pain or a lot of pain when you move your leg. You might see a red spot or a big cut.

If your knee is hurt, your doctor might give you a kind of band to help your leg stay in one place. They might also tell you to rest your leg so it can get better.

You also need to be carefully when you stand or walk. If you stand on a hard floor, you might hurt your knee. You can also hurt your knee if you jump a lot or run quickly.
