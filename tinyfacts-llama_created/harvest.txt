A time in the year when people work together to make food from the green things they grew. They pick the things that are ready to eat before the cold starts and before they get too big.

Imagine you are a person who grows food. You work from the beginning of the growing time to the end. In the middle, you go to a place where many other people work together. They work from morning to night, all day long, to move the big round things that carry the food from the green things to the houses where people live.

When the time is over, they go back to their own homes to rest and to make sure everything is ready for the next time. They might share the food they made with their friends and family. This is a time to be together and to share what they have.

People also do this with animals. They help to bring the animals back to their homes when they are ready to give them a safe place to rest. They do this to keep the animals safe and to help them grow stronger for the next time they go out to find food.

In some places, this time is also a time for people to think about what they have. They look back at the things they did and the people they met. They remember the good times and the bad ones. They try to learn from their past so they can be ready for the next time.

This time is important because it shows us how we work together with each other. When people work together to make food and care for each other, they can make a big change in a small time.
