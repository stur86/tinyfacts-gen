When people have different ideas, they get angry. They might shout, use words they do not like, or even hit each other. This kind of fight is called a fight. It happens when two or more people do not agree on what is right.

Imagine you and your friend are playing a game. You both want to be the one who gets the ball. If you do not give it to the other person, you feel angry. You want to stop the game and have it over. This is a kind of fight too.

Sometimes, things go wrong because of a problem. A person might say something that hurts another person. This can make the other person very angry. They might want to hit the person who said it. They might also want to get ridden what they think is wrong.

When many people are in a fight, it can be very hard. They might not listen to each other. They might not want to stop. This can cause more hurt than one fight.

But fights are also important. They show us how strong we are. They help us learn how to stay calm when things get bad. They help us find a way to fix the problem.

Sometimes, we have to decide which fight is better. We might say, a fight is better than a fight. This is because one fight might be more important than another.
