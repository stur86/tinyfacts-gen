To go forward is to move toward the front. Imagine a line of people. Each person is standing in a different place. If you want to reach the end of the line, you need to move forward. You do not look back. You just keep walking.

It is like when you are in a room and you want to get out. You can only go forward. You do not stay where you are. You go toward the door. This is what we do when we move. We move forward, and we do not stop until we reach the end of our line.

Sometimes, we want to reach a far place. We go forward. We walk, run, or fly. As we move, the world looks different. The trees are taller and the sky is bright blue. We feel a feeling of being safe because we are moving.

We can also go forward in our minds. If you are afraid, you might go forward and think about what you are afraid of. You might imagine a good time in the past or a happy moment in the near past. This helps you feel better.

If you want to start something new, you can say, I am going forward. This means you are ready to begin. You are going in a new direction.
