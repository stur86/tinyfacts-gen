To make a plan is to think about what you want to do. You might want to learn how to play a game, or you might want to build a thing. When you make a plan, you tell everyone what to do and how to do it.

You can make plans for many things. You might make a plan for a trip. You can decide where you want to go, how you will get there, and what you will do when you get there. You can also make plans for your life. You might decide how you will spend your time, what you want to learn, and how you want to grow.

A computer can also make plans. If you give it a job, it will follow the steps you tell it. It can find answers to questions, send you pictures, and even make music. It works like a person, but it does not feel the same way.

Sometimes, people make plans for things that do not happen. They might plan a party to make people happy. They might plan a new job to make a better life for themselves. When people do not follow their plans, they might feel sad or angry.

To make a plan is important. It helps us know what to do and when. It helps us decide if we are on the right way. It helps us learn from our problems.
