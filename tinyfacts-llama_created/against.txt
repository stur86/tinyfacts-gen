When you say something is against, you mean it goes against what is already there.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friend. If you do something that makes your friend very angry, you are against them. You are not doing what they want you to do. You are doing something that makes them feel bad.

Sometimes, you might do something that is against the way things are. Maybe you want to do something new, but your family says no. You are against what is already there because you want to change it.

You can also be against things in your mind. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might be against it. You are afraid because it makes you feel scared.

Sometimes, you might be against things that are bad. If a doctor tells you that a bad thing will make you sick, you are against it. You do not want to get that bad thing.

But being against can also mean you are standing up for what is right. If someone is doing something very wrong, like taking money that does not really go to them, you are against them. You are standing up for the people who were hurt.

So, when you say something is against, you are telling people what you think. You are saying that what you are doing is not okay.
