A year in a group of ten is called a year. We use these years to tell time. When we start with one year and counters ten years, we reach the end of a group of ten years. This group of ten years is called a year.

A year is like a short time when we walk around the world. It is one hundred and twenty days long. In one year, the same days and nights happen many times. We go from one year to the next like this: year one, year two, year three, year four, year five, year six, year seven, year so on. This way, we can have a whole group of ten years and see how the year goes.

A group of ten years is like a big number with ten numbers. We can use this group to talk about many years at once. If you want to say that a year is one of ten years, you can say, this year is one of ten years. This helps us to talk about the same time over and over.

When we talk about a person who is old, we often say they are in their thirded year. This means the year when they are three years old. A person who is very old can be in their hundred and first, hundred and second, hundred and third, and so on years. This is because they have lived for many years.

People also say that a group of ten years is like a big change. In one year, a baby is a tiny child. In the next year, the child is a little bigger. In the year after that, the child is a bigger kid. After ten years, a person is an old person. This is because time goes very fast when we are young. When we are old, the years seem to go very slowly.
