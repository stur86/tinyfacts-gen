An thing can be a thing you hold. You use it to do work. You might have a book, a chair, a computer, or a phone. These are all things that do not change.

An animal is a thing with a body and a head. It has legs. Some have hair. Some do not. Some fly, and some walk. Animals need to eat to stay strong.

A house is a thing that stands on the ground. You live there. You make it warm. You put food in it. You make it safe. You also keep your family and friends there. A person lives in a house.

A word is a thing that tells us something. It is like a letter. A word can be short or very long. When you write a word, you are sending a sign to the people who read it. This sign is important because it helps us understand each other.

An idea is a thing that happens in your head. You might have an idea while you are walking. You might wonder what you will do later. An idea is a feeling in your mind. It is something you think about.

A person is a thing that lives in the world. They are not just one thing. They are many people together. Each person has their own face, hair, and clothes. They are the ones who live on the ground with you.

Things and people are different. Things are the ones you can hold. People are the ones who live. An animal is a thing that moves around. A house is a thing that stands. An idea is a feeling in your head.
