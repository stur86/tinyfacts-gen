To have a thing is to know it is there. When you see a thing, you know it is in front of you. You know it is not a thing you made or a thing that is only in your head.

If you are sure you are in a place, you have a feeling of being there. You know it is the real place. You feel safe.

To be a fact is to be true. A fact is something you know is real. It is not a guess.

If you have a fact, you can tell others. You do not have to hide it. You can say, I know this is true.

To have a thing also means you have it. You have it if you see it, if you touch it, if you hold it. You have it if you are sure you have it.

You can also have a thing if it is your mind. If you believe something is true, you have it. You do not need to see it to know it is there. You just know it is true in your heart.

Sometimes, when you have a thing, you can be sure you are doing the right thing. You can be sure you are being kind or safe. You have a feeling of being right.

Having a thing can help you. It can help you know what to do. It can help you feel safe.
