To fight over something is to have a problem and try to find a way to be right. When you say you have a fight, you mean you do not agree with someone. This is usually about something that is important and you both want to have control over.

When you fight, you use your words. You might tell the other person how you feel. You might use words that hurt. This is why you have a fight. You are trying to make the other person say yes or no.

Sometimes, fighting is about a thing that is not allowed. You might fight with a friend because they did something you do not like. You are trying to stop them from doing it.

There is also a word that is about a story. When you fight, you do not tell the truth. You might lie or tell the same story many times. This is a bad way to act.

Sometimes, people fight because they are angry. They might say things they do not mean. They might shout. This is a way to show that they are very angry.

You should not fight every time you have a problem. It is better to talk and try to understand each other. This way, you can find a way to live with the problem and still be happy.
