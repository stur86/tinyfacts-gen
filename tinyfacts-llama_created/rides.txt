A ride is something you go on to move around. It can be a car, a horse, a big round thing in the air, or a walk.

Imagine you are with a friend. You both want to go to a place that is high up in the sky. You can ask a grown person to take you. They put you in a big round thing and it goes up. You feel happy because you are moving and seeing the world from a new place.

If you ride a horse, you are moving very fast. You might feel like you are flying. You can go over fields and over trees. Some people ride horses for fun and to feel free.

A car ride is when you are in a car. You sit in a seat and the driver moves the car. You might drive on a road, go through a city, or go to a place far away. You can listen to music or watch a movie.

Sometimes you ride on a big round thing in the air. This is called a hot air ball. People fly on these balls to see the world from a new place. You can see the ground far away and look at many places at once.

You can also ride your own feet. This is called running. If you run very fast, you can go very far. You might feel like you are flying because you are moving so fast.

Rides can be fun. You can laugh and enjoy the moving. You can see many things from a new place. You can also meet new people and make friends on a ride.

But a ride can also be a little bit hard. You might feel a bit scared if you are going very fast. You might feel a bit cold if the wind is strong. You might hear strange noises that you do not like.
