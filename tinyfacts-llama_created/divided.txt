When you are told that something is done or that a group of people have decided on a thing, it means they have found a way to act together.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You all want to keep the ball. One person says, I want the ball. Another person says, No, I want it. There is a big fight, and you end up with the ball.

If many people decide the same thing, the group becomes a group. This group can make a new set of orders. They may decide what game to play next week. They may decide who gets to sit where or who gets to be the leader.

Sometimes a group decides that things are not the way they should be. This is called a problem. They might want to do something new or different. They might ask, Why are we doing this? Why are we doing this way?

A person might feel this way if they are in a group where they do not agree. They might feel angry or sad. They might want to change what is happening. They might want to be alone with their own thought.

When people are told that something is done, it means they have found a way to act together. It is about finding a new order or a new way of doing things.
