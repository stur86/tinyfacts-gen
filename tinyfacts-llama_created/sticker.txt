A thing with a picture on it that you stick to a wall, a table, or a piece of paper. It is like a small piece of paper with a picture or a word on it. When you put it on something, the picture stays there.

Sticks can be made of paper or hard stuff. Some sticks are soft and easy to pull. Others are hard and can be cut. You can draw a picture on the stick before you put it on. This makes the stick look nice before you use it.

You can also put a word on a stick. The word says something important. You can put a picture on a stick too. This is called a picture stick. You can put a picture of a cat or a dog on your wall. This keeps the cat or dog in your room so it does not leave.

Sometimes people use sticks for fun. They put a stick on a table and walk around. They see who can move the stick the farthest. Some kids have a game where they try to put a stick on a picture. If they do not get it right, they have to try again.

You can also put a stick on your face. This is for a party. You wear a funny face and a stick on your face. People laugh at you. It is a fun way to show you are happy.
