To enter something is to move into it. It means you step inside a place or a door. You go past the front of it. The door closes after you. If you enter a room, you sit down or stand in front of the desk. You might be waiting to talk to a person or to see the person.

If you enter a game, you join the other people playing. You can be the first one to touch the ball or the first one to cross the line. This happens when you step into the game, like when you enter a school or a hospital.
