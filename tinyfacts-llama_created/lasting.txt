To last is to stay for a long time. When you eat food, your body uses it. If you do not eat, you will feel very tired and you might feel sick. A lasting thing is something that stays in your mind or your body for a very long time.

Think about your friends. If you stay with them for many years, you will still remember them and love them. You will always think about the happy times you had together. You can remember the way they made you laugh, the way they helped you when you were sad, and the way they told you stories.

A lasting thing can also be a thing that never changes. A tree stays the same for many years. It does not change color, it does not change how tall it is, and it does not change how big it gets. It stays the same always.

When something lasts, you can be sure it will not go away. It will be here for a long time. This is good because it means you have something to look forward to.
