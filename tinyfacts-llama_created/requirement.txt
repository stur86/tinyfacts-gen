A need is when you have to do something. You might need food to eat, a bed to sleep, or a car to drive. When you have a need, you must act. You must go to the place where food is made, eat, and go home. This is important because it keeps you safe.

Sometimes, you might need something that is not here. You might need money to buy a new computer or a book you want to read. If you do not get what you need, you might feel sad. This is because you wanted it very much.

To have a need is to be ready to act. It means that you are waiting for the moment when you can do what you need to do.

When you do not have a need, you have a want. A want is when you want to do something. You might want to play with a ball, see a movie, or talk to a friend. If you have a want, you can act. You can go out and do what you want.

In life, you must have both needs and wants. You need to live a life that is good for you. You want to live a life that is fun and happy. You must find a way to do both so you can be happy and stay safe.
