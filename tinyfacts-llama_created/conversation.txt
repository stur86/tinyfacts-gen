A conversation is when two people talk to each other. They might talk about what is happening in their world. They might talk about their day. They might talk about their friends. They talk so much that they forget to eat or sleep.

Sometimes a conversation happens when two people meet. If you meet a new person, you might start talking. You might ask them how they are. You might talk about your job. You might talk about your family. After a while, you both start to feel like you know the other person. You might laugh and smile.

You can also have a conversation while doing things. If you watch a movie, you are talking to the person in front of you. You are talking about the story. You might ask questions. You might say, What happens next? You are in a group, and everyone is talking at the same time. You all talk about the same thing.

A conversation can be about many things. You can talk about your life. You can talk about your dreams. You can talk about your fears. You can talk about your hopes. You can talk about the world. You can talk about your friends. You can talk about your family. You can talk about the things you like.

A good conversation is when both people feel happy. They feel like they understand each other. They feel like they can tell anything. They feel like they can trust each other. It is when you feel like you are a part of something bigger than you.

Sometimes a conversation is about a problem. You and a friend are talking about something that is wrong. You are trying to find a way to fix it. You might ask each other questions. You might say, How can we help? You are trying to talk through the problem together.
