A floor is the part of a room that is on the ground. It is usually a hard thing that people walk on. It can be made of wood, or it can be made of hard white stuff that you walk on. Sometimes, floors are very wide and you walk on them for many steps. Other times, the floor is very small and you have to step very fast to stay on it.

Some floors are for people who work. They are made of strong hard stuff that keeps them safe while they work. Other floors are for people who are very old. They might be made of a soft stuff that is easy to walk on, so they don't hurt their feet as much.

When you stand on a floor, you feel the ground. You hear a sound when the floor hits the floor. You also see the floor when you look at it. The floor is always there, even when no one is standing on it.

Floors help us stay safe and make our rooms feel nice. They can also be very pretty. Some floors have bright colors or pictures on them. They can make a room feel happy and fun to be in.
