A small box with many keys is a thing you play to make nice sounds. You press the keys with your fingers. Each key is a place where the sound comes out. When you press a key, it makes a sound that is like a soft voice.

There are many kinds of these things. Some have only one key. Some have many keys, but they are all the same. Some are very small. Some are very big. You can hold one in your hand.

You can play this thing by moving your fingers over the keys. If you press a key and then another key, it makes a different sound. You can play many different songs. Some are for big groups. Some are for one person. You can play it alone or with others.

These things are very old. People have played them for a very long time. They help us make music that makes us feel happy or sad. Sometimes they make sounds that are like animals, like a cat or a dog. Sometimes they make sounds like a person speaking. They are a very important thing in the world of music.
