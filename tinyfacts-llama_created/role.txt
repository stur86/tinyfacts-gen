A person has a job. This job is what they do. They do this work every day. They might work with others. They might work alone. They might do this work for a long time or just for a short time.

A person can have many different jobs. Some jobs are easy and some jobs are hard. Some jobs pay a lot of money. Some jobs pay little money. Some jobs need you to be very strong. Some jobs need you to be very quiet. Some jobs need you to be very fast. Some jobs need you to be very good at writing.

A person can also have a job that is not just about what they do. This job is about who they are. Maybe you are a teacher. You help people learn new things. Maybe you are a doctor. You help people get better when they are sick. Maybe you are a police person. You keep the town safe.

When people act in a movie, they often play a job. They wear clothes and use words. They act like the person they are playing. They might say words like, I am a person. I am happy. I am sad. This helps the people watch the movie.

In our world, you can have a job that is not always the same. Some people go to work every day and then go home. Other people work in a place with many people. They work with a group. They work together. They help each other.

Sometimes, you might have a job that is not one job at all. You might have many jobs at once. You might be a teacher and a dog walker. You do this job because it helps you and it makes you happy.
