An uncle is a person who is your father, but not your mother. He is your father, but he is not the one who gave you your mother. He is like a father, but he is not the one who has a child with you.

Uncles are usually older than your father. They have lived for a long time and have seen many things. They can tell you stories about when you were a little boy or girl. They can also give you money when your father is not around.

Some people say that an uncle is the person who helps you when you need it. If you are feeling sad or angry, your uncle might listen to you and tell you to be calm. He might also help you fix something if you broke it.

Because he is not the mother, an uncle does not have to care for the same kind of things as a mother. He might not have to change clothes for a child or give them a bathroom story. But he still loves them and wants to see them happy.

You can call an uncle anything you like. You can call him a man, a father, or a big brother. Some people just call him uncle because it is a word that is easy to say and remember.
