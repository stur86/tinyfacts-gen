When someone is a part of a story, they are a person who does not do the wrong thing. They are the one the police or the people in the movie ask to show.

In a story, a person might be the one who was the bad person. They might have done a thing that made many people very angry. The police ask the person to come and answer questions. They might ask them why they did what they did.

The person who is not the bad person is the one the police ask to stay. They must come to the place where the people in the story live. The police want to know what they did. They want to know if they did the wrong thing. The person must answer their questions.

If the person does not answer, the story goes bad. They might get in trouble. The police might say the person is a bad person. The bad person might have to go to a place where they have to stay for a long time. They might not get to see their family again for a long time.

This is how the story ends. The person who was not the bad person stays safe. The story is done.
