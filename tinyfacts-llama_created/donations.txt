To give something is to give it to another person. When you give money, food, or a car, you are helping someone. You are helping them because you care about them.

You can give money to a person who is in trouble. This is a way to show you are sorry. You give them the money so they can buy food or a place to stay. You want to help them feel better.

Sometimes, people give things that are not money. They give books, clothes, or a box of food. These are also ways to help a person. They give these things because they want to help the other person feel good.

You can also give money to a group of people. If many people in a town want to build a new school, they can give money to the group. The group uses the money to build the school.

Giving is important. It helps people and makes the world a kinder place.
