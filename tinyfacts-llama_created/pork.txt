To eat something is to take it into your body. You can eat food that comes from animals. One of these animals is a big animal that lives on the ground. It has hard parts in its body. Sometimes we eat parts of this animal. We call these parts the same as the animal. We eat the soft parts, the white parts, and the brown parts.

Another kind of food comes from the bottom of the water. It is made from the dead parts of the water. We pick this food out of the water. We do not eat the water. We only eat the dead parts.

Sometimes we eat parts from one animal and use parts from another animal to make food. We take the parts from one animal and cut them into smaller parts. We then use these parts to make food for the body. This way, we keep the parts of the animal that are hard. We use the soft parts and the brown parts. We only use one kind of animal. We do not eat the other kind.

When we eat, we take the food into our body. Our body breaks down the food. The body uses the food. The body makes things that help us live. The food gives the body the power it needs to move, to grow, to stay warm, and to keep us going through the day.
