A long time ago, people built a big building in a city. This building was for people who had died. When someone died, their family would put a picture of them on the wall. They would write something about how the person was important to them. This helped people remember the person.

Today, we build something different. We do not put pictures on a wall. Instead, we remember a person by doing what they did. If a person does something very good, we remember them. If a person makes someone happy, we remember them. If a person helps others, we remember them.

Sometimes, we build a big thing to remember a person. This is a big building with a lot of words about the person. We put the words on the wall and around the building so everyone knows about the person. We also put pictures and other things that remind us of the person.

We also build a small thing to remember. This is a small box or a paper that we put in our home. We put money in the box or write a note on the paper. We put the box in a room so everyone can see it.

We build these things because we remember the good times. We remember the person who helped us. We remember the happy times. We remember the people who are still with us.

Sometimes, we build a place to remember a person who died very young. This is a small room with a picture of the person. We sit in the room and think about the person. We remember what they did. We remember how they were loved.

We build these things so people do not forget. We remember the people who came before us. We remember the people who are still here today. We remember the good times and the hard times.
