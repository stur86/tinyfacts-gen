To repeated something means to do it many times. You can do this with your hands, your feet, or even your body. Imagine you want to show a picture to a group of people. You might tell them the story many times, so they remember it better.

Sometimes, we repeat words to make them easier to understand. We use the word again and again because it is the right word for our idea. This helps us say the same thing over and over until everyone understands.

We also repeat things to remember. Think about a time when you saw a movie. You might watch it many times until you know every story and every move. This helps you remember the story and the people in it.

Sometimes, we repeat things because we are afraid. If you are afraid of a dog, you might say, over and over, that you are not going near the dog. This helps the dog know that the person is not going to come any closer.

We can also repeat things because we want to feel strong. If you are feeling sad, you might repeat the words to yourself, over and over, to help you feel better. This helps your body and mind feel safe.

Repeating something is like putting a mark on a piece of paper. The mark lets you know that the story is important. It helps you remember and feel the story.
