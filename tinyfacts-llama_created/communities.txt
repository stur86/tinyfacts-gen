A group of people who live near each other and work together is called a city. There is also a group of people who live in a small area. This group might be made up of people who share the same job or who like the same kind of music.

A place where people go to be safe is called a town. Many towns have a police and fire team that helps keep everyone safe. There are also places where people go to buy food and other things they need.

A group of people who live in one place and work in another place is called a town. They might drive cars to work in a city and come back home to their town. This is often the way people live today.
