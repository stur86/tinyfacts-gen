The word surrounds means to move around all around something. If you are standing in front of a big tree, you are surrounding it. You walk around the tree so you can see both sides.

A thing that surrounds is something that is always near. It is always moving around. If you put a ball on the ground, it is surrounding the ground. The ground is not the only thing it is surrounding. The sky and the ground and the tree are all surrounding the ball at the same time.

Surrounding can also mean to stay near something even if you are far away. If you are walking very far from your house, you are surrounding your house. Your house is always there, no matter how far you walk.

Sometimes, people use this word with animals. If an animal runs around in a big open space, it is surrounding the space. It is always moving around the space, even though it is very far away. It is like it is watching the space, waiting for something to happen.

In some places, people say that a city or a town is surrounding a body of water. This means that the city or town is always near the water. The water is always there, and the city or town is always near it.
