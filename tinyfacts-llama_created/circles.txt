To make a round thing means to form a round place. You can do this with paper, a ball, a cup, or anything you hold in your hand. A round thing is a place that has no ends. You can look at it from many sides without ever getting tired of seeing it.

Imagine you are in a room with many chairs. Each chair is a round thing. If you walk to the chair next to you, you will still be in the same room. You can keep walking around the room without ever leaving it. This is because there is no beginning or end to the room.

When you make a round thing, you are using your hands and your brain. You start with a line of paper. You draw round and round with a piece of paper. You make the line go in one direction. Then, you use your hands to push the paper in the other direction. This keeps going until the line is a perfect round thing.

You can also make a round thing out of a small piece of wood. You can use a small piece of wood and round it with a round thing. After a while, the wood is a round thing that can hold your hand.

Sometimes, you make a round thing that is not just a place. You can make a round thing out of air. Imagine a big round thing that goes through the sky. It is not a place, but it is very round. It goes around the world many times each day.

In our lives, we often use the word round. We might say a friend is round. This means the person is nice and has many good friends. Or we might say a book is round. This means the book is a good thing to read.
