To go to a small place in the middle of the big water is to go to a land. It is like a tiny piece of land that is very small and very high. People call it New Land because it is in the middle of the world.

A long time ago, people went there and made a new home. They built houses and roads, and they grew food. Now there are many people living there. Some people live in houses with green on the top. Some live in houses with big rooms with many rooms inside. Some live in houses with many floors, so many people can live there at the same time.

There is a big city with many streets. The city is very busy. People drive cars and walk. There are many people. They go to work every day. There are also small places where people go to buy things. There are shops with food, clothes, and things people need to live.

The people in this land like to play and have fun. They go to the park and play on the ground. They go to the water and ride on the water. They go to the big fields and grow food. They also go to the big fields and watch the animals. Some people go to the big fields to take pictures of the beautiful things.

People from other lands also visit this land. They come to see the bright blue water and the green land. They see the tall trees and the small green houses. They learn about the people and the way they live. They talk with the people and ask questions.

Even though it is a small land, the people in New Land are very strong. They work hard to build their homes and their city. They help each other and make sure everyone is safe. They also have a good way of living. They are kind to each other, and they help when someone is sad or hurt.
