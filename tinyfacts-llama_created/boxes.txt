A box is a thing that holds many things. You can put many books in one box. You can put small things in a box too. Sometimes a box is very big and it holds a whole room. Or a box is very small and it holds a tiny thing you might find on the floor.

You can also use a box to carry things. If you have many things to move from one place to another, you need a box. You put the things inside the box and then you walk or drive the box to the new place.

Some boxes are made of hard stuff. They can hold fire. If a box is made of hard stuff and it is very heavy, it is called a heavy box. You can also make a box out of wood. Wood is a strong stuff that can hold a lot of things.

People use boxes to carry money. They put money in the box and then they walk to the store to buy food or other things. This is a way to keep your money safe and easy to carry.
