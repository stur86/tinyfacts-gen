To look at something. This is when you see something that is in front of you. You use your eyes to see the light and the colors. You see the world around you.

Imagine you are in a big room. There is a high wall in the middle of the room. You see people on the wall. They look like a big group of friends. You might want to see them again.

You can look at a picture that someone makes. The picture shows a place with tall trees and a bright blue sky. You can see the green of the trees and the bright blue of the sky. This is a way to see a place.

You can also look at a picture of a thing. If you want to see how a car looks, you look at the picture. You might see the soft parts around the car, like the soft parts on the front and the soft parts on the back.

Sometimes you look at a place where you are going. You look at your road. You see the cars and the houses along the way. You know where you are and where you are going.

When you look at something, you are using your eyes to see the world. You are taking in what is around you. This is how you know where you are and what is happening.
