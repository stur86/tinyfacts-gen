To comment something means to talk about it. When you talk about a story, a picture, or a thing, you are commenting on it. You might say why something happened or how it feels.

Imagine you see a movie. You might feel angry, happy, or sad. You could also think about why you feel this way. You are commenting on the movie. You are telling others about it.

In a different way, you can comment on a person. You might say nice things about their face, their clothes, or their voice. You are telling others that you like them. You are not telling them their real name or their story, but you are still talking about them.

When you comment, you use words to talk about what is happening. You might use words that mean you are surprised, you are excited, or you are worried. You use your voice to share your ideas with others.

Sometimes, we comment on things that are not people. We might talk about a game, a song, or a book. We use our words to explain why we like it or why it is important. We are still talking about the thing, but we are also talking about how it makes us feel.

To comment is to share your ideas with others. It is a way of being a part of a group. When you comment, you show that you care about what is going on.
