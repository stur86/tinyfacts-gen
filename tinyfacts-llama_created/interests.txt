To have an interest is to care about something or someone. It is to feel happy or sad because of what happens. When you have an interest, you want to learn more. You might watch a show about something you like. You might read a book about a story. You might talk to a friend about a game they play.

You can have many interests. You might like to read stories about far away lands. You might like to play games that make you feel happy. You might like to help people or animals. You might like to watch movies with your family. You might like to walk in the park or run around with your friends.

Having an interest is good because it helps you grow. If you like a game, you learn how to play it. If you like to read, you learn new words and ideas. If you like to help people, you feel a sense of power and a way to make a good change.

Sometimes, you might feel a little confused. You want to do one thing, but your mind is on another thing. This is because your interest is strong. Your mind wants to learn more about that thing. You might feel happy when you are doing something you love. You might feel sad if you are doing something you do not like.

To know what you like is to have an interest. If you do not know what you like, it is hard to find a job or a place to live. But if you know what you like, you can find a way to make it happen.
