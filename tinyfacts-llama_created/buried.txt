To be covered is to rest on the ground. You sit on the floor or on a high bed. When something is covered, it is hidden from the world. We cover our beds with soft things so we can sleep. We also cover things that are broken so people can see they are not hurt anymore.

If something is not covered, it can be seen. A tree is covered with green things from the ground to the top. The ground is also covered with soft things. When you look at the ground, you see the world under it. You see the houses and the houses of people.

Sometimes, people cover things that are not safe. They cover old things to keep them from being broken. They cover food so it does not go bad. They cover books so they stay safe. This helps us keep our lives safe.

If a person is very sad, they might cover their face. This means they cover their eyes and their mouth so they do not see the world. They might cover their nose to stay quiet. They might cover their body to stay warm. They are trying to hide their pain and to feel better.
