To work means you do things that help people. You might work in a big building where you fix things that are broken. You might work on a road so cars can go around a city without problems.

You can also work at home. This is when you sit at a desk and do jobs for people. You might write papers, make things, or help friends with their work.

Some jobs need people to be on the ground. You might work as a doctor, a teacher, or a police man. Your job is to keep people safe. You help them feel better when they are sick or hurt.

You can also work in a store. If you are a store person, you help people find what they need. You answer questions, show them things, and help them buy things.

When you work, you do not just sit around. You usually do things that need to be done. You might work hard for a few hours, and then you can rest. It is a way to pay for living.

If you work very hard, you can get money. Money is something people use to buy food, clothes, and other things they need. When you work, you become part of a team. You work with many other people to get things done.

Being a worker is important. It helps our world stay moving.
