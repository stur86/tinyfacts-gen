A small, moving water that runs across the ground. You might see them in many places. They are often very quiet. But they can also be loud when they are full.

The water goes from one place to another. It goes over many rocks and over many small things. The water often changes as it goes. In the beginning, it is very clear and cold. As it goes over the rocks, it gets darker and warmer. It can even become green or blue if there are many tiny living things in it.

Some of these small water ways are very deep. You can jump over them. They can be very wide. You can walk along the bottom and see many different kinds of things living there. You might see tiny animals, small pieces of rock, and other small living things.

You can also go across them by a small thing that people use. They sit on a wide piece of wood and go across. The water is under them, and they feel the water moving under their feet.

Sometimes, these small water ways join together. They form a big water way that goes far across a land. This water way can carry people and things from one place to another. It can also carry tiny living things that go through it and then get out into another water way.

The water can also change the ground around it. It can wear down rocks and pick up small pieces of rock and tiny living things. It can also fill in small places where there are no more water.

In the end, the water goes back to where it started. But sometimes it goes far away and comes back many times. It changes over and over, never quite the same as it was the first time.
