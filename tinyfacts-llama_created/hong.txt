To hang something means you put it in a place where it is not on the ground anymore. You might hang a picture on the wall, a bag on a line, or a light thing on a long line.

Imagine you are a parents. You want to keep your child safe. You might put them in a room that is not the room they play in. You hang a window so the light does not go out.

A long time ago, people used to hang things on the wall. They hung things that told stories from the old days. They also used to hang things that kept people safe. Today, we still use this word to mean something is hanging there.
