To often means that something happens many times. If you want to know what happens in a long story, you can say, usually, that the story happens many times. This is because you know that the same story is happening many times.

If you say that a person is often very happy, you mean that they usually feel very happy. If you say that a place is often cold, you mean that it is very often very cold.

You can also use this word with animals. If you say that a dog often runs around the house, you mean that the dog usually runs in this place. The dog might do this many times a day.

This word is important because it helps us understand how things really are.
