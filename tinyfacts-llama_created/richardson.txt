A person who is very good at their job or a job that they love is called a person with a good job. This person may work in a school, a hospital, a big building, or a place that makes things. They do a lot of work every day and are very good at it. They can do many different things and are always busy.

A person with a good job is not the one who gives money. They are not the one who buys things for people. They are the one who makes the things that we need. They are the ones who build roads, make cars, and build houses. They are the ones who help us live in a world that is full of people and things.
