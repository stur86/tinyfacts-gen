When you are worried, you feel a big, dark feeling in your chest. This feeling is often because you are afraid about something that might happen in the near time. You might think about bad things that could happen to you, your family, your friends, or even your job.

You might feel like you should do something to stop the bad thing. This can make your heart beat very fast. You might also want to hide or run away. The feeling can make your face look red and your eyes look wide.

Sometimes, people get worried because they are afraid to do something. They might be afraid of being alone or afraid of a new situation. Other times, people feel worried because they have a big problem to fix. They might feel like they can't do it alone and need to find help from others.

You can also feel worried when you are waiting for something to happen. You might feel nervous or your mind is full of questions. This is a normal feeling, and it happens to everyone.

If you are worried, you might say things like, I am worried about this. I am afraid of this. I feel bad because of this.
