To be the first is to act before everyone else. It happens when no one else does anything. It is the start of something new.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. If you are the first one to pick it up, you are the first. You do not have to wait for anyone else. You just start.

In a group of people, if you are the one who starts talking, you are the first. You do not wait for anyone else to join you. You go first.

It is also about time. If something happens before everyone else, it is the earliest. You get there before everyone else. It is a good thing to be the first.

You can also be the first in your school. If you do something that no one else has done, you are the first. You did it before everyone else. You are a leader.

The word can also mean the very beginning of a story. If a story starts with someone, that person is the first. They are the one who begins the story.
