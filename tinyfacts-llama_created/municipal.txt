A city is a place where a lot of people live. This is where you buy food, where you go to school, and where you work. Many cities have many streets, many houses, and many places where people go to buy things.

A city has many people who work together. They share the same area and make things happen. They also share the same water and power. They help each other with roads, schools, and police. This is because they are all under one name.

In a city, you might have a big building for the police. There are many people who watch what people do and keep the area safe. You also have many stores where you can buy food, clothes, and other things you need. There are many parks where you can go to play or sit.

Sometimes, a city is very big. It has many more people than a small town. It might have many buses and trains so you can move around easily. It might have many different kinds of houses and lots of different kinds of food. But even if a city is very big, the people who live there still have to work together.

There are also cities made for people who live far away. These cities are big and have many many houses. People go to these cities to work or to go to school. They might have to pay money to live there, and they have to follow the city ways. They might not have all the same things as people who live in small towns, but they have their own way of living.
