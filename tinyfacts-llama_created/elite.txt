An order of people is when they are the best in their group. They have done many good things and are very good at their work. They are often the ones who get the most money and are the ones who get the best jobs.

They are also the ones who go to the best schools and have many friends. They are often the ones who have been in the top of their schools for a long time. They are the ones who know everything and are the ones who get to do the things that are important.

Some orders are very high. They are the ones who are the head people in their groups. They are the ones who make the big plans and give the orders. They are the ones who are very important and get the most power. Other orders are lower. They are the ones who do the work that helps the people who are higher. They are the ones who fix the cars, clean the houses, and do the other jobs that are very important.

People are often sorted into these orders by how well they do. You are not an order if you do not do well. If you are the best, you are an order. If you are just okay, you might be lower. If you do not do at all, you might be the very bottom.

Some orders have a name. There is the high order of the world, the high order of the land, and the high order of the mind. There is also the order of the blood, the order of the fire, and the order of the wind. Each order has its own way of doing things and its own power.
