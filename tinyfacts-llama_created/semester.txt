A month is a long time for a person. We often have many of these. A group of people in school sits together for some of these months. They are in one group for one whole group of months. Then they are in another group for another whole group of months. This way, they get to be with many different people.

During one of these months, people usually learn a lot. They do many classes, read books, and learn from a teacher. They also do work. Sometimes, they go to a school that is far away. They stay in a different house each night.

After these months, the group of people moves to another group. This is why they have many different groups. Each group has different classes. They also learn from different teachers. Sometimes, they see a different kind of person each month.

At the end of these months, the group of people gets ready to move on to the next one. They do not always know who they will be in which group. They are a little afraid because they do not know what the next group will be like.

People usually have one or two groups. Some people have many groups. They like to have many because they get to meet many different people and learn from them.
