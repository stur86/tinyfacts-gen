Friends are people you like. You do things together. You tell each other things that are not true. You laugh with them. You like to be near them. Friends do not care if you are big or small. They do not care if you are good at games or if you can not read. They just like you for being you.

Some friends are people you meet in school. You sit next to them in class. You talk with them during lunch. You talk about your day. You feel happy when you see them.

You can also have friends from other places. You see them at a park. You talk with them. You learn about where they are from and what they like to do.

Friends can help you when you are sad. They listen to you. They tell you things that make you feel better. They are there when you want to cry.

Sometimes friends do things that are not good for you. They go to a party that is too loud. They drink too much. They say things that hurt your face. But even then, you still like them because they are your friends.

You need friends to be happy. A person who is alone is not happy. They feel like they are missing something. Friends make you feel like you are not alone.
