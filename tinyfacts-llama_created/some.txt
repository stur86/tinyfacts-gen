The word some is used to talk about many things.

When you say you have some of something, you mean you have a few. This can be food, a book, or a ball. You might have one of these things in your hand or in your bag.

Think about how you feel when you have some money. You can buy something you want. You can buy a drink or a small thing to play with. You do not have all the money, but you have some.

You can also use this word when you are talking about people. You might say, I have some friends. This means you have many people you like to be near.

Some things are small. Some things are big. Some things are made of hard stuff. Some things are made of soft stuff. You can have many of these things if you look at them all.

The word some can also mean you do not have any of something. If you have no food, you feel very tired and sad. If you do not have any money, you might have to ask for help.

So, when you say you have some, you mean you have more than one.
