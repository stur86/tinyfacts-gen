A drink that has a small glass and a few pieces of something is called a drink. The drink has a few parts. One part is the clear stuff from the bottle. The other parts are the small pieces that are not clear. You can put the small pieces in the bottle after you drink the clear stuff. This is why you see the small pieces in your glass.

Some people make drinks by adding small pieces of things to the clear stuff. You can add small pieces of a sweet thing. You can add small pieces of a strong drink. You can also add small pieces of food. The small pieces give the drink a different look and a different feel.

Coffees are like drinks that have a small glass and a small piece of something. You can drink a small glass of coffee and feel a bit like you are in a quiet room. The small pieces of coffee are what make the drink. Some people like to drink them with a small glass. Others like to drink them in a big glass.
