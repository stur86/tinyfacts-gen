To suggest something is to give an idea or a plan. You might say, you have a great idea for a new movie. You think it should be a story about a young girl who finds a way to fly. The person listening might hear these words and think, that sounds like a good idea. They might then ask you to explain why you think this is a good idea. This is how people often learn new things.

You can also suggest things you would like to do. If you want to go to a new city, you might suggest it to your friends. They can help you decide if it is a good idea to go there. They can ask you questions about what you want to do and what you are looking for in a new place.

Sometimes you suggest things that are not a good idea. If you want to start a new business, but you do not have any money, you might suggest you just stay home. This is a bad idea because you have a job and you need money to pay it. It is better to think about what you really want to do and what you can do to get there.
