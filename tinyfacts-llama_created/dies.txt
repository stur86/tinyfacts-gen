To die means you are going to a place where there are no more living people. It is the end of your life. When a person dies, their body stops working. Their brain stops thinking and they do not feel anything anymore. After this happens, no one can come back.

Sometimes, a person dies from their own body. This happens when the heart stops beating, or the blood does not reach the body. Other times, death comes from outside. This can happen when a person is very sick and their body stops working because of the bad things in the body. A person can also die from things they eat or from things that hurt their body.

When a person dies, they stop being a living thing. They are gone from the world. It is the end of their story.
