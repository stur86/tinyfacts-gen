To show something is true can be a big job. Sometimes you hide things for a long time. You might keep a surprise for a friend or a family person. Or you might keep a problem to yourself.

When you show something, you make it clear. You tell everyone what is happening. You let them see what you have done. This can be very important. It helps people understand each other.

Imagine you keep a deep, dark story. You tell a friend, but they do not want to hear it. The story stays hidden. You do not tell anyone else about it. But if someone shows the story, everyone gets to see it. They can understand why you did it.

Sometimes you show a picture. You show a face you did not know. You show a moment that happened a long time ago. This can help people remember or learn something.

In a way, showing is like giving. When you show something, you give it away. You are letting others see it. This can be a good thing. It can help people learn. It can bring people together.

But showing can also be hard. You might be afraid to show something. You might be afraid that people will not understand. You might be afraid of what others will think. When you show, you are letting your guard down. You are being your self.
