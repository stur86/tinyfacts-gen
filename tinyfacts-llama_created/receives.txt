To get something is to get it. This happens when you want it. You might want a book. You look for the book in a store. When the store finds the book, the person inside puts it in a bag. The bag goes to you. You have it now.

Sometimes, you give something to someone. You might give your friend a big hug. This makes them very happy. Or you might give a child a small thing. You are showing them you care.

When you do this, you are the one who gets the thing. The thing is the one that moves. It is what you want to get.

You can also say that something is getting ready. If a car is getting ready to leave, it is moving. It is going to go to a far place. It is ready to go.

To be gets ready is another way to say the same thing. You can be ready to go or you can be ready to get ready. Both mean you are ready to do something.
