To drink something is to put it into your body. You can drink from a bottle or a cup. You might drink water, or you might drink a drink from a store. When you drink, the water goes down your throat and into your chest. It then goes through your body and into your stomach.

Inside your body, there are tiny things that help you live. When you drink, these tiny things take in the water and the other things in the drink. They go into your blood, and your body uses them to make you stronger. Some drinks help you feel better when you are sick.

You can also drink when you are happy. When you are happy, you might drink from a cup or a bottle. You might drink a drink that makes you feel good. Some people like to drink during movies or when they play games.

Drinking is also important for babies. Their bodies need to get the right food to stay strong. The mother or a close person gives the baby the food through the mother or a bottle. This helps the baby grow up.

Sometimes, people drink to help them remember things. They might drink before a big game or a talk with a friend. This can help them feel more calm and ready to do things.
