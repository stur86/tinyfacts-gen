Sometimes a person who lives in one place is called a town person. They know the streets and houses and can go to any house in their town. But a person who only goes from one place to another and stays in each place for a short time is called a free man. They have no home to go back to.

A person who goes from one place to another can have many jobs. They might be a teacher, a doctor, a police person, or a person who helps people cross a big water. They might also be a person who moves to another city for work.
