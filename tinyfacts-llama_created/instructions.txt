To tell someone what to do is to give them a way to do it. This is when you give someone a set of steps to follow. You might write these steps down so they know what to do.

Think of this like a road. You tell a person where to go. They follow the road and get to where they need to go. They do not think they know how to get there. They follow what you say.

Sometimes you need to tell someone how to do something that is not easy. You might say, you need to pick up something from a far place. The person must follow your words. You do not tell them to think about it. You just give them what to do.

You can also give someone how to do something. If you are a teacher, you tell your students how to do a job. You show them how to do it and give them the steps to follow.

Sometimes, you give someone how to do something that is hard. You might say, you need to fix a thing that is broken. You tell them what to look for and how to fix it. You do not tell them to try to figure it out on their own. You give them the steps to follow.

You can also give someone how to do something in a group. You are the one who tells them how to act. You are the one who tells them what to say and how to act. This is how you lead people when you are part of a team.

When you give someone how to do something, you help them. You tell them what to do so they can do it.
