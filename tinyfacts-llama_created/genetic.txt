Your body is made of many parts, all working together. Every person is like a set of numbers and words put into one one piece of paper. These numbers and words tell your body how to work, what things to make, and where to put your tiny living things.

Inside you, there are many groups of tiny things that carry these numbers and words. These groups are like long lines of paper. Each line has a set of numbers and words that tell how the tiny things should work. When you look at these lines, you see how your family is built.

Some groups have many of the same numbers and words. These groups are like the same family. The people in those groups have more of the same things, and they do not change much.

Other groups have different numbers and words. These groups are like different families. The people in those groups have different things, and they change a lot.

When a group has many of the same numbers and words, it usually stays the same for many years. But when a group has different numbers and words, the people who are part of that group might change over time. This is why you and your friends are different from your mom or dad.

The numbers and words in your body are what make you you. They tell your body how to grow, how to stay warm, and how to make the tiny living things that are inside you.

You can also get some numbers and words from the people around you. If your mom or dad has some numbers and words, you might have them too. This is why you might look like them or share some of their ways.

So, the numbers and words inside you and the people around you are both from the same set of lines. They are built together in different ways, but they both tell you who you are and where you come from.
