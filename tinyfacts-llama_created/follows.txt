When you follow something, you move after it. If you follow a dog, you go behind it. If you follow a ball, you chase it. When you follow a word, you listen to what it says and try to do what it wants you to do.

Imagine you are in a room and someone says,  Go to the table. You go to the table. You do not just stand there. You move to the table. You follow the word.

Sometimes you follow people. If a teacher says,  Do this, the student follows. The student does what the teacher says. They follow the teacher.

We also follow things that are not people. We follow a light when we drive. We follow a road when we go from one place to another. We follow a song. If you like a song, you follow it. You listen to it and try to feel like the song is in your heart.

Following is important. It helps us learn and grow. If you follow a story, you learn about what happened. If you follow a game, you learn how to play it. Following can also help you stay safe. If you follow a warning, you might stay away from a place that has a big fire.
