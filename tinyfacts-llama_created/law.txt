A thing that is set by a group of people is a way to act. It is something you do when you follow the way of that group. This way of doing things is called a way or a set of ways.

The people who make a way have thought about how to act. They have looked at what happened before and have found out what is right. They write this down so that everyone knows it.

When a group of people says a way is the right way, everyone must follow it. If someone does not follow the way, they might do something bad. This can cause trouble for everyone.

Sometimes the way is very old. People might not know if it is still the right way. In these cases, a group of people can help decide if it is still okay. They look at how the way is doing and decide if it still works.

There are also ways that are made by many people. These can be very important for a whole area. They help make sure that everyone is following the same way.
