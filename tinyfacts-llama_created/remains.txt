When something happens to a person or something that has been gone, the thing that remains is the part that is left after the parts have been taken away. This can happen many times in a person or a group of people.

Imagine you have a group of friends. One day, one of them leaves. The friends are sad because they miss their friend. This is like the person who left. The friends still remember their friend and the time they had together. Even though the friend is not there, the friends still have the memories of their time with them. Those memories are the remains of the friend.

Or think about a fire. When a fire burns, it takes away a part of the building. But even though the building is gone, the remains of the fire are still there. The fire left behind is what we see and feel after the building is gone. It is the part of the story that is left after everything else is gone.

Sometimes, something that is very old can leave behind a small piece. This piece can be small, like a tiny rock or a tiny bit of wood. But even this small piece can still be a part of what happened. The old thing left behind is like the small piece that stays after we have taken everything away.

The word remains also talks about things that are not gone at all. When you grow up, you leave your home and start a new life. This new life is the remains of your old life. Your old life is still there, even though you are not in it anymore. You are still a part of who you were before.

In a story, the remains of a person or a thing can tell us what happened. If a person dies, their remains can help us understand what was happening in the story. If a thing dies, the remains can show us what happened to it.
