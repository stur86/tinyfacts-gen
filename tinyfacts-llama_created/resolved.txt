To be in a good state can be important. When you feel safe, you can be happy. If you feel sad, you might cry. If you feel angry, you might get angry again. This happens when your body is ready or when you do something you do not want to do.

When people say a thing is over, it usually means the problem or the thing that was causing trouble is gone. This can happen in many ways. If you have a fight, you might say the fight is over. If a person has a bad word, they might say it is over.

Sometimes a person has a problem. They might feel afraid. They might be worried about what will happen next. They might feel a lot of trouble inside. If they talk to a friend or a doctor, they can find a way to make the problem better. They might learn a new way to feel or do things. This is a good thing, because it makes the person feel better.

In this way, when a problem is gone, we say it is over. It is like a big stop sign.
