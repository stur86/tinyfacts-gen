To decide something means to pick the right one. You might think about a job, a place to live, or a person you like. You ask yourself, which one is best? You look at the words, the job, the place, or the person. Sometimes you like one more than the other. Sometimes you are sure.

You might also decide to do something. You might want to go to a park in the morning. You decide to leave the house. You are making a plan.

Sometimes you pick a friend. You like them and you want to spend time with them. You want to laugh and talk. You are happy they are your friend.

In a game, you might pick a number. You pick the number that feels right. You do not look at the numbers the other people picked. You just feel your number. You hope it is the right one.

To decide something can be hard. You might feel not sure. You might want to do many different things. But you must pick one. You must decide.
