To see something is to look at it. When you see a tree, you look at it. You look at its green color, the round part at the top, and the tall part that reaches the sky. You look at the way it moves in the wind and the way it makes sounds when it is heavy with water.

To know something is to understand it. If you want to know why a thing happens, you look at what is happening. You look at how it is made and how it works. You look at how it is done and how it has always been done.

A group of people who study the world and the way it works can be called a group of people who know things. They use their eyes to look at the world and their minds to understand what is going on. They look at the world and then they write what they think.

Sometimes, when you see something, you also feel something. You might feel a feeling of calm when you look at a picture of a happy place. You might feel a feeling of fear when you see a picture of a dark place. Your mind and your body work together to show you how you feel.

In the world, we use the word to talk about many things. We use it to talk about things that are new. We use it to talk about things that are very old. We use it to talk about things that are not yet here. We use it to talk about things that are very far away.

When we look at the world, we see how it is. When we know things, we understand how it is.
