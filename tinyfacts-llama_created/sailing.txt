To go by the water is to move from one place to another by a small thing that is pushed or pulled by the water. You do not use your hands like you do on land. You sit in a chair with a seat that is not on the ground, and the chair is held by a man who pulls on a long line with a bit on the end.

Imagine a big piece of wood with a hard top. The wood is held up by a strong line, and a bit on the end of the line is held by a man. The man uses his hands to hold the bit, and the bit pulls on the wood. The wood turns and goes round and round, carrying you across the water.

Some of these things are very fast. They can go very high up in the air and cross great wide bodies of water. They can also carry many people and things at once. They are an important way for people to go far places.

There are also some small ones that sit on the ground. They have a long line and a bit on the end. A person sits in the chair, and the chair is held by the bit. The person pulls on the line, and the chair turns round and round. This is how you go across a small body of water or a long way on land without using your hands.

People also use these things for fun. They can go very fast, and the wind makes them go even faster. They can also cross the water while it is moving, and people are often on these things during a big cross of water to get from one side to the other side of a wide water body.

You can also use the word to mean being very strong. If someone is very good at going by the water, they are strong. They can carry heavy things and go very far in a short time.
