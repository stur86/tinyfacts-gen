An air is the stuff around us. It is very important for our lives. The air around you is different from the air in a big room. The air in a room is often heavy and can make things heavy. Our air is lighter and does not make things heavy.

The air in the sky is also different. When the air is very high, it is cold and very clear. When the air is very low, it is warm and full of tiny white things that we can not see. The air in the sky is what keeps us from falling down.

We need air to breathe. Our bodies need it to keep going. Without air, we would not be able to live. If the air was gone, there would be no more air to breathe, no more life on this world.

The air also makes things happen. When hot air moves into a room, it cools down. When cold air moves out, it cools down too. This keeps the world changing and moving.

Sometimes, the air can change. When the air is very cold, the sky can look dark and cold. When the air is very warm, the sky can look bright and warm. This is why we have different days in summer and in the cold months.

The air is also important for the trees and the animals. Trees need air to grow and make food. Animals use air to breathe. The air helps give us the power to run and to fly.

In short, the air is everything we need to live. It keeps us safe, gives us the power to move, and helps the world stay changing.
