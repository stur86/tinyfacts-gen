A tall person is a person who is taller than most others. This can be a good thing because they can see over tall things like trees or high walls. It can also be a problem if they have trouble walking through a small room because they stand out.

You can also say someone is tall if they have a strong body. A tall person might have a long neck and long arms. This can be very good if they can do things they do not think they can do. They can reach high things that other people can not. They can also stand in front of a group of people and be very important.

Sometimes, a tall person might be afraid if they see a small dog. They might feel that the dog is going to jump on them. But they usually do not get really scared. Most tall people just think the dog is a fun animal. They like to talk to dogs and learn about them.
