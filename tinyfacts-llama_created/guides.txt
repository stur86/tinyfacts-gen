To show someone where to go is to be a kind of person. You might want a friend to help you find the way home at night when you are far from your house. You can ask them to point out the street you must walk on.

This is what a teacher does too. They show their students where to learn new things. If a teacher has a long story to tell, they might point at the words so the student can see the right words.

It is also about being the one who knows where you are. If you are lost in a park, a person who is there might say, I know the way out. They help you get back to your home.

Sometimes, a person helps you try to do something you are afraid of. If you are afraid of a tall house, a friend can go with you until you feel safe again. They are there to help you feel better when you are scared.
