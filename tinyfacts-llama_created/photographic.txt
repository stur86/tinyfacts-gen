A picture is a thing that shows what is happening right now. You can see these things with your eyes. A picture is made from many small parts.

The first things we use are things that take a picture. You hold the things in your hand and point them at a thing. This makes the parts on the picture move. The parts are so fast that the picture is made very quickly, very fast.

Then, we use a box with many small pieces. These pieces take the picture from the box. The box has the parts that know what to look for in the world. It sees the bright parts and the dark parts. It takes the bright parts and puts them into the picture.

After that, we use a computer to make the picture look nice. The computer looks at the picture and makes it look like a good picture. It can make the colors, the light, and the way the picture looks, all at once.

Sometimes, we use things that can remember what is happening. These things are like a memory box. They keep a picture of what has happened so we can remember it later. We can look at the picture and remember what happened.

Pictures help us tell stories. They show what is happening and what is important. They are a way to keep our memories safe.
