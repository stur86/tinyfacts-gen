To free something means to let it go so it can move on its own. It can happen with many things.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it up high. Then you let it go. It falls down and lands on the ground. You did not hold it anymore. It is free. You did not need to keep it in your hand.

The word can also mean a cold thing. If you put your hand in a cold cup of water, the water starts to move and the cold goes out. The water does not need your hand anymore. It is free to be cold.

In a very big group, the cold gets so strong that it pushes the warm part over. This is why when it is very cold outside, the air feels cold too. The cold is free to move and push the warm air around.

Sometimes we use this word with people. If someone is in a bad place, they might feel like they need to free themselves. They might try to leave or do something to get away from the trouble. This is when they are free from the problem.

In a small group, the leader might use this word. They might say, I am free to make a different plan. Or, I am free to do what I want. This means they are not held back by the group.
