To hand something means to give it to someone else. This can happen many times in life.

Imagine you want a book for your sister. You pick it up and want to give it to her. You hold the book in your hand. You look at her and say, hand it over. This is when you hand the book.

Sometimes you hand something so it can be used by someone else later. If you see a food you want to eat, you hand it to your friend. They can eat it when they want.

If you hand a ball to a team player, you are giving them the ball. This helps them play the game. You are not the one who will be the one to hit the ball.

In a store, you might hand a box of food to the person who will take the money. This is when you give the person the box.

You can also hand something to someone who is sick. You hand them a drink or a small thing that will help them feel better. This helps them feel better.

Sometimes, you hand something to someone you do not know. You might hand a friend a small thing you found. This can be a fun way to show you care.
