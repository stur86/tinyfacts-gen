To put something through a thing means you take something and let it go so the clean part can come out. This is how you clean your hands when you do not want anything on them. You take a hand full of water and a hand full of the stuff that makes you sick. You put both hands under the same water. The water gets all the bad stuff and the clean water comes out.

Another way to use this word is when you use a thing to keep the bad things out of something else. You put a paper thing through a thing that makes things clean. This thing picks out the bad parts and keeps the good ones in. Your paper is clean because it has passed through the thing.

Sometimes you put a thing through a thing that makes you feel bad. This is not good. You might feel tired or sick after you go through it. You did not want to go through it.
