An idea is when you think about something. You do not need to see it. You just need to know what it is and how it feels.

Imagine you are in a room. You see a picture on the wall. You do not look at the picture. You just think about the picture. You are not looking at the picture. You are thinking about the picture. That is an idea.

A story is another kind of idea. You do not see a story. You just hear the words. You do not look at the words. You just think about what the story means.

Sometimes you have a feeling. You do not see the feeling. You just know it. You do not look at your body or your face. You just know that you feel a way. That is an idea.

An idea is important for a person. It helps them decide what to do. It helps them know what is right and wrong. It helps them understand the world.

But ideas can also be hard to understand. Sometimes you look at something and you do not get it at all. You might see a picture and you do not know what it means. Or you might hear a story and you do not know how it makes you feel.

Ideas are all around us. They are in the air. They are in the books we read. They are in the words we say. They are in the way we think about the world.
