To go somewhere is to move from one place to another. You can go on foot, by car, by train, or by a big moving thing in the sky.

Imagine you live in a city and you want to see a far place. You can go to that place by car. You drive your car for a long time. The road goes over many lines and roads. You pass over tall high places and under low places. The car moves very fast.

After a while, you get to the far place. You have seen many cities before. You feel happy because you have seen new things. You may want to stay for a little while to see more. If you stay, you stay in the same place, but you are in a new city.

Sometimes you go by train. You sit in a car that goes very fast. You look out the window and see trees, houses, and fields. People sit next to you. You talk to them and hear their voices. You can read books or listen to music.

When you go, you might feel a little nervous. You might think, I am going far away from home. You are leaving behind people you know and things that are easy to remember. You feel a little sad at first, but then you feel happy because you are moving to a new place.

People also go to work. They drive to a far place where they work. They go to a building with many rooms. In some places, they work in a room with many people. In other places, they work in a room with only one person. They are part of a big group with many people who work together.

Going is a way to change where you live. You go to a new place with new things and new people. It helps you learn about new ways of life. It can be fun and exciting.
