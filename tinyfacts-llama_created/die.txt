To die means you are very, very dead. It is the end of your life. When you die, your body stops working. Your brain stops sending out what tells you what to do. Your heart stops beating. Your blood stops going through your body.

You can die from many things. You can die from a big cut. You can die from a very bad cold. You can die from a thing that eats you. You can also die from a very bad thing that happens to you. This is the hardest part.

Some people die by their own hands. They might take a drink or a thing that makes them feel very bad. Some people die in the dark. Some people die in a car. Some people die in a fire. Some people die in a fight. Sometimes you do not know why until later.

When you die, your body stops being you. You are gone. You are no longer here. You do not come back. You are gone.
