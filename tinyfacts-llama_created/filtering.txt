To clean something means to take away the things you do not want. This can happen with water, air, or food. You can use a hair cleaner to get ridden of hair from your face and hands. This is a good way to keep your skin clear.

You can also use this word with people. Imagine you have a group of people you want to be friends with. If you only want to talk to them about books, you can ask them if they want to talk about books. They are only talking about books. They are not talking about anything else.

In a big group of people, you might want to keep only the people who talk about music. You would not say they are all friends because they only talk about music. This is how you keep things small so you can see everyone who is important.
