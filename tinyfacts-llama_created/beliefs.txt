To believe something is to trust that it is true. When you believe something, you feel safe. You do not think your mind is broken. You are sure that what you see or hear is real.

Believing can be about many things. You can believe that a person is kind. You know they are nice and help others. You feel happy when they smile at you. You might even trust them when they tell you a story.

You can also believe in things that are not real. You might think a story is true. You might feel sorry for a person who is sad. You want to help them feel better.

When you believe, you let go of your fear. You stop thinking about the worst thing. You start to see the good. This can make life feel better.

There are many kinds of believing. You can believe in your family. You trust them to keep you safe. You can believe in your school. You trust the teacher to teachers. You can even believe in your body. You trust that your heart can beat and your hands can move.

Believing is important. It helps us feel happy and safe. It helps us form close relationships. It helps us know who we can trust.
