To go down is one way to go. Your feet push you. You walk or run. You are moving. You are going toward the ground. But the ground does not want you to go. The ground wants you to stay where you are.

The world is like this too. Every thing is pulled toward the middle. The air around you pulls you toward the sky. The ground pulls you toward the bottom of the world. The sky pulls you toward the sky. The middle pulls you toward the middle. It keeps you on the ground.

You can not escape this. It is what holds you to the place where you are. If you go too fast, you will fall down. If you go too slow, you will stay in one place. It is a big pull. It is what makes things fall.

The pull is not just for things. It is for people too. The pull of the world is what keeps your feet on the ground. It keeps you from falling into the water. The pull of the sky is what keeps you in the air. If you let go, you will fall down.

The pull is also what keeps the world in place. If there were no pull, everything would fly away. The pull is what keeps the world standing still.

So, the pull is everything around us. It is what holds us to the world. It is what keeps us on the ground.
