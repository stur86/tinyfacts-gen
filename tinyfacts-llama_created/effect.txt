An answer. To show how a thing works.

When you do something, it often has an answer. The answer can be a word, a number, a picture, or a sound. It can also be a feeling.

Think of a word. If you say a word, the word comes from you. It is not a thing in the world. It is only a part of you. If you do something, the word is the answer. You did something, and the word is the way you feel.

You can also say that something is an answer. If you watch a movie, the movie is the answer. You watched it, and now you know how a story goes. If you do something and you feel a good feeling, the feeling is your answer. You did something, and the feeling is the way you feel.

Sometimes, you do something. You do not just think about it. You act. This act is the answer. The act happens because of what you did before. If you did something and then you got angry, the anger is the answer.

An answer can also be a thing. If you answer a question, you give the answer. You tell the person what you know. You give them the word to help them understand.

The word can also mean how things change. If you make something, it gets better or worse. When you do something, the way things are can change. You can change your life, or you can change how you feel.

In a very big way, an answer is what is happening now. It is what is happening right in this moment. It is the now.
