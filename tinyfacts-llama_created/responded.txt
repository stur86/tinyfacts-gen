To respond means you show how you feel when someone says something to you. It happens when you hear a word, a voice, a sound, or a word that is not your own. You might feel angry, sad, or happy.

When you respond, you use your own words. You might say yes, no, or something else. Your words help you show how you are feeling and what you think.

Think about a time when you see a friend. You might laugh. If the friend says something funny, you might laugh too. You are both responding to each other. You are showing how you feel when you are together.

A computer can also respond. If a person types a word, the computer might type back a word. This is how computers talk to each other. They are responding because they are listening to the words the other person has sent.

Sometimes we respond when we feel afraid. When you are afraid, your heart might beat fast. You might look around to see if anything is wrong. This is your body responding to a feeling. You are showing how you are feeling in the moment.
