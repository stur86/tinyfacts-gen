A group of many people is called a group. If you have many friends in your school, you have a group. If you have many books in your room, you have a group of books. But the word you are looking for is much bigger than that.

If you counters a number a few times, you get a few. If you counters the same number many times, you get a many. If you counters a number a long time, you get a big number. A group of many people is a big number of people.

Think of it like a long line of people. If you are in the line for a few minutes, you are one person. If you are in the line for many minutes, you are many people. If you are in the line for a long time, you are a big number of people. 

This word is used when you talk about things that are very many. You might say, I am happy for ten, twenty, or even one hundred people. This means you are very happy.
