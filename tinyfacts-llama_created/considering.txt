To consider something means to think about it. It is a way to look at how things are. 

Imagine you want to decide if you want to go to a party. You might ask yourself: What is the party about? Who will be there? Will I have fun? What will happen? You are really thinking about the party. You are considering it. You are looking at all the things that are important and trying to decide what to do.

Sometimes you consider things that are far away. You think about what will happen when you get there. You are thinking about how your life will change. You are considering a big change. You are looking at the long way ahead and trying to figure out what to do.

You can also consider things that are right in front of you. You look at a picture and think about how it makes you feel. You consider the colors and the way the light looks. You are looking at the picture and thinking about how it makes you feel. You are considering the picture.

When you consider something, you are not always sure. You might feel a little confused. You might ask yourself: Is this right? Is this true? You are trying to figure out what is true. This is an important way to learn.

Sometimes people consider things they have done. They think about how they acted and what they could have done better. They are looking at what happened and trying to understand how they did it. This is a way to learn from your life.

You can also consider things that are not happening right now. You might think about a day that has gone by. You might remember happy times or sad times. You are looking back and trying to understand why things happened. This is a way to look at the past and understand what happened.
