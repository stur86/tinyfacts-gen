An animal that is not like the ones next to it is called an animal that is different. This is because all animals are not the same, and there are many ways they can look, move, and act.

An animal that is different can have a body that is very different from the bodies of the animals next to it. It might have a long neck, a very long body, or a body covered in many long hair. This is because animals need to find food and stay safe in their world. A long body can help them run away from big animals. A long neck can help them see over tall things. A many hair body can help keep them warm.

Animals that are different can also have very different ways of moving. They might run very fast, jump very high, or walk very slowly. Some animals can even change color to help them hide from the sun or from other animals.

People also have different ways of being. Some people like to be alone and stay inside. Some people like to go out and play with friends. Some people like to do things that help others. Some people like to do things that make them happy.

Because of all these different ways, the world is a big and interesting place. We learn about each other by seeing how we are different.
