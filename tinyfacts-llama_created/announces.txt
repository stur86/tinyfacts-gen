To Annoy Someone Means They Are Very Filled With Anger

When you annoy someone, it is because you make them very mad. This happens when you talk too much or when you do things that they do not want to do. You might say a lot of words and make them want to stop or go away. This is called a strong feeling of anger.

People also say they are annoyed. This means they feel this way because something happened and they need to get ridden of it. Maybe you do something that makes a person angry. They might be very angry and want you to leave the room or the house.

To Annoy Someone Is Not The Same As To Be Angry

Being angry is a normal feeling when you are hurt or very surprised. It helps you keep your body ready to fight or run away. But when a person is annoyed, it is a strong feeling of anger that can not be stopped. It is like their blood is running fast and they do not want to stop.

Sometimes, when you are very sad or very happy, you might feel a little bit of this kind of feeling. But if you feel this kind of feeling for too long, you might become very angry. This is why it is good to be kind and not to annoy others.
