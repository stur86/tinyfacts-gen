To remember something means to keep it in your mind. You can remember things from your day, from your school days, or from a movie. When you remember something, you feel a small push in your chest. It is like your brain is saying, I know this.

If you want to remember something for a long time, you can try to see it. You see a picture of a big tree, or a bright sun, or a fun face. The picture stays in your mind for a long time and you can remember it easily later.

Sometimes, you might remember something from the past but not from the present. You might remember a song from when you were a child, or a place you went, or a time you had fun. This is important because it helps you understand how you feel and how you have changed over time.

You can also remember things from other people. If you talk to a friend or a teacher, you might remember what they say or what they did. This helps you build a relationship with the person and remember important words or stories.
