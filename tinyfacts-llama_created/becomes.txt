To become means you start and then you are no longer the same. You are getting into a new place, a new state, or a new way. It happens when you get better at something. You might learn a new word, a new way to walk, or a new game to play.

Imagine you are a dog. You are a small dog. One day, you learn to walk on a long line. You can go far without your mother. You are becoming a better dog. You are not still the same dog. You are a new dog.

You can become strong. You can become fast. You can become good at a new game. Or you can become old and tired. This happens to many people in life.

We can also become friends. If you like a person, you start to care about them. You listen to what they say. You smile at them. You want to be near them. You are becoming a friend.

In a big city, you can become a person who knows many other people. You might meet them at a coffee shop. You might talk about your work or your life. You are becoming part of the city.
