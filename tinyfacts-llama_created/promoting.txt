When you want something, you are trying to make it happen. You might try to get a new job, or you might try to get a game. You are making an idea into a real thing. You are helping people see how good it is.

If you are a person, you might be a leader. You are making things happen for your group. You are telling them what to do. You are helping them get their job done. You are helping them feel safe and happy.

If you are a business, you want to tell other people about your thing. You are saying, this is the best thing to buy. You are making it known to everyone. This helps people decide whether to buy it or not.

Sometimes, you try to make a place feel better. You put up pictures that make it look nice. You put food on the table that makes everyone happy. You are making the place a home. You are helping people feel at home.

You can also try to get something for a group. You might ask, what do people think about this idea? You are trying to find out what they want. You are trying to see if the idea is good. You are listening to their answers.
