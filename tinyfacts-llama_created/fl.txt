To flashes something is to make it move quickly and far. You might see a person running or a small animal moving across a field. The word comes from the sound of air moving over hot things. When you hear a hot piece of glass or a hot piece of food, the air from the hot thing goes out quickly, and you hear a soft sound.

In this sense, you can also say that something is flashed. It means you have moved it quickly and far. You can use this word in many different ways: the ball flashed the white paper, the dog flashed the wall, or the light flashed the room.

Sometimes the word is used to mean to talk quickly and with a lot of words. You might hear two people talking very fast when they are angry. The word is like that because it means something is happening very quickly and with a lot of sound.
