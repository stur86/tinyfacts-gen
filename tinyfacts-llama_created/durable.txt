During means to stay in one place for a long time without moving. It is a word that we use when something is strong.

A piece of wood that is very hard and does not break is an easy thing to say about. It means the wood will not break when you hit it. A car is also a thing that is very hard to break. It keeps going for a long time because it is strong.

You can also use this word to talk about people. A person who is very strong and can not be broken is called a person who is very big. A family with many children is a big family. A person who is very old and still going is a person who is very good at their job.

When you say something is strong, you are telling a story about how it stays in one place for a long time. You are saying it does not fall down or break easily. It is like a rock or a hard thing that can not be moved.
