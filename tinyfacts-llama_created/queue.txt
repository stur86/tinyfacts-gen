A line of people waiting for something is called a line or a line of people. You might see this in stores. When you go to buy food, people stand in a line. They wait for the person with the food to come out. When the food person is ready, they give the food to the first person in line. The first person gets the food. The next person waits. This is how people get what they want when there is a long line.

You can also see this in movies. When a movie starts, people wait to see what happens next. They are waiting for the story to begin.

Sometimes, a line can be a group of people. If many people want to go to a place, they can all stand in one line. They wait together until they reach the front.

In some places, people stand in a line to get a number. They write their numbers so they can get the things they want at the same time. This helps everyone get what they need in the same order.

Sometimes, a line is a way of saying we can't do it right now. If a line of people is long, the people in front of them might have to wait a very long time. This can happen in schools, hospitals, or any place with many people waiting for something.
