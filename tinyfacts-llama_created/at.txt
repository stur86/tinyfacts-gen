At is a word that you use to talk about time and places.

When you say you are at a place, you mean you are there. This can be a house, a school, a park, a store, or a big city. You are there right now. You are in a different room than you are in when you wake up. You are in a room with other people.

Think about how you feel when you walk to a place you know. You know the way because you have done it many times before. You might feel happy when you see a tree or a bright light. The world around you changes as you move from one place to another. At a place, you are in the middle of all the other places.

You can also use at to talk about the day. You might say you are at school. You are in a building where children go to learn. You are sitting with your friends. You are not at home with your family. You are at a place where people work and learn.

Sometimes, when you are with a friend, you say you are at their house. You are in their home. You are with people you know and people you care about. You are safe and you can talk. You can do things without worrying about what others think.

So at is a word that tells us where we are and how we feel.
