To floor something means to cover it with something hard. This can be wood, hard stuff made from very hard rocks, or even things that are very bright. When you put this on the floor of a house, a room, or a room in a school, it makes the floor look nice and safe. It also makes the room feel warmer when the sun is out, and it can help keep the cold floor from getting in.

If you put a soft thing on the floor, like a big chair or a table, you are not covering it with hard stuff. You are just making it look nice for people to walk on. In this way, the word flooring is a bit like the word flooring a car. It means to make something look nice for people to walk on, even if the thing is not made of hard stuff.
