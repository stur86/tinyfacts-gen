The word is about being very sick. It is a word you use when you are not feeling well. If you feel very bad, you might say you are feeling  sick. This means you have a bad feeling inside you and it hurts your body.

You can be sick because you have a cold. You might feel a lot of cold. You might have a run. Or you might feel very tired. You might not want to move. You might feel very sad.

You can also be sick because you hurt your arm or leg. You might feel a pain. You might feel like you want to cry. If you are sick for a long time, you might need to stay in a room with a doctor. The doctor can help you feel better.

Sometimes, people feel sick because of a bad thing that happened. They might feel very angry. They might want to shout. They might want to cry. This is because they feel hurt inside.

No one can tell how long a person feels sick. Some people feel better in a few days. Others feel sick for weeks or even months. It is like a big, hard thing inside your body that makes you feel very bad.
