To be whole means you are not broken. You are not a tiny piece inside another bigger thing. You are the whole thing. You have all the parts and everything inside you.

Imagine you have a box. You put many small things in it. Each small thing is only one thing. But when you put them all together, the box is one thing. You are the box.

You are whole when you are not missing any part. You have everything you need. You are not a tiny piece inside something else. You are the same thing.

If you have a small hurt on your hand, you are still whole. You are not broken. You are still the same person inside your heart and inside your body.
