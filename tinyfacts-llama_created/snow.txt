When the sky gets cold and the ground gets wet, white stuff falls from the sky. This white stuff is made of tiny bits of water that come from the air. When the air is very cold, these tiny bits of water come together to make a small ball. When many of these small balls fall from the sky, they form a big white cover on the ground and the trees.

You can feel this white cover when you step outside on a cold day. It is like walking on a thick white bed. The ground is soft under your feet and it is easy to step on. You can see things far away because the white cover makes the light from far away look very bright.

The white cover can also make things look strange. When the ground is covered in it, the ground looks like a big white box. The trees look like they are covered in white stuff. The white cover can be very deep in some places, but it can also be very light and cover only a few feet of ground.

Sometimes, the white cover can stay on the ground for a long time. This is called a white cover day. People usually go to work or school on these days. They wear warm clothes to keep themselves warm. The white cover can also make it hard to see things at night. The bright light from the sky makes it hard to walk around in the dark.

The white cover is a sign that the air is cold. It happens when the air can not get warm enough to keep the water inside the tiny balls from turning back into water. The tiny balls start to fall out of the sky and cover the ground and trees.
