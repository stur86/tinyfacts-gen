To be in the middle of something means you are in the middle. Imagine you are standing in a big room. There is a big wall on one side and a big wall on the other side. In the middle, there are many people. You are in the middle.

If something is not in the middle, it is on one side. It might be on the left side or the right side. It is like a line that goes across the room. If something is on the left side, it is on the wrong side of the middle line. If something is on the right side, it is on the wrong side of the middle line.

A thing can also be in the middle if it is not the biggest or the smallest. It can be in the middle and still be important. Think about a house. If you move the house to the right side, it is no longer in the middle. It has changed. It is no longer in the middle.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about people. If a person is a big person, it means they are not small. They are in the middle of the group. They are not on the left or the right side. They are right in the middle.
