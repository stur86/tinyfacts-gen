To be good is to have a kind heart. You are not afraid to help someone who is sad. You try to make things better. You show love and care. This is what we mean when we say a person is good.

When things are good, everyone is happy. You feel safe and you have a lot of fun. You have friends who like you and you like them. Everything is right. You do not feel angry or scared.

If something is not good, it makes you feel bad. You might feel sad or angry. You might want to get ridden. You might want to go away and forget. This is when people act in a way that is not kind.

If you are good, you show you care about others. You listen and you try to understand. You are a friend. You help people when they need it. You are a person to be loved.

Being good is very important. It makes the world a better place.
