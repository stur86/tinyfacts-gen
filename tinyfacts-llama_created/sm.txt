Small is a word you use to say how small something is. It is about how much less something is than a bigger thing. Think about a tiny room next to a big house. The room is small because it is much smaller.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If a person is very small, they are small. If you are small, you are not as tall as a big person. If you are very small, you are very quiet and you do not say much.

When you say a word like this, you are telling someone how the thing looks or how it is. It is a way to tell how much one thing is smaller than another.
