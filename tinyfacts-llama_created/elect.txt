To decide is to pick. It is when we talk about who should do a job. Sometimes people pick by how they feel. If you like food, you might pick food. If you like a game, you might pick that game.

We also pick people. In a group, you might pick the person who is funny. This means you like them. You like them because they make you laugh.

In a story, we might pick a person to be the good guy. This can help us know who is the one doing good. When we pick, we are telling the story who is important.

We can also pick things. If you are looking at a picture, you pick the thing you like. You might pick a dog because you love dogs. If you pick a book, you pick a book that you want to read.

When you decide, you are making a change. You are saying who should do something. This is important because it helps us know what to do next.
