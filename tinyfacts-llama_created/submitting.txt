To put something down.

When you are afraid of something, you might feel a big feeling in your chest. This is because your body is ready to run away. Your legs are ready to move fast. Your heart is ready to beat very fast.

If you know this feeling, you can decide to do something. You can say, I am afraid. Or you can put your foot down. This means you are not afraid anymore. You are ready to do what you want.

You can also put something in a box. If you want to leave a job, you might say, I am going to leave. This means you are putting your job down. You are not going to do it anymore. You are moving to a new job.

You can also put a book down. If you read a book for a long time, you might feel tired. You put the book down and you go to sleep. You do not need to look at the book anymore.

Sometimes, you have to put something in a bag. If you have something you do not want, you might say, I am going to throw it away. You are putting it down. You are not keeping it anymore.

You can also put a picture down on a table. This means you are letting it stay there. You are not going to move it. You are letting it be there for now.

Sometimes, you have to put down your phone. You might say, I am going to go to sleep. Or you might say, I am going to do something else.

Put down is an old word. It used to mean you were afraid. Now, it means you are ready to do what you want.
