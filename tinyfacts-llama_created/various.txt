To be different means to be something other than what everyone else is. You are different from your friends, from your family, and even from the people you walk by on the street. Sometimes this is a good thing because it makes life interesting. You are not like anyone else and this can make you feel excited and happy.

Another way to be different is to have a job that is not like any other job. You might work at a place where you get to build things, or at a place where you get to help people. You are doing something new and you like it very much. This is a very good way to live because you are always doing something different and you are not like anyone else.

Sometimes being different is hard. You might feel like you do not fit in. You might feel like you are the only one who feels this way. It is okay to feel this way. It is normal to want to be like everyone else. But at the same time, you are still you and you are still important.

Being different is also about being happy with who you are. You might like your hair in a different way, or you might like to wear bright colors. You might like to play music or to draw pictures. You are being different because you like these things over the things everyone else does.

Finally, being different is important for the world. Think about the animals that live in the woods. If all the animals were the same, there would be no one to watch. There would be no one to learn about. There would be no one to help. So we must be different so that the world stays full of life and change.
