Years ago, many people did not know what a group of lands was. It was not one big land like the ones in the middle of the world. Instead, it was many small lands that were close to each other. There was a land that was very cold, a land with cold water that was very far away, a land with warm land that was very close, and a land with a cold and a warm land right next to it.

These small lands were all tied together. People could go from one land to another without much change. They shared the same money, the same clothes, and the same food. They also had a big leader who made sure everyone was following the same way.

One land was very close to a big city. People from the land went to this city to work and to buy things. Another land was very far away. People from that land went to the city for the same reason.

In the end, the lands were not as one group as people thought. The cold land and the warm land fought with each other. The cold land wanted to be alone, but the warm land wanted to stay close to the cold land. They fought many times.

Eventually, a man from one land became the leader of the lands. He wanted to make the lands one group again. But the cold land and the warm land were too strong. They fought the man and his men. He was killed in the fight.

The lands were then broken into many smaller groups. The cold land became the land with the cold water, the warm land became the land with the warm land, and the land with the cold and the warm land became the land with the cold and a warm land next to it.

Today, the lands are still not one group. But they are still all close together. They have their own people, their own cities, and their own ways of life. They are part of a group that is trying to stay together after many fights.
