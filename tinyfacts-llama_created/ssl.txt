A strong word for a computer.

When you talk to a computer, you need to tell it what to do. You do this with numbers and words, but sometimes you want to make sure what you say is safe. This is why you need an idea that is very strong.

Think of a big door. When you open it, you can go in, but you must be sure the door is closed. If you open it too many times, the door becomes easy to open. An idea like this is like a strong idea.

An idea that is strong is an idea that is safe. When you say this idea, you are telling the computer that the numbers you give it are the right numbers. This makes the computer know you are talking to it, and it will do the right thing.

Most computers use this idea to talk to the world. When you send a picture, a word, or a voice from one computer to another, you are sending an idea. The computer uses this idea to know what to do with the idea. If the idea is strong, the computer can trust it, and it will do what you want.

In a way, you can think of it like a lock and a key. The lock is the idea, and the key is the numbers. If the lock is strong and the key is right, the lock opens, and the computer can do what you want.
