To give someone the name of someone is to give them their name. You can do this with a person you know. If you like a person, you might say,  I like you. This means you like the person. You are the one who is nice to them.

You can also give a name to a thing. If you find a small ball, you might say, This ball is your ball. This means the ball is the one you found.

Sometimes, people give names to animals. If you see a dog, you might say, That dog is a dog. This means the dog is the one.

The word comes from a story. A long time ago, a man had a friend. He was very good at doing things. He could make things and play games. He was the best at his work. He was so good that everyone said, He is the best. This story helps us know that the word for the name is important because it is the name of the person or thing that is important.
