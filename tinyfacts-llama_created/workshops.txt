A work is something you do. You might make something new or fix something that is broken. You might write a book, make a picture, or build a thing. A work is a thing you are making.

A shop is a place where people buy things. You can go there to buy a book, a dress, or a food to eat. Some shops have many things. Some shops have just one thing. A shop is where people go to buy things they need.

A work can also mean something that is hard to do. If you work hard, you can do a job very well. You might feel tired, but you keep going. You are doing something important.

Sometimes, people have a work that is very important. They might work in a big office or in a hospital. They do things to keep people safe. They are the ones who help people get better when they are hurt.
