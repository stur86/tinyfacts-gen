To close something means to stop it from moving or opening anymore. It happens when a door is shut, a box is closed, or a room is closed.

Imagine a tree. When you are young, you can move to different places and touch many different trees. But when you grow older, you want to stay in one place. You close your arms and hands to the world. You want to stay in your home. This is closing.

You can close your eyes too. When you are afraid, you might close your eyes. You are not looking at the thing that scares you. You are not seeing it anymore. You are safe.

People close their doors in houses and shops. They close the door to keep people out. They close the door when they go away or when they are not wanted anymore.

Sometimes, we close our hearts. When we love someone, we feel happy. But when someone we do not like is gone, we might feel sad. We close our hearts to show we are still there for them. This is closing our love.

You can also close a book. When you finish a book, you close the cover so you do not touch it again. You close your eyes and you are done.
