A word is a thing that you use to tell what something is. When we say a word is big, we mean it is very large or very many. If you say a word is small, you mean it is small.

A word can also be big in other ways. A big word might mean something that is very hard to understand. A word that is big in this way is not easy to use. If you say a word is big, you might feel afraid or confused.

Sometimes, when we say a word is many, we mean it is many small things. You can make a big word by adding many small words together. The word big is made from many small words.

There is another kind of word that means we have everything we need. This word can be very strong. If you say a word is complete, you mean you have all the things you need to know. You do not need to look at one more word to understand.

Finally, if a word is very much a part of what we say, we say it is complete. This word is like the end of a story. You tell the whole story and you are done.
