A house has many rooms. One room is for sleeping. Another room is for eating. There is also a room for making things. This is what a house does. In this room, people make things to buy. They make clothes, food, and other things that people need.

A car is a thing that people drive. It goes very fast over the ground. Many people use these cars to go to work. Some people drive them to buy food or to pick up friends. The car is like a big box that moves around the world.

A place where people keep their things is called a box. This box is in a house. Inside the box, there are many things like a book, a box of food, or a small thing that someone bought. People keep these things there because they are important.
