To continue something means you keep doing it over and over again. You start it and you do not stop.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are very good at it. You are the best. You play every day and every week. You do not want to stop. You want to keep playing for as long as you can. You do not think about stopping, you just keep going.

If you have a job, you do it every day. You go to work, you do your work, you go home. You do not leave. You keep doing it until the day comes when you need to go home. That is a long time.

You can also continue a story. If you start to tell a story and you do not stop, the other person can keep listening and following the story. You keep adding more and more until everyone is following the story together.

Sometimes, you might say you are doing something for a long time. This means you are doing it for a very long time. If you are tired, you might say you are going to the store. You are not going right now, but you are going soon. You are continuing your trip.

To continue is important. It helps us feel happy when we see something we love happening. It helps us know that things are not going away. It is a way to keep things going even when they become hard.
