Your hands have fingers. These are the parts that you use to touch other things. You use your fingers to pick up things, to hold a cup, or to play with a ball. Your fingers are also very good at feeling. You can feel if something is hot, cold, soft, or hard.

Your hands are also good at making things. You can use your hands to build a house, to make a car, or to make a piece of food. Your hands help you use things that you buy from a store.

Sometimes, people say, your hands are the hands of a person. This means they are the parts of a person that help them do things. It is a way to say that a person has strong hands because they can do many different jobs.

Your hands are also very important for your body. They help you breathe. They help you move. They help you eat food. They keep your skin and your clothes clean. They help you keep your face and your hair looking good.
