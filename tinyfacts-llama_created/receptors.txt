A small part in your body can pick up things that are not there. These parts are very good at catching tiny bits from the air or from the water you drink. They work so well that your eyes can see colors, your ears can hear sounds, and your skin can feel the touch of a soft thing.

Inside your body, these small parts are often found near the end of lines that carry food or air from your mouth or nose to your brain. When a tiny bit from the air reaches these parts, it sends a short word to the brain.

The brain then tells you what the word means. For a person who can see, these small parts help them see. For a person who hears, they help them hear. For a person who feels, they help them feel. They are very important parts of your body.

These small parts are all around us. They are found in the skin of a tree, the hair of a dog, and even in the air inside a room. They are even found in the water you drink and the food you eat.
