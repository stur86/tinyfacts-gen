A small push of power. A small move that makes things happen.

When you turn a light on, you are giving it power. This power is what makes the light stay on. It is like a tiny push from your hand to the light.

Every power is one second. This is the time it takes for one push to happen. Think of it like a quick move. One second and it is done. But if you had a hundred of these quick moves, it would take a hundred seconds!

The word comes from a place where people made and kept power. They would push tiny bits of power to make things work. The power is very small, but when you put it all together, it can make a lot of work happen.

Some things need more power than others. A car needs a lot of power to go fast. A computer needs a lot of power to do all the work it does. A small phone needs power to work.

When we talk about power, we usually use numbers. The bigger the number, the more power there is. But the number is only a way to show the power. It does not tell you what the power is like. You have to know how much power there is to understand it.

You can also talk about the power of a person. A strong person can push a heavy thing. A small person can push a small thing.

The word is very important in our world. We use it to talk about things that need power. We also use it to talk about how strong or small something is.
