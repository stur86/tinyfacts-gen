A big problem happened in a city near a big water way. People had to move to other places for a while because the water way was filled with big red things. These red things were big and hard to get through.

The red things were coming from a big place in the water way. The water way was usually calm, but this time it was very strong. The red things were very big and were going very fast. They were going to break the big place and make a big change in the water way.

People thought they should close the water way and wait for the red things to go away. But they also thought they should try to stop the red things before they broke the big place. They built tall walls around the city and tried to close the water way.

The big place in the water way had a very strong power inside it. It was making the red things grow bigger and bigger. The power was very strong and was going to break the walls and the city if it kept going.

A man who was good at fixing things came to the city. He said that the power was the cause of the big problem. He said that if they could find a way to stop the power, they could save the city. He also said that they should not wait, they should act fast.

The people decided to act fast. They built big boxes to catch the red things. They also built tall walls to keep them out. The man who was good at fixing things helped them. They worked day and night to stop the power and close the water way.

After a long time, the red things stopped going. The power went away too. The city was safe, and people could move back home. The man who was good at fixing things was a big help. He helped save the city from a very big problem.
