When you say something is on the computer, you have put it into a big box of words. This box holds many many words about the thing you want to say. If you want to tell a friend that you love them, you can type this into the box. The words go into a line by line, one after the other. This line is like a short story. Sometimes the words are very long and take many lines to write. Other times the words are very short and can be put into one line.

When you want to share a picture with others, you put it into the computer. The computer looks at the picture and makes it look nice on the computer. This makes the picture easy to see from many people. You can share it by sending it to a friend who has a computer too. You can also share it on a big computer that many people use at work.

If you write a short story, you put it into the computer. The words go into a line by line. When you are ready, you can press a few keys and the story appears on the computer. This is how people write and share stories with each other.
