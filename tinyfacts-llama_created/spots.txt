To have spots means to have many small white or light things on your body. These spots can be on your face, your arms, your legs, your hands, or even your face while you sleep.

The white things are small pieces of skin. They are like tiny pieces of paper. They do not hurt you. But they can make your skin look different. Sometimes, the spots are small and round. Other times, they are long and straight. They can be one color or many colors together.

You can have spots because you were very happy while you were very young. You might have been in a game with your friends. You might have laughed a lot. You might have been very close to everyone around you. These happy times leave marks on your body. When you look at them now, you see many small white pieces of skin.

You can also have spots because you ate a lot of food. Some people have spots when they are very old. The spots get bigger over time. But the spots are always there, like a small picture on your skin.

People use these words to talk about bodies that are a little bit different. They are not like everyone else. They have their own way of looking. They are happy and strong. They are a good kind of weird.
