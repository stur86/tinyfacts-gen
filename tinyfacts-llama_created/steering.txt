To give direction means to move a thing so it points to a place. If you are in a car and you turn the handle, you are giving direction. You are telling the car which way to go. If you are walking and you want to go to a place, you are also giving direction. You are telling your legs which way to move.

In a room, if you want to move from one corner to another, you are giving direction. You are telling the walls which way to move. You are pointing the way to the room you want to go to.

Sometimes, to give direction means to ask for help. If you are lost and you need someone to find you, you are giving direction. You are telling the person where to look. You are giving them a way to find you.

When a person is very good at giving direction, they are called a leader. They know where to go and how to get there. They help others follow them.
