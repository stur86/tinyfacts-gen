To have a long thing means you have a very big body. A long thing is a thing that can go far from one side of a big room. It is not a small thing like a dog. It is a thing that can be seen for many steps.

You can have a long hair on your head. This is a good thing to have because it makes you look nice. You can also have a long neck to lift food from high places. A long thing can also be a long piece of wood for a table. This helps people sit and eat.

Even the sun is long. It takes many days for it to come up high in the sky after you go to bed. The ground is often covered in dark colors during this time.
