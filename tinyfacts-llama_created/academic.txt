An idea that is studied by people who want to know about the world, often in a place where many of them go to learn. They might do this because they want to understand how things work or because they want to become good at a job.

Sometimes, it means someone who is very good at learning. They might pick up a lot of facts very fast. Other times, it means someone who is very good at writing, making sure they are right and that their words are clear.

It is often used to talk about people who do this. Some are in a high school, some in a college, and some in a school that is much bigger than any one of them. They are all learning how to do their jobs or how to stay in school so they can get a job later.

It is also used to talk about books and papers. If someone writes a paper that is very good, they are doing something like this. They are using all they know to show how they think about the world.
