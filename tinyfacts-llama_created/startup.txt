A start is something that begins. It is the first thing you do when you start to do something new.

A person who starts a business is called a start. They work very hard to make the business grow. They try to find people to help them and find a place to work. They also try to make money so they can pay their family and pay for things they need.

A start can also mean a beginning to a story. If you start a story, you begin the first part of the story. You tell what happens next. A start is like the start of a new thing that happens.

To start something is to give it life. It is to take the first step. If you start to eat food, you begin to drink it. If you start to walk, you start to move. This is the beginning of the thing you want to do.

It is also important to start small. Starting with a small thing can be easier than starting with a big one. It can help you learn and build your way up.

Sometimes, you start something that does not work well at first. You try again and again until it starts to do what you want. This is a good way to learn and grow.
