Arrives is a word that means to come or come to a place. It is about what happens when you are in one place and you are going to another place.

Imagine you are going to school. You leave your house at a time. You have a bag with things you need. When you arrive, you are the first person in the room. You have found the right way and you are the first one there.

You can arrive in many ways. You can arrive by car, by bus, by train, or by a long walk. You can also arrive when the day starts or when the night begins. Some people say that an hour arrives when time starts.

When you arrive, you might feel a bit tired. You have been moving for some time. But you are happy to be in your new place. You might meet friends or see a family you love. You might feel a bit nervous, but you are excited to see what the day brings.

You can also arrive in a different way. If you are late, you have arrived. If you are very early, you have arrived before anyone else. If you arrive with a big bag, you have arrived with a lot. If you arrive with a happy face, you have arrived with a bright day.

In your life, you might arrive at work, at school, or at a big eventually. You might have arrived at a new job or a new home. You might have arrived in a new city or a new town. When you arrive, you are in a new place and you are ready to start your new life.
