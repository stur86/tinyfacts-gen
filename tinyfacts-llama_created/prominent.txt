When you talk about people, a word can mean many different things. One word tells you who something is. Another word tells you why something happens.

The word we are talking about today is about a person. To be a person is to have a face and a body. Everyone has a face and a body. But some people stand out more than others. They are not just like anyone else. They have a way they move, a way they talk, a way they wear clothes, and sometimes they have a look in their eyes. All these things make them more important.

A person can also be important when they do something for others. If a person helps a child who is hurt, they are important. If a person helps a person who is afraid, they are important too. When a person is important, people listen to them. They want to hear what they have to say.

The word is also used in other ways. Sometimes, it means to stand out in a crowd. If you are a person who is very good at a game, you are a person who stands out. You are not just like everyone else, you are the best at that game. This is important because it makes you feel good. You feel like you are the one who has the power.
