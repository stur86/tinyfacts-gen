To be something is to have it. When you buy a car, you are buying a thing you do not have. You are getting it for yourself. It is for you. The car is for you.

To be something also means to add a part to it. If you have a book, you can add a cover. The cover is a part that you put on the book. It is not the book. The book still has the same words and the same stories. The cover is a new thing that is on top of the book.

You can also be something that is not like you. If you are a small person, you are different. You are not like the people you see. You are not like the people you have known. You are a different person. You are your own person.

Sometimes, you can get something that is already there. If you walk into a room, you are in the room. You are inside. You are not out. You are already there. You are a part of the room.

To be something is to have a life. A thing does not have a life. A person has a life. A person can laugh, cry, and feel happy. A thing can sit, stand, and walk. But a person can not sit or stand like a thing. A person is the life of the thing.

You can also be something that is not here. You can be something that is in your head. You can think about something. You can remember things from the past. You can be afraid or happy. Your mind is always working.
