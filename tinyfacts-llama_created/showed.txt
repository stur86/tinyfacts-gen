To show something means to act on it. Imagine you see a picture. You want to know what it is. You look at it again and again. You see the colors, the lines, and the words. You are learning. You are showing the picture to your friends. You tell them what it is so they can understand.

You can also show things that are not there. If you have a box with a ball inside, you can show the ball to someone. You can open the box, take the ball out, and show it to them. This is a way to share what you have with others.

Sometimes, you show a story. If you tell a friend a story about your life, you are showing them. You are giving them a picture of what happened. They see the words and the pictures in your head. They can understand what you are saying.

You can also show how to do something. If you are a teacher, you show your students how to write. You write words on a piece of paper. You show them how to put the words in order. You are helping them learn.

When you show something, you act. You do something with your body or your mind. You tell them, you show them, or you act on a story.
