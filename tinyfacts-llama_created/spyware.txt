To be a spotted one is to know and see the things that are there. It is to notice people or things you do not want to notice. A person might not want a child to watch what they do, or a computer might not want a person to see what they are doing.

You can be a spotted one in many ways. You might see a person who is not supposed to be there. You might see a thing that is not supposed to be there. You might see someone who is trying to hide something. This is often done for a bad reason, like to catch a bad person.

There are also things that are not people, but still spot them. These are things that watch you. They might watch you when you are on the phone. They might watch what you are writing. They might watch what you are doing in the room. They are always looking for something new.

Some of these things are not good. They might take your pictures. They might take your money. They might tell your computer what to do. You must be carefully about what you let these things do. You must not let them watch you when you are alone, or when you are sleeping.
