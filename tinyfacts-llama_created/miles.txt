To go a long way is to move a long way from one place to another. This can be done by walking, by driving a car, or by flying in a big thing in the sky.

One way to look at it is like this: if you have a ball that is one foot long, and you want to go to a place that is two feet far away, you need to walk two feet. If you walk five feet, you have gone a long way. This is the same with cars and big flying things. When you drive a car for a long time, you are going a long way. When you fly in a big flying thing, you are going much farther than a short walk.

The word comes from a time when people wanted to know how far they had gone. They would counters and numbers to see if they had really gone. If you say you have gone one hundred feet, you are sure you have gone. If you say you have gone a hundred and two feet, you might be wrong. The number is important.

Sometimes people use the word to talk about how big a place is. A big city might be one hundred hundred hundreds of people. That is a very many people. A small town might have ten or twenty hundred people. That is many, but not as many as a very big city.

It is also used for very long numbers. If you say you have gone one hundred and two hundred and four hundred and five hundred times, you are saying you have gone one hundred and two hundred and four hundred and five hundred times that one. This is a very, very long way.
