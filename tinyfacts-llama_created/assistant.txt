An helper is a person or thing that helps another person. They do things that you are not sure you can do. They might make a phone call for you, or they might do a job for you.

Think about a teacher. A teacher is an important person who helps you learn. They are not your parents. They do not live with you. They just go to a school and give you things to know. An helper does the same job. They go to a work place and do things that help the people there.

An helper can be a person or a thing. Sometimes a helper is a small thing that you keep on your desk. It is a box with numbers or a box with a light. Sometimes a helper is a person who sits next to you in a room. They talk to you and help you understand things.

You can also have an helper at home. Your mom or dad might have one. They clean the house, do the money, and help you with your school work. This is very important. They make sure everything is okay at home.

Sometimes, we need an helper when we are feeling very bad. We might need someone to tell us to stay calm. Or we might need someone to help us when we are scared. An helper is there to help you feel better.
