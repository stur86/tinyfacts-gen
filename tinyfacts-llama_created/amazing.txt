When you say something is amazing, you mean it is very good. You feel like you are in a world of your own. This word is used to tell how something looks, how it moves, or how it works.

Imagine you have a very big dog. You are amazed when the dog runs because it is much bigger than you. You might laugh and say, I am amazed! The word helps us to talk about what is happening.

If you see a big, bright star in the sky, you might feel amazed. This is because the star is so big and light that it is hard to believe. You can feel like your eyes are wide open.

You can also use this word when you do something that makes you feel happy. If a child does something very well, they might feel amazing. They feel like they are the best.

In some places, people use this word to mean something is so good that it makes you feel like you are walking on air. You might feel like you are walking on water.

When you say something is amazing, you are showing that it is very good.
