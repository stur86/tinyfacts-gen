Sign is a word that means something important or a fact that is noticed. It can be a word, a number, a picture, or a sound. It tells us about the world or the people in it.

Think of a piece of wood. If you put a small mark on it, people can see that the mark is important. That mark is a sign because it lets them know something about the wood.

A sign can be a word. If you see a sign that says, you are here, you know that a place is where people live. You are following the sign to find a place to stay.

Signs can also be things that happen without a word. If you are walking down a street and you see a big sign that says, do not stop, you are following the sign. You are doing what is important for your safe moving through the city.

Sometimes, a sign can be a person. If a person is saying, follow me, they are giving a sign to the people around them. They are telling everyone to follow what they are doing.

The word is also important for computers. When a computer needs to know if a person is real or not, it looks at a sign that says, is this person you? The computer checks if the sign is the right one. If it is, the computer knows that the person is real.

In our lives, we use signs every day. We use them to find our way, to remember important things, and to follow the order of what is happening. Signs help us stay safe and help us understand the world around us.
