An person who plans things is called an owner. They put things in the right order. They tell others what to do. They are also the ones who make sure people follow the plan. 

Imagine you want to have a game with your friends. You need to decide who will be the one to run around and who will be the one to catch the ball. You pick a person to be the first one. They are the owner of the game plan. They set the order of the game so everyone knows what to do.

Another person is the one who makes sure everyone follows the plan. They tell the owner what to do. They might say, if the owner says, the game starts, then everyone must start. If the owner says, the game is over, everyone must stop.

Some people are also the owner of a store. They make a plan for how much money they will get from each day. They decide how much money to keep for themselves and how much to give to the people who work in the store. They set a plan for how to run the store so it stays running well.

The word can also mean a group of people who all follow the same plan. They are the same people, but they do things in different ways. This is why you might have a group of friends who all do the same things. You all follow the same plan and you all do them.

In a big city, there are many people who follow the same plan. They all drive on the same road and follow the same signs. They all go to the same places and do the same things. Everyone follows the plan, and it keeps the city running well.

In short, an owner is the one who makes a plan and tells others what to do. They keep everyone following the plan so everything goes right.
