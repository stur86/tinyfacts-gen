When you are talking to someone, you need to be able to hear them. You use your ears to pick up the sounds. If you want to talk to a friend who is far away, you need to find a way to send those sounds to them.

This is what a computer does. It can send and get words. It uses many many small boxes that work together. These boxes are like your ears. They help the computer understand what you are saying.

Imagine you want to tell a story. You start with a word. Then you add another word. You do this many times. When you finish the story, you have a long line of words. This line of words is like a long story.

When you are writing a story, you need to tell the reader how to picture the story. You use words and tell how the people feel. You want the reader to see the story in their own mind.

A computer does the same thing. It takes a set of words and uses them to make a picture. It can make pictures of people, animals, and things. It can also make pictures that look like a movie.

When you are talking to someone, you are sending words to them. You are using a computer to send those words to the person. When you are writing a story, you are sending words to the reader.
