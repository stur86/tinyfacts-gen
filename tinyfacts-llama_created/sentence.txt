A word is a thing you say or write. A group of words together is a word. When you use a word, you tell the world something about yourself or what is happening. You might say something nice, something bad, or something funny.

A group of words can have one idea in them. If you have only one idea, you are saying a single thing. If you have many ideas, you are telling the story of those ideas.

A word can change how we feel. If you hear a word that makes you happy, you might smile. If you hear a word that makes you afraid, you might get nervous.

Some words are short. Some words are long. Some words are hard to write. Some words are easy to understand. Words help us talk and write things.

When you write a story or a letter, you use many words. You use one word for a little bit, then you use another word, then another word. This helps your story feel strong and clear.

Everyone uses the same words. You, me, she, he, they, and they all use the same words. But each person adds their own ideas and ways to use those words.
