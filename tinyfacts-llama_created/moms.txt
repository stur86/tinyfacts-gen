To be a mom means you love a child very much. You take care of them when they are little. You make them feel safe. You are always near them when they need you. You are their friend.

A mom is a woman who has a baby in her body. The baby grows inside the mom. After a long time, the baby is ready to come out. The mom goes to a hospital to help the baby be safe and well.

A mom also helps the family. She makes sure everyone is happy. She talks to them when they are sad. She makes them laugh when they are happy. She helps them learn how to be good people.

Sometimes a mom is the only child. Other times, she is one of many children. She loves all her children very much. She is always ready to help them. She shows them the way. She is a very good mom.

To be a good mom, you must be kind. You must listen to your child when they talk. You must be sure they are safe. You must show them love. And you must be ready to help them when they need you.
