A person who has something to give is called a buyer. A person who has something to give is called a giver. When a person wants to give something to another person, they use these words. 

A person who has a thing to give might be a teacher. They give you a book. A person who wants to get something new might say, I want a new phone. They want to buy it with their money. 

In a store, there are many people. Some of them are the ones who give things. Some of them are the ones who want to buy. They stand next to each other and look at each other. The person who has the thing they want to buy looks at the person who gives them it. If the person who wants to buy is happy with what they see, they say, Yes, I want this thing. The person who gives it to them says, Here, take it. 

Sometimes, people do not just want a thing. They might want someone to talk to. A person who wants to talk might say, I am sad. They do not have anyone to talk to. A friend might say, I am going to a party. They want to go with others. They want to see a movie or go to a game. This is called a friend. They do not have something to give to anyone else. They just want to be with the people they know.
