To book something means you buy it for later. You are telling the person or place that you want it now.

Imagine you want a seat on a bus. If you buy the seat for now, you have it. Later, you can go to the bus and get in. This is like booking a thing.

You can also book a room in a big place. If you want that room for a few days, you are booking it. This is important so you know it is taken.

Sometimes, you need to book a thing for a long time. You might book a table for dinner at a big place. When you arrive, you are ready to eat. You did not have to wait for the table to open.

You can book a car for a day if you need it. You are telling the person that you want the car. They will give it to you when the time is right.

In some places, you need to book a time to do a thing. You might book a time to go to a place or to do a job. This helps people plan and make sure they have enough time.

You can also book a thing for later when you are not sure if you want it. You might book a book for later if you want to read it. This is like leaving a door open until you are sure you need it.

To book something is to buy it or tell the place that you want it now.
