A word is a thing that people use to talk about the world. To talk about something means to tell other people about it. We use words every day to tell the story of how things work, of what is happening, of what happened before, and of what will happen next.

One word is for one person. It is like a name. You can not have two names at once. You can only have one name. If you have two names, it is not the same word.

Another word is for many people. It is like a group name. You can say, like, a group of people, a group of animals, or a group of things. This word does not have a name. It is just a way of talking about a group.

There is also a word for every thing that is in the world. It is like a name for a book. If you have a book, you can say, you read the book. This word is not a name. It is just a way of talking about the book.

The word perhaps is different. It can mean, you might think, you might find out, or you might try to do something. If you are afraid, you might say, I am perhaps afraid. This word does not tell you what to do. It just shows how you are feeling.

Finally, there is a word for when you have a small part. You might have a small part of a food, a small part of a thing, or a small part of a person. This word is like a name for a small piece.
