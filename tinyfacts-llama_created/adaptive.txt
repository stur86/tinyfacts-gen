To be an animal is to have a body that can do many things. Your arms are for making things and your legs are for moving around. You can use your arms to reach food and your legs to go fast.

An animal that can change how it looks is an animal that is good at changing. If a cat is a good cat, it can change its color to look like a ball of fire. It can also change its eyes so it can see at night.

When you say an animal is an animal, you mean it is not a person. Animals are very different from people. They do not need to drink water all the time like we do. They breathe air and they need to eat food to stay strong.

Some animals can fly. Some can run very fast. Some can see very well. Some can even feel the ground under their feet. All these things show how well the animal is at its job.
