A shoe is a thing you wear on your feet to walk and stay on the ground. A shoe has a bottom part that you put your feet in. This part is made of hard stuff that is strong and can hold your feet. The top part is the part that goes on your head. It is usually made of soft stuff so it does not hurt your head.

You wear these shoes to keep your feet warm. In the cold or wet days, you put them on before going out. You can wear them with other clothes to keep them warm. Some shoes are very tall so you can stand up straight. Some are very short so you can walk quickly.

There are many kinds of shoes. Some are made for running. Some are made for walking. Some are made for standing. Some are made for playing games. Some are made for working. You can pick one that is good for the kind of work you do.

You need to change your shoes when you are done. You take off the top part and put them in the bag. You do this so they stay clean and do not get old. You change them for the next day so you are ready to walk.
