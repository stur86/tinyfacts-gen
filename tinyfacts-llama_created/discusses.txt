To talk about something is to use words. When you say this, you are talking to others. You are not just saying one thing, you are talking about many other things.

The word talks about the idea. It tells you about the thing that is the focus. It might say something is very big. It might say something is very small. It might say something is very hard to do.

You can also use this word to talk about what you think. You might say, I like this or I do not like this. You are telling others your point of mind.

Sometimes people use this word when they want to say they are going to do something later. They might write it down in a book. They might make a plan. They are talking about the thing they want to do.
