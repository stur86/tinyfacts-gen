To help something is to make it easier for people to do. You can help someone by giving them a drink. If they are very tired, you can help them sleep by moving a heavy thing away from them.

When you help something, you are doing a good job. This makes the person or thing you are helping feel better. You might help a friend move to a new home. You can also help people who are sick. You can give them food or water so they do not get very sick.

You can also help with work. If you are working in a big office, you can help your friend who is doing work that is hard. This is called working together. Everyone helps each other so that everyone can do their job.

Sometimes, to help someone is to make them feel safe. If you see a child who is afraid of a dog, you can help them feel better. You might talk to the child in a soft voice or show them a picture of a dog that is not really a dog. This helps the child feel more at home.
