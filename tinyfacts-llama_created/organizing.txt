To have a plan

When you have a plan, you are ready for things to happen. You know what you want and how you will get there. You are not afraid. You feel strong. You are in control.

Imagine you want to go to the park. You want to play and run. You do not just sit around waiting. You go. You walk. You run. You play. You are in the right place.

If you have a plan, you can do many things at once. You can see which way to go and which way to turn. You do not have to think about every step. You just follow your plan.

You can plan your day too. You might wake up early. You might eat breakfast. You might go to work. You might do your work. You might go to the store after work. You follow your plan and you do not worry.

A plan is like a road in your brain. It is how you move through life. It helps you feel safe and sure. It helps you know what to do when things happen.
