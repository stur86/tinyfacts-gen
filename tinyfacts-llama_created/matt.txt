A man who works with his hands is called a worker. A man who works with his hands so well that he can do many things quickly is called a good worker. He might build houses, fix cars, or make clothes. He works at a place with many people to make things for the world.

A man who sits and talks is called a person. A person can say many words and understand many words. They tell you what they think. People need them because they help us understand the world.

A man who lives on land and a man who lives in water are different kinds of people. One can stay on a big piece of ground. The other can go to a place that is high in the air. They use different ways to stay and stay safe.

A man who works with a team is called a team leader. He helps many other people do their jobs. He makes sure that all work is done well. He also helps people feel safe and happy. He is the leader of a group of people who work together.
