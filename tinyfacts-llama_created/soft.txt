The word soft is about how things feel.

When you touch something, you can feel it. If it is hard, it feels like a rock. If it is hard to the touch, it feels like a tree. If it is soft, it feels like a bed. You can put your hand on a soft thing and feel it. Your hand might feel very nice.

You can also say something is very soft. This means it is very easy to hold. You can hold a soft thing for a long time without it breaking. A soft thing can be a soft drink or a soft bed. It is a way to show you care about something.

Sometimes, you might feel something that is too soft. It is like your skin. You might be afraid because it is so soft. It is a feeling you get when you are scared.

In a house, you often have soft things like a bed or a couch. These are very nice to sit on. You can feel them and rest your body.

In some words, soft can mean you are very kind. If you are soft, you show you care. You might be very nice when you talk to someone. You are not like a mean person. You are like a friend.

So, soft is about how things feel. It is about being nice and easy to hold.
