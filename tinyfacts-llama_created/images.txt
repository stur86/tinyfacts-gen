An idea is a picture or a word that tells us something. It is not a real thing you see. It is something you think about.

We use these ideas to talk about our lives. When you tell a story, you are using an idea. You are telling the story of what happened to you. You are not showing the story. You are using the idea to explain it.

Think about a picture. The picture is a picture of a tree. It does not show the tree. It is a way to tell you about the tree. If you look at the picture, you can see the tree. But the picture is not the tree. It is the idea of the tree.

An idea can be very strong. It is like a big word in your head. It helps you feel. It helps you understand. It helps you know what to do.

Sometimes, we make pictures. We draw or use a computer to make a picture. We can add colors and make it look nice. We can also change the picture so it looks different. We can make it look like a story. This is how we tell stories with pictures.

We also have pictures in our minds. A picture of a child is in our mind. The picture is not a real thing. It is only in our head. But it helps us remember the child. It helps us feel happy when we see the picture.

In some places, people use these ideas to make money. They make pictures of things. They show them to people. People buy them. The pictures are then kept in a big house. Many people look at them every day. They help us remember things.

All these ideas are about how we understand the world. They help us live our lives. They help us know what to do.
