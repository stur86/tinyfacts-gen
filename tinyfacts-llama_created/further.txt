To further something means to make it even more. When you start doing something, you can only do it one way. If you want to do it fast, you have to go very quickly. If you want to do it quietly, you have to be very quiet.

If you are doing something, you may have started. You might have begun. But now you want to do it better. You want to do it more. You want to do it in a different way. You might want to use more power. You might want to use your hands. You might want to use your head.

You can also further a problem. If you have a problem, you might think you know how to fix it. But then you find out it is not that easy. You have to look at it from another side. You have to see it from a different point of time. This helps you understand better.

Sometimes, you have to further a person. If a person is doing something wrong, you want to help them. You want to show them that it is not right. You want to help them see that they can do better. You want to show them that you care.

To further something is like taking things to the next step. It means you are getting closer to where you want to be. It is about moving forward in a way that is good for everyone.
