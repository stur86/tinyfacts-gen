A rock is a hard piece from the ground. It is made of long lines that go up and down. When the ground moves, these long lines change and form the rock.

You can find rocks all around you. They sit on the ground, on the side of a road, or in a deep place in the ground. Some rocks are very big and heavy. They can fall down and hit a person.

Some rocks are white, some are brown, and some are black. They can be very old or very new. Some rocks are covered in tiny things that help keep them from breaking. These tiny things are like a soft cover.

You can use rocks for many things. You can use them to build houses or cars. You can use them to make fire by putting them in a hot fire box. You can also use rocks to make a clear drink from the ground.

But rocks can also hurt people. If someone hits a rock with their hand, the hand can get hurt. If the rock falls on a person, they can die. So, we need to be carefully around rocks.
