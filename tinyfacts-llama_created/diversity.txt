People, animals, and things that are different can be a good thing. When many different things are around, it makes life more interesting. You might see a dog, a cat, a horse, and a car. Each one is different from the others.

Dancing is another kind of this. When many people dance, it is fun to watch. Each person does their own way of moving on the floor. Some people jump high. Others walk fast. Some use their hands to make music. When people from different places and with different ways of living dance together, it makes the world feel more like a big place.

In the world, we have different kinds of food. You eat food that comes from a tree. Your body gets the good parts. Other people eat food that comes from a ground animal. The ground animal gives the good parts to the person. We need to be friends with other people so we can share what we have.

The world is also full of different kinds of words. You might say, I love to read. Or, I love to write. These words are not the same. They mean different things. A word is a thing that you say. It can tell you what to feel, what to do, or what to think.

When we are different, we can learn from each other. A child might learn how to play a game by watching an older person. An older person might learn how to make food by watching a young one. We grow and learn from each other.

You can also say that a world is full of different kinds of people. This means there are many people who are different from each other. If everyone was the same, the world would be very quiet. But because we are all different, we have more to learn from each other and more fun.
