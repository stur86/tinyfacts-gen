To make a set of things that are the right way can be a very important job. When a group of people or a place has set ways to act, everyone must follow those ways.

Think about how you walk in a school. You must not run, you must not push other people, and you must stay on the line. If you do not follow these ways, you might get in trouble. The teacher says, you are breaking the way, so you must change.

Grown people also follow these ways. They do not do things that are not allowed. They pay their money, they follow the roads, and they follow the food they buy. If they do not follow these ways, they might get in trouble.

Sometimes, things are not always clear. A person might think they are doing something right. But if they do not follow the set ways, they might get in trouble. This is why it is so important to follow what is written.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are not allowed in a place. If you are in a store and you touch something that is not yourself, you might get in trouble. The store has set ways to keep people safe, and you must follow those ways.

In some places, there are even more ways. The city might have more ways than the school. The city might have ways that are different for cars and for people. These ways help keep everyone safe.

The word is important because it helps us understand what is right and what is not. It helps us know what to do and what not to do.
