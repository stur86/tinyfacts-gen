To be strange is to be different. It is when something does not follow the usual way. You might be different in how you walk, the way you speak, or the way you think.

If you are a very fast runner, you are very strange. You move in a way that no one else is. You might be the fastest person in the world. This is because you are different from everyone else.

It is also strange when you are very happy. You might laugh and smile a lot. You might feel like you are on top of the world. This is because you are so much better than other people.

Sometimes, being strange can be a good thing. If you are a person who thinks a different way, you might be the best person to help others. You might be able to see things that no one else can see.

But sometimes being strange can be hard. If you are different and people do not like you, you might feel sad. You might want to hide who you are. You might feel like you are the only one.

Even in the world of animals, being strange is important. Some animals are very quiet, while others are very loud. Some have bright colors, while others are very dark. This is because each animal is different and these are what make them work well together.
