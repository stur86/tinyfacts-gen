To have a good or nice thing means you have done many things that show you are ready. A person who is good at a job has a good job. A person who is very nice has a kind heart. When you have a good job or a nice heart, you are often called a good person.

You might see a group of people who have done many good things. They are all together because they have done what they think is right. They do this work to make the world a better place. They help people who are in trouble and they work to keep the streets clean.

You can also have a good thing that helps you. A computer that is very good can help you do many things. It helps you write papers, talk to friends, and watch movies. A good car can help you move from one place to another quickly.

When you have a good thing, you feel strong and happy. You know you have done what is right. You feel like you are a good person because you have done many good things.
