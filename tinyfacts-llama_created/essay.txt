An answer is a group of words that show you think and why you think this way. You might write about a person, a thing, or an idea. You might use your own words and show how they fit into the world.

An answer can be long. It might have many lines. You might write about one thing and then another, and another, and many more. You might write about how this thing or idea makes you feel. You might write about what you think is right or wrong.

An answer is not just one piece of writing. It is also the ideas you use to begin or end it. You might write about how you started to think about this. You might write about what you learned. You might write about why you are sure this is right.

Some answers are for a teacher. The teacher asks you to write about this. You write what you know and how it fits with what they asked. You might write about what you think is important. You might write about how you feel.

Other answers are for people who want to know more. You might write about what you think is true. You might write about why you think something is important. You might write about what you do to live your life.

You can also write about people. You can write about how a person feels. You can write about how they act. You can write about how they live their life.

An answer is important because it helps us understand the world. It helps us know what to do. It helps us know why. It helps us learn.
