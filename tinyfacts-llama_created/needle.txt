A thing that feels like a small point is called a need. It is a small part that you use to fix things that are broken. You can use it to fix a piece of paper that is torn, or to fix a thing that is hurt.

To use a need, you must hold it with your fingers. You must turn it around in your hand to make it point into a thing. Then, you can push the point into the part of the thing that is broken. The point makes a small cut in the part, and the part gets fixed. After that, the thing is strong again.

You can also use a need to make a small mark. You put the thing in a small piece of paper and press it down. The paper is left with a small line where the point touched it. This line is like a small word or a number that tells you where the mark is.

Some things have many needs. A book has many needs. A child needs a book to learn. A doctor needs a book to read about how the body works. A person needs a need to breathe. They need air to stay living.

A need is something that you need to have. You can not live without a need. If you do not have a need, you will not be the same person.
