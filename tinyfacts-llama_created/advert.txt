An admit is a word that people use to say you are sorry. If you did something wrong, you can admit it. You do not hide it and you do not try to change what you did. You just say you are sorry.

Some people use this word when they tell a story. They might say, you admitted to the trouble. They did not try to hide it. They just said the truth.

Sometimes, you admit that you love something. You might say, I admit I love my dog. You are happy and you want to play with him. You do not try to hide your love.

In some places, you admit to a wrong. If you did something that hurt someone, you can say, I admit it. This word is used when you are sorry for what happened.

In the world of work, you might admit when you did not do your job. You might say, I admit I did not do my work. You are sorry for not doing it.
