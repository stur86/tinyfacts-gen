To put something in a box

Think about a box that holds things you need for a game. You might put a ball, a book, and a small thing that makes a sound inside it. When you are done, you close the box and put it down on the floor. This is how you put things away when you are done with them.

You can also put things in a box for a very different reason. If you are very angry, you might throw a thing at someone. This is a bad thing to do. It makes the other person sad and the two of you become very angry. If you put a thing in a box, you are putting it away from people you do not like.

Sometimes you put people in a box too. You might put someone in a place that is not a home. They are not allowed to stay there. This is because the place is not safe for them. This is a way of making sure everyone is safe.

The word is very important. It is how we talk about how to deal with things we do not like. It is also how we talk about how to put things away so we do not have to think about them later.
