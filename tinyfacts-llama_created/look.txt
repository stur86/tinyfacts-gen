Look is a word that we use to show we see something. When you look at a person, you are looking at them. You are looking at their face, their eyes, and their hair. You are looking at what they are doing.

You can look at a thing in a room. If you want to see the bed, you look at it. You are looking at it. You are not just sitting there. You are doing something.

You can also look at what is happening. If you see a ball going around the room, you look at it. You are watching it. You are seeing it.

It is easy to look. You just close your eyes and look. It is not hard. You do not need to think about it. You just look.

Sometimes, you look at something that is far away. You look at a tree in a far field. You are looking at it from a far place. You are not right next to it. You are looking at it from a place where you are not.

Looking is important. It helps us understand what is happening. It helps us know what is there. It helps us know who is there.
