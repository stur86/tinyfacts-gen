Understand means to know how something works. It is about understanding how things are made and how they act. When you understand a word, you know what it means. You can use it to talk or write.

Imagine you are trying to help a baby. The baby wants to eat a thing. You show them how to take a small piece from a bag. You say,  This is the thing you want to eat. The baby understands. They know what to do.

If you are talking to someone, you are talking to them. You are sending your words to them. The other person is listening and understands what you are saying. They can feel the words in their heart.

Sometimes, when you know something, you do not need to say it. You just show it. You can show a picture or a story. You can show the way to a place. You are telling them. They see. They understand.

But sometimes you need to show them. You need to tell them. You need to say,  This is what you mean. You need to say it in your own words. This is when you use the word understand.
