A long, thinking thing that you use to eat food. It has two parts. One part is very long and has a point at the end. The other part is shorter and has a part that goes around the middle. You use this thing to hold food and pick up the food from your mouth.

You often use a table for this. A table is a thing that sits on the floor. It has many long lines going across it. You sit on one end of the table and you use a long, thinking thing to hold food. You put the food on the table and you eat.

You also use a table for other things. You might use a table to write words. You hold a paper under the thing and press your hand on it. This makes the paper move and shows what you want to write.

Some people use a table for games. They put a small ball on the floor. One person tries to move the ball to a different spot on the floor. They use their hands to push the ball. This is fun because it is different and it is easy to do.
