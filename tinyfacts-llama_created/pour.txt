To give something into a cup or a bottle is to let it go. You can do this with water, or with food. When you drink water, you are taking it from a bottle. The bottle is empty, so the water goes into the cup. This is what we mean when we say something is being given away.

Sometimes, we give something to someone. If you give your friend a book, you are letting them have it. You are not keeping it for yourself anymore. You are letting them use it. This is good because it helps the person who needs it.

You can also give something to a place. Imagine a store. If you go into the store, you are giving the store money. The money is like the water that went into the cup. It helps pay the person who runs the store. The store uses the money to buy things to keep its place clean.

Giving also means letting go of a thing you love. Think about a game you played with a friend for many hours. You did not want the game to end. You were very happy. But now, you need to go to bed. This is like giving the game away. You are letting go of the memory of the fun you had together.
