To be on a horse is to be in a place where a tall animal with strong legs and a hard body is standing. When you say someone is on a horse, you mean they are sitting on the back of the animal.

The person might be holding the horse by the neck or the sides with their arms. They might also hold a long thing that goes across their body so the horse can move faster.

If you are on a horse, you are in a place where the ground is high and there is not much room. You can see everything around you. You can also see the sky and the trees. The sound of the horse is loud and it can help you stay safe.

Sometimes you might have to hold the horse so it does not run away. This can be hard because the horse wants to go. But if you are strong and you can hold it, you can help it stay in place.

If you are on a horse, you might feel like you are the top of the world. You are in a place where the air is strong and you can see far away. You can feel the wind in your hair and the sun on your face. It can be a great feeling because it is different from walking or running.
