An animal that can walk and run is an animal. These animals are very important because they help our world. They help make food for us and their babies. They carry heavy things on their backs.

Most of these animals are not the same from the moment they are a tiny thing in a mom. The mom does not have babies to help her. The young ones grow and learn how to be safe. When they are a bit older, they can live on their own without the mom. Some of these animals can live for many years. They can be very different from one another.

Some animals are very small. They are very fast. They can fly or run very fast. They do this so they can find food before the sun goes down. Some animals are very big. They can be as tall as a house. They have very strong bodies and can move heavy things.

Many animals are not very bright. They do not understand how to stay safe. They might eat things that are not good for them. We must make sure we do not hurt them. We must also keep them safe. We can do this by following what they tell us and by keeping the world clean.

Some animals are very good at hiding. They can hide in trees, in the ground, or in the water. They can stay there for a long time until they feel safe. This is very important. If animals hide, people stay safe too.
