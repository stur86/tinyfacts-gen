An animal has many tiny parts inside it. Some of these parts are for eating. Other parts help the animal fight. One kind of tiny part does both.

When an animal feels something bad, like a tiny piece from a friend that has hurt it, it makes more of these tiny parts. It sends them out into the air. These tiny parts go to many other animals. The tiny parts ask them if they too have the bad tiny part. If they do, they say yes, and the animal feels better.

One kind of tiny part is very good at finding the bad tiny part. It looks at it and says, yes, this is the one I am looking for. The other kind of tiny part is not good at this. It looks at the bad tiny part and says, no, this is not it.

The tiny parts that fight are like a team. They work together to keep everyone safe. They are very important because they can help us get better when we get sick.
