To deal with something is to find a way to live with it. It means you must be ready to face what is happening.

Imagine you have a big problem at home. You feel very sad and angry. You want to shout at the person who made the thing that broke. But you do not want to hurt them. So you go to a quiet room and you write down what you feel. After you write, you start to think about how you can fix the problem. You look at the picture of the thing on the wall and you see the problem better. You think about how you can make the thing work again. You might tell the person who made it that it is not good enough.

When you are dealing with a problem, you are not always happy. You might feel afraid, confused, or worried. But you keep going because you know you are trying to be strong. You are learning how to live with the problem.

Sometimes you need to deal with a hard thing that hurts your heart or makes you cry. You might cry for a long time, but then you start to feel better. You find a way to remember happy times with the person who made you feel this way. You keep moving forward, even though it is hard.

To be able to deal with a thing is to show that you are a person. It means you are not afraid to face the hard parts of life.
