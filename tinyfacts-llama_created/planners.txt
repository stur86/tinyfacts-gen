To plan means to think about what you want to do and how you will do it. You think about the day, the week, the month, or even years ahead. You decide what you want to be, what you want to do, and when you want to do it.

You can plan for many things. You might plan a trip to a far away place. You think about where you want to stay, what to wear, what food to eat, and what games to play. You also plan how you will get from one place to another. If you have a car, you plan your way, the roads, and the time.

Planning is important for many reasons. When you plan, you can feel safe. You know what to expect. You can make a plan for your work, your school days, or your free time. You can also plan for things that will happen later, like when you might need money or when you might want to meet a friend.

Some people use the word to talk about how they live their days. They plan their work, their food, and their sleep. They plan how they will spend their money. They might plan to go to a store to buy things, or to do something for fun.

In some places, people use a word called a plan. They write a plan for a big thing that is going to happen. They make a plan for how to move the people, how to build the houses, and how to give money to the people who need it. This is important because it helps everyone know what is going on and how to work together.

To plan can be hard. Sometimes you want to do something, but you do not know what you should do. You might feel like you are in the middle of something. Planning can make you feel safe and sure. It is a way to say you are ready for what comes next.
