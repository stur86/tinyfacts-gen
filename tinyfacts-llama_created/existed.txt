To have something means to be there. When you say a thing is here, it means it is in this place. This is the beginning.

Then, you can also say something has been. If a thing is here, it has been in this place many times before. You can look at a picture of a place you went to and know that it is the same as the place you are in now.

But if something happens, it does not stay the same. You can change something. If you say a person is happy, they are happy now. If you say they were happy before, they were not happy anymore. Everything changes, even if it stays in the same place.

Sometimes, when you say something has been, you mean it is still there. But sometimes, you mean it is gone. If you say a book is gone, it is not in the same place anymore. It is not in your room. It is in a place where people keep things.

When you say something has been, you are talking about time. Time is like a line that goes from one moment to another. Each moment is a little bit of time, and when you look at the line, you see how the moments are moving. If something happened a long time ago, it is gone now. If something is still there, it has been for a long time.

The word is important. If you say a thing is gone, you know it is really gone. You can never say a thing is still there if it is gone.
