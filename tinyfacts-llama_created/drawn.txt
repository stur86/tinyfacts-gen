To draw something means you make lines on a piece of paper. You start with a small line. You add more lines next to it. The lines go in a straight line from one end to the other. You keep adding lines and turning the paper so the lines go around you.

You can draw people, animals, and things that look like they are in front of you. You use your fingers to push and pull the paper. Sometimes you use a hard round thing that pushes the paper. You can also draw with a small stick.

Drawing is a way to show what you see. When you see something, you can draw it. It helps us remember things. It is a way to tell stories with pictures.
