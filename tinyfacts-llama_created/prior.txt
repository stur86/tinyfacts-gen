To come before something means you are first. This is true for many things. A child is the first child in a family. An animal is the first one of its kind. A book is the first book in a group of books.

You can also say something comes before another thing. If you eat a food before you drink a drink, you have come before you have to drink. This helps you stay safe while waiting for your drink to come.

In the world of computers, you can say a number is before another number. This is important because it helps you write the right order. If you want to write the number of hours before the number of minutes, you use this word.
