To get an idea of what an act means, imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You run around, try to hit a ball into a box, and you feel very good when you do it. You have a feeling that you are doing something good.

When someone does a thing and it is seen by many people, they say they have an idea of what they have done. This is what we call an idea. If you run around and hit the ball into the box, many people see you and think you are a good player. Your idea is that you are doing something good.

The word is also used when someone does a thing that is not easy. If you are afraid of a big animal and you see it, you have an idea. You know something is happening. You feel a little scared. This is what we call an idea.

Sometimes, the word is used when you get a thing you want. You work hard and you get a new car, a book you wanted, or a job you like. This is when you have an idea of what you have done.

It is also used to talk about how a thing grows. If you start a small thing and it grows big, you have an idea. You see how it is becoming.

The word can be a bit hard to understand at first. But when you think about it, it is about seeing what is happening, feeling good when it happens, or knowing what you have done.
