A promise from one person to another. When you say you will do something, you are making a promise.

Imagine you want to buy a book. You do not know if the book will be in good order. But you trust the store. You say you will pay for the book. The store says yes, and you pay. This is a promise. You have agreed to do something and you have paid for it.

A person can also give a promise. If you promise to help a friend, you are making a promise to be there for them. You are saying you will be there. This is a strong kind of promise.

Sometimes people give a promise to a business. They might say they will pay a lot of money for a car. If the person does not pay, the business might take their word. This is not a good idea because the person has not really promised anything.

You can also promise to do something on a day. You might say you will go to a movie on a day you do not have school. This is a small promise.

The word is used to show that something will happen. If you say you will do something, you have made a promise to do it.
