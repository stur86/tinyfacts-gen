To study something is to think about it in your head. If you want to know about a movie, you can watch it. If you want to know about a book, you read the words. If you want to know about a game, you play it. This is what people who work with papers do.

Some people go to a school. They go there every day for many years. They learn many things. They read many books. They talk to other people. They do not just watch. They think. They write things. They make things. They use what they learn to do something new. This is how they become good at their job.

There is a word for people who do this kind of work. They are called people of letters. They are people who like to think. They are people who like to write. They are people who like to learn. They are people who work with papers.

Sometimes, a person says they are an actor. This is a good job. They act in movies and plays. They pretend to be something else. They make people believe they are that person. They act so good that people watch them and laugh.

To know something is to understand it. To study something is to try to understand it. To be an student is to be a person who tries to learn.
