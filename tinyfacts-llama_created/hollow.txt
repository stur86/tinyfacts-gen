A thing that is made of nothing is called a room. You can close it and no sound can go out. But if you leave the door open, the sound can come out. This room is not full of air.

A box can also be a room. You can put things in it and close the top. The box is not full of air. When you open the top, the air goes out and the box is empty again. We say the box is empty.

Sometimes, a tree is a room. The room is inside the tree. It is not open to the air. The tree keeps the room inside. You can not go inside. The room is only for the tree.

A room can also be a place to sleep. You can lie down in a room and rest. The room is a place for you to be alone.
