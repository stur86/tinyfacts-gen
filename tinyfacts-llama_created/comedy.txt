A story that makes you laugh. A person who makes you laugh. A game with words and a body. People watch them.

They start with a problem. Maybe a person is sad. Maybe they do a thing that is not right. Then the person says, no, I am not going to do it. They do another thing. Another thing. They laugh and laugh. The people watching laugh too.

A man can do this. He says things that are not good, but the people laugh. He is not afraid. They like him. He is funny.

There are many kinds. There is a story with a man who does a job. He does it very well. There is a story with a man who is a bad man. He does bad things. There is a story with a man who is a good man. He does good things. Everyone likes the story.

You can watch them on a box. You can also watch them live. They are people. They act. They make you laugh.
