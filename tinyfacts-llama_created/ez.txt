An easy thing is when you can do it quickly and without much trouble. It is a way to do a job that is not hard. You do not have to think a lot. You just do what you have to do.

Imagine you are playing a game on a computer. If you pick the right numbers, you will get a good time. It is easy to do. But if you make a wrong move, the game is over. You had to think a lot and do many things.

An easy thing is like this: you can walk to a place in a short time. You do not have to run very fast. You just walk. You can stop walking and look at a thing. This is an easy thing to do.

Sometimes, an easy thing is a word. If you look at a book and find a word you know, it is easy. You did not have to think a lot. You just saw the word and knew what it means.

An easy thing can also be a life. If you live with your family, you have an easy life. You do not have to go to school every day. You can watch movies while you sleep. You are safe.

But an easy thing is not always easy to keep. If you are very happy, you might feel a little sad. If you have a good day, you might feel tired. If you have a lot of things to do, it can be hard.
