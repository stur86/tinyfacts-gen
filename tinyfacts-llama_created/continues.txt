When you tell a story, you start with a beginning. The first word shows how you started. You might start with a loud sound. You might start with a small thing that is close to you.

The beginning of a story can also show what happens next. You might show how the person is feeling. You might show what they do. You might show how they change.

After you start with the beginning, you keep going. You tell the story for a long time. You tell about what happened, and you tell about how the story ends.

When you finish a story, you say it is done. You say the story is over. This is important because it makes everyone feel safe. Everyone knows what happened, and everyone can move on to the next story.

A story can also continue when you tell it to someone else. If you tell a friend a story, they might ask questions. They might want to know more. You can keep going and tell them more. This is how we learn new things.

In a story, you can also show what happens later. You might show a big thing that happens many years later. You might show how the person changes because of what they did. You might show how they learn or grow.

Sometimes, a story continues in your own mind. You can remember things from the story over and over. You can use the story to help you feel happy or sad. You can use the story to learn about people or places. You can use the story to imagine what might happen next.
