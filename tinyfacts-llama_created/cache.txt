To save something is to put it away so you can come back later.

Imagine you are going to a big party and you have a lot of food to eat. You could just walk to the table every time you want a bite. But instead, you decide to save some food in your bag. You put the food away so you can come back later.

You are saving things for later. You might save money so you can buy something you want now. You might save clothes so you can wear them tomorrow. Or you might save a picture so you can look at it again later.

When you save, you are keeping something safe. You are not giving it away or letting it go. You are keeping it in a safe place so you can use it when you need it.

Sometimes, you save things you are afraid of. You might save a thing that makes you nervous. You put it away so you can look at it later when you feel better.

Saving is important. It helps you stay strong when things get hard. It helps you feel better when you are sad or scared. It gives you a way to plan and to feel calm.
