To do something is to try. You might try to be strong so you can run. You might try to learn a new word to speak. You try to pick up a ball so you can throw it.

When people work together to do a thing, they are making an act of help. They put their hands together to help a friend. They work as a team so everyone can do their part.

To have an idea is to have a thought. You might have a bright idea that you want to do. You are thinking about something and you want to make it happen.

To do something for someone is to give them something. You might give your friend a book. You are showing you care.

To try to do something is to use your brain. You try to learn a new game. You try to make a new plan. You use your body to do something.
