When someone says something is happening, they usually say it is happening now. But what if something is happening right now, even if no one is watching? We say that it is happening. This word is used for things that are happening right now.

Think of the word like this. If you are standing in front of a big door, and you know that you can open it, but you do not open it yet, you are not sure what will happen next. You are not waiting for something to happen. You are doing something else. You are taking a step. You are moving. You are the one who is doing the thing. This is what it means to be the one doing something.

We also use this word for things that are going on in the world around us. If you know that your friend is driving a car and they are going very fast, then you know that they are going. You do not watch them to see what they do, but you know that they are doing something because of the way they move.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about how things feel. If you are feeling very sad, you are not waiting for the day to come. You are doing something. You are taking a deep breath. You are thinking about what happened. You are feeling the way you are right now.

In some cases, we use this word to talk about things that are not happening yet. If you say that something is going to happen later, you are not sure. You are not sure if it will happen at all. You are just waiting to see what happens. This is the word for things that are going on in the past, or things that are going to happen later.
