The word later comes from a time before now. In the past, people used this word to talk about the time after they had done something. They would say, for an hour later, or for a few minutes later. This means something will happen a bit later than the time they already know.

In the morning, you might know when your family will get up. Later, you might see them. This is because time moves forward.

When you are doing a job, you might start at  tomorrow morning. Later, you will finish. This is because time keeps going.

It can also mean something that happened after something else. If you watch a movie and then go to bed, the movie is later than you were ready to go.
