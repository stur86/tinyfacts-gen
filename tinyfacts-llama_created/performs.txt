To do something is to act. When you do something, you bring that thing into being. You might do it while you are moving from one place to another. You are moving your body, and the thing you do is happening in your body.

When a person does something, it is an act. It is not a thing. It is not a thing you can see. It is a thing that happens. When you hear a song, you are hearing a thing. But when you hear the voice of a person, you are not hearing the thing. You are hearing what is happening in their body.

When a person works, they act. They do things to get money. They do things to make food. They do things to make clothes. They do things to fix things so they do not break. They act to get what they need.

When a group of people acts together, they act as one person. They all do the same thing and they all act at the same time. This is what happens when many people work together. They are all acting as one person.

When a thing does something, the act happens. A dog acts when it wants to eat. A baby acts when it needs to be held. An animal acts when it runs to find food. A person acts when they try to tell a story.
