People go on these trips for many reasons. You might want to learn a new thing. You might want to see new places. You might want to have fun. You might want to escape from your home. These are all good reasons to go on a trip.

Trips can be very different. You might go to a place with many tall houses. You might go to a place with many green fields. You might go to a place with many cold places. You might go to a place with many hot places.
