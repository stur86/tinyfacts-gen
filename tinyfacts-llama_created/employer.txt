An owner is a person who owns a house, a car, or a very big office with many computers. They pay people to work in their office. The owner decides how the money is spent. The owner also decides who does what job. The people who work for them are called their friends.

People can be the owner of a small store. They buy the store and keep it running. They work hard to make sure the store is safe and has good food. The owner also pays the people who help with the store. The owner must follow the big plan made by the owner when they were young.

Sometimes, a small group of people becomes the owner of a group of schools or a big city. This group must decide how to spend the money and what to do with the city. They also have to work with many people who help them.

People also have an owner when they pay money to watch a movie or a game. This money is given to the person or the group that owns the movie or game. The owner then gives the money to the people who watch it. The owner does not get to decide how the money is used, but they get a small piece of the money.
