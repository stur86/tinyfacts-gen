When you allow something, you give it the power to happen. You do not stop it from happening. You let it go. This is important because it helps you feel safe.

Think about your dog. If you keep him inside the house all day, he gets very sad. He wants to go out and run around. But if you allow him to go out, he feels better. He is happy. He can play with the other dogs and run around.

You can allow a friend to stay in your room when you are busy. You let them sit on the bed. This is a good thing because they need to rest. You are letting them do what they want.

Sometimes, you allow things to happen because they are right. If a police car stops a car, the driver might get a fine. The car driver did not do anything wrong. The police are just doing their job. They allowed the car to stop.

You can also allow a person to do something they want to do. If a girl wants to go to a party, you might allow her to go. You let her decide what to do. This is a good way to help her feel happy.

Sometimes, you allow something to happen that is not safe. If a person says they are going to run away, but they do not, it is not safe. The person might get hurt. If you allow a person to do something that is not right, you are letting them act without checking.

In a big group, you can allow each person to speak. This means you let every person have a turn. It keeps everyone happy because no one feels left out.
