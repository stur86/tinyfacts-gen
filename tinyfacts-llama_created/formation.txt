To form something means you put many small parts together to make a new thing. You start with a small piece of something. Then you add more pieces one after another. You keep adding parts until everything is in the right place.

Think of a tree. A small baby grows into a small tree. The small parts are the tiny green things from the ground. The baby grows from the green parts until it is a tall tree. Each part is part of the tree. The tiny green things give life, the green parts hold the tree up, the wood parts give the tree its hard part, and the hard parts give it a strong side.

In life, you can form many things. You form your friends. You form a job. You form your home. You form your family. These new things help you live in a world with other people.

You can also form words. You take one word and you change it. You add, you take away, you change the sound. You make new words. You can form words from other words. You can make new words from sounds that are not in your word.

When you form something, you are building from small parts. You are taking parts and making them work together. You are making new things.
