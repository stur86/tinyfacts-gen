When you want to do something, you usually do it. If you want to eat a food, you go to a place and you drink the food. If you want to play a game, you find a friend who likes the game and you do it together.

But sometimes you want to let someone else do it. If you want to go to a movie, you tell your mom or dad to take you. You do not do it yourself. This is what we call a word: a word that tells us what to do.

We use this word all the time. If you tell someone to do a job, they must do it. If you tell a store to give you money for a thing, they must give it to you. We use this word to tell people what to do.

It is also used when we want to show that we have done a job. If you do a job well, you might say, I am glad you are done. This shows that you are happy because you did it.

Sometimes, if you are afraid, you can say, I am afraid. This means you are afraid to do it. This shows that you are scared.
