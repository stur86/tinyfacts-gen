A group is a set of people who do the same things. You might have a group of friends. You all like the same kind of music. You all like to watch movies together.

A group can also be a group of things. Imagine a group of animals. The group is like a single animal. It is all the animals in one place.

A group can also be a group of people who do the same work. If you are a doctor, your group is the group of doctors in a hospital. Everyone in this group does the same job.
