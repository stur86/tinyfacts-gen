An animal is a living thing that moves around on land or in water. It has a body, often with hard parts that help it stay strong. Some animals have feet, while others use their bodies to walk or run. Many animals have hard parts that help them keep their bodies safe.

Animals need to eat. They look for food in the ground, in the water, or on the trees. They use their mouths to take in the food. Sometimes, they drink from a big green thing in the ground. Animals live in groups with other animals. They work together to stay safe.

Animals can be very different. There are big animals that are as tall as a house. There are small animals that are as small as a hand. Some animals are very fast, while others are very slow. Some animals can go very high into the air. Some animals can go very fast through the water.

Some animals can change color to hide from bad people. Some animals can make their bodies look very different to help them stay safe. Some animals can even change from day to night to find food.

Animals are important. They help us by making food and keeping the air clean. They also give us things to use like clothes and things to hold water. Many animals are very good at hiding and can help us learn about the world.
