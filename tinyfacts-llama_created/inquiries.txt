To ask a question is to ask someone about something. You want to know the right answer. You might want to know if something is true or if someone said something wrong.

When you ask a question, you are making a sound. You are using your voice. You use your words to ask the question. You can ask about many things. You can ask about a person, a thing, or a place. You can ask why, how, or what.

Sometimes you ask a question to learn. You want to know so you can understand better. You might ask your parents, your teacher, or your friend. You might ask a question about a problem you are having.

You can also ask a question to see if someone is telling the truth. You might ask if a word is right. If you are not sure, you can ask again. You are trying to find out what is true.

People use this word to talk about many different things. You might ask a question about the world around you. You might ask about the sky, the ground, or the water. You can ask about the sun, the stars, or the animals that live in the woods.

To ask a question is to be interested. It shows you care about the answer. It shows you are trying to learn. It is a way to show you are thinking about something.
