To clearest something means to make it clear. It is a word that you use when you want to tell a group of people that something is true or that the way is right.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are all running around and trying to catch a ball. One of your friends is very fast. You want to catch the ball, but you do not know where he is. You are looking at the ground and the other friends, but you do not see him.

You want to tell your friends that he is there. You want to clear him from your mind. So you start to shout and point at the ground. You are saying, the person is over there! You are making it clear that he is the one you are looking for.

You can also clear the room. This means you want to stop everyone from talking and everyone to be quiet. You might use a word or a sound that makes everyone stop. It is like a big order that says, everyone be still for a minute.

Sometimes, you might clear the air. When you are breathing, you are clearing the air in your body. You are taking out the bad things you are not using. It is like you are making space in your body so that you can breathe easier.

So, to clear something means you are making it clear or stopping everyone from talking so you can understand what is going on.
