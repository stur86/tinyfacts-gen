To be a good person means you act in ways that are kind, and help others. You do not take things that do not give you anything in return. You are not mean or angry, even if you are sad. You are glad when good things happen.

A person can have many different kinds of good. They can be good at making things. They can be good at helping a baby be strong. They can be good at making their home feel safe. They can be good at making friends. They can be good at making their family feel happy.

If a person is not good, they might do things that hurt other people. They might take money that is not their own. They might say bad words. They might do things that make others afraid. These things are not good at all.

Good people can also have bad days. They might feel sad, or they might feel angry. But they try to stay calm and find ways to make things better. They might talk to a friend, or they might find a way to do something nice for someone else. They always try to act in a way that is kind.
