To go somewhere is to move from one place to another. It means you are going far away.

Imagine you live in a small town. You play with your friends every day. One day you decide to see the world. You pack your bag, say good day to your mom and dad, and you go out. You walk to the bus stop. The bus is a big car with many people on it. You hold the hand of the person sitting next to you. The bus driver tells you where you are going. You might be going to a city far away, or a place in a far land. When you reach the place, you go to the place your family has told you about. You meet your new friends there. You do new things, like eat food from a store, buy clothes, and watch movies.

You can also go by the water. You sit on a big thing that goes over the water. The water moves under you, and you feel like you are flying. You might go to a place that is very cold, or a place with bright bright light during the day. Some people go by the water to find work, or to move their home to a new place.

Sometimes you go by car. You drive your own car, with the person sitting in the back, or with the person sitting in the front. You follow a road that leads to your place. You might go through towns with many people, or through big cities with many tall houses. You might go through fields with many green things, or through dark dark woods.

When you go, you leave your old life behind. You might have to leave your friends, your house, or your school. You might have to learn new things. But you also get to see new things. You might see new animals, new cities, or new ways of doing things. You might find new places that are very pretty.

To go is to change where you are. It is a big change from your old life to your new life.
