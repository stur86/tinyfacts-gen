When you say a thing is busy, you mean it is doing many things at once. It is working very fast. One person might be cleaning the house while another person is playing a game. They are both doing different jobs.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about how they feel. If you are busy, you are very busy. You do many things and you do them quickly. You might feel happy because you are doing so many things at once.

Also, if you have a lot of things to do, you say you are busy. It is like you are in a rush to do everything at once. You do not have time to think about each thing. You just go from one thing to another. When you are busy, you might feel a bit angry because you feel like you have too much to do.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If a person is busy, they are going around and meeting other people. They might be talking to many different people. They are not alone. They are with many other people in the same time.

In some places, people call it a busy night if it is very late at night and there are many people out. It means there are many people doing things at the same time.
