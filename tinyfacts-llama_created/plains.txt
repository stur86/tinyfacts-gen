The word is about a big area of land. It is like a very wide open space. Most of the world is made of this kind of ground.

Imagine a long, long line that goes across the world. Between this line and the ground that goes up, there is no ground at all. This is the area we are talking about. It is very big and very wide. Sometimes it can be as wide as two big bodies of water put together!

There are many kinds of these areas. Some are very high. Some are very low. Some are made of tall green things all over the top. Some are only black and brown, and they look like nothing at all.

People move across these areas. Some go by car or by train. Some walk. Some fly. Some go on big moving things that can carry many people at the same time.

These areas are also where many animals live. Some of them are very big. Some are very small. Some can fly. Some can walk or run. Some can even live in the water and walk on the ground at the same time!

In some places, these areas are very hot. In other places, they are very cold. At night, the ground can be very still. In the day, it can be very warm. The ground can also be very wet or very dry, and that is why the areas are so different.

You can find these areas all over the world. They cover most of the land under our feet. Even though they are very big, they are still made of one piece of ground that goes on in every direction.
