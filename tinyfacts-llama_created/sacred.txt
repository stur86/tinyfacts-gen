Think about the word family. You are a part of your family. You live with them. You eat their food. You play with them. You do what they tell you to do. When we say something is a family, we mean it is a group of people who care about each other.

The word also means something that is kept in a place for a long time. A book is a thing you read for a long time. It has words in it that you do not understand at first. Later, you learn how to understand them. The book is a safe thing for your mind.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about how we feel. If you are very scared, you might say you are scared. If you are very happy, you might say you are happy. This word tells us how we are feeling at that moment.

You can also use it to talk about something that is very important to someone. If a person is very sad, you might say they are sad because of something. When we say something is a dream, we mean it is something that happens in your head when you are not there. It is not something you can touch or see.
