To be kind is to act with a heart that is good and not mean. When you are kind, you help others. You do not say bad things. You do not laugh at them when they are sad. You listen to them and you try to understand how they feel. You are a friend who cares about them.

Kind people are not afraid to be different. They do not try to change who they are to be like everyone else. They are happy to be themselves. They are not afraid to show their love. They love their family and their friends.

You can also be kind when you help someone who is not happy. You might give them a drink or a bed to sleep in. You might tell them that everything will be okay. You are showing them that you care about them. This makes their heart feel better and they feel less alone.

Being kind is easy when you do small things for other people. You smile at a friend. You say hello to a stranger. You hold the door for someone. These small acts make a big change in the world.

We all need to be kind sometimes. We need to be kind when we are angry or sad. We need to be kind when we are afraid. Being kind helps us stay calm and makes us feel better.
