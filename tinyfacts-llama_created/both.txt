The word both can mean two different things.

First, when we talk about two people, we say they are both friends. If you are friends with a girl, you are also friends with the boy. You do not have to be friends only with one of them. You can be friends with both.

This means you can be happy for both of them. You can say, like, I am happy for you both because you are friends. This shows you care about both of them.

Sometimes, when we talk about two things that are at the same time, we say they are both. If you are in school, you are both studying and doing your work. You are doing both at the same time.

Another way to use this word is when you say something is both big and small at the same time. A house can be both big and small. It is not one thing or the other. It is something that is both big and small at the same time.

So, the word both means that something happens for two people or two things at the same time.
