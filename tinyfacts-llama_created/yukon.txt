Sometimes, people use this word with a cold. If they feel very bad, they might say, yeah. They are feeling better because they tell themselves they are okay.

Another way to use this word is with a word that means a long, hard road. If you drive very far, you will say, yeah. You are very tired, but you are getting to your place.
