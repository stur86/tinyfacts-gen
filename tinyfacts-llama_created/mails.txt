To send something through the air is to send it. You can do this with a small box. You write what you want to send on a piece of paper. Then you put the paper into the box. The box goes to a place where it can go to many other places. When the box reaches the other places, it drops the paper down to the ground. People at those places pick it up and read what is inside.

You can also send something through the water. You put the paper into a box. The box goes to a place where the water is cold and the water goes around the world many times. When the water goes back to the place you picked it up from, the box drops the paper down to your hands.

Sometimes, people send words or pictures through the air. They use a small box that sends the words or pictures. You can hear them on your phone. They can go to many places at once, and people can listen to them at any time.

You can also send something through the air with a small thing that flies. This flying thing goes very fast and can go very high. It carries small papers or small pieces of paper and drops them down to your hands. People use these things when they want to send something quickly, like money to someone who lives far away.

Many people send letters. A letter is a box with many pieces of paper inside. The person writing the letter puts their name and their home on the first piece of paper. They write to the person they want to write to. They tell them what they think. They can write many lines. When they are done, they put the box into a box that goes to other places. The box goes to many homes and drops the box at different homes. The person at the home reads the letter and responds.

In some places, you can send a small thing that flies. You give the person you want to send the thing to a box. The box goes to the person you want to send it to. The person you want to send it to can pick it up and send it back. This way, you can send many things at once and it is very fast.
