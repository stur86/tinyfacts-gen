After means you do a thing before another thing happens. You do something first, then the next thing happens.

Think about a story. If you are a boy, you might do a thing. After you do it, a girl might do something else. She might smile or laugh. This is after you.

You can also say after a time. If you go to bed at tonight, tomorrow you will go to sleep. You are not going to sleep until the next night.

Sometimes you do a thing after you are tired. You might watch a movie. After you watch the movie, you feel happy. You were tired before, but after you watch the movie, you feel better.

You can also use after to talk about the past. If you have done something, you might say, I did that after I was finished. This shows that you did the thing before you finished other things.
