To appear is to show up or be seen by people. It is a word that tells us what is happening in the world.

When you appear, you are not hidden. You are there. You are a part of what is happening. You might be standing in a line with other people. You might be sitting in a room. You might be doing a job.

Sometimes, we do not see something at first. Then, it appears in front of us. This can happen in a small way. If you look at a book and then you see a picture, you have appeared. Or, if you are waiting for something and then a person comes, you have appeared.

When you appear, you show that you are there. You are not hidden. You are a part of what is happening in the world.

Also, we can appear in our lives. We might appear in a movie. We appear in a story. We appear in a book. We appear in a picture. We appear in a game. When we appear, we show that we are here.

The word also means that something has happened. If you appear in a story, you have happened. If you appear in a game, you are doing something. If you appear in a picture, you are a part of a picture. When something appears, it is true. It happened.

So, to appear is to show up. It is a way to show that you are there.
