A tire is the round part at the back of a car. It sits over the ground so the car does not hit the ground too hard. The tire makes the car go very fast.

Inside the tire is a soft stuff. This stuff is very strong and helps the car move. The stuff is made from many small pieces of hard stuff. When you put many of these pieces together, they form a strong wall that keeps the car moving.

The outside of the tire is made of hard stuff too. This hard stuff is very strong and helps keep the tire from getting too small. It also helps the tire stay on the ground so the car does not roll over.

When you drive on a hard road, the outside of the tire makes a sound. This sound is like a big, soft noise. The sound comes from the way the air inside the tire pushes against the hard stuff. This sound is very important because it helps people hear you.

The inside of the tire has a line on it. This line shows how much air is in the tire. If the air is too low, the car might not start. If the air is too high, the car might go too fast. The driver can use the line to fix the problem.

When you put air into the tire, the air makes the stuff inside the tire move. This makes the tire go round. If you take too much air out, the stuff does not go round as fast, and the car might go too slow. If you put air into the right place, the stuff goes round at the right time, and the car goes very fast.

Tires are very important. If the car does not have a tire, it can fall down and break into pieces. The soft stuff inside the tire helps keep the car moving and the hard stuff on the outside keeps the tire from getting too small.
