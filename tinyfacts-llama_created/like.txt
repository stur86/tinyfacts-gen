To like something is to want it. You might like a food, a game, a person, or a thing. When you like something, you feel happy. Your heart feels warm. You might smile when you think about it.

If you are not sure, you can use the word like to show how you feel. You might say, I like you because you are nice. You feel happy when we talk.

Sometimes, you like the way a thing looks. You like a bright blue sky, a green tree, or a soft white bed. You feel calm when you see these things.

You can also like a place. You like the city where you live. You like the park where you go in the morning. You like the smell of the air when it is warm.

To like a person is a different thing. You might like a friend because they are fun to talk to. You might like a teacher because they help you learn. You like being around them.

Being liked by someone is a nice feeling. It is when people want you and want you to be their friend. It is a way to feel happy and safe.
