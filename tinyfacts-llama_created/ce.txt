To cell or to cover something means to keep it inside a hard place. Imagine you have a cup. When you drink water, the water goes out of the cup. But if you cover the cup, the water stays inside. You are holding something until you want to be done with it.

You can also use this word for a place. If you want to keep something away from people, you can cover it. You can cover a door to keep people out or cover a box so that only one person can open it.

Sometimes, to cover something is to hide a bad thing. If you are afraid of a dog, you might not go near it. You cover yourself so you do not get hurt. This is how we keep our bodies safe from things that might make us sick.

In the world of the stars, a big group of stars is called a group. This group of stars is like a big cover for many small stars. The small stars are like tiny balls that are too small to see. The group of stars is much bigger than all the tiny balls together, but it is still one big group.
