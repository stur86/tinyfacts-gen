Directions are ways to show you where to go. When you want to move to a place, you need to follow the right directions. You might use a piece of paper with numbers on it. You look at the numbers and follow them as you walk. You follow a road to a city. You follow a road to a park. You follow a road to a house.

You can also use words to show you where to go. You might say, I want to go to the store. I follow the words. I walk down a street. I cross a street. I walk down another street. I finally reach the store.

When you give directions to someone, you tell them what to do. You say, Go to the left. Then they follow you. You give directions to a group of people. You want them to go to a place. You give them a listened to order. You say, Follow the road. Go to the next stop. Go to the next stop. Keep going until you reach your place.

You can also give directions to a group of people who need to do something. You tell them what to do. You say, Go to the left. Go to the building. Go to the room. They follow your words and go to the right place.

Directions help us know where things are. We use them every day. We give directions to a friend who is lost. We give directions to a group of people who are going to a party. We give directions to a person who is moving to a new home.
