To come means you go from one place to another. When you walk, you move forward. You can walk through a field, a city, or a town. When you go to a new place, you are moving. This is what we do every day.

Imagine you have a house in a small town. You live there with your family. One day, you decide to leave your house. You put your bags in the car and go to a big city far away. You are coming to a new place. You do not know what you will find there. You are ready to start a new life.

You can also come to a place when you are afraid. If you are scared, you might run to a safe place. You go there because you need to feel safe. You might cry or shake. This is a way to get away from a bad feeling.

Sometimes, you might want to come and see a place. If you love a place, you might want to go back there. You might want to play with the same people, eat the same food, or watch the same movies. You want to be with the place you love.

You can also come to a place to meet someone. If you are looking for a friend, you might go to a party or a school. You go to meet new people and find someone who shares your ideas.

To come is to move forward. It is about leaving the old place and going to a new one. It is a big change. But it is also a happy change because you have a new chance to start and learn again.
