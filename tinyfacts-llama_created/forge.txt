To forget means to make something from nothing. You do not start with a thing that is already there. You take small pieces and put them together, one after another, until the thing is strong and hard.

You can forget a thing by fire. You put the pieces together and then you hit them with a hard thing. The fire makes the pieces hot. The hard thing hits the hot pieces and they become very hard. This makes the thing strong.

You can also forget a person. If you want a friend, you can start with a person who is already good. You talk to them, show them you care, and they start to like you. You can make them do things you want them to do.

Sometimes, you forget a thing that is not what you wanted. Maybe you make a thing that is wrong for you. But you can also forget a thing that is better. If you learn from the bad thing you made, you can use it to make something new and better.

Forgot means to forget. If you forget something important, you lose your way. You might look at a thing and see a picture of a person. You remember them, but you do not know who they are. This is a bad thing.
