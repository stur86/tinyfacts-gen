To rest means to stop moving or doing work for a while. You might need to rest if you run a long way or if you are very tired. This helps your body and brain feel better.

Imagine you are playing a game on a computer. You keep running and jumping for hours. After that, you feel very tired and your body hurts. You might want to sit down and close your eyes. This is rest. You are stopping your work and letting your body and brain rest.

You can also rest in a quiet room. When you are in this quiet room, you do not talk to anyone or look at a computer. You just sit and breathe. Your body and mind feel calm and safe. This rest is good for you.

Sometimes, we rest in a bed. When we sleep, our body and brain get a good rest. We do not think about things that worry us. This rest helps us feel strong and ready for the next day.

Rest is important for everyone. If you do not rest, you can get sick. You can feel bad for days. You might not be able to work or play. So, it is good to stop moving and let your body and brain rest.
