An idea or a thing you do because you have to is an order. This means you are not doing anything, but you still do it.

Think of a job. You do not really want to work, but you have to. The work is a part of your life. You do it because someone paid you, and this is an order. If you do not do it, you will not get paid. Your life is about this job.

Sometimes, you might feel a bad feeling if you do not do something. You might feel like you have to do it even if you do not want to. This is because you feel you have to follow the order.

You can also have an order because you are a part of a group. You might be a student in a school. You have to follow the school orders. You do not want to go to school, but you must go because you are a part of the group.

In life, you might have an order to be kind. You might feel a strong feeling to help others. This is not because you want to, but because you have to. You do not want to hurt anyone, and you feel a strong pull to do this.

An order can be a person, a thing, or even a feeling. It is always about following a set of things.
