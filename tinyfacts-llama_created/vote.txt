To talk about a group of people is to talk about how they decide what to do. A group of people makes a set of things that everyone must follow. This is often done through a set of numbers. Each person has one number.

When people get these numbers, they go to a big room. In this room, they have a chance to say what they want. They can write down their number and their idea on a piece of paper. Many people write many numbers. After they write, they go to another room.

In this room, they sit next to each other. They listen to what each other is saying. Some people may not like the idea at all. They may say it is bad. If many people want the same thing, the group decides on that idea.

The group then writes down who they want to follow. If there is a tie, the group might have to go back to the start and try again. They do this many times until everyone is sure.

Everyone gets a set of numbers. They have to follow those numbers. If you follow the numbers, you are a part of the group. If you do not follow the numbers, you are not part of the group.

Sometimes, people do not like the group. They might say the group is bad. This can cause trouble. The group might have to go away and come back with a new group.
