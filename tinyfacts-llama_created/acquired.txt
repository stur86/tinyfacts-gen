To get something through your mind or body is to learn it. You may not have known it before, but as you learn, you start to feel it inside you.

When you are a child, you get things very quickly. You know your name, your family, and what you like. As you grow up, you do not always get things so easily. You may have to try hard and ask many questions to find out what is going on.

Sometimes, you get things from the people around you. A teacher may teachers you how to write. A friend may show you how to do a game. You may listen to a story and then remember it later.

Sometimes, you get things from your own brain. You may remember a word from a story. You may be able to do a thing that you have never done before. These things happen when you learn.

To get something is to take it into your body. You might get a piece of paper from a box. You can feel the paper under your fingers. You can see the words on the paper. The paper is inside you now.

You can also get something through your mind. You might learn a new way to walk. You might hear a song and remember it later. You can get ideas from books, movies, or talking to other people.

In this way, getting is about how you get things inside your head or body.
