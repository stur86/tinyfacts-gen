To pair something means to put two things together so they are near each other. You can pair a dog with a cat. This can be for fun or for work. If you have two books, you might pair them so the one with the cover on the left is next to the one with the cover on the right.

In school, you might pair students who want to learn something together. One student might be good at numbers, and the other student might be good at stories. This helps everyone learn and understand better.

You can also pair things with other things. If you have a big box and a small box, you can put them in the same bag. This is like pairing two things that are very different. You might pair a book with a chair. The book is the thing, and the chair is the other thing.

Sometimes, you pair people. If you have a friend who likes to run, you might pair them with someone who likes to play a game. This way, you can both be happy.
