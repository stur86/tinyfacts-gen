To have a friend or a good person is to be glad you are near them. This happens when you are happy to see them. You might want to talk to them or laugh together.

A person can be a good friend. This is when they always listen and help. They do not say bad things. They are kind and make you feel safe.

You can also have a friend in your life. This is when you are glad when you see them. You might see them at school, at work, or at a game. You can tell them you are glad they are there.

A person who is a friend of a friend is also glad. They are glad that their friend knows someone they like. They are glad that their friend is in the world.
