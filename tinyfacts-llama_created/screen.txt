To put a picture on a box is to make it appear as if it is in front of you. This box is often in a room with other boxes. People use these boxes to watch movies or to show pictures of animals. The person who makes the box sits behind the box and uses a small piece of paper to send the pictures to the box. The person who sits behind the box looks at the pictures and turns them around so you can see them.

A long time ago, people did not have these boxes. They would draw pictures on a wall or a piece of paper and put them in a box to keep them safe. Later, they made these boxes so you can watch pictures from far away. Today, we have many of these boxes all around the world.
