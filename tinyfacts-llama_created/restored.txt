To be rest is to be safe. When something is broken, it is not safe anymore. You must fix it before it is safe again. If you fix a car, you are making it safe to drive. If you fix a picture, you are making it safe to look at again.

When you rest, you are not really resting. You are just waiting for your body to fix the hurt. You might lie down, close your eyes, or go to sleep. You are not doing anything new. You are just waiting for the time to be safe again.
