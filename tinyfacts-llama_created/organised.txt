To be ordered is to be in a right way. You might have a group of people who all work together. There is a person who is in the lead. Everyone else follows what they say. This is an ordered group.

You can also order a room. The person who is in the lead says, you must clean your chair and your table. You do this. After you finish, you can sit or stand. Everyone follows the same way.

When you order a food, you say, I want a cup of coffee and a small piece of food. The person who is in the lead gets the food first. Then the other people get what they want.

Sometimes, we order things that are not made by people. We might order a car. We tell the car place what kind of car we want. They build it for us. When we are ordered, we must follow what we were given.
