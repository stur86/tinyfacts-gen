To roll something means you move it around on the ground or in a place. You use your hands to push the thing. It can be a ball, a piece of paper, or a small animal. When you roll something, it goes in a straight line and then comes back to the start.

You can roll a ball in the ground to play a game. The person who gets the ball the farthest around the ring is the one who is best. You can also roll a small animal like a cat or a dog. This is fun to watch and it helps us understand how the world works.

Rolling is a way to move something from one place to another. It can be a bit hard to control, but it is also fun.
