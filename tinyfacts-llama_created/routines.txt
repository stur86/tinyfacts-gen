To do something in a way that is very easy and often done every day, and to do it at the same time every time, is to follow a set of steps. This can be for a person, a dog, or a car.

You might have a morning set of steps. You wake up, eat a small thing, and then you go out to play. You do this every day, even on a very busy day. You know what you do before you start, so it does not worry you.

Your family has a set of steps too. They go to the kitchen, make breakfast, and then they go out to buy food. They do this every morning, and it is always the same thing. They are doing the same work, but in the same order, every day.

Doing something in this way can be very good. It keeps you safe, so you do not have to worry about something bad happening. It also helps you feel very calm. When you follow a set of steps every day, you know what is coming next, and you can feel ready.

Sometimes, this can happen with people. In a school, some people do the same thing every morning. They sit in the same seats, eat the same food, and get ready to go to class. This helps them feel safe and ready for the day.

It can also happen with animals. A dog might follow a set of steps to get to the door. It knows the door is the place where it gets food. It follows this set of steps every time, so it does not have to think about it. It is just doing what it is supposed to do.

In short, following a set of steps is a way to do something very easily and at the same time every time. It can help keep you safe, make things calm, and help you feel ready for what comes next.
