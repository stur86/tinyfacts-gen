To fly is to go very high up in the sky. You are in a big moving thing that can carry you from one place to another.

The world is a big round thing, and we can fly over it. But if the wind is strong, it can pull you down. You must be very strong and have a good feeling to stay up in the air.

You can fly over big cities, over green fields, or over the wide blue water. Some people fly to far away places to visit. They carry many people and things. You can also fly over your own city to get to a different part of town.

Flying is a good way to go. It can take you to where you want to go quickly. You can see many things from the sky: trees, houses, fields, and water. It is a fun way to feel free.

Some things fly very fast, like small animals or things that go very high. Other things fly much slower, like cars that go along the ground. You can also fly a small thing that is like a box in the sky. This is a fun thing to watch.

Flying can be hard. You must be ready for things to happen. If you get too close to the ground, the wind can pull you down. If you lose control, you can fall very fast. But if you are very strong and follow the right way, you can stay up in the air for a long time.

Flying is a way to go where you do not have to walk or run. It is a way to go very high and see many things at once.
