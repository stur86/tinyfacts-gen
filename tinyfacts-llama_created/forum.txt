A place where people talk to each other about many things is called a group. There is a big one in many cities. It is a place where people from different places go to share their ideas and talk about how they live.

If you want to talk about a book, a movie, or a game, you can go to this place. People here ask questions. They want to know how you felt while you read the book. You can tell them. They listen and write down what you say. Then other people can read what you wrote and learn from it.

A group can be about very different things. You can talk about your work in a job. You can talk about your favorite food. You can talk about your friends. You can talk about how you feel about a movie. You can talk about a game you played. Or you can talk about the world as a whole. You can talk about how we can help each other.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about things that are big. They talk about things that will happen in the near time. They talk about how we should live. They talk about how we should act. They talk about how we should help each other.

You can also use this word to talk about how you feel. If you are sad, you can go to a place where people listen. You can tell them how you feel. They might feel the same way or they might feel better after talking with you.
