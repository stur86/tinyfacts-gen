A day is when the sun is up for a very long time. People usually do their school work or play games on this day.

In the morning, the sun comes up and stays in the sky until it is time to go to bed. Many people like to look at the sun when it is up, and they write down the day in their books. Sometimes, people also have a big dinner on this day.

After school, some people go to a big building with many rooms. In these rooms, people work together on big jobs. Some rooms are for making things and others are for writing papers. Everyone works together to make the world a better place.

On this day, some people also go to a place where they can hear music. They listen to songs with many notes and use their hands to move them. This is a fun way to feel happy and calm.

At night, people often watch movies or watch games with friends. They laugh and shout at the people on the phone. Sometimes, a group of people from different parts of the world come together to watch a movie or play a game.

This day is also a time for people to talk about what happened during the day. They say how they did things and what they saw. This is a good way to share stories and learn from each other.

In some places, people take a short walk on this day. They go to a park or a green space to feel the air and see the trees. This is a good way to get some air and feel calm.

Finally, people often go home and rest their bodies. They might eat a good dinner and watch a movie before going to bed. On this day, everyone is happy to be together and feel safe.
