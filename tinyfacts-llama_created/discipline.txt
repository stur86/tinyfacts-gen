To be in a good place means you act well. When you are in a good place, you do not fight with others. You follow the way. You are not afraid to wait. You know when to say yes and when to say no. You keep your words and your body clean.

Being in a good place is like being a team. When everyone is in the same place, they all work well together. They understand what they need to do. They do it all at once. The person who keeps things clean is the first one. They pick up the things that go out. The person who helps is next. They help the person who keeps things clean. This way, everyone is safe and happy.

Some people do not like to be in a good place. They want to fight. They like to use their words to hurt others. They think they are the only ones who should act. But being in a good place is not about being the one who acts. It is about being good. It is about following the way and being kind to everyone.

You can also be in a good place when you are not doing anything. You are sitting quietly in your room. You are not afraid to say what you think. You are not afraid to look at people who look different. You are not afraid to be different. You are happy to be you.

Being in a good place is important for everyone. It makes our world a better place. It makes us feel safe and happy.
