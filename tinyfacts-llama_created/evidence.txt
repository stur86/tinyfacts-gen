To show something is true is to give it as a fact. When you say something is true, you are showing it happened. You show it with words or things.

Imagine you see a boy who is not in his room. You see his feet on the floor. You see his clothes hanging on a line. You see a piece of paper on the floor. You can see that something is not right. You have found something that shows the boy is not in his room.

You show this with your eyes. You are telling the story with what you see. It is an easy way to show something is true.

Sometimes, people show things they know are not true. They say something is true, but they are not sure. They might not show it with their words. They might just say it because it sounds good.

People also show things they know are true by doing them. If you show that a book is about a story, you are showing it with your hands. You read the book to show that the story is real.

In a school, you might show a picture to show how a person feels. You might show a picture of a person who is sad. You are telling the story with what you see. You are showing how the person feels with your words.

In this way, showing something is true is about showing it with your hands and your words.
