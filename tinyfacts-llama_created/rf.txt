A small round thing is a word you use to talk about things that send power from one place to another. When you use this word, you are talking about how things work.

Think of a power line that goes from a big building to a small house. The power line is like a long road with many small round things on it. The round things carry power from the big building to the small house. When a person turns the power on at the small house, the power goes to every room. The round things help the power go through the long line.

You can also use this word for other things that send power. There are power lines in cities, power lines in the middle of the ground, and power lines in the sky. They all carry power from one place to another.

When you talk about something, you often ask if it is a round thing. If you say a car is round, you mean it is a round thing. If you say a table is round, you mean it is a round thing that sits on the floor.
