To be close to something means to be near it. If you are close to a person, you are near them. You can see them or hear them. If you are close to a thing, you are near it. You can touch it or see how it looks.

Think of a small dog. If you reach the dog, you are close to it. You can hold the dog and feel its soft further. If you are close to a big city, you might hear the sounds of many cars and people. You might see people going in different directions.

To be close to someone can also mean you feel happy when they are around. If a friend is smiling, you might feel happy too. If a teacher is talking, you might be interested in what they are saying. When you are close to someone, you feel a strong feeling.

We can also say you are close to a place. If you live in a city, you are close to the city. If you are on a long road, you might feel you are going to the city. Being close to a place means you can go there easily.

Being close to something can make things easier. If you are close to a door, you can open it quickly. If you are close to a book, you can read it fast. When things are close, we feel more safe.
