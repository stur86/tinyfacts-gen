To be an end is to be the other side of something. It means that two things are so different that they are not even the same. You can be the end of a line and the line is the beginning. You can be the end of a story and the story is the beginning.

Imagine a line of people going to a big party. One person is the first to the party, then there are two, three, and four people on the line. But if you go the wrong way, you are not the end. You are the first person. So, being the end is about not being the beginning.

Sometimes, to be an end means to be very strong or to have a lot of power. A strong wall can be an end to a road. A very big tree can be an end to a field. In a game, the end of a game is the moment when all the people have done what they are supposed to do.

It also means to be at the very far away end of a long thing. If you drive a car down a long road, you can reach the end of the road. But if you drive to the other end of the world, you are at the end of your own world. You are the end.

Finally, to be an end can mean to be the end of a story. If you finish a book, you have reached the end. If you finish a game, you have won't have to play more. If you finish a hard work, you have done it.

So, being the end is about being very different, or being at the very far end of something, or to be the end of a story, or to be the end of a line of people, or to be the end of a long thing.
