Games are things you do to have fun. You might play with your friends or family. You might use a box, a ball, a book, or a computer. You try to be the best. You do things that are hard or easy.

Some games are for one person at a time. They are for fun. You might play a game of numbers or a game with words. You try to get the right number or the right word.

Other games are for many people at the same time. People stand in lines. One person is the first to do the things. The person who does the things first goes first. Then the next person does. This goes on until everyone has done everything.

Games can be about moving. You might run. You might walk. You might jump. You might climb a tall thing. You might climb down and go back inside the house. You might climb up a high wall.

Games can be about numbers. You might play with a box that has numbers on it. You try to get the right numbers. You might use a ball with numbers on it. You try to catch the ball.

Games can also be about stories. You might listen to a story. Then you tell a story. You use your voice and your body. You act out what the story is about.

Games can help you feel better. They are a way to be happy. You might play to laugh. You might play to feel a little bit scared. But the most important thing is that you have fun.
