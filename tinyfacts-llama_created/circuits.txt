A thing that makes things work is called a computer. This thing is very good at doing many different jobs. It can do a lot of numbers, remember many words, and show pictures.

Inside this computer, there are many tiny lines. These lines carry power from one part of the computer to another. The power helps the computer do its work.

One of these lines is very long. It goes through many boxes and small rooms inside the computer. This line is like a big road. It carries power from one place to another. The power is sent to the computer, and then it goes to the other computer parts.

Inside one of these computers, there are many tiny parts that work together. Each part does one job. When many parts work together, they can do many different jobs.

The long line from the first computer to the second computer is like a long road that many cars drive on. The computers talk to each other, sending power and words, and using the road to get the job done.

When people write a thing to this computer, they send the words and numbers that they need. The computer reads the words and numbers and does the work. The computer does not think, it just follows the words and numbers.

All of these tiny parts and long lines work together. They are like a team of people who all work on the same job. This team makes many different computers and helps people do their work every day.
