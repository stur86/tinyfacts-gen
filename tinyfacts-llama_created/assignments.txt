To do an answer

You have a job to do. This is called an answer. You have to find out the right answer to a question. This can be a big word, a hard one, or a small one. You read about it, you look at books, you ask your teacher. Then you write down what you think. You have to write it down so that everyone can see it.

Sometimes you do this work with other people. You all sit down and talk. You write what you think. This is how you share your work with others. It is a way to show that you are ready for the job.

You can also do this work by yourself. You read a book. You look at the words. You try to understand what the writer means. You write down what you think. This is how you learn new things.

When you do an answer, you are showing that you are ready to do the work. You are telling the teacher that you can do it. You are showing that you are a person who can do things.
