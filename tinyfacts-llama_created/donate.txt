To give something is to send it to someone. You can give money, food, clothes, or other things to help people. When you give something, you are making the other person happy. This can happen when a person is sick and you give them food to eat. You can also give money to a person who does not have enough to buy food or a house.

You can also give your time. If you work hard for many hours and do not get paid, you gave your time to help others. You gave people the power to live their lives.

If a person is very sick, the family and friends give them money so the person can get better. In this way, people help each other and make the world a better place.
