To make a sound is to put something into a way that the air can carry it. You can do this by hitting a hard thing or by moving your mouth.

You can make a sound with your voice. You can laugh, cry, or shout. When you laugh, the air inside your body goes up. The sound is like a loud push. When you are very happy, your voice goes very fast and very loud.

A long time ago, people did not have computers. They did not have a way to keep a listened sound. So they had to make a new way to keep track of things. They put a piece of wood or a small piece of hard stuff into a box. This box has a tiny round thing inside. The round thing catches the sound. When you press the round thing, it makes a sound on the outside. You can hear this sound later.

Today, people use these boxes to keep many sounds. You can put a piece of music, a movie, or a talk on a box. When you turn on the box, the sound comes out. You can listen to it as many times as you want. You can even carry the box with you. If you lose the box, you have to go to a store and buy a new one.
