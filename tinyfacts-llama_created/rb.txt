To run is to move quickly on the ground. When you run, you can go far in a short time. You might feel a bit of wind in your face. Sometimes, running is fun. You might go to a park and run around the big green area. You can also run with a group of friends. This is often called a game.

When you are running, you use your legs. You push them down on the ground. This makes your feet hit the ground many times. This makes you keep moving. Your legs are the parts you use to run.

You can also run on a hard floor. This is called a track. The floor is very hard and long. It is often used for running races. A person might run around a track many times to get ready for a big race.

Running is good for your body. It helps your heart work well. It also helps your feet get strong. This is why you should run often, so your body stays strong.
