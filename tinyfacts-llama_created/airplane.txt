An air way is a way to move through the sky. It is a big piece of wood with many strong arms that go under it. The air way goes very high into the sky and then falls down again, so the ground is always in front of you.

You can fly on an air way with people or with animals. Some air ways are very big and have many rooms inside. Others are small and only have one room. Many air ways have windows so you can see outside.

The air way goes very fast. You can go very far in just a few minutes. It is like a long car that goes in the air.

Some air ways go very high. They can go as high as the top of a very tall building. Others go much, much higher. They go into the sky where there are no tall things at all.

Air ways can carry many people and things. People use them to go to school, work, or to visit other places. Some air ways carry animals to far away places where they live. Others carry food and other things that people need.

Sometimes air ways are used for fun. People fly on them and feel the wind under their seats. It is like being in a big car, but the car is flying through the sky.

You can also use an air way to get away from problems. If you are afraid of a big city, you can fly away to a place far away. You can leave the noise and the people so you can feel calm and safe.
