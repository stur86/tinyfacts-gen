To grown something means you start with a small thing and make it bigger. 

Imagine you have a small ball. You hold it for a long time. Then, you pick it up and put it in a box. The box keeps it safe, and the ball becomes a ball again. This is a part of growing.

You can also use this word for animals. A baby animal comes out of a mother. It is very small. As it grows, it gets bigger and stronger. The baby becomes a new animal, like a dog or a cat. This is how all living things grow.

Sometimes, when you are feeling happy, you might say you are gazing. It means you are looking at a beautiful thing for a long time. You might stare at a pretty picture or a picture of a place you love. This is a way to be happy and calm.
