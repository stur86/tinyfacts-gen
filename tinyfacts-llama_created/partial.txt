A word is an idea that helps us understand other ideas. It is what you do when you think about something.

Think of a room. You see the floor, the walls, and the table. You know where everything is. But if you are in the middle of the room, you do not see the floor or the table. You only see the part of the room in front of you.

This is like a part. A part is a small piece of something big. You can not see the whole thing with one eye. But with one eye, you can see a small part of it. You know it is there.

If you have a word for a small piece of something, you use that word. If you have a word for a small part of a story, you use that word too. If you have a word for a small piece of a picture, you use that word too. All these words help us understand what is going on.

Sometimes, a word can mean more than one thing. If you say you are in a good mood, you mean you are happy. But you can also say you are in a bad mood. This is because your body is showing you are not happy.

People also use the word to talk about the way of life. If something is a part of a life, it is part of the life. If something is not a part of a life, it is something else.
