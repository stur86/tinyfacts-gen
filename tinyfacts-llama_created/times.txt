A time is when things happen. You can think of it as a big group of hours and minutes.

A short time is a few minutes. A long time is a very many minutes. One big group of minutes is an hour. An hour is one half of a day. A half day is half of a week. A week is seven short times. You can also think of it as a number of days.

Time is important because we use it to plan our days. We use it to decide what to do. If you know what time you have to do something, you can go and do it. If you are late, you will be in trouble. If you are early, you will feel happy.

Time is also important for the world. The sun moves through the sky. It moves very fast. When it moves, the day changes. At night, the sky is dark. In the morning, the sky is bright. This is because the sun has moved far enough across the sky so that the part of the sky that was dark before is now bright.

Time can also change our lives. When you are young, you have many hours to learn and grow. As you get older, the hours go by very fast. You learn more, you make friends, and you grow into a person. Time is what makes life happen.
