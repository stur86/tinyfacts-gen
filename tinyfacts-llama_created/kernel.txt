To start something is to begin it. You start a story, a game, a walk, or any other thing that will happen next. Before you start, there is nothing. Nothing is the beginning of everything.

Think about a tiny thing that grows from a green thing. The green thing is the beginning of the thing. It starts to grow. Over many years, the green thing grows into a tall green thing, then into a small green thing, and then into the tall green thing again.

A small part inside a green thing is the beginning. This part is what grows from the green thing. It is the small green thing that starts to grow. The rest of the green thing is the green thing that comes after.

A tiny part of a ball can start a whole game. If this part falls, the game is over. But if it stays up and the rest of the ball falls, the game can go on. The tiny part is the beginning.

You can also start a story with a single word. This word starts the story. After the word, the story goes on, and the story ends with a finally word.

A thing that starts something can have many parts. There is the part that begins the story. There is the part that starts the game. There is the part that starts the walk. Each of these parts is the beginning of the thing we are talking about.

So, a beginning is the start of something. It is the first step.
