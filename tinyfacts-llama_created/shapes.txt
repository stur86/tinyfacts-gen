To see something is to look at it. A thing can be many things at once. A wall can be a floor and a top and a side all at once. You can see the floor first, then the top, then the side, and finally the other side. It is all put together.

Things that are round have many sides. They look like they are made of one piece. You can see a round thing in your hand. You can look at the inside of your eye. Inside your eye there are round things. They hold light and let it go so you can see.

Things that are not round have many straight sides. They look like they are made of many lines. You can see one side after another. You can look at the front of a car and see the lines that go around the front. You can look at the back of a book and see the lines that go around the back. These lines help the car stay on the ground or the book stay closed.

Things that have many sides are called many things. You can have many round things. You can have many long, straight things. You can have many things in between. The more sides a thing has, the more things you can see.
