Waves are big moving things in the water that go across the water. You can see them when you look at the water from the side of a high place.

When the sun is up, the water in the big blue thing in the sky gets warm. This makes the water want to move. It goes up and then goes down. It keeps doing this many times each minute. Because of this, the water makes a line of moving things that we see.

The line of moving things is what we call a wave. A wave is made of many of these moving things. When one part of the line is moving up, the water above it is going down. When another part of the line is going down, the water above it is going up. This makes the water move back and forward, and that is what we see.

Sometimes, a wave is very small. These are tiny waves that you might see on a small water place in your town. They go across the water very slowly. Other times, a big wave is very tall. These are the ones that might hit the ground and make a lot of noise.

You can feel the power of a strong wave when you are on the water. If the wave is going too fast for you to stay on it, you might feel like you are going to fall over. But if you move your feet and hands fast enough, you can stay on top of the wave.

There are also other kinds of waves. A line of moving things that is very long can go across the water from one side to the other. This is how water can move from one big area of water to another. A wave can also go across the water from one side to the other, but it does not go as fast as a line of moving things across the water.

Sometimes, the wind makes waves instead of the sun. This happens when the wind is very strong and is pushing water up and down. The wind can make waves that are much taller than the line of moving things. These can be very hard to cross.

Waves are important for many things. They help keep the water clean by carrying away the things that fall off the top. They help keep the ground safe by keeping the water from going over the top. And they give us a way to see the world from a far away place.
