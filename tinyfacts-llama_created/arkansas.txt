The word for a big water area is called an arming. This word comes from the old days when people built huge houses on top of the water. These houses were like big rooms in the middle of the water. They were often very tall and had a big open floor on top.

People used these houses for many different reasons. Some built them to move from one place to another during the cold months. They would carry the house with them on top of a big moving thing. They would walk or ride on the water for many days until the cold went away.

Other people built these houses to keep food inside and water out. They would store food and water inside the house and keep it on top of the water. They would also keep animals inside the house that would not go into the water.

These big houses on water are often called arming. They are very old and have been around for many years. Some are still standing today. They are very important for people who live in the places where the water is very high.
