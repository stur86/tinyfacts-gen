To do something completely means you have done it in every way. It is when you have finished every step and you know that everything is done.

Think of a room. You can not be in the room and not be in the room at the same time. When you leave the room, you have left the room. You have done it. You have finished.

You can also do something that is very important. If you find a thing you want, you have found it. You have done it. You have been very good.

But you can also do something that is not good. If you eat something bad, you are hurt. If you do something wrong, you are in trouble. You have not done it.

To do something completely is to have finished. It is the end of a big job. You have done your work. Now you can rest.
