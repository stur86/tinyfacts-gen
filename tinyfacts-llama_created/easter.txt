Easier is a word that means you can do things more easily because you know how. When you are a child, you might get a small thing that you have to carry. It is a thing that is easy to carry and it has a small light on it that tells people where you are.

When you grow up, you might have to carry a bag full of things. Some bags are very big and heavy. If you carry a bag that is too heavy, you might get tired and feel sad. But if you know how to carry the bag, you can do it easily.

Easier is also a word for things that happen in a day. You might go to school and then you go home. You do not have to go far. You can sit and read books. This is easier than going to a far place and doing many things.

Sometimes, we say that something is easier for someone else. If someone is very strong, it is easier for them to move heavy things. If someone is very good at a game, it is easier for them to play the game. They do not have to work as hard.

We also say that something is easier when you can do it more times. Imagine you try to play a game on a computer for the first time. It is harder than playing the game for the first time when you know how to use it. But if you play it many times, you get better and it becomes easier.

Finally, we say that something is easier because it is not hard. If you go to a place and it is very hot, you might feel tired. But if you go to a place with a cool water, you do not feel tired. It is easier to go to the water because it is not hot like the place with the hot ground.
