To put something into a new way is a very important thing. You can use this word when you are doing a job.

When you are making food, you start with the beginning. You find the hard part of the thing that grows on a tree. You cut it. Then you make the white stuff that grows on the top. You put the white stuff inside the hard part. After a while, the food is ready.

You also use this word for how things change. Imagine you want to be a tall person. You start with being a small person. You eat food that makes you strong. You go to the doctor many times. You do not get better. You are still a small person. You are not a tall person yet, but you are getting there. This is a kind of change.

Sometimes, you put people into a new way. You put a person who is afraid of the dark into a room with a bright light. At first, they are scared. But after a while, they feel calm. They can see the room. This is a kind of change.
