To share something with many people is to put it on the air so they can watch it. You are on the air. You send your words and pictures to a big room far away. The room has many people who watch. They see what you are saying. They hear you.

A way to do this is to use a computer. You put your words in a way that people can see and hear. You send the pictures and words to the computer. The computer sends them to the big room. People watch on their phones, computers, or big boxes in homes.

You can share your life. You might show your home. You might show your job or your school. You might talk to your friends. You might show how you feel. This way, many people see and hear you.

It is also a way to learn. You can watch a teacher show you how to do something. You can watch how a doctor works. You can watch a movie to learn about the world.

People use this when they are far away. They might watch a game or a show. They watch a movie. They watch a talk. They are not in the same room as the people in the room.
