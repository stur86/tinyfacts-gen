To be well is to feel safe and happy.

When you are well, you are in a good place. You feel like everything is going right. You might be smiling. You feel you can do what you want.

The word can also mean to come to a place that is new and different. Imagine you are in a big city. You do not know where you are. You see many tall people and many cars. A kind person says,  Hi, I am here to help you. They tell you, you are in a new place. You feel a bit scared, but the person is there to help.

Sometimes you might feel well in a group. You are with friends who know you. You all laugh and talk. You feel like you are part of something nice. You feel happy and safe with the group.

You can also be well in your own home. You feel like you are safe at home. You do not have to worry about anything. You can relax and do what you like.

Being well is important. When you are well, you can be yourself. You can laugh and play with your friends. You feel like you can handle anything that comes up. It makes you feel happy and strong.
