To play something means you make it sound again. You might hear a song you like, or a story that happened before. You use a thing that can make sounds. This can be a small box with many tiny parts, or a big box with many many small parts inside. When you push the right parts, the box makes a sound.

A person can also play a thing. If you are a good player, you can make the box sound very nice. You might hit the small parts with your fingers. This makes a lot of sound. You might make it fast or slow, or make it grow louder. You might even make it happen in your brain, so you feel happy or sad.

Sometimes, people play things for children. They show the child the sound on the box. The parents might also watch. They like to see their child happy or learn.

You can also say a story is played. If a book is full of stories, the stories are played. You listen to the words and imagine what happened. This is how we learn about the world.

To play something is to make it sound again. It is like the music in a movie or a game. It makes you feel something.
