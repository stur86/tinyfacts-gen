To pack something means you put it in a way that it is safe and ready to go. You might pack a bag for a trip. You fill the bag with clothes, shoes, and other things you need. You put the bag on a car so it can move quickly.

You also pack things for a house. You put books, papers, and things you need for school in a box. You pack your food for the week so you have enough for many days. This helps you have everything you need without having to go out.

Sometimes, people pack things for a job. They might pack papers and money for a meeting. They might put their computer and papers in a bag so they can go to the office.

When you pack something, you are making it safe for when it goes somewhere. You make sure it does not break or get lost. When you are ready, you can move it from one place to another.
