A small box of paper and hard stuff is often kept in a room. Inside this box, a person writes about their day, their week, their life. They write about what happened, how they feel, and what they think. They might write about a good thing that happened, like a new day or a nice person they met.

This box is like a story about a person. It shows how they feel and what they think about things. It helps a person know themselves and what they need. Sometimes, a person keeps a box because they feel sad or angry and need to write about these things.

People keep a box of these kinds of papers for many years. They might start when they are very young and keep them for a long time. They might stop writing one day and start the next day. Sometimes, a person keeps the box for many years, and then they throw it away and buy a new one. This is because the box is full and the person does not need it anymore.

A person who keeps a box of these papers is called a writer. They write about what they see, what they feel, and what they think. It is a way to keep a story about their life.
