A tone is how something sounds. You can hear a tone when you listen to a song. A sound can have many different tones. The tone of a sound can change when you move it from one place to another. It is like a line on a piece of paper that shows the sound.

Think about a low sound. It is a heavy sound. It makes your feet feel the ground. It makes your mouth open a little. The low sound is a big tone. It is like a deep line on the paper.

Think about a high sound. It is a light sound. It makes your voice come out. It makes your mouth close. The high sound is a small tone. It is like a long line on the paper.

The same sound can have many tones. A big sound can have a low one. A small sound can have a high one. When you hear a sound, you are hearing the tone. You are hearing how the sound looks to your ears.

In music, tones are the parts that make the sound. The middle part is usually a middle tone. The high part is a high tone. The low part is a low tone. Each part has its own tone. The whole sound is made of many different tones.

You can also hear tones in your body. When you hear a sound, your ears send the sound to your brain. Your brain then tells your body what to do. If the sound is too loud, your body might shake. If the sound is too quiet, your body might feel heavy. Your body is always hearing the tones of the world around you.
