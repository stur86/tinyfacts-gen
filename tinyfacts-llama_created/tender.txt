Toward something is a feeling you have when you want to be near it. It is a soft feeling that makes you want to hold it and keep it. When you are this way, you feel safe.

Imagine you have a small animal that you love. You might hold it close and whisper to it. You want to stay with it until it is safe again. This feeling is toward that animal. It is about being near something and wanting to care about it.

You can also feel this way toward a person. Maybe a mother loves her child very much. When the child is safe in her arms, the mother feels this way. She wants to keep them safe and be near them.

This feeling is important because it helps us stay close to the people and things we care about. When we are toward something, we feel a little bit of love. We do not like to let go because it makes us feel safe.
