A person who wants to do something is called a person with a plan. They set a thing they want to happen. They work on it every day.

We all have a plan. We plan to go to a place, to buy food, or to play with our friends. We write down what we want to do and when we want to do it.

A person can have many plans. One person wants to run fast. Another person wants to be the best at a game. They all have a plan, even if they do not know the end yet.

When a person does something, they feel good. They might feel happy. They feel like they are getting closer to their plan. If they do not follow their plan, they might feel sad or angry. They might want to change what they did.

We also have a plan to learn new things. We might want to learn a new word, a new song, or a new way to do a job. We read books, watch movies, or ask teachers. We follow a plan to get better.

A person can also have a plan to help others. If you want to help a friend who is sick, you follow a plan. You give them water, you listen, and you stay near them. This helps the friend feel better. You follow the plan until they feel better.

In life, we all have plans. We plan to grow, to learn, or to be happy. We plan to meet new people or to find a job. We plan for the day after tomorrow. Every plan has a beginning and an end.
