To call someone means to ask them to come to a place. You can call a friend, a family person, a teacher, or anyone you want to talk to.

Imagine you are in a room and you want to talk to your mom. You say, my mom, and she hears you. She comes to the room. She does not have to see you. You just say her name and she comes.

You can also call a phone. When you press the right number, the phone says, your number. Then you talk to the person on the other end of the line. This is how people talk when they want to be heard.

In school, you might have a teacher who wants to talk to you about a problem. You say, teacher, and the teacher hears you. They talk about the problem and then they give you a new idea to try.

Sometimes, you call someone to say sorry or to ask for help. If you do something wrong, you might call a parents number and say, sorry, mom. This is how people show they care about each other.

You can also call a place. If you want to go to a store, you say, let me go. The person at the door lets you in. You do not have to say, my name, they just let you through. This is how people go to places without going through other people.
