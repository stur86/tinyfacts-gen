To make something different is to change it. You start with the same thing and you do something to it. You might change how it looks, what color it is, or how it works.

Think of a car. You start with a car. You do not change it in the same way. You put new parts in it. The car is now different. It is not the same car you had before.

You can also make a word different. If you say the word is the same, you are wrong. The word is different. You are changing how the word is used. You are making the word your own.

Sometimes you can change something so it is better. You might learn how to play a new game. You learn how to do it quickly and well. You are making the game your own. This is a good thing.

To change something is to change it. It is a way to make things new.
