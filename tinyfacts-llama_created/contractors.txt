A person who works on a big job is called a builder. They are often very good at making things that need to be built. They might make houses, schools, or roads. They are the ones who do the hard work so that someone else can not do it.

There are also people who work on smaller jobs. They might make something for a small business. They might fix a phone or a computer. They do not have to build something from the ground up.

Sometimes, people who work on these jobs are called a team. They all work together to finish the job. They share the money they get from the job to make sure it is done well.

The money people pay to these people is not for them to keep. It is for the job they do. The money is like a big round that people give to many people at once. It is used to buy the things they need to do their job.

Some people have a job that is very different. They might be a doctor or a teacher. They do not work on a big job with many people. They work with only one person or a small group. They care for the people who are sick or who need to learn.

There are also people who work on jobs that are not big. They might fix things that break around the city. They might walk to a building if a light is broken. They help people who are in trouble.

Everyone needs these people. Without them, we would not have many of the things we have today. They are important for making our lives better.
