A cover story is a way you lie to people. It is a story about why you are doing something. Most of the time you tell people this story so they will not ask you another question.

You might lie about where you are going. You might say you are going to a big city, but really you are going to see your friends. Or you might say you are going to a place where you work, but really you are going to go to a house you are paying money to. This is a way to keep people from asking you more questions.

You also use a cover story when you want to hide something. If you do something bad, you might tell someone that you were doing something else. This can help you avoid getting in trouble. It is like a safe word.

Sometimes you use a cover story to get money. If you tell a store that you need money to buy food, the store might give you the money. This is not a good idea. You should tell the truth if you need money.
