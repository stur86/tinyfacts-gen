A time of year is a part of the year when the sun is in a different place.

Imagine you live in a place with warm days and cool nights. In the summer, the sun is high in the sky for many hours every day. When the sun is high, it is very hot outside. People often go to the park or the water to have fun. The days are very long, and the nights are short.

Then, in the fall, the days get shorter. The sun is lower in the sky. The air gets colder, and the leaves on the trees turn to brown. The smell of the leaves is very good. People often go to the park or to a big building to watch the sun go down. This is a time of change.

In the next part of the year, the days are shorter again. The sun is lower in the sky more of the time. The air gets even colder. The leaves fall from the trees. People often stay inside because it is cold.

The time between these three is called the fall. It is a time of change.

In the next part of the year, the days are very long. The sun is high in the sky for many hours every day. The air is cold, and the leaves are all gone. The ground is covered in white or brown stuff. People often go to the park or to a big building to see the sun come up every morning. This is a time of cold.

Then, in the next part of the year, the days are shorter again. The sun is lower in the sky. The air gets colder again. The ground is still cold.

This time is also called the fall. It is a time of change.

The time after is a time of cold, dark days. The sun is low in the sky. The air is very cold. People often stay inside because it is very cold.

This time is also called the fall. It is a time of cold.
