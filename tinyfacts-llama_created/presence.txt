To have a present is to be right there in a place. You are not far away. You are in the room, in the street, or in the house. You are with the people you love, or with the things you want.

You can have a present when you see something you want. You might see a book you want to read. You can also see a movie you want to watch. This is when you see the things you want with your eyes.

Sometimes, you might be in a room with many people. You feel a little nervous because you do not know the names of the people. But then you hear a word, and suddenly you feel a big calm. You are still in the same room, but you feel like you can do things.

You can also have a present when you are near a place. You might feel a present when you are near a park, a school, or a store. You feel like you are in the right place. You are not far away from what you need.

Being present is important because it helps us feel. If you are not there, you feel like you are missing something. But if you are right there, you feel happy and safe.
