You can get a small piece of money from someone without paying them. You do not pay them, but you still have the money. This is what a friend or a family man or a person you like gives to you. If you buy a car, your friend gave you the money.

You can also get this kind of money from a computer. If you buy a book or a game, the computer gives you the money. The computer does not pay you, but it helps you buy the things you want.

This is how people do business. They give money to each other to buy things. They promise to pay later. The money is like a very strong word that everyone knows.
