When you say someone is entered, you mean they are inside something. They are not on the outside anymore. They are inside.

Imagine you are in a room with a door. The door is closed. You are standing in front of the door. You are not in the room. You are on the other side of the door. This is where you are, and you are not inside the room.

If you enter a room, you are going inside. You are taking a step into a new place. You are not going back to where you were before. You are moving forward.

You can also enter a game. If you are playing a game with a friend, you can enter the game. You are now a part of the game. You are doing the same thing as them.

Sometimes, you might feel very happy when you enter a room. You might feel like everything is going well. You might feel like you are in a great place.

But you can also enter a room in a different way. If you are feeling very angry, you might enter a room. You are going inside because you need to calm down. You are moving away from the problem.

The word is also used when you enter a place with a person. If you enter a school, you are going to a place where people learn. You are going to a new place with new people. You are not in your old home anymore. You are in a new one.
