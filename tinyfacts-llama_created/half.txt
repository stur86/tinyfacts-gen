Half is a word that shows part of something. It is a number that is not one. It is between one and two.

Imagine you have a box with one ball inside. If you take the ball out, you have the other half of the box. You still have a half inside.

You can also have half of a thing by eating it. When you eat half of a piece of food, you have taken one half out. The rest is still there.

If you have a number that is half a number, it means you have taken one half of the number. For a number like ten, ten half is five. It is half the number.

People often use half to talk about time. If it is half ten, it means it is ten minutes past something. It is almost time.

You can also use half to talk about things that are not whole. If you have two half glasses of water, you have two parts of the water. You do not have one full glass.

There is also a word that means to act in a half way. If you act in a half way, you are not the whole person. You are only part of it. You are not the full story.
