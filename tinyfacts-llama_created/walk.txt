To walk means you move your feet from one place to another. You start in one spot and go to another. You move your feet in a straight line or a line that goes around a round thing.

When you walk, you use your legs. Your legs are the parts of your body that make your feet do the work. You use your legs to push yourself up and down. This makes you go forward.

Sometimes you walk so you can go to a place you want to see. Other times, you walk to go home from work. You might walk with a friend or a dog. Walking is a good way to get some air and to move around.

You can walk very fast or very slow. Some people walk quickly to go to school or to a store. Other people walk slowly to be quiet or to enjoy the street.

Walking can be a good way to be alone. You can walk in a park or a quiet street. You might see new things, like a tree that is different from the one you see at home. You might hear animals or hear music. Walking can also help you feel happy and calm.

If you walk around a round thing, you are going in a full turn. You are moving your feet so that you keep going in the same direction. If you walk in a line, you are moving along one side of a line. You are following a line that goes around a round thing.

Walking is an important part of life. It helps us move from one place to another. It can help us feel safe and in control.
