To be a leader is to have the power and the right to tell others what to do. A person who is in this position is called a leader. They might be the one who decides what game to play at school or who gets to be the one to read the story at night.

Sometimes, a leader has many people under them. This is because the person is very good at what they do. They can make a plan and make sure everyone follows it. They can also tell people if they are doing something wrong.

People also have a top person. This is the person who is in a group and has the power to decide what happens. The top person is like the leader of the group. They are the one who says, yes or no.

A person can be the top leader of a city or a big group of people. They might be the one who decides what happens to everyone or who makes the hard parts of the day. A top person is important because they help keep everyone safe and make sure everyone is happy.

To be a leader is to be the one who makes things happen. It is a big job that only a few people get. When a person is a leader, they have to be good at making people want to follow them. They must be able to talk and be heard so everyone understands what they want.
