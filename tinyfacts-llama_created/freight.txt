To carry something means to move it from one place to another. You might carry food, clothes, or books. Cars, buses, and trains do this all the time.

A long time ago, people used to carry heavy things on their backs. A strong person would go through the day and night, carrying a big bag full of food, clothes, and money. This was hard work, but it helped the people have what they needed.

Today, we use many different ways to carry things. You can drive a car, or you can ride a bus. You can also use a box on a train. Some people carry boxes on their feet. They walk with the boxes and walk to their home.

To carry also means to give something to someone. If you have a big book, you can carry it to school. You carry it in your bag. When someone does this, it is called carrying a thing.
