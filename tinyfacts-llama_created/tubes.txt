A long, straight thing that carries water, air, or food through a place. These things go through many rooms in a house. They carry water from a big building to a room where people sit to eat. They carry food from a room where people eat to a room where people drink. They also carry air for people to breathe when they are in a big room with many people.

Sometimes these things are made of hard stuff that is cut into long pieces. They have a hard top and a soft bottom. When water goes down the top, it goes away. Water on the bottom stays. This is why the water comes out of the bottom of the long thing.
