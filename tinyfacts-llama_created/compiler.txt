A computer is a very fast thing that can do many things. It can do things on your computer, phone, or any other thing that has a brain inside. It can read what you type, follow your words, and change them into more clear or fun things. This is called a computer.

A computer works with numbers. It takes a number, adds two numbers together, or changes one number into another number. It also reads the words you type and changes them into numbers so it can understand them.

One part of a computer is the brain. The brain makes sure everything happens in the right order. If you type a word and press the key, the brain says, okay, I understand. If you want to go to a different place, the brain says, okay, let me move you there.

There is another part inside the computer. This part does not understand numbers. It only understands the way you type. When you type a word, the brain sends the word to this part. This part reads the word, changes it into numbers, and then sends the numbers to the brain to understand.

A computer also has a part that looks at the numbers. This part is like a book that keeps track of who did what. If you use the computer to write a story, the part reads the words, adds them together, and keeps a listened to the story. This part is very important because it keeps your story safe and makes sure you did not do anything bad.

Sometimes, a computer does not do what it is supposed to do. If a computer does not follow the right order, it can do things that are not safe. If a computer reads a word that is not safe, it can give you a bad idea. So, a computer must always follow what the person who built it says.
