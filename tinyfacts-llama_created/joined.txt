To join something means you add it to another thing. If you have a ball and you throw it to a friend, you are joining the two things. You are making them one.

Imagine you have a book with many pictures. You want to read a story with a picture. You can not just put the book and the story together. You need to put the book in the hand of the person who will read it. That is joining them. You are now part of the story and the book.

You can join a group of people who like the same thing. You join a school. You join a team. You join a group of friends. When you join, you become a part of it. You are not alone anymore. You have many people who are with you.

You can also join a thing that is not a person. You can join a team of animals. You can join a team of people who do work together. You are adding to the group. You are making it bigger.

Sometimes, you join something that is already there. You join your body. You join the ground under your feet. You join the air around you. When you join something, you are making it bigger and stronger.
