The word is used to talk about how things are set up in the world. You can find this kind of thing in your life. A world is like a big area that is under one person. This person is the top person and everyone else is under them. They are the ones who make the many things work together.

There is also a world that is not under one person. This is the world of the animals. This world is not one person. It is a place where many animals live. They do not follow one person.

Some things are in the world of people. These are the big things that you see every day. You go to school, you work, you play games. These are not the same as the world of animals. They are not things that live and breathe.

Some things are in the world of ideas. These are things you think about. You might be angry or you might be happy. You can feel things that are not there. These things do not have a real body. They are only in your mind.

Finally, there is the world of numbers. Numbers are the things you use to counters. You use numbers to build things or to help you remember. This world is very strong and it does not change.

In this way, we can see that there is a big world under one person and a smaller world under many people. There is a world of animals and a world of ideas. There is a world of people and a world of numbers. Each of these worlds is important in its own way.
