So is a word you use when you are talking about where something is. It tells you where a person, a thing, or a house is. You can say, I am going to my house. This means I am moving to a place that is far away from my home.

Think about it like this: if you have a cup of water on a table, you can say, this cup is on the table. This is like saying the cup is in one place. You can also say, the cup is next to the chair. This means the cup is near the chair but not in the same room.

You can use the word to talk about many things: a place you visit, a thing you want, a friend you like, or even a feeling that makes you happy. When you use the word, you are telling others where the thing is or how it feels.

Sometimes, when you say, I am going to the store, you are also asking, where am I going? The word helps you know where you are going. It is a very important word to use when you want to tell where things are or how they are feeling.
