When you are told to do something, you have to follow what the person says. This is how your job starts. If someone is very good at their work, they might be asked to help another person. This is called a job. They do the job for a long time.

If you want to help someone, you can ask to be their friend. You tell them you care about them. This is like being picked to do a job. They have to follow you and do what you say.

Sometimes, a group of people is made to decide what to do. The leader says, and everyone follows. This is like being put in a job to do. They talk about what they want and what they think.

You can also get a job with a person you like. This is a very nice thing. The person lets you do the job. You might help them or do a job for them. This is like being picked to help.

All these things are about being told to do something. You do what the person says because they told you to.
