A place where things are made. These places are often very big and have many many rooms. People work there, using power from the ground or the sun to move things from one room to another. They add parts together and put them into a big box. The box then goes out to stores where people buy the things.

Some places make clothes or food. Others make cars or things that fly through the air. Some make things that go over water. They make things that people use every day. They are very important because they help make the world a better place.

You can also have a small place where things are made. This might be in a house or a room in a building. A small place might make things for people who live in the area. These places are good because they help people have what they need.
