To be done is to have been done. It is when something is finished. You have done your work for the day. The sun has set and the stars are coming out. The work is done.

You are done when you have finished what you wanted to do. You can stop doing it now. You do not have to keep going. This is a big word. It means you have done something very well. You can feel a strong feeling of being done.

If you are done, you can stop thinking about it. You can go to sleep or play a game. You do not have to think about what you did anymore. You can just feel the feeling of being done.

You can also be done with a person. If someone has done their job, they are done. They can walk away and go home. This is a good feeling. They have done what they needed to do.

Sometimes, to be done can also mean to have learned something. You may have learned a new thing. You might have picked up a new way of doing things. You feel like you are a better person now. You know you can do more things because you have done them.

There is also a different meaning. When you do something, it can leave a mark. You might leave a mark on a tree, on a wall, or on your skin. This mark can tell others that you were there. It is like you are leaving a sign that says, I was here.

To be done with something is also about to stop. When you are done with a book, you do not have to read it anymore. You can put the book away and not think about it anymore. You are done with the story.
