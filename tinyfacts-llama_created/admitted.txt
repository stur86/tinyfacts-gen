Admitted is a word that means you are allowed to be in a place. When you admit, you tell the person in power that you are the person you say you are. You are not hiding.

You might be admitted to a school. If you are admitted to a school, you are allowed to go there and learn. You do not have to pay money for a room. You can also be admitted to a job if you show that you are the right person for the job.

Sometimes, people admit things that are not true. This is when you say one thing but mean another. If you admit that you love someone, but really you hate them, you are lying.

Admitted also means you have done something right. If you admitted a book, you say it is good. This is like admitting you are right.

One important thing to remember is that admitting something does not mean you have to let it go. You can still remember it and learn from it later. It is about being open and showing that you are okay with what happened.
