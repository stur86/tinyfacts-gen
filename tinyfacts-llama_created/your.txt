Your is a word that means you are a person. It is a word you use to talk about your self.

When you talk about yourself, you use this word. You might say, I am you, or I am the one in front of you. This word tells us that you are the person doing the talking.

You are a person because you live in the world. You have a body and a brain. You breathe air, drink water, and eat food. You do things, like go to school or work, and you learn new things.

Think about a tree. A tree is a living thing that grows from the ground. It makes its own food from the sun and the ground. It is a person too. It is not a person with a mind like you or me. But it is a person in the world, and we call it your tree because it is your part of the world.

You are also your friends. Your friends are people you like and care about. You play with them, have fun together, and tell each other things. You are your friends because they are people you care about.

Sometimes, you might feel sad or angry. You might cry, yell, or shake your body. When you feel this way, it is because your body is trying to show you something. Your body is a person too, and it needs love and care.

In short, your is a word that tells us who you are and what you do. It is a word you use to talk about yourself, your friends, and the world around you.
