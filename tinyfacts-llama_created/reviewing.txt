To look over something is to think about it. You might look at a book you read. You might look at a picture of a place you went to. You are not just looking at the things you see. You are also thinking about what they mean.

Sometimes you need to look over your work. If you write something wrong, you might look at what you wrote and see how you did it. You might look at what people think and what they say about your work. This helps you learn.

You can also say you are going to look over a thing. You are going to look at it and see if it is okay. You might look at a computer or a phone. You might look at how it works. You are checking it.

To tell someone that something is bad is also to look over it. If a movie is not good, the person who made it might look at it and see if it is true. They might look at the parts they like and the parts they do not like. They are checking if the movie is what they think it should be.

To look over something can be hard. You might see things you do not want to see. You might feel angry. You might want to stop thinking about it. But if you keep looking, you can learn and grow.
