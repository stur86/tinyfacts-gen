To be self means you are the person you are. You are not another person. You do not have to be like someone else. You are the one in your life who is you.

Think about a tree. A tree is a thing that stands in one place. It does not change. It is always the same. You are like a tree. You stay the same year after year. You do not become another person or change like a person does.

You can also be self to something. You might say, I am happy. I am doing well. This means you feel good about yourself. You are the one who likes how you are.

Sometimes people want to be self like a cat. A cat is very quiet and does not talk. They just sleep and watch. You can be like this too. You can stay quiet and not talk much.

You can also be self to your work. If you are a good doctor, you can be self to your job. You know your work and you do it well. You do not need to be told what to do. You are the one who does the work.

Being self is important. It means you are not afraid to be the same person you are every day. It means you are not trying to be someone else. It means you are happy with who you are.
