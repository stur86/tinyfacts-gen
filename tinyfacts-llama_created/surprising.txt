When you say something is surprising, you mean it is not what you expect. It is a big change. You might expect one thing to happen, but instead, another thing happens that makes your heart go fast.

Think about a game. You know the game is going to be easy. You think you can do it quickly. But then, the game starts, and suddenly you are doing something you have never done before. You feel surprised because you did not expect it to happen. You might laugh and say, wow, that was amazing!

Surprising can happen in many ways. You might see a person you love doing something different. You might hear a sound that makes you jump. Or you might see a strange animal in the woods. These are all surprising because they change how you feel.

Surprise can also be about what happens when you do something. If you walk into a room and find a surprise party waiting for you, you are surprised. You did not expect that.

Sometimes surprise can be a little bit bad. If you are walking down a street and see a car with bright lights, but it is not moving, it can make you feel afraid. You were expecting it to move.

A surprising thing can also happen in your mind. You might see a picture that is not your own. It is a strange picture of a place you have never been. This can be a surprise because it changes how you think about the world.

So, surprise is about a big change that catches you. It can be good or bad, but it always makes you feel something.
