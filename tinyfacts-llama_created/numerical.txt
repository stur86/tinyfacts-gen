To number something means to tell it a number so everyone can know how many there are. You can write numbers on a piece of paper in a line from one to many. This helps us keep track of how many animals, how many books, or how many people there are.

You can also number a thing that is not a number. If you have a book with many stories, you might number the stories so you can find them later. Or you might number a chair so you know where to sit. This helps you find the chair when you need it.

Sometimes, when we talk about time, we number the days of the month. We start with the first day and number each day until the last day of the month. This helps us keep track of how many days have passed since the start of the month.

We use these numbers in many ways in our lives. We use them to buy things, to plan our days, and to keep track of who is doing what.
