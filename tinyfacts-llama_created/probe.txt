A long, hard thing you use to ask questions. You hold it under the ground or in a room. It feels the hard stuff around it. It can see if there is anything there. It can also see what is behind it.

You can use it to find out if there is a thing you are looking for. You can put it under a tree, under a house, or under a big rock. If the thing you want is there, the thing you use can tell you. If the thing you want is not there, the thing you use will not find it.

Sometimes you use it to see what is under a floor. If you are trying to figure out if a floor is safe, you might use a long thing to look under it. If you find something you do not want to see, you might get hurt. You must be very carefully when you use it.
