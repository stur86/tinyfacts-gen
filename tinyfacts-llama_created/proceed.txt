To move forward means to act on a plan. You have a story to tell or a job to do. You start to do it. You take one step after another. Every step moves you closer to the end. When you do this, you are moving forward.

If you are waiting, you are not moving. You are standing still. You are not doing anything. You are waiting for something to happen. But if you start to do something, you are moving. You are moving forward.

Imagine you are trying to reach the top of a big, green wall. You are standing at the bottom. You start to walk up. You are moving. You are not just standing. You are doing something. You are going to the top.

When you are moving forward, you are changing. You are not the same person you were before. You are becoming something new. You are becoming ready for what comes next.
