When light moves through space, it can take different ways. This is why you can see things that are far away as clear as a picture and things that are right in front of you as small as a tiny line. Light is made up of many different ways. Each way is a different kind of light. When light from the sun goes through a window, some of it passes straight through and some goes in at a very small turn, like a tiny corner, and comes out the other side.

We use these turns to make things work. A thing with a turn can see what is behind it. A thing with many turns can see many things at once. A thing with a turn that is very small has a turn that is very big. We use these turns to make things that can see light from far away.
