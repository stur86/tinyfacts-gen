To die means you are very close to your end. Your body starts to stop working and you do not feel well anymore. You might feel like you are going to sleep or feel very cold. This is the end of your life.

You are not alone. Many people have died. Some were very old, and some were very young. They all went to the same place. It is a place where all the dead go. You will not go there if you live.

The word is often used with people who are very old. They might say, I am almost dead. This is a sign that time is running out.

Sometimes, when you are very old, you might talk about what you think is the end of life. You might ask if it is okay to die. This is a hard thing to talk about. Many people do not know how to answer.

In some places, you might see a sign with numbers. The numbers tell how many days you have left. It is like a number from a game. You are running out of time. You can only do so many things, and then you are gone.

Even though you are dying, you are still a person. You have loved and lived your life. You have made a mark on the world. This is something to remember.

If you are very old, you might hear things that people did not see. You might hear about people who lived a long time ago. You might hear stories about how things were before. You might even hear about things that are not yet here.
