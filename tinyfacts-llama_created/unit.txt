A group of things that work together is called a group. In a group, everyone has the same job or one thing to do. Each person has a small job and they all do it together. This way, they can do many things at once.

Imagine you have a team of kids in a class. You all work together to build a box. One kid can build the top, another can build the bottom, and another can put the pieces together. Each person does only one thing, but together they build something nice.

In a group of people, you can have many different jobs. One person is the leader. They make sure everyone follows the right way. Another person might be the one who knows the most about a thing. They help the group learn. There might also be people who can speak many different kinds of words. They help everyone understand each other.

Sometimes, people are in a group because they are all about the same thing. They might all be doing the same job or they might all be working on the same problem. This group can be very strong because they all work together and share what they know.

In the world, we also have groups of animals. Some groups of animals live in one place. They stay there for a long time. Other groups of animals go far away. They meet new places and people. They learn from each other.

A group can also be a group of people who are not the same. They might all have different ideas about something. They can still work together and find a way that makes everyone happy. This is important because it shows that there is still room for different people to work together.
