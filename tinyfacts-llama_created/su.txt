To sure something means to be very sure or strong about a thing.

Think of a game. You and your friend are playing a game. You are both playing. But one of you might say, I think the ball is going to go this way, and the other one says it is going to go this way. You both are sure of your own answer. If you both are sure, you can stop thinking about it and do what you want. You can go to the kitchen and eat a drink. Or you can go to the store and buy a new ball.

If you are surest about something, you do not need to think about it. You know for sure. You do not worry. It is easy to be surest because your mind is clear.

Sometimes, you can be surest about things that are not clear. You might think you know something, but you are not sure. You feel that something is wrong. You might want to ask another person to help you, or you might want to think about it some more before you act.

Being surest is important. It helps you feel safe and calm. If you are surest, you can trust your own ideas.
