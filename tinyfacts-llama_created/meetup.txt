To meet someone or something can mean different things.

One way is when you meet a friend. You might see them in a big room. There are many people there. You talk to them for a while. You do not know if this is a good time to talk. If you are happy and your friend looks happy, you feel good. You might even decide to spend more time together.

Another way is when you meet things. If you are working on a big problem, you might need help. You ask your friends to join you. They come and help you. You work together. This is how you do things when you have a group of people. They all work together to get the job done.

When you meet a group of people, you usually say hello. You might be talking about something. You talk about a movie you saw or a game you played. You share your ideas with each other. This is how people meet and learn from one another.

Sometimes, you meet a person who is different from you. You might not know much about them. But when you talk to them, you learn more. You might find out you have a lot in shared. You might decide to be friends.
