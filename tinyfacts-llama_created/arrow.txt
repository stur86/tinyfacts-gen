An thing that goes from one place to another, often in the sky, is called an arm. You can use it to throw a small thing that moves through the air. This is the part that flies. It is usually made of hard stuff and has a point at the top.

You can also use an arm to pull on something. If you are afraid of a dog or a cat, you can use your arm to stop it. You might use your arm to pull its leg or to stop it from moving.

An arm can also mean something that helps you. A doctor has a long arm that they use to feel inside a body. A person who works with water might have a long arm. They use this arm to move a big round thing that makes water go around in a round way.

Sometimes, an arm is used to talk about how a person acts. If someone is very nice, they might act like an arm. They might give you a hug or a high smile. They are trying to help you feel safe.
