To give something away means to let it go for free. You can do this with money, food, or an old thing that is not needed anymore.

Imagine you have a big bag of money. You want to buy something you really want. But the money is not enough. You decide to take the money from the bag and give it to the person who needs it. Now the person has the money they need and you are free.

You can also give away things that are broken. If you have a broken chair, you might give it to someone who needs it to fix it. This is a kind way to help others.

When you use a water thing that sends water out, you give the water away. You turn the water thing on and it sends water out into a room. This is what happens when you take a shower. You let the water go out of your body. It is like the water is giving you a clean feeling.

In a room, you might find a door that is closed. You want to get out. You pull the door open. The door goes away. The room is now free to other people.
