To be the one who made something means to have power over the things that are around you. This is when you make the air, the water, or the ground. You tell the world what to do.

You can also mean something that is made by a computer. If you write a word, the computer makes it from your words. If you tell it to play music, it plays the music.

Sometimes, people use this word when they talk about a big group of people. If many people work together, they can make big changes. They work for one person, but together they do many things for everyone.

It can also mean a small thing made by someone. A small child might make a small paper thing. A small animal might have small further on its body. This word is often used when we talk about small, easy ways to make things happen.
