To use a word is to talk about it. Think about the word water. You use water to drink. You use water to clean your face. You use water to fill a bottle. You do this every day.

The word is about a part of a whole. It shows that one thing is not alone and is part of another thing. Think about your hand. Your hand is a part of your body. It is not the whole body but it is still part of it.

We use this word when we talk about numbers. If you say a number is half of another number, you are saying the first number is a part of the second number. This word is not for numbers only. It is for any part of a whole.

Imagine you have a group of friends. You are the person who is in the middle of the group. You are not the whole group. You are a part of it. You can not be alone. You are part of a bigger group of people.

To use the word is to show you understand. If someone says a word is a part of something, you can say you understand.
