A study is a way to learn how people feel or what is happening. It is usually done with many people. Sometimes a person is in a room with you. They ask you questions and you tell them if you like or hate something.

You can also use computers and papers to study. You write things about what you see or hear. You look at numbers and pictures.

Sometimes, people go to a place where many people live. They ask them about what they do, how they feel, and what they think. This helps us understand how people are and how they live.

It is like looking at a city. You walk around and see many faces. You hear what people are saying. You get a big picture of how the city works.
