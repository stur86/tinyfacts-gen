To be in a place is a very big thing. You can be in a city, a park, a house, or even a high up place on a top of a tall building. 

If you are in a room, you are not in a place. You are in your own home. You can be sitting, standing, walking, or just sitting. You are in your own room. You are not in another room.

If you are in the sky, you are not in a place. The sky is all around you. You are not in a small room with walls. You are in the wide space above you.

If you are in a car, you are in a place. You are in a car that moves. You are not in a room. You are in a car that can take you far away.

Sometimes, to be in a place means you are in a group of people. You are with the other kids who live in the same house or school. You are in the same area as the other kids. You are not alone.

You can also be in a place when you are in your body. Your body is your self. You are your body. You are not another person. You are you.

Being in a place can help you feel safe. You might feel happy or sad. You might feel calm or angry. The place you are in can change your feeling.
