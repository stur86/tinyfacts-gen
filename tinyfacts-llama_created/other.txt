The word other is a word that helps us tell about other people, other animals, other things, or other ideas.

When we talk about people, we use other. This word helps us say that one person is different from another. It shows that we do not only talk about one person. We talk about many people, and we use other to mean each of them.

Think about your family. You are the child of your mother and father. Your mother is the mother of your sister and your brother. You are also the child of your father, so you are also your sister and brother. This is because your family is many, and we say your family is many.

We also use this word when we talk about things. A book is one thing. A car is another thing. A tree is another thing. A dog is another thing. Each of these things is different from the other ones.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about ideas. If we say that the sun is the most important thing in the world, the word other can help us. We can say that there are many important things and other are also important. This shows that there are more than one important thing.

So, the word other helps us tell about many people, things, and ideas. It is a word that is used in many different ways.
