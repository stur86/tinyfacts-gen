To think is a very big thing. Our brains are like very strong computers inside our heads. When we learn new things, our brains use those new pieces of computer word to do the work. When we think, our brain sends out tiny lines from our brain to other parts of our body. These lines tell our body to do different things. Our eyes see, our ears hear, our skin feels, and our heart beats.

We use our brains to remember things. When you see a picture of a cat, your brain stores that picture. Later, when you see the same picture again, your brain says, here is the cat. You can even remember things from the past. Our brains are always working, even when we are not thinking.

People also think about their world. They think about their families, their jobs, their friends, and their places. When we feel happy, we think about those happy times. When we are afraid, we think about the things that are wrong. Our brains help us stay safe and know what to do.

Sometimes, our brains get confused. If you try to remember a word, but your brain does not want to help, you might feel sad or worried. Or, if you think you see a person, but you are really looking at a tree, you feel surprised. Our brains are very good at thinking, but sometimes they need our help to stay right.

We can also think about things that are not here. We can think about a place we have never been, a story we have heard, or a feeling we have never felt. Our brains can take us on a trip to another time or place. This is why we can remember stories and songs, or why we feel happy or sad.

So, thinking is how our brains work. They take in what we see, hear, and know, and they send those pieces to other parts of our body. We use our brains to learn, remember, and stay safe. Our brains are like the windows of our bodies, and thinking is how we use those windows to see the world.
