To carry or to hold something means you have it in your hands or in your bag. When you do this, you are taking something that is heavy so you can move it to another place. This is important because it helps you go where you need to go.

Imagine you have a big bag of food. You need to carry it to a school. You can do this by walking or by using a car. If you walk, you are carrying the bag. If you use a car, you are not carrying the bag, but the car is carrying it.

You can also carry things for another person. If you know you need to give your friend a book, you can carry it. When you do this, you are taking something from your bag and giving it to the friend. This is a kind thing to do.

Sometimes, you carry things you are afraid to do. Maybe you are afraid to tell a story. You might carry the story around in your head. As you think about it, you feel more and more nervous. You might want to stop, but you need to keep going. This is like carrying a heavy box of fear.
