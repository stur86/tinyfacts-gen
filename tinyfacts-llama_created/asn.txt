As in the story of the three little boys, the three little boys went to the dark house and they did not go back. One of the boys went to the room and he found a great, heavy box. He opened it and there were many small, hard things in the box. He took one out and looked at it. The small thing was a small piece of a girl. He did not like it. He said,  I will take it. He went to the room where the girl lived and the girl said,  Take this, but he did not take it. She said,  I will show you. She took a small thing from her head and showed it to him. He said,  This is the thing you wanted. She said,  No, this is the thing I wanted. He said,  I do not want it. She said,  You will not be happy. He went back to the dark house.
