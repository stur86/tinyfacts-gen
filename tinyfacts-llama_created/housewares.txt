To make things for a home.

A house is a place where people live. Inside the house, you can find many things you need to live. You need clothes, food, and things to clean your room. These things are not for people to play with. They are for people to use.

You might have a table, a chair, and a bed. You use these things every day. When you are done with them, you put them away. You do this so your home stays clean and your life is easy.

House is also a word for many things you keep in a home. You keep your books, your pictures, and your small things. You keep them because they help you remember happy times or because they make you feel happy.

You might have a box for papers or a box for clothes. These boxes are for keeping things you do not want anymore. You throw them away when you are finished with them.

In a small house, you might have a small table and a small chair. In a house with many rooms, you might have many tables and chairs. You use these many times a day.

House is also a word for things you buy for your home. You might buy a new table, a new chair, or a new bed. These are new things for your home.

All of these are part of what makes a home. They help you live your life in a warm and safe way.
