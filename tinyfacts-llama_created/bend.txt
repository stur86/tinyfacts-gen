To be in a line means you are standing on the ground. It is like a line of people or animals. You are following a straight line that goes in one direction.

When you are in a line, you do not move. You stay in the same place. The line is like a big, straight wall.

Sometimes, things fall down when they are in a line. They go down and come to the ground. This happens when the ground is very hard and the thing is heavy. It is like a ball. When the ball falls, it goes down to the ground.

If you try to move a line, it does not move. It is not a moving thing. You can not push it. You can only follow it.

You can also say something is in a line of thought. This means a thing is following a way of thinking. When you learn something, you are in a line of thought. You follow the way you learn and you get better.

In life, there are lines that you follow. Some lines are straight. Some lines go around in a round way. These lines can help you know where you are going.
