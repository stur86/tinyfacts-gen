A known thing is a thing that is already in a story. Think of a story where you know the name of a person who is not even there. You know who they are, where they live, and what they do.

You can also know things about a place. If you have seen a place before, you know its name, what it looks like, and where it is. You can tell the people around you if you have seen a place before. This helps us stay safe and find our way around.

Sometimes we know things about the people who are around us. If you know a friend, you can tell them you love them. You know their face, their voice, and their way of talking. This helps build a strong feeling between you two.

When we say someone is known, we mean they are already in our minds. We do not need to think about them anymore. They are already part of our story.
