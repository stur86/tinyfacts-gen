To say that something will happen is to tell the world that it is going to be true. You use this word when you are going to do something before you actually do it.

Imagine you are planning to go to a big movie. You know you have to leave in an hour, but you do not know when. You tell your friends, and they tell the people who help you get to the movie. They tell everyone that the movie starts at that time. The word is like a big yes to everyone.

If you were to tell a friend, your friend would know that something is about to happen. They would not have to ask you again. This word helps make plans happen before they start.

You can also use it to talk about if a person will do something. If you are asking a parents if they will let you watch a movie, you are saying, you will let them. You are telling the parents that you are sure.

Sometimes you want to say that something is going to happen even if you do not know the time. You might be sure that it will rain tomorrow. You are not saying that the rain will happen at a very small time, but you are sure. You are saying, rain is going to happen.

You can also use this word to tell that something is about to happen to a group of people. You might say, we are going to the game. Or, I am going to talk. You are telling everyone that the time has come.

This word is a very important one in our lives. It lets us know when things are going to happen and when they will not happen.
