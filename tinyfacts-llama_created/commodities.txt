Coming from old words for money, this word has changed over time. It started with buying and making money from things people need or want. People would buy food, clothes, or other goods and then give the money they had. This made them the people who buy and make money.

Now, people use it for many different things. They buy things that are made far away and give them money. The money goes to someone who makes the things. They then keep the money and give it to others who want it. This makes them the ones who give money.

Some are the ones who buy things for people who do not have money. They buy food or clothes for those who are in need. They also buy things for people who are sick or hurt so they can get better. Some are the ones who buy things for people who want to have fun.

People who work with money, like doctors, teachers, and police, are also important. They help keep everyone safe and make sure the money goes where it should. They do not buy things, but they give money to those who need it.
