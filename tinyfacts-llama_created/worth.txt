To be of a high or low kind means to be one way or the other. A high kind is one that is strong, good, and often very important. Animals, people, and things that are high are often seen as better than those that are low.

A low kind is the other way. It is one that is small, not very good, and often not important at all. Low things can be very small, like tiny animals or small rocks, or they can be very small pieces of things that people use to make things.

If you are high, you might feel happy or strong. You might feel like you can do anything. If you are low, you might feel sad or afraid. You might not feel like you are in the right place.

Sometimes, you might be in a middle kind. You are not very high, and you are not very low. You are just right. This is a good kind because you are not too strong or too small. You are not too big or too small either.

Being of a high kind is important because it helps us know who is important in a world full of many people and many things. It helps us know who to trust, who to fear, and who to be kind to.
