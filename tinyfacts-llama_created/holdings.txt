Hold something means to keep it. You can hold a small thing that you carry in your hand. You might hold a book so you can read it later. You can also hold a person. If you are the person, you hold them in your arms. If you are afraid, you might hold your breath. This is when you feel a bit of fear.

You can also hold a thing that is money. You might hold your money in a safe place so you do not lose it. This is important because money is how people buy things they need.

In a bigger way, a group of people holding money together is a group that works well as a team. They share the money so everyone can have what they need. This group helps people who are in need.
