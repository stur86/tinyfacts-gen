A story has many parts. One part is the beginning. The beginning is usually the first thing you see. The next part is the middle. It shows what happens. The end is the last part. It shows what happens after the beginning.

Sometimes, all these parts are in one place. This is a place that is full of what happens. It is a place that makes the story easy to understand.

You can also use the word in another way. If you are in a room, you are in a room. If you are in a city, you are in a city. You can not be in two places at the same time. This is because you are one person.

This word is very important. It helps us understand what is happening in a story.
