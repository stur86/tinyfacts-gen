When you send a letter, you write down what you want to say. You tell the person you are writing to the right person and what you want them to do. This is like a set of steps that you follow.

A computer also follows these steps. When you want to talk to another computer on the same big group, you follow these steps. First, you talk to the computer you want to talk to. You send the words you want to say. The computer listens and then sends back the words in the same way.

We can also say that when you follow these steps, you are following a computer word. This computer word tells you what to do and how to do it.

Sometimes, people use these steps in a work place. If you want to send a paper to a person, you might follow these steps: you write down what you want to say, you tell the person how to get the paper, and then you send it. This is like following a computer word.

You can also say these steps are like a set of ways to do something. A doctor follows these steps when they are doing a check. They follow the steps to make sure everything is okay and that the person is not hurt.
