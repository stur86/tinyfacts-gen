A painting is a picture made by putting colors on a hard thing. People do this to show how they feel or to tell a story. They use bright colors to make the picture look good. They might use many colors at once to make the picture very bright and strong.

Painting can be made on paper, wood, or even on the wall. The picture is made by adding tiny drops of color. The color drops are put on the paper or wood in a way that makes the picture look nice.

Sometimes a picture can tell a story. The colors can show how a person feels. They might use red for anger, blue for fear, and green for calm. This helps people understand how the person is feeling.

Painting is a way to show how we see the world. The colors and the way the light falls on the picture help tell a story or make us feel.
