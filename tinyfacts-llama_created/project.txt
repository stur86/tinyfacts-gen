To start something is to begin a work or a plan.

If you want to build a new house, you start with a plan. You decide where the house will be, how many rooms it will have, and how much money you will spend. Then you start building the walls and the floors. You build the house one day at a time. You do not start the whole thing at once. You work on it every day.

If you want to go on a trip, you start by looking at your money. You decide which places you want to go to and how much money you have. Then you start packing your bags and buying things you need for the trip. You start thinking about the places you will see and the things you will do.

Sometimes you start a new job. You do not know what your job will be. You learn as you go. You meet new people and learn how to do things. You start to feel like you are a part of the team. You are working for a person who wants to make something new happen.

To start something is to begin a new life. It means you start from the very beginning and try to do something new. It is a time when you are not sure what will happen, but you are ready to try.
