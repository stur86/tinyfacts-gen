A room where young children live with the people who care for them while their parents are away. This place is often near the home but not inside it.

Inside the room, there is a big bed where the children sleep. They also have many small tables and chairs where they eat and do their work. The room is usually very quiet because the children are very good at sleeping. The people who care for them often play with them and give them small things to play with.

There is also a desk where the people write letters and send money to the children when they are very far away. Some rooms have pictures of the children on the walls. Children often look at these pictures every day and feel happy.

In this room, the people help the children learn how to be good people. They teachers lead the children in games and stories, and the children learn to share and be kind to each other. The room is a safe place where children can feel happy and safe when their parents are not there.

Names for this place come from the word for baby. A baby room is like a room for a baby.
