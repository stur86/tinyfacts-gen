A person or thing has a number of things it is not allowed to do. This is a way to keep what is important from getting too big or too strong.

Imagine you want to build a tall wall to keep animals out. If you build it all the way around and make it very strong, no animals can get in. But if you only build it high up and then stop, animals can still jump over it. They just have to go around the wall. They can't climb it.

This is like a way to stop people from doing something. It helps us stay safe and keep things in order.
