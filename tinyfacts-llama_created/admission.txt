To admit something means to say it is true. You are not afraid to tell the truth. It is about being open.

If you are in a room with people you do not know, and you see something that makes you feel bad, you might admit it. You say, I am sorry, the food is bad. This shows you are not afraid to be wrong.

When you do this, you are taking the first step. You are opening your heart. It is the start of a new conversation.

Sometimes, you admit things that hurt you. You might admit you were wrong. This can help you understand better and change how you act in the next day.

You can also admit when you do not know something. If you are not sure if a game is right, you admit it. You ask the teacher or another person who knows.

Being admitted can feel like a big thing. You might feel happy or a little nervous. It is a sign that you are trying to be the best you can be.
