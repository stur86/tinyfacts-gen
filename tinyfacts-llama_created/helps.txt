To help means you give someone another person to do the job for them. This can be important when a person is very busy or needs to do a job that is hard.

Imagine you are working in a store. You are very good at how to make the clothes and hair of the people. But one day, the person in the back who is in control of the store comes to you and says,  Please, you can help me for just a minute. You go to the back room and do the job for them. After a minute, the person in the back room says, thank you, and they let the person in front of them know that the job is done.

You also can help if a friend is sick. You might bring them food or do things that help them feel better. This is because you care about them and want them to feel better.

In some places, you can help if you are the one who breaks something. You might tell the police if you see a person who did something wrong. Or you might help a person who is lost. If you find a lost child, you can look for them and tell their parents where they are.

You can also help if you are in a group and someone does not want to follow the plan. You might step forward and say, I think we should do this instead. This helps the group because you are showing them that you care about what is best for everyone.

Finally, you can help if you are working with a group. You might do a job that is hard, but you do it because you know it is important for the group. This is because you know that together, you are stronger than you are alone.
