To find something means to look for it. You might look in a room, a park, or a book. If you find a thing you want, you feel happy. You might find a friend, a book you love, or a game you play.

To decide means to pick which one is best. If you have many things to do, you need to decide which one to do first. You might want to go to a movie, play a game, or talk to a friend. You use your brain to think about which one is the best.

To be something means to become a thing. If you are happy, you are a good person. If you are angry, you are a person who is very mad. You might become a child if you are very young. You might become an old person as you get older.

To have something means to have a thing. If you have a book, you can read it. If you have money, you can buy things you want. If you have a friend, you feel safe with them.

To know something means to understand it. You know that a tree is green because green is a color. You know that a car goes fast because it has a hard body. You know that a day is a time when the sun is up.

To do something means to act. If you say you are going to do something, you must do it. If you say you love a person, you must follow your heart and do what is right.
