To send something from one place to another means to move it so it reaches another person or thing. This happens when you write a word or a picture on a piece of paper and give it to a friend or a child to hold.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it to your friend who is standing far away. The ball goes to your friend and then it goes to the top of the wall. The wall is the second place the ball is sent. Then, the ball goes down and lands in the hand of a child. The child picks it up and brings it home.

Things can be sent through air, water, or through the ground. We send food to people who live far away. We send pictures to people who are very busy. We send money to people who work hard. And we send our voices so we can talk to each other.

When you send something, you are sending it into the world. It goes across the space to reach another person. This happens even when you are not there. You send your heart to someone who loves you. Your love is in your heart, and it will reach their heart when they see you.

The word is important because it helps us understand how things move and how they reach us.
