To check something means to see if it is true. You do this when you look at a piece of paper. If the paper says a word is there, you have checked it.

You can check a person too. If a person is telling the truth, you are checking them. You want to make sure they are not lying. You look at their face and their words. If they are being themselves, you know they are telling the truth.

You can also check a thing. If you want to know if a thing is there, you check it. If the thing is in a room, you are checking it. If you are looking for a book, you are checking to see if it is under the table.

Sometimes, we use this word with numbers. If you have a number, you are checking it. You want to make sure it is right. If you find out the number is wrong, you tell the people who made the number.

In life, we need to check our plans. If you have a plan to go to school, you need to check if you are going. You look at your book and your clothes. If everything looks okay, you feel safe.
