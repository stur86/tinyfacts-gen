To reach a point is to come to a way about something. It is the end of a story. If you know the whole story, you know the way it ends. You can see the end before you start the story. If you do not reach the end, the story is still going.

You can also reach a point by moving. If you walk a long way, you reach a place. You can reach the top of a tall place. You can reach your bed. You can reach a place where you can sit and rest.

When you have an idea, you reach it. You are the one who thinks about what you want. You may think for a long time. You may think about many things before you reach a point. You reach a point when you know you are right.

Sometimes, you reach a point by doing something. You might learn to play a game. You might learn to do a job. You might learn to tell the truth. When you reach a point, you know you have learned or found something new.

To reach a point is different from to decide. To decide is when you think about something and you want to do it. You might decide to go to a place. You might decide to do something. To reach a point is when you have done it.
