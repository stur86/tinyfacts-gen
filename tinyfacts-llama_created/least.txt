To have the least means that something is not the most. It does not say you have more. You can have many things, or one thing and one other. The one is not the best, but it is the one you have.

You can also say you are the least. If you are the person who is not the best, then you are the one who has the least. It is not about being the one who is best. It is about having the least.

Imagine you have a bag of money. You give some to a friend. You still have the bag. You did not lose the bag. You just took some of it. The bag is still the same, but it is now smaller. This is like what it means to have the least.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about things that are not very good. If a movie is very bad, you might say it is the least good. It is not the best, but it is the one you think is the least good.
