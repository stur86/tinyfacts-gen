To grow something is to make it start to grow. It happens when a small thing is dropped into a ground that can not stand on its own. This tiny thing is like a tiny child. It needs something to drink and to use the ground to make it grow.

The ground needs to be warm. When the ground is warm, the tiny child starts to drink. The child takes in water from the ground and from the air. It uses the water to grow. It also uses the ground to make the ground strong.

After a while, the child starts to grow leaves. These leaves are like tiny green things. They drink the ground and the air. They make the ground and the ground have food to give to the next tiny child.

When the ground has many leaves, the ground is ready to grow more. The ground can grow many more tiny children. These tiny children will go and drink from the ground, make the ground strong, and grow into new trees, or into new tiny children.

People use this word to talk about things that grow. They say a tree grows from a small thing. They say a person grows from a tiny child. This word is very important because it shows how things start and grow into what we see today.
