A person who watches a thing happens when they see it. They do not do it because they want to, but because they are there. They are watching the people, the things, or the stories.

You can watch a movie. You are in a room with many other people and you are not doing anything. You are watching a story with sounds and pictures. You see the people and the things on the picture. You feel like you are with them.

You can also watch a person talk. You sit in a room and you listen. You see their face and their body. You see their words. You understand what they say. You might learn something new or feel a way.

Sometimes, you watch a game. A team of people is doing a thing. They are pushing a ball or throwing a thing. The people are watching the game. They watch to see who will windowing. They want to know if their team will be the best.

You can watch a show too. A person makes a story or makes a show for many minutes. They are acting. You watch them do it. You might laugh or feel happy. You might think, I like this person.

In the day, you might watch a show. You watch a show while you eat or while you move around. You might watch something when you are tired. You might watch something when you need to relax.

Sometimes, you watch a thing that is not a person. You watch a movie with a story, a story with sounds, or a story with pictures. You do not see a person. You see a place or a thing. You see the story.
