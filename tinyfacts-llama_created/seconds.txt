Seconds are small parts of a big group. You can put many of them together to make a very long one. A second is one hundred ten of a hundred. This is why one second is one hundred ten of a hundred ten of a hundred, which is one hundred ten of a hundred hundred of a hundred.

Imagine you start a very fast race. You run one second before the other person. You are right behind them. Then, in one second, you are in front. You cross the line first. This is how a second is important.

In our lives, seconds are the time between two things. When you watch a movie, there are many seconds. When you eat a thing, there are many seconds. When you talk, there are many seconds. You can not see a second. You can only hear it.

When you do a small job, you might do one second at a time. You do one small thing, then you do another small thing, and another small thing. After many seconds, you finish. You feel a feeling of being done.

We also use seconds to talk about time. If it is one minute and one second, that is two minutes. If it is one minute and three seconds, that is two minutes and three hundred ten of a hundred.
