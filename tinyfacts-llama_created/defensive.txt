To be in a state of being ready can be very important, especially when things get moving. You might be feeling your heart beating fast because of a big idea or a strong feeling. You want to keep yourself safe and make sure you are not hurt.

Imagine you are in a room with people you do not know. You want to stay in your seat so they do not attack you. You are in a state of being ready. This is what you do with your body. You stand up, you look around, and you are ready to act if something happens.

In a game, a player is in a state of being ready. They are ready to hit a ball or to run with the ball. This is when they are not thinking about something else, and their mind is focused only on doing what they need to do.

Sometimes you are in a state of being ready to give a talk. You want to show your ideas to others. You are ready to answer questions and to share your ideas. You are ready to be heard.

This is also what you do with your mind. If you are feeling safe, you can relax. You feel calm and your mind is quiet. You are ready to think about your day.
