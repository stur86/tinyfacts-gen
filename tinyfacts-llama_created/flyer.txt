A paper thing that you send through the air is called a fly. You can make a fly by taking a piece of paper and a small bit of stuff from your mouth. You cut out the middle and the top. This makes a small room in the middle, and a bottom part. The room is round and it lets the air go in and out. The bottom part has a hard cover.

When you want to send the fly to a person, you fill the room inside the paper with air. You might put some stuff into the room so that the fly flies in the right direction. You then put the paper with the room and the stuff in a bag. When the bag gets close to the person, the bag gets heavy with air. This makes the bag go up and the room goes down. The room opens and the air goes out, and the room closes again.

The room is very small. Inside the room, there is a tiny bit of air that is not enough for a person to breathe. It is too small to be seen. But because the room is moving through the air, the tiny bit of air gets to the person. The person feels a little bit of air when the room passes over them.

You can also make a fly that flies by moving the paper around in the air. This is like a tiny car. The person holds the paper and pulls it around in a round way. The paper makes a sound, like a small moving thing, and the person feels the wind.

Some people make fly things to tell stories or to show pictures. They use a small bit of stuff to make the pictures, and a paper to hold them. They can fly above a city, over a field, or over a wide water. The fly moves around in a round way, and the person watches from a window.

Fly things can also carry money or other things for people to send to each other. When a person wants to send money to someone far away, they make a fly. They fill the room with the money, and the person can pick up the fly from a box. The fly flies to the person, and the money is sent.
