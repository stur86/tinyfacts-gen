A thing that crosses a big body of water is called a water way. A long time ago, people built these things so they could move from one side of a wide water to the other. They used trees with strong parts or hard stuff from the ground to hold them up.

Now, these long pieces of wood are not always made of the same stuff. Some are very wide and strong, while others are very small and not as strong. But they all help us cross the water.

When a water way is on land, it is like a long road that people walk on or drive on. Sometimes it is very high and goes over a big round thing. This is called a round way. It goes over many of these long pieces of wood.

Sometimes, a round way is on land, and a long piece of wood in the middle goes over it. This is called a round way with a round. The round way in the middle goes over the wood, and the wood goes over the round way.

There are also long pieces of wood that go across many water ways. These can be many hundreds of feet long. They are very strong because they are made of many pieces of wood.

We need water ways so we can get from one place to another. We use them to get food from far away places, to get to places where the air is good, or to get to places where the water is nice for drinking.
