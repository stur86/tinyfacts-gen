An area of something is the part that is seen by everyone at the same time. It is the part of a picture or a story that everyone understands. Think of a big room with many people. You do not see every single person, but you can see the faces of many of them all at once.

Imagine you are at a big music place. There are many people playing different things. You can hear the sound of one person and the sound of another person, but you do not see them. You see the whole place and all the people together. Every person is in the same area of the music and everyone hears the same sound. This is the same with a story. When many people read the same story, everyone understands the same things and sees the same pictures.

An area is not just a thing you see. It can also be a way to look at something. You can look at an area of a room to see how it is laid out. You can look at an area of a city to see how many people live there. You can look at an area of a picture to see what is happening. An area can be a place, a time, or a feeling.

You can also say that something has many areas. Imagine you have a box of food. You can open the box and see inside. Each part of the box is an area. There is the top part, the side parts, the bottom part, and the inside parts. Each area is a way to look at the food inside. You can see what is in each area by looking at the food in that area. This is how we understand many things in life.
