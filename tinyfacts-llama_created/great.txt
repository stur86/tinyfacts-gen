To great someone means to tell them how much you like them. When you are great at something, you are very good at it. You might do a job that many other people do not do. You are very strong or very fast or very good at making things.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If someone is great, it means they are very important. They have a lot of power and make a lot of money. They are usually the ones who get the biggest deals.

Sometimes, to great a person is to tell them that you like them. This can be a big thing to do. You might feel happy or nervous. You might want to go to their house or see them again.

It is also about how much you care for someone. If you are great at someone, you care about them very much. You want them to know that you like them. You might write a letter or draw a picture.

So, to great someone is to show that you really like them and that you want them to know it.
