To be nice is to be kind and not to be mean. You do not use words that hurt. You do not use your feet to hurt someone. You show them you care. You give them a small thing they want. You also listen to what they say.

To be a bad person is to act in a way that hurts many people. You might say a word that is very bad. You might do something that makes a child cry. You might do something that makes a dog run away. You act like you are the one in control. You do not care about anyone else.

A person who is nice is a good friend. They are the ones you go to when you are sad. They listen to you and help you feel better. They make you feel safe. They help you do things you want to do. They are the ones you trust.

A person who is bad is the one who does not want to be nice. They say bad things. They do things that hurt people. They act like they are the one who gets to be in control. They do not care about anyone else. They only care about what they want.

To be nice is to show love. It is to put another person first. It is to want them to be happy. To be a bad person is to want to be in control. It is to want to hurt others.
