To climb means to go up a long way on a high ground. You can climb a tree to get a better look at what is around you. You can also climb a rock wall or a big white piece of ground that is hard to walk on.

Imagine you are a cat. You want to get to a high place where you can see the sky. You walk across a green field. You run up a long walk with many steps. You go over a small green top. Then you go up more steps and reach the top. You feel like you are flying! You are climbing the ground.

You can climb a big building with many floors. You walk up the long walk with many steps. You go up the long steps that go up the side of the building. When you reach the top, you can see everything that is around you.

Sometimes you climb a thing that is very high up in the air. You can see the city from far away. You might climb a long walk made of hard white things. You go up high and feel the wind in your hair.

To climb is to go up on your own. It is a thing you do when you want to be high up in the world.
