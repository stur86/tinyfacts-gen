To be a student is to be young and to learn. Many people do this job. They go to a school or a place with a teacher.

A student is usually a person who is still learning. They might be very new to a place or a thing. They ask questions and try to find answers. They do not know everything yet.

A student might study things that are very hard. They might learn about a world that is very far away or a way of making things that you use every day. They might also learn how to do things with their hands.

Sometimes students do not learn well. They might not understand a word. They might get confused. Or they might do something that is not right. But most students try their best. They want to learn and to become better.

Students live in a place with many other people. They share a room with other students. They sit at a table with a table and chairs. They write in a book. They talk to a teacher or a friend. They might make a picture or write a story.

Being a student is a big job. Students need to listen and learn. They need to be quiet and not make a lot of noise. They need to follow a teacher and do what the teacher tells them. They need to be kind and show they care about the other students.

At the end of the day, a student goes home. They might feel happy because they learned something new. They might feel a little sad because they do not see their friends for a long time. But they are always looking forward to the next day.
