To be younger means you are less old than other people. When a baby is very young, they are younger than the people who are older. An older person might have been in the world for a hundred years or more. A younger person has not yet grown very old.

Think of this like a line. The line shows how old you are, from very young to very old. When you are younger, you are on the left side of the line. When you are older, you are on the right side of the line.

A younger person is often more happy and full of life. They might feel like they can do anything because they are not yet tired. They might want to go to school and play games with their friends. An older person might feel a little more calm, because they know they have lived through many years of things.

You can also be younger in the way you feel. If you are very excited or very happy, you might feel like you are the age where you are. In this case, you are younger than the people who are older than you.
