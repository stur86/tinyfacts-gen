Night air is the dark air at night. You can feel it when the sun goes down and the sky turns to dark grey. This air is cold and can make the wind feel like a big hand on your face.

The night is when the stars are up. They are like a big group of tiny lights hanging in the sky. You can see them with your eyes, but they are much smaller than the bright lights we see during the day.

Some nights are very quiet. There are no cars or people moving. The only sound is the wind and the way the trees cold in the night. It is a time to rest and dream.

In some places, the night is warm. The air can feel like a soft cover over a bed. People like to sit in the dark and listen to music. It is a time to be alone with your mind.

Night air is all around us. We need it to breathe. It makes our bodies feel good when we sleep. Without it, we would not be able to move or think.

Sometimes, people say that the night is a time for power. They say that the dark is where the strong ones live. They say that when the night is dark, bad people are more afraid. But this is not true. The night is just a time like any other.
