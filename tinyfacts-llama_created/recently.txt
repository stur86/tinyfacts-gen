To do something is to act now. When you say you are going to do something, you are saying you are going now.

When you say you did something, you tell everyone you are doing it right now. You tell people you went to the store or you made a call. 

You can also say you did something very quickly. If you went to the store and picked up a book, you did it in a few minutes. You can do things very fast or very slow. 

Think of a time when you did something with your friends. You might have gone to a movie or played a game together. This was a time that was just for you and your friends. It was a moment from your day. 

Sometimes, people say we are in a new time. This means that now is different from before. We are seeing a world that is different. We have new things, new ways of living, and new ways of talking. 

You can also use this word when you are talking about the past. If you tell someone you did something, you are showing them what happened before. This helps us remember what has passed.
