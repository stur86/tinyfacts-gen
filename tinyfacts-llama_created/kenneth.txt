A person is a human being. They are the ones you know and the ones you do not know. They live their life in the world, with their own way. A person can be a boy, a girl, or a person who is in between. They have their own name and a face and a body and a mind. They can do many things, like walk, run, work, or play games.

A person can also be a part of a group. A group is a group of people who are close to each other. They share the same ideas or the same things. They work together and help each other. In this way, the group is like a family.

Sometimes, a person is a person who has done something very bad. They have done something wrong that hurts many people. This is when a person is called a bad person. They should not be around people because they do not follow the right way to live.

A person can also be a person who is very old. This is when a person has lived for a very, very long time. They have seen many years and have seen many things. They have learned many things and have seen how people change.

People are important because we help each other. We share our food, our clothes, and our houses. We talk and listen to each other. We are all the same, but we also have different ways. We live our lives and make our own way.
