To have a use is to have a reason. Everything has a use. If you have food, you use it to eat. If you have clothes, you use them to cover your body. If you have money, you use it to buy things you want.

If you are a person, your life has a use. You live so you can live. You work so you can buy food and clothes. You love so you can spend time with the people you care about. You die so you do not leave others behind.

If you are a thing, your life has a use too. A chair sits so you do not step on it. A car moves so you can go to where you want to go. A book tells you stories so you can learn. A tree gives you air so you can breathe.

You can also have a use for a person. If you help someone, you are using them for a good reason. If you tell a story, you are using your words to reach others. If you listen to someone, you are using your ears to understand them.

Having a use is important. Without it, you would not be able to live.
