To make something means to change a thing so it is ready to use. This can be done by many people in different ways. One way is to put food into a box. When you open the box, the food has come out. Another way is to use your hands to make a thing. If you make a book, you write words on it with a small thing you hold. You also add pictures. You put the book in a place where people can read it.

We also make our own bodies. When a baby is growing inside a mother, the mother makes food for the baby. The mother drinks this food, and it goes into the baby. The baby grows and becomes strong. The mother also makes the baby breathe air. This happens inside the mother too. So, the mother is like the one who makes the baby.

You can also make things that are not from a body. You can use your hands to make a thing from a piece of wood, paper, or something else you found. You might make a thing to put your hands on. You can also make a thing to hold things. A box, a bag, or a chair are all made from these kinds of things.

When you say someone makes something, you are talking about how they work. They might work with other people. They might have a plan. They might use a computer or a thing to do their work. The person also does the work by their own hands. They do not need to wait for someone else to do it for them.
