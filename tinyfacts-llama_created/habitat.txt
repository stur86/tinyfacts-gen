To live in a place is to live in a home. Every animal and every thing needs a place to rest and a place to eat. If a thing is alone in the dark night, it does not feel safe.

A home is a place where a person or a group of people live. It has a big room where they sit and a room with a bed. Sometimes the home is inside a big thing that moves from place to place. Other times the home is in the ground. The home is where a person or a group of people are safe and stay warm.

When a thing lives in a place, it is living in a home. This is important because the home is where the thing gets food. Food is what makes the body strong. When a thing is in a good home, it can stay and grow. When a thing is alone, it can not find food or stay safe. The home helps the thing to stay in one place.
