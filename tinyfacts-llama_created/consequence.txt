To have a person or thing as a direction means you are doing what you decided to do. This is because of what you did. If you pick a book you like, then you will read it and learn new things.

Think about a game. You play a game with a friend. You are happy and your friend is happy. But then, something bad happens. You fall down and hurt your hand. This is a direction because you did what you decided to do.

You can also have a person as a direction. This is when you do something wrong and then the person you did it with has to feel the pain. They might be angry or sad. This is a way of saying you hurt the person you love.

Sometimes, things happen that you did not plan. You might have wanted to go to the park but it was raining. You still have to stay home. This is also a direction because you did not plan it, but you still did it.

You can also have a thing as a direction. This is when you do something that is not right. You might take a thing that does not go with your room. You might eat food you do not like. This is not a good thing. It is a direction because you did what you decided to do, even if it was wrong.

So, a direction is when you decide to do something and you follow it.
