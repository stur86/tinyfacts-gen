Does is a word that means you are sure something is true.

Imagine you see a tree that is green. If you look at the tree again, it will still be green. You are sure it is green.

This word is used when you ask if something is true. If you do not know, you can ask. If you are sure, you can say you do. You are doing something because you are sure it is right.

You can also use it to talk about what you feel.

If you love someone, you might say you do. You are in love.

This word is important because it helps us talk about what is real. It helps us know if something is true or not.
