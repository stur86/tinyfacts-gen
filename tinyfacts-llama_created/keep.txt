To keep something means you keep it safe. You do not throw it away, even if it is old or broken. You keep it in a box or a bag so it is not hurt. You might keep a picture from a happy day with your family. You might keep a small thing you love like a book or a small animal. 

You can keep your heart safe too. When you feel safe, you do not worry about what might happen next. You stay calm and can think about other things. This helps you feel happy and strong.

Sometimes, keeping something means you do not want to give it away. You keep a word you did not say, a book you read, or a piece of music you like. You keep it because you are happy about it, even if it is not for everyone to see. This can help you feel safe in your own way.
