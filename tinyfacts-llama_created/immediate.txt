To act immediately means you do something right away. Imagine you see a cat running across the street. You do not need to think. You just act. You jump over the wall and get to the other side. This is what immediately means.

If you are waiting for a friend to come home, waiting is not immediately. You are still waiting. You do not do anything. You just sit and wait. It is a long time before your friend comes.

But if you see a problem that needs to be fixed, you act immediately. You might call a friend to help you. You might go to a store to buy a thing you need. You are doing something right away. This is what immediately means.

Sometimes, when you act, you might feel a rush of power. You might feel like you can do anything. But this is not always good. If you act too quickly, you might hurt yourself or someone else. It is better to wait and think before you act.

In life, you need to act sometimes. You need to act to get help. You need to act to tell a friend you care. You need to act to fix a problem. But you also need to wait. Waiting can help you think. Waiting can help you feel safe. Waiting can help you feel calm.

So, to act immediately means you do something right away. It means you act without thinking too much. It means you act without waiting for a long time.
