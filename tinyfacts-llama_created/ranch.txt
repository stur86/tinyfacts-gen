A big area of land where you grow many kinds of food. This is where people live with many animals. The animals are usually big and strong to help take care of the land. The person who owns this land is the owner. The owner gives the animals food and takes care of them until they are ready to be taken away to a new home. The land is often green because many trees grow there. In the summer, the land is hot and the air can be very heavy with water from the rain. In the fall, the trees start to change and the air gets cold. In the cold months, the animals drink a lot of water to stay warm.
