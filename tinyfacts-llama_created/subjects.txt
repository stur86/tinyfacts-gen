People use the word this and that to talk about things. This is for the people you are talking to. This is for your friends. This is for your family. It is the name of a person.

You also use this to talk about things you are learning. You might read a book. You might watch a movie. You are learning about a new thing. This is the book or the movie.

Sometimes, you say, you are studying this. This means you are learning about a thing. You are thinking about it and trying to remember it.

In a school, you might have many classes. One class is about reading. Another class is about writing. A third class is about talking. You study each of these classes. You learn new things about each one.

The word also means the people you work with. You might work with friends. You might work with people you know. You learn from them and they learn from you.

If you say, I am a good student, it means you are doing well in your classes. You are learning and growing. You are becoming a person.

So, the word this and that is about names and what you are talking about. It helps you know who is there and what to do.
