To warm something means you make it feel warmer. You can do this with your body. When you run or you get cold, your body gets warm. Your face feels better and your arms feel better too.

You can also warm things. If you put a hot cup of coffee in a cup, the coffee gets warm. If you put a cup of cold water in a hot cup, the water gets warm. This makes everything feel nicer and makes it easier to drink.

Sometimes, things that are made of hard stuff get warm. A rock might feel warm to your hand. Or a hard piece of wood might feel warm when you touch it. This happens because of the sun or because of your body.

You can also warm a room. If you put a few pieces of wood in the fire, the room gets warmer. The fire makes the air get warmer and the walls get warmer. This makes everything feel nicer and makes the room feel more like a home.
