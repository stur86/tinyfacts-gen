While is a word that is used to talk about time and how things happen.

When you are talking to a friend, you can say something while you are listening. You are not really paying attention to what they say at that moment. You are still thinking about what you want to say next. This is called being in the middle of a conversation.

Imagine you are watching a movie. You see something very interesting. You might say, while the movie is happening, I am not looking at the words on the paper. I am looking at the picture.

This word can also mean that a thing is happening while another thing is still going on. You can be working and at the same time you are watching a funny movie. You might laugh while you work, but you do not stop.

Sometimes, you might be in a situation and you are not sure what to do. You might feel like you are standing in a cross street while you are trying to get to the other side of the city. Your heart is in your chest, and you are feeling very much afraid.

In these situations, people try to stay calm. They might ask you to help them, or they might just sit quietly and wait for what happens next.

So, while is a word that helps us talk about how time works and how we move between different moments in life.
