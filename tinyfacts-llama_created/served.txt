To be seen is to have been seen. It is the act of being noticed by another person. If you walk down the street and everyone looks at you, you have been seen. This is a good thing. If you are in a room with many people and you do not notice them, you have not been seen.

You can also see things. When you look at a book, you are seeing its words. You see the words that tell a story. You see the pictures that help to show the story. You can see the colors of a picture. You can see the way a light falls on a room. You can see the way a person looks.

Sometimes, you see things that are not there. You see a thing that is not really there. You see the air moving around you, even when there is no one around. You see the light from the sun. You see the way the street looks at night. You see the way the world looks when the sun is not there.

When you see something, you are part of it. You are a part of the world. You are a part of the people around you. You are part of the light and the air and the ground. You are a part of everything.
