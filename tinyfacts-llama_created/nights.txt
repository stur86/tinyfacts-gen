To nights is to go from the day to the night.

When the sun is up, it is day. During the day, the sun is in the sky. People go to school, work, and play. After the day ends and the sun starts to go down, it is time to go to bed.

As the night starts, the dark sky comes out. The stars and the light from the world go out. This is when the night is most quiet. Some people like this time because it is still dark, so they can see things that are not there.

In the night, animals are more quiet. They are not looking for food like they are during the day. They are resting.

People often use this word to talk about things at night. They might say, something that is done at night is important. They might talk about how people do things when they are alone at night.
