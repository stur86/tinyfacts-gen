To carry something heavy is to move it around. You can do this by walking, by driving a car, or by pulling a big box on a long line.

Imagine you have a heavy bag. You want to go to the store. You can carry the bag on your back. This is easy. If you have many bags, it is even better. You can carry them all at once.

Sometimes, a big group of people carry a heavy thing. They use many lines and many people. They walk or run. They move the thing from one place to another.

If you have a lot of work, you can do it in one night. This is called a lot. You are carrying many things at the same time. It is a busy night.

To carry a lot is to move it many times. You do not just move one thing. You move many things. This makes the job harder. But it also makes you strong because you are moving something heavy many times.
