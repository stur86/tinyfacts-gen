To class someone or something is to put them into groups.

Imagine you have a big box with many small boxes inside. You put a cat in one box and a dog in another. Then you put a dog in the box with cats because both are animals. You put a book in the box with other books. You put a book in the box with other books too.

This helps you see what you have and what is different. You can look at the boxes and see how many boxes each one has. You can also use this to decide what to do next. If you find a person who is different from everyone else, you might want to look at the boxes to see where they fit. You might ask them questions or look at their clothes to see if they are in the right box.

In a school, a teacher might want to put students into groups so they work together. They might put the ones who like to read in one group and the ones who like to write in another. This helps them learn from each other and work together to do their jobs.

People also use this word to talk about things. If you say a tree is a green thing, you are classing it as a green thing. This helps us understand what we mean when we talk about things.
