To go to a place where people are not living is to go to a place where they are sleeping. It is like going to a friend.

Imagine you are very cold. You want to go to a warm place where the air is warm. You go to a small area where many people live. They have hot food and warm beds. They say, let us go to your place for a day. They bring you food and help you to feel better.

You can go to a place for many reasons. One reason is to have fun. You can go to a far place where you do not know anyone. You meet new people. You play games with them. You learn about their way of life.

Another reason is to help those who are sick. You go to a place where a person is very sick. You help them. You give them food to eat. You give them water to drink. You keep them safe.

Sometimes you go to a place to work. You go to a big place with many people. You do a job. You make things for people. You help them. You make their lives better.

You can also go to a place for school. You go to a big building where many children go. They learn. They study. They make new friends. They learn about the world.

Going to a place is a big thing. You have to be very ready. You must know what to do. You must be kind. You must follow the people who are in control. If you follow them, you are safe. If you do not follow them, you might get hurt.
