A hospital is a place where people get very hurt. If you have a cut that needs to get better, you go there. A doctor is a man with a high school job who helps fix your body. They use things that can stop you from getting worse. There are many rooms. Some rooms are for people who are very sick. Some rooms are for people who are not very sick. Some rooms are for people who are between these two things.

There are lots of people in the hospital. They all work together to help the people who are there. They give them food, drink, and things to make them feel better. Some people stay for a few days. Some people stay for weeks or even months.

Sometimes, people go to a hospital if they are not sure what is wrong. A doctor might not know right away. They might go to see other people who have the same problem first. If they find out that you have the same problem, they can go to the hospital later.

You might go to a hospital if you have a baby. A baby needs to be very small and tiny to stay safe. The people in the hospital help the baby and the mother so the baby can grow well.
