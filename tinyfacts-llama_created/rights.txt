To have a right means you are allowed to do something. It is about what you can do because of who you are or what you own.

Imagine you are a child. You want to play with a ball. You have the right to do this. This is because you are a part of a group of people who tell what is allowed. When everyone agrees, you can play. If someone says you can't, the group says you are wrong. This is a right.

You also have a right to say what you think. When you tell your teacher that you feel sad, you have the right to say these words. This helps people understand how you feel.

Sometimes, someone might take away a right. They might take the ball from the child or tell you you can't speak. This is not right. You still have the right to be heard and to feel safe.

Everyone has a right, no matter who they are. This is why we have things like a job, a home, and the right to live in a safe place. These are the rights we should always try to follow.
