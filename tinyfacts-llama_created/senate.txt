A group of people makes big hard but important jobs. They sit in a place with many chairs. They talk a lot and try to find the best answers for the whole town. They help people make sure everyone is safe.

Some big jobs are made by the people from each state. The ones from the state with the biggest land have more power. But other jobs are made by people from all the states together. The group makes sure everyone in the town has the same rights.

They hear from people and do things that help everyone. They also help people who can't talk for themselves. They are like the town police, but instead they help the whole town.

There are many different kinds of groups like this. One group is in a city. Another is in a state. Another is in the whole land. They all work together to make sure everyone is safe and happy.
