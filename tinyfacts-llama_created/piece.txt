A piece is something that is made from many small parts. These parts are made to work together. When you have many pieces, you can make many things. You can build a table, a car, or a house.

Imagine you have a group of people. Each person has a small job. They work together to make a big thing that is stronger and better. If one person does not do their job, the whole group falls down. So, the person who did the job is important.

A piece can also mean a small part of something. If you cut a book into many small pieces, you have many pieces of the book. Each piece can be read by you alone. If you are feeling sad, you might feel a piece of yourself is broken.

In games and movies, a piece can also mean the last word before the end. When you know the last word, you know what happened. You can feel a sense of what is happening. It is like you are the one who decided what happens next.

You can also say a piece is something that is broken. If a ball is broken, it is not good anymore. You might need to get a new one. This is because the parts are not working well together anymore.
