Some things are hot when they are very hot. A fire is very hot. A fire makes the air around it warm. You can see the light of the fire and it looks bright.

Other things are hot because of how they work. A car has a hot part inside it that makes the car move. The hot part is very strong and makes a lot of power. This power makes the car go very fast.
