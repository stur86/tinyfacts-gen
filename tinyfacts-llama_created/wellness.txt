To feel well is to be in a good state of being. You are not sick. Your body is not hurt. You do not feel pain. You are not worried. You are calm.

Well being is about how you feel in your body and in your mind. It is about how happy you are and how safe you feel.

When you are well you can do what you want. You can play with your friends. You can go to school. You can eat the food you like. You can do what makes you happy. You can laugh and smile. You are not afraid of anything. You are happy.

But being well is not just about how you feel. You must also take care of yourself. You must eat good food. You must drink water. You must go to bed at night. You must be kind to others. You must do things that make everyone else happy.

Sometimes people say that being well is like being a tree. The tree is standing tall. It is not broken. It is strong. It is not afraid. It is happy. A person can feel like a tree when they are well. They feel safe and happy. They are not afraid of anything.

You can feel well in your body too. You can feel your heart. You can feel your feet. You can feel your mind. You can feel calm. You can feel happy. You can feel like you are doing what you want.

Being well is important. It is important for your body, your mind, and your friends. It is important for your life.
