To fight means you use your body to try to stop someone else. This happens when two people or animals want to be the one in control.

Imagine you want to eat a big piece of food. If you want it very much, you might want to run to get it. But if you are very strong, you might also want to use your body to hold the food. You might hit the floor or push the food away. This is fighting.

In a big fight, many people are there. They might hit each other with their arms or hands. Some use their feet. Some use their heads. Some even use their eyes to look at the other person. The person who has the most power usually gets to decide who is the one in control.

Sometimes, fighting is about a dog or a cat. A dog might fight with another dog if it feels it is in trouble. A cat might fight with another cat if it feels it is in a place it does not like.

It is also about a fight that happens on a high place. People might go up a tall building to fight for a high spot. They might use their arms and legs to try to reach a spot on top of the building.

Fighting can be very bad. It can hurt people and animals. It can break things and make a lot of noise. It can even make the sky look red. So, it is not something to do all the time.
