To find something means to look for it. You might look in the dark, or you might walk through a crowd. You might use your eyes to see a small thing that is very small. Or you might use your hands to feel it.

Think about a child. They want a ball. They look around. They look on the floor, under the table, and behind the chair. They find the ball. They got it.

You can also find something when you are not looking. You might find a book that you left in a room. You might find a door that you forgot to close. You are surprised.

To find something can be hard. You might look all day and still not see it. But you keep looking. You are not giving up. You are finding it.

It is like a game. You are looking for something. You are trying to find it. When you find it, you feel happy. You have done it.

You can find people. You can find things. You can even find a way. When you find a way, you can do things faster. You do not have to keep looking.
