A group of people who are in the most number is called a group. When many people are in the same group, they are said to be in the most number.

Think of a big room with many people sitting in a line. If you counters all the people, you find out that many more people are sitting there than people are standing. In this case, the room is in the most number.

Sometimes, when you have a group of people who are in the same way, you can call them the same group. This is when the people are very good at what they do and the group is like a family.

A group can also have a leader. This person makes many of the group decide what to do. They are like a strong leader. When many people follow this person, they become the group. In this way, the group is under the leader.

In a group, people often talk to each other. They may ask questions or share their ideas. They can also laugh together or cry together when they feel sad.

In a school, there are many students. Some students do better than others. In a group of students, the ones who do better are often called the most important. They are the ones who get the best marks and get to sit near the front. In this way, the group is in the most number.

A group can also be a group of things. Imagine you have a box with many small balls. If you take a ball out, you have one less ball. In this case, the group of balls is the same group. You are still in the same group.

When many people say the same thing, it is called a group. When many people agree on something, it is called a group. When many people do the same thing, it is called a group.
