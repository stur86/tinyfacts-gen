To have a friend or a person you work with who is very good at what you do is called a friend. A friend is someone you like and trust.

If you have a friend you do things with, you talk, you laugh, you play, or you run around. You are happy when the other person is happy. You feel safe with them. You can be open with them.

But in the office, you also work with other people. You have to share ideas, tell them what you think, and listen to what they say. You may have a person you like in your team. You work well with them. You help each other. You understand each other.

A person who works with you and is very good at their job is a good friend. You like them because they are great at their work. You trust them because you know they will not do anything bad.

You can also say a person is a friend. This means you are happy to see them every day. You know they will be there and they will help you if you need it.

Having a good friend is important. It makes you feel good when you are happy. It makes your life better.
