A sound is a way to use your voice or make things that make a sound. Every sound is different. Some sounds are high, some are low, some are fast, and some are slow. When you make a sound, you are using your body to make the air go in and out. This makes the air hit different parts of your face.

Imagine you are playing a long thing. You push the long thing with your fingers. The long thing makes a sound. If you push the long thing very hard, the sound is very loud. If you push it very little, the sound is very quiet. That is because the sound is coming from different parts of the long thing.

A person who makes sounds is called a sound maker. They make sounds every day. Some sound people make happy sounds. Others make sounds that are very sad. Some sound people make sounds that tell stories or talk to people. They use their faces and hands to make the sounds.

When you hear a sound, you listen. You try to understand what the sound is saying. You might hear a sound that is very high. You might hear a sound that is very low. You might hear a sound that is very fast or very slow. Your brain takes in the sound and makes it into a picture in your head.

There are also sounds that are in the air. You can hear them when you are near a big body of water. You can hear sounds from far away, even if you do not see the water. These sounds come from many places at once. They are like a big group of many sounds all happening at the same time.

Sometimes, people use sounds to tell a story. They might use a sound to show that something is happening. They might use a sound to show how something feels. This is called a sound story. It is a way to use sounds to help people understand what is happening in a story.

Finally, sounds can change. One day a song might sound happy. The next day the song might sound sad. This is because the world changes. What sounds good today might sound bad tomorrow. What sounds good tomorrow might sound good next week. Because sounds change, we have to listen to them every day to understand what is happening.
