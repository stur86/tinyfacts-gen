To years is to go one hundred years. This is a very long time. If you go one hundred years, you have been in the world for a very long time. You are not a baby anymore. You are not a child. You are a grown person.

Years are like the days in a week. But years are much bigger. There are seven days in a week, and one hundred years are like many hundreds of weeks. You can not even imagine how many years are in one hundred years. It is a very hard thing to think about.

Years are used to show how old someone is. When you are one year old, you are still a baby. When you are two years old, you are a little bit bigger. When you are ten years old, you are a child. When you are twenty years old, you are a young man. When you are one hundred years old, you are very old.
