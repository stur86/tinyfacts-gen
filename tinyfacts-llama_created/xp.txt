To do something with power is to use a strong part of the body to change the world. It is to feel the power and then act with it. It can mean to be very strong or to do a great job.

You can also do this when you play games that need a lot of power. These games can be very fast and you need to be very fast to play them. You use your body and your mind to do this. When you do this, you feel like you are on top of the world.

In some places, it means to use a computer to do many things at once very fast. You type words and the computer does them for you. You do not have to think about each word. The computer is like a very good friend that helps you do many jobs at once.

You can also do this when you work hard to build a thing. You build a house, a car, or a book. You use your hands and your brain to make it strong and safe. When you finish, you feel a great feeling because you made something new.

Sometimes, when you use power, you might feel like you are losing control. If you are very angry, you might want to hit someone. But you must not do this. This can hurt people and make them sad. So, you need to learn how to use power and how to keep it safe.
