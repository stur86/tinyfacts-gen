To fit something means to make it right for a person or an animal. When you are small, you fit into a box. When you get older, you grow into clothes that fit your body. This happens because your body is not as small as a box anymore.

When something is not quite right, it is often called not fitted. It might be too big, too small, or too heavy. Imagine a shoe that is too tight. You can not walk around because your feet hurt. This shoe is not a good fit.

Sometimes, you might try to fit something that is too big. You might wear a big shirt or a bag that is too heavy. This makes you feel strange and you might feel sad.

When you fit something, you have found the right way to hold it. It feels like it was made just for you.
