To class something means to put it with other things that are the same. It is a way to group things together so you can learn about them.

Imagine you have many books on a table. Some books are about animals, some are about stories, and some are about games. You could put all the books with animals together. You could also put all the books with stories together. You could also put all the books with games together. These groups are all the same. You can find the same kind of thing in all these groups.

In school, you might have many classes. Some classes are about reading. Some are about writing. Some are about drawing. Some are about speaking. You go to each class and listen to the teacher or read what the teacher writes on the paper.

Classes are important because they help you learn new things. You learn about the world around you. You learn about how people are. You learn about how to act in different ways. You learn about what is right and wrong.

Sometimes, you might have a class with many different people. You learn how to work with them. You learn how to listen to each person. You learn how to share your ideas. You learn how to be a team player.

In some schools, there are many classes for different kinds of work. Some people do work with computers. Some people do work with paper. Some people do work with people. Some people do work with things that fly. All these classes are the same because they all teachers teachers how to do their work.

So, classes are a way to group things together so you can learn about them. They help you get ready for life after you finish school.
