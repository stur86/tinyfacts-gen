To show something means you give a sign so everyone can see. This can be a word, a face, a hair, or a sound. You might say, I am happy. This sign lets everyone know how you are feeling.

You can also show something by doing it. If you want to tell a story, you might act like you are in a place. You act like you are a person who lives there. This helps everyone understand what you are saying.

Sometimes you want to ask a question or show you need something. You might hold up your hand and say, can you help me? This is a way to ask for help without using words.

If you are about to do something, you can also show that you are ready. You might start to move or say, I am ready. This lets everyone know you are ready to do the thing.

You can show something even when you are not doing it. You might think about a thing you want to buy. You can imagine the look on a store window or the sound of the money being taken. This is the start of your show. Your mind is doing the work, even if your body is still in the room.

When you show something, you are giving a piece of yourself to others. It helps them understand you and know how you feel.
