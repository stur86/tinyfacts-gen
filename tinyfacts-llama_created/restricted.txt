To keep something is to keep it from others. You might keep a book so you do not read it. Or you might keep a room so you do not leave it alone.

Sometimes a place is not allowed for some people. If a park is too crowded, the people who run it might close it for a few days. They can do this because they want the place to be quiet. They do not want people to be loud when they are trying to rest.

A computer has a set of words it follows. If you ask it to do something, it follows those words. But if you ask it to do something it does not know, it will not do it. The computer is not allowed to do anything wrong. If it does something bad, the people who make it might change the words.

In a room, a person has a set of words they can use. They use those words to talk to others. They are not allowed to say words that are very bad. They might have to leave the room if they say something they do not like.

In a place where people are allowed to stay, there are usually some things you can and some things you must not do. You might not drink from a cup that has been thrown away. You might not touch something that has been broken. These are the ways to keep the place safe for everyone.
