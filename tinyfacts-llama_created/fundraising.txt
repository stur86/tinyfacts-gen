To raise money is when you ask people to give you money. You do this so you can buy things you need or so you can do something that is important.

Imagine you want a new book for your school. You do not have enough money to buy it. You ask your friends and family to give you money. You write a note and give it to them. They tell you how much they want to give. You ask them to write down how much they want to give.

Most of the time, you ask people to give money in small ways. You might ask if they can give you one, two, or five pieces of money. You do this so many times. After many people give, you have enough money to buy the book.

You can also use this word to talk about a big thing that needs money. Maybe you want to build a new school in your town. People need to give money to build it. They give money to the people who are building the school. The school is not made out of money, but because many people gave it money, the school is strong.

Sometimes, people ask for money to help someone who is sick. They want to make the person feel better. They give money to help the person. This is a way to show love.

In some places, people raise money for their own fun. They do this by running a race or by doing a big act. They want to show people how strong they are.
