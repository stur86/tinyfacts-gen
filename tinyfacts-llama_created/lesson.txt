To learn something is a big job. When you are small, you need to learn how to do things that are hard. A teacher helps you. They tell you what to do and why. They also show you how to do it so you can do it later.

When you learn, you pick up things from many people. You hear from a parents, you see from a friend, you read from a book. You hear the words of a book and you remember them later. You try to do what they say.

Sometimes, you see a thing that makes you wonder. You look at it many times and you still do not understand. You ask a friend. You ask another friend. You ask a teacher. They help you understand.

Learning is a very important job. It helps you grow. When you learn, you become stronger. You learn to do things that you did not know before. You learn to be a person who can do many different things.

We also learn from our life. When you are young, you learn how to walk. You learn how to eat. You learn how to talk. You learn how to be a person. You learn how to be a person who can live with others.

Learning is always happening. You learn every day. You learn when you wake up. You learn when you go to bed. You learn when you eat food. You learn when you see a picture. You learn when you hear a song. You learn when you hear a word.
