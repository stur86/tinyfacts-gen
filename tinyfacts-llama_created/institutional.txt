In an office, there is usually one person in a high place. They are the one who makes the most important things happen. This person might have a job that is not just one job. They might be the one who decides what the office does and how it is run.

There are also groups within offices. These groups are like smaller teams. They work together on smaller things. One group might focus on making money, while another group focuses on how things are done.
