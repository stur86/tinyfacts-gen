Like means you are very much like another person or thing. You are both the same, but you also have a small bit of something else that makes you different.

When you say a person is like a dog, you mean that they look like a dog. They have the same kind of further on their head. They walk on the ground like a dog. They also use their nose to find food.

But you are not a dog. You have a hard hard thing in your hand. You use it to write words. You also have a soft thing on your feet that keeps you on the floor. You are different from the dog, and that is why they are not the same person.

You can also say someone is like a tree. They are both tall and strong, like the hard parts of a tree. They have a wide wide part on the ground, and they live on top of the ground for a long time.

Sometimes, when you like a thing, you really want to have it. You want to touch it, or you want to have it in your hand. This is a happy feeling. You are happy to be near the thing you like.

A long time ago, people did not always know that words were like this. They would use the word to mean the same thing, but they did not really know how it was different. Now we know that words are like this, and we use them to tell stories and show how we feel.
