A town is a small group of houses and people who live together. They have their own leader. This leader helps keep everyone safe and makes sure the town works well.

Think of it like a group of families. Each family has a person who does work for them. They do things like clean the streets, pay money, and help each other. When all these families work together, they make a big town that is very nice.

Some towns have a big room where everyone talks. This room is called a town hall. People go there to talk about things that need to change. It is like a big group meeting.

Some towns have their own police and fire. They also have people who keep the roads clean. This is important because it keeps people safe.

Mentioning a town can help you understand where something is. If you hear about a town, you know it is far away.
