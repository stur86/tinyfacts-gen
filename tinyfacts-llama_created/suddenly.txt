When something happens very fast, you say it happened suddenly. This means that the time between when it started and when it finished is very short. Sometimes this happens when you hear a loud noise or when a door opens. You might feel like you are moving through time because everything happened so quickly.

You can also use this word to talk about small things. If a small animal jumps out of a bag, it happened suddenly. You do not know how it got out. It just happens very fast.

Sometimes people use this word in a different way. If you do not know something, you might feel surprised. You look at something and you are surprised. This is because something new and different happened very quickly.

You can also use this word when talking about people. If a friend tells you a funny story, you might laugh very quickly. This is because the story happened so fast. You are surprised by what they say.

To say something happened suddenly is often used when a big change happens. If a school year starts, everyone is surprised. They did not expect it to happen so quickly.
