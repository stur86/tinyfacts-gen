An across is something that goes over a thing. Think about a tall thing that has a top. When you go over the top of the thing, you are going across. You might walk over a road, or you might go over a high part of a field. You are moving from one side to the other.

It can also mean something that makes you feel a bit strange. Imagine you are in a room with many people. If you see a strange thing, you might feel like you are going to laugh. This is because the thing is new and makes your mind think. It is like a tiny bit of a surprise inside you.

People also say this when they are moving very fast. When you are going very fast across a street, you are going across. It is like the road is a big open space. You are not really in one place, but you are moving from one place to another.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a person. If someone is across, it means they are in a different place. They might have moved to a new city, or they might have gone to a different school. This can be a good thing because it means the person is learning and growing.
