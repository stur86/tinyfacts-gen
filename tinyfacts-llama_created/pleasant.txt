To be happy is to feel good in your heart. A happy person does not feel sad or angry. They do not worry. They have a warm feeling in their body.

You might feel happy when you see a bright sun after a long night. You might feel happy when you hear music you like. You might feel happy when a good friend is near you. A very small child might feel happy when they see a bright light or a small animal. The feeling is like a warm light in your chest.

To be happy does not mean you are always smiling. Sometimes you feel a little sad or worried. But if you are happy, you are not afraid to be happy. You can laugh, smile, and tell stories to yourself.

It is also easy to feel happy when you do things you love. If you paint, you feel happy. If you fly a car or ride a horse, you feel happy. Your body feels light and you do not feel heavy. You feel like you are flying.

To be happy is important. It helps you stay strong. It makes you feel like you can face anything. If you are happy, you can go to a place you love. You can see a beautiful sight or talk with a friend you like.
