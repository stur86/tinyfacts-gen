To match something means to find something that is the same as you. You are looking for a friend. You find a friend. You feel happy because you found a person who is like you. You both laugh and talk. You can talk all day.

You can also match a thing. If you have many books and you want to keep them, you can put them in a box. You put one book in the box. Then you close the box. You did not take any book away. You kept them all for yourself.

When you match something, you do not take it. You just find it. You do not use it. You keep it for yourself. It stays with you. You can keep it in your bag or your room.

You can also match a time. If it is time for school, you match the time. You go to bed at the right hour. You wake up in the morning. This helps you feel calm and know what to do.

Sometimes you match a feeling. If you are happy, you feel happy. If you are sad, you feel sad. You do not take the feeling away. You keep it inside you. You can feel it when you see a beautiful picture. You feel happy.

To match something is to find it. It is to know that you have a friend, a book, a time, or a feeling. It is to be the same as you.
