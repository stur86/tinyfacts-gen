A long time ago, a person would hang a thing on a high wall or a strong part of a house. They would use long lines to hold the thing. The thing was not on the floor. This is what we call a thing that is on a high place.

You can also hang things on a line. You can put a bag of food, a box, or a book on it. The lines go up high and the things go on top. You can have many lines. This is how people keep their clothes, books, or things they want to keep safe.

A person can also be called a person who hangs things. They might hang things that are old or things that are broken. They keep them on the wall so everyone can see them. It is like they are keeping a story or a memory.

Sometimes, when people talk about things they like, they say they love to hang things. This means they like to keep them on the high places. It is a way of showing love or a way of keeping a thing safe.
