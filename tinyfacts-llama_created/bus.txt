A bus is a large car that carries many people to different places. You can sit in the back with other people, or you can sit in front with a driver. Some buses are very big and have many rooms inside for many people to sit. Others are smaller and can only carry a few.

People use buses to go to school, work, or to a friend. You can also use one to go to the movies or a store to buy food. Some buses are green and have bright colors. Others are blue and have many lines that go around a town. Some buses have windows so you can see the road and the other cars.

In some cities, you can buy a small thing that lets you ride on a bus for a few minutes. You can pay with money or with a card. This is a good way to go to a place without having to walk for a long time.

Even though a bus looks like a car, it is not a car. It has a long body with many rooms inside, and it has a big round thing in the front to help it move. When you drive a bus, the people in the front tell the people in the back where they should go, and the bus goes to the right place.
