To point something means to show it to someone. It is when you are in a room and you see a book or a picture. You look at the thing and you want the other person to see it too. You are not really looking at the person, but you are looking at the thing. You are giving them a clear picture of what you see.

If you are walking down a street and you see a dog, you can point it out to your friends. They will see the dog and they will go to see it. They may even stop and look. If you point to a door, you are telling the people inside that you want to go in. You are using the word to tell them that you have seen something that you want to act on.

Sometimes, you point at a person. You might point at a friend or at a person who is doing something wrong. You are not trying to hurt them, but you are showing them that you see what is going on. You are trying to get their attention.

You can also point at the sky. If you look up and you see a big bright thing in the sky, you might point at it. You are showing the people around you what you see. This is how you tell them that there is something big up there.

In some games, you have to point to a ball. The person who is playing the game is trying to catch the ball. The person who is not playing the game is trying to hit it. If you point to the ball, you are helping the person who is playing. You are showing them where to hit the ball.
