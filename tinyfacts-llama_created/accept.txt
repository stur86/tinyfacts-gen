To accept means you believe something is true. You may think a story, a word, or a plan is not right. If you accept something, you say it is real. You do not try to change it. You might still wonder why it happened, but you see it as the way it is.

You can accept a friend who is mean to you. You do not want them to be angry all the time, so you accept them even if they act bad. You might still feel hurt, but you see that the friend is a person. You are not the friend, but you live with them.

You can also accept a thing that is not good. If you see a car that is very bad, you might say, I accept it. It is a car. It is here. You do not try to change it, but you live with it.

In life, you accept things that are not good. You accept that you have a bad day when it is cold. You accept that you might not get the job you want today. You see that things happen and you do not fight. You live with them until they go away.

Sometimes you accept things you do not want to do. You might say, I accept a walk. I do not want to walk, but I do it because I need to. You are still doing it, but you see the need. You do not try to fight it. You accept it and move on.

To accept someone is to be glad they are there. If you are sad because someone is moving away, you might say, I accept it. You are glad they are leaving. You do not try to stop them. You live with the change.
