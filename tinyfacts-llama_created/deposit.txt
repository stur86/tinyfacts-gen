To put money into a place is to give it to someone or something. This can happen in many ways.

One way is to give money to a store. Imagine you have some money to buy a new game. You walk to the store and say, I want to buy this game. The store person will take your money and put it into a box. This box is like a safe. The money is now safe with the store.

Another way is to give money to a person who works hard. Maybe a person did a hard job to make food for your family. You can give them money as a thank you. This is a way of showing you care. It is a way to help someone who does a job that is important.

You can also put money into a home. Imagine you have money for a big house. You can put it in a place that is for people who need a home. This money helps make a home safe and warm for many people.
