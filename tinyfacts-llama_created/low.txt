To low something means to make it go down. When you walk down a long way, you are low. You are on the ground. The ground is under you.

You can also low a thing that is heavy. If you carry a big box, you are low. The box is hanging down from your arms.

If you are low, you feel sad. You might want to hide. You might cry. This happens when you are afraid or when you are very tired.

Sometimes, we use this word in a different way. If you are low on money, you do not have enough to buy what you need. You might have to wait until your money gets better.

If you are low on power, you have no more power to do things. You feel very small. This can happen when you do not have enough to eat or when you are too tired to walk.

You can also low a person. If someone is low, they are very sad. They might cry a lot. This happens when they are hurt inside.
