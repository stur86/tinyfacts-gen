A tone is how you sound when you speak. It is the way you use your voice. When you are happy, your voice is high. When you are sad, your voice is low.

You can change your tone by how you say things. If you use a high voice, it is like a loud song. If you use a low voice, it is like a soft sound.

You also change your tone when you talk to people you know. If you say something nice, you might use a happy tone. If you say something mean, you might use a hard tone.

Some people use their tone to show how they feel. If you are afraid, you might say a small word. If you are angry, you might say a loud word. This helps us know what to do when we feel different.

We also use tone to show how we feel about something. If you say a word that is bad for the world, you might use a bad tone. If you say a word that is good, you might use a good tone. This helps us understand each other.

A tone can change when you grow up. When you are a little child, your voice might sound very high. When you get older, your voice might sound deeper. Your tone changes with how you feel and with how you act.
