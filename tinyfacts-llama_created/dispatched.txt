To send something is to give it to someone else. When you send a letter, you write the words you want to say. You put them into a box with the words and the money to pay for it. Then you give the box to someone who lives far away.

If you want to talk to a person quickly, you can use a phone. When you use a phone, the person you want to talk to hears your words. They listen and can answer back if they want to.

Sometimes people send things to people who work at a building. If you want to buy a book, you can send it to a store. The store will bring it to you when you are ready to buy it.

When you send something, you are giving it to another person. You are letting them know that you want them to have it. It is like sharing a thing with someone you trust.
