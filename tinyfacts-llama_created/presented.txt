To present something is to show it to the people. When you present something, you are telling them about it. You might be a teacher, a doctor, or a person who has a job. You come into a room and you start talking. You use words that are easy to understand so everyone can hear.

You might say things like: This is a new idea. This is how we make food. This is a way we live. You are showing them what you know. You want them to know about the thing and to understand it.

Sometimes you present a picture. You show a man who has done a great job. You show a story about a person who has been very kind. You want to show them how the person felt and how they acted.

When you present something, you are sharing your idea with the world. You are helping people understand the world better. You are helping them learn new things.

To present can also mean to show something in a way that is easy to see. If you are trying to tell a story, you might use pictures and words. You can show the story to someone so they can understand it. You are using the words to show the story.

You can also present a thing that is not a person. You might show a book or a movie. You might show a song. You are showing the thing to the people so they can see it and understand it.
