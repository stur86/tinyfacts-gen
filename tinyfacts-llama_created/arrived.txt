When you arrive, you have reached a place. You might have walked a long way or flown in a car. You have been waiting for a very long time. Now you are here.

You can arrive in many different ways. You might walk into a room. You might take a bus or a train. You might fly in a big flying thing. When you get to a place, you can feel a big feeling. You might feel happy or a little bit sad. You might feel like you have found home.

To arrive also means to be at a place that is not where you were before. Imagine you were in a big city. You were in a small town. You had never been in that city before. Now you have arrived. You have seen many things and met many people. You feel like you know this city well.

Arriving can be a big word. It means you have been on a long road to a place where you can be safe and happy. When you arrive, you can rest your body and your mind. You can eat food and drink water. You can do things you like. You have finally reached your home.
