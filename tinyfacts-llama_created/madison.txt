To mad is to act very angry or to feel a lot of fear. It can also mean to act like a person who is very happy or excited.

Imagine you are in a school and you see your friend who you like. You might feel very happy. You could shout and jump up from your seat. This is mad. You are so excited that you can not stop it.

A person might also be mad if they are very angry. They might say something that is not nice. They might shout or use their hands. They might also want to get even with the person who made them angry.

Sometimes, people feel mad when they are afraid. They might feel their heart is going fast and their face is getting red. They might be worried or scared. This is also mad.

You can also be mad at a place or a thing. If you see a person doing something that makes you angry, you might get mad at them. You might shout or walk away. You want them to stop.

Being mad is a way to show that you care. If someone is mad, they are telling you that something is wrong and they need your attention.
