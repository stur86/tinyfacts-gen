Back off the wall means to go from one side to the other. Imagine you are in a room with two people. One person is happy and the other person is sad. If you go and sit next to the happy person, they will be the one with the happy face. If you go and sit next to the sad person, they will be the one with the happy face.

But if you go to the wall and you go back to the start of the room, the people will still be the same. The sad person is still sad and the happy person is still happy. So going back is like going back to the start. It does not change anything.

This is why we say a story can go back and back. You can start at the beginning and end at the end, and it is still the same story.
