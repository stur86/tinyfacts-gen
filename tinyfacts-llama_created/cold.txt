When you feel cold, your body gets very low. This is because your body uses all its warm blood to keep you warm. If you are cold, you feel very small and you want to find something to warm up.

You can feel cold when you are in a room with many people. If everyone is holding a cup of hot water, you might feel cold because your body is not getting enough of the warm water. You can also feel cold when you walk on a very hard floor.

Sometimes, you might feel cold at night. This happens because your body is keeping you warm while you are sleeping. If you are cold, you might feel very tired. It is like your body is saying, I am glad you are here, now I want to be warm.

Cold days can make the ground hard to walk on. You might trip and fall because the ground is very hard. But it is okay to be cold. It is a normal part of life.

You can also feel cold if the air is very still. If the air is very still, there is no wind to push the warm air around. This makes your body get very cold.

If you are cold and it hurts, you might feel a lot of pain. You might want to shake your arms and legs or move around to get warm. This is because your body is trying to get warm again.
