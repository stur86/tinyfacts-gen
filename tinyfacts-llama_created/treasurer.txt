A person who holds money for a town, a city, or a school is called a treed. This person is like a middle man. They take the money from people who pay their money to the town. They put the money in a big box that is kept in a safe room. They use this money to pay for things the town needs. The person also has to tell the town when they have money, so everyone knows how much money is left.

The person who holds the money is called the treed of the town. This is a big job. They must be very carefully who they let work for them. If they make a bad deal with a person, the town may have to pay a lot of money later. They have to be good at keeping money and making sure the town does not spend too much.
