To change something means you take something old and make it new.

Imagine you have a book. The book has a story inside. One day, you find a new story that is better. You do not want the old story anymore, so you keep the new one. You take the old story and put it away in a box.

You can also say you are going to change your life. You want to be a better person. You want to learn new things and do good things for others. You will try new ways and maybe some of them will work. You will learn from them and use them to become a better person.

Sometimes, you need to change things that are broken. A chair might be broken and you need to put it back together so it works again. You might use a new piece of wood to fix it. When this happens, you have taken something old and made it new again.

We also use this word when we talk about people. If a person does something wrong, you might say they have to change. They have to be a good person now. This means they must not do that again.
