A small part of a bigger thing. You can break a thing into many parts. Each part is still a bit of the whole. You can see each part by looking at it alone.

Think of a line in a book. If you cut the book into many parts, each part is a small piece of the line. You can read the story from each part one by one.

Imagine you have a tree with many long arms. Each arm is a small piece of the tree. You can see each arm by looking at it. If you cut the tree into many parts, each part is a small piece of the tree.

Things that are made of many small parts are called many parts. You can also say a person is many parts. One person is one group of many parts. Each part tells the story of the whole person.

A small part of a picture can be a face. The face is a part of the whole picture. It tells us about the person who made the picture.
