Ears are parts of the head. One part is on the front of the head and the other is on the back of the head. They are very small pieces of stuff that people wear.

One part is made of hard white stuff. It is put into the ear and stays there. The hard white stuff helps the sound of a song or a voice go out from the body. This is why you can hear a person when you are far away.

The other part is made of a soft red stuff. It is put into the ear and stays there. The red stuff helps to move around when you move your head. This makes the sound go out more easily. It also helps keep the sound from getting in your ear.

Most people wear the red stuff part. They put it into the ear before they put on clothes. The hard white stuff part can stay for a very long time. It is very strong and does not break easily.

Some people wear only one part. They put it into one ear. This is a very pretty way to wear a piece of stuff. Some people also wear both parts. They put the red stuff part into the back of the head and the hard white stuff part into the front of the head. This is like having two small pieces of stuff in two different places on the head.
