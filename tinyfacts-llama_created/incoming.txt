The word you are looking for is called an answer. An answer is when you find the right way to do something. It happens when you think about a problem and then you find the right thing to do.

Imagine you are trying to get a book from a tall person. If you ask the person, they might say they are in a room on the other side of the street. You then go to the street and look around. You see a sign that says the room is on the right side of the street. You go to the right side of the street and find the room. Your answer is the right way to do it.

When you answer a question, you are giving the person the right words to use so they can understand what you mean. You are helping them. This is why we call it an answer.

Sometimes you might not know how to do something on your own. You might ask your friend for help. You ask them to show you how to do it. This is also an answer. You are asking someone else to give you the right way to do something.

You can also answer questions with numbers. Imagine you want to buy a computer for school. You ask your mom how much money you need. She tells you that you need to spend around one hundred and twenty five hundred. This is your answer. You are giving her the number she asked you to use.

In this way, an answer is anything that helps you understand something or does not change anything.
