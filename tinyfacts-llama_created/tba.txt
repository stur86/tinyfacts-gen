Tone

A tone is how you say something. It is the way you feel. A happy tone is like a little bit of air that goes up and down. A sad tone is like the sound of a door that is closed. When you are with people you like, you usually want to feel that way. This is why you like to laugh and smile.

A tone can also mean the sound of a thing. A happy song has a bright tone. A loud noise can have a strong tone. A soft song has a quiet tone. When you listen to a song, you hear the tone of the music.

Sometimes, a tone is about the way you feel about something. You might feel a strong tone when you are afraid. You might feel a quiet tone when you are calm. You might feel a happy tone when you are happy. Your tone is like a feeling inside you.

We also have a tone when we talk to each other. We use different tones to show how we feel. A friend might use a happy tone when they say something nice. A person who is angry might use a loud tone. We use tones to help each other understand each other.

You can also say that a person has a tone. If someone is very serious, they might have a strong tone. If someone is very happy, they might have a bright tone. This is how we see how people are feeling.
