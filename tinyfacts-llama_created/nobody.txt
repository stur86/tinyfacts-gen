To not do something means you do not act. It is a way to say you are not doing it. If you say you will do something, if you do not do it, you are not doing it. You are not the one who does it. 

Think of a person who does not have a friend. They do not talk to anyone. They do not go to the movies. They do not see a movie. They do not watch a game. They are not doing any of those things. They are not doing anything at all. 

You are not doing your work. You do not do your school work. You do not eat your breakfast. You are not doing anything. You are just there, not doing anything.
