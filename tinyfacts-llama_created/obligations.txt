An order is a way to tell someone to do something. When you order a friend to come home, you are telling them to go. You are giving them a job to do. You are not asking them to do it, you are telling them to do it.

An order can be made by a person, a group, or a group of people. If you say, you must go to the movies, the people who say it are the ones who must go. If you say, you must help your mom, you are the one who has to do it.

When you do something, you feel a little bit of a pull. This pull is what we call an order. It is the reason we do things. It is what makes us act.

You can also say you feel a pull when you are afraid. If you are afraid of a big dog, you might say, I am going to run. Your heart is telling you to go.

An order is a way to share this pull with others. When you give an order, you are sharing the pull. You are saying, here is what we are doing, and everyone must do it. This is how we work together in a group.
