To make something is to start from a small piece and add more to it until it is the thing you want. Most things we use start as a small piece of wood or paper. A person who makes something does this. They use a box that can hold the pieces and a power box that makes the pieces move.

Imagine you want to make a box for your cat. You start with a small piece of wood. You cut it into the right pieces and add more wood to make it strong. Then you use the power box to push the wood together.

You are also the person who makes a book. You start with a small piece of paper. You add many other pieces of paper to it. You write with a tiny thing on the paper that makes the words appear. You also add pictures of the things you want to tell. After many hours, you have a book with many words and pictures.

A maker is a person who makes things. They use their hands and a box to make new things every day. Many people are maker because they like to build and fix things.
