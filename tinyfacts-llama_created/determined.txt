To be decided means to be true. A person or thing is decided if it is the one they want to be. It is often about who is in a fight or who has a job. You can be decided if you are going to do something. You might be decided to be happy or decided to be afraid.

To decide also means to know what you want to do. You might want to go to school or stay home. You might want to play with your friends or watch a movie. A person can decide what to eat for dinner. They might decide they want a hot food or cold food.

When you decide, you are not afraid. You know what is best. You are ready to act. It is like a small light that shows what you want to be.

Sometimes, you might not know what to decide. You are like a child who is looking at many books. You see many words that are good. You want to read them all. In this case, you are not decided yet. You need to look at each one and decide which one feels best.

You can also decide on a plan. If you want to go to a place, you need to decide which way to go. You need to think about which road is better. You might decide to go on the road that is far away. You are making a plan, and it is helping you feel sure about what to do.
