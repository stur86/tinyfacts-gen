When you talk about people, animals, or things, you often say they are one kind. You might say they are all the same or that they are only a few different kinds.

A word that helps us understand this is called a word about many. This word says that there are many kinds in one thing, place, or group. You can see this when you look at a big group of people, animals, or things that are not all the same. They are different from each other and from the kinds you started with.

To be different is to not be like others. If you are very tall, you are different from a short person. If you like to eat sweet things, you are different from someone who likes to eat green things. Being different is what makes life interesting.

There are many kinds of words that show this. If you have many friends, you are a different kind of person. If you learn many new things, you are a different kind of brain. When you look at a wide group of pictures, you are seeing many different kinds of faces.

Because there are so many kinds, each one has their own way of doing things. If you look at one kind of hair, you see one kind of hair. If you look at another kind of hair, you see a different kind of hair. This is what makes the world a big and interesting place.
