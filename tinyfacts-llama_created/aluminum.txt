When we make something from air, we are making it change. Air is light and can go very fast. When we push it into a place where it can stay, it gets heavy and forms something. Then when we take it out, it has changed back to air.

One of the most important things about this is that air can not be broken. Nothing can break the box that makes air and stay inside it. Once the air is in the box, it stays there.
