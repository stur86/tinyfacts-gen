To have a bit of power

When you push something, you are using your body. You are making the thing go forward. This makes you have a bit of power. If you keep doing this for a while, your power gets stronger. This is what we call a bit of power.

But power is not always easy to keep. Sometimes you might feel like you are going to fall down. This is because the power you are using is too strong. If you do not stop, you might hit the floor. This is a big problem.

If you have a bit of power, you can move things. You can pull a door, or pull a chair. You can even push a car. This power is what makes you move.

There is also a bit of power that is inside you. This is what keeps you going when you are tired. It is like a tiny bit of power that is always moving inside you. You do not need to push anything to get this power.

To have a bit of power is to have the power to do something. It is about having enough power to move around or to do the work you need to do.
