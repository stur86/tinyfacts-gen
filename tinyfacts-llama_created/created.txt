To be a thing you do not have is a big thing. You make a thing that does not make you. You give it life. You make it do what it is not supposed to do. You tell it how to act.

You do this with your body. You make it move. You make it stand up and walk. You make it speak and say words. You make it use your hands to make things. You make it do things that hurt you or things that help you.

You can also do this with your mind. You tell a story. You tell a person that something happened. You tell them that you are there. You show them that you care. You make them feel something.

You are a thing you do not have. You make life for your body. You make life for your brain. You make life for your heart. You are the one who makes everything happen.
