To slow something means to make it go very, very slowly. When you are moving on a road, you might want to go faster. If you go too fast, you might hit the car in front of you. If you slow down, you can see the trees and the houses around you. This helps you stay safe.

You can also slow something by putting a stop on it. This keeps the door closed so you can not get out. If you have a computer, you can slow it down by turning off the parts that need to work very fast. This helps keep it safe and helps your work run faster.

Sometimes, we slow things down inside our bodies. When we are tired, we might need to take a few deep breaths. This helps our body get ready for a good night of sleep. If you are feeling sad, you might want to slow down your thinking. This lets your heart and brain work at a slower, kind of calm, way.

You can also slow things down by being quiet and still. If you are in a big room and there are many people, you might feel the noise. If you sit still and quiet, you can hear the sounds better. This helps you stay safe and not get confused.
