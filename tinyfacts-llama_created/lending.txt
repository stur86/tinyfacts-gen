To give something to someone means to let them use it. Imagine you have a book that you really want to keep. You decide to give it to a friend who loves to read. You are letting them use the book. After they finish reading it, you must give it back to them.

In this way, you are giving something to someone else. This is how people give money to each other when they need it. They give it to someone who will pay it back later. They might promise to pay the money back at a later time. This is how people help each other.

You can also give a thing to someone if they are in trouble. If a friend is very tired and they do not know how to do something, you might give them your phone. They can use it to find answers to their questions. You are helping them.

Sometimes, you might give something to someone because you think they will use it. If you see a person who is very happy, you might give them a small thing you have. You want them to be happy. This is how we show love and care.

To give something is to let it go into someone else. It is a way to share what you have with others. When you give, you are not keeping it for yourself. You are giving it to someone who can use it better than you do.
