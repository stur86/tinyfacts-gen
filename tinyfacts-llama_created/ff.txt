The word you are looking for is called a silent letter. It is a letter that does not make a sound when it is written. This happens when the sound the letter would make is not important for the word you are writing.

Imagine you are writing a word that has a lot of the letter Beside it. If you write the word, the Beside sounds are there. But if you write the word with the silent letter, the word will still be the same. It is as if the word has lost some of its important sounds.

The silent letter is not always the same. Sometimes it is a letter that is not part of the word at all. Other times it is a letter that is in the middle of two words. The important thing is that it is a way to make a word without making a sound. This can be very good when you want to keep a word very short. It is also good if a word has many of the same sounds. If a word has a silent letter, it is more like a group of words than one word.
