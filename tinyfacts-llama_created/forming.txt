To form something means to make it from nothing. You can form a thing by putting parts together. Imagine you have a small ball. You can put many small pieces of wood together. Each piece is one part of the ball. Now the ball has many parts. You did not start with one ball. You started with many small pieces.

When you say a word forms, it means the word is made from other words. If you look at a word, you can see how many other words it is made of. A word that is made of many other words is said to be a form of the word that came before it. This is how we learn new words. When a word is a form of another word, it is a part of that word.
