To be me means you are the person you are today. You live your life as you see it. You do things that make you happy. You might like to play games with your friends. You might like to watch movies. You might like to read books.

You are the person who is in the room. You are the one who is speaking. You are the one who is writing. You are the one who is eating food. You are the one who is sleeping. You are the one who is standing up.

You are the person you were before you became you. You are the person you are now. You are the person you will be in a few years. You are the person you will be in ten years.

Being me is about being real. It is about not trying to be someone you are not. It is about being yourself. It is about enjoying the day and the life you live.
