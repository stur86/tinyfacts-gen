To type is to use a small box on a computer or other kind of box that can write words and numbers. You press the right side of the box with your fingers many times, and the box makes the words come out.

When you first start, your fingers get used to the way the box feels under them. You press the right side in the right spot, and the box makes a sound. Then, you move your fingers to the left side, and the box makes the words start on the left side. You keep pressing and moving until you get the words just right.

The box has a set of numbers and letters that it uses. You tell it which number and letter to use. If you want to say a word, you type the words in the order that looks right to you. The box reads your words and then writes the words on a piece of paper.

You can type fast or slow. Some people are very good at it and can type hundreds of words in one minute. Others might take longer. But you can always stop the box and look at what you wrote.

You can type words for a lot of things. You can write a letter to a friend. You can make a story. You can write a book. You can type music. The box can make your words come out in a way that makes them look like they are from another person.

You can also use the box to talk to other people who are far away. You can type words and send them through the air to someone else. This is called writing a word. You are writing a word for the other person, and they are reading it for them.

Some people use the box for work. They type papers and make money. Others use it for fun. They write stories and make pictures. They write to people they like. They write to friends they do not know. They write to people who are far away.

To type is to use a box to make words. It is a way to write.
