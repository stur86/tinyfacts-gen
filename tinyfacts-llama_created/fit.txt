To fit something means to make it go in the right place. Imagine you want to put a small thing inside a big box. If the small thing is very small, it will go far away on the bottom of the box. If you put it in the right spot, it will look good and not fall out.

People also use this word when they talk about how a person looks. If a person is very tall, they fit in a room. If a person is very short, they do not fit. It is like a picture. If the picture is right, the person is also right.
