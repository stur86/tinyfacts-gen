A front is the front of a building or a house. It is the part that is closest to the street. It is usually the part that is made of strong wood, and it is often the part with the door and the windows.

Imagine you are walking on the street. You see a big house or a building in front of you. This house is like the front of a city. People go to this house to buy food, clothes, or to see movies. It is the first place you see when you walk into a new town.

Sometimes, a front is also used for computers. When you see a big picture of a person on a computer, that is the front. This picture is like the front of a book or a movie. You look at it first to see who is there and what is happening.

In some places, the front of a building is also a place where people meet. It is the area where people go to talk. If you are on a bus and you see many people in this area, you know it is a busy front.

In another way, a front can mean the beginning of something. If you start a new game, you are starting at the front. You are the first person to play. It is the start of a new thing.
