To present something is to show it to others. When you present a book, you read it to them. You read the words and show them how the story is written. You do this so that the other person can understand.

Imagine a teacher. A teacher is the one who shows you how to do something. They do this by writing, by speaking, or by doing. They show you the way to do it so you can learn.

You can also present your own ideas. If you want to tell your friends that you are happy, you can go up to them and say, I am happy! This is presenting your idea. You are showing them how you feel.

Sometimes, people present a picture. They show the picture to a group of people. They might tell why they made the picture and how it shows their life. This helps others understand what the picture means.

To present something is to show it to others. It is a way to share your ideas, your pictures, or your stories with the world.
