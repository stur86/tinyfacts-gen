A cat is a small animal that moves around on the ground. It has soft, long hair that is often bright in color. It can be very small or very big. Cats live in houses and trees, and they like to sleep in quiet rooms. They have bright eyes that see very well. They can also make sounds that sound like a cat crying.

Cats are very good at hiding. They can go under a table or in a box. They have a hard cover on their body that helps keep them safe. They can also use their long legs to run very fast. Some cats are very fast and can go very far in one step.

Cats need to eat a lot. They like to eat small pieces of food that are not too big. They drink water from a bottle. They also need to go outside at night to do their business. This is why they like to come out of their house when the sun goes down.
