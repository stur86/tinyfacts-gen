A ball is a round thing that people use to play with. You can hit it with your hands or use a long stick to hit it. Sometimes, a ball is thrown by one person to another person. It is often made of hard stuff that does not break easily.

People also use balls to run. They wear a soft cover on their feet to keep the ball from hitting them. They can run very fast because the ball goes straight across the ground.

You can also use a ball to play a game. One person is the ball player. They try to move the ball from one end of the room to the other end. The person who catches the ball is the other person. If the ball player drops it, the other person gets to play.

A ball is also used in a different way. It is a thing that is made to go up high. When you throw a ball, it goes up and comes down. People watch what happens and try to guess where the ball will land next.

In the story of a girl, she loves to dance with a man who is a ball player. She wants to be his friend. When the man does not want to be friends, the girl gets sad. She wants to be happy again.

You can also use the word ball to talk about something that is very strong. A ball can be thrown many times and still go very high. A ball can also fall from a great high place and land on the ground. This is why we say that the ball is strong.
