A hard cover is a way to cover a book. It is not a soft cover like a couch or a chair.

Imagine you have a small ball. The ball is very small and round. You can put it on a table. The table has lines on it. These lines are like the cover of the ball. The lines go all around the ball and cover it completely.

A book has many lines. If you cut one line, the book is broken. But if the lines are strong, the book stays together. This is what a hard cover does. It has many lines that go all around the book. These lines hold the book together so it does not break.

There are two kinds of hard covers. One is for books with many words. The cover has many lines on it. The lines are very thick. This kind of cover is very strong. The other kind of hard cover is for books with only one word. The cover is smaller. The lines are fewer.

Hard covers are usually made from things that are hard to break. They are often made from hard parts that are cut together. This makes them very strong. People use these hard covers for books because they do not fall apartment like other covers do.
