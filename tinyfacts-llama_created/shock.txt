To shock something means to make it move or change very quickly. It can happen to people, animals, and things.

Think about a ball. If you throw it, it goes from a slow to a fast, high place. Your eyes see it move, and your brain thinks it is there. This is a kind of shock.

You can also get a shock when you hear a loud noise. A big car might make a loud sound and you feel like you are moving. Your heart might beat faster. This is a shock because your body is surprised.

Sometimes, a shock can happen to your body. If you touch a hot thing or see a bright light, you might feel a sudden pain. This is also a shock.

You can also get a shock from a surprise. Imagine you have a friend who you like. Then, they say something that makes you feel strange. This can be a shock because your mind is not ready for the new idea.

To shock someone means to make them feel strange. If you talk about something that is bad, they might feel a shock. This can be because they did not think about it before.
