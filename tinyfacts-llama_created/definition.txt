To have a meaning means you know what something is.

Think about a word like the word water. You use it to talk about how wet things are. You also use it to talk about how wet your hair feels. You use it to say that you are feeling wet or to say that the ground is wet. This word has a different meaning than the word water. The word water is about the thing that is wet, but the word water is about how you feel when you are wet.

Another word is a name. A name is what people use to call you. If you are a person, you have a name. If you are a dog, you have a name. You can also give someone a name if you do not know their name. You tell them you are their name so they know who they are.

People also have a way of talking. They use words, words, and words to get their point across. If you say something wrong, the other person might get confused or angry. If you say something that is true, the other person will be happy and thank you. This way of talking is how we all learn to understand each other.

Sometimes, a word can change its meaning over time. If you learn to say a word in a way that is different from how it is used, you are using it in a new way.
