A short, soft food from green parts of a large tree. People eat them when they are green, then they turn red, white, or brown.

You can eat the white round part inside the green part. This is the part that people eat. You can also eat the green top and the soft part at the bottom. Some people make food from the green top so it is not as green.

To make food from the green top, you use water to cover the green top. You then put the green top in a hot water bag for a while. After this, you take the bag out and the top is soft. You can then cut it into small pieces and put it in a hot bag with more water. The hot water makes the inside soft and the outside hard. You can eat this or use it to make food for your children.
