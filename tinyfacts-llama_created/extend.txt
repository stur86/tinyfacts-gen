To use an adder to an end of a thing is to add more to the end. You can use this word when you want to add more words to a story, a name, or a thing.

Imagine you are writing a story. You start with a name. You might add more names. You might add a time. You might add a place. You can keep adding more and more, and the story gets bigger and bigger.

You can also use this word when you want to get ready to do something. If you want to go to a far city, you might use an adder to your shoes. You need to add more shoes to your feet so that your feet can walk far. This is very important.

Sometimes, you use this word when you talk to a child. You might say, I am going to the park. You are adding more to the end of your words, so the child knows what you are talking about.
