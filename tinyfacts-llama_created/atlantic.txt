To go across the water is to move from one side to the other. If you are standing on a wide, open place of land, you are on one side. You can walk to the other side. But if you are on a big water, you can use a long, moving thing that goes across the water. This thing can carry people, animals, and even food to other places.

The water where this is happens is very cold. It is also very deep. The water is far down than the places you can walk. If you were to go this way many times, you would go very deep into the ground. This deep water is where things that live under the ground live.

There is a big water body where this happens. It is much bigger than many places on land. This water body is so big that you can not see the bottom. Many people go there by moving big things. They use the water to go, the land for the places they land.

People have been going this way for a very long time. They started by moving small moving things across the water. Later, they built bigger things that could carry more people and more things. Now, there are many ways to get there. Some go by moving big things that can carry many people at once. Others go by moving things that move across the water, like the kind you see in pictures.
