To take a thing that is already there and use it for a time that you do not pay back. You give the thing to someone else, and they give you money so you can use it. After you use it for a time, you have to give the money back to the person you took it from. If you do not pay back the money when you should, you have to pay more money. This is called being in trouble.

You can take a money thing to buy a car, a house, or a thing you want but you can not pay. You give the money and then you pay back the money later. You pay the money back in small parts so it does not hurt you all at once.
