An actor is someone who makes people believe they are doing something else. They act like they are a person in a story. They use their face, body, and words to tell a story.

Imagine you are in a movie. The person playing you is not you. They wear a different face and clothes. They act like the person in the movie. They do not really say the words, but they feel the words in their heart. They act like they are the person.

Acting is like being a part of a big group of people. You share the same story with many other people. You all act together. Sometimes you act with other people. Sometimes you act alone. You might act while you sleep, or you might act while you are waiting for a movie to start.

Acting is a work of the mind. It is not just about doing things with your body. It is about using your heart and brain to tell a story. An actor must learn about the story, the person they are playing, and how to make the person look and feel.

There are many kinds of acting. Some act in movies. Some act in plays or in front of a group of people who watch them. Some act in front of many people at a time in one place. Some act in front of many people at a time in many places. Some act alone in their own room. Some act with many people in a group.

Acting is important because it helps people imagine other worlds. It helps people feel happy, sad, angry, and scared. It helps people understand other people and their stories. It helps us see the world in a different way.
