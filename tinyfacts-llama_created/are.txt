The word are is about people and animals.

When you say, I am here, you are in this place. You are the person you are talking to. You are the one doing the talking. You are the one with the words.

When you say, she is there, the woman is in this room. She is not the person you are talking to. She is a person, too. She is not you.

If you have many friends, you are happy. You have people you like. You are part of a group.

If you are tired, you need to rest. You are not doing well. You need to stop and catch your breath.

If you are a boy, you are a man. If you are a girl, you are a woman. These are the ways people are.
