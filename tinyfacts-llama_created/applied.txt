To use something means to make it work for you. When you do this, you are using the thing. You put it in the place where you need it to be. You might put a book on your table. Now you can read it.

You can also use this word when you take something and make it do something else. You might take a piece of wood and make a chair. You put the wood in a way that the person who makes the chair can use it. This is when you use the wood.

Sometimes you have to use a word that is not there. If you want to say you are sad, you can say you are feeling sad. This word is for people who feel this way. You might feel sad because you lost a friend. You can also feel sad when you are very tired.

You can also say you have used a word. This means you have tried to do what the word means. If you try to do something and you are very afraid, you might feel afraid. You can say, I am afraid. This shows that you are trying to be strong.

To use a word is to show that you understand it. If you say a word and you mean it, you have used it.
