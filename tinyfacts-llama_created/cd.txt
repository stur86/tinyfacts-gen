To put a small round thing into a big round thing.

You have a big box. Inside the box, there are many small round things. These small round things are like tiny pieces of music. You put the tiny pieces of music into the box so that they can play a song.

One of these small round things is for the power. This small round thing goes into the box. The power inside the small round thing makes the tiny pieces of music move. When you put the small round thing into the box, the music starts to play.

Another small round thing is for you. You can put this small round thing in your ear. When you put it in your ear, the music comes out of your ear. You can listen to the music while you are doing other things.

Sometimes, you have many small round things. You can put the small round things into a box and then put the box into a car. The small round things go into the car so that the music can stay on your phone or in your ear while you are driving.
