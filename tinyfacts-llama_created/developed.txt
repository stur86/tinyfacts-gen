When something is built, we often use the word built. But when something is done, we use the word done. A car is built. We can build a house. A person built a book. We built a house for a family. We built a car to go far.

We also use this word when we change one word into another. If you say you are happy, you are doing this. If you say you are going to the store, you are doing this too. You are doing something.

This word is also important when we talk about people. If a baby is very small, we say it is small. But when the baby is very old, we say it is big. We are building the baby. We are helping it grow. We built the baby, and now it is a person.
