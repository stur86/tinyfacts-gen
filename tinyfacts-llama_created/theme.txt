A word is a thing you use to talk about or to show. It tells us about something. A word can be short, like a single word. Or a word can be many words put together. It can tell us what to do, what to feel, or what to think.

A group of words that all tell the same story can be called a story. A story has a beginning, a middle, and an end. It shows what happened and why. People can hear a story. They see it on a computer, read it, or watch a movie.

Sometimes a story is about what to do. It shows you how to act in a situation. It tells you how to feel if you are afraid. It also tells you about people who have been there before you.

You can also use the word in other ways. You might say, a movie is a story. Or, a game is a story. These words help us understand what a story is and how it works.

When people talk about a thing, they often use words from a story. They might say, the room looks like a school. This means the room looks like a place where people go to learn. It is telling us about the room and what it is like.

The word also helps us think about things. You can say, the city is big. This means the city is very large. It helps us understand what the word means.
