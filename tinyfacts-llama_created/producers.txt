A person or a group of people who make things is called a maker. They are the ones who start the work. They decide what to make and how to make it. They often work with money or with things from the ground.

In the world of music, a person who makes the sound is called a maker. They sit down with a box that makes the sound, and they press the right keys at the right time. This makes people want to listen to the music.

In a big group, the person who makes the food is a maker. They plan how much food to make and how to make it. They buy the food from the store, and they work with the people who make the food.

In a team, the person who makes the games is a maker. They plan the games, buy the ball and the stick, and they tell everyone what to do. They work with the other people to make sure everyone is happy.

To make something is to start it. You start a fire to warm your hands. You start a book so you can read it later. You start a new day when you wake up.
