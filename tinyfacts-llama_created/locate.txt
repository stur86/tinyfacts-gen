To find a place is to know where something is. You might use your phone to find where you are if you are in a big city. You look at the street number on your phone and it tells you where your house is. You use the street name and the number of the house. You can also use the name of the city and the name of the street to find your way.

You can also find a person. If you want to meet a friend, you need to know where they live. You look at a piece of paper with their name and their house number. When you find them, you say hello. You are finding them in a place.

Sometimes, you might find something you lost. You look in a room, a desk, or a box. You ask your parents if they know where you put your phone. If the phone is not there, you might find it in the bed or on the floor. This is finding something that was not where it was before.

Finding is about knowing where you are or where something is.
