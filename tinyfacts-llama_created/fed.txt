To be the person you are is very important. When you eat, you are the one who does it. If you did not eat, you would not have the power to move. Food is what you need to live.

You can also say that someone is the one who does it. If a dog is the one who does a job, it is because the person gave it to it to do. You are the one who decides what you want to do with your life. You are the person you are.

Sometimes, you have to pay for what you do. If you go to a store to buy a thing, you are the one who pays. You give money to the person who owns the thing. This is how you help people and give them what they need.
