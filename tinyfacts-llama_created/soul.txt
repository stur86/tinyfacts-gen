Your heart is inside you. It is the part of you that feels things. It can feel happy, sad, angry, or scared. You use your heart to care about others. It helps you love a person, feel a feeling when you see a picture of a loved one, or feel fear when you hear a loud noise.

You also have a mind. Your mind is the part of you that thinks. It helps you remember things, learn new things, and figure out how to do things. Your mind is like a computer inside your head.

When you are very young, your heart and mind are growing together. As you grow older, your heart and mind get very different. Your heart becomes stronger and more focused on your own life. Your mind becomes more focused on things that are big and important to you.

You can not see your heart or mind. You can only know they are there by how they make you feel. Your heart can be very soft and give in to a lot of things, or your heart can be very strong and keep going even when you are very sad. Your mind can be very clear and know what to do, or your mind can be very confused and want to do something different.

Your heart is like a person. You love people and things. You want to keep them safe. You want to be happy with them. You also want to be able to say, if you are sad, I love you. You want to be able to say, if I am happy, I love you. Your mind is like a book. It holds all the things you have learned and all the stories you have heard. It helps you remember things and find answers to your questions.

Sometimes, people say, if you love someone, you are in love. If you hate someone, you are angry. If you are sad, you might cry. If you are happy, you might smile. Your heart and mind work together to help you feel these different ways.

You can not stop your heart from beating. It is always there. You can not make it stop. But you can learn to live with it. You can learn to be strong when you are sad, or you can learn to be happy when you are happy.
