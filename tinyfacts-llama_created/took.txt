The word took means you got something. You might take a drink from a cup, or a ball from a game. You take it so you can use it later.

Imagine you are going to a big room with many people. You need to find a place to sit. You look around and see a chair. You walk over to the chair and sit down. Now you are in the chair. You took it. You did not ask anyone to sit for you. You just sat down because you found a good spot.

You can also take something that is not yourself. If you find a box in a dark room, you take it. You do not say, you took it from someone else. You just take it because you need it.

Sometimes, you take something that is hard. If you have to work for a long time to do a job, you might feel tired. You take a break to rest your body and your mind. This is taking care of yourself.

You can take something that is hard to understand. If you see a word you do not know, you might look it up in a book. You read the word and then you know what it means.

Taking is a way to get what you want. You take something to eat, to sleep, to be happy, or to play. It is a way to live your life.
