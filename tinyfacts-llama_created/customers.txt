A person or a group of people who buy things you make is called a user. They want the things you make. They want to buy them so they can live a better life.

Think about a store. A store is a place where you can buy things. You go there to buy food, clothes, books, and other things you need. If you buy something, you are a user of the store.

If you want a drink, you go to a store. The person at the front of the store looks at your money and says, yes, you can buy it. Then you get your drink. You are a user of the store.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If a person works at a store, they are a user. They help you buy things. If a person likes to buy things, they are a user. They are people who use things to live a good life.

In some places, people are called a user when they use a computer. They type words and pictures on the computer. They use it to learn, to make money, or to play games. They are people who use the computer to do their work.
