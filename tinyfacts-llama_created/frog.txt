A small animal that lives in the water has green skin and can jump. It has long legs and a short body. It eats tiny things that live in the water and small animals that fly into its mouth. It can also make a strange sound, like a soft song, and it can say a word like a human being.

Some people keep these animals as friends. They put food in a box and the animal comes to the box to eat. They also watch it so the animal knows where it lives. Some people like to watch these animals when they jump and see how fast they can jump.
