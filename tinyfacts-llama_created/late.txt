When you say something is late, you mean it happened after the time it was expected to happen. It is behind where it should be.

Imagine you have a friend who is going to a party. You tell them you will be there at a time, and you expect them to be there at the same time. If you are late, you have been behind your friend. You could be waiting in line and talking to a friend while your friend is still at the door. This is a bad thing because it makes your friend feel left out.

In a house, if a person does not finish a job, they are late. They are not the first one to do the job, and they do not have the right to be the first one to do it. This is because they started later than they should have.

In a car, if you start your car before you are ready to leave, you are late. You start moving before you are ready to go out. This is not good because you might get a fine or a warning.

Sometimes, you might be late because you are afraid of a thing. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might not go to bed at night. This makes you late because you are staying up too late, and you might not have time to do other things you need to do in the morning.
