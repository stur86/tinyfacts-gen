When you want to talk to someone about a problem, you often ask them to talk with you. You might say,  Can you help me with this? Or you might say, I need your help. This is called a conversation.

A different way to use this word is if many people need to talk about the same thing. They might meet in a room and talk for a long time. This is a group talk. Everyone is there to share their ideas.

Sometimes, a group of people want to talk about a big problem that is not clear. They ask a person who is an animal or a person with a lot of money to help them. They ask him how he did it. This person might give them a way to do things. They learn from him.

You can also use this word for a meeting where many people are there. They all sit and listen to someone speak. This is a place for people to share their ideas and work together.
