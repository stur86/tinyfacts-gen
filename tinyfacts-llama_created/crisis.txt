A time of big trouble is called a hard time. This happens when things are very wrong and it is hard to stay safe. A person might feel very angry or scared. They might say they are going to act in a bad way.

Sometimes, this happens because a big problem is happening. Maybe there is a lot of water that is going out of a town. The people who live there are very worried because they do not know what to do. They might start to fight with each other.

Another time, a person might get very sick. They feel very bad and their body is under a lot of pain. They might need to stay in a hospital for a long time.

Hard times can also happen when a person loses a job. They might not have money and they feel very scared. They might ask their family for help.

Sometimes, a hard time happens because of our own doing. Maybe we act in a way that makes the world feel bad. This is called a problem. We might do something that hurts someone else or something that makes the world feel very wrong.

It is very important to talk about hard times. This helps us know that everything will go through and that we can get through it together.
