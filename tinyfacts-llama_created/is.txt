To is is a word you use to tell who is doing something. It is a word that says a person is doing a thing.

When you say, you are going to the store, you are using this word. You are telling the story of what you do. You say, I am going. This word is saying you are the one doing it.

If you say, he is the one who does it, the person is the one. If you say, she is the one, the person is the one. If you say, it is me, the person is the one.
