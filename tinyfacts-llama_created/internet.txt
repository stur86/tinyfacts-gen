The word you are looking for is very important. It is a way for people to share things with each other over long lines of power. These lines go through many places and many computers. When you send a word, a picture, a song, or a picture to someone, it goes through these lines.

The power is like a huge box that can hold a lot of words and pictures. When you want to see a picture, your computer sends the picture to the power. The power looks at the picture and shows it to you. It works for every computer in the world.
