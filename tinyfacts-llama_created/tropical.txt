The word we use when we talk about a place is very important. It tells us where things are. Think about your home. Is it near the water? Is it in a green area where trees grow a lot? Or is it in a big city with many tall houses?

Things that live in water, like animals and big moving things, are usually found near the water. They like the warm water. The warm water makes them happy. They go there to play and eat. They sleep at night and wake up at night.

Tall places are far from the water. They have big high walls around them. The high walls keep the warm water from going in. The high walls also keep the cold air out. In these places, the day is very hot. The night is very cold. People who live there have to wear a warm cover to keep from getting very cold.

Sometimes, a place has both hot and cold days. The hot days are very warm. The cold days are very cold. This is called a place with changing days. The hot days are called summer days. The cold days are called fall days or year days.
