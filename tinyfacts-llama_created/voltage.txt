To use a power thing is to send a small push of power into a wall. This power thing makes the wall do work. It makes things turn, light, and water go.

When you use a thing to power a small thing, you need to tell it how much power to send. This is like a number. The number tells the thing how strong to be.

If you send too many power, the thing might get very hot. This can make it break. If you send too little power, the thing might not work at all. This is why we need power things to keep our homes, cars, and other things going.

There are many kinds of power. Some send very strong power to light up a room. Other power sends power that makes things move. Some power sends power to make the air go around your head. Every kind of power is different.
