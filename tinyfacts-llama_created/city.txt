A city is a place where many people live and work. There are usually many tall houses and big blocks with many stories. These tall houses are often very tall and can go up into the sky.

Cities are usually very busy. There are many cars, buses, and trains that go around the city. People move in and out of the city all day. They go to work, go to school, buy food, and buy clothes. Many people live in the city with their families and friends.

Cities also have many shops and places that people go to buy things. There are places to eat, places to drink, and places to watch movies. Some cities have many big parks where people can walk and play.

In a city, there are many different groups of people. Some people live in high houses and have lots of money. Other people might live in small houses with not much money. There are also people who work in big offices with many people. Some people might live in small apartments in the city.

Cities have big spaces that are open. People might walk or ride their cars through these open spaces. There are also parks and green areas where people can sit and relax.

Cities are often very safe. People are usually very carefully about their bodies and their money. There are also police and other people who help keep the city running.

Cities can be very big. Some cities have many more people than small towns. But all cities are made of many small towns and cities that are joined together.
