To build a thing or a place is to make something new. When you build a house, you make a room for people to live in, and you make a way to go around it. You use wood and other things to make it strong and safe.

You can also make a place for animals. A dog might build a small house in a field for its young ones. A cat might build a small house in a house for its babies. This is how the animal keeps its family safe while it is away.

Sometimes, we build a thing that is for everyone. A school is a place where people go to learn together. A park is a place where people can go to play or sit. A hospital is a place where people go if they are very sick.

We also build things to help us move. A car is a thing you drive to go to another place. A house is a place to live. A tree is a thing that grows in the ground and gives us food.

When you build something, you do a job that helps the world. You make the world a better place for all of us.
