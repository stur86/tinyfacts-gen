Drinking water is how we get water from the ground into our bodies. When the rain falls on the ground, some of it goes into the water that is under the ground. This water then goes down into the ground, and it can go many hundreds of feet down into dark places under the ground.

Sometimes, water from a high place goes down to a low place. This is what happens when you drop a water bottle on the floor. The water from the bottle falls down to the ground. It goes down through the ground and into the places under the ground where water is waiting.

The places under the ground are very deep. They are like very long ways down into the ground. Water from many high places goes down to one of these deep places. The water goes down very slowly. It is always going down, but it does not go very fast.

If the ground gets wet and heavy, water can go down through the ground. This is why you might see water coming from a green area. The ground is getting ready to give up some of its water. You can also see this happening if you look at the ground after it rains. The ground looks wet and heavy, but it is still standing up.

Sometimes, the ground can get very heavy with water. This is called a big water problem. The water can fill up the ground and make it hard for people to walk. This happens when a lot of rain comes down at once. It is like a big drink of water for the ground. It fills up the ground and makes it hard to move around.

We also have places under the ground where water can go very quickly. These places are very deep and have a lot of water. If you were to drop a rock into one of these places, the water would come out very quickly. This is why you can see water coming out of a place under the ground if you look at it from far away.

In some places, the ground is very high and the water goes up very fast. This can be very strong. You might feel a pulling feeling when the ground is under water. This is why people can fall down when the ground is moving under them.

Water under the ground is important for many things. It gives us places to live and work. It is what makes our world look green and blue. It is also what makes our ground soft and soft places to sit.
