To enter something is to go into it. You can enter a room when you walk into a house. You enter a game when you start playing. You can also enter a book. If you read a book, you are entering its world.

When you enter a place, you show you are coming. You might be going to a store to buy food. You enter a school to learn. You enter a car to drive. You enter a room with your friends. You all enter the room together.

If you enter something that is not safe, you can get hurt. A dog can enter a house and look for food. A cat might enter a room and see a ball. You must be carefully when you enter something that might be broken.
