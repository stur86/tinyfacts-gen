A word used to talk about money and things.

A person who works in a job, like a doctor or a teacher, has many things they want. They might have a job, money in a box, and a car. They might have a home or a house. They might also have things they bought to make money, like books or clothes.

When you have many of these things, you are in a group. Many people in this group are working in jobs. Some are doctors, some are teachers, some are in the money world, and some are in other jobs. They are all working for the same person.

A person who does not have a job is also in a group. They might be a student or a person who helps with things. They are part of the same group because of the things they have.

In a group like this, there is one person who is the head. This person helps the others and makes sure everything works. This person is important.

You can also talk about a group of things. If you have many books, a group of books is a group. If you have many money, a group of money is a group. If you have many friends, a group of friends is a group.

To put all this together, a group is a group of people, things, or money that work together.
