The word they is used to talk about people. When we say someone is they, we mean they are the person we are talking about. It is a name for the person.

Think of the word like this: if you are the one who is sitting at the table, you are you. If you are the one who is drinking a cup of coffee, you are also you. This word does not tell us which person you are. It is the person we talk about.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about a group of people. If I say, they are the people who live in this town, I am talking about all the people in that town. This word helps us when we want to say many people at once.

We also use this word to talk about things. If you say, they are the book on the table, you are talking about the book. It does not matter if the book is the one you read or the one your friend reads. You are talking about the book.

In this way, the word they is an important word to use when you are talking about people, things, or groups of people.
