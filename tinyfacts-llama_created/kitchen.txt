A kitchen is a room where food is made. It is usually part of a house, but it can also be a small building that stands alone.

Inside a kitchen, there is a table where people sit to eat. They use the table to put their food in front of them and to eat. There is also a big top part with many small windows. The top part lets the bright light from the sky come in and makes the room feel warm.

People who work in a kitchen are called kitchen people. They make food. They use big things to cut food into small pieces. They also use big things to move food around. Some kitchen people are very fast and can make food very quickly. Others might take their time and make food one piece at a time.

There is a part of a kitchen that is very small. This part is where the people who work here make food for one person at a time. It is very quiet because there are no many people inside.

In some homes, there is a kitchen with a door. This door is closed when the family is inside. When someone wants to eat food, they open the door and the food comes out. This is a way to share food with the people they care about.

Kitchen people also do other jobs. Some work in a store near the kitchen. They help people buy food. Others work in a building that makes food for people to eat on the road. They pack food into big bags and put them in cars.

In some places, there is a kitchen with many people. This is called a big kitchen. Many people work there at the same time. They make food for many people at once. This is a very busy place. They use many big things and move food around very fast.

Some people use a kitchen to make food for animals. They have big things that make food for the animals. This is important because animals need food to stay strong.
