To deal with something is to handle it. You can deal with your body after you have eaten. Your heart might be dealing because you feel happy. You might also deal with a job. You might feel like you are doing a good job. When you deal with a person, you try to understand them. You listen to what they say. You might agree or you might say you do not agree.

To deal with money is to manage it. You might spend money on food. You might save money for a big thing. You might save money for a car. You do this so you do not run out of money.

To deal with a problem means you try to find a way to fix it. You might talk to a friend. You might think about what you did wrong. You try to learn from your past. You try to do better next time.
