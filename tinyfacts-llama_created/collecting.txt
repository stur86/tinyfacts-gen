To go around and pick up small things you want is called going out and getting things. You can do this when you are looking for a book to read or a game to play.

You can also go out and get things you do not need. If you see food that you want to eat, you go to the store. You buy the food and take it home.

Sometimes, people go out to see places they have never seen. They might walk, ride a car, or fly in a big thing in the sky.

When you go out, you go from one place to another. You move through the world. You see new things and meet new people. This helps you learn about the world.

You can go out in the morning to go to school or work. You go out in the afternoon to meet a friend or go to a place to watch a movie. You go out at night to have fun.

Going out is important for us. It helps us learn about the world, meet new people, and have new ideas.
