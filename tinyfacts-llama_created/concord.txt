To be in a group that agrees is to be in a group where everyone has the same idea. When you say this, you mean that no one is different from the others. All the people are happy with the same thing.

Imagine you are in a room with many people who love music. Everyone is there because they all like the same kind of music. They are all smiling because they are together and they know everyone else likes the same thing. They are all happy and they feel like they are a group.

Sometimes this happens in school or at work. If you are in a group of people who all like the same book, you are all in a group that agrees. You all like the book because you all like the same story.

It is good to be in a group like this. It makes the people feel safe. It makes them feel like they are part of something important. When everyone agrees, the people feel happy and they are more ready to work or talk together.
