To break something into many parts is to make it smaller. Think of a big box. If you open it, you have many small pieces inside. Each piece is a part of the box. If you take one piece and break it, you are taking one part of the box.

Things have many parts too. A tree has many parts. It has a top, a bottom, and many in the middle. When we say a tree is broken, we mean it is broken into many parts. We can pick one part to make something new. We can use the wood from that part to make a chair.

A person is made up of many parts. Each part works together. The brain does what the heart does. The head does what the body does. The arms do what the body does. This is why we are all made of many parts.

You can break a small thing. You can break a glass or a piece of paper. But the thing that is made of many parts can not be broken so easily. The body is made of many parts. If one part is broken, the body can still stay in one piece.

Sometimes, when we say something is broken, we mean it is not working. If a door is broken, it does not open. If a word is broken, it is not the way it should be. We can fix these things. We can put parts back together. We can make the word right again.

In life, we break many things. We break a promise. We break a friend. We break a plan. But we can also break things. When we break a glass, it is hard to fix. When we break a heart, it can hurt a lot. But we can also break a plan and learn from the bad things that happen.
