Always means something that is always the same. It is a word you use when you want to talk about a thing that does not change.

If you see a tree, it is always there. It does not go away. It is always there on the street or in the park. This is why people say the word always.

If you have a book, you can read it every day. The words stay the same every day. The book does not change. This is why people say the word book always.

In a game, the player must always play the same way. They must never change the way they move or what they say. This is why people say the word game always.

It is also a word for a far away place. If you are in your house and you hear music, you can say you are in a far away place. This is because the music is not coming from your room. It is coming from another place.

Sometimes, people use the word always to mean something is true for the whole time. If you say your dog is always happy, you mean the dog is always happy. You do not say it is happy sometimes. You say it is always happy because it is always happy.

In this word, you can also use it to mean something is the same in every way. You can say a person is always kind. This means the person never does anything wrong. They always do the right thing.

The word is important because it helps us understand how things work. If something is always the same, we know what to expect.
