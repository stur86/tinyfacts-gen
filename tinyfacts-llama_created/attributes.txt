An animal or a person has many things that are true about them. These are called the animal or person word. You can say the word is big, fast, or can fly.

A word can also have many other words that are not part of the word. These are called the word part. For a dog, the word part is word, fast, and can. The word as a whole says that the dog is a fast animal that can fly.

Think of a tree. The word part is word, tall, and green. The word as a whole is a tall green tree.

You can also say that a person has many good words. If a person is kind and nice, you say they are a nice person. If a person is funny, you say they are funny. These are all part of the word part.

An animal has many of these words too. If an animal is a dog, it is a fast animal. If an animal is a cat, it can climb. Every animal word part is found in every animal word.
