To have a head is to have a top part of something. It is the part that sits on your head. When you are sitting, you have a head. When you are standing, you also have a head. When you are running, you have a head. It is the part that holds your body up.

When you write something, you might put a picture on top of the paper. This is called a head. You can also put a name or a number on top of the paper. This is called a top line. This helps people understand what the picture or number says.

Sometimes, when you talk about a computer, you might say a picture on top of the computer is the top line. This shows how the computer works and what you can do with it.
