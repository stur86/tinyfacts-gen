To be the newest means you are the first one of something. It is when you are the first person to do it. If you are the first one, everyone else is behind you.

Imagine you are at a big party with many people. If one person does something first, you are the newest person. Your name is now the one that is first on the listened to name. Everyone remembers you now.

If you have a dog, and you get a new dog, it is the newest dog. If you get a new book, it is the newest book. This means you are the first person to have it.

You can also be the newest person in a group. If many people are working together to do a big job, and one person does it, everyone is now the newest person. They are the first one to do it.

Being the newest is a good feeling because it is a new thing. It means you are in a new place and doing something new.
