To begin something is to start it. It means you start to look into a problem or a thing that is not clear. You ask questions. You look at things. You try to find out what happened.

Imagine you see a person who is doing something wrong. You want to know why. You might ask that person. You might also look at their words. You might look at what they did. You might look at what other people think. If you find out why they did it, you can tell the people in power. You can tell the police or the teacher. You can tell the person who is in a bad position so they can change.

Sometimes you start with a question. You ask if something is true. If the answer is yes, you have found out what you were looking for. If the answer is no, you know there is a problem and you can find a way to fix it.

It is not the same as to think about something. To think about a thing is to have a feeling about it. You might think about a picture. You might think about a word. You might think about a story. These are different. When you start to look into something, you are trying to find out the facts. When you start to think about something, you are trying to understand why.
