People wear clothes every day. They are things you put on your body. They keep you warm when it is cold and make you look nice when you go out. You can wear many different kinds of clothes. Some are for work, some are for school, and some are for fun.

When you are young, you wear clothes that are small and have pictures on them. Later, you might wear clothes that are bigger and have more colors. Some clothes are for just one day, like a shirt for one day. Some clothes are for many days, like a pair of shoes that you wear every day.

Your clothes are made of different kinds of things. Some are made of soft stuff that feels good when you touch it. Some are made of hard stuff that keeps you safe. You can also wear clothes with numbers on them so you know which one is which.

Some people only wear clothes they bought. They might pick a few clothes and keep them for a long time. Other people buy many clothes at once. They like to have many clothes so they can change their look easily.

Clothes are very important. They help you stay warm in the cold, keep you looking nice when you go out, and help you feel good when you wear them. If you do not wear clothes, you will feel very cold and very sad.
