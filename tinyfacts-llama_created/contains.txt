To put something inside something else means you place one thing in a larger space. This happens many times in our lives.

Imagine a big box. Inside this box, you can keep your favorite book. You put the book in the box. Now the box is full. The box has what you need. If you put a small ball into the box, the ball will not go into the wall of the box. The ball will stay inside.

If you have many small things, you can put them inside a bigger box. This box is called a bag or a bag with many pockets. Inside the bag, many small things are kept. When you want to find one of those small things, you can look inside the bag.

Sometimes, when you put a thing in a box, you are not always sure if it is safe. You might put a rock into a box to make it look bigger. But a rock is not a good thing to put into a box. It can make the box look strange and make people feel afraid. If you put a bad thing in a box, you must get it out and throw it away.
