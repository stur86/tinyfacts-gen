To write a computer is one thing. To write a computer that can do things for you is another. This is what a computer does when you tell it to. You give it numbers and words, and it does what you want.

Think of it like a phone. When you want to send a word, you type it into the phone. The phone takes the word, puts it in a box, and then sends it to another phone. The other phone gets the word and sends it back to your phone. This is how computers send and get things.

A computer can do many things. It can read numbers and words, do what you ask, remember what you have asked before, and do not make a problem worse. It can also do things without your help. It can do many things at once, all at once, very fast.

You can use a computer for many things. You can look at pictures and movies. You can write stories and books. You can talk to friends far away. You can learn new things and remember them.

But computers are not all the same. Some are very good at one thing. Some are good at many things. Some are slow, and some are very fast. Some can only do one kind of job. Some can do many jobs at once.

You also use computers to keep your money safe. You put money in a computer box, and the box keeps it safe. The computer is like a safe for your money.
