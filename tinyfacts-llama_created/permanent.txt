When you talk about things that last, you say they are not going away. A tree is a thing that stays in the ground for many years. It does not change at all. If you kill the tree, it does not come back. This is a very important idea.

You also say something is not going away. If you do not eat your food, you become very sick. You will not be able to walk or run like you do now. This is because the food in your body is not being used.

In this way, a word is very strong. It means that something will stay the same for a very long time. You can use it to talk about your life, your family, your home, or even your job. It is a word that helps you understand how things stay the same over a long time.
