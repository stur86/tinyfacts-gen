Facts are things that are real. They happen in the world we see. We learn this from our lives and from what others tell us.

Follows are things that are made up. They are not real. They are things we make up to tell a story. These stories can be about people, animals, places, or other things. They are not about what really happened.

When you read a story, you are following a set of facts. You know what is happening before the story starts. You know the names of the people and the places. You know what happens next. But then the story changes. It becomes something new. The story might be about a person who goes on a trip, or about a group of friends who have fun. The story does not follow the same set of facts. It is made up.

Sometimes, people make up stories to tell us a way to feel a way. They might tell us they are sad when they are really happy. They might tell us that something is big when it is really small. This is called using a word that is not true.

You can also make up stories to play a game. You might make up a name for a thing, a name for a person, or a name for a place. You might make up words to make a story sound more interesting. You can use these made up words to build a story that is fun to read.

People often use the word to talk about things that are not real. They might say something is a story if it is not true, even if it is about something that happened. They might say a movie is a story if it is about people and things that did not really happen.
