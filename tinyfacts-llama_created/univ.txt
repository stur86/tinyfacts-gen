Until

You can use this word to talk about time. It is the word we use to say how long something happens.

To start, you look at a long line of days. Every day after the first one is the same. So, one day is the same as the next day is the same. This line is called a line of days.

Then, you take the line of days and you cut it. This is called a cut. Each cut is like a small piece of the line. When you cut a piece of a line, you are changing how long the line is. But you are still on the same line.

You can have many cuts. Each cut is like a new piece of the line. The line of days is now made up of many pieces that you made by cutting. When you are done cutting, the line of days is finished.

The word until is used to say when something happens. You can use it to tell a story. You might say, I went to the store until I got home. This tells the reader what happened until you got home.

You can also use this word to talk about how things change. If you have a problem, you can try to fix it until the problem is gone. If you have a friend, you can talk to them until you understand each other.

The word until can also be used to talk about a long time before something happens. You can say, I have until tomorrow to finish my work. This means you will finish your work tomorrow.
