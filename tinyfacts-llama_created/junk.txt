To break something down is to take it into parts. This is how we do it with old things.

Imagine you have a big box. In this box, there are many things you do not want anymore. You have a chair, a table, and a book. All these things are very heavy. You need to get ridden them because they are not needed anymore.

You start by picking the things you want to keep. You look at them and decide which ones are important. You might keep the chair and the table. You might throw the book away. Then you go to a place to get ridden. This is a big building where people take things that are broken so they can be made into new things.

The people at the place look at the things you have to get ridden. They look at the chair and the table. They see that they are very strong and can help people. They decide that the chair and the table can be made into a new table or a new chair.

The things you threw away go into a big ground. There, they are burned. This is like a big fire that breaks down the old things. The fire is very hot, but it does not hurt you. It only breaks the old things into small pieces.

After the burning, the ground is clean. The old things are gone, and the ground is ready for new things to grow.
