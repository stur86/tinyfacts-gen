Accepting something means you let it happen. It is about being ready to deal with a problem when it comes up. Sometimes, you might feel surprised. Your friend might say, I have a booked face. You might feel a bit strange. You do not know what to do.

In your body, there are tiny things that live. When you use some things on your face, these tiny things can get out of control. They make red spots and come out in big lines. This is why you might have a booked face. These red spots are like white marks.

It is not because you are mean. You did not do anything wrong. You just have a booked face because your body is fighting with the tiny things. If you stop using those things and do not touch your face, the booked spots go away.

You can also get booked on your arms or legs. The red spots are the same as on your face. They might be smaller or bigger. Sometimes, the booked spots are very red and look like a big mark. Other times, they are more like tiny red lines.
