To go fast is to go very, very quickly. A car goes fast because it has many big parts turning very quickly. When you hear the word, it usually means something that is moving very fast.

One way to look at it is like a group of people. Imagine you are in a long line of people going to school. If everyone is moving at the same time, it looks like you are all moving at the same time. This is because everyone is turning around the same way. In this case, the group is moving as a whole.

Another way to look at it is like a small thing. If you move a small thing quickly around a round thing, it does not keep going in the same line. The small thing will turn around the round thing many times before it stops. This is because the round thing turns around many times too.

A car can have many of these kinds of things going on at the same time. The car might have many small things turning around it, many big things moving across its floor, and many people in the back who are all moving to the front. When all these things are moving together, the car goes very fast.

A thing that turns a round thing is called an hour. An hour is a big group of minutes. One minute is one hundred seconds. So one hour is one hundred hundred seconds. This is why the word hour is used to talk about big groups of time.

You can also use this word when talking about people. If someone is working very hard and they are going very fast, you might say they are going at a high hour. This means they are doing a lot of work and they are doing it very quickly.
