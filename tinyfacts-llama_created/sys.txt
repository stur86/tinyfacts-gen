A computer has many small parts that work together. When one part does something, it tells the others to do the same thing. If one part does not work, the others can still do their jobs.

Think of it like a group of people in a school. Each person has a job to do. If one person does not do their job, the others can still go to school and learn. The computer is like a group of people working together.

There are many types of these small parts. Some are very strong and can do many things. Some are very small and can only do one thing. Some are very heavy and need a lot of power to work.

When people say they are working on a computer, they often mean they are using a computer to do a job. They type words, numbers, or pictures into the computer. The computer then does the work. They can also look at the computer to see what the computer has done.

In some cases, people use computers to help them in their work. If a doctor wants to look at a picture of a body, the computer might help them find the right picture. If a teacher wants to show a student a picture, the computer can show it.

Computers also help us talk to other computers. When you type a word on a computer, it sends that word to another computer. This way, you can write a long piece of paper that many people can read at once.
