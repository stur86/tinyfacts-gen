To frown can mean two different things. One way is when you look at a picture of a person. If a person looks at you and their face is in a very sad way, they might frown. This is like a sign that they are not happy. It is like saying, I feel bad right now.

The other way is about a group of people. In a big school, there are many different groups. Some people like to read books. Some people like to play games. Some people like to draw pictures. They all live together under one big top. When they want to say they are happy or that they like something, they use a word. This word is called a sign. The word can be short or very long. If many of them use the same sign, the sign becomes a way that they all talk. It is like a way of saying, I am happy.

People also use this word when they are talking about a story. If someone tells a story that is very sad, they might frown. This can help the person feel less alone. It is like they are sharing a very hard thing that happened to them.

Also, if you are very tired, you might feel like you want to frown. You might look down at the ground. Your face might feel heavy. Your eyes might feel like they are heavy too. This is because your body is trying to rest. Your brain is thinking, I need to stay here for a bit, so I can feel better.

So, when you see a person frown, you know they might be sad or not happy. When you see a group of people use the same sign, you know they are all happy and talking about the same thing. When you see a person frown because they are tired, you know they need to rest.
