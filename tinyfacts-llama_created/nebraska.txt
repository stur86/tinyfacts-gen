Near. A long time ago, the word was a bit different. It started with the word for water, but people thought it should be for a place. They wanted a word that said, well, this is where you are. They used the word for water because it had a clear name. They put the word for water before the word for land. This made a word that said, this is where you get your water.

Later, people found a word for the big wide land. They used this word because it said, this is the land. The word for water was left behind.

Today, we know this word is for a state in the middle of the big wide land. It is not on the top or the bottom. It is in the middle, near the big water. People say, go to the place in the middle of the big wide land. This place has a lot of water. There are many small towns. There are also many big cities. The people who live there might not always have as much water as people in other places, but they have a lot of open land.

The word has nothing to do with the animals. You would not say, go to the land of the horse. It is a word for a place, not for a kind of animal.
