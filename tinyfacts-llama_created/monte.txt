A place with many high points is called a high top. On the top of a high top, there is often a great wide open area. You can see for many days in each direction. The top of a high top is often very cold. People go there to run fast and feel the wind in their hair. They often climb the high top on foot or by a long long way of moving cars. In the cold, the air is very still. This is why the top of a high top can feel very quiet. In the warm sun, the high top can feel very hot.
