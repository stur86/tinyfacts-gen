To see something, but not pay it much attention.

You might think about a fight. You might remember what happened and how you felt. You might not really remember the words the people said. You just notice that things are not the same.

Sometimes you look at a picture of a place you love. You see the green trees and the blue water. You see the houses that are like your home. You do not really think about what happened there. You just see the place.

You can also see things that are not real. You might see a number that is strange. You might see a word that does not make sense. You just look at it and do not try to understand it.

You can also see a person who is not there. They are in another room, talking to another person. They are laughing and smiling. You do not see them, but you notice their face is happy.

Sometimes you need to see something to know what to do. You see a sign that says stop. You see a word that says you are not allowed. You act. You do not just stand there. You act on what you see.

To see something is to notice it. It is to look at it. It is to pay attention.
