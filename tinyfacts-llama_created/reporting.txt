To write is to tell people about what you saw. You might write about a fight that happened in a school. You might write about a dog that ran fast. You could write about a strange thing that happened at night. You tell others what you think happened.

When you write, you are also saying that what you saw happened. You are telling them about the story. This helps others understand what is going on in the world.

People who work in the press write like this. They write stories about what happens in cities. They write about what people say. They write about what is happening in the world. They help people know what is going on.

A person can also write about things that are not happening. They might write about a dream they had. They might write about a feeling they have. They tell people about their own life.
