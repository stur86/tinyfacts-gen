To buy something is to pay for it. This happens when a person wants a thing they want or need. They go to a store and look at many things. They pick one they like. The store person helps them. They give them the money they paid for the thing. Once the person has the thing, they can use it. They are happy because they have it.

Buying is important for people. It lets them get what they want. If a person does not have money, they might not be able to buy a thing they need. Buying helps make life better.
