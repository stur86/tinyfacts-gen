To grow something is to make it come into being. It starts as a tiny thing that is not there. It needs the right thing to happen. The right thing is what makes the tiny thing grow. The tiny thing gets stronger and bigger. It makes new tiny things so it can stay. This happens in the ground. It happens in the air. It happens in the water.

You do this with your body. You breathe in air that has good stuff. You drink water. You eat food. You use your body. When you use your body, it gets stronger. Your skin is made stronger. Your hair is made stronger. Your eyes are made stronger. You grow new parts. Your hair comes out of your body. Your body becomes bigger. You become a new person.

You also do this with things you buy. You put the right things into a box. You put water in it. You put food in it. You put the right air in it. You keep it in a warm place. After a while, something comes out of the box. It is a thing you need to live. The thing is not a person. It is just a thing you made. You made it so it can live.

There are many ways to grow things. You can grow food in the ground. You can grow things in a box. You can grow things in the air. You can grow things in a box with water and food. You can make a box grow by putting the right things into it. You can make a box grow by keeping it in a warm place. You can make a box grow by giving it the right air.
