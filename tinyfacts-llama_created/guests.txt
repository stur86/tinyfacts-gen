To have a person as a friend is to be happy they are there. This is how people usually feel when they are at a party, a dinner, a movie, or a game. You are glad they came.

If you are a person, you can also say you are a friend. This is when you have a close person you like. You might talk with them a lot, or you do things together. You feel safe with them.

Sometimes, we have a friend who lives far away. We do not see them often, but we still care. We write letters, talk on the phone, and send pictures. This is a way to stay close even when we are far apartment.

We can also say you are a friend if you help someone in need. If a person has a bad day, you can be there to give them a hug, a word of hope, or a new idea.

A group of people who all like the same things can also be friends. They may have a big party, a game, or a movie night. They are happy to see each other and can laugh together.

The word can also mean someone who is not very good at their job. If you have a friend who does not do their work, you might feel sad. You might want to help them. But you still care about them.

In a way, to have a friend is to share your life with someone. You show them you care, you listen to them, and you might even do things together. It is a big feeling to be a friend, and we all need these people in our lives.
