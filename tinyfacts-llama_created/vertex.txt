A point in a line where three lines cross is called a point. Every line has three points. Imagine three lines in a room. One line crosses a wall, one line crosses a table, and one line crosses the floor.

A line can cross many points. Think of a line that goes through many houses. There is a point on each house, and all the points are on the line.

A point is a single point. It has no sides, and it is not a line. There is only one point at a time.

You can also talk about a point in space. Imagine a room with many walls and a floor. There is a point in the middle of the room. This point is not on any of the walls or the floor. It is a single point in a very big space.

If you have a line, you can find two other points on the line by looking at the line. You can find these points by looking at the line many times. If you find the same two points, you know you have found two points on the line.

A point can also be a point of a person. A person has many points. One point is the front of their body. Another point is the back of their body. Another point is the top of their head. Another point is the bottom of their feet.

There are also points in music. A point is a small number in a song. It tells the person who made the song how to play it. The point can be a number that is one more or one less than a number. It can be a number that is half of a number, or a number that is one third of a number.

In a story, a point is a place where something happens. It is the moment when the story starts or when something changes.

A point of a thing is the middle of the thing. It is the point where the thing starts and ends. Think of a ball. The point of the ball is the middle of the ball. If you reach the point with your hand, you have reached the ball.

All the points in a line are on one line. The points are all on the line and not on any other line. This is why a point is a single point.
