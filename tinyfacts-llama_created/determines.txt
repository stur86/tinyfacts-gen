To decide means you have to think about it. You look at a problem and try to find the answer.

One way is to see if there is a right answer. If you look at a piece of paper, you can see if a number is right or not. If the number is not right, you know you made a wrong move.

Another way is to try different ideas. If you are trying to decide if a drink is good, you might try drinking it first or try it with something else. You want to see if it is better than the drink you already had.

Sometimes, you need to decide between two things that are both important. You might want to go home now, but you also want to watch your favorite movie later. You have to think about what is more important.

You can also decide on a plan. If you want to go to a party, you need to decide what you want to do. Do you want to go to the party alone or with your friends? You are deciding what to do next.
