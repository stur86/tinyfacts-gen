A rider is a person who moves a thing from one place to another. This thing is called a horse. A horse is a big animal with strong legs and a body that can carry a person. The rider sits in the back of the horse and uses their feet to control the horse.

The rider holds a long thing that helps them control the horse. This thing is called a brushed. The brushed is a hard thing that sits on the horse. When you pull on the brushed, the horse moves. You use this power to go fast or slow.

Some people ride horses for fun. They go to a place with green fields and follow the horse. Other people ride horses to work. They go to a city to bring food or money. The rider must be very good at holding the horse and using the brushed to stay safe.

Sometimes, people ride horses to help a doctor. They carry a box with the doctor and the box goes from one place to another to bring help to the people who need it. This is a very important job.

We also use the word to talk about people who work for a big group. They do the same job every day and help people who work for the group. The people in these jobs are called the group. The group is like a big team that works together to do things for many people.
