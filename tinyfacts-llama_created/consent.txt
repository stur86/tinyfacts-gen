To give your word is to say you are okay. It is when someone agrees with what another person is doing. When a person says yes to a thing, they are giving their word.

Think about a game with a group of kids. One person might say,  I want to run the first round. If you agree, you are also letting them run the round. You are giving them your word.

In this way, everyone knows what is happening. No one can do anything without a word from everyone. This is important because it keeps the group safe.

Sometimes, people use this word for a very different reason. If someone says they will do something if you let them, it is not okay. You still need to know if they are really going to do it.

Also, if you say yes to something, you must follow through. If you say yes to a game, you must be ready to play it. If you say yes to something, you must do it.

The word is very important. It helps us know if we are doing the right thing.
