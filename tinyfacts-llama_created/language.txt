A word is a thing that you use to talk or write. Each word is made of many small parts that come together. When you say a word, the sound of the parts comes out as a sound you can hear.

A word is not just a small thing. It is a part of a story. It tells you who is talking, where they are, what they are doing, and what they are saying. It helps people understand each other.

Think about a word you love. Maybe you love your mom. If you say her name, you are talking about her. You are not just talking about a thing. You are talking about her. You are showing her how much you care.

People have many words to talk about things. You can talk about a friend, a dog, a car, a tree, or a house. Each word is like a tiny piece of a story that helps you share your ideas with others.

Some words are easy to say. Others are hard to say. Some words tell us what to do. Others tell us how to feel. A word can make us laugh, make us cry, or make us feel happy.

You can also learn new words. When you hear people talk, you start to see words. Later, when you start to talk, you learn which words are good to use. This is how you become a better person.

A word can change how we feel. If you say something nice, a word can make you feel happy. If you say something mean, a word can make you feel bad. We use words to make our lives better and to show love to each other.
