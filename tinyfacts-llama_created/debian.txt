Deepest: This word means something is very deep. It is about how something is going inside your body or inside the world. When you are deep in a book, you are deep in the story. When you are deep in a tree, you are very high up. It is a word for how something is.

Deepest: In a group of animals, the one at the top is the one that is most important. This animal has a job that no other animal can do. It finds food and makes sure everyone else has enough to eat. The one at the bottom may not know the same things as the top one. But it helps too. It keeps everything moving so everything stays together. This is why the top animal is the deepest animal of all.

In a city, the one who works the most hours is the one who gets the most money. But in a group of people, the one who is the most kind is often the one who is deepest. They are the ones who help others and keep everyone safe. They are the ones who are most important in the group.

To be the deepest: This means to be the one who is most important. You do this by doing the best job you can. You are like the top animal in your group or the top person in your city. You are the one who makes everything work.
