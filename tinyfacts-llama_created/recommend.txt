To mean something is to say it is good for you.

You might tell a friend that a new movie is very funny. You are saying that this movie is a good thing to watch. You are not saying that it is the only good movie, but you think it is a good one.

You also might say that you will try a new food. If you try it and you like it, you are telling everyone that it is a good idea to eat this food.

This word is often used when you want to suggest that something is a good idea. You might say,  I think you should go for a walk. I think it is a good idea to get moving and clear your head. This is because you are trying to help the person do something that is good for them.
