When something is very small, but still you can buy it, it is called very small. If you can buy a small thing with money, it is very small. This is how we use the word to talk about things.

To be very small is like a tiny thing. You can not see it. It is not a thing you see. It is a tiny piece of something. Most of the time, when you say something is very small, you are talking about a tiny thing.

Another way to say it is that you can buy it. If you can buy a tiny thing, the thing is very small. You can buy a tiny piece of paper that is only one tiny bit of paper. Or you can buy a tiny piece of wood. Both of these things are very small.

To be very small also means you are very quiet. If you speak very little, you are quiet. This is like the small tiny thing. If you speak very little, you do not make a sound. You just whisper.

A very small group of people is a group with only a few people. There are not many people. They are small. They do not have many friends.
