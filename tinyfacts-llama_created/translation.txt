To understand a word means to know what it is and how it works. Sometimes words are very different from each other. If you hear a word, you might not know what it means. You might think it means something else.

Think about a song. You hear a word, and you think, I am happy, I am sad, I am angry, I am afraid. But this word is not in the song. The word is just a word. The song is the real thing.

A person can help you understand a word. They read it out loud. This lets you see the word, so you can know its meaning. They might use the word many times. The more they use the word, the more they know what it means.

If you want to say something in a different way, you can use a word. You might say, I am happy. But you did not use the word right. You said, I am sad. This is because you used the word, but you did not understand it. You used it the wrong way.

This is what a different word is. It is a word you use instead of another word. It is like a different name for the same thing.
