To be fuller can mean two different things.

One way is to be a large thing in the middle of a water way. It is a big round thing that keeps the water from going past it. If you look at a small round thing, it is small. If you look at a big round thing, it is bigger. This kind of thing is full.

Another way is to be a big group of people or animals. If you have a lot of friends, you can say you are bigger than one person. This means you are more many.

You can also say a thing is fuller if you have more things to show. Imagine you have a box that is full of small things. Now you add a few more small things to the box. It becomes bigger. You have become fuller.

Sometimes we say a thing is fuller when we talk about how we feel. If you are very happy, you might feel fuller inside. This is because you have more good things in your body than before.
