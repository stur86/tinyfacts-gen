A key is something you use to open a door or a box. It is usually long and has many lines on it. You put your finger in the lines and turn the key. This makes the door open or the box open.

Some keys are very big and heavy. They are for the door of a house. You can use them to go inside and stay safe. Other keys are small and light. They are for a computer or a box that makes music. You use these keys to make the music come out.

People also use keys to keep things locked. If you put a key in the lock, the door will not open. You need the key to be the right one to get into the room.

A key can also be something else. You might have a key to your car. This key opens the car door so you can get in. It is a way to get to the car when you need it.

When you play music on a box that makes sound, you use keys to make the sounds happen. The keys are like the numbers you write down to tell the box how to play. Each key is for a different sound. When you press the right key, the box makes the right sound.
