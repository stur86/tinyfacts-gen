To suppose something means you think it is true. You might think a person is happy. You might not see them at all. You are not sure. You are not sure they are happy. This is how you act.

You might put a picture of a happy face in your head. You might see a smile. You might feel a little happy inside. This is your way of believing something is true. You are not sure, but you are sure it is possible.

Sometimes you might not have enough to know. You do not have enough facts. You do not know everything. But you still think it is true. This is how people act when they do not know the answer to a question.

It is like you are saying, I do not know, but I want to believe it is true. You are not sure, but you want to believe. This is why people use the word supposed.
