A picture made with many lines is called a picture. This can be from a book, a movie, or a computer. When we look at this picture, our eyes see colors and make sense of what is happening.

Imagine you are in a room. You look at a wall. The wall has many lines and colors. You can see which side is which and where the wall starts and ends. If you were to write down all the lines and colors, you could write a long story.

A computer has a very fast way to put these lines and colors together. It can do this very quickly. This means we can look at pictures many times fast. We can see how a car moves, how a building looks, or how a person is feeling.

The word also means something that is made of many lines and colors. When a person draws a picture with a small line, they are using this word. The word does not say how the lines are put together. It only says that the picture has many lines and colors.
