A person who pulls a heavy thing over the water is called a nodding person. They use a long line with a heavy end to pull the thing. They stand in the water and use their arms to move the thing across the water. This is how a big thing that helps people get across is moved from one side of a big body of water to the other.

There is also a person who is very strong and helps keep the people on a big thing that moves across the water. They are called a nodding man. They help the people stay safe while the big thing is moving over the water.

In a fight, a person might say, I am a nodding man. This means they are very strong and can help keep people safe.
