To deal with something means you are trying to get rides of it. If you are a person, you might try to deal with a feeling that is very sad or angry. You want to stop thinking about it so you can feel better.

Sometimes, you might deal with a thing that is hurting you. If your arm hurts after you use a computer, you might try to deal with it. You might rest it, use ice, or take a drink. You want to make it stop hurting so you can go back to doing your work.

You can also deal with a problem in your life. If you do not do well at a job, you might try to deal with the situation. You might talk to a friend, write a letter, or just stay quiet and try to move on.

To deal with something is to take control of it. It means you are the one who decides what happens next. You are not afraid of what is happening because you know you can fix it.

Sometimes, people deal with a big problem. They are the ones who fix everything. They are the ones who make sure everyone is safe and happy again. This is a very important job.
