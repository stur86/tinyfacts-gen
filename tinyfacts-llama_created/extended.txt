To get something longer means to add more parts to it. 

Imagine you have a small box. You start with one small part. Then you add another small part on top. This makes the box bigger and longer. You have now a big box.

A person can also get something longer. Imagine you start with a short walk. You do it many times. After many short walks, you get a long walk. You have walked many many times. Your legs get stronger and your feet become bigger.

Sometimes, you get something longer because of the things you do. If you eat a lot of food, you might get bigger. When you are very full, your body feels heavy. This is your body getting longer.

We also get something longer when we learn. When you read books, you learn new words and ideas. This makes your brain bigger and stronger. You can remember things from the books for a long time.

A longer thing can also be something that is bigger. Imagine a small door. A bigger door is longer. You can open it more easily. A longer road is also bigger. It is easier to drive on it because there are more parts to follow.

To get something longer can be very good. It means you are growing or learning.
