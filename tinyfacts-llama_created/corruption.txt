To let something go to the ground means to let it fall down. If you drop a ball, it goes down to the ground. This happens when you let a thing fall without stopping it.

But if you drop a ball, you are not the one who lets it go. You might have dropped it, but someone else did it. The person who lets it go is the one who allowed it to fall. They did not stop it. They just let it go.

Sometimes, people let things go into a deep place. This is when they let things go that they do not want anymore. This can be very hard. If you keep things in your mind for too long, you might feel sad or angry. You want to get ridden. This is what people mean when they say something is going down.
