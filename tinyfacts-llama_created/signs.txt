A sign is something that shows a word or a picture. It tells you what to do or what is happening. You can see a sign on the road, a sign in a store, or a sign in the sky. It might say, in big letters, to turn left or to go straight. It might show a picture of a dog or a cat. It might say, in a small way, to be quiet.

Sometimes signs are made of paper. They might have words on them. Other times they are made of wood. They can be very big or very small. Some signs are very good at telling you what to do, while others are not so good. You have to look at them carefully to understand what they say.

In a city, there are many signs. Some are on the street, and some are on a wall. They help people know how to go around and how to stay safe. They are like a big group of words and pictures that help everyone move around together.
