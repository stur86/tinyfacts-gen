To winding a thing is when you do something that makes a group happy. It is a way to show you are the best.

Imagine you play a game with your friends. You run around a big room, and you do a few more steps than the others. The group of people who watch you decide to give you a small box with a round thing on it. This is like a tiny piece of paper that says you are the best.

In this way, the person who gives you the thing is like a teacher or a friend. They are saying, you are the top one. You did what everyone else did not do, and you did it better.

Sometimes, this happens with a person. Maybe you work very hard and do a great job. You get a big box with a picture on it. The picture is of your family, and the words say, you are our best person.

We also give these things to people who do things that are very important. If a doctor saves many lives, a teacher gives them a big box. A person who helps people in a place where there is no light gives the box to them.

These boxes are not just for one person. Sometimes, two people get them at the same time. If two kids do something very well and the teacher says they are the best two, the two kids get the boxes together.
