The word other is used to talk about people or things that are not like the one you are talking about. If you say something is the only one like it, you are being very sure. But if you say something is also like it, you are being more sure.

When you want to tell someone that a dog is not a cat, you might say,  The dog is other than the cat. This means the dog is not a cat, and you are not sure if it is the same kind.

Imagine you and your friend are playing with a ball. You both like the ball. But if you see a small ball, you might say,  That is other than the big ball. This means the small ball is not the big ball, and you are not sure which one it is.

We also use this word when we talk about people. If a person is other than the one you know, it means they are different from you. Maybe the person has a different name, a different job, or a different way of doing things. This makes their life different from your life.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about things that are not like a thing we already know. If you are thinking about a book, and then you see a picture of a tree, you might say,  That is other than the book. This means the picture is not the book, and it is not the same kind of thing.
