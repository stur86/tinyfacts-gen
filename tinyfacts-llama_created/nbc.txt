To be on the air means to be on a show. A show is when people talk and do things on a computer or a box with a small window. Many people are on the air to talk about their day or to do something funny. Some people talk about their work and other people talk about their lives.

A person might be on the air for many hours. They might talk about their favorite food or a game they played the day before. They might also tell stories to make people laugh or feel good.

Some shows are about books. A person will tell the story of a book and act it out with their voice and body. They might use words from the book and make sounds like the people in the story.

On the air, people also talk about the world. They might talk about what is happening with the people in their city. They might also talk about things that are not happening, like a big problem that happened in another place.

To be on the air can be a good thing. It can help people learn new things or see things that they might not have seen before. It can also be fun to watch and listen to.
