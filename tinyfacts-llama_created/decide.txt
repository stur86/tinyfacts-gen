To decide means you make a clear answer about what to do. You look at the facts and think about what is right. You might think about what is wrong and what you want to do instead. This helps you know what to do.

When you decide, you might use your brain. You might think about the people around you, the money, the time you have, or the things you want. You might also use your words. You can say, yes, no, or maybe.

To decide can be hard. Sometimes there is no clear right answer. You might feel sad or angry. But you can still decide. You might decide to stay calm and listen to both sides. Or you might decide to do something different.

Deciding can be for many reasons. You might decide to go to a movie. You decide to eat a new food. You decide to play with a friend. You decide to work hard. Deciding is a way to show you care about what happens.

Sometimes you decide for others. If a teacher says you are doing a good job, you decide to feel happy. If a friend says you are doing a good job, you decide to feel glad. You are helping them feel better.

To decide is not the same as to agree. To agree means you both think the same. To decide means you have a clear answer. To agree can be easy. But to decide can be harder. It takes time and thinking.
