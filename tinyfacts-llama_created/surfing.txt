To go fast on water is to ride the big blue thing. You stand on the top of it and push with your feet. The water pushes back, and you go up and down. The world goes round and round around you. You feel the wind in your hair, and the sun on your face.

The big blue thing is not a car. It does not have four lines with moving parts under the back. It has a bottom. The water under you holds you up. The top of it pushes you down. You have to feel the bottom and the top at the same time. If you feel the bottom and the top, you stay on the water.

You can do this on a small water thing with a round top. You can also do it on a long piece of wood that is pushed by people. The wood goes fast over the water, and the people push it. The people are on the bottom and the top at the same time.

Sometimes you go fast. You feel like you are going over the top of the world. You feel like you are not on the ground at all. You are on the water, and the water is under you. You are free.

But it is also very hard. You have to know how to push. You have to know how to feel. If you do not know, you fall in. You can hit your head on the top of the water. You can lose your foot or your leg. It is a big job.

Many people like to go fast on the water. They do it for fun. They try to go as far as they can. They try to go faster and faster. They push their bodies and their things to be the fastest. They are very strong. They can hold their breath for a long time.

But most people go to the water to relax. They go to sit in a chair and watch the blue thing go by. They look at the water and see the trees and the houses. They see the sky and the stars. They feel the warm sun on their skin. They feel the wind in their hair. They feel the water under them.

You can go fast or you can relax. You can feel the world around you. You can feel the wind, the sun, the water, and your own body. You can feel like you are the water.
