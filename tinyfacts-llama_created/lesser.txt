When you say something is less, you mean it is smaller or not as strong as another thing. It might be a little bit smaller, or it might not be as much as another thing.

Imagine you have a box. If you take the top off the box, what is left inside is smaller. You can hold it in your hand. If you take a dog and put it in the box, it will be smaller because it has to go in a smaller place. If you put a very big thing in the box, it will make the box look even bigger. This is what you mean by something being less.

You can also say something is less strong. If you are very strong, but you have to work hard to do it, then you are less strong. If you have a friend and you are very happy, but you do not want to talk to them, you are less happy. This is because you are not as happy as you were before.

It is also easy to say. You do not have to say how much you are. You just say you are less. This is why it is easy to talk about things in this way.
