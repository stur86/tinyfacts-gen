To follow something means to do it after you are already doing it. It is about keeping your eyes on the things you want to see.

Imagine you want to run to the park. You start running. After you are already running, you look to the side to see if there are any people. You look for the blue shirt of a boy who is playing a game. You follow him until you reach the top of a small green area. You are following him.

You do not stop in the middle. You keep going until you reach the park. You followed the boy so you could see him and the game. You followed a story so you could understand what was happening.

It is also about following a person. If you see a person walking down the street, you might follow them. You might want to know where they are going. You follow them because you want to see where they go.

You can follow a book. You might read a story and then you look back to see what happened next. You follow the story so you can know what happens next. You follow a game because you want to see how the other team does.

You can also follow a thing. If you see a car, you might want to see where it goes. You follow the car so you can see how it moves. This is important because you can learn from what it does.

Following can also mean following a way. If you want to go to a new city, you follow the road that leads there. You follow the signs and directions to get to your new home.
