The word best is used to talk about the things that are the best.

When you say a thing is the best, you mean it is the most good. It is the one that is very good in every way. If you have many nice things, and you pick one, that one is the best. It is the one that you like the most.

You can use this word to talk about many things. You might say, a book is the best book I have. Or you might say, a dog is the best dog. You are not being mean. You are just telling the truth.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about people. If a person is the best friend, it means they are the friend you like most. If a person is the best teacher, it means they are the person who makes you learn best.

To use this word, you must know what you mean. You must know that something is the best, not that it is better. If you say something is better, you are not talking about the best. You are talking about something that is still good, but not the best.
