To hear a sound is one thing. To make that sound is another thing. A cat does this all the time. It makes a sound with its mouth and then it hears the sound with its ears. Sometimes it is a high sound. Sometimes it is a low sound. Sometimes it is a sound that makes a small bit move. The cat hears the sound and then it does not make the sound anymore. It is gone.

People use this word with things that make sounds. The sound of a car can be heard by many people. If you are in a town and you hear a car sound, you know that a car is moving. The sound of a dog can be heard by a small dog. The sound of a dog can tell you if the dog is happy or if it is afraid.

We also use the word with things that move. A dog moves when it wants to go. A cat moves when it wants to climb or when it wants to jump. A small thing that you hold can make a sound. If you press the thing, the thing makes a sound. You can hear the sound if you listen.

Sometimes we use the word with sounds that are not very loud. A small sound in a room can be heard by everyone. A small sound that is very far away can be heard only by one person. We use this word to talk about these kinds of sounds.
