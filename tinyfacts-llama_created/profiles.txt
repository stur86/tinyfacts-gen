A person who has a name and a face is called a person. A person has a name, a set of things they do, and a picture of their face. This is the first way to know a person.

You also have a story. This story is about who you are and what you believe. It is a way to tell who you are and why you are there. You may have stories about your family, your school days, and your work. This story is inside you and it makes you who you are.

But when we talk about a person, we usually talk about their face and their name. We look at their picture and we hear their name. We do not look inside their brain like we look inside a book. We do not hear their stories like we hear a story. The name is the outside part of who they are.

Think of a name like a word. A word can be small or big. It is a word that tells us who you are. A name does not tell us about your stories. It is only the word.

People also have pictures that show who they are. These pictures are like a sign that says, this is who I am. A picture can help us remember a person. If you see a picture of a person, you know who they are even if you do not remember their story.

In a group, people may have the same name. This is like a team. They all say the same word. They all use the same sign. They all act the same way. This is how they work together and understand each other.

In a story, a person may have many stories. Each story is like a different picture. Each story tells a part of who they are. When we look at a story, we see the picture of who they are.
