To think is to be in your head. It is when you are very busy with your own brain. Your brain is like a computer. It takes numbers and makes them into words. It also takes your own ideas and makes them into words. When you think, you are not looking at the world outside your head.

You can think about many things. You might think about a day you had. You might think about a game you played. You might even think about a person you like. Your brain is always working, even when you are not paying attention.

But your mind is also about what you feel. You might feel happy or sad. You might feel angry. You might feel scared. These are all your own inside stories. Your brain is not always telling you what to do.

Sometimes, you might feel like you are not in your head. You might feel like you are in another room. You might feel like you are moving. This is because your body is also doing things. Your heart is beating. Your legs are moving. Your eyes are looking in a different way. Your mind is not always sure which one of these things is happening.

You can also have many minds at once. One mind might be in your head. Another mind might be in your heart. Another mind might be in your feet. Each one is like a different way of seeing the world.
