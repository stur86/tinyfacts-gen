When you use the word in this way, you mean something more than one thing.

Think of a story. You have one story. Then you tell another story. Each story is a new thing. So, if you tell a story, you are telling two things.

The word helps us tell more stories. We can say, for one thing, that you are going to the store, and for another, that you are going to eat a hot food. This adds more to what we know. You know where you are going. You know what you want to eat.

This word is also used in other ways. You might say, if you work hard at your job, you are doing more than you have to. This means you are doing more than one job. You are doing more work, and this is a good thing.

The word is often used in writing and talking with friends. It helps us find more things we want to say. It is a word to add more to our words.
