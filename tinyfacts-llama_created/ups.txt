When you are happy, you feel a good feeling. This feeling can happen very fast. It is like a push. You might jump up and smile. Your face is bright and your eyes are wide. You are very happy.

When things go well, we often say, up. We might be looking at a bright sky. The sun is coming up over the houses. The world is moving away from us. This makes us feel better.

Sometimes, things go wrong. You might feel sad or angry. You want to cry or shout. This can happen when you are afraid. It is a way to show you are not okay.

We also use the word up to talk about time. If you finish a book, you have reached the end. You can look at the words and see how far you have come. If you do not finish a book, you have not reached the end.
