To was means you have something. You are something. You are not something else. When you was, you were that. You were a boy, a dog, a tree, a house, or anything else.

You do not have to say it all the time. You just say it when you know it. You say it when you are a person. You say it when you are a thing. It is a word that helps us talk about our lives.

If you are a person, you were a person before you became a person. If you are a dog, you were a baby dog. If you are a house, you were a small thing. Every time you say you was, you tell a story about who you are and where you came from.
