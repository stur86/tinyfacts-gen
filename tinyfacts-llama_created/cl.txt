To climbed means to go up a tall place. People often go to the top of a big building to see what is far away. When you climb a wall, you use your hands and feet to reach the top. It can be hard to climb because the wall is very high.

You can also use this word for other things. If you are very angry, you might want to climb your high chair. This means you want to get up and show the other person you are mad. If a dog is very strong, it might try to climb a high place to get to a better spot to sleep.

In music, when you play a small thing, you might press it hard. This makes the sound go up. If you press the keys of a big box in a song, it makes a very loud sound that goes up and comes down. This is how music makes you feel.
