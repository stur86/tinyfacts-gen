To suggest something means to let someone know a thing you think is important. You do not tell them to do it, but you tell them it is a good idea. You might say, 

If you see a dog in a street and you think it is a bad dog, you might say,  Look at that dog. It looks very angry. It might be in a bad mood.  This is a way of helping someone. You are helping them see what you think.

Sometimes you suggest a plan. If you are working with a group of people, you might suggest we go to the park. This is a good idea. You are helping everyone think about what to do. You are not telling them to follow you, but you are showing them a way to move forward.

You can also suggest a way to do something. If you want to eat a new food, you might say,  Try this food. It is different from what you have eaten before. This is a way of letting someone know that you think a new idea is good.

When you suggest something, you are telling the person that you believe it is a good idea. You are helping them make a better plan.
