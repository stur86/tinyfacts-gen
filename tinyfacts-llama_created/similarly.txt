To be the same as someone or something means you have the same things in your mind, body, and heart. You are not different. You do not do something or say something that is different from what they do or say.

Imagine you want a drink. You want a cold drink. A friend wants a hot drink. They both want something that makes them feel better. They are both looking for something that will make them happy. They are not doing different. They are doing the same thing.

You can also be the same as a person who is dead. When a person is gone, they are still the same person. Their body is gone, but their mind and heart are still there. They are not a different person. They are still the same person.

If you say you are the same as your friend, you are not really the same. You have different ideas, different ways of living, and different ways of feeling. You are different people. You are not the same person you were five years ago.

When you say something is the same as something else, you are saying that it looks like or feels like that thing. If you look at a picture of your mom and see her, you are the same. You see her face. You see her hair. You see her eyes. You are looking at her.
