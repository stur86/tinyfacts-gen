When light from a thing that is near hits the window, the tiny things inside the window help make a picture that is not too clear.

People have many of these small parts. Sometimes, there are many more than one in each eye. One eye has many tiny things. The other eye has fewer, but still many. These small parts are very important for us to see the world. Without them, we would not be able to see at all.
