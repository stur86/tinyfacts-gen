To marry is to join two people. It is a big word that most people do not use every day.

A long time ago, a man or a woman would get married. They would say they would love each other for the rest of their lives. They would live together and raise a family. This was a happy time for them. But if a person wanted to be with another person, they could not get married. They could only love that person.

A girl who loves a boy is called a girl friend. She likes him, but she is not ready to get married. She might be a little afraid. She might think, maybe he loves her too. She does not know if he wants to be with her for the rest of their lives.

A boy who loves a girl is called a boy friend. He likes her, but he is ready to get married one day. He wants to be the one to say, I love you. He wants to be the one to marry her.

The word marries is also used for animals. A dog and a cat are two friends. They play together and love each other. If a dog gets married to a cat, they are now a team. They will work together and have a family.

So, marrying is about joining two people together. It is about love and building a life with the one you love.
