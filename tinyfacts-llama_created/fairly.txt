To do something is to use your mind and your body. You must think about how to do it and then you do it. If you do something and it is good, people say you did it well. This word is very important in many ways.

When a person says a thing is hard, they mean it is very hard to do. This does not mean it is not possible. It just means the person thinks it is very hard. If you try to do a thing that is very hard and you do it, you did it well. You showed that you are strong and you are able to do it.

Sometimes, you have to give something up for another person. You might give a book to a friend. You are giving away a thing you love. If you do this, you are being kind. You are showing that you care about the other person.

When you say something is nice, you mean it is good for you. If a place is nice, you feel happy. You might want to go there and play with your friends. If a food is nice, your stomach feels good. You want to eat it.

In many ways, this word is about what you do and how you act. It is about showing that you are a person who cares about what others think.
