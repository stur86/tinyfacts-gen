To stop something happens when you decide to stop doing it. You might decide to stop playing with a ball or to stop walking. This is important because it keeps you safe and helps you feel calm.

Imagine you are in a busy street with many cars and people. If you keep walking, you might run into a car or get hit by a falling thing. But if you stop and look around, you can see the people and cars, and you can decide what to do next. You might turn around and go the other way. This is stopping.

You can stop by moving your body. If you stop walking, you stop moving. If you stop talking, you stop saying words. You can also stop doing a job. If you are making food for a group, you can stop adding something to the pants so the food stays safe and the people do not get sick.

Sometimes, you stop things that are not right. If a person is being very mean to another person, you can stop them. You can stand between them and say, I am not going to let you do this. This stops the mean act.

You can also stop things that are going wrong. If a car starts moving, you can stop it. If a house is falling down, you can stop it by pulling the floor out from under it. This keeps people safe.

Stopping is important for everyone. It keeps us safe, helps us feel calm, and keeps things moving in a way that is safe for everyone.
