When you say you have enough, you mean you do not need more. You are not short on what you want or need. You do not have a lot of money or food. You can buy what you need for the day.

Think about a game. You are playing with your friends. You have not lost many games. You still have some games left. You are not very far away from the top. You are not a very good player yet, but you are doing well.

In a house, you might have a small kitchen. You can make breakfast, lunch, and dinner with what you have. You do not need many things. You can do all the work you need to do. You are not very full of food.

If you have a job, you might work for a few hours. You do not have to work all night. You can go home and do other things. You are not very tired all the time.

When you say you have enough, you usually mean you do not need more money. You have enough money for what you need. You do not need to buy more things.
