To feel excited means you are very happy. When you are excited, you start to jump up and down, or you might laugh a lot. You might want to do something very fast. You want to see what is happening.

You might feel this kind of feeling when you see a big animal. Or you might feel it when you hear a loud sound. It is a strong feeling that makes you want to move your body.

To be excited is like being ready to go. Imagine you are waiting for a game to start. You are checking your phone over and over. You are thinking about who will play next. You are ready to shout out your name. You want to see the game.

Sometimes, people feel excited when they do something they love. If you love to run, you might feel excited when you see the starting line. If you love to build things, you might feel excited when you start building. You are very happy to do what you love.

To be excited is also about the moment. You might feel excited when you see a beautiful picture. You might feel excited when you hear a song. You are listening to the music with your ears. Your body might start to shake. You might jump up and down.

In life, you can feel excited in many ways. You might feel excited when you get a new job. You might feel excited when you meet a friend. You might feel excited when you go on a trip. You might feel excited when you do something fun.
