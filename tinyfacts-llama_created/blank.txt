A word is a thing that people use. It tells us what something is or what it is for. You can use the word to talk about a person, a thing, a feeling, or a place.

A word can have many different parts. Some words have two parts. One part tells you what the word is. The other part tells you what it means. This is like a key to a box. The key is a word that opens the box. You need to know both parts to use the word.

Sometimes, a word is empty. It does not tell you anything. It is like a box that is not filled. You might see a word that looks like it should be a word, but it is not. You might not know what it means until someone tells you.

To fill something with a word is to put the word in it. You can fill a piece of paper with words to make a story. You can fill a room with words to make it feel quiet and calm.

A word can also be broken into many parts. If you take one part of a word, you might not know the whole thing. You might only know one part and then you have to figure out the rest. This can be fun if you like to learn new words and build stories.
