A bell is a small thing that makes a sound when you ring it. It is usually made of a round part with a long piece hanging from the top. When you hit this piece with your hand, it makes a sound that we hear all the time.

There are many different kinds of bells. Some are very small and small enough to fit in your hand. They are called tiny bells. Other bells are very big and heavy. They hang from a high place or from a big tree in a park. When you hit these big bells, they make a very loud sound that can be heard for many blocks around.

People use bells to tell time. On the hour, a person might ring the big bell to let everyone know that it is time for lunch or to tell people to stop what they are doing. This helps keep everyone safe and on the right track.

Some bells are used for music. A person might ring a small bell while they play a song on a tiny thing they hold in their hand. This makes the music sound very clear and nice.

Bells can also be used in homes. People might hang a small bell from a door to let their children know that someone is at the door. This can help them feel safe and happy.

So, a bell is a thing that makes a sound and helps us hear and remember important times and things.
