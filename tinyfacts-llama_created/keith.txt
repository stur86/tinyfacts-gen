The word keeper means to keep things safe or to be strong. You might see a man who keeps a small dog in a small house. He keeps the dog inside because the house might be too big or the dog might run away. The man keeps the dog safe and so the dog stays.

You can also use this word for a person who keeps their things. They might keep money in a box or a bag. This is because money can be very important and they do not want to lose it. They keep it safe so they can use it later.

A person who is kind is often called a keeper. They help other people because they care about them. They might help a child who is afraid or a person who is very sad. This kind of person is like a small house that keeps everyone safe and happy.

Sometimes we use this word for a person who is very good at keeping things. They are like a strong man who keeps a house safe. They are often seen with a big bag or a box to carry things around.
