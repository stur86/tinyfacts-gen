An idea that is never the end is called an idea that is not a number.

When you think about a number, you use it to help you. You use it to buy things, to pay for work you do, to keep track of how many people you have. Numbers are very good for us because they help us understand the world.

But some ideas are not like numbers. You can not use them to buy things or to keep track of numbers. An idea that is not a number is always bigger than a number. It is always going on and never ends.

Imagine you are standing in a room with a friend. You talk for a while and then you stop talking. The room is still there, and the friend is still there. The room is not the end of the talk. The talk is still the start of the room. The friend is always there, even if you stop talking.

This is like an idea that is not a number. The room is always there, and the talk is always going on. There is no end.
