To approach something means to move closer to it. It is when you are near the thing you want to see. This can happen in many ways.

One way is if you want to meet a person. You might walk up to them. You are getting closer. You are almost there. You are now standing right next to them.

Another way is if you want to do a job. You are doing it. You are moving your hands and your feet. You are moving your body. You are getting closer to the job you want to do. You are getting closer to the end of the job.

Sometimes people approach a place that is not their home. They go there for work. They drive or walk. They go past their house. They go into a new building. The building is where they do their job. They are now inside the building.

When you approach something, you are taking a step forward. You are moving into the area. You are becoming a part of it. You are a part of the world around you.

To approach something can also mean you are afraid. If you see a dog you do not like, you might feel afraid. You are not sure if you will stay in the same place. You are moving away from the dog. You are getting closer to the back of your chair or the back of the room. You are trying not to be near the dog anymore.
