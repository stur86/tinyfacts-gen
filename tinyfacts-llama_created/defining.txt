To definitely means you are very clear in what you mean.

Imagine you are talking to a friend. You want to tell them what a dog looks like. You can use many words. You might say, a dog is a small animal with a long body and four legs. You are very sure about this. You have shown the friend that you know what you mean.

If you say, I am a dog, you are not definitely saying that. You are only saying you are a dog. You have not shown that you are a dog. You could be a cat or a cat in a funny way. You are not sure what you mean.

To definitely means to show you are sure. You must be very clear that you mean what you say. If you are not sure, you are not definitely. You are just using words.

Another way to use this word is to talk about a problem. If you say, this is a problem, you are not definitely. You are only saying you think something is a problem. You have not shown that you know what it means. You are not sure you can fix it.

So, to definitely means to show you are sure. It is a way to be very clear about what you mean.
