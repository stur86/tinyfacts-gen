A small area of land for sitting or eating outside a house is called a back step or a back room. The word is from the past, before the word for a house was used. It is a place where people sit and drink coffee when they are home.

You can also call this area a small room with a door. It is not a big room, but it is a place for people to relax. The words can be the same.

To put a small area outside a house in a line with the line that goes around the house is a form of building. It is a line of rooms and doors that goes all around the house, but it is not a house. It is a way of making the house look bigger.
