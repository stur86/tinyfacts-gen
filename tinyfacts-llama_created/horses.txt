A horse is an animal that is very good at running. They are very strong and can go very fast. They have a long body with four legs. Each leg has a foot on the bottom and a long part in the middle. The foot is very important because it helps them walk and run.

Horses are very different from people. They do not walk like we do. They go very fast and then slow down very quickly. They are very good at jumping over small things. They can jump over a wide open area in just a few steps.

Horses are often used for work. They help people carry heavy things. They help people move from one place to another. They are also used for fun. People ride them in big groups to go over long roads or to cross wide bodies of water.

Some horses are very small. They are called tiny horses or baby horses. They are very cute and can be very fast. Other horses are very big. They are called great horses or heavy horses. They are the strongest animals on land.

Horses are very important for people. They help us move around. They help us get to places we need to go. They help us carry food and other things. They are also a big part of many games and stories.
