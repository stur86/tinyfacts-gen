To put something into less space, or to make something smaller, is to do this. Imagine you have a big ball. If you put it in a box, it is still the same ball. But if you put it into a smaller box, it is smaller. It is now in less space.

If you want to be smaller, you can also do this. You might not want to be as tall as someone else. You can put yourself into less space. This can help you feel more calm and less worried. It is like you are not in the middle of a big crowd anymore. You are in your own quiet space.

It is also true for things you do. If you make a small change, it can make a big change. You did not need to change a lot. But when you do something small, it is like a small step down a long road. When you do many small steps, you get to where you want to go.
