To be the same as something means you have changed or changed your old way. A person might change their name. They might change what they wear or how they act. If you are the same as a book, you are still the same person you were before you read it.

If you make a word wrong, you are not the same as the word. You are doing something different. This happens if you type something that is not what you mean. If you say you are happy, but you are really angry, your words are different.

A computer can also be the same as a word. It follows the same way of doing things. This is why people use computers to do work. They can change numbers and words in a way that makes them ready for people to use.

In the story of a baby, a person is the same as a thing that is dead. They are the same thing. The baby is still the same person that was being raised. A dead thing can not change. The person is the same as the thing that has been dead for a long time.
