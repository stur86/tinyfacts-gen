There are two ways to get money from this work. One way is to get a job where you are paid. When you do this job, you get money at the end of each day. You can use this money to buy food, clothes, or other things you need.

The other way is to get money by doing work for a long time. Sometimes, people do this work for a week or a month. In these times, they work very hard. They do not get paid at the end of each day like they do in the other way. They get money at the end of the whole time they are working.

Some jobs pay more money than others. The job that pays more money is often the one that is harder or the one that has more power. If you work for a big team or for a place with many offices, you might get more money than if you worked for a small group of people.

You can also get money by doing work that is important. If you build a house or fix cars, people pay you. You did not just work for free. You did a job and you got money for it.
