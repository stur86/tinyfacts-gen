To be led or to be in control of something is to have someone or something helping you move through a place. 

Imagine you want to go to a far away place. You do not know the way. You look at a big book with many lines. You follow the lines. As you walk, the lines show you which way to go. A person who follows you is your head. They tell you what to do and keep you on the right road. This is when someone is led.

When you are in a room and you do not know how to find the door, a person can help. They might point to a door and say, do that. They are leading you to the door. They are taking care of you.

Sometimes you need to be led by a thing that moves. A car has a driver inside it. The driver is the one who follows the lines of the road. They keep the car on the right road and help it reach its place. The car is led by the driver.

In life, we also need to be led. We might have a plan to do something. But if we do not follow the plan, we can end up in a bad place. A head or a group of people can help us stay on the right road. They give us the right words and help us understand what to do.

In a very strong way, to be led means to have someone or something inside you who is telling you what to do. This can happen in your heart or in your mind. When you feel you are being led, it means you are following what your body or your mind is telling you to do.
