To make a picture of someone is to take a picture of them. You sit next to them for a few minutes. They look at a small thing that you hold in your hand. The thing has their face on it. You might want to show the picture so everyone can see how they look.

Sometimes you make a picture of a child. You use a thing with a light. You point the light at the child. The light makes their face look happy or sad. A picture is like a story from a time that has already passed.

You can make pictures of people and animals too. You might take a picture of a dog so everyone can see how nice it looks. You can also make pictures of places. You might see the blue water of a big body of water. You might see the green trees in a big green park. You are making a picture of where they are.

When you say someone is a picture of you, it means they are a picture of you. It is like they are standing in front of you and they are you.
