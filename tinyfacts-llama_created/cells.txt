Cells are the tiny building blocks of our world. They are the very things that make us live. Every living thing is made up of many of these tiny cells.

Imagine you have a big box of blocks. Each block is like one of these tiny cells. They can all fit inside the box. Without all of these blocks together, you would not have a box.

These tiny cells are very small. You need a strong light to see them. They are very good at keeping the inside of the body clean. They help keep the food we eat from turning into bad stuff.

Each cell has many tiny lines that go out from it. These lines are like roads. They carry the stuff that the cell needs from one part of the cell to another part. Some of these lines are very long. Some are very short. They all work together to make the cell do what it needs to do.

Cells can do many different jobs. Some cells help us breathe. Some cells help us make food. Some cells help us fight off bad guys. Some cells help us keep our skin and hair nice. Every job is done by many of these tiny cells working together.

There are many different kinds of cells. Some cells are very small. Some cells are very big. Some cells are very round. Some cells are long and thinking. Some cells are hard. Some cells are soft. They all do the same job in the end.

Our body has many different kinds of cells. Our skin has cells that keep it strong. Our heart has cells that make it go. Our brain has cells that think. Our eyes have cells that see. Our mouth has cells that eat. Our stomach has cells that break food. Our body needs many of these tiny cells to stay living.
