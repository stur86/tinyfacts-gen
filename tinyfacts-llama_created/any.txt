The word any is a word we use in our day.

When you use any, you are telling the world about something. You say, for this, or for that, any is the word.

You might say, I am any person in this room. You mean, I am not a boy, I am not a girl. I am not a man, I am not a woman. I am any kind of person.

Or you could say, I am any kind of food. You could eat any food you like. You do not have to be a boy or a girl, or you have to be a tall person. You can eat a piece of rock if you want.

You can also use any in the place where you buy things. You are looking for any book. You do not want a book with a picture. You want a book that is quiet. Any book will do.

Even when you are looking for a friend, you can say, I am any friend. You mean, you do not look for a boy. You look for a person who is nice. You look for a person who listens. You look for a person who is happy.

So, any is a word that means you are not sure which, you are not sure which one. You do not know which one is the right one. You are open.
