A color is a part of the world that looks like a line of many colors all put together. When you look at a bright blue sky, the sky looks blue because the light from the sun is in many colors, and our eyes see all of those colors together.

A light is a thing that moves through the air. When light from the sun comes into our world, it is made up of many different colors. Each color is like a small piece of the light. The light from the sun is in the middle of all those pieces.

When you put many of those pieces together, you get a color. The color looks like a line of many colors that are all in one place. A bright red color is like many red pieces all together. A bright blue color is like many blue pieces all together.

Things in our world can have many different colors. A car can be bright red or bright blue. A tree can be bright green or bright brown. A person can be very bright blue or very bright red. People are made of many different colors.

The word we use to talk about colors is the same word we use to talk about the lines of many colors that are all together. When we talk about the line of many colors, we are talking about the color. When we talk about a person, we are talking about their color.
