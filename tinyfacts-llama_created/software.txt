To build something is to make it happen. You start with a plan and then you do many small steps. Each step is like a piece of a bigger picture. You use words and numbers to tell the computer what to do.

People make many kinds of this. Some are for making things you can see. Some are for making things you can touch. Some are for making things that go on the phone. Some are for computers that do not need a person.

You can also make things that do not need to be turned on. You just put them in a box and they are ready to work. These are called computers. They can do many things at once and can remember what you did before.

People use these things to make money. You make a game and it helps people play. You make a word that people can look at and understand. You make a story that people can follow. You help people learn new things.

The word is important. It is about how we build and use things to help others.
