But there are two different ways of making food sweet.

One way is to take many small things from a big animal. You take the white soft stuff that comes from under its skin. You cut it into small pieces and you press it so that the tiny hard parts come out. Then you put the white soft stuff into a big thing that holds air. The air inside makes the white soft stuff grow and become soft. You can eat it straight or you can put it on food. This makes food sweet. It is often used on food that is not sweet on its own.

The other way is to take a hard round thing from the bottom of a big animal. You cut it into small pieces and you press the pieces so that the round hard part comes out. Then you put the round hard part into a small thing that holds air. The air inside makes the round hard part grow and become soft. You can eat it straight or you can put it on food. This makes food sweet. It is often used on food that is not sweet on its own.

The round hard part is what we call but. When you cut the round hard part into small pieces, you are making it into small pieces. When you press the small pieces, you are making the hard part smaller. When the hard part is smaller, it is softer and it grows more as it sits. This makes it sweet.

When you put the round hard part and the white soft stuff together, the white soft stuff does not make the round hard part sweet anymore. The round hard part is made from the white soft stuff. The round hard part is made to stay hard. This is why we can keep round hard parts in our food for a long time.
