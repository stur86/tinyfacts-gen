To use someone or something is to act on their word. If you say you are going to do something, you are using your word. If you do that thing, you have used your word.

Imagine you have a friend who says, I am going to buy a new car. You do not buy a car. You act like the word was wrong. You have used your word. You did not follow what you said.

Sometimes, if you say you are going to do something, and you do not do it, you can still use your word. You might say, I said I would be here at evening, but I am not. You did not follow your word. You acted on the word, even though the word was wrong.

In a big group, you can use your word many times. People act on what they say. If you say, I am going to tell the truth, and then you lie, you have acted on your word. You followed the word.
