The word around means to go around in a big way. When you go around a big tree, you go around the tree. You start from one side and end up on the other side. This is a big round. Animals and people also use this word.

In the cold places near the water, there are big cold water things. They are found in the water and ice. They have a round body and can go under the ice to find food. This is why they are called around animals.

When the cold air is very low, the ground can be very cold. People wear warm clothes to stay safe. They go around the cold ground to stay inside their houses. This is also called a round.

A round can also mean to go around something many times. If you ride a round, you go around a big round many times. You keep going around until you get tired. You do this for fun and to see how many times you can go.
