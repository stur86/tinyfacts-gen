To be able to do something is to have the power to do it. When you are able to do something, you are able to keep your word or to follow through on what you say. You are able to do things that you promise to do.

You can be very good at this. A man might be a good friend, a good teacher, or a good doctor. He keeps his word and he does not break it. He is a person you can trust.

If you say you will do something, and you do not, you broke your word. Your word was not good. It was not true. It was not what you said you would do.

Sometimes, you might say you will do something. You start, and you do it. Then, you stop. You do not follow through on what you said. You do not keep your word. You have broken your word.

When you are able to do something, you are not afraid. You do not feel bad if you do not follow through. You know you can do it. You are safe. You are not in trouble because you did not do what you said you would.

Being able to do something is important. It helps you build trust with others. It helps you build your own self. It helps you feel good about yourself.
