To fall means you go down. You can fall from a high place. You can also fall when you hit the ground. This happens when you are moving quickly and your feet hit the hard floor.

You can also fall when you are afraid. If you fear something, your heart is beating fast. You might feel a bad feeling in your chest. You might want to hide. In this kind of fall, you are trying not to show how you feel.

Sometimes, we fall because we make a wrong move. If you try to run and you hit a wall, you have fallen. Your body is moving, and then your legs hit the hard stuff. This is a hard fall.

We can also fall when something goes wrong. If you try to build a book and the wood falls down, you have fallen. You did not want the wood to fall, but it happened anyway.

Sometimes, we fall because we forget. You might remember to do something, but you do not act. You fall into a bad way because you forgot to do the right thing.

To fall is also to let go. When you fall from a high place, you are letting go of the ground. You are giving up your grip on the floor. It is a big change, and it is often very hard.

You can fall in your life too. You might fall into a bad job. You might lose your job. Or you might fall in love. If you love someone, you are falling into a new kind of life. It is a new way of living with the person you love.
