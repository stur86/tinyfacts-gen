To be an idea is to show how something works. But to say something is an idea is to say it is not real. You can have a great idea for something new. You might think, I want to build a house that moves. Or you might have an idea for how to make food better for people. When you say something is not real, you are telling everyone it is not a thing you can see or touch.

Think about money. A thing that is not real is money. You can not touch it or see it. You can only know it is there. But an idea is a thing you think about. It is not something you can see, touch, or hold. You can not give money for it. But you can tell many people about it.

Sometimes, you might want something that is not real. You might want to fly to a far place without a thing that can carry you. This is a bad idea. You need money to buy the thing that will take you there. Or you might want to have a big house with many rooms. But your idea might not be real because it is not something you can pay for with money.

But you can also have an idea for how to make a thing better. If you want to build a car that can go very fast, you are making an idea. You are thinking, I want to build a car that can go fast. And then you can do things to make it happen. You can buy parts, learn how to build them, and use them to make your car. Your idea is not something you can pay for. It is something you decide to try.

So, an idea is something you think about. It is not something you can see, touch, or hold. It is something you decide to try to make real.
