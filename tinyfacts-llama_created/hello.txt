Hello is a word you use when you want to start a conversation. It is a way to say, I am here. It can be said by a person, a dog, a cat, a baby, or even a computer.

When you say hello, you are showing that you are ready to talk. You are not afraid. You are ready to share your ideas, your stories, your music, your pictures. You are ready to be friends.

You can say hello to someone you know. You say it to your mother. You say it to your best friend. You say it to the person who gives you a drink. You can also say hello when you see a person walking down the street.

You can also use the word in a different way. If you are waiting for something, you can say, I am waiting for you. You are looking forward to seeing your friend. You are hoping they will arrive soon.

Sometimes, you say hello when you are surprised. You hear a loud noise and you look up. You see a big animal or a strange thing that is moving on the street. You say hello, and you are surprised that there is something new there.

Hello is a word that shows we care about each other. It is a way to say, I am here, I am interested, I am happy to talk to you.
