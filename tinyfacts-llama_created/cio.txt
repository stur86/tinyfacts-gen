A cover is a small piece of something. It is usually made from a piece of hard stuff, like glass or hard wood, and it covers a small part of something. Imagine a small piece of water that is covered by a small piece of clear stuff. The clear stuff is like a cover. When water falls on the cover, it hides the small part under it.

You can find cover thing on the floor. A cover for a table is the soft thing on the top of a table. It keeps your hands from getting wet when you walk across the table.

A cover can also be a small cover for a door. It is a small piece of hard stuff that you put over the door when you do not want people to see inside. This helps keep the door closed when you are not home.

Sometimes, a cover is a small cover for a thing that you carry with you. It is a small piece of paper or a small cover made of hard stuff. This cover keeps your hands dry when you walk in the rain.
