To be good for something means you make it a better place. If you do this, you are helping people or the world. You might give someone a job, or you might fix a road so that many can move easily.

A good thing can be in your body, in the air you breathe, or in the water you drink. When the air is good to breathe, it makes you feel better. When the water is not full of bad stuff, you feel happy to play in it.

You can also be good for a person. If you listen to them when they are sad, you help them feel better. If you tell them that everything is going to be okay, you give them hope.

Sometimes, to be good means you do not hurt anyone. You keep your words and your body. You do not act in a way that could cause trouble. This makes the world feel safer.

You can be good for many things. You can be a good friend, a good teacher, or a good parents. You can help people learn, or you can help them build a strong body to stay safe.

Being good is not just about one person. It is about how you act for everyone around you. When you are good, you show that you care about what others think and that you want the best for everyone.
