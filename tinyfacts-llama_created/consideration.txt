To consider something means to think about it before you decide on what to do. You might look at a problem, a person, or a thing. Your mind is quiet for a while, and then you see how it feels.

Imagine you are talking to a friend. You are very angry. You want to be the one to do something. But then you see the friend is crying. You start to feel sorry for them. You start to think, what would happen if they did not get mad? You start to think about how your friend feels. You are considering how they are doing.

You can also consider a big idea. You do not want to decide on that right now. You want to think about it some more. You read a book or talk to someone who has more understanding. After a while, you feel you have enough to say.

Sometimes, you have to consider the things that might not happen. You can plan for what is going to happen next. You might think about how you will feel if something bad happens. You are considering the what and the how of a situation.

Consider is also about looking at yourself. If you are feeling good, you might consider doing something nice. If you are feeling bad, you might consider doing something to feel better. This helps you feel better when you are not happy.
