To sense something means to feel it. When you see a bright light, your eyes are the ones that do it. When you hear a loud noise, your ears are the ones that feel it. Your body is full of these tiny parts that help you know about the world around you.

Imagine you are in a dark room. You do not see anything, but you can hear every sound. A cat might be sleeping in the corner. The sound of its own breath is the only thing you can hear. You do not see it, but you know it is there.

You can also sense things that you do not see. You might feel a small touch on your skin. This is your body. Your skin is like a soft cover for your whole body. It keeps you warm and helps you breathe.

Sometimes, you might not even notice you are doing something. You might be sitting in your chair, and you are not thinking about it. You are just living. But your body is still working. Your heart is beating, your feet are touching the floor, and your brain is thinking about other things. You are all of these at once.

You can also sense things through what you have learned. If you have never eaten a food before, you might not know how it feels in your mouth. You might not want to eat it because you do not know if it is good for you. But the fact that you are trying to know is the sense. You are trying to learn about the world.

Sometimes, a person might not be able to sense anything. If you are in a very quiet place, you might not hear a sound. If you are in a dark place, you might not see anything. You do not know what is happening around you. This is because your body is not being told anything. Your brain is just not paying attention to anything because nothing is happening.

In short, to sense something is to feel it, to hear it, to see it, or to know it through what you have learned. It is a part of how you live your life.
