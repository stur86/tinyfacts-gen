To have something means you already have it. You do not need to go to a store to get it. You can just look at it.

Imagine you have a book. You have it in your hand. You are the one with the book. You are the one who reads it and learns from it. You do not need to go to a store to get a new book. You already have one.

You also have a friend. You and your friend are together. You talk and laugh with them. You feel happy when you are with them.

You can also have a job. You work in a place. You do things that make money. You are the one who does the work.

When you have something, you are not alone anymore. You have it with you. You can remember it later.

Sometimes, you might feel sad. You have a problem. You do not know how to fix it. You feel like you are stuck. Having a problem is not the same as not having it. You still have the thing you need to do.

Having a dog is also an important thing. You care for the dog. You give it food and water. You take it to the doctor when it is sick. You love the dog because it is your friend.
