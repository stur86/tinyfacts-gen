To put a mark on something is to make it look different. A small piece of paper with a picture or a word on it is called a paper thing. You can give one to someone. When you do this, the person gets a mark that says they have it.

A mark is what people use to show they have done something. A picture can be a mark. A word or a number can be a mark too. Some people use these marks to send money through the air. The money goes from one person to another and then stays in their pockets.

You can also put a mark on things you make. When you make a paper thing, you can add a mark to it. This makes it look like a part of your work. If you do this with every paper thing you make, the paper thing becomes more like a thing you made.

The words we use when we talk about these marks are different. We say a mark is a way to show. We say a paper thing is a thing made of paper. We say a money thing is a thing that gives us money. We say a work thing is a thing that you make. These words help us understand what we are talking about.

Sometimes, a mark can be a tiny thing on the ground. If you walk on the ground and you step on something with a mark, the ground gets the mark. This is why people say the ground is a place where marks go. They are the marks on the ground.

You can also put marks on your own body. When you grow hair, you get small pieces of hair from your body that come off. When you put these pieces on a paper thing, you can keep them and look at them later. This is how people keep track of things that are already happened.
