To make something new from old things is to add them together. You can take a piece of wood and a piece of paper and put them together to make a table. The wood is the old thing, and the paper is the new thing. You are taking the old and the new and making something new.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a group. If you want a group of people to do something, you need to let them know. You might write a letter to say, here is what we want. Then, everyone who reads the letter is part of the group.

You can also use this word to talk about things you buy. If you want to buy a new book, you are taking an old one and adding a new one to it. This makes the book new and ready to read.

In a big group, people often try to make sure everyone feels safe. If a person does something wrong, the group can talk about it and try to fix the problem. This helps everyone feel better and stay safe.

So, to make something new from old things is to add new ideas or things to old things. It is a way of building new things from what already there is.
