A long thing with a hard head is called a ball. People use this ball to hit other things. When you hit the ball, it makes a sound. You use this sound to make things move or to fix things that broke.

You can use the ball to hit wood. If you hit wood with the ball, the wood gets broken. But if you hit the wood with the ball and hit the head of the ball on the ground, the wood stays the same. This is why you use the ball to hit things that need to be broken.

You can also use the word to talk about a person. If a person is very good at this kind of work, they are called a strong hand. They use the ball to hit and to fix things. This is why people say that a strong hand is a good hand.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about a story. A ball can be the ball that moves through the story. It can be the ball that makes things happen. The ball can also be the ball that is hit. It is a very important word because it tells us what is happening.
