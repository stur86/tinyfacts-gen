To happen is to be done. It means something that is not yet here is going to be. You do not know when it will start. You just know it is going to happen.

Imagine you are waiting for a friend at the door. You are standing there for a very long time. You are looking at the door and thinking about what you want to say. Then the door opens and your friend comes out. This is an eventually happened thing. You did not know it would happen. You were not waiting for it to happen.

You can also happen to a person. A person does something and it changes them. When a person is afraid, they happen to have a bad feeling. When they are happy, they happen to feel a good feeling. Things happen to us all the time.

We also say that something happened to a thing. If a ball flies through the air, it happened to the ball. If a tree falls down, it happened to the tree. The word happened is important because it tells us that something did not happen by the time we look at it.

Sometimes we talk about things that happened a long time ago. We look at old pictures or hear stories about them. This helps us remember the past. We can look back at things that happened to us and things that happened to others.
