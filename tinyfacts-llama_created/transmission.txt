To send something through something else.

Think of a car. The car moves because it is pushed by a person. But the person does not carry the car. The car is the thing that carries the person. The car has a way to send the person to another place.

A person can send a word to another person. They type on a computer and send the word. The word goes through the air and reaches the other person. The other person reads the word.

A car has a way to send power to the parts inside it. Power is like the push that makes things move. When the car is moving, the power is sent through the car. The power makes the car turn, the car go up a road, and the car go down a long line of roads.

A computer has a way to send numbers to other computers. Numbers are like small bits of power that move from one computer to another. When a computer does something, it uses these numbers to tell other computers how to act.

When a person sends a word, they are sending power. When a car sends power, they are sending power through the air. When a computer sends numbers, they are sending numbers through the air, too.
