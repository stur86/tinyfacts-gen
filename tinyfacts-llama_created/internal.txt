An inside part of a thing you use to live. Your body has parts that help you breathe. Your heart is one of them. Inside your heart, blood goes around and comes back. This keeps you strong.

Your brain is another inside part. It is the place where your mind works. It tells you what to do and how to feel. Your brain helps you remember things, understand words, and know when to be happy or afraid.

Inside your mouth, you eat food. You use your teeth to break it into smaller pieces. Then you use your tongue to move the pieces into your throat. From your throat, food goes down into your body through your stomach.

Inside your head, there is a part that helps you see. You use your eyes to look at things. Your brain tells your eyes what to look at and how to focus the light.

Inside you, there is a hard round thing. This is your skin. It keeps you safe from the world. Your skin also helps keep your body warm.

Inside you, there is a long white thing. This is your blood. It carries food and other things your body needs. It also carries bad things that need to be thrown away.

Inside you, there are many tiny moving things. These are your cells. They are like tiny building blocks that make you what you are.

Inside you, there are also tiny tiny things that go around your body. These are tiny animals that can only live inside your body. They help break down the food you eat. They also help get ridden of bad things that can make you sick.

All these inside parts work together to keep you living. Without them, you would not be able to breathe, think, or feel anything.
