To be a person is often easy. You live in a house, go to school, and work. You have a job, a family, and friends. You do things that make you happy and things that make you sad. You act like you care about others and you act like you want them to be happy too.

But if you have a lot of power, you can use it to hurt people. You might take things that do not feel like yourself. You might make someone cry because you are angry. You might make someone afraid because you are mean. You might use your body to hurt someone.

You can also be a person who is mean. If you are always angry, you might scare people. If you are always wrong, you might make others want to fight with you. You might use words that hurt people.

Sometimes, being a person means you are very strong. You might hit a man or a dog to make him stop. You might take something that is not yourself. You might act like you are not afraid of anything. You might act like you are in control.

But being a person is not just about being strong or being mean. Being a person is also about how you act when things are not easy. You might feel sad, angry, or scared. You might cry. You might shout. You might want to be alone.

People need each other. We all need to feel safe, to feel loved, to feel happy. We all need to know that someone cares about us. Being a person is about being there for others when they need us. It is about being kind, being strong, and being real.
