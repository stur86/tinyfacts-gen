To look at something for fun that you know is not for you. This can be about a picture or a place that others like. You might go to this place because you want to see how people live in a different way. Or you might look at this picture because it is funny or interesting.

This word is often used when someone is doing something they should not do. If you are looking at a picture of a bad thing, you are showing you are not interested in the real life. This can make the person who made the picture feel sad.
