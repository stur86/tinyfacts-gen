The word once is used to show when something happened before.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it to your friend. You are the one who threw it. Then your friend is the one who got it. This is the first time the ball is given to them.

After that, someone else might want to play with the ball. Maybe a new friend gets it. But you are not the one anymore. You are not the one who threw it.

This word is used in many different ways. You might say, I once loved you. You did not love me. Now you do love me. You have changed your mind. Or you might say, I once thought it was true. Now I see it is not true at all.

Sometimes, we say something is once a day. This means we do it only once a day. We do it many times and it is the same thing each time.

The word once is also used to tell if something is gone. If a tree falls, it is gone. This tree was once there. Now it is no more.
