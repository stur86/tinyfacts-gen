Mood is how we feel. When you are happy, your face might smile and you might jump up. When you are sad, your face might look sad. When you are angry, your face might be red and you might say, you are mad.

Imagine you are playing with your friends. You are having fun. Your face is smiling and you are laughing. This is a happy mood. Later, you might feel sad when you think about a friend you lost. Your face might look sad and you might cry.

Sometimes, you might have a bad feeling inside. This is when you feel very angry. You might want to hit something or shout. This is not a good feeling.

We also have a kind of mood inside our heads. This is how we feel when we are eating food. If the food is too hot, we might feel angry. If the food is too cold, we might feel sad.

You can change your mood quickly. If you see a bright light, you might feel happy. If you hear a song you love, you might smile. If you get a new game, you might feel excited. If you have a bad day at school, you might feel sad.
