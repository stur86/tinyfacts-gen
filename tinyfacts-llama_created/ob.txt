The word obviously means you can see that something is true. When you know something, you can easily see it. You do not have to think about it very hard. This word is often used when people are very sure about something, like their name, their home, or what they like.

Think of a person who always answers questions. They can answer a question right away. You can see that they know the answer. This person is very good at what they do. They are not like other people who might say,  wait, let me think about that for a moment, or  I don't know.

When something is obviously true, you do not have to think about it at all. You can see it right away. This word is used to show that something is clear and not a big problem. It is like a light that tells you where you are going.

Sometimes, this word can also mean that you see the person you are talking to. When you look at someone, you see their face, their hair, their clothes. You see the person you are talking to. This is also a way to say that something is clear and you know exactly what it means.
