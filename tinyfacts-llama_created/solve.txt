To have something is to have it already. When you have a thing, it is not a new thing anymore. It is the thing that you already know. To have is to be the one who has it.

When you want to play a game, you do not have the game. But then you find it. You are the one who has it now. You have the game, and the game has you.

You can also have a problem. If you do not do something, you have a problem. You have to fix it. Once you fix it, you have it again. You are the one who has the problem now.

Sometimes, you might not want a thing. You might want to be alone. But you do not have it. You still have it inside you. You are still the one who has it.

To have is important. It shows you are the one who is in the right place at the right time.
