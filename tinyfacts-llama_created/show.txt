To show something means you are giving people an idea. You are not telling them, but you are making them see it with your body and words.

Imagine you have a big box full of small, round things. If you just say, you have a box, no one knows what it looks like. But if you show them, you point to each small thing and say it is this. You are giving them a picture with your eyes.

You can show things in many ways. You can draw something on paper. You can make music that makes people want to move. You can tell a story that makes people feel the same way you feel. You can even show something by making a loud noise that makes people cover their ears.

When you show, you are not just telling people. You are also making them feel. You can make them smile, or make them feel happy. You can show that something is real and important.

Sometimes, you show people something you do not have. You might show them how to do a thing that you already know. You are giving them a way to learn something new. You are showing them that you care about them.

Showing is important because it helps people understand each other. When you show, you are building a relationship. You are showing that you are there for them, that you care about what they think and feel.
