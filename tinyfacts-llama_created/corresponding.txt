When two things are like one person and another person, they are often called the same. This happens when each person has the same name, the same job, the same family, the same life story.

Think of a person who is a teacher. They are the same person as a person who is a writer. They both have the same job, the same clothes, the same home. They both love reading and writing. They both love to learn.

This is because one person is a part of the world, and another person is a part of the world. They are two different people in one world. They are not the same person, but they are the same person in the world.

People also say this when they talk about things. If you say, the sun is going up, the sun is in the sky. This is the same as saying, the sun is up in the sky. The two are the same. The sun is going up and the sun is up. They are the same thing, even though the word is different.
