To return or change something.

Imagine a big room with a lot of chairs. Some chairs are old and not good. Some chairs are new and nice. You want to put the old chairs away. You tell the people who live there to move the old chairs out. They take them away. Now the room is ready for new chairs.

You can do this with many things. You might want to move money from one place to another place. If the money is not in the right place, you need to change where it is. You are returning the money to the right place.

You can also return things that are broken. If a child breaks a glass, you take the glass away and put a new glass in its place. This helps the child feel safe again.

Sometimes, people change their jobs. They move from a job they like to a job they think is better. This is a return of power. The person who was in the job now has more power.

To return something can also mean to give back. If you help someone, you are giving them something. This is a way of showing that you care.
