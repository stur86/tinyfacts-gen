To go away from something

Think about a person who always wears the same clothes every day. They are very sure of how they look. But then, one day, they see a movie with a new look. The person feels confused. They might try to change their clothes. They might look in the mirror and see a different face. This is a kind of going away.

It is also about how things are different from what you think. Imagine you are in a room and you think everything is the same. But when you step outside, you see trees and a bright sky. The room was a small space. The outside was much bigger and full of new things. This is a kind of going away from what you think.
