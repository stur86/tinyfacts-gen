Light is a kind of power from the sun. It comes out of the sky when the sun gets very hot. When the hot air gets moving fast, the air gets so hot that it breaks into tiny pieces and flies around very fast. These tiny pieces are like tiny balls of light.

When many of these tiny balls fly together, they start to move in a line. At first, they are very slow. But then they start to go very fast. They go so fast that they start to break the air around them. This makes the air go very hot. The hot air from the top of the line goes up, and the air at the bottom goes down. This makes the air get very hot and very loud.

When the air gets hot and very loud, it becomes a big ball of light. The light is very bright and can reach the ground very fast. This is why we see the bright light from the sky during the day. But when the sky is dark, the light goes away very quickly.

Sometimes, the light goes up into the sky and stays there. This happens when the air is very cold and the light from the ground can not reach it. The light stays there for a very long time, and it can be seen for many days.

Light can also go very high into the sky. When the sun is very high in the sky, the light can go far away. The light from the high sun can reach our world from far away, and we can see it even on a dark night.

The power of light is very strong. When it is very bright, it can hurt our eyes. We use things to make light stay in the air for a longer time. These things help us see at night when it is dark. They also help us see far away when we fly in a car or a big moving thing.
