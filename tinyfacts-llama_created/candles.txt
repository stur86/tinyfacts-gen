A small box with a clear cover is used to make a bright light. The cover is made of a strong stuff that does not break easily. When you put the cover on a box that holds water, the water gets hot and the cover starts to move around inside. This makes the cover turn and push the hot water out.

Inside the box, there are many tiny lines. When you put a small piece of stuff inside the box, the lines start to burn. The burning lines make a bright light. This light is what we need to see at night.

People use these boxes to make their homes look warm and nice. They put them on tables or on the floor. They also use them to make a bright light for a party. The light is very bright and can see from a far way.

You can also use a small box that burns a hard stuff to make a bright light. This stuff is very strong and can burn for a very long time. People use these boxes to make their homes look very nice at night.
