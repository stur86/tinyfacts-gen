The word locked is used to talk about a door or a box that is shut and can not be opened by someone else.

When you lock a door, you put a heavy piece of wood or a hard thing on the door. This makes the door hard to open and close. No one can enter the room where the door is.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friend. You both want to enter a room. If you put your hands in your pockets and the door is locked, you have to wait for someone to come and open it. If a person tries to open the door, the door will make a loud noise and it will not go down.

A locked box is a box that is not opened. A person can not get inside. Only the person who has the key can open it. If a box is locked, you need to find the key to open it.

Sometimes, a person who has a job is locked in a room. They have to stay there until the end of their work. This is like being locked in a room.
