When you say something, you usually say it with your mouth. But if you want to show that you really know what is happening, you might say it again. This is called being asked to repeat what you said.

If you are asked to do something, you might feel a little nervous. You might say, I will do it. This is called being asked to do something. It means you are ready to follow the person who is asking you.

Sometimes, a person tells you a story. You listen and you feel happy. You might even want to hear the story again. You say, I heard that story. You have said it again. You are trying to show that you remember what the person said.

It is also possible to be asked to say something. If you are told to speak, you might feel a little afraid. You need to be ready to talk. You can say, I am ready. This means you are ready to say the word.
