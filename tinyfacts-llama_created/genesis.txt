To begin something is to start a new thing. You start with a small thing and then you add more and more things to it. This makes the thing bigger and stronger.

Imagine you want to build a house. You start by making a small room. Then you add more walls. You add a floor. You add windows and a door. You keep adding things until the house is big enough for you and your family. This is the beginning of the house.

If you are looking at a picture, you start with the first line. You see the words and the pictures on the line. Then you look at the next line and you see the words and the pictures on that line. You keep looking line after line until you see the whole picture.

The word is also about the first day of the world. Some people say that the world began when the sun and the stars were still very small. They say that a big, hot ball of fire started to move and it kept moving for a very long time. After a long time, the fire became a bright ball in the sky. This bright ball is still there today.

You can also use this word to mean when a story starts. It is the beginning of a story about people, places, and things. You can find a story in books, movies, or in your mind. When you start reading, you begin a new story.

The word is often used in a different way when people talk about how things start. If you are going to do something new, you might say, I am starting something. This means you are beginning something.
