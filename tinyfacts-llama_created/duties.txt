To do your work is to act. A person has a job. They must do the work. Their job is important. This is your work. Everyone has a job. It is a part of life. It is how you help others and keep things going.

You are a worker too. You work every day. You do things to buy food, pay money, and keep your home clean. You work with your body. You work with your mind. You work with your heart. When you do your work, you are helping. You are making things happen.

Sometimes, you do work that is not for money. You work on a problem. You work to fix something. You work to help a friend. You work to keep the world safe. This is important too. Everyone needs work.

You do your work so that people can be happy. You do your work so that things are right. You do your work so that everyone can live.
