An area where people live and work together is called an area. A group of people who live there can form a family. There is usually a person who is in control and makes the plans. They may have a name for the area.

An area can be a city. A city is a very big area with many streets and many houses. Many people live there and go to work every day. There are many shops and food places.

An area can also be a place where animals live. This area is for the animals. There are many trees, and there is a big water area. Animals come to this area to drink water and eat the green stuff that grows.

An area can be a place where people grow things. This is a big area where many people grow food. They use the ground, the water, and the air to make food for their families.

An area can also be a place where a person starts their life. This is the area where a person was first. This area can be a city or a small town. When a person starts their life, they go to this area to learn and get a job.

All of these areas help people live together. They give places to live, food, and work. They help people know where they are and where they are going.
