The word house is used for a kind of house. It is often found in the city. You can see them on the street. They are usually small. You can walk past them many times before you see one. Some are very high. Others are very small. Some are made of wood. Some are made of cold hard stuff that does not break.

A house usually has a small room in the front. This room is for the people who live there. It is like a small house. Inside the room, there are many small rooms. These rooms are for the people who work there. There is also a room for the people who keep the house clean. This room is very clean. You can see the floors and the walls. You can see the windows. You can see the door. The room has a light. This light is very bright. It helps people see at night.

A house can also have a very small room. This room is called a tiny house. A tiny house is very small. It is like a box. People live in tiny houses when they are very far from the city. They have to carry things with them. They carry food, water, and clothes. They carry a box with food and water inside. This box is very strong. It keeps the food and water for many days.
