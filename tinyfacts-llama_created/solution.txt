When something is a problem, it is hard. We try to find a way to make it better. This is called a problem. If you have a hard time doing a work, you need to find a way to finish it. This is the first step to fix the problem.

A problem can be many things. You might have a small thing that is stuck. You try to push it out with your hands. This is a problem. Or you might have a big thing that is too heavy. You need to find a way to move it so you can go. This is another problem.

Sometimes a problem can be broken into many parts. If a car breaks down, it is not one thing. It is many parts: the big thing that makes the car go, the small thing that makes the car stop, and the small thing that makes the car turn. Each part is a problem that needs to be fixed.

A good problem is one that you can fix with your own hands. If you fix a small thing, you feel happy. If you fix a hard thing, you feel strong. This is a good problem.

A problem can also be a chance. Think about a place where you want to go. If you do not have a way to get there, the place will not be the same. This is a problem. But it is also a chance to learn about how to move and to plan your way.
