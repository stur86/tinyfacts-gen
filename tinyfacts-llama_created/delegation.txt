To give something to someone else means to send them a piece of work to do. This can happen when you want to be away for a short time. You might decide to give your work to a friend or family person.

Imagine you are the person in a school. You love to write stories. One day, you feel very tired and your teacher asks you to go to a school far away. Your teacher says, you are going to be the one to write the stories. This is a big job! You must work hard and write many stories without your teacher being there.

Another way to use this word is when you want to let someone else do something important. Maybe you are the person in a big group. You feel you are the best at a big job. But, you realize that you do not have enough power to do everything. So, you give the job to someone else. This is called a team. The person who works with you is like your team. Together, you can do many things that are harder for one person to do alone.

Sometimes, giving power is very important. If a person is in a place of power, they might give a job to someone they trust. This way, everyone is safe. They do not have to worry about the person they trust having bad ideas.

Dealing with power is a very important job. It means you have to be ready to give power when it is needed. It also means you should listen to others and follow their ideas.
