When you talk about something that is inside another thing, you say it is inside. If a small piece of paper is inside a book, the book has the paper inside it.

When you use this word, you are talking about a part you put into another part. You put the part in, but it is not part of the thing anymore. It is now a part of the other thing.

You can also say it when you put something into a box. You are bringing something new into the box. You are adding something to what is already there.

Think about a room. You walk into the room. You do not know what is in the room. Then, you see the food on the table. You see the books on the floor. You see the chair. Everything is inside the room now. The room is full of things. It is not just one thing anymore. It is many things all together.

When you talk about a thing that is inside another thing, you are talking about how the things are changed. You are talking about how the inside of one thing becomes part of another thing.
