To cut something means to use a thing with a long line to make it much smaller. You can use this thing to cut the hair of a person, to cut the paper, or to cut a big piece of wood into smaller pieces.

Imagine you have a long stick. If you move the stick, the long part gets shorter and the short part gets longer. This is what happens when you use the long line to cut. You are moving the line along the stick until everything is small and the stick is very short.

You can also use this word for things that are not living. If you take a hair from a person, you are cutting it. If you cut the paper, you are using the same kind of thing. This is like taking a piece of a thing that is not your own.

Sometimes, people cut things that are broken. If a table is broken, the person who fixed it may have to cut the pieces to make new ones. This is important because the person can then use the new pieces to make a new table.

To cut can also mean to make something look better. If you cut a picture, you are taking away parts that make it look bad. This helps the picture look better so people can see it more easily.
