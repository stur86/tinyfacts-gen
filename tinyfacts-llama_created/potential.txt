To have the power to do something means you are ready to act. If you want to be a great writer, you need to have the power to write. You must be able to think of stories and make them come out of your brain.

You can also have the power to change the world. If you are very strong, you can lift heavy things or run around a big field many times. You are ready to act.

But the power is not always about being strong. Sometimes you just need to believe in yourself. If you really want something, you can make it happen. You do not need to be as strong as the one who already has it. You just need to want it very much.

Think about a tree. If it does not have the power to grow, it will not grow. It has a deep inside that tells it to grow. It is ready to act, even if it does not know how yet. You can feel the power of a tree when you see how it moves in the wind.

We all have power inside us. We can do things every day that change our world. We just need to believe and act.
