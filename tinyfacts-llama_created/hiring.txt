To find a job is to look for a place where you can do a job you like. You go there and talk with people who want to work. They ask you about how you feel and what you want to do. You try to find a job that is a good fit for you.

When you find a job you like, you start doing it. You work at the office, or you walk the streets, or you help other people. You do things that make you feel happy. Your job is the thing you do every day.

People often find jobs that they love. This is because they are good at what they do. They like the work and the people they work with. They feel like they are a part of a team. When you find a job you love, it is a great feeling.

Some jobs need you to work alone. You might sit at a desk and type papers. You might talk to people on the phone. Other jobs need you to go out and talk to people. You might go to a store and help people buy things. You might go to a house and fix a problem.
