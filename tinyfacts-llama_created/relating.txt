When you say something is a part of something, you are talking about how the things are together. If you have a book, the words are a part of the book. The words are the part you read. Without the words, there is no book.

If you take a chair, you are taking a part of the room. The chair is a part of the room, and it is a part of the floor, the wall, and the other chairs.

If you talk about a person, you are talking about how that person feels. You can say they are happy. You are saying that the person is part of how they feel.

You can also say something is a part of a story. This means that the story is about something that happened. You might read a story about a boy who goes to a far away place. The boy is a part of the story. Without the boy, there is no story.

To show that a person or a thing is a part of something else means you are explaining how they are a part of the whole.
