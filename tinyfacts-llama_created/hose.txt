A long, strong piece of hard stuff is used to carry water from a big box at the top of a house or a building to the rooms in the house. This water helps clean the floors, fills the water in the rooms, and makes the water warm for drinking or to make a drink.

You can find these things in many places. You might see them in your kitchen, in a bathroom, or in a house with many rooms. They are very important for keeping your home clean and your food warm.

When you turn the thing on, it starts to move and carry the water. The water goes over the ground, under the ground, and into the rooms. Sometimes, the water goes through small clear parts inside the thing so you can see what is in the water.

These things can break easily if the water is very strong, or if they are not in good order. But they are usually easy to fix. You can get new parts if something breaks, and you can put the thing back together.

You need to be carefully when you are near these things. If you touch them with your hands, you might get hurt. You should not try to use them if the thing is not in good order.
