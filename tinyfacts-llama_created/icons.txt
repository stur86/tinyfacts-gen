An word is a thing you use to talk about other things. It might be a word, a name, or a word that means a thing. You can use this word to tell others about a person, a place, a thing, or a story.

An thing is a thing in the world. It is the ground you walk on, the air you breathe, the water you drink, the sun, the stars, and every other thing that makes up our world.

When you use the word, you are talking about how to feel or how to act. If you say, a man is a leader, you are talking about how to feel about this man. You might think he is strong, kind, or funny. You are also telling others who to trust and who to fear.

Think of an animal. You might say, a cat is a good animal. This word is talking about the cat. You are telling others about the cat. You are not saying the cat is a cat. You are saying, the cat is a good animal. This is a different kind of word. It is about how you feel about the cat.

Sometimes, you use words to point to a picture or a thing. You might say, look at that. This word is not talking about the picture. It is telling you to look at it. You are not saying the picture is important. You are saying, look at the picture because it is there.

The word is also used in the world of ideas. When you write about a person, a place, or a story, you use the word. You are telling others about what happened. You are not just saying the words. You are telling them what it means.

In a group, people might use the word to talk about a problem. They might say, we need to act on this. This word is not just about the word. It is about the group and how they feel about the problem.
