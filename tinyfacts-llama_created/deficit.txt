A number that is less than the totally of something is called a number that is smaller than one. Think of money. If you have  ten little pieces of money and you spend five of them, you have a number smaller than ten. This number is called a number of money that is smaller than ten. It is a way to show how much is less than a whole thing.

Imagine you have a big bag that can hold many, many things. If the bag is empty, it is a number smaller than one. If you put one small thing in it, the bag can hold more things, so it becomes a number larger than one. But if you fill it all the way, it is still one, because there is nothing left to show.

In the world, a number of money that is smaller than ten is called a small number. If you have a number smaller than ten, you can add one more to it. This makes it larger. For numbers bigger than ten, you can add ten more to it to make it bigger.

In a house, a number smaller than ten is the number of people in the room. If there are ten people, the room is full. If there are more, the room is smaller. This is how we show how many people are there.

We also use a number smaller than ten to show how much money is not in a box. If you have one hundred little pieces of money and you give two to a friend, you have one less. This is a number smaller than ten. It tells us how many pieces of money you are left with.

Sometimes, a number smaller than ten is used to talk about how much is left in a box after you take out some things. If you take out ten things, you have ten left. If you take out a hundred, you have a hundred left. This is a way to show how much is left after you do something.
