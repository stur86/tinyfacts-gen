A long time ago, people made things by hand. They would put small pieces of wood together many times. Each piece was made from a small round piece of wood. When they made a long thing, they put all the pieces together so that the round pieces were inside one after another.

This makes a thing that you can hold in your hand. It has many sides. If you turn it one way, you go around the round piece once. If you turn it the other way, you go around the round piece in the other direction. If you turn it many times, you go around many, many times.

You can also do this with your mind. If you do something many times, your brain gets used to it. You do it so fast that you don't even think about it. It is like a long, round round that goes around and around in your head.

A group of people doing this is called a group. A group of many people doing the same thing is called a group of many. A big group of many small groups is called a big group of many small groups. This is why a big city has many small groups of people who do the same job.

This is also why things go around. When you do something many times, you go around and around. You do not stop.
