Imagine you have a long piece of paper with many words on it. You want to know what the whole thing means, so you break it down. You look at each word one by one. Each word tells you a little bit about the whole thing. You might see that the paper is about a person, a place, or a time. Then you look at the words that tell you where the person lives, what they do, or how they feel. You are breaking down the paper into smaller parts.

Each part has its own story. One part might tell you about the person. Another part tells you about the place. Another part tells you about the time. By looking at all these parts, you can see the whole picture.
