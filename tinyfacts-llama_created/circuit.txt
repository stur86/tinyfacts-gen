A short way of saying a round thing that lets power go from one place to another. It is usually made of many small parts that work together. When you turn a round thing on, it sends power to other round things. This power helps other things work. It is like a long line of power that goes through many houses, cars, and computers.

You can have many of these short ways in one house. They help the house get power from the big power line. Or, you can have many short ways in a street. Cars and lights use these short ways to work.

Some short ways are very strong. They carry a lot of power. Other short ways are not so strong. They only carry a little power. They are used in small places like a room or a small house.
