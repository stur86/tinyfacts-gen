A big animal that goes to the green land to eat the green parts of the green things. It has a soft cover on its back and a neck that is very strong. People use the hard stuff from its feet to make things like shoes.

These animals live with many other animals. They are very good at hiding when it is dark. They can make a very loud sound and it can be very far away.

People keep these animals because food is hard to get. They give them food and let them live. Some people keep them for money.
