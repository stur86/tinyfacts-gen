To direction means to tell people what they should do. Imagine you are a teacher. You tell a group of children to stand up and single. You give them a few words, and they follow your voice. You tell them what to do. When a movie is made, the person who makes it is a direction. They tell the people who make the movie what to do. They decide who is on the team, who should act, and what to say. They make sure the story is done well.

Sometimes, a person is a direction because they are not a person who makes things. They are someone who watches how a thing is made. They look at what is done and think, if this is right, or if this is wrong. They might write a book about what happened, so others can learn from it.

A person who is a direction is important because they help people see what is important. They help people understand how to act, how to feel, and how to work together. Without them, a story or a movie would just be words on a piece of paper.

In a big building, there are many people who work together. One person is a direction. They tell the people who make things what to do. They are the ones who make sure the building is safe and that everything is done right. They are the ones who help the people make their jobs easier.

So, to direction means to tell people what they should do, and it is important for making sure things are done right.
