To expect something is to think you will get it. It is when you are looking for a person, a thing, a feeling, or a moment to happen. When you expect something, you are ready for it.

You might expect a call from a friend. You have been waiting for this call for hours. You are looking at your phone to see if it is there. If the call comes, you will be very happy. You had a feeling that it would happen, so you are ready.

You can also expect something that is already true. If you expect a day to be good, you are not afraid of any problems. You are ready for the day to start. This means you are not worried. You are calm.

Sometimes, you expect something that you do not know yet. You do not know if it will happen. You just wait and see. This is a big wait. It is like waiting for a sign from the sky. You are not sure if the sign is a yes or a no. You just wait.

You can also expect a change. You might expect the sun to come up in the morning. Or you might expect a new game to start soon. When something happens, you feel glad. You are happy that it is happening.

To expect something is to have a feeling. It is like having a picture in your mind. You can see what is going to happen before it happens. You can feel the air before a loud sound. You can feel the ground before a heavy thing falls.

So, expecting something means you are ready for it. It means you have a feeling about what is going to happen.
