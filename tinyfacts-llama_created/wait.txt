To wait means you are waiting for something that will happen later. You do not do anything with your time. You just sit and wait.

Think about a time when you want to see a movie. You know it is about to start. You look at your watch. The time is right. You get up and go to the room where the movie is being shown. When the movie starts, you sit down and watch. You are waiting for it to end.

You can wait for a friend who is late. If you know you will see them soon, you might stand near the door, looking at the street. You are waiting for them to come out.

You can also wait for a thing to grow. If you have a small ball, it will grow bigger as it gets bigger. You watch it every day to see how much bigger it gets.

Sometimes, you wait for the day to end. When night falls, you feel calm. You might sit in a dark room with your eyes closed, waiting for the night to go away. This is a kind of waiting.

Waiting is a good thing. It helps you feel safe. It helps you think about what to do next. It makes you not jump up and shout. It lets you stay calm and be ready when things happen.
