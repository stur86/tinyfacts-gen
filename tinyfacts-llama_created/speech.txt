To speak is to use your voice. When you talk to someone, you use words. Your voice goes out of your body and into the air. You are speaking. You are talking to someone who lives far away or to a person who is right next to you.

You use your words to tell stories, to share your ideas, and to ask questions. Your words can be loud or soft. They can make people laugh, cry, or think. When you speak, you are sending a sign to the people around you.

To speak does not mean you have to be a great person. Everyone speaks. Even a child speaks to a parents. You speak when you are afraid, when you are happy, or when you want to tell a story. Your words can help others understand you.

Sometimes, we speak and we do not know what to say. We are afraid to start. But then we take a deep breath and we begin. Our words come out slowly at first. We try to be sure before we finish. We might stop and think before we say something important.

Speaking is a way to show you are there. When you speak, you let others know that you are in the room with them. It is a way to share your ideas and your feeling.
