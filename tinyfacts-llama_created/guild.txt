A group of people who do the same thing together is a group. A group is a way to work together. When many people do the same job, they can be more strong and better at it. They help each other.

Imagine you are a person who makes clothes. You work in a store. You make many clothes every day. One day, you need a new kind of clothes. You go to a place where they make new clothes. They tell you they need this clothes. So you go to this place and buy it for you. This group of people is like a small team. They work together to make clothes.

A group can also be a group of people who are friends. They have fun together. They go to a place and laugh. They play games. They share food. They talk and say nice things to each other. This is a good way to be happy.

In a city, there are many groups. There are groups of people who drive cars. There are groups of people who walk. There are groups of people who play games. There are groups of people who work. Everyone is a part of the same group because they all do the same thing.
