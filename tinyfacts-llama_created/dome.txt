A big cover is what you use to keep the air in a room. If you walk into a room and it is too hot, the cover helps to keep the air out. If the cover is too heavy, you might feel the air pushing against your face. A small cover on the top of a tall thing is also called a cover. It helps keep the rain off when you walk in the rain.

A round cover on a door is called a cover. It is like a big piece of stuff that you put on the door to keep people out. If you have a door with a round cover, you can lock the door so you are safe inside.

A cover can also be a thing that keeps things inside. Imagine you have a box full of food and you close the box. The cover keeps the food inside and the air from getting out. You can close the cover so the food stays there.

Sometimes, a cover is something you wear. You might wear a cover on your head to keep the sun out of your eyes. Or you might wear one on your hands while you work. This cover helps you stay safe.

You can also use a cover to keep things clean. If you have a big piece of paper on your desk, you can put a cover over it. This helps keep the paper clean when you use it.
