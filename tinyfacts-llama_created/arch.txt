An arming is a long thing with a round top. It is made of one piece of hard stuff. People use them to keep their hands safe when they fight with another person. The round top is what stops the hard stuff from flying out and hitting the other person. This keeps the fight from getting very bad.

The top of the arm is often very high. It is like a big round thing on top of the hand. When you hold the arm up, the round top looks like a ball. This helps the person who makes the arm see the top better.

You can also use this word for a high place. Imagine you are on a tall building. You are in a high place and you can see for a very long way. The word helps us talk about where people are standing when they are very high.
