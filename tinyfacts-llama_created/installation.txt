To set something up or put a thing in a place. When you put a computer in a room, you are putting it in a place where it can work. You turn it on and it starts doing its job. This is an act of putting a thing where it is needed.

You also put things in your home. You put food in the kitchen. You put clothes in the bedroom. You put pictures on the wall. This is putting things where they will be seen.

Sometimes, you put people in a place. You put a teacher in a school. You put a police person in a city. This is putting a person where they are needed.

You can also say that something is set up. If you say that a game is set up, it means that the game is ready. The people who run the game are waiting for you to start the game. They are ready to begin.

It is also important to put things that are broken. If a car has a problem, the person who fixes it will put the car back in the place it was. They fix the car so it can move again.
