To have a bathroom is to have a room in your house where you do not have to go out in the middle of the night. Inside this room, you can go to sit down, or you can go to the floor and sit on the floor. Sometimes, you can go down a long hall and turn a round thing that opens. This opens and closes to let air in and to let water out.

There is also a box you can open that holds a bottle of water. When you need water, you open the box. You can also use the box to get water if you are very tired and your body is full of bad stuff.

Some bathrooms have a door that locks so you can not go in. This door is like a strong box that only you can open. You can lock it because you are in a room with people you do not know, or because you feel afraid.

Inside the room, there is a chair you sit on. You sit down and you can rest. Sometimes you sit on the floor with your feet up on the floor. This is a good way to relax because your legs are not under your body.

Bathrooms are very important because they help our bodies stay clean. We go inside and we use the water to make our skin feel soft and nice. This keeps us looking good.
