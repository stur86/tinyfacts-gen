A very important word in many places.

When we talk about big groups of things, we often look at them as one group. We might say,  A group is the group of all people who share the same things. If everyone in a city shares the same food, they are all in the same group. This helps us to understand how big the group is.

But sometimes, a group can break into many smaller groups. This happens when a group has many parts that are not the same. Each part might have its own way of doing things. The small groups are like tiny groups within the big group. They might have different names, but they all share the same things.

Think of a tree. The tree is the big group. The parts of the tree that grow from the ground are the small groups. Each part has its own way of growing and making leaves. The way the parts grow and the way they make food from the sun together make the whole tree.

This happens with many other groups too. Imagine a city with many small towns. Each town might be a small group with its own people. They all share the same way of doing things, but they are not as big as the city. This is how groups can become smaller and still be important.

When we talk about a group, we usually ask two questions. First, what are the things that people or things share? Second, how are these things shared? If we know the first question, we can understand the second one.
