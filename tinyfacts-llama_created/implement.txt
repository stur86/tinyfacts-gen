To put a plan into work means you act on it. You do the things you have decided to do and you follow the steps that were given to you. This helps the plan become real.

Imagine you want to build a new house. You have a plan for how the house should look and where the rooms should be. You make a listened to plan that says how to make the walls and the floor. You tell your friends who are building it how to put the wall parts together. Then you go and watch them do the work. When you see them doing the work, you say it is done. You did not just talk about it. You actually acted on the plan.

You can also put a plan into the computer. If you want to play a game, you put the game into the computer. The computer follows the steps in the game plan. It does not just read what you tell it. It works by following the steps. This is how you can play games on a computer and a phone.

Sometimes you have to follow a plan to get a job done. If you are a worker, your plan might say how many hours you must work and what you must do during those hours. If you follow the plan, you will have a job done in the right time. If you do not follow the plan, you might do more work than you need to do. This can be a problem because it can cause you to be late or to work too many hours.
