To join two pieces of something together is to use the power of fire or strong white stuff. You can put these pieces together and make them strong so they can hold each other.

Imagine you have two big pieces of wood. One piece is very hard and the other piece is very soft. When you use the power of fire, the hard piece can be broken and the soft piece can be made hard. You can put the two pieces together so they fit like a big hand. When the fire is done, the two pieces are joined and the hand fits around them.

This is how a car is made. The hard pieces of the body of the car are made and then the soft pieces of the body are put inside. The car is then put together by putting the hard pieces and the soft pieces together.

You can also use this to join things that are very small. If you have a small piece of paper and a small piece of wood, you can join them together by using the power of fire. The hard piece of wood can be broken and the soft piece of paper can be made hard. The two pieces fit together and the paper is now part of the wood.

Sometimes, when you join two pieces of something, the inside of the pieces is not the same. The inside of the hard piece might be soft and the inside of the soft piece might be hard. When you join the pieces, the inside of the pieces gets the same kind of power. The hard piece of wood becomes hard and the soft piece of paper becomes hard too.

This is why the power of fire is used to join all the pieces of a thing. It can join pieces that are very different, like hard and soft, and pieces that are very big and very small.
