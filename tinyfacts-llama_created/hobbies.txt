To have a job means you do work for someone else. You go to a place every day. You do things that help people. You might work with your hands to make things or with your mind to fix things. You might write papers, fix computers, or teachers. Some people have jobs in big houses with many people and many computers. Some people have jobs in small houses with fewer people and more time.

Sometimes people just like to do things alone. They like to walk, read books, or draw. They do these things because they feel happy. They might do these things every day, or sometimes just for a few hours. They might play with their kids, watch movies, or listen to music. These are their own jobs.

You can also have a job that you like. This is when you are very happy when you do it. You feel like you are doing the right things. You do it because you love it. You might paint, play music, or work in a store. You do this job every day and feel very good. This is what we mean by having a job.

Hoping is a word we use to talk about things we want to happen. We can hope for many things. We hope for a better day, a happy family, or a good friend. We hope that the sun will come up tomorrow. We hope that the water will be cold and clear. We hope that our favorite book will be ready.
