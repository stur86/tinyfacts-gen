When you go to a school, you pay money to go there. Some schools need money to pay for things like a place to live, food, and teachers. This money is called a feet. You pay the feet each week or each month so you can go to school.

Think of it like this. If you have a little money in your pocket, you can buy food. But if you want to go to a big school with many students, you need to pay for the food for everyone. So, you pay the money to the school so you can go.

Most of the time, you pay for this money when you are a small child. But when you grow up, you might pay it later. This is called a feet when you are an older person.

You do not pay the money when you are a baby. The baby grows in the mother. When the baby is big and can walk, run, and talk, they start to pay for their own food. This starts when they are about three years old.

Some schools also give you money for books. This is like buying a book for your own use. If you like to read, you can pay money for a book. This helps your teacher know that you want to learn.

In some places, you might pay money to go to a school that is far away from where you live. This is like paying for a bus ride. You pay money so you can go to a place where you need to go.

Teeth is a word that some people use to talk about money. It can mean both a feet and money. You can also use it to talk about how much you pay for things. For a long time, people used to say, if you pay for a thing, you pay it. Now, we use the word teeth to mean the money you pay.
