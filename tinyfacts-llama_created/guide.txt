To show someone the way is to help them move through a place. Imagine you are in a big city with many roads and many streets. A person who knows the city can tell you which roads to take and how to get from one place to another. They show the way so you do not get lost and you can find what you need.

If you are on a big water, you need to follow someone who is already on the water. They can point out what to look for, where to be and when to stop. They help you find the right place to reach the other side.

You can also use this word for a person who is in a room and shows you where things are. If you are in a big hall with many rooms, you might ask a person who is already there to help you find a place. They help you know where to go.

To show someone the way can be a kind thing. If you are feeling scared, a friend can help you feel better by showing you the way out. This can be very nice because it makes you feel safe and you are not alone.
