To avoider means to stay away from something. It is a word you might use if you are afraid.

Imagine you see a big dog. You are afraid to go near it. You do not want to get close to it. You are trying to stay away from the dog. This is avoiding the dog.

You can also use this word for the sun. If you are going to bed early, you might want to avoider going out into the bright sun. You do not want the bright light to hurt your eyes while you are sleeping.

Sometimes, avoider is used for a person. If someone is afraid of a thing, they might say they are avoider of it. This means they are afraid to see or do that thing.

It is an important word because it shows you care about yourself and the world around you.
