To be a leader is to have power over others. This can happen in many different ways. One way is to be the top person at a school or in a business. They make many important jobs for everyone else. They decide what is best to do and what not to do.

Another way is to be the one who starts a new game or a new story. If you start a game, you are a leader. You decide who goes first and who gets to play next. You also decide what the game is about.

A leader can also be someone who helps people feel safe. If you are afraid, you might feel very scared. A leader can make everyone feel better by being kind and calm. They might say,  Let us all stay inside the house because it is safe. This helps everyone feel safe.

Sometimes, a leader is a person who is very good at their job. They do their work very well and make sure that everyone follows them. They are like a teacher, but they do not give books or papers. They tell people what to do and how to do it.

Being a leader is not just about doing things. It is also about leading by showing what you want. If you want everyone to move to the right side of the road, you show them. You point to the right and say, Come! They follow you because they trust you.

In some places, there are many different people who are in this kind of job. They all have the same job, but they each have a different way of leading. Some might use their words, others might use their body, and others might use their brains.
