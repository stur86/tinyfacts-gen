To barely means you are only just getting enough or you are not very strong. It is often used to talk about things that are very small or very easy. You can barely open your mouth. You are barely able to say a few words.

If you say you are barely able to do something, it is because you are not very good at it. You might be barely able to walk a short way. You are barely able to get a glass of water from the high counter.

You can also barely get through a long day. You are barely able to keep your eyes open. This is when you feel very tired and your head feels like it is going to fall off.

When you are barely happy, you are not very happy at all. You are barely smiling. You might look very sad. It is like you are not really sure if things are okay.

In some places, you say a person barely speaks. This means they are very quiet. They do not say much, but they still seem to know what they are talking about.
