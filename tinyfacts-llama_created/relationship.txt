A relationship is how you feel with someone. It is about the way you act toward another person. You do things for them, they do things for you, and you both feel happy or sad together.

To be in a relationship means you are not alone. You have a friend or a person you trust. If you love someone, you have a deep feeling. This feeling can be very strong. You feel like you can be your best self around them.

Sometimes, you can have a relationship with a person you see every day. You know them well. You talk to them every morning. You know how they laugh. You know their voice. This kind of relationship is easy because you know each other.

Other times, you have a relationship with a person you see less. You see them once a month. You talk about things. You laugh together. You feel a little bit of a pull. This kind of relationship is good too. You have time to talk, to laugh, and to feel happy together.

People also have relationships with animals. You care for them. You give them food. You keep them safe. This kind of relationship is very important. Animals are our friends. They help us feel safe. They make our lives better.

In a relationship, you and another person do not always see eye to eye. You might fight. You might be angry. But you both try to understand each other. You try to find a way that makes both of you happy.

Sometimes, a relationship ends. You and your friend grow very far. You learn new things. You grow as people. You might not like the way one person acts anymore. You might decide you want to be alone. You are sad. You both feel a little bit of a hurt.

But even if a relationship ends, the memory of it can stay in your heart. You remember the good times. You remember the love. You remember how you felt when you were together. This can help you feel better in the end.
