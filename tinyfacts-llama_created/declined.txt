When someone says a thing is done, it usually means they did it. But if you use the word, you are talking about how that thing happened.

Imagine you wanted to play a game with a friend. You were very excited and ready to start. But then, you saw a dog. The dog was running very fast and you were afraid. You decided to stop and go inside the house. This happened because of the dog.

We can also use the word to talk about how things change. If you love a person very much, you might feel sad when you think about them. This is because you are not happy anymore. Your heart feels like it is breaking.

Sometimes, something happens to someone. They might be very happy or very sad. They might feel a lot of different things. The word helps us understand how things move.

We also use the word to talk about how people act. If someone is very good at a job, they might do it with a happy heart. But if someone is very bad, they might do it with a mean face and a heart that is not happy. This helps us see how people act when they do things.
