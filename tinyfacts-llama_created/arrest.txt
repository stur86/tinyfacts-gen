When a person is caught doing something wrong, they are said to have been caught. This means they were found by others who saw them doing the wrong thing.

The police are the people who do this. They are like the people who help keep the city safe. They go from house to house, asking questions and looking at papers to see if the person did the wrong thing. They want to know who did it and why.

If a person is caught, they might be taken away. This is called being taken into the police building. It is like being put in a strong room where you are locked. The people there keep you until the police decide what to do next.

The word can mean two different things. When someone does a bad thing, they get a time to stay in the room. This is a time to think about what they did. If they do not change, they might have to stay there for a long time. This is called a time to stay.

If a person is caught doing a good thing, they might just be asked to go home. They do not have to stay in the room. They can go back to their home and family.

To be caught is a way to show that something is real. It shows that people are watching and waiting.
