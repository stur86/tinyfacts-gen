An earlier person is one who lives before the time of today. A long time ago, a person who lived in a place would be called an earlier person. The word is from the word for a man with power.

In the past, a person who was very important would live in a big house. They were called the earlier of that house. This person would give orders and help the family of the house. They were like a top person.

Today, we use the word in a different way. If you are an earlier person, you are the first one in your line. You are the one who starts it. You are the one who makes the first move. This is how it works in the world of business. If you are the first person in a group, you are an earlier person.

You can also use the word to mean someone who is very good at making things. They might work in a big building where they make cars or build houses. They are the first ones who do the work. They are the ones who start it all.
