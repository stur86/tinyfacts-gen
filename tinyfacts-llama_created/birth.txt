To be a baby means you are very small. You are not yet able to walk or talk. Your body is still growing. You need to eat often so your body can stay strong.

A baby comes into the world. This happens when a woman goes into her body and pushes the baby out. A woman is the one who gives a baby life. She helps the baby get its first breaths and its first things to drink.

After the baby is out, the baby needs food from its mother. The mother drinks a drink every day. She gives the food to the baby through her body. This is why we say a baby needs a lot of food.

As the baby grows, it learns how to walk, talk, and do things. It sees the world around it. It looks at things and tries to understand them. This is why babies are so full of wonder and love.

Even though a baby is very small, it is the start of everything new. It makes the world feel new and full of life. When a baby is grown, it becomes a person. It can walk, talk, and do many of the things that grown people do.
