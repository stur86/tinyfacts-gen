To box is to use your hands to hit a small, hard thing that makes a loud sound. You hold your hands in front of you and your body. You stand still and wait for your body to say it is ready.

A person who box is called a fighter. They often fight in a room with other people. They use their hands to hit the other person. You can also box with a ball. You throw the ball against a wall. Your hands go on top of the ball and your body is the floor. You try to stop the ball from going into the wall.

In a game, the person who is doing the hitting is called the fighter. They wear a cover on their hands. The other person is called the one who tries to hit the cover. If you hit the cover, you are in the game. If you are hit, you go down.

A box is also a thing that holds things. You can find boxes in stores. They are usually round or have a long side. Inside the box, you can find many things you need to live. If you have a box, you know you have a place for your things.

Sometimes, a person might say they are in a box. This means they are in a place they do not want to be. It might mean they are in a room or a school. They might feel sad because they do not want to be there.
