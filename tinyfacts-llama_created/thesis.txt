A very important word that many people use when they start to study a big idea.

When you start to study a big idea, you usually make a plan. This plan is called a plan. It is like a road that you follow. You write down what you think, what you want to learn, and how you will do it.

But sometimes, you do not just follow a road. You start your own road. This is what a person does when they start to study a big idea. They write their own plan. They think about this idea for a long time. They might write many papers. They might talk to other people. They might go to a meeting. They might even talk to the person who made the plan before them.

When you start your own road, you are doing something that is not done by others. You are showing everyone that you can do it. You are trying to be the best.

There is also a thing called a name. When you start your own road, you give it a name. This name is like a sign. It tells everyone who you are. If you go to a school, you might have a name. If you write a paper, you might have a name. If you talk to people, you might have a name.

Sometimes, people ask you to show what you have done. They want to see how you did it. They might ask you to talk about it. They might ask you to show them your work. They might ask you to show them how you felt.

This is what happens when you finish your plan. You have done what you started to do. You have shown everyone what you are doing. You have followed your own road.

Also, sometimes people say that someone is doing a thing. They say the person is a person who does it. This is like saying you are a person who makes things. It means the person is the one who makes it happen.

So, a person who starts to study a big idea is a person who follows their own road. They give their plan a name. They try to be the best. They try to show everyone what they are doing.
