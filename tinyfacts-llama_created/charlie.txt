A person who is a good friend is called a friend. You can be a friend to a person, a dog, a cat, or even a tree. A friend is someone you like and care about. You might spend time with them, talk to them, or just be near them.

If a person is a good friend, they are kind. They listen when you need to talk. They help you when you are sad or scared. They might tell you the truth even if it hurts your face. They are there for you when you do not feel like smiling.

Sometimes, a person might be a friend because they are happy for you. They might say, I am so glad you are doing well. They might smile at you when they see you. Being a friend can make you feel happy inside. It can make you feel like you are not alone in the world.

A friend can be different for different people. Some friends are small. They are a few people you see every week. Other friends are many. They are people you meet at school, at the park, or at work. Some friends are people you know from a game. They are people you like to play with.

A good friend can stay with you for many years. They might not always be there when you need them, but they will always remember you. They will always know that you care about them. This can help keep your heart full and make you feel safe.
