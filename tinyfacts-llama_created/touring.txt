To go on a trip is to move from one place to another. One person might go to see their friends. Another person might go to visit a city that is very big. A person might go to a place where they want to eat food that they like.

A person does not just go. They go on a set date. They plan the days, the places, and the times. They buy a bag with food, clothes, and a thing that helps them stay safe while they are away. They also buy a thing that they can use to take pictures when they see places they have never seen before.

Sometimes, a person goes to many places in one go. They go to many cities, towns, and small places. They stay in each place for a while and see what they think. They talk to people and hear stories about their lives.

When someone says a place is a good place to go, they mean that it is nice to be there. They like the way it looks, the way it feels, or the way it makes them feel. They might tell a friend they should go to this place because it is a great place to visit.
