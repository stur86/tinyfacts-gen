Frown is a word that people use when they are sad or angry. It is like a face with a line across it. When you frown, you look down and your eyes are a little bit heavy. You might raise your arms and shake them a bit. It is a way to show that something is wrong. You might also frown when you are afraid.

When people talk, they often use this word. If you say you are happy, a friend might say, I am frowning. But if you are really sad, your friend will say you are frowning. It is a word that helps us show how we are feeling.
