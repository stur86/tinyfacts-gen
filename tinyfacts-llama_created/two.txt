Two is a number that stands alone. It is the number before the number three. It is the first number in a group. You can not have one without two. When you pick up a ball, you hold the ball in your hand. You do not pick up a hand. You pick up the ball.

Two is also a number that has many friends. Imagine you have a dog and a cat. Both dogs and cats are two animals. You can not have only one dog and one cat. You need both of them to live together.

Sometimes, when you have two things, you can do two different things. You can watch a movie with your friend or you can play a game alone. You can also have two friends and one friend who is very close to you.

If you have two books, you can read one book first and then read the other book. You do not have to pick one book and then the other. You can pick both books at the same time.

Two is a number that helps us understand how many things there are. It is a number that is always the first number in a group. It is the number that you use when you say, like, two friends or two dogs.
