To talk to a person who helps with your mind and body can be very good. This is called a job. A person with this job might have a big room with a soft chair and a table. They sit with you and listen. They ask you many questions. They also try to understand why you are feeling. They might say things like, what is wrong, why you are sad, what you think about, or what you need.

Sometimes, a person with this job works with a person who is very angry. The person who is angry might say many things. The person with the job listens and tries to help. They help the person say what they really feel. They help the person find ways to stay calm.

A person with this job can also help people who are afraid. They might talk about what the person is afraid of. They help the person see that they are not alone. They might give a person a small thing to carry. This small thing is like a friend.

People who work with this job are called people who help with their mind. They help you face your problems. They help you say what you really think. They help you find ways to be better. They help you feel less alone.
