An old word for a small computer. It was before the word computer. Some people still use it today. It is for a thing that can play music and movies.

To play music with it, you use a small thing that is put inside the box. You press a few lines of numbers and the thing makes the music. You can also use it to listen to music from your phone.

To watch movies with it, you sit in a dark room and the thing shows a picture of a far away place. You can watch movies from your home or buy them from the store.

It is small, like a box, but it does many things for you. You can play music and watch movies, just like a small computer.
