To respond means to give a thing back. When you speak to someone, you are responding. You answer their words. If you say yes, your friend is responding to your question. If you tell a story, you are responding to the story they heard.

In a big group, some people listen and some people speak. Everyone is trying to respond. If you are quiet and watch, you are responding by listening.

Sometimes, you might feel angry and want to say something. You are responding to the person who made you angry. You are giving them your words.

In a long story, the person who wrote it often has many different ideas. They may ask many questions and then write about them. Every person who reads the story has a different idea. This is an interesting way to respond to a story.

To respond also means to be part of a group. If you are in a group of friends, you are responding to them. You are showing your love and care. You are saying you are there for them.
