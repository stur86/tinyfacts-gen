A plan is a way to do something. You can plan before you start to do it. You think about how you will act. You might think, What will I do if a person asks me? Or, What will I do if the time is short?

You can plan for a long time. You might plan for a year, a month, or a day. You might plan for a big thing, like a game, a movie, or a big eventually. You are thinking about the end and how to get there.

A plan is not always easy. You might have to make hard answers. You might have to deal with things you do not want to do. You might feel afraid or confused. But a plan helps you feel more sure.

Sometimes you make a plan for a time that is very far away. You might plan for a time when you are old and your body is not strong anymore. You might plan for a time when your children are grown and you do not have to care for them anymore. This is a plan for the end of your life.

A plan can also help you stay safe. If you know how to act in a very bad situation, you can stay safe. You can plan for how to get out of the bad place to a safe place. You can plan how to tell your family what happened so they can help you.

A plan is a way to act so you do not get hurt. It is a way to be ready for any thing that comes up.
