To set something up means to make it work so it can do what you need it to do. If you want a computer to play music, you might set it up so it can hear the music and show the picture that you want to see. You do this by telling the computer which music to play and how to show the picture.

A computer has many of these things inside it. One of these is what we call a hard drive. The hard drive is where you keep all the things you want to store on your computer. Think of it like a big box. You put things in the box and then you can look at them later.

A computer also needs a thing that helps it talk to other computers. This is the word we use for this thing. When you send a piece of word or picture to another computer, the computer reads it and does what you want it to do. This thing is like a person who talks to other people.

When you send a piece of word or picture to another computer, the computer sends it through the air. This is why you can use a computer from many different places at once. You might be sitting at your desk and a friend is sitting at another desk. You can talk to each other and send words and pictures to each other in a way that is fast and easy.
