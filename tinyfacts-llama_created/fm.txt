A sound is a thing that makes you feel. When you hear a sound, you know what it is. It is a sound that comes from a thing. A car makes a sound when it moves. A dog makes a sound when it wants to play. A bell makes a sound when you ring it.

An ear is a part of your head that helps you hear. The sound comes into the ear and goes into your brain. The brain then tells your body what the sound is. Your body feels it. You might hear a sound when you walk, when you eat, or when you talk.

A long time ago, people did not have a word to talk about the sound of the air. They called it a sound. Now we have many words. We use them to tell stories, to show how people feel, and to explain what is happening.

You can also hear a sound that is very far away. This sound is from a place you have never been to. You might hear the sound of a big water when you are in a small room. You can also hear a sound from a far away house when you walk very far.

A song is a group of sounds that you play together. A person who makes a sound might singled out one sound and use it. They might use all the sounds together to make a song. You can hear a song on a computer or on a phone. You can also hear music from a car or from a movie.
