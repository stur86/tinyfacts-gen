The word is used to talk about people or things that are very good at what they do. When someone is very good at something, it is an out doing. It means they do not just do it well, they do it in a way that is very clear and easy to see.

Imagine a person who can run very fast. If they are the fastest person in the world, they are an out doing. They are the best in their own way. They are not just running, they are doing it in a way that is very good for everyone.

It is not always about the best thing. An out doing can also be about how well you work with others. If a team works well together, it is an out doing. They are doing a good job of helping each other.

Sometimes, people say an out doing is when you are very happy with what you have done. It is a way to say, I am very glad that I did this.
