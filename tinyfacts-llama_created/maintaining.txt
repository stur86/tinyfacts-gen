To keep something going is to help it stay strong. You do this by doing things that do not break it. You do not let it fall down or get very dark.

Imagine you have a ball. You keep it up in the air by using your arms. If you stop using your arms, the ball will fall down to the ground. This is what it means to be in control. You are in control of the ball.

In a home, you keep a table by cleaning it every day. You do this so the table looks nice and can hold food for many people. This is also about being in control.

When a person does not keep their body clean, they get sick. They may have a cold or a run. This is because their body is not strong enough to fight off the bad things that are there.

To keep something is also to keep it safe. If a child keeps a box with their favorite book, they are keeping it safe. They are not letting anyone else touch it. They are keeping it for later when they want to read it again.

In some places, people keep money in a box for many days. They keep it so they can buy food or clothes if they need them. This is about keeping things going so people can live.
