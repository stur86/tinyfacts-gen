To be an animal you must not fight or hurt other animals. It is very kind to all living things. If a baby animal is very small, a human can pick it up and keep it safe. This makes the animal very happy.

When a person is an animal they are also not afraid. They do not fight or try to kill other people. They just want to live in the world without fear.

Sometimes, a person acts very well for a long time. They do not do anything that makes other people angry. This is a good thing to see. It shows that the person is kind and does not want to hurt anyone.

In life, you will meet many people. Some people are very strong and make a lot of noise. Some people are very quiet and only think about their own things. An animal is like a person who is very kind and does not want to hurt anyone.

In a story, a person might act very nice. They help a friend who is in trouble. They do not try to hurt the person. This is an act of being an animal.
