A man is a person who does work. You might see him when he drives a car. He might drive a car to go to work. He does not just sit in the car. He has to pay money for the time. He must follow the road. He might drive in many cities.

Another person is a man who makes things. He is a builder or a maker. He uses hard things to make new things for people. If he makes a chair, it is a thing you sit on. If he makes a car, it is a thing that moves.

A man can also be a man of words. He talks a lot. He can say many things and many words. He might give a talk at a school or at a big place where many people are. This is when he is a teacher.

Some men are a man who flies through the air. They have a big thing with a round top. They go very high and very fast. They fly over many cities and over the water. People watch them from the ground.

In some stories, a man is a man who is afraid. He is not sure about things. He might hide or run away. But he is still a man.
