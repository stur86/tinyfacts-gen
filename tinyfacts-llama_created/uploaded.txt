When you write something on a computer, you put it into the computer. The computer looks at what you wrote and puts it into a place where other people can see it. This is how people share things.

When you want to share something, you can take it from your computer and put it into a place where many people can see. You can do this with pictures, words, or other kinds of things that computers can understand. The computer sends the thing to where you want it to go.

This is how people share things they did not make. You can see what someone else is doing. You can look at how they are doing things and learn from them. You can also see what they are writing and share what you think with them.

Sometimes you might want to send a thing that is already there. You might want to send a picture from your phone to a friend. You tell the computer what to do and it sends it to your friend. This is how you stay in touch with people even when you are far away.
