A place where you can get on a bus, a train, or a car. It is a stop where people get off. Many cities have many of these. 

Sometimes the word is for a big stop with many lines. You can get on one line and go to many different parts of the city. Other times, it is for a stop only on one line. You go there and get on the line, then you go many times to get to where you want to go.

You might see many lines at one stop. If you are moving fast, the lines might be moving too. If you are walking or sitting, you can wait. 

Some places are very busy. Many cars get on and off at these stops. People may talk on their phones while they wait. Some places have food and drinks. You can buy a drink or a food thing at these stops.
