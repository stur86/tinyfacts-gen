To find something means to look for it. When you are looking for a ball, you are looking at the ground. You look in the middle of the field and then in the area where the ball is. You keep looking until you see it. You did not just sit there. You were moving. If you were sitting, you would not have looked.

Sometimes you look in a place where you know the ball is. You look at a picture of the ball. You see the red and white parts. You see the round body. You see the long neck. You see the arms and the legs. You see the way the ball looks. You are sure now. You know where to look next.

You can also find something that is hidden. You do not see it at first. You look and look. You do not give up. You keep looking until you see it. It is like a game. You are looking for a thing that is not there. You are looking until you find it.

To find something can also mean to understand it. If you are trying to learn something new, you are finding it. You are looking at the words and trying to make sense. You are looking at the picture and trying to see. You are looking at your own brain and trying to know why things happen. You are finding how things work. You are finding what is important.

Sometimes you find something hard. It is a big piece of wood. It is heavy. It is hard to carry. You must use your hands. You must work with your body. But you do not give up. You keep pushing. You keep trying. You find a way to open it. You find a way to use it. You have found something important.

Finding is a part of our life. We all do it every day. We look for our friends. We look for food. We look for a place to sleep. We look for a way to go to a place that is far away. We look for things that make us happy. We look for things that help us feel safe.
