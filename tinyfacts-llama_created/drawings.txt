To draw means you make things by moving your hands on a piece of paper or a wall. You use a small mark that you make on the paper. The mark is like a tiny line or a small piece of paper.

When you draw, you start with a little bit of paper. You place it on a table or a desk. Then you use a piece of soft stuff to make lines on the paper. You can use a small thing that makes lines or a long stick that moves in a straight line. You can also use your fingers to make lines and small marks.

You might start with a big idea. You might draw a person, a dog, or a car. As you draw, you might add more lines and marks. You might also change the lines to make the thing look different.

Sometimes you draw things that are not real. You might draw a tree in the middle of a big city. This is called a picture. You can also draw things that are only in your mind. You can draw a bright thing that makes you feel happy or a dark thing that makes you feel sad.

To draw is to use your hands to make things. It is a way to show what you see and what you want.
