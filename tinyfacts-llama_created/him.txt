To him is a word that tells how we feel about a person, a thing, or an idea.

Imagine you are very happy. You might smile and laugh when you think about this person. You might feel like you are the one in the story. This person is the one you are thinking about.

You can also use this word to talk about an animal. If you see a cat, you might say,  This cat is to me. The word shows that the cat is important.

Sometimes, when you read a story, you see a person who is important. You might feel like you are the one who is living the story. You might feel like you are the one who does something. This is why you say,  That is him or her.

This word is very important in our lives. It lets us know who we care about and who we do not care about.

You can use it to talk about your own life. You might tell a friend,  I am in love with him. This word shows you are very happy. You feel like you are the one in the story.

So, to him is a word that tells how we feel about the things in our lives. It is a word that helps us understand what is important to us.
