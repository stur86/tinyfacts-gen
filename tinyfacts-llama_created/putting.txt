To put something is to move it from one place to another. You can put a book on a table. You put the book down from the high top where you sit.

You can also put food on a table. If you have a big bag of food, you put it on a table for people to eat later.

If you put a child in a car, you put them in the back seat. The child sits down and looks out the window. When you are driving, you put the car on the road.

You can put a thing in a box. You put a small thing in a box to keep it safe. This keeps the thing from being hurt.

You can put money in a bag. If you have money, you put it in your bag to carry it with you.

Sometimes, putting something means to change it. If you put a broken thing back together, you fix it so it works again. You do not just put it in a box and forget about it.

Putting can also mean to give something to someone. If you give a friend a book, you are putting the book in their hands. This makes them happy because they have something new to read.
