Hellos is a word we use to say when we meet someone for the first time. It is like a big hello.

Imagine you are walking down a street. You see a friend. You say, hello. This is a hello. It is a way to say you are there. You are not saying, you are here, or you are there. You are just saying you are.

Sometimes we say hello to people we see every day. We might say hello to the bus driver, the teacher, or the person who cuts the hair at the bar. This is a hello we use because we know them. We are not surprised. We know they are there.

We can also say hello to things. We say hello to the sun when it is up in the sky. We say hello to the trees when they are green. We say hello to the water when it is moving. This is because the thing is here.

When we say hello, we are not just saying the word. We are showing that we are ready to talk. We are saying, I am here. I am ready.

You can also say hello when you are leaving. You say hello to the people you see on the street. You are saying, I am going away. I am leaving.

So, when you hear someone say hello, you know they are ready to talk. You know they are here.
