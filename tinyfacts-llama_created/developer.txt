A person who works with computers is called a computer worker. They build things for the computer. They write the lines of words that the computer follows so it can do things. They make the computer do work for people. They also fix the computer when it breaks.

A computer worker does not just build things for one person. They work with many people from all over the world. They talk with them and write notes. They make sure the computer works well so everyone can use it.

A computer worker also makes new things. They build computers for schools and for work. They build computers for games that let people play far away. They build computers for the police so they can look at pictures of people and find them. They build computers for doctors to help them see inside the body of a person.

Some people are very good at these jobs. They build computers that can do many things at once very fast. They make computers that can learn and change on their own. They build computers that can talk to other computers and send things over the air.

These people are often called computer people. They are very important because they build the computers that help us live our lives today.
