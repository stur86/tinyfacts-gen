A thing that is made by people is called a built thing. It is not found in the ground. You did not see it there before. It was brought into the world by people who had a plan.

You can build a house, a car, or a chair. You use many parts that are made by people. You put them together. You also use your own hands. You can build a thing that is very heavy. Or you can build a thing that is very small. Many built things are for people to use every day.

Sometimes you build a thing that will help you later. You might build a computer so you can do work and watch movies. You might build a thing that can carry people or things from one place to another. You might build a thing that can make food or clean things.

A built thing is not like a tree or a rock. It is made by people and is not a part of the ground. It has its own power and can do things on its own.
