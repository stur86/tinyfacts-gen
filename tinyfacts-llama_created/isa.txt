An idea is a thing you think about. It is not a thing. You do not have it in your hands. You can not see it. It is just in your mind.

Sometimes, you have an idea about how you should live your life. You might want to be kind to everyone. Or you might want to work hard and get a good job. Your idea is like a set of lines and numbers in your head.

Another kind of idea is a person. You might think about a friend. Or you might think about a family man. This is a real person in your world.

You can also have an idea about something that does not yet happen. You might think, I will go to the store tomorrow. Or I will learn to play a new game. This idea is for the day after tomorrow.

An idea is like a light in your mind. You can not see the light, but you feel it. You can not touch it, but you can feel its power. Ideas help us understand the world and how we live with each other.
