To put something in front of someone means to tell them about it. You might want to tell a friend that you have a new book. You are not going to just walk into the room and say, like, I have a new book. You have to first say, I have a new book. This is the beginning of the story. It sets the story going.

Sometimes, you are not sure what to say. You want to find the right word. You might ask a friend, Can you tell me about your day? They look at you and say, Okay. This is a good start. You still have a long way to go. You need to keep going until you find the right words.
