To trust someone means to believe that they are not going to hurt you. It is a feeling of having a friend who you can tell anything to, because you know they will not tell you a bad story or keep your money. When you trust someone, you are letting them know that you have your back.

Imagine you have a bag with money. You give it to someone you trust. They take it and go to a place where they buy things. When they come back, they give you the money back. You did not worry that they would take your money.

Trust is important because it makes people want to be around you. If you do not trust someone, it might be hard to talk to them. You might feel afraid or angry.

Sometimes you trust a person, but then you find out they are not a good person. You felt happy at first, but now you feel sad. This is because your trust was broken.

There are also things you trust that are not people. You trust the ground under your feet. You know that the ground will not fall away from you. You trust that the water will not go over you when you walk in the rain.

Trust is like a strong word that helps us feel safe and happy.
