To build something means you use many pieces of wood, or other stuff, and you put them together to make a new thing. This thing can be a house, a car, a table, a chair, or even a big group of people working together.

When you build something, you start with a plan. You think about how you want the thing to look and how it will help people. You make a listener of what you need and how you will put them together. Then you start to build.

You start with the big parts. You put the big pieces of wood together to make the floor of the house or the table. You use long pieces of wood that go across the table to hold it up. You use short pieces of wood to make the legs of the table.

Next you add the sides. You put the long pieces of wood across the table to make it strong. You put the short pieces of wood under the long pieces to keep the table from falling over.

After you have the sides, you put the top on. You use many small pieces of wood to cover the front of the table. You use big pieces of wood to cover the back so they do not show.

You keep adding the pieces together until the thing is all built. You check the thing to make sure it is strong and safe. Then you are done.

This is how people make many things every day. They build the roads so cars can drive. They build the schools so people can learn. They build the places where people work and live.
