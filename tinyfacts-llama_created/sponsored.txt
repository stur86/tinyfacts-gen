To be your own person is a big thing. You are the one who does what you want, who says what you think, who does not follow anyone else. It is when you take care of yourself, when you say you are happy and you are fine.

Sometimes, people help you. They pay for things, or they tell you to do something. This is when they give you money or let you do a thing they say you can do. They do this because they want you to be happy, or they want to see you have fun.

We also have a word that is different. When you are your own person, you do not need to ask anyone else what to do. You just do it. You are the one who decides, who takes the steps, who follows your heart. This is how you build your own life, your own world.

But sometimes, people are not your own. They want to control what you do, how you live, and who you are. They want you to be like them, to do things they want you to do. This is not okay. When we are our own people, we can be free. We can make our own way. We can be the person we want to be.
