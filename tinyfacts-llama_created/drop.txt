To drop something means you let it go. You do this when you are moving and you let the things you carry fall down. It can happen when you are running very fast or when you are trying to get away from someone.

Imagine you have a bag of food. You carry it on your back. You are moving. Then, you let the bag go. The food falls down and lands on the ground. This is a drop.

You can also drop things in a room. You might drop a book, a phone, or a small thing. This happens when the thing falls from a high place.

Sometimes, when you drop something, it does not just fall down. It might go over your head and land on the floor. This is a very cool thing that happens in the sky during the night.

In a game, you might drop a ball. The player throws the ball to a friend. Then, the friend catches the ball. This is a drop because the ball leaves the player and goes to the other person.

To drop a word means you use it less. If you use a word too many times, it is like a heavy bag. After you drop the word, you can stop talking about it. It is a way to let go of what you are saying.
