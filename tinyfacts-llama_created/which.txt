Which is a word that helps us talk about who and what.

When you want to know who is in a group, you might ask, who is that person? This is a question. The person who answers might be the one who is standing next to you, the one who is speaking, or the one who is writing something down.

You can also ask about what something is. You might say, what is that thing? This is another question. The person who answers might tell you it is a book, it is a car, or it is a dog.

Sometimes, you just want to know if something is true. You might ask, is it true? If the answer is yes, then you know that the thing is real. If the answer is no, then you might not know yet.

There are many other types of questions too. You can ask about how something works. You might ask, how does it work? Or you can ask about why something happens. You might say, why is this happening?

Sometimes, we use the word which in other ways. When you are looking at a group of people, you might say, which person is the leader? This is a question about who is in the middle. You are trying to find who is in control.

You can also use which when you are talking about things that are already there. You might say, which chair is my favorite chair? This is a question about what is already there.

In short, which is a word that helps us talk about who, what, and why. It is a word that helps us figure out who is there, what is true, and why things happen.
