An act is a thing you do. You might do this by moving your body or by talking. It is something you do with your own power.

When you act, you change something. You make a new thing happen. You are not just a quiet person. You are a person who is doing things. You might act to get a job. You might act to say a word. You might act to show you are afraid.

You can act many ways. You can act with your words. You can act with your body. You can act with your heart. You can act to help other people.

You must follow the way you act. If you act in a bad way, you can hurt people. If you act in a good way, you show you care. Acts show us who you are and what you think.

Sometimes, you act in front of many people. This is a big act. You show them what you are like. This can be a good act or a bad act. It shows how you feel and what you want.

You can also act when you are scared. You act by moving your body. You act by talking. You act by doing things to stay safe. Acts help us deal with how we feel.

In life, you act all the time. You act when you wake up. You act when you go to bed. You act when you talk. You act when you laugh. You act when you cry. You act when you are angry.
