To begin something is to start it. When you start something, you start moving. You can start a new day, a new game, a new story, or a new idea. You are at the start and everything else happens from there.

Imagine you are a person. You want to go to school. You are not sure if you can do it yet. But you decide to start. You get ready, you eat, you walk, and you go to school. You begin your day. You learned something new that day.

When you start a story, you begin the first part. The story starts where the person was before they started. They may have been happy, sad, angry, or afraid. The story then goes on from there. The story has many parts. You read each part until you get to the end.

You can also start a game. You might say, let us begin the game. Everyone follows your words. Then, you play. You use your hands, your body, and your mind to do things. You try to beat the other person. The game is fun because you are working together and trying to get better.

To start something new is important. It shows that you are ready and that you want to try. It is a first step. After you start, you can keep going.
