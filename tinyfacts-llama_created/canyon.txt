A big cut in the ground is what a can is made of. But a can is not a place like a room or a house. It is a place in the ground where water or food can go in.

Imagine you are in a wide field. You look out and see a long, deep line in the ground. This line is the top of a very high place. The ground under the line is much lower. This is what a can looks like. The top part of the line is the top of the can. The bottom part is the ground under the can.

Cats like to jump over these lines. They climb up the side and then jump down to the other side. If you jump over a can, you are not going up. You are going down. If you jump over a can that is very high, you are in more trouble than if you jumped over a small one. The higher the can, the more you have to go down.

Some places where a can is found are in the ground where a big water body has cut out a wide area. Other places have can lines in the ground where a place has been cut out and then put back together.
