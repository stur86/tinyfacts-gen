To make something better is to move forward. When you walk, you go forward. When you build a house, you add more rooms. Each room is better than the one before it. This is a kind of change.

Things do not stay the same. As time passes, you grow. You learn new things. You become stronger. You can do new things. This is a kind of change.

When a group of people do things together, they are making their world better. They fix a street. They build a new high school. They help each other. They are moving forward.

Sometimes, we see people who are getting better. They work hard. They learn from their friends and from the world. They try new ways to be kind. They try to help others. They are getting closer to being the best.

In a big group of people, there is one person who is doing better than everyone else. They are the leader. They are making sure everyone follows them. They are moving the group forward.

To make something better is to show that you care. It is to help. It is to move forward together.
