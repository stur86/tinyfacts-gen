The word often has two different uses.

When you say something happens, you are usually talking about what is happening now. This is like saying a baby is crying. The word happening is in the middle of the word. It is not the start or the end. This means the thing that is happening is the part in the middle.

Think of a big city. The city is the place where people live. The people are the people. The streets are the roads that go through the city. The cars are the things that go on the roads. The people and the cars are all happening together in the city. This is how we use the word happening.

Sometimes you see a story. The story has a good part and a bad part. The good part is when the good things happen. The bad part is when the bad things happen. The story is like a building that has many rooms. Each room tells a story about a different time. When you hear the story, you get to know the whole building.

This word can also mean something that is happening today. If you say a thing is happening, you are talking about what is happening right now. You are not talking about something that happened before. This is like saying you are sitting in a chair. You are in the chair right now.
