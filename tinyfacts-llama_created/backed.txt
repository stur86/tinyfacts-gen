To backed something means you are ready to help it. If you want to go to a new city, you can say you backed it. You told your parents you are ready to go and you will pay for the food.

When you back something, you are the one who will do the work after the other person is done. You do not ask to be the one who does it. You just do it when it is your turn.

If you are backed by someone, you are helped by them. They give you money or they help you with a problem. This helps you feel safe because you know you have someone you can turn to when you need it.

You can also back a team. If a team is doing well, the people who backed them are happy. They believe they can do it and they feel good about it. They are backing the team all the way.

Sometimes, you back something that is not your idea. You might back a new idea because you think it is good, even if it is not your own. This helps others see that you are a good person who wants what is best for them.

In the end, backed means you are ready to help or be part of something.
