To make something new

When you make something, it often starts from nothing. You take some things and put them together. You add them in a way that makes them different. This takes time and a lot of work.

Sometimes you make things that are for people to use. You make a car so a person can go from one place to another. You make a book so someone can read it. You also make food so people can eat it. You make a computer so a person can use it.

Other times you make things that are not for people. You make a tree so it can grow and give us air. You make a rock so it can stay in the ground. You make the sky so it looks like the world we know.

To make something new is to start a new thing. It is a way to change what already is. It is a way to build on what is there. It is a way to be different from what has come before.

Think of it like water. Water is made from the sun, the ground, and the air. But you can make more water from this water. You can use it to drink, to make food, or to make things. You are making new water from the old water.
