A word is a thing you use to talk. It is a small group of letters, numbers, or words that tell a story or expressions. You can use a word to ask a question, to tell what you think, or to say a thing is true. When you use a word, you are saying something new.

A group of words that tell a story is a story. If you say, you are a good student, you are telling a story. Each word is a small piece of the story. The words are put together to form a complete picture.

You can also use the word in other ways. If you say you are very happy, you are telling a story. You are not just telling the word, you are telling the feeling you have. You are showing how you are feeling.

Sometimes, one word tells another word. If you say, you are going to the store, the word is a kind of story. You are telling a story about where you are going. You are not just telling the word, you are telling the place and the time.

A group of words can be very short or very long. You can use one word to tell a story about how you feel or you can use many words to tell a story about a thing you see or a place you visit.

You can also use a word with other words. If you say, I am going to the park, the word is a kind of story. You are not just telling the word, you are telling a story about the place. You are not just talking about the word, you are talking about the place and how you feel there.
