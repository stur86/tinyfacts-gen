A word is a thing you use to talk about things.

When you say something is the same as everyone else, you use this word. You say your word is the same as someone else because they say it too. They are both telling the truth.

If you say something is not like everyone else, you are wrong. You have used the word in a way that does not mean it.

Sometimes, the word is used to talk about how things look. You say a thing is normal. You say it is like how other people see the thing. If you see a thing that does not look normal, you might call it strange.

You can also use this word for how things work. If something is the way that is normal, you say it is the same. If something is different, you say it is different.

We use this word every day. We use it to talk about how things are, how people act, and how things look.

Think about your day. You wake up. You go to school. You eat breakfast. You go to work. You come back home. You watch a movie. You go to bed. This is your day. Your day is the same as everyone else. You are like them.

So, an ordered thing is the way things are. It is the way the world works. It is the way we live.
