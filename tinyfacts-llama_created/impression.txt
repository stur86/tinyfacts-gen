To make an idea from what you see or hear is to get an idea. You can see a picture or a movie and your mind gets a feeling about it. You might feel happy if you see a bright, happy picture. You might feel sad if you see a picture of a person you love being very sad.

You can also hear a sound. If you hear a loud noise, your heart might jump. If you hear a song you love, you might want to move your body. The idea of the sound is inside you. It is what makes you move or feel a way.

When you see or hear something, you make an idea inside your mind. This idea is what you think about. It might be a feeling, a story, a picture, a song, or a memory from your life. Every time you see or hear something new, you are making a new idea.

Think about a game with a ball. You see the ball move. You hear the sound of the ball hitting the ground. You see the ball go up. You might feel excited because you think the team is going to winding. This is making an idea. You are making a picture in your mind of what is happening.

You can also make an idea when you write. If you write a story, you are making a picture in your mind. You are telling a story about what happens. This is making an idea. Every time you write, you are making a new picture in your mind.

Making an idea is important. It helps us understand the world. It helps us remember things. It helps us feel things. It helps us understand how things are. When you have many ideas, you can learn a lot.
