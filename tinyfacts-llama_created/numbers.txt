Numbers are things that help us tell how many things are there. They are the number before you when you start a story.

One number is one. You can counters one thing. If you have one thing, you are done.

Two numbers are two. You can see two fingers on a hand. Three numbers are three. You can see three blocks in a box.

Four numbers are four. You can see four lines in a paper. Five numbers are five. You can see five dogs in a park.

Numbers help us do many things. We use them to write numbers in a book. We use them to help a doctor fix a broken body. We use them to play games with friends.

Numbers are all around us. We see numbers on a wall. We see numbers in a book. We see numbers in our body when we breathe. We see numbers in the sky when the sun is high.

Numbers are like a road. Each number is a step on the road. Each step tells us how many steps are left to go. If you reach the end of the road, you are done.
