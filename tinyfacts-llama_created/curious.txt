To be a good friend means you care about what others think. You want to know what makes them happy or sad. You might listen to what they say or ask them questions. You want to understand them and make them feel safe.

Being a good friend is also about being kind. You show you care by being there for them when they need you. You might help them when they are in trouble or make them laugh. You show you trust them.

Sometimes, you might be a little bit different. You might like things that other people do not. You might like to read books or watch movies. You might be a little bit weird. That is okay. You can still be a good friend. People need friends who are a little bit different too.

We are all different. Some of us like the bright sun, while others like the dark night. Some like to play with colors, while others like to play with numbers. But no matter how different we are, we all need friends to be happy.
