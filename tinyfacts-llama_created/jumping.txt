To jump is to go very fast and then stop on the ground without hitting it. You use your legs and your arms. When you jump, you go up in the air.

You can jump when you are happy. You might jump up and down when you see a fun thing. Or you might jump to get away from a person who is following you. Sometimes you jump when you are afraid. Your heart is going fast and you feel a lot of fear.

People also jump when they are very tall. If you are very tall, you can jump over a small wall. You can jump over a high wall while standing on the ground.

It is good to jump when you are young. You can jump high and land on the ground without hurting yourself. If you jump too much, you might break a leg. But when you jump at first, it is fun and you feel strong.
