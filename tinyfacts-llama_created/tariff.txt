Imagine you are buying food from a store. The store has to pay money to the city. If the city says, the store has to pay a lot of money, the store might not want to buy from them anymore. This means the store might go to a different city to buy things. Or, the store might just close down and not buy anything at all. This is how the money people use in places like cities and big cities helps people.
