To show someone a picture of a place or a person can be called a picture. But when you show a picture to someone, you are not telling them the whole story. You are only telling them how the picture looks. The story is in your head.

Think of it like this. Imagine you are in a room. You see a picture of a tree on the wall. You know the tree is tall and has a big round top. You know the tree is in the room because you saw it. But you do not know the room. You do not know how the tree got there. You do not know if the tree is in the city or the woods. You only know the picture.

A person can be a picture of a city. They do not know the city. They only know a picture of it. They do not know how the people are dressed or how many cars are on the street. They only know the picture.

A picture can be of a person. The person may be happy or sad. They may be with friends. They may be standing in front of a big building. The picture tells you nothing about their life. You do not know what they do or what they like. You only know they are in the picture.

A picture can also be of a place. It tells you where it is. You know it is in a far away land with a cold cold water and tall green trees. You know it is near a big city. You know it is not near your city.

A picture is only a thing you see. It does not tell you anything about the story. You must listen to the story in your head.
