To have a high or very good thing means you can do many things. If you can speak many words, you have a strong voice. If you can run fast, you are strong. If you can write many books, you are good at writing.

Think of a small animal. The animal is not very strong. It can not lift big things. But if the animal has a small hard thing on its back, it can carry heavy things. That small hard thing is its strong part. It helps the animal move around.

People have many strong parts too. Some have very hard skin. Some have very strong arms and hands. Some can hear very well. They are very good at what they do. They are the ones who can do things that help us.

You can have a high or very good thing in many ways. You might have a high high or very good high. You might have a high good high. You might have a high small high. You might have a high very good small high. Or you might have a high very good very small high.
