A new thing is something that is not like anything else before. It is a thing you have just made.

Imagine you have a small ball. You throw it. It goes far across the room. Now, if you throw the ball again, it will go farther than the first time. This is because the ball has changed. It has become stronger.

A new car is something like this. It is a car that you have never seen before. It is a new way of moving from one place to another. It is a new way of getting from one stop to another.

You also have something new in your body. This is when your body gets a tiny piece of hair or a tiny bit of skin. This makes you look different and feel different. You can feel when your hair starts to grow.

Sometimes we give something new to a baby. This is when a man gives a baby a thing that is not from his family. This makes the baby a new person.

When you say something is new, you mean it has not been made before. It has not been done before. It is a new way of doing things.
