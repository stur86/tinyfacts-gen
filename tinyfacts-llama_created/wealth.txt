To have money is to have many things you want. It is what you are left with after you pay your school money, pay for food, and pay for other things you need.

A person can be very well off. They live in a big house with many rooms. They have many cars. They can buy many things they want. They can buy new clothes, nice food, and things to play with. A well off person has a lot of money.

But a person can also be very well off in other ways. They may have a great job that pays a lot. They can buy a nice car or a big house. They can give money to their friends and family. They can buy things they love. They can have many good friends. This is also a way to be well off.

In some places, money is not just about having a lot of money. You can also have a lot of friends. You can have many people who like you. You can have many people who help you. You can have a big group of friends. This is a kind of well off too.
