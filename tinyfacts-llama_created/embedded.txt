When you say something is not on the outside, but is inside something else, it means it is there in a way that is hard to see. It is part of the thing.

Think about a rock. The rock is a hard thing that you can hold in your hand. But inside the rock, there is a tiny bit of water. This tiny bit of water is not hard, it is soft. It is small enough to be kept inside the rock. This tiny bit of water is not on the outside of the rock, so it is not seen by your eyes. It is only there because the rock is holding it inside.

You can also say that a person is not on the outside, but is inside a group. This group is a set of people who share a feeling or a way of thinking. The person might like to dance or make music. They might not say much, but they show that feeling through what they do. They are not seen by the people outside the group, but the group sees them and feels the same way.

Sometimes, something is inside you. You feel a bad feeling for a long time. You try to fight it, but it is strong. You feel like you want to hide or run away. You are not seeing the world, you are seeing the bad thing inside yourself. You are not seeing the world at all.

A tree is also a kind of hidden thing. It grows in the ground. It grows from a small part of the ground that is not seen. It takes in the air and the water around it. It uses the light from the sun and makes food from the air. It grows big and strong. You do not see it growing, but it is there, inside the ground, doing its thing.

These are a few ways to think about a thing that is not on the outside. It is part of something else. It is hidden inside.
