To burn is to make something turn into fire. You can burn food, a thing that gives you power, or a thing that is old and must be thrown away.

When you burn food, you put the food in a fire. The fire is made by hot air and small bits from the food. As the food goes into the fire, it gets very hot and starts to change. The food turns into a hard, bright, and hot piece. This is what we call burned food.

You can also burn things that are made of wood. A fire can burn wood very fast. The wood gets hot and turns into air. The air goes out and the wood falls to the ground.

To burn a thing means to make it go up in the air and then fall down. This happens with many things that burn in the fire. It can be a thing that is old and must be thrown away, a thing that gives you power, or a thing that is broken and must be fixed.

Sometimes, when you burn, you see bright colors. This can happen if you burn things that have small bits of bright colors. The bright colors go into the air and come out as light. We see these colors when we look at a bright fire or a bright piece of paper after it has been burned.

Burn is also a word for to feel very angry or sad. If you feel very angry, you might want to burn things. You might throw a chair or a book at people who are mean to you. This is a way of showing that you are very much against what is wrong.

In some places, burning is done for fun. People build a tall thing in the air and let it burn. They watch as the fire goes higher and higher. Some people also burn things to remember a person who died.

In another way, burning is also about change. When you burn something, you are changing it. You are taking it and turning it into something new. This can happen with your body too. When you feel a strong pain, your body starts to burn. Your body is changing to make it stronger.
