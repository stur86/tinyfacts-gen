A computer is a thing you use to do many things at once. It is like a very big box with a computer inside. You can sit in front of it and it does all the work for you. Some of these computers are small and can hold only one thing on the hard drive. They are very good for doing work. You can use them to write papers, look at pictures, or play music.

A different kind of computer is bigger. These are often found in homes or schools. They have many more things on the hard drive and can do many more things at once. You can use them to do many things at the same time. They are good for watching movies, playing games, or playing music. Some people use them all day.

A computer can also talk to other computers. When this happens, the computers can share ideas. It is like having many friends in your brain. You can share ideas with other people who have computers. This is very important for work and for learning new things.

Some people also have a small computer that they carry around. These are called phones. You can use these to look at pictures, watch movies, or talk to people. They are very good for staying in touch with friends.
