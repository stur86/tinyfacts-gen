To buy something with money is to pay for it with your money. When you want a new thing that you need to live, you go to the store and show the money you have. The store person looks at the money and says if it is the right kind of money and if you have enough money to pay for the thing.

Sometimes, the money is in your pocket. Other times, it is in a bag or in a box with many numbers on it. You use this number to pay for a thing. The store person takes the money and the number from you.

Money is important because it allows us to buy things we want and need. It also helps us buy food and clothes. Without money, we could not buy the food we need to stay strong. We can not buy a house to live in if we do not have money.
