To escape something is to get away from it. It can mean many different things.

When you are in a room with a person you do not like, you might feel scared. If you find a way out of the room, you are escaping. You are leaving the room and going to a different place. This is a good thing.

Imagine you are in a very hot car. The car is moving very fast. You feel the hot air on your skin. If you get out of the car, you are escaping the hot air and the car. You are leaving the hot air behind.

Sometimes, a person might try to escape from a place they do not want to be in. They might run away or hide. This can be very hard and sometimes very sad.

You can also escape from a problem. If you are in a room with a big problem, you might find a way out. You might think of a plan and do it. You are getting away from the problem. This is a good feeling.

To escape is to move away. It is to go somewhere else. It is to leave behind what is wrong. It is to start a new life.
