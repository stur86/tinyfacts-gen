To make something bigger is to make it larger. Imagine you have a small ball. You can hold it in your hand. Now imagine you put a small rock on top of the ball. The ball feels heavy and the rock feels heavy too. The ball is still the same, but the rock is on top of it, so the ball looks bigger.

When you put more and more small things on top of a ball, the ball gets bigger and bigger. The things on top are like tiny pieces of stuff that cover the top of the ball. As the things get bigger, the ball gets bigger too.

You can also make something bigger using things you make. If you use a lot of wood to make a table, the table becomes bigger. The wood covers more of the floor and the walls. The table is not the same as the floor and the walls.

Sometimes, making something bigger can be good. It can mean you can hold more things or you can make a room that is bigger. But sometimes, making something bigger can mean it becomes too heavy or it falls down.

People use this word to talk about how things change. When you make something, you are changing it. You are making it new.
