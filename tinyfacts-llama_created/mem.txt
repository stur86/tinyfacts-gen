Memories are things that help you remember things. When you learn something, you put it into your brain. Later, when you want to remember it, your brain looks back on the thing you learned. Your brain uses the same words and pictures from the past to do it.

Memories are very important. If you forget something important, you might feel very sad or confused. But when you remember a happy time or a fun day, you feel happy again.

You can remember things in many ways. You might write them down in a book or draw them in a picture. You might listen to someone tell you the story over and over again, so it becomes easy to remember. Or you might just remember them by heart.
