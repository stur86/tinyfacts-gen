A step is a small, hard thing you walk on. You step when you go from one place to another. A person might step on a floor, a table, or a wall. It helps you move from one spot to another.

Imagine you are in a room with many chairs. You want to get to the chair that is far away. You do not just move forward. You step from one chair to the next. You are moving from one place to another.

You can step in many ways. You can step on your foot to get up. You can step on a rock to go across a small water. You can step into a room to find a door. Or you can step into a bed to get ready for bed.

A step is also about taking a step forward. When you step forward, you are moving your body. You are moving your feet away from one place and toward another. This is what happens when you walk, run, or jump.

You need to step when you walk. If you do not step, you will not get from one place to another. You need to step to feel the ground under your feet. You need to step to move your body.

You can step in a group with many people. When everyone steps together, it makes a line. The people step in a line so that everyone can see the one in front of them. This is how we move through the world.
