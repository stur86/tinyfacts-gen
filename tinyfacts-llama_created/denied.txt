To be blocked means you can not go where you want. It happens when you try to go into a place that is not for you. The walls or the doors are closed. You are not allowed to enter. You might try to push or try to run around the walls, but it is not safe.

Sometimes, people are blocked by other people. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are happy and you are ready to play. But then, your friend says, you are not allowed to play. They say, you are the one who breaks the game. You feel angry and sad.

You can also be blocked by things that are not real. If you try to fly to a place that is far away, your body might feel afraid. It is like your body is saying, you are not going to do it. You might also feel angry because you do not want to go.

Sometimes, you are blocked because you are afraid. You do not want to do something. You feel like you should be doing something else. You are afraid to go into a room that makes you feel sick. You do not want to go near a thing that you think is going to hurt you.

You can not block the sun. The sun is always there, even when you are not looking at it. But if you try to block it, nothing happens. The sun keeps going around the world.

You can also block your own ideas. If you try to think something is a good idea, but you feel it is not, you might block it. You might push your ideas away like you do not want them.

To be blocked means you are not allowed to do what you want. It is like being stopped in a way that does not let you move. You might feel sad, angry, or afraid. But sometimes, it is a good thing. It keeps you safe or helps you understand what is not right.
