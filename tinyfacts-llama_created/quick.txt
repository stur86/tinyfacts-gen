To be quick means you act fast and move quickly. You do this when you need to get to a place before other people. You might be running to catch a bus or a dog. This helps you stay safe and get to where you need to go.

Think about a dog. Dogs are quick. They can run very fast and catch small animals. They also help keep people safe by being on the road and watching for people who might try to hurt them.

When you are quick, you are not slow. You are doing things before other people. It is a good thing to be quick because it shows you are ready and can act fast.

You can also use this word to talk about things. If you have a lot of work to do, you might feel quick. This means you are doing your work fast and you are able to get it done quickly.

Sometimes people use the word quickly for things that are not really quick. They might say, like, a big thing happens quickly, but the big thing is not really quick. It might take a long time to happen. This word is not always clear.
