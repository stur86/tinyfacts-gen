To make a wrong move in a game or a fight is to do something that is not right. This is called a wrong turn. You might have been trying to do something and you did it the wrong way. Your body might have moved in a way that was not what you wanted.

It is also to put the wrong thing in the place where it should be. Imagine you are making a bed and you put a book on the floor instead of on the bed. You did something wrong. This is what a person means when they say they made a wrong move.

Sometimes people make a wrong turn because they are afraid. They might think something bad will happen if they do what they think is right. Or they might try to do something but they do not know how, and they end up doing the wrong thing.

It is also important to remember that everyone makes wrong turns sometimes. Even you can have a wrong turn if you are walking and you do not see the stop sign. But if you learn from your wrong turn and you try to do things right next time, you can become a better person.
