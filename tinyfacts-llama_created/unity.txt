To be one is to be as one. It is when people, animals, and things all join together to make one group. This can happen when you decide to work with someone or when two animals live together in the same home.

You can also be one with your body. If you hurt your foot, you might feel pain. But if you take care of your foot, you can feel better. You are one with your body because your foot is part of you.

Being one with the world is also an idea. Imagine you are sitting in a green field. You do not see the sun or the trees. You are not alone. You are part of the green world around you. You are one with the world.

Sometimes, being one with something can be hard. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might be one with fear. You feel like the animal is going to hurt you. But you can also use this feeling to learn how to be strong.

In life, we often try to be one with others. We might try to be nice to everyone. We want everyone to like us. This can make us feel sad if people do not like us. But it is also important to be one with the people you care about. When we are with people we love, we feel like we are home.

Being one is not always easy. Sometimes, you have to let go of who you are to be with others. You might have to share your things or give up your ideas. But if you are one with the world or with others, you are more sure. You know you are not alone and that you can work together.
