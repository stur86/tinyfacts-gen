A hundred years is a very long time. You can stand in front of a big house for a hundred years. You can see many children grow up and get married. You can see a whole group of families live in the same house for a hundred years.

A hundred years is also a big number. If you have a hundred small numbers, one after the other, they are all the same number. This is why we say a hundred years. It is a very long time, but it is also a small time. You can do many things in a hundred years.

Sometimes, people use a hundred years to talk about a time that is very far away. They might say, this is a hundred years from now. This means that a hundred years from now is a very far time. It is not a short time, but a very long time.

You can also use the word in a different way. Imagine you have a hundred dogs. If you keep all the dogs together, the dogs are all the same dog. If you move them to different houses, they become different dogs. This is what happens when you counters a hundred. You start with one dog and then you add one more dog every day for a hundred days. After a hundred days, you have two hundred dogs. After another hundred days, you have three hundred dogs. And so on.
