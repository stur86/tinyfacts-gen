To do something means to use up your time and your money. When you drive a car, you do this every day. You go to the city, you go to the park, and you go back home. This is your job. You are doing something to get from one place to another.

One way to think about it is that if you drive a car for a long time, you might go  how many hundred feet? If you do this many hundreds of times, you have done a lot of work. You have done a long way. You have used up a lot of time.

To do something also means to show how far you have gone. Imagine you are on a road with many many other roads. You drive along one road for a long time and then you turn to another road. The road you are on is your way, and the road you turned to is your other way. You have done a lot of work on one road, but you are still on a different road. This is the idea of doing something.

Sometimes, you do something to help others. You drive to a place where a doctor is waiting for you. You drive to a place where a teacher is waiting for you. You do this so that the person you are going to see can help them. You are doing something to help.

We also do this when we buy things. If you buy a book, you are doing something to your life. You are getting a new thing that will help you learn. You are doing something to your life so that you can be happy later.

So, doing something means to use up your time and your money, and it can also mean to show how far you have gone, and it can mean to help others or get new things.
