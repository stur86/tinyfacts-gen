To show someone something means to act in a way that shows you believe it. You are not just talking about what you think. You are doing it so that others can see.

Imagine you want to tell a child to stop being loud. You might say,  Stop making so much noise. You are not just saying the word. You are actually making a sound with your body. You are moving your arms and your face so that the child can see that you mean it.

You can show how to do something by doing it yourself. If you want to show how to tie a shoe, you should do it first. This is the best way to show because you are doing it from start to finish. You are not just saying,  Look at me, I am doing this. You are really showing them.

Sometimes you show how to do something by showing how it feels. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might be quiet and hide. You are showing them that you are afraid. This shows them that you care. You are not just telling them. You are letting them know.

You can also show how to do something by showing the way it is done. If you want to show how to write, you should write. You are not just talking about writing. You are doing it. You are writing the words on a piece of paper. This is the best way to show because you are doing it all at once.

Showing is important because it helps people understand. If you are trying to tell a story, you are showing the story. If you are trying to show someone how to do something, you are showing them how.
