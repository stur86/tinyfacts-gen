They tell the person they are going to be there for a few hours. The person then goes into the room and makes sure everything is ready.

You can also do this for a very good thing. If you want to sit in a place with a lot of people, you might ask a school if you can sit there for the day. The school looks at the papers you give them and decides if you should be there. If they say yes, you get a seat.
