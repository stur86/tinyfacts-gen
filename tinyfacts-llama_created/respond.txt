To respond means you give an answer to what someone else said. You use your words to tell them how you feel. This can happen many times in a day. Maybe you are talking to a friend. You might say something and your friend responds with their own words.

You can also respond to a problem. If you see a big dog on the street, you might say,  I am scared. This shows you are afraid. Your friend might say,  You are right. If you are afraid, you should go home and be safe. This helps everyone feel better.

Sometimes, you need to respond to a question. When a teacher asks you a question, you must think about the answer. You are the one answering the question. You are the one who talks back.

You can also respond to a joke. If someone says,  Why did the cat cross the road? You might say,  To get to the other side. This is your way of playing along with the joke.

To respond is important because it helps you stay in the same place. If you do not respond, you might get confused or angry. But when you answer, you show you care.
