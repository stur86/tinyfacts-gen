Phones are the sounds we make. When you say the word, all the sounds are different. If you say the word, the word is different. But if you only hear the sounds, you do not know what the word is. This is because each sound is made by moving parts in your body in a very different way.

Think of it like this. If you say the word, the air from your mouth goes into your throat, and your tongue moves. The tongue is like a long, round thing that goes over the hard part in your mouth. This makes a sound. If you say a different word, the air goes into the throat in a different way. The tongue does not move as much. The sound is the same, but it is not the same word.

When you are talking, you use the sounds in your head. You move your lips, your tongue, your teeth. You do not just say the word. You say the sound. The sound is the word inside your head. This is why you can say a word and it is not the same word as before.

There are many different sounds. You can hear the sound of a car, a dog, a tree. Each sound is like a different word. The same sound can be the word of many different things. The sound of a door is the same sound as the word of a house.

The word you are talking about is a word that has many sounds inside it. If you know all the sounds, you can build the word from those sounds. But if you only know the word, you can only hear those sounds. You need to know how the sounds fit together to build the word.
