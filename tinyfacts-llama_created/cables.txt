A long line of many small parts held together is what a power line is. These lines carry power from a place with lots of power to many houses or schools. They are under the ground. Some are very long and go all the way to the city and then to your home. Other ones are much shorter and go only a few blocks.

To make these long lines work, we use many tiny parts. Some are very strong and can hold a lot of power. Some are very light and can carry almost as much power as a strong one. Some parts are very small and fit inside the line. They make sure the power is not going to the wrong place.

We use these lines to send power to many places. When you turn on a light, a computer, or a phone, the power comes from a line that is very far away. It is safe to be near these lines because the power is very strong. But we still need to be carefully when we walk near them because we do not want to get hurt.

Covers are also about long lines. They are very strong and hold a lot of power. They go from a power place to many houses. They are very safe, but we still need to be carefully when we walk near them.
