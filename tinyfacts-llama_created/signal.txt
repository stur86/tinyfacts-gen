A sign is a word or a picture that tells you something important. You can use a sign to tell someone you are coming. You might hold up a sign with a word that says,  I am here. Or you might use a sign to tell a child that the door is open.

Sometimes a sign is a word or a picture that a person makes to get your attention. If you hear a loud noise and you think it is a problem, you might use a sign to tell the person who is in a different room that there is a problem. They will stop doing what they are doing and listen to you.

You can also use a sign to tell a group of people what to do. A police sign is a sign that says,  Stop. People follow the sign so everyone can stay safe.

In a place where people work, a sign is a word or a picture that tells people what to do. It might say,  Go to the next room. It helps everyone know what is happening and what they should do.

Sometimes signs are made from paper or from hard stuff that can break. A sign from paper is usually written so you can read it. A sign from hard stuff can break if you touch it too much. But even a broken sign can still be important because it tells you something.
