The word would is used to talk about what will happen next. When you say you would go to the store, you are saying you plan to go there later. You do not know when you will go. You just want to happen to be there. The word is like a promise.

You can also use it to talk about things that are coming. If you say you would eat dinner, you mean you plan to eat it soon. It is a way to say you are looking forward to it.

Sometimes, you might use the word to tell others you are not going to do something. If you say you would go to the movies, but you decide to stay home, you have broken your promise. You did not follow through.

The word is important because it helps us plan our day. If you say you would finish your work, you can check if you have done it. If you did not, you need to figure out what you need to do next.
