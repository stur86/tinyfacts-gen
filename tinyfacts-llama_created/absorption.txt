To accept something is to take it into your body or mind. You can do this with food. When you eat, your body takes the food and changes it so it can be used. You feel full after you eat.

You can also accept a problem. If someone is being mean, you might want to leave. But if you stay and accept the mean words, you are showing that you are not afraid. You are letting the mean person know that their words are not right.

When you are under water, you can feel the water around you. You are not seeing the world because your eyes are in the water. You are in your body. This is how you feel when you are in a deep place.

In a game, a team might give a person a ball. The person does not touch the ball. They just let it go. They are not holding the ball. They are letting the ball move through them.

Sometimes, we let something into our mind. We might see a picture that is not really there. This can be because our mind is busy with other things. It is like our brain is doing something else while we are looking at a picture.

If you drink something, it goes into your body. It changes who you are. If you drink too much, you might feel sick. Your body is saying, this is too much for me.
