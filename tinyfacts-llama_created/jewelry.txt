Jacket is a thing you wear on your back. It covers your chest and arms. It is usually made of hard stuff like glass, wood, or bright white stuff that looks very nice. You can wear a jacket with your pants and a shirt. It helps keep your clothes warm when it is cold outside.

People wear jackets all the time, even when it is warm. Some jackets are very small and hang on your neck. Others are very big and cover your whole body. Some jackets have many long lines that go down from your neck to your feet. Others have a long line that goes across your chest.

A jacket can also have a name. You might see a man wearing a green jacket with a long line. Or you might see a girl wearing a pretty blue jacket with a funny picture on it. Sometimes, people wear a jacket to show they are friends. If you see a group of friends walking together, they might all wear the same jacket.

You can also buy a jacket from a store. These stores make many different kinds for people of all ages. You can get a jacket for a baby, a small kid, a tall person, or a very tall person. The jacket is usually made of soft stuff and has many soft lines around it. When you wear a jacket, you feel safe and happy.
