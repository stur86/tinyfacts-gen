To be strange is to do something that is not like the way things are. It is a word that we use to talk about things that are not usually seen.

Think of a cat. Cats are small animals that walk and give soft sounds. They are not like dogs and horses. They are not like animals that fly. But they are still animals. You can still say that a cat is strange if it does something that is different from how other animals do.

If you go to a place where no one speaks, and you start talking, people might look at you in surprise. This can make you feel strange because you are doing something that is not usual.

Sometimes things are strange because they are new. If you have never seen a star before, you might feel strange. It is a big thing in the sky that looks like a ball. You have never seen it before, so it is new to you.

It can also mean that something is different in a way that is not clear. If your friend says they want to go to a movie, but they are really going to a game, it is strange. You do not know what they are talking about.

We also say a person is strange if they act in a way that is not like how we think. If a teacher has a funny way of talking, or if a person in a movie is very different from what we expect, this can be strange.

So, being strange is when you do something that is different from what is usual. It can mean you are new, or you are doing something that is not clear. It is a way to say that something is not like other things.
