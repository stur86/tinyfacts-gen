To reach something means you are getting close to it. When you walk or run, you reach the middle of the street. You might reach your house before you finish your walk. You are getting closer and closer.

You can reach something in your body. Your heart reaches for air when you want to breathe. Your eyes reach to look at a picture. Your head reaches to touch a book. Your feet reach to walk on the ground. Your hands reach to hold things.

Sometimes, people reach for things they want. If you want a piece of food, you reach for it. You do not have to walk a long way. You can just walk up to it. This is an easy way to get what you need.

You can also reach things that are not on the ground. If you fly, you reach the sky. If you jump from a high place, you reach the ground far away. This is a big way to go up into the air.

When you reach something, you are almost there. You might feel a little nervous. You are getting very close. You can feel the ground under your feet. You are getting ready to stop and stand still.

To reach something can be about life too. If you want to learn a new thing, you reach for it. You study. You listen to books. You watch movies. You try to learn. You are getting closer to knowing.

So, reaching is about getting close. It is about feeling the ground and being ready to stop. It is about wanting something and working hard to get it.
