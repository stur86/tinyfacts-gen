A memories is a thing that holds memories. It is a story or a word that keeps you thinking about a past time. People write them so others know what happened. They might write about a fight or a new day. They use these things when they want to remember something important.

You can also make a memories in your head. You might think about a place you visited. You can remember the food you ate and the people you met. When you want to remember something, you can write it down in your brain. This is how you keep your memories.

Memories are like small pieces of a big picture. Each one is a tiny part, but together they help you understand the whole story. When you hear a story, you do not just remember the words. You remember the sound of the voice, the feeling of the words on your skin, and the way it makes you feel.
