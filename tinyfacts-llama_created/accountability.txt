To hold someone or something to be in the right and to answer for what you did is to be held as a person or thing. When you do something wrong, you are held. This means you are not just the person doing it. You are also the person who made you do it.

Imagine you are a small child. You break a glass. The parents are the ones who have to hold you. They are the ones who said you could not touch the glass. They are still in the same house. They still have to tell the truth about what happened. They are not just the child. They are the ones who made the child do it. They are the ones who have to answer for it.

You can also hold someone in a place. If you are very bad at school, you can be held in a place to learn. You might stay in a room with other people who are also in trouble. They are all in the same place because they are all in the same trouble.

Sometimes, a person who has done something bad has to answer for it. If a teacher makes a student do work they do not want to do, the teacher has to say, they did it. If a parents does not do what they say is right, the parents have to answer for it. This is how we learn to live with each other.

It is also important to hold people who are in power. If someone is doing bad things and they do not stop, the people who are in power should answer. They should not just say, nothing happened. They should say, we did it. We are not letting bad things happen anymore. This is how we keep our world safe.
