Leaves are things we grow from the ground. They are the parts of a tree that hang from the top of the tree. They are green and look like long pieces of food.

Most trees grow leaves in the summer. During this time, the leaves are very big and strong. They make food for the tree. The food gives the tree power to stay standing and to make more leaves. This is why the tree looks tall and green.

In the fall, the leaves change color. Some leaves are red, some are brown, and some are green with white spots. The colors are like a big show. The trees are getting ready for the cold months.

During the fall, the leaves fall down to the ground. Some fall to the ground and cover the ground, making it look like a big green match. Other leaves fall into the water in the ground. The water picks up the leaves and carries them away.

In the cold months, the tree goes into a sleep. The green color goes away, and the tree starts to look brown and dry. The tree is waiting for the right time to grow new leaves again.

Leaves are very important. They help keep the air clean. They take in good air and give out bad air. They also help make the ground soft for the feet in the cold months.

You can see leaves on the ground in many places. In the ground, they cover the ground. On the ground, they make the ground look like a big green match. On trees, they hang from the top of the tree. On trees, the big parts of the leaves are usually the top parts.

Leaves are also a sign of the time of year. In the summer, they are new and green. In the fall, they are old and brown. In the cold months, they are brown and dry.
