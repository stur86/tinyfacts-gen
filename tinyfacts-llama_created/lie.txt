To lie means you tell a story that is not true. When you lie, you are not telling the truth. You are making up a story or a set of words to make it seem like you did something that you did not do.

Imagine you say you are going to the store, but you are not. You are going to do something else. You are lying to the person who asks you. You are making a story that is not real. You are not being open about what you are doing.

Sometimes people lie when they are afraid. They might tell a story to hide their true feeling. They might say they love someone when they really do not. They are afraid the other person will find out.

Another time, you might lie to get something. You might say you have a job when you do not. You might say you are a student. You are trying to get a job so you can pay for things you need. You are not being true to yourself.

To lie is to break the trust. When you lie, you are not being kind to the person you are talking to. You are breaking the trust in the relationship.

There is also a word that is about lying. It means you do not tell the truth. This word is used when you are not telling the truth. You are lying about something.
