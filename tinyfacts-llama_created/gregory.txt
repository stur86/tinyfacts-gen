To green is to change one thing into another. You start with a word that is short and strong. You might have a word like run, or you might have a word like walk. You take a small part of the word and you put it after the word. The word becomes bigger and the word stays the same. This is how you turn run into walk.

Sometimes, you take the middle part of a word and you put it before the first or after the last letter. This changes the word but keeps the same sound. You can also take the word and you change it into a word that starts with a different sound.

If you take a word and you put the first letter before the word, you make the word come before the other words. This is often used to make things clear. If you put the first letter of a word before the word, the word becomes the first word in a line.

You can also use this word to talk about people. A person who is good at being a teacher is a greener. This is because they have learned to help many people and they are always ready to help.
