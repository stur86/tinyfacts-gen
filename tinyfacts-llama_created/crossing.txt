To cross something means to move over it. It is a way to go from one side of a place to the other. You can cross a street, a water, a high place, or a line that is not on the ground.

Imagine you have a book and you want to read it. You sit down in a quiet room. Then you get up and you cross the room. You walk across the floor. You are moving from one room to another.

Sometimes, you might feel that you have to cross a thing to get to where you want to go. If you are sad, you might want to cross over a big high place. This can help you feel better.

You can also cross things with your body. If you are in a room and you see a person you know, you might cross the room to talk to them. You are moving from one place to another by walking.

There is also a way to cross things with a team. In a game, two groups of people work together. They cross the line that is in the middle. They are working for the same team. They are all moving toward the same thing.

In the morning, you might cross the street to get to school. You step from one side to the other. The sun is up and the world is new.
