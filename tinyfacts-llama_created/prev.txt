The word is short. It is a word that starts with a word that is the same as our word for before. So it is like a word that says you are getting close to something.

You can use it to talk about what is coming up. You might say, I have a problem before I go to bed. Or you can say, I am going to the park before I go to dinner.

It can also mean a thing that happens before another thing. If you see a picture, then you see a story. The story happens before the picture.

Sometimes you use it to talk about how you feel. If you are feeling sad, you might say you feel like you are going to cry before you get ready to go out. Or if you are afraid, you might say you feel afraid before you go into a room.

It is a word that is easy to remember. You can just say it once and you know what it means.
