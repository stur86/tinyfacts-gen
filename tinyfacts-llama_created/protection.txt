To be a person who keeps you safe is to be a friend. You help each other when the world is bad.

Imagine you are a child. Your family is going to a place where many people live together. You do not know what will happen. But you have a grown man or woman who lives in that place. They tell you what to do and where to go if things get wrong. They keep you safe.

This is what a doctor does. They help people get better when they are hurt. They keep them safe so they do not have to be in pain any more.

You can also be a person who keeps your things safe. If you have money, a lock on your door helps. If you have a book, you keep it in a safe place so you do not lose it.

Some people work in places that help people who are in trouble. They are like a team of friends. They talk to the people, help them, and then they go away so the people are safe again.

Everyone needs to feel safe. When you feel safe, you can be happy. It is a feeling that comes from knowing you are not alone and that you can trust the world around you.
