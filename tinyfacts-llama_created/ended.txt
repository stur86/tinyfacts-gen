When something ends, it means it is finished. This can happen to many things in your life.

Think of a book. You read a story. You know the end of the book. You do not need to keep reading anymore. The story is over. You can close the book and feel a sense of calm.

You can also end a time. If a day ends, it is over. The sun is up and the light is strong. Then you can go to bed and start a new day.

If you end a game, you have won't be able to play it anymore. You did what you wanted, and you are done.

Sometimes, ending something is hard. Maybe you wanted to stay in a place where people were nice, but you had to leave. You felt sad because you loved that place. The end of the time is very hard to accept.

But often, ending is also a good thing. When you stop doing something, you can start doing other things. You might have a new plan or a new idea. This can be very exciting.

So, when you say a thing has ended, you mean it is finished. You can stop doing it, you can close a book, or you can stop playing a game. It is the end of a time, a story, or a moment.
