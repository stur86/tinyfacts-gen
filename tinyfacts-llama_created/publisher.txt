To be a book is to have a story inside you. A person who writes this story is the one who is the book. They decide what things happen and who is the one doing them. They also decide how many people should get to read the story.

The person who keeps this story is the one who is the book. They buy the book. They take it from the store. They put it in their car. They drive to the place where people live. There, the book is given to the person who wants to read it. The person reads the story and then they might give it to others.

The book is not only a story. It can also have pictures. These pictures are like the book is a picture book. They are the ones who are made by a person. They are put together from many small pictures. When many people make many pictures, they can make a book that has lots of pictures.

Some books are for children. Others are for grown people. Some books are for people who want to learn about the world. Others are for people who want to feel happy. Every book is different.
