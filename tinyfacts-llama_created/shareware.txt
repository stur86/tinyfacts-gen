To share something is to let others see it. You can share a book, a song, or a game with your friends. Sometimes, you give a small part of your computer to someone else so they can play your game or listen to your music.

The word share is often used with money too. You might say you share a room with a friend. This means you live together. You do not have your own room, but you share it with the other person.

If you share something, people learn from you. Maybe you show them how to fix a computer. You are helping them.
