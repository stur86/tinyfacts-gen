A huge building where lots of things are kept. It is often for stores that do not have enough space. In it, you can find things you need, but you might also find things you do not want. People use these places for work, to buy things, or to store things for later.

Imagine a place with many rooms. Each room is very big. In the room where you buy food, there are many boxes of food. In the room where you buy clothes, there are many boxes of clothes. If you need something, you can go to the room where it is kept. If you do not need it, you can leave it there.

Sometimes these places are for things people do not need anymore. If you have a big box of old books you no longer want, you can take it to the building. They will keep it for a while until you decide what to do with it.

In some places, people keep things that are very important. They keep money, important papers, or things they need for work. It is like a very big home where many people keep things they need.

It can also mean a place where you can find what you need if you do not know what to look for. If you do not know where to find a book, you can go to a huge building. There you can see many books and ask a person who works there for help.
