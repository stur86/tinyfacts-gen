To send something through the air is to send it through the sky and into other places. This is how we send words and pictures to each other. You can send a word to a friend or a computer. The word goes over the air until it reaches your friend or computer.

You can also send short stories, pictures, or music. These are called short books or pictures. You can send one at a time. Each person can read it or listen to it when they get it.

You can also send things that are not words. If you have a word, you can write it down and send it to a friend. The friend can read it when they open the word. This is how you learn new things and keep your memory.

When you send something, you must tell the computer where it should go. You tell it the name of the friend or computer that should get the word. If you do not tell the computer, the word will not go where it is going. This is why you must be carefully when you send things over the air.
