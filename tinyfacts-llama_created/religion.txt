To believe something is to have a way of thinking about it. Every person has their own way of doing this. Some people say things like this:

Life is very hard. We must act fast.

Life is very hard. We must act fast and stay safe.

If you say this, you are telling people what to think. You are not letting them decide for themselves. You are telling them what to believe.

Most people believe in things that help them. Some of these things are about how to live. Some are about how to feel. Some are about what is real.

Some people believe in a big group that is one way. They follow their leader and do what the leader says. Some people believe in a group that is different. They follow their own leader and do what they say.

There is a thing that is about the person who makes the things. They are the ones who write the books, make the pictures, and put the words together. They are the ones who decide what people believe.

There is also a thing that is about the place. People go to that place to feel safe and to remember the past. They may say words or singling songs when they are there. They may sit on the floor and tell stories.

Some people believe in things that are not real. They might say things like, their family is the most important thing in the world. They might say things like, they are the only ones who know what is true. They might not want to listen to others.

But most people believe in something. They believe in the good things that happen. They believe that things will get better. They believe that people are kind to each other. They believe that life is a story that is still being made.

So, to believe something is to have a way of thinking about it. It is to decide what to think and how to act.
