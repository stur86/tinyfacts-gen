To group things together is to make a group. You can do this with many different kinds of things. You might put many books together. Or you might put many small animals together. This makes it easier to see what is there.

An important word is when you take many small pieces. You put them into one bag. This bag is the group. Inside the bag, you can see all the pieces. The pieces are now a group of one thing.

Sometimes, when many people do the same thing, you get a group of people. This group can be very big. It can be made from many different places. You can also have a group of animals. This group is made of many different animals.

To group things is also how we do with numbers. We can put many numbers into one group. This group can help us find the answers we need. It can help us see if something is true.
