The word pink is a color. It is not red and it is not blue. When light from the sun hits something, and the light is made of all colors, pink is one of those colors. It is the color of a soft, warm light that looks like a warm, bright color.

To make something look pink, you need to add a little bit of a different color. This color is more red than blue. When you add more red than pink, you get a darker color. When you add more blue than pink, you get a lighter color. This is why a pink ball might look different from a red ball.

Pink is also a color that is often used for things that are very young. Babies are usually very pink. They are a very bright color because of how much light is being put into their tiny bodies. As they grow, their skin becomes less pink and they become more like the color of their hair.

You can also make pink by adding red to blue. This is why some soft drinks have pink or red parts. The red makes the drink look more like pink. If you only add blue, the drink might look more like clear water.
