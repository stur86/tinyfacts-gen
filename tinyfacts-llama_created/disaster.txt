A big problem that happens very fast is called a hard situation or a hard time. It is something that you did not plan for.

Imagine you are a child. You play with a ball all day. Then, one day, the ball falls down from the sky and hits you on the head. You are very hurt. This is a hard situation because it makes your life very hard.

Another kind of hard situation is one that happens when many people are very sick at the same time. This is a very bad time for everyone. It is hard because many people are very afraid.

There are also hard situations when many things happen very quickly. A hard time is a time when many things happen at once. This can be very hard to deal with because your brain is busy trying to understand everything.

Hard situations need people to work together. They need people to help each other and to make sure everyone is safe. Sometimes, people try to fix the hard situation on their own. But often, they need help from others or from a group of people who are doing the same work.

A hard situation can also be a time of change. When many things change at once, it can be very hard for people to understand. But it can also be a chance for new ideas and new ways of living.
