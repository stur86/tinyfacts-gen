A place where people go to drink and talk is called a bar. It is a big room with a long table where people sit. There are many people who work there. Some are the ones who clean the floor. Some are the ones who make the drinks. Some are the ones who tell jokes. Some are the ones who play music.

People go there at night to have fun. They drink from cups and glasses. They laugh and smile with other people. They talk about their lives and their friends. Some drink too much and do things they do not want to do. Some go there alone to be alone.

A bar is often near a street. People walk down the street to get there. It is a place where people go to be together.
