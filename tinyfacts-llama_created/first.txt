The word first means the very beginning of something. It is the thing that happens before any other thing. When you have a new day, the first day is the one you wake up to. It is the day before the day that starts later.

Think about a story. If you start the story, you are the first one who gets the word. The first person you meet is the first one you talk to. If a friend says, I love you, it is the first time they ever say that to you.

In a game or a race, the first person is the one who goes first. They are the first one to cross the line. They are the first one to reach the end.

Sometimes, you might say, first, to show that something is the beginning of a line. This means the thing that comes after is not first yet. It is the thing that comes after the first one.

The word first is important because it helps us know where things start. It is a word that helps us remember the beginning of a story, a day, or a line of people.
