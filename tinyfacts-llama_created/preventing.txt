To keep something from happening.

Imagine you have a box of food. You are eating from it every day. One day, a friend comes and takes a small piece of food. The food inside your body starts to get sick. You feel very bad. This happens because you did not have enough food.

If you keep the food inside the box, it will not be taken. You can eat from it again and again. This way, you keep your body strong and your body does not get sick. You can eat many more days without feeling bad.
