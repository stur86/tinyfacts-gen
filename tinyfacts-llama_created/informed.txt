When you are told something, you have become more sure about it. If you do not know something, you are not yet sure. When you are told, you have a new idea, and you start to believe it.

You might be told to be quiet because you are afraid of the loud noise. You might feel a little scared. You were not sure before, but now you know what to expect.

You can also be told to do something. If a teacher says, you must clean your desk, you are told. You did not know you had to do it. You were not sure what you had to do.

Sometimes, you might be given a piece of paper with numbers on it. You do not know what those numbers mean. Later, you learn that the numbers are the hours of the day. You were not sure at first, but now you know the time.

In some places, you might be told to follow a line. You are not sure where the line goes. But after you follow the line, you see where it leads. You were not sure at first, but now you know what to do.

Being told is different from knowing. Knowing is when you understand something. Being told is when you learn something new.
