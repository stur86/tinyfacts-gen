To car is to move a round thing. This round thing goes very fast. It has a box on the front. People use these things to go from one place to another.

Most of these things have a round seat for the person inside. The seat is like a soft bed. The person sits in the seat and feels the road under them. They also have a head and a back for their head. This helps them see the road and stay safe.

The round thing has many parts. One part is the front. It is the part that lets you see. The other part is the back. It is the part that lets you see what is behind you. There is also a part in the middle. It is the part that lets you go fast.

People also put things on the round thing. They put money on the round thing so it can go. They put food on the round thing so it can keep going. They also put their hands on the round thing to control it.

There is a man who built these things a long time ago. He wanted people to be able to go very fast. He made the first round things. Now many more round things have been made. Some are very big and some are very small. Some go very high. Some go very slow.
