To do something and be the best at it. When you do something well, you are in control. You are not afraid. You feel happy and you feel good.

When you try something new and you do it very well, you can say you did it well. You did not make a lot of trouble. You were not afraid of any things. You were able to stay calm and you did not break anything.

If you play a game or work very hard, you might feel happy. You did not get hurt. You did not cry. You were able to do what you wanted to do. You felt good because you were able to do it.

To do something and be the best at it is like being the top of a big rock. There is no one like you. You are the one with the right moves and you use them very well. You are the one who is very strong. You can do many things and you are always safe.
