To feel something is to be inside it. You are the thing. You live it. You know what it is like to be in a cold room or to feel a pain in your hand. You do not just watch it happen. You see it, hear it, feel it, smell it, and think about it. You can tell if something is bright, dark, hot, cold, or quiet.

You can also feel things without thinking about them. You might feel a bit of fear when you hear a loud noise. This happens right away. Your heart might beat faster. Your eyes might close. Your legs might feel like they are going to go.

You can also feel things when you remember them. When you think about a happy time with your family, you feel a smile. You feel happy. You remember the food you ate and the people who were there. You remember the music and the fun.

Sometimes you feel things when you are afraid. You might feel a bit of fear. You might be ready to run. You might feel your legs get tight. Your heart might beat faster. This is your body telling you to be safe.

You can feel things that are not real. You can feel the ground when you walk on a hard floor. You can feel the air when you breathe. You can feel the water when you touch it. You can feel the cold when you are outside at night. You can feel the warm when you are in a warm room.

To be in a place is also to be in a body. You can feel the ground when you walk on a hard floor. You can feel the air when you breathe. You can feel the water when you touch it. You can feel the cold when you are outside at night. You can feel the warm when you are in a warm room. You are inside the place.

When you are with people you love, you feel happy. When you are with people you do not like, you feel afraid. When you are alone, you feel quiet. When you are with yourself, you feel safe. You feel things because you are here and you are a part of it.
