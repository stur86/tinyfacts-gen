To buy something means you use money to get it. When you buy a thing, you are taking it from someone else. The person who made the thing or gave it to you has already made it. You are just taking it away from them.

Imagine you want a book. You see a book that you like. You want to read it. You go to a store. The store has many books. You pick the one you want. Then, the store owner tells you how much money it should be. You pay them that money. After you pay them, you have the book. You do not own it anymore, but you have it.

You can also buy things you do not need. You might go to a store and buy a small thing like a bag or a ball. You do not need it for your life, but you want it because it looks nice or it is fun to have.

Sometimes, buying something is about getting something for your family. You might buy food for your mom or a thing for your brother. You are showing them you care.

Buying can be easy or hard. Sometimes, you have to wait for a long time. You have to wait for the thing to come out of the store. Other times, you can buy something right away. You can buy a thing from a store or a person who works at a store.
