A student is a person who learns from a teacher. They go to a school or a place where they learn. They do not just watch the teacher. They think about what they hear. They write things down, and they talk about them with their friends.

A student has school days. They wake up early and go to a room where they sit with many other people. They sit at a desk and listen to the teacher. The teacher gives them things to read. They do not always listen. They might think about other things.

During school days, students might be asked to do work. They might write papers or draw pictures. They might also do short work. This is so they can show what they know. They might also go to a place where they can talk with the teacher or other students.

When a student is not in school, they might be in other places. They might go to a school in a different town. They might go to a school where they learn about the same things as in their own school. This is called going to school.

A student can be a boy, a girl, or a girl who is a boy. They can be a child or a young person. Sometimes, a student is a person who is learning about a big thing that happens in the world. This is called learning about a big thing.
