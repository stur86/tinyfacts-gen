Tracking means to walk in a way where you keep looking at the ground. It is a word people use to talk about moving around with a lot of looking at the floor.

Imagine you are walking to school. You see a tree. You walk behind it for a bit. Then you look up and see your friend. You walk to them, say hi, and then you keep walking. You do not look at the ground as much as you do when you walk to school.

Sometimes this happens when you are walking home from work. You see a dog. You walk by it for a bit. You look at the dog. Then you keep walking. You do not look at the ground as much as you would if you were walking to work.

This word is used by people who like to walk. They might say, I tracking the road today. It means they are walking in a way that keeps them looking at the ground. It does not mean they are looking at the ground all the time. They are just keeping their eyes on the floor.
