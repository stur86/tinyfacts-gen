The word suggests things.

When you say something, you are telling people about it. A person might say,  This is a great movie.  They are suggesting that the movie is good.

The word also means something that is easy to do. If you are feeling tired, you can just sit down and rest. This is a suggested way to act.

People often use this word to talk about a place. If you go to a town, you might say,  This is a nice town. The word is suggesting that the town is a good place to live.

Sometimes, you might hear a person say,  This is a good idea. This means they think the thing is right. They are suggesting it without being sure.
