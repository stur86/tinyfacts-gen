A word is a thing you use to show what you mean.

When two people do not agree, they have a problem. This is called a fight. It can be very loud. They might shout and push each other. This is not good. It makes it hard to be happy.

Think about your family. You have a mom and a dad. You are a child. You do not have to fight with them. You live in the same house and share food and bed. But sometimes, your friends do not agree too. They might want to play a game. But your friend wants to watch a movie. They do not want to talk. This is also a fight.

Sometimes, people have a fight with the city. The city has many people. They have many jobs and many cars. But another city has more money. They both want the same things. They fight over it. They do not want to share. They want to keep their own money.

Sometimes, people fight with each other. This is a big problem. It can hurt many people. It can make people afraid. It can also make people sad.

Sometimes, people fight with each other that is not about money. They fight because they have different ideas about how to live. They might want to go to a different school. They might want to wear different clothes. They might want to live in a different house. When people have these kinds of fights, it can be very hard to understand what is happening.

We need to talk about these kinds of fights. We need to listen to each other. We need to try to understand. We need to find ways to share.
