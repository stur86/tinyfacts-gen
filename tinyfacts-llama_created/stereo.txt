A group of three things that work together to help us hear sounds better is called a speaker. These things have three parts. One part is a round thing that makes sound when you press a thing. The round thing is very strong and makes a loud sound. The second part is a box with many small round things. When the small round things move back and forward, they make the sound go in a different way. This changes how loud the sound is. The third part is a set of three small things. When the small things go up and down, they help change the sound so we can hear it better.

There is also a thing that has three parts too. This thing is used to watch moving pictures. The round part moves in a way that makes the picture go. The box part has many small round things inside it. The small round things go up and down as the round part moves, making the picture go by quickly. The set of small things inside the box helps the picture look clear and the colors look right.

When you put a speaker in your house or a room, it helps your friends hear you better. If you put a speaker next to a speaker, the sound gets stronger because the two round things are working together. If you put a speaker in the middle of a room, the sound comes out of all the walls, and it is very quiet.
