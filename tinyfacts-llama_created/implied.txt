To mean something is to use your mind to understand a fact without you saying it. You do not say the word, but you show how you think about it. This is different from saying the word out loud. When you say a word, you are telling everyone else what you think.

Think about a game. You are not saying the word, but you show how you act. If you do what the person said, you have meant it. If you do not do what the person said, you have not.

A person might not say a word, but you know from how they act that they mean something. You can be sure. You do not need to say the word. You just see the way they act.

This happens in the world too. When a person says something, they do not always mean it. Sometimes they do not really want to say it. They might be hiding something behind their words. You can see the truth behind their face.

The word can also mean to show a picture in your mind. If you see a picture of a dog and your friend says, you can tell they mean the word. You remember the dog and know what they are talking about.

It is important to remember that to mean something is not always the same as to say the word. You can be sure about a thing without saying it. But you need to know that you are sure so you can act on it.
