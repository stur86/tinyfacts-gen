To mumble is to talk very quietly. You might do it when you are trying to sleep. A person might say, they are going to the kitchen to get a drink. Then they go and close the door behind them. They do this quietly so that the people in the room do not hear.

A person who does this work is called a madder. They use a small hard thing that is made of hard white rock. They place this thing on their hands. They press the thing and push it. This makes the rock push into the wood. The wood does not break, and the thing stays in place.

Sometimes the word is used for a person who is a little bit old. They might wear old clothes and walk with a slow step. They might have a face that is not very bright. They are not very strong and can not do very fast work. But they are still important because they have lived for many years.

In a place where people build things with strong wood, a madder is very important. They use the hard rock to make the wood strong. They make the wood so strong that it can hold heavy things. Without them, the world would not have many of the things we use today.

The word comes from a word that was used a long time ago. It started as a word for something that is made from strong wood. Over time, people started to use it for the people who make the hard rock.
