A week is a long time of time. It is between two big days.

One day is the day we start. It is the first day of the week. It is where we begin. We use it to tell how many days we have left.

A week has seven days. On the first day, we can do many things. The next day, we might do something different. The third day we might play with a friend. The next day we might go to school. The days go round and round until we are on the last day.

A week can also tell us how many weeks are in it. If we have three weeks, that is three big sets of days. We can use those sets to plan our life. We can plan what we do before and after the week.

Sometimes people use the word week to talk about a time that is very long. If you have a week of school, that is a very long time. You are in school for many days and have a lot of work to do.
