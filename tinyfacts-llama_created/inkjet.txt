To make something you can't see is a thing that uses power to join tiny drops of clear stuff together. These tiny drops are the tiny bits that make up a picture or a word. You can put many of these tiny drops together to make a big clear thing that holds your picture.

The power does this by moving tiny drops of clear stuff through the air. When the power turns on, it moves the tiny drops in a very fast way, so they go very far from each other before they touch again. This makes a line of clear stuff that goes across the air. The air goes over the thing you want to make, and the clear stuff goes around it.

The power does not use water. It does not use fire. It only uses power from a box. You put the box into the thing, and the power comes out of the box and into the air. The power makes the tiny drops move, and the tiny drops join together to make the clear stuff.

When the power is turned off, the thing stops moving. When you turn it back on, the power comes out again and the tiny drops start moving once more. This is why you can keep making the same thing over and over without it getting old.
