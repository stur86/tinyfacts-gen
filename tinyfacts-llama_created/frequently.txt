To often something means you do it a lot.

When you do something, you are doing it over and over. It is like a song you singled out in your head. Your brain is always thinking of it, but you are not really listening. You are moving your body, but you are not really paying attention.

You often do this with your body. You do this with your heart when you feel happy. You do this with your eyes when you watch a movie. You do this with your ears when you listen to music. It is a way to make your life feel like a song.

Sometimes you do something and then you do it again the next day. This can feel like you are stuck in a long line of people. You are waiting, waiting, waiting. But then you see a person, and you are like, oh, this is my time. I am going to do this thing.

You can also often do something when you want to. You may want to eat food, or you may want to play a game. You are thinking about it, and you are trying to decide.

Sometimes, you do something and then you do not. You feel like you should be doing it, but you are not. You are like, why am I not doing this? I want to, but I am not. This is often the same thing as being often afraid.

To often something is like being in a place where you are always there. You do not leave, and you do not go away. You are the one doing it.
