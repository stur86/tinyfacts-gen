To be a good friend is often about being kind. A good friend listens to you when you are sad or angry. You feel safe around them. They try to make you feel better.

A person who works with people with a bad heart is called a doctor. They help people feel better when they are sick. They give them things to drink. They help them breathe. They also make them feel safe in their body.

A person who helps people who are hurt or sad is called a helper. They listen to what you say. They give you things to eat or drink. They help you get to a place where you can get more help. They try to make you feel better.

Nights can be hard. When the sun goes down, people might feel sad. They might want to cry. But a person who stays up all night and looks after them is helping. They keep them safe and make them feel better. They are like a warm light in the dark.

Some people work with animals. They take care of them. They give them food. They make sure they have water. They help them feel safe. They help animals when they are hurt.
