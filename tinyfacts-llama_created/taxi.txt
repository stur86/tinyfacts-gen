A car that takes you where you need to go is a very important thing. It is usually small and has many seats. The driver sits behind the car and drives the road. You can tell where you are going by telling the driver. You can say, for a short time, or for a long time, or for a place, or for a city, or for a house. If you say, for a house, the driver will take you to a house far away.

You pay the driver money when you get there. Sometimes you pay by putting money in the car. Other times you pay with a small card. The money goes into the car and the car keeps it. The driver is the one who drives you to where you want to go. They know the way by heart and can help you if you are lost.

Sometimes you call a car that takes you to a place. You call it a ride or a car. You tell the driver where you are going. The driver listens and drives the car to your place. You do not tell the driver where you are going. The car just goes.
