To make something easier to understand is to make it less hard. This happens when you use short words instead of many words. You can also make a word stronger by adding a word in front of it. This makes the word more important.

Sometimes, when people change how they do something, they become different. They do not do the same things as they used to. Their way might be better for them. This change is called a change. To change something is to make it new.
