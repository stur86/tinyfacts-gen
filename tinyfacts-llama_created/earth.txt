The word for our world is something that sits in the sky. It is a big piece of ground that is covered with water and land. It is where we live and the air we breathe comes from. We call it home.

The word also means the stuff that makes up most of what you see around you. It is very hard and can not be broken. It is the ground you walk on, the ground under your feet, and the ground that makes the ground. It is what keeps us safe and what gives the world its color.

There are many different ways to say the word. Some people say, the world. Others say, the place. Some say, the ground. Some say, the thing that sits in the sky. It is a big word that shows how much we love and need this place and the world inside it.
