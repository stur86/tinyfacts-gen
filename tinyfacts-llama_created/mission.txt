A person who goes on a long trip, often to a far away place, to do something important. This person might be a doctor who goes to a city to fix a man who is sick, or a man who goes to a place to build a new building.

They usually have a plan. They know what they want to do and where they will go. They might meet many other people along the way. They might learn a lot about the place they are going to. They might meet new friends.

The person who goes on this trip is doing something important for many people. They are helping to make the world a better place. They are showing us that even one person can make a big change.

Sometimes people go on this kind of trip because they want to learn. They want to see new things. They want to meet new people. They want to try new foods. They want to be happy.

They might also go on this trip because they want to help others. They want to fix a problem. They want to make a friend. They want to help a person who is sad.

No one is alone on this kind of trip. Many people work together. They share their ideas. They help each other. They are doing this for everyone.

When we say someone is on a job, we mean they are doing something important for many people. We use this word to talk about people who work very hard to help others.
