To turn something means to change its way. You can turn a chair or a car. If you sit in a chair, you are sitting. But then you turn the chair around and now the chair is facing another way. You are not still, you are moving.

You can also turn a thing around. If you want to go to the other side of a room, you turn your body around. This is when you turn your head and your legs. It is a way to change your direction.

Sometimes, you turn something that is not moving. You might turn a door, a book, or a thing that is on the ground. This is when you use your hand to make it go in a new direction.

To turn something also means to change how it looks. If you look at a picture, you turn the paper around. This changes the way you see the colors and the words. You see the picture in a different way now.

You can turn a time too. If you start a new day, you turn the hour. You turn the minute. This changes the start of your day.
