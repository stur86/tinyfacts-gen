To bring back something after it has gone away is to start again. When you are old, you have lived many years. You have seen many things and done many things. But time goes on and time goes on. One day you might feel tired and sad. You might want to stop doing things.

To change this feeling and to feel happy again is to have a new start. You can do this by doing new things. You can move to a new place. You can meet new people. You can do things that make you feel good.

You can also do this by being kind to others. When you are kind, it makes everyone feel happy. It makes them want to be friends with you. It makes them want to help you.

Sometimes things go wrong. A tree falls down. A dog gets sick. A person loses a job. But if you can start again, you can do something new. You can learn from the bad times. You can try to do better the next time.

We also bring back things by making them better. When you clean a room, you make it look nice again. When you eat food, you bring it back to your body so it can be strong again.

It is good to have new starts. It is a chance to start again and to do things in a new way.
