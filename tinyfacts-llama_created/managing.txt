To manage something means to control how it is done. Imagine you are a person who owns a store. You need to buy food, clothes for the people who work there, pay the money of the people who come in, and keep the store clean. If you do not manage these things well, the store might not be able to stay open. You have to decide how much money to spend on food, how much to pay for clothes, and how to keep the store clean.

You also manage your time. You decide how many hours you will spend on each thing. You might decide that you will work for two hours in the morning and two hours in the afternoon. This helps you stay on top of your work and do not get too tired.

Sometimes, you manage people. You are a teacher or a manager at a big building. You have to decide who gets to do what job, how to give orders, and how to keep everyone safe. You also have to know when to let people go and when to keep them in.

If you manage a person, you help them do their job. If you manage a thing, you keep it running well so that people can use it.

To manage something is to keep it safe and work with it so that it stays in a good way.
