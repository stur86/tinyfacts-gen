To make a thing is to start it. You are the one who starts it.

Sometimes we make things to help each other. A doctor makes a paper to show what happened to a person. A teacher makes a book to show what they know. A person makes a book for their family.

When you write something, you are making a thing. You are starting a story or a word. You have to decide what to write and where to write it.

A person can make a thing for many reasons. They might want to tell a story. They might want to show how something feels. They might want to show what happens. They might want to show how they feel about something.

To make a thing is to start to build. You start with a small idea. You add one thing after another. You build a picture. You build a house. You build a car. You build a house for your family.

Making a thing is a very important job. It takes time and hard work. It shows you are strong. It shows you can think about what is important. It shows you are a person who can make a good thing.

You can also make a thing for fun. You can play a game. You can make a game to play with your friends. You can make a game to play alone. You can make a game to see how fast you can do it.

Making a thing is a way to show you care. It is a way to show you are there for someone. It is a way to show you want the best for them.
