To be serious means you are very close to the truth. It is not just a word, but a feeling that comes from inside.

Imagine you are in a room with a very important person. If you act like you are happy and laugh with others, they might think you are not to be trusted. If you keep looking at the person with a look of deep thought, they will know you are thinking about them. This kind of thought is serious.

People also say something is serious when it is a very big thing. A doctor might say,  You have a big problem with your body, and that is a very serious thing to say. If you tell a friend you are very tired, it is not serious. You are just tired. If you tell a teacher you broke a chair, it is serious because the chair is important to the school.

Being serious is about showing you care. It means you are not just talking, but you are really thinking about what you are saying. If you are very serious, people will listen to you. They will look at you. They will not just shake their heads. They will look at you with a look that says, I am listening.

Sometimes, being too serious can be a problem. If you are not happy, you might look very sad. You might not want to talk. You might just sit and look at the floor. This can make people think you are not to be trusted. You might be a person who does not like to say anything. This is not being serious. This is being sad.
