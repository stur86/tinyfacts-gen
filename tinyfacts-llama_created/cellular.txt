Cells are very small things that are inside us and inside the world. They are the tiny building blocks of everything around us.

Imagine you have a box with many small rooms. Each room is like a cell. Inside each room there are many tiny living things that can move around and do their job. The rooms are so small that we can't see them with our eyes.

When you breathe, tiny rooms inside your body are taking in air. Inside those rooms, the tiny living things are breaking the air into smaller pieces. This makes the air you breathe go into your body and into your blood. Your body then uses that air to make you move and keep your brain working.

The rooms are also where food is broken down. The food you eat becomes tiny pieces of what you need to live. These tiny pieces go into the rooms and the rooms break them down so that your body can use them.

The rooms are also where the tiny living things that make things grow live. These tiny living things can give off colors and make things grow. Some rooms have many of these tiny living things, and together they can make a big thing grow.

When a room dies, it can break down into tiny pieces. These pieces can go into the rooms of other things. This is why you get hurt if you cut yourself. The tiny pieces can go into your body and cause pain.

Cells are very important because they make everything around us. They are the stuff that grows things, makes food, and keeps us living.
