To be in a deep space where there is nothing and no light. It is a place where there is no air and no water. Everything you see and everything you hear is gone. The ground is hard and the sky is very high. You can not see anything but the sky. It is a place that is very hard to understand.

Imagine you are in a room and everything is gone. You are left with nothing. There is no room, no floor, no walls, and no air. It is a very big empty space. It is not a place you can go to. It is a place that is always there, even when you are not looking.

Sometimes this word is used to talk about something that is very small. A tiny room or a tiny box can be full of nothing. It is not a place you can see, but it is a place that is always there.
