To watch something is to stay there and see what happens. When you are a police person, you are a part of the police. You watch where the people go. If a person does something wrong, you tell them to stop and then you go to the place where they were.

When you walk around a city at night, you are doing a job. You look at the streets and see if everyone is where they should be. If there are people who do not want to be there, you tell the police. They will come and stop them.

A group of people who watch the city streets is called a police team. They use cars and buses to keep people safe. They also use a long walk stick to catch people who are trying to run away. The people who watch the streets do this job so that everyone is safe.

You can also watch things that happen in a home. A parents watch the children when they are at school. A teacher watches what the students are doing so they can know if they are doing well or not.
