To pay a person to do work is called paying them. This is when you give money to a man or a woman to help them do their job. They work for you for a few hours a day or for a whole day. You pay them money so they can buy food, a house, or other things they need.

Some people pay for work by giving them money. Others pay by giving them a thing that is already made. This is like paying money for a food you eat or a drink you drink. You give the person money because they help you. They help you get things done, and you help them get money.
