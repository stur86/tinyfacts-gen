To eventually means you will not be here soon. You are going to do a job, and then you will be gone. You will leave your house, your car, and your friends behind. You will go on a long way, maybe to a new city or a far away place.

This word can also mean you will change a lot. You started as a small thing, but now you are a big one. You have more power or more money. You are becoming stronger or better.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about the end of a story. A story is a set of words that show what happens. If you read a story, you have seen the whole thing. The end is the last words. If the story ends, the story is actually over. This means you have seen all of it.

We can also say something is the most important. If you are going to do something, it is the most important. You want to do that first. It is the best thing you can do.

You can also say a thing is very far away. If you look at the stars, you can see them very far out. The sun is far away in the sky. The stars are so far that you need a long time to see them.

This word is often used with numbers. If you wait a long time, you will see a number that is very high. Or if you wait for a long time, you will see a number that is very low. It helps us understand how time works.
