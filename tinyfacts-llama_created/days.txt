To day something means to tell a story about it. You tell the story over many days. It is often about people. People often live in houses with many rooms. Each room can be many stories. You tell a story about the room, the people who live there, and the things that happen.

Some days are for fun. You play games or watch a movie. Other days are for work. You go to school or go to work. You do your job. You buy food and clothes. You see your friends. You do things that help you live.

In the morning, you wake up. You get out of bed. You look at the sun. You see how bright it is. You feel a bit warm. You eat a breakfast. You go to a school or a work place. You learn new things. You make new friends.

At night, you go home. You eat dinner. You watch a movie or a story. You listen to music. You feel calm. You think about what you did during the day.

Days are important because they help us know where we are. We know what day it is. We know if it is morning, afternoon, or night. We know when to go to bed and when to get up.
