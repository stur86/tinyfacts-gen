To warm something means to make it feel better. You can warm a cup of coffee or a soft chair. A small fire can also make things feel warmer.

Imagine you are in a cold room. Your body is very cold and you feel very tired. You need something to make your body feel better. If you drink warm water or sit near a warm place, your body starts to feel better. You feel more warm inside and you are more ready to do things.

You can also warm things outside. If it is very cold and the ground is hard, you can use a warm thing to make the ground feel softer. This is so people can walk or run without hurting their feet.

Sometimes you use a thing to make air feel warmer. This is the air that comes out of a hot thing, like a box that makes hot air. When you breathe in this warm air, it makes your chest feel better and you feel more warm inside.

In life, you can warm a person. If someone is very sad, you can talk to them and make them feel better. You can also warm a room by putting more light in it.
