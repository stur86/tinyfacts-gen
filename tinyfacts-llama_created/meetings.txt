A meeting is when many people get together. They talk about something important. They usually sit in a room or a chair room. Some people are the leader. Others are the person who brought the idea. They talk and try to find a way.

In a meeting, people ask questions. They try to know more about each other. Some people speak a lot. Other people keep quiet. They listen to what the others say. Sometimes people get very angry. Other times, they laugh. They all try to see the same thing.

Sometimes people meet for a short time. They talk for only a minute. Other times, they talk for many minutes. A meeting can be about a problem. A meeting can be about a plan. They may talk about money. They may talk about jobs. They may talk about what to do next.

When people say they will meet, it means they will talk soon. They will do this at the same time. They will all be there. They will listen to each other. They will find a way to do what is important.

You can have many kinds of these. There is a meeting for people who work together. There is a meeting for a group of people who want to change the world. There is a meeting for people who want to share food. There is a meeting for people who want to talk about their lives. There is a meeting for a group of people who want to learn.
