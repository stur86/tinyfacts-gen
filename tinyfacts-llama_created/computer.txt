A computer is a very big box that can do many things. It can do things for you, tell you answers, and even play games.

Inside the computer there are many parts. There are two kinds of parts. The first kind is made of many small parts that move around very fast. These small parts are what give the computer power.

The second kind of part is made of many many lines of tiny numbers. These lines tell the computer what to do. When you give the computer a word, it reads the words and follows the lines to do something.

The computer has a brain inside. The brain is like the mind of a person. It looks at what you tell it and makes a plan. It then follows the plan to do the work.

You give the computer a piece of paper with numbers on it. The computer reads the numbers and does the work. You can also tell the computer to do things that are not on the paper. The computer listens to your voice or what you write, and it does what you want.

Computers are like friends. You talk to them, and they do what you want. You can ask them to show you pictures, play music, or tell you a story. You can also use them to learn new things, like how to do a new job or how to grow food.

There are many kinds of computers. Some are very big and sit on a table. Others are small and you carry them around in your pocket. There are computers in homes, schools, and offices. Some computers do only one thing, like play games, while others do many things like show pictures and tell stories.

Computers are very important today. They help us do work faster, keep our memories, and help us learn new things. They also help us talk to people all around the world.
