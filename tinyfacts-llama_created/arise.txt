To begin something is to start. Imagine you are in a quiet room. You are very still. Then you start to move your feet. You take a deep breath. You open your eyes. You are now in a new place.

This happens to many things. If you are in your bed, you are asleep. But then you wake up. You are new and ready for the day. This is what it means to arrive.

You can also start a fire. You add wood to the ground. The wood starts to burn. It grows and gets bigger. The fire makes a bright light. It makes a warm feeling. This is what it means to begin something that will give you light and make you feel warm.

Sometimes people start a new job. They are very nervous. They do not know what to do. They go to the office. They look at papers. They talk to other people. They try to do their work. After a while, they feel better. They start to enjoy the work.

To begin something is to start a new story. It is to make a new day. It is to make a new moment. When you begin, you are ready to start.
