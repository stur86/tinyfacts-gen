To be marked is to be put on something. You can put a small thing on a book or a picture so that when you see it, you know it is that thing. You can also mark a door so that people know it is closed.

When you walk in a room, you might see a picture on the wall. If you see the picture, you know it is there. Your eyes are marking the picture. This is like being the first one to see something.

A long time ago, people put marks on the back of animals so they could find them later. If a dog is found, the owner can pick it up. The mark helps the person find the animal.

You can also put a mark on a phone when you write a word. When you send a word to someone, the computer adds a mark to the word. This mark tells the computer who the word is from and where it came from.

When you look at a movie, you see the marks on the picture. These marks tell you who the people are, what they did, and where they were. The marks help you understand what is happening on the picture.
