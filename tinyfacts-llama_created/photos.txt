To take a picture is to make a picture. You use a thing that holds light to see what you want to remember. This thing goes into a box that keeps the picture. Inside the box, the light and the picture are kept together for a long time.

When you take a picture, you are showing the world what is happening now. The picture can be seen many times and in many places. It is like a small piece of the world that you can keep inside your mind.

Sometimes, you can use the picture to remember things that happened. You can look at the picture many times and remember the story. You can also show the picture to others and share what you saw.

The word comes from a word that means to hold a thing in your hand. To take a picture is to hold the moment in your mind.
