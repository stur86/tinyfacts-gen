Plays are big things made of many tiny tiny parts. They are usually very hot. If you put many small round parts together, they make a hot round thing. But when the hot parts get very big and very hot, the parts start to break away from each other. This makes the hot parts go out and join other hot things. The hot parts are like a big group of tiny moving water.

The hot part is made of tiny parts that are very small and round. There are more of these tiny parts in the hot part than in any other part. These tiny parts are very good at moving past each other. The hot part gets hot by moving around and around. When the hot part is hot, the tiny parts are moving fast and the parts are very close together.

Plays are often found in the sky after a big rain. The rain is made of tiny water drops. When these drops fall from the sky, they are not very big. They keep falling because they are very heavy. But when the drops get very hot from the sun, they become very fast and very small. They go out of the sky and join other drops like them.

Plays can also be found in the ground. They are what you see when you look at the ground at night. The tiny parts of the hot part of the sky join the ground and form what we see. The ground is made of many tiny parts of the hot part that have come down from the sky.
