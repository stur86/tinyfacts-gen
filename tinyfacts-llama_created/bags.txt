To carry something is to move it from one place to another. You might have a bag in your hand. It is a thing you use to hold things. You can put food in it. You can put clothes. You can carry a book.

Bags can be made of many things. Some are made of hard stuff. Some are made of soft stuff. You can find them in many colors. You can find bags with numbers on them. You can find bags with pictures.

When you carry a bag, you move it from your room to the kitchen. You move it from the kitchen to your car. You move it from the car to the street. You carry it when you go to the store. You carry it when you go to work.

Some bags are very big. They are used to carry many things at once. You can carry a whole box of food. You can carry a lot of clothes. You can carry books and papers.

You also carry a bag when you feel sad. You might put your head in it. You might cry. The bag is a safe place for your things. It keeps you safe while you are feeling sad.

To carry means to move. It is a way to move things from one place to another.
