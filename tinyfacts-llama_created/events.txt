To happen is to become. It is what you do that makes you not be the same as who you were before.

Think about time. You are now. You have done many things. But you are still the same person as you were five minutes ago.

An eventually thing happens. It is a thing that will happen later. You do not know when it will happen. You do not know where it will happen. But you can tell it is happening.

You can have a good eventually thing. You do something that makes you happy. You meet a friend you never knew. You do something that makes you feel safe. These are good things that happen.

You can also have an eventually bad thing. You do something that makes a room feel hot. You do something that makes a door break. These are bad things that happen later.

But sometimes an eventually thing happens and it is the best thing ever. You do something that makes a room feel warm and happy. You do something that makes a door make a soft noise. This is a good thing.

In life, you have many of these. Some are good. Some are not good. But they all happen, and they make us who we are.
