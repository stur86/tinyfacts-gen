To part means you move away from someone or something. Imagine you and your friend are playing a game. You both want to be the one who gets the ball. You both reach for it at the same time. Then, you step away from each other. Now you are not touching the ball anymore. You are not together.

When you part, you are still in the same place. But you are not near each other. You are in different areas. This can happen many times in life. You might part from a family. You might part from a friend. You might even part from a job.

You can also part from something that is important to you. If you love a book, you might part with it. If you love a place, you might part with it. When you do this, you are letting go. You are giving something that is important to you to someone else.

Sometimes, you part because you are not a good fit. If you are not a good friend, you might part. If you are not a good team player, you might part from a group. If you are not a good person, you might part from your family.

Part is also about space. If you are part of a group of people, you are a part of that group. When you part, you are not a part anymore. You are on your own, and you are not in that group.

In a story, part can mean a time when something happens. It might mean the end of a story. Or it might mean a time when a person does something. It can also mean a time when a thing is broken so it can be fixed.

In your life, you might part because you want to try something new. You might part from a place you love because you need to move to a different city. You might part from a job because you want to do something else.
