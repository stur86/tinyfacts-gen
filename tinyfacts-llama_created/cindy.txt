To cry is to make a soft sound with your voice. It is usually a sad sound. When you cry, your body feels very heavy. Your eyes feel like they are going to fall down. Your nose and your mouth get wet. You might start to shake. Your eyes might well up with tears. This happens when you are sad or angry.

Sometimes, you cry because something is very wrong. You might cry because you lost a friend, or because you got into a fight with someone. You might cry because you are feeling very sad. You might cry when you are afraid or scared.

You can also cry for happy times. When you see a beautiful place, or when you hear a song you love, you might cry. This is because you feel happy inside.

A person can cry because they are afraid, or because they are very sad. A child might cry when they are afraid of a dark place. An animal might cry when it is hurt. A person might cry when they are happy.

To cry is to let go of your hard feeling inside. When you cry, you are letting your body know that you are okay. You are letting go of your hard feeling and letting your heart feel nice again.
