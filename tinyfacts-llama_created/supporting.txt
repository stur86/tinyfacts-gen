To help someone is to give them something they need. If you are happy for a friend, you are showing you care. You might do this by letting them know that you are there for them if they need to talk.

Think about money. If you have money and you give it to someone, they have more to buy things they want. This helps them live better. You are helping them when you give money.

You can also help someone when they are sad. A kind word can make them feel better. If you sit down next to a person who is crying, you are showing you care.

In a group, we all help each other. If one person is doing a job that is hard, the others can help them. This is how a group works. Everyone is there to help so that everyone can be happy and work well together.
