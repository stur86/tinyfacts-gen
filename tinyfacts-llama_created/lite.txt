To light something means to make it bright. You can use a light from a box to see or to make a room look nice.

Imagine you are in a dark room. Your eyes are tired and you can not see very well. But then you turn on a light. The light comes from the box and goes through the air to reach your eyes. Now you can see everything. The light makes things stand out and makes the room look nice.

You can also light things by burning them. A fire is a big light. When you burn wood or paper, tiny parts of the wood get hot. These hot parts make light. The light goes out of the fire and into the air. When you breathe it in, you feel warm. You can also see the light coming from the fire.

Sometimes you light things to play music. A person can turn a round thing. This round thing has a small piece inside that makes a sound when you push it. When you turn the round thing, the piece inside starts to go around very fast. This makes the air around it get moving. The moving air makes a sound that is like music. The round thing makes light too. The light comes from the moving air and the moving parts of the round thing.

You can also light things to keep them warm. If you put a cup of hot water in your hand, the water gets warm. You can keep your hand warm for a long time this way. The light from the water makes things warm.
