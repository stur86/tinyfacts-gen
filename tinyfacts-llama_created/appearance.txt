To appear means you are there. You do not hide. You are right in front of the people or things you are looking at. You show up without trying. You are not a thing that can be seen, like a tree or a house. You are a person or a thing that is in a place. You might be a person you know, or you might be a thing that is there because of the way it looks. 

If you are in a room, people see you. If you are in a park, people see you. You are the thing that makes the space feel more full. You help people know if they are in a place. If you are in a room full of people, you are one of them. If you are in a park, you are one of the many people there.
