To act means you do things. When a thing is ready, you can start. A car has a part that turns the round thing. If this part is not working, the car does not move. The car acts by turning.

A person can also act. If you want to go to a new place, you act. You tell the bus driver where you want to go. You act by telling him what you want. He acts by going to the place.

Sometimes, when you act, you might make a sound. A small animal acts when it sees food. It jumps and makes a loud sound. A person acts when they tell a story. They tell people about what happened.

You can act in many ways. You can act when you speak. You act when you smile. You act when you write a story. You act when you try to do something you want to do.

To act is to do and be. It is about the things you do.
