A person or a business that gives things to other people is called a suppose. This word is often used in the word for a person who helps another person. It is also used to talk about the things that are needed for a job.

Think about a store. If you buy a book from that store, the store gives the book to you. This is a suppose. The store is the one who gives the book to you. If you buy a drink from a coffee shop, the coffee shop is the one who gives the drink to you. Here, the coffee shop is the one who does the giving.

In a big group, there is a person who is the one who gives all the things they need to the people. This is the person who is the top person in the group. They are the ones who decide what to give and how often.

In a room with many people, there might be one person who gives food to everyone. This person is the one who is the top person in the room and is giving the food. If you do not know who is the top person, you can ask other people if they know.
