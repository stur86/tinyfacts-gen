When you give something to many people, it is said to be in a way that many are getting it. You may give a book to a friend, a food to a family, or money to a person who is in need. This is how you make the world a better place.

Think of a big box with many small boxes inside. Each small box has a different thing in it. When you put all the small boxes together, you have a whole set of things that everyone can use. This is what happens when you put many things into one place.

A person may give a word to another person so they can understand. Or a group of people may give money to a person who is very sick. This makes it easier for everyone to live in a better way.

Sometimes, when you give a thing, you have to decide who gets it. If you give a book to a person who is very young, the person who is older gets the book. This helps everyone learn new things.
