To catch someone means you find them. If you see them doing something wrong, you stop them. You might use a phone to see if they are doing something bad. Or you might catch a ball while they are running.

You can also catch a word. If someone says something that is not true, you catch it. This makes them say sorry. You catch the word so the person knows they did something wrong.

Sometimes people use this word to say they did not follow the game. If a person is playing a game and another person is not following the way, the first person catches them. They might say, I caught him doing that. This means you saw them do it.

You can also catch a feeling. If you are feeling angry, you catch it. You might try to breathe hard or look at a picture to calm down. If you feel sad, you might cry. Catching a feeling helps you know what you need to do.
