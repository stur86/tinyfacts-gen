To be a little bit of something means you have a small part. You might have a small piece of paper, a small rock, or a small animal in your hand. You are not as big as the whole thing, but you are still a part.

When you say something is small, you are often talking about how many people are in a group. If there are ten people in a small group, that means there are only a few people.

Sometimes, when you say something is big, you mean it is very large. A big house can be very tall. A big car can carry many people. A big city is often many stories high.

If you say something is a little bit, it means it is not as much as something else. You can say that a small dog is not as strong as a big dog. But you are still talking about the same dog.

You can also say something is very little. This means it is not one bit. It is nothing at all.
