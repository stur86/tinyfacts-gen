The word for the smallest thing is one. It is the thing that is not bigger than any other thing. If you look at a very small room, the wall is the smallest part of that room. If you look at a tiny ball, the ball is the smallest part of the ball.

One can also be the number one. This means the number is the first one. If you have one book and you get another book, you now have two books. This means one is the number before the number that follows.

Sometimes we use one to mean a person. If you say one is a very small person, it means the person is very little. If you say one is a very good person, it means the person is very kind.

We also use one to mean how much money you have. If you have one hundred pieces of money, you have one hundred. If you have a hundred pieces of money and you give ten more, you now have one hundred and ten.

In some places, one is used to mean the end of a story. If you tell a story for a hundred words, you are done. If you tell a story for a hundred hundred words, you are done for the day.

One is also used to show when things happen. If it is one in the morning, it is time to wake up. If it is one in the afternoon, it is time for lunch. If it is one in the evening, it is time for dinner.

Finally, one can mean the start of a new day. When it is one, the day starts. When it is two, the day has already begun. When it is three, the day is almost over.

So, one is a word that tells us how small something is, how many people are in a group, how much money you have, what time you should be at, and how to start a new day.
