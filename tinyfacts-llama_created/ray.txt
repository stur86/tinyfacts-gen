A light is made from many tiny parts. When the parts move, the light is made. It is very bright. You can see it from far away. If you look at a bright light, the light goes out. The light is not in front of you anymore.

A thing with a light is a light thing. It has a very bright part at the top. When the light goes out from the top, it looks like a long line. This line is the light thing. Sometimes we call it a line, but it is really a thing that makes light.

You can also use the word to talk about a line of people. If many people are moving in the same way, it is like a line. If many cars are going the same way, it is a line of cars. If many dogs are going the same way, it is a line of dogs.
