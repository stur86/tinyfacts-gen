A day with a set time is called a time. When you know what time to do something, you can plan it. You might have a work time from  morning until lunch, then you go to the park, and after that you go home.

A person who makes this plan is a time maker. They decide when to do things and how long they will be. They might also decide which things are most important.

When you have a time, you can make sure you do not forget anything. You do not leave your work late because you did not plan it. You are not late because you were doing other things at the same time.

Sometimes you need to follow a set time. If you are working, you might have a set time for when you work. If you go to school, you follow the same time every day.

You can also have a time for fun. You might plan to watch a movie, play a game, or have a sleep. This is a time to relax and have fun.

A time can be for work, for school, for playing with friends, or for doing your job. It is the time we set for doing what we want to do.
