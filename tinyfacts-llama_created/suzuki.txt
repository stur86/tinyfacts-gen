The word sucking comes from a story about a boy who wanted to catch a small animal. The boy had heard that a cat could not be caught if you did not use the right words. So, the boy went to a far away place and told everyone he saw that the cat was there. Many people believed him.

But when the boy went to the cat, the cat ran away. The boy tried again and again. He said, again and again, the cat was there. People started to think he was crazy.

One day, a man saw the boy and asked him what he was doing. The boy said, he was trying to catch a cat. The man said, you are not the cat. The boy said, I am the cat. The man said, no, you are not the cat. The boy said, I am you. This is what the story is about.
