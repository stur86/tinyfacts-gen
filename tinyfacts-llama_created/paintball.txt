To paint and to fight are two different things.

Paint is a bright color from a box. You use it to make things look nice. You paint on a big table. People paint on their faces so they look nice for a party. They use small hard things that go round and round to hit the wall.

When you hit a wall with paint, the color goes up. The wall gets a funny look. If you hit a person, the color goes on their face and clothes.

There is a game where people go to a big area. They are all wearing the same color so they do not stand out. One person is the top person. The others are on the ground. They are all wearing the same color. The person who is the top one is the one who is the best.

The person on the ground is trying to get to the top one. They run and jump. If they hit someone, they have to go down and try again. If they hit the top one, they get a round. They get to go again.

Sometimes people use a big round thing to hit the top person. They throw the round at the top person. The top person must stop moving. If they stop, they lose. If they keep moving, they are the top person.

Paint ball is like a game of hide and look. The people are all hiding. The top person is looking for them. When the top person finds one, they hit them. The person gets to the top one. The game is over.

It is a fun game. People wear the same color for many games. Some people love to paint. Some people love to fight. Many people like both.
