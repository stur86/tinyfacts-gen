To be in a moving line can be easy. You walk in a straight line and you get from one place to another without stopping. But when you are in a group of people and you want to watch a movie, you are in the middle of many other people. You have to look at them and not at a picture in front of you. You are in a line that is moving, but you are not moving.

A computer can do this. It is a box that can look at many pictures at once. It can go through them very fast. It does not stop, even if you close the box. It keeps going until it has seen all the pictures. You can watch them all without you having to look at them one by one.

Sometimes the line is very long. You might be watching a movie for hours. You are not moving. You are just sitting and looking at the pictures. This is like being in a moving line.

Other times, the line is short. You watch a movie for a little while and then you stop. You are not moving. You are sitting there, but you are not moving from one place to another. You are just sitting.
