To breathe is to take air into the body and send it out. Your body needs air to stay moving and to make life happen. Every living thing, from tiny tiny things you can't see to huge animals like you and your dog, needs to breathe.

When you breathe, you take in two kinds of air. The first kind is the air that goes into your chest and stomach. This kind of air has good things in it that help you stay strong. The other kind of air goes down into your body and into your blood. This kind of air has bad things in it that can make you sick.

You breathe in this kind of air first, and then you breathe out the bad air. When you are happy, you breathe a lot of good air into your body. This helps you feel good and gives you the power to move around. When you are afraid or worried, you breathe more of the bad air in and more of the good air out.

You also breathe when you sleep. At night, your body is ready to rest. You take in more good air and let out more bad air while you are sleeping. This helps keep you safe while you are not watching your body.

Breathing is very important for everyone. It helps you stay strong, keep your body clean, and give you the power to do what you need to do.
