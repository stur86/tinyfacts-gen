To lose something means you have it and then you give it away. You keep it for a long time. Later, you do not have it anymore. This is a very sad thing.

You can lose a thing in many ways. You might drop a ball and it goes over the wall. You might leave a book on the floor. You might lose money. This can be very hard to understand because you feel like you should not be in this situation.

Sometimes, you lose people too. You might see a friend you love. You do not see them anymore. This can hurt a lot. You might feel sad, angry, or even want to cry.

You can also lose your way. If you walk in a dark room and you do not find your way back to the light, you are lost. You need to find a way out so you do not stay there.

Sometimes, you lose things you love. Your favorite food, your dog, or your home are gone. This is a very hard thing to do.

To lose something is to let go. It means you decide to give up something that is important to you. This can help you feel better when things are very hard.
