A star is a big ball of hot, bright light in the sky. It is very hot and very heavy. The hot light from the star makes the night feel bright and warm.

Stars are made of the same stuff as the sun. They are made of white hot stuff that is very hot under our eyes. When this stuff gets hot enough, it goes out into space and comes back to form a new star.

The light from a star comes out in many different colors. When you see the night sky, you see many different colors of light. Red light is the warm color, the color of a fire. Blue light is the cool color, the color of the sky on a clear day. Green light is the color of a green tree. The different colors of light tell you which star you are looking at.

Stars are very far away from us. The farthest star we know is one that is more than    hundred hundred of our suns away. You can see this star with a big eye, but you would not see it if it were much closer.

Stars come in many different ways. Some are very small and very hot. Some are very big and very cold. Some are very old and some are very new. Some stars are burning out and become very dark. Some stars will stay bright for a long time and then go out.

Many stars are like big friends in the sky. Some are very bright and very close. Some are very small and very far away. Some are very cold and very quiet. Some are very hot and very bright. Some stars are like old friends, and some stars are like new friends.
