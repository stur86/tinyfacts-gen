To bother someone means to make them feel a bit annoyed or worried. It is a way to tell them you are thinking about them, but you do not want to talk.

Imagine you are walking down the street and you see a friend. You want to say hello. But you are too quiet. You start to think about your own problems. You are bothering your friend because you are not thinking about them. You are not letting them know that you care.

You might ask a question in your head that makes them feel confused. You are not really talking to them. You are just thinking about how you can make them feel better. It is not a nice thing to do. It makes them feel like they are the only one in the world.

Sometimes you might bother your family. You might call them and say, I am thinking about you. You do not want to talk about your day, but you are trying to be nice. You are not really being nice. You are just doing something you do every day.

You can also bother animals. If you leave your dog outside, it may get very cold. You might say, I am going to the store. You are bothering the dog by leaving it alone. You are not letting it know that you care about it.

So, to bother someone means to make them feel a bit of a problem. You are not really trying to help. You are just doing something that makes them feel angry.
