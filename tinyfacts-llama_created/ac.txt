The word across is a bit old and some people use it, while others do not. It is about moving from one place to another across a wide body of water, or over the ground, or even in the sky.

When you cross the water, you do not walk. You use a big thing that goes under you and over you. This thing is strong so you can stay up in the air while still being on the water. You might cross a very wide body of water, like the great blue one that is in the middle of the world.

Sometimes you cross from one side of a big land to the other side. This is called going across a wide land. Other times you cross from the water to the land. This is called going across a wide body of water.

It is hard to say if you are going across or if you are going from one place to another. It is like you are moving around a big thing in the sky or under the ground.
