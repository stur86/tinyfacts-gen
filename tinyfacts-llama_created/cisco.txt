A computer is a thing that can do many things on its own. It can work, play games, show pictures, and even talk to other computers. Most of these computers are made by a group of people.

When you type on a computer, it sends your words to the computer. The computer then reads the words and does what you want it to do. This computer is often much smaller than a room and does not need a person to work it. This is why people often use computers for work, school, and games.

There are many different kinds of computers. Some are very small and can fit in the hand. Others are very big and sit on a table. Some are for making money, while others are for fun. Some can only do one thing and are very good at it. Others can do many things at once and are very good at many things.

You can also buy a computer that you can carry with you. These are called a bag computer. They are very good for people who like to move around. You can work on them while you are walking, eating, or driving.

A computer also has something called a memory. This is where it keeps the words and pictures it has seen so far. It does not keep everything in its mind at once. When you want to look at something, the computer has to look at the memory first.

There are also computers with a small box on the top. This box is the brain of the computer. It is the part that decides what to do. It can be made by many different people or one person can make it all by themselves.

People use computers for many reasons. They can work for hours without getting tired. They can look at pictures of beautiful places. They can talk to friends and family who are far away. They can even do things that take a long time, like making a book, very quickly.

Computers are also used in many homes. They keep your phone numbers and your money safe. They help you stay in touch with your friends. They also help you find what you need when you are looking for something.

In short, a computer is an amazing thing that can do many different things on its own. It is a way for people to work, play games, watch movies, and talk to each other, all from a room or from another place in the world.
