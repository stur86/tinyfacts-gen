A computer has many small parts that work together. One of these parts is a kind of cover. This cover sits on top of the part and helps keep things out.

Imagine you are making a drink. You need to get the right colors out. The cover helps get the wrong colors out. If you put the drink through the cover, it will not look like a bright drink anymore. It will look like a drink with a lot of brown on top.

This cover is like a small window. It lets some light through, but keeps the wrong things out. It is very good at keeping things clean. This is why it is so important in the computer.

Another cover is on the part that reads words. When you type a word, the cover helps catch the right letters. If the cover catches a wrong letter, the word will not be right. This is why the cover is also important.

There are many other covers. There is a cover for the part that makes music. A cover for the part that makes pictures helps keep out the wrong colors. There is a cover for the part that makes a sound. The cover on this part helps keep out the wrong sounds.

All of these covers work together. They help keep the computer clean and make sure the words, pictures, and sounds are right.
