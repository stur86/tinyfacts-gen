A computer does many things very fast. It can answer questions, move pictures, and send words to other computers. It also tells the user that something is happening.

Think of it this way. If you send a word to a friend, your friend sees it and knows something. The computer does something like this too. It sends a word to the person who asked for it.

When you press a key, your computer sends a word to you. It might say, you have a new picture, or you have a new word. This word is called a computer word, and it helps you know what is happening.

Sometimes, your computer tells you if something is wrong. It might say, your picture is wrong, or the word you asked for is not there. This word helps you fix the problem so you can keep using your computer.

It is also used in the world outside computers. If a doctor sends a word to a different doctor, the first doctor knows that a new person is coming to help. The word helps the doctors know who is coming and what they need to do.

In a long way, this word is about sending words to other people so they know what is happening. It is a way to share what is happening in your life with others.
