Things that are heavy are much bigger than things that are light. A big rock is heavy. You can feel it when you put your hand on it. A heavy book is one you do not want to lift.

Air is also heavy. When you breathe, you take in the air and your body feels heavier. This is why you might want to sit down after you eat a lot of food.

You can also have heavy things inside you. When you are very tired, your body can feel heavy. This is why you might want to lie down and rest.

Sometimes, things that are heavy can be hard to carry. If you have a heavy bag of money, you might feel very tired and want to sit down. If you have a heavy book, you might want to put it down on a table.
