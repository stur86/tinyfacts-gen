To see someone or something for who they are

When you know someone, you know their face. You know their hair. You know their clothes. You know what they like. But you also know that they are not like you. They have their own way of thinking. They have their own way of feeling. They do things that you do not do.

If you are in a room and you see a girl, you might see her as a girl. But if you see her, you know she is a woman. You know that she is not a boy. You see her as a woman. This is because you are looking at her with the way you see the world.

You can also see the world like this. You see the world as it is. You do not try to change it. You see it as it is. You see the bad things that happen. You see the good things that happen. You see the people who are kind and the people who are mean. You see the world in the way that you see it.

We also see the world in different ways when we are young. When we are very young, we see the world as a bright and loud place. We see things that are very big. We see bright colors. We see people as big and loud. As we grow older, we see the world as a smaller and quieter place. We see things that are small and quiet. We see people as small and quiet.

This is why we see people and things for who they are. We see them because we know who they are.
