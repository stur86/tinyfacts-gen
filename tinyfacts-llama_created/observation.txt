To watch something that is happening. You see a dog running across the street. You watch how it jumps over a small thing. You are not in the dog. You are not doing what it is doing. You are just watching.

You can watch a person walking down the street. You might see them smile. You might see their hair. You might see how they walk to a place. You are not part of the story. You are just looking at what is happening.

Sometimes, you watch a big group of people doing something. You might see how they move. You might see how they talk to each other. You might see how they work together. You are not doing what they are doing. You are just looking at them.

You can also watch a person doing something that is hard. You might see how they try to do it. You might see how they feel when they are struggling. You are not the person doing the hard thing. You are just looking at them.

Sometimes, you watch a thing that is not moving. You might see it sit on the floor. You might see it look at the wall. You are not doing what it is doing. You are just looking at it.
