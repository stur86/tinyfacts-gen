A paper thing that says you did something is called a piece of paper. It is something you can hold in your hand. You can write on it, draw on it, or draw a picture on it. Some people give these papers to work, to be a teacher, or to work in a big building. It tells them they did a job well.

You also use it to buy things. If you buy a car with a money thing that says it is paid for, you can use this paper. The paper makes it real.

A different kind of paper is one that says you did something for money. You work very hard to do this job. If you do this job well, the money thing tells the world that you are a good worker. This paper makes people know that you are a good person to work for.

Sometimes these papers are given to kids when they finish school. This is a way to say that they did something well. They can show these papers to people to show that they did their work.
