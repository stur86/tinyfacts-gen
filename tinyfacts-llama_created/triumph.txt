To trip means you fall down on the ground. It happens when you move your body very fast and you lose your feet. You might hit your head on the hard floor or your bottom. If you are running, people might laugh at you and say, like, you are a tripping dog.

It can also mean that someone or something is the best. If a team is the best team in a game, they get to play against the other teams. If a person does a great job, the people around them are happy. They might give them a big smile.

Sometimes, to trip is not good. If you fall and hurt your leg, you have to rest for a while. You need to feel better so you can get back up and keep moving. It is a time to be quiet and not to jump on the floor.

To trip over your own feet can be very funny. If you are in a room and you walk in, you might hit your head on a table. Then you fall down and laugh. It shows that you are able to laugh and find the funny parts of a situation.
