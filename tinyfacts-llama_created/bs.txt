The word that starts with an Sends a strange idea to your mind. It is not for people. It is for computers. When you type it, a computer says the word. The word is like a very fast way of writing down things.

To say this word is to break it into tiny pieces. You write these pieces down one after the other. Then you put them together. The computer reads the pieces and makes something new.

Sometimes you use this word to talk about how fast a computer is. If the computer says it is going to do something, it means it is going to do it fast. If the computer says it is slow, then it means it is not going to do it at all.
