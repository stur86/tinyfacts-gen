To relax means you stop thinking about things that are hard or make you feel worried. When you relax, you let go of what is bothering you. You might lay down on the floor and close your eyes.

You can relax when you are feeling tired. After you sleep or after you have done a hard job, you can relax. You might watch a movie or listen to music to help you feel better.

You can also relax when you are feeling sad. When you are sad, it can be hard to think. Relaxing helps you feel less sad. You might do something you like to do, like play a game or read a book, to help you relax.

Sometimes, people relax in a warm place. A warm place can feel very good when you are feeling cold or tired. You might sit in a chair with your back against a wall and let your body feel the warm air.

Relaxing can help you feel calm and safe. It can help you feel like everything is okay. When you relax, you are not thinking about the bad things that are happening. You are not worried about the things that are going wrong.

You can also relax in your mind. You can imagine a very calm place. You might see a beautiful picture or hear a song that makes you feel happy. This can help you feel better when you are feeling sad or worried.

Relaxing is important for your body and your mind. When you relax, your heart beats slower and your breathing becomes easier. This helps your body to feel safe and your brain to feel calm.
