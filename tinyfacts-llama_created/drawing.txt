To draw is to make things on paper. You start with nothing and you make it look like a person, a thing, or a place. You use lines and small marks. You start with a small line and then you add more lines. Each line tells the person what to do next.

The person who draws is a person who uses their hands to make the things they want. They might draw a dog, a house, or a tree. They might use their eyes to look at what they want to draw.

Sometimes people draw things that are not real. They might draw a cat and then a dog at the same time. They are making a picture that is not the real cat, but a picture that looks like a cat.

People also draw when they are happy. A child might draw a picture of a dog when they see a dog. They might draw a picture of a bright day so they remember the day.

You can also draw with your hands. You might use a piece of paper and a small thing. You press the paper down and then you move your hand around to make different lines. This makes a picture.

To draw is to use your hands and your eyes to make what you want. It is a way to tell a story or to show the world what you see.
