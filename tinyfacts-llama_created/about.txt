About is a word you use when you want to tell people a little bit about something. It is like a small story.

Think about a friend. You want to tell them you saw a movie the night before. You can say, about the movie. This tells them what happened. You did not say, I saw the movie. I just said about it.

Another time, you might say about a place. If you went to a park, about the park, you are talking about the park. You are not saying the whole park. You are just telling them about it.

To say about a thing can help you remember what it looks like or what it feels like. If you see a dog, you might say about the dog. This tells you more about the dog than just seeing it.

Sometimes, you might use this word when you are writing. You might write about a book or a movie. This is a small story about the thing. It gives the reader a bit to know about it before you show them everything.

So, about is a way to tell people what you are talking about. It is a word you use when you want to share a bit of your life.
