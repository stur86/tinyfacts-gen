To waking someone means to wake them up from a sleep. When you wake a person, you open their eyes and tell them it is time to go. You might say,  wake up, or get up, or come on. This is important because the person needs to know what day it is and what time it is.

Imagine you are in a house with a family. You hear a sound that means a person is moving. You open the door and find a young child standing there. This child is saying,  wake up, I am here! The person who opened the door is like a person who wakes a child.

Sometimes, to wake up means to feel a bit angry or sad. If you are tired and you go to bed, but you do not sleep, you might feel a bit of a heavy feeling in your chest. This feeling is like a big rock that is inside you. It makes you want to cry. If you wake up and you feel this way, you might want to sit down and rest.
