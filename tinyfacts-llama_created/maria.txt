The word marries has many different uses.

One way to use it is to talk about a woman. A man and a woman can get married. This is a big eventually where a man becomes a part of a group of people who love each other. He can also give the woman a ring. This ring is like a sign that says, I love you. When a person marries, they are no longer alone. They become a team with the other person. They work together and share their lives.

Another way to use this word is to talk about a place. A city or a town can have a name. A woman might move to a new city. She might love the place and decide to stay. The city can become her home.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a time. A long time ago, a woman could get married only if her mother said it was okay. Later, the idea changed. Many women now have the right to marry at a younger age. The word can also mean a time when many things are happening at the same moment.
