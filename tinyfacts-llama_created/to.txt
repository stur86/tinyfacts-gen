To is a word that helps us talk about moving things and people.

It starts with a small sound that is like a step. When you use it, you are saying you want to do something.

You might say,  I want to go. Here, you are going. Or you might say,  I want to see. You are looking at something.

You can also use it to talk about how things are. If you have a dog, you might say,  The dog is to my house. The word shows that the dog is near your home. It is not far from your door.

Sometimes, when you say  I am going to, you are not really going. You are just talking about a plan. You might be saying you want to do something later. But if you actually go, the word  to helps your brain remember. It makes your brain think,  I should go now.

In another way, if you have a job and you have to work, you are to work. You are the one who does the work. You are the one who follows the orders of your manager.

You can also use it to talk about how people feel. If someone is very happy, they might say,  I am to happy. Or if someone is afraid, they might say,  I am to afraid.

To is very important. It helps us talk about moving things and people, and it helps us remember when we should do something. It is a word that is easy to use and it is a big part of how we talk every day.
