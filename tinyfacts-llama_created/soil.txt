So what is this thing you walk on? It is the ground under the green stuff that grows. It is made of small pieces from the sky and the ground. It is also made of dead things from the ground and the living things that grow on it.

You can not see it. It is not a thing you see. But it is all around you. It is what your feet touch. It is what your hair grows into. It is what your clothes feel when they touch it.

So it is a place that grows things. It is a place where tiny living things go to make more tiny living things. It is a place where old dead things go to become new dead things. And it is a place where new living things grow from old dead things.

You need this thing to live. If the ground is hard and does not give, you do not have food to eat. If the ground is too hot, you can get a burn. If the ground is too cold, you can get a cold. If the ground is very wet, you can get a wet foot. But if the ground is just right, you can walk and run and play.
