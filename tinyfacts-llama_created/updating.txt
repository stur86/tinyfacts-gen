To use something means to change how it works or looks. You might use a computer or a phone. If you do something many times, it becomes easier and faster. You can do many things quickly now because the computer or phone is always ready.

Imagine you play a game with a friend. You are very good at it. One day, the game gets an easy part of it. The game now lets you winding things more quickly. You are still good at it, but now the game does all the hard work for you. You only need to play.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about people. If a person is very old, they might not know as much as a younger person. But as they get older, they learn new things and become better at their job. They can understand how the world works better.

It can also mean to change how a place looks. If you go to a new city, you might see a different kind of building, a new kind of street, or a different kind of food. This is like using a computer or a phone. The city is always changing, and you are always learning something new.
