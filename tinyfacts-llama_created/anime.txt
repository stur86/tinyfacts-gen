An animal is a living thing that goes around and moves through water or air. It has a head and a body, and it has legs or no legs at all. Animals need to eat and drink to stay strong.

All living things need air to breathe. Animals breathe in and out through their mouths and noses. They also need to drink water from the ground, from the sky, or from a big green thing in the ground that gives out water. Animals also use their heads and bodies to feel their way and find food.

Animals are very good at moving. Some can run very fast and jump high into the air. Others can move very slowly, but they can always find a way to cross a wide water or a high wall. Many animals can change color to hide from other animals or from the sun.

Some animals are very small and only a few hundred years old. They live in the ground, under rocks, or in dark places in the ground. Other animals are very big and can live for a very long time. Some animals are only a few feet long, while others are as big as a car or a house.

Animals also have babies. When an animal is ready to have a baby, it gets ready to hold its baby inside its body. The baby grows inside the mother for many months or years before it comes out. When the baby is ready to come out, the mother opens her body wide and the baby comes out.

Animals are very important to our world. They help us by eating bad things and keeping the ground clean. They also help us feel good when we watch them in their movies or see them in pictures. Many people love to watch animals because they are so fun to watch and they remind us of the power of life.
