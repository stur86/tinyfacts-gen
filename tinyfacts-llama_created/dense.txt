When you say something is very hard, you usually mean it is very strong. A rock is very hard because it does not break easily. This is because the tiny bits of the rock are very close together.

Think of water. Water is very soft. You can easily break it with your hand. This is why we do not drink water from the street. Water is not as strong as rock.

When something is very hard, it has a lot of small bits of hard stuff. These small bits do not let the other bits out. They keep everything together. This makes the thing very strong.

Imagine you have a ball. If the ball is very hard, it will not let anything push on it. It stays in one place. This is because the small bits of the ball are very close together. If the ball were not so hard, it would break very easily.

A room can also be very hard. If a room is very hard, it has a lot of people inside. The people are very close together and do not move much. This makes the room feel very small. It is like being in a small box with many other boxes.

Sometimes, when we talk about something, we say it is very hard to do. This means the thing is very strong and we need a lot of power to do it.
