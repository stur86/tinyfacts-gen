When you do something, it is working. If you build a box, the box is working. If you eat food, your body is working. You are moving your arms and legs. Your mind is thinking. If the box breaks, the work stops. If the food is gone, you feel sick.

A computer also works. It is a big box that can do many things at once. You can give it words to make it do things or you can tell it what to do. If it is not working, it does not do anything. If you give it the right words, it works again. It can help you with your work, play games with you, or show pictures.

A car also works. You put in the right kind of power and it moves. It can go very fast or very slow. It carries people from one place to another. It is an important thing for people.

Sometimes, people say a thing is not working. This means it is not doing what it is supposed to do. It might be broken. It might be in a bad place. This is when we need to fix it or try to make it work again.

To be working is to be moving or doing. It is a way to show that you are doing something.
