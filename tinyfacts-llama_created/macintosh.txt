A computer is a thing that can do many things. It can play music, show pictures, and talk to other computers. People use them to do work, play games, and talk with friends.

A computer has many parts. The parts are like people. There is the brain, the computer brain, and many other parts that work together. The brain is the part that tells the computer what to do.

Some computers are very big and heavy. They need many power to work. They also need a lot of space to hold all the parts and the memory where the computer stores its work.

There are two kinds of computers. The first kind is a box with many parts. It sits on a table and you tell it what to do. The computer brain in this box is the one that really makes the things happen. You type what you want on a small window, and the computer brain follows what you say.

The second kind is a small thing you hold in your hand. This thing is not as big as the box computer. You can carry it with you all day. You type on a small window on the hand thing. The hand thing tells the small computer brain what to do.

Most people use the computer brain for work. They type papers, check numbers, and make plans. They also use it for games and movies. Some people even make their own games or music for computers.

A person who works with computers is called a computer user. They use the computer to do their job. They might write papers, make money, or work with other computers.

There are also computers for fun. Some people play games, watch movies, and listen to music on these hand things. They can also watch old movies or play old games that you can not play on a computer anymore.

Computers are very important today. They help us stay in touch with each other, buy things, and learn new things. They also help our doctors make new kinds of drinks and give us new ways to stay safe.
