To start something means you begin it. When a person starts a thing, they move it from a place where it is to a place where it will finish. You can start a game, a story, a day, or a work.

Imagine you are playing a game. You put your ball on the ground. Then, you pick up your friend and you both start running. You are the first person to reach the other end.

When you start a work, you are the first person who does it. You do the first steps. As you work, the work gets bigger. You might make a table, build a house, or fix a car.

In many cases, the beginning of something is a key idea. If you start a new idea, you might ask yourself, What will this new idea be about? What will it look like? This helps you understand where you stand.

Starting is important because it is the start of something new. Without a start, there is no beginning. Without a start, you do not know where you are going.
