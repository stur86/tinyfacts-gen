A small table for one person can have a top that is high and a bottom that is low. It has many small pieces that fit together. These pieces are usually made of wood, but some are made of other hard stuff.

In a room, this kind of table might be in a high place where people sit. It might be on a floor in a room that is not very big. The top of the table might have pictures on it. People might sit on the low part of the table.

You can also use this word for a group of people. If many people are in the same room, they can all sit on these small tables. This is often for a meeting or a big group of people to talk.

A table is a thing that holds things. It keeps things in one place. It helps people work together. People might work on papers and write on these small pieces. They can also use them to talk and listen.
