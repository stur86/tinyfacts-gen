To stating means you stop doing something and then start again later. You might do this because you feel ready, because you have a plan, or because you want to try something new.

Imagine you are a person who loves to play games. You play for a long time every day. One day, you decide you want to try something different. You might go to a new place or learn a new game. You start this new thing when you leave home. You do not do anything during the day. You just sit in a strange place. Later, when the day is over, you go back home and start playing the game you left behind. You play for a few more hours, and then you take a break. Then you do it all again.

This is a way to stop and start again. It is like a stop and start for your life. It helps you try new things, learn new things, and remember what you did before.
