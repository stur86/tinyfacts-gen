A computer is a thing that you can sit in front of. When you look at it, you see many pictures and moving things. People use these things to stay in touch with each other when they are far away.

One kind of computer is for people to watch things. It has a small box inside it. The box has many tiny pictures on it. The computer looks at these pictures and shows them on a big box. You can see what is happening to someone who is doing something far away, like a person playing a game or a dog running around.

There is also another kind of computer. It is for people to watch what happens in a room. The computer sees many moving pictures of people and things. It shows you what is happening right now. You can see a child playing, a woman smiling, or a man talking. This is very fun because you can see things that no one else sees.

Sometimes, these computers show you pictures of the whole world. You can look at the blue sky, the green trees, or the bright white sun. You can even see the city lights at night. It is like having a window into another world.

People use these computers for many reasons. Some use them to work. They can see their papers and do their jobs. Others use them to watch movies. They can see the whole movie at once, so they do not have to watch it many times.

Some people also use these computers to talk to each other. They can see each other and hear each other. This is called real time. You can talk to your friend right away, even if you are far away. It is very easy to stay in touch with people you do not know.

In short, a computer with a box that looks at many pictures is called a computer with a box. It is a way to see and talk to other people from far away.
