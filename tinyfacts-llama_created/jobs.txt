Jobs are what people do to get money. When you have a job, you are doing work for someone else. This work is usually in a place with a desk and a chair. Sometimes, people work in the ground or in the air. If you are working on a road, you might drive a car or walk on the road.

Jobs are important because they help make money for people. When many people work in one place, it can become a big group of people doing the same job. This can help make things happen. It also helps keep the world going because there are many people doing different jobs.

Some jobs are easy to do, like cutting hair or making clothes. Other jobs are harder, like being a doctor or a police man. Some jobs pay a lot of money, and some jobs pay very little. You can pick a job that fits your life and your money.

You can have many jobs at once. Some people have many jobs. They might be a teacher, a doctor, a police man, a police woman, a teacher, and a mom. This is called being a full time job. Some people have only one job. They might work in the office, and they do not go out and walk around a lot.

Everyone needs jobs. If no one works, there is nothing to buy. If no one works, people do not have money. Jobs are important for keeping our world going.
