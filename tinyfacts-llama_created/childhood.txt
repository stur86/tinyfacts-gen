Child is a word for a young person. A baby is very small. A young person is not very old. They are still learning how to do things.

A child is a time when you are growing. You are going from being a baby to being a person. During this time, you learn about the world. You make new friends. You go to school. You play with other children. You learn to be kind and to follow the teacher.

A child is very different from an old person. An old person has already learned many things. They know how to do things. They have stories to tell. They know how to take care of themselves. A child is still learning.

Some children are very small. Some are very big. Some have lots of hair. Some have lots of teeth. Some have very bright eyes. Every child is different.

In the first year of a child, they learn to move their body. They learn to walk. They learn to stand up. They learn to use their hands. They learn to say words. They learn to use their face to show they are happy or sad.

In the second year of a child, they start to learn how to act. They learn to follow the teacher. They learn to follow the other children. They start to learn how to share. They start to learn how to be quiet when they are not supposed to be.

In the third year of a child, they start to learn about their own body. They learn to use their mouth to drink. They learn to use their hands to make things. They start to learn how to be afraid. They start to learn how to be angry.

In the next few years, children grow more and more. They get bigger. They get stronger. They get more able. They learn how to do things on their own. They learn how to be a person.

Eventually, a child becomes a young person. They are now older. They can go to school and work. They can pay their own money. They can make their own food. They can do things with their hands. They can be a person.
