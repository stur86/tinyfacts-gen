A thing that is in a book is called a word. A word tells us what to do. If you say, you are going to the store, the word tells you that. You need to do it.

A person who makes things can also have a word. If they build a house, they have a word. They follow the word to make the house.

If a person does not follow the word, they might break the house. They might break the door. They might break the wall. This can be very bad. The word is like a line that tells you what to do.

There are many different words. Some words are for big things. Some words are for small things. Some words are for things that are funny. Some words are for things that are hard to understand.

You also have words in your head. You use words every day. You talk to your friends. You talk to your family. You use words to show how you feel. You might be happy. You might be sad. You might be angry. Your words help you say what you mean.

A word can also be a name. You can give a name to a person or a thing. You say, this is my friend. You say, this is my car. The word helps you remember the person or the thing.
