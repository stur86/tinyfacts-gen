To expects means you are waiting for something to happen. You might have something that will come later. You do not know when it will start, but you are ready. You are not afraid because you know it is coming.

Imagine you have a friend who is going to meet you. You want to see them. You feel a bit nervous. You are waiting. You are looking at the time. You are thinking about what they will say. You are ready to hear them.

You also expects money. If you buy something, you expect it to be there when you need it. You expect the money to be in your hand. This means you have a plan. You know what will happen.

Sometimes you expect bad things to happen. You might feel sad. You are waiting for something that will not happen. This can make you feel very sad or angry.
