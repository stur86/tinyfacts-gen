To be a big business is to have many people who work for you, doing work for your money. One of these people is called a writer. A writer writes stories, books, or other things people want to read. They might write about people who are happy, or about things that are very important. They write these stories so that many people can know about them.

Another person who works for a big business is called an office manager. They are the one who keeps the business running. They make sure the money is coming in and out, and that all the people know what is going on. They might also have to make sure the people are doing their jobs right.

There are also people who work for big businesses who are not just writing, but also making things that people want to buy. They make sure the things are made in a way that is safe, and that they look nice. They work very hard so that people will buy these things.

Then there are people who work for big businesses who are not making things at all, but are in their offices. These people might be the ones who answer phones, or who write letters to other people. They help the people who are making things know what to do. They might also talk to people who want to buy things, so they know what they want to say.

The person at the top of the big business is the leader. They make the big business do what it wants to do. They decide who should be in each job, and who should be in what job. They also decide how much money the business should give to each person, and who gets what.

All these people work together so that the big business can stay going. Without them, the business would not be able to pay people, or to buy things for people.
