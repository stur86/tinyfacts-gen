To straight means you move a thing without turning it. Imagine you are walking in a line. If you walk forward and then turn to the right, you are not straight anymore. You are still moving, but your body is not in a straight line. That is not straight.

If you have a long piece of wood and you push it across the floor without touching the ground, it is straight. You are not turning it. You are just moving it from one place to another without going around.

You can also straight a line of paper. If a line is not straight and it is not a straight line, you can straight it. You are putting the line in a straight line so it does not go around the corner.
