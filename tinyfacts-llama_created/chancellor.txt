A high person in a big group of people who work together is called a chancing. This person helps keep everyone on the same line. They do this by making sure everyone knows what to do and by fixing any big problems that might have come up.

The word comes from a place where the old days were. In those days, a man with a big top made the whole area. He was like the head of the whole group. When he was gone, the top went to another man. This man then made sure the whole area was running well. He was the one who kept the people doing their jobs.

In a big group of people who work together, this person is like a top. They help each other stay on the same line. They are important because they keep everyone working well together.
