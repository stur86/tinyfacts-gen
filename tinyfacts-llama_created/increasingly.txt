To become more and more of something means that you are getting stronger. When you do something, you start small and then you do it again and again. After a while, you are doing it every day.

Imagine you love a game. You start to play it one hour a day. One week you play for two hours. One month you play for three hours. One year you play for four hours. You are doing it more and more each year. This is what happens when something becomes more.

You can also say the thing is getting worse. If you have a cold, it starts to make you feel worse. You have a lot of pain and your body is tired. This is a bad thing. It is not good to be sick.

Sometimes, to get better, you must stop doing something. Maybe you are drinking too much water. You need to stop. This is an important thing because your body gets hurt and then it starts to feel better.

To become more, you need to take steps. You can try something new or do more of what you already do. This can make you feel stronger. It can help you learn and grow. It is a way to get better and become the person you want to be.
