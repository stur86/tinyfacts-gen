A computer has many parts that work together. Some of these parts are very small and can move around quickly. These small parts are called computers. The computer can send and get many pieces of paper very fast. The pieces of paper go through the computer, and the computer puts them together.

But there are also other parts that are much bigger. These parts take many pieces of paper from the computer and put them into other computers. They take the pieces of paper and give them to other computers. This way, all computers can use the same papers. The big parts are like a road that computers follow.

Some of these big parts are called a box. Inside this box there are many lines. The lines send the papers from one computer to another. The lines are very fast. They can go very many times before they are broken.

Another part is a box that can show pictures. The box can show many pictures at once. If you want to see all the pictures, you have to tell the box which ones you want to see. The box then shows you the pictures.

There is also a part that is like a box that holds things. This part keeps the papers and pictures safe from people who do not want them to see. If a computer does not follow the lines, the papers and pictures can go to the wrong place. The box stops them.

All these parts work together. Without them, computers would not be able to share papers and pictures. They help us work and play on the computer.
