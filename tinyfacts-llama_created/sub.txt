To take something under something else means to move it under. This is different from to put something under another thing. When you put a thing under a table, it stays in the same place. But if you move a thing under another thing, it goes to the other side.

Imagine you have a big box of things. You want to put a small ball inside. You put the ball under the box. Now you have the ball inside the box. The box is still the same, but the ball is in a different place.

Sometimes you might put food under a table so it stays warm. Or you might put a book under a bed so it does not fall off. This helps keep things in a safe place.

Sometimes, when you say you are going under something, you mean you are going under the ground. You might go into a deep part of the ground to look for old things or to see what lives down there. This is different from going under a high wall or a big door.
