To do something in a way that is true

This means you are not just doing a thing. You are doing it with your whole body and your mind. You are not just looking at a picture or a word. You are really doing it with your hands and your feet.

Think about a word. If you say a word, you are not really saying it. You are just using it as a word. But if you take the word and you do it, you are really saying it. You are doing the word with your whole being.

This is how we know if something is true. If we see something, we look at it. If we hear something, we listen. If we feel something, we feel. When we do it in our own way, we know it is true. We know it is what is actually happening.

We can also do this with people. If you say something is true, you are not just telling people it is true. You are showing them you know the truth. You are showing them that you really care about the truth. This makes the people believe you.

Sometimes, we see a thing that is not quite right. It looks okay at first, but when we look closer, it is not quite the way it is supposed to be. In this case, we say, it is not quite true. This is because we did not do it in a way that is true. We did it with our whole being, not just with our eyes.
