An eventually happens. It happens to one person, two people, many people, or even a group of many people. It might be a big thing that changes the world or a small thing that happens every day.

When an eventually happens, the people around it might feel something. They might think, feel sad, feel angry, or feel surprise. Some people might want to talk about what happened. Others might just want to be left alone.

Sometimes the eventually happens because of what people do. A person might do something wrong. A group of people might act together. A person might say something that makes others afraid. A person might do something that is not right. In each of these cases, the person is the one who happens.

An eventually can also happen because of what already happened. If something is happening, and then something new happens, that can be an eventually. Imagine a city street. The cars are moving. A person is walking. Something new happens. The cars stop. The person runs. This new eventually happens because the first eventually happened before.

We might also use the word to talk about how things are. If you have a fight with a friend, you might feel angry or sad. This is an eventually. It happens because of what happened before.
