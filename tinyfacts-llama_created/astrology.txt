To study the stars and their places in the sky is to follow the way they move and see how they change. People who do this are called star people. They look at the signs of the animals and the days that are between the stars to figure out what will happen tomorrow.

In the beginning, it was only about the big bright light in the sky that was always there. Later, people started to notice that some days were bigger than others. They found out that the same sign of the animals comes together at the same time each year, and that the days between them change. This helped them know when to do important things and when to rest.

Today, we know that the stars move very fast and that many of them go around the world many times each year. We can use this to know what the time is and what the day will be. People also use the signs of the animals to know about people and the world around them. Some people think that the sign of a person can tell them if they will be happy or sad in the next few days.
