To be sure means you have no doubts. You know something is true. You do not wonder if it is right. You feel you can trust the person or thing.

Imagine you are walking in a dark room. You are not sure if the door is open or if there is a bad person on the other side. You are afraid to open it. But then you find a light. You feel safe. You are sure. You did not have a doubt. You know the room is safe.

You can also be sure of your own word. If you say you love someone, you do not wonder if they really love you back. You know for sure. You feel you can trust them.

Being sure is important in life. It helps you make good plans and keep your words. It helps you feel safe and calm.
