To give up something you want or need is to let someone else take it for you. It is when you let a person or an animal do what they want. This does not mean you are afraid. You are still in control.

Imagine you have a game you love. One day, the teacher says you have to play with the people in the other room. You do not like this. You want to play with your friends. But the teacher says you must play with them. You are not happy, but you still follow the teacher. This is a kind of giving up.

Sometimes, you give up something because you are afraid. You might give up your money so you do not have to pay a fine. Or you might let someone take your car so you do not have to go to work.

You can also give up something because you feel you should do it. If you are a kind person, you might feel like you should give money to those who need it more than you. This is not always easy, but it is a way to show you care.

Giving up can be hard. You might feel sad or angry. But it is a way to show that you are ready to let go.
