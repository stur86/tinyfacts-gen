To watch over something means to keep an eye on it. When you are a mother, you watch your child. You do not leave them alone. You stay by their side. This helps them feel safe.

If you work in a big office, you might be under a man. He is the one who gives orders. You listen to what he says and do what he tells you. This is part of the job you did. You are watching over him so he can do his work well.

Suppose you are playing a game with your friends. You need to follow the game. You follow what the person who is the game says. If they say to do something, you do it. You do not just do things on your own. You follow what they say.

Sometimes, a teacher watches over their students. They help the students learn new things. If a student does not do well, the teacher talks to them. The teacher helps the student understand better.

You can also watch over things that are not people. If you watch a car, you watch it so it does not go too fast. You help keep it safe on the road. This is important for many people who drive their cars.
