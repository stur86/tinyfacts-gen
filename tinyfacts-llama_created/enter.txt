To enter is to move into a room or a place. You do this by walking in. You can enter a room when you go inside. You can also enter a game or a story. When you do this, you start to be part of it. Your words or your body can be seen by the people in the room.

Imagine you are waiting in line to buy something. You stand there for a while. Then you see the person in front of you is finished with the line. You step in after them. You have entered the line. You are now part of it. You can wait, or you can start to buy the thing.

Entering a place can also mean to show you are going away. If you are in a room and you need to go home, you might say, I am going to enter the house. This means you are leaving the room and going out into the world.

Entering can happen in many different ways. You might enter a school to learn. You might enter a movie to watch a new story. You might enter a game to play with your friends. Each time, you step into a new world.
