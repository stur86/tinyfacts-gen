To do something before another day.

Think of a day and the day after. The day you are in is the day before the day you are in tomorrow. You are doing things now and things will be done the next day.

If you go to sleep at night, you are doing something before the next day. You did not do anything today. Tomorrow you will go to sleep at night and do things the next day.

You can also say that something happened before you started your day. You got ready and then you did what you did. You were doing things before you started your day.

Sometimes people ask, what happened last night? They are asking what was happening before they woke up. They want to know if there was anything new that happened.

This word helps us remember the days that have passed.
