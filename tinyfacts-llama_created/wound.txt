A wound is a mark on your skin that comes from a cut. It happens when something breaks through the skin and the skin tears. This can happen when you fall, or when you get hit with a hard thing. A small cut might feel okay at first. But if it stays open, it becomes a bigger problem.

You might see a wound if you look at your hand after you play a game with a ball. The ball could have hit you and left a mark on your skin. Or if you fall and hit your knee, you might get a cut. It might feel a little bit hot and you might feel a bit pain.

Some people have many cuts at once. These are often from things they do every day. When you work with your hands, you might get many small cuts. Or if you play a game with your friends, you might get some cuts. Your skin might feel red and it might take a while to close up.

Winds can also cause cuts. If a strong wind pulls on your hair, your hair might pull out. You might feel a bit of pain, and your skin might be red. This is why you want to cover your face and arms when it is very hot or very cold.
