A year is a long time of many months. Months are like weeks. We have four of them. There are six more. We call them a year because each set of four weeks is like a year.

A month is a short time. We have one month with  four weeks. We have another month with three weeks. We have one month with two weeks. We have one month with only one week. The month with only one week is the shortest. This month goes round and round in the year.

A week is a very short time. It is only one day. There are many weeks in a month. In a year, there are many years. Each year has a month, and each month has many weeks.

A year helps us know how many days are in it. There are  year days in a year. This helps us keep track of time.

We use a big round thing to keep track of years. This thing goes round and round. We start on a year that begins with a month that starts with the first day of the year. The year goes round and round, and we keep going as long as we need.

A year can also start in another way. This is called a different start year. In this way, some months start on different days of the week. This can make the year different.
