A computer is a thing that you type on with your hands. It is a box with many small keys. The keys are like tiny pieces of paper. You press one key, and the computer says, yes, that is what you did.

When you want to write a word, you press the keys one by one. You press the first key for the word, then the second key for the word next to it, and so on. This is how you make words. The computer does not understand words like we do. It only understands the sounds of the keys.

Some computers have a small box on top. This box has many small keys. You press the keys on this box, and the computer says, yes, I understand now. The keys on the top box are like the tiny pieces of paper you press on your computer.

The keys are very important. If you press the wrong key, the computer will not understand your words. You need to press the keys in the right way to tell the computer what you want. This is why you need to learn how to use a computer.

Computers also have a way to show numbers. You press the keys for the numbers. You press the number keys for the numbers, then the number keys for the numbers, and so on. The computer reads the numbers and does the right thing with them.

There are many different kinds of computers. Some are small and fit in your hand. Some are big and sit on a table. Some are in a room with many computers. Some are in a box that you carry with you. All of them are things that you use to do things on your computer.

A computer is not a living thing. It does not breathe or move. It just sits there and waits for you to tell it what to do. When you give it a word or a number, it follows what you told it. It is very good at following your words.
