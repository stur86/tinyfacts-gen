To act or to be acted upon means the same thing. When you act, you do something. When you are acted upon, you are the one who does not do anything. You are the one who is changed.

When you act with a friend, you do things together. You both laugh or smile. You help each other. This is a good kind of acting. In this kind of acting, no one is the one who does all the work. Everyone is together and does the same.

When you act with a person who is afraid, you are not the one who makes them afraid. You act in a way that makes them feel better. You are not the one who is the bad person. You are the one who helps them feel safe.

Sometimes, you act in a way that makes you angry. You might shout, you might hit something. You are not happy, but you are trying to show how you feel. This is acting too. You are the one who is acting.

You can also act against what is right. You might break a glass. You are doing something that is wrong. This is a bad kind of acting. The person who is acting is the one who does the bad thing.

When you act with a person who loves you, you act in a way that shows you care. You do things that make them happy. You listen to what they say. You show them that you love them. This is a good kind of acting.

In our lives, we act every day. We act when we go to work. We act when we buy food. We act when we talk to a friend. We act when we do something we do not like. We act to stay safe. We act to be happy.
