When you miss something, you do not see it. But when you forget to do something, you have done it. You have not done it, and it is not there anymore.

In the same way, when you forget to do something important, it happens without you noticing. You did not see it. You did not hear it. You did not feel it. But the thing you forgot about is still there. It is still a problem. It is still a thing you need to take care of.

Sometimes, people forget to say hello. They think it is okay, but it is not. It is a way to show you care. When you forget to say hello, you have hurt a person. You have made them feel bad.

We also forget to be kind. We forget to use nice words. We forget to be nice to people. We forget to be happy. When we forget these things, we hurt the people around us. We hurt our friends. We hurt our family. We hurt our own hearts.
