To reason is to think about something. When you reason, you use your brain to understand what is happening. It helps you decide what to do next.

To reason is different from to think. When you think, you just have ideas in your head. You might not know why. To reason is to have a clear idea about how things work. You might see a picture and know why it is so.

If you reason a problem, you try to find a way to fix it. You might ask why something happened. You want to know so you can do it again. This is a very important thing to do.

Sometimes you reason with your body. If you are afraid, you might feel your heart beating fast. Your body is trying to tell you something. You might feel a cold face or a tight chest. This is your body reasoning to you.

You can also reason with your mind. If you reason about a problem, you are trying to find a way out. You think about the past or the things that happened. You look at what you know and what you do not know. You try to figure out what to do next.

To reason is to be a good person. It is to think about how you act. If you reason, you are not afraid to say what you think. You are not afraid to be different. You are being true to yourself.
