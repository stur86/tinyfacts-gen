To grow is to break down. It is a word you use when you are tired.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You run and run, but you are very, very tired. You feel like you are going to fall down. This is when you start to groan. Your body is saying, I am done. I need to rest.

When you groan, you are not shouting. You are not angry. You are just saying that you are very, very tired. Your mouth is making a low sound. It is a way to show that you are not strong anymore.

You can also groan when a tree breaks. The wood is breaking down, and it makes a very loud sound. It is like a big grow.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about food. If you eat too much food, you feel like you are going to get sick. You feel like you are going to groan because your body is saying, I am full. I need to stop eating.

So, to grow is to break down. It is a way to show that you are tired or that something is falling apartments.
