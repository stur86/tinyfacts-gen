To be pleased means you are happy because something happened or a person did something nice for you. You feel a warm feeling inside. It is like your face has a soft light around it and your heart feels full.

Imagine you were playing a game with your friends. You had fun running and jumping. But then, a friend gives you a small, pretty thing they made. You feel very happy because they did something nice for you. You might smile a lot and say, I am so happy you did that for me.

Sometimes, you might feel this way if you got a job you wanted. You did all the work to get there, and now you get to do something you love. This feeling is very good because it means you are doing well in life.

You can also feel this way if you see a beautiful picture or hear a song you like. Your eyes get bright, and your face feels warm. You might want to listen to it again and again because it is so nice.

To be pleased does not mean you are always happy. It is about how you feel when something good happens. If you are very tired and then you eat a good food, you might feel a bit more happy than usual. But if you are very sad, you might not feel this way at all.
