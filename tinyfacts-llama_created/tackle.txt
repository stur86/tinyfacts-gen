To take something means you are ready to do it. You are not afraid anymore. You have planned how you will do it. You have thought about what you need to do.

When you take a ball, you are not just holding it. You are ready to run with it. You are ready to throw it to a friend. You are ready to catch it when it goes through the air.

You can also take a problem. You feel very sad and worried. You want to find a way out. You want to fix the thing that is wrong. You start to think. You try to find a way to make it better. You are taking the problem. You are facing it head on.

A player in a game might take a ball. They use their hands. They use their feet. They use their body. They are taking the ball. They are taking control of the game.

You can take a person. You can take a dog. You can take a car. You take them because you need them. You need the person to help you. You need the dog to help you in the house. You need the car to go to work or school.
