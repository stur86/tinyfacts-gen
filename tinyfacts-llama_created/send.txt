To send something means you give it to someone else. You put the thing in a box or a bag and then you move it to their house.

Imagine you have a small ball and you want to give it to your friend. You can pick the ball and hold it in your hand. Then you open the box where you keep the ball. You can see the ball inside. You put the ball into the box. Now the ball is in the box. You have given the ball to your friend.

You can also send a picture or a word. If you have a phone and you want to send a picture of a dog to a friend who lives far away, you can do this. You type the word and the picture goes to the friend. The friend looks at the picture and they tell you how they feel.

Sometimes you send money to someone you do not know. You use a computer or a phone to send the money. You tell the person you want to give them some money, and they get it. You do not see the person.

You can also send a thing that makes a sound. If you want to tell a story to a child, you can put the story into a small box. You close the box and you give it to a teacher. The teacher reads the story to the children. The children hear the story and they enjoy it.

Sending is a way to share things with others. It helps us stay in touch when we are far away.
