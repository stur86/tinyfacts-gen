The word largest is used to talk about the biggest thing in a group. You can say something is the largest if it is bigger than all the other things in that group.

Imagine you and your friends are playing a game. You all want to see who can pick the biggest ball from the bag. The person who picks the ball is the largest. They are the biggest because their ball is the biggest.
