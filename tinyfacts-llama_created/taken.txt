The word taken can mean different things. It can mean you get something from another person. Imagine you have a box of your favorite food. You want to eat it. You go to the table and take a piece. You have taken the food. Now you have it.

It can also mean you use something for a long time. If you have a book, you are taking it. You are holding it in your hands and looking at the words. You are reading it. This is taking a thing that is not yourself.

Sometimes, taken can mean you are taken away. If you are late to school, you might get in trouble. This is a bad thing that happens to you. It is not something you can control.

In some places, you might say taken because someone did something you did not want. You might have been trying to sleep, and the person in the room did not let you. They took you in their room because they wanted to be alone with you. This is a very bad thing to do.
