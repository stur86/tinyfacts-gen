A picture is made from many lines. Each line tells us something about the world. Sometimes we need to show how many steps are in a long story. Other times we make a picture of how a car moves. In this case, the lines show the car going left, then right, then down, and then up.

We can also make a picture of how things work. If you look at your hand, you see lines on your skin. These lines tell you how your skin is made. You can use these lines to learn about your body and how it works.

In the world, we use many different kinds of pictures. We use them to talk about things. We use them to show how things are. We use them to show how we feel. We use them to show how things are in space.

Sometimes we make a picture for fun. We draw pictures of animals. We draw pictures of houses. We draw pictures of people. We use these pictures to show how we feel when we see them.
