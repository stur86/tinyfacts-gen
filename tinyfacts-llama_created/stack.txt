To put things on top of one another is to make a tall thing. A book is a type of thing you can put on a table. You can also put papers, clothes, or food on top of each other.

Imagine you have many small pieces of paper. You put them one after the other on top of each other to make a long line. You keep doing this until your line is very tall. This tall line is called a big group. You can see many big groups put together to make a long line of many tall groups.

Sometimes, you use a tall thing to hold many things. You put a small box on top of another box on top of another. This is like making a tall group of tall groups. This tall thing is the top of many tall groups.

You can also put a small thing on top of another small thing. This is like making a tall group of small groups. The small thing is the top of many small groups. This is how we make tall groups from many small things.
