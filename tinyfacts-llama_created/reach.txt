To reach means to get near something. When you want to get close to a place, you reach it. Imagine you are in a room and you want to go to the table. To reach it, you walk around the room until you are right in front of it. You do not jump over it. You just walk and stop right in front of it.

You can also reach something that is far away. If you are standing on the top of a big building and you want to go down to the city, you reach it. You walk down the stairs until you are at the ground. This is a big step because it is a long way.

Sometimes, reaching something is hard. If you want to learn a new game, you have to keep trying and trying until you can do it. You reach the game when you finally know all the moves and you can play it without getting confused.

You can also reach something inside yourself. When you are sad, you reach out and touch your face. You reach out to your heart because it hurts. This helps you feel better.

To reach a high place is also important. If you want to go to the top of a tall building, you have to use your legs to push yourself up and your hands for help. You keep going until you are at the top. This is a big thing to do because it is hard and you have to be very strong.
