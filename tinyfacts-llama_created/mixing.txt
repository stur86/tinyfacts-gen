To add things together can make a new thing. When you add blue water and red water, the colors from both get together. You do not see the blue anymore, but you see red and blue together.

You can also add things that are hard. If you have a rock and a piece of wood, you can put them together to make a new thing. The rock does not change, but the wood is changed into a new thing that is strong.

Sometimes, you add air to a bag. The air makes the bag feel lighter. You do not see the bag change, but it feels different inside.

You can also add sound to music. When you put different sounds together, you get a new sound. A song with only one kind of sound is not very nice. When you add different kinds of sound, you get a new sound that is nice.

You can add a person to a group of people. When many people are together, they become a group. They all have their own ideas, but they work together. You can also add a thing to a world. When many things are together, they make the world a better place.
