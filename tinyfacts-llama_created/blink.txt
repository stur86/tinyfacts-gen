To blink is to close your eyes and open them again very quickly. This is how your eyes keep moving while you are sleeping.

Think of it like a door. When you close the door, you are stopping the air from going out. But your eyes are like a window. When you are watching something, your eyes are always looking at it. If you close your eyes, you are taking a quick look at what is in front of you.

When you blink, you are taking a quick look at your hand or your face. You are looking at your fingers or your teeth. This helps your brain know what is happening around you.

Sometimes, you blink a lot when you are feeling happy or excited. Your eyes are going very fast. You might blink many times in one second. This is because your brain is trying to keep all the pictures in your head at the same time.

You can also blink when you are afraid or surprised. You might close your eyes and look at something else. This helps you calm down and feel better.

It is a very small thing, but it is very important. It helps you stay safe and know what is happening around you.
