To Practically Explain

Practically is a word that means you do things that are real and true. It is about what you do in your life. It does not mean you are making a plan for the whole world.

Think of it like this. You are in a room. You see a picture. You want to see the picture again. You go to the room again and again. You look at it many times. You are doing what you do. This is practically.

Some people use this word when they talk to a god. They are not sure what they want. They ask the god to help them. The god might not answer. But they still try to do what they think is right. They are being practically.

You can also use this word when you are afraid. You do not want to go out in the dark. You stay inside your home. You are not really doing anything. You are just waiting for something to happen. This is practically. You are waiting.

To practically is not the same as to believe. To believe is to hold something on your mind. To practically is about doing what you need to do. It is about living your life.
