To search is to look for something. You can search for a word in a book. You can also search for a person. If you do not know where a thing is, you might look. You walk around the room, or you look at the floor, or you look in a bag.

Sometimes, you search for something you want. You think about it a lot. You ask your friends if they know where it is. You look in the house, the car, or your bag. If you do not find it, you might feel sad or angry.

You can also search for a person. If you do not know a person, you might look for them. You go to the school, the park, or the street. You ask people if they know who it is. You might look at signs that say who they are.

The word is important because it means you are doing this. You are looking in the dark, or you are walking through a room. You are trying to find what you are looking for. It is a way to find the right thing or person.
