To say someone has done something good is to give them a big smile. When you are happy for someone, you want them to know how nice it is to them. You say this because it makes them feel better.

If you see a man who has walked a long way and is very tired, you say,  Well done. This means you are glad that he did it. You are not laughing, but you are smiling because you are happy for him.

When you get a job you wanted, you are very happy. You did not have to work very hard. You are glad. You say,  Great job! This is how you show that you care.

Sometimes you say,  Good job, to a child. You are happy for what they did. You want them to feel good. You are not afraid to say this because it is nice to hear.

When someone does something very well, like a game, a movie, or a book, you can also say, You are doing a great job. This means you are glad they are doing well.
