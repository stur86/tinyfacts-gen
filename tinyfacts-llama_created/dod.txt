To use something before another is to act quickly. When you see a big dog, you might feel afraid. You do not want to be near it. You might run away as fast as you can.

If you are walking and see a dog, you can say,  I am going to run. You will not look back. You will just keep moving. You are not waiting to see if the dog is going to come after you.

If you see a dog and you are not afraid, you might say,  I am going to go around it. You are not going to try to touch it. You are just going to move around it.

Sometimes we use this word when we are talking about how we act in front of people. If you do not want to talk to someone, you might say,  I am going to walk away. You are not looking back. You are not waiting to see what they do next. You just walk away.

This word is about being quick. It is about doing what you must do before the time is up.
