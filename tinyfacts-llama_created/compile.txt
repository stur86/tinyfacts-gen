To build something is to put many small parts together. You start with a small thing you need. Then you add more parts to it until it is the thing you want. You keep adding parts until it is as good as you want it to be.

Sometimes you build things by hand. You take wood and make it strong. You use a power thing to cut the wood into long pieces. You use a power thing to make the wood go together. You might use a power thing that makes the wood move. You can also build things with computers. You write a set of words and numbers that the computer understands. The computer does the work for you.

You can also build things with people. You work together with many people to make a big team. Each person does a small part. They talk and agree on what they want to build. Then they start building. The team works together until they are all happy with what they made.

To build something is to put many small parts together. It is a way to make new things that you need.
