To be seen or to show something is to be known. This means that a person or thing is not hidden any more. When you open a book, you do not see the words inside. But then you read them, and now you know what they say. 

Sometimes you are not sure what is happening. You do not know the answer. But then you see it. You are able to see because you are watching. You see a dog running around a corner. You see a tree. You see a room. You are able to see because you are looking at it. 

You can also see something that is not there. If you close your eyes and then open them, you are seeing the room again. But if you do not close your eyes, you see something that was not there. You see a picture. You see a song. You see a word. You see a picture of a person. 

Sometimes people see a person and then they know they are a friend or a family person. You see them and you know that they are the person you want to be near. You know them because you have seen them many times before. 

Being seen is an important thing. When you are seen, you are noticed. You are heard. You are understood. You are known. You are not alone.
