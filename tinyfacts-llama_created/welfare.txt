The word for the well of something is called a well. A person or a group of people who do this work are called well people. They go into the ground and bring water up. They put the water in a big bag that they carry on their backs. In some places, the well people go far away into the dark under the ground. They stay there for a long time under the ground and bring the water up when they are done.

But the word for the water that is given to people when they need it is very different. This word comes from the word for being kind to a child. When you give a child a cup of water, you are being kind. When you give a person a box of food and a bag of clothes, you are also being kind. This word is used when there is not enough food or water for everyone.

If you do not take care of the well, the water can go away. People who work with the water have to make sure it stays strong and clean. They do this by putting things in the water that keep it from getting bad. They also help to keep the ground around the well safe so that people who go to drink the water do not get sick.

Sometimes, the word for the well of power is used. This is when people give money to the group that works with the well, so the well people can do their work. This helps the group to stay strong and keep giving water to everyone.
