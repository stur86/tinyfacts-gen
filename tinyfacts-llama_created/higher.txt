The word higher is used to talk about things that are bigger than you.

When you are standing on the ground, a tree is higher than you. It goes up into the sky and reaches far away. You can not see the top of the tree, but you can see that it is much taller than your head.

If you go up a lot, you can get higher and higher. You can see the sky all the way to the top. The air gets very thinker as you go up, but the sky stays the same color. The sky looks blue and very clear.

Sometimes, you might say that a thing is higher than another thing. This means that one thing is bigger or has more power. If you are in a room and you see a picture of a cat, the picture is higher than your hand. The picture is bigger and looks better than your hand.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If someone is higher up in a group than another person, it means they are in a better position. In a school, a teacher is higher up than a younger person. The teacher has more power and can give more important things.

The word higher is important because it tells us about things that are bigger or stronger. It helps us understand the world around us.
