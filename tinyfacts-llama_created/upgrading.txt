To use something new is to take it and make it work better. This happens when you buy a new car that is different from the one you have. The car might have more power or a better seat.

Imagine you have a small book and you use it for a long time. One day, you decide it is not for you anymore. You decide to buy a new book that is bigger and has more pictures. You tell the old book you are done with it. The old book is still the same, but the new book is new and better.

When you say a computer is getting an older, it means the old computer is not as fast as the new one. The computer in the new one has a better way to do things, so it works faster.

Sometimes, you might want to change the way you play music. You have a computer that makes music, but you do not like its sound. You decide to buy a new computer that makes music better. The computer in the new one is made to make the sound sound nicer, so it is a better way to play music.
