Things that are made by a green part of a thing grow from the ground can be called the colors of a place. These green parts make the air and the ground warm. They use the green parts from the ground to make food for themselves.

On a green part, tiny parts are inside the green part. These tiny parts make a soft white stuff that grows from the green part. This soft white stuff grows in long lines and makes the place look like a big bed of white. People call this soft white stuff the color of a place.

The long lines of the soft white stuff go out in many directions. Some of them go down to the ground. Some go up into the air. Some go across the green part. The long lines go to many other places. They carry the color of the place and the green part.

The long lines can go very far. They can go to the water. They can go to the ground. They can go into the air. They can go to the ground again. When the long lines go to the ground again, they bring back the color of the place.

The long lines are important for many things. They help grow food. They help make clothes and things to hold. They help make places look nice. They help make our world look like many different places.

Some green parts are only for one day. After that, they dry up and the green part stops growing. Other green parts keep growing all their lives. These green parts are the ones that grow many long lines. They keep growing and making the same color of place for many years.
