A place on land where the water is very close to the ground. People go there to play, walk, and have fun. There are often many tall things on top and many small ones on the ground. The water is usually clear and warm. You might see children playing in the water and dogs running around. There might also be tall green things growing on the top. Some places are very small and only a few feet wide, while others are very long and cover many hundreds of feet.

Places like this are often found near big bodies of water like the searches or big white water bodies. They are also near the side of a high ground where the land drops down very fast. This makes it easy to reach the water. In some places, the ground drops very quickly from one side to another. In other places, the ground drops very slowly. The ground in these places can be very hard to walk on.

People also use this word for a place with many small white things growing on it. These things are very small pieces of rock that grow in the ground. When the water from the big bodies of water comes into these small white things, they grow even bigger. These places can be very pretty and help keep the water clean.
