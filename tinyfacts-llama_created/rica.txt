To be nice is to show you care about people. You do this when you smile at them, or when you listen to what they say. You might ask them how they feel, or you might say hello. You are being kind.

Being nice is important because it helps people feel good. When you are kind, you make the world a better place. You make everyone feel like they are important. You are making the world a softer place.

People often say they are nice because they feel happy. They might be happy to see a friend, or they might feel happy when they do something they love. Being nice is how we show love and care.

To be nice does not mean you are perfect. Everyone makes a few tiny wrong moves. A child might say a bad word, or a dog might do something that is not right. You do not have to be perfect to be nice. You just have to want to help and to be kind.

You can also be nice to animals. If you see a dog that is afraid, you can be kind to it. You might say hello and move away. This helps the dog feel safe. Being nice to animals shows that you care about them.

Being nice can also mean you are happy to see your family. If you are happy to see your mom or dad, you are being nice. You are showing them you love them. This makes everyone happy.
