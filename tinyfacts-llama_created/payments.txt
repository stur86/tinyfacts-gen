To pay something means you give money or other things to someone else. This can be for things you already have. You give money to buy food or clothes. Or you pay for something you need to do. You might pay for a job, a school day, or a book.

When you pay, you show that you are ready to give your money. It is important to pay so you do not have to worry about it later. You do not have to remember to give your money every time you want to buy something.

You can pay money to the person who runs the store. You give them the money so they can buy things for you. Or you might pay for a ride. The person who drives the car gives you the money so you can go where you want.

Sometimes, you pay money for a game. You give your money to the person who makes the game, and they let you play. This is a way to share money with others so everyone can have fun.
