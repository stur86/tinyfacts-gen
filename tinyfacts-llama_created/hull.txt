A big thing in the water. It is like a box. Sometimes it is made of wood. Sometimes it is made of strong stuff from the ground.

You can build a small one that is not very big. It is for a small car. It has four sides and a round top. It is held up by four strong lines that go across the top. This keeps it standing up on the water.

A bigger one is for a big car. It is not held up by four lines. It has many sides and a round top. It is made of strong stuff that is very hard. This one can carry many people and many things.

You also use the word when you talk about a thing that is made to move on water. The water is like a big room. The box is the one that moves. It goes up and down, side to side, and back again. This is what we call a big thing on water.

In a building, a big thing is a tall one. It is for a house. It is the one that stands in the middle of the street. People can see it from far away. It has many windows and a big top that can go around. This is how we know that a big thing is there.
