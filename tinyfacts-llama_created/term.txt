To use a word means you are saying it. You might say it to a friend. You might say it to a teacher. You use words to talk about your day. A word is a thing that stands for a feeling, a thing, or a way of doing something.

Think about a word that tells you to be quiet. This word is like a sign. It says, when you hear this word, you should stop talking. You do not need to think, just listen. If you do not listen, you might get a time out or a warning.

Some words are very strong. They make you feel angry. If you hear these words, you might want to hit your head. Other words are very kind. They tell you to be nice to someone. These words help us feel happy and help us stay friends.

When you talk, you use a number of words. A word is one part of a group of words. If you have many words, you have many parts. A group of words can tell you something about the world. You can use words to tell a story, to explain a problem, or to show how you feel.

You can use a word many times. You might say a word many times when you are happy. Or you might say a word many times when you are sad. The word is the thing you are saying. It is the word, not you.

You can also use words in a group. If you have many words that are the same, you can put them together. This makes a group of words. You can use these groups to write a story or to talk about your day.
