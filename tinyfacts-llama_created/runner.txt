A runner is a person who moves quickly to stay ahead of others who are running. They wear a piece of hard stuff on their feet to help them run far. They often wear a shirt and a bag on their back to carry food and water.

To run, a person starts by standing still. Then, they start to push their legs and their feet together. They go forward one step after the other, over and over. This makes their legs get very hot. They have to use their arms to help. They use their arms to push the air out of their chest, and this makes their arms get very tired too.

A long time ago, running was a way to carry food from one place to another. Now, people run for fun. Some run in a line so everyone is running together. Others run in a straight line, but they do not carry food. They just run because they want to go very fast.

Some people run to be the first one to reach a place. They do this to beat others. Other people run because they like it. They like the feeling of moving quickly and the sound of their feet on the ground.

Running is good for people. It helps them get more air in their bodies. It makes their legs strong and their feet better. It also helps them feel calm and happy. If you run a lot, you can become very good at it.
