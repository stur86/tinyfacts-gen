To causes something is to make it happen or to help it. This can be done by talking or by making sounds with your voice. If you are angry, you may cause a person to be afraid. If you are happy, you may cause others to smile.

You can also cause a big problem. If you make too much noise in a quiet room, it can be a problem. You are causing the other person to feel angry.

In a small group, you may cause a person to do something. You might say,  Stop doing that!  This means you are telling the person to stop doing something and to do something else instead.

Sometimes, you can cause a person to feel a strong feeling. This can happen when you speak very loud or when you use a word that is very strong. You are making them feel the thing you are talking about.

If you are a mother, you cause your child to be quiet. You use your voice so they do not cry. This is an important way to show love and care for your child.
