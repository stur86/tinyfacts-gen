A person who wants something often makes a small paper. They write the name of what they want, and why they want it. They might tell why they need it and how it will help them. They send this paper to a person they trust, usually a head of a group or a high person.

This person looks at the paper. They might think it is a good idea. If they agree, they will talk to the head of the group and tell them what they want. The head might say yes or no. This is how people get things they want.

Sometimes, a group of people want something. They write a long paper. They tell why they want it. They might ask a head to listen. If a head agrees, they will tell everyone who wants it.

In some places, people can make a small paper to ask for a thing. They are like the people in a story.
