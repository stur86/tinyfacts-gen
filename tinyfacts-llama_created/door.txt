A door is a thing you open to let something in or out. It has a round part in the middle and long sides that go all around it. You can push it open or pull it open. When you push it open, the world on the other side comes in.

You use a door to get into a house, a school, a store, or a car. You might need to use the door to walk through a park or to go to a movie. You can also use a door to close the window when it is dark outside.

Sometimes, you open the door with your hand. You can pull on the door or turn a small round thing inside the door. This helps the door open. If you open a door too many times, the wood can get wet and the door can fall down.

There are many kinds of doors. Some are small and fit into a space. Some are big and cover a room. Some have a lock on them so you can not open them. Some have a bright light on them so you can see what is on the other side.

You also need to be carefully about doors. If you do not close them, bad things can come out. You must always be ready to close them before you leave a room or a car.
