To barely means you are not doing well. It is not the same as being very bad or very sad. When you are barely, you are still standing, but you are not as strong as you should be.

Imagine you have a very bad cold. You are not feeling well at all. You are almost gone. You could not even get out of bed. You are barely there. You are not really here.

You can also barely be happy. If someone is very sad, they might not even try to be happy. They are barely holding it together.

To barely something means you are letting something go. If you have a big bag of money, you might barely have enough money to buy food. You are not really getting enough money to buy what you need. You are barely in the right place.

Sometimes, you barely know something. You do not remember something at all. You are not really sure what happened. You are just not sure.

It is also possible to barely feel something. If you are not feeling very happy, you might feel very sad. Or, if you are not feeling very strong, you might barely be able to move your body.
