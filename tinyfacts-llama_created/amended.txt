An am made something that is wrong.

You and your friend have a fight. You both say the same thing. But then you both write it down. You both sign it. This makes it clear that you both agree. This is an am made.

You can also make an am for your family. You and your mom or dad make a promise. You promise to be good. You show them that you care. This am is between you and your family.

Sometimes, an am is about money. A group of people want to do something. They want to pay each other. But one person says they will not pay. This makes everyone sad. The group can be very angry. They might fight. This is an am made because of a problem with money.

Another am is about a house. You and your family all live there. You all have the same chair. You all use the same table. This am makes everyone happy. Everyone knows the same thing.
