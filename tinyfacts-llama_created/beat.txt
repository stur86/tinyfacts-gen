To beat someone means to out do them. You can beat a person at a game or a fight. A person who is very strong can beat another person who is very fast. If you beat a dog, you have shown that you are better than him.

You can also beat something. If you beat a book, you have found a book that you like. You have read it and you like the words.

To beat a time means to do something before another person. If you beat a run, you have done it before everyone else. This can happen in many ways. A person might run very fast and beat the time. A person might beat the time to run a very hard race.
