To be a part of something means you are a small part. You are not the whole, but you help make it work.

Think of a family. The mom and dad are the top. The kids are the small ones. The mom and dad help the kids, and the kids need the mom and dad to live. If the kids go to school, the mom and dad are still a part of the family. They help the kids learn.

An office is also a part of a big group. The manager is the top. The people in the office help the manager do their job. The manager tells them what to do, and they do it. Without the office, the manager would not have anyone to work with.

You can be a part of a group by helping. If you like a game, you might join a team. You do not play the game alone. You work with other people who share the same love for the game. You all try to do well and have fun.

You can also be a part of a story. A book has many parts. There is the first part, the second part, the third part, and so on. Each part is important. When you read a story, you are reading each part. You are a part of the story.

Being a part of something does not mean you are the only one. There are many parts. You might be part of a group of friends. You are part of the same group. You help each other.
