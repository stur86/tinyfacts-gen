An answer is something you give to a question. You write or say it. You must be ready to tell the right thing. In this, a person does not just hear a story. They have to show what they know.

You might sit in a room with many other people. You are the only one who will answer the same question. The teacher asks you the question. You think about it for a minute. You write down what you think. Then, you read the questions from the book. You read them very carefully.

When you are ready, you give your answer. If you answer the question, you get a mark. You do not get a mark if you do not answer at all. The teacher looks at each person. You might get some marks for being good and some for being bad. The person with the most marks is the one who did the best.

In this way, we learn. We learn what is important. We learn what we should not do. We learn how to be good at a job or how to be a person.
