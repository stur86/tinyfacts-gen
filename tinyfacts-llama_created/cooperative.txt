To work together is to do things with other people. You might be part of a group of kids. You all have a game to play. One kid is the one who keeps the ball. Another kid is the one who keeps the other ball. Everyone does their part. They all work together.

This can happen with money too. If many people want to buy a big thing, they can form a group. They all give their money. The money is put together. Then they buy the thing. They work together to make sure everyone gets what they want.

Sometimes, people work together because they are different. One person is very good at fixing things. Another person is very good at writing. Together, they build a house. They use what each person is good at. This is a way to use the good things of many people for one place.

In a group, people also have to follow a set of things they all agree on. This helps keep everyone happy. It is like a big game that everyone works together in.
