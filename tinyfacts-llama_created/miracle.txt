A person or thing that does something that is not supposed to happen can be a surprise. It can also happen when a group of people all work together in a very good way to help each other.

Think about a child who is afraid to go to a big room. They might shake or cry. But then something strange happens. The child is standing in the room and they see a dog that is bigger than them. The dog is not afraid. The child is amazed.

This is a kind of surprise because it happens when no one expects it. It is not a thing that happens every day. It is something that happens one time only.
