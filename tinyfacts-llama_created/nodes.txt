A group of things that help a long line or a big group work together is called a group of things. In a computer, a group of numbers is a group of things. If you want to find where something is, you can look at each number in the group. You can see where each number is.

A group of things can have many parts. Each part does a small job. Some parts help with the beginning, some parts help with the middle, and some parts help with the end. When all the parts work together, the whole group does a job.

Imagine you are walking in a long line of people. Each person has a job. The person at the front of the line helps the person next to them. The person next to them helps the person after them. The person after them helps the person before them. This is like a group of things.

In a group of things, you can also look at the number of people in the group. If there are many people, the group is big. If there is only one person, the group is small.
