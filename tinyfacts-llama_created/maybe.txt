Maybe is a word you use to think about something. It is like a tiny word that helps us decide what is true or what is not. You can use it when you are not sure.

Imagine you see a cat. Maybe the cat is black. Maybe the cat is white. You do not know which one. You do not want to say if you think the cat is one color or the other because you do not know. You just say maybe so you keep your mind open.

You can also use it when you are thinking about a plan you want to do. Maybe you want to go to the park today. Maybe you want to watch a movie later. You do not know which one you want to do. Maybe you want to do both.

Sometimes, maybe is also about not doing anything. Maybe you do not feel like going out tonight. Maybe you just want to stay home and watch a show. You might feel a bit sad, but you do not want to do anything else.

You can also use it when you are talking to a friend. Maybe they are feeling sad. Maybe they need a friend to talk to. You listen and you say maybe you can help them feel better later.

Maybe is a word that helps us be kind. If you say maybe, you are not sure you are right. You are trying to find out. You are not afraid to ask questions. This makes us feel better.
