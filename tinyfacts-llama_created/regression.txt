To go back is to start from a place you are at now and go back to a time before. This is important when we are trying to find out why things happened.

Imagine you are a person who loves to play music. You play a song every day and feel very happy. Then you learn a new song and you love it even more. You play it many times. After many weeks, you feel sad because you are not playing the song as much anymore. You start to think about why the music is not as good as it used to be.

You go back to the time when you first started to play. You think about the days when you were very happy and excited to play. You remember how you felt when you were the first one to play the song. You start to wonder why the music is not as good now. You start to think about what happened.

You go back to the time when you were a little child and you loved to play with a ball. You remember how you felt when you held the ball. You remember the way the wind felt when you threw it and the way it felt when it hit the ground. You go back to the time when you were very young. You see your older brother and you feel happy to see him. You remember how he used to play with you and how you used to play with him. This makes you feel a little sad because time goes by very fast.

You went back to a time before. You learned something new. You remembered why you started to play the music in the first place. You remembered why you loved it so much.

This happens with people too. When we are happy, we often go back to a time when we were younger and we felt more care free. We remember the way things were. We remember the people we loved. We remember the fun we had. This helps us feel better when things are bad.

So, going back is about remembering the past and why things happened. It helps us learn from our past so we can do better in the present.
