To have something is to own it. If you had a small dog, it is a thing you have. You care for it and it cares for you. You are the owner of the dog.

If you had a big house with many rooms, the house is a thing you have. You pay money to live there.

When you had a friend, you were friends. You liked each other. If you still had your friend now, you are still friends.

You had money in your pocket before you spent it. You had things in your bag. You had a book to read. You had a ball to play with.

Now, you do not have those things anymore. You spent them. You lost them. You are not the same person.
