A big body of water where people catch many animals. People use big moving things with long lines to catch the animals. When the water is cold, the animals are often found in the deep parts. When the water is warm, the animals are found near the bottom where the water is not too deep.

These places are very important for us. The water gives us food and helps keep the water clear so we can see what is around us. The animals give us something we need to stay strong. Many people work together to make sure these places stay safe and so we can keep eating from them for many years to come.

Some places have many different kinds of animals. Some have only one kind of animal. Some have many kinds of animals. The animals can change how the water looks and how the water feels. The animals can also help keep the water clean if they eat food from the water.
