A group of people who want to talk about a problem or a new idea often stand near a door that leads to a room where important people are talking. They might wear clothes that show they are there or sit on chairs near the door. This is the name of this kind of group.

Imagine you want to ask a teacher if you can work on a paper. You might go to the office and tell the person who is in the room with the important people. They might say yes or no. If yes, you can sit down and talk to them. But if no, you might have to wait for another day.

A group like this is called a group of people who want to talk about a problem. They might be worried about something that is happening. They might want to fix it or see what is going on. They might want to get the people in the room to listen and help.

Sometimes, people in the group are very good at what they do. They know about the problem and how to fix it. They might give their ideas and listen to what others have to say. This helps everyone work together.

If you are in a group of people who want to talk about a problem, you should be ready to listen. You should also be ready to say what you think. This is important because everyone has an idea. By listening, you can learn more and help the group find a way that works for everyone.

The word comes from the word for a room where people from different places go to talk. It is like a place where people can talk about things they care about.
