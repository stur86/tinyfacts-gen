To open something means you make it bigger or more wide. You can open a door or a box. If you look at a box, you might open the top so you can see inside. You are taking away something that is closed and making room for more air.

When you open a book, you are turning the cover around. You are making the cover so the words are not covered up. You are letting the words come out and share their stories.

You can also open a tree when it is still a small one. As it grows, the tree gets bigger and bigger. It turns out to be much bigger than you could ever imagine.

Sometimes, you open a bag to see what is inside. You might find food or clothes. You are taking something out of the bag to see what it is. This helps you know what you need for the day.

Opening can also mean to start something new. When you open a box, you are starting a new game. You are letting a story begin. You are moving forward into a new place.

In some ways, opening is like letting go. When you open a door, you are letting something leave. You are giving room for more to enter. This can be a good thing because it makes space for new ideas.
