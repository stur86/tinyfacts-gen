To begin something is to start it. It is the first step. If you start a story, you begin it. If you start to build a house, you start the building. You use the word to talk about the start of a thing.

Imagine you are at a new school. You are very happy. You feel a big smile on your face. This is the beginning of the school year. Everything that happens after this is new. There is no beginning before the beginning, so there is no end.

You can also begin something new. If you decide to learn a new game, you start to learn it. You play the game every day. This is your beginning. You are starting something that will take you a long time.

Sometimes, the word can mean the very first day of a week. If it is your first day of school, you begin the week. You start the day with a new day. This is like the beginning of the beginning.

The word can also mean the start of a big thing. Imagine a big building. It has many floors. The first floor is the beginning. From there, you go up one floor at a time.

In our lives, we begin many times. We start a new job. We start a new school year. We start a new year. We start to feel a new feeling. We begin to learn something new.

So, to begin something means you start it. It is the first step.
