To live is to be here now. It is about the things that happen in your body every day. Your heart beats and your brain works. You breathe in air and you give out the same air again. You eat food and you grow stronger. Your body keeps you going.

A person lives when they are living. This is about feeling and enjoying the moment. You might be happy when you see a beautiful picture. You might be sad when you hear a sad song. You might be angry when you see a mean word. These are all different ways of living.

You can also live in a big city or a small town. You can live in a house or a car. You can live in a place that is far away from your family or your friends. You can live alone or you can live with many people.

To live is also about being ready. You need to be ready for things that might happen. You need to be ready to help someone if they are hurt. You need to be ready to love your family and your friends. You need to be ready to face your own fears.

Finally, to live is about being part of a group. You live with people who have the same hopes and fears as you. You share things with each other. You laugh together and you cry together. This is a good thing because when you live with others, you feel less alone.
