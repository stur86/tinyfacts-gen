The word filled something means to add many things to it. When you fill a bottle with water, you put more water inside. The bottle becomes bigger. You have filled the bottle.

Imagine you have a bag of food. You do not have enough food to eat. Then a friend gives you more food. You put the food in the bag. The bag becomes full. You have filled the bag with food.

You can fill a house with books. You put many books on the floor and in the walls. The house becomes a book room. You have filled the house with books.

You can also fill a bottle with air. When you put air into a bottle, the bottle gets bigger. The air makes the bottle heavy. If you put too much air, the bottle might break.

Sometimes we fill a cup with food. We drink the food from the cup. The cup becomes empty. We have filled the cup with food.

The word filled is also used when people talk about people. If someone is very happy, they might feel filled. This means the person is full of good things and does not want to stop.

You can also fill a room with music. When you put many songs together, the room becomes filled with sound. You feel happy and want to move your body.

The word filled is used in many ways. We use it to talk about many different things, like books, houses, cups, and people.
