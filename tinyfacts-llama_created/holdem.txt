To hold something is to keep it in your hand. You might hold a small ball, a piece of paper, a key, or a small animal. If you are very good at something, you might hold a job.

In a game, you hold a card. You look at the card and try to know what number it is. Then you play it. If you hold the number, you get points. If you hold a number that is not on the card, the other person gets points.

A person might hold a drink. They might drink it slowly, one small drink at a time. This helps them stay calm and think. If they feel too much, they might need to hold their drink.

To hold something is to keep it close to your body. It is like holding a small friend. You can feel the hand, the skin, and the soft parts inside. This helps you feel safe and happy.

Sometimes, you hold a word. You might feel a strong feeling when you say it. It is like holding a key to a place in your mind. When you say it, you open the door to a new idea or a happy moment.
