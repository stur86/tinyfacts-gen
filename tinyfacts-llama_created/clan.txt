A group of people who share a family or a place can be called a family. But some people also talk about groups that have a leader. This leader might have a name, a face, and a way of doing things. They are the ones in the middle.

Think of a group like a big living room. There are many people sitting there. If one person does something wrong, the others might get angry. The people in the middle might say, the person who did that should leave. This is how a group can stay together, but it can also make things more hard.

In some groups, the people are very close. They share their food, their clothes, and their stories. They might even help each other when things get bad. These groups are like a big family.

In other groups, the leader is the one who makes the plans. They decide where to go, what to do, and who should do what. This is why they are called a group with a leader. The people follow the leader because they trust them.

Some groups are only for people who share the same place. If you move to a new city, you might need to start a new group. But if you all move to the same place, you can be called a group.

The word has another meaning. If you are part of a group, you are also part of the whole. You are a part of something bigger than you. This is why we say, you are part of a group.

In the end, a group can be a family, a team, or a place. It is a way to stay together when things are hard.
