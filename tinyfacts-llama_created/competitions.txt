To have a big fight with someone

When people want to see who is better than another, they do a fight. This is a way to show who has more power, or who can do something the best. In this kind of fight, people do not use their hands. They use their faces, their arms, and their legs. They try to hit the other person.

There are many kinds of fights. One is for a game. A man and a woman are in a game where they have to run. They run as fast as they can. Then the man or woman who gets to the end first is the one who is the best. Another kind of fight is for money. A group of people go to a place and they fight. The one who is the last person standing gets the money.

Sometimes people have a fight because they are angry. They want to tell the other person that they are wrong. They may shout and scream to show that they are mad. Other times, people have a fight because they are afraid. They might fight because they are afraid of a thing that is in the room. They might fight if someone comes into their room and says they are wrong.

Fights can be very hard to watch. Some people want to stay inside and watch movies, but they also want to see what happens when two people fight. It is like looking at two different stories at the same time.
