Life is the time you spend in the world. It is the time you live in the here and now. Every moment is a part of life.

Imagine you are a small animal. You go out in the morning to find food. You eat and you move. You find a safe place to rest. You sleep. Then you go out again in the morning to find more food.

Life is like this. You do many different things every day. You live, you grow, you learn, you love, you cry, you laugh, you become older. You do not know what the next moment will bring. You must always be ready.

You are a person. You are a being. You are a thing that can feel, think, and act. You are a part of the world. You are part of the people around you.

Life is also about change. You grow as a person. You learn new things. You meet new people. You do new things. You change who you are. This is a big part of life.

Sometimes life is hard. There are many things that can go wrong. You may lose a person you love. You may get hurt. You may feel sad. You may feel scared. But even in those hard times, you are still living. You are still here.

Life is also about the world around you. You see the bright sun in the morning. You hear the sound of a dog in the night. You see the stars at night. You feel the wind in your hair. You feel the ground under your feet.

Every person has their own life. You do not have to be like anyone else. You can be who you want to be. You can do what makes you happy. You can love the way you feel. You can be kind to others. You can do good. This is what life is about.

So, life is about being here. It is about living in the moment. It is about finding the good in every day. It is about loving and being loved. It is about growing and changing. It is about being part of the world and the world being part of you.
