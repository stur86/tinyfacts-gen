A thing that joins many other things together is called a person or an animal. This is also how you join your shoes so they fit. It is the thing that holds the world together.

Imagine you have a small box. You use the box so your drinks do not go out. You put a small piece of wood or a hard thing from a tree on the box. This small piece holds the box to your hand. Without it, the box would fall off.

A person can also be a person who joins other people. If many people want to go to the same place, they use a person who can carry them. This person carries them from one place to another. They are like a person who holds many people together.

In another way, a person can join things in your body. If you want to be strong, you might use a person who makes you feel better. They give you power so you can move around and stay safe. They are like a person who holds your body together.

You also use a person who lets you see. The person you look at can show you things that are far away. This person lets you see the world in a different way than you could without them.

All these are about joining many other things so you can move, stay safe, or feel better.
