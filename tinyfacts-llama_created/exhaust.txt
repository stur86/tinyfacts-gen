To wear out means to use something until it is no longer able to do its work. When you wear your shoes, your feet get tired. You feel the ground under your feet. Your legs become heavy because you have been moving around a lot.

When you talk about an animal, it can wear out too. If you give a dog too many pieces of food, it might start to feel very full. It will want to lie down and rest. This is because its body is tired from eating all the food. It needs time to eat and then to sleep.

Sometimes, people wear out their clothes. If you wear a shirt for too long, it gets old. The color may start to change and the shirt might feel heavy. You might want to throw it away because it is no longer good for you.

An old car can also wear out. The parts inside the car start to break down. The paint on the car gets old and the car becomes smaller. You might hear the car make a strange sound when you start it. This is because the old parts need to be fixed before the car can move around.

When something is worn out, it is no longer able to do its job. It needs to be fixed or changed. This is why we say that something has worn out.
