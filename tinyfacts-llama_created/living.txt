To live means you are here and you are living. You are doing things. You are eating and drinking. You are getting ready for the day. You are doing things that make you happy.

Living is about being. You are not just a thing. You are a person. You have a heart and you have a brain. You can think and you can feel. You can love and you can cry. You are a part of the world.

You live in a world with other people. You share a place with trees and animals. You use things to stay safe and to move from one place to another. You need air to breathe and water to drink. You need food to eat and to stay strong.

Living is also about change. Things change every day. The sun is up in the morning. The sun goes down at night. The wind moves through the trees. The ground is wet with rain. The world is different at different times. You have to learn to live with those changes.

Sometimes people live in small houses. They have a bed and a table. They eat food from a box. They watch a small box with pictures. They listen to a small box with music. They live in a world with other people who are not their friends. They have to work and pay money. They live in a world where some people have more power than others.

But most people live in big cities. They have many houses and many cars. They walk to work or they drive a car. They eat food from a big box. They watch a big box with pictures. They listen to a big box with music. They live in a world where many people are together and many people are different. They have to work together to build a better life.

To live is to be here and to be with the world around you. It is to do things and to feel. It is to be part of a group and to be part of the world.
