To study something is to think about it. You might look at a book, listen to a teacher, or watch a movie. You want to learn more. You might ask a friend, a family person, or a computer to help you.

When you study, you use your brain. You look at words and numbers. You try to understand why things happen. You also listen to the words and try to remember them.

Many people study for school. They read books, do work, and listen to talks. They study their hands and body too. This helps them know how the world works and how to do things.

You can study anything. You can study a person, a place, a thing, or a story. You can study how people act, how things grow, or how things change. This helps you understand the world and how to live in it.

Sometimes, you study something hard. This can be a number, a word, or a story. When you study hard, you are learning new things. You are trying to remember and understand them. You are building a bigger picture.

To study is to be ready for the next step. It is to know what you need to do next. It is to be ready to learn more and to do something new.
