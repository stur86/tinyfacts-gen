To be in a middle place is to have two sides. One side is the side you like. The other side is the side you do not like. When you are in the middle, you are not leaning to either side. You are not very much on one side and not very much on the other.

Imagine you are at a table with your friends and family. You sit next to your mother. She is the person who takes care of you. You like her. But your father is the one who buys the food and makes you go to bed. You do not like him. You do not want him to be in the middle of the table with you. You want him to be the one who does not sit next to you.

You are not very much on your mother side and your father side. You are in the middle. You have to listen to both sides and try to understand why they are angry. You try to feel what they feel, even if you do not agree with them. You learn that everyone has their own way and that you are not alone in your own mind.

In this way, being in a middle place is about not wanting to take sides, but still being a part of what is happening. It is about having two parts of you that are not quite the same.
