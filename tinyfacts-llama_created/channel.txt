A line is a line of things that are put together. You can put many things on one line. This line goes through a place. The line might go under the ground. It might go over a high wall. It might go through a city. Each line has a name. People use these names to talk about the line.

When you watch a movie, you see many lines on the floor. Each line shows the story. If you move the line so that the story starts from the top, you get a different picture. The line is like a story about a place or a person.

Sometimes, you might put things on a line for fun. You might put music on one line. You might put pictures on another line. You might put words on a third line. The lines can be very long. They can go around the world.

There is also a line for water. This line goes under the ground. It carries water from the top of the world to the bottom. The water goes through many lines of the ground. Each line helps the water reach the next line.

You can also use this word to talk about how people act. If you are a good person, you act like a line of people. You follow the lines of what is right. You do not break the lines.
