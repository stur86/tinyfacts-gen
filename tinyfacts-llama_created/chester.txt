To carry something or to hold a thing for you is to take it. This happens many times in our lives. A person may carry money, food, or clothes. A child may carry a small bag with their books. A dog may carry a small thing that is important.

In some places, a person who carries a thing is called a carrier. They might carry a bag that is full of things that people need. They move from one place to another, bringing things to where people need them.

We also say this word when we say a person is a carrier of a story. They carry the story with them. They might tell it to a child while walking, or they might read it while sitting in a room.

Sometimes, when a person wants to tell you something, they use this word. They might say, I am a carrier of a story about a man who lived a long time ago. This means they are telling you a story they carry with them.
