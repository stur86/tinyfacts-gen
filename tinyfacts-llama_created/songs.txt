A song is a short piece of music that you hear. It is often made of only three things: notes, a beat, and a story. Some songs are very short, while others are very long.

When you hear a song, you can move your body. You might want to jump or dance. You might want to single your voice. Or you might just want to sit and listen. The song tells a story or makes you feel a way. Some songs tell stories about a person or a place. Other songs tell you to feel a way or to be happy.

Some songs have only a few words. These are called pop songs. They are often very short and easy to remember. Other songs are very long and have many words. These are called long songs. They might have many different parts, like a long story or a long beat.

People make songs for different reasons. Some make songs for fun. Some make songs for their friends or family. Some make songs for work. Some make songs for money. Some people write songs because they love the way music sounds.
