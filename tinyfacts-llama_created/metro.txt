A word you hear every day.

A long time ago, people who lived in cities had a way to go from one place to another. They used a lot of money and a lot of people. They walked a lot. They would go to the store, then to the school, then to the place where people go to talk, and then back home. It took a long time.

One person had a great idea. He said, why do we need to walk all this way? He said we can take a box of wood with a round top. You put the top on, and you can go very fast. You go through many streets. The top keeps you on the right road. You do not have to walk all the way. You only go where the road goes.

Many cities built this kind of thing. It is under the ground. You can not see it from the street. You can only hear the sound of people inside. It is very quiet. You can walk by it, or you can go through it. It is a great help to people who need to go many places in one day.

In big cities, you can use many of these boxes. You can buy them and ride them. You do not need to walk. You can go fast and see a lot of places at once. Some people use them to go to work. Some people use them to go to school. Some people use them to go to the store. Some people use them to go to the movies.

Some of these boxes can go very far. You can go to a far city and come back to your city. You can go to a city far away and come back to your city. You can go to a city you have never heard of and come back home. You can go to a city you love and come back there again.

You can also use them to go to a place where people go to rest. Many big cities have these boxes. You can go there, sit down, and rest. You do not have to walk or stand. You just sit down and wait for the box to go to the place.

These are the things people call a box under the ground. They help us go from one place to another quickly. They are very important for cities with many people.
