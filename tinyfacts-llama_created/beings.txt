Being is a big word that all living things use. It is about who you are and what you are like.

You are a thing that is here. You are not a far away thing in space. You are right next to the people you love and the things you see every day. You are a living thing with a body and a head. Your body is made of parts that hold your body together. Your head is where you see and hear and talk to the world.

Being is not just about what you look like. It is also about how you feel. You feel when you are happy, when you are sad, when you are angry, or when you are scared. You feel your heart beating in your chest. You feel your legs and arms moving when you walk. You feel the wind on your skin when you go outside. You feel the ground under your feet when you run. You feel the sun on your face when you are warm. You feel the cool night air when you are quiet.

Being can also mean how you act. You act like you are the top of the world when you are very happy. You act like you are the bottom of the world when you are very sad. You act like you are the most important person in the room when you are the one in the middle. You act like you can do anything when you are the one who can.

Being is not just about one person at a time. It is also about many people at the same time. You are part of a group of people who all live in the same town. You are part of a school with many other kids. You are part of a group of animals living in a place with many other animals. You are part of a group of things that go around the world.

Being is also about what you do. You are a person who works. You are a person who studies. You are a person who loves to play. You are a person who loves to read. You are a person who loves to be with your family. You are a person who loves to go to the park and play with your friends.

Sometimes being can mean how you feel about yourself. You might feel like you are the best person in the world. You might feel like you are very strong. You might feel like you are very good at your job. You might feel like you are the most important person you know. You might feel like you can do anything.

But being can also mean how you feel about other people. You might feel happy when a friend laughs at your joke. You might feel sad when a friend does not want to talk to you. You might feel angry when someone does something you do not like. You might feel scared when you hear a loud noise. You might feel happy when you see a beautiful picture.

Being is a big part of our lives. It is what makes us who we are. It is what makes us feel and act. It is what makes us friends and loved ones. It is what makes us feel like we are home.
