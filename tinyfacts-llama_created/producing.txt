To make something new can be very important. This word talks about how things are made.

You can make a thing from nothing. You start with a piece of paper, a ball of wood, or a small rock. You use your hands to move the pieces until they become the thing you need. This is how a house is made. People make houses from wood, and then they put on the walls and the floors.

You can also make something from old things. If you have a broken chair, you can use the wood to make a new chair. You take the broken pieces of the chair, and you put them together again. This is how a new chair is made.

Sometimes, you have to start with a small thing. You might start with a tiny piece of paper and build up to a big book. You make the book word by word. You add the first few words, then the next few, and so on. This is how a book is made.

We also use this word to talk about how people work. If a person is a teacher, they are making things for others. They read the words on a piece of paper and then they tell the words to other people. They are making things that help people learn.

When you work hard to make something, you are often said to be doing something. This means you are moving from one thing to another. You are building a new thing from old things, or you are making a new thing from nothing.
