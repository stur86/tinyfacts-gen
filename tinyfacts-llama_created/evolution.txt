To change is one of the most important things that happens in the world. Everything is made of one kind of thing. This thing is the very start of everything. The thing goes through many changes. Each time it changes, it becomes different from the one before it.

The first thing is a tiny thing. It is very small and very hard. It can only make one kind of thing. This tiny thing is the start of everything. It makes many other things, one after another. As it makes more and more of these things, it becomes less and less like the one thing before it.

The tiny thing changes into a living thing. The living thing can move on its own. It can eat other tiny things. It can make more of these things. It can also make more of the ones it already has. This is how life starts.

The living things keep changing over many, many years. They are all different. Some are big. Some are small. Some have hard parts. Some have soft parts. Some can fly. Some can walk on water. Some can live in the dark. Some can live in the light.

Some living things change very quickly. They can grow and change in just a few hundred years. Other living things take many, many years. They change very slowly.

Things that start as one kind of thing can change into many kinds of things. This is how new kinds of animals, new kinds of trees, and new kinds of people come into being.

Sometimes, living things change in a strange way. They might grow in a way that makes them look different. They might make new parts from the parts they already have. They might even change their way of looking at the world. This is what happens with animals that can change color, with animals that can change their body parts, and with animals that can jump high.

People also change. We grow older. We learn new things. We get new ideas. We change how we feel. We change our bodies. We change our minds. We change the things we believe.

So, to change is to go from one kind of thing to another. It is the way the world changes over time. Every living thing, every animal, every person, and every thing on the ground, is going through this change.
