To look at the past is to see how things have changed over time. It is about what happened before and how it has become. People and places have changed a lot, and what we see today is not the same as what we saw before.

When we talk about a person, we are talking about how they lived. We look at their life and their work. We see how they made things and how they lived. We see how they were and how they became.

A story is like a long look at the past. It starts with a beginning and ends with a end. It tells us what happened before the story starts. It shows us what happened and why. It can tell us about people and places, about time, and about how things change.

A person can have a long life. They might see many years go by. They might see things happen that they never saw before. They might see their family change. They might meet new people and see new places.

Time is the way we see the past. It is the order of what happened. One day is before another day. One year is before another year. We move through time, looking back and forward.

We learn from the past. We learn about how things work. We learn about what happened before. We learn about how to stay safe. We learn about our own lives and how they fit into the world.

People work to remember the past. They write about it. They tell stories. They keep old things. They look at pictures. They talk about how things were.

Sometimes we want to change the past. We want to make things better. We want to fix the bad things. We want to make our lives better. We try to do things that will help us.
