A computer is a box that can do many things. It can remember things from the past, like numbers and words. It can also do new things that are not in the past. It can help us work, play games, and talk to people over the phone.

A computer uses numbers to do its work. Each number is like a tiny piece of paper. When you give the computer a set of numbers, it uses those numbers to make the computer do what you want. It might add two numbers together to make a new number, or it might find a number in a huge book of numbers.

You can tell a computer to do many different things. If you ask it to look at a picture of a cat, it will find the cat. If you ask it to write a story, it will write words that make sense. You can also give it a set of numbers and it will do a quick check to see if they are right.

Computers are like very good books. They remember everything you have asked them and can do it again without you having to tell them. They can do a lot of work very fast. This is why computers are so important in our lives today.
