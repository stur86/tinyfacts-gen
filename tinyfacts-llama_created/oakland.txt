A city in the place where the water from the big water goes into the ground is called a big city. This one is in a place where the big water goes into the ground. This place has a lot of trees. The trees are very strong. Some of them are as old as the world. They can stay standing for many years. People in this city like to walk in the woods behind the city. They go there to get away from the city noise.

The city was very important in the time when the first people came there. They made homes out of the wood from the old trees. Later, the city became a place where people went to work and to learn. Today, the city has many schools and places where people go to be heard. There is a big building with a big room where people go to talk. The city is also a place where people from all over the world come to be heard.

The city is near a big water. The water goes into the ground and then comes up again. This water is very important for the people who live here. It gives them water to drink and makes the ground stay together.
