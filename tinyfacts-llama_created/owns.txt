To own something means you are the one who has it. You keep it and you do not let anyone else have it.

Imagine you find a big rock in the ground. You could keep it and keep it for a long time. You could show it to your friends and they could see it. But if you do not find it again or if someone else finds it first, you do not own it anymore.

You own things that you buy with your money. You might own a car, a house, or a dog. You are the one who pays for them and you get to keep them. You can also own things that are not money. You might own a book, a computer, or a piece of land.

When you own something, you are safe. You do not have to worry about someone else taking it from you. You can do what you want with it and you are the one who decides.

Sometimes, you might own a job. This means you work for a person or a business. You do the work and you get paid for it. You own your time and your space. You can decide when you work and how you work.

The word is important because it helps us talk about people and things. When we say a person owns something, we mean they are the one who has it. It helps us understand who is in control.

To own something is not just about money. You can also own a feeling or a memory. You might own a happy memory from a time with your family. You can own a piece of your heart because it is a very important thing.
