To everyone means you are the biggest person. When you say, everyone, you mean that no one is better than you. You have the most power and the most money. You are the one everyone looks up to.

Think about the things you do every day. You do the same things as everyone else. You walk to school, eat breakfast, and go to work. You are like every other person. You do not have a different life.

Some people think that everyone is the same. They say that everyone has the same problems and the same hopes. But this is not true. People are different. Some people have big dreams. Some people have small worries. Some people love to laugh. Some people love to cry. Everyone has their own life.

You can also use this word to talk about a group of friends. When you say everyone is there, you mean that all of them are there. You might be at a party with a friend. You are all together. You are a group of people who are all friends.

Sometimes, when you say everyone, you are trying to tell a story. You might say, everyone loves music. Everyone likes to dance. Everyone is a person who enjoys this.
