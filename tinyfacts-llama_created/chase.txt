To chase means to run after something. If you see a cat, you might want to chase it because you are very interested. You might run across a park or through a field. You might even go down a long street that is dark and quiet.

Sometimes, you chase things that are not very big. You might chase a ball that is moving very fast or a dog that runs in a long line. You might keep running until you catch up to it. This can be fun because you feel like you are doing something.

You can also chase things that are not there. If you see a bright light, you might want to follow it. You might walk quickly to find what the light is. This is like a game where you try to find something that is not really there.

To chase a person can mean to try to catch them. If someone is running away, you might try to catch them. You might follow them and hope to stop them before they get to a place they want to go to. This can be a hard thing to do because the person might be very fast.

In a different way, to chase something can mean to keep an idea in your mind. If you want to learn something new, you might want to chase it. You might read books, ask people questions, or watch things to learn more about it.
