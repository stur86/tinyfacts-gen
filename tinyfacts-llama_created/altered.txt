When you say something has been changed, you are talking about a change in how it looks or how it works. This can happen to many things at once. It is like a big change that happens to many people in a group.

Sometimes, a person might change their name. This means they become a different person. They might use a different name or a different family name. This change is big and important. It is like a new start for the person.

People also change their clothes. When you get a new shirt, it is different from the old one. The color might be different, the way it looks, or the feeling it gives. You might feel happy or feel cold in a new shirt.

Sometimes, people change how they act. If you are afraid of a big thing, you might become very quiet. You might start to hide. This is a change in how you feel. It is like a new way of being.

A car can also change. The car might have a new sound when it starts. It might have a new color or a new way of moving. When a car has many changes, it is called a different car. This means the car is not the same as before.

Changes can happen to many things in life. They can happen to people, animals, and even things we build. A new book might be written. A new song might be made. A new game might be played.
