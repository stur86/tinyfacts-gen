A word is a thing that helps us talk. It can be a word you use to say a word, or a word that tells you how to feel. Think about the word you use to say you are happy. It is a word that tells you how you are.

Think about a big building. It has many floors and many rooms. Each room is a part of the whole. You go up the floors to get to the room where you sleep. You go down the floors to go to the room where you eat. You walk down the floors until you reach the room where you do your work. The whole building is a big, hard thing.

A thing that is not one of these things can also be called a word. If you are in a room and you hear a word, it means the word is telling you something. If you hear a word, you might think it or you might feel a way. You might be afraid or you might be happy.

Sometimes, a word can be very hard to understand. It might tell you a thing you have never heard before. It might make you feel something you do not know how to feel. It is like a door to a new world. It opens a new way to see or to feel things.

We use words every day. We use them to talk to each other. We use them to write stories. We use them to tell jokes. We use them to show how we feel.
