To sign something means you use your name and the date. It is a way to say, I am the one who did this. You do not need to show your face. You might sign a paper or a computer word.

Imagine you are a teacher and you give a paper to your students. You write your name and the date on the paper. The next day, you hand the paper to your student. Your student has the paper now. They can read it and remember what you said.

Sometimes, people sign things they do not want anyone to know. They might sign a book so a child reads it. This way, the child knows the book is safe. They have a sign that says, I am the one who read it.

You can also sign things you have made. If you make a picture, you might sign it. You sign it so others know you made it. This is important for people who make things that people like to buy.
