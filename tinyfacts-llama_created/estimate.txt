To make an idea about something is to use your mind. You do not know everything. You have to guess or use your brain to think about what you are looking at.

Imagine you are trying to guess the number of things in a room. You have seen the room many times before. You think about the small things that can be seen. You think about the big things that are hard to see. You also think about the way the light looks. You might think that the room is more than one hundred. You are not sure.

You might use numbers. You might say, I think the room is between one hundred and one hundred and ten. You are using numbers to help you think. This is an idea.

Sometimes we make these kinds of ideas for people. If you know how many days it will take to do a job, you can plan your day. You have an idea about how much time you need. This is also an idea. You are using your brain to think about what will happen.

Sometimes we make these kinds of ideas for things that are far away. If you know the time when the sun will come up tomorrow, you can plan your day. You have an idea about what to do. This is also an idea.

To make an idea is important. It helps us understand the world and our place in it. It helps us make plans and take care of things.
