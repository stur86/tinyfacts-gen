A mirror is a thing that makes you look at yourself. It sits on a table or on a wall. You can stand in front of it and look into its clear glass. The glass makes your face look the same as when you wake up in the morning.

You can use a mirror for many reasons. You can look at yourself when you go to bed. You can look at your hair and your face. You can see how you look in the morning when you wake up. You can also use a mirror to see what is behind you. If you are in a room and you do not see the door, you can look at the wall behind you to see the door.

Some mirrors are very big. They cover the whole room. You can stand in front of them and see everything around you. These are called tall mirrors. Some are small and you can stand in front of them and see only your face.

There are also small, round things that you hold in your hand. These are called glasses. They sit on your face and make you look like a person from a movie. You can wear them when you go out to play with your friends. They help make you look like you are in a different world.
