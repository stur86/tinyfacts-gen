To give something out to the world means to share it with everyone. This is how you tell people about a story, a picture, a thing that happened, or a thought.

Imagine you have a book. You want to tell the world about a story you like. But you are a person and you do not know if anyone will read it. So you ask a friend to tell the story to everyone they know. This is how you make the story known.

When you are ready, you write the story down. You might write it in a book, on a computer, or even in your own words. Then, you send the story to many people at once. This is like sending a long piece of paper to every house in a town.

Your work is then seen by many people. They read the story, think about it, and share it with others. This can change the way we think about something or make new ideas.

Sometimes, you want to share something with everyone, but you do not want it to stay in your brain alone. You might share it through a word, a picture, or a song. You want to show it to the world so everyone can see it.

You can also use this word to talk about making money. If you write something that is good and interesting, you can make money from it. You are giving the world a part of your work, and in return, people pay you to see it.

This word is important because it helps us share our ideas, our stories, and our pictures with the world. It lets us talk to each other and shows us that we are not alone in our minds and our imagines.
