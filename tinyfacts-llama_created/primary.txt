The word is about the first things you do.

When you are very young, you learn how to walk. Your legs are very strong, and you can stand up on your feet. You start to move around when you are three years old. This is your first step.

Later, when you grow a little bigger, you start to do the same things again. You might use a chair to sit on while you are talking. You also start to make your own food with a box that can make food.

These are the first things you do. They are the very beginning of a long story.

Sometimes people say, a person is the first of something. This means they are the very first one in their group or in a long line of people. It is like the start of a story.

You can also say, a person is the first to do something. This means they were the first one to try to do it. They might feel afraid, but they did it anyway.
