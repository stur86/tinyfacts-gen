To add something means to join more things together. You can add a number to a number. You can add a word to a story. You can add a picture to a book.

Imagine you have a bag of food. You have one bag. You want to buy more food. You go to a store. You buy two bags. Now you have three bags. The three bags are more than one bag. You added more things to the first bag.

You can also add more things to a number. If you have a number that is already very high, you can add more numbers to it. Then the new numbers will be added to the old numbers. This makes the number much bigger.

When you add many numbers, you counters the numbers together. You start with a number and then you add each number in a line. This makes a new number.

Adding is an important way to show how many things you have. When you are happy, you might add many small things to your face. You might add a happy face, a smile, or a laugh. This makes your face look happy and bigger.

You can add things in many different ways. You can add numbers, words, pictures, or even ideas. The same thing can be added in many different ways.
