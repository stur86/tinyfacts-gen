After is a word that talks about time and what happens next. When you do something, you start at one time and end at another time. After you do something, you are in a new time.

Think of it like this. Imagine you have a cup of water. You drink it. Now you are done with the water. You are in a new time. You are after the time you drank the water.

You can also use this word when you talk about a story. You tell the story before you start. Then you tell it after. The story happened before you heard it, and it happened after you heard it.

It is also used when you talk about a day. If you go to a movie after you finish dinner, you are after the night. You are after the time when you went to dinner.

You can also use it when you talk about a time to be. If you want to go after the sun, you are looking for a time when the sun is not up yet. You want to wait until it is later.

So, after is a word that talks about time and what happens next. It helps us keep track of how long things have been going on.
