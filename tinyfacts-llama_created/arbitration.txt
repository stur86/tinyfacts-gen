Armed with a little money, a man or a group of men can tell a fight to stop. They pick a man who is not in the fight but is a friend of the man who is fighting. They talk to the man who is fighting and the man who is not fighting. They listen to both sides. If the people who are not in the fight are happy with what the two men say, the fight stops. If the two men agree, the man who is fighting goes home. If they do not agree, the fight continues. In the end, one man or one group of people gets to decide what is right.
