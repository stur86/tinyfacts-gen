To make a good move is to do something that is right for you. This can happen when you see a movie or a book. You might like it. If a friend says a movie is good, you might watch it. You are following what your friend says.

A friend might tell you to try a new food. If you like food, you will try it. You are following their word. You are not the one deciding. The friend is the one who is telling you what to do.

A teacher might give a student a job to do. The student has to follow what the teacher says. They are doing what the teacher says, not what they want to do.

When you say a person is great, you are making a good move. You are telling people that they are doing something right. This makes others think about the person. It is like a sign that says, this is good.

If you see a song you like, you might tell a friend. You are following what the song is. You are not saying you like it. You are just saying what you think. You are doing what you think is right.

Making a good move is important. It helps you feel good and it helps others understand you.
