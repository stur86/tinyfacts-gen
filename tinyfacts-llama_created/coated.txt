To cover something means to put a hard piece of stuff on top of it. This piece of stuff is often white, but it can be other colors too. It can be made from many different things like glass, paper, or even the hard part of a rock.

Imagine you have a piece of paper that has a picture on it. If you want to keep the picture from falling off, you can put a small piece of clear stuff on top of the paper. This clear stuff is like the hard piece of stuff we just talked about. It keeps everything from moving around and makes the picture stay on the paper.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If a person has a lot of money, they can cover themselves with clothes that make them look nice. This keeps them looking good for a long time. Or, if you are very tired, you might cover your face with a soft clothes so you do not feel the cold air on your face.

It can also mean to cover the bottom of a big thing. If you are walking on a long road, you might cover the bottom with a soft thing to keep your feet warm. This helps you to stay warm and safe while you are moving.
