Babies are very young children. They are usually under the age of three. They grow and change very fast. Their skin is soft and their eyes are very bright. They learn to walk and talk.

A baby needs to be looked at and held all the time. They need food from a bottle or from you. They also need a place to sleep. When a baby is very young, they do not speak. They make sounds to tell others that they are here. When they are a little older, they start to say words. They might say their first word, like the word for a ball.

Babies are very small. They can not walk yet. They sit on a person or a thing. They also use a thing to breathe air. They drink from a bottle. Their tiny hands move when they are happy or sad. They are very cute.

As a baby grows, they learn to use their arms and legs. They start to walk. They might fall down. This is okay. It is a part of growing up. A baby is very small for a long time. They need many people to care for them.

When a baby is very old, they become a child. They can go to school and play with other kids. They learn more and they can tell you things they have seen. A baby is a very small person, but they are also a great friend.
