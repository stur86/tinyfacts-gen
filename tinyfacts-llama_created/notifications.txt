When you do something, a small piece of your brain sends a little word to another part of your brain. This word tells the other part where you are and what you are doing. It is like a little word that says, you are standing near the door. The word helps you remember what you need to do.

Think about a phone. When you send a word, it goes to the other phone. It does not leave the first phone. It just goes to the other phone. If you send a word to a friend, the word is stored in your brain. If you want to know if someone has sent you a word, you can look at the words they sent. If they sent you a word, the word is still there in your brain.

Your computer does something the same way. When you type on the computer, the words are sent to the computer. The computer stores the words. If you look at the words, you can see what you typed.

People also use this word when they are watching a movie. Sometimes the movie shows a little piece of the world. If you see something bad happening, the computer says, the person is being bad. You see this word before you see the bad person.

A computer also uses this word when it tells you that a word is ready. When a word is ready, the computer says, the word is ready. You can hear this word by listening to the computer.

The word is very important. It helps you know what is happening and what you need to do. It is like a little watch in your brain.
