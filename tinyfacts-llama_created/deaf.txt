To be dead is to be gone. It means your body is not here any more. Your heart stops beating, and your brain stops thinking.

When you are dead, you are not living. You are not breathing. You do not feel anything. You are not here to see the world around you or to hear the sound of a dog belling. You are gone.

People say you are dead when you have died. You are gone, and you will never come back.

You can also say you are dead when you stop living. You might feel like you are dying inside. You might feel your body getting cold, or your breath getting short. You might feel like you want to be gone.

Sometimes, people say you are dead because you are very, very sick. Your body is so hurt that it can not work anymore. You might not be able to move or you might not be able to breathe. Your body stops working, and you go away.

You can also say you are dead when you give up. If you are in a fight and you know you can not window, you might say you are dead. You have given up. You have given up on the fight and on life.

Being dead is not the same for everyone. Some people die very quickly. Some people die slowly. Some people die from their own bodies. Some people die because of other people.
