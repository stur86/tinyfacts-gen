Jacket can be a thing you wear. You put it on to keep you warm. It is usually made of hard stuff. You can buy one to wear every day. Some people wear it to work. It helps keep your clothes clean.

It can also be a thing from the ground. It is a green color and comes from a tree. The tree makes the green color. When the tree is old, the color turns brown. When you cut the brown part, the green color comes out. This green color is the one people call jumper. It is a very old thing. Many people think it is beautiful because it is green and it comes from the ground.

People also use the word to mean something is very old. If you have a lot of things, you can say your things are old. This means your things are many years old.

In a school, a person who works with books is called a teacher. They help you learn new things. They also give you a jacket to wear, so you look nice while you are learning.
