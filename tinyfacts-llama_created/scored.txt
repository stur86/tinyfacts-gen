To have something means you have it. You are the one who does it. You use it to help you or to play with others. When you do something, you get a point.

A game is a way to use this word. One team plays and the other team tries to stop them. The team with more points at the end is the one that did well. If a team does not get enough points, they are the ones who did not do well.

You can also use this word to talk about a person. If a person does something good, they get a point. This point is important because it helps them get a better job or a higher place in a group. You can see points on a paper with numbers that show how good a person is.

When you say a team has a point, you mean they have a way of doing something that helps them. It is like a way to show that they are doing well.
