To be the best means you can do more than anyone else. If you do something, you are the first person. It is the same for animals and people, but it means different things for each person. If you are the best at something, you do it well and everyone wants to do it too.

When you are the best, you are not the only one. Many other people also do it. There is no one in the world who is better than you. You feel happy. You feel the same way all the time.

But if you are the best, you have one big problem. You do not want anyone to be better than you. You do not want them to beat you. You are afraid. You do not like to be the first. You like it when everyone is the best.

This is why you might not want to learn a new thing. You might not want to try new ways. You do not want the world to change. You like the old way of doing things.

Sometimes, being the best is not a good thing. It is very hard. You have to do everything. You have to be very good all the time. If you do not do it, you are the worst. This makes you very sad.

Being the best is also important for people who are very small. If a tiny animal is the best, its family is the best. The mother, the father, and all their children are the best. They all do the same thing and they are very happy.

In the world, being the best is a big idea. Many people try to be the best. They work hard. They learn a lot. They try to be the best at every thing. But it is hard. Sometimes, you just have to be happy with who you are.
