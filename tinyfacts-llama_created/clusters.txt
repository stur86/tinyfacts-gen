To form a group of things is to make a group. This happens when you put many of the same kind of thing together. When you have many of the same kind of thing, you can do many things with them at once. You can work with them, play with them, or talk to them.

Imagine you have many balls. You pick a few of them and put them in a box. After you do this, the balls are not alone anymore. You have made a group of the balls. You can still play with them later, but now you are doing it with a group of balls.

You can also form a group of people. If many friends are together, they form a group. They can do things together, or they can just stand around and talk.

When you form a group, you have to decide what to do. You have to decide what you want to do with the people or things in the group. You might decide to work together, or you might decide to go your own way.

Sometimes, a group can be very big. It might have many hundred or even many many more people. When this happens, it is called a large group. People in this group usually do the same things, but they also do their own jobs and work on their own.

Groups are important because they help people work together. When many people are working on the same job, they can do it faster and better than one person doing it alone. This is why many schools and hospitals have groups of people working together to make sure everything runs well.
