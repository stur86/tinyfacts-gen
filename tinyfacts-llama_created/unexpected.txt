When something happens before you plan, you might feel surprised or confused. If you know something is going to happen, you usually try to think about it. But if something happens without you knowing, it can be a big surprise.

Think about a time when you went to buy food. You had a plan. You knew you would go to a store near your home. But then you saw a sign that said, right there, a new store opened. You did not know it was there. You were not expecting it.

You could feel happy because you got something you wanted. You could also feel a bit worried. You had to decide whether to go to the new store or stay where you were. You might have asked yourself, Is this a good idea? Or, Should I just stay where I am?

Sometimes, things that are not expected can be very good. A surprise party for a friend can make them feel very happy. A surprise visit from a family you love can bring you closer together. It is a way to catch people off guard and make them feel happy because they did not expect it.

You can also use this word to talk about how things go in a movie. If a person does something they were not expecting, the story can go on. It can change the way you see the story and the people in it.

In your life, you might have an expected day. You plan what you will do, who you will see, and when you will finish work. But then, something happens. You see a funny picture, you meet a friend, or you have a surprise call. This is an expected moment, but it is also an expected surprise. It changes how you feel about the day.
