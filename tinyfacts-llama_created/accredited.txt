To be an accepted person means you have been seen and heard by many people. This is important for work and life.

Imagine a group of doctors. You go to one of them and ask them a question. They listen and write down what you said. After you leave, they look at the paper. They see you were right. The group then says, you are an accepted doctor. This is a good sign because it means you did what you said and people trust you.

A school may have a person who tells what they know about things. The teacher says, this is an accepted answer. This person has been checked and their answers are seen as true by many people.

In life, you might have a job. Your job is to do work for a group. If your work is accepted, people know you did it well and they trust you to do it again.
