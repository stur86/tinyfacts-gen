To add something means you counters something else. You take two numbers or words and you put them together.

Imagine you have one book with one hundred words. Now you add another book with one hundred more words. You have two hundred words now. You have more words than the first book.

You can add many things. You can add numbers, words, and even pictures. When you add them together, you get a bigger picture.

If you have one bag and you get a bag with two more bags, you now have three bags. You have more bags than you had before.

Adding is a way to show how many things you have when you have a few of them.
