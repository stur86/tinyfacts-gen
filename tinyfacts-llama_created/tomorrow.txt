Tomorrow is a word you use when you know what day after today is.

Today is now, and tomorrow is the next day. Tomorrow is always after today. You can not tell tomorrow until you know what day it is today.

You can say, I have a movie tomorrow. This means you know what day tomorrow is, and you have a movie to watch. Or, I have a big plan for tomorrow. This means you know what day tomorrow is, and you have a plan to do things.

Sometimes people use this word when they are very excited. If a person says, I am going tomorrow, they mean they will go the next day. It is like a word that tells you that a day is coming.

People also use this word to talk about things that will happen later. If a teacher says, I am going to read tomorrow, it means they will read the next day. This can help you plan what to do.

So, tomorrow is a word that tells you what day after today is. It helps us plan, remember, and know what to expect.
