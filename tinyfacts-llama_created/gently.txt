To gently means you move things very carefully so they do not hurt anyone. It is like when you are playing with a ball. If you pick it up too fast, it might roll away. But if you hold it very tight and move it in a slow way, you can keep it safe.

You can use this word to talk about many things. You might say, I am gently talking to him. This means you are speaking softly so he feels no fear.

Sometimes, we use this word for people too. If a doctor is talking to a child, they use it to mean they are speaking very quietly so the child can understand.

You can also use it to talk about how you act. If you are being very kind to another person, you are being gently. You are not saying mean things, but you are showing you care about them.

To gently can mean many different things. It might mean you are not afraid, you are not too loud, or you are not too hard. It is a way to show you are making things easy for other people.
