To coffee is to make a drink. You need a small round thing with a round bottom, a round top, and a long handle. You put hot water in the bottom part. Then you add tiny bits of green round stuff from a tree. After that, you add some soft white stuff from a big animal.

The hot water makes the green round stuff start to break. The green round stuff gets all tiny and falls down into the bottom part. The white soft stuff stays on top.

When the drink is ready, you turn the round thing on the handle. This makes the tiny bits fall down and the white soft stuff stay on top. You can see the white soft stuff through the round top.

You can drink the coffee hot or cold. Some people like to drink it with sweet things. Some people like to drink it with other drinks. It is a nice drink for a morning or after lunch.

People often say that coffee is a way to wake up. It helps the body feel more full and ready to move.
