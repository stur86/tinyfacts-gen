A place that is not the same all the time. Sometimes it is cold. Sometimes it is hot. It can be very dark or very light. It can be deep down in the ground or high up in the sky.

Imagine you are standing on a road. The road goes from one town to another. If the road goes through a big city, the air might be different. If the road goes through a small town, the air might be different too. There can be more or less of everything in the air.

Sometimes the ground is not one color. It can have many colors. It can have dark colors and bright colors. It can even have colors that are not seen by the eyes. This is why we say the ground is a place that is not the same.

There are many different kinds of places like this. Some are very far away from other places. Some are very close to other places. Some are in the middle of two places. Some are under the ground and some are above the ground.

People move around these places. Some go very far. Some go very little. Some go by car or by foot. Some go by water. Some go by air. Where you go and how you get there can be different from place to place.

It is very important to understand these kinds of places. If you know where you are, you can plan your day. If you do not know, you can ask others for help.
