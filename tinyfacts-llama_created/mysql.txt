When you want to look at a group of people, you can ask them to stand up. But, if you ask a dog to stand, it will not do it. You need to tell it what to do. You do this with words and signs. The word says what to do, and the signs tell the dog. This is how people and animals work together.

A computer works in a way like this. When you ask a computer to do something, you tell it what to do. You tell it to look at a group of numbers. The computer looks at the numbers and does the work. It does not ask you what to do. You do not need to tell it again. This is why you can ask a computer to do many different things with one word.

You can also ask a computer to remember things. You tell it to remember a word, a number, or a picture. The computer keeps the word, number, or picture in a box. When you ask it again, it looks at the box and gives you the word, number, or picture.

Sometimes, you tell a computer to do something that is not a word. You tell it to look at a picture, or to play music. The computer understands what you are asking and does it. This is how computers work. They can do many different things with just a few words.

In a big group of computers, all of them work together like people. Each computer tells the others what to do. This way, you can get things done very fast. You do not need to tell each computer what to do. The computers do it for you.

A group of people who work together is also like this. You tell the group what to do. You tell them to work on a job, or to help each other, or to tell each other what to do. The group works together to get the job done.
