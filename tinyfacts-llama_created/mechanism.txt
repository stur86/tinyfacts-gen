A thing that makes things work is a word for a big group of parts. When you pull a small thing, it moves around. This pulls other things around. The pulling makes a door open. The door opens because of many parts. If one part is broken, the door does not open.

The same is true with a car. The car has many parts. There are parts that make the car go fast. There are parts that make it stop. There are parts that make the car go around in a big round way. When all these parts work together, the car moves. The car is a kind of thing that makes work happen.
