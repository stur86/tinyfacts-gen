To need something is to want it but you do not have it. You might want a big cup of coffee after you wake up, but you are still in bed and do not get up. You need the coffee to feel better.

Sometimes you need to buy something so you can do something. You need money to go to a store and buy a book. If you do not have money, you do not get to read the book.

You also need food to stay strong. If you are very tired, you need to eat something to give you power. You need water to drink so your body can work well.

A tree needs water and light to grow. It needs the sun and the ground around it to make new leaves and grow more. If it does not get enough of these things, it will die.

Sometimes, you need someone to talk to when you are sad. A friend can help you feel better. You need people in your life because they make you feel like you are not alone.
