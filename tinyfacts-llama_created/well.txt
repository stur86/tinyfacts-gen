A well is a deep box with a round top. It is used to get water from the ground. You can put a long hard piece in the ground near your house or your land. This piece is very hard and keeps the water inside.

When the water gets out, it comes up fast. You can see it in a small water line that comes from the top of the well. The water is clear and white. If the well is not deep enough, the water will go down into the ground. It can go under the ground for many feet before it reaches your house or your land.

You can also use a well to get food. Some people grow food in the ground and bring it up to their houses. They put the food in a big box or a box with a cover. The food grows good food, like green leaves or bright green stuff, and then they bring it up to their homes.

There are also small wells in the ground. They are small and can go only a few feet deep. These wells are often used for small animals. They bring water up to the animals so they can drink.

A well is important for many people. It gives them water, food, and even money. It helps keep our water clean and makes our lives easier.
