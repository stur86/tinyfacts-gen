When you say someone is not ready, you mean they do not have the time or space to do what you want them to do. This is often when you ask them to do something, but they are busy or have another plan.

Think of a phone call. If you call a friend and they are in a meeting, they are not the one you want to talk to. You are trying to talk to them, but they are not there.

This word can also mean something is not working because it is not there. If a movie is not going to show that night, the movie is not up yet. The thing is not ready to be seen.

You can also say a person is not ready to talk to you. If you ask them to meet, but they do not show up, you are saying they are not ready. They do not want to talk to you or hear what you have to say.

It is like when you want to play a game with your friends, but your mom tells you to do your work first. You want to play, but you have to follow what she says. This is what happens when someone says they are not ready.
