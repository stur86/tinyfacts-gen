To walk ahead is to move before you are there. When you are in a line, the person in front of you is the one you see. If you walk ahead, you are moving to the right side of the line. This helps you get to your place before everyone else.

Imagine you are walking to school. If you start walking when the others start, you will be the one who arrives first. You are walking ahead of everyone else. This is why you want to move before you are in the way of other people.

You can also walk ahead in other ways. If you are on a road, you can walk ahead of the cars. This way, you are safe because the cars are going very fast. If you are walking in a city, you might walk ahead of the people who are going the same way. This is because you are moving before they are.

Sometimes, we walk ahead to meet a friend. If you know where they are, you can go to the door and say, I am here. This shows that you are coming. It is like walking ahead of the group so you can be the first one to meet them.
