To have a set of facts or a set of ideas as your start point is to start with something.

Imagine you are standing in front of a big building. You do not know what the building is. You only know that it is big and has a floor and walls. If you look at the floor and the walls, you see that they are made of a hard stuff that is not green. You do not know if the floor is made of wood or if it is made of a white hard stuff. You do not know if the walls are high or if they are low.

One day, a friend tells you that the building is the home of a group of people. You do not know who they are, what they do, or where they live. You only know that the building is not your home. This is your first fact.

You start to ask questions. What do they do there? Do they live there? What are they eating for breakfast? You start to build a picture of what the building is like.

You start with a small piece of fact and build from there. You look at your own facts and build on them. You might start with a fact that the people inside the building have a big desk. You ask, what kind of desk is it? You find out that it is a desk with a soft top. You build on this and ask, what kind of top is it? You find out it is a top with long lines on it. You ask, what are those lines for? You find out they are used for writing. You build on this and ask, what kind of paper do they use? You find out they use paper with a soft feel.

Now you have a picture of the building. You know its name, its kind of floor, its kind of walls, its kind of top, and what kind of paper it uses. You started with a small fact and built from there.

You can also start with a set of ideas. You think that the world is very big. You think that there are many different things in the world. You might say, the world is made up of many different things, some of them are very big and some of them are very small, and in between there are many different kinds of things that are not so big and not so small at the same time.

You start with the idea that the world is big. You do not know how big it is. You do not know if it is many times bigger than your world or if it is only a little bit bigger.

You build from there. You look at the idea and build on it. You ask, what does it mean to be big? You find out that it means something is very large. You ask, what does it mean to be many different things? You find out that it means there are many different kinds of things. You ask, what does it mean to have many different kinds of things in the world? You find out that it means there is a lot of change.

You keep building. You ask, what does it mean to have many different kinds of things in the world? You find out that it means there is a lot of different kinds of things. You ask, what does it mean to be very big? You find out that it means something is very large. You ask, what does it mean to be many different things? You find out that it means there are many different kinds of things. You ask, what does it mean to have many different kinds of things in the world? You find out that it means there is a lot of change.

You keep building until you have a big picture of the world. You know that it is very big, that it has many different kinds of things, and that there is a lot of change. You started with a small fact and built from there.

This is how we learn and grow. We start with a small piece of fact or a set of ideas. We build from there, asking questions and finding answers. We keep building and building until we have a big picture of the world.
