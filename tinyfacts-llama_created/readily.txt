The word ready can mean different things.

One way it means you are ready for a fight. If you are ready, you are not afraid. You are not nervous. You can go out and do what you want.

Another way it means you are done with something. If you finish a job, you are ready. You can leave the house and go to bed. You do not have to think about what to do next.

Sometimes, to be ready is to be ready to do something. If you are ready for a party, you are ready to go. You can put on your clothes and go to the party. You are ready to have fun.

It can also mean that something is ready. If food is ready, you can eat it. If a book is ready, you can read it.

In a different way, to be ready can mean that you are not waiting. You are doing something. You are moving. You are doing what you want.

So, ready is a word that means you are not waiting. You are doing something. You are ready to go or to do something else.
