To live in a house or a part of a city is called to be a part of it. A house is a room with a small door and windows. You can walk in and out many times. Inside, you have a bed and a desk. You eat food from a kitchen. You watch the sun or the night sky.

A big city has many houses, schools, and stores. People live in some of these houses with their families. They work at jobs. Some work in offices, some work in stores, and some work in schools. They pay money each week to live there.

A part of a city can be a small town with many houses. It might have a school, a store, and a park. People go there to buy food, to talk, or to play. If you live in a part of a city, you are part of the city.
