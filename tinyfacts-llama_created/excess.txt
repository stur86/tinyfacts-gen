An empty bag can hold many things. When you fill it, it can hold many things. But if you keep adding things and the bag stays full, it becomes an over bag. This happens when you take too many things. You have more than you need.

Imagine you have a big bag for food. If you put a lot of food in it, the bag gets too heavy. You can't carry it anymore. This is what happens when you have too many things. You have an over bag.

It is also about money. You can have a lot of money. But if you spend too much, you have an over money bag. You have more money than you need. This is bad because you do not have enough money for things you need.

Sometimes, we have too many things we do. We start to do many things and we do not stop. This is a problem. We are using too much time. We should stop some of the things and do one thing at a time. This can help us be more calm and happy.
