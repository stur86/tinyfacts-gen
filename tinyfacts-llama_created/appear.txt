To appear means you are seen or heard by someone. When you appear, you are not really there. You are only a small part of a larger picture. This happens when you go into a room and people notice you.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. You are not a person on the wall or in the corner. You are not a person in a book. You are only one person in the room with them. When you appear, you take up space and make a sound.

Sometimes, things appear without you doing anything. A tree can appear outside your window at night. It is not there when you are looking at the wall. It has been there for many years. When you see it, you know it is there. The tree is not really there. It is only your mind that sees it.

When you appear, you can change the way people feel. You can make them laugh, smile, or feel sad. You can also make them feel afraid or excited. You are a part of the story and help make it happen.

In a way, you appear every time you are seen. You are always there, even if you do not want to be.
