The word oldest is used to talk about things that are very old. It tells you which one is the first.

Think about your family. You are the youngest of many children. If you have many children, there are older children. If you have a brother, he is older than you.

An animal has an age. A dog is old. A cat is old too. They have lived many years. An old house is very old. It has been standing for many, many years.

People also use this word. If you are the first one at a party, you are the oldest. If a child starts to learn to speak and says their first word, they are the oldest. It means they are the first one to do it.

You can also use it with people. If an old man says something, you listen. You are listening because he is older than you.

The word oldest is important when you talk about the past. It helps us know how long things have been.
