To put things on a table or a wall is to make them look nice. You use things you have, like small papers or colors, and you place them where they will be seen by people.

Imagine you are a teacher. You have a room full of students. You want the room to look nice for the day when everyone comes. You pick a nice color for the walls. You put a few nice pictures on the table. Then you add a small table with books and a chair. The room is now ready for the day.

People also do this when they are at home. They might put a pretty thing on their table. They might put a small picture of a loved one. They might put a small box of food. These things make the room feel more warm and happy.

It can also mean to make something look like a thing that is not there. If you have a big box of clothes, you might put a small picture of a person on it. The picture looks like the person, so the box looks like it has a person inside. This makes the box look like a real person.
