To recognize means to notice something. It is about paying attention to a person, a thing, a sound, or a feeling. You might recognize a friend, a person you know. You know who they are, and you can feel a big happy feeling when you see them.

You can also recognize things that are not very big. A tree, a rock, or a small animal can be recognized. When you see these things, you know they are there.

Sometimes, you recognize a word. If you hear a word, but you do not know it, you must try to remember it later. You might write it down, or you might try to remember it by thinking about the words you already know.

In a big group of people, you can recognize one person. You know their face, their voice, and their way of talking. If someone you know comes up to you, you can feel a warm feeling because you know them.

In a game, you might recognize a number. You know that a number is big, but you do not know its exactly how many. You might guess the number because you see it many times.

Recognize can also mean to show that something is true. If you recognize that a thing is dead, you are telling the world that the thing is really gone.
