Were is a word that is very important. It helps us talk about the past and the present.

To say something were something means you are looking back at a time from the past. You are thinking about what happened a long time ago. You might be thinking about a day you had, a time you watched a movie, or a story you heard. You are looking at the past and trying to remember what happened.

Sometimes, you might use this word with people. If you tell your friend that you were at school this morning, you are saying that you were in the past. You were not in the present. You were in a different time.

There are also other ways to use were. When you are talking about a place, you might say something like, I were going to the store. This means you are going somewhere now. You are not there right now.

You can also use were to talk about what happened. If you did something, you might say, I were scared. This means you were afraid.

It is an important word to know because it helps us talk about time and places. It helps us understand how things are.
