Especially made things are often called this. They are made to work only on one kind of paper. If you want to use them on other kinds of paper, they will not work well. They can also do things that other things can not do.

A long time ago, people made things this way so that only one person could make them. This was very good because one person had to make all the things. But it was also very hard. Many people tried to make them, but they never became as good as one person.

Now, most things are made in many different ways. Some are made for one kind of paper, and some are made for many kinds of paper. But still, many things still work only for one kind of paper.

This is why we say that things that are made this way are often very good at one thing, but not at many things. They are very good at one kind of work, but not very good at many other kinds of work.
