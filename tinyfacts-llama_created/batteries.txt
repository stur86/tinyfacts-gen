Bottles are things that hold water or other drinks for people. They are usually made from glass or hard stuff. Inside the bottle, there is a round thing that makes tiny drops of power. This power makes the water move and makes the drink hot or cold.

When you put the bottle in your hand, you give the power to the round thing. The round thing keeps giving the power until the bottle runs out. When the power is gone, the round thing stops working. Then the bottle is empty and you need to get a new one.

A small round thing inside the bottle is the power. It is like a tiny heart inside the bottle. The power makes the drink hot or cold. If the power is broken, the drink will be very hot or very cold.

Some bottles have many of these round things. These many round things are like a group of tiny hearts. When all the round things are working, the power keeps coming. If one round thing breaks, the power might not come at all.

You can use these bottles to power many things. You can put one bottle in your hand and it will make the light on your phone work. You can put one bottle in a box and it will make the music on the box work. You can even put one bottle in a car and it will start the car.
