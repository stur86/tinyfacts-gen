A thing that is set with many pieces is a kind of game. People often play this kind of game for fun. The pieces can be small or big and some can be moving and some can be hard to move.

You start with a small part of the game. You use the pieces to build something new. As you add more pieces, the thing gets bigger and more interesting. Sometimes the pieces are not in the right order. You must figure out the order so that the thing is complete.

There are many different kinds of games like this. One is to build a house with many rooms. Another is to move a ball into a big box. Some games are for kids. Others are for grown people. Everyone likes to play these games because they are fun and they help you learn new things.
