To be in a different place is to be in a different area. You can be in a city, a state, or a far away land. Sometimes, it means you are not in the same area as another person.

Think of the ground. The ground is the area under your feet. If you move to a far away land, you are in a different area. You are not the same person anymore. You are in a new place.

Sometimes, you may be in your home area. Your home is the area you live in. You know the streets, the schools, and the food. You feel safe there. When you leave your home area, you are moving to a different area. You are in a new place.

The word is also about the people. If you live in a city with many people, but you do not speak the same words as them, you are in a different area. You are not the same person. You have a different way of thinking.

In this way, the word is about the different areas of life. It is about the places you are and the people you meet. It is about being part of many different groups and the many different ways of living.
