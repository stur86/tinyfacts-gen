Present means you are right now. It is a word that people use to talk about things in their day. You can see it in a word like this: the present moment is the right now.

Imagine you are in a big room with many people. You want to know what is happening so you can decide what to do. If you are present, you are right there in the room. You are not looking at the room from another place. You are really there.

You can also be present when you do things you enjoy. If you are playing a game with your friends, you are present. You are not thinking about anything else. You are in the moment and you are having fun.

Sometimes, you might be present in a hard time. If you are sad because a friend is gone, you are in the present. You are feeling the pain in your heart. You are not looking at the past or the next day. You are right there with the sad feeling.

Being present can help you feel better. When you are in the present, you do not worry about the past or the things you will do tomorrow. You are just here now.
