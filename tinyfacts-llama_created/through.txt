The word through goes across something. It is a way to get from one end of a line to the other end. If you are walking through a field, you are moving across the ground. You are not standing still. You are moving forward.

You can also use it to talk about the world. If you are going through a city, you are moving through the streets. You are not in one place for a long time. You are moving from one place to another. Sometimes, you go through many cities before you get to where you want to go.

Sometimes, people say you are going through a hard time. This means you are facing a lot of problems. You are not happy or you are feeling sad. You are moving from one place to another because you need to find a way out.
