To hear something means you can use your ears to understand it. Your ears are the parts inside your head. They are like long sticks that help you hear voices, sounds, and other noises.

When you hear a sound, your brain picks up the words. You might hear a dog belling, a car going by, or a baby crying. Your brain is like a computer that takes in the sound and makes sense of it.

If you are in a quiet room, it is easy to hear your own voice. But if you are in a busy room with many people talking, it can be hard to hear what someone else is saying. This is because there are many sounds around you, and it is hard for your brain to pick out what is important.

Sometimes you hear a word when it is not there. This can happen if a person is talking and you are not looking at them. You might hear the word, but the person is not saying it. This is called hearing a word without paying attention.

You can also hear things that are not sounds at all. You can hear a small animal moving under the ground. You can hear a light in a dark room. You can even hear your own heart beating. This is because your body is making sounds that you can hear.

To hear something is to understand it. It is like getting a picture from your ears.
