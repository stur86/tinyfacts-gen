A group of people who work together to decide what is best for a place. They are usually made of many different people, and they have a leader. They talk and listen to each other to see what everyone thinks. They might talk about big problems like how to pay for a road or how to make sure everyone is safe.

They can also talk about small things. They might decide which games to play at school or which color to use for a game. They help make sure everyone is happy.

Sometimes, a group of people who work together to decide what is best for a place might have more power than the people who live in the place. They can make things that everyone has to follow.

The word comes from the word for a group of people in a town. In this town, many different people would meet together and decide what to do. Over time, the group became a group that made many of the big, hard things that the town had to do.
