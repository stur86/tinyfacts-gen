To responder means to talk back to someone. It can also mean to act again when a computer or phone has stopped working.

Think of a person who works in a store. They buy things to give to others. When they are busy, they often let the store manager know that they need to take a break. This is a way to say, I am busy, but I will be back soon.

If the store manager does not answer, the person might go to another store and tell the manager that they need help. This is like saying, I need someone to answer my question, but I am not sure how.

In this way, people who work in stores are the first ones to respond. They help when things are wrong. They also help when you need to buy something.
