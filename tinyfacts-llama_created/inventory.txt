An old word for things you keep. To have an old word means you remember how things were many years ago. You can remember what you had, what you gave away, and what was left. This word is for keeping things that are not for you.

Imagine a small house. You keep things there. There is a box where you keep money, a box where you keep a book, and a box where you keep a small thing. You keep these boxes so you do not lose them.

A store also uses this word. When a store buys things, it adds them to its old word. The old word now has more of those things. If the store does not have a box for those things, it must buy more. This helps the store stay in business.

You can also use this word for things that are not for you, like a thing you do not want. If you do not want a book, you can say, I do not have this old word. This means you keep it so you do not have to look for it later.

Sometimes, this word is used for a group of people. If a group of friends keeps a listened word, they can remember each other. If a group keeps a word for a game, they can use that word to play the game. This word helps them stay together.
