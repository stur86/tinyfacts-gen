To somehow means you do something that is not easy or clear. It is a way to say you did it, even if it was not the way you wanted to do it.

Imagine you want to walk across a wide open space. You have a small bag and you do not want to carry it. You find a place where the ground is soft and low. You step on the ground from one side to the other, one step at a time. You walk, but you are not sure if you are going in the right direction. You have to keep looking at the ground and at the trees. You are doing this because you want to reach the other side of the space. You are doing it in a strange way because you are trying to reach a place where you want to be.

Sometimes you do something that makes no sense. You might pick a bright color for your clothes and then put them on. You might go to the bathroom and then eat a sweet thing. You are doing this because you want to make yourself feel better or because you like the sound of the word.

You can also use this word to talk about how things are. If you are in a place that is very hot, you might feel like you are going to die. But you are somehow still standing. You are not falling down because you are standing on the ground.

Sometimes, when we say we are doing something, we use this word because we do not know how to say the word in a different way. We might say, I am going to the store, but we do not know the word for going to a store. So we use this word. It is a way to fill a small space with words that mean we are doing something.
