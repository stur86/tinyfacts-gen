A word you use to talk about a thing.

When you want to talk about something, you say a word. This word tells the other person what you are talking about. It is like a small word that says, this is what I am talking about.

You can talk about a thing that is in your home. You talk about a chair. You talk about a book. You talk about a picture. This word does not tell you which thing, but it tells you what you are talking about.

You can also talk about a big thing that is not in your home. You talk about a city. You talk about a big group of people. You talk about a big problem. This word tells you a bit more than the word for a small thing.

Sometimes, you talk about two different things at once. You might say, I like to walk. I like to read. This word is for both of them. It says, this is something you like.

You can also talk about a thing that is not a real thing. You might talk about a story. You might talk about a movie. These words do not tell you what real things are. They tell you what you are talking about.

The word for a thing is important. It helps you know what to say when you want to talk about something.
