The word you are looking for is called a number. A number is a way to show how many things are in a group. It is a number that is big and not small like the number one.

One is the smallest number. It is a single thing. Two is the next number. It is two single things. Three is three single things. Four is four single things. Five is five single things. And so on.

A group of ten things is called ten. A group of twenty things is called twenty. This goes on for many, many groups of numbers. People use these numbers to buy things they want or to give money to each other.

A small number is one. A small number is two. A small number is three. A small number is four. A small number is five. And so on. This is why you need a hundred, two hundred, three hundred, and so on.

A big number is one hundred, two hundred, three hundred, and so on. These numbers help us know how much money is in a bag or how many things we have. People also use these numbers when they buy food or clothes.

There is a number that tells how much money a person has in their pocket. This number is called the number of money. It is like a key that opens the bag of money.

You also need a number to buy things with money. When you buy a book, a movie, or a game, you give the money to the person who is telling the story. The money makes the story happen.
