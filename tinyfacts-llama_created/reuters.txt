To reader something means to look at it. You might read a book, a story, or a word on a computer. This word helps us understand things.

A place where people bring their stories is called a new. The new is like a big room where people from different places tell their stories. You can hear what someone from another city or town says. It can be about their life, their family, or their work.

The word reader comes from the word read. To read is to look at the words in a book. When you read, you are not looking at the words as a child. You are looking at them as a person who has written them. You are trying to know what they mean.

Sometimes you read about things that are not real. You might read about a person who is very tall or a place that is very high. You do not see the person or the place. You only see words on the paper. Your brain is trying to understand why the story is true.

We also have other words for this. We say you are reading something if you are looking at the words. We call it a book or a story. This word is important because it helps us know what we are doing.
