A big box that keeps food and drinks cold is called a cold box. It is like a big, tall house that you can sit in. Inside the house, there are many small rooms with a soft white cover on top. Each room keeps the inside room at a very low number, so the cold stays there.

You put food and drinks inside these rooms and close the cover to keep the cold air inside. Then, you close the door and the cold box stays very cold for a long time. This is very important because you can keep your food and drinks for a very long time without them going bad.

Some cold boxes have many small rooms inside. These rooms are so small that only tiny living things can fit. These tiny living things live inside the room and breathe air into it. They also move water through the room so that the cold stays inside. When you turn a handle, the tiny living things breathe in air and the water moves. This keeps the cold air inside the room.

Most people do not need to make food inside the cold box. They buy food from stores and take it home. But some people like to keep their food in the cold box. They put their food and drinks inside and close the cover. They sit in the house and let the cold box do its work.

Cold boxes can also be very loud when they work. They make a sound that is like a big, slow moving thing. Some people like to listen to this sound when they are sitting in the house.
