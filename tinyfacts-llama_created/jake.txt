To jacket someone means you are making them feel less safe. You might do this by telling them they are safe, but you are not really sure. You want them to feel that way so you can use them for your own fun. This is not a good thing to do.

If you jacket someone, you are taking away their power. You are taking away their right to say they are safe. They feel like they can not trust you. This is why we say this is not a good thing to do.

People do this to animals too. A dog might feel this way if a person is trying to hurt them. The person might say, but you are safe, or you are not, but you are. This is not a good way to make a dog feel safe.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about money. If you take money from someone, you have taken away their money. You have taken away their right to keep it. This is not a good thing to do.
