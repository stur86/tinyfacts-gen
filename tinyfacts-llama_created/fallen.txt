To fall can mean different things when you are a person or when things are falling down.

When a person falls, they hit the ground. This can happen if they run very fast and their feet hit the ground before they reach the bottom. This is a big thing. If you fall from a high place, you might not hit the ground at once. You might fall many times before you stop.

When things fall, they go down from something they are on. If you drop a ball, it falls to the ground. If you drop a book, it falls down to the floor. When you drop things, they start to move very fast and then they slow down and stop. 

Sometimes, when things fall, they do not hit the ground. They fall into a deep water or a dark place. This is not safe. It can hurt many people.
