A thing that is made in many small parts is called a box. You can see all the parts on the outside. Then you can take one part and put it into another box. This is how we make many boxes all at once.

This is also how computers work. You can write a story with many small words. Each word is like one small piece of the story. When you put all the words together, you get a story. The computer does the same thing. It takes many small parts of a picture or a word and puts them into a new picture or word.

When you build something with many small parts, you need to follow a set of steps. If you do not follow those steps, the thing will not work. This is what a teacher does. They tell you how to do the steps so you can build a box or a computer or a car.

Sometimes, a word can mean many different things. To talk about something is to use this word. To be a good friend is to follow the set of steps. To make something new is to follow the set of steps.
