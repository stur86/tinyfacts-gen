To then is a word you use when you talk about something that happens after something else. It is like a line that goes from one thing to another.

Imagine you are walking down a street. You see a friend standing in front of you. You want to talk to them. You tell them how you are doing. Then your friend starts talking back to you. This is because your friend is the next thing you want to hear after you talk.

If you were talking to a computer, you would use this word. You could say, then. You are talking about something that happens after you finish talking.

You can also use this word to show that something is still happening. If you are waiting for a movie to start, then it will start. This means you are waiting for the next part to start.
