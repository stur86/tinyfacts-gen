To cry is to make a very sad sound. When you are afraid, you might cry. This happens because your body is very happy, but your face is also very sad. You might drop your water because you are crying. You might also fall to the ground because you are so sad.

Sometimes you cry because you love someone very much. You might cry when you see your friend smile. You might also cry when you are angry because someone did something bad. When you cry, your eyes get wet. This makes your face look red and sad.
