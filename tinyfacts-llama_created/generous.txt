To be very kind and give money or things to people is to be very good. When you are very good, you help others without asking. You do this because you care about them. You want them to be happy.

Imagine you have a lot of money. You could keep it all for yourself. But if you do this, you are not very kind. You are just keeping the money. If you give some of it to your friends and family, you are being very good. You are showing them that you care about them.

Some people are very kind. They give money to people who are in trouble. They give food or clothes. They also give money to those who are sick. They do this because they want others to be happy.

You can also be very good by being kind to animals. If you walk a dog or give a cat food, you are being kind. You are helping them feel safe. This is a good thing to do.

Being very good is important. It helps people feel better when they are sad. It makes the world a better place.
