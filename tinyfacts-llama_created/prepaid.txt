To have something ready before you need it is to be ahead. When you buy things like a phone or a book, you usually pay for them after you use them. But if you have money set up before you use it, you are ready. You have the money, and you can use it when you need it.

Imagine you are a student. You have a job to pay for food and school. But before you start, you put some money in a box. When you need to buy a book, you use the money in the box. You are ready. You do not need the money until you need it.

Pretended is when you act like someone is there. You might pretend to be a doctor so you can get money or a job. You say you are a doctor even though you are not. This is not good because you are not really a doctor. But you act like one so you can get what you want.

Present is something you have right now. It is not something you will get later. You can see it in front of you. If you have a present, you do not need to wait for later. You can watch a movie or read a book right now.

You can also have a present ready for a person. If you know you want to give a surprise to a friend, you can set something up. You might buy a small thing you think they will like. You can put it in a box and keep it until the right time. Then, you surprise them.

Present money is money you keep for later. You put it in a box so you can buy things when you need them. It is like having a box of money in your pocket.
