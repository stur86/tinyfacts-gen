To be in a group with others is to be in the same place. You might see this in a big room. If you are in the same room as many people who look like you, it makes you feel like you are part of a group. You might feel happy because you know you are not alone.

When people put things in a box, they make it easy to find the things you want. They do this so you do not have to look through a big box full of many different things. You can look at one box and find many things. This helps you decide what to do.
