Years is a way to show how many years have passed. Imagine you start a new book. You write one story. Then you write another story. After a few years, the stories are old and you might not remember them anymore. Years are like those stories. We use them to know how many years have gone by.

One year is when you are one year older. Two years are when you are two years older. Three years are when you are three years older. This goes on for many years until you become a very old person. If you live a very long life, many years will pass.

We also use years to talk about things that happened a long time ago. A hundred years ago, things were very different. A few hundred years ago, people did not have cars. Today, many of those things are already gone. People now live with computers in their hands, and cars fly through the sky.

When we say a person is a few years old, we mean they are not very old but not young either. It is like a small step between being a baby and being an old person.
