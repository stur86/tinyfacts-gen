To give something as a way to do a thing is to give it when you do it.

Imagine you want a box of sweet things. You do something you like, like play with a friend. The friend gives you the box. This is a way to show you did something good. You are happy because you got the box.

You can also give something to someone if you help them. If you help a friend move things, you give them a small thing that says thank you. This is a way to show you care.

Sometimes, a person works hard to get a job. They do a job for many hours. After they do it, they get a small thing from the person who owns the job. This is like a small way to say, thank you, for helping them.

You can also give a thing to someone when you do something nice for them. If a teacher is kind to a student, the student might give the teacher a small thing as a thank you. This is a way to show you care about the person.

To give something is to share what you have. When you give, you are letting someone else have it. This is a good thing to do because it makes everyone happy.
