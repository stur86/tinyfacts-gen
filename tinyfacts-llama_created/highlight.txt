To light something means to make it very bright or to show it on a wall. You might use a light to show a room or to make a person see a word.

Imagine you are in a dark room. The walls and the floor are all black. A person holds a bright thing up. The light makes the black parts of the room stand out. Now you can see everything.

In a story, the word might mean something important or the person who does it. If a person does something good, the story might say they are a light person. This means they help others or make things better.

You can also use it for words. If you write a long story, you might use the word to show a strong idea or a key word. It is like a sign that shows you are talking about the right thing.

To light a small thing is another way to use the word. You might use a small light on a phone or a small light on a computer. This helps you see the small words or pictures on the phone or computer.

In a game, you might see a word on a big wall. The word is what you are trying to find. If you find the word, you are a light person.
