An arming is a thing you hold. It is made of hard stuff that keeps you safe. You can hold it in your hand. You can use it to keep a door open or a window closed.

When the sun moves, the air around the world goes. It moves in big turns. This turns make a round thing. This round thing is the sun. The round thing is the sun. People call it the round thing in the sky.

We use the word to talk about turning. If you turn a thing, it moves. If you turn a car, it goes in a round way. If you turn a door, it opens. You turn it when you want to move it.

Sometimes, to tell what something is, you say it is an arming. This is how you talk when you see a thing you hold. You say it is an arming, and it is a thing you use to stay safe.
