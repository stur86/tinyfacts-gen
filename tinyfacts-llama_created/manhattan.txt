Man is a big group of people and animals that live on land. They make a world with houses, roads, and big green spaces. In this world, most people live in one city called New One.

New One is very big. It is on a big water that goes across two big pieces of land. The water is very strong and it goes around the land many times. This water is called water. It is black at the bottom and white at the top.

Inside the city, there are many tall houses. The top of these houses is so high that people can see the whole city from there. In the city, there are many different groups of people. Some live in tall houses, and some live in small houses with many rooms. Some live in houses with open spaces where they can go outside and play. Some live in houses with big rooms where they can sit and talk.

Many people drive cars in this city. They go fast on the roads and drive many hours. Others walk. Some walk for days to get to the other side of the city.

There are also big green spaces in the city. Some are big and cover many hundreds of city blocks. These spaces have green green stuff. The green stuff keeps the air clean. People sit on the green stuff in the hot sun.

There are many different kinds of animals in the city too. Some are big and strong. Some are small and fast. Some are big and slow. Some are very small and can fit into a box. There are many kinds of food that people eat in this city.

In the city, people have a lot of work to do. Many work in big offices in tall houses. Some work in small offices with many rooms. Some work in places that make things that people use every day. Some work in places that fix things that break. Some work in places that make food for people.

But in the city, there is also a lot of fun. People go to places with big lights. They go to places that have many movies. They go to places that have music. They go to places with food that is very good. They go to places with games. They go to places that have big balls that go up into the air.

People also have many different ways to show love. Some give money. Some give things that people use. Some give time. Some give words. Some give hugs.

In the city, people are very different. Some are very tall and strong. Some are very small and fast. Some are very big and slow. Some are very quiet, and some are very loud. Some are very kind, and some are very mean. Some are very happy, and some are very sad.

All these different kinds of people make the city what it is. They make it a place where many different lives can happen at the same time.
