A book with many pictures is called a picture book. The pictures are very small. You can read the words next to the pictures. The pictures tell a story. You can read it many times.

A book with many words is called a book. The words are very long. You read them one by one. You might not know what to look at first. You look at one word, and then you look at another word. As you read, you start to understand the story.

A thing you buy that you keep in your home is called a book. You might buy a book about a car. You can read it when you want. You can look at the pictures of the car. You can read about how it works. You can learn something new.

A book with many words about a person is called a book about that person. The person is a story. The words tell about their life. You might learn about their family, their friends, and their work. You can read the story many times.

A book with many pictures of people is called a book about people. The pictures show how they act. They show how they smile. They show how they laugh. They show how they work with each other. You can see how people are like.
