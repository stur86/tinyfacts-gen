The word is used to tell something will happen now or later. It is often used in the beginning of a story or a letter. It is the first word in a new part.

To say something happens now means you are saying it now. To say something will happen later means you are saying it will happen soon. It is not the same to say something will happen very soon and to say something will happen after a long time.

Imagine you are talking to a friend. If you say, this will happen now, you mean you are telling them that something is going to happen right now. If you say, this will happen later, you are telling them that something will happen after a while.

This word is important in many ways. It helps us know when things are happening. It helps us plan our days and our lives. It is a word we use every day.
