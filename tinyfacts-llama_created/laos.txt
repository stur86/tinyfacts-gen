Losing is the act of giving up and leaving something behind. It is the end of a story. When you lose, you are no longer the one in the story. You are now a place or a thing that is no longer yourself.

Sometimes people lose money. They spend it all and can not get it back. Or they lose a game. They did not window. They do not have the money they need to buy food or a place to live.

There are also times when a person loses a person. A child loses their mother or father. A person loses a friend. This is a very hard thing to do because you feel like you are missing them.

A place can also lose. A city loses its power when it is not the same anymore. A small town loses its people and its life when it is gone.

In the world, many things are losing. The air is getting clean, but still it is not as clear as it was. The world is full of people who are going in different directions. Some people are moving to a new city. Others are moving to a new home in a different part of the world.
