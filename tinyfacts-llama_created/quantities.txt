To have a lot of something means you have a big number of it.

Think of your room. You have a lot of books. Or you have a lot of clothes. You can not hold them all in your hands. You need a box or a bag to carry them.

If you have one book, that is a small number of books. If you have many books, you have a lot of books. If you have a huge house, you have a lot of rooms. If you have a huge car, you have a lot of cars.

We use numbers to help us. A number is a way to write how many things we have. If you have ten books, you have ten books. If you have a hundred books, you have one hundred books.

You can also have too many things. If you eat too many food, you feel sick. If you have too much money, you can not buy what you want. These are problems.

Sometimes, you need something in a big group. If you have many friends, you are part of a group of friends. If you have many animals in a park, you are part of a group of animals. This is how we show we are part of a group when we are many together.
