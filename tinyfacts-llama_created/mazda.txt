To make something or to change it: a car that can go very fast, or a feeling when you are sad

A car is a thing that moves on the ground. It has a round body and four big round things on the bottom that push air out and push it forward. You put your feet in the front seat and hold the round things so you do not fall out. You close the door and sit down and then the car starts moving.

A car can go very fast. If you want to go to a city that is far away, you can drive the car. You can use the round things to go around the road. If the road is very long, you might need to follow the road and then turn onto a smaller road. You keep following the roads until you reach your city.

Some cars are green, some are red, and some are blue. You can pick one that you like. If you like a car that makes a lot of noise, you might want a car with a loud round thing on the front. If you like a car that is quiet, you might want a car with a soft round thing on the front.

A car is also a person who drives it. If a man is a car driver, he follows the roads and makes sure the car is safe. He watches out for other cars and people. He also drives carefully so he does not cause a big problem if something goes wrong.

You can also say a car is a thing that can do many things. If you have a car that can go very fast, it can also go very far. If you have a car that can carry many people, it can go many roads at once. This is because the car has many round things under it.
