Heading means you are moving in a direction. It is when you go forward on a road or a line. You are not looking at the ground. You are looking at the road ahead.

Imagine you are in a car. You are going to a place you want to be. You are not looking at the road. You are looking at the road ahead. You are moving. You are on your way.

You can also head to a place in a school. If you are going to a class, you are heading there. You are going in a direction to get to that place.

In a game, you might say a player is heading for a high spot. This means they are moving toward something good. It is a good feeling when you are moving toward a place you want to be.

You can also head to a place when you are afraid. This is when you are afraid to walk in front of a group. You are afraid of what might happen. You are trying to stay close to someone who can help you.

In some words, you can say you are heading toward something. This means you are moving in the right direction. It is like a sign that says you are doing well.

To head a dog means to walk in front of it. A dog likes to follow its owner. The owner walks in front of the dog. The dog follows the owner because it knows they will keep it safe.

Sometimes, you might head away from a place. If you are at a park and you want to go home, you might head away. This means you are moving in a different direction.
