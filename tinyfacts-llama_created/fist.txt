To make a hand into a thing that can hit or throw is to use your hands. You hold one hand in the other. Your hand parts into two or three parts. You can push the parts together or push them apartment by apartment.

When you make a hand into a thing to hit, you use the parts to push against something. You might use your hand to hit a ball in a game. You use the hand to push the ball in a way that makes the ball go far away from you.

If you make a hand into a thing to throw, you do the same thing but you throw it instead of hitting it. You hold the hand with the parts together and push the hand down toward the ground. You then let the hand go. The hand makes air move in a way that the air goes to the other side of the hand. The air goes out of the hand and goes to the other person. The air makes the person move.
