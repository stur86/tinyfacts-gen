When people are not happy with a thing that happened, they might start to fight about it. They will say things that are wrong to each other. They might use words that are not nice to use, and they might even do things that hurt each other. This kind of fight is called a fight about a thing.

Imagine you and your friend are playing a game. You both thought you should get the ball. But then you both want it for your own reasons. You start to say, you did it first, and you did it better. You both get very angry. You start to shout at each other, and the game becomes very hot.

A fight is not always about a small thing. Sometimes it is about something that is very big. A fight can happen when a person says something that is not true, or when a person does something they do not want to do. They might even fight with their bodies. In a fight, people use their hands, feet, and other parts of their bodies to hit each other.

Sometimes, a fight can go away if both people decide to stop. They might say, we are done. They will go back to their own side and live the rest of their lives without this problem anymore.

But sometimes, fights can go on for a very long time. This is called a fight that never ends. A person might fight about a thing for years, or even for a life. They might hurt their bodies and their minds. The fight can also hurt people in their families and at school.
