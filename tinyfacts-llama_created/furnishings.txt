Further means to do something a second time. It also means things that help you live. When you buy a chair, the person who makes it has made it many times before. So, the word can mean both the act of making something and the things that are made.

Think about a family. Every year, they get a new table. The table is made many times before it becomes old and the wood starts to hurt you. At first, it is very new and very nice. But after a while, it becomes hard to clean and it starts to look old. That is why the word is used to talk about things that get old and then become something new again.

You can also use this word for people. If a teacher has to give another teacher a set of books, they are both doing the same job. The teacher is making the books many times before they become old and the people who get the books are getting a set that has been made many times before. This is why the word is used to talk about people who do the same job many times.
