To make something is to change it. You start with one thing and you do something to it. This makes the thing new. It has a different form.

Some things come from the ground. We eat them to give us power. The power is inside the green parts. The green parts grow from the ground and give us food.

Other things are made by living things. Animals give us food. They breathe air and give us power. They also give us water. They also give us food. They live from the green parts and from the ground.

We also make things from other things. We take wood and make a chair. We take a piece of paper and make a book. The wood is from trees. The paper is from old things. We change them into new things that we use every day.

Things made from other things are often called things made from the ground. They are not living things. But they come from the ground.

Some things are made from many parts. The body of a dog is made from many parts. It has skin, hair, and soft parts that move. It is not one thing. It is many things put together.

There are also things made from only one part. A small ball is made of one hard white thing. It has no green or blue parts. It is only one thing.
