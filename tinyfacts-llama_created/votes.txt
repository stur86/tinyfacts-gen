To have a voice means to be able to tell the people who you think is the right one to do things. When you give your voice, you are saying what you want to happen. You are saying if you like a person, a place, a game, or a way of doing things. When people listen to what you say, they might agree with you.

A group of people can also decide what they want to do by using a way that is like a big talk. They ask each person to tell them what they think. They ask the people who are already in power to tell them what to do. They ask the people who are not in power to tell them what to do too.

Sometimes, a group of people decide to do something by putting a number on a piece of paper. This number tells them how many people say yes and how many say no. The person who gets the most numbers is the one who gets to decide. This is how a group can make a big change happen.

You can also have a voice in a game. If a team of people is playing a game, they want to decide what to do next. They talk to each other and decide if they should go to the next part of the game or if they should stop playing. This way, the team is making a call with their voice.

In a city, people may have a voice in how the city is run. They talk to the person who is the top person and tell them how they feel about things. They might ask for more money for the roads or for more police. The top person listens to what they say and can change their plans.

When people all have a voice, it is very important. It is how we stay in control of our world. We use it to tell the world what we want and what we do not want. It is how we keep the power and the money.
