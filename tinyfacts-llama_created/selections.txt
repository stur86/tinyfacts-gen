To make a group of things is to pick them from many others. When you pick your favorite book to read, you are making a group of books. You are not keeping all the books. You are taking a few and putting them together.

Sometimes we do this with people. You might pick a few friends to play with. You do not keep all your friends. You take a few and you play with them. You have a group of friends.

It is also about the things we do. If you want to see a movie, you make a group of movies you like. You pick the ones you want to watch. This is how you find things you like.

You can also make a group of people who do the same thing. If you work in a school, you have many people who do the same job. You are a group of teachers, a group of students, and a group of other people who help.

When you make a group of things or people, you are building something new. You are taking many things and putting them together in a way that makes them a group.
