The word you are looking for is called a word that has a name. This word is very important. When you say a word is the word you are looking for, you are saying that the word is one of the many words we use.

Every word has a name. We tell the first word. We tell the second word. We tell the third word. And so on. This is true for all words.

When we say a word is a name, we mean the word is the word. It is the word. It is the one we are talking about. This is why we use the word to name other words.

For this reason, we do not use the word to name more words. We use it to name the word. If we want to say a word is a name, we do not use the word. We say the word is the word. The word stays the same.
