To do a job is to act and act well. This is how you show you can do things for a person or a group. Many people do this work every day. They might be a doctor, a teacher, or a worker who makes things for money.

A group of people usually do the same job. They might work together to make a thing, write a story, or fix a car. If one person is not doing the job, others have to do it instead.

When you do a job, you are doing a thing. You might feel a bit sad when you are tired. You might feel happy if you are doing something you like. You might feel a bit angry if you are not doing the job right.

You also do a job to help others. If you are a teacher, you give kids new ideas. If you are a doctor, you help people who are sick. This is how you help people and make the world a better place.

You can also do a job to learn about something. If you are a student, you read books and try to remember the facts. If you are a writer, you write stories and try to make them clear.
