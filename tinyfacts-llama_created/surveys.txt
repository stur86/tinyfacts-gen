A group of people is asked to give their answers about a thing. This is called a group question. They are not told what the thing is at first. Later they learn what the thing is.

The questions are often about how people feel. They might ask how they are doing or what they like. They might ask how much money they have. They might ask if they like a place or a way to get from one place to another.

The answers give us a picture of what people think. It tells us about their hopes, their fears, and their plans. It helps us understand how to change things. It can also tell us where there are problems so we can fix them.

Sometimes people do this kind of question with a group of children. They ask kids to tell them if they like a game. They want to know if the game is fun. They want to know if the kids like playing it.

People also do this kind of question with a group of people who are very old. They ask old people if they remember the past. They want to know if the old people have learned new things or if their memories are still strong.

Surprises are also a kind of question. You might surprise your friend by giving them a small thing they like. You might surprise them with a new book or a fun thing to play with.

In some places, you have to do a group question. You have to ask people if they agree with something. If many people agree, it is a good thing. If many people do not agree, it might be a bad thing. This helps us understand what is right and what is not.
