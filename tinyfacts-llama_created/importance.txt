What does it mean?

To have a big job means you are very important. You do things that help people. You fix things that break. You tell people why things are happening. You help people feel better.

Think about a school. There are many children. Some children know a lot. They help their teachers. They help other children. They are very important.

You are important too. You are an important person in your family. You are important in your city. You are important in your world. What you do can change people.

What if someone is not important? They might be a friend. They might be a person you like. They might be a person who can help you. Not being important does not mean you are bad. It just means you are not as important as someone else.

Important things are things that help us live. They help us feel safe. They help us stay in our homes. They help us be with our friends. They help us do what we want.

Important things can change. Sometimes they change very fast. Sometimes they change slowly. But the idea is the same. They are things that matter.

You can feel important if you do good things. You can feel important if you help others. You can feel important if you are kind.
