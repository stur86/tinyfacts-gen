A thing you use to do a job is called a computer. But many people use things that make the computer work. These things are very small and are put into the computer. They are like a key that makes the computer do its work.

When you put one of these things into the computer, it does not need to tell it what to do. It just does the job. You do not have to tell it what to do every time.

Think of a hair cut. A hair cut is a job. A hair man does the job. But if you want a new hair cut, the hair man must go to a new place. He has to bring his things and his way. If you just want a new hair cut, you do not have to go to the hair man. You can do it on your own. This is what a thing like a hair cut is like.

When you put a thing into a computer, you are doing the same thing. The computer does not have to tell you what to do. It just does the work.
