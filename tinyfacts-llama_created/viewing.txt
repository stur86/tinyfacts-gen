To see something is when your eyes look at it. You open your eyes and look at what is in front of you. You look at a person, a house, a car, a tree, or a thing on a table.

When you see, your brain gets the picture. Your eyes send the picture to your brain. Your brain then uses the picture to know what is happening. You can see a picture of a cat. You can see a picture of a cat. You are seeing the picture.

To watch something is like to see it more than once. You watch a movie. You see the same thing many times. You see the same words, the same pictures, and the same story. You watch it because you like it.

Sometimes, you watch something while you do other things. You watch a game. You are watching a game and you are also eating food. You are watching the game while you eat. You see the ball go up in the air.

You can also watch things that are far away. You watch the sun. You watch the stars at night. You watch the sky. These are all ways to see what is happening in the world.

To look at something is when you look at it with your eyes. You do not use your brain to see it. You just look at it. You are looking at the thing.
