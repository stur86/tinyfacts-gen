A person who works before another starts something. They are the first to do the work.

In a room, a person might be the first one to pick up a book from the floor. This is like a start. They do it before someone else picks it up.

In a car, the person who drives is the start. They turn the key and start the car. This makes the car go. They are the first one to do the work.

Sometimes, a person who works before someone else is the one who does the work first. They are the start. They do the job before another person starts doing it.

Also, a person who starts a game is called the start. They get the game going. They decide who plays next and what happens next.

In a kitchen, a person who starts the work is the one who gets the food ready. They put the food in a pants and turn the fire on. This makes the food hot and ready to eat.
