To pay for something is to give money for it to stay. When you pay for a house, you pay the owner so they can live there.

You can pay for a room in a big building. You might pay perfect every month. This means you pay the same money every day, and you stay in the room for one whole week.

Sometimes, you pay to use a car. If you drive a car and you are not the owner, you have to pay to use it. This is because the owner needs the car to go to work.

You can also pay to use a box. If you need to keep a book for a long time, you might pay for it. This is like paying for a room in a big house. You pay the owner so they can keep the book for you.

When you pay for something, you are giving the person money so they can do what they want.
