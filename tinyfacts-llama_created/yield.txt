To give something up means to let it go. You might feel this when you are very tired. You need to rest. You can not keep watching a movie all night. You must close your eyes and go to sleep.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are very good at it. You are the first one to catch a ball. But then, you get a little sad because you do not want the game to end. You want to keep playing. You feel you have not given enough. You give up and you walk away.

Sometimes, you give up because you are afraid. You do not want to try. You are afraid of something. You do not want to do it. You give up and you stop. This can happen with a job, a book, or a plan.

Another way to use this word is with money. When you give, you give something of your own money. You are giving up your hard work. You are giving up a piece of yourself. This can be sad because you are losing a part of yourself.

You can also give up a small thing. If you have a box of books and you want to keep them, you might give them away. You give them to someone who loves to read. This is a good thing because the books will find new homes.

To give something up is to let go of it. It means you are letting something go from your life. You are not keeping it anymore. You are moving on.
