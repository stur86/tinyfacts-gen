Sun is a big ball in the sky. It makes a bright light that can make things warm. When the sun is up high, the light is very strong and you feel warm on your skin. This is why you need to wear clothes when it is bright outside.

When the sun is low in the sky, the light is not so strong. This is when the sun is behind you. You feel a bit cooler and the light looks more like a soft white or a grey color. It is still a beautiful thing to see.

At night, the sun is gone. It has gone down to the place where it started. The sky is dark and you do not see the light anymore. The night is when you can see the stars and the world is quiet.

People use the word to talk about days when the sun is up high. They say the days are warm and bright. They say the night is dark and cold.
