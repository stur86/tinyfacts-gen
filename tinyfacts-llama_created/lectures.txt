A person who gives a talk is called a teacher. They know a lot about a thing. They want to share their ideas with you so you can learn.

When a person gives a talk, they stand up and face you. They might use books, pictures, or words to tell you what to think. They might also ask you to join them. This is why you sit and listen.

You can also listen to a talk. If you want to know about something new, you might watch a person talk. They speak very fast and move their mouth. You listen so you can understand what they say.

Sometimes, people have long talks with many friends. They share stories and ideas. This can help you feel better or learn more.

You can also have a talk with a doctor. They help you feel better if you are sick. They listen to your body and talk about what you should do. This is how you can get well.
