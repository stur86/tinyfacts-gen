When you do something, you can have many different ways to happen. An eventually happens because of what was done before. You might move from one place to another because of a road. Or you might meet a friend by doing something different.

Every time you do something, there are many different ways the story can go. The same thing can happen in many different ways. This is why we say that an eventually happens.

Think about a game. You start with a ball and a friend. You throw the ball. If you throw it straight, the friend might catch it. If you throw it very high, the friend might miss it. Or if you throw it low, the friend might hit it. Every time you throw the ball, there are many different ways the story can go.

When something happens, we might have many different ways to feel about it. We might feel happy when we see a friend we love. We might feel sad if our friend is sick. Or we might feel scared if we hear a loud noise.

Out comes something new. It does not stay the same. It changes. This happens every time you do something. Even if you do the same thing many times, every time you do it, something new comes out.
