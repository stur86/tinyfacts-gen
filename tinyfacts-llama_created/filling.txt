To fill something means you make it bigger or more than it is now. You put things inside a box that is already full. You also put food into your body when you eat. When you fill your mouth, you are full. It does not leave the room.

You can fill a room. You can fill the floor with clothes or books. You can fill the air with music. This can make people happy and excited. 

You can also fill a bag with things you need for a trip. You put clothes, food, and money into the bag. When you reach your house, you have everything you need. You are done.

Sometimes, we fill our brains with ideas. If you are thinking about a problem, you are filling your brain with ideas. You are using your mind to learn and understand.
