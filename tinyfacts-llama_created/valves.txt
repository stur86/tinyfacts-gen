A door or a thing that opens and closes has a small opening in it. This opening allows air to go in and out. When the door is closed, the air inside is hard. When you open the door, the air comes out and the door closes again.

On a big box that makes food, there are many small parts. Some of these parts open and some close. When the food inside is ready to eat, the parts open. This lets the food go out. When the food is not ready, the parts close and keep the food inside.

A person might have a small thing on their hand. This thing helps to keep blood moving. When the person needs to go to the bathroom, the thing opens. It lets the blood go out. When the person is done, the thing closes. This keeps the blood inside the body.

The word comes from the word to open. The door and the small parts are open and close. The word helps us think about how these things work.
