To have an answer is to know something. When you ask a question, you want to know the truth. An answer is what you find when you look at something. If you want to know why, you need an answer.

An office has many people. Some are like a manager who tells people what to do. They help people know what to do. Some are like a person who listens to a problem. They help people find a way to fix it.

An office might also have a person who helps the manager. They talk with the manager about what is going on. They tell the manager if everything is okay. They tell the manager if there are any problems.

When you say someone is an answer, it means they are the person who helps you know something. They are the people you turn to when you want to know how things are.
