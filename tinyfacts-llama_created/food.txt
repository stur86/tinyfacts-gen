Food is the stuff you eat. It makes your body strong and gives you power. You need food every day to live.

There are many kinds of food. Some are sweet. Some are green and grow on trees. Some are red from animals. Some are made from tiny bits from the ground. Some are from the water.

Food is made by living things. Animals eat other animals to get the food they need. Some animals eat green things from the ground. Some eat the water from the ground. Some eat the skin of other animals. Some eat the blood of other animals.

When you eat food, it goes into your body. Your body breaks it down and makes it into the power you need. Your heart works to move the food through your body. Your brain uses the power to think. Your skin and hair need food too.

You can eat food by yourself. You can also share food with your family and friends. You might eat breakfast in the morning, lunch in the middle, and dinner in the evening. Some people like to eat the same food every day. Some people like to try new foods every day.

Food is important for life. Without food, you would not be able to move. Without power from food, you would not be able to think. Without hair, skin, and other parts, you would not be able to stand. Food is the very thing that makes life happen.
