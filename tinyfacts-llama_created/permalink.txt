To share a long piece of writing with a group of people.

You might see this word on the side of a long story. It lets you go to a place where you can read the story again. It is like a word that shows where to go to read the next part of the story.

If you search for a word, you might find many places with this word. One place is a word on the top of a picture. It shows the number of the place where the picture begins. Another place is a word on the side of a picture. It tells you how to get there.

Sometimes, people use this word to share their own writing. They will put a number before a word, and then put the word after the number. This lets you read the next part of their writing. It is a way to share your work with others without sending it to them one letter at a time.

It is like a word that lets you jump from one part of a story to another. You can start at any place and go where you want.
