Nicest is a word you use to tell how nice a person is. When you say someone is nice, it means they are kind and they do not hurt anyone. They are very good to others.

A person who is nice is like a big soft ball. They are not strong like a big ball, but they are very good at not hurting people. They like to help others and they want everyone to be happy.

You can also say that something is nice. This means it is beautiful or very good. A nice day is a day when the sun is out and there is no rain. A nice food is food that is sweet and makes your body feel good.

If you are nice, you are happy. You smile and you are kind to everyone. You want people to be happy and to like you. Being nice is a good way to live and to be a friend.
