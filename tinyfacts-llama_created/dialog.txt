To talk is to use words to share your ideas. When you talk to a friend or a family person, you are talking to each other. You are not doing this alone. You are using your mouth to send a set of words that you think and want to say.

Sometimes, two people talk at the same time. They might not use the same words, but they both want the same thing. They use their voices so they can understand each other. This is how they find out if their ideas are the same.

When you have a long conversation, you might have many different ideas. You might be talking about what to eat for dinner, or what game to play later. You are using your words to show your point of mind.

A person can have a big talk with a friend. They might talk about their day, their work, or a movie they saw. They use this time to share their feeling and their story.

Also, you can talk to a book. If you read a story, you are talking to the story. You are using your voice to tell the story to your brain. This is how you learn about the world and about people.

A group of people talking is called a group. They use their words to share their ideas. They might work together to build a house, or they might talk about a problem. Everyone in the group is there because they all want the same thing.

When you talk to a computer, you are using your words. You send the words to the computer, and it gives you a picture. You did not talk to the computer. The computer just did the work that you gave it.
