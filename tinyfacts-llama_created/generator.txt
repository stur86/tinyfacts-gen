A small thing that makes more of something else can be called a maker. It can be a person, a house, a car, or a computer. These things do not start by themselves. They need other parts to work. One part gives them power, and the other part makes sure they keep going.

Think about a light in your home. You turn a round thing that is pulled by a power line. This thing makes the light. It is a maker because it uses power and makes something new. It does not just sit there and do nothing. It is always working to give you what you need.

Sometimes, a maker is a thing that makes a sound. A small thing in a music box moves around inside it. When it does this, it makes a sound. The sound is the maker. The box does not just sit there. It is always moving, making the sound.

In some places, a maker is a person who does this work. They might make things to buy for you, or to help you do your work. They might make clothes, or houses, or cars. They are the ones who make many things that you need to live.

A maker is important because it gives us what we need. It is the thing that helps us live our lives.
