Admit is to say something is true. It is a word that we use when we are talking to a friend, a family person, or a teacher. It means we are letting them know that the thing we said is right.

You might say, I admit I was wrong. This means you are sorry for what you did. Or, you admit you are tired. This means you are tired and you need to rest.

The word also comes from the world of work. If a person works very hard, they might admit they did it. This means they are happy that they did it.

In some places, people wear clothes with the word on them. These clothes are for people who love running. They wear the words of a team they follow, or a person they like. The words are like a name for the team, but they are not a real name. They are just a way to show that the person follows the team.

Sometimes, people admit they are afraid. This means they are afraid of something. They might say, I admit I am afraid of the dark. This shows that they are not very sure about what is happening at night.

We also use the word to talk about things that are not right. If you see a person doing something that is not good, you might say, that is an admit. This means you are saying that what they are doing is wrong.
