To have an area of work that you know very well is a good thing. It means you have done many of the same kinds of jobs before and are able to do them very well.

If you are an old man and you know how to fix a car, you have a kind of job that is important. You can go to a place and tell the car what is wrong with it. They will fix it for you.

It is also about how you know things. If you are a doctor, you are the one who knows how to make people feel better when they are sick. You are an important person because you can help people.

You can also say you have an area of work if you know how to play a game. You have seen other people play it many times before. You can play it and do it very well.

It does not mean you are the best at everything. If you like to play with balls but you are not very good at running, you do not have an area of work. You do not know as much as someone who is very good at running. You are not as good.

If you have an area of work, you are not just doing a job. You are the one who knows how to do it. You are the one who is very good at that job.
