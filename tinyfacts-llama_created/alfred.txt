Alone means you are not with other people. You are alone in your own body. You have your own things to do. You do not need anyone else.

One person is alone. Another person is with many friends. They talk and laugh together. They have fun.

A person might feel alone if they are in a big city with a lot of people. They are not sure who to talk to. They feel they are the only one in the world.

You can feel alone too. You might be in your room. You are not doing anything. You feel sad. You want to talk to someone. But you do not know how to reach them.

To be alone is to be by yourself. It is to have no one else in your life. It is to be the only person in the world.
