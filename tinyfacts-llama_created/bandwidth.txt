To share something means to give it to others without taking anything back. Imagine you want to watch a very long movie on your phone, but you can only watch it for a few minutes at a time. You can not stop watching. You are always moving. This is like sharing a big room with many people. You are giving them a small piece of space to watch their own movies in their own time.

A computer or a phone has a lot of power inside. If you want to send a picture to someone who is far away, you need to give them a small piece of space. You tell the computer what to send, and it takes the piece of space and sends it. This is how you share the power. The computer is not taking the power from you. It is just giving it to the person you want to send it to.

When you use the word with a group of people, it means they are all sharing the same power. They are all giving each other small pieces of space to do what they want. This is why many people work together to make a big building. Each person is giving the building power, but the building is still the same building.

Sometimes, when you use the word for a very long time, it means you are using the power too much. You are not giving it back. You are using it every moment of the day. This is bad because your body and brain need to rest. If you keep using the power, you will get sick.
