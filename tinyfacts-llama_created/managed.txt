To manage something means you are in control of it. This is different from letting something happen without you. When you manage a group of people, you decide what is best for everyone. You plan and plan and make sure everyone is doing what they should do.

If you manage money, you decide how much to spend and how to keep it safe. If you manage a car, you know when to start it and when to stop. You are the one who decides what to do and how to do it.

To manage can also mean to keep something safe. If you manage a house, you make sure the doors are closed and the windows are shut. This keeps people and things inside safe.

Sometimes, managing is about keeping things going. If you manage a group of children, you find ways to keep them happy and safe while they are playing. You help them learn and grow.

You can also manage a big problem. If you manage a problem, you try to find a way to fix it. You look for ways to make things better and then you do those ways.

To manage something is to stay in control and know what to do. It is a way to be strong and keep things going.
