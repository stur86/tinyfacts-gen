The word for the place where the first big meeting of the people took place is often called the city of the first big meeting. It is where the very first group of people who wanted to make a better world for everyone came together.

The city was named after the man who was the first one to write in the book about the group. He was a man who was very important in the land before the city was built. He wanted to write down what people thought about so that everyone could see it.

This big meeting was a very important moment in the story of the people. It was the start of a long time of work to build a world where everyone is safe and happy. Many people came from far away to be a part of this big group.

Today, the city is still an important place. It is a big city with many people and many different kinds of houses. It is a place where people from many different places come together to talk and work.

The city is also a place where people can go to remember the big meeting. They can go to look at the book and see how the story began. Some people go to visit the old house where the man who wrote the book lived. They want to remember the day when the idea for a better world was first started.
