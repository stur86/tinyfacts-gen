The word take is used when you are ready to do something. When you say you are ready, you are taking it.

Imagine you are waiting for a friend to come to your house. You have been waiting for a long time. You have not eaten. You are very ready to see them. You can not wait any longer. You take a deep breath and step out of the house.

You can also use this word with animals. A dog is ready to go. The dog is ready to run. The dog has been waiting for the right moment. It is ready to move.

Sometimes, people use this word to say they are taking a drink. You are taking a drink. You are going to have something to drink. You are ready.

But the word can also mean something is in the way. If you want to walk, but a big tree is between you and the road, you have to take a different way. You have to take another way to get to where you want to go.
