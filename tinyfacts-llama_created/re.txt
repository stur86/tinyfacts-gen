The word replies. When you say something, you are giving a answer. If you are angry, you might say a word to the person who is doing the thing that is wrong. This is a reply.

Sometimes, you might reply to a picture you saw. If the picture is a person, you might say, this person is nice. You are telling the person, this is what you think of them.

You can also reply to a word. If you hear a word and you do not like it, you can say the word back. This is a reply. It is like you are saying, I do not agree with you.

Sometimes, you might reply to a story. If you like a story, you might think, that is a good story. You are telling the person, that is what I think of the story.

In this way, replies are ways we tell people what we think.
