The word now comes from a time very far back. It is about when things were still new. A long time ago, people did not have things like we have today. They did not have cars or computers. They did things by hand. They made their own food. They made clothes from the hair of animals. They lived in houses made of wood. They did not have the things we have now.

Now, the word now means a time in the middle. It is between the past and the present. It is when the past is going away and the present is coming. It is the middle ground. Imagine you are watching a movie. You see a story that happened a long time ago. Then you see a story that happened a little while ago. In this time, you do not know the story yet. You are in the middle of it.

You can also use the word now in your own life. If you are talking to a friend, you might say, I have a problem now. This means you have a problem right now. You are in the middle of trying to fix it.

Sometimes, we use the word now to say something is happening right now. If you are waiting for food to come out of the kitchen, you are saying, I am now waiting. You are in the middle of the waiting time.
