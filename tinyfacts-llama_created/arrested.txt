To be under the power of a person who is in a place of power means you are in trouble. This happens when a person says you did something wrong. If you do not do what they say, you might be taken away. This is called being in a place of power.

Imagine you have a game with a boy who is in a high place. The boy is the one who decides what happens. If the game is stopped, the person in power has to let the game go. If the game is stopped by the boy, the person who is in power has to let the boy go.

When a person is under the power of a person in a place of power, it is like the person in power is the one who does not want to let the person in power go. They might say, no one leaves this place without a good reason. If you do not follow the person in power, the person in power might take you away.

This can happen in many different ways. If you say something bad to a teacher, you might be told to leave the room. If you break a thing that is not yourself, the person in power might take you away to a place where you will stay until you learn to act right.

Sometimes, being under the power of a person in a place of power is not a bad thing. It means you are doing what is right. But sometimes, it means you are in trouble because you did not follow the person in power.
