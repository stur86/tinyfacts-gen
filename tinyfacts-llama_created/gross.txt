To be a bit too much is to be a lot. If you eat too many big pieces of food, you feel full at once. If you wear too many clothes, you feel hot. A big, strong dog can move fast. A small child can run as fast as a big dog.

We also use this word when we talk about money. If a person has a lot of money, it is a big number. If a person has a small number of money, they are short. If a person has a lot of money, they are very happy. If a person has a small number of money, they feel sad.

To be a bit too much can be a problem. If you do something that makes others angry, you might be in trouble. You might have to say sorry. If you do not stop, you might hurt someone. But if you learn to control your words and your body, you can stay safe.
