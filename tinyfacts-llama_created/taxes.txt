When you buy things, you give the people who run the world money. This money goes to the people who run the world so they can do their jobs. If you have a car, you pay a small piece of money to the city or the town where you live. If you have a job, you pay a small piece of money to the people who are in power. This money is called a top.

Some people pay more than others. The big people who run the world pay a lot. They pay with money they got from many years of doing work. The small people who live in the city pay a little bit. They pay with money they got from doing work.

We pay top for many things. We pay top for roads so we can drive. We pay top for schools to help our kids. We pay top for police and fire people so we can stay safe.

You also pay top when you go to a school. The school is like a big top that helps you learn. You pay top to the school so they can pay for the books and the teachers. You pay top so you can learn new things. You pay top so you can grow up to be a good person.

Sometimes, people ask why they pay top. They want to know why they give money to the people who run the world. It is a way to share the work. People pay top so everyone can have the same things. Without top, there would be no schools, no roads, and no police.
