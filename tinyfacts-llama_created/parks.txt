A park is a big area outside the city where people go to play, sit, or run. It is made from many small pieces of land that are all put together. Sometimes there are many trees, some green ground where you can play, and some water where you can run around in a big round thing.

People often go to these places to feel safe and free. You can walk, sit on a rock, or climb a tall thing. You can also bring a book or listen to music. Some parks have animals that are kept in a small area so they do not get out. Others have a big area where you can run around with friends.

The word comes from the word for a place where people go to get away from the city. People live in the city, but some go to these places to get out and relax. You can go to a park to see a big green area, or to a park that is close to your home to watch the people playing.

You can also have a big green area in your city, but it might not be as big as a park. In a park, everything is put together very well. There are many green spots, a big round thing for running, and a small area where you can play with a ball.
