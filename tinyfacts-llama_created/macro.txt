A word is a thing that people use to talk about the world or things.

A small word that means a very big group of things is called a group. A group of people who all say the same thing is a group. A group of animals that all eat the same food is a group. A group of people who all do the same thing is a group too.

A group of things that are very far apartments from each other is a group. If you are in a city and you see a person who is in a city far away, you are in a group with them.

Sometimes, we make a group of many people and give them a name. This name might tell us which city they live in or which school they go to. We use this name when we talk about them in a group.

A group can also be a group of things that are very different. If you are in a school and you see a person who is very tall and has very bright hair, you are in a group of people who like that kind of person.

We also use this word when we talk about big things that cover a lot of space. The world is a big group of many different places all put together.

So, a group is a way to talk about how many things are together and what they have in mind.
