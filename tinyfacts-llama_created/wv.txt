To write a word or a short piece of writing with one hand while the other hand works on something else is to use the hands in different ways. 

You might sit at a desk with your right hand holding a piece of paper and your left hand moving words on a computer. Your right hand is doing what you want the computer to do. Your left hand is doing what you want your computer to remember. This way, you can give your brain a different kind of work to do at the same time.

Sometimes, people do this with words. They write with one hand while the other hand listens to music. Or they write a short story and use their other hand to make a picture of what they saw. This is a great way to be both very good at two different things.
