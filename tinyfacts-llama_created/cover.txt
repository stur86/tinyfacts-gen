To cover means to keep something under your body or around it.

Imagine you are playing a game where you must keep your hands safe. You cover your hands so you can see what is happening around you. You cover your hands with a piece of paper or a soft thing so you do not hurt yourself.

This is how we cover things when we are afraid. We cover our faces so we do not see the things that make us scared. We cover our bodies so we do not touch things that are bad.

You can also cover something with a soft thing. If you have a small baby, you might cover the baby with a soft thing to keep them warm. You cover the thing with a soft clothes.

Sometimes, to cover something means to make it look nice. You cover a picture with a soft paper so it looks nice for people to see. You cover a book with a cover so it looks pretty and people will want to read it.

So, to cover is to keep something under your body or around it. It is to keep things safe so they do not hurt you.
