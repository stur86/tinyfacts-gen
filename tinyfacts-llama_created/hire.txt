To hear something is to listen. When you hear a sound, your brain works to understand what the sound means. You might hear a voice from a friend, a loud noise from a car, or a quiet sound from a tree. You might see a picture with moving parts. Your brain uses all this to know what is happening.

To work is to do a job. You might work in a school to learn things. You might work in a big room to keep people safe. You might work in a store to give people things they need. When you work, you are doing something for someone else. You are helping them.

To take something is to get it. If you take a book from a friend, you are taking it. If you take a ball from a child, you are taking it. When you take something, you are moving it from one place to another. You are moving it to a place where you want it.

To buy something is to pay for it. When you buy a food, a thing you wear, or a thing to play with, you are giving money. You are giving the person who made the thing your money. In return, they give you what you need.

To work for a job is to do the job. When you work for a person, you are doing the job they give you. You are following their orders. You are doing what they want you to do. You are helping them make money.

When someone works, they are not alone. They work with other people. They share ideas and do things together. They talk to each other, move around, and use their brains. They are a team.
