To say something is great often means to feel how strong or important it is. You can use this word to tell how big a thing is or how good a feeling is.

If you are very happy, you are being great. Maybe you did something you loved and you felt so happy that your face was bright and your eyes were big. Your smile might have been so wide that people could see it from far away.

If a game is very good, you might say it is great. Maybe you played a game with your friends and had a lot of fun. You were not afraid to try new things and you enjoyed how the game went on. You were so happy that you laughed a lot.

You can also say that something is great when it is very strong. If a tree is very tall, you can say it is great. It stands up high and does not fall down. It is like a big friend in the ground.

Sometimes, you might say something is great for you. Maybe you are feeling better after eating a good food. You feel happy and your body feels nice. You are not thinking about anything else.

To say something is great can help you feel happy. It makes you want to shout or do a happy dance. It makes you feel like everything is okay.
