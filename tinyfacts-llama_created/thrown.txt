To thrown something is to move it far away. You can do this with a ball, a small rock, or even a small piece of paper. You start with your hand and push it. The force from your hand pushes the thing out. It goes across the ground and into the air.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You throw a small ball to your friend. The ball goes to your friend. Your friend catches it. The ball is now in their hands. They hold it and can play with it. You did something and the thing went to another person.

You can also throw a thing that is heavy. You stand up and push. Your legs are strong and push the heavy thing. The force from your legs makes the heavy thing go far. It goes across the ground and into the air. This can be fun if you are a strong person.

Sometimes you throw a thing to make it go back to you. You might throw a ball to a person in a game. They catch it. The person then throws the ball back to you. You are now holding the ball and ready to do something with it.

Throwing is a way to move something. It is a way to make something go across a wide space.
