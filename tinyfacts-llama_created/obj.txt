An thing you use every day is an important thing. This word is about these kinds of things.

When you use a thing, you are using it to do a job. You might use a phone to talk to a friend. You use a chair to sit down. You use a book to learn something new.

Everything has an owner. The owner is the person who holds the thing. The owner might keep it in their home. They might keep it in a box. The owner might give it to another person.

An owner can also be a thing that is given to another person. When a child gets a new car, they become the owner. Later, when the car is time to be given back to the owner, the child becomes the new owner.

Sometimes, we use words to talk about things that we do not own. We might say, a friend is an important person in our life. This is because friends are people we care about. They are not things, but people.

In a group of people, we use this word to talk about how things work. If a person is doing a good job, we might say they are the owner of the job. If a group of people work together, they can be the owner of a big thing. This means they are working together to make something happen.

So, an owner is the person or thing that holds the thing. It is the one who does the job or keeps the thing.
