To exactly means you are very sure about how something is. It is not far from the real thing. It is like you have seen it with your own eyes.

Imagine you want to know if something is the same as the other thing. If you say it is, you are exactly. You did not just guess or say something that was almost the same. You knew for sure.

Think about a line. If you put a line on the ground and another line across the top of a book, they are almost in the same spot. But if you put a line on the ground and a line on a wall, they are not even close. One is very close and the other is not at all the same.

You can also use this word with numbers. If you say a number is exactly, you mean the number is the same as another number. If you add two numbers together and the answer is the same, then you know the numbers are the same. You are not just guessing or getting a wrong answer.

This word is important in many parts of life. In school, when you write your work, you must use this word. In games, if you say a number is exactly, you are saying it is the same as another number. In your own life, if you say something is exactly, you mean you are very sure.

So, exactly is about being very sure.
