An air sound. When you breathe, air goes in and out. You can hear air moving inside your chest. This sound is what everyone hears all day. It is like a soft moving. If you hold your breath for a long time, you feel the air going away. Your ears tell you this.

Sometimes you hear this sound when you listen to music. When a group of people single, their voices make a soft air sound. It is like a group of small moving things. The sound comes from the people and goes out into the air. You can hear it even when you are far away.

People also use this word to talk about money. If you spend money on something, the sound you hear is like the sound of money going in and out. It is a sign that you have done something with the money. When you pay for something, you are making an air sound with your money.
