To be or to have something is often the same, but the words give different ideas.

To be is about how you are now. You are here, you are there, you are this way. This is when something is true. You can not be nothing. You can not be dead. You can be very old, very big, very heavy. You can be a person, a dog, a car, a tree, or a star. You are the one you are.

To have is about what you have. You have a house, a car, a book, a friend, or a job. You have things that are important to you. If you do not have these things, you feel sad. If you do have them, you feel happy.

Sometimes, one thing is like another thing. You can be a person and you can be a thing. You can be a person who is standing in a room, but you are also a person who is not standing. You are the same person, but in different places. You are the same person, but in a different time. You are the same person, but in a different world.

You can also have something in your mind. You can have a big fear or a small fear. You can have a happy feeling or a sad feeling. You can have a strong idea or a small idea. You can have a plan to do something or a plan to do nothing. You can have a feeling that is always there or a feeling that changes every day.

Being and having are both important. When you are, you are living. When you have, you have life. You can not live without having. You can not be happy without being. This is why we love to be with people, to have things we like, and to have the things that make us feel good.
