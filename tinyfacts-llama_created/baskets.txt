A bag is a thing you use to carry things. It is made of one piece of strong stuff. You fill it with many things. You can put many books in one bag. You can also put many small things like tiny pieces of paper or a few small pieces of paper for writing.

A bag is also a thing you put things in. You put clothes in it. You put a small bag with a key in your bag. This bag is in your pocket. You can take it out when you want to go somewhere.

Sometimes a bag is a thing that holds food. You put food in it to eat later. It keeps the food from falling out. You can also buy a bag with a long top. You can put your lunch in it. This bag helps you carry your food.

People also use bags to carry money. You can buy things with money from a store. When you finish buying, you put the money in your bag. This bag helps you keep your money safe.

A bag can be small or big. A small bag is easy to carry. A big bag is heavy. You may use it to move things from one place to another.
