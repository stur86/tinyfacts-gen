A thing you put on something else is called a thing that sticks. This is when you put a small piece of paper or a small piece of hard stuff on a thing you use every day. You might put a picture on your desk or a name on your shirt.

Sometimes the thing that sticks is very small. You can put these small things on a book or on a table. They are very easy to put on because they do not hurt.

Other times, the thing that sticks is not so small. It is a big piece of paper with many pictures or words. You can put these on your face or your arms. When you put it on, it stays there and you feel a little happy.

You can also put things that stick on things that are not made from paper. You can put them on the floor so people do not walk on them. Or you can put them on a wall so people can see them when they walk by.

Some things that stick are good for children. They are a fun way to show you care about them. You can give them a small thing and say, I love you. This makes the child feel happy.
