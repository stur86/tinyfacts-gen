A person who is a high leader. In a group, they might be the one who makes the big moves. They are the ones who are important and help the group work well together.

They are often very strong at making sure everyone follows the right way. They might be the one who decides what is important and what is not. They have to be very good at making good ideas and keeping everyone safe.

Sometimes, these people are very good at making money. They might have a big name and many people like them. This makes them very important in the world.

Even though being a high leader is a good job, it can also be very hard. They have to deal with many people and make sure they all feel safe. They have to be strong and calm when things get very bad.
