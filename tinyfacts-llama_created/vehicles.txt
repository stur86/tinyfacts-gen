A car is a thing that you drive. It is made to go very fast. It has a hard seat for the person who wants to sit. There is a big round thing in the front that makes the car go. There is a box in the back that holds food for the person. You put your feet inside the box and you are ready to go.

Cars are used to get around cities. They go past big high ways and through streets with many houses. Some cars go very fast and can go many times a minute. Others are slower and go very little. Some cars are very big and have many seats for many people. Some cars are small and only seat one person.

You can buy a car from a store. You pay money for it and it is ready to go. Some cars are very old and need to be fixed many times. You must always follow the car maker and the police when you are driving. It is very important to be safe and not go too fast when the road is wet or dark.

Cars also have many things that make them go. There are two big round things that push the car. These push the car around so it does not hit the ground too much. There are also two round things that make the car go in a line. These round things are like a long line of moving things.

Sometimes people drive cars to go to work or to school. They drive to places where they need to be. Some people drive alone and some drive with friends. Cars can also go to places where people do not walk. They help us get from one place to another quickly and easily.
