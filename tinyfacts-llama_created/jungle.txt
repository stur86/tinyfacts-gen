A big green place in the middle of a land with many high ground. It is home to many kinds of animals that can not live in cold places. The ground is soft and the trees are tall. The air is warm and it is very bright.

You can not see the ground at all. You can only feel it. The trees are very big. They are tall and they have many long things that go out from them. These long things are like big arms that hold the sky over them. The ends of these long things go down into the ground. The long things help keep the air from going out.

In the ground there are big round things that make things that go round. These things help make the ground turn. The ground turns and the air moves over it. The animals use this to move around. They run and they jump and they fly.

The ground is very full of green things. The green things grow from the ground. Some of them are green and some are brown. Some are red. Some are white. The green things are like big parts of the ground. They make the ground look like a green floor.

Animals like to live in the ground. They have strong bodies that can hold them up. They have a hard skin on their back that keeps them safe. They use their long arms to move and to find food. They eat things that grow in the green things. They drink water from small clear things.

Some animals like to live on the big round things. They climb up high and then they fly across the sky. They go very fast and they see many things from above. They can see many high ground and many wide water ways.

The ground is also home to many kinds of small animals. These animals have legs and they go very fast. They can fly and they can run. They have small bodies that can fit into tiny spaces. They eat things that grow in the green things.

The ground is very big. It is many times bigger than a city. It covers a huge area of land. It is the ground under the big round things. It is the ground under the high ground. It is the ground under the many green things.

A long time ago, there were no houses on the ground. People went into the big round things to find food. They went up high and down low. They walked on the hard ground and they ran on the soft ground. They carried things on their back and they used the air to go.

Now people live on the ground. They build houses and schools. They make things to use. They use the big round things to make power. They use the green things to make food. They use the air to go.

Many people want to live in the ground. They like the green things and the big round things. They like the sound of the water and the sound of the wind. They like the bright sun and the dark night. They like the feeling of the ground under their feet.

But people must also care about the ground. They must keep the ground clean. They must not cut down the big round things. They must not hurt the green things. They must not make too much power from the big round things. They must take care of the animals and the ground so that they can stay there for many years to come.
