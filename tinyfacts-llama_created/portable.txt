To carry something is to move it from one place to another. You can carry your books, your phone, or even your food. You do this when you need to go to a different room in your house or to a place where you do not have a chair.

Imagine you have a bag with your clothes inside. You are carrying the bag. You do not need to sit on it or put it on the floor. You can walk quickly from one room to another, so you do not have to stay in one place for too long.

Sometimes people carry things that are heavy. If you carry a heavy box, it is heavy. You must carry it so it does not fall down. You can walk slowly with the box, or you can ask someone to carry it for you.

You can also carry things that are small. A small bag can hold many tiny things like a ball, a small book, or a piece of paper. You can carry it in your hand or on your back. This is a good way to move around when you need to go to a different room.

If you carry money, you carry it with you. You do not need to put it in a box. You can carry it in your pocket. When you are feeling sad or angry, you might carry a small thing that makes you feel better. It could be a small stuffed animal, a funny picture, or a piece of paper that says you are strong.
