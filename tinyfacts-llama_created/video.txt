A short time on a box, where you can see a person or a thing, moving. People watch these short times to learn about far away places, or to see what happens in a movie. They also watch them to laugh, to feel happy, or to be scared.

A long time later, something like this is made for your computer. You put your phone into the computer, and it starts to show you a moving picture. The picture moves around your room, or it shows you a far away place. You can look at it in the morning, or at night, and you can watch it over and over again.

Sometimes, you can make your own. You can put your voice into the computer, and it makes a moving picture. You can tell a story, show a joke, or just do something funny. This is called a movie.

You can also watch a short time that is made for a game. The game is about a person, and you use your brain to do things. You press a few numbers, and the person does what you want them to do. It is like a game, but with moving pictures and sounds.

In some places, people make short times that are very funny. They show things that are not real, like a cat that jumps on a table, or a dog that does a funny thing. These short times are often called funny movies.
