A long time ago, people would decide how to act. They might say if you can do something or if you must do something. This is called a plan. It helps everyone know what to do.

Today, people use this word more. They make plans for how to do things. If a school has a plan for how to teachers, it means they have decided how the school will work. They are following a plan. This helps make things clear for everyone.

Some plans are about money. A city might have a plan for how to pay for things. They decide how to spend the money. This is important because it helps make sure everyone gets what they need.

Sometimes, a plan is about how to act in a situation. If you are afraid, you might have a plan. You know what to do. You can walk away or you can stay. This helps when you are feeling scared or worried.

A plan can be about a person, a group, or a group of people. It can be about how to work together or how to act in a world that is changing fast.
