To be a good person means you act in a way that makes everyone around you feel safe and happy. You follow the way that is right for your town or school. You listen to what other people have to say. You try to be kind and not make others afraid.

A person who is very good at this is called a person who is kind. They help others when they need it. They are good at being a friend. They do not use their words to hurt people. They use words that are nice and make everyone feel better.

You can also be a person who is not kind. You might use your words to laugh at or yell at a friend. You might make someone feel bad. You might act in a way that makes everyone afraid.

Being a person who is kind is important because it helps everyone feel good. It helps people feel safe. It makes the world a better place.
