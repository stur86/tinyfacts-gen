To use a word means to use it in your talk. When you use a word, you are telling people what you think. You tell them that you mean this word. Sometimes, you just use the word without thinking. You might say, you like this drink. You did not mean to tell them to drink it.

When you say a word is usual, you are telling them that it is the way things are. It is what you do every day. If you always do something, it is usual. You do not have to say it is usual every time you do it. It is just what you are doing.

Think about how you feel when you are tired. If you are always tired, you say it is usual. You are not saying you are always tired. You are telling people that this is the way you feel. It is usual to feel tired.

You can also say, it is usual to go to bed at night. You do not have to say it is usual every time you go to bed. It is just what you do. You follow this.

Sometimes people use this word to talk about things that are not usually done. If someone says, it is usual to laugh, it means they do not think it is funny. They are surprised.
