To reach something means to get to it. If you want to eat a food, you need to walk or run until you reach it. If you are playing a game, you need to move your body until you reach the end of the line.

If you reach a high place, you have gone up. You have reached the top of a tall building. You can see for a long time because the ground is far away. You can see the city or the water and the sky.

If you reach a friend, you have found a person you like. You talk to them and they talk to you. You understand each other. You are happy to see them.

To reach a place is also important. Imagine you are walking through the woods. You walk for a long time and at last, you see a green green area. You walk some more and you see a small house. You enter the house and you see a nice room. You feel happy because you found the house.

When you reach something, you are close to it. You have done some work to get there. You are very happy because you have reached the end of the line.
