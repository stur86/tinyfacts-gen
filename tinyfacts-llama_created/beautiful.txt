The word beautiful is very important. To be beautiful is to make someone feel happy. When we see a person who is very pretty, we smile. We might want to touch them or play with them.

A thing can be beautiful if it is made to look nice. A white dress is beautiful. It is made from long, soft white stuff. A bright blue sky is also beautiful. It is very high in the sky and it looks very nice.

A person can also be beautiful. A girl who has bright hair or pretty eyes can be very beautiful. A man who has a strong body and a happy face can also be beautiful. We all like to see people who are beautiful.

Sometimes, something that is not very big or not very strong can be very beautiful. A small dog can be very beautiful. A small tree can be beautiful too. It may not grow very tall, but it is still nice to look at.

Being beautiful is not just about how we look. It is also about how we act. If we are kind and smile at everyone we meet, we can be very beautiful. We can show that we care about the world and the people around us.

You can also be beautiful if you are inside. A person who is very kind and gives to others is beautiful inside. They make the world a better place.
