Learner is to learn and understand something new. It is about being ready for the things you will do next. You might learn how to play a game, or you might learn how to speak a new word. When you learn, you feel like you are getting closer to being a person who knows many things.

There is a thing that people use to keep their hands and feet. It is made from the skin of animals. The skin is cut and made into long pieces. These pieces are then put together to make a strong cover. People use this cover for many things. They put it on their shoes so they do not get hurt, or they put it on their clothes so they do not get marks.

The word comes from the word for hair. A long time ago, people made the cover from the hair of the animal. They would cut the hair and then put the hair into the cover. This way, the cover had hair on it. Today, people make the cover from the skin of other animals. They do not cut the skin, so the cover stays the same for many years.

You can also learn about a place. If you go to a new city, you learn about its people, its food, and its way of life. You might learn to speak the city talk. This is like learning a new word. You are learning to understand how to be a person in that place.
