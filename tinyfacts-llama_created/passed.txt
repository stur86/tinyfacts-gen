To pass something means you move it from one person to another. It is when you hold a thing for a long time and then you let it go to another person.

Imagine you have a ball. You keep it for a very long time. Then you give it to your friend. Your friend takes the ball and they play with it. Later, they give the ball to someone else. The ball has gone from you to them.

You can also pass a thing for money. If you have money, you can give it to someone who needs it. This helps them buy food or a house. You are helping them without taking anything back.

Sometimes, you pass a story. If you like a book, you tell your friends about it. You tell them about the people and the places. You make them want to read the book too.

A person can also pass a job. If someone is good at a job, others might want them to do it. They pass on the job to another person so the job stays being done.

When you pass something, you show that it has been given to you. It is a way to share something with someone else.
