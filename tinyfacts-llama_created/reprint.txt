To really mean something is the same. When you first see a thing, it is new. After you look at it many times, it becomes like the thing you first saw. You do not feel the same way anymore.

If you want to show you really like something, you can really do it. You might go back to the place where you first saw it. You might read it again, or you might watch it one more time. The thing is the same, but you have a memory of it. You remember how it felt.

You can also really do something that you do not want to do. You might feel a lot of pain when you cut yourself. After you do it, you feel happy because it is over. You did it, and you can stop. You are not the same as when you first did it.

Sometimes, we want to show that something is new. We might put it in a box and say it is a new thing. But when we look at it, it is the same old thing. The box is only a way to show we care. You can not put a thing in a box and then say it is the same as when you first saw it. The thing is always the same, no matter how you show it.
