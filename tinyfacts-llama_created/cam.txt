A came is something that opens. It is a round thing that you put inside a door, or a window, or a box. When the door opens, the round thing moves.

You can use a came to let light in, or to open a window. It is also used in cars and on the floor of a bus to open a door and let in air. This helps to keep the room or the car cool and makes it feel better.

When you use a came to open a door, you must push it. You press the round thing with your hand. If you do not push it, it will not open. The came is like a hand that is turned to open a door.
