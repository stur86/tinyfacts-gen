A sad person is someone who feels very sad. They might cry a lot. They might feel the ground is hard to walk on. They might want to stay inside their house and not go out.

Sometimes, a sad person just feels that they are not happy. They might feel that they did not do the best thing. They might think about the past and remember a bad thing that happened.

It is also a word for a thing that makes you feel sad. You might see a picture that makes you feel sad. You might hear music that makes you feel sad. You might see a person who looks sad.

You can also be sad if you miss a friend. You might feel very alone and wish you were with them. You might want to call them and talk. You might want to see them again.

Sad days are days when you feel the sad inside your heart. It is a time to feel what you feel.
