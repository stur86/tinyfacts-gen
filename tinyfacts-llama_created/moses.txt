A man is a person who lives on a place with green ground and big water. He can walk, run, or fly through the sky. He also does work for a person or a group. When people say he is the one, it means he did a great thing.

A long time ago, there was a man who was very old. He was sent by the people who lived near a big water to go to a far away place. This was a hard way. Many days went by, and he did not have food. He was very sad and angry.

One day, he saw a high green ground where people did not have to walk or run to get from one place to another. He saw a place where the water was calm and clear. He saw a green ground where people did not have to walk. He saw a green ground where people did not have to run. He saw a green ground where people did not have to fly through the sky. He saw a place where the ground was soft and the water was calm.

He was so happy that he wanted to go there. He took his children and went. He walked on the green ground, and his children went on a small flying thing. They were all happy.

Later, he came back and told the people that he had seen the green ground where people did not have to walk. He told them that it was a place where they could live without much work. They were very happy.

The story of the man who went to the green ground and told them about it is called the story of the man who brought us the green ground.
