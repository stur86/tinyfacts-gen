To join something means you put it together so that it becomes one thing. It happens when two or more things meet. One of them goes across, and the other one goes around, so they can fit together.

You can join people. If you are friends and you want to talk to someone else, you can join them. You all speak the same words and you are part of the group now.

You can also join things. If a book has a cover, the cover is the part you put on the book. If you put the book together with the cover, you join them. The book is now a one thing with a cover and a cover that goes around it.

If you join your feet, you are putting them together. You are making one foot from many feet.

You can also join things that are not people. If you put two balls next to each other, they become one ball. The two balls are the one ball now.

Sometimes, joining can mean being part of a group. If you go to school, you are joining many other children who are also going to school. You are all in the same group.

Joining is important because it helps us stay together. If you join a team, you are working with other people to do a job. You are all doing the same thing.

You can also join the world. If you watch a movie, you are joining the world of the story. You are seeing what happens next.

So, joining is about putting things together so they become one thing. It can mean being part of a group, joining people, or joining two things that are not people.
