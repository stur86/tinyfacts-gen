To Promises

A person who promises is a person who says they will do something. When you promise, you say you are going to do it later. You do not have to do it right now. You might promise to help your friend if they are sad.

A person who promises can be a friend, a teacher, or a leader. They say they will do something for you. If they do not do it, you are very sad. This can hurt your heart.

Sometimes you promise to be nice to someone. If you are nice, the other person feels good. This is good because we like to be happy.

You can also promise to do a job. If you say you will do a job, you need to do it. If you do not do it, your job is over.

To Promises is also about words. When you say you are going to do something, you are promising. You are telling people that you will do it later. This word is very important in our lives.
