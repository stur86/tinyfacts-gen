To see someone or something is to look at them. It means you want to know who they are, what they do, and where they are. It is like trying to find a thing you have lost.

If you see a person, you might want to talk to them. You might want to ask them a question. You might want to know why they are there. This is one way to see someone.

Another way to see is to look at a place. If you walk into a room, you see the things that are there. You see the table, the chair, the books on the wall. You see the floor under your feet. This is seeing a place.

Sometimes, you see things that are not there. You see a light in the dark. You see a small animal that is hiding behind a tree. You see a person who is afraid. This is also seeing.
