To do something means you act on a thing. You use the thing to help others or to learn new things. When you are given a job, you must follow the orders the person who gave you the job tells you to do. This is called an answer.

In a school, you might have to write a story. You write the words using your brain and your hands. Later, you show the story to a teacher. The teacher looks at the story and says, yes, you did that right. You are happy because you did the job well.

You can also do something for a person. You might help them move from a house to a new home. You carry their boxes and their things while they walk. This is helping someone.

Sometimes, you get a job where you do a work for a long time. You work every day and every week. You do not get to do anything else. You have to follow the orders you get from your person who paid you.

An order is a word or a number that tells you to do something. When you hear this word, you must follow it. If you do not, the person who gave the order might be very angry. This is why it is very important to follow orders.
