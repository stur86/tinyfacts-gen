To share means you give something to another person or people. When you share a book, you give the book to a friend. You can share a game with a group of people. This is when everyone plays together.

Sometimes you share a thing that you own. You can share your phone with a friend. This means you let them use it. You are not keeping it for yourself anymore.

When you share, you help others. You are making room for new people. This is important because it lets everyone have something they need.

You can also share your time. If you have a lot to do and you can only do one thing for a long time, you might share it with a friend. This means you do the work for them.

Sometimes people share money. If you have a lot of money and you want to help people who do not have much, you might give some to them. This is a way to be kind to others.
