To care means to have a feeling that makes you want to help other people. You might care about your friends, your family, your home, or the world around you. It is about showing that you are there for them when they need you.

Imagine you have a cat. You take care of it every day. You give it food, sit next to it, and make sure it is safe. If the cat gets hurt, you go to see a doctor or find help. You care about the cat because you want to see it live a good life.

You might also care about your body. If you feel sick, you want to feel better. You might take care of your mouth when you are not eating, or you might run to the bathroom so you do not get hurt. You are caring for yourself so you do not get too bad.

Care is about showing love and care in your life. It is about making sure people are safe and happy. It is not just about doing things, but about how you make other people feel.
