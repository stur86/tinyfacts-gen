To become something means you start as a small thing and then you grow and change into a bigger thing. This happens in many ways.

Imagine you are a small animal. You can only live in one place at a time. One day you get a tiny cut that needs to be fixed. You go to a place where you can get help and the help people fix the cut. After they fix it, the cut is gone, and the animal becomes a new kind of animal. It has grown bigger and is now in a different place.

You can also become something by learning. If you want to play a song you have never heard before, you have to listen to it many times. You start to understand what is being said and how it sounds. After many times you listen, you become the one who can play that song. You are becoming the person who can play that song.

Sometimes you become something by making a new thing. You might make a small box from wood and paper. You start with nothing. You cut, you put the wood together, and you make a box. The box becomes a new thing. You have become something new because you took something old and made it into something new.

Sometimes, you become someone you are not. Maybe you start to act like a person you do not feel like being. You might wear clothes that are not your favorite or say words that are not your usual way. This can make you feel different. You are becoming something you are not because you are changing who you are.

Being becomes something when you are growing and changing. It is when you are moving from one thing to another. It is when you are learning, growing, and becoming new. It is a big change that is happening all the time in our lives.
