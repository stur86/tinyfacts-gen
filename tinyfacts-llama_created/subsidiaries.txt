An idea that is made in one place can become many places. This happens when a big group of people decide to start many smaller groups. The big group is called the parents. The smaller groups are called the children.

The parents give money and set some ways for the children to work. They might also decide what the children do and how they do it. This way, the children can learn from the parents and start doing things on their own.

The children can do many different things. One might work in a store. Another might work in a kitchen. Another might work in a school. In this way, the children become many different groups with many different jobs.

This is how a group can grow. It starts with one person, then many people join. It can become a big group with many people all working together.

People often start a child to learn how to do something. They give them money and help them get started. As the child works and does well, they become more and more in control. They might start to work alone or with other children.

This can be good for everyone. The parents get money from the children. The children get to learn and do things they love. The parents are not always in the same place as the children.
