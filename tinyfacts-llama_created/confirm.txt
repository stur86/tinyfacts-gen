To be sure something is true is to know it is right. You can do this by looking at what is happening, or by talking to people who know about it.

If you say a thing is true, you are saying it is real and it does not change. If you tell a friend that a book is very good, you are telling them something is right.

If you try to know if something is sure, you look for the facts. You look at what people say. You look at what happens. You try to find out if what you think is true. If you find out you are wrong, you must change what you think.

Sometimes we say a thing is sure. This means we are sure it will happen. If a person is sure that a thing will happen, they are trying to be safe. They do not want to be in a place where the thing will not happen.

You can also be sure about your own life. If you know you are who you are and you do not change, you are sure.

In a group, if everyone says a thing is true, it is sure. You can not question this because everyone is saying it is true. This is how you know something is real and what is right.
