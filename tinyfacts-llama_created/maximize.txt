To make the most of something is to use it to the fullest. It means to find out how to use a thing so you can do the best you can with it. If you have a computer and you only look at pictures, you might not use it as well as you could. But if you watch movies and read books, you use it more.

You can also use this word for your own life. If you are very happy, you have made the most of your day. If you are trying to do something and you do not get it right, you might try again. This is how you grow. You use your chances to get better.

Sometimes, to make the most of something can mean to use it for a good reason. If you want to be the best in a game, you try very hard. This is to make the most of the time you have. It is a way of being strong in the face of a problem.
