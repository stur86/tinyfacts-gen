To start something means to begin it. This is when you start to do a job or to play a game.

Imagine you want to run. You have to start from the beginning. You must put your feet on the ground and move forward. You do not just stand there. You start moving and then you keep going. This is starting.

You can start with many things. You can start a new day. You can start a new book. You can start a new movie. You can even start a new year. This is when you begin a new time and start a new way of being.

When you start a new thing, you do not know everything yet. You learn as you go. You may find problems early on. But you do not give up. You keep trying. This is what makes things start.

You can also start something that is already there. You might start to build a house. You are starting with the ground and the walls. You are moving forward from where you are now to where you want to be. This is starting a new way of living.

Sometimes, starting means to let go of something. You start to leave a place or a person behind. You start to move away from a problem. This is when you let go and you start a new life.

In short, starting is when you begin something new. It is the start of a new way, a new time, or a new life.
