An end is a word that tells you when something is over. Imagine you are playing a game. You run around a big round thing. After you do many steps, you feel tired. You stop. The game is over. The end is the point where you stop playing.

You can also use this word with computers. When you write a story, you need to know when the story will end. You might write many lines, and then you say, the story is over. This helps you know when to stop writing and when to stop reading.

Sometimes, you might use this word with people. If you are in a class, the teacher says, the end of the day is coming. This means the school day is almost over. You might feel a little sad because you want the day to be over.

In a song, the words tell you when to stop. The song might have a long part at the beginning, and then it ends. This is like the end of the day or the end of the story.
