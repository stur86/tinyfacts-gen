A long piece of soft, strong stuff used for holding together many things. You might see it in a room when a person is about to walk in. They might hold the long piece behind their neck, so it does not fall down.

You can also use it to show love. A person might give their friend a small, round piece of soft stuff. This shows they care. The small piece is like a little story. Inside, there is one word that says how much they love the friend.

Long ago, people made the long piece from the skin of animals. They cut it into small pieces and put the pieces together. Later, they made it from other things, like from the skin of the animal. Now, you can make your own.

You can also use it to keep things closed. If you have a box with a small round part, you can put the round part over the top. This keeps the air out. You can use this kind of thing to keep food in your bag when you are going to a place where the air is bad.
