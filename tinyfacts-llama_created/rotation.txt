To turn

A long time ago, people didn't have cars or computers. They used things that went round and round to move around places.

Imagine you are standing in a room. You look at a ball on the floor. The ball goes around the person who holds it. The person does not move. The ball does not stop. It keeps going around them.

A thing that turns like this is called a turn. It keeps going around in a round way. People use these to move from one place to another. You might turn around to go back to your own home. You might turn to go to a friend who lives far away.

A turn can also mean to change. Imagine you are in a line of people waiting to go into a room. If the line turns around, everyone moves to the other side. This is a turn. You are moving to a new place.

Sometimes, things turn so much that they fall down. A small ball will go around many times and then it falls down to the floor. This is a turn.

In the world, things turn in other ways too. The sun turns around in space. The world turns on its side. The days turn around, so the sun comes up in the morning and sets at night.

So, a turn is something that goes around, keeps going, and changes places. It is a way to move and change.
