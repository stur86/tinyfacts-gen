To summered means to make a short story about many things. It helps us know what happened and why. You can do this with your life, with a book, or with a movie.

You start with the beginning, and then you tell the middle part. The end is the most important part. You are not telling the whole story, just the bits you think are most important.

Sometimes you must cut out parts that are not so good. You can only keep what is good and clear. This is why you need to get the best from the story.

To summered someone means you are making them know what they did. You are helping them see how they acted. This is important for schools and for people who work with others.

You can also use this word to talk about a story that is very big. If you are talking about a long book, you might say, the book is the whole story. If you are talking about a movie, you might say, the movie is the whole story.
