An animal is a thing that grows from the ground and moves around. It has a body that is hard and does not break easily. Animals need to eat to have power to move.

Most animals are small. Some are big and can fly. Some are very small and can only see very well. Some animals can see in the dark and can hear sounds that are too quiet for a person to hear.

Animals need food to stay going. They look for food in the ground, in the water, or in the air. Some animals catch food with their mouths. Other animals have to eat the green parts of the ground or the parts of other animals.

Animals live in many different kinds of homes. Some live in the ground with many other animals. Some live in the water. Some live in trees high up in the air. Some live in the ground under the ground.

Animals are very important. They help make the world a better place by eating the green parts of the world and taking out dead animals that might hurt people. Some animals help keep the water clean. Some animals give us food to eat.
