To put words together to make something new is to do this job. You start with a piece of writing and add your own words. You change how a person feels, what they say, or what the story tells.

Imagine you are making a picture of a bright day. You start with a picture that is dark and cold. You look at the picture and decide to add some bright colors. You move the bright colors to the top and bottom of the picture. You also move the dark parts so they are near the middle. Now the picture is bright and warm.

You did this same work many times before you became a good writer. You learned how to put words together to make new pieces of writing that tell stories, explain ideas, or make people feel.

People also use this word to talk about music. When you want to make a song sound better, you might use a word to change the way the sound looks. You change how high or low the sound is, how fast it goes, or how much it moves when it reaches the end. This makes the song sound like it was made by someone else, but it is still the same song.
