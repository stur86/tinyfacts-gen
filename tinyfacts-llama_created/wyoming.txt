To go to the high place in the sky is a long way. This place is in the middle of the big water. People who live there go there many times a year. They go there to see the water and to breathe air that is very different from the air we breathe.

In this place, there are many big rocks that go up very high into the sky. These rocks are not very big, but they are very hard. You can stand on top of them and see the whole sky. Some people go to the top of these rocks to see the stars at night.

There are also many green places in this place. The green is from the tall green things that grow on the ground. These things grow very fast in the warm sun. In the summer, the green is so green that it looks like a big green cover over the ground.

The people who live here work with the land. They build houses that sit on the ground, and they grow food to eat. They also work with the rocks and green things. They make things from these things that people use every day.

This place is also home to many animals. There are animals that can fly, animals that can live in the cold water, and animals that can run very fast. There are also animals that can go very high into the sky on their backs.

The name of this place comes from the word for the sun. In the old days, people thought that the sun was a big thing in the sky. They said that the sun was the one that gives light to the world. Today, we know that the sun is a big ball of fire in the sky, but we still call it the one that gives light.
