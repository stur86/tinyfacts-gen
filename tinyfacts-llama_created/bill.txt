A thing you pay money for.

When you say someone has a problem, you are talking about how they do things. If they do not follow a way, they have a problem. You might say,  They have a problem with their job. They do not do what they say they will do.

A person or a group gives money to a place. The money is for the place to do something. They say,  We are going to pay for the food. They pay in money. The money is like a sign that says,  I have paid for this.

When you buy something, you pay money. You might buy food, clothes, or a book. If you do not pay, the thing you buy will not work. It will be broken. If you do not pay, you must face the problem.
