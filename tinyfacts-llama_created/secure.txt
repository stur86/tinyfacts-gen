To be safe means you are in a place where there is no fear. You are not afraid of anything. You feel calm and happy. You can walk around without feeling worried.

Think about your home. You have a door that keeps you safe. You close the door when you are not in the house. This keeps people and animals that might want to get in from going away. You feel safe because you know you are not alone.

You can also be safe in a place that is not a house. A park is a safe place to be. You can play with your friends and do not worry about anything. The park is a place where you can be safe and have fun.

Sometimes, you might be safe because you are not doing anything bad. You are not doing anything that you should not do. You are just being you. You are safe until something happens that makes you feel afraid.
