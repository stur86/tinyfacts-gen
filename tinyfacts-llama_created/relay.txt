To run a long way in a group is to be a runner. You are in a line with many other people. One of the people is very strong. They start and run. They go a little ways. Then another person runs a little ways. Another person runs a little ways. This goes on and on. When one person is tired, they hand the ball to another person. This is called a run.

The person who runs first does not stop until the ball is given to them. If they do not run, the ball goes to the next person. This is why the first person is the one who is very strong. If a person is not strong, they might not make it to the end.

A team of people runs this way. Many people work together. One person might run for a short time. The next person runs for a little longer. This goes on for many hours. Sometimes many people run at the same time. This is how teams can be very strong.

A person who is very good at this can be called a runner. They might run many times in a day. They might run a long way. They might run many times. This is a great way to stay strong.
