The word empty is like a room. A room is a space inside a house or a building. It is where people live. If you walk into a room that is empty, you see nothing. There is no one there. It looks like a big empty space. You can feel the empty space. The air feels heavy. It is like the room is waiting for something to happen. 

Imagine you are at a big party. Everyone is there. There are many people, many drinks, many music. The room is full. But then, someone leaves. The room is empty again. The empty space feels strange. It is like the room is going through a big change. 

The word empty is also about the way things look. If you look at a room, you see walls, floor, and chairs. But if you look at a room that is empty, nothing is there. There is no wall, no floor, no chairs. The room is just a big empty space. This is what an empty word looks like.
