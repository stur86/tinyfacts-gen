To do something many times means you do it a very many times. You do not just do it once. You do it many times.

Think about a group of people. If one person says, you can not do it, then the group does not have to try. They say, we are going to do it many times. Then they all do it together.

When you do something many times, you start to get used to it. You do it so fast you do not even notice. You do it so easy that you can not stop.

You can also do many things at once. If you have many books to read, you can read one book at a time. You can also play many games at once.

This word is also used with numbers. If you have many numbers, you say you have many. This can mean you have a very big number. It is like saying you have many, many, many things.

You can also use it with time. If you do something many times, you go through the day many times. You go through the night many times.

In short, to do something many times is to do it many times. It means you do it a very many times.
