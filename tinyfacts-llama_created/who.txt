The word who is used to ask about people. It is a word we use to talk about who is in a group.

Imagine you are sitting with your friends. You talk about your day. Your friend asks, who are you? You say, I am you. You are the person you are.

When you talk to a computer, you can ask it, who is it? The computer answers, it is me. It is the one doing the work.

The word who can also be used to talk about time. You say, who is going to the party tonight? It is you. You are the person going.

In a story, a person might say, who am I? They are the one telling the story.

The word who is also important when you talk about things you have done. You might say, who did the work? You did it. You are the one with the job.

In some places, people use this word when they talk about a baby. A mother says, who is the baby? The baby is her. She is the one holding the child.

So, who is a person who asks about people, things, or time. It is a word that helps us talk about who is there.
