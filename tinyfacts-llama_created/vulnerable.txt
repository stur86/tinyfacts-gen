People who are in a situation where they can not be sure if something good or bad will happen are called those things. A person who is very happy is also in this situation. A child who loves another person is in this situation. An animal that is afraid of a person is also in this situation.

When you are this way, you are not as sure as you think you are. You have to be ready for anything. You are not alone. Many other people care about you.

To be this way is not bad. It is just a part of life. All living things go through this. Even a tree that is not a person can be in this situation. It is ready for rain or for the sun to be strong.

You can stay this way by learning to trust and by being kind. You can stay this way by being quiet or by being still. You can stay this way by being ready for what is to come.
