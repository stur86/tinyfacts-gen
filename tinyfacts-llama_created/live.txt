To live is to be in a place. It is to be with other people who are like you. It is to have air to breathe and water to drink. It is to have a warm body and a heart that goes.

You can live in many kinds of homes. You might live in a small house with your family. Or you might live in a big city with many people. You might live in a house in the woods. Or you might live in a house in the middle of a high place.

You can also live in your own mind. If you are happy, you live. If you are afraid, you might feel a lot of trouble. If you are sad, you might feel like you want to cry. But if you are happy, you live and you are strong.

Sometimes people live in a far place. They are far away from their families and friends. They have to do work and eat food that is not like the food they grew up with. But they also have a new home and a new group of people. They might meet new people and learn new things.

You can also live in a world of ideas. You might read books about far away lands. You might watch movies about people who are doing great things. You might even think about big questions about how the world works. This kind of living is very interesting.
