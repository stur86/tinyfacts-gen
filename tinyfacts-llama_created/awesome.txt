When you say something is very good or very cool, you are usually talking about something that makes you feel happy or excited. This is the kind of word you use when you see a great movie, hear a very nice song, or see a very strong person doing something.

The word can also mean something that is really big or strong. A car that goes very fast is an amazing thing. A dog that can run as fast as a car is a very good animal. A bright light in the night sky can be really amazing.

Sometimes, we use the word to tell a story. If someone is doing something very well, we might say they are a great person. Or if you feel very strong, we might say you are an amazing person. This word helps us show how good or strong we think something is.

You might also use the word to talk about things that are very fun. If a game is very good, you might say it is an amazing game. If a movie is very good, you might say it is an amazing movie.
