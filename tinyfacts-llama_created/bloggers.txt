To write for others

A person writes about their life or what they think about. They write for many people. Many people read their words. Some like to read about what others do. Others like to read about what they think about their life.

Some people write about how they feel. They write about their friends. They write about the world around them. They write about their own life.

Sometimes, people write about things they do not know. They write about the things that happen when they go to work. They write about what they eat. They write about how they feel after a long day.

Many people write about things that are not real. They write about stories that do not happen. They write about things that are only in their head. They write about things that make them laugh.

Some people write about animals. They write about how they feel when they see a cat. They write about what a dog looks like. They write about what a cat does.

A person who writes is called a writer. They write for the world. They write to help people know about each other. They write to make people feel something.

Some people write for money. Some people write because they love to write. They write because it makes them happy. They write because they want to share their ideas with the world.
