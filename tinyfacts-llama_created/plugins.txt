To use a thing you need is to make it work for you. When you say a computer is your friend, it means you use it to do things for you.

When you use a thing, you do not start from nothing. You start with something already there. If you want to play music, you do not start with a piece of paper. You start with a computer that has the music already. You use the computer to hear the music.

If you want to watch a movie, you do not start from nothing. You start with the movie you want to see. You put the movie into the computer. The computer then plays the movie for you.

Some things you need to use can only be used with other things that are the same. A computer you buy might not work with a book you buy. But if you have both, you can use the computer to read the book.

Sometimes, you need to add more power to a thing to make it work better. If you have a computer that can only do one kind of work, you need to find a thing that can do another kind of work. This thing is a power thing. You put the power thing into the computer. The computer then uses both kinds of work.

You can also have things that you can not buy. You can not buy a thing that makes you feel happy. You can not buy a thing that makes you feel safe. These things are inside you. They are always there, even when you are not using them.

Plays are another kind of thing. You can not buy a play. You get a play by watching it with other people who already have the play. You watch the play and you feel like you are part of it. You are not alone.
