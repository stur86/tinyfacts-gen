Before is a word that tells us about a time that is still in the past. It is used when you want to talk about what happened before a time.

Think of it like a line on a line. If you are standing in front of a table, you are on the first line. If you are standing on the line after the table, you are on the second line. If there are many lines and you are standing in the middle, you are on the third line. Because you are on the third line, you are before the table.

You can use this word to talk about many things. You can say, before breakfast, before school, before the day starts, or before a time you want to know about. It helps us show that something happened before another thing.

Sometimes, you can use the word before when talking about a past time. You might say, I saw a person before they became a star. This means you saw this person a long time before they became a well known person in the world.

You can also use this word to talk about a time that is still coming. If you say, I have before me a big day at work, you are talking about the day after today. You are talking about the day after you are done with your work.

So, the word before is a way to show that something happened before another thing. It helps you understand how things are in time.
