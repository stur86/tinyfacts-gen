To confuses people is to cause them to think in a wrong way. It means you say or do something that makes others think you are wrong. This can happen when you do not follow what a friend, family person, or teacher says. You might act as if you think something is true when it is not.

It can also happen when you do not tell the whole story. If you say one thing but another happens, people might think you are lying. This makes them not trust you. They do not want to listen to what you have to say anymore.

Sometimes, you might get confused because you hear two different ideas from two different people. One person tells you a story, and the other person tells you something different. This can cause your brain to make two different pictures. You might feel like you do not know what is true anymore.

To cut a problem means to stop it from getting worse. If you are angry, you can cut the problem by talking to the person who made you angry. You are not still angry, and the problem is no longer a big one.
