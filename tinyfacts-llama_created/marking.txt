To mark something means to leave a sign or a mark on it. You can do this with your finger or with a small thing that goes on the paper.

Think about your name. Every person has a name. If you do not write your name in a book, it will be hard for others to find you. When you write your name, you are marking it. You are putting a sign on the paper that says, here is me.

You can also mark things in a place where people go to buy things. If you see a sign that says, buy now, you know you have to act quickly. You mark the sign so you remember it for later.

Sometimes, we mark things that are broken. If you drop a glass, it breaks into many pieces. You might put a small piece on the floor to show where it fell. This helps us know about it so we can be more carefully.

In a room, you can mark a place where you sit. You might use a small piece of paper and write your name and the number of the chair. This helps you find your seat when you get up from the table.

You can also mark your face. If you wear a funny hair color, you might leave a small sign on your cheek. This is a way to show that you are happy or that you like something.

To mark something can help us remember. It is a way to leave a sign so we can find it later.
