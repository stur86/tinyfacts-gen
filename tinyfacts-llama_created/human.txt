To be human means you are a person. You are a living being. You have a body, a head, and a heart. You breathe and you eat. You feel things. You love things. You want to be happy.

All living things are humans. We all have the same parts. We all need air to breathe. We all need food to live. We all have a heart that works. We all need love and friends to feel complete.

Humans are the only ones that can hurt each other. We use words to talk. We use numbers to write and talk. We use our hands to make things. We use our feet to walk. We use our faces to smile and show we care.

Humans are also the only ones that can make a lot of noise. We laugh, we cry, and we shout. We use things to make things move. We use things to make food. We use things to make clothes. We use things to make houses.

Because of all these things, we are very different from other living things. We are able to think and understand. We are able to remember and learn. We are able to feel and love.

Even though we all share the same parts, we are different. Each person is like a small piece of a big picture. Each person has a different story to tell. Each person has a different way of feeling. Each person has a different way of seeing the world.

Because we are different, we must learn from each other. We must listen to each other. We must be kind to each other. We must share our ideas and our ways. This is what makes the world a better place.
