A word is a thing you use to talk. It is a group of letters or a set of words. We use these words to tell stories, give orders, or just say how we feel.

A line is a short group of words. This group of words is like a small story. You can read one line and know what is happening. You can read many lines one after another and hear a story.

A group of words is a story. This group of words is many lines long. You can read this group of words many times and hear the story many times.

A story starts with a group of words. This group of words is the beginning. Later, you might add more words. The words get longer and the story gets more clear.

We also use words to talk about other things. If you say you love a person, you are using words to tell how you feel. This is a group of words you have made up. You are not just talking about how you feel. You are also telling the story about the person you love.

Sometimes, when we are writing, we use words in groups. These groups of words are called a line, a word, a word, a word, a word, a word, a word. This is like a short story with many lines. Each line is a word.

When people read a story or a book, they listen to the words. They follow what is happening. They see pictures in their head. They feel things when they think about what is happening.

A word can have many different uses. You can use words to tell a story. You can use words to order people. You can use words to tell a joke. You can use words to talk to a friend. You can use words to talk to a teacher.
