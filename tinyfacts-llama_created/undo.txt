To go back can be very good. You might want to change what happened while you were doing something. You could have eaten a bad thing when you should have eaten a good one. You could have said something you did not mean. You could have left a room when you should have stayed in it.

This is what you do when you want to go back. You start over. You look at the beginning of the time. You remember what you did before you started. Then you do the same thing again. But this time you do it right.

Sometimes you go back to make things right. If you did something wrong, you might go back to do it again. You might ask to go back and do something over and over until you do it the right way.

We also use this word when things are wrong. Imagine you bought a thing you did not want. You wanted a new one. But the new one is old and broken. You feel sad. You want to go back and take the old one. You go back to the store where you bought it. The shop owner looks at you. He asks why. You say, I want to take the old one. He says, Okay, but you have to pay for it. You go home and pay for the old one.

It is also about how things change. If you build a thing, then you put it down. You think it is done. But then something happens. The wind moves the building. The building falls down. You had to go back and fix it. You go back and put the building back together. You change what happened.

In life, we have to go back a few times. We might go back to a time when we were very young. We might go back to a time when we were afraid. We might go back to a time when we did something we were afraid to do. But going back is not always bad. It can help us learn. It can help us understand what happened. It can help us be ready for what comes next.
