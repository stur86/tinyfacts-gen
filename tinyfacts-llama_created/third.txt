The word third is used to talk about people and things in a group.

When you see three people, you know they are not the first one. They are not the second one either. They are the third one.

Think of a line of people waiting to go to a movie. If there are two people in front of you, the line is first. Then comes the third person. They are not first, second, or third at the same time. They are just in the third place.

In a story, a person who did something first is the first one. Then comes the second one, and after that the third one. This is how we understand who is in what order.

You can use this word to talk about many things. You might say,  The third book in the story is the one about the boy who loves dogs. Or you might say, I am the third person in the movie. This means you are not the first or the second person, but you are the third person.

So, the word third is important for people who like to order things or follow lines. It helps us know who is where and what happens next.
