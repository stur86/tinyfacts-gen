To eat dinner is to have a big food thing for the day. It usually happens at lunch time when people are done with school or work.

Most of the time, you eat with your family or friends. You might sit at a table with a nice table clothes. There is usually a big round thing made of paper or hard stuff. You put food on the paper first. Then you eat it. This is usually the best part of the day.

You might eat foods that are easy to eat. They are often cold or you put them in your mouth and then you drink something. You might eat food that you buy in a store. You can also eat food from a box that has many foods inside. These things are often easy to carry around.

After dinner, people often go to a place that is called a bar. They go there to drink drinks and talk with other people. This is a nice way to end the day. You can also go to a place that has a big room with a lot of people. You might eat food there too.

Sometimes, you might go to a place with a big room and a long line. You stand and wait for a few minutes. Then, a person comes and gets food for you. You sit down and eat your food while you wait. This is a good way to wait for food.

In some places, you do not eat dinner. You might eat a small food before you go to bed. This is called a light dinner. You do not eat a big food thing like you do at night when you are very tired.
