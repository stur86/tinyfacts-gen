To have a name

When people want to do something, they might ask other people to help them. If you want to be the first one to run in a race, you can be a runner. You are the one who gets the job. This is called a name.

The person who gets the name is called a name giver. They help the person who wants to do something get the job. They write down the name and put it on a paper so everyone knows it.

Sometimes, people give names to things. If you have a dog, you might name him. The dog is now called his name. This helps people remember him.

Also, when you are in a group, people might give each other names. This is a way to show you are part of the group. It is like wearing a shirt with your name on it. This name gives you a place in the group.

A person who gives names can be a teacher, a friend, or even the police. They help us know who is who and what is happening.
