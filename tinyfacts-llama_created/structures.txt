To build something is to make it from the ground up. You start with a tiny bit of wood or a small piece of paper. You use strong lines and small pieces to join it together. As you add more pieces, the thing gets bigger and stronger. It becomes a house, a car, a table, or a chair. Every building is made by many people over many years.

A building can have many sides. Some are very tall and have many windows. Others are round and have a big round top. Some are long and go across a wide place. All of them are made to hold people or animals. They give people a place to stay and work. They make it safe and bright inside and out.

Some things are made of many many long pieces. These are called long things. They are held together by strong lines. People use these long things to make many different things. The long things are very important because they help everything stay together.

A building can also be made by people who are very good at making. These people are called a team. They work together for many years. They plan what the building will look like, how it will be made, and who will do the work. They talk and share ideas. They plan so the building is safe, strong, and looks nice.

When you build a thing, you use your hands and your mind. You decide what the thing will be and how it will look. You use your hands to move pieces and your mind to decide where each piece goes. Building is a way to use your brain and hands together. It is a way to make new things.
