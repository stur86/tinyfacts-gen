To be barely covered means you are almost completely not covered at all. You are not hidden by any clothes. You are not covered by a bed. You are not covered by a wall. You are completely open.

Imagine you are a tree. You grow from the ground. You do not need to cover yourself with leaves. The wind can see you, but you do not feel fear. You are not afraid because you are not hidden. You are free.

You can also be this way in a room. If you walk into a room and you are the only person there, you are barely covered. There is nothing to cover you. You are not covered by any clothes. You are the only one there.

Sometimes, to be barely covered means to be in a place where no one is watching. You might be in a room full of people, but you are alone. You are not afraid because no one sees you. You are free to be yourself.

In a way, being barely covered is like being a little bit broken. You are not whole, but you are not broken either. You are just in between. You are not completely covered, but you are not completely broken. You are still yourself.
