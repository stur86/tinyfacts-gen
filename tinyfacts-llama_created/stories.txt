To tell a story means to use your words to share what happened. You might use your voice and your face to show how a person felt. You might also use pictures or things you saw to help tell the story.

Sometimes, we use stories to make other people feel less alone. When you hear a story, you might think, How did this happen? Or, How does this make me feel? Stories can help us understand the world and the people in it.

You can tell a story with many people. This is called a group story. You might tell a story that happened many years ago. You might also tell a story about what you are doing now. People listen to these stories to learn from them.

You can also tell a story about a place or a thing. This is called a place story. You might tell a story about the sun or the night sky. You can also tell a story about a house or a car. These stories help us remember things that happened in our lives.

Stories are important because they help us understand the world. They also help us feel how we feel when things happen to us. They help us remember happy times and sad times. They help us learn about the world and about how to act.

You can tell a story with words or with pictures. You can also tell a story that happens in your mind. This is called a story you tell yourself. These stories are important because they help us learn and remember.
