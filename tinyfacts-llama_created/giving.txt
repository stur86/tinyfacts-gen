To give something means to share it with another person or animal. When you give a book to a friend, you are giving it to them so they can read it and learn from it. You are not keeping it for yourself anymore.

Sometimes, giving can mean to give money to someone who needs it. If you have a lot of money and you decide to give some to a person who is in trouble, you are giving. This can help them buy food or a new home.

You can also give something that is inside you. If you are feeling sad, you might give your heart to a friend. This means you are letting them know you care about them. You might also give your time when you help someone who is in need.

To give is to show that you care. It means you are not alone. You are part of a group and you are working together to make things better.
