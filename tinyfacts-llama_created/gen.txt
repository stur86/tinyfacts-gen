To begin with, a gently means in a very short way. It is about making something start. Think of a tree. A tree starts with one small green thing. This green thing grows and becomes a tall tree. It starts with a tiny thing that grows and becomes a tall thing. This is how a tree starts.

A group of tiny things can start to make a thing. This is how a lot of tiny things can start to make one big thing. When a group of tiny things work together, they can make a very big thing happen. This is why we have many tiny things in our bodies. Our tiny things work together so our body can work well.

A word can also mean how something starts. If something starts with a word, it means it began with that word. If something starts with a number, it means it began with that number. Think of the first word of a story. This word tells us where the story starts.

Sometimes, a word can mean a lot more than that. It can mean the very start of something. Think of a book. The first book is the very start of the whole story. It is the start of how the story goes.
