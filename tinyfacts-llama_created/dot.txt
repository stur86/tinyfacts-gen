A small point of light in the sky that you can see with your eyes. It is a very small thing that is much bigger than your hand.

You can see many of these in the night sky. You can also see them in the air when you are flying or when a bright thing moves across the sky.

When you write, you use small lines to make words and pictures. These lines are very small and you have to be very fast to write many of them. If you try to look at a line too quickly, your eyes will get tired. That is why you can only see a few lines before you need to look away.

If you look at a small light from the sky for a long time, your eyes will feel tired. It is like looking at a very bright window. You need to stop looking for a short time to let your eyes rest.
