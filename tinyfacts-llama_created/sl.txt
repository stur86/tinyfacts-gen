To slow down means you are moving very slowly. If you are running to school, you should not just stand there. You need to keep moving your legs or your feet. If you keep standing, you will get tired quickly.

Sometimes, when you are very happy, you might start to feel like you are going very fast. But then you will stop and breathe. You are not really moving anymore. You are just standing still. This is not good for your body.

To slow down is to slow your body down so you do not get tired. You can do this by walking, running, or even just moving your feet. You can also try to be kind to people around you. If a friend is being very loud, you can say, slow down. This can help everyone feel more calm.
