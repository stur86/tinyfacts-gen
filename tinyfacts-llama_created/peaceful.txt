To be in a quiet place

Being in a place that is quiet is a kind of feeling. It is when there are no loud sounds. You can hear a few very small sounds, like a small animal moving around. The air is still. No one is talking. The trees are not moving. There is no wind. You feel safe.

This kind of place makes you feel calm. It is like a warm hug from the world. The world is not moving too fast. Your heart feels easy to breathe. You can think about what you want to do. You can feel happy or sad without getting very angry. You can just be.

You can be in a place like this. You can sit in a park. You can sit on a rock. You can sit in a room with a nice person. You can even be in a room with books. You can read a book and not move. You can feel calm and your mind is clear.

Sometimes, people want to be in a place like this. They might want to be in a place that is quiet and still. They might want to be away from big crowds and noise. They might want to be alone with their mind. They might want to feel safe and calm.

Being in a quiet place is important. It can help us feel better when we are sad or angry. It can help us think better. It can help us remember what we want to do. It can help us feel happy and calm.
