To trip something means to move around and go from one place to another. You might trip while you are running or while you are walking. You can also trip in a car. If you are in a car, you might trip over a small thing. This happens when your feet touch the floor before the car stops moving.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You start to play. Then, you run to get another ball. You jump on the floor and hit the floor many times. After a while, your feet get tired. You get up and try to walk. You might trip. You fall down. This happens too when you walk.

When you go on a big trip, you usually use a computer or a phone to look at where you are and where you want to go. You can see many places on a big picture. You can also see what people say about a place. Some people write their own stories about a place. You can read these stories and learn about the place.

Many places have a big computer. This computer looks at many people who have been to the place. It finds out what people like and do not like. It shows this to people who want to visit the same place. This helps people make their own plans.

There is a place on the computer that lets people give money to a place they like. This money is for the place to do things to make it better. If you give money to a place, you are helping the place to be a good place for others.

Sometimes, people use this computer to find out if a place is safe. They look at many stories about the place. If a place has many bad stories, the computer might tell the person to stay away.
