When you say something is for a short time, you are talking about a time that is not very long. It might be one day, one week, or one month. This time is not very big, and it ends before you know it.

Sometimes we use this word when we are talking about things we need to do. You might say, you are going to the store. The store is a place where you buy things. You are going there for a short time. You will be gone for a few hours. When you come back, you might say you are tired.

When you are going to do something, you might not stay for a long time. You might only stay for a few minutes. You might leave very quickly. This is okay if you are in a place where you need to go. You do not have to stay there all day.

You can also use this word for people. When someone is going to a place, they are going for a short time. They might be going to a school, a work place, or a place to see a movie. They might only be there for a short time. When they go away, they are gone.

Sometimes, we use this word when we talk about life. When you are very young, you are living in your home. You are not going to do anything big yet. You are just growing. Your life is very short, but it is also very good because you are learning and becoming a person.
