To have an idea is to know what something is. To have a job is to work for a man who gives you money so you can live. To have power is to be the one who tells the other people what to do. When people get to pick who is in power, they are holding an idea.

Imagine you are the one who gets to pick the game for your school. You want the game to be one that you like. You tell the other kids what you want, and they do it. You have the idea. You are the one who gets to decide.

Sometimes, this happens with big groups of people. A group of people might get to decide what the whole city should do. They pick what the city should build and what kind of streets should be made. They are the ones with the idea.

If the group of people is not in power, they might try to get the power for themselves. They might tell the other people who are already in power that they are not good enough. They might say they should be the ones who decide what happens.

It is an important thing because it lets people have a voice. It lets them tell the world what they think and what they want. It lets them be the ones who decide what happens in their world.

You can also have an idea about how a city should look. You might think it should have green streets. You might think it should have many parks. When you tell people about your idea, you are sharing your idea with the world.

In a big city, people often have many ideas. They might all be different. They might have different wants. They might all have different ideas about how to make things better. But when they all share their ideas and listen to each other, they can build something that is good for everyone.

Having an idea is a good thing. It lets people be a part of making things happen. It lets them tell their story to the world.
