To hide means to keep something or someone safe from people who want to find them. It also means to change how you look so you can not be seen.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You love to run and jump. You run very fast and jump high. One day, you see a big dog. You are afraid. You want to hide so the dog does not see you.

You go to a room with a door and a window. You close the door and pull the window part to keep the wind out. You are hiding. You are safe now. You do not have to worry about the dog or anyone else.

You can also hide things. You might put a book under the bed. You hide the book so no one finds it. You hide it so you do not have to go looking for it later.

Sometimes, you hide inside your house. You might hide in a dark room with a light on. You hide so you are safe while it is dark. You hide to feel safe.

To hide is to be safe from people or things. It is to be quiet and still. It is to not be seen. It is a way to feel safe, even if you are not near anyone.
