To do something again is to change it. You might try to fix a broken thing. You look at it and see where it is wrong. You make the parts that are broken new. Now the thing works again.

You can do this with words too. If you write something, you might change it. You might change the words or the way you use them. This is called a change. It is the same as doing something again.

Sometimes, you do something for a long time. Then you stop and think about what you did. You might look at your work again. You might think, Why did I do that? You might change what you did. This is another way to do something again.

There is also a word for changing how you feel. If you are afraid, you might do something to make the feeling go away. You might move your body or talk to a friend. You are trying to make the feeling go away. You are doing something again to fix your body.

To change something is to change the way it is. It is about taking something and making it new.
