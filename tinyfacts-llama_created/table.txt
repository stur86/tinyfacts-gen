A table is a thing that sits on the floor. It is long and it has four sides. One side is wide and the other side is short. The table is used for many things.

You can sit on the table to eat. You can eat food from a box or from a bag. You can also put your hands on the table while you talk to a friend. People also use the table to work. They write papers or do work on computers.

A table can be made of wood, or it can be made of other stuff. Some tables have a top made of glass. You can see what is on the top from the other side of the table. Some tables have a soft part in the middle. This part helps you feel safe while you are sitting.

Sometimes, you might have a table in a room with a big table. This table is for many people to talk or to eat together. The table is very important because it brings people together.
