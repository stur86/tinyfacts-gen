To be the person who holds something is to be the one who keeps it. This can be a person who works at a big place. They work there every day and make sure everything is ready. They are the one who keeps the things that people use and the rooms where they meet.

You can also be the person who holds a game. A person might say,  This is the game. They are the one who tells people what to do. They help the people decide who is the best or who is the person who did the best thing.

Sometimes, to be the one who holds a story is also the same as being the one who holds a thing. If a person tells a story, they are the one who holds the story inside their brain. They keep the story going and make it happen.

When you say a place is held by someone, you mean the person is in control of it. They are the one who decides who is allowed to enter. They are the one who makes sure everything is safe.

This word can also mean to give something to someone. If you have something you love and you want to share it with someone, you are being the one who holds it. You are keeping it for them.
