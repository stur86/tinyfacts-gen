An edging is a person who helps a writer make their words better. They look at what the writer wrote and pick out the parts that are wrong or need more work. They might change the words so they are not as wrong, or they might make the words look better by putting them in the right order.

Sometimes the edging is the one who tells the story. They might decide if the story is clear or if it needs more. They might also decide if the ending is good or if it should be longer.

An edging is very important for people who write books, stories, or talk on the phone. They help to make sure everyone is happy with what they have to say.
