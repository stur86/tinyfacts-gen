To reacher is to become better. It means you have been hurt or wronged. You try to change so you are safe again.

Imagine you cut your hand while playing with a ball. You feel pain and the cut is red and very hot. You want to stop the blood from coming out. You put a band on your hand so the blood will stop.

After a while, the red gets smaller and the hand feels better. Your hand is no longer hurt. You are reacher. You are becoming better.

People do this with their bodies too. If a person is very mean and does bad things, they can get into trouble. They might go to a place where they can learn to be kind. The place helps them understand that they should not act like this. They learn to be nice and to think about how they act. They become reacher.

You can also reacher a person who is very sad. If a child is afraid to go to bed because they are scared of dark things, a teacher or a parents can help them. They give the child a night light so they feel safe. They talk to the child and help them feel better. The child becomes reacher.

In some places, people who do things that hurt others are sent to a place with a kind person who helps them. They learn about how to be nice and follow the way of the person. They become reacher.

To reacher is to change your way of being. It is about becoming a better person.
