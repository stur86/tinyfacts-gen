To place something means to put it in a place. Think about a book on a table. You put the book down so it can stay there.

You can place something in a room. You might put a table in the middle of the room. Or you might put a chair in a corner. This makes it easier to look around.

We also use this word with people. If you are placed at a job, you work there. This means you are the person who is in that job.

Sometimes, you place something on the ground. You might put a cup down. This is a way to keep things safe until they are used.

If you have a friend, you might place them in a group. This is when you put your friend with others who are the same as them. It is a way to help them feel at home.
