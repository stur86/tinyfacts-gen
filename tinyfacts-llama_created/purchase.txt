To buy something means you get it from a store or from a person. You give them money and they give you the thing you want.

Imagine you want a new book. You walk into a store and look at the books. You see a book that looks nice. You pick it and put it in your bag. When you leave the store, the person is the one who gave you the book. You did not buy it. You just took it from them.

You can also buy things from the phone. If you want a new game, you use your money to buy it. The store is like a person. They are the ones who give you the money and the game.
