To have an idea or to know something means you have done it before. You are not the first person who did it, but you have learned from them.

Think of a place you have never been. You do not know the look of the sky, the sound of the water, or the feel of the ground under your feet. But after you go there one more time, you start to know it. You remember how the sun feels on your skin, how the trees look when they are full of green, and how the night sounds.

You also have an idea of how things work. You know that a car has a big round thing that turns round and round. You know that a door opens and closes. You know that a phone has a small box that you talk to through the air.

When you do something, you get a feeling. You might feel happy if you eat a sweet thing. You might feel scared if you hear a loud noise. You might feel calm if you are in a quiet room. These are your ideas about how things work.

Sometimes you do something that you are not sure about. You might feel nervous. You might feel a little afraid. You might wonder if you will do it again. But as you do it many more times, you start to feel more sure. You start to know that you can do it.

An idea is not just one thing. It is many things that you have learned from your life. It is a story you tell yourself about how you feel, what you did, and what happened. It is a way you talk to your mind so you can understand yourself and the world.

So an idea is a story you tell yourself about your life. It is how you feel about what has happened, what you think, and what you want.
