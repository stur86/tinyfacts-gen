A thing you wear on your head is called a head cover. This cover keeps your hair from falling off. It is usually made of a soft stuff or a hard stuff that does not break easily. People wear these things for many years. Some people wear them for fun, for work, or for a very important job.

A person who is a leader wears a small thing with a round top and a long stick. This thing is called a head of power. The person with the head of power is very important. They make many people follow them. They say what to do, who to trust, and who to not trust.

When people say a person has a head of power, it means they have a head cover that is very important. It is like a sign of their power.
