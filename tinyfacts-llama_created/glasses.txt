Glasses are things you wear to see better. They sit on your face. They have small pieces over your eyes. These pieces help you see colors, numbers, and words.

You can get glasses if you have trouble seeing. Sometimes people see fine things that are far away. They might see letters that are hard to read. Other people have trouble seeing people or things that are moving fast. Glasses can help. They can make your eyes work better.

There are many kinds of glasses. Some are for kids. They help children see before they go to school. Some glasses are for grown people. They help people see if they are tired or if they have a problem with their eyes.

You can wear glasses every day. You can also take them off when you go to sleep. Some people like to wear glasses at night. This helps them see better when they wake up.
