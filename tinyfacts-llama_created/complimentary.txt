To be complete means you have everything you need to stay safe and go about your day. If a bed is not complete, you need to add a cover and a side to stay warm and safe.

Think about a group of friends. If they all have the same clothes and the same food, they can still have fun together. But if one person wears a different shirt, it makes the group feel strange. That person is not complete.

Sometimes things are complete when we look at them. If a picture is complete, we know it is the right picture. If a book is complete, we know it tells the right story. We know the story is finished.

Also, if a person is complete, they are happy. They know they do not need anyone else to be whole. They can be alone and still feel safe.

In some ways, being complete is like being a good friend. If you are a good friend to someone, you help them feel safe and happy. You give them the things they need to stay well.
