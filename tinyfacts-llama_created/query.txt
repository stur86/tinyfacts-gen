A person who asks questions is called a question. They go to a place with many computers and ask the computers to give them answers. The computers help them find out the answers. They might ask about a person, a place, a thing, or a fact.

When you ask a question, the computer looks at what you said. If the computer understands, it gives you the answer. If the computer does not understand, it keeps asking until it finds the right answer.

Some people use this word to talk about the computer. They might say,  Can you do this? If the computer says yes, the person is happy. If the computer says no, the person might ask again.

You can also use this word to talk about a search. When you search for something, you ask a computer to find it. The computer looks through many lines of words to find what you want. If the computer finds what you are looking for, it shows it to you.

In this world, we use the word to talk about how we use computers and find what we need.
