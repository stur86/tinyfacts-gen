When you are in a room, and you hear a door, you know it is time to go out. You might feel a little nervous, but you also feel you have to go. You do not have to go, you can just stay, but you feel you must leave. This is what it means to have to do something.

If you are waiting for a friend, and you are not very happy, you might feel a little angry. You do not want to wait anymore, so you say, I have to go. You are not afraid, you are just ready to go.

Sometimes you have to do something that is hard. You might feel a little scared, but you do it anyway because you must. This is a good thing. It shows you are strong.

You can also say you have to do a job. You are the one who has to do it. You might feel a little sad because you do not want to do it, but you know you have to. When you do it, you feel like you have done something important.
