To check something means you make sure it is true or right. You look at it one by one to see if it is as you think it is. You might read a word, look at a picture, or listen to a story to make sure it is not wrong.

Imagine you are buying a book. You want to make sure the cover is not torn and the words on the inside are still the same. You open the book and look at the first word. If it is not the same, you do not buy it. You look at the next word and so on until you find the one you were looking for.

You can also check things that are not written. You look at a person to see if they are telling the truth. You look at a picture of a person to see if they look like the person you know. You might check a phone to see if the person on the phone is really the person you are talking to.

Sometimes, checking can be hard. You might think something is true, but then you learn something else. You feel confused or sad. This is when you need to go back and look again to see if you were wrong.

In your life, you often have to check things. You might check if you are ready to go to school. You check your bag to make sure you have everything you need. You check your phone to see if you have any important calls. You check the time to make sure you have enough time.
