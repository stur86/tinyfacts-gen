Smoke is a dark thing that comes from burning things. When you light a small thing, it makes a small white light. When you put that light in a room with air, it gets cold and turns dark. This dark thing we call smoke is like the white light from the small thing, but it is much smaller and darker.

Smoke can come from many things. You can see it from a fire in your home. You can also see it when you breathe out. When you walk through a wet place, the water from your body goes out and into the air as tiny drops. These tiny drops are like the tiny white light, but they are much smaller and are made of water and tiny pieces of your body.

Smoke can also come from animals. When an animal dies and breaks down, tiny bits of its body go up into the air as a dark thing. This is why you might see a dark thing in the air if an animal is dying.

Sometimes, smoke comes from things that are not burning. If you use a thing that makes a bad smell, the bad smell comes out as tiny drops into the air. This is why you might see tiny drops of the smell in the air.

Smoke is important for our lives. It helps us breathe. It carries tiny pieces of the air that are good for us. But if too much smoke comes into our homes or our cars, it can make our eyes hurt and our throat feel funny.
