An animal can be an animal and also a thing that you drive. If you drive a car, you are using an animal. You pay money to use it. The car moves on its own. It does not need to eat like a dog does.

You can also drive a car that is made by a man. This car is not an animal, but it is still a thing that moves. It has things inside that help it go.

People use words to talk about these things. They say, if you are driving a car, you are an animal. If you are driving a man made car, you are not an animal, but you are still a person.
