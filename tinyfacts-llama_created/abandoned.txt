To be an animal is to have life and to be able to move. You are not alone. There is another living thing in the world with you. You have to stay with them. When you are not alone, you feel safe.

Sometimes, one of the two living things dies. The dead one does not breathe anymore. The other living thing still breathes. It looks at the dead one and feels sad. It wants to stay near the dead one, but it does not know why. It just wants to be near the one it loved.

Sometimes, the dead one leaves the place where the living one lives. The living one follows them. This happens when one place is better than another. The living one might follow the dead one to find food or to stay warm. This is not good because the living one is not safe without the dead one.

Sometimes, one living thing is very old. The other living thing has not been able to move in a long time. The old one is so tired that they do not want to move. The old one is like a tree in a field. They are still there, but they are not moving. The young one might go to find food, or they might stay with the old one to be safe. This is good because the old one is still there and can give them food.

Sometimes, one living thing is very small. The dead one is too big for the small one to carry. The small one goes to find food, and the dead one stays behind. The small one has to carry a heavy thing that is not very good for them. This is hard for the small one.

Sometimes, one living thing dies alone. No one is there to see them. They are alone in the dark. This is very sad. The dead one does not have anyone to talk to, to laugh with, or to feel safe with. They are left to face the night and the cold alone.
