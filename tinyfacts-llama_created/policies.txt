When a group of people decide to do something, they often use a plan. This plan is like a set of words that tell everyone what to do. It helps everyone know what is expected of them.

Think of it like a school. The school has a plan for how to run the day. If the plan says that everyone must be quiet after lunch, the kids can be quiet. If the plan says that everyone must eat their food before playing, kids can eat before they start playing. Everyone follows the plan.

Sometimes, a plan can change. If the plan says that everyone must be quiet after lunch and then the plan says everyone can play after lunch, the plan changes. Now, kids can both be quiet and play.

In a big group, like a business or a city, there are usually many plans. Each plan might be for different things. One plan might say how to work the office. Another plan might say how to deal with people who are being mean. Both plans help the group know what to do.

There are also plans for the things that happen over time. A plan for a long time might say what will happen next month, next year, or next week. These plans are important because they help everyone know what to expect.

People also make plans for their own lives. If you want to move to a new city, you might make a plan. This plan tells you what to do. It helps you decide what to buy, where to live, and when to start your new job.

In short, a plan is a set of words or ideas that tell everyone what to do. It helps people work together or make big changes.
