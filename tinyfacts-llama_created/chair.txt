A chair is a thing you sit on. It is a box with four legs and a seat. People use these to rest when they are tired. You can find chairs in many rooms in houses. They come in all kinds. Some chairs are very tall and have a back. Some chairs are small and look like a cup. Some chairs are round and have a soft seat inside.

You can sit in a chair to watch a movie. You can sit in one to talk to a friend. You can sit in a chair to read a book. Some chairs are very heavy. They can make the floor feel heavy too. Other chairs are light and easy to move around with. You can sit in one and then get up and walk away.

Sometimes a chair is a thing from the floor. You might sit on a chair while you walk. A man might sit in a chair while he is writing a story. A girl might sit in a chair while she is thinking. This is because a chair can help you feel safe and calm.
