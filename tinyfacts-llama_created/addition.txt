To add a number is to find how many things you have together. It is like putting one thing after another.

Imagine you have five pieces of food in your box. You give one piece to your friend. Now you have six. You have more food than before. This is what we mean by adding. You are adding five and one.

You can add many numbers. If you have ten cats and there are two more cats in the house, you have twenty cats now. You added ten and two.

This word is very important. When you add numbers, you are making a new number. It is the right way to show how many things you have when you put them all together.
