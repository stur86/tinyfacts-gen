To change something means you make it bigger or different. You can do this in many ways. 

One way is to put more things around a small thing. This makes the small thing look bigger. You can put a few small pieces of paper around a small book, and it looks like a big book now. You are making the small book look bigger. 

Another way is to take something small and make it bigger. You can do this with paper. You write a long story on a small piece of paper. The paper becomes much bigger because the words are on it. Now you have a very long story that you can share with others. 

Sometimes you change things by moving them. If you keep a small ball in your hand, you can keep it for a long time. But if you throw the ball, it goes far away. This is what happens when you change something. You change the way it looks or the way it feels.

You can also change things that are not in your body. You can get a new job. You can move to a new city. These changes happen inside you. You feel different and you want to be in a new place with new people.
