To have a plan means you are ready for the day. When you do this, you do not feel afraid. You know what to do and you can do it. You have things you need for the day. You have money and food. You are ready for the work you are doing.

Sometimes, people need to stay in a place for a long time. This is called a stay. A stay is like a home away from home. You do not go back home. You stay in a new place. You have to learn the way this place is. You have to follow the ways of the people who live there.

A stay can be for many weeks or many months. During this time, you do not leave the place. You do not go to a different town or city. You do not go to a different town where you live. You stay in the same place.

You also need to have food and water. You need to have clothes and a bed. You need to have a way to get money. When you have these things, you are ready for the stay. You are ready for the work you are doing.

A plan can also mean a thing you do before you start. If you have a plan for your day, you can do the things you need to do. You can go to the office, eat food, or go for a walk. When you have a plan, you know what to do.

In a group, a plan is when everyone agrees what to do. They decide on a way to do the work. They agree on who will do what job. Everyone knows what they are doing. Everyone works together. This helps everyone stay safe and do the work well.
