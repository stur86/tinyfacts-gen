To make something less bright

Imagine you are in a room with bright lights. The lights make everything look very good. But then you go outside and you see the sky. The sky is dark and the stars are very bright.

You can feel your eyes getting tired. The lights from the room are still there, but they are not as strong as they were when you were inside. This is what it means to be in the dark.

You can also use this word to talk about a person. If someone is in a bad mood, it means they are not happy. They might feel a little sad or angry. This is because the person is not able to see the good things in the world.

You can also use this word to talk about a thing that is not working well. If a light is not working, you might say it is in the dark. If a car is not moving, the person might say it is out of the dark.
