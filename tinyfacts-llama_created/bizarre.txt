When you say something is strange, you mean it is not like other things. It does not follow what people usually say or do. It is a big change.

A person who is very strange might be very tall or very short. They might have hair that does not grow in the usual way. Their eyes are very large. They might have a very different kind of face. Or, they might say things in a way that is not like other people.

Sometimes, something that is not strange can be very interesting. You might see a strange animal. This animal has a body that looks like it is made of many different parts. It can move in ways that do not follow the usual way people move.

The word is also used when you are talking about the world. If a place is very strange, it is not like the places you have seen before. It has a new kind of feeling. It is a place that makes you feel like you are in a different world.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are very different from how people usually live. If you live in a city, and then you go to a place with a different way of doing things, you might say your life is a little strange.
