To go round is to keep going in a round way. You can do this with your hands, a car, a dog, or a ball. The ball goes round the ground. The dog goes round the house. The car goes round a big round road. The hand goes round your hand.

You can also go round on a top. If you stand on a top and you push, the top will turn round. The top is round because it keeps turning round. It is round because it wants to keep turning round.

Sometimes we use this word with people. If a person is going round and round, it means they are doing something that is easy but not very interesting. They might be talking to themselves or thinking about nothing at all.

But we also use it to talk about things that are round. If a book is round, it is round. If a ball is round, it is round. If a chair is round, it is round. A round thing has many sides. This is why you can see it from far away and it looks the same on every side.
