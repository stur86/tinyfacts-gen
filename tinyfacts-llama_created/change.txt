To change means you do something new. You might change your clothes, your food, or how you act. You might also change how you look or how you feel.

Imagine you are a tree. You have been growing for many years. One day, a big wind comes and knocks you down. You are broken and need to be fixed. You have to be made strong again. This is a change. You are changing how you look and how you feel.

People change in many ways. You might change your job or how you work. You might meet new friends or people who help you. You might learn new things or change your way of thinking. These are all changes.

You can also change something that is already there. If you are angry, you might change your words and your face. You might try to be happy again. This is a change in how you act or feel.

Sometimes, we change because of what is happening around us. A place might change because of new people moving in. A town might change because new roads are built. This is a change that happens because of the world.

Being a child is a big change. You learn new things every day. You play and you learn. Your body changes as you grow. Your hair grows out, and your face becomes different. This is all a change.

You can also change your way of thinking. You might see things that you did not see before. You might feel a new way about life. This is a change in your mind.

Changing is important. It helps us grow and learn. It makes us different from what we are today. It makes our world a better place because of the changes we make.
