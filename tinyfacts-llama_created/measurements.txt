A word is an answer to a question. One can ask, why are you? Another person answers with a number. In this way, a word helps us know the world and how we live.

When people talk, they often use numbers to tell how big things are. If a cat is three feet long, you know it is three feet. You can use numbers to know how many people are standing next to you, how long a line is, or how many times a thing moves.

We also use numbers to tell how many times something happens. If you eat a whole bag of food, you have eaten one bag. If you play a game for three hours, you have played for three hours. Numbers help us keep track of these things.

Sometimes, we use numbers to tell how things are not. If a house is one story tall, it is not two stories tall. Numbers can also tell us how much money is in a bag.

To make sure, we use words like these. We say, if it is iced, it is cold. If it is hot, it is warm. If it is green, it is growing. These words help us stay safe and know what is happening.

You also need numbers to do work. When you make a cup of coffee, you need to know how much water and how much coffee to put in. You need to know how many times you press the big round thing. Numbers help you make things that you need.

People also use numbers to write books and make music. When you write a story, you need to know how many words are there. When you write a song, you need to know how many notes to play. Numbers help you make words that people want to hear.

In short, numbers help us know the world, keep track of how much, and tell us how things are not. They are a way to tell stories, make work easy, and help us stay safe.
