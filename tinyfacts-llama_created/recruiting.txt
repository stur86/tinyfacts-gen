To pick a group of people to do a job is to find the right ones. The person in the top makes a listens to what the other people want and what they need. Then, they go out and they talk to the people they want to join. They ask them if they want to do the job. If they say yes, they are added to the group.

The group is often made up of people with different jobs. One person might be a man who can build things. Another person might be a woman who can write. A third person might be a person who can fix things. When all these people are together, they can work very well.

If you want to join a group, you can do this too. You might find out about the group by talking to a friend or by looking at what the group does. You can then go to the group and say, I want to join. The group can decide if you are good for the job.

Sometimes, a group is made up of people who are doing the job for a long time. They are the ones who are already there. They help the new people learn and they show them how to do things. This can be good because the people who are already there can help the new ones.

You can also pick people for a job for a short time. You need to find the right ones for the time you have. The person in the top picks the people who are the best for the job. They pick them because they have the right ideas and the right way to do things. This is often done for a small time, like a week or a month.
