Jumps Can Be a Problem

A jump is a way to go very high. You might jump over a small wall. You jump because you want to get to the other side. You feel happy because you are going fast.

A computer can also jump. It can jump from one place to another. This is how it remembers many things. It keeps a listen of many numbers and many words so that it can answer your questions.

A jump can also happen when you are very strong. When you jump, you use your whole body. You lift your legs and your arms. You use your legs to lift your body up.

In the world of music, a jump is when a group of people start to play a song. They jump because they are excited. They jump and shout. They jump in the air, and people watch them.

Sometimes, people jump at a party. They jump up and down on the floor. They laugh and say, Oh, I am so happy! They are having a great time.

But a jump can also be a problem. If you jump too high, you might fall. If you jump too fast, you might hurt your head. You need to be carefully about how you jump.
