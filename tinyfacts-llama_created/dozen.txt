To do something a few times means you do it many times. A group of ten of these things can be called a group of ten or a group of ten. A group of ten is a big group of these things. Ten is a number that we use to show how many of these things we have.

You can not do a thing a group of ten times and then say you did it one time. If you do the same thing a few times, you are doing it many times. If you do it only once, you are doing it only one time.

One group of ten is the same as ten groups of one. You have ten of them, and you have one more. This is ten one times.
