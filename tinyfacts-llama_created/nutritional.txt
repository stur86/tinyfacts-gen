To be in a good state is to be living well. This is often done by eating well. You need many kinds of food to stay this way.

The word comes from a time when people did not know how to grow food. They had to walk to far away places to find what they needed. They were always tired and not strong. Later, other people found ways to grow food in their own places. They made sure they had a lot of the good food the body needs.

Some foods are good for you in many ways. They help you feel strong and give you the power to run, to jump, and to do work. They also help your body stay clean by getting ridden of bad stuff that comes from the air and from the ground.

Other foods are good for your brain. They help you think and learn new things. They also help you stay happy and calm when you are afraid or worried.

When you eat foods that are good for you, you give your body the power it needs to stay in good state. This makes you feel good, and it keeps you from getting very tired. A good state is an important part of being a person.
