When you say something is done, you often write it down. You tell the whole group that the person has done it.

To be done in this way is called a fact. If you say a fact is true, you have made it known. This is important for people who need to know what to do next.

Sometimes, you might also say something is done. This is different. You might be asking about it. You want to know why it happened. You are trying to understand.

If you are done with a problem, you can say you are finished. You have reached the end of it. This can be good because it means you have saved your time.

You can also say a person is done with a job. They have worked hard for many years and are ready to move on. This is an important moment for them because they are about to start a new life.

The word is used when you are about to do something or when you have done it. It tells us what has happened.
