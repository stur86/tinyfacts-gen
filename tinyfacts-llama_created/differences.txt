To have a different is to have a different way. Imagine you and a friend are playing a game. You both want to get a ball. You might reach for it at the same time. But then you see a different ball on the ground. You feel surprised because your friend did not see it.

This kind of surprise happens with things, people, and ideas. There are many different ways to do things. Some ways are fast. Some ways are slow. Some people like the quiet way and some like the loud way.

There are also different ways with words. One person might say they love a book. Another person might say they love a movie. If you know both, you can see they are different. But if you do not know, it is harder to understand.

In life, we also have different days. Some days are bright and happy. Other days are dark and sad. We might feel like we are in different places at different times. This is what makes life interesting.
