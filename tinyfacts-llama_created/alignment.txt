To be in line with someone means you are standing next to them. When two things are in line, they do not go around each other. They face each other straight.

Imagine you are walking in a straight line. You are always moving forward. If you turn and walk the other way, you are no longer in line. If two people are in line, they can talk to each other. They do not have to go around each other.

If you are in a line for a game, the first person is at the front. The next person is behind them. The person at the end is last in the line. The line helps people know who is first and who is last.

We also use the word to talk about how things are set. When you are in line with the world, you are not moving away from it. You are in the middle. If something is not in line, it is going the wrong way.
