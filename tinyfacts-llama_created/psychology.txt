To understand something is to know how it works. When you think, you use your brain. Your brain is like a very long and very strong thing inside your head. It helps you remember, learn, and feel things. Everything you do is because of what happens in your brain.

A person who studies this thing is a very important person. They look at how people act and what they say. They try to find out why people do things. They ask people and look at how they act when they are happy, sad, angry, or scared. This helps them understand how people work and what they need to feel well.

Some people want to know about the parts of the brain. Other people study how people act when they do things. Some people even study how the world works so that they can make new things or better ways to live.
