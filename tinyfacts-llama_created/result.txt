A lot of words are about how things work. When you do one thing, you usually get a new thing in return. This new thing is what we call a form of the word.

When you walk, you move forward. The ground pushes back on you. Your feet hit the ground. This is a form of the word. You are moving from one place to another.

If you drink a glass of water, you feel it going down your throat. The water comes out of your body. This is another form of the word.

When you look at a picture, the picture comes into your mind. The picture is the form of the word.

Sometimes, when you do something bad, you get a form of the word. This is called a form of the word with a bad feeling.

Sometimes, when you do something good, you get a form of the word. This is called a form of the word with a good feeling. This is important because it helps us understand how we feel.

A form of the word can be short or long. It can have many words or just one word. It can be about how you are feeling or how things are.
