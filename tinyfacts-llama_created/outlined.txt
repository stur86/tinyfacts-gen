To be in a state of being is when you are not really there. It is like you are already in a place. You are not waiting to go. You are already where you are.

Think about a room. You are in the room. You see the walls and the floor. You know where you are. You do not have to go to the door. You just sit down and stay.

When you are in this state, you are not thinking about anything else. You are only thinking about the thing you are in. If you were going to a party, you would not think about your work. You would just think about the party. You would not even remember where you are.

You can be in a state like this with many things. You can be in a room, a house, a car, or even your bed. You can be in a place where you feel safe. You can be in a place where you are happy. Or you can be in a place where you are afraid.

Sometimes, we try to be in this state. We try to be calm. We try to think about nothing. We try to be still. This can help us feel better. It can make us feel like we are not really there. It is like being in a quiet room.

But being in this state is not always easy. Sometimes, you might get angry. Sometimes, you might feel a lot of fear. Sometimes, you might feel sad. These are times when you are really there. You are not just sitting in a room. You are in the middle of something.
