When you say something is on the side, the word you use is often from a different word. We use this word for things that are not at the top of a picture. The top is the top part, the middle is in the middle, and the bottom is at the bottom. A word like this helps us talk about how things are around us.

Imagine a big building. The top part of the building is the room where people sit to talk. The top part has a high wall and a big window. The middle part has the rooms where people work. The bottom part has the rooms where people sleep. This is like the top, middle, and bottom of a big thing.

Now, think about a group of people. One person is at the top, another person is next to them, another person is next to the person next to them, and so on. Each person is on the side of another person, but they are not at the top. The person in the middle is the one in the middle. The person at the bottom is the one at the bottom.

This word can also mean things that are near but not part of a group. If you are very tired, you might feel like you are on the side. You are not in the middle but you are not at the top either. You are just between the people in the middle. You are not the top or the bottom but you are in the middle.

The word is also used to talk about how something works. If you have a problem, you need to figure out why it is happening. You might need to look at the top part of the problem. If the top part is broken, the whole top might break. If the middle part is broken, the middle part might break. If the bottom part is broken, the bottom might break. So, if a thing is broken, the top part, middle part, and bottom might all be broken at once.
