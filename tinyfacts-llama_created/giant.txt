A big animal is a very large animal. A man or a woman who is very large is called a big person. A tree that is very tall is also a big thing. In a city, you might see a huge building that is taller than the tall houses. Or you might see a huge car moving very fast.

Animals that are very large can be very good to watch. Some have very bright colors. Some have long hair. Some can fly. A big animal eats a lot of food every day. It needs many drinks of water to stay strong.

People also use the word to talk about things. When we say a house is big, it means it is very tall. When we talk about a computer, it is a big thing that can do many jobs. When a car is big, it means the seat is very far away. These words help us talk about the world and our lives.

We also use the word to talk about people. If a man is very good at his job, he is a big person in his field. If someone is very kind, they are a big person with a heart. We use this word to show how important something is and how much it means.
