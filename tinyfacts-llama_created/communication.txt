To talk is to use words to share your ideas, your stories, or your plans with other people. You can talk to a friend, a teacher, a family person, or a computer. When you talk, you are sending a sign to someone else.

Your words can be short or long. You might say a few words and then stop. Or you might keep talking for a long time without stopping. Your words can be happy, sad, angry, or calm. They can tell us about how you feel, what happened, or what you want.

People use this word in many ways. You might talk to a shop person to ask if you can pay for something. You might talk to a police person if you feel scared or hurt. You might talk to a friend to ask how they did on a day.

This word is also about how we use our bodies too. When you speak, you use your mouth, your nose, your tongue, and your ears. You use your face to show your smiling face, your eyes, and your hair. You use your hands to point or shake your head.

We all need this word to stay together. We need it to share our lives, to tell stories, to laugh, and to talk about things that are important.
