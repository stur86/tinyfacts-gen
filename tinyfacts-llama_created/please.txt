To please someone means you do something and they are happy. You might be very nervous before a big job at work. You are worried that you will not do it well. But when you do it, they smile and say, thank you. This makes you feel happy because you did something that made them happy.

Sometimes, you want to do something very much. You might want a new game for your phone. You have been waiting for a long time. You want to play it right now. If you get it, you feel very happy and you say, please.

You also need to please a parents. If you do something nice for them, they are happy. They might say, thank you. This makes you feel good.

If you please a friend, you are making them feel happy. You are doing something for them. This can make them smile and say, I like you.

To please someone is to show they are important. It is a way to show you care about them.
