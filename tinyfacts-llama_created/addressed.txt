To have something said about you is to be told about you. You do not know who is talking to you. You do not know why they are talking to you. You can not change what they say.

If you are angry, you might shout. You might point at someone. You might use your words. But you do not have a piece of paper that says, here is what I am saying. You do not have a word that says, this is what I am saying.

If you are sad, you might cry. You might say words that tell others how you feel. You might not know why you are crying. You do not have a word that says, this is how I am feeling.

When you are told about something, you are not the only one who knows. You have to be ready to hear what is said. You have to listen and try to understand. You do not have to know what the other person really means. All you need to know is what they are saying.

You can be told about something many times. You can hear the same words over and over. You can hear the same story a hundred times. But each time, you are still not the only one who knows. You have to listen again and again.
