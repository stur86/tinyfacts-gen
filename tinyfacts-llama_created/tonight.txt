Tonight is a word we use to talk about time at night. It is about the time that is after the day and before the morning.

When the day is over, we move to the time that is at night. This is when the sun is not up in the sky, and it is dark. People often go to bed at this time, so they can get a good rest.

Some nights are very quiet. The stars are bright in the sky. There are no cars or people moving around. It is a time for rest and thinking. Other nights are very busy. People are going out to eat, or they are working. The city is full of people.

When you say you are going tonight, you are telling someone you are going to do something. You might go to a movie, a game, or a party. You might also go to sleep. It is a time to focus on the things you want to do tomorrow.

Also, you can say you are waiting tonight. This means you are waiting for something to happen. You might be waiting for a phone call or a person to arrive. It is a time to be ready.

So, tonight is a time when we live at night. It is the time when we rest, and it is the time before the day starts again.
