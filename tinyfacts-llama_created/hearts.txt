A heart is something you hold close. It is a part of your body. Inside your chest, there is a round thing. This thing is very strong. It keeps your body warm and safe.

You can feel a heart. It is like a heavy thing in your chest. When you are happy, your heart feels lighter. When you are sad, your heart feels heavy. It is like a big ball.

People also use a heart to show love. If you love someone, you can give them your heart. You give your heart to show that you care. When someone gives you their heart, you feel happy inside.

You do not have a heart. You are made of air and water. You are made of many small pieces that fit together. When you breathe in air, you get inside your body. When you breathe out, you give air back to the world. The heart is only in your body. It does not go out.
