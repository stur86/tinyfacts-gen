A tiring thing is something that makes you feel very much. When you do a hard work, you feel tired. You want to stop and rest. This word is used when something is very hard to do. It helps us know when we are tired and when we need to rest.

You can also say a person is tiring. This means the person is very tired from doing many things. Maybe they worked a lot at night and now they are very tired. Or maybe a person who works in a big office all day is tired.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about things that are also tired. A book can be tiring. If you read a book for a long time, your head might feel tired. Or a dog can be tired when it has eaten many times. It is also a word for things that are old. You might say your old shoes are tired. They have been worn a lot and are not as good as they used to be.

The word can also mean something that is very hard to understand. If you see a word you do not know, you might feel tired. You want to look at the word again and again before you can understand it.

The word is often used in the same way as the word for things that are hard. If you do not feel like doing your work, you might say you are tiring. If a group of people feel tired, they might decide to stop.
