To go very fast is to move across the ground quickly. When you are going this fast, you might feel like you are flying. Your body feels like it is heavy and your hair might be in your face.

This is how fast things move in the air. An animal might fly very fast to get away from a big animal. A car can go much faster than a dog. A car going very fast can move very many feet in a very short time.

You can also go very fast when you walk. If you walk very fast, you can cover a lot of ground in a short time. You might feel like you are running when you are this fast.

To write the number, you can write it using numbers. The number before the number tells you how many feet you are going. If the number is one, you are going one foot perfect second. If the number is two, you are going two feet perfect second. If the number is three, you are going three feet perfect second. This number tells you how fast you are going.
