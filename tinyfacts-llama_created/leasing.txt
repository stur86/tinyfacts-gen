To buy something with money is a good thing. When you buy something, you pay for it. You pay money every month for a year. You get the thing you want at the end. But if you do not use it, you must pay the money back to the person who gave it to you.

A car is a thing you drive. You can buy a car with money. You can pay for it over many years. Then you drive it every day, and you pay a little bit of money each month. After the money is gone, you have nothing to pay. You can keep the car until you want to buy another one.

Another kind of thing you buy is a house. A house is a big room with many rooms. You pay money for the room. You pay every month for many years. At the end of those years, the house is yourself. You do not have to pay the money anymore. You can live in it with your family.

Sometimes people buy things they need but do not want. They do not want a new computer or a phone. They buy an old one. The old one still works. They pay money for it, and then they keep it for many years. After they pay, the computer or phone is their old thing.

Least is also a word. To let something happen is to give it to someone. You let a person use your book. You give it to them, and they read it. You are not the one who is reading it.

Least can also mean to give up on something. If you are afraid, you may least. You start to feel scared. You want to hide, but you are not sure if you should. You are afraid to move. You are afraid of the dark or of the loud noises. This is a very bad feeling.

Least is a word that is used to talk about how we act. It tells us about our own lives.
