A thing that turns is called a round thing. It has a round top and four sides. When you turn it, the sides move around the top. This makes the top turn around too. The top keeps moving in a big round way.

This kind of thing is often used to make things that we use every day. You might see a round thing that holds your drink. Or you might see a round thing that turns your music. The round thing turns, and the music comes out of it.

Some round things are very big and heavy. They sit on the floor and help hold things up. Other round things are very small. They are in your pocket and make it easy to carry your phone.

We also use this word to talk about things that go around in a round way. If you go around a big round thing, you are moving around it. You are not walking in a straight line. You are moving in a big round way.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about things that are not strong. If you feel a round thing is going to hurt you, it might be too heavy. You need to stop moving it.

The word is also used to talk about things that change over time. If you watch a round thing turn, you see how it changes. It starts one way and ends up a different way. This can be a fun thing to watch.
