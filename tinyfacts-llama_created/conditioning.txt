To train someone means to help them do things they already know. A person who is an animal might learn to walk and run. A person who is a rock does not learn to walk, but a person can teacher them what to do. This is called training.

Sometimes, a person trains a dog. They say the word, and the dog does it. They also teacher the dog what to do when they hear a sound. The dog does not remember the sound, but they remember what the word is.

You can also train a person to do things that might be bad for them. If a person is afraid of a big animal, they can learn to walk past it. They might even feel safe while they are doing it. This is a kind of training.

Training is different from making someone do something. Making someone do something is when you tell them to do it, but they do it anyway because they are afraid of what might happen. Training is about how you help them learn and feel safe.
