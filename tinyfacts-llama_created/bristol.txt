Bathroom

A bathroom is a room in a house where people go to clean themselves. Inside the room, there is a big water thing that goes round and round. It makes the water warm or cold. People use this water to get clean. They put their hands and their faces under the water and rub them. They use a thing with long parts that they hold in their hands to clean their bodies.

There is also a box in the room with many small parts. You put your hands in the box and shake them. You can shake the water out of your hands. After that, the water goes down the long parts and comes out of the box into a big round thing that goes down to the ground.

Some bathrooms have a window that lets you see outside. Some have a door that you open to let people in or out. Some have a door on one side and a door on the other. Some have a door at the top and a door at the bottom. People use the door to go in or out.

Inside the room, there are many small boxes that hold many things. There are boxes for water, for cleaning things, and for the things you use to eat. There are also boxes for hair and for things to put in your mouth.

Bathrooms are very important. They help us feel clean and feel happy. Without them, we would not be able to go to school or work. We need to go to the bathroom many times a day.
