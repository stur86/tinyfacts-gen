To cover something means to keep it safe from the outside world. Think of a big room. If you put a table in the room, you cover it. The table is now hidden from people who walk by. You keep it safe from them.

You can also cover a person. If a person is afraid, they might cover their face with their hands. They hide their face so no one sees them. This helps them feel safe.

You can cover a thing with a big piece of clothes. If you cover a book, it keeps the words safe inside. You keep the words hidden from people who want to read them.

Sometimes, you cover something that is broken. If a ball is broken into many pieces, you put the pieces together again. You cover the broken parts so they do not hurt anyone.

In a big city, people often cover their faces when they walk down the street. This is a way to stay safe at night. Some people cover their faces with a long piece of clothes or a hated. They keep their faces hidden so no one sees them.

To cover something can mean to keep it quiet. If a teacher says, we must cover what happens in the story, you must not talk about it. You keep your mouth shut so the teacher does not hear you.
