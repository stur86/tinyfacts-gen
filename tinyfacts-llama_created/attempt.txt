To attempt something is to try to do it. It means you try to do something you want. You do not know if you will be able to do it, but you want to see if you can.

You might attempt to run a long way. You might also attempt to fly in a small flying thing. You are trying to go very far or try to go very high.

Sometimes you attempt a thing that is hard. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might attempt to walk near it. You are trying to go near it and not to be afraid.

You can also attempt to do something you do not want to do. If you are afraid to go to a place, you might attempt to go there. You are trying to go even though you are afraid.

If you attempt to learn a new thing, you are trying to get better at it. You watch a teacher, you read books, or you try to learn a new way to do a job.

The word attempt can also mean to try to do something you think is wrong. If you think something is a bad way to act, you might attempt to change it. You are trying to do the right thing.

In this way, attempting something is about trying and not giving up.
