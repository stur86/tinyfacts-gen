To run is to move quickly, often over a long area. People do this for fun. They might go very fast or just run around the field many times. This helps their bodies and makes them strong.

Some people do this in a place with a big ground. They wear a hard cover on their feet to help them run fast. They might jump over things or run around a big ring. They try to beat each other, so they can see who is the best.

Another way to use this word is to talk about a team. Many people watch a game where many people run. They try to do better than the others. The team with the most points at the end is the one that did the best.
