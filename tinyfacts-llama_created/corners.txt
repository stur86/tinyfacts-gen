A corner is where three lines meet. You can see it when you look at a picture. Imagine a line that goes out from a wall. It turns around a corner. This makes the line go in a new way.

In a room, a corner is a place where a wall meets another wall or a floor. It is the area between two walls. This is important because it is where people often stand. If you are standing in a room, you are standing on the corner.

You can also talk about corners of things. A corner of a book is the small part where the cover meets the paper inside. The cover has a picture on it and the words on the inside. When you open the book, you see the cover and the words inside. This is the corner of the book.

Some things also have corners. A dog might have a small corner where its ears meet. A small thing might have a corner where it goes into a box. These corners help us understand how things are put together.
