An area where the ground is very soft is often a place where there are lots of small jumps. When water from the deep goes into this area, it is pushed up and then falls back down. This makes a lot of noise and makes the ground shake. This is what is known as an area with a high chance of being a big jump.

The ground is not always the same. At the top of a high area, the ground is very hard. But when the ground around this high area starts to shake, the ground at the top goes down. This is why people are afraid of high areas.

Sometimes the ground is very quiet. People might say, it is a calm area. But when the ground starts to shake, it is not quiet anymore. This is what is known as a sudden jump.

An area with a high chance of being a big jump is called a place where you might feel the ground under you. It is a place where the ground is going to go down.
