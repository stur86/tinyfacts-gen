To be early is to do something before the rest of the people. If you wake up at five, you are early. This means you started before everyone else. It is good when you want to get a head start on your day.

Imagine you are waiting in line to buy a book. You are the first one in the line. You are not late. You are early. This is how it works with time. When you act before everyone else, you are the first one.

It can also mean something is happening before the end of a day. If you are early in bed, you are getting ready for sleep. You are not tired yet. You are in a good place.

Sometimes, we use this word with people who work. An early person is someone who starts the work before the rest of the people. They work alone for a while before everyone gets up. This is important because it helps the work go quickly.

We also talk about early days. If something happens early in the morning, it is before the rest of the day. This can happen with people, with animals, or with things that are new. It is a good time to do it before the rest of the world knows about it.
