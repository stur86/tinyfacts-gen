To go is to move away. It happens when you walk or run. You start from one place. You go until you reach another place. This can happen by walking, by car, by bus, or by a big moving thing that carries many people.

You might go to a school or to a work place. You might go to see a friend or to buy things you need. When you go, you change your body. Your legs move under you and your heart goes fast.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about things that are very big. A group of animals can go to a new home. A group of people can go to a far away place. It is a way to move from one place to another.

You can also go to a place that is not on a ground. You can go to the water, to the sky, or to a high place. This can be fun because you see new things and feel a big change.

Going is important for us. It helps us find our way in the world. It keeps us moving and changing. Without going, we would stay in one place for a long time.
