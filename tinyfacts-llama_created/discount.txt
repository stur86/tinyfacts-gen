A very important word in our world. It is about things you buy.

When you buy something, you pay money. This money helps the person who made the thing. In return, the person gives you the thing. If you buy something, you get it. You are happy.

Sometimes, you might see a sign. This sign tells you about a time to buy the thing. The sign is like a word. It tells you what you should do. If you see this sign, you should go to the store. You should buy the thing before the time is up.

There are two kinds of this word. One is about money. It is called a money word. The other is about how you feel. It is called a feeling word.

If you buy something and you feel very happy, you might say, I love this thing. The word helps us say this feeling. It is like a word that tells you how you feel.

Sometimes, a store gives you a small thing for free. This is a way to show you like the store. They give you a small thing so you will come back. It is a way to say, I like you.

So, a money word is about buying things with money. A feeling word is about how you feel. Both are important in our lives.
