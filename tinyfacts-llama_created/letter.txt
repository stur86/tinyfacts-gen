A letter is a word or a short set of marks that you write. You use these marks to tell people you care about them. You write them by hand on paper.

Each letter is made by putting one mark after another. The first mark is often the top left mark. You might put a mark that looks like a line and another mark that looks like a line next to it. The next mark is another line, then another, and so on. You keep making these lines and marks until you write the word.

Sometimes, a letter has two words. One word is on top and the other word is on the bottom. You write the words on top first, then you write the lines for the bottom word.

You can write a letter to your mother, your friend, or even someone you love. You might write a funny story or a picture. You can also write a letter to a teacher or a doctor. They read your words and understand what you want.

The word is important because you can use it to talk to people. If you write a letter, you are telling the person you are writing to that you care about them. It is a way to show you are thinking about them.

You can also use this word when you are playing games. Some games have letters. When you write these letters, you are trying to find the right words to get a point. It is a fun way to use your brain.
