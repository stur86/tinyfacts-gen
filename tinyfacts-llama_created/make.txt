To make something means you use things to change it. You are the one who does it.

Imagine you have a small box. You want to fill it with small balls. You do not have many of those balls. You go to a store and buy a lot of them. Then you go home and fill the box with the balls. The box is now full.

You are the one who did this. You are the person who made the box. You did it by using the boxes and the balls. You took the things you had and used them to make something new.

You can also make a sound. If you are in a room with many people, you can make a loud sound. You are making the sound. You are the one who made it happen.

Sometimes, you make your own food. You might grow a tree and then eat the leaves. You are making your own food. You took a thing from the tree and used it to give you power to stay inside.

Making things is a big job. It takes time and many steps. But when you do it, you are very strong. You have made something new.
