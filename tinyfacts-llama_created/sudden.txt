To sudden someone means to act very quickly. If you are waiting for someone, then suddenly they appear. This can happen because a door opens, or a car goes very fast.

You can also use this word to talk about a big change. If you are happy, then suddenly you become sad. You might cry. This change happens very fast, like a sudden move.

Sometimes, a sudden thing is a surprise. You do not expect it. You are not ready for it. This can be very big or very small. If you are walking and then you see a dog, you feel happy because you saw a friend.

You can also use this word to talk about a loud noise. If you are quiet and then you hear a loud sound, it is sudden. It happens very fast. You can feel the sound in your ears.
