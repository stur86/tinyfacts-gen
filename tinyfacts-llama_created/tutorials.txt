A group of people, often young, show how to do a thing. They might be from a school, a work place, or a game. They are there to help people who want to learn a new thing.

You might watch a person who plays a game show tell you how to play. You might see them pick a game, how to start, and how to use a small box. You can learn from their way.

Todays, these people use computers. They show you how to work with pictures or words. They might show you how to make a picture or write a story. They help you learn how to use a computer.

Sometimes, these people give you a book to read. They tell you what to do if you get stuck. They show you how to do things. You might learn new words, how to use a computer, or how to do a thing you do every day.

Todays, people watch these groups on the computer. Some watch them while they play a game. Some watch them while they work at their jobs. They like to see how someone else does things. It helps them learn and get better at the thing they want to learn.

People also use these groups for fun. They watch a person dance or tell a story. They might laugh at a funny thing. They enjoy the show and the music. It is a way to be happy and have fun together.
