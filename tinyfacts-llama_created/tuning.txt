To get something just right is a big job. You start with something not quite what you want. You try to move it around until it feels just right. The word has many different uses.

When you play a long song, you might want it to go faster. You use your fingers to press the right keys. You are changing the sound. You are making it fit better. This is what we do with a person who is not the right fit for a job. We are making them fit. We are making them work.

A person who is very good at something is often called a good thing. If you are a good thing at a game, you are doing it well. If you are a good thing at a job, you are doing it better than others. This is because you are using what you know to get the job done.

Sometimes, we change things that are already there. We might change the way a car goes. We might change the way a computer works. We are not just making new things. We are also fixing old things. We are making them better. This is how we make the world feel more nice.

You can also say that something is out of place. If a person is not in the right place, it is out of place. It is not where it should be. This happens when something does not fit with the rest.
