When you talk to someone, you are sending words to them. You can use a computer or a phone to do this. You type the words in your brain, and the person on the other end reads them. This is called writing.

But you can also talk to people who are not in the same place. If you want to ask them a question, you can write it down. Then, you send the words to them by sending the computer. They read the words and can write back. This is how we talk to people who live far away.

Sometimes, you might write a letter. You use words to tell a story or explain why you want something. You use the same words over and over, so the person who reads the letter knows what you mean. This helps you stay in touch with your friends and family.

When people say they are in a different place, they often use the word to mean they are in a different time. They might be saying that they are late for a meeting or that they are not going to be there at all. This is because they are not in the same moment as the other person.

Sometimes, the word is used in a different way. If a person is in a good mood, they can say they are in a good state. They mean that they feel happy and relaxed. If someone is angry, they might say they are in a bad state. They mean that they feel very sad and mad.

In a way, all of these ideas are about how we use words to talk to each other.
