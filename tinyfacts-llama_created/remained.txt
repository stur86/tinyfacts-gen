To remain is to stay in a place where you are. It is to keep things or people around you. You do not have to go away. You can stay at a school, a house, or a park. When you are in this place, you are not moving. You are still here.

You can also say that something remained. This means that something that was broken or changed still is the same. A dog that was happy when it was a baby still is happy now. A book that was read many times still has words inside. The people who are friends, the ones you love, and the things you know all remain.

If you are afraid, you can remain. You do not have to move. You can stay in a room or go to a safe place. You are still there. You are safe. If you are afraid, you can also stay in your own house and wait for it to be safe.

To remain is also about staying in a way that is not good. If you do not leave, you can get in trouble. You might not be able to go to a different place or see a friend. It is good to leave when you are not safe.
