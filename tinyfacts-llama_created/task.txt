To do something means you are doing a job. You have a job to do, and you work on it. You might be doing a job for a person who owns the business. You might be doing a job for a school. You might be doing a job for a family.

A job is when you are working. You are not playing games. You are doing something to make money. You might be moving things from one place to another. You might be fixing things. You might be writing words. You do this job every day.

Sometimes, a job can be hard. You might feel very tired. You might feel angry because you are doing a job that is not fun. But you do it anyway because you want to pay your money.

When you do a job, you feel a small bit of good inside. You are making money. You are doing a thing that is important. You are helping others.

You can also do a job with a friend. You work together. You help each other. You do this job because you like the other person. You like the way the person is being. You like the way they do things.

Sometimes, you do a job that you do not really want to do. You might be a teacher. You have to talk to people all day. You might have to say things that are not nice. You might have to write things that you do not like to write. But you do the job anyway because you want to help the people.
