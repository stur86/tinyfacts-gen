To face means to look at something in front of you. When you face a wall, you turn around and look at the wall. When you face the sun, you look at it so you can see it.

When you are in a room, you are facing the door. You can see what is on the other side of the door. If you want to go to the other room, you must face the door.

You can also face a person. If you want to talk to them, you must stand up and face them. This is important because you want to be seen and heard.

Sometimes, we face a problem. We might feel scared or worried because the problem is in front of us. But if we face it, we can work it out. We might look at the problem, think about what to do, and then we can move forward.

If you are facing a chair, you are not sitting on it. You are standing in front of it. This is how you are facing the chair.
