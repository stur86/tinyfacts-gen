A ring tone is a sound that plays before you start a phone call. It is a short sound that you hear before your phone rings. Most phones have a few ring tones that you can pick from. These ring tones are usually from someone you know, like a friend or a family person. You might also hear a ring tone that is made by a computer.

When you answer a call, the phone rings. But before the person on the other end speaks, the phone plays a short sound. This sound is the ring tone. It helps your phone remember the person you are calling. It is a way for your phone to keep track of who is trying to reach you.

You can also make your own ring tone. If you have a computer and a music player, you can make a sound that sounds like a person. You might use a picture of a person or a sound you like. You can then save this sound to your phone. This way, you can play the sound before you answer a call.

Ring tones are important because they help you know who is trying to reach you. If someone calls and you don't hear a ring tone, you might feel confused. It is like the phone is saying, I am here, but I am not playing a sound yet.
