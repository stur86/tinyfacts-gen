When you say something is under a lot of problems, you are talking about the things happening around you right now. This can be things you see, things you hear, or even your own body.

Think of your day. You wake up and find out that you forgot to put on a shirt. You feel a bit worried because you do not want to look strange. This is a situation. It is not a story from before, but something that is happening now.

Something is also a situation if you are in a place. If you are sitting in a small room with other people, or if you are driving on a busy street, you are in a situation. You are not doing something you love, but you are doing something important.

Sometimes, people say that a situation is hard. This means that it is very hard to do what you want to do. This can happen if you are in a new place, if you have a lot of money, or if you have a job that makes you very tired. You feel like you are under a lot of problems because your life is very busy.

You can also say that a situation is good. If you have a new job, or if you meet a friend you have not seen in a long time, this is a good thing. You are learning and growing, and this is an important step in your life.

The word is very important because it helps us understand what is happening around us. It tells us if something is hard, good, or okay.
