When we say something is an entire thing, we mean it covers every part. If you cut a book into pieces, you still have the book as a whole. The pieces do not cover the whole book. A book is not an entire thing. It is a part.

Think about a person. You love your family. You like them. You love them so much that you feel a big, happy feeling inside. You do not just love them. You love them completely. You love them without any doubt. You love them because they are your family.

In this way, the person is an entire thing. They are a whole person. They are not just a group of pieces. They are a person in their own right.

Everything is not an entire thing. A rock is a part of the ground. A tree is a part of the ground. A dog is a part of the group of dogs. But a person is an entire thing.
