An empty thing is a thing that has no things inside it. If you look at a box, it looks like it has a face on it and some parts around the middle. But inside, there are no things at all. It is like the box is empty. You can fill it with many different kinds of stuff, like paper, food, or small rocks. But after you fill it, it will still look empty because it has no more room inside.

Sometimes, we say a house is empty. This means it is quiet and there is no one living there. Maybe a family went away and left the home for a few days. Or maybe the person who lives there is away at work or school. The house is still there, but it is not full of people.

An empty thing can also mean a room in a tall building. If you walk into a room that is empty, you might feel strange. The walls are not covered with anything, and the floor is not covered with a soft floor. It feels like a big empty space. You might look around and see that there is nothing in it at all. This can be a very strange feeling because a room is usually filled with people, books, and other things.

Sometimes, an empty thing can also mean a place where you can not go. If you try to go to a place, but it is closed, you might feel sad. You need to go somewhere, and the place is not there. It is like the door is shut and you can not get in.
