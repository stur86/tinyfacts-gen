Neck is a word that you might hear in a funny way. It is made of two parts. The first part is a big word with a lot of letters. The second part is a word that means a short, hard thing that you might break.

Think of it this way: if you are very strong, you can use the big word. But if you break something, you are left with the second part. You still know what it means. You just know it is a small, hard thing now.

You can also use it to talk about a person. If a man is very good at a job, people might call him a nodding man. It means he is a good man who always smiles.
