To be a good person is to act with love. This happens when you put the other person before yourself. You do this because you care. You want them to be happy.

A person who is a good one is called a good person. People who are good do not hurt others. They are kind. They are not afraid to say what is right.

A good person might help someone who is in trouble. They might give them money or food. They might tell them they are wrong so they can leave the bad thing behind.

A good person is also one who does not give up. If they are fighting for what is right, they will not give up until they get it. They do not give up even when things seem very hard.

When people act like this, it makes the world a better place. It makes everyone feel safe and happy.
