A user is a person who uses a computer, a phone, a table, or any other thing that can make things happen. They use it to look at pictures, watch movies, write words, or talk to other people. They might use it to buy things from a store, to talk to a friend, or to learn new things.

Imagine you are a mom. You use the computer to look at pictures of your children. You watch their faces, their hair, and their clothes. You might laugh or cry because you are happy to see them. This is like a user.

Another person might use a computer to send money to a friend. They type words on the computer and hit a key. The money goes to the friend in real time, without a person in the middle. This is how the computer works and how people can send money to each other.

People also use computers for work. They type words and numbers into the computer. The computer does not understand words. It only understands numbers. So, a user must tell the computer what they want to do.

Some people use computers for fun. They play games, watch movies, or write stories. They might use the computer to draw pictures or make music. This is a way to relax and be happy.

In a school, some people use computers to do work. They look at pictures of pictures, write papers, or make movies. This is a way to learn new things and do school work.

In our world, we need people to use things. If no one uses a computer, the computer will just sit there, waiting. We need people to use it so it can do its job.
