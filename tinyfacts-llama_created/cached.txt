To save something is to keep it until you need it. You put it in a safe place so you do not forget it later. When you need it, you go back to the safe and take it out.

You can save money in your pocket. You put it in a bag so it stays safe and you can use it later when you need it. Or you can save food for a day. You keep it in a box so it stays food and you do not eat it before you need it.

You can also save things that are hard to find. If you love a book, you might put it in a box. You keep it safe in your room until you need to read it. This way, you do not forget to read it when you do not have time.

Sometimes, you save your ideas. If you think of a good idea for a story, you write it down so you remember it later. You can look at the paper and see what you wrote. This helps you remember what you wanted to say.
