A group of letters that have a lot of power is what we mean by a type of thing. These letters are very important in what we call a word. They are the ones that show how the word starts.

When you see a word, you see how it starts. If the first letter is a short one, like a small number, the word might be short. If the first letter is a long one, like a word that means a room, the word might be long. But the group of letters that shows how the word starts is always the same.

Think of it like a line. If you go to the end of the line, you will be back where you started. The first letter of the word is like the first point on the line. If you move the line and the first point stays the same, the group of letters is like the line.

The group of letters is the most important part of a word. If you change one letter, the whole word changes. If you change two letters, the word might be different. If you change three letters, the word is different again. And if you change four or more letters, the word is almost nothing to anyone who reads it.

There are many groups of letters, like the group of letters in the word family. Each group of letters has a set of letters that follow it. If you go to the end of one group, you go to the beginning of the next group.

You can also see groups of letters as a way to build words. If you pick a group of letters and add one more letter, you get a new word. This is how we build new words from old ones.
