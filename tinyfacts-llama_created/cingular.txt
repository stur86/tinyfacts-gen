A caring person is one who is very kind. When you are a caring person, you show that you care about others. You might give them a small thing they need. You might listen to them if they are sad. You try to help them feel better. 

You can also be a caring person when you help people who are not well. If you see a person who is hurt, you might go to help them. You might give them a drink of water. You might tell them they are not alone. This shows that you care about them and want them to feel safe.

Being a caring person is about being there when someone needs you. It is about being a friend. If you are a caring person, you make the world feel a little better. You show that there are still good people around who care about each other.
