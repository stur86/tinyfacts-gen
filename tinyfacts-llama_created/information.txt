To know something is to understand it. When you learn a new word, you are getting to know how it works. You are building a picture of what it means.

Think about a book. If you read a few lines, you start to understand what is going on. You start to see how the words fit together. You know what the story is about. This is an idea.

But there are many other things you can know. You can know how the world works. You can know how to do things. You can know how to stay safe. You can know how to make a house. You can know how to work with a computer. You can know about the world and your own life.

Sometimes you get this kind of idea from other people. You hear them talk and you start to understand. You see how they think and you start to think in the same way. You are learning together.

You can also get this kind of idea from things you see. You look at a picture of a tree and you start to know about trees. You see how the leaves are green and how they grow from the ground. You start to remember that green. You are building a picture of a tree.

The word we use to talk about this is different. When you say you know something, you are usually talking about facts. You are saying, I know how this is. I know how this works. You are sure about it. You are not just talking, you are really knowing.

Sometimes we say we are learning something. This is the same as knowing. We are building a picture. We are understanding. But with this word, we are talking about a new kind of thing. We are talking about things that are not just facts. We are talking about how things change. We are talking about how things move.
