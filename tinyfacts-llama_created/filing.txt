To fill something means to add many small pieces of paper, small parts, or other things inside a box. These small pieces help keep the box strong and safe for things inside.

Imagine you have a box for your money. You put your money in the box, but you still need to add more money to it. If you do not add any money, the box will be very heavy and will break. But if you fill the box with many small pieces of paper and small parts, the box will stay light and safe.

People use this word to talk about many things at once. If you are trying to remember many words or ideas, you might say you have to fill your mind. You need to add these things to your memory until you can remember them all.

You can also fill a room with many people. When you fill a room with many people, it gets very crowded. This is because there are too many people and not enough space. This can be very hard to do.

Sometimes, you fill a bag with small things. You put many small pieces of food in the bag so that you have enough for the day. This helps you stay safe and keep your food warm.

In a school, you might fill a paper with answers to questions. You write down what you know and what you learned. This helps the teacher see how you did and what you need to work on.
