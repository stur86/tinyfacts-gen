A scaring wind is the kind that makes your hair stand on your head. It is a very strong wind with very strong pieces of air that go very fast. When the wind is like this, the ground and the trees shake and the water on the water goes very fast. This kind of wind is usually found in the high places where the air is not as thick. People use words like this to talk about the kind of wind that is very strong and makes you feel a little scared.

The word comes from old words for far away places. The old people used these words to talk about the places that are very far from them. They did this because they had never been to these places before. They heard these words and they kept them. Now everyone uses these words when they talk about very strong winds.

Some places have very strong winds. The wind can be so strong that it can knock down trees and break power lines. It can also make it hard to breathe. People live in these places to be safe. They stay there when the wind is very strong and they have power lines to keep them safe.
