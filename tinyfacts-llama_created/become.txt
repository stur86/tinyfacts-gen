The wind carries your tiny body and your food away from your body. Your body is no longer on the ground. You are now in the air.

You are not the same animal you were before. You have changed. You have become something new. You are not still a tiny animal. You are something bigger and you are still a living thing. You are still your body, but you are also a different living thing.

You can become a person with a different way of thinking. You can become a person who loves another person. You can become a person who is very happy and very kind.

The word become is used to talk about how things change. It is a word that shows how a person or a thing becomes something else. It is like a door. The door is the old thing. The door is the new thing. When the door opens, the old thing is gone and the new thing is inside.
