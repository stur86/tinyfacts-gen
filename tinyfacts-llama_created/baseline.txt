To set a line as a start point for something is to make it known how far it stands from a point that is already known. This is often done with the help of a line that is very long and straight. When we talk about a line, we usually talk about its ends. One end is the point from which we start and the other end is the point from which we end.

Imagine you are standing in a room. You know how far you stand from the wall because you have been standing there for a few seconds. Now you want to know how far you are from the chair in the corner. You can use your body to move until you are right next to the chair. This is the same thing that happens when you set a line as a start point. You are setting a line at the point where you stand.

Sometimes we set a line at a place where the ground goes very slowly down. This is to help us know where we stand. It is like a line that says, here is where we are at the moment, and now we know how far we have come.

In the world of work, a person might set a line at the place where they start to work. This line marks the start of their work day. They know how far they are from the place where their work ends, and they have a plan to get there on time.

In the world of games, a team might set a line for their place on the field. This line is like a start point for their game. When they move to this line, they are ready to play. It is the place where they know they stand and where they know they go.

To set a line is to give a point of beginning that is used to start a new thing. It is about being sure you know where you stand in a situation.
