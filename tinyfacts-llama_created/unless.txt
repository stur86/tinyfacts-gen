To do something or to not do something

When you say you will do something, you are saying you will not do it. This is because the word before the word is usually the word for not.

If you are going to a party, you are going. But if you are going to leave, you are not. You are doing something, but you are not doing it.

You can also use this word with people. If you say you will be friends, you are promising to be a friend. But if you are going to be angry, you are not going to be a friend. In this case, you are doing something, but you are not doing it.
