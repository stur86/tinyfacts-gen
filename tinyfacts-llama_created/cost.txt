To pay for something means you give money so you can use it. When you buy something, you pay money to the person who makes it. You might pay money to buy food, clothes, or a book.

Think about money. Money is the thing you use to buy things. If you do not have enough money, you have to wait until you get more money. This is a hard way to live.

Sometimes you pay for a thing so you can do it later. If you buy a car, the car is your money. When you pay for a car, you pay money now. Later, when you need to drive to school or work, you use your car money.

You also pay money when you pay for a job. If you work in a big office, you give money to the person who runs the office. This money is for all the people who work there.
