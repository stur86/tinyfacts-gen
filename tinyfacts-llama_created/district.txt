A big area of land is called a city. It is like a group of many towns. People from many different towns all go there. There is a big building where the city is the head. There is a police car, a bus that goes to other cities, and a big place where the people make the plans.

Sometimes the area where the city is is not one big piece of land. It is many small areas. Each small area has its own police, schools, and stores. The people in each small area are close to each other. They can walk to the other small areas if they need to.

A place where people go to buy things is called a store. There are many stores in a city. Some are very big and have many things. Some are small and only have one thing. People go to these stores to buy food, clothes, or things they need.

There is also a place where people go to eat. It is a big room with many tables. The people there sit and eat together. They talk and laugh. Some places are very quiet and some are very loud.

A group of people who live in one area is called a city. They share the same school and the same police. They are all part of the same group.
