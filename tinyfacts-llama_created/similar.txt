Something that is like another thing is called the same thing. It means that two things look the same. If you look at a face and see the same eyes and the same hair, you are the same person.

Think of a book. If you read a story, you know what happens next. You do not start reading from the beginning. You know what the story is about because of what you have read so far.

You can also say that two things are the same. This means that they are both the same thing. You do not have to read a book if you know what a book is. If you look at a picture of a house, you know what it is. The house is the same picture.

In your life, you might see a friend and think they are the same person. You know them because you have talked before. You know their voice and their way of talking. You know how they act when they are happy and how they act when they are sad.

When you say something is like something, you mean that it is very much like it.
