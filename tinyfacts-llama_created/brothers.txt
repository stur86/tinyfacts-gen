To be a brother is to have a brother. A brother is a person you live with. You share a room with them. You eat food together. You play games with them. You tell jokes together.

A brother is usually the boy who is the older one. But a brother can be the girl who is the older one too. Sometimes, a girl can be a brother. This happens when a girl has a baby. She has a boy. So, a girl can be a brother too.

You can also be a brother to your friends. If you like a friend, you can be a brother to them. You can give them a hug. You can tell them you are a friend. You can be a brother to them.

Being a brother is a good thing. It means you have a person you care about. It means you feel happy when they are around you.
