To ignore something is to not pay it any attention. When you ignore a person, a thing, or a sound, you are not thinking about it. You do not hear it, see it, or notice it. It is not important to you.

Imagine a child. If you play with them, they might get very sad. They might feel angry. But if you ignore them, they do not feel sad at all. They just stop playing. They do not care.

This happens with people too. If you do not talk to a friend, they do not notice you. You are not important to them anymore. They walk away, and you are left alone.

You can ignore a word too. If you hear a word you do not like, you might say it is a bad word. You are not going to think about it. You do not care what the word means.

Sometimes, you might ignore your own body. If you are tired, you might not move. You just sit there. You do not want to do anything because you do not feel like it.

In a way, ignoring something is like putting it in a box. You are not looking at it, you are not thinking about it. It is gone from your mind.

But ignoring can also be a bad thing. If you ignore a problem, it may get bigger. You might not fix it, and it will stay the same. You might get more worried and angry as time goes on.

So, when you ignore something, you are not really doing anything. You are just letting it go.
