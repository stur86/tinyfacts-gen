To play means you use your hands to make things happen.

You can play with your hands. You might shake them to make a sound. You can also use your hands to move a small thing around. You move it so it goes in a line.

You can also play with a ball. You throw the ball into a long piece of ground. If you hit the ball with your hand, you are playing. You are having fun. You are doing something you enjoy.

Sometimes you play with a friend. You sit together and you say things. You might laugh. You might do things that are not right. You are being your self.

You can also play with your mind. You might think about things. You might try to remember a word. You might try to remember how to do something. Playing with your mind helps you feel better.

When you play, you are not doing work. You are not thinking about the things you need to do. You are being free. You are doing something that makes you happy.
