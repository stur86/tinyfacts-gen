To clear something means to get ridden it. When you say a person or a thing is cleared, it means they are not doing anything wrong. They are not in the right place or they are not doing what they are supposed to do.

Imagine you are playing a game with a group of friends. If one person is not following the game, you can tell them to stop. This is clearing them. They are not in the game anymore.

Also, if a person is being watched all the time, it is being cleared. They are not alone. There is someone looking at them.

Sometimes people get a clear. This means they do not need to show their papers or their money. They can go to a place without a line. It is like a big sign that says, you are free to go.

You can also clear a room. If a group of people want to go home, they can ask the person in the front to let them go. They can walk out while the person in the front is still there.

If a thing is cleared, it is safe. The person or thing is not a problem anymore. They are gone.
