When you talk about a person, a thing, or a place, you often talk about what they look like, what they can do, or what they are like. This is because these things are part of what makes them who they are.

Think about your face. You have a nose, a mouth, eyes, and hair. Each of these parts is a part of your face. They are all there together in a way that makes you, you.

Your hair is a part of your body. It grows out of the top of your head. Some people have hair that is very long, and some people have hair that is short and grows close to their head. Your hair is a part of who you are.

Your eyes are another part of who you are. They help you see the world around you. You use them to look at people, at things, and at the ground. Your eyes are very important because they let you know what is happening.

You can have many different kinds of hair. You can have hair that is very dark, or very light, or hair that is a funny color. You can also have hair that falls down, or hair that falls up. Your hair is your own way of being.

Things are also a part of who we are. A car is a thing that people use to get from one place to another. A book is a thing that people read for fun. A tree is a thing that grows in the ground. All of these things are part of what makes the world what it is.

When we talk about a thing, we are usually talking about its parts and how they work together. We might say that a car has a big round thing on the front and a thing that goes round too. We might say that a book has words on it and a cover that keeps the words safe.

We also talk about a person. A person has their own body, their own face, and their own voice. They have their own way of thinking and feeling. A person is a living thing that is part of the world.

When we talk about a place, we are talking about where people live. We talk about cities, towns, and the big wide places where there are many other places. We talk about how far away one place is from another. We talk about the things we do there, like going to school or working at a job.

So, when we talk about a thing, a person, or a place, we are talking about what makes them, them. We are talking about the parts, how they work together, and who they are.
