Over the night is when you are in your house, and the sun is not up. You are in the dark. The sky is black and the stars are bright. You can hear the wind moving through the trees.

When you are in this time, the world is still. People are asleep. They do not move. The dogs sleep too. Everything is quiet. The night is a time to rest and dream.

This time happens every night. People use it to sleep. They stay in their rooms and wait for the morning. They may watch the stars or read a book. They do not go out to play.

Sometimes, you can hear strange noises at night. A dog might move around in the dark. A cat might be quiet and look for food. A person might hear their own name or their phone ringing.

In the morning, the night ends. The sun starts to come up. The world becomes bright. People wake up and start their day.
