A person who helps others. They often do this to show that they care. This word can mean different things.

One kind of help is when you step on a floor. You step before you go. This helps you move from one place to another. You are moving. You are doing something.

Another kind of help is when a person steps up to a table. They stand in front of a person who wants to eat. They help make sure the food is ready for everyone. This is important because everyone wants to eat at the same time.

In school, we use these words. One student is the one who steps first. They go first. The next student steps. This goes around many times. Everyone steps a few times. This helps everyone know who is in front of them.

Sometimes, a person steps up to a room. They say, they want to speak. The person who wants to speak sits down. The room stays quiet. The person speaks. This helps everyone listen.

This word is often used when a group of people want to help each other. They step together. They work as one team. They help each other. This makes everyone feel good.
