To be enough means you are not too small or too big. You are just right. If you are not enough, you are not strong enough. If you are too small, you can do nothing. If you are too big, you might hurt others.

Think of a book. You need a book to read a story. If the story is too big, it is hard to read. If the story is too small, you can not see the words.

You are enough if you do your job. If you do not do your job, you are not enough. You must do your work and help others. This is what it means to be enough.

A person can be a good friend. They listen to you and do not laugh at you. They help you when you are sad. You are enough because you have a good friend. You do not need to be the strongest or the best. You just need to be you.
