The word is made of two parts. The first part is called a word. It is a word you use to talk. We use it to tell other people what we think or want. It is like a short story.

The second part is called a thing. It is a real thing you can see or hold in your hand. It might be a chair, a book, a car, or a tree. It is not a word.

When we put a word together with a thing, we make a story. The word tells what happened before the story starts. Then the story tells what happened while the word was there. The word and the story are together.

You can also use this word to talk about how things work. If you say something is a thing, you mean it is a real thing. You do not say it is a word, even though it is a word.

Think of it like this. Your name is a word. Your name tells who you are. Your house is a thing. Your house tells you where you live.
