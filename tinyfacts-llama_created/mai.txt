May is a word that many people use to talk about the time of year when things get warm and the days are long.

In the year, May is the month after the first month. It starts in the middle of the year, and it goes until the end. During this time, many things happen.

One thing happens in May. In some parts of the world, the first growing things on the ground start to grow. These are things you find in the ground, like green things that grow from the ground, or small hard things that grow from the ground. These are the things that grow before the green things that grow from the top of trees.

Another thing happens in May. People often wear clothes with colors in them during this time. Some people wear red and white clothes. Some people wear blue clothes. People also wear clothes with pictures of animals on them. These clothes are called clothes with pictures.

In some parts of the world, the days are very hot in May. The sun is up in the sky for most of the day. People like to go to the park or to the water to cool off. They also like to go to the movies or to the park to watch movies.

In other parts of the world, the days are not as hot. The sun is still up in the sky, but it is not as bright as it is in May. People still like to go to the park or to the water to cool off.

May is also a time when people start to think about the end of the year. They start to look at the days and think, what will happen next year? They might plan their jobs or their lives for the next year.

In the end, May is a time of change. The days get longer, the growing things get stronger, and the clothes start to change. It is a time of new things and of moving from one time to another.
