To walk is to move your feet from one place to another on the ground. You stand on the floor and take small steps. Each step is a bit of your body moving from one place to another. Your legs, your feet, and your arms all work together so you can keep moving.

When you walk, you keep your eyes on the ground. You look at the ground, and you see the tiny things that are all around you. Sometimes you see a tree, a house, a car, or a person. This helps you know where you are and where you are going.

Walking is a way to move through the world. It can help you feel safe. You know what is ahead of you, and you can plan your steps. It is also a way to get from one place to another. You can walk to school, to a friend, or to the store.

Sometimes people walk in the dark. They use their eyes to see the road and the ground. They might carry a small light on their hand. This helps them see what is around them. Other times, people walk with a friend. They can talk and laugh together as they move.

Walking is a normal thing for most people. You do it every day, even if you do not think about it. You walk while you listen to music, while you watch a movie, or while you think about your day ahead.
