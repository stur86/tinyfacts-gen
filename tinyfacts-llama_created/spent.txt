To spent something means you use it all up. You do not have any left. You have nothing. You have gone through the money or the power or the time.

Think of it like a small bag. You fill it with small things like pieces of paper, small rocks, or small pieces of food. You put them into the bag. After you fill the bag, you can not put any more in. You have used it all up.

If you are a person, you can also spend your time. You go to a place, watch a movie, eat food, or play games. This is spending. You are using your time to do something you like.

Sometimes you spend money. You buy things you want. You buy a book, a game, or a new pair of shoes. You use your money to buy those things.

You can also spend your love. You love a person or a place. You feel happy when you are with them. You spend your time with them. You give them your heart.

To spend something is to give it up. You are letting it go so you can have more. It is an important word because it shows how we live our days and our lives.
