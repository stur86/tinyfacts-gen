To change something is to make it new. You can do this with a word, a thing, or even a person.

Imagine you have a word you use to say something. Maybe you say it every day. One day, you decide to change it. You become angry because you think the word is wrong. You want it to be a different word. You start to say it in a different way. You might say it with a smile, or you might say it in a funny way. Now you are using the word in a different way, and you have changed it.

You can also change a thing. If you have a book, you might get a new book. This is when you take an old book and put it in a box so it is safe. The old book is still there, but it is not part of your room anymore.

Sometimes, you change the way you act. If you are very angry, you might become quiet. You might stop talking and just sit. This is changing how you feel. You are not the same person anymore.

When you change something, you are changing the world around you. If you change your word, you change how people think. If you change a thing, you change how it looks or feels. If you change your way of acting, you change how others see you.
