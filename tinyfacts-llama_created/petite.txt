The word we use when we talk about small people is very important. To say a thing is small is to say it is little. It does not mean the person is not strong. If you are small, it just means you are not as tall as another person.

When we talk about a small dog, a small cat, or a small child, we are talking about how much they are. A tiny baby is very small. A tiny animal might be the smallest you have ever seen. Even a tiny bit of paper can be very small.

To be small does not mean you are afraid. It just means you are a little bit different. Sometimes, being small can be a good thing. A small house might be more quiet and safe. A small car can be very fast and easy to drive. A small child can do things that a bigger child can not do.

The word is used to talk about many things. You can say, in a way that is nice, a small house is a very nice place to live. Or you can say, a small dog is very good to have around because it is so small. It shows you care about the person and the thing.

We also use this word when we talk about how we feel. If you are small on your body, you might feel a little bit heavy. If you are small in your heart, you might feel a little bit sad. But if you are small in your mind, you might feel a little bit calm.
