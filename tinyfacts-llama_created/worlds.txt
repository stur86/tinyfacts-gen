The world is the place where everything lives. It is where people, animals, and trees are all together.

Imagine you are in your room. You can see the wall, the floor, and your bed. But you are not in another room. You are in your world. Your world is where you live.

There are many worlds. One is where the sun is. Another is where the sky is bright. Another is where the water goes. Each world has its own things. In one world, the water is blue. In another, the water is green. In another, the water is red.

Some people have worlds they never go to. They live in a city or a big place with many people. They have cars and big houses. They are safe and have many things. In their world, everything is new and different from your world.

There are also worlds that are far away. These are places you can only dream about. They are very big and have many worlds inside. If you go there, you might see strange things. You might meet people who are very different from you.

There is also a world that is not really a world. This is a place where no one can go. It is a place where no one can leave. Some people say this is a place where the people who built it are still there. They say the people who live there are still living and still making things.
