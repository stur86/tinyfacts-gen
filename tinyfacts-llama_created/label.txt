To a word, a thing that a person puts on something else is a word. You can put a word on a book, a picture, or a thing you make. This makes it clear what the word means. If you have a book with many words, the first word is the one you put on it. This word tells the reader what the book is about.

You can also put a word on something you eat. This word tells the person how to eat it. If you eat a food with a word on it, the word says what to do. If you eat the word instead of the food, you might feel sick. This is why we do not put a word on our bodies.

A word is not the same as a picture. A picture shows you what something looks like. A word only shows what the thing is, without how it looks. If you have a word that is true, but the picture is not, the word is a good help. But if you have a picture that is true, but the word is not, the picture is better.

You can also put a word on a person. If you are afraid of a word, you might be afraid of people who use it. If you are happy about a word, you might feel glad. A word is a word that stays in your mind.
