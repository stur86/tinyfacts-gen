To share something means to give it to many people. When you share a book, you read it for yourself. Then you tell your friends about it. Now many people know the book.

Sometimes people make a story. They write it and then they give it to many people who watch their show. When many people watch the show, the story gets more and more people. The story is shared from person to person until it is known by everyone in the world.

In this way, when you share something, you are helping many people learn. You are breaking down a thing into many small pieces so that more and more people can understand it.

This is how many people share their work. There are many ways to do it. Some share their work one person at a time. Others share it all at once. Some share their work on the computer so that many people can watch it at the same time.
