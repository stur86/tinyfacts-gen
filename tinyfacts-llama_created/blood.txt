Blood is a clear stuff that moves through your body. It is very important. It carries many things that your body needs to stay going.

Inside your body, there are many tiny moving things that go round and round. These moving things are like tiny animals. They carry food from one part of your body to another. They also carry bad things that you get from other people. The moving things also carry things that you need to grow big and strong. When the moving things get old, they die and fall down.

The blood in your body is made up of many different kinds of tiny moving things. Some are very small and help your body fight against bad things. Some are even smaller and go into the tiny spaces around your brain and other things in your head. Some are very big and go through the walls of your heart. When they get old, they die and fall down.

The blood is also made up of many tiny white things that help keep you safe. These white things are like tiny pieces of paper that your body makes. They cover the bad things and help keep them from getting into your body.

You can feel the blood when you press on a cut. The red part of the blood comes out. The white parts stay inside. This is why you see red when you cut your skin.
