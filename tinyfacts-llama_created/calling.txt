To call something is to send a word or a sound to it. You can use your phone to call a friend. If you say the word, the person you are calling hears it. If they hear it, they know who is there.

To call someone means you are asking them to come to you. You might call a teacher to tell them you are late or to ask for help. You can also call a phone number that is not your own. If you call a number, you send a word to the person at that number.

Sometimes, you call something that lives in the water. You might call a small animal that flies in the sky. You can also call a place where you go to rest. You might go to a call to go to a place that is far away.

To call a thing can also mean to use it. If you call a chair, you are asking someone to help you or to give you something. If you call a friend, you are saying you need them.

We use this word every day. We call people, things, or places. We call a friend when we need help or a fun time. We call a phone number when we want to talk to someone.
