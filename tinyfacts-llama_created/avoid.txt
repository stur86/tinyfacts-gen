To avoid something means you try not to do it. Imagine you really want a book but you know it is not what you need. You look around and see that there is a door. You push it. You are not going into the room where the book is. You are trying to stay away from the thing you do not want to look at.

You can also avoid something in your life. Maybe you feel afraid of a big dog. You walk quickly and stay close to a wall so you do not get hurt. You are not going near the place where the dog lives. This is because you do not want to be the one who gets hurt.

Sometimes you might avoid a fight. If someone is making you angry, you might walk away. You are not going to the room where they are. You are staying away so you do not have to face the person who is being mean.

You can also avoid a problem. If you know that money is hard, you might look for a job that is easy to do. You look at places where you can work. You do not want to spend your time looking for a job. You are trying to stay away from the hard work.

To avoid something can help you feel safe. It means you are keeping yourself away from a place or a person. You are not going near them because you do not want to be near them. This helps you feel calm and safe.

So, to avoid something is to try to stay away from it. It is a way to keep yourself safe.
