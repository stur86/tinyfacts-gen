A normal is a word that means a way to do things. It is something that people usually do or expect. If you do not do it, you might be the one who is wrong.

Think of a time when you and your friend do not play the same game. You both might like the game, but your friend says it is not fun. That is not a normal thing to do. Your friend is right. You should do it.

There are many types of normals. One is the way a family lives. Another is the way you act in a school. A normal way is one that is good for everyone.

Sometimes, people make their own normals. They decide what is the right way to act or what is the right way to do things. This is called a normal for your group. You are the one who decides what is right for your people.

We also have normals in our lives that are not about people. We have a normal way to act when we are in front of a big group. We are not supposed to be afraid. We are supposed to be happy and make the people around us happy too. This is a normal for the world.

In some places, people have a normal way to act in front of other people. They might not speak at all. They just smile and wait for the right moment. This is a normal way to act when you are not sure what to do. It is okay to be quiet.

In a way, normals help us know what is right and wrong. They give us a way to decide what is good for us and what is not. They help us stay together as a group and as a family.
