To say something is true, but not actually happening, is a big word. It is often used when you are talking about something that happened in the past. 

You can say,  The car is red. It is a true fact. Now, if you say,  The car is red. The car is red,  but it is not actually red,  is not true. The car is red because the person who drives it says it is. If something is not actually red, it is just a lie. You can say,  The dog is happy. The dog is happy. The dog is happy,  but it is not actually happy. The dog is happy because it feels that way. It is not actually happy.

You can also use this word when you are talking about a story. You might say,  The story is about a man who went to a far place. The story is true,  but it happened a long time ago. People do not actually go to that far place. They tell stories about it, but they are not really there.

Sometimes, you might say,  The idea is good. It is a nice way to think about things. You can say,  The way I feel is good. The way I feel is not actually good. The way I feel is because I am happy or sad. If something is not actually good, it is just a way to make things sound nice.
