To have an open door means you can go in. You do not need to knock. You can just walk in and go to where you want. This is true for a door in a house or a school. It is also true for a computer. You can go to the computer and look at things without a person telling you. This is how you learn new things.

Sometimes, you might not have an open door. You need to ask for it. You ask a teacher if you can see a picture on the computer. Or you ask a friend if you can go to their house. When you ask, a person usually says yes. They open the door and let you in.

You can also say this when you are not allowed to do something. You might have a book that is not for you. You want to read it, but your mom says it is not right. In this case, you can not read it because you do not have the right to do it.

To have an open mind is to not be afraid of what other people think. You are not sure if you agree with them. You try to understand their point of sight. This is a good thing because it helps us learn from each other.

A computer can also have an open door. If a computer does not work, you might need to open it. This lets you see what is wrong. You can then fix the problem.
