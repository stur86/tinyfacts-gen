To mine something means to get it out of the ground. Imagine you are a person who wants to make a bed. You need to go under the floor to get the soft things out of the way. You use a long thing with a round part to push the things out. It is like a big dog that finds what you need.

A person who works in this kind of job is called a mining man or woman. They go into the ground and take out the things that people need. They might find old things from a long time ago, like old books or things from a far away place. They might also find things that are very hard to get from the ground, like a lot of money.

Sometimes, people do not go into the ground to get things. They go into a big area with many small rocks. They pull out the rocks that are like tiny balls. They need a big hard thing to pull them out, but the hard thing is much bigger than a person. This is why it is called a big job.

To mine something is to find it. It is also to get it and take it out of the ground where it is. It is like a long story with many parts.
