To work together is the best word for this. You are a group of people. Each person does their own job. If the jobs are not the same, they can be very hard. But if they are the same, they can all do the job at the same time.

Think of a city. There are many people who live there. They each have a job. A doctor works at a big hospital. A teacher works in a school. A police man works in a town. If they all work together, the city is safe and happy.

Sometimes, two groups work together. They are different, but they are friends. One group is good at fixing cars. The other group is good at fixing things that break. When they work together, they fix many things that need to be fixed.

When you watch a movie, the people in the room are working together. The people who play the parts are doing the same job. They follow what the person in front of them is doing. This helps you understand what is happening.

In a game, the team works together. The people who throw the ball are working together. The people who run are working together. They all move the ball to the other team. This is how you get better.
