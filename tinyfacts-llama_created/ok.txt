To say something is okay means you think it is right. It is a way to say that a situation is not bad. This can help you feel better when things are not going the way you want them to.

You can use this word in many ways. You might say, if you have to do a hard work, you feel okay. Or you might say, if you see a good friend, you feel okay.

Sometimes, you might use this word with someone else. If a person says, you are okay, the other person feels safe. They know they can talk to you without fear.

You can also use it in a funny way. If you eat a thing you do not like, you might say, that is okay. The word is about not being mad or worried, just being okay with what happened.
