Always is a word you use when you talk about things that happen every day. It is a word that does not have a beginning or an end.

When you say you will always do something, you mean you will do it every time. If you say you will always love someone, you mean you love them always. If you say you will always be happy, you mean you are always happy.

It does not mean you will never do something. You might say you will always eat food or you will always watch a movie. These are not the same thing. If you always eat food and you never watch a movie, you are not always both. You are in between, but you are not always both.

You can always go to the park. You can always read a book. You can always speak with someone. These things do not change. You are always doing them.

When you say you will always do something, you tell the world. You tell everyone that you will never stop doing it. This can make them worried. It can make them think about what they should do.

But in some ways, always is a good word. It shows you have a plan. It shows you are ready for what happens next. It is a word you use when you want to be sure.
