To follow someone means to go behind them. You do this when you want to show you care. If a friend wants to go to a party, they might ask another friend to go with them. The second friend has to go because the first friend is going alone.

If you are with a group of people, you are often following them. This is because you want to be near them. You might walk behind them so you do not trip. You might also follow them so you know where they are going.

You can also follow a sound. If you hear music, you might want to follow it. You might walk in the direction of the music because it is the best way to find it. This is called moving to the beat.

Sometimes, people follow a thing that is moving. If a car is moving from one place to another, people might follow it. They might not follow it every step, but they keep going with it because it is moving and it is a good thing to watch.

To follow someone can mean you are their friend. If you are following a person, you are showing you care about them. You are not afraid to tell them what you think. You might follow them in a quiet way so you do not scare them. You follow them because you trust them.

You can also follow a story. If a book has a story, you might want to follow it. You follow the words to see what happens next. This is how we learn new things and get better at understanding the world around us.
