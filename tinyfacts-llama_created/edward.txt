A person who lives in a place is called a city. A city has many houses, stores, and places to eat. People from many different parts of the world come there to live and work. It is often very busy. There are many cars, buses, and trains that go around the city. In the city, there are big streets with lights and many people walking.

A person who is very good at a job is called a person who works. They go to a place every day and do their job. They may work in a building or a field. Sometimes, a person who works is the one who makes things. They use a computer to make things. They also work with other people to make sure everything is done right.

A person who is very old is called a person who is grown up. They have had many years of life. They have seen a lot of things. They know how to take care of themselves. They may have children of their own. They may also have friends. People who are grown up are often important in a group. They help lead the group and make sure everyone is safe.

You can also say someone is a person who is small. A small person is very short. They can not reach the top of a very tall building. They might have to use a chair to stand. In a game, a small person might be the one who does not have a ball and has to stay close to the other people.
