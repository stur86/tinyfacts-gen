A book is made of many small pieces of paper. Each piece is a story. The stories are in a different order. The first story is in the first piece of paper. The second story is in the second piece of paper. The third story is in the third piece of paper, and so on.

When you read a book, you go through the pieces of paper one by one. You read a story, and then you move to the next story. There are many stories in one book. The stories are about people who do things, people who are sad, people who are happy, and people who do strange things.

A group of these stories is called a book. The book is like a big box with many small boxes inside. Each small box has a story inside. When you look at all the small boxes, you see the whole story.

Some books are very long. You read one story after another story. Each story is a bit different from the last one. You see many different ways to live, many different kinds of people, and many different ways to feel things.

Other books are very short. You read one story, and then you are done. You can finish reading a book in one night. This kind of book is like a short story.

Sometimes, you read stories that are not in a book. You might watch a movie with a story that is not in a book. You can watch a story many times and still see new things. You can watch a story and think about it later.

There are also books that are not really stories at all. They are like a way to show something. Maybe you see pictures. Maybe you hear a voice. Maybe you read something that is not a story at all, but it is still important to know.

In some places, people write stories on walls. They write many stories in one place. They do this so that everyone can see the same story. This is like a big book that everyone can read.

In some homes, there is a big box that has many small boxes inside. This box is the book of the family. Each person gets to pick a small box. When you open a small box, you find a story that is about your family. You can read the story many times and see how your family has changed over the years.

The word books comes from the word for small pieces of paper.
