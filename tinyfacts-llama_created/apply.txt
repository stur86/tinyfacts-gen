You can also use this word to talk about people. If you ask someone to help you, you are asking them to use their time and their power to do what you need. This is important because it helps you get what you want.

Sometimes, you have to use a word that is hard to say. If you do not use this word, you might not understand what other people are talking about. You must use it so that everyone can know what you mean.
