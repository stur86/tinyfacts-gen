To be on a high ground is to be above the ground. You are standing where the sky is bright and the wind is not too strong. You can see a very far way. This is a good place to watch the world go by. You can see many houses, and sometimes you see people you know. It is a place where you can feel calm.

A place with a high ground is often called a town. Many cities are on high ground. This is why the city looks nice. There are many tall houses and wide streets. You can see the top of the city from far away. People move around this town in cars and buses.

Some places are very high. You can see the whole world. The top of a very high place is very, very high. This is why people go there to see the world from the top. These places are not for everyone. They need a lot of money to go there.

You can also say that a place is beautiful if it is on a high ground. If you look at a place from a high ground, you see how pretty it is. The ground is green and the houses are small. The air is clean. You can feel the cool wind. The place looks like a picture from a book. This is a good thing about a place with a high ground.
