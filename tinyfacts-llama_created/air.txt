Air is the stuff we breathe in every day. It is all around us and in every room. We can feel it when we close our eyes and take a deep breath. It is light and goes very fast. You can see it in the sky when the sun is up and the air is clear.

Air comes from many things. The sun makes it by burning things in the sky. The ground and the water give off air too. Even the tiny living things in the ground and in the water have air inside them.

When you cut a piece of wood, the air inside is pushed out. This is why you feel a bit of cold when you cut wood. You also feel it when you cut a piece of paper. The paper is made of tiny bits of wood and air.

Air is very important for us. We need it to breathe. Without air, we would not be able to live. Our bodies need to take in air to keep us going. We can also use air to move. If you are on a fast car and you press the foot on the floor, the car will start moving because of the air pushing out the way.

There are many different kinds of air. There is the air that comes from the ground. There is the air that comes from the sky. There is the air that comes from the water. There is the air that comes from the ground and the water. There is also the air that comes from the tiny living things in the ground and in the water. And there is the air that comes from the big moving things in the sky.
