A smell is a way your body tells you something is there. When something gives off a smell, it sends a tiny bit of the thing to your nose. Your nose picks up this tiny bit and turns it into a picture that your brain can understand.

Think of it like this. Imagine you smell food. You might see a bright green color or a soft light. This is because your brain is telling you that the food is there. You can't see the food, but you know it is because of the smell.

You can also smell things that are very far away. If you are standing near a tree, the smell of the tree will come to you. It is like a big wave of smell that starts from the tree and goes into your body.

Some smells are very strong. They can make you feel a little bit scared or angry. Other smells are very small. They are like tiny drops of air that go into your nose and make your brain say, okay, this is what I am smelling.

Your nose is very good at picking up smells. It is one of the best things your body has. If you have a nose that is not working well, you might have trouble smelling things.
