To give something is to share it with others. When you give money, you are sending some of what you have to someone else. You do this because you want to help the person. It makes them happy.

You can also give things you own. If you have a book you love, you might give it to a friend. This is a way to share the love of the book. You are not taking anything from the person.

Sometimes, you give things that are not money. You might give your time. You could help a family. You could help a person who is sick. You could also give your voice. If you speak well, it can help people feel better.

Giving is an act of love. It is showing that you care about other people. It is a way to make the world a better place.
