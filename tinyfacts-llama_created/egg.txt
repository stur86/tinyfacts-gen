An animal that lays many small white or brown things that are eaten by people and other animals is called an animal. One of these animals can lay many of these small white or brown things at once. When it lays them, the animal pushes them out of its bottom.

Inside the animal, there are many small parts. One of these parts is big and round. This round part can hold many of the white or brown things at once. Another round part is much smaller and sits inside the round part. This smaller part goes in one end of the big round part and out the other end. This round part can hold many of the white or brown things until it is full.

When the animal is ready to give these things to us, it goes into water. It pushes the white or brown things out of its bottom and into the water. When it comes out of the water, the white or brown things are ready to eat. The animal then goes back inside to do it all again the next day.
