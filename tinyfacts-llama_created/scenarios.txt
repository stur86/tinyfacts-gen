To do something in a different way is to set up many different steps.

You can have a big story. One way is the good part. Another way is the bad part. You can also have two stories about the same thing. One is happy, and the other is sad. You can see which one is better.

You can also do this with a job. You have a job, and you do it one day at a time. Or you have a job with many different days. You might do the same work every day, but you change what you do each day. This makes the job feel different.

Sometimes you do this with a game. You play a game with many steps. You might start with a good plan. As you go, your plan changes. You learn something new. You get a better idea. This makes the game more fun.

You can also do this with a story with many people. One person is the leader. Another person is the one who is in the middle. Another person is the one who is at the end. You can see which person is important.

You can set up many different ways to do something. This makes it interesting to watch. It makes it feel like there is no one right way. It makes you think about what is best.
