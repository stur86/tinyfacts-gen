To Yell

When a group of people want to talk to each other, they use words. One person says a word. The next person hears the word. The word goes around the group. The word can be loud or soft. Sometimes people yell because they are very happy. Sometimes they yell because they are angry.

If one person says a word, everyone else hears it. Everyone knows what the word is. People use this word to talk about many things. They talk about their friends. They talk about their jobs. They talk about the world. This word is very important for people to be able to share their ideas with each other.

In this world, people often use a word that starts with the word. When this word starts with a hard sound, the word is strong. When this word starts with a soft sound, the word is quiet. People use this word to show who they are. It is like a sign that says, I am here. I am talking.
