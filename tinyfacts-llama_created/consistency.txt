To be in a state of being is to be in the same place at the same time. When you are in one place, you are in one time. When you are in another place, you are in another time. The time and the place do not change. 

Imagine you are eating a food. You eat the food. You are in one time. The food is still there. You are not in another time. You are still in the same place. You are in the same place at the same time. This is being in a state of being in one place.
