An idea is a thought that you have. It is a feeling or a plan that is in your mind. You might have an idea about how to build a new kind of car, or how to make food that is better for people. You might have an idea about how to help the world because of a problem. An idea is like a picture in your head.

Sometimes, you might see an idea that others do not see. You might hear a song that you love and think about it for a long time. Or you might see a picture that you love and want to make real. You can have ideas about your life, your work, or your friends.

You can also have ideas about things that are not yet here. You might have an idea about a new kind of food or a new kind of computer. These ideas are for the time when they come.

Ideas can be big or small. You might have an idea that changes your life. You might have an idea about what you want to do with your life. You can always change your ideas as you learn more and hear new things.

An idea is not just something you think. It can also be a way to act. If you have an idea, you might decide to do something. You might decide to work hard, to ask for help, or to try a new thing.
