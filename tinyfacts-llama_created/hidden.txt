To hide something means to keep it from others. When you hide something, you put it away in a room or in a bag. You do not show it to anyone. This makes the thing safe. It is not seen by people.

Imagine a small animal. It wants to hide from the big animals. It runs to a quiet place where it can not be seen. This is hiding. You hide in a dark room when you are afraid. You want to be alone.

If you hide a thing, you might not tell anyone. You keep it in your pocket or under your bed. You might not look at it until later. This can be a good thing. You might feel safe. But if you hide a bad thing, you might feel afraid. You might want to tell others, but you are afraid.

Sometimes, hiding can be hard. You might feel sad or angry. You might wish you could tell others about what is happening. But you are afraid of how they will feel. This is why you hide.
