To cut something means to break it so it can be moved away. Trees are cut by a big thing that moves along a line, and the person who works with the big thing pulls the line and hits the tree. The wood is then taken away and made into many pieces. This helps to give more wood to other places where people need it.

Imagine you have a big box with many small things inside. Each small thing is like a piece of wood that grows from a tree. When you cut a tree, you take out one of those small things so the other small things are left. After many cuts, you have a long line of many small things.

The people who cut trees do this for many reasons. Some want to make more wood for houses. Some want to make paper. Some want to make things for cars. Some people cut trees only because they need to give more wood to other places.

It is very important to be carefully when cutting trees. Trees can fall and hurt people. It is also important to follow the way the land is set up so that people and animals can stay safe.
