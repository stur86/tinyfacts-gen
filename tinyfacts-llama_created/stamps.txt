Stands are things that can hold things. You often put a piece of paper with a picture on it in a box. The box holds the paper. When you want to send a piece of paper to someone, you put it in the box. You give the box to the person. They open it. They read the paper. The paper tells them something.

You can also use stands for people. In a big group of people, there are many stands. Each stand is like a person. They all stand in the same place. If one person does something wrong, the other stands can tell them. The stands help keep everyone safe.

In the past, people made their own stands. You could make one from wood. You would cut the wood. You would put the wood together. You might add a hard cover to make it strong. Or you could buy one. You could buy a stand that already had a hard cover. This makes your stand look nice.

Sometimes, people use stands to hold things they do not want. They might put a small thing in a box. The box is like a stand. The box keeps the thing safe. When you want to get ridden a thing, you put it in the box.

Stands can also be in a game. You play with a box. You put a ball in the box. You try to throw the ball to your friend. If you do not throw the ball, the box might fall. The box is like a stand. It helps you play the game.
