An important group of people makes a set of things that everyone must follow. They are the ones who do the work of a city, a state, or a big part of the world.

Imagine you want to decide what to do with the money you got from your job. You would send your ideas to the people who make the money. These people would then pick which ideas are the best and write them down. They do this for many years so everyone knows what to do.

In a place where people get to decide, the ones who make the money are not the ones who do the work. They are there to listen to what you, the people, want. They write down what you say so everyone knows what you want.

The person who makes the money does not always get to decide on their own. Sometimes, a group of people gets to decide. They work together and pick the best way to live. This is how we stay in one place and follow the same set of things.

The word comes from the word for a group of people who act. In this case, the group acts for everyone. They do not decide on their own, but they help keep everyone in line with what they want.
