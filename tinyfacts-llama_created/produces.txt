To make something is to use your body to change the way things are. You might hit a ball in a game to make it move across a field. Or you might use your hands to cut a piece of wood so it can be used to build a house.

An animal makes food from green things. It goes into the ground and breaks down the green. It then uses the broken down green to make its own food. It eats a lot to make enough food to stay strong. This is how animals are made.

A person makes things by using their hands. They can make a paper bag to carry food or a chair to sit in. They use wood and other stuff they find to make these things. A person can also make money by working hard.

A computer makes things for you to use. You type words and numbers into the computer. It then makes a picture, a word, or a short story. You can ask the computer to do many different things. The computer can work all day and night without needing to rest.

Everything makes something else. If a tree makes food from green, then people make food from the tree. If a person makes clothes, then someone else makes clothes from the same clothes. We are all made from other things that came before us.

To make something is to start a new way of doing things. It is a way of changing what is already there. You make a new way when you use your body to change a thing into a different kind.
