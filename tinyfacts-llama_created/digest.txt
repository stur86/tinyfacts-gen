To eat food is a big part of a day. When you eat, you put the food into your body. This makes your body strong and gives you the power to move around.

Your body is made up of many parts. The mouth is the first part. It takes food into the body. The stomach is next. It breaks down the food. The stomach is like a big room where bad parts of the food go away. After the food goes away, it comes out of the bottom of the body.

The rest of the body has many parts. The small pieces go into the walls of the body and break down more. The walls then carry the broken down food to every part of the body. Some parts take the food and make it into the body. Some parts take the food and make the body move.

Your body uses the food for many reasons. It helps you move through the world. It gives you the power to think. It also gives you the power to feel and to remember things.

Sometimes, you might feel full after you eat. Your body says, enough. You stop eating and you feel happy.

To eat is to put food into your body. You eat many times every day. You eat food from a box, a bag, or a car. You eat food from a tree or a dog. You can even eat the ground under your feet.
