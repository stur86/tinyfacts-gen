The word we use today to talk about people and things often gets a bad start. It is the word we use when we talk about what one thing does to another.

A man is a thing. A person is a thing. A dog is a thing. A book is a thing. If you say a thing is a man, you are not saying it is a person. You are just talking about a thing that is like a man.

The word also talks about what one thing can do to another. If you say a thing is a man, you are saying it is a man. If you say a thing is a man, you are saying the man is one thing. You are not saying the man is many things. You are saying the man is only one man.

In this way, the word helps us understand how we talk about the world. We say what one thing is when we talk about what another thing is doing. We say what one thing can do to another when we talk about how they are working together.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about what is happening. We might say, the sun is a thing. Or we might say, the sun is a man. It is the same thing. The word is about what the thing is and what the thing does.
