To pay money to someone when they do a job is called a pay. If you work at a job with a big group of people, you might be paid one day. You do your job, and the group gives you the money. This money is your pay.

Sometimes, the person who owns the job has to pay money to the person who does the job. This is called a pay. The pay is like a small group of money that the person pays to the person. The pay is for the work that the person does.

Imagine you are a girl who helps a mother with her children. She is very happy. She smiles and says, Thank you. You did not have to do anything. She gave you a small group of money. You did not have to work for this. This is a form of pay.

You can also pay money to a person who is sick. If someone is hurt, the doctor says they will be okay soon. The person who helped them might pay them money. This is a way to help the person feel better.

Sometimes, we pay money to a person for doing a job they did not do. They might do a job and the money does not go to them. They do not get the pay they are supposed to. This is wrong. It is like someone who does not do a job and gets paid.
