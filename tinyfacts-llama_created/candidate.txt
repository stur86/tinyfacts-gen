A person who wants to be the top leader of a group or a city is called a person who runs. They must follow the way the group decides who should lead.

In a school, students who want to be the top leader of the school are called a person who runs. They have to work hard and do well in many different things. They also have to answer questions from their friends and the teacher.

Sometimes, when a person wants to be the leader of a group of people, they are called a person who runs. This is when they go to the next town or city to meet many people and get their voice. They are trying to get many people to follow them.

In a game with many people, a person who runs is the one who is first when the game starts. They have to run and do things fast. This is why they are called a person who runs.
