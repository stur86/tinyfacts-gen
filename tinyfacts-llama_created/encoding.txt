When you talk to someone, you use words. These words are like short lines that tell the other person what you mean. But sometimes you want to send a word to someone else. You might write it down, or you might send it through a box. This is called sending a word.

Think of it like a picture. You draw a picture of a cat. The picture shows what the cat looks like. But if you want to send this picture to a friend, you need to write the words that the picture shows. You write these words as short lines. This is like what an computer does when it sends a word.

There are many different ways to do this. Some computers can hold many words at once. They store them like a huge box full of lines. Other computers only hold one word at a time. They write down each word as a line, so the words are not all together in one place.

When you send a word, you are sending it to the computer. The computer reads the words and uses them to make something new. It can change words into other words. It can change words into numbers. It can use these numbers to tell a computer what to do.

Sometimes, when you send words, you are also sending a bit of the word. The bit is like a tiny piece of the word. The computer looks at these tiny pieces and uses them to make the computer understand what the word means.

In short, when you send a word, you send it and the tiny pieces that make it up. The computer then uses those tiny pieces to make something new.
