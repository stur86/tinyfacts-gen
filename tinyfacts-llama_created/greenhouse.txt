A green house is a big box made of glass. It keeps the air inside warm. People use it to grow things. You can put a bed of green things in it.

Imagine you are in a room with a window. You see the sun, the trees, and the sky. But if you are in a room that is very cold, you get cold. This green house helps people keep warm. It is like a big warm room.

Many cities have green houses to keep their air clean. They put tall trees in front of them. The trees give off good air. This makes the air in the city cleaner. People also put green things on the top of the green house so that the sun does not burn the tops.

You can also build one at home. You put a box of glass in your house and put green things inside. The green things make the air warm. This is a small green house. It is like a tiny warm room.

Sometimes people use green houses to keep things safe. They put them in cold places so the animals stay warm. The green house helps keep people safe too. It is like a big warm room for everyone.
