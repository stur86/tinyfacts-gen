A car that can go very fast is a big thing that people drive. It has a round body that goes around in a big round place on the ground. Inside, there are many moving parts that work together. The car has a top part that goes up high and a bottom part that goes down low. The top part is where the driver sits. The bottom part is where the car goes around the round place.

People make these cars for many reasons. Some make them to help people get from city to city. Others make them to carry people and things for work. Some people also make them to play games where you drive fast and try to stop before other cars. A car like this is very good at going fast and is often called a car.

The word comes from a word that means a group of small people who fight with each other. In this case, the people are people who make cars. They work together to make many cars that go around the big round place many times every day.
