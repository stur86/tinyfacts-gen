At the top of a big room, you can see the blue sky above. The air around you is always moving. You can feel the wind when the door opens or closes. This moving air is what we call the air around us.

The air around the world is very different. In the middle of a big city, the air is very different from what you see outside. The air is thick with tiny bits of stuff that come from the ground and the water. This stuff makes the air smell like a big green food. The air is also cold in the middle of the day and warm at night.

When you are far away from a city, the air looks very different. The air is often very high and very still. The sky is a clear blue, and there are no big green bits of stuff. The air is very quiet and you can see the ground far away.

The air is made up of many different kinds of air. The warm air is light and goes up into the sky. The cold air is heavy and comes down to the ground. The air that is warm and light at the top is the warm air, and the air that is cold and heavy at the bottom is the cold air. All of these kinds of air are moving around each other.

The air is also very important for the life of living things. Most of the water on the ground is in the form of water that is in the air. When you breathe out, you are getting air that has already gone through many people and many animals. The air gives life to everything that grows, and it keeps us all breathing.
