To shoulder something means you hold it on your body. You do this when you carry a bag, a small box, or a large thing on your arm. The word comes from the way a person holds a baby or a child. The baby leans on a person with their shoulder. The person keeps the baby safe while they move around.

You can also use the word when you are strong. If a person is very strong, they can lift a heavy thing without getting tired. This is because their shoulders are big and their arms are strong. If you feel like you are about to fall, you might use this word to mean you are trying to stay safe.

In a different way, to shoulder a problem is to take it. If a person has a big job, they might feel the problem is too much. They might decide to do it by themselves instead of asking for help. This is because they feel the problem is on their chest, like a heavy thing they have to carry.

Sometimes, we use the word to mean to put something on your body. If a person wants to feel cool, they might put a wet clothes over their head. This helps them feel like they are in a cool place. They are using the word to mean they are trying to feel better.
