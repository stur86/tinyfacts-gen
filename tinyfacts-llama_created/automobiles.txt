An animal is a thing that moves on the ground. It has four legs and can go very fast. It uses power from inside it. Some animals fly or live in the water. They are very good at getting from one place to another.

A car is a kind of animal. It is not made of wood or animal skin. It has big round things on top that help it go. It has four strong legs under it, like a horse. The car sits on the ground, but it can go very fast. Some cars can go over  hundreds of feet in one second!

You can drive a car. You put in the power from the car and turn the round thing. The car goes. You can drive it to a store, to school, or to a friend. You use the power to make the car go. When you want to stop, you press a round thing. The car stops.
