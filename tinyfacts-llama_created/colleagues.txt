To have a friend or a person you work with is to have someone who is like you.

When you work together, you do the same things. You all do the same job, and you all want the same thing. You try to do it in the same way, and you all follow the same ways. This makes it easy to talk and understand each other. You can ask each other questions and get the same answers. This is a good thing because it makes your job easier.

People have many of these friends in their lives. There is a group of people who do the same job in a city. These people are called a team. They work together and help each other. They share their ideas and work to make the city better. They are like a big group of friends.

You can have a friend in school too. You sit in the same class with them, and you talk with them during breaks. You talk about your day, what you did, and what you are looking forward to. This is a way to feel close to someone.

Sometimes, people have many friends. They have many friends in the city, and many friends in school. They all know each other and have fun together. They might go to the same movies, watch the same games, and talk about the same things. They are a group of friends.

Having a friend is important. It makes you feel happy and makes you feel like you can do anything. It helps you when you need it.
