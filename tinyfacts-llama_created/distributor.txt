A person or a place that gives things or money to many people. They are like a middle man between the person who makes the things and the people who want them.

Imagine you want to buy a new book. You go to a store and say, I want this book. The store person looks at the book and says, I can give it to you. They take the book out of the store and hand it to you. The store person is the person who gave you the book.

Some people work for a big group of people. They get money from the group. They decide how to give out the money. They might decide which people to give it to, how much money to give each person, and when to give it. This is why they are called a group or a team.

A person who works for a small group of people might have more power than a person who works for a big group. They might decide what to do with the money or which people to help. This can be good if they do things that the big group is not doing. But it can also be bad if they make many bad moves and make many people lose money.

A person who works for a small group can also help them. They can tell them about new ways to do things. They can help them find money. They can help them decide who to help. This is important because it makes sure the small group stays strong and can do things well.
