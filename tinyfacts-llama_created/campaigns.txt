To run a big game is to try and get many people to do a thing. When you run a game for money, you are running it for money. You ask people to help you. You show them a picture of a team. You tell them how to play. You tell them when to do it. You ask them to be here at a time. When you ask people to help, you are running a big game.

You also run a game to try and get people to do something. You are trying to get them to join a group. You show them why you want them to join. You tell them that it will be fun. You show them how you will work together. You tell them what they will do. You ask them to be there.

Some games are about making money. You have to do work to get money. Some games are about playing games. You play with a team. You work together. Some games are about fighting. You fight with another group. Other games are about racing. You run around and try to be the first one to reach a place.

To run a game is to think about what you want to happen. You decide how many people will be there. You decide when the game will start. You decide what to do. You decide how to make the game fun. You plan how to get people to come. You plan how to keep them going. You are the one in the game.
