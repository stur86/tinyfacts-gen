When you cause something happens. You put a thing in the way. You do this so the thing will happen.

Think about a tree. The sun gives the tree light. The tree needs water to grow. If you take the water away, the tree does not grow. The tree is the one that causes the water to go away. The water is the one that causes the tree to grow.

You can also cause things that are not living. If you make a room very hot, your body starts to feel the warm air. Your skin gets red and you feel like you want to move. This happens because your body is trying to cool down. Your body is the one that causes the room to feel warm.

Sometimes, you cause things that are good for you. If you eat food, your body uses the food to make you strong. You are the one that causes your body to use the food.
