To make something new is a very important word. It means to use your mind and your body to change a thing that already is. You can use this word for many different things.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it up in the air. As it goes up, it starts to move. It is moving away from you. Then it goes across the room. It goes under the bed. It goes behind the wall. It stays there for a little while. After that, it comes back to you. It falls down to the floor. You did not make it. You only helped it go where it wanted to go.

You can also make something that is not there. You can make a story. You tell a story to someone. You tell them about what happened. You tell them about the people and the places. You do not start from nothing. You start from what you already know.

You can also make a thing for someone. You make them a new book. You make them a new dress. You make them a new bag. You use your mind and your body to help them. You help them find the things they need to know.

This word is also used when you change your mind. If you think something is not right, you might feel angry. But if you decide to say something else, you are making a new idea. You are changing your mind. You are not the same person anymore.

In the world, people make things all the time. They build houses. They make food for their families. They make clothes for themselves. They make music. They make movies. They make games. They make books. And they make many other things that people need.

So, to make something new is to change something that is already there. You use your mind and your body to change it into something different. You can make a story, a person, a place, or a thing. This word is very important because it helps us understand how we change our lives every day.
