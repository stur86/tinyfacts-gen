To lay something down is to put it on the ground. It often means to make a bed. You lay your bed on the floor so you can sleep at night. You may have a big bed with a soft cover and a white top. You can also lay a table or a chair. When you do this, you are making room for something else.

You can also use this word to talk about something that is in a place. If you lay a book, you put it on the table. This makes the table ready to hold the book.

Sometimes, to lay something down means to let something go. If you have a problem, you might say you are laying it down. This is a way of saying you are not going to fight about it anymore. You are letting it go and moving on.

You can also say that something is laid in a way. If a person is laid in bed, it means they are very tired. If a person has a lot of work, their day might feel very laid. They are not busy at all.

One more way to use this word is in a game. If you are going to play a game, you might lay the pieces down. This means you are ready to start the game and everyone knows what is going to happen next.
