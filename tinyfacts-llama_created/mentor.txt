A person who helps another learn how to do a job, or how to make a hard thing work. You might be a teacher, a parents friend, or a person who works in a big building. They give you ideas and show you how to do things. This helps you get better at what you do and makes you feel more sure.

You can also say a person is a good friend. If you need help with a problem, you might go ask your friend for help. They listen and give you a good answer. This makes you feel safe and helps you feel better.

Sometimes, a person you know is your friend. This is a very good thing. You can talk about anything you want. You learn from each other. They show you that you are not alone in the world.

A person who helps you learn how to drive is a good one too. They tell you how to use the car, how to stay safe, and how to drive well. This is important so you do not get a fine or hurt.

In some places, a person who helps a child learn how to walk is called a parents friend. They stand on the street and say nice things to the child. They help the child feel safe so they can learn to walk without fear.
