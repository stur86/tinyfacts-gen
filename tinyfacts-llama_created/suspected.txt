To think something is true

When you have a thought or a feeling, you might say it is a fact. But sometimes, you are not sure if it is true. You have not seen it with your own eyes. You have heard it from someone else. You think about it and try to figure it out.

If you are not sure, you might say you are not sure. This is a way of keeping things in mind. You do not say you are sure, because you do not know for sure. You might ask questions or look at the thing to see if it is what you think it is.

If you are sure, you say you are sure. You say that what you think is true. This is a way of being strong about what you believe.

Sometimes, you might think you have a wrong idea. You try to find out if you are right. If you find out you are wrong, you might feel sad or angry. This can happen when you are sure about something but find out it is not true.

To think something is true is like being sure about something. It means you have a feeling or a thought that you know is right.
