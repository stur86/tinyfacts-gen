An area where people sit and watch things happen. There are usually many people there. Sometimes the things happening are music, movies, or shows with people on a big box. People might be watching a person talk on a computer, or a person who drives a car very fast. They might also watch a person do something they like, like dance or singling.

The word comes from the old days when people would watch a show with a group of friends. They would sit in a room with many chairs and stand up when the show started. They would watch the people on the box for many hours. Now, people sit in a huge room with many chairs and stand up when the show starts. They might even stand up and shout for their favorite people.
