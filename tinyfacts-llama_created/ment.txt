To mean something is when you tell people you want to do a thing. It is a way to show you want to happen.

Imagine you want to go to a new school. You might say, I want to go. This is a way of saying you want to go. If you say you are going, you are actually going.

You can also mean the person you are talking to. If you say you are going to help someone, you are going to do it. But if you say you want to go, you are not going to help anyone.

Sometimes, you might want to do a thing because a person told you to. This is called a way to act. You are following what someone said.

You can also use this word in a different way. To mean a person is the best one can be is to say they are the best. This is a way to show you love them the most.
