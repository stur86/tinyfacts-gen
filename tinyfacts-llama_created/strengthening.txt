To add power or make something stronger is to make it more able to do work. When you walk, your legs get stronger every day. You might feel a bit tired at first, but then you feel more able and can walk faster.

The word can also mean to make a thing more safe. If you drive a car, the car is becoming stronger. It can carry more people and things. If you wear a hard cover on your feet, your feet become stronger because they are able to hold the cover.

Sometimes, something can become stronger because it is made from good stuff. When you make something from wood, the wood becomes stronger. It can hold more of what you put on it. This happens when the wood gets hard and the tiny living things inside it die and do not grow again.

When we talk about people, we can talk about their power to act. A strong person can do many things. They may be able to fight a fight, do a job, or be the leader of a group. A strong feeling is also an important thing. When you feel strong, you might feel happy, calm, or ready to act.
