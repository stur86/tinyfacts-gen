To keep something means to hold it in your hands or your home. You keep things because they are important to you.

Imagine you have a small bag with a ball inside. You do not want to let the ball go. You keep the bag and the ball so you can stay close to them when you go out. You keep the ball so you can play with it later.

You keep things in your home. You keep books for school. You keep your clothes so you do not lose them. You keep a picture of your family so you remember the good times.

When you keep something, you do not throw it away. You keep it safe and ready for when you need it. This helps you feel calm and strong.

Sometimes, you keep a small thing for a very long time. You might keep money you did not want to spend. You might keep a note from a friend. Keeping something like this can help you remember a moment or a feeling.

Keeping things also means you are always ready. If you have a bag with food, you keep it ready for when you want to eat. If you keep a book, you keep it ready for when you want to read it.
