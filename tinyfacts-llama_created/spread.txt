To send something can mean different things. One way is when you send a dog to a far place. You put the dog in a car with a person and tell them where to go. The dog goes there. When the dog is there, the person gives the dog food and a place to sleep. The dog is not alone. Other dogs in the city see the dog and come to say hello. They play and run with the dog. This is how animals can move from one place to another.

Another way to send is when you send a story. You write the story and tell it to someone. The person reads the story and understands what you are saying. This is how people learn new things. When you send a story, you are sharing your ideas with others.

You can also send a feeling to someone. If you are sad, you might want to send your sad feeling to a friend. You write a short note and give it to your friend. Your friend reads the note and feels sorry for you. This is how you show you care.

When you send a thing, you are giving it to someone else. You might send a book to a child who loves to read. You are sharing a part of something with another person. This is the way we share our ideas and our lives with others.
