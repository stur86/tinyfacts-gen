To have a thing done is very important. If you want something to happen, you usually tell others. You tell them so they know what to do. This is called an annoys. An annoys is when a person gives a word. They say a thing is happening. This word is like a sign. Everyone who has the word knows what has happened.

Sometimes, a person tells many of their friends. If everyone knows, the word is a big one. This can be very important. People often use big words when they are very happy. They say, for sure, or I am sure, or I am going to do this. This word is like a way to show you are sure.

You can also have a thing done by a person. If you want to play a game with a friend, you can say, I want to play. This is an annoys. You are telling them you want to do it. After you say you want to do it, they know what to do. They go and they play. This is a way to give a word.

We can also say that a thing is done. If a doctor says a person is better, then the thing is done. This means the person is now in better hands. They are not alone anymore. The doctor has done the thing and now the person can live.
