When something is very light, it is called a color. A color can be bright and big, like the blue sky on a clear day. It can also be very small and quiet, like a tiny bit of light.

A thing that is not very light is called black. It does not let any light come through. It looks like nothing at all. If you put a bright light next to a black thing, the light will not go through. This is why a black box keeps things inside. You can not see what is inside it.

Animals also have different ways of making their colors. A blue animal might have blue further than a green animal. A green animal might have a green back. This helps them find food or stay safe.

There are also things that are not light at all. You might see a thing that is very far away, but it is not there. It is a thing that is hidden. It is like a very quiet sound. You can not see it, but you can still hear it.

Sometimes we use the word to talk about how old something is. If you look at a picture of your mom when she was a baby, it will look very old. This is because the pictures are not very light, like the color of a bright day. They are like a soft, dark thing that makes you feel old.
