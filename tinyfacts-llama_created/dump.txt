To fill something with something else

This word can mean two different things.

First, you can fill a thing with many things. If you have a big box and you put many books inside it, the box is now full. You have made the box ready for someone to take. If you have a bag of food and you add more food, the bag is now full too.

Second, you can do something to a place. If you have a big problem in your town, you can do something to fix it. You might go to the town hall and talk to the people in the town. You might also go to the police or to a doctor to get help. You are doing something to the place so it will be safe again.

Sometimes, people also use the word to talk about when something is thrown out. If you have a lot of things you do not want anymore, you might throw them away. This is a way to get ridden stuff you do not need anymore.
