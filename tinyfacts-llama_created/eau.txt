Eaten is a word that means to eat. To eat is to take food from a box, a bag, or a table. You eat food that is good for your body. You put the food in your mouth. Your body breaks down the food and makes you strong.

Sometimes, we eat because we are very tired. We eat to fill our body with power. If you do not eat enough, you might feel sick.

You can also eat things that are not food. You might eat a drink that comes from a tree, a drink that comes from a big animal, or a drink that comes from a green thing. These drinks are good for you and help your body work well.

When you eat, your body uses the food to build new things. It takes the food and makes your body parts. It makes your skin, your hair, and your eyes strong. It also makes your body parts work so you can move and do things.

To eat is also to enjoy. You can eat a sweet thing that you like. You can eat a big, round thing that has a hard skin. You can eat a small thing that is good for your body. You can eat with your hands or with a cup.

In some places, people say, you eat because you want to. You eat because you are happy or because you are thinking about something. You eat to feel full.

To eat something is to start the beginning of a story. It is the first step. It is the first word of many words that follow.
