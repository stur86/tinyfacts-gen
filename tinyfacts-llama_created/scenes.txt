To take a moment in a story and show what is happening. This is when a group of people act out what is happening in a room. They might be talking, moving around, and doing things. This helps you see what is going on.

Sometimes a group of people act out a story for the people watching them. They may play a story from a book. They might act out a story they made up. They might act out a story from a movie. They are using their bodies to show what is happening.

In a story, these moments are called a part. There are many parts. Sometimes there are a few parts, and sometimes there are many. The parts can show how people are feeling. They can show who is happy, who is sad, who is angry, or who is afraid.

In a story that is about people, these moments can also tell us about the people. If a person is very happy, we see them smiling and laughing. If a person is sad, we see them looking down or crying. These moments help us understand who the people are.

We also use the word to talk about things outside a story. If you watch a movie, you see the story on the big box. If you watch a story on the small box, you see the story in the moving pictures. The words are like the pictures, and the moving pictures are like the story.
