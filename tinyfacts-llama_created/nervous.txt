To be nervous means you are very afraid. It is when you feel your heart is going fast. You might be afraid of something that is not there. You might feel your hands shake or your legs feel funny.

When you are nervous, your body is like a dog. It is ready to run. It wants to get away from the thing that makes you afraid. Your face might turn red. Your eyes might be wide open. You might speak very fast.

Some people are nervous before a big thing. They might feel like they are going to die. Some people are nervous before they eat food. This is because their body is trying to make sure they are safe.

To be nervous is not always bad. It means you care. If you are nervous, you are showing you are thinking about something important. It is like your brain is saying, I am thinking about this.

Sometimes, you can be nervous for a short time. You might feel a little bit of fear, but then you calm down. You can also feel nervous when you are excited. You are happy and your heart is beating fast.
