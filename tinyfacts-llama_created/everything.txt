Everything is a big group of all things that are in the world. It is not one thing, but many many things all at once.

You can not see everything at once. If you look at one thing, another thing comes into your eyes. Your eyes are looking at many things at the same time. Your brain says, okay, I can see this one thing, but I also see another thing.

Everything is all around you. You breathe in air, you walk on ground, you feel warm sun on your skin. The ground, the air, the water, the sky, and everything in between are all part of everything.

Even the tiny things are part of everything. A tiny piece of rock is part of the rock that makes up the ground. A tiny drop of water is part of the water in the ground. A tiny thing like a tiny animal is part of the world.

Sometimes everything is hard to see. You might not see one thing, but many other things are there too. You might not see the sky, but the ground and the air are still there. You might not see a small thing, but you can still feel it in your body and hear it in your ears.

Because everything is here, we must learn to live with it. We must learn to see the world as one big whole, instead of just one thing after another. We must learn to find the good in every person, every place, and every thing.
