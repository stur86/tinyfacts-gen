To be in a group or to follow a set of things means you do what is asked of you. You might do this because someone told you to or because you want to show you are part of a group. 

If you are in school, you might have to follow the teacher. You do what he says so you can get a good job later. This is an act of doing what is said. If you do not follow, you might get in trouble. 

You can also be in a group of people who share the same idea. If you like the same kind of music, you might join a group of friends. You all do the same things and talk about the same things. You are each in your own way, but together you are a group.

Sometimes, to be in a group means you are happy to be there. You might be in a team with a friend. You all work together to get a job done. You are all doing the same thing, but you are doing it together.

Being in a group is a way to be with others who share the same idea. It is a way to do things together.
