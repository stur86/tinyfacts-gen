A person who is the head of a big family is called a head. They are like the top of the family. They make sure everyone follows their words.

Sometimes, the head of a city is also the head of the big family that lives there. They are the ones who give orders to the people who run the city. They must be very good at making sure the city is safe and happy.

A head of a school is like a teacher. They are in a job that is very important to the school. They tell the students what to do and help them learn. They also help the school people make sure everything is done well.

A head of a team is like a person who leads a group of people. They make sure everyone does their work right. They talk to the people and give orders. They help the team stay strong and work well together.

A head of a house is like a woman who takes care of a family. She makes sure everyone is happy and has enough to eat. She helps her children and her parents and other family people. She is like the top of the family.

A head of a group of animals is like a big animal that is the leader. They do what they think is best for the group. They keep everyone safe and make sure they stay together.

All these people are called heads because they are the ones who make sure everything is good. They are the top of the people, the family, the city, the school, the team, the house, and the group.
