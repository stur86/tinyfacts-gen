To finish something is to complete it. Think about a story. You are the one in the middle. You have to do what the other people did before you. If you do not finish, the story is not done.

When you finish a thing, you feel good. You have done something important. You did not leave anything behind. You have taken care of the problem until you can fix it later.

In life, you also finish things. You finish a book. You finish a game. You finish a job. You finish a day. You finish a week. This means you are done for the day.

If you start something but you do not finish it, it is like you left it in the middle. It is still there. You can go back and do it later, but you do not feel the same as when you finished it.

Sometimes, you finish something that is hard. You have to work very hard. You have to be very strong. You have to be very quiet. When you finish something like this, you feel a big feeling of power. You have done something that shows you can do it.
