A must is a hard green thing from the ground. It has four legs and a hard top. It is very strong and can run very fast. People keep these animals because they can help with work.

In some places, these animals are also called horses. They are very tall. A very old horse was the biggest one in the world. It was so big that it took a long time to carry it. Today, people still keep these animals, but they are also often seen in movies where they are not really there.

In the old days, people would ride these animals to find food. If you saw a must, you would run away. The must can be very loud, like a big animal that says, very loud. But they are also very good to eat, so people keep them.

Sometimes people call these animals by other names. You can also call them a horse, a great horse, or a great horse. Some people even call them a rock.
