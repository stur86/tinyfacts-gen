A group of people who want to make sure their town or city is run by them, rather than by a person who is in a high job. They try to decide how things are done and who gets to be in power.

Imagine you are in a group of kids in a school. Some kids want to be the ones who decide what games to play and what colors to wear. They might be very strong about it and make sure everyone follows what they say. This is a kind of power for a small group of people.

Other kids might want to decide for themselves. They might be very happy and say, sure, we will decide how to run the school! But this power is harder to find because there are many kids and not everyone agrees.

Sometimes, these small groups can be very good at making sure everyone is safe and happy. They might decide to build new roads or pay for the police so they can be there to help if someone is hurt. But they can also cause trouble if they do not listen to what other people think.

In a city like a big town or a state, these small groups are called a group of people. In a small town, they might be called a town hall. They use money they get from the town to do things for the people.
