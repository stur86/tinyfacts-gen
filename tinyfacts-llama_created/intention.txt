An idea is a picture in your head about what you want. It is a plan for how you will act. It is something you think about and want to happen.

When you have an idea, you are not just thinking. You are also doing the things you plan. This is called acting. You are moving your body and using your words to do the things you want.

Think about a time when you wanted to be the best. You wanted to run fast or hit a ball. Your idea was to be the best. You wanted to do these things because you thought you could be better than others.

Your idea is important. It tells you what to do. It helps you stay focused on what you want to become. If you do not have an idea, you might not know what to do or how to act.

An idea can be big or small. You might have an idea to learn more about a book or a game. You might have an idea to help your friends. An idea is a way to show you care about what is happening.

Sometimes, you might not like what you are doing. You might feel angry or sad. In this case, your idea might be broken. Your idea was trying to help you feel better. It was a plan to make things right.

But most of the time, your idea stays with you. You keep thinking about it and trying to make it happen. It is a way to stay focused on what you want to become or what you want to happen in your life.
