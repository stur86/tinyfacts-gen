To fly on a big thing that can go very high and fast is to fly.

This thing is usually round or has many sides. It has big parts on top and bottom that help it go through the air. The people who fly it sit inside, and they use a seat to sit in and hold on to things so they do not fall.

The flying thing goes very fast. It can go so fast that the wind goes around the car over and over many times. People sit in the front or the back. Some people sit in the front to see where they are going. Other people sit behind the front so the person in front can not see what they do.

When the flying thing is ready to go, the people who run it give a word. The thing starts to move. It goes up and over the ground. It then goes down and then goes up again. This keeps happening many times. The flying thing can go very high, over the big wide water or over the wide open ground.

Some flying things are very small. They fly very fast and go very high. People can sit in the back and see where they are going. Other flying things are much bigger. They can carry many people and lots of things. They can fly to places that are far away.

Flying is a fun way to go. People use this flying thing to get from one place to another. Some flying things go to places that are very far away. Some go to places in the middle ground. Some go to places near the big wide water.

Flying is also a way to go to school. Some kids go to school in flying things. They fly over the city to get to the school. The flying thing helps them not drive a car to school.

We need flying things so we can get from one place to another quickly. They help us move around the world. They are an important way to go.
