To make something is to take the stuff that grows and put it into a box. When you go to a place where animals go to eat, they walk to a green area with many tall green things. They eat the green things to have food. When they eat, they make their bodies bigger. This makes them strong so they can run, jump, and fight.

Animals have a thing in their mouths. This thing is used to cut the green things. It makes a small piece of green so the animal can eat. The animal also uses this thing to drink the green thing down.

Sometimes, people make green things too. They cut the green things and put them into a box. They put water and other stuff in the box. They let the water go out while they wait. After some time, the water goes away and the green stuff comes out as a soft, brown, white, or green thing. This thing is food for people too. People eat it to feel full and to give them the power to stay going while they work or play.

You also need to give animals food to keep them strong. If you do not give them food, they will die. This is why it is so important to take care of the green things and the animals that eat them.
