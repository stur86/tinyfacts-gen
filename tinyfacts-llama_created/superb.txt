To say something is a thing you love is to use this word. It is about how much you like a person, a place, a food, or a game.

If you love a friend, you want them to be happy. You do things for them and they make you feel good. You are a good friend because you care about them. You can say,  This is a beautiful day. It means you are very happy.

If you love a picture, you look at it often. You feel a good feeling inside when you see it. You might even laugh. You like the colors and the way it looks.

If you love a food, you want to eat it all. You do not care if it is big or small. You just want it. If you love a song, you listen to it many times. You feel happy when the music is playing.

You can say this in many ways. You can say, The sun is a big thing in the sky. Or, The music is a thing you love to hear. These words show you care about the thing.
