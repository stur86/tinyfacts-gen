To hope means you have a feeling that something good is going to happen. It is when you are not sure if it will really happen, but you still want it to happen. You might hope for a better day, a better life, or a better time.

You can hope for many things. You might hope to get a job that pays well. You might hope to find a good home where you can live with your family. You might hope to go to a far away place and see a beautiful place you have never seen before.

Hopes are like a strong feeling inside you. They make you want to act in a way that is good for you and for others. They help you stay calm when things are not going well.

Sometimes, you hope for something to happen in the near past. You might hope to see a friend soon or to hear from a family you love. Other times, you hope for something that will happen very far in the past. You might hope to remember a happy time from your life in the years to come.

You can also hope for things that are not real yet. You might hope to learn something new tomorrow or to find a beautiful thing you have never seen before. These hopes are like a bright light that fills your heart and mind.

It is very important to hope. It helps you stay strong and keep moving forward. When you have hopes, you feel like you can do anything.
