A world that is all one is a very big idea. It is like when you are very close to a person and you feel like everything is in your head. You can not see the person, but you know they are there. The world is like this too. Everything that happens in one place is part of the world.

Think about the sky. The sky is the world. It is where the world starts and where it ends. It is where you, your family, your friends, and all the people in the world go to sleep at night. It is where you see the sun, the stars, and the big bright pieces of rock that go around the world.

The world is also where you buy things. If you buy a book, it is part of the world. If you buy food, it is part of the world. If you buy a car, it is part of the world. All of these things are part of the world.

The world is also where you learn. When you learn something new, you are learning about the world. You are learning about how things work, how to do things, and how to be a person. This is part of the world too.

And then there is the world we do not see. The world is the one under the ground, under the water, and under the air. It is the world of the tiny living things and the tiny things that go round and round in the water. It is the world that makes food and keeps us going. It is the world that gives us the bright colors of the sky and the warm light of the sun.

So the world is everything that is big and everything that is small. It is the one place where you are, the one place where you are not, and the one place that is always there, even when you are not looking at it.
