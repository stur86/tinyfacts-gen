The word can mean different things.

One way it means going forward. Imagine you are on a long road. You start at one end and walk to the other end. You have already been at one part of the road for a while. Now you are at the other end. You have not yet reached the farthest part, but you are getting closer.

Another way it means doing things that help people. If you are a doctor, you are doing things that help the body. You are making people feel better when they are sick or hurt.

It can also mean things that happen over time. If a new car comes out, many people want to buy it because it is a good idea. The car is going to change the way people live. It will make life easier and more fun. This is a big change.

Sometimes, the word is used to talk about how things grow. When a tree starts small, it grows into a tall tree with many big leaves. It is getting bigger every day. This is a kind of change that happens over a long time.

In some places, people use this word to talk about a leader. If a leader says they will change the way things are done, they are being a bit too quick. They might make things worse. It is like trying to change a car in one day. It is much better to do it slowly over many days or weeks.
