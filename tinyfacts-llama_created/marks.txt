To mark something means to put a big mark on it. This can happen in many ways.

Imagine you are drawing with a big stick on a piece of paper. You are making a picture. You put a mark in the middle of the picture. This mark is like a small line that shows where you put your line.

You can also mark something when you tell a story. You might say, you did this, or you did that. The words you use are like marks on a line. These marks help people remember what happened.

Some things are marked so they can be found later. A book with many stories might have a mark at the beginning. This mark lets you know where to start reading. It is like a name on a person.

Sometimes, we mark things so we can remember them. We put a mark on a door in a school. This mark tells a student which room to go to when they need to use the bathroom.

We can also mark things when we make them better. If you are playing a game, you might put a mark on your name. This is like a big number that says you were the one who did the best.
