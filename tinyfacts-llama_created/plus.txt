To add something to another is to make a bigger number. 

Imagine you have five small pieces of wood. If you add one more small piece of wood to the top, you now have six small pieces of wood. 

You can also add numbers together. If you have one hundred and one, and your friend has one hundred and two, you can add those numbers together. The answer is two hundred and three. 

When you add, you are making the number bigger. It helps us tell how many things are in a group. It is a very important word in our work and our play.
