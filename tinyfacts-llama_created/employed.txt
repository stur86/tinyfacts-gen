To be used means a thing that you need or a job that you do. When you are a part of a group, you are often called to do a job. If you are not working, you are not used.

Imagine you have a big box where you keep things. You take the box out and use it to carry things around. The box is not just a thing. It is a part of a team. Without it, the team would not be able to do its job.

If you do not work, you are not used. You are not doing a job. You are just sitting around doing nothing.

You can also use the word to talk about your life. If you are in school, you are using your time. You are doing a job for your family so you can have money and learn how to become a good person.

Sometimes, you might be told you are not needed anymore. This means you are no longer doing your job. This can be sad because you liked being a part of the group. But you can still be used by other groups. You might find a new job or start a new life where you are needed again.
