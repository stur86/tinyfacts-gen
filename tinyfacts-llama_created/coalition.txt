A group of people who work together is called a group. A group can be made of many different kinds of people. Some groups are made of friends from the same town. Other groups are made of people who have the same job. People can also form a group of friends who like the same music. This group of people does not have a leader. They all work together to do what is best for everyone.

Sometimes, a group of people who do not agree can come together. They may have different ideas about how to do things. They may talk and listen to each other. After they talk, they may decide to work together. This can be very strong. They can help each other and make sure everyone is safe. Sometimes, a group of people who do not agree can even become a new group. This can happen when many people say the same thing and want to change it.

A group can also be made of many people who are all very strong together. This is like a team. They use their power to help each other. They work well and do great things as a group. In a group, everyone is important. No one is better than everyone else. They all have to listen to each other and follow the same way.
