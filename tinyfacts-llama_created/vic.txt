To be a good friend is to care about others. It means to be kind and help them when they need it. You might be a good friend to a family person or a person you know at school. You might talk to them or give them a small thing they like.

To be a good friend is to show that you trust them. If you tell a friend that you trust them, it means you believe they will do the right thing. This makes the other person feel safe and happy.

Sometimes, being a good friend is about being there for them when they are sad. You might listen to what they say. You might give them a hug or a cup of coffee. This is a way to show that you care.

Being a good friend can also mean to be a good person. This means you act in a way that is nice to everyone. It is about being kind and not doing things that could hurt other people.

If you are a good friend, you might feel happy when your friend is happy. You might feel a little bit of a warm feeling inside when your friend does something nice for you. This is because you know you are important to them.

To be a good friend is a big job. You need to be kind, trust your friends, and show that you care. It is a way to show that you love them and want them to be happy.
