To deal with something means to handle it. You might have a problem at home and you go to the store. You buy a drink and a book. This is a deal. You are doing something to help you. You are not afraid because you have a plan.

In a different way, when you deal with your money, you are also doing this. You buy something you want. You pay for it with your money. You are using your money to get what you need.

Sometimes you can deal with a person. You say what you think. You tell them how you feel. You might be angry or sad. They listen to you. They might say they are sorry. You are working together to find a way.

Dealing with a problem is important. It keeps you moving forward. When you are ready to give up, you might need to start again.
