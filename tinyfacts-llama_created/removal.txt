To take away something means to get ridden of it. You might see a big dog in a park. If someone wants to play with the dog, they can pick the dog up and carry it away to a different place. The dog is not in the same spot anymore.

This can happen with many things. You might want a book you read. If you do not want the book anymore, you can ask a friend to take it for you. Or you might get a job that you do not like. You can ask a person to take the job away from you so you can do something else.

It is also about people. If a person does not want to be with you, you can ask them to leave. You take away their right to be there. This is a very strong way to end a thing.

Sometimes, you can take away something that hurts you. If you are very angry, you might shout at someone. This can be a way to make them stop. But it can also cause a fight. You take away their right to be happy or to stay calm.

You can also take away something from a place. If you go to a new city and do not like it, you might decide to move away. You take away your old life and start a new one.
