The word loses can mean different things.

One way it means to give up something you want. If you have a big bag of food, but you do not want to eat it anymore, you are losing it. You are giving it away.

Another way is when you run away from a place. If you are in a room and you see a dog that looks like your friend, you might want to hide. This is a losing. You are losing your fear.

Sometimes, losing also means you forget something. If you have a note with your name on it, and you look for it, but it is not there, you are losing the note.

Also, if you have money and you spend it all, you are losing it. You did not have it anymore. You lost it.

It can also mean you are the last one left. If you are the only person in a big room, and everyone else has gone, you are losing. You are the one who is alone.
