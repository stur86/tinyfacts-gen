To be wide means you are very big. Imagine a long street. You walk on that street, and you are not close to anything. You are in the middle of it. If you walk to the other end, you are very far from the middle. You have moved very far away from the middle of the street.

A wide thing is something that is not small. It is a big round thing that you can see from far away. You can see it from the road or from the top of a building. It is a thing that is not tiny. It is a thing that has many sides.

You can also say something is wide because it is very strong. A wide door is one that is very big and heavy. You can open it, but it is hard to go through it. You have to push it very hard. This is because it has a lot of power. You do not need to push it very hard to open it.

Sometimes, to say something is wide, you have to use the word big. A wide tree is a big tree. A wide house is a big house. A wide story is a long story. These words tell you that the thing is very big.
