Following is when you do what someone else is doing. You follow another person, a dog, or a car. You walk behind them so you do not get in the way.

When you follow, you listen. You hear what the person is saying and you do the same thing. This helps the person stay safe. If a dog follows you, you are not going to run away from the dog. You are going to stay in a straight line and follow the dog.

Sometimes, you follow a book or a song. You listen to the words and you think about what is happening. You might feel happy or sad. Following is about being part of a group and doing what is right.

You can also follow a game. If you are playing a game with a friend, you might want to keep up with them. You watch them and you try to do the same thing. If you are following a story, you might want to see what happens next.

Following can be important for people who work. If you work with a team, you might follow the leader. You listen to what they say and you do what they tell you to do. This helps the team work well together.

In life, following can mean following your heart. If you love a person, you might want to follow them. You might want to do what they say and follow them into a new place. Following your heart means you are following what is right for you.
