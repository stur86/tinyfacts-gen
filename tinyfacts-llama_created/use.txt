To use something means to make it work for you. You are using a thing when you use it. You do not just look at it. You do it with your hands. You put your body into it.

Imagine you have a box. You open the box. Inside the box you have food. You take the food out and you eat it. You are using the box. You are using it to give you something you need.

If you use a word, you are telling the people around you what you mean. If you say, you are angry, you are using the word. You are making it mean that you are very mad.

You can also use something to do something else. If you use a car, you are using it to go from one place to another. You are using it to get from your home to the school.

Sometimes you use a word when you are not sure if it is right. You might use a word because it is easy. You might not really think about the meaning of the word. You just use it because it is in your brain.

If you use a word to mean something different, you have made a wrong word. You might have used a word that means something you did not mean. This is a bad thing to do.

To use something also means to learn it. If you learn how to drive a car, you are using it. You are using the car to get from one place to another. You are learning how to do it.
