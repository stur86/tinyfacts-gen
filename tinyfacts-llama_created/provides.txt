To give something means you give it to someone. This can be a person, a thing, or even your own body. You give it so they do not have to have it by themselves.

Think about a child. If you have a book and the child wants to read it, you give it to them. This shows you care about them and want them to be happy. You are helping them learn how to read.

You can also give something to someone who is very sick. If a person is in a lot of pain and they do not feel well, a doctor might give them a drink that makes them feel better. This is because the drink helps the body fight the bad thing that is making them sick.

Sometimes, you give something to a place. If you want a new car, you buy it. This is when you pay money for the car. You did not have the money before. Now you have it and you can use it.

You can also give something to a group of people who work together. If many people work at a big office, they all give their time to help each other. This is how they keep the office running well.

Giving is about sharing your time or things with others. It is a way to show you care about them and want them to be happy.
