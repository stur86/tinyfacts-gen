To promise is to show you will do something. You say, you will be there. You say you will not leave your house. You do not have to do it right away. You can do it later, and you still say the same thing.

When you say you will do something, you are making a promise. This helps people know that you mean it. If you say you will not do something, people might not know if you really mean it.

You can promise with a word, with a picture, or with a thing. If you promise a food, you say you will give it to the person. If you promise a thing, you say you will not break it.

A person who is sure can promise. They do not ask if you will keep your word. They just say, here is what I promise. This helps you know if the person is true to their word.

Sometimes you promise something that is not possible. You say you will run a long way if you are tired. But you might not be able to do it. You have to understand that the promise is not real.

You can also promise a safe place. If you promise your home is safe, you do not have to worry about it. You know that the door is locked and there are no bad people around.

To promise is to show you are sure. It helps people feel safe and happy.
