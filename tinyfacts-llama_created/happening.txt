Something is happening when things are moving around and people are doing things. It is the day you are living. You are here and the world is around you. You see people walk past you, the sun is up, and the air is moving. You hear music from a car or a house. You feel the ground under your feet. Everything is moving and people are doing things. This is what is happening right now.

Sometimes, something happens to one person. You get a job and you work every day. You do your work and you pay money. You might feel happy because you are doing something important. Other times, something happens to a group of people. A big fight happens in a city. Many people are scared and they want to get away. They run to a different place.

Sometimes, something happens that changes the world. A new way of doing things is started. A new word is used instead of an old word. New ways make life easier for people. New ways can help us feel better when we are sad or when we are afraid. Sometimes, something happens that makes us feel bad. We might cry or feel sad. This is when we are feeling the happening.

We also talk about what is happening at a time and place. If you are at a party, something is happening. You are laughing with your friends. You are having fun. You do not know what will happen next, but you are enjoying the moment. If you are going to a movie, something is happening. The lights are going up. The music starts. People are getting ready to go inside.
