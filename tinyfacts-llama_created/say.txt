To say something means you are telling others what you think. You are using your voice to tell them a fact, a story, or a truth. When you say something, you are making a sound with your mouth and your body.

Think about it this way: If you want to show someone that you are happy, you might say,  I am happy! This is a way of telling them that you are feeling good. You are using words to tell them how you feel.

Sometimes, you might also say, I am tired. This is saying how you are feeling. You are using words to tell them how you are doing.

You can say many things: you can say, I like this, or I hate this, or you can say, This is a nice day. The words tell the story or give the truth about what happened.

You can also say, I am sorry. This is when you tell someone you are sorry for what happened. You are using words to make them feel better after something went wrong.

When you say something, you are sharing your idea or your feeling with others. This helps us understand one another.
