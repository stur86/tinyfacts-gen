An important job is to check how a group of people or a big business does things. They look at papers, talk to people who work for them, and check if they follow the right ways to act.

Imagine you have a small store. You buy things and give them to your friends. One day, you do not give the money back. You take the money from the store and keep it for yourself. This is not right. You need to let the store know so it can fix the problem.

An actor does this job. They act out a story in a movie or a play. They make sure everything is real and follows the story. They do not change anything in the story. They are checking if the story is true.

An important person who does this job is a manager. They help people who work for them make sure they do their jobs well. They talk with them, write papers, and make sure the work is done right.

When a person does not follow the right ways, the people who do the job can tell them. The manager can say, you are not doing your job right. You need to fix it.

This job is very important. It helps keep people and big groups of money safe. If something is not right, the job can stop and people can be helped.
