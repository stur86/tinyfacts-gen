To start a show is to begin a game. You might have a person who is in a room. They tell a story. Or you might have many people who are not in the room. They talk to many people at the same time. This way, everyone can hear.

Sometimes, a person is in a room but they are not in the room. They are far away. They send the show to many places. This is like sending a big piece of paper. Everyone gets the paper at the same time.

You can also use this word for music. When a group of people play music, they are sending a show to everyone in the room. The music is for everyone to hear. Some people listen to the show in their homes. Others listen on the road. Everyone can hear the music, even if they are far away.

A long time ago, people used to send shows through the air. They would send the show over the sky using big moving pieces that could carry the sound. Today, people use computers and the air to send shows. This is much faster and more easy than using the sky.

Sometimes, people send shows to other cities. They send the show through the air. People in the city might watch the show later. They do not have to be there right away. This way, people can watch the show at any time.
