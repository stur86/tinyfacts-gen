To book something means to put it in a box or a bag. You might put books, clothes, or food inside a box to keep them safe. Sometimes you put things to sleep in a box so you do not wake up when it is dark.

When you want to go to a far place, you might use a big water thing. The water thing has a strong part that goes into the water. The person in the water thing goes very fast to get to the other side of the water. This is like going on a big trip.

You can also say you are booking a game. This means you are playing a game where you hit a ball with a long stick. The game is fun and you get to run around with your friends.

Another way to use the word is when you are booking a thing. If you have a booked dog, it means your dog is very happy. It might jump and run around a lot. If you booked a book, you are reading it and you feel very happy.
