To track something means to follow it. Think about your friend running in a park. You want to know where they are. You follow them. You watch their feet. You see where they go. If they run around a big round thing, you might know where they are.

You can track things that are far away. You might track the sound of a dog if you hear it. Or you can track a person. If you know how they move, you can find them. This is how a police person tracks a person who did a bad thing.

You can also track things that are moving. You might track a car. You watch how it goes. You follow the road it is on. You see how many people are following it. If the car is going fast, you need to follow it more carefully.

Sometimes, people use a thing that helps them track. If you are lost in a big city, you can use a small thing that is like a computer. You put the city into it. It tells you where the city is. This is very help.

In some places, people use this word with animals. They track animals so they know where they live. They follow the animal to see where it goes at night. This helps us understand how animals live and how they move.
