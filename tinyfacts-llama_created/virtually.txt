To almost everything is almost. Something that is almost there is almost what it should be. It is not quite there, but it is close. To almost is to get very close to the real thing.

If you see a picture of a tree, you almost see the real tree. You can see the top of the tree and the bottom. You can see the green parts and the brown parts. You almost know what it looks like.

But you can't see the middle part. You can't see the round parts of the tree. You can only see the parts that are right in front of you.

To almost is a way to say that something is almost what it should be. It is not a thing in its whole form, but it is close enough to be real.
