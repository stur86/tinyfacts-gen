That is a word that you use when you point to something.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it to a friend. Your friend catches it. You want to know where the ball was. You look around. Your friend says, I am standing right next to the ball. This is a way to tell where something is. You did not look at their face. You just looked at their body.

You can also use it to talk about time. If you are walking to a place, and you see a house, you might say, I am going that way. You are on the road. If you are in a room, and you want to go to another room, you say, I am going that way.

Sometimes, you use it to ask a question. You might say, Where is the door? The answer is, that is where the door is. The door is there.

You can also use it to tell what happened. If you see a dog, and you hear a dog, you might say, That dog is here. You saw the dog.

The word is very important. It helps us understand and talk about the world around us.
