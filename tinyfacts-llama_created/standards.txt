Stand means to be ready to help someone. You are ready when you are waiting to do something important for a friend, a teacher, or a family. You might stand by a door to let people in. You might stand at a table to share food with others. You stand up when you say you are ready.

Stand can also mean to show you are right. When you are standing, you are standing tall and not moving. You are not afraid. You are the one who is right. You show the world that you are the best person in a group.

Sometimes, people stand for a job. A teacher stands in front of a class to tell them what they need to do. A police person stands at a corner to keep people safe. They stand to show that they are there to help.

Stand can also mean to stand for a thing. If you stand for a game, you are ready to play. If you stand for a word, you are ready to say it. If you stand for a feeling, you are ready to show it.

Stand is a word that means you are ready to help. It is about being strong and ready to act.
