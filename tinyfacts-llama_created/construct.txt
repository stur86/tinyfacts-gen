To build something means to make it from small pieces. You start with nothing. Then you use your hands to put the pieces together. You put one piece next to another. Next, you put another piece next to the first one. This keeps going until the whole thing is made. Sometimes you use a hard thing to hold the pieces together. Other times you use your own hands. You make a house, a car, a tree, or a chair. You use your mind to plan what you want to make.

You can also build a story. You start with a beginning. Then you add more pieces to tell what happened next. You might add names, places, and things that happened. You keep adding until the story ends. This is how we learn from the people around us.
