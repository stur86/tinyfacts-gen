To change something means to make it look better. Imagine you have a picture of a friend. You like the picture, so you decide to change it. You might change the color of the hair or the clothes. You are changing the picture to make it look like a new picture.

Sometimes, you change words in a story. You might change a word that means a bad thing to a word that means a good thing. This is an important job because it helps people understand what is right and wrong.

You can also change words in your own life. If you want to learn a new word, you might look it up and read it. You can use a computer to find the word. You might watch a movie to see how people use the word. This helps you remember how to use it.

Sometimes, you change things that are already there. If a story has a small problem, you might change it so that the problem is not as big. You are making the story better.

To write is to change words into new ones. You can write a story, a letter, or even a sign. You are using words to tell a story or to make people understand your point.
