A water way is a long piece of ground that has a clear line running through it and across the ground. The water goes over the land and under the ground. There are many of these water ways in the world. Some run into the wide water and join with other water ways to make one very big water way.

Some water ways go under the ground and stay hidden for a very long time. When the water from one water way meets the water from another, the water comes together and makes new water. This new water can fill a big area or go through a small area and fill the space in between the two land pieces.

Riding a water way is to walk on top of the water. People do this when they are not in a car or bus. They wear clothes that keep them dry and walk around the water way, often going many times over the water. Some people also work on the water ways and make things for the water so it can move more easily and stay clear.

Riding a water way can be very hard and make people tired. The water can be very cold and the water can be very strong. People must watch out for rocks and big pieces of wood that can fall from the land.
