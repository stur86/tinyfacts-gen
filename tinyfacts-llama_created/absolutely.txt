When you say something is true, but it is not, it is not the right word. We use this word to show that something is not the way it seems. If you tell me you are going to a store and you are actually going to the kitchen, you are being not true. You have not done what you said.

The word is like a big stop. It means you have stopped. If you say something is true, but then you show that it is not, you have stopped the truth. You are stopping the truth.

Sometimes we use this word with people. If a friend says they will be late, but they really are early, we say they are not. They have stopped being late. They have stopped telling the truth.

We also use it with things. If you say a thing is the best, but it is not, you are not being. You have stopped saying it is the best.

This word is important because it helps us know when we have stopped. It helps us know when to stop and when to keep going.
