When you agree on something, you both think the same way. You might have a different idea, but you both want the same thing to happen.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You all want to go to the movies. You both say you want to go. You all agree. You all have the same idea, and you all feel happy.

Sometimes, you might agree to do something. If you want to eat dinner, you might agree to eat at a table. You both agree to sit down and eat. You are both happy because you are doing the same thing.

You can also agree to do something hard. If you want to learn a new word, you might agree to watch a few times. You both want to learn the word, and you both feel like you are doing the same thing.

When you agree, you show that you trust each other. If you do not agree, you can have a fight. You are both saying, and your words are not the same.

So, to agree is to say you are on the same side. You are both thinking the same way. You are both happy because you are doing the same thing.
