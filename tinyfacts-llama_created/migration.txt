To go somewhere and live in a new place is called a big move. Sometimes people move because they want a better life. They leave their old home, school, or job and go to a new town or city. They look for work, a better school for their children, or a place with cold water so they do not get sick.

Another time, animals go from one place to another. They move to find food or water. A group of baby animals may leave their mother and go to a new home with a new mother. A big group of animals might move through the water to find a new place with more food.

People also move because of work. If a job is in a far city and the job is important, people might move there. A doctor might move to a city with many hospitals. An actor might go to another city to act.

Sometimes families move. A father might leave his old home to find a job that pays more money. A mother might go to a new home with her children to have more money to buy food and clothes.

Moving is important because it changes your life. You learn new places and people. You see new things every day. You might even meet new friends. You might feel happy or sad, but you are always moving forward.
