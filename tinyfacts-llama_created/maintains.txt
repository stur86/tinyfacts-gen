To keep something going is to help it stay in a good state. You might think of your body when you say this. Your heart beats every day, and your brain works. This keeps you safe and strong.

If you do not keep your body in order, you will feel very bad. Your body might get very hot, or you might feel very cold. You might also feel tired or sick. If you do not eat food, your body will not have enough power to move. You might feel very full and heavy.

You can also keep things in a good state. If you keep a book, it stays in your home so you can read it later. If you keep money, you have money to buy things you want. If you keep a friend, you can stay in touch with them and feel safe.

To keep something is to keep it from going wrong. If you do not keep your car clean, it will get very old and not run well. If you do not keep your money in a safe place, it will be taken away from you. This helps us stay safe and happy.
