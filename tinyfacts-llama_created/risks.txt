To take a chance means to be ready to do something that might not happen easily. If you want to go to a new city, you are taking a chance. You are not sure if the city will be okay, but you are ready to go.

You might take a chance if you are very young. You might go to a big city to see how it looks. You might try a new food that is not in your home. You are taking a chance to learn something new and have fun.

Sometimes, you take a chance if you are afraid. You might walk across a wide road that is very high up. You are not sure if you will be safe, but you are ready to do it.

There is also a word for when you do not think you can do something. You might say, I am not sure if I can do this. But then you do it anyway. You showed that you are ready to take a chance.

You can also say that something is a big chance. If you have a very high chance of doing something, it means you are very sure you will do it. It is like a big sign that says, do this!
