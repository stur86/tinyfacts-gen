When you say something through, you mean you use your voice or your words to tell others what you think or feel. This is how you talk to your family, your friends, or people you do not know.

Imagine you want to tell your mom something. You might say,  I love you, I am happy, or I am afraid. This is through. You are using your words to show your mom how you feel.

If you walk through a room, you go from one end to the other without stopping. You do not look at one thing while you are looking at another. You are moving from one place to another. This is what happens when you move through a place.

Sometimes, to walk through a hard thing means you are going through it. You might walk on a hard road, a hard floor, or a hard wall. This is when you are using your body to move through the hard thing. You might feel pain, but you keep going because you want to reach the other side.

In a game, you might walk through a line. If you cross the line, you are done and you get a point. This is a way to move your body through the game so you can finish.

To go through a story means you read it. You move from one story to another. You follow the people, the places, and the things. This is how we learn about the world and how people live.

In a story, you can go through a room, a city, or even a far away place. You might go to a house, a school, or a hospital. The story takes you on a ride through the world, and you learn about what happens to the people in that world.
