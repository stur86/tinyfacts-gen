The word for a small piece of land is called a covering. It is a place with a low wall around it. Water goes in and out of it many times. This helps keep the water clean and the land safe.

Some places that are part of a bigger land are called small lands. A small land is a tiny piece of land. It is very close to a larger piece of land. People often move to a small land because it is small and easy to walk around.

The word for a small piece of land can also mean a small area where a house or a building stands. A small land might be a small area of ground where a person lives. It is a tiny part of a bigger land.

We also use the word to talk about a place with many small lands. This is a place with many tiny pieces of land. Each tiny land is small and has its own water and land. People live in these tiny lands and work them.
