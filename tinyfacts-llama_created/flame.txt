A fire is a thing that makes a lot of light and a lot of warm air. It is a bright, hot thing from the ground. It starts from small parts of wood or other things. When the small parts burn, they make hot air and tiny bits of wood go up. This air goes round and round, and the hot air goes up too. The tiny bits of wood go up so fast that they go far into the air. This makes the air look white and gives the fire its bright look.

When you make a fire, you use wood or other things to start it. You put them in a box. You cover the box with a hard thing to keep the air out. When you turn on a small box that makes hot air, the wood burns and the hot air comes out. The hot air goes up and makes the air look white. The white looks nice and makes the fire look like a tall, bright thing in the dark.

A fire can be very strong. It can make a house feel very hot. People put things in the fire to make food hot. They can also make it to look pretty. Some people make them look like animals or things from stories. When a fire is very big, it can make a lot of noise. It can shake the ground and make the air feel strange.

A fire can also be very bad. It can catch things that are not wood. It can make the air look dark and make people feel sick. It can make the house feel hot and heavy. In some places, people have to get out of the house and run to a safe place. They do this because the fire is going to be too strong to stop.

Even though fires are strong, they are also a part of how things grow. Trees need fire to grow. Animals need fire to eat. People need fire to make food. So, we have to be carefully around fires so they do not get out of control and hurt us.
