To watch something means you are looking at it. You might watch a moving picture on a box. You might watch a game with your friends. You are not doing anything else. You are just looking at the thing in front of you.

Imagine you are sitting in a room with a person. You are watching them talk. You do not do anything. You just sit and look at them. They are the one doing something. You are just a part of what they are doing.

Sometimes, you watch something because you are interested. You want to know what is happening. You might watch a show with a story. You are not just looking at the pictures. You are paying attention to what is happening in the story.

To watch a person can be hard. You have to be still and quiet. You have to not move. You have to not speak. If you get a little excited, you might make a sound. If you get a little angry, your face might get red. It is a big thing to do.

Sometimes, you watch something because it is fun. You might watch a game with your kids. You are happy to see them laugh. You are happy to see them play. You are just enjoying the time with them.

To watch something can also mean to wait for it. You might wait for a movie to start. You wait for the lights to go down. You wait for the music to start. You wait for the story to begin. When it starts, you are watching.

So, to watch something is to be there. You are looking at it. You are not doing anything else. It is just you and the thing in front of you.
