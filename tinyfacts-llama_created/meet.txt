To meet someone means you are going to see them. You are going to a place where you will find them. It can be a school, a work place, a park, or a big room.

Imagine you have a job and you work very hard. You finish your work at the office. You can not go home yet. You look at your watch. You know that your friend is going to be there in five minutes. You look up and see them. You walk over to them. You say hello and you say how you are doing.

You did not need to say this before. You just knew it was the right moment. You did not think about it. You just did it.

When you meet people, you learn about them. You hear their words and you see their faces. You know more about them than before. You can see if they are happy or sad.

Sometimes you meet a person you have never seen before. You are going to a place you have never been. You do not know how to find them. But then you see them. You smile and you say hello. You start to talk.

You meet many people in your life. You meet your family. You meet your friends. You meet people you have never met before. Each meeting is different. You learn something new.

To meet a dog means you are going to a place where dogs live. You might be at a park. You see dogs running around. You see dogs playing with balls. You hear their happy sounds. You can see how happy they are.

To meet someone is to be near them. You are not next to them. You are not on the same floor. You are on different floors. You are in different rooms. But you can see them. You can hear them. You can see their faces.

You can meet someone you know. You can meet someone you do not know. You can meet someone who is your friend. You can meet someone who is a teacher. You can meet someone who is a doctor. Each person has a story to tell.
