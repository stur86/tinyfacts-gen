To work as a part of a team is to help many people do their jobs better. There is one person in this team who makes sure everything runs well.

Think of a big house. In this house, there is a person who keeps the rooms clean. They tell the people who clean to pick up the floors and to not leave the floors until the next day. This person is the one who makes sure the house stays safe.

In a group of people, each person has a job. Some people clean, some people make food, and some people keep the money. But there is one person who keeps everything together. They tell everyone what to do and make sure everyone follows.

When you work as a part of a team, you do not just do one job. You do many jobs. You help your friends. You help your family. You help the people around you. This makes the world a better place.
