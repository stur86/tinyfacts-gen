When you are surprised, your body and mind are very much moved. You are not expecting this to happen. You did not see it coming. You are shocked.

Imagine you are eating a sweet food. You feel happy because you like it. But then, you hear a loud voice. The sound is so loud that it makes you jump. You look around to see who is making the noise. You are surprised because you did not think anyone would do that.

When you are surprised, your face goes white. Your heart starts beating very fast. You feel like you are in a big rush. You might laugh or cry. You might do something you do not want to do. You are also a bit afraid because you do not know what is happening.

Surprise can happen in many ways. You might be surprised when you find out you have a new friend. You might be surprised when you see a big animal in your room. You might be surprised when you get a surprise from a friend. You might even be surprised by your own body or your own mind.

Sometimes, you are surprised by things that are not happening. You might see a green tree in the middle of a white street. You might hear a loud music in a quiet room. You might feel a strong wind when you are in a cold place. These are all kinds of surprise.
