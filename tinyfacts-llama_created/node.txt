A small thing in a group of many people, animals, or things that grow in the ground, can also be a small piece of wood or other stuff that holds power. This small piece is usually very small and only one part of a much bigger thing.

Think of a person. Each person has many friends. One friend is like a small piece of wood that holds the person together. The wood is small, but it is very important. Without the wood, the person would fall down and be broken. The wood holds the person and helps them live.

Also, a tree has many small pieces that hold it together. These small pieces are like many friends. They help the tree grow strong and keep it from falling down. This is why we say a tree is full of friends. The tree has many small pieces that work together.

We can also use this word to talk about how the world works. A city is made of many small places: a park, a school, a store, a police car, and many other small things. Each of these small places is very important. They all work together to make the city live. The city is like a small piece of wood that holds the many parts together.

We use this word to talk about computers too. A computer is a very small thing that can do many things at once. It is like one small piece of wood that holds all of the many small pieces together. The computer uses many small pieces of wood that help it work. These small pieces of wood are very small, but they are the key to how the computer works.
