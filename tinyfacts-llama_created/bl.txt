To blue something means you change it to look darker. It happens when you put it in water or when it gets wet. The air around the thing starts to run out of it. The blue color comes from the way light looks when it hits the wet stuff.

Imagine you have a blue shirt. If you put it in a room with white walls, the blue will get less bright. The light from the sun will not hit the shirt as much, and the blue will not be as strong. This is what happens when something blue gets wet.

You can also blue things that are made of other colors. If you add water to a red thing, it may turn blue. This happens because the water takes away some of the red light. The red light gets left behind, and the blue light comes forward. This is why things in water look blue.

Some things are blue when you look at them. When you look at a blue sky, the sky looks blue. You see this color because your eyes and brain are taking in the light from the sky. Your brain says, yes, the sky is blue. This is how you know the sky looks blue.

You can also use the word to mean to become quieter. If you are talking very loud, you become a bit blue. You feel a bit sad or worried because the sound is too strong.
