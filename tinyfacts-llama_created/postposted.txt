To put something after something else is to move it after you. This is often done with words.

A person might write a story. They start with a story about their day. Then they write about something else. The story about their day follows the story about something else. They use the word after because they are moving the story to come after the story.

You can also use this word with things you buy. If you buy a book, you are the one doing the moving. You put the book after you get it. This can be funny. Imagine you want a dog. You go to the store. You buy the dog. Then the store puts the dog in the car. The dog goes after the store. This can make you laugh because you feel you are also in the dog.

In some places, people use this word to talk about people. When someone does something good, the other people say, the person is doing after. This means the person is following the good act. It is like putting the person after the good thing they did. This word helps us tell about people and their good ways.
