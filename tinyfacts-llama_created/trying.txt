To try means to do something you are not sure if you can do. It is about trying to be strong when you are afraid. You might try to do a thing that makes you feel bad. You might try to run a long way when you are very tired. You try to do something that is hard because you want to be better.

You also try to do things you want. You might try to be happy when you feel sad. You try to do things that make you feel good. You might try to do something you love. This is a good thing because it makes you feel good inside.

Sometimes you try to do something you want but you do not want to. You might try to eat food you do not like. You might try to do a thing you do not want to do. This is not good because you are afraid.

Trying is also a way to learn. When you try something new, you are learning. You learn if the thing works or if it does not. You learn how to do it. This is important because it helps you grow.

You can try many things in your life. You might try to be a better friend. You might try to be a better student. You might try to be a better person. Trying is a way to show you care about what happens around you.
