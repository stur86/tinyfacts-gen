To be a part of something can mean different things. When you are a part of a group, you are part of them. You might sit next to them in a room or be one of them when you go out into the world. You are not the only one who is part of the group. Everyone is part of the same group.

Sometimes, people have money. If you get a job and you work hard, you get more money. This means you are a part of a bigger group of people who make money. You are part of the group of people who buy things that you need.

You can also be a part of something that is not real. If you are a part of a story, it means you are part of the story. Maybe you see the story and you feel like you lived it. You are a part of something that happened in the past.

Sometimes, to be a part of something is to be a part of a group of people who do something together. You might be a part of a team. You work with other people to reach a big thing. You are all in this together.
