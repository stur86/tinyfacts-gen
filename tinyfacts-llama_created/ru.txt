To rung means to throw things down from high places. This is often because people are very angry or sad. They might feel like they do not want to live anymore. They throw their bags, clothes, and other things down onto the ground.

Imagine you are very angry and you throw your chair, desk, and books into a big room. This makes a lot of noise and makes many people afraid. The word rung is like this, but you throw things down into the ground instead of a room.

When you are in a hard place and you are very sad, you might feel like you want to get away from the situation. You might want to throw away your things and start over. This is a way of breaking free from the pain.

In some places, if you are very sad, people might give you a bag with things to help you. This bag might have food, water, and other things that help you feel better. The bag is like a big hug for your heart.
