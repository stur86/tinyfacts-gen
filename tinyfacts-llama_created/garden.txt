To grow something can be easy. You put it in the ground and let it sit there until it is tall and strong. You then give it water and let it drink. Then it makes its own food from the air and the sun. After a few weeks, it is ready to give you a nice thing to eat.

Sometimes people have a small area in their home where they grow their own food. This is a great way to be close to what you eat. They can pick the food when it is ready and enjoy it right away. It is a good way to learn about where food comes from.

A big place where many people grow food together is called a store. This is where they bring their food from their own fields and put it away for people to buy later. The store also helps to make sure that people have enough food to eat.

There is also a word for a green area where many things grow together. This is a kind of area where you can see a wide area of green. Some of these places are very big and cover many cities. Others are small and grow only in one area.
