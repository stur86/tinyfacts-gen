Sat is a big word we use to talk about things that go around the sun.

The sun is the bright thing in the sky during the day. It makes everything warm. It is the star at the very top of our world. It gives us light and makes the world feel nice.

The world around the sun is like a big round thing. This round thing goes very fast. It turns around many times in just one day. Imagine you are holding a ball. You hold it for a long time, and then you let it go. It goes very fast and does many turns in just one hour. The round thing in the sky is like that.

The round thing is made of many big pieces that are always moving around each other. These pieces are very small and light. They are always moving and never stop. This is why the round thing looks like it is turning.

There are many different kinds of round things. Some are very big and go around the sun many times. Some are very small and go around the sun many times very quickly. Some go around many times very slowly. Our world is one kind. It goes around once every day and once every year.

The round thing in the sky is like a great big ball. It is so big that if you were to go very fast and keep going for a very long time, you would never reach it. It is always there in the sky, even when we are inside our houses or our cars.

People use many words to talk about round things. We say the sun is the big round thing in the sky. We call the round thing in the sky the round thing. We also say the big round thing in the sky is the round thing. This is because the words are the same.
