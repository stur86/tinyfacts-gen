To do something means to start a new thing. You might do a hard job at the office. You sit at your desk and work for hours. After you finish, you feel happy. This is when you do something.

Doubt is when you are not sure. You have a question. You look at something and you do not know what to do. You might feel scared or angry. This is when you are not sure if you are right.

Dream is when you have a very good time. You are not in school. You are in a place far away. You do not know where you are. You see things that are not real. You might see a bright light or a big animal. You might hear a very loud sound. You feel happy and very free.

When you do something, you are changing the world. You add something new. You make a new story. You make a new day.
