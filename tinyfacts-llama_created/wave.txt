A wave is a big, moving thing that goes across the water. When the water is calm, the wave looks like a long, straight line. But when you see the wave, it looks like many lines that are going in different directions. It looks like the water is moving up and down, side to side, back and forward.

The wave can be made by many things. The sun can make big waves in the water. The wind makes small waves. A person can make a wave by standing on the water or by running across it. The water can also make a wave by falling down from the sky when it rains.

Waves go very fast. The water inside a wave is moving from the top to the bottom very quickly. This makes the water push out from the side, and the side that was pushing out goes up. The water then comes back, pushing out from the other side, and the side that was pushed back goes up again.

When you stand on the water, you feel the wave right next to you. The water is moving over your feet. You can feel the wave when the water is moving under you. If the wave is very high, the water might go over your head. If the wave is very low, the water might barely touch your feet.

Waves can be very big. Some waves can cover the whole town. Some waves can reach the top of a tall building. Other waves are so small that they are barely a foot high.

Even though the water moves in many different directions, the wave always goes from the top down. This is why the water looks like it is going up and down, side to side. The wave is always going down to the bottom, but it never turns back.
