An answer is something you give when you are asked a question. When you buy something, you pay with money. If you have money, you have an answer. If you do not have money, you do not have an answer.

An answer is also how you show you are interested. If you are in a game with a friend, you might ask them a question. If you think the answer is right, you say it. This shows you care.

When you have an answer, you can use it later. You can read it again later, or you can use it to write something else. If you do not have the answer, you might ask another person to help you. This is how you learn.

You can also have an answer inside yourself. Think about what you think. If you are happy, you feel good. If you are afraid, you feel scared. Your body and mind are trying to tell you something.

Sometimes, an answer is not just a word. It can be a picture, a song, or a story. This is when you hear something and you remember it later. It helps you remember things you learned.
