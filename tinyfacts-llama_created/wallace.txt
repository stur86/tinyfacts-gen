A wall is a long piece of hard stuff that you use to make a house, a school, or a building. It is usually made of wood or strong stuff that can hold a lot of people.

Imagine a big tree that falls down. The tree hits the ground and becomes a wall. This wall helps keep the people inside the house safe. It keeps the wind and rain out. It also makes it hard for animals to get to the house.

You can also build a wall for a game. A game is when you try to get a ball into a box. The wall is the big thing that stops the ball from going in. It helps the team with the ball winding the game.

Sometimes, a wall is a high thing that is very tall. You can climb up to the top. This can be fun because you feel like you are on top of the world. But it can also be hard to get to because there are many steps.

People also build walls to keep people out. Imagine a bad person wants to come into your town. They can put up a wall so that only you and your friends can go through. This helps keep your town safe.

There is also a man named Wall Street. This is where many people go to buy and change money. It is like a big place where people talk and move their money.
