An animal is a thing that lives. It needs to breathe air to stay. It also needs to drink water. It needs food to give it power. All these things are inside the animal.

An animal has parts. One part is the head. It has eyes. It has a mouth. It has hair. The head is where the brain is. This part is very important. It lets the animal see what is around it.

Another part is the body. It is the long thing that covers the head and the body. The body is where the animal gets power. It is where the animal moves. It is where the animal eats.

The animal also has a way to breathe. It has parts inside the body that take in air. It uses the air to make the body work. The animal can also drink water from the ground.

The animal has many other parts too. Some animals have a long hair around their body. Some animals have a hard skin. Some animals can fly. Some animals can live in the water. Some animals can live in the ground.

All these parts help the animal live. The animal can move, eat, breathe, and feel. It is a living thing that is very important for our world.
