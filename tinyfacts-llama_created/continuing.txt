When you are doing something, you are always doing it at the same time. If you are talking, you are talking now. If you are eating a food, you are eating now.

If you do something and then you stop, you are done. But if you keep doing something after you stop, you are continuing. This means you are keeping what you started doing.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You play and then you stop. You are done. But if you keep playing, you are continuing the game. You are still doing what you started doing.

This is also true in your life. When you learn something new, you are learning. You are continuing to learn. You are keeping the new thing you are learning.

Sometimes, you might want to stop doing a thing. You might say, I am tired. You do not want to keep going anymore. But if you keep doing it, you are continuing the thing. You are not stopping.

You can also continue something that is already happening. If a movie starts, you are continuing it. You keep watching until the end.
