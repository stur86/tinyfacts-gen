When you see something go down, you have a feeling. It is like your heart is beating faster because you know something is happening.

Imagine you are playing a game. You start with a high number of points. You are doing well. But then you play another game. The other team does better. Because you have more games, your points go down. You feel sad. You are afraid that you will not be the best player anymore.

When things go down, they often get smaller. If you have a lot of money, you might have to give some away. If you have a big number of friends, you might have fewer friends left.

Sometimes, things go down because things are not getting better. You might feel tired all the time. You do not want to do anything. You are worried about what is happening.

When you see a number go down, you can see how much things are changing. It helps you understand how the world works.
