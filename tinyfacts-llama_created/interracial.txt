To be different means you are not the same as anyone else. When you are not the same, people look at you and think, this is strange. You are not like them. You have a different look, a different voice, a different way of doing things.

Sometimes, a person has a friend from a far away place. They meet and talk, and they learn about each other. They are happy to know they are not alone in the world. This is what it means to be different.

In a world where everyone is the same, being different is a problem. There are many things to learn and many ways to live. People from different places have different stories, different hopes, and different fears. When we do not understand this, we can feel angry, sad, or scared.

But when people are different, they can also be very strong together. When two people from different places meet, they can learn from each other. They can share their ideas, their food, their clothes, and their ways of living. This can make our world a better place.

There are many ways to be different. You can be short or tall. You can have a long hair or no hair at all. You can have many colors in your skin. You can speak a different word. You can love someone who is not your own. You can live in a place that is different from where you are from.

Being different is what makes us human. It makes us not just one thing, but many different things. When we see that, we are happy. When we see that we are not alone, we feel less alone. We can be friends with people from far away places. We can learn from them. We can laugh together. And we can live a life that is full of many different stories, each one our own.
