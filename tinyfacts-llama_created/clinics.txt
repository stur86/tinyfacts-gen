A small building where people go to see a doctor. These places are like a home for the body. You can go there when you are sick or hurt. The doctor is the one who helps you feel better.

Imagine you are very tired from running around a long day. You go to the building with the green sign. Inside, there is a room with a chair and a table. The doctor sits in the chair and looks at you. They ask you questions to know how you feel. They also take a look at your body with a small light. This helps the doctor find the problem.

The building is usually very quiet. People who are feeling better go there to see the doctor. They might also get a small paper. This paper tells the doctor what to do next. You might have to stay for a short time to rest before you go home.

The building has many rooms. One room is for kids who are very young. Another room is for grown people who are sick. There is even a room for people who are very old. The building is like a safe place where you can go when you need help.
