To get something is to bring it into being. When you do something, you make a thing. This can be a thing you build or a thing you have already. You can get a thing from a person. You might get money from a job. You can also get a thing from the air or water. You might get a thing from the ground that can be eaten. 

You can get a thing from a place. A town has many people. You get to live there if you move. You get to work and play in the same place. You get to know the people who live there. You get a feeling when you are in a town. 

You can also get a thing from a time. You might get a picture of the world. You might get a story about what happened before. You get to know how people lived before. You learn about the past. 

Sometimes, you get a thing that was not there before. You might get something that you did not think was real. You might get a feeling that was not there before. You get to know something new. You get to feel happy or sad. You get to understand how the world works.
