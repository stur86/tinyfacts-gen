A person who helps make something new is called a person who helps. When you make a book, you are a helper. You write words and put them on the paper. When you build a house, you are a helper. You build the walls and put the floor on the ground.

Another way to look at it is that a person who helps is one who gives you something. If you are a good friend, you help your friend. You give them a hug when they are sad. You tell them you love them. This is helping. Your friend feels happy because you are there for them.

Sometimes, a person who helps is someone who does not have to do anything but is there anyway. They might be a teacher. They tell you what to do, even when you are not doing it. Or a mother might be a helper. She keeps your hair clean and makes you a drink. She is there for you when you are feeling sad or hurt.

In the world, people who help are very important. They are the ones who build roads, parks, and houses. They are the ones who help keep our city clean. They are the ones who help us feel safe. Without them, we would be in a lot of trouble.
