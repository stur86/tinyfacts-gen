To have a chance is to have a chance to do something. You are not in a group of people. You are the one who does the thing. You have a few times to try and see if you can do it. You do not know if you will be the one who does it. You have a small chance, but you are trying anyway. Some people have a lot of chances. They do many things and they are always trying. They never give up.

Sometimes you have a chance to do something good. You can say yes to a job or a friend. You can say yes to play with a dog. These are chances. They help you feel happy and make your day better.

You also have chances to do something bad. You might decide to do something that is very wrong. You might say bad words or act in a way that makes others feel afraid. These are not good chances. They hurt people and make the world a worse place.

A chance can also be a time to try something new. If you have a chance to go to a new city, you might want to try it. You might see new places, meet new people, and learn new things. This is a good thing. You are stepping out of your old life and into something new.
