To use something is to make it work. When you buy a computer, you use it to do many things. You might watch movies. You might write papers. You use the computer for many hours.

A group of people working together is called a team. When they work well, they can do many things at once. They use their different jobs to make the work go faster. This makes the team stronger.

If a computer has a problem, it might not start. The user tries to fix the problem. If the user looks at the computer and sees the problem, they might fix it. They are the one who uses the computer to fix the problem.

Sometimes, people use words to talk. When they use the word, they mean the same thing. If two people use the word, they might not understand it. They might think the other person is wrong. This can cause a big problem.

To use a word is to know what it means. When you know a word, you can tell if someone says it. If you see the word and you know it, you can say, I understand you.
