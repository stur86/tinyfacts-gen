To start something means to begin it. It is the first step. You start when you open the door, when you pull out your phone, or when you walk into a room.

Imagine you are waiting for a friend to arrive. You might feel excited and nervous. This feeling is starting. It is the beginning of what is going to happen next.

You can start a game with a friend, a song with music, or a book with words. In each case, the first thing you do is begin.

Sometimes, starting is hard. You might feel a little scared or worried. But if you start to do the thing you want to do, you can go ahead and see what happens.
