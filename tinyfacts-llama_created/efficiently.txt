To do something very well is to act quickly and get the job done. When you do something this way, you are doing it very fast, and you get it done before you want to stop.

Think about how you walk down a long hall. If you walk very fast, you can cover a lot of ground in one go. You can finish your walk before you even start it. You are doing your job very well. You are also very good at getting from one place to another quickly.

You can also use this word to talk about how you work. If you work hard all day, you do your job very well. You do not leave too much to one person. Everyone has to do their part.

Sometimes, you might see a person who is very good at doing a job. They move very fast, and they do not make any big noise. They are very safe because they can do their job very well without making a sound. This is why we say they work very well.
