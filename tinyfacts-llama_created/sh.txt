To shadows something means to make it look smaller so you can not see it. When the sun is very bright, it makes everything look the same. But if you walk very slowly in front of a tall tree, the tree looks smaller and you can not see its top. The tree looks like a small rock.

You can also shadow a person. If you are very tall, you can make a person look smaller. If you are very short, the person looks very tall. This happens when the person walks in front of you. The tall person gets smaller and the short person gets bigger.

Shadows are also found on the ground. When the sun is in the sky, the light from the sun falls down on the ground and makes it look like a big dark line. These lines are called shadows.

Sometimes, a group of people will stand together and make a long line. In this line, the people are all smaller than they are alone. Because they are all together, they look like one thing.
