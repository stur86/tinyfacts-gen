To expect something means you are ready for it. You do not fear it because you know it is coming. You are not worried. You are ready. 

Imagine a person who loves food. They know when a new food is coming. They do not look at the phone. They do not think, they just wait. When they see the food on the table, they smile and say, I am ready. 

You can also expect things from a game. You know what is going to happen next. You have seen the game many times before. You know the moves of the other team. You are ready to play. You are not afraid. You are ready for the game.

Sometimes, we expect things from our families. We know they will be there. We know they will love us. We are not afraid. We are ready for them.

We also expect things from our friends. We know they will be there. We know they will smile when they see us. We are ready for our friends.

When we expect something, we are not afraid. We are ready. We know it is coming.
