The word ends is used when something is finished. It is the end of a line. Imagine you are writing a story. You write the first few words and then you end the story. The end is the last word.

A line can end in many different ways. It can end in a word, a number, or even a picture. Sometimes a story ends with a small word. It might be a word that tells you the end, like a word like this: the end.

Things also end in their own way. A tree ends when it falls down. The sun ends in the night. The stars end in the sky after dark. The world ends when the sky turns a different color.

In life, people end their lives. This is when they stop being strong and they die. A child ends when they grow up and they become a person. An animal ends when it is too old to live anymore. The world ends when the sun goes away from us.
