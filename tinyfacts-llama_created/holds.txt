To hold something means to keep it in your hands. You can hold a ball, a book, or a small animal. When you hold a ball, you keep it close to you. You can use your fingers to grip it. If you hold a book, you keep it in your hand.

You can also hold something because you need it. If you need to drink water, you hold the bottle. You do not let it fall into the ground. You keep it up so you can drink it when you want.

Sometimes, you hold a person. You hold a friend or a family person. This means you are near them and you care about them. You can talk to them and listen to what they say.

You can hold a thing in your mind. If you love a person, you might hold them. You keep them in your heart. This is a strong feeling that stays with you for a long time.

To hold something can be a good thing. It helps you keep your things safe. It gives you power. It lets you act quickly when you need to. But it also keeps you in a place. It means you are not moving around.

Sometimes, to hold something is to feel a strong feeling. You might hold a picture of a child. This means you remember happy times with a family. You feel a warm feeling in your heart because of this picture.
