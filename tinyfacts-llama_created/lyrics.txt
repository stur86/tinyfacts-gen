To write the words of a song is one thing. To write the words that people will listen to and understand is another thing. People often do both. They write the words that go into their heads so they can remember them later. They write the words they want to say to each other. They write the words that will help them tell their story.

Sometimes, people write the words first. They think of the song, and then they write them down. Other times, the words come to them as they hear the music. They might listen to the song and start to think about what the words should say.

Lying is when you tell a story that is not true. You might make up parts of a story or change how things happened. You might say a person did something that they did not do or say a story that is not real. This is not good. People want to know the truth. They want to know what really happened.

Sometimes, people write songs because they want to remember a big eventually. They write the words that are important to them. They write the words that tell the story of how they felt when something happened. They write the words that help them remember.

Music is very important for people. It can make them feel happy, sad, or angry. It can make them want to move their bodies or sit still. People often listen to songs many times before they know the words by heart. They learn the words by listening to the music over and over.
