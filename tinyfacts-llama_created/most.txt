The word most is very important. It tells us how many of something there are.

If you have a group of people and you ask, how many of them are there? You can say, there are many. Or you can say, there are most of them. This means there are more than you. If you have a bag full of things and you take out one, you can say, I have most of the things. This means you have the most.

Think of a house with many rooms. If you go to the kitchen, you are in the most rooms. You are the one in the middle. If you go to the living room, you are in the middle too. You are in the most rooms.

Another way to use this word is with numbers. If you have ten books and you give one to your friend, how many books are left? You still have the most books.

Sometimes you see a picture of a big group of people. You see the face of the top person. That person is most of the group. They are the top one.

The word most is like a number that shows how many of something there are. It helps us know how big or small something is.
