A big animal with a hard brown body is found in many places. It has a long neck and a head that is very round. It is good at running and can move very fast. When it wants to eat, it goes into a green area and comes out with a lot of green stuff. It also has a hard white stuff on its face.

This animal is very strong. If it gets very angry, it can run very fast and hit the ground many times. It also makes a loud sound when it is very angry. People like this animal because it is very good at eating and it is also good to have around.

In the old days, people used to kill these animals. They would take the hard white stuff off the animal to eat. Some people also took the green stuff from the animal to make food. This was not good because it hurt the animal. Today, many people keep these animals and let them live in the green areas.
