A high place is where you can not walk down far. The ground is not soft enough to walk on and you must go up to the top. The top of a big high place is very high in the sky. You can see for many days from up there.

There are many kinds of high places. Some are very, very high and tall. They have great big rocks on top and are very, very hard to reach. Other high places are not as tall, but they are still very high and have great big rocks around them. Some high places are small and only a few hundred feet tall. They are still good to go to and see the world around them.

People often go to these high places to climb and run up. They wear strong clothes and hard shoes to stay safe while they climb. It can be very hard to reach the top, but many people like to do it because it is a great feeling to reach the top of a high place.

High places can also be very dark at night. The sun is gone and the sky is very heavy with big white things. It is hard to see and it can be very cold. Some high places have bright lights at night so people can see.

People use high places to go to school. There are tall houses with many floors and rooms. The rooms are for people to learn and the high place is the top where everyone sits. Some high places have big rooms with many people sitting around a big table. Others have small rooms and a big room for a meeting.

In some places, high places are very important for food. If you go to a high place to pick food, you have to climb many steps. You might feel tired and your feet hurt. The food is very good but you have to go far to get it.
