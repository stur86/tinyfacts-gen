The word for a sign of something happening is called a sign. When you get a sign, you see or feel that something is wrong. This can happen when you get sick, or when you are afraid, or when you are very excited. A sign is something that lets you know that a thing is happening.

Think of a tree. If the tree starts to fall down, that is a sign that something is wrong. This sign tells us to be carefully watch the tree. We need to stay away from it until the wind stops or the tree falls down. The sign is a warning.

Sometimes signs can be very hard to see. If you have a tiny tiny hurt on your finger, you might not feel it right away. You might only notice it after the hurt has become very bad. In this case, the hurt is a sign that something is wrong.

Another kind of sign is a feeling. If you are feeling very sad, you might see a picture of a person you love. This feeling is a sign that something is very wrong. It tells you to feel sorry for yourself.

We also have signs in our bodies. When you feel hot, you might see a line across your face. This sign tells you that your body is getting too hot. You can stay in the dark or go to a cool place to feel better.

Finally, signs can be about the world around us. If you see a dog that is very loud, you might feel afraid. This sign lets you know that something is happening that you should be carefully watch.

In short, signs are things that tell us that something is wrong or happening. They are an important way for us to stay safe and take care of our bodies.
