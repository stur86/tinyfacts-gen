When you hear a low sound in a group of people, you might say that they are making a low sound. But the word you are looking for is different.

A low sound is when a thing makes a very deep sound. The sound is much louder than a high sound. You can hear it when you are very quiet or when you are very far away from a loud place.

A small animal that lives in the water has a very low sound. It makes this sound so you can find it in the dark. It is very good at hiding so you do not see it.

A low sound can also mean something that is very heavy. If you put a heavy box on the floor, it makes a low sound. This is because the sound is going down through the floor.

When you hear a low sound in music, it is often the sound of a big group of people playing together. The low sounds come together to make a very big sound. This is why music often feels happy and warm.

You might also hear low sounds in your body. When you are very cold, your hands and feet make a low sound. This helps your body get warm.
