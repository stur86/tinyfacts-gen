To send something to someone is to give it to them. You can use a thing from your home, like a letter, or a person from a far place. You write what you want to say. You put the thing in a bag with a cover. Then you go to a place where the bag goes to a far away city. The city has a big building that makes the bags go to other cities. People work there and use a computer to send the bags. The bags can go very fast. They can even go through the sky if the right kind of bag is used. This way, you can give your friend a thing you think they want, even if you are far away.

You can also use this word for a job. A person in a city works for a big group. They send papers, money, or other things to people who live far away. They do this so the people can know what is happening in the city. It is a way to help all the people in the world feel part of what happens in a place where you live.

Sometimes, when you send something, you have to follow a set of ways. You must put your name and your house number on the bag. This is to make sure the person knows who to find the bag for.
