To start something can be very exciting. Think about a small thing that you put in the air. It goes up very fast and then it comes back down. This is a kind of move where a person or a thing is sent out to far away parts.

A car that can fly is the same idea. It is put in the air and it goes very fast. It goes over many cities and lands in a new place. This is how we get from one place to another without using a road.

Sometimes you use this word to talk about things that are very big. A huge group of people can be called a group. If you are the leader of this group, it is a group that you lead. You tell them what to do.

You can also use this word to talk about things that start to move. A ball goes through the air when a person throws it. It moves from one place to another and then it comes back. This is how things move when they are on the go.

A person can also use this word to talk about when things are ready to be started. If you have a plan for the day, you might say, I am ready to start. This means you are ready to do what you have planned.
