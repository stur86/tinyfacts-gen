To further means to get closer to something. It is often used when talking about big animals. A big animal has a soft cover on its body. This cover is like hair. It helps keep the animal warm in cold times.

Imagine you see a dog. The dog looks like a big animal, but it is not a real one. The soft cover on its body is what makes it look like a dog. This cover is very important for the dog.

When you further a dog, you are getting closer to its body. You see the soft hair that grows from its skin. You might see the hairs on its back, the hairs on its legs, and the hairs on its head. This is what makes the dog look like a real animal.

Sometimes, you use this word when talking about a person. If you say a person is further than another person, you mean they are getting closer to the truth. They are doing something better than another person. This means the person is doing a better job.

In another way, further can mean you are moving away from a place. If you are further from a town, you are moving away from where you live. This is like leaving your home behind. You are not in the same place anymore.

To further something is also a way to get more of something. If you have food and you want to eat it, you might want to get more. This means you want to have more of the thing. It is like the thing is growing bigger and bigger until you can no longer have it.
