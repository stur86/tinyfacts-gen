To be a word is to be a thing that you can use to talk about something. When you say a word, you are telling other people about what you mean. You are telling them about a person, a place, a thing, or even your own feeling.

One word can mean many different things. If you say you are happy, you are being one word. But if you say you are happy and you are actually very angry, you have changed the word. Now it means something different. This is how we learn to use words and mean different things.

Another word is one that you use to build other words. If you use the word cat, you can make other words like this: dog, house, run, eat, and many more. These words are like little building blocks that help you put together a big picture of life.

Sometimes words can have more than one meaning. A word can mean something in one way and something else in another way. Think of the word walk. You can walk a short way, or you can walk a long way. You can also walk a straight line or walk in a round way. This makes the word walk many different ways.

A word can also change over time. A word might mean something one way today and something else another day. This is because people learn and change as well as animals and trees.
