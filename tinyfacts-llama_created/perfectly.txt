To be perfect means something is not broken or wrong. You can feel this when you do something right, like you did your work on time. Or when you have a word you wrote and it is not any different from what the teacher said it should be.

Think of a picture. If you draw something, you are not perfect. You might make a small line or a tiny face. But if you draw a picture that looks like the ones you see on the street, you are very close to being perfect.

You can also be perfect when you are in a game. If a team is playing well, and they do not make any bad moves, they are perfect. You feel happy because you did not make any wrong moves.

Sometimes, something is not perfect. It might have a small problem. But it is still okay. You might not be perfect, but you can still be very good because you tried.
