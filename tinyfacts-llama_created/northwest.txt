The word for this direction is found in many places. In the morning, you see this when the sun is high in the sky. The first half of the day is when the sun is high. In the late morning, it is in the middle of the sky. After the morning, you see it in the middle of the day. This is the time when the sun is between the two big tall things in the sky.

In the night, you see this when it is very dark. The night is the time between the sun and the big tall things in the sky. If you are looking at a place where the sun does not come up, you can see this. It is a place where the sun is in the sky but is not very high. This is why it is sometimes called the night side.

In the world, there are four directions. The one we are talking about is the one that is between the big tall things in the sky. It is the direction to the side that is not on the ground.
