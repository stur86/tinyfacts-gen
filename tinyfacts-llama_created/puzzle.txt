A problem is when you are trying to figure out how things work. It is not a thing you see. It is a way of thinking. When you have a problem, you look for the right answer. You try to understand what is happening and how it can change.

Sometimes, you need to put many small parts together. Each part is very small and has only one job. When you put them all together, the whole thing becomes a new thing. It is a new way to do something.

You can also have a problem where you have to find a thing. If you are looking for a book, you can ask people around you. You talk to them and ask if they know where the book is. You look at the faces and the clothes of the people you talk to. Then you follow the lead of the people until you find the book.

Sometimes, a problem is not about finding a thing. It is about thinking about how things work. A person might have a problem with their head when they are afraid. They may feel a lot of worry. They might not want to do anything. They might just sit in the dark, thinking about what is wrong.

There are also problems that are about how to act. If you see a person who is being mean to a child, you might feel angry. You might want to hit them or shout at them. But you do not act like that. You think about what is right and you do not act wrong. This is a way of being a good person.

People also have problems when they are in a group. Some people might not like to work with others. They might feel alone. They might think they are better than the others. But when many people work together, they can do many things that are better than they could do alone.

A problem is also about how we feel. If you are sad, you might feel very hard. You might cry. You might not want to play. You might feel like you want to hide. But even when you feel sad, you are still a person. You are still a living thing. You still need air and food and water.

People work on problems every day. They try to find answers. They try to fix things. They try to make life better for everyone. They are always looking for a way to do things in a better way.
