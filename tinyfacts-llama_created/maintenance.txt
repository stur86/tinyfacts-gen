To do something is to stay in a place for a while. If you have a car, you do this. You fill the car with air, so the car does not go into a bad place. You also do this when your home is old. You fix the doors, change the light, and keep the floor clean so it looks nice.

A person who does this is called a person who keeps things. They are like the ones who keep the schools. They keep the money of the money people and the computers of the office so everyone can work well. They also keep the cars and the houses of the people.

Some people do this when they are not working. They fix things for their own home. They fix the light so it does not go out and they fix the table so it is not broken. They do this so their home stays in good order.

If you do this too often, you can get tired. Your hands get a bit red. You might feel the same when you do this every day. But if you do it all the time, you stay very strong. You keep everything in order.
