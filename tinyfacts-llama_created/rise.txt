To go up is to move to a higher place. You might go up a street, up a building, or up into the sky. When you climb, you might go up a very high wall. You might also go up a big tree.

When you go up, you might feel a bit tired. You might be walking and your feet are heavy. But when you reach the top, you feel very strong. You can see for a long time and see a far way. This feeling is often called a high feeling.

Going up can be done by many different ways. You can use your legs to walk up. You can also use a car or a bus. Sometimes, you just go up by your own power. You might be going up a long road and you need to push your feet to reach the top.

When you go up, you are moving away from the ground. The ground is the place where you start. As you go up, the air gets harder to breathe. You might feel a bit cold because the air is very thinker than the warm air you are used to.
