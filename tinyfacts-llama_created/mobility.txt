To move is to go from one place to another. It is about how you get from where you are to where you want to go.

You can move by running. You start running. Your feet hit the ground. Your legs move forward. You are moving. You are not still. You are going.

You can also move by using a thing that goes on your body. You sit on a chair. You stand up and you walk. You are moving to a different room.

You can also move by going through the water. A small car goes over the water. It moves under the water and it goes around the side of the world. It does not walk. It does not run. It just goes.

When you move, you change your position. You are not where you were before. You are now in a new place. You are in a different spot.

Some things move by themselves. A cat jumps from one place to another. A dog runs. A tree falls from one side to another. They move without anyone telling them to.

When you are moving, you might feel a little bit of fear. Your heart might beat faster. You might feel like you are going too fast or too slow. But when you are moving, you are also moving your body. Your body is getting from one place to another. This is what it means to move.
