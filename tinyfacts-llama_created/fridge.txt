A box is a thing that keeps food and drinks cold. You put food and drinks inside it. The cold inside the box makes the food and drinks stay safe for a long time.

There are many kinds of boxes. Some are small and fit in the kitchen. Others are big and go in the living room. Some have doors that you close. Others have a round top. Some have a bottom and sides. You can find boxes with many different colors.

You might have a box in your kitchen for food. You put food that is good to eat like food for breakfast. You might have a box for drinks. You put cold drinks like water, cold water with ice, or cold drinks from a bottle. You can also have a box for making food. You can put food you made in the box and it stays safe while you go.

Sometimes, you use the word to talk about how a person feels. If a person feels sad, they might say, I feel a bit cold inside. This means they feel the cold inside the box. Sometimes, people say they are feeling cold too. They are not just talking about the food. They are talking about their own body.
