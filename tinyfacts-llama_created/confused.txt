When you are confused, you are not sure what is going on. It is hard to understand and your brain feels very strange. Your body might be shaking a lot or your eyes are wide open because you are worried.

Imagine you see a big dog. You think it is a cat. Or you think a cat is a dog. You are not sure which one is what. This is what it means to be confused.

Sometimes, when you are confused, you might feel scared or very nervous. You might want to ask a friend or look at a picture to help you understand. It is a feeling that is not easy to get out of your head.

A person might be confused if they are trying to do many things at once. They might see many things, hear many sounds, and feel many different things. Their brain is very busy trying to make sense of it all.

You can also be confused if you are not sure about something. Maybe you want to eat a food but you do not know if it is good for you. Or maybe you do not know how to do a thing. In this case, your brain is trying to figure out the answer.

Being confused can make you feel sad. You might feel like you are the only one who is worried. But sometimes, when you are confused, you can learn something new. You might see a different way of doing things or you might pick up a new thing that you like.
