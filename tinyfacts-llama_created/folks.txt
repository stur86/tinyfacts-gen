Follow means to follow people or things. It is about moving after someone or something. You can follow a walk, a run, or a story. If you are a big group of people, you can follow them.

Sometimes, you follow a book or a song. You read the words or listen to the music while you move your body. You follow the story and see what happens next.

You can also follow a game. If a team is playing a game, you follow them. You watch the play and see how they do things. You might want to do the same things.

In a city, you might follow the people in a street. You see who is walking, who is sitting, and who is running. This helps you understand where everyone is.

If you follow a person, you do not stop them. You just watch them. Sometimes, you might try to catch up. But usually, you let them go because they are moving in a way that is different from you.
