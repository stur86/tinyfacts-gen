To be in a new place is to move from a place you know to a new one. This can happen in many ways. A child might move to a new school with their family. They have to learn new things and meet new people. This can be exciting because they do not know what will happen next.

A place is where people live. It can be a big city like New City or a small town like Green Town. A place has houses, streets, parks, and shops. It has food stores and places to buy clothes. It has roads to drive on and places to sit and rest.

When you move to a new place, you might feel a bit scared. You do not know where your new home is. You might wonder if you will like the food, the people, or the way the world works. But after a few days, you start to feel at home. You make new friends, you learn new words, and you start to enjoy your new life.

You can also be in a new place if you go to a far away place. You might go to a place that is very hot or very cold. You might go to a place with very high trees or very low trees. You might go to a place with water and you might go to a place with no water at all. When you move to a new place, you are changing your life.
