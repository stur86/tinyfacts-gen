A person you like and want to be near. You might say you are a good friend. You like to go to the same places and have fun together. You have fun things to talk about and laugh. You feel happy when you see them.

You do not always have to say this. You can just be near another person. You can sit next to them. You can play with them. You can help them. You can also fight with them if you are angry. In this way, you are a good friend too.

You can also say you are your friend. This means you like them very much and you want them to be happy. You want them to feel safe and happy. This is a way to show you care about them.
