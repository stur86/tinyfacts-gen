A thing you use for a short time is called a thing you use. You pay money to the person who owns it. When you pay, you are agreeing to keep it for a little while.

Think of a house. When you move into a house, you sign a paper. You pay money for the house and you promise to keep it clean. After a year, you can sign another paper and you get to stay in the house for another year. This is called a long time. But if you want to move, you have to tell the owner you want to leave. You might sign a paper saying you want to go and you will pay the money to leave.

You can also sign a paper to use a car for a short time. You pay money for the car and you promise to drive it carefully. You might sign a paper that says you can drive it until the end of the month. If you are late or you do something wrong, you might have to pay money or you might have to leave the car.

Sometimes, you sign a paper to use a computer for a short time. You pay money to use it and you promise to not break it. If you break it, you have to pay money to fix it. You are keeping the computer for a little while and you are paying for it.

A thing you use is like a person. You pay to use it. If you do not pay, you might get into trouble. You have to follow the person or the thing you use.
