To grow means you get bigger and stronger. Imagine a tiny thing that starts on the ground. Over time, it gets taller and wider. It also gets more food so it can have more power. This is what happens when you grow.

You can grow in many ways. You can grow in your body. If you eat many more food, your body gets bigger. You might feel your skin being made softer. Or you can grow in your mind. You might learn new things and become better at doing things.

Some things grow very fast. A small green thing on a tree can grow into a big tree over a very short time. Other things take much longer. A tiny baby might grow very slow at first, and then in a short time it is almost as big as a full grown person.

Grown things can also change. You might grow taller if you grow taller. You might get bigger eyes if you live in a place with lots of green stuff. You might also grow hair. Or you might grow a big round thing on your head.

The word can also mean to become more important. If a person is small, they can grow to be a big leader. If a group of animals is small, they can grow to be a large one that is good at fighting.
