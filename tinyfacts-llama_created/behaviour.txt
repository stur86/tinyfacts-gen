To act is to do things. This word can be big, small, or between two people. When you act, you show how you feel about a thing. If you are afraid, you act by staying quiet. If you are happy, you act by smiling.

You act with your body and with your mind. Your body tells your brain what to do. Your brain tells your body. Your brain has your heart and your head. They work together. When you act, you are telling the world that you are here.

People act when they want something. They act when they want to be the top person. They act when they want to be loved. They act when they want to be safe. They act when they feel afraid.

Animals act too. Dogs act when they want to play. Cats act when they want to sleep. Some animals act when they want to run away. Other animals act when they want to stay still and look calm.

In your day, you act when you do something that is not right. You act when you say bad words. You act when you do something that makes others angry. You act when you do something that is not safe.

But you also act when you do something good. You act when you help your friends. You act when you are kind to a child. You act when you tell the truth. You act when you show that you care about others.

To act is to show how you feel. It is to show the world what you are like. It is to show them that you are here.
