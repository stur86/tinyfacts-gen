When you are able to do things, you are not broken. You can run, play, and work. But if you have a thing that makes it hard for you to do those things, you are said to be a person with a problem.

Imagine you love to ride a horse. One day, you get a very bad cut on your hand. Because of this cut, your hand is very soft and you can not use it to hold a horse. You still love to ride, but you must do it in a different way. You might use a stick instead of holding the horse.

You might have a thing that makes it hard to speak. You have trouble talking to people. This is not because you are a bad person. You are just not able to say the right words.

You might also have a thing that makes it hard to walk. You have a hard time standing for long. You might need to sit down and rest.

Everyone is different. Some people have many problems. Some people have only one problem. Some people have two or three problems. But all of them are the same. They are a person with a problem.

We call these people friends because we care about them. If you see a friend with a problem, you might be happy for them. You might want to help them. You might want to listen to them.

People with problems can still be very happy. They might have a great life. They might have many good friends. They might even become great at something they love. They might find a job that makes them very happy. They might even love to help other people.

So, being a person with a problem is not bad. It is just a part of who we are. We can be strong and we can be kind. We can be friends to others and we can be happy.
