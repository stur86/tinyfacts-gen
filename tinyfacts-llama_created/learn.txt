To learn is to get better at something. You start with a thing you do not know and then you try many times. You try to remember what is right and you try again with the same thing.

When you learn, you use your brain. Your brain is the part inside your head that keeps you safe. It is where you remember things. When you learn, your brain adds more to what you already know. This makes you stronger and more able.

You can learn many things. You might learn to read or write. You might learn to talk to other people. You might learn to do a new job. You can also learn how to be a good friend.

Learning is a big part of how we grow. When you learn something, you are making the world a better place for yourself and for others.

There are two kinds of learning. One is the kind you do in school. You sit in a room and listen to a teacher. The other kind is the kind you do by yourself. You try things and see if they work. Both kinds are important and help you learn new things.
