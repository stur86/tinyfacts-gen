A house is a big room with many rooms inside. It is where you live with your family and friends. You go there in the morning to sleep and come back in the evening to eat dinner and watch a movie.

You can have a small house in a city or a big house in the middle of a big area. Houses can have many floors. The top floors are usually the most nice and have big windows that let the light in. The bottom floors are usually where you put your cars and boxes.

Inside the house, there are many rooms. One room is for sleeping. There is a bed where you lie down at night. There is another room for eating. There is a room for playing. There is a room for reading books. There is a room for watching movies and a room for playing games with your friends.

You also have a kitchen inside the house. The kitchen is where you make food. You use the kitchen to make breakfast, lunch, dinner, and sometimes a little food for the next day. You need to clean the kitchen after you are done eating.

A house can have many windows. Some windows open in the front to let the warm sun in in the morning. Some windows open in the back to let the cool air in at night. You can close the windows when it is cold outside to keep the warm air inside.

A house can also have a front door. The front door is the door that you enter with your keys and your face. When you come home, you use the front door. If you go out, you use the back door. You can lock the front door at night so you are safe.

Sometimes, houses have a small area outside. This area is like a green space in the middle of the house. This is where you play with your friends or go for a walk. You can sit on the floor or on a chair in this area.

Even though houses are often big, they are still a place where you live and feel safe. You can feel happy and safe when you are inside the house with your family and friends.
