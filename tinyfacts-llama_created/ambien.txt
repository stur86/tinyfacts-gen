To sleep is to rest. When you are tired, your body feels heavy and you need to be still. You might lie down or sit in a chair. Your eyes are closed and your breathing is slow.

When you do not sleep, you feel very bad. Your brain is still busy from the night before. Your body is ready to go to sleep. But when you are not sleeping, your brain stays busy. It is like a computer that is still working.

Some people take a thing called a sleep drink. This drink makes you feel like you are sleeping even though you are still sitting up. It is like a very quiet time for your body.

There is another thing that happens when you are not sleeping. Your body starts to build up a heavy thing. This heavy thing is like a big bag of water. Your brain tells your body to put this water in your body. This is why you feel full.

Sometimes, a person might feel this heavy bag in their chest. This is a sign that it is time to sleep. It is like the body is saying, it is ready to rest. The heavy bag goes away when you go to bed.
