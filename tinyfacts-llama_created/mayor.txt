A person who is in a big group of cities often helps them. They are the top person in the city. They make sure everyone follows the same things. They also decide what is best for the city.

This person usually lives in the city they help. They might be a man or a woman. They are very important because they help make the city a great place to live.

If a person is the one who helps a city, it is called a city. If a person helps a group of many towns, it is called a town. If a person helps many many small groups of people, it is called a state.

The person who is the top one in a city is called the city leader. They have power over the city until they decide to give power to another person. This is a very important job.
