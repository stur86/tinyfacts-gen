To spend something means to use money or other things you have to buy things you need. This can happen when you go to a store to buy food or clothes. You buy food for your body, so you have more money left for other things.

You can also spend money on things that bring you fun. Maybe you go to a game with your friends. You spend money on drinks and food so you can enjoy the game. This is an important way to spend money.

Sometimes, people spend money to help others. They might give money to a doctor if they are very sick. This is because the doctor can help them feel better. They are spending money to show that they care.

You can also spend money on things that you want, but you might not need right now. Maybe you want a new car, but you do not have enough money. You might have to wait or save your money for a better time to buy it.

To spend money is to use it to live. It is a way to use your money to buy things you like and need. It is a big part of how we live our lives.
