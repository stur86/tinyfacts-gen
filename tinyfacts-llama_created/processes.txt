A way to do something is called a thing that works. Every living thing and every thing that does not live has a way to do its job. You have a way to breathe. You have a way to eat food. You have a way to walk. You have a way to stop. Every way is like a long line of steps. One step after another. You follow the line and the thing works. If you break the line, the thing does not work anymore.

A computer is a thing that works. It uses many steps to do its job. It takes in numbers and letters. It looks at the numbers and letters. It finds answers. It writes answers on a paper. It uses many steps to change the answers into words. This way of making answers is called a thing that works. It helps us find the answers we need.

A group of people can also be a thing that works. When many people work together, they can do things that one person could not do alone. They can build houses. They can fix cars. They can help people. This way of working is called a group.

A thing that works can also be something that does not work. If a house does not let the air in and out, the people who live there will be sick. If a car does not go, people will not be able to go where they need to go. This is a thing that breaks and does not work anymore.

Sometimes, a thing that works can break and then become a thing that works again. If you fix a computer, it starts to work again. If you fix a house, the people who live there can live there again. This is a way of things that can change.

We also use the word to talk about how things move. When something moves, it follows the way of the line. If it goes too fast, it might stop. If it goes too slow, it might hit something. If it goes up, it might fall. If it goes left, it might go right. This is how we talk about how things work.
