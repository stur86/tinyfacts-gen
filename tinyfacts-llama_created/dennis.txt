To be a good person means to act with a kind heart and mind. A person who is a good person tries to do the right thing, even when it is hard. They listen when others talk and show they care. They are also very nice to their family and friends.

Dancing is a fun way to move your body. You can do it alone or with a group. You might jump up and down, or you might use your feet to make a sound. It helps you feel happy and free.

A computer is a thing that can do many jobs. It can help you look at pictures, play games, or talk to people from far away. It is like a very strong friend that works for you.

To be a good teacher is to help others learn. You might stand in front of a group and tell them what to do. You give them words to remember and help them understand. It is an important job because it helps people grow and become strong.

A dog is a kind animal that loves people. It often follows you around. Dogs are very good friends because they do not tell you to do things wrong. They just want to be near you and play.

To have a dream is to think about a place or a thing that is not yet real. Sometimes, these dreams are big and exciting. They might make us feel happy or nervous. A dream helps you know what is important and what you want to do in the world.

To be in a group means to be part of a group of people. You might work with them or play together. It is a good way to learn from others and feel happy when you are with friends.
