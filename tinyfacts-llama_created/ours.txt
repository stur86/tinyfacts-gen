Our means something is in our hands. If a tree falls down on a street, people might say, the street is our. We are the ones who are near it.

If you think about the word, it is not like a thing you own. You do not pick it up and say, I own it. You do not say, the book is mine. You say, the book is mine with me. You share it with others, but you are still the one who owns it.

We also have things that are not our own. The sky is over us. The sun is up. We do not own those things. We just live with them. When we say, the sky is our, we mean we live under it, but we do not own it.

Sometimes, when we talk about our things, we use the word with people. If you are the one who does something, you are doing it for yourself. If you do it for someone else, then you are our doing it.
