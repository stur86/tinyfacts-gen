To talk about something in an easy way is to use words that are not the same as the words you use when you talk in a group. This is called an expression. You can use this when you talk to a friend or when you write a letter.

Think about a word. If you say, the dog is very good, the word is the same as when you say the word in front of a group. But if you say, the dog is good, you are using a different way to say the same thing. You are talking about it in a way that is easy for your friends to understand.

This is why you might use these words instead of the other words. You want to be able to talk about things in your own way so that your friends can follow what you say.
