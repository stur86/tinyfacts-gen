To put something on a bed means to make it soft for people to sleep on. You often put a big thing on the floor with many soft pieces inside. This thing is what a person sits on while they sleep. It keeps their body warm and makes them feel safe.

A person might buy a new soft thing to put on their bed. They look for one with a very soft cover and a bottom made of hard wood. Some people like to sleep on their backs on the hard floor. Others like to sleep on their sides with their arms under them. They might have a big one for each side.

You can also use the word in other ways. If a doctor says a person needs a new soft thing to bed, it means they need a new bed to sleep on. This happens when a person has a big problem that makes it hard to sleep.

It is also used to talk about something that is soft and does not feel hard at all. A soft drink is a drink that is easy to drink because it feels good in your mouth. Your hair can feel soft if you brush it with a soft brush. A soft piece of paper can be a book that you read. A soft thing in a game can help you feel better if you are afraid or angry.
