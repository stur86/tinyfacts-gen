To counter means to do the same thing to the other person. When you counter, you are making your point or your words stronger than the other person. It is a way of showing that you are right.

Imagine you have a friend who wants to play a game. You both want to go to the same place at the same time. You could both go, but if you both go, you will miss out on fun. So, you might say, you are going, but I am going instead. You are counter to the other person because you are doing the same thing, but you are doing it in a different way.

You can also counter a bad idea. If someone tells you that something is wrong, you might counter by saying that it is not. This is a way of standing up for what you think is right.

Sometimes, you counter a plan. If a group of people want to start a new business, you might say, that is not a good idea. You are counter to what they want to do.

You can counter a joke too. If someone says, laugh at this, you might say, no, I am not laughing. You are counter to the joke.

To counter is to be one step ahead. It is to be ready for what the other person is going to do next. This way, you can stay safe or stay in the game.

To counter something is to stop it. If a person is being mean, you might counter by being kind. This way, you are stopping the mean act and keeping your own heart safe.
