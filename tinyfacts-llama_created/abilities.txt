An idea is a way of thinking about something. It is about how you can do things. You have many ideas about your life. You might think you are a great writer. You might think you are not good at this game. These ideas help you plan what you do next.

An idea is also about what you can not do. You might know you are not good at a game, but you still enjoy watching the game. You do not try to play, but you watch it and feel the story. This idea is not about being better, but about enjoying what you have.

Sometimes, people have many good ideas. They use them to change the world. A person might have an idea about how to make food better. They work hard and then they make a new food that people love. Another person might have an idea about how to help animals. They work and then they help many animals.

You also have ideas about how you want to be. You might want to be a friend. You might want to be happy. An idea is what you are working for. It is a long time of thinking and trying before you see the end.
