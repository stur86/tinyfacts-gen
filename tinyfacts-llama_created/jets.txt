To fly fast is to go very high in the sky. You might feel the ground far under you. The air pushes on you. You move from one place to another quickly.

Air goes around you all the time. When you go fast, the air around you moves around you in a different way. The air pushes harder on one side of you than the other. This makes you turn. You turn because you are pushing against the air on one side.

Some things fly fast. Cars, dogs, and small animals can go very quickly. They go over many houses and fields. Some of these things are very small. They can fly fast and carry people.

There are also big things that fly fast. They are much bigger than cars. They carry many people at once. They go very high into the sky. Some of these things are used to carry people to work or school. Others fly to places far away. They are like big flying cars.

When air moves around a thing that is not moving, it pushes on the thing. This is why things move. The air pushes the thing forward. This is why you can not just stand still on the ground. You have to move your feet or your body to stay up.

You can also fly fast when you use power. A person might use power from a box to go very quickly. They might wear a thing that goes fast over their body. This power makes the thing go very fast and keeps it up in the air.

Flying is fun. You can see many things from high up. The world looks different from up. You can see trees, houses, and big bodies of water. You can also see the sky. It looks blue and white when the sun is up.

Flying is also good for the air. When the ground is far away, the air gets cleaner. The air is not full of bad stuff that can hurt your body. You breathe in clean air when you fly over the ground.

Some people fly to places that are very far away. They might go across many bodies of water or fly over many pieces of land. These trips can take many days. Others fly to places in the sky. They go very high and stay in the air for a long time.
