To see something means to look at it with your eyes. You use your face to look at a wall or a piece of paper. If you are looking at a picture, you are seeing it. You are not thinking about what you are looking at. You are just looking at it.

A computer has a round thing in the middle. This thing is for people to type on. When you type words, the computer gets them and shows you the words on a big box of words. This box of words is like a big paper with many words on it. The computer can also show you pictures. You can see pictures of dogs, houses, or cars. The pictures are made by people who draw them.

The word you are looking at today is a computer thing. It is a very good way to show you many things at once. People use these things in their homes and in schools. They can look at many pictures, read many words, and do many other things at the same time.
