A word to feel good when you are sick or hurt.

You can feel this way when you have a cut on your hand. You want to stop it so it doesn't hurt more. You might put a cold drink on it or cover it with a band. This makes your hand feel less bad.

We also use this word for a doctor. The doctor is like a friend who helps you feel better. When you are very tired or sad, the doctor can help you feel better. They give you things that make you feel better.

Sometimes, a person feels this way when they are very angry. They might want to yell, but they know it is not good for them. They might do something else to calm down. This helps them stay safe and their body gets better.

This word is also used to talk about a feeling that makes you want to laugh. When you hear a funny joke, you might feel this way. You might smile or laugh because it is easy to be happy.

You can feel this way when you love someone very much. It means you care about them a lot. You want them to be happy and feel safe.
