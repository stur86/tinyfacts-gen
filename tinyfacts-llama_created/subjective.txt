To be a good friend is to care about someone. You want them to be happy. You want them to be safe. You want them to be kind to you.

But when you look at a person, you see them as they really are. You see their face, but you don't see how they feel. You see their clothes, but you don't see how they act. You see what they do, but you don't see how they feel inside.

This is why you can be a good friend even if you don't know everything about the other person. You can still care about them. You can still like them. Because you see them as a person, not just as a friend.

It is also about understanding. It is about not thinking you know everything. It is about being open to what another person has to say. It is about trying to see things from their point of side.
