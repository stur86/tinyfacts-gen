When you make a promise to someone, you are making a big deal. You are saying you will do something. You can be very strong about this. You might want to be the first one to finish, or you might want to be the last one.

There are many kinds of these. You can promise to be there for a friend. If your friend is going to a place you do not know, you are promising to go with them. You are a team. You help each other. You are doing this because you care.

You can also promise to help your family. If your mom is sick, you might say you will help her. If your dad is going to a work meeting that you do not want to go to, you might say you will go with them. You are showing you care.

Sometimes, you make a big promise to your work. You work very hard. You might promise to finish a big job on time. Or you might say you will not let a problem stop you. This is like a big thing you are ready to do.

You can also make a promise to your body. If you are feeling very sick, you might say you will eat more food. Or you might say you will stay up late to finish a work thing. This is about taking care of yourself.

Making a promise can be very important. It shows you are a person who can keep their word.
