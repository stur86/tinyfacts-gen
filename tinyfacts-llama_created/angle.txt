An edge and a corner are two things that come together. Imagine a piece of wood. The top part of the wood is the edge. The part in the middle is the corner.

Think of how a table is made. You start with a big piece of wood. You cut it into long pieces. You use these pieces to make the table. One of the long pieces has a corner. The corner is an edge. The table has many corners like that. Each corner is a point where two sides meet.

Think about a book. The cover of the book is the edge. The cover is the outside of the book. The hard part at the back of the book is the corner. The corner is a point where the cover and the back part meet.

Every time you look at a table or a book, you see an edge and a corner. They are the parts that tell you where the piece of wood or paper ends and where it starts again.
