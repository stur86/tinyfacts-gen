To a person, a person who is in a place far away and sends things to another place is called a person who is in a place far away and sends things to another place. But for things, the person is not in a place far away. They are in the middle.

Think of a box. The box has a door. When you put a thing in the box, you put it through the door. The person who puts things in the box is the one who sends them. They do not have to go to the door. The door is already there. The box is the person.

The box is like a middle person. It keeps things and sends them to other places. It does not have to go to every place at once. It does it one place at a time. This is why it is called a middle person.

Sometimes, a person who is in a place far away sends things to people who are in other places. The people in other places get the things and use them. The person who sends the things is the one who is in the middle. They are the people who keep the things going.
