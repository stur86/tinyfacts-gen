To get something out is to make it happen. If you write a story, the words are the things you get out. You write them on paper. You read them later when you sit down and look at them.

Think about a computer. You type a word into the box. The computer gets the word out and stores it in a place where you can look at later. If you type a picture, the computer gets it out of the box and shows it to you.

Sometimes, you get out things that are already there. If you have money, you already have it. If you have a phone, the phone already has the words inside it. The phone only makes them happen when you press a thing.

Sometimes, you get out things that are not there. If you write a story and no one reads it, the story is still there. It is still waiting to be seen.

To get something out can mean different things. You might say someone got out of a room. This means they left. You might say a person got out of a game. This means they did not lose. You might say a computer got out of a problem. This means it found a way to fix the trouble.
