To carry something means to put it in a way that it can be moved from one place to another. This is often done by people or animals. 

Imagine you are going to the city. You have to pack your bags. You put food, clothes, and other things you need for the day into a big bag. You also put your phone and other things you want to keep. When you are ready, you open the bag and you are gone.

Cars and big boxes also carry things. A car can carry many people and things. A box can carry a lot of small things like books or clothes.

Sometimes, people carry money when they go to a far place. This money helps them buy food and other things they need. It is like a way of moving money from one place to another.

In a store, the person who works there might carry many different things. They pick which things to show to the people who want to buy them. This is a way of carrying things to people.

You can also carry something in your hand. If you carry a book, you are carrying it with you. You can read it later or you can give it to a friend.

To carry something into a place is also to move it. If you carry food into your house, you are moving the food from the store to your home.
