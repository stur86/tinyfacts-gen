To put something into the mind

Think about a picture. You can see a picture in your eyes. But you also know the words that are on the paper. The words tell you what the picture is about. This is what we call a picture.

A picture can also be in your mind. If you hear the story of a person, you can picture them. You see their face, their hair, and their clothes. You remember what they look like.

A person can also put things in your mind. They tell you what to feel. They tell you to be happy, to be sad, or to be angry. They are like a kind friend who is always with you.

You can also put things into your mind that are not there. You might think about a time when you did something nice. You might feel happy because you remember that thing. The things you put into your mind are inside your brain.

Sometimes we put things into the mind that are not real. We might see a picture of a person who is not really there. Or we might hear a story that is not true. This can make us feel confused or sad.

But often, we put things into the mind that are real. A kind word from a friend can make you feel better. A good story can make you laugh. These things help us feel better when we are sad or worried.
