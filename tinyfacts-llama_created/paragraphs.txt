A part of a book is a line of words. Each line has many words. You can put many lines together to make a story, a talk, or a short piece of writing. This is called a part.

When you read these lines, you see a story. You can understand what is happening. You can see how things are, why they happen, and who is doing them. Each line is a small part of the story.

Sometimes these lines are very short. One word is enough to tell the story. Other times, there are many words. The words are short together. They go in a line and the line ends. This part of the book is short.

Sometimes you put many lines together. This is a story, a talk, or a short piece of writing. There is a beginning, a middle, and an end. You tell the story through many lines. You use words, words, and more words.

A part can also have a name. A story might have many parts. One part tells where the story starts. Another part tells what happens next. This is called the beginning, the middle, and the end.

When you read these lines, you can see how they work together. You learn something new. You learn about people, places, things, and how they are. This is the power of a part.
