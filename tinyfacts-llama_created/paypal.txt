Pay your friends money. You give them money and they give it to you. You do this many times. You can pay with a phone, a computer, or even a small thing that sits on your table. This thing is very fast. You can pay money to someone in a town far away in minutes.

When you pay with this thing, you tell the computer who you are and what money you have. The computer then takes the money and gives it to the person you wanted to pay. This is how you pay for things you want to buy.

There are two kinds of these things. One is for money that people give to each other. The other is for money that you keep in your own thing. You can send money from one person to another, but you also keep your money in your own thing. This way you do not have to give the money to everyone all at once.
