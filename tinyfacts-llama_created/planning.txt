To plan something means to think about it before you do it. You might plan how to get to work. You might plan how to do your work. You might plan a trip to a far away place.

You can also plan a game. You might plan the order of the moves. You might plan how many points you will get. This is so you do not forget important steps.

Sometimes, people plan for things that will happen later. They might plan a party for a big day. They might plan how to buy food and drinks. They might plan where to go and how to get there.

To plan also means to make a plan. You need to think about your life. You need to know where you want to go. You need to know how you will get there. You need to know what you will do when you get there.

You can plan your day. You can plan how you will spend your time. You can plan your work. You can plan your life.

Planning can be hard. You might feel worried. You might feel afraid. But it is important. It helps you feel ready for what is coming next.
