An attempt is when you try to do something. It means you decide to do it. You do not know if you will be able to do it, but you want to try anyway.

You might have an idea in your head. You want to run a long way or climb a high tree. You decide to do it. You start by doing small steps. You walk a short way, then you run for a short time. You keep going until you feel tired.

Even if you do not finish the whole way, you have done something. You have taken a step. You have shown yourself that you can do it. This is an attempt.

Sometimes you try to do something that is hard. You might try to build a house or to fly in a small box. You want to see if you can do it. You work very hard. You try to use your brain and your body. You do not give up, even if you feel like you are not doing well.

An attempt is important because it helps us learn. If we try something many times, we learn what we need to do. We learn how to be strong and how to stay calm. We learn that even when we do not finish, we still did something good.

If you attempt something and you do not get it, you might feel sad. But you can still try again. You can learn from your first attempt. You can use what you learned to try again. This shows that you are not afraid to try.
