When something happens, we use words to tell about it. 

First, we talk about what is happening. We say, for this moment, the sun is up. We say, the room is dark. We say, the dog is running. 

Then, we talk about what has already happened. We say, the sun is now down. We say, the room is bright. We say, the dog is in the house. 

When something happens, it is a new thing. It is not what has been before. We use the word because it is about something new. 

Think of a time you played with a ball. You did not play before that. You did not play at any other time. So, you say, that happened. 

You also say, something happened. This means something new is happening right now. You might hear a noise. You might see a person. You might feel a surprise. This is when the moment ends and a new moment starts.
