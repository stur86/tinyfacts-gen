To sweet is to feel very happy or to give a happy sound. When you are very happy, you might laugh and jump up in the air. When you are sad, you might want to cry.

You can also use the word to mean a small animal that jumps over things. It is very fast and can jump very high. Imagine a tiny dog that jumps from one floor to the next.

Sometimes, when you say something is sweet, you mean it is very nice. You might tell a friend that a book is sweet because it has nice words that make you feel happy. This word is often used to show you like something.
