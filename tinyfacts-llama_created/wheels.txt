Things that move around have round parts. These round parts go in a line. They turn while moving. When they turn, the ground moves too. This makes the thing move.

A car has many of these round parts. They are held inside the car. The round parts go around the outside of the car. When the car moves, the round parts go around the outside of the car many times. This makes the car go very fast.

A tree has round parts too. These round parts hold the leaves. The round parts go around the tree many times each day. The round parts help the tree hold onto water and air. This keeps the tree strong and growing.

The round parts of a thing can also be very heavy. When a heavy round part turns, the ground shakes. This is why people say a heavy round thing is loud.
