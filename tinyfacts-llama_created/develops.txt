To begin something is to start it. This is a very important part of life. When you start a thing, you set it in a new place. You put it on a table or under a bed. You tell it what to do, and it starts moving.

Think about your life. You were a small baby. Your mother brought you into the world. Your first steps were very hard. But then you learned to walk. You learned to talk. You learned to eat and drink. Every day, you became more strong and more sure.

A tree is another thing that begins to grow. It starts from a small part that you can not see. It drinks from the ground and makes tiny parts. These tiny parts join together. More and more parts grow. The tree grows taller and stronger. It becomes a tall green thing that gives you air.

You can also begin a new dream. You have a great idea. You tell yourself, I want to fly. You start to learn about flying. You watch a movie. You read books. You try to feel the wind in your hair. Slowly, you become better at flying. You feel the power in your body. You feel free.

A child starts school. The teacher tells them what to do. They sit at desks and listen. They learn to write and to read. They make new friends. They start to understand the world.

A person becomes older. Their hair turns brown. Their eyes become blue. They grow a face that is different from their mother. They learn to drive a car. They learn to pay money. They learn to keep money.

In all these cases, beginning means starting. It means you start to a new part of life. It means you start to a new dream. It means you start to learn.
