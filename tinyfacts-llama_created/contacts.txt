To use a phone is easy. You look at the phone, put your finger in the small line, and press the little round thing. The phone will talk to other phones. You can talk to your friends, talk to a doctor, or listen to music.

But if you want to talk to a person who is far away, you need something else. A phone does not work by yourself. You need something that is like a big box. This box is where people talk to each other when they are far from where they live.

The box is very strong and safe. Only one person can be inside at a time. If you talk to the box, you must say the right words. You must say the word that the person says when you call them. If you do not say the words right, the person you are calling can not hear you.

Sometimes you need to give money to the person inside the box. This money helps the person pay for what they need. You give the money, and the person takes it. You did not give them anything. They just took the money.

A box like this can be in your home, in a place you go to, or in a car. Some boxes are in a big building where many people live. Some boxes are in a small box in your hand. When you hold a box, you are holding a way to talk to people who are far away.

People use these boxes to stay in touch with friends, with family, or with people they care about. They can talk, laugh, and feel close even if they are many places away.
