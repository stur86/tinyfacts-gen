A person who listens to things is called a listener. They do not act, but rather hear what others say. They might listen to a teacher when they learn new things, or they might listen to a friend who wants to talk.

A person who sends things is called a giver. They give money, food, or other things to others. They do not act, but rather put the things they give into the hands of the people who need them.

A person who gets things is called a taker. They take money or food that has already been given. They do not act, but rather take the things they need from the hands of others.

The word also has a different meaning with computers. A computer is like a very fast group of people. When it gets a word, it reads it very quickly. It does not act, it just reads the word. The computer is like a big group of people that works together to do things very fast.
