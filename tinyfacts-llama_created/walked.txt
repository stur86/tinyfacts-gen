When you walk, you move your legs and your feet. You use your legs to push against the ground. This makes you go forward. You can walk on a soft floor, like a floor covered with soft hair, or you can walk on a hard floor. You can walk very fast if you want to go very quickly, or you can walk slowly if you need to go to a far place.

Walking helps your body. It keeps your feet and legs moving. It keeps your arms still. This helps you stay standing and keep your body straight. Walking is also good for your heart. It makes your heart go around and around. This keeps your blood moving and helps keep you strong.

Sometimes, when you walk, you see the world around you. You see trees, houses, and other people. You see the way the light falls on the ground and the way the air moves. You might hear sounds from far away, like a dog belling or a car driving down the street. Walking helps you see the world.

Walking is also an important way to feel safe. When you walk alone, you feel like you are in control. You know that you are the one who is moving and that you are not waiting for anyone else to do anything. This can be very good for your mind and your heart.

In some places, walking is a way to get to school. People walk to school in the morning to start their day. They walk through the streets and along the roads. They might pass by houses, shops, and other people. They might see animals and other things they do not remember.

You can also walk to a place you want to see. If you have a friend in a far city, you might walk to their house. You might walk for a long time before you reach your friend. You might see new things and meet new people along the way.

Walking is a way to be with yourself. It is a way to move your body and to see the world. It is a way to feel the sun on your skin and the wind in your hair. It is a way to be alone and to be by yourself.
