To break something into small pieces is to call it small. This happens to things that are big. If you look at a tree, it is big. You can break it into many pieces. Each piece is small.

You can also break a story into many small parts. You might start with one story and then add another, and another, and another. Each story is like one small piece of the story. When you put all the pieces together, you have the whole story.

Sometimes you break a hard thing to make it feel easier to hold. You might cut a rock into small pieces to make it less heavy. This can make it easier to carry or keep in a bag.

In some places, people use the word to mean a small group of people who work together. You might have a group of friends. You all work together to help one another. This is like breaking a story into many small parts. You are breaking a bigger idea into many smaller ideas.

We also say a person is small. This means they are not very big. You are small in the world. You are small in your family. You are small in your town.

To break something is to change it. You take something that is one way and you make it into something different.
