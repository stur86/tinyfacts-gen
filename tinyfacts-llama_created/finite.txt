To be a number is to have a number. You can have many numbers: one, two, three, four, five. You can also have many less numbers: less than one, less than two, less than three. Every number is a kind of piece of paper that tells how many times you can add one to it. If you start with one, you add one to it one hundred times, and you get two. If you start with two, you add one to it one hundred times, and you get three. If you start with three, you add one to it one hundred times, and you get four. And so on.

Every number has a number of parts. Imagine you have three numbers. The first number is one, the second number is two, and the third number is three. You can not have more than three parts in one number. This is what makes numbers different. You can have a number with one part, two parts, three parts, or more parts. But you can never have more parts than there are numbers.

Sometimes, people say that a number is a number when it has no parts. This is when a number is only one number. One is one number. Two is one number. Three is one number. This is a number because it has only one piece of paper, one line of numbers, one number, and one number.

When you are a number, you can not change. You stay the same. You can not add one to you and then change to another number. You stay one number, always one number. This is why numbers are important in our world. You use them to tell how many things you have, how many times you do something, and how much money you have.

Also, when you say a number is not a number, it is wrong. Numbers are all around us. You need them to build your world. You need numbers to talk about how many people are in a room, how many cars are on the road, or how many times you sleep.

In some ways, numbers are the same as things that have a number. A thing can have more than one number. A number can have many numbers. A number can be the same number many times. But a number is always one number. It is the only thing that is not a number.
