An out is something you use to send power from your home to the world outside. This is why your lights are on at night and your computer works during the day.

Think of a place where people go to get food and drinks. If you walk into a store, the people there have a place in the back where they keep things to power their lights, their computers, and other things. This place is the out.

When you are at home, you can use the out. You press a tiny piece on the floor, and a small line comes out. This line carries power from the power line in your house to the tiny piece. The power makes things work, like the light in your room and the computer on your desk.

Power goes out at night. The street lights go out when the power goes down. This happens because of the way the power lines work. When the power goes down, the tiny pieces stop sending power to the street lights. The street lights stop working, and the people who live there have to use their own lights.

In the morning, the power comes back up. The tiny pieces send power to the power line, and the power line carries power to the power place in the city. The power place sends power to all the houses and stores, so everyone can use the power during the day.

This is how we get power all over the world. Every home has an out, and every out has a power place. When you use your power, you are using the out.
