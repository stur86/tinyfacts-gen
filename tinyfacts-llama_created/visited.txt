To visited something means you go to it. You might go to a big city, a small town, or even a far place like the top of a high ground. When you visit a place, you see what it looks like. You look at the houses, the streets, and the things people do every day.

Sometimes, you might go to a place where people work. You go there to see how they make food, or how they build the ground. You see the big rooms, the small rooms, and the rooms with many people. You hear the sounds of the people and the sounds of the things they use.

You might also visit a place where people live. You see the houses, the streets, and the people moving around. You see how they are happy or sad. You learn about the people and their stories.

Sometimes, you go to a place that is very far away. You might go on a big car or a small thing that flies through the air. You see the sun, the stars, and the night sky. You feel the wind and the sun on your face.

When you visit a place, you learn about it. You learn how it works, how the people live, and how they feel. You remember the things you saw and the people you met. You might remember a funny thing that happened or a nice place you stayed.
