To be different means you are not like other people. You do things your way. You might wear clothes that are not the same as others. You might like a different color of food. You might have a different voice when you talk.

Some people like things that are very bright. Some people like things that are very quiet. Some people like things that are very fast. Some people like things that are very slow. You can be different in many ways.

Being different is okay. It means you are not afraid to be yourself. It means you are not trying to be like anyone else. You can be different and still be happy.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. You see someone who is very tall. You see someone who is very short. You see someone who is very light and someone who is very dark. You see someone who is very strong and someone who is very small. You are all different. And that is what makes our world so interesting.
