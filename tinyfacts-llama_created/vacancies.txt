A person or a thing has a job. When this job is done, there is a space. This space is called a place. When a place is empty, it is called a place. 

When a job is done, a person leaves. This is a time when there is a space. Sometimes this space is filled by someone who is waiting for the job to become open again. This person is waiting for a place. 

If a job is done and the space is filled, the person who filled the place is the one who has the job. They are the one with the job. They are the one who did the work. They get money for doing the work. 

Sometimes a place is empty for a very long time. This is a time when there is a space. People say, this is a time of waiting. They are waiting for the job to become open again.
