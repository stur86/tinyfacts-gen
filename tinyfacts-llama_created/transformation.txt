To change something is a big idea. You can change how you look. You can wear a new shirt. You can be a different person.

If you eat a bad thing, your body gets very sick. If you eat a good thing, your body gets better. This happens because the bad thing has bad parts that break down. The good thing has good parts that stay.

You can also change how you act. If you are angry, you might shout. If you are sad, you might cry. You can change your words. You can change how you talk to people.

Sometimes you change your mind. You might decide to do something that you never wanted to do before. This is a big change. It is like a door opening. You get out of the old way into a new way.

You can also change the world around you. You can build a new house. You can change the way a city is built. You can use your words to help people.

But a change can also be bad. You can change a person you love. You can change a place you live in. You can change a way of life that makes you very sad.

In life, you need to change. You need to move from one place to another. You need to learn new things. You need to be ready for the new things that come up.
