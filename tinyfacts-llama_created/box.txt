A box is a thing that you hold things in. You can make one from wood or from paper. It is often round and has four sides. Inside, you can put many things. A small box is a very small one. A big box is one that can hold a lot of things.

You can give a box to a child. They might keep it for a long time. Or, you might give it to a person who wants to carry things. Sometimes, a box is used for moving people or things that go over water.

A box can also be a place in your mind. Sometimes, you see a box that looks like a real one. But it is not real. It is only a picture you make in your head.

If you drop a box, it can break. You might feel sad because things are falling down. But, you can put the pieces together and make a new one. This is how people build houses and make new things for themselves.
