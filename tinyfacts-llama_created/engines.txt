An old word for something that makes things move.

You know how cars move around? The car has a round thing inside it that makes a lot of power. This power comes from the power inside the ground. The round thing is like a very strong hand that pushes through the ground and makes the car turn. This is an inside job of the car.

But there are other kinds of things that make things move. Some of these things are big and sit on the ground. They push out air and make the air go fast. They are like a very strong hand that pushes through the air. They make things go up and down. They make the ground shake. They make the wind go around. They are the big things that make our world move.

The power inside the round thing of the car is very different from the power inside these big things. The round thing uses the power of the ground. The big things use the power of the air. They need to push air through a big round thing to make the power. The round thing is much smaller and uses a lot of power.

Both of these things need a lot of power to work. The round thing needs to push through the ground a long way. The big things need to push air through a big round thing a lot of times. Without both, nothing would happen.
