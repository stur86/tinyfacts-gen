To be a bit smaller is to have a tiny bit less. You might have a small dog. This dog is not as big as a big dog.

A thing that is very small can be very good. It is like a tiny piece of paper. This paper does not block the light. It lets the light go through and is not heavy.

You can also say a person is a bit of a problem. This means they have a small problem they are trying to fix. It might be a bit of a fight, a bit of a worry, or a bit of a bad idea.

Sometimes you might see a small thing in the ground. This is a tiny bit of green stuff. It is like a tiny bit of food that grows in the ground. It is very small but it is important for our world.

To be a bit of a thing is to be a small part of a larger group. Think about a group of friends. One person is the leader. Everyone else follows him. He is the one who decides what to do. We all work together and follow him.

In a story, a small thing can be a good thing. Maybe a small girl saves a small animal from a big animal. She is a good friend and a good person.
