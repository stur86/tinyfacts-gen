To directions something means to show you where to go. When you drive a car, you need to know where to turn the corner or where to cross the street. You are the one who tells the car what to do.

A computer does this too. It has a set of words that tell it what to do. If you type a word, the computer reads it. Then it follows the set of words to find the right answer. It does not think for you like a person does. It only follows the words.

You can also say a computer is doing something. If a computer is running a game, it is doing the work. It is not thinking about what to do, it is just doing it.

Sometimes, you send something to a person. You write their name and their phone number on a piece of paper. You then give the paper to the person, and they take it and follow the steps you told them. They do not think about the paper, they just follow what you say.

The word can also mean to send something to a place. If you send a letter, you are giving it to a person. They get it and then they go to the place. They do not think about the letter, they just carry it to the right place.

In a world where many computers can talk to each other, we use this word to show how they work together. We use it to say that a computer is following a set of directions. We say it is doing the work.
