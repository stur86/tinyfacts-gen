To be a person who is in a group is often easy. But a person who is a name in a book is not so easy. This person is the one who keeps the book. They write down each name and when the person comes in and out. They keep the order of who is there.

Imagine you have a big line of people going in and out of a store. You keep track of each person so you know who came first. You would write their names down. This person does the same thing for a whole town or city. They keep the order of who lives there so everyone knows who is first and who is last.
