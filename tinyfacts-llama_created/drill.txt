To put a thing in a place is to use it. You might put a small round thing into a hard stuff in the wall. This makes the hard stuff hold more air. You use a long hard thing with a round end. You push the round end into the wall. The round end goes into the hard stuff and the hard stuff holds the air. You can then pull the round end out. The air is now in the wall. You have put the round thing where it is needed.

You can use this word for many things. You put a big round thing in the ground to make a road. You put a round thing in a piece of wood to make a box. You put a round thing in a car to make it go very fast. The round thing is what you use to put the thing where it needs to be.
