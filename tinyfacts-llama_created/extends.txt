To go out into something is to go out into a world. When you say a thing goes out, you mean it starts to happen. If a dog goes out, it means it comes out of its house and walks around. If a child goes out, it means they leave their home.

When you say a thing is going out, you are talking about how it is becoming something else. Think about a ball. When you hold the ball in your hand, it is inside the hand. Then, if you throw the ball, it goes out into space. The ball is going out of the hand. This is an important way to talk about things that move.

Sometimes, to go out means you go to a place where many people are. You might go to a big city, a place with many tall houses and many cars. Or you might go to a place where many animals live, a land with green trees and big water. Going out is about moving from a place you are in to a new place.

You can also use this word to talk about how things change. Imagine you have a thing that is old. It has been around for many years. One day, it starts to break. The old thing becomes a thing that is new. This is an important part of how things change.
