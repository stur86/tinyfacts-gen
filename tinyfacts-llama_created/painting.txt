Painting is when you make a picture on a big piece of paper or on a wall. You use colors from a box or from the sun to make the colors. You also use a thing with hard stuff to cover the paper. This makes the picture look like the world.

You start with a clear thing. You put the clear thing on the paper. Then you add colors one by one. Each color is different, so when you put them together, the picture looks very good. You can make it look like the sky, the water, the ground, or many other things.

You can also paint things that are not on paper. You can paint a car, a house, or a tree. You can make them look old or new, or make them look like they are in a far away place.

There are many ways to paint. Some people paint with their hands, and some people use a big thing to make the colors. Some paint with a brush, and some paint with a small thing that you hold.
