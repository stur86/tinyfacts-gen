To carry a heavy thing is to put it on your back. You put it there so it does not fall. You might carry a bag of food for a child, or a box of books for a teacher. When you do this, you are taking on more work than the thing can carry. You are the one who has to hold it.

Think about a tree. If you pull on the tree, it will fall down. You are the one who is pulling. The tree is the one with the heavy stuff on it. You are the one who has to hold it. If you do not hold it, it falls down and breaks.

We can also carry a feeling. If you are very angry, you might feel like you want to hit something. But you do not hit anything. You carry the feeling inside your body. You do not leave it out on the floor. You keep it inside so you do not hurt anyone else.

Sometimes, you carry money for a long time. You put it in your pocket or in your bag. You do not want to give it away, but you know you need it later. You carry it so you can buy food, clothes, and other things you need.

To carry something is to hold it. It is to keep it on your back.
