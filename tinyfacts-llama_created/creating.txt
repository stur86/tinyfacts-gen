To make something new is to bring a thing that already is into a new form. When you draw a line on paper, you are making a new piece of paper. You are not keeping the old paper. You are starting with nothing and turning it into something new.

When you build a house, you are also making a new thing. You start with a few small pieces of wood and a strong piece of glass. Over many days, you use those pieces to build a tall, strong house where people can live. You take the old and put it into a new form, so it has a new place.

You can also make something from nothing. You might start with a small rock and a piece of wood. You take the rock and break it into small pieces. Then you put those small pieces into a strong box. The box has a cover and a top. You can fill it with more small pieces of rock and wood. When you are done, you have a new box with many small pieces that were never there before.

Making something new can be hard. You might try to build a house and the pieces do not go together. You might try to draw a line but it is not straight. But you can still try again. You can try to use more of the pieces or to make the pieces better. You can also try to find a way to make the pieces fit together so that the house is strong and safe.

Making something new can also change the world. Imagine a world where everything is the same. There are no new pieces of wood, no new lines drawn on paper, no new houses built. If you were to make everything new, people would not know each other. They would not learn from each other. They would not grow. They would not change. So we need to make new, so we can stay strong and keep moving forward.

In a way, making something new is like a story. You start with nothing and end with a new story. You take the old and turn it into a new story. You are always starting a new story. You are always making something new.
