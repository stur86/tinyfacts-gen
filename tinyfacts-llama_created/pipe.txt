A line from a big thing can carry water, air, or food. It is usually made of hard stuff that does not break easily.

You might see a line coming out of a house or a car. This line is the same for everyone in a city. It goes from the house or car to the street. People can walk along it, drive a car on it, or use a thing that carries water to drink.

If water comes out of a line, you can use it to drink. You can also use it to fill a cup, a bag for food, or a thing that holds your hair.

In a place where people do not have a big water thing, a line can help. It brings water from far away to people who need it. This makes life easier, especially in a city with many people.

A line can also be about a thing that is not a living thing. It is a long, straight thing that goes through the ground. It helps make the world feel like one big room. Without these lines, we would not have water, air, or food for everyone.
