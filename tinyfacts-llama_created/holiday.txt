A time to be away from work and school.

You know how you go to a school or work and spend your days in a place that is not your home? In those days, you might have a few days off. You could go to a park with your friends, or watch a movie, or play games. This time is very fun.

Sometimes, when you are not in school or work, you go to a place with many other people. You go to a city or a town with many stores and food places. You can buy things you want or eat food that you like. You can also go to a place with a big water. You can go to the water, or you can go to a place with a big room for music and dancing.

The word is used to talk about these times. If you go to a place to be away from work, you are going on a break. If you go to a place with many other people, you are going to a party. If you go to a place with a big water, you are going to the water.

These times are usually in the summer or the year when the days are very long. This is why they are called a time to be away from work and school. It is a time to have fun with your friends and to remember happy times from the past.

We also have a word to talk about these times. We say that the day is a day off. Or we say that the week is a week off. This is because these times are a break from the usual day.

So, a time to be away from work and school is a time when we leave our homes and go to a different place. We do not have to do work or go to school. We go to see friends, to eat food, or to go to a big room with music and dancing. It is a time to have fun and remember happy times.
