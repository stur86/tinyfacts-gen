To did something means you act on a plan. You follow a plan made by someone else. This plan might tell you what you should do. You follow the plan, and then you do what the plan says.

Imagine you want to go to a party. You did not go. You did not plan to go. You did not follow a plan. You did not follow what the person in front of you said to do. You did what you felt like. When you follow a plan, you act on what is written down. When you did something, you did it because you followed a plan that someone else made.

Sometimes, you did something by chance. You did not plan to do it. You did it because you were there. You did not follow a plan. You did not follow what someone else said. You followed your own mind. This can be good or bad. It is good if you did something because you wanted to, like if you did something you loved. It is bad if you did something because you were afraid or because you wanted to be alone.
