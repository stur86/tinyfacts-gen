A thing that flies very fast is called a fly. You can see it because it makes a long line of water around it. The water goes up and over the top, and the air goes under.

When you push air through this water line, it makes the line go very fast. You need a strong push to make it move. The push comes from the power of the thing making the push.

The air that the thing flies through is moving very fast too. When air is moving fast, the air around it is moving fast too. This makes the water line around the thing go fast. When the water line goes fast, the water goes fast around it.

The air under the water line is under a lot of force. The force is like a big pull that wants to pull everything down. But the water line and the air above it push back hard. This makes the air and water move in the same way, and they stay close together.

When the air and water move fast, they can make a lot of noise. This is why the air looks blue when it is moving fast. Blue air is the air that is moving very fast. The blue looks like the sky on a hot day.

Sometimes you see a long line of fast moving air under the sky. This is a line of fast moving air that goes across the sky. It is like a big line of water, but it is made of air. It goes very fast and can cover a lot of ground in one second.
