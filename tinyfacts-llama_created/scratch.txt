To make a mark on something

You can make a mark on a piece of paper with a piece of paper. You put something hard on top of the paper. The hard thing makes a line or a small round spot. You can do this with your fingers or with a hard thing that is not living.

You can also do this with your skin. If you press your skin on a wall, you make a mark on the wall. The mark tells you that something happened on the wall. If you press your skin on a piece of paper, you make a mark on the paper.

Sometimes, you make a mark on a thing that is not a piece of paper. You might use a small thing that is not living to make a mark on a wall. If you use this thing on a wall, the mark can be very small or very big. If you use it on a piece of paper, the mark might be on top of the paper.

You can also make a mark on your face. If you rub your face, you make a small red spot. This spot tells you that you just touched your face.
