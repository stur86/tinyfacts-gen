A book is something you read. It has many stories and words. You can read it when you have time.

A book is made of paper. It is very long and has many sides. Each side has words and pictures. You can turn the paper to see what is written on each side.

You can hold a book in your hands. You can carry it with you. This helps you carry many books if you go to school or work many days.

Sometimes you read a book before bed. You sit in a quiet room and look at the words. This can help you remember things. You can also learn new things from books.

There are also books that you buy. You buy them from a store. You can pick which book you want. Some books are for kids. Some are for grown people. You can also buy books about animals, food, or money.

When you read a book, you learn about the world. You learn about people and places. You might even learn how to write your own stories.
