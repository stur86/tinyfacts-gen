To be ready is to be not afraid. If you are not ready, you look sad. You might look like you want to run away. You are ready when you feel safe. You do not want to worry. You are ready when you are ready.

You can be ready for many things. You might be ready to go to school. You are ready to learn new words. You are ready to meet your friends. You are ready to do a fun job. You are ready to see a movie.

You can also be ready for the night. You might want to sleep. You might want to drink a glass of water. You are ready to rest. You are ready to wake up early in the morning.

In a funny way, you are ready when you have the right clothes. You do not want to wear a bright shirt. You want to wear a shirt that is dark. You are ready to go out. You are ready to see what happens next.

Being ready is important. It helps us feel safe and happy. It helps us to do what we want to do without a big worry in our head.
