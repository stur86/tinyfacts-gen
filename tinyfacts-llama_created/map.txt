A long time ago, people made pictures of the world. These pictures were called world pictures. They showed how far the world was from one place to another. They also showed the water and the big wide places between them.

Today, we use something different. We make many, many of these pictures. We put them on a big round thing. This round thing is like a big ball. We can see the whole world at once. We can see the water and the big wide places, and the land, and all the small places between them.

We use the round thing to move around. We put a small round thing on the top of it. This round thing has numbers on it. The numbers tell us where we are. We can see where we are next.

Sometimes, we use the round thing to find our way. We look at the numbers and follow them. We walk along the numbers and see where they lead us. It helps us find the place we need to be.

We also use the round thing to learn about the world. We look at the pictures and learn about the land, the water, and the people who live there. We can see how the world is set up. We can learn about the people and the things that make them happy.

In short, a world picture is a picture of the world. It shows you where everything is. Now, we use something called a round thing to move around the world and to learn about it.
