When you listening, you are paying attention to what someone is saying. You are making a group of things one after another. This is often done by writing or by talking.

Think of a listened conversation. You are not just hearing a single word. You are hearing many words that are all together. You might hear a story, a plan, or a plan for a game. The person is showing you what they think about something. They are sharing their ideas.

In a listened conversation, you might feel happy because you are learning something new. You might feel glad because someone is talking about something you care about. You feel like you can follow the story and understand what they mean.

A listened conversation is different from a spoken conversation. In a spoken conversation, you hear one person and then another. In a listened conversation, you hear the person before you. You listen before they start talking. This helps the person know how you feel and what you want to hear.

In life, you can listen to others. You listen when a friend is talking about something important. You listen when a teacher explains a problem. You listen when someone shares their story. This helps you understand their point of mind and feel their way.

To listen is to pay attention. It is to not just hear the words, but also to understand what the person means. When you listen, you are showing you care about what the other person is saying.
