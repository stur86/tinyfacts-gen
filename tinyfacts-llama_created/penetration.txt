To go under something means to go through it. When you do this, you are moving from one side to the other. The world is made of many hard things that cover the ground. If you walk under these hard things, you are going under them. This can be a long way or a small space. It is different from going around them.

Sometimes, you might try to go into a room or a building. This is when you go under the door. You push the door open and walk through it. You are now inside the building. You can see what is happening inside.

In a different way, you can also say that something is going under. If a person has been acting well, they might go under the floor. This means they are hiding behind a piece of wood or a wall. They might feel afraid or they might feel the ground is shaking. If you go under something, you are going down.

You can also use this word when a thing goes into the ground. When a tree falls down, it goes under the ground. Then the tree is hidden from us. The ground is like a big cover that holds everything. It keeps us and everything else down.
