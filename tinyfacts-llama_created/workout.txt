To work out means you move your body so your body gets stronger. You might go to a big room where many people work out at the same time. Or you might do this at home with a small box or a chair. You do things to your arms, legs, and chest.

You might run, jump up and down, or lift heavy things. You might also do things that make your heart beat fast. Some people work out in the cold to get warm. Others work out when they are very tired so they can sleep later.

When you work out, you feel better. Your face might look nice and your hair might feel soft. Your body might feel like it has a big hug from the inside. You feel strong and can do many things.

Some people work out to stay fit. They want to look like they are in good form. Others do it because they feel good. They like the feeling of their body moving and the way they feel after they are done.

To work out is to build up your power. You are using your body to do things that make you strong. It is a way to stay ready for things that might happen later.
