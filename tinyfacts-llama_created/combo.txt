A group of things together can be called a group. It is a way to show many things at once. Imagine you have many friends. You are all part of one group. You do not have to be all friends. You can be friends with some and not friends with others. You are still in the same group.

You can also have a group with only one person. This person is the leader. They help all the other people in the group. This is a group of one. You can have many groups together. Sometimes, one group is much bigger than another.
