To change something means you make it different from what it was. You can change a thing that is small, like a ball, or a thing that is much bigger, like a house.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it to your friend. The ball goes far away. You want it to go back to you. So, you use your arms and legs to move the ball. You use your hands to push it and your feet to pull it.

You are changing the ball. You are making it go in a different way. You are not keeping it the same.

You can also change a person. If you do not like someone, you might say you are angry. This means you are not happy with them anymore. You want them to stop being their bad self.

You can change your mind too. If you think something is wrong, you can change your mind. You might decide you like it instead. This is a good thing. It means you are listening and you are learning.

Sometimes, we change when we grow. We learn new things and we get older. Our bodies also change. Our hair turns brown, our skin becomes softer, and we get taller. This is all part of how we become who we are today.
