A light box is a thing you use to write words or numbers. It has a front that is bright and a back that is not bright. You turn the box around so the bright side is facing out, and people can see what you have written.

The word comes from the word light and box. It means that the box can hold light, and when you turn it, the light is coming out of it.

Some light boxes are very big. They sit on a table and hold hundreds of numbers. Other ones are small and can hold only one number at a time. You can write a lot of numbers on a small one.

When you use a light box, you press a key. This makes the light inside the box turn on. You write your words on a paper and the paper goes into the box. Then the box gives you the word. It is like a computer, but it does not use a computer part.

There are many kinds of light boxes. Some are for writing. Some are for showing pictures. Some are for doing things that do not need to be written. Some are for things that do not need to be seen at all.
