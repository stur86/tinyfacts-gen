To make a case means to show that something is true. This is often done by people who are in a fight about a thing they both want. One person might say that they have something and that they should keep it. The other person says that they want it and should get it.

When you make a case, you must tell the truth. You can do this by saying what happened and what you think. You may also use what you know about the thing to help your side. This shows that you believe what you are saying is right.

Sometimes you make a case about money. If you feel that you were wrong in a fight, you can say that you are the better person. This can help you get what you want. It is like being the one with the right story.

Making a case can be hard. You must think about what really happened and how it feels. You might feel sad, angry, or surprised. You must be ready to talk and to show that your side is right.
