To reason means to think and find out why something is true. When you see a thing, you might ask, why is this? You try to find the answer by thinking about it. You look at what happened and what you know.

Sometimes you might have a question that you can't answer right away. You look for a way to find out. You might ask a friend, a family person, or a teacher. You might look at books or movies. You might even try to think about your own life.

A person who is good at this is called a reasoner. They can find the right answer to a question. They use their brain to see why things are true. They do not just accept what someone says. They ask, why? They want to know the truth.

You can also reason with a group of people. If you see that something is wrong, you can use your brain to find out why. You look at what others say and what you know. You might find a way to change things so everyone is happy.

Reasoning helps us make good ideas. When you reason, you do not just follow what someone says. You think about what you know and what feels right. You find a way that is good for everyone. This is an important part of how we live our lives.
