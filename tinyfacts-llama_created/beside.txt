To be beside someone means to be near them. You are not in the same room. You are right next to them. 

Imagine you are eating a big hot dog. You sit down at a table with your friends. One friend is sitting across from you. They look at you and say, I see you. You are beside them. You are right next to them.

You can also be beside a thing. If you have a book, you can sit next to it. Or if you are in a dark room, you might feel the light from the window beside you. In this case, you are not in the same room as the light, but you are still near it.
