To wonder is to feel a kind of surprise at something you see or hear. You look at a bright light in the sky. You feel like you should not have seen it. You feel very glad that you saw it. You might also feel a little scared because something is moving very fast.

Imagine you are in a room with a big box on the floor. Inside the box is a ball that is very heavy. You have never seen a heavy ball before. You try to lift it. Your arms are heavy. You are not sure if you can do it. You wonder if the ball is as heavy as you think it is.

You wonder about many things. You wonder why the sun is always in the sky. You wonder why there are so many stars at night. You wonder how things move. You want to know how the world works.

When you wonder, you do not have an answer yet. You are thinking about the thing. You are looking at it with your mind. You are not sure if the answer is there. You keep thinking about it until you get a feeling that something is right.

You can also wonder about things that are not here. You might wonder about a far away place where it is very hot and very cold at the same time. You wonder about a world that is very high up in the sky. You wonder about the deep water where the light from the sun does not reach.

Wonder is important. It helps us learn. It helps us grow. It helps us be glad when we see something new. It is a way to feel the world around us.
