To send someone or something

When you want to get something to someone, you must tell them where to pick it up. You write the name of where the person is and the time they should come. You also tell them what to bring. This is called a send. If you want to send something, you use words. You might say, you are sending a book to your friend. You tell her where the book is and when she should get it.

The word send comes from a long time ago. A man with a gun would tell a group of people to go and get something from a far place. They would go and then come back. This was a way to get things done quickly.

You can also send a person. Imagine a police car. The police tell the driver to go to a place where a bad person is. The driver goes there and they help keep everyone safe.

Sometimes you send money to someone. This way, the money reaches the person you want it to go to.

If you send a picture, you send it from your phone to someone else. You tell them your phone number so they can pick it up. They then see the picture.
