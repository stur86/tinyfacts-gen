To notice something is to pay attention to it. When you notice something, you do not ignore it. You look at it and think about it. This can happen with many things. If you are sitting in a room with many people, you might notice a small sound from a phone. You might hear the person say a word. You look at the person who said the word. You are watching them.

Sometimes, noticing is about how you feel. If you are in a room and you see a person who looks sad, you notice their face. You see that they are sad. You do not just walk by. You stop and look. This helps you understand how the person is feeling.

You can also notice things in your own life. If you are eating a food, you notice the smell. You notice how the food feels in your stomach. You notice the sound of the food when you eat it. These small moments are part of what makes life interesting.

When you notice something, you are not just looking at it. You are paying attention. You are thinking about it. You are trying to understand what is happening. This is important because it helps us learn and grow. By noticing things, we can build our world and our lives.
