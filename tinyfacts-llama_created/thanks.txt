To thank someone means you want them to be happy because you did something good for them.

Imagine you see a friend who is sad. You want them to feel better. You do something nice for them. Maybe you give them a drink or tell them you are sorry. When they say thank you, you are happy. You feel your heart feel bigger.

If you do something for a family, they thank you. If you help someone move, they thank you. They say you are a good person. This means you did something nice for them and they are glad.

You can also thank a teacher. They help you learn how to read and write. You feel happy because they are there to help you. If a doctor helps you feel better when you are sick, you thank them. They did something good for you.

When you thank someone, you are showing that you care about them. It is a way to say you are glad they are there and you like them.
