A group of people living in one place is called a group. You might see many houses, cars, and people going around. This many people in one place is called a group.

A group can be small, like a family of four. Or it can be very big, like a city with many, many houses and cars. Sometimes, people move to a new place. This can change how many people are in that group.

When you talk about a group, you might say how many people are in it. If the group has five hundred people, you can say that group has five hundred people. You can also say how many people are in each house, or how many cars are there.

A group can also change over time. New people might move in, or old people might die. This makes the group grow. It can also get smaller as people move away.
