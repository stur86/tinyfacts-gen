To enjoy something means you are happy and you like it. You can use this word with many things.

One time, a person likes to eat food. If they eat something and they feel very full, they say they are enjoying it. They are happy that they are eating the food. This word helps us talk about things that are nice to us.

Another time, you might say you are in a movie. If you watch a movie with a great story and the pictures are very nice, you are enjoying it. You are not looking at the wall or you are not thinking about your work. You are just watching the story.

Sometimes, to say you are enjoying something can mean you are afraid. If you are afraid of a big dog, you might say you are enjoying the feeling. But if you are afraid of a big dog, you are not happy.

You can also say you are enjoying a game. If you are playing a game and you are very good at it, you are enjoying the game. You like the way you move on the ball or the way you hit the ball with your hands.

To enjoy something is to like it. It is to feel happy and to want to do it again.
