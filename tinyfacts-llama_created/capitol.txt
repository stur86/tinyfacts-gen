The word you are looking for is the top part of a building where people go to make big things happen. This is where the leader of a city or a state lives. It is the place where the people of the city or state can go to talk about what they want to change.

Imagine a small school. The top part of the building is like a high chair. This is where the teacher sits and makes sure everyone follows the plan the teacher set. When people go to this top part of a building, they are going to the place where the leader makes the big changes. They talk to the leader about how to fix things, or they can talk to the leader about why they want to change the plan.

The word comes from a time when a man had a hard top part on his head. He would wear it when he was the top of a group of people. Later, people started using this word to mean the top part of a building where a group of people go to make big things happen.
