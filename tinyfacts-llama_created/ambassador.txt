An important person who helps two different groups. They meet to talk and find ways to stay nice. They are like a middle person.

Imagine you have a friend who lives far away. You want to talk to them, but your mom does it first. The mom is like the first person. She says things and helps your friend understand you better. Then the friend comes and talks to you. This is like the mom and the friend.

An other person is like this. They go to a place where they do not live. They go to meet people who do not speak the same words. They learn and find ways to be nice. They help everyone feel better.

Sometimes these people do not speak the same words. They use words that are not the same as each other. They use words to mean the same thing. This helps everyone understand each other.

An important person can be an old friend, a teacher, or a person who works for a big group. They help people from different places feel safe and happy. They are like a good friend who helps people from far away.
