An answer is a word or number that helps us know how many things are in a group. It is a way to tell how big something is.

Think about money. If you have ten little pieces of money, you have ten. If you have more, you have more. If you have less, you have fewer.

An answer can be a number. You can ask for how many books you need to buy. The answer is ten. It helps you know how many to get.

It can also be a number that shows how much something is. If you buy a bag that is three hundred and twenty five, you know you spent three hundred and twenty five little pieces of money.

Sometimes, you might ask how much something is. You do not know, and you ask the person. They say, you have to pay one hundred and ten. You pay the money, and now you know what the answer is.

An answer is also how you show you are ready. If you want to go, you ask the person if you can go. They say yes, and you go. The answer is yes, and you are ready to go.

So, an answer is a word or number that helps us know how many, how much, or how ready we are.
