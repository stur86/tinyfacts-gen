To be a person who studies how the world works is to be a person who uses things to learn. This kind of person looks at the world and asks, why? They try to find the answers to this question by doing things and looking at what happens.

Some people study the way the sky works. They look at the stars and try to figure out where they are and how the world looks at night. They use things like computers and pictures to help them understand.

Other people study how things grow. They watch the green things grow from the ground and try to figure out how they make more of themselves. They ask, how do these green things grow and how can we help them grow more?

Some people study how people work. They look at how the body works and how the mind works. They try to figure out why people feel happy and why they do things. They want to know how to help people feel better when they are sad or scared.

The word is important because it means that you are using your brain to learn about the world. You are not just taking things at face. You are asking questions and trying to find the right answers. This kind of work helps us understand how to live in the world and how to keep it safe for everyone.
