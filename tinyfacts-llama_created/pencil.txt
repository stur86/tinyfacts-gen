A small writing thing you use to write or draw. It is made of a hard white stuff that is not hard like a rock, but hard like paper. You put the white stuff in a small box and then you turn the box around to make it easy to hold. When you press the white stuff onto paper, it makes lines. You can write with it. You can also use it to draw pictures. Many people use them for school because they are easy to carry and you can do a lot of work with them.
