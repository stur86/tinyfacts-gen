To be a part of the brain means you help it work. Your brain is like the head of a computer. It takes in what you see, hear, and feel. It keeps you going. But your brain also needs help from other parts of your body.

Inside your brain, there are many tiny lines. These lines are like very long roads with many little places on them. These places are where you think. When you see a bright light, your brain tells your eyes to look at it. When you feel a pain, your brain sends a word to your body so you know what to do.

The long roads have many parts. Some parts help you move. Some parts help you feel. Some parts help you think. When these parts work well, you can learn and remember things. You can also feel happy or afraid. Your brain is a very good computer, and when all the parts work together, you can do many things.

But the brain is not the only thing that makes you. You have other parts in your body that help you breathe, eat, and move. These parts work together with the brain. Without the brain, you would not be able to do anything at all. And without the other parts, the brain would not be able to do its job either.
