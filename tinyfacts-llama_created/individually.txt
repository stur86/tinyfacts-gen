To act alone means you do things without other people. You do what you want, when you want. You do not follow anyone. You make your own way.

Imagine you have a box of food. You eat all the food. You do not share it with anyone. You keep it all to yourself. This is acting alone.

If you have a friend and you do something, you act with them. You share the food and the fun. You do it because you like each other. You follow the person who told you to do it. This is acting together.

Sometimes you act alone because you feel safe. You do not need to ask anyone. You do what you think is right. You do not have to follow anyone else.

You can also act alone when you are scared. You stay in a room because you are afraid. You do not want anyone to see you. You keep quiet and do not move.

Sometimes, people act alone when they are sad. They might cry or they might be very quiet. They do not want anyone to see them because they feel alone.
