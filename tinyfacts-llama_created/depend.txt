To have something you need is to be able to do it. When you say you need something, you mean you are not strong enough to do it by yourself. You need help.

Think about a child. A child can not run very fast. The ground is hard. They need a chair to sit. They do not have a chair. They ask a grown person for help. They sit on the chair while the person does the work.

You also have something you love. This is a feeling inside you. When you love someone, you want them to be happy. You want them to be safe. If you love a place, you want people to go there and be happy. If you love a book, you want to read it many times.

You can also say you need money to buy things. This is when you need money to buy food, a bed, or a car. If you have money, you can buy what you need. If you do not have money, you are in trouble.

Sometimes, you need to wait. If you want to see a movie, you have to stay in the house until the movie starts. You do not leave until the movie is finished. This is waiting.

To have something you need is important. It helps you live a good life.
