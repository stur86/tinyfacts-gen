A player is a person who does things with a ball or a piece of wood. They move around and try to hit a ball with a hard thing on the end of their hand.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You are the one who gets the ball. You run around the field. You try to catch the ball when your friend drops it. This game is for fun. People also play with many friends or with a group. They all play together and try to do the same thing at the same time.

A person can be a player if they do things on the field. They might have a job that is like this. They work with a team to get a thing done. They might run around and try to get the thing before it goes to another person.

In games like the one you play with your friends, there are many people. Some are the ones who get the ball. Some are the ones who try to stop them. Some are the ones who watch and try to figure out what they are doing. Everyone is trying to be the best.

A person who is a good player is the one who does the best. They know how to use their body and how to hit the ball. They are also very fast and can catch the ball quickly.
