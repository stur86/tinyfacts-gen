To make a low ball is to hit a ball so the ground is near you. You use your hands and feet to hit the ball and it goes far over the ground. This is a game where many people play together.

A man who makes low balls is often called a moneys. He is the one who makes the money. When he makes a low ball, his team gets a free ball. This is a good thing because the team does not have to pay money to get the ball.

People also say a low ball when a person is very good at a game. If someone is very good at hitting a ball, they can make many low balls. They can hit the ball very high into the sky. This is a big thing because it is very strong and far away.

The word moneys also comes from money. It started with a man who gave money to others. He gave the money in small pieces so everyone could have some. Over time, people started to call him a money man because he gave money to help others.
