An idea is a guess. You might think something will happen before you see it. You might think a door will close when you push it. But if you do not see it, the door stays open. This is an idea.

But an idea is not always clear. What if you are wrong? What if you do not know what will happen? If you do not know, you must live with your idea. Your idea is like a picture in your brain. It is the first thing you think before you start to learn.

People have many of these ideas. Some people say, if you are very tired, you must sleep. Another person says, if you are happy, you must be good. They are all ideas. They are not always true, but people hold them because they seem right.

We can have many of these ideas. We might be sure that we are the best person in a group. Or we might be sure that we should not fight. Our ideas are like a set of pictures in our heads.

But ideas are not always easy to change. If you learn something new, your idea may change. If you see a new idea, your old idea may go away. Sometimes, people change their ideas. They learn something new. Sometimes, they keep their old idea.

We also have to be carefully about our ideas. Ideas can be very strong. If you do not think about them, you might not notice what is happening. Ideas can also be very easy to change. If you see something that makes you change your mind, you might change your idea. This is important because it shows you are open to new things.

In the end, an idea is like a picture in your brain. It tells you what to do or what to think. It helps us understand the world. But it is not always right. It is a picture that we have made. We must live with it and learn to change it if we need to.
