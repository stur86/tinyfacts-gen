Together means when many people are in the same room or when many people are doing the same thing. It is often about people who are close to each other. They can laugh and talk. They might be working on a big job together.

When you are together, you do not have to be alone. You can have fun with the people next to you. You might play a game. You can tell stories. You can share your ideas.

You can also be together for a very long time. You might go to a school. You might go to a work place. You might go to a place where people live. You can stay there for many years.

Sometimes, when many people work together, they can do very well. They share their ideas. They work well together. They can do things that you could not do alone.

We also say it when we share a thing with many people. We might share a book. We might share a game. We might share a food. When you share something together, you are showing the world that you care.

You can be together with a person you love. You can be together with a friend. You can be together with a family. You can be together with a group of people you know.
