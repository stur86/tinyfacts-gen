To roll something is to move it around in a round way. You can do this by holding the thing and turning your hand. You can also do it with a paper. You start at one end and roll the paper around. The paper goes in a round line.

When you roll a paper, you change its form. It is no longer a long line. It becomes a round thing that you can hold in your hand. You can use this round thing to carry your work or your books.

You can also roll something that has many small parts. You can pick up one part and roll it around. This is like a game. You try to keep the small parts together.

Rolling is also a way to move things from one place to another. You can roll a ball. You put the ball on a hard floor and push it. It goes in a line until it reaches a far place. You have moved it from one place to another.
