To go to a place in a different land is to move to a new home. In this case, a home is a place you live in. People from different places move to each other to start a new life. When you go to a new place, you are leaving your old life behind.

The word comes from the word for the water that surrounds a land. The water that you drink is called the water of the land. When you go to a new place, you are also moving away from the water. This is why you do not drink the water of the new land. You drink water from your old home, and you drink water from the new land.

Many people move to a new place for work. They go to a city that is very busy. They work in a big building or a school. They learn new things and meet new people. When you move to a new place, you are starting a new life. You are starting to do things in a new way.

Some people also move to a new place to have more space. They have a lot of room to play and walk. They can build a house with many rooms. They can make their own food. They have the power to make their own way. When you move to a new place, you are opening your home to the world.

The word is also used to talk about the places on a big water. There is a big body of water that goes around the world. People who live on the water move to different places. They move to a new land on the water. This is also a way to start a new life.
