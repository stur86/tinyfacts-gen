To pass something means you move it from one person to another. You can pass a small thing like a paper or a ball. Sometimes you have to hold the thing for a short time. If you do it well, the person after you can use it without you.

It also means you go past a place. If you walk to school, you are passing your home. The street is in between the house and the school. You are moving from one place to another. If you drive a car, you are passing a street. You are going in the same direction as the street, but you are also going past it.

In a game, you might have to pass a ball to your friend. Your friend has the ball and you are not holding it. You are moving it to them. This is when you are helping your friend. You are showing them how to do a thing.

Sometimes, you might pass a story to someone. You tell them a story and they hear it. You did not actually say the words. You just moved the story from one person to another. You are passing the story through your voice.
