To shop is to buy things you need or want. A person goes to a store. They look at the things that are on the floor. They pick what they want. The store has many things: clothes, food, books, and games. The person goes to a store many times. They buy more things with money.

You can shop with a friend. You go to a store together. You look at the things and pick what you like. Then you go home and put the things in your bag.

Some people shop for food. They go to a store and buy food. They buy food to eat for dinner. Others shop for clothes. They go to a store and buy clothes to wear to school.

You can also shop for things you need. If you have a cold, you go to a store and buy cold drinks or a box of food to help your body feel better.
