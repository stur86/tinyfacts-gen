To write something about something else is called to write about it. This can be about a person, a place, a thing, a book, or even a story.

When you want to say something about something, you use this word. You might say you want to write about the city. This means you want to tell the story of the city.

You can also use this word when you are talking about a work that someone did. If you want to know how the person did their work, you might ask them to write about it. This helps everyone understand what they did.

Sometimes, when you write about a story, you need to say who told the story. You can do this by writing a word that says who is the person who is telling the story. This word is like a name for the person.

When you read a book, you are reading about what the person who wrote said. You are using their words as a story to learn new things.
