A word is a thing you use to talk. It can be short and hard or long and easy to read. We use these words to tell how things are. A word is made of tiny parts. Each part is small. When you put many of these parts together, you get a word.

In a story, a word might mean a big thing. You might say,  I am happy. This word tells you that you are glad. You can also use it to talk about things that are much smaller. You might say,  This ball is small. This word shows how small the ball is.

In a game, you can use words to play. You might give your friend a word. They can use that word to say something. You can use words to laugh or to be very angry. You can even use words to move around. If you say,  Go, you move. Your body moves to the left.

Sometimes, when we talk, we talk about things that happen. We might say,  I did something. This word tells us what happened. The word is like a story. It helps us remember what happened.

Red is a word too. It is made of tiny parts. When you see a red thing, your eyes see this color. You can also use red to talk about things that are hot. You might say,  This cup is hot. The word tells you that something is very warm.

Red is also a word for a place. You can say,  I am in a red room. This word tells you where you are. It is like a big word that helps you understand things.
