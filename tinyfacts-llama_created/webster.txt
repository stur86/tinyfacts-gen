A word is a thing that people use when they talk. It is a short group of letters that form a word.

To make a word is easy. You just add a few letters to a word. You can add one letter, two letters, or many more letters. If you add the right many letters, you get a new word.

Some words are short. They are only one or two lines long. You might say,  I am happy. This word is short.

Other words are long. They have many lines. You might say,  I love to read books and write stories. This word is long.

A word can also change. If you use the word to talk about a thing, you might change it later. You might say,  He is a man. Later, you might say,  He is a boy. This is because the words have different meaning.

The word we use today is different from the word you used when you were a child. Our words have changed to fit our new way of living.

We also use words to talk about people. We talk about who they are, what they do, and how they feel. This helps us understand each other better.

So, a word is a small set of letters that form a group of meaning. It helps us talk to each other and understand the world around us.
