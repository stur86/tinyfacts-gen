To make a drink with a small cup is to use many small cups. One of these small cups is called a shot. If many people drink these small cups, they drink a lot. In this way, you can make a drink very fast.

Another cup that holds a little bit of something is called a cup. You can put many cups in one to make a big one. This is why you see many cups in a big drink. The cup in the middle is the big one, and the cups in the sides are the small ones.

A small drink that is very clear is called a shot. A small drink with many small pieces is called a shot glass. A small drink that is very strong is called a shot. A small drink that is very cold is called a shot of ice.
