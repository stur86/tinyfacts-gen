A person who dies leaves something behind. This can be their body, their money, or their words. These words can tell us what they wanted to happen later.

Sometimes a person writes down their hopes and dreams. They write so that their family and friends know what is important to them. This can help the family know what to do when the person is gone.

A person might also leave something that is not their body. They might give money to a school, a hospital, or a group that helps people. This shows that they care about other people.

A person who lives until old age often has a story to tell. They might tell about their life, their family, and their friends. They might tell about how they saw the world and how they felt.

A person who dies without their family or friends might leave their home. They might leave their books, their clothes, and their things. This helps other people know who they are and what they believed in.
