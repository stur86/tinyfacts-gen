A computer is a thing that can do many work jobs. It can send things to other computers. It can also send things to people. It does this through the air.

There are many ways to send things through the air. One way is when you send a word. You write the word and press a small round thing. The word goes over the air to the computer. Another way is when you send pictures. You send the pictures one at a time, one after another, so that the computer gets them all at once.

When people talk to each other, they use this too. When one person speaks, the other person hears. The computer does not understand words. It only understands numbers that the computer can read. The computer then uses those numbers to know what to do.
