A very important word.

To take a thing means to put it into your body. When you are sick, you need to take something to make your body feel better. This is called a drink. You drink it from a bottle or a glass. The drink is like a friend that helps your body.

Sometimes, if you have a big problem, you need to take a thing that will make you feel better. This is a very strong drink. You must follow the words on the bottle. You must drink it at the right time. If you do not drink it, the problem will stay and you might feel even worse.

There are many kinds of things you can take. Some are for things that hurt your head. Some are for things that hurt your stomach. Some are for things that hurt your heart. Some are for things that make you feel sad or angry. You must ask a doctor who knows about these things before you take them.

You should not take too many of these things at once. This can make you feel very sick. You should also not give them to a baby. Babies need only what their mom or dad gives them.
