To be just means you follow what is right. When you act this way, you do not do anything wrong. You give everyone a chance to be happy, even if you are not the best person.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. If one person does something that is not right, you do not let them window. You make sure everyone is safe and the game is fun. This is being just.

Sometimes, there are two people who are in the same situation. One of them is very nice, and the other is not. If one person is not doing anything wrong, the other person should be able to live. This is also being just.

In real life, being just is important for many reasons. It helps keep everyone safe. It helps keep people from getting hurt because of how they act. It also helps build trust between people.

Sometimes, people do not act just. They do things that hurt others because they want to be the best or to get what they want. This is not right. When this happens, people have to work together to find a new way of being just.

Being just is not always easy. There are often hard times. But it is a good thing to have in your heart.
