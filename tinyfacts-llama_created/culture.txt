A word that is often used, but not always understood.

To talk about a group of people is to talk about their way of life. If you see many people eating the same food for breakfast every day, you know they are in a same group. This group lives in the same place and shares the same story, the same music, the same jokes, the same words, and the same way of doing things.

One group may live in a city, and another group lives on a far away land. They may not even know each other. But because of what they have in their hands or what they wear, they know they are part of the same thing.

Cats are part of a group that lives in houses. Dogs are part of a group that lives in the green fields. This group is not made of people. They are part of the same world, the same thing, and the same story.

In this way, a group is not a person. It is a set of things that come together. A group can be very small or very large. It can have many different ways of living. But all the things that make it up are the same.

People build groups around them. Sometimes it is because of a place. Sometimes it is because of a story. Sometimes it is because of a thing that they like. When people start to do the same things, they join a group. They start to live with one set of things and to talk about the same things.

Sometimes, a group can become a thing that is bigger than the people in it. A thing that is made of many groups can become one thing. This is why it is so important to understand the things that make a group.

One day, a new group can start. Maybe they have a different way of doing things. They may not follow the same story. They may not share the same food. They may not have the same things to wear. But because they are not made of the same people, they are not the same group. They start a new story and a new way of living.

The word can also mean how things are seen. If a group sees one way, they might not see another way. They might have a different idea of what is right. This can cause trouble. People in the same group might not be happy. They might not like each other. But they are still the same group.
