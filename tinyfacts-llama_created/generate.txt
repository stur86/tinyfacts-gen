To make something new

You can make a thing, a person, or even a story just by thinking about it. This is called a job. You sit down and use your brain. You might want to write a story, so you start to write words. You put the words together. You also do other work while you write, like cleaning the room or eating food.

When you are making, you are not the person who is making. You are the one who is doing the thinking. You are the one who has the idea. You are the one who brings the idea to life.

Sometimes, you want to make something that will not be broken. You might make a house, a car, or a thing that people use every day. This is a very hard job. You have to follow many steps. You have to make sure the walls are strong, the floors are safe, and the doors are easy to open.

Another way to use the word is to give power to someone. If you are a doctor, you can make a person better. You take the person who is sick and give them strong things that make them feel better. You also take away the bad things that make them sick.

You can also say that something is coming. When something is coming, it is going to happen soon. You can watch the sun move across the sky as it comes up in the morning. You can see the stars at night as they come out.

So, the word is about making new things or giving power to people.
