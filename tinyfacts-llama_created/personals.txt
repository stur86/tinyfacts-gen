To have a person as a person is to be alone. It means you do not need anyone else. You are just you.

Imagine you have a dog. If you do not have anyone to play with, you do not feel alone. You are not alone with the dog. You are alone with your own mind and your own heart.

When you are alone with a book, you are in your own world. You read your words and you do not feel like anyone is watching you. You are not alone with the words.

Sometimes people have a person they love. This person loves them back. They feel the same way. They are happy together. This is a happy feeling.

It is also possible to have a person who is a person. You meet a new friend. You like how they talk and how they make you laugh. You feel like you are the one who is laughing and the friend is the one who is making you laugh. Both of you are happy.

If you are alone, you can do many things. You can watch the sun. You can listen to music. You can dream. You can build a world in your head. You are free.

If you have a person, you also have a life. You have a job, a home, and friends. You have to go to school and do your work. But you are not alone. You have them around you.

So, having a person is about being alone and being with the one you love.
