A thing that is big or small is called a number. Numbers are the very beginning of all things. Think about it. If you have one book, you have one number. If you have two books, you have two numbers. If you have three books, you have three numbers.

A thing that is big or small is called a number too. Numbers help us know how big something is or how small it is. If you are very tall, your number is very big. If you are very small, your number is very small.

Numbers can be very, very big or very, very small. They can be like one tiny thing or one very big thing. Some numbers are only two numbers, like one and two. Others are many numbers, like one hundred and two, three hundred, and one hundred and four.

You can use numbers in many ways. You can counters things. You can write numbers on paper so you can remember things. You can use numbers to build things. You can use numbers to play games with numbers. You can even use numbers to tell stories.

Sometimes, numbers can be hard to understand. You might see big numbers that are harder to read than tiny numbers. This is because the numbers are so big that the lines in the paper are very hard to see. You have to use your brain to figure out what the number means.

But numbers are very important. They help us learn about the world. They help us understand how things work. They help us build new things. And they help us tell stories and remember things.
