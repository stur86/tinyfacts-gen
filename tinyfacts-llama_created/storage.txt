To store something means to keep it in a place where it can be found later. Think of a box where you keep your things for school. You might put your books, your lunch, and your clothes in the box. When you need them, you just open the box and they are there.

You can also store things in a big room in a house. There is a room where people keep their clothes and their food. This room is very big and it keeps everything safe. You can not see it, but you can hear the sound of people moving around. It is like a very large box that keeps everything you need.

Sometimes, you store money. You put your money in a box. You keep it there until you need it. You can not see the money, but you know it is there. This is like a big box that holds money for you.

Another way to store is in your brain. Your brain keeps all the things you know. You remember things that happened when you were young. You remember where you live and what your friends look like. These are the things you have stored in your brain.

To store something also means to put it in a way that it stays there. You might put a book in a box so it does not fall. You might put a picture on a wall so it is not broken. This keeps the thing safe and helps you find it later.

Sometimes, we store food. We put food in a box or a bag. Then we put it in the kitchen and wait for it to be ready. We do this because we do not want to eat it before it is time. We are storing food so we can have it later when we need it.

In life, we often store things like money or things we hope to have in the next day. We put these things in a way that they stay safe and we can find them when we need them.

So, to store is to keep things in a safe place. It is about being ready for what comes next. It is about keeping everything you need.
