When you make a plan for the day, the next day, or the week, you are making a promise to yourself. A plan is a way to do something. This helps you feel safe and in control.

Sometimes, people want to stop doing something they hate. If you are tired of being late, you can say, I am going to be on time today. This is a way to break a bad thing. It is a way to change how you act.

You can also make promises to do something. If you say you will eat a whole book, you are making a plan. You are saying you want to do that.

It is also important to have a plan for when things do not go well. If you do not want to do something, you can say, I will not do it. This shows you are ready to stop.

When many people make the same plan, it helps them work together. They can help each other if one person does not follow the plan.

Making a plan is a good way to feel better when you are scared. It is a way to say, I am ready.
