To stuff something means to fill it with small, round things. You can use this to make something big and full. Imagine you are making a big bag for food. You put a lot of small balls inside. The bag is now big and can hold many more food pieces.

You can stuff a bag for animals too. You put food for them inside the bag so they do not get too full. Or, you can stuff a small box with small things. The box looks like a ball and people like to keep small things inside them.

Sometimes you stuff a thing that is not a bag. You might put small pieces of paper inside a small thing. Then the thing looks like a box and it is full of papers. This is a funny way to keep things inside.

You can also stuff a person. If a person is very sad, you might give them a big bag. You put many small, round things inside the bag. When they open the bag, they see many of those small things. This makes the person feel better.

The word is also used to talk about things that are small. If you stuff a word, you are adding more words to it. This makes the word bigger and more important.
