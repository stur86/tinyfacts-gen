To have a direction is to know where you are going. Imagine you are walking down a long street. You are following a line. You are moving from one place to another. Without a direction, you would keep going in the same way for a long time and never reach your end.

A direction is where you point at. It is the line that points to a place. When you have a direction, you can use it to find your way. You look down the line and see a point. You walk toward that point until you reach it. You are following the direction.

You can have a direction when you are moving. You are moving from one place to another. You are following a line. You are using the direction to stay on the line. You can also have a direction when you are standing. You look at the ground. You see a line. You know where you are standing.

Sometimes you have to go in a direction. You do not know where you are going. You need to follow a line that points to a place. You follow the line until you reach the end. This is important when you are on a road, a bus, or a long walk. You need to have a direction to know where to go.

You can also have a direction when you are thinking. You might have a feeling about the right way to do something. You do not know the exactly where you are going, but you know you should be moving in a way that feels right. This is like having a direction in your mind.
