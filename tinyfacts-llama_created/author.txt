An person who makes words is called an actor. They write what they think and put it into books or movies. The words are their own. The person who reads these words makes the story happen.

Sometimes, a person who writes books or stories is called an actor. They make up the words. They think about what people will like. They make the story happen in their head before they write it down.

There is another kind of person who makes words. They do not write their own stories. They tell people what to do. They tell people how to act. They show people what to feel. They are the ones who make people think and act.

Some people who make books are called the people who write the book. Some people who make movies are called the people who act. Some people who make stories for children are called the people who tell the story.
