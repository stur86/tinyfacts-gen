To do something is when you act. You do it to get a job done. This is how computers do their work. They take many bits of numbers and put them together. The more bits they take, the more work they do.

Think about when you buy things. You might buy food to eat. You take the food and put it in your body. This is a kind of doing. When you do this, you are helping your body. You take the food and give it to your body to use.

When you write a word, you are also doing something. You take words and put them together. You use your brain to understand what the words mean.

When you make a game, you are doing something. You take many parts and put them together. You make the game so the person who plays it can have fun. This is a kind of doing.

To do something is to act. It is to use your body or your brain to get a job done.
