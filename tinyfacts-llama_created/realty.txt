To buy a house is a big thing. You go to a place that has lots of land. You think it is a good place to live because it is near your school, your friends, and your work. You also like the way the light falls on the house.

You can buy this house from a person. They are the owner. You pay them money with your money. Sometimes you pay with money from a group of people. The group of people has money and they are trying to buy many houses.

If you buy a house, you are the owner now. You get to make the house your home. You can make it look nice. You can put pictures on the walls. You can even add a new window.

You also get to pay a small money each month. This money is called a month. You pay it every month for many years. After many years, you pay a lot of money. It is like a long story of how much you paid for your house.

Sometimes, when you buy a house, you also buy the land it sits on. You get the land too. This is like buying a small piece of the world. The land is important because it is where the house is. You pay the person who owns the land money.

The word real can be used in two ways. One way is to say something is real. If you say a story is real, you mean it is true. If you say your face is real, you mean your face looks like the real one. People use this word when they talk about life.

The other way is to say something is not real. If you say a dream is not real, you mean it is not a real thing. This is like the world we live in. Nothing we can see is real. The world is made of things we can not see. There are other worlds in space that are not real either.

When you buy a house, you are buying a piece of land and the land next to it. You are buying a small part of the world. This is why people say the real world is the world around us.
