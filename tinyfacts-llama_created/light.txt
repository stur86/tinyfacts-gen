Light is the way that things move around us. It is not a thing like a ball or a tree, but something that can go very fast and make us see things.

Imagine you are standing near a bright window. The light from the window comes down and hits your face. Your eyes see the light and you see the world around you. The light is like a huge wave of color and light that goes all around you.

When the light from the sun comes down and hits the ground, it makes everything look bright. The ground looks like a big, bright spot in the sky during the day. At night, the sky is very dark because there is no light from the sun.

Light can go through many things. Water is a good way to hold light. You can see the light from a light bottle or a light from a light show. The air can hold light too. When you breathe in, you take in light from the air.

Light can also go through things that are very hard to see. You can not see light that is very small, like tiny bits of air. But you can see the light from the tiny bits of water in a drink.

Sometimes, light can be very strong. It is like a big push of power. When you look at the sun, you see a bright, white light. This light can hurt your eyes if you look at it for too long. You need to cover your eyes so you do not get hurt.

Light is also very good at moving. When you move your head, you are moving around a ball of light. The light from the ball of light goes all around you, so you see everything around you.

Finally, light is what makes things look like the way they do. If a thing is black, it is like no light at all. If a thing is very bright and white, it looks like it is full of light. A bright ball of light can look like a huge, round thing in the sky.

So light is everything that moves around us and makes us see the world. It is what gives us our sense of what is in front of us.
