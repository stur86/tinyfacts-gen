The word interests things you like and use in your life. It is a word you use when you want to tell a story. Sometimes you use it to mean a feeling. You might say, I am very happy or I am very sad.

When you use this word, you are talking about how you feel. You might say, I am going to the movies. You are very excited. You want to watch a movie and be happy.

You also use this word when you are learning. You are trying to learn a new way to write. You might read a book and then you understand how to write the words. You are interested in the book. You want to learn more.

In some games, you use this word to talk to a friend. You might say, I need a glass of water. You want to drink something. You are interested in the drink.

So, the word interests is about things you like and the way you feel. It helps us talk about what is happening in our lives.
