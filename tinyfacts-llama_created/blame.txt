When you do something bad, you might want to say who was with you. You might say,  My friend did it. You are not really sure, but your friend says it happened. You did not do it. The person you are talking about is the one who did it. You give them the trouble. If you are the one who did it, you are the one who should be in trouble. If you did not do it, you are not the one who did it.
