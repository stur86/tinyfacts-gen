A person who is still living after something bad happens.

Imagine you are in a place with many strong animals. You are afraid, but you want to stay. You build a small house and wait for the day when the animals go away. Then you are ready to leave and find a new place to live.

If you are in a place with many strong water, you are also afraid. You want to leave, but you do not know where to go. This is a hard time. You may feel very sad. You are not sure if you will be safe tomorrow.

Sometimes, you might fight for your life. If you are in a place where there are many people who want to kill you, you must find a way to stay safe. You learn how to use a gun or how to run fast. You might hide in a house or in a small room. You do not want anyone to find you.

If you are in a place with many bad people, you might also fight for your life. You might use your words to say what you think and to find a way to stay safe. You might go to a school or a place where people are safe. You are trying to stay in one place where you are not alone.

All these people are called a group of people who are still living after something bad. They have found a way to stay safe. They are the ones who are able to live in a place even when things are very bad.
