To have an idea about something means to know it from the start. You know when you see a person, a thing, or a place. You know the name, the way they look, and what they do.

When you first see something, you might not really believe it. You might think it is a big thing or a very small thing. But then you learn more about it. You hear the person say they do that. You see the way they move. You see the way they look at you. You start to understand they are a real person.

If you are in a room with many people, you might hear a word. You might think it is a word that only one person says. But then you see the person who said it. They are the one who said it. They did not just say it. They wanted to tell you something.

Sometimes you see a thing that makes you feel a way. You might feel happy or sad. You might feel angry. You might feel scared. You did not know the thing before. You did not know how it was going to make you feel.

To have an idea about something means you learn about it. It means you get to know it before you know it yourself. It is like learning a new word or a new way of doing something.

If you think you know something but it turns out to be wrong, you have been wrong. You did not really know it at first. You were just guessing.
