To be asked to come is to be asked to join a group or a place. When you are asked to join, you do not know if you will be asked again. You do not decide. You just follow what is asked of you.

If you are asked to be at a place, you must go. You do not have a time to think about it. You just go. You might feel a bit nervous, but you go because you want to be near the people you like or the place you love.

You might be asked to do something you do not want to do. You might have to talk or move around. You might have to say things that make you feel bad. You might feel angry or sad. But you still go because you want to be part of the group.

An important person might ask you to join them. They might say, I like you and I want to have you by my side. This makes you feel happy. You might feel like you are part of something important.

When you are asked to be a part of something, you are not the only one who is asked. You are part of a group with many other people. You are a piece that fits into a larger picture. You are not alone. You are part of something bigger than you.

Being asked to join is a way to enter a new world. It is a way to become a part of a new group. It is a way to start a new story.
