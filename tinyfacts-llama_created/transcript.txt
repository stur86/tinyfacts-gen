To take a word or a set of words and put them into lines is to make a short story. This is what a person does when they are speaking. They speak many words to a group of people. The person does not have to speak in one long story. They can cut the words they said into short pieces and put them into lines.

The person might speak one word for a very long time. Then they stop. They put the words they said in a line so the people who watch can read them. This line is the story. The people can read it and understand what the person was trying to say.

Sometimes the person speaks many times. They say the same thing over and over. The lines are all the same. People put all these lines together to make one big story.

A person might also be asked to take a long thing and break it down into lines. This is what a teacher does. A teacher might ask a person to write down a long story. The teacher then puts the lines into a new set of lines. This new set of lines is the story.

People use this word to talk about how they talk when they speak. If someone says many words to a group, they are saying a story.
