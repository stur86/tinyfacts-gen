A table is a thing you stand on. It is a big piece of wood, often with four sides. People use them for many reasons.

One way is for eating. You can sit on a table and eat food. It is a good place to keep your hands clean. You can also use it to put things down. When you have food, you put it on the table. Later, you can pick it up and go.

Another way is for working. If you are a writer or a computer person, you often sit on a table. You type words and make things happen. This is important because people need to write their stories and make new things.

You also can use a table for games. A long table with many pieces is a great place for a game. You sit with your friends and play together. It is a good way to spend time.

Sometimes a table is a place for talking. People sit on a table in a room and talk. They use words to say what they think. This can be a good way to understand each other.

A table can also be a part of a house. The kitchen is often on a table. There you can make food and talk to your family. The living room has a table too. You sit on it and watch a movie with your friends.

In a school, a table is a place for work. You write papers and do your work on the table. It is a quiet room where people work on their jobs.

So, a table is a piece of wood with four sides. It is a place for eating, working, talking, and playing. It is also a place for understanding each other.
