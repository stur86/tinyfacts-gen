To be in a group can mean different things, but one way to talk about it is to do this.

Think of a school. Some kids are not there yet. They have not been told that they must be there. But if a kid shows up, they are in the group. They have already started to learn how to be a student.

You can also be in a group if you join a group of people to do something. Maybe you are a new person in a city. You do not know many people. You want to learn how to feel at home. You join a group of people who also want to do the same thing. You all do things together. You help each other. You feel safe.

Sometimes, you can be in a group because you are doing something. You are in a group of people who are all doing the same thing. You are a part of a team. You work together to get a job done.

You can also be in a group because you are not alone anymore. Maybe you have moved to a new city. You do not know anyone. You feel scared. But then you meet some new people. They are nice to you. They tell you that you are not alone. You join a group of people who are also new. You all learn how to feel safe and how to be a part of a group.

A person can also be in a group that is not about doing something. They might be in a group that is about being friends. They talk to each other every day. They share their lives. They show each other love. They are a part of a group of friends.
