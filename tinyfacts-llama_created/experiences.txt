Explain what an act is.

An act is a thing you do. When you do something, you are the one doing it. You are the one who is being acted upon. Think about a child. If a child does something, like they eat a food, they are the one doing it. They are the one who is being acted upon. They are the one who eats the food.

An act can be a big thing. You could do something that changes the world. Or you could do something small. You might say a word in a school. Or you might do something that makes a friend laugh. 

An act can be a story too. You might hear a story about someone who did a thing. The story might show how they felt and what they did. The act of the story is what we remember most. 

Sometimes we act without a plan. We might do something and then feel happy. Or we might do something and then feel angry. This can happen because of many things. It can happen because of a friend. It can happen because of a job. Or it can happen because of a feeling inside. 

An act can be an act of love. If you love someone, you might do things for them. You might give them a hug. You might listen to them. Or you might help them. This is an act of love because you are showing that you care. 

An act can also be an act of fear. If you are afraid of a thing, you might act. You might run away. You might hide. This act is because you are trying to stay safe.
