A cleaner is a person who makes things look better. They use things that can take away old stuff and make the air clean. They use these things to clean tables, floors, and other things that people touch.

You can also use a cleaner to clean your body. When you are sick, you might feel very bad. A cleaner can help you feel better by wiping away the white stuff from your nose and mouth. They can also clean your hands so you do not get the same old stuff on them again.

A cleaner can work with many kinds of things. They might clean a car by wiping down the seats and the floor. They might clean a room by picking up clothes and putting them away. They might even clean a big building so that people can work there without getting sick.

Some clean things by using water and a bit of power. Other clean things use a bag that pulls out the old stuff. When a cleaner works, they make the world feel cleaner and brighter. This is why you might feel happy to see a cleaner around.
