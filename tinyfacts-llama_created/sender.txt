A person or thing that sends something is a very important job. You can send a paper to a friend, a book to a school, or a picture to a family. When you send something, you give it to another person or a group of people. They will then take it and give it to the right person.

Think of it like a letter. You write a letter and you give it to a friend. Your friend reads the letter and then they give it to another friend. This goes on and on. The person who started with the letter goes around and around until they reach the person they want to give it to.

Sometimes, people send things that are not words. They might send a song, a picture, or a small thing that can not be held in your hand. These things can move from one place to another without a person holding them. They go through the air or through the water until they reach the person they want to reach.

We also send things to people in far away places. If you want to talk to a friend who lives in a city across the water, you can send a word through the air. This word is like a tiny piece of your heart, and it goes to the person who needs to hear it.

In some places, people send things through the dark. They might send a small light thing or a tiny piece of paper that goes under the ground to a person who lives in a different place. These things help people stay in touch, even when they are far away.
