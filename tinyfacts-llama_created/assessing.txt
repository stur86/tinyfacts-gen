To check something is when you look at it. You try to see if it is right or if it is not. You might look at a book. If the word is not in the book, you know it is not the right word.

You can also check people. If you want to know if someone is telling the truth, you can ask them. If they answer the right way, you know they are telling the truth. If they give a different answer, you know they are not telling the truth.

You can check the ground too. If the ground is hard, you might fall down. If it is soft, you can walk on it. If it is too hot, you might feel a burn. This is how we learn.

Sometimes, we check our bodies. If your heart is beating fast, you are probably afraid. If you are very tired, you might feel like you want to sleep. You are checking how you are feeling.

We also check things that are not there. If you hear a sound you did not make, you might be surprised. This can happen when you are in a room with many people. Or, you might hear a strange noise in your house.

When you check something, you are trying to understand it. You want to know the truth or if it is true. This is a very important thing to do, especially when you are trying to stay safe.
