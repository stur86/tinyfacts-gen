An open road is a place where many cars go. It is a long way that is not a big street. People drive along it to get to work or to visit places. There are many roads that go to different parts of a city or a town. Some roads go across a big water or a big land to get to a far away place. Other roads go through the green fields and the trees.

The word comes from a place where many people go to buy food. Before the cars, people would walk or use a big animal to carry them. Now, people drive these cars and use the roads to get where they need to go. The roads are important for many people because they help get things from one place to another.
