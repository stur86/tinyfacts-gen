A sound is a way to make things happen. You can make a sound by moving your lips or your voice. When you move your lips, air goes in and out. This makes air go round and round. This round air makes a line of air go across your face and over your head. The line of air goes through your body and out the other side. This line of air goes back to your mouth and you make another sound. This goes on and on until your voice is gone.

A sound can be very big or very small. A very big sound is like a loud dog. A very small sound is like a tiny drop of water falling on your skin.

When you make a sound, you are making the air go in different ways. You can change how fast the air goes round and how long it goes. This is why you can make a sound that is high or low. If you make the air go fast, the sound is high. If you make the air go slow, the sound is low.

You can hear sounds when you listen. Your ears are the parts of your head that help you hear. When you put a small piece of glass over your ear, you can hear sounds from far away. This is because the glass is letting the sound go through to your ear.
