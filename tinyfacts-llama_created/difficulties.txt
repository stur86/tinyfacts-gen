When you are doing something, you are doing it. It is a job. If you are happy doing it, you can do it easily. But if the job is hard, you feel a lot of trouble. This is what we call a problem.

You might have a big problem at work. You have to do many things at once. You are afraid that you will do something wrong and the person you work with will be angry. This makes your heart beat fast and your hands shake. You feel very much worried.

You might also have a problem at school. You are learning something new. You do not understand it yet. You feel confused and sad. You want to ask a teacher for help, but you are afraid to say your problem to them. This can make you feel alone and scared.

Sometimes, you are facing a big problem. You do not know what to do. You are afraid to start. You feel like you will not be able to fix it. This is a very hard feeling. It is like your heart is heavy and you do not know how to move forward.

You can also have a problem with your body. You might have a cut that is not going to close. You feel pain and worry. You want to see a doctor so you can feel better. This is a problem that you need to deal with.

When you have a problem, you are trying to figure out a way to fix it. You are looking for help. You are trying to understand what is wrong and how to make it right. This is a very important part of being human.
