A car is a thing that you drive. It has a round back and four moving parts inside. You push on the round back to make it move. You put the car in a way so it can go down a road.

A man who works with cars is called a driver. He drives the car for a job. He must always follow the road and the sign that says where to go. He must not hit any other car, because that is a bad thing.

A car also has doors and a window. You can get in and out of the car easily. Some cars have a seat for a baby or a child. You can also put a bag in the back for your things if you go to a store or a place.

Cars are very fast. They can go very high and very far in just a few minutes. Some cars can even go faster than the wind in the sky. You need to wear a seat and a cover to stay safe inside while you drive.

In some cities, you need a thing that lets you go on a road without a car. It is a way to go that does not use a round back or four moving parts. It goes under the ground or through water.

You can buy a car with your money. You can also buy one from a store or a place where cars are made. Some cars are very old and not very safe. You should always check if the car is safe before you buy it.
