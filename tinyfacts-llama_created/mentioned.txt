When you mention something, you are talking about it. If you talk about a cat, you are mentioning a cat. You are telling the other people in the room about the cat.

Imagine you are at school and you see a girl. You might say, I saw a girl. You are mentioning her. You are telling the people around you about her. It does not matter if you are talking to one person or a lot of people.

When you mention something, you are showing it. You are letting others see it. If you mention a book, you are showing people that it is a book. If you mention a song, you are showing people that it is a song.

Sometimes, you mention something without being asked. This can happen when you think about it for a long time. Or it can happen when you want to make sure everyone knows what is going on.

You can also mention a story. If you tell a story about a girl who loves to run, you are mentioning the story. You are telling the people around you about how the girl feels and what happens.

When you mention a person, you are talking about them. You are telling others about who they are and what they do. You might mention a friend, a teacher, or a family person.

In this way, mentioning is how we show others what we are talking about. It helps us to share our ideas and tell stories.
