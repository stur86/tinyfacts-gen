To care for something means to look after it. This can be a person, a thing you own, or even the air you breathe.

If you care for a child, you make sure they are safe. You keep them warm at night and give them food when they feel sick. You listen to them when they are scared. This makes them feel happy and safe.

If you care for a car, you fix it if it breaks. You find the problem and fix it so the car can go again. This is a big job, but when you do it, you keep the car going and it stays in good form.

You also care for people. If you care for a friend, you listen to them when they talk. You show them you care by being there when they need you. You do not laugh at their jokes, even if they are funny. You just listen and smile.

Sometimes, people care for animals. They give them food to eat and keep them clean. They also take them to a place where they can go to rest. This helps animals feel safe and happy.

The word comes from a place in the world where people care for the dead. In this place, you go to a room where the dead are kept. You clean the room, and you say a small word to the dead person. You are showing you care for them even after they are gone.

So, to care for something is to look after it, to keep it safe, and to help it when it needs it.
