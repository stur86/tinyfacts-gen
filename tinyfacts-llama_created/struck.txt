To be hit is to feel a hard thing from another person. When you are hit, your body goes into a state that makes you feel like you are being broken. You might have your face or your arm hit on the ground.

Sometimes, a ball is thrown to you. You might see the ball coming. You can try to move your body to avoid it. But if you do not move fast enough, you can feel the ball hit your skin.

You might feel this kind of hurt when a person pushes you. If you stand on a hard floor and your foot hits the ground, you feel the ground go under you. This is a kind of hit too.

You can also be hit by a word. If a person says, you are a bad person, the word is a hit. It is a way of telling you that you are not right. This kind of hit can make you feel sad.

Sometimes, a thing is hit and it does not hurt at all. If you hit a ball with a soft thing, the ball might roll away without you feeling any pain. You can also feel this kind of hit when you hit a book. The cover of the book might feel hard to your fingers, but inside, the words are safe.

To be hit can mean many different things.
