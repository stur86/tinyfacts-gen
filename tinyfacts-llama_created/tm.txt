The word you are looking for is time. Time is the big thing that goes around. It is the time between every moment. When you wake up, time starts. When you go to bed, time stops.

Time has three parts. The first part is the past. You lived in the past before you woke up today. The second part is the present. You are right now. The third part is the coming. You are going to wake up tomorrow, or you will go to bed tonight.

Time is very important. If you do not use your time well, you will miss things important. You might miss your family or your friends. You might miss school or work. Time can also be very fast. You can see hours in just a few minutes. Or time can be very slow. You might stand there for a long time without doing anything.

People use time to plan their day. They set a wake up time, a lunch time, and a bed time. They try to do things at these times. They also try to use time for fun. You might watch a movie or play a game. You use time to rest. You sleep so your body can be strong for the next day.

Time is also important for the world. People have to work and pay their money to live. They have to eat food and buy things to keep them going. Without time, life would be very hard.
