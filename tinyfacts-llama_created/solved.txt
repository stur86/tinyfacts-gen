To have something done is to have it ready. This is very important in many ways.

Imagine you want to finish a big game. You run as fast as you can, jump over tall things, and hit the ball that is thrown to you. After you play, you feel happy. You did it all by yourself. You did not need anyone else. You are the one who finished.

If you have a problem to fix, you find a way to do it. You look at what is wrong and change it. You might use a book to learn or ask a friend to help you. After you fix it, you feel better. You did it all by yourself.

Sometimes, you might have a story you want to tell. You write down the words and put them into a book. After you finish, you feel happy. You shared your story with others. You shared a part of your life.

A computer is another thing that can be done. You type words into the box. The computer reads the words and shows you a picture or a word. You did not need to be there. The computer did it all for you.

When you say a word, you are the one who says it. You use your voice and your body. You make the word real.
