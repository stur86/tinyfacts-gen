To begin something is to start it. It means you start to do a job or a thing. You might start by doing the very first thing. You might begin to look at a thing that is new. You might start to ask questions.

A person who begins something new is called a leader. They tell others what to do. They are the first ones to start a new job. A person who begins to learn is also a leader. They start to ask questions and try to understand.

If you begin with a book, you start to read it. If you begin to build a thing, you start to put pieces together. You begin to make it look like the thing you want.

You can begin to do things that are hard. You might begin to try to speak a new word. You might begin to do a new act. You start to feel the feeling of the thing.

To begin is to start something. It is the first step.
