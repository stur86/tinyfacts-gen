To reach something means you are moving toward it. It is a way to get closer to a place or a person you want to meet.

Imagine you want to get to the top of a tall building. If you start from the bottom and walk up, you are moving toward the top. You are going in the right direction. You are not moving away from the top.

When you reach a place, you have arrived. You have done what you wanted to do. You have found what you were looking for. You can feel a happy feeling inside you.

You can also reach something that you want to do. If you want to learn a new song, you are reaching out to the music. You are moving toward the sound you want to hear.

You can also reach a person you like. If you are feeling sad and you talk to a friend, you are reaching out to them. You are sending a word to a person who can help you feel better.

Sometimes, you might reach a point where you are not sure if you will reach your place. You might be in a room and you do not know if you will reach the door. You are moving but you are not sure if you will get out.

In a game, you can reach the end. You are moving toward the end of the game. You are doing what you want to do.

So, to reach something means you are moving toward it. It is about trying and going in the right direction.
