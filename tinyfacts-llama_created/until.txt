Until is a word that means before another time.

Think about a time when you were very happy. Maybe you had a friend who loved you. You did something fun together. Then, something bad happened. The friend said they were sorry. You felt sad. You wanted to hold them again, but they were gone. You felt the same way because you were still in the same time. You were not sure if things would ever be the same again.

If you keep doing something until something happens, you are waiting. You are not sure if it will be good. You might feel a little worried. You are trying to see if the time will change.

We use this word in many ways. You might say, I have until next week to finish this book. You have a short time left. You are not sure if you can do it. You might feel a bit scared. You are waiting for the time to be over.

We also use this word with numbers. If you have an hour until you need to leave, you are waiting. You are not sure if you will be able to get ready.

Sometimes, we use it to talk about how much time is left. If you have a short time, like one hour, you can feel the time going by quickly. You might think, I am running out of time! You are waiting for something important to happen.

So, until is a word that tells us about time before another time. It is a word that helps us understand how we feel when something is near and when it is far away.
