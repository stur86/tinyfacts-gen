To talk about something that is all around the world is to say you care about it a lot. When you say a thing is all around the world, you mean no place in the world is far from it.

Think about how you feel when you are in the city but also in a far away land. You feel you are part of something bigger than your own city. This is because many other cities and lands are also part of the world. Your city is part of the world, and the world is part of your city.

You can also talk about things that are all around the world. A person might say, I love to read books about far away lands. Or a group of people might say, We care about the world because we know there are many different ways of living there.

When we talk about something all around the world, we usually use words like this:

One: one city, one land
Two: many cities, many lands
Three: everything in the world

This is how we show we care about the world and about the people in it.
