To have something done is to give it to another person so they can do it. When someone is a done person, they have a job to do that they follow very well. They are often in a group. The people in the group have a plan. They all follow the plan and work together.

You can also mean a thing that has been made. If you do something, you make it happen. A doctor can do this with a person. A person can do this with a thing that breaks. A person who is a done person is the one who makes it happen.

To have a job is to work for another person. You work from morning until night. You do things that help the person. You may do work that is hard. You may have to be very quiet. You may have to do a lot of work in a short time.

Sometimes you do things for yourself. You might write a story. You might draw a picture. You might play a game. You are doing something for your own fun.

You can also mean a person who has done something. If you have done something, you have been a done person. If a person is a done person, they are the ones who have done the job.
