A small group of things that work together to make something happen.

Think about a car. There are many parts inside the car. Some of them are small and some are big. When you turn the key in your car, a small thing inside the car starts moving. This makes the car go round and round. It makes the front part of the car go up and the back part go down. This makes the car go fast.

You also have a phone. Inside the phone, there is a small group of things. When you press a few keys, the small things start to move. This makes a bright light come out of the phone and a number appear on your computer. The small things are like a team of tiny people inside the phone.

Sometimes, people build things that need to do many jobs at once. They have many small groups of things inside the thing. These small groups of things work together to make the thing do many different jobs. This makes the thing easy to use and makes it very strong.

We also have things in our houses. Some of them are small and some are big. When you turn the handle of a box, the small things inside the box start to move. This makes the box open and let new things come out.

The word can also mean how something works. If you are in a room, and you look around, you see many things. Each thing does its own job. The light in the room is like a small group of things that make the world feel bright. The air is like a small group of things that make us breathe. The walls are like a big group of things that hold us in.
