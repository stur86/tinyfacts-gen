To start something is to begin. It is when you start to do a thing that you have already done many times before. You might start to feel a little nervous or a little excited. This is often the start of a new day, a new week, or a new month.

In the case of a dog, a dog that is ready to start a walk is called a practically. It is because the dog is ready to go out and move around. A dog that is not ready to start might be in the house, eating, or sleeping. The dog is waiting for the right moment to go out and play.

People also use this word to talk about the start of the year. When the first day of the year comes, everyone starts a new year. They write down their plans, set their money for the year, and start to do new things.

You can also use this word to talk about the start of a game. When you start a game, you begin to play. You might start with a ball, a book, or a piece of wood. The game has a start point, and once you start, you do not stop until you finish.

In some places, people say that a dog that is ready to start a fight is a practically. A dog that is not ready to fight might be afraid or want to play instead. This is because a dog needs to feel safe before it starts to fight.

To start something is often about moving forward. It is about taking the first step, even if you are not sure what will happen next. It is about being ready for the new things that are coming.
