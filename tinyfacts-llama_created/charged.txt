To be a good thing can mean many different things.

One way you might say this is if you are very angry. When you are angry, you feel like your heart is going to burst. This is because your body is sending out a lot of power. This power is like a tiny ball of light that goes around you. This power makes you want to hit things or shout at people. It is like the power of a big animal.

Another way you might say this is if you are very busy. If you have many things to do, you are being a good person. You are using your time well. You do not just stand around all day. You do important work.

When something is also called a power, it is because it is giving off power. This power can be from a person, a thing, or even the sun. You can feel the power of the sun when you look at the bright sky during the day. You can feel the power of a running dog when you see it running. This power can also be inside you. When you feel strong, you are like the power inside you. You can do many things.

In a word game, we use this word. We take a word and add a few letters to the end. If we add the word for being angry and the word for being busy, we get a word that means being both. This is because being both is like having both power and time.
