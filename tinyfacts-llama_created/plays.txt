To play with something means you use it to have fun. You might hit a ball with a friend, or use a piece of wood to make a sound. You might also build a thing with blocks or make a story with words.

When you play, you use your body and your mind. You move your feet and your arms. You might laugh and shout. You might also think about what you want to happen next. You might plan how you will act.

Playing is a way to be free. You can do what you want without someone telling you what to do. You can be alone or with other people. You can use your time to learn, to grow, or to just enjoy.

You can also play with your mind. You might think about how things work. You might try to figure out how a thing works so you can fix it. Or you might think about a story you want to tell. You might imagine what might happen next.

Sometimes, you play with things that are hard to hold. You might use a ball or a box that is heavy. You might also play with things that are bright. You might hit a ball with a bright thing or look at something that is very clear.

Playing is important for everyone. It can help you feel happy. It can help you learn about the world. It can help you be kind to others.
