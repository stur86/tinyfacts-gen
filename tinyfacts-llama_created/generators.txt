A very important word.

To make something is to turn things that are not moving into moving things. You can use the word for many things.

Think of a light. You turn a part of the box. It makes the light. The box is like a big thing that makes a kind of power. This power is what makes the light.

You can also use this word for things that make sound. A small thing in a car can make a sound. You press a small part and the car makes the sound. The car is making the sound.

A person who does this is called a person who makes. They use their brain to turn things into moving things or sound.

Sometimes, this word is used to talk about money. A person who can not work because they are sick is said to be on a sick leave. This is because they are making money while they are not working.

In some places, people use the word to talk about the power of a person or a thing. A person who can not do something is said to be not able. A thing that does not work can be called a thing that does not work.

This word is very important. It helps us talk about how things change and how they make us feel.
