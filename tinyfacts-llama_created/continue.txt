To continue means you keep doing something after you started. You do not stop or end. You keep going. If you finish something, you can still keep doing it. It is like a story. You started a story and you keep telling it.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. Your friend is doing something. You want to keep playing. You do not stop. You are still there. You are still in the game. You are not done yet. You are continuing.
