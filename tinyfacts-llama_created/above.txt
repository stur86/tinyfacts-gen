The word above means something is going up. When you are sitting on a chair, if you push your feet up, you are going above the floor. You are moving higher than the ground.

Imagine a tall tree. The top of the tree is above the ground. Everything that is not on the ground is above it. This means something is higher than the world.

You can also use this word when talking about the sun. If you look up at the sky, you see the sun. This is above the world. The sky is above the ground and everything else.

When you are flying in a car, you are moving above the ground. Your car is going very fast. The ground is far away. You are higher than everything you see on the ground.
