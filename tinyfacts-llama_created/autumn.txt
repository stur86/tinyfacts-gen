To fall is to drop from a high place. You can drop things you are carrying from a car or a tall tree. It is a way to go down.

In the fall, the days are getting shorter. The sun is going down before you see it. The light is bright at first and then it gets darker and the stars start to come out.

The trees change their color. Some are green and green and keep it for a long time. Others are red and brown and turn them into warm colors. This is why we call this time of year the fall.

In this time, the ground is covered in green and brown leaves. These leaves are like big green things hanging from the trees. They are made from the green parts of the tree. When the wind moves, they go down and fall to the ground.

When the leaves fall, they make the ground look different. It looks like the ground is covered in a soft green or brown ground. This is why people like to walk in this time of year.

People also say that the fall is a time of change. It is a time when things start to end. The warm days are over and the cold days are coming. This makes room for new things to grow.
