A person who makes things from wet stuff is called a maker. They use hard stuff from the ground and some other hard stuff to make small things you can hold in your hand.

You start with a bit of wet stuff. You let it sit for a while so it starts to change. Then, you take the wet stuff and you make it hard again by pushing air through it. This makes it strong and it holds its form.

Some people make things that people use to eat. They make cups, mums for hot drinks, and small cups to hold food. Some people make things that people use to keep their hands clean. They make cups from the hard stuff so they can pick it out easily.

Some people make things that are for animals. They make small things for the feet of a dog or a cat. They make things that keep the animal warm at night.

Many people work with a hot thing that makes air go through a round thing. They put wet stuff into the hot air and wait for the wet stuff to change. After a while, they take the wet stuff out and the small things are ready to use.
