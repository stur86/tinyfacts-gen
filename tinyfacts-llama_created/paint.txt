To paint something is to cover it with colors. You do this with a thing you hold. There are many colors you can pick. You put the colors on the thing, then you press the colors together. When the colors are all covered, the thing looks different. It might look bright or it might look dark.

People use this word to talk about things that are made of paint. Paint is a thing that comes out of a bottle. You put it on a table, a floor, or a wall. The paint covers the area and changes the look of the thing.

You can also paint your face. A person might paint their hair. A child might paint their hands. They use a soft thing that holds the colors. They make their face look a new color. This is fun to do because you can change your look.

Paint is also used on things that are old. You can use it to cover a broken thing so it looks new again. It helps to keep things from getting hurt.

Sometimes people paint things to remember. If you are sad, you might paint your face with colors that are dark. This helps you remember how you feel. You can also paint pictures that tell a story.
