A strong thing that keeps things from falling down is a thing that is very sure. It does not move much, and it does not change easily. It is like a rock, but it is not a rock. It is very heavy because it is made of hard stuff that will not break easily.

When you are very sure of something, you can trust it. You know that it is the right way to do things. If you are sure, you do not get worried because you know you are doing the right thing. You can feel calm inside your heart.

Some things are not sure. They change very often. One day they are one way, and the next day they are another way. They are always moving. They do not stay the same. If you are sure of a thing that is not sure, you have to be very carefully thinking about it.

Also, a thing that is sure can be very strong. It can hold more power than a thing that is not sure. It can stay up against the wind, against the water, or against the ground. It does not let go easily. It stands tall and does not fall down.
