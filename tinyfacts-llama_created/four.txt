Four is a number. It is the number before five.

Imagine you have four books. You have one book with a story, one book with a picture, one book with a story about animals, and one book with a story about the sun.

Four is also a number of days in a week. We have four days before the week starts again. We call the day before this one day the night before. We also have four days before the month starts again. This helps us keep track of how many days have passed.

You can also use four to mean many things. Four friends is a group of five people. Four colors are red, blue, green, and so on. Four legs are on the ground, on the ground, and in the air at the same time. Four sides of a table are the short sides, the long sides, and the sides that are across from each other.

When we talk about four, we usually think about four as a number. But we also use it to talk about other things. We say the first four steps are the most important steps in a long story. We say the four sides of a house are the two sides that go across the top and the two sides that go down.

Four is a number that is often easy to work with. You can add four easy numbers together. You can find the number of legs on a table, a chair, or a dog. You can counters four things at a time.
