A simply person is one who does not use a lot of words. They usually say what they think. This is because they do not want to use words when they say what they feel. They might just say yes or no. They do this because they are happy with what they have. They do not want to say more than they need to.

Simply people are often easy to understand. They do not have to say a lot. They can just speak their mind. They are like small animals. They can not carry many words inside their head. They just need to say it once and they are done.

A simply person might say something like, if it is the morning and they are not very tired, they say, no, I am not tired. If they like a book, they might say, yes, I like this book. They do not need to write a long story. They just say what they want.

They are not afraid to show their face. They do not hide. If you see them, you know who they are. They are not afraid of people. They can talk to anyone. They are like a small dog. They do not need to be big to be strong.
