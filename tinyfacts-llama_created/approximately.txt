To be close to something means you are not far away, but you are near. It is like you are standing next to a person, but not right next to them.

Think about numbers. A number that is two is close to the number one. You could say the number one is two hundred times bigger than the number two. This is close. You are not one hundred times bigger, but you are close. It is a small step away.

You can also say a story is close to a picture. If you are not in the middle of the story, but you hear the words, they are close to the story. You are not the whole story, but you are part of it.

When you say a thing is close to a number, you are saying it is near. It is not a tiny bit, but a tiny bit big. If something is close to a number, you know it is getting closer.
