A totally is a word you use when you want to mean the very end of something.

Imagine you have a bag full of things. You buy one more thing than you have. You can only have the one you bought. You don't have any of the other things anymore. That is a totally. You have one less thing than you started with.

Think about a game. You start playing a game and you play for a long time. You are very good at it. You can do it very well. At the end of the game, you are the one who won't leave the table. You are the best. That is a totally.

Sometimes, a totally is used to mean something is the best. If you are the best at something, you are a totally. It means you are the very best.

In a story, a totally can mean that someone is the very top of a line. If you have a line of people and they are all going up, the top person is the one at the very top. They are the one at the top of the line.

You can also use this word in a funny way. If you want something, you can say, I want a totally of ice water. This means you want the whole thing. You want it all.

In the end, a totally is a word that shows you have reached the very end of something.
