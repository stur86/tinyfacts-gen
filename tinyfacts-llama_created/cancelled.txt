To be done or to be stopped is a very important word.

When you say something is done, it means the work is finished. You did what you wanted to do. You can say this to a person, a job, a game, or even a thing that happened.

But what if you want to do something but you can't? Maybe you want to go to a movie but you do not have money for the movie. Or maybe you want to go to a party but your mom says you must stay home. In this case, you are not doing what you want to do. You are doing something else.

If you tell someone you are going to do something and you do not, you have done something wrong. This can cause a problem. People get angry if they think you did not follow what they asked.

The word can also mean you are stopped. Imagine you are going to run into a wall. The wall stops you. You are not moving anymore. You have stopped.

When you are done and you stop doing something, you might say you were done. You were finished. You stopped what you were doing. If you are done with a game, you can stop playing. If you are done with a book, you can stop reading it.
