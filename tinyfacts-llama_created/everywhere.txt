When you say something is all around you, you mean it is in every place. You do not need to move to find it. It is there, all the time, in the sky above you, under the ground where you walk, and in the water that moves when the wind pushes it.

Imagine you are standing in a green room. The room is full of green things. Everything is green. The floor is green. The walls are green. The windows are green. The air is green. Even the ground under your feet is green. This green is always there, no matter where you are or how you move. You can walk across the world and still find green. You can go to a city and still find green. You can go to a far place and still find green.

This is why you can say that something is in every place. It means the thing is always there, in every spot, every time, and always.

Sometimes you say something is in a place. You might say the park is in the city. Or you might say a book is in the school. These are easy ways to say you know where something is.

You can also say something is in a way. This means the thing is always there, even if you do not see it. The sun is always in the sky. The air is always around you. The water is always under your feet. The ground is always there. This way of saying helps us know that something is always there, even when we do not see it.

So, when you say something is in every place, you mean it is in every spot, every time, and always.
