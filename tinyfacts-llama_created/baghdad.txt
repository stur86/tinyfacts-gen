Bagged is a word that means you carry something. You put it in a bag so it stays safe while you go.

Imagine you have a big bag that holds many things. You fill it with food, clothes, and other things you need. When you are on a long trip, you put the bag on your back. This way, the bag stays close and does not fall off.

In this way, you are bagged. You are the one carrying the bag. You are the one who keeps the bag safe until you reach your home.

Some bags are very big. They are like rooms inside one big thing. You can put many things in one bag. You can also have a bag with many small bags inside. This is like a box that is full of boxes.

People also bag things when they are moving. They put things they need into a bag so they do not have to carry them all around. This helps when you are going to a place far away.

In some places, people use a bag that looks like a box. This box has a top and a bottom. The top is where you see out. The bottom is where you keep your things. You can open the top and close the bottom to keep the things inside safe.

You can also bag money. When you have money to buy things, you can put it in a bag. This way, your money stays safe. You can carry the bag around with you until you reach your home.

To bag something is to keep it safe.
