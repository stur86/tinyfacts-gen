To form something means to make it from nothing. When you form a word, you put many small pieces of letters together. You start with a few letters and add more. You use your fingers to press the keys on a computer. You use your mouth to type words. You use a brain to think.

You can form a word to mean a thing. You can form a word to mean a person. You can form a word to mean a feeling. You can form a word to mean a place. When you form a word, you are changing what is not there into what is there.

You can also form a word by using the sounds of your voice. When you say a word, you are making sounds together. You are not just saying a single word. You are making many sounds that together form a word.

Think about how you form your own words. You might use a word you already know. You might add a word to it. You might change the word. You might use it in a different way. This is how you change old words into new ones.

To form something is to change what is not there into what is there. It is how you make new things from old things.
