A drop is a small piece of something. It is usually very small and can be seen with your eyes. Drops are often from the sky or from a person when they cry.

When a person cries, water from their eyes falls down their face and off their face into the air. These drops go down and come into the ground. The ground is covered with many of these drops. When the ground gets very wet, the drops start to fall faster and faster. This is why the ground can become very heavy and make it hard to walk.

Sometimes you see drops on the window of a car. The water from the sky falls down the window and forms a bright, small piece on the glass. You can watch the drops fall for a long time before the window gets too full.

Drops can also grow very big. Imagine a tiny drop of water that is so small you can not see it. If it gets heavy enough, it becomes a small ball of water. When this ball of water falls down, it becomes a small rain drop. When many of these drops fall together, they form a small piece of rain.
