A teacher is a person who helps other people learn. They show you how to do things. They tell you what to do when you are doing a job. They also help you understand why you are doing a thing.

When a child is very young, a woman or a man who is older than them is their teacher. They help them learn to read and write. Later, when a child gets older, they become their teacher. A teacher is important because they help people learn and grow.
