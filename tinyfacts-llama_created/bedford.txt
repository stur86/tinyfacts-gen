A city is a place where many people live. It is often big. You can walk for a long time to get to a city. Some cities are very old. People have lived there for many years. You can see old houses and old streets. You can also see a big building that is for work or for school.

A place where people go to sleep is a bed. It is a room in a house. The room is for one person. There is a bed, a table, and a chair. You can sleep in a bed. It is a quiet place to be alone.

A place where people go to eat is a house. The house has a kitchen. The kitchen makes food. The person who runs the house makes sure the food is good. You can eat there many times. The house also has a room for people to sit and talk.
