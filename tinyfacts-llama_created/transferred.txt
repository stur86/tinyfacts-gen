To take something from one person to another is often called to be done with them. You might give your food to a child so they can eat it. You might take something from a house to a car. This is when you move a thing from one place to another. 

Sometimes, when you talk about something, you are talking about the way it is. If you say a word is funny, it means the person you are talking to is laughing. If you say a picture is beautiful, it means you think it is very nice. 

You can also say a thing is in your hands. This means you are holding it. Imagine you are holding a small thing that you found on the ground. You can feel it and you are the one with it. 

Sometimes, when you say you have done something, it means you took the time. You were doing it for a long time. It is like when you spend hours in a room. You are not just sitting there, you are doing work. 

You can also use this word for things that move. If a car is moving, it is being carried. If a ball is flying through the air, it is moving. When something moves, it changes its place.
