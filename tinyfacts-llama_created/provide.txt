To give something to someone means to put it in their hand so they can use it. You give them a book, a ball, or a thing they need. This helps them. If you do not give someone something, they might be very sad or angry.

Imagine you have a friend who has a big problem. You want to help them. You could tell them the answer so they do not feel alone. You could give them a book with the answers. This is like giving them something to help them.

You can also give a person money. If you work very hard and get many pieces of paper that say you are able to pay money, you can use those papers to buy things you want. This is a way to show you care about the person.

When you give a thing to someone, you act like you care about them. You want to help them feel better.
