A word is a thing you use to talk. You might use it to say what you think, how you feel, or what you just saw. A word is a part of your life.

When you talk, you use many words. A word is one word. It is a small thing that helps your voice be clear. If you want to say something, you use one word. You do not need many words to say something.

Sometimes, a word can be very big. It can mean a thing that happens in the sky, in the water, or in the ground. This is a big word. If you hear a loud sound in the sky, you might think it is a big thing moving. You use this word to tell the story.

Other times, a word can be very small. It might be a word you use to talk about a small thing. If you want to know if a person is happy, you might say, I am happy. This word is small because it only has one word.

A word can also change how people feel. If you say, I love you, this word makes everyone feel very happy. If you say, I am angry, this word makes everyone feel afraid. So, a word can change how we feel.
