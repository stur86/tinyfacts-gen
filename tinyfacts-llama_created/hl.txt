A high is a very big number or a very high place. Imagine you are standing on a small rock. If you go a little bit down, the ground gets smaller. If you go a bit farther down, the ground gets even smaller. But if you keep going down, the ground gets smaller and smaller until it is very small.

A high is also about how high up the sky is. If the sun is high in the sky, it is a high day. If the sun is very low in the sky, it is a low day. You can also use a high to talk about how high a building is. If a house is very high up in a tall building, it is a high place.

In some places, a high can mean a time when it is very warm and the air is thick with warm air. This is called a high day. It is a good day to go outside and play or to have fun.

You can also use a high to talk about how strong a wind is. If the wind is high, it is a strong wind. It can make the trees and the leaves go very fast.

A high can also mean a high point on a body of water. Imagine a big body of water like a big room. There is a high point on the top of the water. It is a high place where you can see far away.
