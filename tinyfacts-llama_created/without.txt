To do something without someone else means you do it by yourself. You do not need anyone to help you. You just do the work yourself.

Imagine you need to move a heavy thing. You do not want to ask a friend to help. You can do it by yourself. You are strong and you can do it alone.

You can also do something without someone watching you. You might play a game or watch a movie without anyone telling you to stop. You are doing it because you want to.

Sometimes, you might do something without thinking about it at first. You do it because it is easy. You might eat a whole bag of food without even thinking about it. You just did it because it was there.

But sometimes, doing something without someone else is bad. If a friend asks you to do something, you should listen to them. You should say yes. This helps build trust and makes people feel safe around you.

To do something without someone else can also mean to keep a thing for yourself. If you buy something, you keep it in your home. You do not give it away. You are keeping it for yourself.

So, to do something without someone else means you do it by yourself, without anyone else helping you.
