Computers are things that do many things at once. They do not need to be told what to do, but you tell them what to do. You give them words or numbers, and they make the right answer. They do this very fast.

You can use a computer to do many things. You can ask a computer to look for a word in a big book. The computer looks through the book many times and then finds what you are looking for. You do not have to stand there waiting for a long time.

Computers are also used to make music. You can put in a song and the computer makes it sound like the one you heard. You can also use computers to play games. You can put in a game and the computer does all the work.

Computers are very good at doing many things at once. They can help us do work faster and make our lives easier. But they are also very big and need a lot of power. They need a power box in the same room as the computer so they do not make a lot of noise.
