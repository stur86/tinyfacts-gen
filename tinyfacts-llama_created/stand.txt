To stand means you are not on the ground. You are up. You can not move. You have a body and a head and a long parts that go out from your body. You are not a thing that is made of only one piece. You are many parts that all work together.

When you stand, you look around. You see what is in front of you and what is behind you. You see people, trees, houses, and cars. You see the sky if the sun is high. You can see many things at once.

You also stand for standing up for something. If you are a person, you might stand up for your family or your friends. If you are a dog, you might stand up for your home and your family. You stand up when you say you are here.

You can stand by yourself. You can stand when you are alone. You can stand in front of a big room or a long hall. You can stand when you are afraid but you are still standing.
