To have a free time is to not have to work. It is a word that means you have time to do things you like when you want. This is good because it allows you to relax and feel happy.

Imagine you work all day from morning to night. You go home, do your bed things, and then you watch a movie or a game. Later, you go out with your friends or you go to a place to have food and drinks. This is your time to do what you want, when you want. You can do these things because you did not have to work.

A long time ago, people did not have this word. They just said, something is done. If a man had a child, he said the child was done. If a woman had a baby, she said the baby was done. This word did not mean that the baby was dead. It just meant that the work was over.

Now, we use this word all the time. When you say, you need a break, you mean you need time to rest and have fun. When you say, I love to read, you are saying that reading is a thing you like to do. You do it because you want to, not because you have to.

You can have a free time in many ways. You can play games, watch movies, listen to music, or just sit and think. You can spend your time doing things that make you happy, or you can do things that help you feel better if you are sad or tired.

This word is important. It tells us that we have the power to decide how we spend our time. It helps us to know when to relax and when to act. It is a word that allows us to say, I am happy and I am free, and I am ready to do what I want.
