Plans are things that grow. They take water from the ground and use it to grow. They need the sun to grow. They need air to breathe and to drink. They also need the ground to sit on and to grow up.

Some plans grow in the ground. They have long parts that go down into the ground. They make the ground soft. The ground gets soft and the water goes down into the ground. Then the plan grows up from the ground. It gets tall and strong.

Other plans do not grow in the ground. They grow on a tall thing. They grow on a tree or a high wall. They use parts from other things to make their own parts. They take the things they need from the air and from the ground. They use those to make food.

Plans are very good at making things around them. They take in the air, the water, and the ground. They use what they need to make their own parts. They can make a house. They can make a car. They can make a tree. They can make a big rock. They can make a small thing that you can hold in your hand.

Some plans grow very fast. They can grow in a short time. Some plans grow very slowly. They take years to grow.
