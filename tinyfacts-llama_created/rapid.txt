To do something quickly is to act fast. When you do something this way, you do it before your friends or family or the time is over.

If you are running, you are doing something very fast. You can not stop because you are going so quickly. If you run around a house in a short time, you did something quickly.

If you are feeling sad, you might want to do something to make the day better. You could watch a funny movie, listen to music, or go for a walk. This is an easy way to feel better.

When we talk about a thing that moves very fast, we say it is moving quickly. Cars and buses move quickly. People who drive fast cars are very good at their job. If you see a car moving very fast on the road, you might want to follow it to see where it is going.

Sometimes, when something happens very fast, we use the word fast. We might say, I am very happy because I did this fast. Or we might say, I am afraid because I saw a big animal.

In our lives, we need to do many things very quickly. We might be in a meeting and we must speak soon. We might be waiting for the food at a table. We must also be carefully when we walk or drive.

So, to do something quickly is to act fast. It makes life easier.
