To cut something means to make it small. When you cut a piece of wood, it becomes much smaller. You can use this to build something new or to make a table.

When you cut the ground, you are making it less green. Green is the color that makes things look bright and full. If you cut the green, the ground will look brown and small. This happens because the green color is made from tiny pieces that are under the ground.

You also cut things to make them look different. If you cut a piece of paper, it is now smaller. You can cut it into many small pieces to use for drawing or for making games. You can even cut your hair to make it look longer.

People also cut things to make them safe. In a hospital, doctors cut open the skin of a baby to take out the bad stuff that is inside. They do this because the baby needs to get better. The cut is very small and is done very fast. After a few minutes, the cut is gone and the baby can feel safe again.
