Dies is a thing you use for cars and other big moving things. It is not like the air we breathe. It makes fire instead of air go round in a round thing inside the car.

You put a small round box with many short hard pieces into the car. The short pieces make fire, and the fire makes the car go. It is very strong because it makes a lot of power.

The car does not need air to work. It only needs the short pieces and the fire. This is why you can drive the car on very long roads without stopping to breathe.

Many cars and buses use this kind of power. They are good for long trips because they do not make a lot of noise. They also do not need to stop as often as cars with air inside.

The long pieces inside the round box are made from hard stuff. They are usually made from rock or hard stuff that can be broken into many small pieces. You put many of these pieces into the round box many times, and each time they go into the round box, they make a little bit more power.

Some cars have many of these short pieces inside them. They are called many short pieces. They make many small fires inside the car, and all these fires together make the car go fast.

You do not need to carry a lot of these short pieces with you when you drive. You just put them in the round box and the car goes. It is like carrying money in your pocket, but instead you carry power!
