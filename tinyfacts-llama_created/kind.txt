A kind person is a good person. They are nice to everyone. They help other people when they need it. They are very warm and make everyone feel safe.

Think about a child. A kind child is a child who is very nice. They may give a ball to a friend if they see they want it. They may say please or thank you even when they do not want to. They are always ready to help.

A kind person does not like to fight. They do not yell or hit. They use words to talk and their body is calm. A kind person is like a soft thing that makes everyone feel happy.

People who are kind live their lives with this feeling. They are always looking for ways to help others. They are good friends to many people. They are like the sun in the sky that gives us light and makes everything feel warm.
