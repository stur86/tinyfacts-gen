To give something for free is to give it without asking. This is when a person gives a thing to another person without expecting anything in return. This is often done when a person wants to help someone who is in trouble.

Imagine you are the one who is in trouble. You feel very sad and you need help. You go to a friend who is kind and strong. You tell your friend that you are in a hard place and you need them to help you. Your friend agrees to help you. They do not ask you to pay them. You did not give them anything in return.

This kind of thing happens when a person helps someone else without being asked. It is a way of showing love and care. When you give something for free, you are doing a very good thing.

Sometimes, we might do this for a group of people too. If a school needs money for a new book, the teachers might give the money for free. This shows that they trust the people in the school and believe that they will help the school.

In some places, people give money for food to those who need it. This is like giving something for free. The person who gives the money is helping those who are not getting enough to eat.

So, to give something for free is to share with others without getting anything back. It is a way of being kind and helping others.
