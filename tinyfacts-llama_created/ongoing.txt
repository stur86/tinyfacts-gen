The word means something that is happening in the present moment.

Imagine you are sitting in a room. You are thinking about something. You are looking at the wall. You are listening to a song. The room is quiet. Then, a person walks into the room. They walk slowly. They look at the wall too. They listen to the song. The room is still quiet. Then, the person starts to talk. They say things to you. They are talking. This is what is happening right now.

You are not done yet. You are still in this room. There is nothing else happening. You are only thinking about what is happening now.

If you were to leave the room, you would have stopped. You would leave the room and go to another room. You would not be in the room anymore. The room is gone. The room is not coming back.

This is what an eventually means. It means something is happening right now. It is not going to end. It is already happening.
