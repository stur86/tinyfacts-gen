To use something means to use it. It is to take a thing and make it do its job. Think of a book. You can read it. You can use it. You might read a story or learn new things from it. You are using it for a reason.

You can use a key to open a door. The door is closed. You use the key to turn it. The door opens. You are using the door.

You can also use a phone. You pick it up. You talk to a friend. You use the phone for a reason. You want to talk to someone who is far away.

Sometimes, we use things without thinking. We might use a glass to drink from. The glass is for drinking. We use it to hold the water. It helps us stay safe.

You can use a computer. You type words on the keys. The computer does its job. You use the computer to do things.

To use something is to make it work for you. It is a way to make life better.
