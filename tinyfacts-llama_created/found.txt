To find something means you look for it. It is like a game where you try to find a thing you did not know was there.

Imagine you are in a big room. You are looking for a small thing that is only small. You look at the floor, the table, and the walls. You look in the boxes and the bags. You look under the things you picked up. You look in the dark corners. You look until you feel the thing under your fingers.

You are looking for a small thing that you do not know is there. It is a feeling in your heart. It is like you are on a big search. You do not know where to start. Then you start looking. You try to feel the things. You try to find the small thing. When you find it, you are happy. You feel like you were a good person.

Sometimes you find things that are not there at all. You find a thing that you thought was gone. You are happy. You feel like you have something new to add to your life.

You can also find things in your own life. You find a book you did not know you wanted to read. You find a song you like. You find a way to make your life better. You found something new that makes you feel good.
