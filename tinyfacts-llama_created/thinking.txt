When you think, you are using your brain to understand the world around you. You are not just sitting there. Your body is moving, and your mind is working.

Think is like going over a story in your head. You might remember the first part, but you also remember some of the other parts that happened later. Your mind is like a computer, and the stories you think are like the numbers the computer uses to do things.

Sometimes you think very fast. You might be thinking about many things at once. Your mind is like a big room with many rooms. Each room is for one thing. When you think very fast, your mind moves from one room to another many times in a second.

Think is also about what you believe. You might think that a person is nice or that something is true. Your brain is sending a clear word to your body. If your brain says you are a good person, your body feels good. If you think something is not true, your body might feel bad.

You can also think about the past. You might think about what happened before you were a baby. You remember your parents, your home, and your friends. Your brain is holding a picture of those things.

Sometimes you just think about nothing in a big way. You might wonder why you want to do something. This is a kind of thinking that happens in your head without moving your body.

Think is an important part of life. It helps you understand the world and your place in it. It helps you learn from your past and plan for the next day. It helps you make good and bad things happen.
