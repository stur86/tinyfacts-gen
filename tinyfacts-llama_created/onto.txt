The word onto comes from the word on. When you put something on, you add it to your body. You wear clothes or use a hair straighter. You add the thing you need to make yourself look nice or feel better.

But onto can also mean something that comes after you. Imagine you are in a line. There is a person in front of you. Then there is a person behind you. After you, there are many more people. This line of people is like a road. People are going in this line and then they get to the other end. You are the one after the person in front of you.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about things that come after you. If you do something good, the people around you might say, you are onto something. This means you are seeing what is right or that you are doing something good.

It can also mean that someone is following you. If you are the one who is doing something, then the people following you are the ones who are doing it. They are doing the same thing as you.
