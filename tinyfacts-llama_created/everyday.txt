To do something every day means you do it every single day. You do it over and over and over again. 

If you love to eat food, you might eat breakfast, lunch, dinner, and also a small bit before going to bed. This is your every day. You do not forget any of these times, but you also do not do everything at the same time every day. You change what you do with the days. 

You might do a different thing every morning. One morning you might write, another morning you might play games with a friend, another morning you might do some work at your job. You change the things you do with the days, so you do not feel stuck to doing the same thing every day.

You also do things in your free time. Free time is the time you have when you are not doing anything else. You could watch a movie, play a game, read a book, or work on a computer. You do these things in your free time, and you do them every day. 

Doing something every day is important. It keeps you going. If you did one thing every day, you would get tired quickly. You would feel sad and you would want to stop doing it. But if you keep doing it, you become strong and you feel happy. You learn to be better at your job and at things you like.
