To form a group is to put together many people who share a thing. This thing is often a group of people with the same job, or a group of people who like the same music.

You can also form a group when you have many things. This is when you put together many things that are all the same. You might put many books together to make one book. You might put many animals together to make a big group of animals.

Sometimes, a group is a way to work. If many people work together, they can do more than one job. They can help many different jobs. This is how a big city works. There are groups of people who help each other and work together to make the city safe.

You can also form a group around a thing that is important. If you are very worried about a person, you might form a group of people who care about them. You can tell each other things they should do and help them when they are in trouble.

The word is also about how people feel. When many people are happy, they feel a strong group feeling. This is why groups of people often laugh or shout together.
