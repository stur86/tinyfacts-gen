To search means you look for something or someone. When you search for a thing, you try to find it. You might walk through a big room. You might ask other people if they know where it is. You might look under tables or behind walls.

You also search for a person. If you want to find a friend, you look for them. You ask if they are there. You might look in a house, in a school, or in a park. You are looking for a person to talk to or to find.

Sometimes you search in your mind. You think about a memory. You think about what happened. You try to remember every word. You look at your face. You look at your clothes. You are looking to remember something that is gone.

To search is to be looking. You are not just looking at one thing. You are looking at many things. You are looking at the world around you. You are looking at your life.
