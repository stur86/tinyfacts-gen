Steps are things you do from one place to another. You go one way and then you go the other way. This helps you get where you want to go. When you walk, you do many steps. When you ride a car, you also do many steps.

Think about how a dog moves. The dog goes forward. It steps one way. Then it steps another way. It keeps moving forward until it gets to where it wants to go. This is what we mean by moving.

Steps can be small or big. One step can be very small. Another step can be very long. Some steps are easy to do, like walking up a big street. Other steps might be hard to do, like climbing a very high wall.

You can also have steps in your life. You might go through the steps of growing up. You start small, like taking a tiny step. Then you get a little bigger, and you take another step. At some point, you reach a high step. You feel strong and happy. You can do more of what you want because you feel ready.

Steps help us learn. Imagine you want to learn how to play a new game. You watch how the person does it. Then you try it. You take small steps and you get better. You become more sure. Your steps help you learn and grow.
