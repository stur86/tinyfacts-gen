To carry something means to hold it. If you have a bag of food, you can carry it around with you. If a person has a hard thing to put on their back, they carry it.

Babies carry their mothers inside their bodies. After a few months, they get their own clothes and can carry things too. When a mother gives her baby a drink, she carries it in her hands. This helps the baby learn how to drink and move around.

Some animals carry food to their young. A mother animal with big further than her baby has to go to far away places to find more food. She carries it in her mouth. When she comes back, she gives the food to her baby. This helps the baby grow strong.

In the cold, white things that live in the ground, a baby can carry its mother on its back. This is how they stay warm and get food. Sometimes, a mother can carry many babies at once, and she has to carry them very fast so they do not get hurt. This is very strong work.
