One is a number that comes after the number two. It is the number after every two numbers in a line of numbers. If you look at a line of numbers, you will see two, three, four, five, and so on. When you add one more number to the end of this line, the numbers become three, four, five, six, and so on.

One is also a word that means a person who is the first one in a group. If there are many people in a group, one person is the first. This person is the one who starts the group.

Think of it like a line of people. If you look at the line, the first person is one. If you move one person to the right, you have two, then three, and so on. One is the number that starts the line of people.

One is also the number for the day when you start a new week. Every week has seven days. The first day is called the first day of the week. When you counters the days, you start with the first day, which is one.
