To study something means to learn about it. You might look at pictures of it, or read about it. You want to know more about it so you can understand it better.

A study often happens in a school. People sit in a room with a teacher who talks about a book. The teacher uses words and pictures from the book. The students listen and try to remember what they heard. Later, they might write their own answers or draw pictures from what they learned.

You can also study a person. If you want to know more about a person, you might read their story. You learn about their life, their friends, and their family. This helps you understand who they are and why they act the way they do.

Sometimes, a study is about a thing that is important for our life. You might study how to keep your body clean. You learn about how to use a shower or how to wear clean clothes. This helps you stay safe and look well.

In some cases, a study is about the way things work. You might study how a car moves or how a tree grows. This helps you understand how the world works and how things change over time.

To study something can be a big job. You need to read, think, and remember. You might feel tired or want to give up. But when you learn something new, it feels good. You have done something important for your life.
