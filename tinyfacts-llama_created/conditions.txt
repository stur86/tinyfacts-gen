To meet a set of hard facts is to be in a place and time where everything is done as it should be. If you go to a school and you learn, you are meeting the facts of the day. If you go to a hospital and you get a cut, you are meeting the facts of the day. You are not doing anything wrong.

Think about a game. If you are the one who hits the ball, you are doing something right. But if you are the one who throws the ball, you are doing something wrong. You are not meeting the facts of the day.

You can also talk about the way a world works. If a place has many people and many cars, it is meeting the facts of the day. If a place has no people or no cars, it is not meeting the facts of the day.

Sometimes, meeting these facts is hard. You might be afraid. You might feel like you are the one who is wrong. But if you listen to what others have to say, you can learn. You can see what other people are doing and what they think is important. You can join in and do it yourself.

Even in your own life, you meet facts every day. You meet the facts of who you are. You meet the facts of how you feel. You meet the facts of what you have learned. You meet the facts of what you want.
