An answer that comes back after you speak is called an answer. If you shout a word into the air, the word goes out and comes back to you. This is called an answer because it is the same word, but it is heard from another place.

Imagine you are standing in a room. You say,  The sun is up. A friend in another room hears you and says,  The sun is up. The two of you have the same answer. You and your friend both hear the same word. This is how answers work.

Sometimes you say something and then you hear the same word back from someone else. This is also an answer. You say a word, and then the word comes back to you. This happens with sounds, too. If you make a sound with your mouth, the sound goes out and comes back to you.

An answer can also be a word that a person or animal uses. If you look at a dog, the dog says,  I am here. This is an answer because the dog is using the word. The dog is not the one saying the word, but it is the one using it to tell you where it is.
