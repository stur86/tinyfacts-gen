The word is about how often something happens.

Think of a bright night. You see many stars. The stars are very many. There are many stars in the night sky.

There are also times when one thing happens very slowly. The water in a bottle is very slow. A tiny bit of water can change the whole bottle in a very short time. But if you wait for many years, the water will have almost none left.

In the world, there are things that are very hard to find. A beautiful animal that only lives in a far place. A small, hard thing that is not used by anyone. A place where the air is very quiet. These are all very hard to find.

There are also times when one thing happens very quickly. A dog can run very fast. A child can jump high. The sun can go around the world very fast. These are all very fast.

The word can also mean that something is not as strong as it seems. If a tree is very tall, but you think it will fall down, then it is not as strong as it seems. If a light is very bright, but you think it is not that bright, then it is not as bright as it seems.
