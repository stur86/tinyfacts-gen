To relationships means to be in a way that is close to each other. This happens when you do the same thing or when you feel the same way about something. 

Imagine you are with a friend. You both love playing a game, but you play it in different ways. You might run around and shout while your friend plays with a ball. You both feel happy and have fun. In this case, your friend is your relationship. You are close and you do the same thing. You feel the same way and you both enjoy it.

You can also relationships with things. If you love a book, you feel a strong feeling inside you. This feeling is like a big warm inside. You might read the book every day because it makes you feel happy. You have a relationship with the book because you care about it.

Sometimes you have a relationship with a person. You like them because they are kind and make you feel safe. You feel happy when you see them. This is a good relationship because it makes both people feel good.

You can also have a relationship with a thing. If a car is your relationship, you like it because it moves and takes you where you need to go. You feel happy when you see it driving. This is a good relationship because it makes you feel excited.

In life, relationships can be with people, animals, things, or even ideas. They are the ways we show love and care to each other. They help us feel safe and make us feel happy.
