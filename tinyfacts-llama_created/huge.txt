To huge means you are very big. A huge car is one of the biggest cars in the world. It is as long as three houses are put together. A huge animal is one that has a body as big as a house. A huge tree is one that is taller than a high school building. A huge dog is one that can run very fast. A huge house is one with many rooms, many floors, and many windows.

If you are huge, you are not small at all. You are the biggest. It does not matter if you are standing or if you are sitting. You are the biggest.
