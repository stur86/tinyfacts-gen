This word is used to say a place is right now or to show where something is.

Think of the word like a line. The line can go from one end to the other. If you are standing here, you are in the middle of the line. You have not left the line yet, and you do not have to cross it.

We use this word when we want to tell where something is. If you say, you are here, you mean you are right now in this place. You are not at another place in a different time. You are in one place at the moment.

Sometimes, we also use the word to talk about a time. If you say, I am here, you are also in the same time. You are not in another time but the same time as you.

This word is very important. It helps us understand where we are and what is happening right now.
