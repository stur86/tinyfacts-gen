A room is a space inside a building. You might see one in a home, a school, a shop, a park, or a hospital. It can be small or very big. Many rooms have doors and windows that let in light. Some rooms are for sleeping. Others are for eating food. Some rooms are for playing games. Some rooms are for working.

You can have many rooms in one building. One room might be for a family. Another room might be for children. One room might be for grown people. Each room has its own space. People move in and out. You do not have to be in one room to be inside a building.
