To write something is to tell people what to do. When you write a story or a set of steps, you are writing a kind of word for people to follow. You tell them what to think, what to feel, and what to do in a place.

A set of words is like a group of stories. You write one story and then write another story about the same thing. You can write stories about how people feel or how things happened in the past. You can also write about how to do a job or how to act in front of people.

A set of words is often called a book. You can read one book and learn many other books. You can read about many different things and you can learn about them one by one. You can also read a book that tells you how to live your life.

Sometimes you write words for a group of people who do the same job. If you work in a big city, you might write words for how to drive a car. Your words tell people how to use the roads and how to stop when the light turns green. Your words help everyone stay safe.
