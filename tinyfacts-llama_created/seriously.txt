To say something is seriously means you are very, very sure about it. This means you do not just think it is true, but you are also very strong in your mind about it.

Imagine you are talking to a friend. If you say, you are sure you did something, your friend might feel surprised. But if you say, I am really sure, you mean they must believe you. It is like you have put your mind to it and you are not afraid to say so.

This word is often used when you are talking about something that is very important. If you tell a story that is full of hard facts, and you use this word, people will know you really meant those facts.

It can also mean you are not afraid to be different. If you like to wear a different color or a funny hair cut, you might say, I am being seriously different. This is when you are not afraid to be yourself, even if that is new or different from what others do.

Sometimes, you might say, I am being seriously sad. This means you are really, really sad. You might be crying a lot, or you are feeling very much pain in your heart. You are not just feeling sad, you are really, very sure that your heart is hurting.
