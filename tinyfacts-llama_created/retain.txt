To keep something can mean different things.

One way is to keep a door shut. If you keep a door shut, you are keeping things inside. It is safe and quiet. You do not have to let people in. This keeps people from entering a house when you are not home.

Another way is to keep things for later. Imagine you have a book you read a long time ago. You do not want to read it again. So you keep it in your room so you can find it later. You keep it safe so you can remember what you learned.

Sometimes, to keep something means to keep it away from people. If you are afraid of a dog, you might keep your door closed. You do not want the dog to come into your house. This keeps you safe.
