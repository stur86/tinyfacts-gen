To be clean is to be free from the stuff that makes things sick. When you pick up a chair that has a lot of red stuff on it, you clean it. You take away the red stuff so that it does not hurt your skin. You use water to wet the chair and then you use a long, soft thing to brush away the red stuff. After you clean the chair, it looks nice and does not feel like it has a lot of bad stuff inside it. If you do this to every chair you see, the whole city is clean.

A person who is clean is often very happy. They feel that they are in a good place. This is because they do not have to worry about getting sick. They can go to school or work without being afraid. They can play with other kids and be friends. They can even go to a movie without feeling the bad feeling that comes after they eat something they do not like.

Sometimes, a person who is clean is also a person who is good at their job. They do not have to worry about making a lot of money. They can just do their work and be happy. They might be a doctor who helps people feel better or a teacher who makes kids happy so they learn.

You can also use this word for things that are made by a person. A person who is a nice clean person might make something very nice. They use lots of good stuff and do not use a lot of bad stuff. This makes the thing look nice and feel good to touch. They might also make a food that is very good to eat. They follow the right ways to make the food so that it is not too hot or too cold. This is why the food is clean and good for you.
