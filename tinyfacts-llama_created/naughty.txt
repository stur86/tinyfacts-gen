To be bad is to act in a way that is not right. It is to do things that are not good for you or for others. It can mean doing things that hurt people.

Imagine you have a big dog. The dog is very good and does many good things. But one day, the dog does something bad. It eats a lot of food in one night. This is being bad.

You can also be bad when you act like a child. You might play with things that are not safe. You might talk to a grown person in a way that is not nice. This is being bad because you do not follow the right ways.

Sometimes, a person might be very good. They help their family. They do kind things for others. This is being good. But sometimes, a person might be very bad. They do things that hurt others. They say bad words. They do things that are not right. This is being bad.

To be good is to act in a way that is kind. To be bad is to act in a way that is not kind. You can be both good and bad at the same time. You might do good things. Then, you might do something that hurts someone. This is because your heart is different.
