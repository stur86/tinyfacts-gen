A small town is a place where many people live together. It might be on the side of a big water, or it might be in the middle of a big field. There are usually lots of houses, and people go to the same school. They all live together and help one another.

A small town is usually smaller than a city. In a city, you can find many big stores, many buses, and many people from all over the world. In a small town, you might only have two stores, and there are not many buses. People might know everyone in their town. It is a good place to start a new life.

Some small towns are very close to a big city. The people in the small town might go to the big city for work, but they also stay in the small town. This makes the small town feel like a part of the city, even if it is far away.

A small town can also have a big heart. People in a small town care about each other. They help those who need it, and they work together to keep the town safe. They might have a big high school, a small shop, and many small businesses. They all work together to keep the town going.
