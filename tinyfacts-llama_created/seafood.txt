Search

To search for something means you look for it. You might look in a big room with many doors. You might look in a dark room. Or you might look in the water. 

If you search for a person, you look for them. If you search for a thing you want to buy, you look at the things that are for that thing. You might look at a table or a chair. You might look at a book.

Searching can be hard because there is a lot of things. You need to know where to look. You need to know what you are looking for. But it is also fun. You get to go to the water and see what is there. 

You can search for food. You might search for a thing that can make you strong. You might search for a thing that can make you feel happy. You might search for a thing that can keep you safe.

If you search for a word, you look at many books. You look at words that are like the word you are looking for. If you find the word, you can read it and learn more about it. 

Sometimes, people search for a thing that is very small. They might look at tiny animals. They might look at tiny things that are not even seen by the eye. They might look at the tiny things that live in the water.
