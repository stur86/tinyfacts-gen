To hang something on a long bar

You can use a thing that holds your clothes. It is a tall thing with many short bars. You put your clothes on the bars. Then you hang the thing on a wall or a room. This makes it easy to take clothes out or put them back on.

You can also hang things like books, pictures, or small things you do not want anymore. The long bar stays up in the room, and you can see them all from your bed or from another room.

The word can also mean to put something in a bag. If you have a lot of things to carry, you can put them in bags. The bags help you hold many things at once.
