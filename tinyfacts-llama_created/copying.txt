To be a good friend means to always act with your heart. It means you are kind, you listen, and you help others when they are sad. You also stand up when someone is wrong.

To be a good friend does not just mean you are nice. It also means you learn from the people around you. If you see a friend make a bad move, you might tell them it is not good. This helps them to learn. It is a way to help others grow.

Imagine you have a computer. You make a picture. You want to show it to your friend. You can send the picture to them. When your friend sees the picture, they can understand why you made it. This is like being a good friend. It is about being a good friend in your own way.

You can also be a good friend by being kind to a friend. If your friend is sad, you can listen to them. You can tell them they are not alone. This shows that you care. You do not just sit there. You are there for them.

A good friend is a person you like to spend time with. You might go to movies with them, play games together, or talk about your day. When you are with this friend, you feel happy. You feel safe. You feel like you can be yourself.
