A person who helps another is a good friend. They help you when you need to find a job, a house, or a doctor.

Imagine you are in school and you are not sure if you should go to the front or the back to ask a teacher for help. A person who is already in the front helps you. They go first and say, in a clear voice, what you need to do.

When you need to find a job, you can ask a friend. They know the job and the city. They tell you where to go. They also help you make a good cover letter or a strong cover so that you stand out in the many people who are looking for work.

You can also ask a friend to help you with a problem. Maybe you hurt your foot and you want to see a doctor. Your friend calls the doctor for you and helps you get to the place quickly.

When a person helps you, they are saying, I am here to help you.
