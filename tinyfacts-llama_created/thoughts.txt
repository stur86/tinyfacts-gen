To think is to be busy in your mind. It happens when you are thinking about something. You might be thinking about a problem you need to fix. You might be thinking about a friend you like. Your brain is working very fast. Your eyes are looking at the world, but your mind is busy with other ideas.

You can not think about one thing at the same time. Your brain has many ideas going on at once. One idea might be important and another idea might be less important. You have to pick which idea to keep in your mind.

Sometimes, you only have one thought. It might be about a thing you see. You see a red car and you think, I want to drive that car. You might not have many other ideas for a long time.

You can also think while you are walking. You might think about what you need to buy at the store. You might think about a song you want to listen to. Your mind is always busy, even when you are moving around.

It is like a fire in a box. The fire is always moving, and it is always changing. You can not stop it from moving. But you can watch the fire. You can see how it is burning and how it looks. This is what it means to think.
