To present something is to show it to someone. You might give a story to a group of people. You tell them about what happened. You might also give them a picture. This is how you share your ideas with others.

You can present things in many ways. You might speak in front of a group. You might show a movie. You might even show a picture. You are showing them what you think.

Sometimes, you need to present a plan. If you want to move a group of people, you might present your plan to them. You tell them why you are doing this and how you will do it. This is an important way to show what you are doing.

You can also present a thing that is not a person. If you have a new book, you might present it to your friends. You show them the cover. You tell them about the story inside. You want them to know what they are about to read.

In some cases, you present something that is already there. If you want to show your family that you are happy, you can simply let them know. You might say, I am happy. You are already showing them that you are happy.

To present something is to share it with others.
