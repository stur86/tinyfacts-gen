To burn something means to make it hot and give off fire. This happens when the tiny parts inside the thing start to move very fast and make a lot of hot air. The hot air comes out through the top and the bottom of the thing.

You can burn food. You put food into a thing that makes fire. The food gets hot and the hot air comes out. The hot air and the fire make a big bright light in the air. You can eat what is left after you use the thing to make fire.

You can also burn things that make a loud sound. These things are like the things that make a loud sound when you hit them with a stick. You hit the thing until it makes a loud sound. After you stop hitting, the sound goes away.

Things that burn are not like living things. They do not need air to live. They need only one thing to keep burning. If you take away one of these things, the thing will stop burning.
