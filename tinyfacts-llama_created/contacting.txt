To reach someone or something means to get in touch with them. You can do this by talking to them, sending a letter, or using a phone. If you are trying to talk to a person you know, you are reaching them. This is how you can tell them you are there and you want to talk.

You can also reach a place or a thing. Imagine you are in a city and you want to go to a store. You are reaching the store. You are using your body to get there. If you are in a far place, you might use a car to reach your home. You are using the car to move your body to the place you want to be.

Sometimes you reach something that is not a person. You might reach a computer. You type a word and the computer sends it to the person who wants to see it. You are reaching a thing that is not a person but still helps you.

To reach something is to get to it. It is when you use your body to find what you need. If you reach your bed, you are in your home. You are getting to the place where you sleep.
