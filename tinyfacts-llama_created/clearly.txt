To show something very clear means to make it easy to see or understand. You might see a picture and then you know what each person is doing. If you are talking to someone and you are sure you are speaking the same words, you can show that you are clear.

When you are clear, you do not hide or try to keep things from others. You tell them the truth. This helps people know what to do. If you are clear about a problem, you can fix it. You do not leave people wondering or confused.

In a group, if someone is clear about their idea, they help the others understand. They do not just talk; they show how their idea works. This makes everyone feel the same way.

You can also be clear about your body. If you are not tired, you are clear. If you are not hurt, you are clear. Being clear helps you stay safe and feel good.
