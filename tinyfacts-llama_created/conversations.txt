To speak is to use words. When you talk to a friend, you are speaking. When you talk to a child, you are speaking. When you talk to a group, everyone is speaking at the same time.

When you talk, you are moving your mouth and your voice. Your voice is the sound you make. Your mouth is the thing that moves. You use your mouth to send the sound. This makes people hear you.

You can talk about many things. You can talk about your day, your friends, your family. You can talk about your work, your school, or your games. You can talk about your hopes or your fears. You can even talk about stories from your life.

Talking with other people is important. It helps you understand how they feel. It helps you know what to do. It helps you find people you like to talk to. When you talk with people you know, you are sharing your ideas. When you talk with people you are not sure about, you are trying to figure things out together.

Sometimes talking is hard. You might not know what to say. You might feel nervous. But you can still talk. You just need to use your words. You can say a few words and then stop. You can look at the other person and see if they want to talk more. You can use body words too. You can smile, make eye look, and use your voice. These words can help people feel happy or scared.

Talking is also important for growing. When you talk to people, you learn about them. You learn about their lives. You learn about their hopes and fears. You learn about their families and their friends. You learn about the world and how it works. This helps you understand how people are.
