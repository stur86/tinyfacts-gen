The word is a bit like a middle place. When you look at a line, one end is the left side and the other is the right side. In the middle, two ends meet.

If you are talking about time, you might say,  middled. This means you were in the middle of doing something. You were not at the very start or the very end, but right in the middle.

If you are talking about a room, you might ask, where is the middle? The middle is in the middle of the room. It is not far from the front wall, normals wall, or the back wall.

Sometimes, the word can also mean a middle name. A person might have a first name and a middle name. The first name is the name before the middle name. If the middle name is longer than the first name, the first name is usually the shorter one, and the middle name is the longer one.

In some games, you need to be in the middle of the line to play. This means you have to be close to the front and back of the line at the same time.
