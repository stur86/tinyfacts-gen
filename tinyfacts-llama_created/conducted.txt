To be done is to have happened. You can do many things. You can go to school. You can eat food. You can play a game. But you are not done if you still have to do another thing.

You can also be done with a problem. If a person has a big problem, they are done. They have found the right way to fix it. They can feel happy because they have a clear mind.

You can also be done with a work. If a person is done with their job, they feel they have done their work. They can stop doing their job for a short time. They might watch a movie or talk with a friend.

To be done with something is to be finished. It is the end of a thing or a moment. When something is done, it is gone. You can not start again. This is important. It helps us know when a time is over.

You can also say a person is done with a person. This means they have stopped playing together. They are not friends anymore. They might get angry or walk away. This happens when a person is not happy with another person anymore.
