A light box is a thing you use to make things look bright. It works by sending out light from one side and into the room. You can turn it around to see the other side too.

You use it to make pictures of people and things. It sends the light from the front and out of the sides. This makes the picture look bright and clear. You can also use it to make things look nice. If you have a box with many different colors, you can turn it on and the colors will change.

Some light boxes are very big. They take up a lot of space. You might have to move them around to find the right spot in a room. Other light boxes are smaller. You can put them in your desk or on a table.

Light boxes are also used in computers. You can put pictures or words on them so that they show up when you open the computer. This helps people see things they have made.
