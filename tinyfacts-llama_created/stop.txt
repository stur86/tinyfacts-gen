To stop means to change what you are doing so you do not go any farther. Imagine you are walking along a road. You reach a big stop sign. You do not want to walk any longer. You turn your feet around and stop. You are now not moving.

You can stop a car, a train, or a dog. If you do not want to go any faster, you stop the thing. You can also stop a thing that makes noise. If a dog bites, you stop the dog so it does not hurt you.

When you stop, you are in a safe place. You are not moving. You can sit down and rest. You can look at the people around you. You can also listen to a song or a story. This helps you feel calm.

Sometimes, you stop to think. You stop to look at a picture. You stop to think about what happened. This is a good way to be quiet and remember things.

You can stop a fight between two people. You stop the words. You stop the angry looks. You stop the loud voices. This helps everyone feel better.
