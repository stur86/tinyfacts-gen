To tone something means to make it sound softer. Imagine you speak into a phone. Your voice is loud. If you talk to a friend, you make your voice softer so they can hear you better. If you use this on a computer, you can make the words on a picture look better.

A toning thing is a small box that makes the sound of a voice smaller. It is usually in a box you hold and turn with your hands. You put the box over your head and speak into it. The box makes the sound softer, so you can hear it better on the phone or computer.

Toning can also mean to make something look better. If you use a toning thing on a picture, you turn the colors so the picture looks nice. It makes the picture look like it was made for a person who sees it.

Sometimes, to tone is used to talk about money. If you talk about something that is very good, you might say it is a toned thing. This means the thing is very nice and you want to buy it.
