When you do something, it is often not finished. You started to do it, but then you had to stop because something else happened or you got tired. You left your work in a strange place, or you did not finish all the steps. You did not finish your work.

But if something is completely done, it is finished. It is not in a strange place anymore, and you can not start doing it again. You can look back on what you did and see that it was done. You feel happy because you finished what you started.

Think about when you build a house. You start to put the ground together. You put one piece on top of another, and it gets stronger. After a while, the whole house is built. You have finished your work. You feel a big feeling of being complete.

Sometimes, we might feel like we are not done with a thing. We might want to do something else, but then we remember that we already did it. That is a kind of not being complete. We have done it, but we are still looking for another way to do it.

To complete something is also to stop doing it and to move on to another thing. You can stop doing a game and do another one. You can stop writing a story and start another one. You are not the same person anymore. You are in a new place.

If you do something for many years and you never stop, you are completing your life. You have lived a full life. You have tried many things, and you have learned a lot. You can look back and see all the good you did. You are a person who is done with their work.
