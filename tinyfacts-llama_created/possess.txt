To have something means you are the one who has it. If you have a small ball, you are the one holding it. You are the one who plays with it.

When you say you have something, you are telling the world you own it. You are not looking at the ball, you are already holding it in your hand. It is in your hands.

If you do not have something, you are still there. You are still standing in the same place. But you do not have it. You look at the ground or the wall. You are looking at something else.

Sometimes, you are holding something because you want it. You are trying to get it. You are not ready yet. You want to be the one who owns it. This is a good feeling. You feel happy when you have something you want.

You can also say you have a friend. You like this person. They are your friend. You do not have to say you have them. Just because you are around them, they are your friend. They are in your life.

Sometimes, you have something inside you. You are strong because of it. You feel good when you are this way. It is inside you, but it is also in your body.

To have something is to be the one who is in control. You are the one who decides what happens. You are the one who makes the words come out of your mouth.
