To know something means you understand it. When you know something, you have seen it with your eyes. You have felt it in your body. You have heard it from others. You have learned it from books or from people who have told you things.

Think about how you feel when you see a picture of a tree. You know what the picture is. You know it is a tree. You know it has a big top part and a long part that goes down into the ground. You know it has a round top and a point in the middle. You know it is a thing that grows in the ground.

You also know things about your body. You know how your heart works. You know how your arms and legs move. You know how your eyes see. You know how your ears hear sounds. You know how your brain works so you can remember things and understand what is happening.

When you know something, you have a feeling of understanding. You feel like you know the answer. You feel like you have found the right answer. You are sure that what you know is true.
