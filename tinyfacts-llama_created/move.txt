To move something means to change its position in space. Think about a ball on a floor. If you stand next to it, you are in the same place. But if you walk away from it, it is now in a different place. This is moving. You are changing where you are standing.

You can move your body too. If you sit down on a chair, you are in one place. But then you stand up and move to the next chair. This is a different place. You are now in a new spot. This is moving.

You can also move things that are not on the floor. If you pick up a book, you are moving it from one place to another. You are moving it from your hand to your pocket. This is a change of place. You are changing where the book is.

Sometimes, to move something means to change how it looks. If you take a picture and change the way you look, you are moving your face. You are changing the picture so it looks different. This is another way of moving.

Moving is a way to change how we feel and where we are. It can be hard to move when you are afraid. But it can also be easy to move when you are excited. When you walk, you are moving. When you run, you are moving fast. When you jump, you are moving high up in the air.
