To store something in a box is a way to keep things safe. You can put things like books, papers, or pictures in a box so that no one else touches them.

Imagine you are a teacher and you write important papers on your desk. You put these papers into a box. Then you close the box so the papers stay safe. Later, you can take the box to school and give it to a friend. The friend can look at the papers and understand what you wrote.

A computer also uses this way. When a person makes a word or a picture, they put it in a box. The computer keeps the word or picture safe so that everyone can see it later. People use this box to store their work so it is not lost.
