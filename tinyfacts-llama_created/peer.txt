To be a friend is to be a person you like and trust. A friend is a person who is not your parents, but you still feel safe with them. You can talk with them, play with them, and laugh with them.

When you are a friend, you can tell them how you feel. You can tell them if something is wrong, or if you are happy. They listen to you and might even say nice things back. You can also show them things you like, like a book, a game, or a car. This makes the other person happy and they like you even more.

Friends help you when you need to. If you are feeling sad, you can talk to your friend. They might know more about what you feel than you do yourself. If you are worried about something, your friend can help you think about it.

A friend is not someone you follow. You do not follow them around like a dog. You do not do what they say without thinking about it. You listen to them and follow your own way. This is because friends show you they trust you and care about you.

Being a friend is also about being kind. You do not always have to do what is right. You do what feels right for you. You show the other person you care, even if you are not always sure what to do. This is a big way to show you love them.

Everyone needs friends in their life. They help us feel safe and happy. Without friends, we would be alone. They help us grow and learn. They show us that we are not the only one who is sad or scared. They make us feel part of something bigger than us.

So, a friend is someone you care about. You feel safe with them. You trust them and they trust you. They help you and you help them. You show them you care, even if you do not always know what to do.
