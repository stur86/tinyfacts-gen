To consider means to think about something. When you consider something, you look at it and you think about it. You might think about what it means, or how it looks, or what it feels like.

You can consider many things. You might consider what to wear to a party. You think about which clothes look nice with the other clothes you have. You might also consider what to eat. You think about what food you like and what food you should have.

Sometimes, you consider the hard things. If you have a problem at work, you might consider how to fix it. You think about what you can do to make it better. This is important because it helps you find a way to move forward.

You can also consider your own life. You might think about your day. You might think about how you did things, or how you felt. This helps you learn from your day.

When you consider a person, you look at them. You think about what they do and how they act. You might think about their words and how they speak. You can consider someone you love, a friend, or a family person. This helps you understand them better.

To consider something means to look at it and think about it. It is about paying attention to what is going on and what you think is important.
