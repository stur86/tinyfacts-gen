To put something in a place is to make it stay there. When you build a house, you need to put the walls up. You put the wood under the walls so they will not fall down. You put the floor on top of the walls. You put the walls up so high that the top of the house is close to the sky.

To put something in a car is to make it move. You put the car in the ground. You push it with a big round thing. Then you turn the round thing so the car starts to move. You tell the car to go by pushing the round thing. When the car moves, it does not stop. It keeps going until it reaches a far place.

To put food on a table is to keep it safe for everyone. When you eat, you take the food from the table. You put the food in your bag or your pocket so you do not forget it. When you are ready to leave, you put the food back on the table. This keeps the food safe for everyone.

To put a picture on a wall is to show it to people. You find a place with a good light. You take the picture and put it on the wall. The light makes the picture look good. You can see the picture from many rooms. It is a way to share what you love with others.
