To have an idea about a thing means you think about it. You might not know the answer yet, but you feel sure about something. You want to know if it is true.

You can have an idea about a person. You might think they are kind. You can think about their face, their voice, their way of acting, and you know it is not the way they really are. Or you might have an idea about a place. You know it is not a good place to live.

You can also have an idea about a thing you want. If you want to run fast, you think about it. You think about the feeling of running, the way you run, and the kind of body you have. This idea helps you feel ready and strong to do it.

Sometimes you might have an idea about a problem you want to fix. You think about what you need to do to make it better. You might think you need to talk to the person who is in the bad place, or you might think you need to do something different.

Having an idea is important. It helps you plan what to do next. It makes you feel ready to act. It can help you feel safe. You can have an idea about your life, your job, your friends, or anything else that is important to you.
