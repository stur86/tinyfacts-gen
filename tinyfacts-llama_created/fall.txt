The word fall is a very important word. It is about the changing of times and the change of the day.

When the sun comes up in the morning, the world is full of light. The light is bright and makes everything look new. But when the sun starts to go down, the light gets darker and the world becomes quieter. The light is gone, and the world is dark.

This is because the sun is moving away from us. It is like the world is moving away from the light. The light is the one thing that stays in one place while everything else moves. When the light leaves, the world has to change. The world can no longer have the light to make it look new. The world has to find its own way to look bright again.

When you are a child, you might feel this change. You might feel a bit sad because the light is gone. You might want to hold onto the light so it does not leave. But the light is always there, even when you are not looking at it. It is in the air, in your eyes, and in your heart.

The word fall is also about the ground. When you walk on the ground, the ground is under you. But when you jump or run, the ground is moving. The ground is pushing on you, and you have to push back. This is why you feel the ground when you jump. It is the ground trying to hold you.

In the story of a tree, the fall is a big change. A tree can live for many years. But eventually, it reaches the end. The old wood starts to break, and the tree falls down. This is a new beginning for the tree. It starts from the ground and grows up again, making new wood.

The word fall is also about the end of a time. Think about the end of a year. At the end of the year, we close the book and start a new one. The end of the year is a time of change. We say good things happened and not good things happened. This is the fall of a year.
