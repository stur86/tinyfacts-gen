To be bigger means you are larger than you were before. You might be taller, or your hands are bigger, or you have more space inside you. Sometimes, you might look at a picture of your mom and dad and see that you are not as small as they look.

It is also true that things grow. If you have a small animal, you might grow it. You might add more hair or more further. A house might get bigger as you add more rooms. Your car might get bigger so you can drive faster.

Sometimes, we say a person is bigger because of how they act. If you are a kind person, the world might see you as bigger. You have more power because you show you care about others. But if you act mean or hurt people, you might be seen as smaller. Your act can make your face look smaller, and you might feel like your power is gone.

In life, it is okay to be bigger sometimes. Your voice might get louder, or you might have more power in a game. But it is also important to know when to hold back so you do not hurt others.
