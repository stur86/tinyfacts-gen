To accept someone or something means to agree with them. You can accept a friend or a teacher. If a teacher gives you a good job, you are accepted by the school. You feel happy and you do not want to leave the school.

You can also accept a plan. If you want to play a game, your parents might say,  You can play if you accept the game. You agree to do it. You do not need to be told. You just know it is okay.

Sometimes you might feel afraid. If you are afraid of a dog, you might not want to go near it. But if you are accepted by a dog, you feel safe. The dog sees you as a friend. They do not try to hurt you.

You can also accept a time. If a movie starts a few minutes after you want it to, you might not be happy. But you still watch it. You are still a part of the story. You are not mad at the person who made the movie.

When you accept someone, you show that you trust them. You know they are not going to hurt you. You are okay with them.
