A person who works in a place where you buy things you need is called a worker. Some of these people do the work you see in stores. Others work in places that have many rooms. They might clean the floors, make things, or help people find what they need.

In some houses, people work in the kitchen. They make food for families who live there. They use big boxes, cut food, and put food in bags for people to take home. They also use big hot things to make food that is hot when you eat it.

There are also people who work in big places with many rooms. They are called people who work in offices. They sit at big computers and use keys to make things. Some work in places that build houses. They use big power things to make the houses. Other people work in places that make things for cars, buses, and things that fly in the sky.

When a person works in a place, they usually have a job that is clear. They know what they have to do and when they have to do it. They work with other people to make things that help you. They are an important part of how things work in our world.
