When someone dies, it means they stop living. They do not breathe anymore, and they do not feel anything anymore. Their body is very still, like a big rock.

Imagine you are playing a game. You run very fast, jump over a high thing, and run to the other end. But then you fall down and hit your head. Your body stops moving. You do not get up. You are gone.

Death is a big change. It happens when a person is very old or when they are very, very sick. They might feel like they are going to die too, but they do not. They are still here.

Sometimes, people say that death is like a sleep. When you sleep, your body is resting. When you die, your body is still, and you are not living anymore. But when you wake up, you are still there. You just feel like you were gone for a long time.

Death can also happen to animals. A dog might die from a bad cut. An old tree might die from the wind. When these things happen, the body stops living, and the animal is gone.

Death is a part of life. We all go through it. But it can be very hard to understand. Imagine you love someone very much. When that person dies, you might feel sad. You might cry and say, I am going to miss them. But death is still a part of life. It is a change that happens to everyone, no matter how old they are or how happy they are.
