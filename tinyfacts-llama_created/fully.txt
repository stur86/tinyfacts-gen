To be full is to have all you need. You are not just one thing. You are many parts together. You are not empty inside.

If you have food, you are full. You can not eat more. Your body feels safe. You can stop eating and still be safe.

When you are full, you want to stop. You do not want to eat more. You feel happy. You feel your body is full.

Sometimes, you might be full of a feeling. You feel happy, sad, or angry. It is like your body is full of those strong words.

If you are full of money, you have enough to buy what you need. You do not need more. You feel safe.

If you are full of love, you feel happy inside. Your heart is full. You feel like you are safe with the people you love.
