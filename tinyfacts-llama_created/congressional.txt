A group of people who make the big plans for a land have a thing they call a group that is in power. This group is made by the people who live in the land. They are the ones who decide what happens and who gets what.

The group is made by the people. They pick some of the people to be their first ones. Later, people from other places go to the same group. They also pick some of their own people to be part of the group. This makes it bigger and stronger.

The group makes big plans. They decide how to spend the money the land gives them. They decide how to use the power to help people. They also decide how to deal with the people who do not want to follow them.

The group is made up of many people from different parts of the land. They listen to what people in their area say. They try to find a way that works for everyone. This makes the plans more sure to work.

The group can have problems. Some people might not agree. They might have different ideas. They might try to change the plans. The group has to work with each other to find a way. They can also have fights. These are the times when they try to decide who gets what.

The group is also the group that the other people of the land look to. If something goes wrong, the people of the land look at the group for help. They might ask the group to tell them what to do. If the group does not help, the people might be angry.
