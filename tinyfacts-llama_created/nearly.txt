Nearly is a word that means almost. It is a word that you use when you want to say something is almost something else. It helps us talk about things that are close.

Imagine you are looking at a picture. You see the blue sky behind it. But you also see the green trees in front of it. You do not see the white ground. You see the two together. If you say the sky and trees are nearly the same, you mean they are almost the same as each other.

You can use nearly when you are about to say something, or when you are trying to stop a big thing from happening. If you are about to leave your house, you might say, almost home. This means you are very close to going home.

You can also use nearly when you are trying to remember something. If you remember something that happened a few days ago but you want to remember it more, you might say, nearly. It is a way to say you are trying to be sure.

In some ways, nearly is like almost, but it is more about how close you are. If something is nearly a thing, it means it is very close. If something is almost a thing, it means it is not quite there yet.
