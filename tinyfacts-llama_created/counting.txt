When you counters something, you are making a listening of the things. You start with one thing and you tell each thing. It is like a short story. You say, one, two, three, four, five, and so on.

Imagine you have five small balls. You start with one ball. You take the ball and you say, one. You have two balls now. You take the second ball and you say, two. You have three balls. You keep going and you tell each ball you find. After you have gone through all the balls, you say, five.

This is what we do with numbers too. When you read a number, you are saying the story of the number. One is one, two is two, three is three, and so on.
