To be the case means something is true. You can use this word when you want to tell how a thing is. It is like a key that opens a door.

Think of a dog. He is the case of a dog. This means he is a dog. He is not a cat or a house. He is a dog.

You can also use it to talk about how big a thing is. If something is the case of a book, it means the book is bigger than other books. It is not smaller.

Sometimes, you use it to say you are not sure. If you are the case of something, you do not know if it is true or not. You are still thinking about it.

To be the case is important in our lives. It helps us understand the world and what is happening around us.
