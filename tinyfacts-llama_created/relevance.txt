To be of a thing means you are a part of it. If you are of a book, you read it. If you are of a town, you live there.

When you talk about a thing, you are talking about how it is important to your life. If a song is important to you, you might want to listen to it every day. If a game is important for school, you might want to watch it before bed.

A word can be of many things. You can be of a word, a book, a story, or even a person. When you are of a word, you can tell the story. When you are of a book, you learn new things. When you are of a person, you learn about how they act.

Think about a game you play with a friend. The game is a thing. If you are of the game, you are a part of it. You follow the game. You do what the game tells you to do. You do not make the game.

When you talk about this, you are saying that one thing is important to another thing. You are saying that the first thing is important to the second thing.

This is why the word is so important. It helps us know how things are.
