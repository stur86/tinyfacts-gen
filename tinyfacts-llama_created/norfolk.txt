Normal people live in the land between the big water and the green land. They live in a place where the water is very cold and the air is very cold too. They live in houses with many small rooms and a big room in the middle where the whole family sits together.

They have a way of doing work that is very different from the way people in other places do things. They use long things that go across the water to move things from one place to another. They also use small round things that go into the water to move air. This way they can carry food, clothes, and other things from one place to another, even when the cold water makes it very hard to breathe.

They are not like the people who live in the green land. They do not grow food in the ground. They grow food in the air, and they grow it in houses that are built high above the green land. They use the green land for growing food, and the cold water for moving things and air.

They have a different way of talking. They use words that start with a soft sound. They also have a way of using numbers that is different from the way people in other places do things. They use numbers to show how many people are in a group or how much money they have.
