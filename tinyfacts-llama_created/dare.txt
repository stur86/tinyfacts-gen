To ask to do something is to be afraid but you do it anyway. You might feel your heart is going fast or your legs are shaking. You also feel a little bit of a rush. This is what it means to be a bringer of light. It means you have the power to change things.

You can do this because you are not afraid of what might happen. You are not afraid of the things you do. You are sure you can do it.

When you say you will do it, you are making a promise to yourself. You are saying you are going to do it. You are not afraid anymore. You are ready.

This is also how you change the world. When you do something new and different, you are taking a step forward. You are being a bringer of light to the world. You are moving the world in a new direction.
