To go fast means to move your body very quickly. When you are moving, you are not sitting still. You are moving from one place to another. This is how you get from one home to another, from school to work, or from a park to a place you like to be.

Think of a car. The car has a big round thing in the back. The round thing is the part that pushes the ground. When you turn the round thing round and round, the car moves. You are the one pushing the car. You are the one who is moving. If you keep pushing, you go faster and faster.

You can also go fast when you are running. If you run very quickly, you might feel like you are flying. Your feet hit the ground all the time, but your body is going very fast. This is what happens when you are in a race with other people.

Sometimes, you might feel a bit of a rush. This is when you are moving so fast that you feel like your heart is going to fly out of your body. You might feel a bit of a bright feeling, like the world is very bright and very loud. This is because your body is moving very quickly and your brain is sending a lot of power to your body.

You can also say that something is moving fast. This means that it is moving very quickly. If a tree is moving very fast, it might fall. If a dog is moving fast, it might catch a ball. If a car is moving fast, it might get to where you want to be quickly.

When you say a thing is moving, you are talking about how fast it is. You can say a car is moving if it is going over  hundred. You can also say a person is moving if they are running or walking. You can say a thing is moving if it is turning or if it is falling.
