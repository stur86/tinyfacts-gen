To feel something happens in your body is a big feeling. It starts when blood inside your body breaks. This is what happens when you get a cut or a wound. The blood comes out of your body and goes down to your legs or arms.

When you feel this, you might want to stop it. You might use your hand to press on the cut. This helps stop the blood from going out so fast. It also helps your body stay safe. Your brain and body want to keep everything inside your body. If you can feel this, you can act fast to stop it.

Sometimes, this feeling happens when you are very happy or very angry. Your face might get red and your eyes might get wet. It is a strong feeling that tells you something is wrong.
