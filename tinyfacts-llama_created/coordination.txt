To work together is when many people do the same thing. It is when you are not alone. You and many others do the same job.

Imagine you are building a big wall. You are all doing the same job. You are pushing the wood on the ground. You are all moving the wood. You all push. You all pull. You all lift. This is how you build the wall. You are all working together.

The word also means to make things that are ready at the same time. If you are making clothes, the people who are making the clothes are all doing the same thing. They are all making the same clothes. You can see them all standing in front of the table, each one doing their part.

When you work together, you can do more than you could alone. You can do many jobs at once. You can help each other. This is why people in work groups use this word.
