When you do something, you are the one doing it. When you make a fire, you light the wood. This is because you put the wood in front of the fire and it makes the fire start.

Sometimes, you do not have to light the wood. The fire is already there. You just push the wood against it. This is called an act. It is a thing you do without the wood being there.

You can also do this with your mind. You might feel a strong feeling. You do not have to have the feeling. The feeling is already there in you. You just let it come out.

If you want to make a room feel warm, you might put a few things together. You put the chair in the room. You put the table in the middle. You put the small table in front of the chair. This makes the room feel like home. You did not have to put the chair and table there. The room already felt warm because of the things you put together.

You can also do this with your body. You can put on a jacket to stay warm. You do not have to have the jacket. The jacket is already there in you. You just put it on.

So, an act is when you do something without the thing you are doing being there. It is when you let the thing happen on its own.
