To have a life is to live. This means to be living in a world with other living things. Many living things need to eat and drink to stay. They grow and change. They make their own food from the air and water around them.

The word life comes from the word that means to grow. It is a word that is used to talk about living things.

When you say a thing lives, you mean it is not dead. It is still there. It is doing something. A tree lives and stays on the ground for many years. A dog lives with a family and runs around in the park.

We also say a person lives. This is when you are living in a place or a city. You are here and you are doing things. You are not gone. You are in your home.

A life is not just about being. It is also about how you act. You act with your words. You act with your face. You act with your heart. When you act, you leave a mark on the world.
