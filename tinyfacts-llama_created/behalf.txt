To be a part of something means you join others to help them. You might be a friend, a teacher, a doctor, or a police person. When you do this, you are not alone anymore. You are now a part of a group where everyone works together.

Think of a group of people who love to play music. They all have different voices. But when one person has a bad day, they can still be a part of the group. They are still a part of it because they are still listening to the music. You are not just one person. You are a part of the group.

Sometimes, you might be a part of a story. You might be the one who wants to tell the story. You are the one with the words. You are not the one who made the story happen. You are only a part of it. You help people understand what is happening.

You can also be a part of a plan. A plan is like a set of steps that will help you do a job. You can be a part of the plan. You might be the one who does the hard work. You might be the one who helps other people. You are all working together.
