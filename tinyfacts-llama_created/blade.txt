A cut is something you make with a hard piece of wood. A person might use a piece of wood to cut a piece of paper. They hold the wood very tight and then press it down on the paper. The wood cuts into the paper. This can be for cutting food, for making clothes, or for cutting things that are hard to cut.

A different kind of cut is made with a thing that cuts through a thing. If you cut a line through a box, you are making a cut. This cut is not always done with a hard piece of wood. You can use a thing with a point to cut through a thing. This kind of cut is quick.

You can also cut with a thing that looks like a cross. This kind of cut is done with a thing that has a long edge. You put the edge of the thing into the thing you want to cut and then you pull the edge through the thing. This kind of cut is very safe because the edge of the thing cuts the thing, not the person.

A very strong cut can break a thing. If you hit something with a hard thing, the hard thing can cut through the hard thing and make a big break. This is why you do not want to hit a car with a hard thing.
