To eat lunch is to have food at middles. It is a time in the day when people eat after they have had breakfast and before they eat dinner.

Most people have lunch at work. They sit in a room with other people and eat food from a big box. They eat their food while they talk and laugh with the people they know. They often drink a cold drink.

In the morning, when you are a child, your mother or father may give you a small box with a few pieces of food inside. You eat this food while you are getting ready for school. This is a lunch.

Some people eat lunch at home. They sit at a table with their family. They eat food from a box or a bag. They might eat a big piece of food, like a hot dog, or a small bag of food with many pieces.

Lunch is an important time. It is a time to rest and feel good. People eat lunch to give their body the food it needs to stay strong and keep going.
