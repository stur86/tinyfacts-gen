To make something from a hard stuff is the first step. You take a piece of wood or a rock from the ground. You cut it into small pieces. Then you put the pieces in a big box. The box makes the pieces stay close together. This keeps the sound inside.

Next, you make a soft cover around the box. This cover is made from many pieces of a hard stuff that you also make. You put the cover on top of the box. The cover helps keep the sound from getting out too easily.

Then you make the cover soft again. You put the cover under the hard stuff, so it feels nice to hold. You also put a line around the cover to keep it from breaking easily.

After this, you put the cover on a hard thing that makes the cover stay. The hard thing holds the cover in place. You put the cover on the hard thing that makes music. Now the hard thing holds the cover and the sound inside.

People use this kind of cover to make music that sounds very good. They make the cover from many small pieces of a hard stuff, and they put them together in many ways. When you listen to the music, the cover helps the sound get out in a way that feels nice to your ears.
