A child is a person who is very young. They are not yet big like an old person. Children are usually very happy. They love to play with their friends and run around in the park. They learn a lot from books and from people around them.

Children go to a place where they go to school. They do things in a room with many other children. They write words in a book and they listen to a teacher. They learn to read and write. They also learn to do things like tie their shoes and use a computer.

Children need a lot of love and care. A parents love a child very much. They give them food to eat and they give them a safe place to sleep. Children also need to see a doctor if they feel sick. A doctor helps them feel better.

Children grow and change. They start to talk like a grown person. They learn to use words that mean more than just the words they say. They learn to do things by themselves, but they still need help from their parents. As they get older, they become more like the people they want to become.

Sometimes, a child will make a bad thing happen. They might say something that hurts a friend. Or they might do something that is not right. But even in these moments, a child is still learning. They are still growing. They are still trying to understand the world around them.

In the end, a child is someone who needs love and care to grow up into a strong person.
