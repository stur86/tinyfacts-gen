An open office space for groups of people who work together is an apartment with many rooms. There are usually many people who share a large room with computers, tables, and chairs. They sit at tables and talk to each other. They also work on computers to write papers and make money.

These spaces are often found in big cities and are usually very busy. People from many different jobs and places come to be there. They sit and talk for a long time. They often share food and drink.

In some places, people also work from home. They sit in a small room in their own home. They use computers and phones. They work on papers and money. This is a good way to have a job while still living at home.

These open offices can be very different. Some have many people and many computers. Some have only a few people and only one computer. Some rooms are very small and dark, while others are very big and bright.
