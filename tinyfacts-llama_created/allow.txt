When you allow something, you give it space to happen. You do not stop it. You let it stand. If you allow a child to run, you are not watching them all the time. You let them move around and learn. If you allow a dog to enter your home, you are not afraid to let it in. You let it see and smell and feel inside your house.

If you do not allow something, you block the way. You close the door. You tell the child not to come in. You tell the dog not to enter. This keeps things safe inside your home.

You can also allow something to happen in your life. If you let things go, they do not stay there. They go to the next place. This is like allowing your heart to feel. If you let love in, you feel it more. It hurts when love goes away.

To allow is to be open and ready to share. It is to let go and trust that everything will work out.
