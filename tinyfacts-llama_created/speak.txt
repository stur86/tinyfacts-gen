To speak means you use your voice to tell others what you think. You stand up and use your words. You talk to people you know, like your family or friends. You might speak in a group or in a quiet room. You use your mouth to make sounds that are different from one person to another.

When you speak, you tell others about your life. You share how you feel. You might tell them about your day, your school day, or your work. You might also tell them about the world around you. You use your words to show how you think.

Sometimes, you speak with your voice. You might speak when you want to tell a story or when you want to tell someone that you care. Your voice is like your way of saying things. It helps people understand what you mean.

You can also speak in your head. You think about what you want to say and then you say it out loud. This is how you learn new things. You hear others speaking and you listen. Then you use what you heard to help you understand and to remember.

When you speak, you are sharing your part of the story. You are adding to the story of what is happening. You are showing the world who you are and what you do.
