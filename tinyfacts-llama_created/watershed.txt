A water way is a place where two big bodies of water join together. This is called a water way or a water way. The water goes over the land and under the ground. It is a very important place for many animals and people.

Imagine you have a big glass of water. You drink it. The glass is like a water way. If you drink the water, it is gone. But the glass stays the same. It is still the same glass, even though you drank from it.

When two water ways meet, the water changes. It goes from one way to the other. This is why we have a water way. It keeps the water clean and helps keep the land safe.

Sometimes, a water way is so big that it goes over many high places. On top of this water way, there are trees and other green things. These green things help keep the water clean. They take in the bad stuff from the water and let the good stuff go out. This helps keep the water clear.

There are many different kinds of water ways. Some are very small and join together in one spot. Others are very long and go over many high places. Some water ways are only for animals. Others are for people. They carry water from one place to another, and people use them for drinking, growing food, and many other things.

Water ways are very important for us. We need them to live, to grow food, and to stay safe.
