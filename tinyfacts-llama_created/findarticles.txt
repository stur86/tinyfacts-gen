To find something is to look for it. It is when you use a computer or a book to look through many places and find the word you are looking for.

When you search, you start with a word. You type this word into the computer, and it looks for this word in many places. It looks in the words of the books you read, the words of the words of the words, and the words of the things people said.

The computer finds many places where the word is. It shows you these places and tells you which one is the best place to look. You can then read the words in that place to learn more about the word.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If you want to know what someone said, you can look at what they wrote. You can find their words and understand what they meant.

To find an answer to a question is to find the word or thing that helps you understand the question. It is like finding a key that opens a door. When you find the right key, you can start to understand what the door says.
