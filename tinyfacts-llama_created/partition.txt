To cut something into pieces is to use a hard piece of wood, a hard thing, or a hard thing with a long part. This part goes across something so big that it can not be broken into many pieces. It is used to share things when many people have the same thing. It is also used to keep different groups together.

Imagine you have many books. You want to share them with a friend. You go to the friend and say, I am giving you some books. The friend takes the books. You still have the books.

If the friend takes some of the books but keeps the rest, you have two groups now. You and your friend are in one group, and the other group has all the books. This is what a cut does to a thing. It makes two groups.

It is also used when a place is cut into many parts. If a big area is cut into many small areas, it is like many groups. This makes it easier to look at the different parts.

Sometimes the word is used to talk about people too. If you cut a person, you hurt them. If you cut the mind of a person, you change what they think. If you cut a group of people, you make them fight with each other.
