The word every, every one, and every other are often confused with each other. Here is how they work.

Every is a word that means one and only one. It does not have any other word that it can be put into. The word does not have a middle word. It is the first word of a line, and the last word of a line. You can not say, for a long time, that you say something is every thing, because it is not. You can not say that you say one thing, that you say two things, that you say three things. Every is the word you use when you want to say you are the only one.

Every one is a word that means one. It is used to talk about people. When you say, every person is happy, you mean every one is happy. It is a word that is used to talk about your friends and family. It is not about how many people there are.

Every other is a word that means not one. It is a word that is used when you want to say something is not the only thing. If you say, every other day, you are talking about days. You are talking about the days between the days you are not happy. You are not talking about one day, one night, one week. You are talking about many days. It is a word that helps you to put together many things.

Think of it this way. Every person is not the only person. Every one is not the only one. Every other is the word that shows you are not the only one.
