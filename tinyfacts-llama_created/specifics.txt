To have something is to have it in a way. You can have a book, a dog, a car, or a house. Each one has its own ways of doing things and being there. 

A person might have a very good job. This means they do not have to work very hard and they get money. A person with a bad job might have to work very hard and get less money. They have to deal with many problems and might feel very angry. 

You can also have a lot of money. This is when you have a large number of the things that people spend money on. If you have a lot of money, you can buy many things you like: a new car, a big house, many clothes, and many food.

You can also have a very small number of things. This is when you have just one thing. If you have a small number of money, you might not be able to buy many things you want. If you have a small number of friends, you might feel alone.

Sometimes, you have to have someone. You need a teacher to learn how to read and write. You need a doctor to feel better when you are sick. You need a parents to help you when you are very young.
