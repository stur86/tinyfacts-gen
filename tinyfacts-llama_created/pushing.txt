To push something means you use your body to move it away from yourself. This happens when you want to go somewhere. You might push a ball to run faster. You stand in a line with your friends and push each person to move forward. This makes the group move together.

You can also push something that is heavy. You might use your arms and legs to lift a box. You are pushing it up a high floor or across a table. This makes the heavy thing go up or across.

Sometimes, you push things that are hard to hold. If you are afraid of a thing that looks very strange, you might push it away. This is because you do not want it to touch you.

In the world of people, a push is like a fight. If two people are standing and look angry, they might push each other. This is a way of showing they are strong. It can also mean you are trying to get a point in a game or a conversation.
