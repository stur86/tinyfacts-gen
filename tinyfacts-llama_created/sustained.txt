When something is kept going without stopping or giving up, we say it is being kept going. This is often with people, animals, or things that need to keep moving.

If you are in a room and you are afraid to leave, you are not being kept going. You are stopped. You are afraid. You do not want to move.

But if you are walking through a big city and you feel tired or sad, you can keep going. You keep moving your feet and your body. You do not stop. You do not give up.

Sometimes, when you are trying to do something hard, you do not want to keep going. You might want to give up. But if you keep going, you learn. You learn how to do it. You learn how to stay strong.

In a game, if you are playing a game where you have to run and jump, you are being kept going. You keep moving. You do not stop. You keep playing until you are the one who is not playing anymore.

Being kept going can be very important. It can help us get to a good place or do what we need to do to stay safe.
