Right is a word you use when you mean something is in the right place.

If you stand on the right side of a street, you are in the right place. If you stand on the left side, you are in the wrong place. The street is like a line. If you are on the line, you are in the right place.

When you walk, you might want to go left or right. You want to be on the side where the other people are not. If you walk on the wrong side, you might hit a car. Or you might get into a fight.

A long time ago, people grew food that grew on a tall green thing. The green thing is like a big green box with many tiny green things inside. The tiny green things take the water out of the ground and make food. People use the food to eat. They do not use the green box anymore. Now, the word is used to talk about the food people grow.

A kind of food from a green box is called food. A type of food that grows on a green thing is called food too. You can eat food from a green box. You can also eat food from a green thing. Both are food.

In the world, people often use the word when they want to say where something is. If you say a place is in the right place, you are telling the world where it is.
