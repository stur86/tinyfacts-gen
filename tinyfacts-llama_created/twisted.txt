To be in a state of being turned around. Something that was once straight or up now lies down or is hanging. A person might be feeling this way when they are angry or afraid. Their body might be in a strange way and they are not sure what is happening.

You can also say a thing is turned into something else. If you walk across the street and you hit a car, the car changes. The car was not a thing anymore. It became something else. This is how things change.

Imagine you have a ball. You throw it up into the air. The ball goes up. Then it falls down. This is the ball turning around. This is why you always see the same thing when you look at a ball in the sky. The ball is turning around, and this is what makes it look like it is moving.

In life, you might feel this way when you are in a bad place. You feel like you are stuck. You try to move, but you can not. You are turned around and you do not know how to get out.

Sometimes, a person turns a story around. They tell the story in a way that is not what happened. This makes the story different from how you remember it.
