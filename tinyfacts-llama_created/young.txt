To be young means you are very young. This is when you are very small. You are not old yet. You are still growing.

You are young if you are in school. You are in a big group of children. You go to a place with a teacher who helps you learn. You learn to read, write, and do things with your hands.

You are young if you are very small. Your body is still growing. You may not be able to walk yet. You may not have a hair on your head. But you are still very new.

You are young if you are a baby. Babies are very small and very strong. They can drink from a cup. They can do things with their hands that make them happy. Babies are very new and very much fun to watch.

You are young if you are a child. A child is a small person who is still growing. They like to play. They like to run around. They like to hear stories. They are still learning how to do things.

You are young if you are a person. A person is a grown person. They are not a baby anymore. They can do many things. They can work. They can go to a job. They can have a family. They are a person, and they are very new.
