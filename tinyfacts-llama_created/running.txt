When you run, you are moving your feet very fast. You are moving your body from one place to another. You are using the power in your legs to go forward.

People often run when they are happy. They might run while they watch a game. They might run while they listen to music. They might run to clear their head. When you run, you feel your heart beat faster. You feel a little bit of a rush. You are moving and you are not stopping.

You can also run with friends. You might run with a group and you all walk together. This is a good way to stay together. You can talk and laugh while you run. You can feel the wind on your face.

Sometimes, people run because they are afraid. They might run to the police if they see a person they do not know. They might run to go to a safe place if they are in a bad area. Running can help you feel better when you are scared. It can give you a chance to move away from a problem.

You can also run to build up your power. You might start with a short walk and then you run for a longer time. This helps your legs get strong. You can do this many times.

Running is a great way to stay strong and to feel good. It helps your body move and it helps your mind be calm.
