An eventually means you are about to happen. It is a word you use when you are going to do something. It does not mean what you are going to do. It only means the thing happens later.

Think of a game with many rounds. Each round is an eventually. In the first round, you do not know what will happen. In the second round, you know what will happen because you have done it before. In the third round, you already know what will happen.

In life, the eventually is when you do something and then something better happens. Maybe you are not happy. Then you do a new job and find a job that is better. This is an eventually.

You can also use this word to talk about how things turn out after you do something. If you walk on a hot street, you might burn your feet. But then you go to a cool place with a drink, and you feel better.

The word is important because it shows how life is. We do not always know what will happen next. But with an eventually, we know that what happens is going to be good for us in the end.
