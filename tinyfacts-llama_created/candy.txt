Crazy is a word that means something is very strange or hard to understand. You might feel this way when you see a big animal that flies very fast, or when you hear a very loud noise. Your heart might beat fast and you feel your face go bright.

A thing that is crazy is one that is very different from how we think. It might be something that makes you feel happy, or something that makes you feel very sad. It can be a person, a thing, a sound, or even a feeling.

If someone is crazy, they act in a way that is not right. They might shout, jump around, or say things that do not make sense. This can happen when they are very happy, very scared, or very angry. It can also happen when they are afraid of their own body.

To be crazy is not always bad. Sometimes, being a little bit different is a good thing. Imagine you are a person who loves to play music. Your music is so good that it makes everyone want to dance. In this case, you are being crazy in a good way.

But being crazy is also something that can happen because you are not thinking right. If you see a thing that is not there, or if you hear a sound that is not real, your brain gets confused. Your body might start to act in strange ways, and you might feel very afraid.

So, being crazy is about feeling like you are not quite right. It is about being different from what everyone else thinks is true. It is about finding the strange and the hard to understand.
