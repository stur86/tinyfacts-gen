A group of things that are near to each other, often in the same place, can be called a group. This group can be made of many different kinds of things. Some groups are very small and have only a few things. Other groups are very big and have many, many things.

When you have a group of people, you can do things together. You can talk, laugh, and share food. You can work together as a team. This group is important because it gives you a way to do things that are easy and fun.

On a big place, like a town or a city, you might have many groups. There are groups of people who go to school. There are groups of people who go to work. There are groups of people who play games. There are even groups of people who go to movies and watch things. All these groups can be very strong because they are all there together.

Sometimes, a group of things that are not people can also be called a group. This group might be a group of animals. Maybe a big group of animals is trying to stay safe from a bad thing. Or, a group of things that are not people might be a group of things that are very small. One group of things can be made of tiny pieces that are not even a little bit big.

In your brain, there are groups of things that are very important. When you are happy, you might feel a group of things that are very happy. When you are sad, you might feel another group of things that are very sad. These groups are inside your head and help you understand how you feel.
