Hug is a word that you use when you want someone to be close to you. It is a way of showing love and care. You can give a hug to a friend or a family person.

To hug someone, you put your arms around them. Your arms go over their head. Your hands are inside the front of their body. You hold them tight. Your face is near their face. This makes them feel happy and safe.

A hug is not just about being close. You can also use it to show you care about something. If you are sad, you might give a hug to a person who feels the same way. This can help you both feel better.

You can hug your own family too. When you are happy, you hug your mom or dad. You might hug your little brother or sister when you see them. This is a way of saying, I love you.

In some places, people say, you should not be too hard on someone. If you are being too strong, you might hurt them. But if you are being kind and you still want to show them love, you can give them a hug. This is a way of being a good friend and a good person.
