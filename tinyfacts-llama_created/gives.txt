The word gives means to share something with another person, a thing, or an animal. You might give your money to a friend who is in trouble. This is when you give them what you have so they can live better.

You can also give something else. If you have a book you want to read and your friend asks if they can read it, you give it to them. This is like sharing the story.

Sometimes, you give something to something that needs it. If you have food and you give it to a child who is very sick, you help them get better.

You can also give your time. If you work in a school, you give your time to help your class. You do not get paid, but you help make sure everyone is safe and learns.

When you give, you are showing that you care about the other person. It is a way to show love and make people happy.
