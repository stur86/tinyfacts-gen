To continue means you do something after the time is over. When you start a story, you might begin at the first step. The next step is the one after that, and the next one is after that. You keep going until you reach the end.

Imagine you are playing a game where one team is on the ball and the other team is trying to stop them. If the game starts and then the game stops, then you have not continued the game. You have not shown the other team what to do in the next steps.

If you are walking, you might start at one place and end at another. If you keep walking, you are continuing. You are not done yet. You are still moving from one place to another.

In life, we all go through different times. One day you might be a child. The next day you might be a young person. The day after that you might be an old person. You are always moving forward.

Even when something is over, it is still in your memory. You remember the day you saw your favorite movie or the day you went to a place you love. These are all parts of what you continue.

So, to continue is to keep going, to keep moving forward, and to never stop in the middle of what you do.
