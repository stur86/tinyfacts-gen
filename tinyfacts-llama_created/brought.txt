To bring something means to move it from one place to another. When you bring a book, you move it from your desk to the table. You carry it on your arm or in a bag. This is often done for people or animals.

Imagine a child. If a mother brings a baby to her home, she moves the baby from a car to a room. She carries the baby until it is safe and happy. This is what we call bringing.

We also bring things to a work place. If you are late, you bring your work home so you can start it. You bring money to buy food for the day. You bring your phone so you can stay in touch with friends. We bring many things into our lives to make them better.

You can bring a friend into your life. If you meet a new person at a party, you bring them into your group. You help them feel safe. You bring them a drink or a book to read. You bring them to a place where you can talk and laugh.

If you are afraid, you might bring a thing to help you feel better. If you are sad, you might bring a friend or a song to make the feeling go away. Bringing helps us move from one place to another and from one state to another.
