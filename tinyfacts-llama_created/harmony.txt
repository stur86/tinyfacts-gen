To have a good time with people you like is to feel happy and not want to stop. When many people are happy together, it makes everyone feel better.

When you hear nice music, you might feel your heart feel happy inside. You might want to dance or single. This kind of feeling is what a group is like when they all play together. It is a good way to share a moment with others.

You can also have a good time with animals. If a dog and a cat are friends, they might play together. They might chase each other around a room. This kind of play makes them happy. It is like they are happy to be together.

Sometimes, people who are very different might find a way to be happy together. A person who likes to eat food that is not their favorite might find a friend who likes the same food. They might laugh and talk about their food. They are happy because they are not fighting.

A group of people who all like the same things can feel happy. They might go to the same movies, watch the same games, or read the same books. They are all there because they like the same things. This kind of group is like a big happy family.
