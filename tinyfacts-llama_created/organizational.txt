An office is a place where many people work together. They do work for one person or one group. The people who work there might be in the same city or far away.

A group of people who work together is called a team. This team does the same job. They share a room, a desk, or a computer. They talk and plan when they need to. One person is the head of the team. They make sure everyone is doing their job.

The team also has a leader. This person helps the head of the team. They are the ones who make the plans. They also make sure everyone follows the plan. The leader is like a very good teacher. They help everyone know what is expected.

When many people work together, it is called a group. A group can be small or very big. It can have many different jobs. Some groups work together to build things. Some groups work together to make money. Some groups work together to help people.
