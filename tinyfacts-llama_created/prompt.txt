To ask someone to do something means to ask them to do it. This can be a very big word. People often use it to ask for help with their work or their lives.

When you want to ask a friend to do something, you might say,  Can you help me with my book? This means you need their help. You are saying,  I need your help.

If you want to ask a family person to do something, you might say,  Can you walk the dog? This means you need their help to walk the dog. They have to go out and let the dog run.

Sometimes, you need to ask to start a thing. If you want to start a movie, you ask your friend,  Let us start the movie. This means you are ready to begin.

You can also ask to be told. If you want to know a funny story, you ask your friend,  Tell me a joke. This means you want to hear a story.

In short, to ask someone to do something is to ask them to help you. It is a way of being nice to others and to show that you care about them.
