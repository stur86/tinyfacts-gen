Changing is when you change something. If something is one way, you change it into another way. This happens when you move from one place to another. You go from the cold to the warm. You go from the dark to the light.

A long time ago, people drank a drink from a tall thing. They would drink it one way and then drink it another way. This made the drink very good for them. They called it changing drink.

Today, we use this word for a drink. We call it changing drink. We put it in a tall thing. We shake it. The top falls down and the drink goes up. The drink is very cold at first. It is very hot at the end. This makes it feel strange on your face and hands.

We also use this word for a person. If you are changing, you are changing. You are moving to a new life. You are moving from one place to another. You are not the same person anymore.

We drink this drink to feel happy. It is a drink that makes us feel like we are doing something good. It is a drink that makes us feel like we are on top of the world.
