Windows are parts on a house or a building that let light into the room or out into the street. They are usually made of glass, and they have a clear part on top. When the sun is high, the glass lets the light in and makes the room bright. When the sun goes down, the glass lets the night air in and makes the room quiet.

Windows also let you see out. You can look at the street, the trees, or the sky. Sometimes you can even see animals or people walking in the street. You can use them to look for a good place to sleep at night.

Windows are important for people who live in big cities. They let in light, which helps you see in the dark. They also help keep the cold air out when it is very cold. This keeps your room warm and makes you feel happy.

Windows can also be made of many different kinds of glass. Some are very clear, so you can see a lot of the sky. Others are thicker and more dark, so you can see only a little of the sky. Some windows are even made of many different colors together.

Windows are usually held onto by strong lines around them. This keeps them in place and does not let them fall down. This is very important because windows can break if a strong wind hits them.

Windows can also be made of other stuff. They might be made of wood instead of glass. Or they might be made of a hard stuff that does not let light through. You might see a window that looks like a door but is really a clear glass window.

In houses, windows are also important for the inside room. They let in light and help keep the air inside clean. This keeps you and your family happy and safe inside the house.
