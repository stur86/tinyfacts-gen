A letter is a small piece of paper that you write on. It is made of one side covered with hard stuff and the other side covered with a soft white stuff. When you write on a paper, the hard side of the paper cuts through the paper and the soft side gets wet.

You write the words you want to write. You can write big words or small words. You can write a word, a name, or a name of a person. You can also write a short story with many words.

When you write a letter, you tell the person you are writing to. If you are writing to a person you know, you can tell them about what you did. If you are writing to someone you do not know, you might write something nice to make them feel happy.

Letters are used for many reasons. You can write to a friend who is far away. You can write to a family person. You can write to a teacher so they know what you did. You can even write to a person who wants to hear a story about a day.

Sometimes, you might send a letter that is not so easy to read. You might use words that are hard to understand. If you write something that is not right, your letter might not make sense. But even if it is hard to read, the person you are writing to will know it is coming from you.

In this world, we use letters every day. We send them through the air, or they are picked up by people who carry them. We can not see them, but we know they are there, and they help us talk to each other far away.
