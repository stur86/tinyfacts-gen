To do something in a big way

Imagine you are a very strong person. You want to see what is on the other side of a deep water. You look at the water and see a big rock. You want to go over the rock and see what is on the other side. You need to be very safe.

You are standing on the water. You look down at the water and see the rock. You want to go over the rock. To do this, you need to drop down into the water. You drop down so fast that your body feels like it is going very, very fast.

When you drop down, your body starts to go down very fast. You feel like you are falling. Your body and your brain are thinking about what to do. You need to keep your eyes on the ground so you do not fall into the water.

As you go over the rock, you feel a rush of air. The water is very fast. You are moving very, very fast. The rock is very small. It is like a tiny top. As you go over the top of the rock, you feel like you are almost there.

You reach the other side. You are safe. You have done something very strong. You have gone over a huge rock. You have seen what is on the other side of the water.
