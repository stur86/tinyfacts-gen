The word gone comes from old times when people and animals went away. A long time ago, a person or a thing would move very fast. As they went, they would leave behind their old world. When they were gone, there was nothing left. This word is still used today to mean something is gone. It can mean a person has gone to another place. It can also mean a thing that is gone. You can say, if you go to the store, the thing will be gone. The thing is not there anymore.
