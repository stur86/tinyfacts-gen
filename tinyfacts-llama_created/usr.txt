To use something is to make it work for you. You use the word to talk to a computer or a phone. When you type, the computer reads what you type and does what you want. The computer thinks in a way that is different from a person.

The word also means the person who makes things. If you buy a computer, the person who made it is the user. You are the one who uses the computer to do your work. You ask the computer to show you pictures or to write a word. This is using the computer.

You can also use this word to talk to a person who works in a big office. They are the user of the computer. They decide what the computer should do and how it should work. They are the person who makes sure the computer is working well.

To use something does not mean you have to be a good person. It just means you are using what is there. You can use a thing to get food, a thing to talk to someone, or a thing to play games.
