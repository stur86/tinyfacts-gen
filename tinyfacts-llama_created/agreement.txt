An idea can be broken down into many parts. One part is what you think. Another part is what others think. A third part is what they decide together. This third part is what everyone agrees on.

When many people agree on the same thing, it is an understanding. This is what happens when you agree on a thing. You all see the same picture. You all say the same word. You all want the same thing.

An idea can also be broken down into many small parts. One small part is a little idea. Another small part is a little group of people. A third small part is a small group of people who agree with the first two. The small parts all add up to the whole idea.

In this way, an idea can grow. It starts as one small part. It is picked up by many people. It is shared with more and more people. Before you know it, everyone agrees. This is how a new idea can start.
