To be tight means something is very strong and holds well. Imagine you have a piece of clothes. You make it very small so it feels like it has a very hard cover. It looks very small and does not fall down. This is because the clothes is very hard and the edges are very strong.

When you say something is tight, you mean it is not easy to move. You can not easily pull it. If you try to pull it, it does not go. It stays in one place. This happens with many things: a door, a shoe, or even a person. When you are very tight, it feels good. It is like a big hug.

You can also say a word is tight when people are very quiet. If you say something, but you do not want to say it, you might feel a bit short. Your voice becomes quiet and you look at the person you are talking to. You are trying not to show how you really feel. This is a kind way to act.

Sometimes, being tight can mean you are afraid. If you feel very afraid, your heart might be beating very fast. You might feel like you can not breathe. This is because your body is ready to run away. It is a sign you are worried about something.

In some words, being tight can also mean you are keeping something inside. If you feel a bit of a problem, you might try to keep it under your skin. You might not want to tell anyone because you do not want them to know. This is a way to hide what you are feeling.
