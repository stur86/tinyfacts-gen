To give an order means you tell someone to do something. When a teacher or a parents tell you to do something, it is an order. You must follow this order.

When you are a child, you hear your mother say, Please eat your food. This is an order. You must eat the food. If you do not follow this order, you will get a time out or you will get in trouble.

When you drive a car, you follow a set of orders. If you are on the right road and you see a car coming, you must stop. You must also look left and right to see if there are other cars around. This is an order to stay safe.

You can also give an order to someone. If you want to fix a computer, you can tell the person, Fix the computer. They will try to fix it. They might need to turn it off, open it, and try to fix the problem.

An order can also be a way to make something happen. If you want to go to the park, you can order a friend to take you there. They will go to get you.

Orders are important because they help us stay safe and follow the right way.
