When someone tries something, they do it anyway. They might not be sure if it will work, but they want to see if it is true. This is a way to face your fears.

Think of a child who wants to fly a small thing. They try it many times. They might feel afraid at first, but they keep trying until they feel the wind under their clothes and the ground far under them. They are trying because they want to see if they can do it.

You can try things in your own life too. Maybe you want to eat a new food, so you try it. You might like it or you might not like it. If you try something and it does not work, you might feel sad. But even if you do not like it, you tried it. You tried something new.

Trying is also about learning. When you try to do something, you learn. You see how you do it and you see what you need to change. This helps you grow.
