A promise is when you say you will do something. You say you will do it later. You are not sure if you can do it now. But you are sure that you will try.

When you make a promise, you are telling the world that you are a person of your word. You are not afraid to say that you are not sure, because you know that if you break your word, the other person will be very angry. This is why people keep their promises. They want to be safe.

You can promise to eat a food or to go to a place. You can promise to be a good friend or to help someone. You can even promise to be quiet when you are not supposed to be. If you do not follow through, the person will be very sad.

Sometimes, you promise to do something that is hard. Maybe you promise to finish a big job before you go home. You want to feel strong and sure about what you are doing. You want to know that you can do it.

You can also promise to be kind to someone. If you promise to help a friend, you are showing that you care. You are showing that you want to help them and that you are not afraid to ask for help.

A promise is like a small act of trust. It helps us feel safe and sure about what is happening. When you tell someone you will do something, you are building trust. If you follow through, the other person is more happy and less afraid.
