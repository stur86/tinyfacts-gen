To start something is to begin. This can happen in many ways. One way is when you start a new school year. You do not know what the day will bring. You see your friends, you learn from the teacher, and you make new friends. This is a new start. You start from a place you are not sure about.

Another way is when a person decides to do something. They may want to walk across the world, ride a big animal, or build a house in a city. They start by looking at their ideas and planning how they will do it. They might talk to many people, ask for money, and find a way to make it happen.

Sometimes, a person starts a business. They have an idea for a thing they want to make. They use their money to build a place, buy things, and find people to help. They work hard to get everything ready for the day they open their store. This is a start to a life of work and money.

A third way is when you start to learn a thing. You might watch a movie, read a book, or listen to a story. You start to understand what is happening. You begin to see how things work. This is a start to a new way of thinking.

To start something is to begin a new thing. It is the first step. After you start, you build and do more. You use what you have learned and use it to make more. This is how you grow and change.
