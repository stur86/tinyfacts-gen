Your is a word that means something is your own. It tells us that a thing is not owned by anyone else. When you say your book, you are saying that it is your book and you are the person who is holding it.

You can also use your word to talk about how you feel. If you are very happy, you might say, I am happy. When you say this, you are telling the world that you feel that way.

Sometimes, we use your word to talk about what someone else is doing. If you see a person walking, you might say, That person is walking. This tells the world that the person is moving on their own feet.

We also use your word when we talk about things that you have made. If you build a thing with your hands, you have made it your own. You are the one who put your mind into it and made it real.

The word is important because it tells us how we feel and what we are doing. It helps us say who is who and what is happening in a group of people.
