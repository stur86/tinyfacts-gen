To watch something means you are looking at it. You see it with your eyes. You do not touch it. You do not move around it. You stand still and look at it.

Imagine you are at a game with your friends. There is a man who is a good player. He is running around the field. You see him jump over a high thing. You see him throw the ball to a friend. You see him smile. You are watching him.

You might watch a movie. You sit in a room with many people. You see a story. The story is about a boy and a girl who are friends. They do things together. They laugh and cry. You feel the story inside you.

You might also watch a person. You see them work. You see them talk to people. You see them make things. You learn about them. You might wonder what they do at night or how they feel.

Sometimes, you watch animals. You see them move. You see them eat. You see them play. You learn about their lives. You might feel happy or sad.

To watch something can also mean you are waiting for something. You might be waiting for a friend to come home. You might be waiting for a phone to ring. You are looking at the time.

Watching is a quiet way to spend your time. You do not have to move around or do anything. You just sit and look. It is a way to relax and enjoy things.
