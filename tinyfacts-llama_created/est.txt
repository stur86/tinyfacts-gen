To use a word is to use it. You show it to your mind. You tell it what it means. This word is a part of our talk. We use it every day.

Think of it like food. You eat a food. You tell your body what to do. You use the word. The word tells you what you need. You eat food, your body does what you say. You use words to tell what you need.

We also use this word in other ways. We say a word is the end. This is when we finish talking. We use it to end a story, a song, or a game. The end of the story is the word.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about how things change. If you say a thing is the end, you mean it is gone. If you say a thing is the start, it is new. You are telling how things work.

We also use this word in different ways. To say a thing is the best is to say it is the best way. To say a thing is the worst is to say it is the way that hurts most. This word is about how we feel about things.

You can also use it to talk about how things grow. A tree grows. The tree starts small. It gets taller. It becomes stronger. This is the word. The tree is growing because it is getting bigger and stronger.

When you use the word, you are showing it to your mind. You are telling your mind what it means. You are taking it in and making it part of your life.
