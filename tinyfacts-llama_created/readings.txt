To read something is to use your eyes to see words or lines on a piece of paper. You look at the words and try to understand what they mean. You do this with a book, a computer, or a phone.

When you read a book, you might start at the beginning and go all the way to the end. You might read one story, then another, and another, until you finish the book. This helps you know the story, learn about the people, and feel how the story makes you feel.

You can also read things that do not tell a story. You might read a book about how the world works, or a book about how animals live. Reading these books can help you learn new things and understand how things work.

Reading is important for many reasons. It helps us learn about the world and our place in it. It lets us know about other places and how they live. It also lets us understand how people feel and what they want. When you read, you are using your brain to understand and remember what you have seen.

Sometimes, we read things that are not real. This can be a story that someone made up, or a picture of a place or a thing. We might read about a game or a movie. Reading these things can help us imagine how it feels to be in that place or to play that game.

In school, you often do this kind of work. You read a story, or you read about a thing that happened. You try to remember what the story is about and what it means. This helps you learn and understand the words and the story.
