To hang over something means to stand or sit above a part of a building, a road, or a ground.

Imagine you are a person. You stand in the middle of a street. Behind you, there is a big wall. You can look at the wall and see all the people on the other side. You are hanging over the wall. You are above it.

If you are in a car, you are hanging over the road. You are not on the ground. Your body is in the air. You can look out the window and see the ground far away.

Sometimes, you hang over the ground while you are walking. You are holding onto a line or a wall. You are not on the ground anymore. You are hanging. You are not touching the ground, but you are still standing on the ground.

There is also a word that means to hang on something. To hang on a thing is to hold onto it. You can hang a picture on a wall. You can hang a bag on a line. You can hang a thing you love from a tree.

So, to hang over something is to stand above it. It means you are looking at it from a different place. It can mean you are above a wall, above a road, above the ground, or above a thing.
