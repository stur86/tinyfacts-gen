You can also say a thing is in the water. This means it is wet. You might say a glass is in the water. It is now wet and heavy because of the water.

Sometimes, a thing is very heavy. You might not be able to move it. You might have to use a strong person to lift it. This is why you might say the thing is heavy.

A thing under the floor can also be called a floor thing. It is a thing that is not a wall. It is a long piece of wood or a hard stuff under the hard ground. It can be very big. It can cover the ground and make it feel like a big room.
