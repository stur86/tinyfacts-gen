To put something after another word is to move it. You can put a word after a word that already happened. You can also put a word after a word that is still coming.

Think about a story. If you tell someone something, then you put it after you. You did not put it in the middle. You put it after the things you already said.

You can also put a word after a word that is coming. This happens when you tell a story. If you tell a story, the word after you is the next thing that happens. You are building on what you already said.

The word is also used for things that are sent to people. When you send a letter, you put the letter after you. The letter is the thing you are sending. You are putting the letter after the person you are writing to.

The word is also used for pictures. You put a word after a picture. The word tells what the picture means. It is the story behind the picture.
