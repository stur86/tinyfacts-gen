To give something away means to let go of it so another person can use it. Imagine you have a box full of food that you want to share with your family. You decide to open the box and let the food go out into the world. This is a kind act because it lets others enjoy the food you gave.

Sometimes, you give something because you need it. You might give your car keys to a friend so they can drive it while you are busy with work. Or, you might give your money to a school so they can build a new building for the kids.

You can also give something when you are afraid. Maybe you feel like you are going to cry. You might let a friend see you so they can help you through your hard time.

To give means you let go of something. It is not about hiding the thing. You must be ready to give it away at any time. When you give, you are showing that you trust other people and that you are not afraid.

Think about the time you had to say a big word in front of your class. You felt nervous. Then, you took a deep breath and said the word. You gave the word away so everyone else could hear it. This is an act of trust that helps you feel more safe.

Giving is a way to show love and care to others. It is a way to make life better for everyone.
