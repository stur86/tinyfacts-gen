When you have something and then you change it, you have something new. This happens when you take a thing that is old and make it look different. You might cover something red with a white cover or put a blue cover on something that is green.

You can do this with people too. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You love to run and jump. But then you get a new chair that is very tall. You do not like the chair. You want to make the chair look like the old one. You pull out the old chair and put the new one in its place. Then you feel happy because the chair looks like the old one again.

People often do this with money. Imagine you have some money that you spend on food and clothes. One day you get a new job and you start to have more money. You can buy more food, clothes, and other things you want. You have something new. This happens with money, people, and other things you buy.
