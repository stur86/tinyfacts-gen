An act or a thing that looks to see how something works. You might see this done at a school. They go around the room and ask the students if they understand. They ask if the door is closed and if the light is on. They do this for a few minutes and then they are done.

This is also how people check their cars. They go around the car and look at the parts. They check if the lights are working and if the air is not full. If the car looks good after they are done, they feel they have done a good job.
