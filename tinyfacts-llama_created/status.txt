To be in a high place means you are important. You are not afraid to tell others what you think. You are the top person in a group. You have more power than the other people.

A person who is high in a group is called a leader. They tell everyone what to do. They make the big plans. They decide who gets to be the top person or who gets to be the new top person.

You can also be in a high place if you do very well. If you do a job very well, you get a good job. You might be a teacher, a doctor, or a police person. You are important because you help people.

Sometimes, a person is not in a high place. They might be afraid. They do not want to tell others what they think. They might hide. This is not good. When someone is in a high place, they help others. They make things better. They make the group work as one.

In a house, a high place is the top room. The top room is usually the room with a big window. It is the room where people go to be alone. This room is not for everyone. It is for those who are important.

In a city, a high place is a place with a lot of people. These places are usually the big cities with many tall houses. They are important because they have many jobs, many schools, and many shops.

Being in a high place does not mean you are the best. You can still have problems. You can still make a lot of people angry. But you are important for a long time because you help others. You are important because you are strong enough to tell others what to do.
