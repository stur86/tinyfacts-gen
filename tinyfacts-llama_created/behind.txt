To stand behind something means to be in a place behind it. Imagine you are in a room. If you have a chair, you can sit in it. If you have a table, you can sit in front of it. If you sit behind the table, you are closer to the room.

Think of a line. If you stand behind a line, you are not on the line. You are on the other side. If you stand in front of the line, you are on the line. If you stand behind the line, you are not following the line. You are moving away from it.

Sometimes, when you talk about people, you say they stand behind you. This means they are following you. They are trying to get your attention. They are trying to talk to you.

Also, you can stand behind a thing. If you stand behind a wall, you are not touching it. You are just behind it. You are watching it from the other side.
