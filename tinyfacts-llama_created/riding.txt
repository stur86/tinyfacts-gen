To ride means you use a thing that moves around on the ground. People usually ride things that go very fast on the road or in the sky.

Imagine you are on a horse that is very strong and fast. You sit on the horse and hold a long thing that helps you stay in the seat. The horse starts moving, and you feel the wind in your hair. You can look around and see what is happening. You can also use the long thing to help the horse turn.

Some people ride animals that can fly. These animals are very strong and can go very high into the air. You can sit on the back of the animal and hold onto the long thing. The animal moves through the air, and you feel the wind around you. It is a great feeling to be in the air with a strong animal.

You can also ride things that go over the water. These are big cars that can carry many people. You sit inside the car, and the car goes very fast over the water. You might feel like you are in a big, round thing that is moving over the top of the water. Some people like to ride these cars because they feel like they are flying.

In a house, you might ride a thing that goes round on the floor. This is a small car that can carry one person. You sit in the car, and it makes you go round. You can feel like you are moving and see the room moving around you.

Riding can be fun. You can feel free and happy when you are on a fast horse or a flying car. You can also learn how to ride a thing that goes over water or a thing that goes round on the floor.
