A word is something you use to tell others what you mean. When you talk, you use your voice. You use your lips, your tongue, and your throat to make sounds. These sounds are like a story, a picture, or a feeling.

A word is made of letters. Each letter is a small piece of the word. When you put many of these pieces together, you get a word. Each word has its own meaning. If you say a word many times, people start to understand what it means.

Some words are short, like a single word. They tell you something quickly. Other words are long. They take many words and put them together to tell a story. These words can be very important because they help us understand each other.

You can also use a word to show how you feel. If you are happy, you might use words that mean you are good. If you are sad, you might use words that mean you are not well. This helps people know how you are feeling.

Sometimes, a group of words is called a word. This is when many people use the same word to talk about the same thing. This helps everyone understand what is going on.

People also use words to talk about things they see. If you see a house, you can say, This is a house. This word helps us understand the world around us.
