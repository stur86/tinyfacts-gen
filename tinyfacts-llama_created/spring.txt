The word for this time of year is often the same as the word for when you start to feel better after being sick. It happens when the ground starts to warm up and the days get longer.

The ground feels the warm air in a different way than the cold ground does. The ground does not feel the cold air at the same time. When the warm air comes up, the ground can feel it. This makes the ground want to be warm too.

As the ground gets warmer, the old green stuff that was on top starts to fall off. This green stuff goes down into the ground and makes the ground feel wet. It is like the ground is being covered in a soft, green stuff. When the green stuff falls off, the ground can feel cleaner.

This is the time before new green stuff starts growing. The ground is still warm and the air is warm. It is a time when the ground and the air are ready to make the green stuff grow again.
