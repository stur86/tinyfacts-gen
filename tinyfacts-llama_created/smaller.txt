To be smaller means you are not as big as you were before. You are smaller now.

Imagine you are a tall tree. You can see for many houses. One day, a small animal moves into the house next to you. The animal is very small. It is almost as small as a tiny thing that you hold in your hand. Now the tree is not as tall anymore. It is smaller.

You can also be smaller in other ways. You might be smaller when you eat less food. You feel less full. You have less power than you had before. Or you might be smaller when you sleep. Your body is smaller because you are resting.

Sometimes we say someone is smaller than another person. This means one person is not as strong or as tall as the other. It also means one person is not as old as the other. If you are older than a friend, you are smaller.
