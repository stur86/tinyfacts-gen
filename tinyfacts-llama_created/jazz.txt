Joke

A joke is a thing you say to make someone laugh. It is a way to play with words and make them funny. People often use jokes to make others feel better when they are sad or scared.

Sometimes, a joke has many parts. One part tells a story, another part makes a funny sound, and a third part says something that is not true. This way, the words and sounds fit together and the person feels the laugh inside.

You can hear jokes when people say them in a room. They might say a little sound with their lips, a funny word, or a story that is not real. Sometimes, a group of people laugh together because the joke is funny.

But jokes can also be very hard to understand. They are like a story with a few words at the end. If you do not know what is happening, you might not understand the joke at first. It is like trying to read a story that is not written for you.

Sometimes, people say they are laughing at a joke, but they are not really laughing. They might just smile because they are trying to be nice. This is because people often say this when they are not sure if the joke is really funny.

Jokes can also be different in different places. One place might have jokes about animals, another place might have jokes about food, and another place might have jokes about things that make you feel sad. This is because jokes are like a way to share and understand what is happening in a group of people.

In a way, jokes are like a game. People make them and try to get others to laugh. This game helps us stay happy and make new friends.
