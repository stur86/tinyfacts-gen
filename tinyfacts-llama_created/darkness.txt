Dark is a word we use to talk about how light is not there. Think of the sun. When the sun is up, it gives light to everything. But when the sun goes down, everything gets very dark.

Everything that is not light is dark. A room that is very dark is not a room at all. It is nothing. The ground is also dark. The ground is not light, so it is dark.

We can not see in the dark. If you walk into a room and you can not see anything, you are in the dark. You are not safe. There are things that can fall on you and hurt you in the dark. You need to be carefully.

Dark is not just things that are black. A dark room can be a room with no light at all. A room with no light is dark. A room with no light at all can not be light. It is not a room. It is nothing.

Dark can also mean something that is not light in the mind. If you are scared, you are in the dark. You are afraid, and your mind is not sure what to do. Your heart is in the dark.

Sometimes, we say a room is dark because it is cold. A room that is very cold is dark. The cold air makes everything look dark.
