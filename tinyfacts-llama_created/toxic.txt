To be too much can cause a lot of trouble. If you are very bright or very happy all the time, you might feel like you are walking on air. You might talk very fast and laugh a lot. You might even feel like you want to jump up and down all the time.

But if you are too much, you can also cause other people trouble. You might make them sick. Your words might hurt their heart. Your loud voice might scare them. Or you might make them feel afraid to talk around you.

This happens when something is not good for you. It is like a ball that is very heavy. If you carry it around for too long, you might get tired. Your body and mind might feel sick. You might want to stop.

But you do not have to be too much all the time. You can be happy and strong when you need to be. You can slow down and listen. You can share your ideas and make friends. You can take a deep breath and calm down.

If you are very sad or very angry, it can be good to let it out. You can cry or shout. This helps you feel better when you are hurting. It is a way to tell yourself that you are not alone.

So, being too much is not always bad. It can be a sign that you need to slow down and be kind to yourself.
