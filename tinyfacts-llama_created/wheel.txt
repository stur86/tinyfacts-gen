A big round thing that turns around is called a car or a thing that moves. It has many round parts that are stuck together. When you push it, the parts start turning round and round, and it goes very fast.

Imagine a thing with a round part that is like a hard ball. When you push this ball, it turns round very fast. The round ball is what makes the thing move. The round parts are inside the ball and turn around it.

There is also a round part on top of a round ball. This part is very heavy. When the round ball turns, the heavy part turns around with it. This makes the round part go round too. This is why the round parts turn together.

Things that move around in the water have this thing too. It keeps them turning and going through the water. Sometimes these things move along the ground too. They are very important for our lives because they help us get from one place to another.

A person can also hold this thing and use it to go places. They put their feet on it and turn it. The round parts turn the ground, and the car moves. It is a very good way to go quickly and easily through many places.
