To put something away is to make a space in your home for it. You might move a small thing to a corner or under a table. When you do this, you are telling your brain that the thing is gone.

Sometimes, you put things that you do not want to let go into a box. You close the top and put the box on the floor. You feel a bit sad because you are moving something from your life to something else. But you also feel a bit safer because you are not looking at it anymore.

You can also put a word into a book. If you write something that you like, you can put it into a book with other people who like the same things. This makes it easier for them to read and enjoy.

When you want to say yes, you often have to put your hand down. This means you are letting the person know that you are ready to do what they ask. You are giving up your power for a little while so that you can follow what they say.
