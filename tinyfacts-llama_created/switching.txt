To change something means you move it from one place to another. You might move your books from your desk to your bed. You can also move your friends to a new group in your school.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are the one who does the things. If you want to play a game with someone else, you might pick them. You have to tell them how to play so they know what to do.

Sometimes, you might want to change your day. If you are tired, you might want to sleep. You can also change the way you act. If you are feeling angry, you might stop being angry. You can also change what you say. If you want to be kind, you might use nicer words.

To change also means you change yourself. You might learn a new thing. You might try to do something you have never done before. If you are afraid, you might face the thing that makes you afraid. You are becoming stronger because you are trying.

You can also change how you see things. If you think something is bad, you might decide it is good. You might see the world in a new way. This is how you change your mind.

To change something in your head is when you think about a problem. You might think, I want to do something. You might think about what you can do. You might think, I need to be strong.

To change the way you act is when you change how you act in real life. If you are a kind person, you might act kind even when you are alone. You might help a friend or give a small thing. This changes how people see you.
