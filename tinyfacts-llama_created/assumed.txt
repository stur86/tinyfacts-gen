To be or to do something is a way you show you really think or feel. It is how you act when you know what is true. You can be an idea in your mind or a real person who does what you say.

If you say you are a great person, you are supposed to do things that make you feel this way. You are not just talking, you are doing it. You are not just saying you are something, you are actually becoming that. Your words help people know if you are true or not.

You can also say you are an idea. An idea is a thing you think about a lot. It is a plan you have for the world. You are not just thinking, you are actually planning, making plans, and trying to get your ideas to happen.

Sometimes, you might be the person who does something. You are the one who takes the act and turns it into something real. You are the one who does what you said you would do.

We all have our own ways of being. We all act in our own way. To be someone is to act like that person for a long time.
