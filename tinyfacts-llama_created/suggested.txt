To suggest is to put forward a thought without being asked. When you suggest something, you are telling the other person that you think it is a good idea. It does not ask the other person to agree.

Think about a time when you wanted to eat a food. You might think, You should eat this food. If you say this out loud, the other person might ask, Why? You need to show them that you have a good idea. You are not asking them to do it. You are just telling them that you think it is a good thing to eat.

This can also happen with people. If someone thinks a new idea is a great way to do things, they might suggest it to the group. The group listens and talks about it. They might even decide to try it.

Sometimes, you can suggest something you do not really want to do. You might say, I should go to bed. But you are not really sure you want to go. You are just telling the other person that you are tired. This is not the same as saying, I am going to bed. You are not making a promise. You are just telling them what you think.

To suggest something is to put forward a thought. It is not a way to get things done. It is a way to be heard.
