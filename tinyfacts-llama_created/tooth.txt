A thing you eat is what you eat. You drink water or food that is not hard. This food goes down into your body and does not hurt you. But the food that is in your teeth does hurt when you bite it. The hard parts in your teeth help keep the food from going down your throat. You need to brush these hard parts many times a day so they stay clean and do not break.

Inside your mouth, there are many tiny moving parts. One part moves food from your mouth to your stomach. Another part cuts the food into small pieces so you can eat it with your mouth. Your teeth are made of hard white stuff. Over time, these hard white things wear down and you need to get new ones.

To get a new set of teeth, you go to a place where people do this. The person in this place takes a small piece of the old teeth and makes a new one from the white stuff. They put the new hard white thing in your mouth and let it hard. After a few days, the hard white thing is strong enough to eat food like a person. You can then eat, drink, and play like you did before.
