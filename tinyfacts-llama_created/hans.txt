A hand is the part of a person that you use to do many things. It is the part of your body that you use when you write, when you play with your fingers, and when you hold things.

Your hands are made of strong parts inside your body. There are five of them. Two are on the sides of your body. One is in the middle of your body. One is on the top of your body. One is on the bottom of your body.

Your hands can be very different. One hand is very strong. One hand is very small. Some hands have many fingers. Some hands have few fingers. Some hands are very long. Some hands are very short.

You use your hands to pick up things. You use your hands to hold things. You use your hands to write and draw pictures. You use your hands to make music. You use your hands to play games.

When you are happy, you might smile and shake your hands. When you are angry, you might classed your hands to show you are afraid. When you are sad, you might cry and hold your hands.

Your hands help you to do many things. They help you to write your name. They help you to draw pictures. They help you to play games. They help you to make music. They help you to do many other things that you do every day.
