A word you might use in a song or a movie.

A long time ago, people used this word to talk about a feeling. When you are happy or you feel very good, you might say,  I am happy. If you feel sad, you say, I am sad.

To put a thing on your body is also to use this word. You put a light on your face so you can see at night. You might wear clothes that are light. You put a thing on your head so you can see without looking at it.

Sometimes, to make something fun, you use this word. If you are playing a game with a friend, you might say, Let us play. This means you want to do it together.

To use this word can mean two different things. You can say, I am a happy person. Or you can say, I am a person who is sad. It is like a word that tells you how you feel.

In a story, people might use this word when they are talking about their lives. A person might say, My life is very hard. Or they might say, I am very happy because I have a good job.
