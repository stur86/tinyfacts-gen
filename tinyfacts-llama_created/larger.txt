A larger thing is a bigger one. A child might be smaller than a dog, but a car is larger than a dog. A house is also larger than a small room.

Think about water. If you fill a small cup with water, you have a small drink. If you fill a big cup, you have a bigger drink. Everything in the world is made up of tiny pieces. The bigger pieces are larger than the tiny pieces. So, the things that are bigger are always larger than the things that are smaller.
