To do something because you have to

This is when you do something without thinking it is wrong. You might be told to do a thing, and you do it anyway. This is because the person who told you did it, or because you feel like it is the right thing to do.

Imagine you have a friend who is always telling you things you should do. One day, you do not follow their words. You do what you want, and then you feel bad. This is because you did not do what you were told.

You can also do this because you are afraid. If you are afraid of a thing, you might do it anyway. You might not think about the bad things that could happen.

Sometimes, you do this because you want to be alone. If you are feeling sad, you might do something that makes you happy. You might watch a movie or play games. This is because you want to feel better.

This word is important because it helps us understand why we do things. If you did something because you had to, you are following the order.
