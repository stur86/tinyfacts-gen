To happen or come into being is to get started. It means you start doing something and you are not finished yet.

When you say something happens, you tell the story of what happened. It is like making a story with words. If you tell a story about a person who went to school, the story happened because the person went to school.

Sometimes, something happens to you. You might hear a sound that you have never heard before. You might see a bright light in the sky. These are things that happen to you and to many other people at the same time.

You can also say something happens if it happens to a place. If a big rain falls on a city, the city is wet. This happens because of the rain from the sky. It makes the ground and the water close together.

Sometimes, you might say that something is happening to you. If you are feeling sad, you might say you are having a bad day. This means you are getting a little worse. If you are feeling happy, you might say you are having a great day.

We also say that something happens because of a reason. If you hit your friend, it is because you were angry. If you do not do something, you might get in trouble.
