To talk is to use words. When you talk, you use your voice. You say things with your mouth. Your words are the things you say. You can talk to your friends, your family, or a person you know. You can also talk to a group of people. When many people talk at the same time, it is called a conversation.

You talk when you want to tell someone something. You might talk to a person who is sad. You tell them how you feel so they can know. Or you talk to a person who needs help. You help them feel better.

You talk when you want to share your ideas. You might talk about a thing you saw. You talk about the sun, the sky, or a game you played. You share your own story with the other person.

Talking is a way to show you care. When you talk, you show the other person you are there for them. You listen to them too. This helps make the world feel bigger and more safe.

Sometimes you talk while you walk. You might talk to yourself. You say words to your body. You might say, I am here. I am strong. You are doing good. When you talk while walking, you are showing your brain that you are okay.

Talking is one of the most important things in life. It helps us make friends, share our stories, and feel less alone.
