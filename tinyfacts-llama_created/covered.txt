To cover something means to put a thing on top of another thing. When you cover something, you are keeping it safe inside a bag of paper, a box, or a box on the floor.

Think of a car. It has a long hard thing on the back that is covered in soft stuff. This soft stuff keeps the car from hitting the ground when it drives on the road. The car is covered so it does not get hurt by the hard lines of the road.

You can also cover a room. A person might cover the floor with a big piece of paper. When you cover a room, you are making it look nice. You can put pictures on the paper so it looks pretty.

Sometimes, you cover a thing to hide it. If you have a thing that you do not want anyone to see, you might cover it. You cover it with a piece of paper or a bag so it looks like it is not there.

There are also things that cover the sky during the day. When the sun is very high, you can cover it with your hands or a piece of paper to keep it from hurting your eyes.
