When you pick a thing that is wet and it gets hot, it starts to change. The water inside it turns into a different kind of stuff. This happens because the hot stuff takes the water away and the hot stuff makes the water go away too.

Imagine you have a wet piece of paper. You put it in the hot sun for a while. The water inside the paper turns into a clear stuff. The paper is now dry. It feels hard and does not have any water in it anymore.

When this happens with a person, their skin gets dry. The air from the air we breathe is full of tiny drops of water. When you walk or run, the water on your skin gets taken away. After a while, your skin feels very hot and dry. You might feel like you need to drink something to put the water back in your body.

Things can also get dry if they are very hot for too long. A dry room is one where the air is very quiet and the walls and floor feel hard. A dry place for food is one where there is little or no water left. When food gets dry, it becomes hard to eat. It might break into pieces or fall apartments.

You can also dry things in the sun. When the sun is very hot, it can dry out the wet stuff on your skin or clothes. You might feel the sun on your face and neck. You can sit in the sun for a while to dry out your skin.

Sometimes, things get very dry. This happens when water goes away from a place very quickly. Think of a dry place at the bottom of a deep water. The water does not come out anymore, and the ground is hard and dry. This is not good for many things. Animals might not find food or water. People might get very tired and sick.
