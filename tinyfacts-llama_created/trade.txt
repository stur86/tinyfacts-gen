A deal where two people or a group of people buy or give things they want. They agree to give the things they want in return for the things they need. This makes everyone happy.

Imagine you have a bag of sweet food. You like it. A friend has a bag of a different sweet food. You want to buy some. You go to their home. You say, I want some of that sweet food. You give them your money. They give you the sweet food. You are both happy.

There are many types of deals. You can buy a car from a man. He gives you money. You give him your car. You both feel happy.

Some deals are very big. A big group of people buy a huge piece of land. They are buying it for a very low money. The people who own the land are happy because they get a good deal.

There are also small deals. You buy a book from a store. The store owner gives you the book. You are happy because you got the book you wanted.

A deal can be about money, things you like, or people you know. It is a way to get what you want from another person.
