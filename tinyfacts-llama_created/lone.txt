To be alone means you are not with other people. You might be sitting in a room by yourself, or you might be walking down a street without anyone around.

Sometimes we feel alone. This happens when we do not have any friends. We might feel sad because we do not have anyone to talk to. We might feel a bit scared if we are the only one in a big room. 

When we are alone, we can think about our own lives. We might think about our jobs, our homes, or our families. We might also think about the things we want to do tomorrow. This can be a good feeling because it gives us time to think.

But sometimes being alone can feel hard. You might feel like you are missing someone. You might want to talk to them, but they are not there. This can make you feel very sad.

There are also times when we are alone in a way that is not bad. We might be alone in a quiet room while we listen to music. This can help us relax and feel calm. We might also use this time to read a book or do a thing we like.
