To share something means to give it to many people so they can use it. Imagine you have a box of food. You are the one who keeps it safe. If you give it to a friend, they now have it and they can eat it. You are not the one who eats it anymore.

When you say something is shared, you are saying it is given to a group of people. This can happen in many ways. If a person is very good at a job, a group of people might want to work with them. They share their work so the person can do more. They share ideas so everyone can learn.

You can also say that something is being shared if it is moving from one place to another. If a book you love is sent to a friend, the friend is now holding it. This is a way of showing you care.

Sometimes, when people share something, they feel happy. It is like they are giving something that was their own to another person. This makes everyone feel good because they can see that the person is kind.
