A business is when people work together to make money. They start a new thing or a place where people go to buy things. The people in the business buy and make more money. This money they have is what they use to buy things for themselves, buy more of the same, or give to others.

Some people start a business by doing what they love. They build a house where they live. They work hard to make sure the door is open and the lights are bright. They take money from their own money and from other people to buy things they need.

Some people start a business by making money from other people. They work for someone else who gives them money and a place to work. They do not build anything from nothing. They just work for someone else.

Both types of businesses need people. You need to work to get money. The person who runs the business needs to make sure everything is going well. They pay the people who work there, pay for the things they need, and make sure the money gets to the people who want it.

Sometimes, a business is very big. It has many people working for it. It makes lots of money. Other times, it is small. It only makes a little money. But all of them are important because they all make money for each other.

A business can also be about how people act. If people are very kind and nice, they can make a business feel happy. If people are very mean and try to take all the money, the business can feel sad.
