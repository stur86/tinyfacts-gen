When people say they have a few things they like, they usually say those are their favorites. But what this really means is they have a few things they do not like as well. If you like eating ice, but you do not like eating green things, you have a small group of things you do not like. This is because you like one more than the other.

Also, people have lots of different ways they act around other people. If you like to play games, but you do not like to go to the movies, you have a few things you like. You do not like going to the movies as well as you like playing games. In a group of people, you might be the one who likes the same things as many others.
