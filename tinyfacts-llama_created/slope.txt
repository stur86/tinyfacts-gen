A line that goes up or down is called a line. This line can be very short, or it can go for a very long time across the ground.

Imagine you are walking on a road that has a small turn. As you turn, the ground under you starts to go up. This is the line. You are following the line as you walk. You do not need to look at the ground all the time. You can just look at the line to know where you are going.

There are many lines in the world. Some lines go up into the sky. Some lines go down to the water. Some lines go across the ground. Each line has its own way of going up or down.

When you are moving from one place to another, you are following a line. The line goes with you, and it helps you know where you are. You can use a line to find a way to a place you want to go. You can also use a line to see how far you are from your home.
