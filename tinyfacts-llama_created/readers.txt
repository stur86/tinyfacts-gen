A reader is someone who reads books or other things that have words. When you read a book, you use your eyes to look at the words. Your brain then tries to understand what the words mean.

You can be a reader in many ways. Maybe you read a book to learn about a person or a place. Maybe you read a book to feel happy or sad. You might even read a book that is funny to laugh at.

Some people read books every day. They might read a short story or a book with many words. Other people might only read one book at a time. They might read a book before going to sleep or during a quiet time.

A reader needs a brain. They need eyes that can see the words. They need a heart that can understand what the words mean. They also need a mind that can follow the story or learn from it.

You can also be a reader of other things. You might read what a friend writes. You might read what a teacher writes. You might even read what a computer does when it is working.

In a group, when people read, they can talk to each other. They might ask questions or make comments. They might laugh or be quiet. This is how we learn about the world and about each other.

To be a reader is to use your mind and your eyes to understand words. It is to be able to think and feel and learn in your own way.
