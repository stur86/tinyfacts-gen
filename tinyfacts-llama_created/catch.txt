To catch something means to hold it so it doesn't go away. You might try to catch a ball when you are playing with your friends. You stand still and wait for the ball to come near you. When it does, you reach out and grab it. You keep it safe until you decide to throw it again or you are done playing.

You can also catch things that are not a ball. If you are walking down a street and you see a small animal running away, you can try to catch it. You might use your arms and legs to slow it down. You will hold on to it until it is safe and you can let it go.

Sometimes, you catch things that are not in your hands. If you see a thing falling from a high place, you can try to catch it before it hits the ground. You might jump into the air to stop it. This is a good thing to do because it can save a person from getting hurt.

To catch something with your eyes is a different way. You watch a movie or see a picture. You do not touch it. You just look at it. Sometimes you see something you want to see but it is hidden. You look at it many times until you find it.
