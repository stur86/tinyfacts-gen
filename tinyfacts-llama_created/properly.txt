To do something is to act in a way that is right. It means you are following the way that is best for a person or thing. When you do something the right way, you are being good. When you do something the wrong way, you are being bad.

You can do something in a few different ways. One way is to follow a group. You follow what others are saying. Another way is to think for yourself. You use your own brain to decide what is right. A third way is to break the group or your own brain and do what you think is best. This is often very bad for you and the people around you.

When you act well, you show that you care about the things around you. You show you are a part of the group and you want to be safe and happy. You act well when you are kind and not mean. You act well when you help others and are nice to the people you meet.
