To explain something means you tell someone why it is the way it is. If you are trying to tell a child about a big thing that happened, you can explain it in many ways. You might use words like this: 

A long time ago, a man from a far away land came to the place where we live. He was very strong and could do many things. He could run fast, jump high, and fight with other people. He was also very good at making things from wood and other things.

You can explain this by saying, 

This happened a very long time ago. There was a man who came here. He did many things and was very strong. People here remember him because of what he did.

To explain something is to break it down into many small parts so that everyone can understand it. You can explain a big thing like a city, a school, or a car in parts. You might say, 

This is a city. It has many streets, a big building, and many houses. It is where people go to buy things, eat food, and see movies. There are many people living here.

You can also explain something to someone who is not sure. If you see a strange thing, you might ask, 

Why is that thing? What does it do? What is it made of? This helps the person understand the thing and feel safe.

In life, we often need to explain things to others. Maybe you want to tell your friend that you are sad. You can say, 

I am feeling very sad today. I miss my family. It is because I was hurt. I want to talk to you about it.
