A person or a thing can have a story. If a person lives for a long time and does many things, people remember them as a way of showing love. We might say, this person is like a star in the sky. Their name is known all over the world.

You can also talk about a thing. A car can carry many people and things. It moves from one place to another. A computer can do many jobs very fast. It can help us write, play music, and show pictures.

A story is a way to share these things. It tells us who, where, and why. It shows us what happened and how it made us feel. We learn about the past, and we can build on what we know to make our own life better.

Sometimes, a person leaves a thing behind. This is called a be left behind. They might give a book to a child. They might give a house to a family. This shows that they care about others and want to help them grow.

Being a leader is also about leaving a story. A person might be a leader in their city or in a school. They make sure everyone follows the right way. They help people feel safe and happy. Their story shows us how to work together and follow the right things.

In some stories, a person has a good thing. This thing is important for the person and the world. It can help them stay safe, bring people together, or make the world a better place. When we think about a person or a thing, we are talking about the story they left behind.
