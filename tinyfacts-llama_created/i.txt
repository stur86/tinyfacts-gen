To i is to be in a place at the same time. It is a word that does not tell you how you are feeling. You can be in a room, in a car, or in a tree at the same time.

Imagine you are looking at a picture. You are looking at it. You are in front of the picture. At the same time, you are also looking at the picture from the side. You are both looking at it at the same time.

You can also say you are in the middle of many things. You are standing in a school hall. You are in a room with your friends. You are also in the street outside the building. You are in many places at once.

This word is very important in writing. When you write something, you might put many different things in one place. You might write about your day, about a story, or about a place. In this case, you are saying that many things are happening at the same time.
