A car is a thing that you drive from one place to another. It has a seat for one person, and it has many other parts that make it go. These parts are usually made of hard stuff and moving parts that turn round and round.

A big car is much bigger than a small car. It has more seats and more room inside. It is often blue or red or white, and it looks very strong and safe.

People often buy these cars because they are very good at carrying many people and things. They are also very good at going very fast. Some people buy them just because they are beautiful to look at. They can go very high and very fast on a road.

The word comes from a story. A long time ago, a man had a big dog. He loved the dog very much. The man decided to name the dog a big dog, too. Later, he said the dog was a car, because it could carry many people and go very fast.

Now, many people have these cars. Some are very fast. Some are very safe. Some are very good at carrying things. People all over the world have these cars.
