To move up the sides of a high place is to go from the ground to the top. You can use a foot to walk up a small side, or a long line to climb a much taller one. When you do this, you go against the ground. The ground is the floor that you are standing on. The top is the very top of what you are trying to reach.

Imagine you have a long piece of paper. If you want to move it, you can pull it along. You are going up the paper. If you want to move it from the middle to the top, you are going up the paper.

On a long, high place that goes over the ground, you might use a long piece of wood to help you move. You might use a long line or a small line to pull the wood. You can hold onto the wood with your hands or stand on top of it with your feet. This helps you stay on the high place while you move around it.

People also use the word when they are going up a long walk. If you are walking with a group of friends, you might say, we are going up. This means you are moving away from the ground.
