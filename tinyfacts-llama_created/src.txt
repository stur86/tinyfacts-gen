The word you are looking for is called a starting point. Every story or picture starts with a place. This place is very important because it tells us who is there and what is happening. It is like the first word in a book. Without a starting point, there is no beginning. The story then goes from there.

Think of a song. The first line of a song is a starting point. It tells you what is happening and where the song is. The next lines continue the story. The words are like the start of a story. They help you understand what is happening.

In a picture, the starting point is where the eyes are. This is the first thing you see when you look at a picture. It tells you where the picture starts. If you look at a picture of a tree, the starting point is the top of the tree. From there, you can see the rest of the tree.

A starting point can be a person, a place, a thing, or even a feeling. It is a point that begins a story, a picture, or a game. Without a starting point, we would not know how to go from there.
