To give something means to share it with another person. Imagine you have a big bag of food. You want to give some of it to your friend. You put a small piece in their hand. This is giving. You are making the other person happy. You are showing you care about them.

Sometimes we give money. We give our money to the store so you can buy what you need. Or we give a book to someone who loves reading. We are giving them something they can use.

You can also give your time. If you work hard at school, you give your time to your teacher. You help them so they can teachers other kids. You are giving them your time.

Giving is important because it makes people happy. When you give, you are doing something kind. You are showing that you care about the other person.
