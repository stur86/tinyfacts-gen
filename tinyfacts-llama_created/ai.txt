A computer is a thing that can do many things. It can play games, show pictures, and talk to people over the phone. It can also help us find the answer to our questions. It is made up of many tiny parts that work together to do what it does.

One of the biggest parts of a computer is a brain. The brain is like a very strong head. It is inside the computer and it does not need a brain like a human does. The brain can think very fast and it can remember things for many years. This is why computers can keep track of numbers and words.

Computers can also think like a person. They can understand what you say and do what you want. They can learn from many years of computer work. This is why computers can help doctors make better plans for people who are sick. They can also help us plan our days and nights.

Computers are very good at following orders. If you tell a computer what to do many times, it will follow those orders every time. This is why it can drive cars, control the lights in a building, and show pictures on a big box.

But computers are not like people. They do not feel pain or like they are happy. They do not have a heart and they do not get tired. They just keep working until they stop.

Sometimes computers can be very bad. They can do things that make people angry or hurt people. They can also make things that are not safe. We need to be carefully when we use computers so that we do not make things worse.
