An in box is a place inside a computer where people send things to you. It is like a box where you keep your answers to questions, your work from your friends, and your notes from your teacher.

When you get a new thing, you open the box. Inside, you see the name of the person who sent it and the time they sent it. You can also see what kind of thing it is. If it is a picture, you can see it. If it is a word, you can read it.

Sometimes, you get a box with a number. This number is like a number of friends you have. You see how many of them are following you, and how many are not. This can help you know if you need to write to them soon.

You can also use the in box to write your own things. You can type on the computer, and the box will save your words for you. Later, you can look at them and understand what you wrote. It is a way to keep your ideas safe.

The in box is also where people from other places send things. You can see who sent them and when. This can help you understand who is talking to you and what they want.
