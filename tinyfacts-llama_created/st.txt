The word states is used to talk about how people live and how things work.

When you say a place is a state, you mean it is a part of the world where you live or where you work. Think about where you are right now. Is this a city? Is this a town? Is this a far away place you never went to? All these places are states. Each state has its own way of doing things, its own people, and its own things that are very important.

When a person says they are from a state, they mean they are from that part of the world. They might love their state because it is where they were brought up, where their family lives, or where the food is good. They might tell you stories about what life is like there.

We also talk about states that have the power to make their own things. In a state, the people get to decide if they want to have a big group of people or if they want to have many small groups. This is important because it makes sure the state has a way to make its own hard stuff, like money, that the people use to buy things they need.

There are also states that have a lot of power, but they are not in control of everything. These states have to follow the power of the one that is in control. This is like a big game where the game is decided by the people who set it up.
