Spaces are places that you don't have. When you walk, you move from one place to another. In the air, the sky is a big space above you. The water is a big space that surrounds your body. And the ground is a big space under your feet.

You also have spaces in your brain. Your brain is like a computer. It helps you think, remember things, and feel things. Inside your brain, there are many rooms, like rooms inside a house. Each room is a space for a different job. When you are happy, your brain is in a happy room. When you are scared, your brain goes to a room that helps you feel safe.

Sometimes people use the word spaces to talk about things that are very far away. If you go far away from your home, you might see a bright thing in the sky. This bright thing is a space. It is like a huge room in the sky that holds many other bright things.

You can also talk about the spaces between your words. When you speak, words go from your mouth and into your body. In between each word, there is a small space. This space is like a small room that holds the word.

Finally, you can use the word spaces to talk about how you feel. If you are very tired, you might feel like you have a lot of space inside you. Your body might feel heavy, and you might want to rest.
