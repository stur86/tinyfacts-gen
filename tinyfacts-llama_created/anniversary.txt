An old word for a day that happens a lot.

When you are one year old, you get a small thing that you hold. This thing keeps your hand. It is like a tiny friend. You hold it for a very long time. When you are one hundred years old, you get a big picture. You hold this picture in your hands for a very long time. This is your first big day. Many old people have many of these days.

Today, you have a day to remember a person. You give them a small thing. You say, thank you for being there. You say, I love you. You might give them a book. You might give them a piece of food. You might give them a small thing that you think they like.

Today is a day to remember how old you are. It is a day to remember how many years you have lived. It is a day to remember how much you have grown. It is a day to say, I am still here.

Some old people do this every year. Some old people do it only once. Some old people do it many times. They remember many days.

The word comes from old times. It is from the time when people lived in houses with many rooms. The word was used to talk about these days. Today, we use it to talk about our own lives.
