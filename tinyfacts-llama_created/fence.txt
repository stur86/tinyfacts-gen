To build a high wall around a piece of land is to make it safe. A person who does this is called a builder. They want to keep people from going into the land that is not their own.

Imagine you are playing in a big green field. You might want to stop the people who are pushing a big box across the field. You can put a long piece of wood around the box. This wood is like a big wall. It stops the box from going into the green space.

A house is a kind of building. It has a strong top and a bottom that keeps people inside. You do not need a wall to keep people in. But if you want to keep your home safe, you might build a high wall around it.

In a city, you might see high walls around the big areas that have many people. These walls are called city walls. They keep people from going into the city that has many houses and shops.

Sometimes, a person builds a wall to keep animals from going into a park. This is called a park wall. The wall stops the animals from eating the green parts of the park. This is important for the animals and the people who want to play there.
