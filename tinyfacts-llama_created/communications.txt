To send words through the air is called a long way of sending things. You can do this with your voice, with words you write, or with pictures and sounds.

Imagine you want to tell your friend that you are going to the store. You can speak into a small box. The box sends your words through the air to your friend. Your friend can then hear your words.

This is how we talk. It is how we share our ideas with each other when we are not in the same room. It is also how we share things we saw or heard from other places.

There are also ways to send things without words. You can send a picture of a dog to your friend. You can send a short sound that your phone makes when you pick a number. These ways do not use your voice at all.

Sometimes, people send words to each other while they are flying high in the sky. When a person is driving a car, they can send words to other cars using a long thing that is in the air. This helps cars to know where to go and to stay safe.

In our world, we use these ways to talk to each other when we are not in the same room. We use them to send ideas, to tell each other where we are, and to share things we saw or heard.
