To keep a set of plans for how you will do things is to use a word for this. It is a way to say when you want to do something and when you are free to do something else.

Imagine you have a job and you need to be there at a time. You can plan this in your mind. You might say, I will be at work from   room until  room. You do not say this because you are not sure if the time will come. You only know that you will be there in this set of times.

You can also keep a set of times for doing small things. If you have a meeting at  room, and you also have a phone call at  room, you can say you have a meeting at  room and a phone call at  room. This is like a set of plans for your life.

Sometimes, you need to keep plans for when you will do things. If you are going to school, you need to know the times you have classes so you can go. You can also keep plans for when you are going to do things. If you have a day to clean your room, you can plan this in your mind so you do not forget.

In some places, this word is used for when you do things for money. You might say you are working a set job at a set time. You are not really doing anything, but you are doing this work until a set time.
