To send something through the air is to move it from one place to another. You might send a letter to a friend. When you write your name and a few words on a piece of paper, you put it in a box. Then the box goes to a place that is far away. When the box arrives, you open it and read what the person wrote. You can also send things to places you do not visit right away. You can send money, food, or a small thing that you think another person might want.

You can also send things through the water. If you drop a small thing into a bottle, it will go down and come back up later. This happens with a computer. When you send a picture, the picture goes through the air or water until it reaches a place that can see it.

Sometimes, you send things that are not a living thing. You can send food to someone who is sick. You can also send a book if you do not want it anymore. People often send things to their friends when they want to say a word or a feeling. This way, they can talk to each other even if they are far away.
