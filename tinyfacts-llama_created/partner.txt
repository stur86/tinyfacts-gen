A friend is a person you are very happy to be with. They share fun times, laugh with you, and help when you need it. A good friend is always there for you. You can talk about your day, your school, or your home. You feel safe with them.

To be a good friend is to show you care. You do not just say things. You act on your words. You listen and try to understand what the other person feels. You help when they need help. This is how you build a strong and happy life with a friend.

In a group, a friend is the person who is close to you. If you are in a school group, the person you sit with in class is your friend. If you have a group of friends in a city, they are all your friends. In a big city, you might meet new people every day and make many new friends.
