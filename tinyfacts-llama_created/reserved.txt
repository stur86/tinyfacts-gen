To be left alone means you do not want others to touch you right now. It can happen if you are very happy or very afraid. You might feel this way because you are in a big group and you do not know who will be your friend next.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are all laughing and having fun. One person says, you are the best player. Then they all want to play with you. You might feel this way. You do not want to be the one they want to play with anymore. You want to be alone.

This feeling happens with many things. You might be in a movie. You see a great act. You feel so happy that you want to stay there and watch it again. You do not want anyone to take a picture of you. You want to be left alone in this place.

You can also be left alone when you do something that makes you very happy. You might want to go to a place you love. You go there and you are alone. You feel safe. You did not need anyone else in this place to be happy.
