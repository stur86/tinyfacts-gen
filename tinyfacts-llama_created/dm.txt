To do something with your hands is a big part of how we live. When you do something with your hands, you use them. You use your fingers to touch things. You use your hands to pick up food. You use your hands to play games. You use your hands to write words. You use your hands to make things.

When you play a game, you are doing something with your hands. You throw a ball. You hit a ball with your hands. You use your hands to catch a ball. You use your hands to make things. You use your hands to hold a book. You use your hands to make food. Your hands are important.
