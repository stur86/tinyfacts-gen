To avoid something is to keep away from it. When you see a big dog, you might not go near it. You want to stay safe. If you have a very hot day, you might wear a big shirt so you do not feel the sun.

You can also say that a thing helps you. If you have a friend who tells you the right way, you are safer. You do not have to worry about what happens next. The thing is there to help you stay calm.

In life, you often have to decide what to do. You might want to go on a trip, but you also need to do your work. You need to find the right time to do both. If you decide to go, you are doing something. If you decide to stay, you are not doing anything.
