To be easy is to be not hard. When you do something, you are done quickly. If a place is easy to get to, it is near the road. This makes it a good idea to go there. You do not have to walk around a long way. You can just walk to the door and go inside.

The word can also mean a thing that is not heavy. A small thing that sits on your desk can be a very easy thing to carry. It is not a heavy bag of paper. It is a small thing you hold.

Sometimes we use this word when we talk about people. If a job is easy, it means you do not have to work very hard. You can just sit and watch the computer. You do not have to do many things. On the other hand, if a job is hard, you have to work a lot. You are very busy. It is not easy to do.

You can also use this word with things that are fun. If a movie is easy to watch, you do not have to think about it. You just sit down and watch. You do not have to think about the story or the people.

There are also times when you want to be easy. You might want to eat a small food quickly, so you do not feel full. You do not want to sit and wait for something to come. You just want to eat now and be done. This is when you want to be easy.
