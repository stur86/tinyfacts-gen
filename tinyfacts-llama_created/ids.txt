An ideas is a thing you think about. It is a thought in your head. You might think about how you feel. You might want to do something. You might be afraid. You might be happy.

Think about the time you were a little boy. You loved to play with your friends. You wanted to run and jump and fly. Your mind was full of ideas about how you could be a great player.

When you grow up, you do not just have ideas anymore. You do things. You learn how to walk and talk. You learn how to eat and drink. You learn how to do your job. But sometimes, you still have ideas.

You might have an idea to start a new business. You might want to fly around the world. You might want to build a big house. You might want to go to a far away place where no one has seen before.

You can have many ideas at once. You can have ideas for a long time. You can not stop thinking about them. Your mind is always working.

Sometimes, you might feel like you do not have an idea anymore. You might feel like you are stuck in a place you do not want to be. But you can always start thinking again. You can start to feel again. You can start to see the world through a new eye.
