To break something down is to take it apartment by apartment. You start with one thing and then you add more and more pieces. You are not done until you know every small part.

Think of a car. You start with the big round thing in the middle. You add the front thing that opens and closes. You add the thing that makes it move. You add the thing that makes it stop. You add the thing that gives it power. You add the thing that makes the seat move. You add the thing that makes the seat stop. You add the thing that keeps the car safe. You add the thing that lets the person behind the car know how fast you are going.

You can also break down a story. You start with the beginning and then you add more and more parts. You are not done until you know every small part. This helps you understand what happened.

Sometimes you need to know every small thing about something. You might need to know the color of a person. You might need to know what they eat for breakfast. You might need to know how they feel. If you do not have these small pieces, you do not know everything.
