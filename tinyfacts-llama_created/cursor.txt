A small moving thing you use to point at a word on a computer or at a picture. It is like a hand that you can not see, but it is there all the time. You use it to tell the computer what to do.

When you want to write a word, you can place the small thing on the paper. It moves very fast across the paper, writing the word. You can see where the small thing is, so you do not have to look at the paper.

You can also use a small thing to point at a picture. You move the small thing around on the computer until it is in the right place. Then you look at the picture and see the person or thing you wanted to see.

Sometimes you use a small thing to draw a line. You move the thing around, and the line goes in a straight line. This is how you make a picture with a computer.
