A person who works at a place has to give money to the owner for the work they do. This money comes at the end of the week, at the end of the month, or at the end of the year. It is the money they get for doing their job.

Think of it like this. When you work for a school, you do things. You walk to class, you do your work, you help your friends. At the end of the day, the teacher gives you some money for what you did. This money is the person who works at the school.

When you work at a big place, like a store or a city, you do the same thing. You do the work, you get paid. Sometimes, the owner gives you money for every hour you work. This is how they pay the people who work there. It is an important job to help the owner run the place.

The word also means money that comes at the end of a long time. Imagine you work for many years and you get a lot of money at the end. You have paid yourself a lot of money over time. You feel happy because you have done well with your job.
