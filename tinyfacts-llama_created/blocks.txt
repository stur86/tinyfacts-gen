Blocks are things you can hold in your hand. You can pick them up and put them down. They are made of hard stuff that does not break easily. You can use them to make a room, or to build something new. Some blocks are red, some are blue, and some are white. You can also use them to make a big room for a game.

When you are a child, you often use blocks to build a house. You put the blocks together to make a door and a window. You can also use them to make a car or a dog. You can also use them to make a story. You might say, a person, a dog, a cat, and a ball. The blocks are like small pieces of something important.

Sometimes, you might put a block in your mouth. This is not good. You should not put a block in your mouth because it can hurt your teeth. If you do this, you will feel a big pain. It is best to ask a grown person for help.

Blocks are also something you can do with a friend. You can build a thing together. You take the blocks and the friend takes the other blocks. You put them together and make something new. You can laugh and say, we made that together.

You can also use blocks to learn about the world. You can look at the different colors of the blocks. You can find a block with the color you like. This can help you remember the color.

In some schools, you use blocks to learn about numbers. You take a set of blocks and you have to make a number of blocks. You can use the blocks to show how many you have or how many you need. It is a fun way to learn about numbers.
