A man is a person who lives on a land. People call him a person. He or she has a name and a body. We use the word to talk about him.

A person is a human. Humans are the ones who make things. They build houses, make food, and make clothes. They also have a family. A mother and father, a father and daughter, a mother and son, and a sister and brother are all people.

To be a person is to have a heart. A heart is a thing in your chest. It makes you feel. It makes you happy when you are with people you love. It makes you sad when you are alone. It makes you angry when you are wrong. It makes you scared when you are afraid. It makes you want to stay safe.

We also use the word to talk about a person in a different way. If someone is very good, they are a person. If someone is very bad, they are a person who does not follow the right way. If someone is very happy, they are a person who is full. If someone is very sad, they are a person who is not happy.

People are always changing. We grow. We learn. We become new. This is why we say a person is always changing.
