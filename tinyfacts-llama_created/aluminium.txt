Alone means you are the only person in the room. You do not have any other person to talk to.

Alone is often used to talk about things that are strong. A strong car can carry many people. An alone dog can run a long way. An alone tree can stand for many years.

But we also need to talk about alone. When you are alone, you can think about what happened. You can feel sad or angry. You can also feel happy if you had a good day. You are the only one who knows your own face.

We need to have alone time. This is time when you do not talk to anyone. You can read a book, watch a movie, or play games. This is a good way to rest your mind and your body.

Sometimes, alone can mean being alone with your own mind. When you are alone, you might feel a bit strange. Your body might feel slow. Your mind might feel hard to understand. But this is okay. You are the only one in your own head.

You can also say alone is about being happy. If you are alone on a beautiful day, you can feel very happy. You might smile a lot. You might feel like everything is okay.
