To put something into a plan means to think about it and decide what to do. When you want to start a new thing, you might want to ask a group of people if they like the idea. You might say, you want to start a new kind of food store. If many people say yes, you can start the store.

Sometimes, a person wants to do something that is not easy. They might want to do it in a very short time. They might not have enough money. They might also want to do it without asking many people if they are okay. To do this is called a plan. The person is trying to be ready for the moment when they start the new thing.

You can also put a plan into a book. When a writer wants to write a story, they make a plan. They write down what they want to happen. They think about who the people are and what the story should say. They write down the story in a way that helps others read it.

When you put something into a plan, you are starting to do something that is going to happen. It is a start to a new thing.
