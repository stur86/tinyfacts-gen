When you are standing in a place, you are not alone. You are standing with other people around you. If you are afraid or if you are in a hard time, you can ask people you trust to be there for you. They can be your rock. This is when something is under the care of many people. It is a way to keep things together when things are very hard.

Think about a tree. The tree does not stand alone. It has many people around it. It gets water and food from the ground and air. It makes its own food from the green things around it. The people of the town are the ones who help the tree grow. They tell it to not grow in that way. They tell it to grow in the right way so it does not fall down. They are the ones who keep the tree safe.

This is how we keep things together in our lives. We do not stand alone. We are all under the care of other people. We are all under the care of the world. When we work together and help each other, we can do many things that we could not do alone.
