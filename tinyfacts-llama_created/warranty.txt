A long time ago, when things were made by people and not by computers, there were people who made them. They said, if you buy a thing, it is good for a long time. If you want to stop it, you have to pay money. This money is the thing we call a promise. This promise is like a word that says, if you do this, you will not be mad.

Think of it like a friend. If you buy a book, your friend says, you can read it as many times as you want for free. But if you break the book, you have to pay the friend money. Your friend has to help you. They fix the book. They give you a new one.

Today, people say the same thing with a word. They say, if you buy something, it is good for a long time. But they do not say you have to pay money. They just say, it will stay good for a long time. This is not as strong as a promise. It is more like a word. You can break it without paying money.

Sometimes, a thing will break. You might say, the car is broken. Or you might say, the computer is broken. But if you bought it, the person who made it said you would not be mad. They did not say you had to pay money. They just said you could try to fix it yourself.

The word helps people. If you buy something and it breaks, you do not have to pay money. But it does not mean the thing will stay good for a long time. It means you can try to fix it or you can buy another one. It is a way to say, if something goes wrong, you can do something about it.

We also have something called a return. If you buy something and it is not what you wanted, you can go back and get a different one. It is like a word that says, you can try something else. You do not have to pay money. You just go back and find another one.
