To get a cut on the head can hurt. You might use a thing that has a long part on it. When you hold the thing over your head, the part can go right through your hair. You press the thing down on your head. The hair does not stand up very much because the part is going right over it.

The part is made of hard stuff. You can use this stuff to cut your hair. When you cut your hair with this kind of part, you make a cut that goes right through the hair. The hair grows back from the bottom of the cut.

If you get a cut with this part, the hair might stand up more because it is not cut right. It might look funny in a way that makes you want to cover it up. This is why people might wear a head cover when they get a cut.

In some places, you get a cut on your hand. This is when you cut a piece of skin. You use a thing that has a hard part at the end to cut the skin. The hard part can go right over your hand or under your hand. You press the hard part down on the skin. The skin does not stand up very much because the hard part is going right over it.

A cut is something you get on your body. It can happen on your head, face, hand, or foot. It can be small or very deep.
