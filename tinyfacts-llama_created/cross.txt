To cross something means to go over it. Imagine you are trying to get to the other side of a wide water. If you walk along the bottom, you are not crossing the water. You are just going around it.

If you have a piece of wood and you throw it over a small wall, you are crossing it. You are moving from one side to the other. You are not going around the wall. You are going over it.

Sometimes, to cross a thing, you need to walk around it. You are not going to the other side of it. You are going to the other side in another way.

You can also cross a group of people. If you have a group of friends and you want to go to a place that is far away, you might cross them. You are going to the other side of your group.

There is a word that means to cross something that is hard. If you are afraid of a big animal, you might feel afraid to cross it. You are afraid of going over it. You are afraid of what might happen.
