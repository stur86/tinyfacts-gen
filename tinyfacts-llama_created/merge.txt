To join two things together means you put them into one place. It is like putting a small piece of wood and a long one together. You put the small piece under the long one. The small piece is now a part of the long one. They become one thing.

Imagine a big group of people. Each person is like a small group of friends. Some people are friends with other people in different cities. If you put all the groups together, you now have a group of friends in one city. The people who were once alone are now part of the group. They are all part of the same group.
