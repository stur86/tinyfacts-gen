The word can mean two different things.

When you say something is strong, you mean it has a lot of power. You can use this word to talk about a person who is very good at what they do. If a person is strong, they can do a lot of work very fast. They can lift a heavy box or run around a big field for a long time. This makes them very important.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are very much. If you have a strong feeling about something, you might feel happy or sad. You might want to act quickly because you feel very strong about it. It is like your heart is telling you to do something.

Sometimes, when you are very angry, you might use this word. You might say you are strong and that you will not let them stop you. This means you do not like what the other person is doing. You want to stay safe and be the one in control.
