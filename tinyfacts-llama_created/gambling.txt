To play with money is to use the money of others for a game that is not about the game. When you play, you try to get more money or things than your friend or family. You might betters how much money you have, or you might betters which game will be the best.

Some people play this game with money. They put their money into a box. Then they take turns to pick a number. If the number you picked is the right one, you get the money. But if the number is wrong, the other person gets the money. This game is not safe and can lead to a big problem.

Some games are not played with money at all. You might play with your body. You try to hit a ball with a long thing. If you hit the ball into a corner, you are the one who gets the ball. Some games are played with a ball and a small piece that you throw. The person who gets the piece first gets the ball.

Gaming is a problem that can hurt your life and your money. It is also a problem that can break the trust between people. If one person takes all the money or the game, the other people do not trust them anymore. This makes it hard to do any other kind of work or to be friends with them.
