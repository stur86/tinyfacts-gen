To be on a floor means you are standing on the ground. You walk around the room, step from room to room, and stand on the same floor many times. The floor is the hard part of the room where you stand.

If you look around, you see many different kinds of floors. You might see a floor with hard white things that make a soft sound when you walk on them. You might see a floor with soft, green things. You might see a floor with hard, hard things that are very hard to walk on. You might see a floor with soft things that feel like a soft bed.

If a car drives on a floor, it means the car is on a hard, hard top part of the road. The car has a hard part at the front and a hard part at the back that help it stay on the road. The floor of the car is not a floor in the room, but it is still a floor.

If a tree is standing on a floor, it means the tree is standing on a hard piece of ground. The tree does not go up into the air like a tall building. The floor is the ground under the tree. The tree has a strong part at the top and a strong part at the bottom that hold it up.
