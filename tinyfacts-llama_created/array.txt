An area is a place with many things in it. It is a set of numbers and words that show where things are. Imagine you are in a city and you are looking at a picture of the city. You see many streets, many houses, many cars, and many people. You have seen these things one by one, but now you want to see them all together.

You can put these many things into a table. Each line in the table is like a street. Each number is like a house number. If you look at the table, you can see how many houses are on each street. You can also see which street is the one next to another street. This helps you understand how the city is put together.

An area can be a place in the ground or a place in the air. You can look at a city, a park, a field, or a water body like this. These places have many things in them. You can see many people, animals, trees, and water.
