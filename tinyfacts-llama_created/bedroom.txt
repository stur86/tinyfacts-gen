A bedroom is a room in a house where a person sleeps. It is a quiet room with a bed, a table, and sometimes a chair. Inside the room, you can keep your clothes, books, and other things. In the morning, you go to this room to get ready to go to school or work.

A bedroom is usually a small room. It can be very small, with only a few walls. Or it can be a big room with many walls and a high window. Some bedrooms have a window that looks out to a street. This is nice because you can see the world outside while you sleep.

You can also have a bedroom with a window that looks out to a house or a park. This is a good way to feel like you are in a different place. You can hear the sound of a park or the sound of a house from your room.

In some houses, there is another room for a person who does not sleep. This room is for a person who works at night. They might sit at a desk and type on a computer. This room is usually very quiet and has a table with papers and a chair.

Sometimes, a room has a door that opens into another room. This door can lead to a kitchen, a bathroom, or a hall. This way you can go from one room to another without having to walk all the way out the door.

A bedroom is a place to rest and feel safe. It is a room where you can be alone and have your own space.
