To cool something means you make it not hot anymore. You do this by moving it far away from the sun or by putting it in a box with cold air inside.

Imagine you have a drink that is very hot. You drink it and it makes your face hot. Then you sit down next to a big green thing. The green thing gives off cold air. The cold air goes into your body and makes the drink go away. In this way, you make the drink not hot anymore.

You can cool things in many different ways. You can put a drink in the box with cold air. You can sit next to the green thing for a long time. You can even sit in front of a window that looks at the sky. The sun will not be so bright and the drink will not be as hot.

This happens when the hot thing has too much warm air inside it. The warm air gets very big and then it goes out. This makes the hot thing not hot anymore. This is how we stay cool when we walk in the hot sun or when we are playing games.
