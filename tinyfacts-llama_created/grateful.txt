To be glad is to feel happy inside. When you are glad, you feel like something good happened. You might laugh or smile. You might feel a warm feeling in your chest.

When you are very glad, it can be because you got what you wanted. Maybe you got money you needed. Maybe you got a thing you love. You might be happy to see your friends.

You can feel glad when you do something good for someone. If you give money to a person who needs it, you are glad. If you help a friend who is sick, you are glad for them. You are glad because you did something nice.

You can also feel glad when you see the sun come up. When the sun starts to go up and the sky gets light, you might feel glad. This is a good day. You are glad because the sun is coming up.

Being glad can help you feel better when you are sad. If you are sad because you lost a friend, you might feel glad when you think about the good times you had with them. You feel happy because you remember happy times.
