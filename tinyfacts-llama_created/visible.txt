To see something is to notice it with your eyes. You might see a bright light in the sky when it gets ready to go down. You see a person you know in a room. You see a thing that is moving, like a ball or a car.

When something is not hidden, it is seen. It is like a big sun in the sky. You can see the sun when it is up in the morning and the sun when it is in the evening. You see the bright parts of things when they are very clear.

If you close your eyes, you can not see anything. Your brain uses what it sees inside your head to help you understand the world. Even if you are asleep, you might still see things that are not really there. You might see a dream, a picture from your mind, or a tiny light from a far away star.

Sometimes, something is only seen if you look at it with your eyes. A person might be very tall, but if you are looking at them from a great place, they might seem small. Your eyes help you see the world and keep you safe.
