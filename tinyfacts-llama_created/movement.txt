To move means you change your position from one place to another. You start where you are now and go to a different spot. You might walk, run, jump, or use a car.

Imagine you are sitting in a chair. You are still. The chair is not moving. But if you get up and walk around the room, you are moving. You are changing your position from the chair to the floor.

You can also move in a different way. You might go up a high wall. You are moving in a way that goes up. Or you might go down a long road. You are moving in a way that goes under you.

When you move, you need something to help you. A car needs roads to go on. A dog needs to run to find food. A person needs to walk or use a thing that can carry them.

Sometimes, moving is easy and fun. You might play a game where you run and jump. Other times, moving can be hard. If you are afraid, you might be afraid to move. But if you are scared, you can try to find a way to feel better so you can move your body.

In your life, you move every day. You move from one school to another. You move from your home to a new place. You move when you go to work or school. You move when you go to the store or to a friend.
