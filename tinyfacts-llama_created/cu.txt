To cut something means to break it so it can be used in another way. It is to take a small piece from a hard thing, a rock, or a thing made of wood, so you can use it for a new job.

Imagine you have a big piece of wood. You want to make a box from it. If you cut the wood straight across, you will get two pieces. One piece will be the front of the box, and the other will be the back. You can use these pieces to build the box.

If you cut a soft thing, it becomes smaller. You can use the pieces to make a new thing that is smaller. You might use the soft pieces to make a soft bag for clothes.

You can also cut a thing so it looks different. If you cut a book, you can make the cover look new. This helps people see the book is still the same, but it looks different.

In a kitchen, you might use a cut to make food. A cut can mean a cut made on a piece of food, like a piece of a piece of food. It can also mean a cut made on a person, like a cut on a hand.

Cuts are very important. They help us change the world.
