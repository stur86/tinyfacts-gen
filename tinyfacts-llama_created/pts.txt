The word is about a small piece of hard stuff that you use to make your hands feel better.

When you are very sad or angry, your body feels the pain. This is because your heart is telling your brain that something is wrong. Your brain then sends a little bit of that pain to your hands. This is like a very small, very fast way for your body to send a feeling from one part of your body to another.

The small piece of hard stuff is what your body uses to carry that feeling. It is like a tiny road for the pain to follow. Sometimes the body makes it feel like the pain is right there in your hand. Other times, the pain is carried to your arm and then to your hand. You might feel the pain in your hand when you are crying or when you are very happy.

People often use this word to talk about how someone is feeling. If a person is very sad, they might say they are having a bad day with their hands. If someone is feeling angry, they might say they have a bad feeling in their hands.
