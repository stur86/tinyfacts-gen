Our is a word that people use when they talk about things they have or are a part of. When you say this word, you are telling the other person that you are the one who has or is a part of that thing.

Imagine you have a dog. You are the one who gives the dog food to eat. If you are the only one who does this, you are our dog. You are the one who is doing the thing.

If you have a friend, you are our friend. If you have a house, you are our house. If you have a car, you are our car. You are the one who is in that thing.

This word is also about how you feel. If you are our friend, you feel happy. If you are our dog, you feel very much. You are a part of the person or thing.

Sometimes, this word is used to talk about people. You can say, Our family is the people we love. This means you love those people very much.

We can also use our to talk about things that are not living. If you say, This is our book, you mean it is a book that you own. You are the one who has it.

So, our is a word that helps us talk about things and people in a way that makes sense.
