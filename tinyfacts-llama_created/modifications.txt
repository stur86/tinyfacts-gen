To make something different is to change it in some way. It is when you take something and turn it into another kind of thing. This can happen for many reasons.

One way is if you want to make food better. You can add more food to it. This makes the food more good for you. You can also add colors or sweet things to make it look nice. You are making it different from the way it was before.

Another way is if you want to change how a car works. You might put a new part under it. This part makes the car go faster or stops it from going very high. You are changing the way it works.

Sometimes, you might want to change something because it is not right. You might think it is not safe or not nice. You might ask a grown person to tell you how to fix it. This is also changing something.

You can also make things that are not made of the same kind of stuff. If you take a thing and make it out of a different kind of stuff, you are making it different. This is how we grow our hair when we are very small. Our hair grows out of tiny parts we get from our bodies. 

All these ways are about changing something. They help us make the world a better place, or they help us feel better about our own bodies.
