To have something in your pocket is easy. You just put it there. But if you are really carefully, you have money in your hand. This money is the money that people give you for doing work or for buying things. You can use it to buy food, clothes, and a house. It is the money that makes life real.

You do not need to walk to a store to get this money. You can just walk to the street and look for people who give it to you. They are called money people. Some people give money because they work hard. Some people give money because they are happy. Some people give money because they love you.

You can keep your money safe at home. You can put it in a box with a strong lock. This is like keeping a very safe place in your heart. You do not need to tell anyone where you keep it. The money stays there, and you can get it later when you need it.

Sometimes, people give money to each other for a good thing they did. If you help a friend, they might give you money as a thank you. If you do a hard job, you might get money from a store. If you write a good book, you might get money from people who like it.

You can also use this money to buy things you want. You can buy a new book, a game, or a thing you like to watch. This money is the way to buy the things you need or want.
