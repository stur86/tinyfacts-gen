A thing you carry around is a small computer. It can talk to other computers. It can also talk to a phone. It can even talk to other things like a car. This thing is called a small computer. You can carry it in your pocket.

When you want to talk to a person on the other side of a room, you use this thing. You put your phone near you. The phone gets the word. Your phone sends it to your computer. The computer then sends the word to the person.

This is why people use small computers to talk to each other. They can talk to each other without using words. They use sounds and words from far away. This is how people talk to each other in a big city.

Sometimes people use this thing for work. They talk to other computers at work. They send papers and pictures. The computers send them back. The computers do not understand words. They understand numbers. So they send numbers instead.

This thing is very good. You can carry it with you. You can do many things at once. You can talk to someone, look at a picture, and send a paper. It is a very small computer.
