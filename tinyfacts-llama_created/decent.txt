To be a good person means you act with a good heart. You try to do what is right, even when it is hard. You do not hurt others or take their things without asking. You show that you are kind and that you care about how others feel.

A person who is not very good is one who does not act this way. They might do things that hurt others or take things that do not really feel like their own. They might not say nice words or show they care. They act in a way that makes others afraid or angry.

If you are a person who is good, you are a good friend. You are a person you like to be around. You make others feel happy and safe. You help them when they need it. You are a person of good word. You keep your promises and do what you say you will.

Sometimes, people say that a person is very good, but they do not know. They are a person of good word, even if they are not always very nice. They try to be kind and to act well, even when they do not always do it well.
