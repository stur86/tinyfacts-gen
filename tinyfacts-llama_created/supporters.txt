A group of people who are very good for someone is called a group of people who are friends. They like the person and want them to be happy. They might sit next to them at a party, buy their food, or write nice things about them.

Sometimes these people are very strong. They are like a big group of friends who shout and laugh when one person does something good. They want everyone to see how happy they are.

A group can also be about a game. If you love a team, you are a big part of the team. You might wear a shirt with the team name on it. You feel happy when the team does well. You want them to winding.

People can also be a group when they are about an idea. They think something is good or bad. Some people like to buy things that are good for the world. They want to change the world for the better. Others like to buy things that make them feel happy. They want to make life better for everyone.

In every group there are people who like the same things. There are also people who do not like the same things. They all work together to make sure everyone has what they want.
