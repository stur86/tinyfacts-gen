To give someone a job is a very important thing to do. When you do this, you are saying that you trust them to do their job.

Imagine you are the head of a big team. You need many people to help you work. You could pick one person to be the leader. You then tell them which job they are going to do and how they should do it. This is like giving them a job. They have a new job to do, and you are saying they are safe to do it.

Sometimes, you might give a job to someone you know. If you know a person who can build a house, you might say,  You can build a house. This is a way of saying,  You are the one who will do the work.

You can also give a job to a person when you need something done. If you have a very important job that needs to be done right now, you might ask a friend to help you. They will do the job for you.

To give someone a job is a way of showing you believe in them. It is a way of letting them know that you trust them to do a good job.
