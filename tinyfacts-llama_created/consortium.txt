A group of people, money, or things that work together to do a job or make a plan is called a group. They share the work and the money. They are not one person. They are many people who all help each other.

Sometimes, a group of people start a business. They make a plan, pay money, and work together. They buy things and make things to give to people. They also make money from their work.

In a group, everyone has a job. One person might run the business. Another person might help with the money. Another person might help with the things they make. The group works together to make sure everything goes well.

A group can also be many people who are not all friends. They might have different ideas or be from different places. They still work together because they share the same job or plan.

A group can be very big. There might be hundreds of people in a group. Or a small group might have only a few people. They all still work together.

When people work together, they can make something new or do a job better. It is a good thing.
