When something happens, it is either early or late. You can be early if you arrive before other people. This makes you the first one to see the people you are going to meet.

You can also be late. This happens when you are not ready to go. You might be sitting in your room, but your mom is already getting ready for work. You go to the door, and she says,  You are late. This is a bad thing because you did not do what you should have done.

Sometimes, you are early and you feel good. You have a chance to do something before other people start. You might see a movie with your friends before they leave home. This is a good feeling because you can do something fun without everyone else doing it.

In your life, you need to be both early and late. You need to go to school on time. This is important so you do not miss your classes. You need to be ready to go when the bell rings. You also need to be ready to go when other people need you. This is why you are a good friend or a good family person.

In a story, a person might have a plan. They know they need to leave the house at a very early time. They want to be there before the police start to look for them. They are doing something early because they know what will happen later. This is why the person acts in a way that helps them stay safe.
