To put things into a book is to write about them. You might have a lot of things you want to know about one kind of thing. Maybe you have a lot of books to read, or a lot of clothes to buy. You need a way to keep everything straight so you can find what you are looking for.

You do this by making a listened book. This book has many things you have. You might write the name of the book, the name of the person who wrote it, and the place where the book was made. You might also write a few words about what the book is about. This helps you remember what you found.

Sometimes people use this word for other things too. You might say a place is in the book of things that happen in a city. You might say a person is in the book of things that they do. You are saying the person is a part of a bigger story.

You can also use this word for things that are made by a person. You might look at a book that a person made. The person wrote the words, made the cover, and put the book on a table. You can then read the book and remember the words.

To be in a book is to be part of something bigger than just yourself. It means you are a part of a story or a story about others.
