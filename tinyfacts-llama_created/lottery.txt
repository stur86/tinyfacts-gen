A lot is a very big number of things. One lot is ten, another is one hundred, another is a hundred and twenty, and so on. We use these lots to decide who gets a very small money that people give away.

In a game, you put a small piece of paper with your number on it into a box. Every day, you pick a number from the box. If your number is the one that came up, you get the money. You do this many times. Each time you pick a number, you are more and more sure you will get the money.

The big numbers are the ones that are not in the papers you give to people. They are the numbers that are not yet in the papers. The people who pick these numbers get the money, and the papers are given to those people. The papers are then put away so no one else can pick them.

Some lots are run by people who do not care. They give away money to anyone who picks a number. They do not follow any of the usual ways. They do not keep the papers. They just give the money to the people who pick the numbers.

Other lots are run by the city. They follow a very small way. You must put your papers in a box, and then you must pick a number from a set of numbers that are already given to you by a computer. You do not know the numbers before you start. The computer does the work for you. The people who pick the numbers get the money.

Everyone is very interested in which lots are run by who. Some people think that the city lots are better because they are run by people who care. Some people think that the people lots are better because they do not follow any of the usual ways. Some people think that both are the same, because the papers are the same in both cases.
