To pick something means you are looking at it and you decide what to do with it. You might pick a ball if you are playing a game with your friends. You might pick a book if you want to learn more about a story.

Sometimes, you pick a thing you like. You might pick a color, a food, or a small thing that is very pretty. You might pick a book because you want to read a story.

You can also pick a person. You might pick a friend if you like them very much. You might pick a teacher if you like how they teachers do things. You might pick a dog if you like the way they walk.

When you pick something, you are taking it away from the world. You are not letting it stay. You are deciding what to do with it now.

Imagine you have a bag of food. You pick a food you like. You take that food out of the bag and you eat it. You are using the food. You are not keeping it anymore. You let it go out of the bag and into your body.
