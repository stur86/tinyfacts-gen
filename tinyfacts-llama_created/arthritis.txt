Arms

You use your arms to do many things. You can hold things. You can make things. You can throw things. You can reach the top of a tall building. Your arms are very strong.

Sometimes, your arms get very hard to move. This happens when you get older. Your body starts to make the red stuff in your body more. This red stuff builds up inside your arms. It makes your arms feel tight and cold.

The red stuff can make it hard to move your arms. Your hands might feel heavy. You might feel pain when you touch your arms. You might also feel pain when you move your arms very fast.

There are different kinds of arms. Some people have arms that are very strong. Some people have arms that are very small. Some people have arms that are very long. Some people have arms that are very short.

Arms are very important. They help you to move around. They help you to hold things. They help you to pick up food. They help you to throw things.

If you have arms that hurt a lot, you should see a doctor. A doctor can help you feel better. They can give you a drink or a top shot to make the pain go away. They can also give you a small thing to put inside your arm. This can help you feel better very fast.

Your arms are a part of you. They help you to live.
