To be in a line means you are standing in a straight line. Imagine you are sitting in a room with your friends. You might be the one who is sitting in the middle. You are not in front of anyone or behind anyone.

When you want to put something in a line, you do this. You look at the things you need to put together. You decide which one goes next. Then you put each one in the right order. This is like making a plan so you do not get confused.

If you have many things you want to do, you might make a listened plan. You write down each thing. You put them in the right order. You can also use a picture to help you. Imagine a table with many things. You look at the picture and you know where each thing is.

If you put things in a line in a way that makes them easy to see, you have done this. You have made it easy for everyone to know where each thing is. This is important in work, school, and even in games.
