When people talk, they often use words that help us understand what is happening. One of these words is called a word. A word is a short group of letters. You can hear a word when you listen to a story or a song.

Another word is a thing. It is a thing you see, a thing you touch, a thing you eat. You might see a tree, a dog, or a ball. You might see a book, a chair, or a table. This word is about the thing you are looking at.

Sometimes, we use words to talk about people. We use words to talk about how someone feels. If a person is happy, we might use the word happy. We might also use words to talk about how someone looks. If a person is very good at a game, we might say they are a great player.

We also use words to talk about plans. If a person says they are going to do something, they are making a plan. This plan is like a short story that says what will happen next. It helps everyone know what is going on.

Finally, we use words to talk about stories. A story is a long group of words that tells a story. You might hear a story about a boy who found a big cat or a girl who fell in love. The words help us imagine what happened.

We use these words every day. We speak them, write them, and read them. They help us stay in touch with each other and share our lives.
