To give an order or a call can mean two different things. One way is to send a person to another place. If you want to tell a friend to come, you might write their name and their home on a piece of paper. This is an order. You are telling them where they must go.

The other way is to give a word to people. Imagine you want to tell the whole town that a new store has opened. You can write the words on a piece of paper and send it to everyone. This is also an order. The person reading the words has to let everyone know the store is there.
