To wish is to hope that something will happen to you. It is a way to feel that things are going well even if you are not sure. You might wish for a good day at school because you are looking forward to playing with your friends.

Sometimes you wish for things that are hard to get. If you wish for a new game to play, you are hoping that someone will buy it for you. You might also wish for a fun time with your family or friends.

You can also wish for things that might not happen. If you wish for the sun to stay in the sky, you are hoping that it will not go away. This shows that you care about the world and want everything to be okay.

Wishing is different from wanting. Wanting is a clear idea of what you need. Wishing is about feeling something inside. When you wish, you are letting your heart know that you want things to be the way you hope they will be.
