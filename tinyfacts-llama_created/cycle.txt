To go around something is to move in a round way. You can go up a road, down a street, or through a park. You might also ride a big round thing on top of water or air.

You can also say you are in a round of things. This means you are very important. Maybe you are the head of a group of people. Maybe you are the person who does a job that is very hard. You are the top one.

Sometimes, we say things are in a round and wrong. This is when we are wrong. Maybe something is wrong because it is not the way it should be. If you say a thing is in a round, you are trying to tell the truth. You are not just making it up.

In the world of running and playing, a round can also mean the end of a game. When you finish a round of a game, you have played all the games. You have done all the rounds. You are done!
