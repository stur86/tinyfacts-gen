To join means to come together with others. Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. One friend is very good at running. Another friend is very good at jumping. If you join them, you can all do more things together. You can run and jump and play games faster. This is what joining means.

Another way to join is when you want to do something. You might want to go to school. If many people join your school, you are all on the same team. You are doing the same thing, and you are all working together to learn.

Sometimes, people join a group because they have some things in mind. Maybe they want to make money or make a change in their town. They all join and work together. This is a way to build power and make their ideas happen.

To join something can also mean to stop doing something. If you are on a team, you might stop playing if the team loses. You are not there anymore. You are not part of the group anymore. This is a way to leave a group when you do not agree or when you feel the group is not right.

Sometimes, joining is about being part of a story. A story has many people who are all there. You can be one of them. You might join a movie, a book, or a game. This is when you become part of a bigger story with many other people.
