To be excited means you are very happy. You might jump up and down because you are looking forward to something. This can happen when you hear good music, see a movie, or play a game that you love.

You can also feel this when you are in a room with many people you know. This room can feel like a big party. Everyone is talking and laughing. You might feel your heart beating fast because you are so happy to be there.

Sometimes, you might feel this when you are about to do something you really like. You are so ready that you can barely wait. Your face might turn red, and your eyes might light up with a bright look. You feel like you are going to shout or scream with how happy you are.

To be excited is to feel a lot of happy and nervous at the same time. It is a strong feeling that makes you want to do something fast.
