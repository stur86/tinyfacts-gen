A game with a ball is what people call a game. The team with the ball tries to move it. They do this by running with it, by throwing it to their friends, or by hitting it with their hands. The other team tries to stop them. If the ball goes out of the lines, the other team gets it. The team with the ball has four chances to move it in. If they do not move it in these chances, the other team gets the ball.

The game is played on a big green field with lines around it. The lines are important because they mark where the ball must go. The team with the ball starts at one end of the line. The team without the ball has to move the ball to the other end. The team with the ball is trying to get the ball to their friends at the other end before the other team does.

There are many different ways to play. Some teams use a ball with four points. Other teams use a ball with many points. Some teams run very fast. Some teams throw the ball a lot. Some teams hit the ball with their hands. Some teams use their feet. Some teams use their heads.

People watch these games. They like to see how the teams work together and how they use their bodies. Some people also like to see the ball move quickly.
