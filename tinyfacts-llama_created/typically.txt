To use a word means to use it in a way that is clear to everyone. When you say a word, you are telling the other people what you mean. You tell them how the word looks, how it is used, and what it means.

A word is often made up of many small parts. Each part is a tiny piece of the word. You can pick one part at a time and use it. If you use all the parts, you get a word. You can change one part and the word changes too. This happens when you change a word.

A word can also mean how things are. If you say a word is big, you mean it is very large. If you say it is small, you mean it is very tiny. You can use a word to show how something is.

Sometimes, a word can mean more than one thing at once. If you say a word is both big and small, you have found two different things. This can happen because the word has many parts that are different, or because the word means more than one way.

A word is like a way to talk. When you use a word, you are showing people how you think. You are showing them what you mean and how you mean it. The word helps the other people understand you better.
