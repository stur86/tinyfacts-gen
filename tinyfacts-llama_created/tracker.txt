A tracker is a person who follows animals. They are usually good at finding where they live and where they eat. A person who tracks might go into the woods to see a big animal. They move very quietly so they do not scare the animal. They look at the animal to see how big it is and how fast it can run.

Sometimes, a tracker helps a person who wants to catch a bad animal. If a person is afraid of a dog, they might try to find it so they can take it away. A tracker can also help people who want to see how animals live in their homes. They can learn about the food they need and the places where they live.

A tracker uses things they build to follow the animal. These things can be things like a long line with a small thing at the end. The person can see the animal on the line by watching the end. They can also use the line to follow the animal if it runs away and then comes back.
