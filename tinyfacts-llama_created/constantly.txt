When you say something is always, you mean it never changes. It stays the same every moment. You can not think of a time when it was different. It is always there.

If you are in a room, and you hear your friend talking, you can always hear them. They are always there. The sound of your voice is always coming from the same place. If you leave the room, the sound of your voice goes with you.

This is true for many things in life. A tree stands in one place for many years. It does not move. A dog stays with its family for many years. It never changes its family.

Sometimes you might be in a room and you start to think about a thing. You are looking at a picture, or you are reading a book. The moment you start to think, the room is still there. But then, something happens. You look up and the room changes. The picture is gone. The book is closed. The room is different. The room is always different.
