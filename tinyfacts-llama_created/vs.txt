To use a word means to use it in a way that is clear and easy to understand. If you want to talk about a person, you can say they are a person. This shows that you know who they are and you are talking about them.

If you say you are tired, you are not saying you are very happy. You are saying you are not very strong. You are using the word tired to tell what you feel.

When we write this, we use words. We put words together to form a story. We use words to show who we are, what we feel, and what we think. This helps us to be clear and to show our ideas to others.

Sometimes, we use words to show that two things are different. If you are happy and your friend is sad, you are showing that the two things are not the same. You are telling the other person that the two things are different.

We also use words to talk about things that are not real. If you say your dog is a dog, you are using the word to show that something is true. But if you say your dog is a cat, you are making something up. You are not really sure if the dog is a cat.

This is why it is very important to use the right words. If you use the wrong word, you might confuse someone. You might say you are happy when you are not. You might say you are a cat when you are a dog. This can cause trouble because people might not understand you.
