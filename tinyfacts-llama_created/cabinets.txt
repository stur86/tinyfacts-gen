A box is a thing with many small parts inside. It is usually made of wood or a hard stuff that can not be broken. You can find them in many places where people live. They have doors so you can open them and close them when you are inside. You can put things inside them. Some are very tall and have many doors. Others are short and only have one door.

People use these boxes for many things. You can keep your clothes inside one. You can store your books. You can keep your money in one. Sometimes, a box is a room in a house. You can move a box from one place to another. It is a way to move things from one place to another.

Cup and box are two kinds of these things. A box is often round. A cup is usually round too. People use a box for things that are heavy. A box helps you keep things safe. You put things inside and close the box. This way, the things are not moved around.
