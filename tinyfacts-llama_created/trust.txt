Trust is when you believe a person or a thing is going to do what you need it to do. When you trust someone, you feel safe and you know they will not hurt you.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You give them a small ball and say you will catch it. You trust them to catch the ball. If they drop it, you feel sad. You were afraid they would break the ball. You were not sure if they would follow the game. But because you trusted them, you did not get angry. You just smiled and said, okay, let us try again.

You can trust a person in many ways. You trust a teacher to help you learn new things. You trust a parents to keep you safe. You trust a good food to make you strong. You trust the sun to give you light during the day. You trust the water to keep you clean.

You can also trust something that is not a person. You trust a computer to do your work. You trust a car to drive to your home. You trust the money in your pocket to buy things you need. Trust is like a big feeling in your heart. It helps you feel safe and it makes you want to act with the person or thing.

Sometimes, you need to trust someone to do something that is hard for them. If a friend tells you they are afraid of a thing, you can trust them. You can trust them to be there for you. You can trust them to keep their word. This helps you feel better when things are very hard.
