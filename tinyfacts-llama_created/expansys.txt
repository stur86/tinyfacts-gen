A big word.

This word means to make something very wide or to cover a great area. You can do this with your body or with things that hang from the ground. When you walk, you are moving and making the ground around you go away. This makes the air feel lighter and it is easier to breathe.

Another way it is used is when you talk about a big thing. If you have a huge problem, you might say you are going through a big time. This means the hard work you are doing is very important and it will take a long time.

You can also use it to talk about how much you care about something. If you love a person, you might say you are going to take care of them. This means you are going to help them and be strong for them.

It is a word that helps us talk about how big or wide something is.
