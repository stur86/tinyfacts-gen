To suppose something means you think it might happen. You do not know if it is true, but you think it is possible. You use this word when you are not sure if something will happen.

Imagine you are walking to school. You have not seen the school before. You look around and see a tall building with a bright sign. You wonder if this school is real. You might think, I suppose, that this school is there because people want it to be. This is because you are not sure, but you are just thinking about it.

This word is often used before we do something. We might say, I suppose we will go to the movies tonight. It means we are not sure if we will go, but we think it is a good idea. Or, I suppose I have enough money for the trip.

You can also use it when you are talking to someone who does not know. You might say, Can you suppose how you felt when you were in school? This means you do not know, but you are asking the person to imagine.

The word is very good for when you do not know what will happen. It helps us think about the possible ways things can be.
