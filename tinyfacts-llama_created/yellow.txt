To be in a state is to be in a place or to feel a way. When a thing is bright like the sun or a bright animal, we say it is bright. We also say a person is bright when they are happy. If you are bright, you are not sad.

The color of a thing can tell us how it feels. A bright color is usually very strong. It is like the color of a bright day. It makes us want to play and is often the color of a ball. A bright red is the color of a stop sign or a fire. It tells us to stop.

A thing can also be bright if it has a very strong light inside. This light is what makes the thing look bright. Imagine you are looking at a bright light from the sun. The light from the sun is very bright and makes your eyes feel happy.

We can also be bright when we are very good at what we do. A bright person is someone who does not give up when things are hard. They are very strong and can do many things at once.

When we say a thing is bright, we usually mean that it is very strong in color. But we can also say a thing is bright if it is very strong in other ways. A bright dog is a dog that is very good at finding food. A bright person can be a person who is very good at their job.
