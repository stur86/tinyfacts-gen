A thing with many parts is a thing that is held together by strong lines. It is not just a single thing. It is many pieces joined together.

Imagine you are building a house. Each room is like one part of this thing. If you break one room, the whole house falls down. The rooms are all the same but different, like different colors. They are all held together with strong lines that do not break.

A group of many things like this can be a group. A group of people is a group of many friends who all work together. A group of animals is a group of many animals that live in the same place. A group of things can also be a group of ideas. When many people have the same idea, they can form a group.

In a world with many people, there are many different groups. Some groups are very strong. Some groups are very small. Some groups are very big. Every group is different.
