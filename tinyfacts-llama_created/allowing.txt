Allowing is when you let something happen. You do not stop it. You do not try to stop it. You let it be.

Think about a baby. A baby wants to touch a soft thing. You might say, no, the baby must not touch it. But the baby is very small and the thing is very heavy. To let the baby touch is to let them learn. You let them feel the soft thing. This is a good thing.

We also allow things to happen in our world. If you are playing a game with a friend, you allow them to be there. You let them join in. This is important. It shows that you trust them.

Sometimes, we allow things to happen because they are already there. A tree is standing for many years. It is already there. We do not try to move it. We just watch it grow.

When we allow something, we are giving up some of our power. We are letting go of the need to control everything. This can be a good thing. It can make life easier.
