To be done with something is to have nothing left of it. A house might be built with many strong walls, but if a big fire comes, they can fall down and the house is gone. The people inside can run away and find new homes.

The word also means when you do something bad and then you pay for it. If you lie to a friend, you have broken their trust. They might not speak to you for a long time. If you drink too much and then you get sick, you have done something wrong.

In a fight, if one person is much stronger than the other, the strong one might hit them. After they fight, the person who was hit might be very hurt. They might need to go to the hospital to get better.

Sometimes, when something is made of hard stuff and you hit it, the hard stuff breaks. The wood of a tree can be hard and strong, but if you hit it with a rock, it will break and fall down.
