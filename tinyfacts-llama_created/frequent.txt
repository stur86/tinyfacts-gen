To do something is to act on a thing. You do this every day.

Think about a ball. If you throw the ball, you do it many times. You throw it many times in the day, many times in a week, and many times in a month. You are always doing it.

If you eat many times a day, you are doing something. You do this because you are always thinking about food.

We use this word when we talk about things that happen many times. If you see a friend many times, you say, I see them many times. This means you see them more than once.

It can also mean you do something many times. If a person works many hours, they do it many times. Or if you play a game many times, you are doing it.
