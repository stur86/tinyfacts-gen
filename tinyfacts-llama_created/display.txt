To show something can mean two different things.

First, you can show something in front of many people. You put things up on a wall or a big box so everyone can see them. This is often done for a party or a meeting. You might show a picture, a movie, or a game. People watch and talk about it.

Second, you can show something in a small way. You might show a picture of a thing you saw in a book. You can also show a small thing that can help you. A computer can show you pictures and words. It can also help you find things you want to buy or where to go.

Sometimes, we show things that are hard to understand. You might explain a big idea in a way that is easy to follow. You show how it works so others can see why it is important.

We also show things to remember. You might write down a story or a word so you can remember it later. You can also write a picture so you remember it.

In the day, we often show things on a computer. You can watch moving pictures, play games, or look at pictures of far away places. You can also look at books or watch movies.

In a kitchen, you might show a person how to make something. You show them how to put the right things into a box and how to move the things around.

In a house, you can show a person where things are kept. You show them where the keys are, where the food is, and where the clothes are.

In some places, you show things to people who have lost their way. A teacher might show them a picture of a house. They look at the picture and try to remember where they are. Then they can find their way back home.

So, to show something is to show it to others so they can see it and remember it.
