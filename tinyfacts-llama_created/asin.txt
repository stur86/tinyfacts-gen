The word as in this story is very important. It is the thing that is left behind when something is done. You can hold it or keep it in a box. It does not go away. It stays with you.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You run fast and jump high. When you stop, you give the ball to your friend. But you still have the ball in your hand. This ball is the thing you left behind. It is what you remember from the time you played.

People also use this word to talk about things that happen later. If you say something will happen, you are telling people what you think will happen next. But the thing that happens is not the same as what you said. It is something new that has not happened yet.

Sometimes, you might say something is as if. This is when you think something is true, but you are not sure. You might think, I hope he likes me. Or, I am not sure if it is going to rain today. You are not sure if it is going to happen. This is like holding a ball in your hand. You do not know if it will go away or not.
