Your body needs a small round thing to stay inside. It is inside your stomach and your arms. The thing helps your body use the food you eat. It also helps keep your blood from getting too big.

When you eat food that is high in food, your body uses the food. It takes the food and breaks it down. The food thing then goes into your arms and into your stomach. The body takes the food thing and gives it to the cells inside your arms. The cells need the food thing to grow. The body also gives the food thing to the rest of your body so it can do its job.

If you do not put food in your body, the food thing stays in your arms and in your stomach. This makes your blood go up. Your arms and your stomach feel heavy. This can make you feel very sad. It can also make you feel very tired and cold. This is not good. Your body needs to go back to the food you ate so it can stay inside and do its job.

The body has a power that helps keep this happening. It is like a small round door that you open and close. When the door is open, the food thing goes in. When the door is closed, the food thing stays inside. This power is inside every one of you.
