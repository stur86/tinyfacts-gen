The word comes from the word for a year in a long time. You go through many years with the same changing times. One year has the same sun in the sky and the same days. After a few years, the days go faster and the year starts to feel different.

There are four of these long years, each with many months. One is very warm and the next one is very cold. You can tell the year by the kind of days. When the days are very warm and the sun is high in the sky for much of the day, the year is warm. When the days are very cold and the sun is low in the sky for much of the day, the year is cold.

In the warm year, the days grow longer and the nights grow shorter. In the cold year, the days grow shorter and the nights grow longer. This is how the world changes.

During the year, the world gets ready for the next year. In the warm year, the growing green starts to show. In the cold year, the growing green goes away and the world is barely covered in green again when the warm year starts.

The four long years are often called the years of the sun. The cold year is called the year of the cold and the growing green. The year of the warm is called the year of the warm and the growing green. The year of the growing green is called the year of the cold and the warm.

Because of these four long years, we have a way to mark the year. From one year to the next, we countering the years: the year before the warm year, the year of the cold and the growing green, the year of the warm and the growing green, the year of the cold and the warm, and the year after the warm year, the year of the warm and the growing green, and so on. This way, we know when to start a new year and how to countering the years.

So, the word comes from the idea of going through many years at once, and it is the way we tell time by four long years.
