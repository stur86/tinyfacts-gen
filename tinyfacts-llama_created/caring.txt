To care is to pay attention to someone or something. When you care for someone, you show they are important. You might listen to them, tell them you love them, or help them when they need it. You are kind.

You care about the world too. You might care about animals, the air we breathe, or the water we drink. If you care, you try to keep things clean and safe. You do not want to make the world a bad place.

Caring is not just about people. You can care for things too. If a book is broken, you care about it. You want it to be fixed. You might feel sad or angry. But you also want it to be whole again. You are showing you care about what is broken.

Sometimes, you care about things you do not know. You care about how your dog feels. You care about how the world looks. You care because it makes you happy. Caring is a big word. It means you are always looking out for others and for the world around you.
