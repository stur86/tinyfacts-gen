To like something is to feel a strong feeling about it. You might smile when you see a friend. You might feel happy when you hear music you like. You might even want to play with something you like.

When you like a book, you might read it many times. You like the way the words make you feel. You like the story and the pictures. You might feel like you are learning something new.

If you like a food, you might eat it for lunch. You like the way it feels in your body. You might feel better after eating it. Some people like to watch movies in the evening. They like the way the pictures look and the sound of the music.

To like someone is to feel happy when you are with them. You might laugh and smile around them. You might feel safe when you are near them. When you like someone, you do not feel afraid.

If you do not like something, you might feel angry. You might want to throw things. You might say things you do not want to say. You might feel scared. But if you like something, you feel happy. You feel good inside.
