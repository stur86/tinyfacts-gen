Practically means you do things without thinking. When you do something, you act on what you know. You do not ask why, and you do not think about how you will be seen. You act because you are sure you should.

When you do something, you are making a way. You are changing what is. This is what makes things happen. When you act, you are changing the world.

In a city, people act every day. They walk, they talk, they buy food, and they go home. They follow the way things are. They act because they know what is best for them.

Sometimes, people act in a different way. They do not follow the way things are. They act because they want something different. They try to change the world. This can be good or bad.

The city of Practically is very big. It has many streets, many houses, and many schools. There are many people living there. People go there to work, to buy food, or to play. They act like they are one with the city.

In some parts of the world, people say, the city is practically the whole world. They see the world in the city. They see all the people, all the houses, and all the streets. They see the way everything is, and they act on that.

But sometimes, the word is not used in this way. When people say, the city is practically the whole world, they mean something else. They are talking about how big the city is. They are talking about how much the city covers. They are not talking about how the people see the world.

In this way, people can act in many different ways. They can follow the way things are, or they can act in a different way.
