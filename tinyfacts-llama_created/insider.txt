An inside person is a person who has a lot of power and is not allowed to talk about what they see. They often work in a place that is not for the outside world. They see things that no one else does. They know how things work and how the people in the place are afraid to tell them.

Inside people often have jobs that are very important. They can see how money is made and how the people who run the place are getting money. They know how to make people do things for them. They can also help the people who run the place make more money.

Inside people are not always happy. They might feel like they are being watched all the time. They might have to answer to many people at once. They have to be carefully kind to the people they work with so they do not get in trouble.

Sometimes inside people help the outside world. They tell the right people about the wrong things. They help to stop bad things that are going on. They can also help to make things better for everyone.

Even though inside people are very good at keeping things quiet, some are not as good at keeping things hidden. They might tell bad things about the place or the people in it. They might be the ones who talk to the press or the police. They might tell people that something is not right.

In a way, being an inside person is like being a friend who is always watching. They are always looking over their shoulder, waiting for someone to see them. They are trying to stay safe and keep their own life going.
