A window is a thing that lets light in. You can open it and see out. This is why you have windows in your house. You can look at the street, at the sky, or at the trees. You can also see people on the street.

Sometimes, you use a window to go out. If you are scared, you might use a window to go to a different place. This is called a walk. You hold onto a person so they do not fall while you are going.

You can also use a window to watch the things inside a room. You look at a picture or a book. This is good for people who like to read or watch movies.

Windows are usually round or long and tall. They are made of clear stuff that lets light pass through. You can find windows in houses, cars, and small rooms. Some windows are very big and let lots of light in. Other windows are small and let only a little light in.

Some windows have bars or lines on them. These help keep animals or people inside the room safe. You can close them if you want to keep the light out. You can also close them at night so you do not have to use a light inside the room.

Windows help us see and stay safe. They let light in so we can see the world around us. They let air in so we can breathe. They let us look at the world outside, even when it is dark or cold.
