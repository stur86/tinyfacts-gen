To struggle means you are trying very hard to do something. You might feel a lot of pain or a lot of trouble in your body. You push against things that are hard to move or things that are heavy.

Imagine you want to climb a big rock. You start to push and pull, but you are very tired. Your arms and hands hurt. You try to use your legs, but they are not strong enough. You are struggling because you are working very hard to reach the top.

Sometimes you struggle to stay up while you are walking. You feel like you might fall down. Your body might shake a lot. You try to stand still, but you are not sure if you will be able to stay up.

You can also struggle with your mind. You have a problem that you do not know how to fix. You are worried about what might happen next. You try to think about what to do, but your brain is very busy.

In your life, you might struggle with a job. You work very hard, but you do not feel like you are doing something important. You feel like you are just going through the days without really finding a way to make things better. You might feel sad or angry.

It is okay to struggle. It means you are trying to do something that is hard for you. You are doing it because you want to learn or because you care about the people around you.
