To hold something is to keep it. It is a way to keep things from going away. If you hold a ball, you do not let it go. You keep it in your hand. This helps you play games with the ball. It keeps you safe from other children who might want to play with it.

People also hold things when they remember something important. When you learn a new word, you hold it in your brain. Later, you might see the word and remember what it means. This helps you understand the world.

In a school, teachers often keep notes and papers. They hold these papers so you can see what you wrote. This is important because it helps you learn. If a teacher does not keep the papers, you might not remember what the teacher said.

Sometimes, people hold money. When you have money in your pocket, you hold it. You keep it until you need it for something. This helps you buy things you want or pay for a thing you are waiting for.

In the world, we use things to hold things. We hold things that are heavy so we do not move them. We hold things that are warm so we can keep them safe. We even hold things that are hard to see, like a book or a computer.
