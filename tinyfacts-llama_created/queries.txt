A question is when you ask a person if they know something. You might ask a teacher, a parents child, or a person you know. You are trying to learn more about the world or about the person.

Sometimes a question is very small. You might ask if you have money. You are just looking to see if the answer is yes. If the answer is yes, you are happy.

A big question is one that has many parts. You might ask why you are here. You are looking for the answer to why you are in a place. If the answer is that you are there to learn, you are happy.

When you ask a question, you are showing that you care. You are interested in what the other person is saying. This helps build a good relationship between two people.

You can also ask questions in writing. When you type words on a computer, you can ask a question. You can ask if a word is the right one to use. Or you can ask if a picture is a good one to show.

A person can also ask questions in their head. They might wonder why something is happening. They might ask if something is true. This is how they learn and grow.
