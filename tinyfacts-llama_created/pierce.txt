To piece something together means you take small pieces from different things and put them together to make something new.

Imagine you have a small cut on your hand. You can use a small piece of paper to cover the cut. This is like a piece. It is a small part from something else. You take one piece and put it next to another piece. Now the two pieces are together.

If you have a broken glass and a small piece of wood, the glass can still be a glass. The wood is not the glass. It is still a different thing. To piece them together means you take the wood and put it next to the glass. Now the two are one thing.

You can also piece your memory together. You have many happy memories from your life. You take one memory and you add another one. Now you have two happy memories. This makes the memory bigger.

Sometimes, if you are feeling sad, you might want to piece your heart together. You take your broken pieces of your heart and you put them back together. You feel whole again. This is like a piece of a broken thing.
