To be done with something means to use it up. When you eat food, you use it. If you play a game for a long time, you do not play anymore. When you clean your room, you are done with it. Your room is clean.

When you work, you are doing work. You use your mind and your body to get things done. This is important because it helps you stay safe and makes life easier.

The word can mean different things. It can mean to make something less bad. If you make a bag of food, it is now a bag of food that is easier to eat. It has fewer bad things in it.

It can also mean to change your body. If a person gets a new job, their body changes. They might have more power or be able to run faster. This is what happens when we grow up. Our bodies grow and change as we live more life.

Sometimes, to be done with a person is to be done with them. If a person is very mean, you might want to leave them. You do not want to be around them anymore.

To be done with a thing is also about how it looks. If you paint a picture, it is done. The colors and the way the light hit the paper are all done. The picture is finished. It is no longer a work in the making.

Think of it like a story. When a story is done, the words are gone. No one can hear the story anymore. But the story stays in your mind until you remember it.

In a kitchen, you might say something is done. It is finished. The food is ready to eat. You do not have to do it anymore.
