A long piece of hard stuff is called a power line. It carries power from a big place to houses and cars. The power comes from a place far away and is sent through many of these lines to reach each house. Each house has a small piece that brings the power to the inside.

These long pieces of hard stuff go over the ground and under the ground. They go through the water and under the ground to reach the far away place. When you turn on a light, the power line from your house goes to your small piece and then to your home. Your small piece then sends the power to your light.

Some long pieces are very high up and go through the sky. These are the ones that carry the power from far away places to far away cities. People have these high pieces to stay in places that are very far away.

Power lines also go through trees, houses, and other places. They help make sure the power goes where it is needed. If there is a problem in a line, the power is moved to another line. The people who fix the lines make sure they do not hurt the power or the people.
