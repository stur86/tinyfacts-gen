People who live in the same house or building are called your friends or your family. We also call them our friends because we like them.

If you live in a big city with many houses, you might have many friends. One friend lives next to you. They live in a house with a big window that looks out to the city. You can hear their voices from their room.

When you say hello to your friend, you are saying, I am your friend. This helps you feel safe and happy because you know you are not alone.

Sometimes, your friends are also friends of friends. They know other friends of friends. This is how a big group of friends becomes a very strong group of friends. They help each other and they are there when you need them.

You can also say you are your friend. This means you are happy for them. You might want them to be the best or to have a good day. This is like giving them a big hug because you care about them.
