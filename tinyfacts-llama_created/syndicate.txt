A group of people who do things together is called a group. This can be a group of friends who play games or a group of people who work in a building. Sometimes, a group can have a leader who makes sure everyone follows what they say.

A group that does things for money is called a group that owns things. These groups often buy land or build houses. They also pay money to the people who work for them. This money is used to pay for food, clothes, and other things people need.

When many people work together, they can do things much faster than one person can do it alone. This is why some big groups are important. They can buy things and make money quickly.

Some groups do things for fun. They might go to a park, watch movies, or play games. They might also do things that make people laugh. These groups are often happy to see each other.

A group can also be made up of people who do the same job. If many doctors work in the same place, they are a group. They work together to keep the people in the city safe.
