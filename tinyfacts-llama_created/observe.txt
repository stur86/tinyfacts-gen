To watch something is to see it. You sit in a room with many people. You do not do anything. You just look at them. You see a man who is very old. You see a boy who is very small. You see a person who is very sad. You watch them for many minutes. You do not move. You do not say a word.

You can also watch things that are happening far away. You watch a game. You watch a big fight. You watch a car move on a street. You watch the sun go down. You watch the stars come out.

If you are a person, you can watch a game. You watch a movie. You watch a story on a computer. You watch a thing that goes round. You watch a thing that moves on a big round thing in the sky.

If you watch a dog, you see the dog. You see the way the dog moves. You see the way the dog looks. You see the dog act like a dog. You do not know what the dog is doing until the dog tells you.

Sometimes you watch something that is not happening. You watch a picture. You watch a story in your head. You watch a thing that is not there. You watch a memory. You watch a story that happened a long time ago.

To watch something is to understand it. It is to see how it is. It is to see what is happening. It is to see what is not happening.
