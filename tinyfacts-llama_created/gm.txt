A game is when people play with each other. They do things that are fun. They might play with a ball, or with a piece that moves on a line. They might run around in a park.

Sometimes, people play a game with a computer. They tell the computer what to do. The computer does it all by its own. It is the game. It does not need any help from a person.

You can also play a game with a friend. You sit together and do the game. You look at each other and laugh. You do not speak while you are playing. This helps you stay focused on the game.

Some games are very hard. You must be very good at them. If you play a game for many hours, you get better. You learn how to do things quickly. You can beat the computer or your friends.

A game can also be a game with money. You buy a game with money. You play it for a few days. Then you have to give the money back. This is the end of the game.

There are many kinds of games. Some are for kids. Some are for grown people. Some are for people who like to be alone. There is a game for everyone.
