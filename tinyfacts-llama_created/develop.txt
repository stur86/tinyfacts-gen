To grow is to become bigger, stronger, and more complete. It is about becoming your own person.

When you are a baby, you are small. You are not able to walk or talk. You need to be held and cared for. But as you grow, you learn to walk and you start to use your words. You become more and more like the person you are going to become.

Things grow in many ways. A small green thing can grow into a tall tree. A tiny bit of water can become a big body of water. A small idea can become a great idea that changes many people.

You can also grow in other ways. You can learn new things, change your way of thinking, or become a better friend. As you grow, you learn to be stronger and to do more.

Sometimes, growing is hard. You may feel afraid, sad, or angry. You may want to stop or go back. But most of the time, growing is a good thing. It helps us become who we are and what we can become.

We all grow in different ways. Some grow fast, and some grow slow. Some grow in their own way, and some grow together with others. We all have our own story of growing and becoming.

So, to grow is to change, to become, and to become a better person. It is a big and important thing that happens to us all at some time in our lives.
