An order is a way to give things to another place. When you buy a thing from a store, you are getting it for your own use. You might want to use it later. An order is different. It says, instead, you have to give the thing to someone else. This person will take it to another city, state, or even far away where they are not from. This is how many people work outside of their own city.

Imagine you are a teacher. You make a book for your class. You write on it and give it to a friend. Your friend can then give it to another teacher, who gives it to another, and so on. This keeps going until the book reaches a place where it is needed most. The order is like a long line of people who take things from one place to another.

Orders are used for many things. People use them for work, food, clothes, and even money. You can write an order in a paper or use a computer to do it. Sometimes, you need to give an order when you are moving a big thing from one place to another. This is why you see cars and buses with their orders on the side.
