When something is burning, it is very hot. This happens when tiny bits of stuff that are all around us get very hot. They go up into the air and then fall down.

Fire is like this. When you throw a small thing that is made of wood and stuff into the air, it goes up and falls down. It makes a big noise and makes the air feel hot. This makes the world look bright for a moment.

You can also burn things on your body. When you touch something very hot to your skin, it feels very bad. It makes your face red and your skin feels hot. This can happen if you are in a place with a lot of fire or if you get too close to a very hot thing.

Burning can happen in our home too. When you burn food from food you eat, you are making the air hot. This makes the air feel warm and can make the walls of your home feel hot.

Sometimes, when we talk about burning, we mean something that is very hot and moving. This can be a thing that is in a car or a thing that is flying in the sky. When a car burns, it makes a lot of hot air and makes a loud noise. When a thing flies, it is burning and makes the air around it hot.

We also burn things that are old. When these things get very hot, they start to break down and make new things. This is how we make new things from old things.

Finally, burning can mean losing something important. If you burn a book, the words and pictures are gone. If you burn a house, the people and animals inside are gone.
