A thing that makes a sound is called a music thing. This thing can be made from wood, from other soft things, or from hard things that are hard to hit. When you hit it with a stick or with your hands, it makes a clear sound.

Some people use this thing in places with many people. They stand on the ground and hit the thing with their feet. This makes the air around them move. When the air moves, it makes a sound. The sound goes out to everyone in the place and everyone can hear it. This is why people say the place makes noise.

You can also use this word for animals that make a sound. Some animals hit things with their feet or with their bodies. The sound is like a big wave that goes through the ground. When many animals do this, the sound becomes very loud and can be heard far away.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about the beat of a song. A beat is like a strong beat in your head. When you hear this beat, you know that the music is about to start. It is like a start of a big story.

You can also say this thing is an important part of a group. If many people are playing the same thing, it helps keep everyone moving. It keeps everyone in time so they do not get out of line.
