A writer is a person who makes stories and words. They write things that people read and watch. They use their brain to come up with ideas and put them into words.

Some people write books. They write about what happens in a story. They also write about their own life. They might write about their friends, their family, or their own dreams.

Some people write things that are not books. They might write short things like words on a paper. They might write things that tell a story without words. They might write things that make people laugh, cry, or feel happy.

A writer does not have to be a big person. They can be a small person who writes a lot. Some people even write when they sleep. They write down what they see, feel, and think while they are lying down.

A writer must have a brain. They must have the power to think and to use their words. They must be able to write in a way that makes the reader feel. When a writer writes, they are making a story that will be remembered for a long time.
