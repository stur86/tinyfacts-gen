To break something means to make it fall down. When you break a glass, you make the glass go into many pieces. You can pick up the pieces and put them back together again. But if you break a person, you do not pick up the pieces. You are very sad when you break a thing because it makes you feel bad.

You can also break a word. If you say something is broken, it means the word is not right. If you try to do something that is not safe, you might break your hand. Or if you tell a lie, you break the trust of your friends.

Sometimes, you break a thing by hitting it. If you hit a ball, the ball breaks and it goes into the air. The ball can fall many times and hit the ground before it stops moving.

You can break a plan too. If you have a plan to do something, and you do not follow it, you break the plan. This makes things wrong and can cause you trouble later.

In some cases, you break things that you own. If you do not take care of a thing, it gets old and it falls apartment. This means it is not good anymore and you must throw it away.
