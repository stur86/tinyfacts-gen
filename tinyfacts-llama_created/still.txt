Still is a word that is easy to use. It means something is not moving. You can see the water of a big body of water or the ground after it has been covered with a thick white stuff. The air is also still when there is no wind. Everything is calm.

Imagine you are in a room. If you are moving around, the room feels different. If you stop and look at the wall, the room is still. The wall is there, and you are not moving in the room. This is what it means to be still.

If something is still, there is nothing happening. You do not hear a sound. There are no moving things. It is quiet.

You can also still a fire. If you keep the fire in one place and do not let it get too big, it will not go out. This is like being still. The fire is not moving and it is not getting bigger. It is a calm thing that is working quietly.

Sometimes, we want something to stay in our minds. If you see a picture of a person, you can still remember it. The memory is not moving. It is a thing that stays in your brain.

So, the word still means that something is not moving or happening. It is a quiet thing that is standing still.
