To be missing means you are not where you should be. You are not in the same place as the people you love. You are far away from them.

Imagine you are in a big room with many other people. You like to sit in a chair with a nice look. You feel happy when you see the people you know. But then you look out the window and see a different room. You are not in that room anymore. You are missing it.

You might feel this way if you are in a far city and you want to see your family. You want to see the big tree where you grew up or the house where you grew up. You are missing the place you love.

Sometimes you are missing something from your life. You might be missing a job. You work hard to get money and you feel happy when you do it. But without that job, you are missing something important.

You can also be missing something from your mind. You might have a big idea. You know it is important. But you are not thinking about it because you are busy with other things. You are missing the moment.

Missing is a big word. It means you are not where you should be. It means you are not happy. It means you are alone.
