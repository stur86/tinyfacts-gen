The word for the land that is far away from the water and the other land is called the world land. It is the big area of land that is not a small piece of water. It is where all the different people live and grow food. It is the land that is far from the water and the land that is next to the water.

Imagine you are standing on the water and you look at the ground. The ground is the land that is between you and the water. If you walk a little bit, you see more ground. If you walk a long way, you see more land. The land is the big area where all the people live.

There are also other words for the land. You can use the word home to mean the land where you live. You might feel at home when you are standing on your land. You feel safe and happy. You are the person who lives there.

You can also use the word land when you talk about a big group of people. If many people live together, they are all on the same land. They share the same space and the same food. They work together to make the place a good place to live.
