To get a thing is to have it. If you buy a book, you get it. If you get a job, you get it. But if you do nothing and just sit, you still have it. You have it and you are still the same person.

Sometimes you get something for doing good. You might get a free thing at a store or a movie if you help people. Or you might get money. Money is like a set of numbers that you can use to buy things. When you have money, you can buy food, clothes, and other things you need.

You can also get something for being very good at a game. A person who is very good at a game gets a high number on their paper. This number shows how well they did.

In a store, you can buy things with money. If you have money, you can use it to buy a thing. You pay the store person and they give you the thing. You do not own the thing. The store owns it until you pay for it.

Sometimes, you give money to someone so they can buy something they want. This is a way to help people. You are helping them because you give them money.
