People often do this thing when they want to know what other people think. They ask many of you. You tell them what you really think. They need this kind of help to make good plans or to know if something is a good idea.

Imagine you want to start a new kind of school. You ask a lot of people how they would like to be in a new kind of school. Maybe they like a school that is very quiet. Maybe they like a school with a lot of games. They tell you these things so that you can build a school that they will like. This helps you make the school better.

In this way, people who give their answers are called the people who are asked. They are called the people who respond because they are the ones who answer the question.
