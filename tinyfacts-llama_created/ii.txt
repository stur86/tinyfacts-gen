The word you are looking for is a number. When you counters something, you start from one thing and move to the next one. Numbers are very important because they help us know how many things are there.

One is the first number. It is one. It is the number before two. Two is two. It is the number after one. Three is three. It is the number after two. And so on.

Numbers go up very high. There are many numbers that are more than ten. Some numbers are so big that you can not even write them down. But we use them to tell stories. We use them to show how many things we have. We use them to help us understand the world around us.

One number is one hundred. Another number is one hundred and one. A third number is one hundred and two. And so on. These numbers help us make sense of the world.
