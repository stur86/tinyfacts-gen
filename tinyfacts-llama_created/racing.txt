To race means to move quickly in a way where you are moving faster than other people. This is usually done on a road, a track, or a water way. You start at one end and go to the other end. As you go, you try not to stop. You might use a small car or a big horse. Some people use their bodies to beat others.

There are different kinds of races. One is for people. In this kind, many people start and run as fast as they can. They try to finish before the others do. It is a way to show who is the fastest.

Another kind of race is for animals. A person might put a horse or a dog in a race. They give them food and water before the race starts. The person who reaches the end first is the one who did well. It is a way to see how fast something is.

Sometimes, people race each other in a way that makes them feel strong. They might be racing on a hard floor or a soft floor. They might go very fast or very slow. This can help them feel better after they run.

To race is to be the first. You are the one who moves first.
