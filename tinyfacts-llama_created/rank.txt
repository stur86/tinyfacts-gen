To have a high or low place in a group can be very important. If you are the best at a game, you are in the top place. If you are the second best, you are in the second place. If you are the third best, you are in the third place.

People also use this word for things that are made by many people. If many people build a house, the house is in a high place. If many people write books, the books are in a high place in the world of writing. This means the house or the books are the best.

But sometimes, a group can be made of people who are not the best. If there are many people who are not good at a game, they are not in the top place. They are also not in the second or third place. In this case, the group is not in a high place.
