To go much higher than what you are used to is to be a better or a stronger person. You might feel like you are going up a high wall or a tall tree. You might look at the top and wonder, how did you get there? Sometimes you feel like you are going to fall.

When you say a thing is a big one, you mean it is very strong. A car is a very big one. It can carry many people and a lot of things. A big dog is a very strong dog. It can run very fast and is very good at catching things.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If someone is a very good teacher, they are a big one. If a person is very good at their job, they are a big one. If a person is very nice and helps many people, they are a big one.

When you use this word, you are saying that the thing or person you are talking about is much better than what you started with. It is better because it is bigger, stronger, or more nice.
