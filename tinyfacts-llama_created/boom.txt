A big sound that makes things fall down is called a loud noise. When a big group of people or animals shout, they make a loud sound. You might hear it when a car drives very fast or when a fire starts.

Sometimes, a big group of people shout at the same time. This makes the sound go around the room many times. It is like a great wave that goes through all the people.

You can also use this word for a time when many things happen at once. Imagine you are waiting for a friend to come home. You are sitting and waiting for a long time. Then, the door opens and your friend comes in. The sound of the door opening is like a big loud noise.

A loud noise can make people feel scared. If a big loud sound is heard while you are sleeping, you might feel afraid. But if the loud sound is a good sound, like the sound of a big dog, it can be fun.

You can also use this word for things that move quickly. A big car can make a loud noise as it moves down the road. A fast animal can make a loud sound when it runs.
