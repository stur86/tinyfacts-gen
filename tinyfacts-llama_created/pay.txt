To pay is to give money to someone. You might pay your mother if you want to buy food or clothes for her. You could pay your teacher if you do well on a school paper. You might also pay to go to a movie. When you pay, you are giving the person or the place the money.

You pay your brother when you want to give him money. You might put the money in his hand. You pay your friend when you want to buy something from him. This means you are giving him money so he can buy the thing he wants.

You can also pay for things like a phone call. If you talk to your friend and they speak with you, you have paid for that call. You paid for the time you used to talk.

Sometimes, you pay for things that hurt you. If you cut your hand, you might pay money to a doctor. You pay your mom if you get a cold because she wants to help you feel better.
