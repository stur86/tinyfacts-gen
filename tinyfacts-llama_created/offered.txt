To offer something means to give it to someone. When you offer something, you are letting someone know that you want them to have it. You are giving up something you have for their good.

Imagine you have a book. You do not want to give it away. But you want to share it with someone who will like it more. You take the book and walk over to the person. You say, You can have it now. You are offering the book to them.

You can offer to help someone too. If you know a person who is hurt, you could say, I am here for you. I want to help you feel better. You are offering your help.

Sometimes you offer something for fun. You might offer to play a game with someone or to go for a walk. You are giving them a chance to have fun with you.

Being offered is also about being asked. If someone asks you if you want to go to a party, you have to say yes. You are giving your answer. You are saying yes to the person who asked you.

So, to offer is to give, share, or let someone know you care. It is about showing you are there for them.
