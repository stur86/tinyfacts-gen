To call something is to use a name that is already in your brain. You might call a friend, a car, a dog, or even a thing that you see. When you say the name, the thing or the person knows that you are talking about it.

Sometimes, you call something that is not in the same room. You might say, I call my bed. This means you are thinking about your bed. You are not really saying the name, but you are thinking about the thing.

You can also call a place. If you go to a store, you are going there. You are calling the place. You are using a name to tell others where you are going.

If you call a person, you are telling them to stop doing something. If you call a friend, you are telling them to be quiet so you can talk. You are using a name to make the person understand.

In the world of animals, you can also call a thing. If you see a cat, you might call it. This helps you find it if you have many cats. It is like a word that you use to find what you are looking for.
