To sleep is to rest your body and mind. When you sleep, your body does not do any work. It does not move. You are not thinking about anything. You are very quiet.

You usually sleep at night. During the day, you are busy doing things. You go to work, you study, you play games. But when the day is over, you need to rest. You go to a place with a soft floor or a bed with a cover. You lie down and close your eyes. Your body starts to feel good. You feel your body relax. Your heart beats slower. Your mind becomes quiet.

When you sleep, your brain does not work. It does not think about anything. You do not remember any dreams. You do not remember what happened before you fell asleep. You just rest.

Some people sleep a lot in one night. They sleep for many hours. Others sleep only a few hours. Some people sleep a lot during the day. They go to sleep at different times and wake up many times. Some people sleep a lot at night and wake up early in the morning. This is called a morning person.

You can also sleep while you are moving. This is called a light sleep. You are not really asleep. You are just moving around. Your body is still. You might feel like you are falling. But you do not fall down.

Some animals sleep standing up. They can be in the middle of the street. They are safe. They can sleep while keeping their eyes open. This is very good for them.
