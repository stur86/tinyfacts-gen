To make something hard is to use a lot of power. You might try to push a heavy box across the floor. It is hard because it is heavy and you need to use your arms and legs to move it.

When you do this, you feel a bit of pain in your hands and arms. Your face is also red because you are breathing hard. You are not easy to move because you are using so much power.

Sometimes, you might try to do something you do not want to do. You might feel a bit scared or angry. You are very hard on yourself. You are not happy and you feel sad.

When you are in a room with many people, you might feel like you are the only one. You feel like you are the one who has to do all the work. This can make you feel tired and alone.

To make something easy is to use less power. You are not as hard to move. You feel less pain in your body. You are happy and you feel calm. You do not have to push yourself as much.

Think about a time when you wanted to play a game but your body was very tired. You did not want to move your legs and arms. You wanted to rest and do nothing. In that moment, the game was easy for you.
