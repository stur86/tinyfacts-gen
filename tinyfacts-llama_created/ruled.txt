To be the one who is in control is to be a leader. A person can be the head of a group or an office. They make sure everything goes well and everyone follows them.

When you are the one in control, you have to make good things happen. You might decide what game to play, what food to eat, or what to do. This helps everyone feel safe and happy.

Sometimes, people are the ones who make the world a better place. They work for the people they care about. They fix the roads so cars can move easily, help those who are sick, or stop bad things that can hurt people.

People can also be the ones who are in control of a small group of friends. They help keep everyone safe and make sure everyone has fun. This is an important job because it helps build strong relationships.

It is also possible to be the one who is not in control. In this case, you might not know what to do. You might feel afraid or angry. It is hard to stay calm when you are the one who does not know what to do.

When you are the one who is in control, you can feel a big feeling in your heart. You are the one who has power and makes things happen.
