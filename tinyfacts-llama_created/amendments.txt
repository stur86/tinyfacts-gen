An act is a set of things that tell how a group of people make a place. The act might tell how people live in a city, how they act when they talk to each other, or how they do things when they are angry.

An act can change in time. Maybe the first act says that people can walk in a way that is safe. Later, another act changes the way people must walk. Or maybe the first act allowed people to have a drink before dinner. The second act says that it is now not allowed.

Sometimes, a group of people want to change one thing. They might want to change how they deal with money, how they decide who is in power, or how they share things. They write down these changes in a new act.

There is also a word for changing the writing on a book. You can write a story, add a new word, or change how the story goes. This is like an act for people who read books.

In the world of people, an act is also something that people do. You might act when you say you are sorry for hurting someone. You might act when you try to help a friend. An act is how we show how we feel inside.
