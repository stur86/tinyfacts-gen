To have a heart that is full means you feel very good inside. Your heart is the part of you that is very important. It makes you feel happy, sad, angry, and scared.

When you die, your body stops working. Your brain stops thinking and your body stops moving. But your heart keeps going. It keeps beating, even after your body is gone. This is why people say you do not really die when you are gone.

Inside your heart, there are three things that make it work. One thing is love. Another thing is fear. And a third thing is a part of your brain that makes you feel love.

People believe that when you are very sad, your heart is sad too. You might feel like you want to cry. Your heart is like a soft place inside you. When you are happy, your heart feels light. You might feel warm inside.

There is also a story about the night sky. When the sun goes down, the sky turns red. It looks like the sky is on fire. Some people think that is what happens when your heart is full. Your heart feels like it is going up into the sky.

Sometimes, people say that your heart is like a door. When you are happy, your heart is open. When you are sad, your heart is closed. This is like a door that is open or closed. When you are open, people can see inside you. When you are closed, you are safe inside.

Your heart is a very important thing. It is what makes you, you. It is what makes you feel things. It is what makes you love and hate. Without a heart, you would not be the person you are.
