A user is someone who uses a computer, phone, or another kind of box to do things. They might look at a picture, read a word, or send a word to another person. They might also play games, watch a movie, or talk to a friend.

A person who is a user is called a user. This is different from someone who makes things. A person who makes things can build a chair or a car. A person who is a user is more about what they do with what is already there.

Think about a store. When you go into a store, you are a user. You are not the store. You are the person who is looking at the things in the store. You are the one who decides what you want to buy.
