To hold something means you keep it in your hands. You might hold a cup, a book, or a small animal. When you hold something, you do not let it go. You keep it near you.

Sometimes, you hold a thing that you need to do later. You might hold a piece of paper to remember what you did. You hold a phone so you can call a friend. You hold a picture of your family so you can look at it later.

When you hold a friend, you show they are important to you. If you are very sad, you might hold a stuffed animal. You hold the thing that makes you feel better.

You can also hold a feeling. If you are angry, you might hold the feeling inside your chest. You try not to let it out so you do not hurt anyone.

If you hold a thing that is not a person, you might hold it with your hands. You might hold a small thing that makes music. You might hold a ball for a game. You hold the thing because it is fun to play with.

To hold something is to keep it close. It helps you remember or feel a strong feeling.
