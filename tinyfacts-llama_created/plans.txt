To plan means you think about what you want to do. You make a listened or a piece of paper with your ideas. You think about how you can get there. You make a plan so you can follow it and know what to do when you get there.

When you plan, you also think about the things you need. You think about how to buy food, how to get to work, or how to get home at night. You look at the days before and the days after to see if you can do everything.

A plan is not just about one thing. It is about many things. It is about how you will live your life. It is about how you will spend your time, how you will work, and how you will be with the people you love.

You can plan for many things. You can plan a trip to a new city. You can plan a big day at work. You can plan a game with your friends. You can plan a party with your family.

A plan is important because it helps you feel safe. When you know what you are going to do, you feel ready. You do not feel afraid. You can go to your house at night and know that tomorrow will be a good day.

You can also plan for things that happen later. You might plan for a big party with your friends. You plan how you will spend the money you get. You plan how you will act if there is a fight. This helps you feel safe and makes you feel happy.
