To make something is to change it so that you can see where everything is.

Imagine you are in a big city. You want to know where the police are, where the schools are, where the stores are, and where the roads go. You look at a big paper. The paper shows you where everything is. You can see the police cars, the schools, the stores, and the roads.

You tell the people who make the paper how to show you where everything is. They look at your words and make the paper. Now you have a way to see where everything is.

A way to make is the same as making. You take something that is already there and you change it so it looks new. You tell the thing what to do and it does it. You did not change the thing at all. You just changed how you see it.

You can also make a way so you can go from one place to another. You can walk, drive, or fly. You use a paper, a computer, or a phone to tell where to go. Then you go there.

Making is important. It helps us know where things are and how to move around. It helps us build houses and roads. It helps us learn about the world.
