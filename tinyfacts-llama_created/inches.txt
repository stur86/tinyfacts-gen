An hour is a big number of seconds. One hour is one hundred twenty five minutes. Each minute has ten seconds. So, one hour has one hundred twenty five times ten seconds.

Think of it like this: if you have a big bag full of things. You can put one bag in the bag, then another bag, and another bag. The bags are all different. One bag is one hundred twenty five times smaller than the big bag.

A foot is another way to say the same thing. You can use one foot to put one foot under a table, or one foot in front of you. It is a number that is easy to remember.

In a room, if you walk around the room, you are moving. You are not standing still. You are moving. This is why we say things move.
