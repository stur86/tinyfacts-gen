To be a red head is to have hair that is very bright red. This happens because the red color comes from the tiny things in your hair. When you cut your hair, you can see these tiny things. If you have a lot of red things in your hair, the hair will look bright red.

Some people have hair that is a deep red color. Other people have hair that is more light red. You can also have hair that is a funny color that is not just one color. If you have hair that is more red than other hair, you are a red head. This is why people call you a red head if you are the one with the bright red hair.
