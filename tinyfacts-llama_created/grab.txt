To grab something means to take it from someone else without letting them have it. You might feel a sudden move to get the thing before a friend or a family person does.

Imagine you are playing a game with a ball. You want to catch it. If you grab the ball, you are the one who holds it. If you let go and the other person catches it, they are the ones who got it. This is what happens when you grab something.

Sometimes, you grab something that is hard to reach. If you are trying to get a book from a high place, you might grab the edge of the table and hold on. This helps you to stay safe until you can walk down to get the book.

In a different way, you can grab a word or a feeling. If you are very angry, you might grab your face and cover your eyes. This shows that you are very mad. You are not letting the feeling go. You are holding it like a thing.

When you grab something, you do not let go easily. You must hold on for a moment. This is important because it helps you keep what you want.
