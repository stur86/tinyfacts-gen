To make a thing is to start a new work. When you want to eat at a table, you are making a thing. You are telling the people you are going to be there. They know you are coming and they have a seat for you.

A long time ago, people were very busy. They did not have enough time. One man said, let us make a way for people to sit. He told the people to write down their names. They put their names on the paper. When they are done, they close the paper. Now everyone knows who is coming and who is not.

You can make a thing for many reasons. You can make one for your family. You can make one for a work. You can make one for a school. Sometimes you make a thing for a big group of people. This is important when you are going to a big place, like a city.

You can also make a thing for a person. If you have a friend, you can tell them you like them. When you like a person, you want them to know that too. This is a way of showing love.

When you say you need a thing, people hear it. They look at you. They ask, what is it? They want to know if you are ready to do the work. If you say, I need a seat at a table, the people say, okay, let us make you a seat.

A room is a thing with a door. Inside, you can sit. You can eat. You can talk. You can do many things. You do not need to make a room. It is always there.
