An old computer thing. It was very good at doing many things at once. It could show pictures, talk to other computers, and write words. It had a long box with lots of keys. The keys were very important. You had to press the right keys in the right order. This was the key to the computer.

The person who made this thing was a very good man. He was very good at making computers. He gave the computer a name. This name was a play on words. He said, the computer does many things for you. It does the hard work. It makes it easy for you to use.

This computer was very important in the  late   ties and the early days of the   computer age. It was like a person. It could do many things at once. It could work on many things at the same time. It could learn and get better. It was like a little brain.

Today, computers like this are very different. They are much smaller. They do many things at once. They do not need to press keys like this one. They do many things in a very short time. But the word still means the same thing. It is about a computer that does many things very well.
