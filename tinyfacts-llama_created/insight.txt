To see something with your eyes is easy. You look at a picture or a person. You see what they look like and what they are doing. This is a kind of understanding.

But to see something new is different. You do not see it at first. You do not know what it is. But as you look, you start to see it. You see how it fits into your mind and your life. You see how it is a part of you.

Sometimes you have an idea. You do not know how it will work. You do not know if it is right or wrong. But when you look at it, you see it. You see it as a part of what you know.

This is what we call an eye open. When you are open to a thing, you are open to seeing it. Your mind is open. You are ready to understand.

Sometimes you see things that are not there. You see a face in a crowd. You see a word in a book. You see a sound that is not really there. This is like seeing a new light. It is a new way of looking at things.

Seeing is important. It helps us know what is going on around us. It helps us understand how people are feeling. It helps us make new ideas. It helps us change our lives.

But seeing is not always easy. Sometimes we have to look at things we do not want to see. Sometimes we have to look at things that are hurt or sad. It is hard to see the bad when we want to stay happy.

Sometimes we see things that are not really there. This can happen when we are afraid. When we are afraid, our eyes are closed. We do not look at the world because we are afraid of it. But even when we do not look, our mind is still open. We are still seeing.
