An empty space between lines is called an empty space. It is a place where there is no word or picture. When you fill in a space with your words, you have filled in the empty space. If there is no empty space, the line is full.

You can also use this word for other things. If a person does something that is not allowed, they might get into trouble. An empty space can mean that a job is not yet filled. If a car is in an empty space, it means it is not moving and it is safe.
