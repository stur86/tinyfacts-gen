A car you sit in to go places for people is called a car. This car is often big with many seats for many people. It has a long box that goes fast on the ground. You use the car to go from one city to another city. You can also use it to go to the store, to the school, or to the home.

The car you ride in is a car too. You sit on it and push it. You can go very fast on the road. You can also go in the dark. In the dark, the car makes a soft sound so that people can hear you coming. The soft sound helps to keep people safe.

Sometimes, a car you sit in is a car for a person. This car goes to the place where a doctor works. The car is very nice. It has many rooms. The doctor has a room with many beds. The car also has a room with many computers. The computer can show the right way to the place. The car is very good at helping people.

The word here means many cars are put together. If you have many cars, you can go many places at once. You can go to the city, to the school, and to the home. The cars can also go to the places where people live. This is why many people use cars.
