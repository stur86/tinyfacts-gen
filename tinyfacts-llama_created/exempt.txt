To be an easy thing is to have your work done by someone else. This is when you do not have to do it. 

If you are an easy thing, you do not have to use your brain. You can just do the work. You do not have to think about it. You can just follow the steps. This is a good thing when you are tired. You can do more things and then you can rest.

If you are not an easy thing, you have to do the work by yourself. You have to use your brain and your body. You have to plan and do the work. You have to be strong. This is a good thing when you are working on something important. You are important because you are doing the work.

People also say you are an easy thing if you can not do something. You are not strong. You need help from others. This is a bad thing. You should try to be strong. You can do things by yourself.
