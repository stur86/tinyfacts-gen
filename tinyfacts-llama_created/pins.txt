Pains are things that hurt. They are very bad. When you cut yourself or feel something wrong, a pain comes. It feels like a big, hard thing inside your body. You try to move away from it. You might want to sit down or lie down.

You can have a pain because of a cold. You might have a cold and feel a lot of pain. Or you can have a pain because of a fight. A fight can hurt your hands and arms. You might feel a pain in your chest or in your head.

Pains can be very strong or very small. Some people have a lot of pain all the time. Other people have only a little pain at a time.
