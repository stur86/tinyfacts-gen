To cut something means to make a line across it. You can use this line to break a big group into smaller groups. Each group is in one way. You can also use this line to cut a thing so it is smaller.

Imagine you have a box of food. You put all the food into one group and all the food that is not food into another group. After you cut the line, you have two groups. You can go to the group with the food and the group with the drink. You do not have to look at each one.

You can also cut a thing in many ways. One way is to cut it in lines, like the lines we talked about. Another way is to cut it into many small pieces. This makes it bigger and more different.

When we talk about things, we often use this word. We cut a story into many parts. We cut a book into small pieces so we can read it one piece at a time. We cut a picture into many small parts so we can look at each one.

Sometimes, we cut people. A doctor might cut a person to make them better. This is when we use a thing with a hard part to make the person feel less bad. We also cut a person so they can see the world better. This can happen if a person has a bad eye.
