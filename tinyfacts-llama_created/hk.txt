A horse kick is when a horse makes a long jump into the air. This happens when the horse gets ready to run. The horse starts to move quickly and then jumps up. The front part of the horse jumps high and then the back part of the horse goes up too. The horse falls down after the kick, but it comes back up quickly so the horse lands on its feet.

When a person kicks a ball, they do the same thing. They push the ball with their feet. This makes the ball move across the floor. The person can kick the ball to their friends, to their team, or to a person on the other team. Sometimes, a kick helps a person get away from someone who is trying to catch them.
