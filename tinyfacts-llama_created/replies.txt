To reply means you send a word to someone. When you reply, you are saying something to them. This word is important because it shows you care about what they said or did.

Imagine you are talking to a friend. You want to tell them about something that happened. You might say, I went to a movie. This is a reply. You are showing them what you did.

You can also reply when you are angry. If you are mad, you might send a word. This word is different from what you are saying. The word is not about the same thing. You are still talking, but now you are also telling them how you feel.

Sometimes you reply when you agree with someone. You might say, Yes, that is right. This shows that you trust what they said.

When you reply, you are building a story. You are adding to what someone has already said. You help them understand what is happening.

You can also reply when you are asking a question. If you ask, Can you help me? You are showing that you need help. Your reply is a way to say, I can help.
