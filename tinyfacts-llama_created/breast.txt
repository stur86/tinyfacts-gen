A baby is very small at first. The baby is in the mother. When the baby is about a year old, the baby starts to grow. The baby starts to have a soft white thing under its arms. This thing is the baby. As the baby grows, the soft white thing goes under the skin and goes into the chest.

Inside the chest, there is a long thing. The long thing is made of soft, white stuff that grows from the body. This long thing does not have a hard cover. It is very soft. The long thing is very strong. It keeps the baby inside.

The woman has a thing that hangs under her arms. The thing is round and soft. It has a small opening at the top. The woman uses the thing to give food and air to her baby. She does this by putting her baby in the thing and closing the opening.

A man has a thing too. The thing is under his arms and over his chest. This thing is very strong. The man uses the thing to carry his baby. The man also uses the thing to keep warm when it is cold.

All the things in the chest are very important. The baby uses them to grow. The woman and the man use them to keep their baby safe and to give their baby food and air.
