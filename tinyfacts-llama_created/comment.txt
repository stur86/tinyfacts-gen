To comment something means you think about it while someone is talking. You might ask a question, say something, or just think about what they are saying.

If you see a movie, you might think about the story. You might wonder why someone did something or how they felt when they were in a place. If you are talking with a friend about a movie, you are both commenting on it. You might say, you like how the person acts, or you think the music is cool.

Sometimes people use this word for writing too. They write a story or an answer to a question. They might say, I have a problem, so I will try to fix it. If you write a comment, you are adding your own ideas to what is already written.

When you comment, you do not stop what is happening. You keep thinking about what is being said. This helps you remember the story later. It also shows you are interested in what is going on.
