To make something hot is to give it power. This power is from deep inside the ground. The deep inside the ground has a very high number of tiny bits of rock. When we cut into these bits of rock, many of these tiny bits of rock get hot and turn into big pieces of rock that can make fire.

In the old days, people used to use these hot pieces of rock to make things warm. They would put them in a big box and the fire in the box would make the room warm. This was very good for people who lived in cold places and did not have power from the sun.

Now we use power from these hot pieces of rock to make cars, power lines, and other things that go around the world. When we make power from these hot pieces of rock, we give them the power they need to move and to make things happen.

Sometimes, these hot pieces of rock are also used to make white stuff that we use to make paper. The white stuff is very hard and white. We make it from the hot pieces of rock by putting them in a big box and letting the power from the rock make the white stuff grow.
