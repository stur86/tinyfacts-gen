To have a piece of space where no one can follow you is very important. When you do not share your life with others, you have your own time. You can be alone and you feel safe.

Some people like having this space because it keeps them from being hurt. If you do not know what is going on around you, you are not afraid. You feel you can do what you want, and you are not worried about what others think.

People also have this space to think. When you are alone, you can think about what happened or what you want to do tomorrow. You do not have to answer to anyone else. You can just sit and think.

But having a piece of space does not mean you are safe all the time. You can still get hurt by what others say or do. You can still get in trouble if you act in a way that is not good for you or for others.

Everyone needs this space. We all need to be able to be alone sometimes. It is a part of being human.
