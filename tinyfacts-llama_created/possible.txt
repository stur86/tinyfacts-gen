To be possible means that something is not yet happening. You can do things that are not yet done. You can learn, you can grow, you can change.

Think about a tree. A tree is not yet a big tree. It is not yet strong. But one day, it will grow and become a tall tree. That is because it has the right things inside it.

When something is possible, you can do it. You can try to do it. You can think about it. You can feel it. You can see it happen in your mind.

Sometimes, a thing is not possible. It is very hard. It might not be safe. It might not be right. But you can still try to do it. You can learn how to do it.

Being possible is important in life. It helps us know what is possible and what is not. It helps us decide what to do and what to wait for.

We also use this word in games. If you can do something, you are possible. If you can not do something, you are not possible.
