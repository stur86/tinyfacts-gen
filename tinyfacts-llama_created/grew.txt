When something starts small and gets bigger, it has grown. This happens with people, animals, and things.

Imagine you are growing a small tree. You start with a tiny piece of wood. Over time, the wood gets bigger and bigger. The tree becomes taller and stronger. You have grown.

Animals also grow. A baby animal is small and soft. As it grows, its skin becomes harder and its body gets bigger. A cat is a small animal that grows from a tiny tiny thing. It gets bigger and its further, and then you see a cat. You have grown.

A thing that grows is something that gets bigger. It does not change the same way a person does. A ball grows when you throw it. A small rock grows when it sits on the ground for a long time.

You can also grow in other ways. You might grow a new hair or a new word in your mind. This happens when you learn new things and your brain changes.

Sometimes, something that started small can get very big. This can happen with ideas or with money. When you start with a small idea, you can grow it into a very big one. This happens because you keep adding more and more people who believe in you.
