To hold something is to keep it in a place where it does not move. You can hold a ball, a book, or a small animal. A big animal that lives in the woods might live in a small box or a small room. This room has bars so the animal can not get out.

Sometimes, a person holds a person. This can happen when a family or a group of friends want to keep someone safe. They might keep the person in their home or in a room with many beds. This way, the person is not alone and can be safe.

You can also hold something that is hard. When you hold a rock, you keep it in your hand. You do not let it fall. This helps your hand stay strong and safe.

In some places, you hold money. When you have money, you can buy food, a bed, or other things you need. You keep the money in a box or a bag. This way, you do not have to wait for someone to give it to you.
