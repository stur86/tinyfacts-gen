To go through something is to move from one place to another. You start at one end and walk until you reach the other end. This is a part of life.

Think of a small thing that grows. At first, it is a tiny thing. It grows and becomes bigger. It gets bigger and bigger until it is as big as you can see. Then it stops growing and stays the same.

You also go through times in your life. When you are a child, you learn to walk. When you are a young person, you learn to use a computer. When you are a grown person, you work and pay money. When you get older, you might have children of your own.

Every living thing goes through these steps. An animal might go through many of them. A person might go through many of them, and then they stop. When a person dies, they have gone through all the steps.

You can also use this word for a long time. If you are in a school, you go through many years. After many years, you get a job. You work for a long time and learn new things. You get a high job and a good place to live. This is a big part of life.

It can also mean to change. Imagine you are a person who always wears the same clothes. One day, you see a new friend. You like this friend. You want to be like them. You start to wear new clothes. You change who you are. You have gone through a change.
