A moment is a very short time. It is a little bit of time that happens only once. This time happens in one second. A second is a very small number that is so big that there are more seconds in one second than there are people in the world.

When we say a moment is happening, we mean a very short time. It is like a tiny, tiny piece of time. Think of a single second. You can not see it. You can not hear it. You can not remember it. It is only there.

A moment can be very important. It is the time when you are in a place with your loved ones. It is the time when you are with someone you love. It is the time when you feel happy. You might be laughing with a friend. You might be smiling at a picture. A moment can be a very big thing. It can be the moment when you see a person you love for the first time.

Sometimes, we say, something is happening now. This means a moment is happening. You are right. The moment is right now.

Another way to say this is. If you are going to a place, every moment is important. You are getting ready. You are putting on your clothes. You are eating a breakfast. You are walking to the door. This is a moment. You are moving. You are going somewhere.

We also use this word in our lives. When you want to do something, you might wait for a moment. You might say, I am going to the store. You are waiting. You are thinking. You are ready. You are ready to buy food or clothes.

A moment can be very small. It is the time when you touch a book. It is the time when you hear a song. It is the time when you see a picture. This moment is very important. It stays with you.
