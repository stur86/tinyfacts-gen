To break something down means to take it apartment by part. The word can also mean something you eat.

In the body, there are parts inside you. These parts are very small. You eat food that gives you power. When you eat, the food breaks down. The parts then break down into tiny pieces. The body uses these tiny pieces for many things.

Your skin is made of parts that cover you. These parts keep you warm. They help keep you dry. Your hair is made of parts that grow from your head. These parts grow and then come out. When you pull your hair, you are breaking it down.

When you have food, you are breaking down the food into smaller pieces. Your body then uses these pieces to build strong parts. This keeps you moving and growing.

Also, you can break down a story. You take a story and you cut it into smaller parts. You might break the story into parts that show what happened first. You might show what happened before and after. You might even break the story into parts that show the good and the bad. This helps you understand the story more.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about bodies. They might say, you are breaking down. This means you are becoming smaller. Your body is breaking down into smaller parts. This can happen when you are very cold. Your body uses the cold to keep warm. This is like breaking down parts of a box to make it smaller.
