To keep track of how much money you have and how much you spend is a very important job. People who do this work are called an actor.

Imagine you have a big box of food. You buy a lot of food, and then you eat it all. You also buy a small box of food, and now it is empty. If you do not keep track, you will not know how much food you have left. You would be in trouble.

An actor does the same thing. They make a story about people and money. They write down how much money a person spent on food, clothes, and other things. They also write down how much money they kept. This helps everyone understand what happened.

The word comes from a word that means to counters. An actor is a person who keeps counters. They use numbers to show how much money is gone and how much money is left. They also use numbers to show how much money they keep.

In a big building, the people who do this job are often the ones who make the money go in. They look at the numbers and tell the people who pay what they should pay. This helps everyone know how the money is being used.

Sometimes, people use the word actor for people who do not work with numbers. They might tell stories or act for money. They do not keep track of their money like the ones who work with numbers.
