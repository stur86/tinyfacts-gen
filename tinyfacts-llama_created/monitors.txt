A computer usually has one or two small boxes in it. These boxes are like eyes. They watch what is happening inside the computer. If something is wrong, the boxes tell the computer to do something different.

Imagine you are in a room. You want to know if someone has taken a book. You ask a friend if they saw the person. If the friend says yes, you know the book is gone. You do not need to look inside the room. You only need to ask the friend.

The boxes inside the computer work in a different way. They watch the computer and tell it what to do. If the computer makes a bad thing happen, the boxes will tell the computer to stop. This keeps everything running safe and good.

Some boxes can also watch other things. If you have a box that can watch the street, it might show you what cars are doing. You can see which cars are coming, which cars are going, and where they are going. This can help you decide how to act.
