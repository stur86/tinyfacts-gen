A word is a thing that helps us tell what is happening. It is a word you use to say something or to mean something else.

A word is made of small parts that come together. When you put these parts right, they form a word. You can make a word from one word, two words, many words, or even from many small words put together.

The word we use today is the same as the one you used. The word you used today is the same as the word you used last week. When two people speak the same word, they are talking about the same thing. But if you use a different word, you are talking about a different thing.

You can use this word to talk about what is new. If something is new, it is not old. It is something that happened for the first time. Think about a new game. You have never played it before. You do not know how to do it.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are very easy to do. If you do something quickly, it is new. If you can do something in less than a few seconds, it is new. It means you are learning something fast.

Sometimes you might say, I am new to this place. This means you have never lived in this city before. You are starting to learn about it. You are not used to the way of life here.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are very bad. If something is new and bad, it is something that will hurt you. It is a word that tells you to watch out.
