But when you say something is different, you mean it is not the same. If you have a book with the word happy on the cover, but then you find out the book is about a sad story, then the book is different. It is not the same. This is what we mean by different.

Also, when you say a word is different, you mean it is not the same as any other word. If you say a word is different, it means it is not like any other word. This word is very important in our lives. It helps us to know what is new and what is old, what is easy and what is hard.

Sometimes, we say a word is different in a way that is not good. We might say, a dog is different from a cat. But this is not true. Dogs are dogs. Cats are cats. They are the same kind of animal. This is not what we mean by different.
