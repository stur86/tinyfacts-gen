To talk is to use your voice to tell others what you think. When you talk, you are speaking words that mean you want to share your ideas or your story.

You talk to people, animals, or even things that do not have a voice. You might talk to a friend, a family person, or a person you just met. You are saying how you feel and what you want. You can talk about things that make you happy, things that make you sad, or things that make you angry.

Talking is an important way to show we are not alone. When you talk, you are telling others that you care about what is happening. Your voice can help people understand each other better. It can help you find out why someone is angry or why they want something.

Sometimes, talking can be hard. You might say something you do not mean or you might say something that makes others sad. When you talk, you are taking a step toward understanding and being kind.
