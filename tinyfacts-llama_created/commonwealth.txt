A world where many people share the same land and the same way of life. This is a place where a group of people, often with many different kinds of people, work together to make sure everyone is safe and everyone has the same chance to live well.

Imagine a big house with many rooms. Each room has a different job. One room helps with food. Another room helps with money. Another room helps with the people inside the house. All these rooms work together so everyone can live in the house.

In a world with many of these kinds of houses, one room becomes the top one. It decides what is best for everyone. This top room makes sure everyone follows the same way of life. It also makes sure everyone pays their part. The top room is like the head of a very big family.

Some of these world houses are very small and only have a few people. They live in a small room. Other ones are very big and have many rooms. They live in many rooms and have many people.

The world houses often have their own ways of doing things. They might use money to buy things or they might use food. They might have their own places where people go to rest or where people go to see a movie. Everyone in the world gets to see what the top room decides.

There are many of these world houses. You might live in one house and you live in another house at the same time. Some houses are in far away places, and some houses are right next to each other.

Even if you are in a different house, you are still part of the world. You follow the same way of life. You share the same money. You help each other when you need it. You are all part of the same world.

This is a big idea. It means we are all one world, but we are also many worlds. We have our own ways, but we all live under the same top one.
