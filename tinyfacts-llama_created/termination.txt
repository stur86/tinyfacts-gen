To end something is to stop it. This can happen when you finish a book, or when you play a game. When you say something is over, the time is done.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You all start to play and have fun. Then, one person gets very angry and wants to stop. They might say, I am going to end this game now. The game stops because one person decided to stop it.

You can also end something that is not a person. Imagine a tree that is very old. Over time, the tree gets smaller and smaller. After a long time, the tree falls down to the ground. This is an end. The tree is no longer standing.

Sometimes, we end things that are hard to do. If you have a big problem and you do not know how to fix it, you might stop trying. You can say, I give up. Or, you might change your plan and try something new.

To end something also means to stop doing something. If you want to stop eating too much food, you might stop eating at night. You stop eating when the sun goes down. This is an end of eating. You are not eating anymore.

To end something can also mean to stop a fight or a problem. If a group of people is very angry, they might end the fight. They decide to stop talking and to go away. This is an end. The fight is over.

In some cases, we end a job. When you finish your job for the day, the work is done. You can close the door and stop working. You have reached the end of your day.
