To be given something means you get it from someone else. You do not have to do anything. It is already there. You can only tell others about it if you want to share it.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friend. You do not have to play. You just get to watch. If you have a ball, your friend has it. If you have a friend, you have a friend. If you have a job, you have a job. You do not have to do any of it.

Sometimes you get something that is not what you want. You might get a book you do not like. You do not have to read it. You can just keep it and put it away. But if you are given something you love, you feel happy.

You can also be given something that is hard to do. If a person is very good at a game, others might think they are very good. But if you are given a job that is very hard, you feel afraid. You do not know if you can do it. You need to try.

When you are given something, you must decide what to do with it. If you do not like it, you can keep it or give it away. If you like it, you can keep it or do something with it. This is how we live with the things we get from others.
