A noise is a sound that is not a word. When you are in a quiet room, you can hear the sound of a cat or a dog. But if you move to a busy street, you hear many different sounds at once. The sound of cars moving, the sound of people talking, the sound of a car stopping, and the sound of a dog belling can all be noise.

Noise can come from many places. A loud voice from a phone is noise. A car driving very fast is noise. A group of people laughing is noise. A baby crying can be noise. Even a wind in the trees can make a noise.

Sometimes, noise can be a problem. If a noise is too loud, it can hurt your ears. If a noise is not quiet enough, it can wake you up. If a noise is very loud, it can be very hard to ignore.

We use the word noise to talk about all these sounds. We say that a room is quiet if it is very quiet and there are no noises. We say that a car is loud if it is very loud. We say that a dog is a noise if it is making a sound.

We also use the word noise to talk about things that do not need to be quiet. A fire can be a noise. A moving thing can be a noise. When you are playing a game with a ball, the sound of the ball moving through the air can be a noise.

So, a noise is a sound that is not a word, and it can come from many places and be a problem or a fun thing to hear.
