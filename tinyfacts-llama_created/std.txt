To have a thing is to have it. It is a word you use to talk about what you have.

You might have a book. You read it and it makes you feel happy. You might have a dog that follows you around. You feel safe with it.

A small, hard thing that people use is also called a thing. It is the thing you hold to write or draw. If you have a thing, you can write your name, draw a picture, or write a story.

When you have a thing, you are not alone. You are with many other people who have things too. You can share your thing with them if you want to.

Sometimes, the word can also mean that something is not right. If you have a thing and it is broken, you need to fix it. Or if you have a thing and it is making you sick, you need to go to a doctor.

In a way, you can never have something completely on your own. Things are always shared with others. This is why we need to be kind to each other.
