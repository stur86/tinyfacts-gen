To begin something is to start it. You start with nothing. You might feel a little nervous. You look at the door. You say, to the door, you are going out. You do not say where you are going. You just walk out.

You can also use this word when you are very first. It is like the start of a story. You do not know what will happen. You do not know if the story will be good. You just start the story and see what happens next.

If you start a game, you are the first one to play. If you start to eat food, you start to eat. You do not wait for someone else. You start your own food.

Sometimes, when you are very first, you say, I am the new person. This means you are the first person who does something. You are not the first person who does it, but you are the first person who is doing it now.
