An animal that has been made from other things is called an animal. An animal can grow hair, or it can not have hair. It can also have a hard skin, or it can have soft skin that is very soft to the touch.

When you make an animal, you take some of the things from an animal and change them. You take the hair and make it grow, or you take the skin and make it hard. You add new parts to make the animal strong. You also change the way the animal moves. It might run very fast, or it might fly through the air.

Animals need to eat. They need to drink water. They need air to breathe. They need a home to live in. A made animal might have a home that looks like a house or a tree. It might have a bed to sleep in.

Animals can have babies. The babies grow inside the mother animal. When the baby is ready, the mother animal gives it to the world to live in. Some animals can only have one baby at a time. Other animals can have many babies.

Animals are very important. They help us and the world around us. They give us food to eat. They help keep the world clean. They are living things that give us life and make the world a better place.
