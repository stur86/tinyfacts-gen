To control something means you are in control of it. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are the one who decides what moves to make. You tell the other person which way to go or which thing to hit.

When you control something, you do not let it act on its own. You are the one who decides what happens next. You might say, I am going to run. You are not just standing there. You are moving.

This happens with people too. A person might control their body. If they have a problem with a leg, they can tell it to not move. They can control how their leg feels. They are in control of their own body.

It also happens with computers. A computer can do many things. It can play games, write stories, or show pictures. But it is not a living thing. It does not have a brain or a heart. It works because of the things it follows. A person might write a word or a picture. They are the one who tells the computer what to do.

Sometimes, people say a thing is controlled by another person. This means the other person is in control. They are the one who decides what happens to you. This can be true in many ways. A person might control a city. The person who runs the city makes all the big plans. Or a person might control a group of people. They decide who gets to say what and who does what.

To control something is to have power over it. It means you are the one who decides what happens next.
