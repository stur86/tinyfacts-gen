To have a word is to understand it. You know something because you hear it. When you see a word, you know it too. You can not live without knowing what you are doing.

Words tell us what is there. They tell us what is old, what is new, what is big, what is small. They help us understand the world. You can not say you know everything with your head, but with your heart, you know many things.

A word can be one word or many words put together. A word is like a small piece of paper. You can write it down and keep it. You can give it to someone. You can not give a picture to someone. You can only give them a word.

Sometimes a word has a different meaning. It can mean a thing that is very small. It can mean a thing that is very big. It can mean a thing that is very hard to do. It can mean a thing that is very fun to play with.

You can also have a word for a feeling. If you are sad, you say, I am sad. If you are happy, you say, I am happy. This word is inside you. It is not something you see.

Words are used in many ways. You use them to talk to each other. You use them to write a story. You use them to tell a story. You use them to laugh. You use them to cry. They help you stay in the same time and the same place.

Sometimes you get a word from a friend. They say, You are a great person. This word is like a big hug. It makes you feel safe and happy. Other times, you get a word from a book. A book is a big box of words. When you read a book, you are taking a word and a story. You are using it to learn.

Words are like a door to another world. They open the door to many other ideas and ideas about people. They help us understand each other and the world.
