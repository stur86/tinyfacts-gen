A high place is the top of a big land. It is higher than other places. A high place can be very cold because the air is thick. People often go to a high place to see a beautiful sight. They might go to a high place to run or to jump from a high place to feel strong.

You can go up a long road to reach a high place. Or you can walk a long way to reach a high place. Sometimes, you have to carry heavy things up to reach a high place. This is hard because the ground is hard and the air is not very good to breathe.

Some high places are very dark. The ground is so high that the sun is not able to reach it. At these high places, the air is very cold and it can be very hard to breathe. But there are also high places with green fields and trees. These high places are very beautiful and are often lived in by people who love to walk and run.

When you are at the top of a high place, you can see many far away places. You can see the wide open space and the many small land pieces. You can also see the sun, the stars, or the night sky. This is a very great feeling. It is like you are on top of the world.
