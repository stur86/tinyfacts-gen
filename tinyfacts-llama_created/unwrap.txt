To wrap something means to cover it with many small pieces of paper or other stuff.

Imagine you have a box with a small door. You want to keep the door closed. You can wrap the box with many small pieces of paper or with a long, thick thing. This way, the door is kept inside the box, and the box looks nice and full.

If you have food that you want to eat later, you might put it in a box. Then you put a cover on top of the box. The cover is like a big piece of paper. It keeps the food inside the box and makes it look pretty.

Sometimes you wrap things when you are giving them as a present. You put many small pieces of paper around the thing. This makes the thing look nice and the person who gave it to you looks happy.

You can also use this word for a person. If a man is very good at his job, he might feel like he has to wrap his body in many soft pieces of clothes. This is because his body is very warm and he wants to keep it looking nice for the people he meets.

So, to wrap something means to cover it with many small pieces of paper or other stuff. It helps keep things inside and makes them look nice.
