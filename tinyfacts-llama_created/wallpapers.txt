To set something on your wall means to put it there so it looks good. Most of these things are pictures, but you can also find pictures in books or on computers. You can also find words and pictures together to make a picture of a house or a city.

When you set a picture on your wall, you are giving it a home. You make sure it is straight and not crossing. If the wall is very high, you might need a long piece of wood to hold it up.

People use these things for a few reasons. They can make a room look nice. They can help people feel happy. Some people even like to have a big picture of their family or their favorite animal on their wall. This can make them feel more at home.

Sometimes, people put these things on walls that have no pictures. This is okay too. It can add to the room. The wall looks different and it can be fun to see.

You can also set things on your wall for fun. You might put a few small things, like tiny balls, on a table. You put them there and watch them go around the room. It is like a game for you.
