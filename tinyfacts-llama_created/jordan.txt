To join something means to join people or things together. When you join a team, you become a part of the team. You do not have to be the one who plays the game. Everyone has to work together and be ready when the game starts.

Imagine you are in a room with your friends. If one person says, we are going to play a game, then you can join in. You do not have to be the one who says it. You just follow what the person says.

A long time ago, people used this word to join a group of animals that were fighting. They were fighting for food and land. When they joined together, they became stronger. The animals that were not joining together could not fight as well.

In some places, people use this word to join a group of people who are fighting against a bad person or a bad group. When you join a group, you are part of something bigger than yourself. You are helping to fight for what is right.

Sometimes, people join a school or a city to do something new. They join the group because they want to learn more or because they want to make a change. This is a way to start a new beginning.

To join something can also mean to change your own way of thinking. Imagine you see a picture and you think it is a big animal. Then, you see a close look at the picture and you realize it is a small animal. You have changed your mind. You are now joining the small animal.

In short, to join something is to join people or things together. It is about becoming part of a group that works together to do a thing.
