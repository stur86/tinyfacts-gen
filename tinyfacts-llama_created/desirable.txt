To be a thing you want is to feel you are the right one. It is a feeling when you think about something and you want it. You might want a new car or a pretty book. You might want to go to a far place so you can see the world. You might even want to be with a person you love.

Being a thing you want is not the same as being real. Real things are things that are already there. You can see them or touch them. You can hear them. You can smell them. Real things do not change. They stay the same from the start to the end.

Desks are things you use. You sit on them and write. You write papers. You write stories. You write things you want to say. A desk is a piece of wood with hard lines around it. It is where you do your work.

People want things for different reasons. Some want to get a new pair of shoes. Some want to go to a movie. Some want to play a game. Some want to be alone. Some want to be with others. Each person has their own way of wanting things.

You can also want to do something. You might want to run or dance. You might want to singling with another person. You might want to learn a new thing. You might want to be the best at something. When you want something, you feel a pull inside. Your heart wants it.

Sometimes, wanting something is hard. You might feel sad if you are not happy. But if you really want something, you try to find a way to get it. You look at pictures. You read books. You talk to people who have it. You do things until it is in front of you. Then you feel happy.
