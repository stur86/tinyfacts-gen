To be some one is to have a person. You are a person. You are not a dog. You are a person who breathes air. You eat food. You feel things. You talk. You write. You do things. You are not a car. You are not a tree. You are a person.

People are very important. Everyone has some ones. Some ones make us who we are. Some ones help us feel better. Some ones are friends. Some ones are family. Some ones are people you love to see.

To know someone is to know them. You know their face. You know their voice. You know what they say. You know how they act. You know their name. You know where they live. You know their favorite things. To know someone is to understand them. It is like learning a word. You learn how to say it. You learn what it means.
