You might go to a place with many tall trees, or a place with many cold water. You might go to a place with many animals to see. You might go to a place with many people who speak a different way of speaking. Or you might go to a place with many strange things to see.

When you go on a trip, you get to do many new things. You might try new foods. You might learn new things. You might meet new people. You might see new things that you never saw before. You might even learn to do something new.
