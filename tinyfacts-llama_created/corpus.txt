A group of people can be a group of friends, a group of people who work together, or a group of things that are all the same.

Think about a group of animals. Some animals are big, some are small, some are brown, some are white, and some are black. All these animals can be in one group because they are all of the same kind. You can tell this group by one word. If all the animals have the same word in their mouths, it is the same group.

A group of people can also be a group of things that are all the same. If you have a group of books, all of them are books. You can look at one and you know that they are all books. This group is all the same because everything is the same.

In a group of things, people or animals, you can have a leader. The leader is like the top person. They tell the others what to do. This leader is important because they help everyone follow the same way.

Groups are also important for us. When many people work together, they can do more than one thing at once. This is why people work in groups in their homes, schools, and in the city. They do many jobs at once, like cleaning the streets, fixing cars, and making food.

Sometimes, groups can cause problems. If one person wants to do something different, the group might not like it. The person who wants to do it might feel alone. This can cause anger and hurt. In this case, the people need to talk and find a way to work together again.

Finally, groups can change. Sometimes, people get tired of being part of the same group. They might want to leave and start a new group. When this happens, new people join and the old group has to start over. This can be a good thing because new ideas and people can bring change.

In short, a group is a group of people who are all the same or all the same. They can be a group of friends, a group of things, or a group of things and people.
