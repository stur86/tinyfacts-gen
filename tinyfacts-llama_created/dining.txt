To eat is to take food from a table. You sit down with others. You look at the food and at the people you are with. Then you pick a thing to eat. You take a bite. Your body uses the food to give you power.

When you eat, you use your mouth and your teeth. Your tongue breaks down the food into tiny pieces. Your body uses those pieces to make you strong and to keep you going.

Some people eat a full dinner before they go to bed. They eat food that is good for them. They drink a glass of water. This helps them feel full and happy.

Other people eat smaller bits of food. They eat quickly. They do not stay at the table for very long. They go to a different room and then they go to their own bed.

Some people do not eat. They are very quiet. They do not talk much. They do not eat with other people. They eat when they are alone.

You can eat many different kinds of food. You can eat food from a box. You can eat food from a bag. You can eat food from a table. You can eat food from the ground. You can eat food from a tree. You can eat food that grows on a big green thing. You can eat food from a small animal.
