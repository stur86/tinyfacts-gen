To appear means you are there. You are in a place. You are not gone. You are a part of what is happening.

Think about a story. There is a person who is afraid. They hide behind a wall. The person is afraid because of what they think. But then something happens. The person sees the person they are afraid of. The person is afraid again. This is the first time the person saw the person. They are now in two places at once.

You can also appear in pictures. A person looks at the picture. They see the person in the picture. This is their way of seeing the world. We use this word to talk about what happens in front of us.

Sometimes we say a thing appears to us. This means the thing is not really there. We see the thing because of our eyes. We do not see it because it is really there.

The word also means you can become something. A person can become a leader. A person can become a writer. This happens when you do something that changes the way you feel or what you do. You start to believe in yourself and want to show the world what you are like.

Finally, to appear can mean you are noticed. You are seen by people. You are heard. You are seen when you are not trying to hide. You are there because someone sees you.

So, when something appears, it is in front of us. It is in our eyes and our ears. It is in our minds and our hearts.
