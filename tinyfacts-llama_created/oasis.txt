An over is a place where water is kept in a big box. It is like a home for animals and people. In the hot sun, the water gets very small. When the sun is down and the water is cold, the over fills up again and becomes big again.

This over is very important for many animals. The animals that live in the hot place go there in the middle of the day to get a drink. After they drink, they go back to their homes at night. This way they stay safe and do not get very tired.

People also use over boxes to keep water for their towns. When the water from the big over runs out, people use the over boxes to keep water for their families. These boxes can hold a lot of water. When you fill one box and then you fill another, the water becomes stronger.

There are also small places with water in them. These are called small over. They are often found near the big over. The water in these places is not as big as the big over, but it is still important. People go there to stay cool and to eat food.

Sometimes, a small over is made by people. They cut the ground and put the water inside. Then they cover the ground with a hard white top. This top keeps the water inside and makes the place feel like a big over. People use these small over to stay in the hot place during the day.

In the story of the man who lived in the city, there was a small over. The small over was very strong because it was made by people. The people lived there for many years and never had to go look for water. They were very happy.

The word over comes from the word for water. It is a word that you use to talk about things that are about water.
