To take a picture is to put something in a box so it looks like it happened. You hold a small thing, like a thing that moves, and you press it many times. The light from the world goes into the box and makes the picture.

You can use this box to tell stories. You can put a story in the box and then look at it later. You can see the world in a different way than you do every day. You can see the people, the trees, and the ground. You can see when someone laughed or when they were sad.

Some people take pictures of things that are far away. They stand far away and wait until the thing they want to see is in front of them. Then, they press the box and the picture comes out. This makes a new picture of what happened.

You can also take pictures of things that are moving. You can hold the box close and press the pictures as fast as you can. This makes a new picture every second. This is how you make pictures of running people or cars.

Many people take pictures of their lives. They might take pictures of their children, their friends, or their home. They might take pictures of the things they love, like a book or a car. They use these pictures to remember what happened.

Some people take pictures of things that are not in the world. They might take pictures of a building that is not there. They might take pictures of a room that is not a room. They use these pictures to imagine other places or other times.
