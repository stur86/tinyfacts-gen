To decided something means you have made a plan or a way to act.

Imagine you want to go to a big city. You look at a listened to talk about where to go. One person says you should go to the city that is far away. Another person says you should go to the city that is near your home. You listen to both and then you decide. You decide which city to go to.

You might decide to go to the city that is near your home because it is easier to get there. Or you might decide to go to the city that is far away because you want to see new things. This is how you make a plan and decide what to do.

You can also decide something that is not yet clear. You might decide to try a new game at a school. You feel like you want to be the best, but you are not sure if you can do it. Then you decide to try the game.

Sometimes, you decide that something is wrong. If a friend tells you that they love you, but you feel like they do not, you might decide that you do not like them anymore. This is how you see if something is right or wrong.

Deciding is important because it helps you feel sure about what you want to do. It helps you make a plan and follow it.
