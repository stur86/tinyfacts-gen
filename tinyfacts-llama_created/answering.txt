To answer something is to give an answer. An answer is a word or a piece of writing that you give when someone asks you a question.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You want to know if your friend is happy. You ask your friend if they are happy. Your friend says yes. You ask again if they are really happy. Your friend says again that they are happy.

When you answer the question, you are telling the person who asked that the answer is true. If you do not answer, the person might feel confused or angry.

Sometimes, you might answer a question while you are talking about something else. If you are talking about a book and a friend asks you about it, you might answer about the book, but you are still listening to the friend. You are answering while you are also talking.

You can also answer questions when you are trying to find out more. If someone asks you if you like a game, you might answer that you do like it. This shows that you are listening and trying to understand what the other person is asking.

The word answer is important. It helps us understand when someone is trying to tell us something or when they are trying to show us something.
