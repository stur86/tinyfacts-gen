To fine someone means you are making them better. You might be the person who works at the store. You have to follow all the small things that make sure everyone buys the right things. If you do a job well, you get a new job. You are making the job easier for the next person.

Imagine you have a small dog. If the dog does not act well, you can fine him. You give him a small number of money. This number makes him feel bad. He knows he did something wrong. This helps the dog become a better dog.

You can also fine someone if they do something they should not do. They might break a glass or talk bad to a friend. You tell them they must pay for the glass and stop talking like that. This is to make them understand they did something wrong.

Sometimes, to fine someone means to stop a bad thing. If a person is driving very fast and does not follow the road, you can fine them. This makes them stop and think about how they act.

A fine is like a small number of money that you give to someone. It is a way to make them better and to help keep everyone safe.
