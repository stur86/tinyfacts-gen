The number five is a big number that goes around in a line. When you see a line of five things, it is a group of five. You can not have more than five of these things, and you can not have less than five. This is why five is an important number.

In a room with five doors, you can open one door at a time. You can open the first door, then the second, then the third, and so on. This is how we use the word when we talk about a group of things.

There are also five days in a week. You have one day on the first day, another day on the second day, another day on the third day, another day on the four day, and then the day after that is the last day. This helps us keep track of time.

Sometimes we say five in a word. This means you are five years older than someone else. You are also five years younger than yourself. You are five hundred years old. You are five times as old as someone else. You are five times more than someone else. This helps us talk about how old things are.

The number five is also used when we counters. When you say you have five pieces of food, you have five things. You have five small pieces of food. You have five small parts of food. This helps us keep track of how many we have.

In the morning, five is the number of stars in the sky. In the evening, five is the number of hours until bed. This helps us stay on time.
