To add something together is when you put many things together to make one thing. This is a way to get more power from what you already have.

Imagine you have a small box. You can fill it with small rocks. If you add many more rocks, the box gets bigger. You have more power now. You can do more things with the box.

This can happen with people too. If many people do the same thing, it gets easier. Everyone does the same job. This helps a big group work together.

Sometimes you might add things to make them stronger. You might put a strong piece of wood in a box with many papers. The box becomes much bigger and more heavy. This helps the papers stay in place.

You can also add things to make them look better. You might put many colors together to make a bright picture. Each color is important. When all the colors join, the picture becomes much more beautiful.
