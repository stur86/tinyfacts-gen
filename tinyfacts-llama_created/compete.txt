To fight and be the best at something can be a very strong feeling. When you want to do something, you usually want to be the first one to do it. You want to be the one who does it right before the rest.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You all want to see who can hit a ball the farthest. You all start running and trying to hit the ball. The person who does the best is the one who hits the ball the farthest. You are all fighting for this.

The word can also mean to try and see if you can do something. If you want to be a good driver, you might want to try to drive a car. If you do not know how, you will try to learn. You are doing this so you can do it well.

In some words, to fight means to use your body. You might have a fight with a person who is trying to take your thing. You use your hands and feet to try and stop them. This is a fight because you are using your power to try and keep what you want.

Sometimes, to fight means to be angry. If you are angry at a friend, you might shout at them. You are fighting your own face because of how you feel. You are using your voice and your body to tell them that you are very mad.

To fight is not always easy. It takes a lot of hard work. You must be able to stay calm when you are angry. You must be ready to use your body if you feel like you need to. This is why you need to know how to fight.
