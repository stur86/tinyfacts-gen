An act is when you do something. It is how you act. If you do a kind thing, it is an act of love. If you tell the truth, it is an act of being good.

An act can be good or bad. If you do something that is good for everyone, it is an act of love. If you do something that hurts many people, it is an act of hate. An act is how you act in the world. It is how you make the world feel.
