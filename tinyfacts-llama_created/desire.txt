To have a strong feeling is to want something. This feeling is inside you. It is what you want right now. It does not tell you what to do, but it makes you act. You might want a food you love. You might want to play with a ball. You might want to go to a place you have never been.

Sometimes you have a strong feeling about a person. You love them because they are kind to you. You feel happy when they smile. You might not say anything, but you are happy inside.

You can also have a strong feeling about a thing. You might want to buy a new car. You might want to move to a new town. You feel excited and maybe a little scared. This is what you want right now.

When you have a strong feeling, your body tells you to do something. Your heart might beat fast. Your face might get hot. You might look at the thing you want. This is because your body is saying, you want it!

Sometimes, you have a strong feeling for someone you do not know. You might feel a big rush of love. You might feel like you can not breathe. This is a very strong feeling.

You can also have a strong feeling for something that is not a person. You might want a color you love. You might want a song you like. You feel happy when you see it. You feel like you are in the right place.

To have a strong feeling is important. It shows you care about what happens. It helps you decide what to do. It can make you act quickly. It is a way you show you are interested in the world around you.
