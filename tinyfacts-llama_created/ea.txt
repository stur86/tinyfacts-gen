To eat something is to take it and put it into your body. When you eat, you use your mouth and your teeth to break things into small pieces. Then you use your tongue to move the pieces around your mouth. You also use your teeth to bite the pieces.

You eat food from a box, a bag, or a table. You drink water. You can eat many different kinds of food. Some foods are sweet. Some foods are hard and need to be broken before you can eat them. Some foods make your body grow strong.

When you eat, you give your body power. Your body uses this power to move, to think, and to keep you going. You need to eat so you have enough power to stay safe and to do your work.

If you do not eat, you get very tired. Your body says, I am not ready. You might feel like you want to cry. Your face might get red. You might want to throw things. Your body is trying to tell you to eat.
