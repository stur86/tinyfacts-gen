To leave out means to get ridden of something. You can do this with words, with things, with people, or with time.

Imagine you have a box full of books. You want to keep your favorite book. You can pick that book and put it away. You are not leaving out the rest of the books.

You can also get ridden of something by not taking it anymore. If you have a big bag of food and you eat all of it, then you have left out the food.

Sometimes, people use this word when they are talking about people. If someone is the best person in a group, then they are not leaving out the others. They are the one who stays.

You can also say that you are leaving out someone. This means you are not talking to them anymore. You are not saying hello or good day.

It is like when you are playing a game with a friend. You might play with them for a long time. After a while, you start to think, you start to think they are not there. You are leaving out the friend. You are not talking to them anymore.
