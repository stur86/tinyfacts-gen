To read a book is to open a big box with many lines inside. You look at the words that tell stories. You learn about a person, a place, or a thing. You might feel happy, sad, or angry while you read.

A book is not a thing you can hold in your hand. You need to sit in a quiet room or a park with a chair and a table. You sit with your eyes and your face. You read the words on the paper.

You can read books about many different things. Some books tell stories about people who went on long trips. Some books tell stories about animals. Some books tell stories about big, strong things that live in the ground.

Many people read books before they go to school. They read books about their favorite animals, their friends, or their family. Some people read books after they finish school. They want to learn more about the world or about their own lives.

You can read books alone or with a friend. You can read books before you go to bed or after you finish a long day. Reading books is one way to learn and to imagine.
