To put something in the wrong order is to change the way it is. Imagine you have a box with many small things inside. You put the biggest thing first. Now the things are in the wrong order. You need to move them back to their right place.

If you want to go back to the first place, you must start again from the beginning. You do not go forward, you go back. This is what we call a round. If you go around a place many times, you are doing the same thing over and over again.

In the world of numbers, when you write a number, you start from the one that is far away. You start with the number that has many many many ones. When you read the number from left to right, the ones are the right ones. If you read it from right to left, the ones are the wrong ones. This is what we call a number that is written in the wrong order.

We also use this word when we talk about time. When it is morning, the day is starting. At first, the day is in the wrong order. But as the day goes on, the order changes again. We are always putting things in the right order.

In the story of a man, he had a bag with many things. At first, he had many small things. Later, he had many big things. But he lost the small things. He put them in a bag with the big things. Now the bag is in the wrong order. To put the things back together, he must take the big things and put them back in the bag with the small things.
