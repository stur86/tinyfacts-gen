To be an edging person is to work with words. They take what is already there and make it better. They look at every word and every line. They change some words to make them sound right. They change some words to make them sound like they should be.

They work with a book. The book has many stories. The edging person reads the stories. They look at what the writer said. They make sure the stories are the right way. They do this so that the book is easy to understand.

They also work with people. They talk to them. They hear their voices. They try to make them sound nice. They help people write things they want to say.

In some places, you are an edging person if you work with pictures. You look at pictures. You change some pictures so they are better. You make sure they tell the story well. You might also make new pictures from things you saw.

Being an edging person is important. It helps people find what they are looking for. It helps people say things they want to say. It helps people share their ideas with others.
