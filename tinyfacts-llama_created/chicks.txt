Chairs are things you sit on when you want to be quiet or feel safe. They are usually long and have four sides. One side is wide and the other three are short. People use chairs in many different ways. You can use one to read a book. You can also use one to watch a movie. Sometimes you sit on a chair while you wait for the food to come.

Chairs are made from wood, but you can also make them from other hard stuff. Some chairs have soft seats. You can sit on them and feel the soft floor under your bottom. You can also sit on them and watch the world go by from the window.

There are also chairs for people who are very small. These chairs are very small and have very little room. You must sit very close to the floor. They are good for young children or very tall people who want to see what is going on when they walk down a street.

Sometimes you use a chair to talk. You can sit across from someone and talk about your ideas. You can also sit next to a friend and talk about your day. When you talk, people often use chairs because it feels more like a conversation than a talking room.
