To easily means you can do something quickly and with little trouble. It is when things happen without a lot of work or worry.

Imagine you have a book with many stories. If the book is heavy to carry, it is hard to read. But if the book is small and light, you can carry it easily. You do not have to feel much pain. You can just pick it up and go.

This can happen for many things. You might easily walk to a place that is far away. You might easily pick a ball from a high place in the sky. You are not trying very hard.

You can also easily do something in your mind. You might easily remember a word you just learned. You might easily think of a word that is important to you. This is when your brain works quickly without you even thinking about it.

To easily means you do not have to try or work very hard. It is when you are able to do something because it is very easy for you.
