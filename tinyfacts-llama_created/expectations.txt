To expect something means you are waiting for it to happen. When you say you expect something, you are telling others that you are looking forward to it.

Imagine you have a friend who goes to a new city every year. You know they will come back home after a few days. You are always looking forward to their story. You are waiting for them.

You do not know what they will do. You do not know how they will feel. But you are sure they will come back. That is what you are expecting.

You also expect your phone to ring. You know your phone is going to ring soon. You are waiting for the sound. When the ring comes, you are happy. You feel glad that your phone is working.

Sometimes, we expect bad things to happen. We are waiting for a fight or a bad day. We are afraid. This is a heavy feeling inside.

Sometimes, we expect good things. We are waiting for a good day. We are happy. We look forward to what will happen next. This is a good feeling inside.

In life, we always expect something to happen. Even when we do not know what it is, we still expect it to come. This is how we live.
