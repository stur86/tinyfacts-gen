A warning is a way to tell people when something is about to happen. It is like a voice that says, here comes this. You need to be ready.

You can give a warning to a person who is going to do something that might be bad. If a driver is going very fast, a warning might say, slow down. This helps keep people safe.

You can also give a warning to a place. If a building is about to fall down, a warning might say, stay away. This helps people get out quickly.

A warning can be about a person, a thing, or a place. It is a way to tell you what is coming so you can act before it happens.
