Often means you do it many times. It is used when you want to say a thing happened a lot.

Think about your day. You went to bed. You woke up. You did your work. You played with your friends. You ate breakfast. You went to school. This happened a lot. You do this many times each week.

You can also use it to talk about things you see. If you see many houses, you say they are often houses. If you see many dogs, you say they are often dogs. It is a way to say that something is very easy to see.

Sometimes, you might say something that is not true. You might say you love a person. But really, you do not. You often say this because it is easy to say. It is like a joke.

We often do things that make us feel happy. We often laugh. We often smile. We often do things that make us feel like we are part of a group.
