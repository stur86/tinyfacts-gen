Never means something that never happens. It tells you that something will never be true. If you say something is never, you are telling the world it will never be.

Imagine you have a friend who loves to eat a sweet thing. If you say your friend will never eat that sweet thing, you mean it will never happen. Your friend might be very sad because they were looking forward to eating it.

Never is also used for things that are always the same. If you say something is never going to happen, you mean it will always be the same way. You can never do something better than that one time.

It is also used when you say you will never do something. If you promise to never go to a place, you are saying it will never happen. You are making a strong promise to stay away from that place.

Never is a word you use when you want to keep a promise or when you want to tell the world that something will never happen. It is a word that helps you stay safe and keep your plans straight.
