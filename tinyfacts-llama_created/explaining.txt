To explain means you tell others what is happening so they can understand. It helps them see how things work.

Imagine you have a big box of books. You want to tell someone about the books inside. You look at each one and tell them what it is. You tell them what the cover looks like, what the words say, and what the pictures are like. This is explaining.

You can explain a thing by saying it, by doing it, or by writing about it. You might explain a word, a song, a story, or a big idea. When you explain, you help the other person see why the thing is important.

Sometimes, explaining is hard because you have to think of the right words. You might want to say a few words, but you also want to show them how it works. You might draw a picture, use a computer, or show them a movie. This helps the person understand and remember the idea.

Explaining is important because it helps people learn. When you explain, you show them how to do something new. You help them see the world in a different way. This is why you want to explain things to your family, friends, or even to a child.

You can explain things in many ways. You might explain something big in one short story, or you might explain it in many short answers. Sometimes, you explain something by asking questions. This helps you learn and remember too.

So, explaining is about telling others what is happening. It helps us see how things work, understand new ideas, and learn from each other.
