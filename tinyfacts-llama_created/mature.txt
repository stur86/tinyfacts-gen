To be a thing that is ready is a big word. It means a person or an animal is not young anymore. They are not little. They are not young. They are grown up.

Imagine you have a small baby. The baby is very small. You hold it. You do things for it. After a while, the baby is not a baby anymore. It is a young person. It is still learning. It is not sure of anything. It is not sure what it wants. But now it is ready to go out. It can do things on its own. It can be alone. It can do its own work.

A thing that is ready can be many things. A person might be ready to go to work. A cat might be ready to go to sleep. A tree might be ready to break off a big piece of wood. An animal might be ready to go out and find food.

Being ready is not always easy. Sometimes you are ready and then something happens. You are ready to go to a party but then you have to go to school. You are ready to sleep but you are still up at night. Being ready is about being strong and not being afraid.

It is also about being able to do things. If you are ready to give, you are ready to give. If you are ready to say, you are ready to say. When you are ready, you do not feel afraid. You feel like you can do it.
