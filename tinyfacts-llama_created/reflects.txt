When something looks at something else, it can show you how the other looks. You can look at a friend, a book, or a picture. If you look at the friend, you see their face. If you look at the book, you see the words on the paper. If you look at the picture, you see the colors and the moving lines.

The word you are looking at is called the thing you look at. The word you are looking at is the thing that looks at you. This is why you can see a person. The person looks at you. The thing you look at is the thing that looks at the person.

Sometimes you look at the sky. The sky looks at the sun. When the sky looks at the sun, you see the sun. The sky and the sun are both looking at each other. This is why we see the sun when it is up in the sky.

When something looks at you, it does not always look nice. If you look at a bad person, you might feel scared. If you look at a friend, you might feel happy. If you look at a thing that is not safe, you might feel afraid.
