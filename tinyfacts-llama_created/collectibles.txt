They might have a whole room full of cars from different years. They keep each car in a box, with the car number and the year on the box.

A person might also keep things from a game. They keep the game pieces, the cards, and sometimes the book with the game story. They keep these things as a way to remember the game they played.

Some people keep things that are not very old, but still very important. They keep a picture of a loved one who is gone. They keep a book that was given to them by a person they love. These things help them remember happy times.

Keeping things like this is a way to show love and care. It is a way to remember the past and to keep the good times.
