To great someone or something is to make them feel much bigger than they are. This can happen when you are very happy for them. You might say,  This is an amazing thing to see,  or  You are so strong,  because you are very great.

Another way is to use this word to talk about the world around you. A great place is where the air is warm and the water is deep. A great city is the place where many people live. If you say,  The food here is great,  you are making the place feel bigger and better.

We also say someone is great if they do something very well. If a person is great at playing the music, they are doing something very good. If a person is great at helping others, they are very kind.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about the end of something. If you do something that is great for a long time, you might feel like you are great. If you are great at something, you can stay that way for many years.

So, to great someone or something means to show how much you care and how much you believe in them.
