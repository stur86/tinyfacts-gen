To quite means to stop doing something. You are doing something, and you decide to stop. It is like you say, I am done.

You might quite a job, a game, a school day, or a thing that hurts you. You might decide you do not want to do it anymore.

You can also quite a person. You might tell a friend you do not want to talk to them anymore. This means you stop talking to them.

Sometimes, you might quite a place. You might leave a home, a school, or a city. This is when you move to a different place where you are not alone.

If you quite a thing, you are letting it go. You do not need it anymore. You might get ridden of books, clothes, or things that hurt you.

To quite something is not always easy. Sometimes you feel sad or angry. You might want to go back. But you do it anyway because you are tired of it.

You can quite a plan too. If you are going to do something for a long time, but then you get a new idea, you might decide to quite the plan. You can still keep the idea in your head, but you are not doing it right now.

All these mean to stop doing something so you can start something else.
