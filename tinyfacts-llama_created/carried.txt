To carry something means you move it from one place to another. When you carry a book, you hold it in your hand or on your back. You walk or run with it. This is because the book is heavy and you do not want to let it fall down.

Sometimes, people carry money. They take it with them when they go to work, run to a store, or go to a place where they need to pay. If a person has a lot of money, they might carry many small pieces of money in their pocket. They keep them safe in a bag.

If you carry a small animal, you hold it and move it to a new place. You might carry a dog or a cat. This helps the animal stay safe until it finds a home or a place to rest. Humans also carry animals when they go to the store or to a school.
