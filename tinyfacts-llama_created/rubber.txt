Rub is a word you use to show that something is soft. You can rub a piece of wood or a ball on your hand to make it feel soft. This is why you can not hurt your hands while playing with a ball.

Rub also means to touch a thing. You might rub your hands after you eat a sweet food. This makes your skin feel warm. You can rub your face to feel the soft skin on your face. You can even rub a piece of paper to make it feel soft before you use it.

In a different way, rub is a word for a way of making something strong. If you rub something a lot, it gets very strong. You can rub a car to make it go very fast. You can rub a hair to make it look nice. You can even rub a piece of paper to make it feel strong so it does not break when you write on it.

Rub is also a word for a small round thing that you use for fun. You might rub a ball on your hand to make it roll around. You can rub a ball to make it feel soft so you can hold it in your hand. Sometimes, people rub their feet to relax after they walk a lot.
