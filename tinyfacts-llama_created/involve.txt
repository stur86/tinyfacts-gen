To In In 

When you are in a group, you are a part of it. You might sit near the person who talks. You might hear what they say. You might laugh with others.

When you do something, you are also a part of it. You are doing it with your body or your mind. If you are in a game, you are a player. You are moving around and hitting a ball.

To In 

To In can mean two different things. One way is to enter a place. You enter a room in your house. You enter a school to learn. You can also enter a fight. You are not going to hurt anyone. You are just trying to keep yourself safe.

Another way is to make something happen. You can say,  The fire is going to burn the house down. This means the fire is going to happen because of the things that are happening inside. You can also say,  The book is about a man who is sad. This means the book tells a story about a person who feels bad.

When you say you are in, you are already a part of the thing. You are already a part of the story. You are already a part of the group. When you say you are doing something, you are also part of it. You are using your body or your mind to make it happen.
