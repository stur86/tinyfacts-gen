A very important word for people who love to run or do other things that make them very fast.

When a group of people do something, they can get a thing that is for them. If one of those people does it the best way, they get a big thing to show that they are the best. This thing is called a big thing or a top thing.

This top thing is often a cup or a small box with numbers on it. The team with the top thing is called a team. They are the ones who did it the best.

Sometimes, there are many groups of people. Each group does something different. They all do it the best way. Then, they all get their top things at the same time.

The top thing is not just one thing. There are often many top things. The team that gets the top thing the most times is usually the best team.

To have a top thing is to be the best. It is a great feeling for people who love to run or do other things that make them very fast.
