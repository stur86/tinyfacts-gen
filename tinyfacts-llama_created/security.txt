To be in a safe place is to be in a place where no one is trying to hurt you. You are safe if you are not afraid. This is because you know that the place is strong and that no one is going to come in and take you away.

Think about the street. If you are walking down that street, you might hear strange noises. You might see a person who does not want you to be there. But if you are in a house or a school, you feel safe. You know that no one is going to jump out and attack you.

You can also feel safe at home. Your family is there to help you if you are scared. Your home is a place where you can go when you need to be safe.
