Todays is the day after tomorrow. The day after that is the day after today. The day after that is the day after the day after today. Think of it like a line that never ends.

You can also think of it like a game. You start with a number one. You do a thing, and then you get a new number. The new number is the old number of the thing you did. If you do the same thing, the number stays the same. If you do a different thing, the number changes. This is how numbers work.

In the same way, when you talk about days, you are talking about how many days are left before a new day. If you have ten days left, the new day is ten days from now. If you have one day left, the new day is today.
