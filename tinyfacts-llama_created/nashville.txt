Nights

One night can be very quiet. You might stay in your bed and listen to your favorite music. The world outside is still and you can hear the sound of the wind.

Another night can be very loud. You might go to a big building in the city. There are a lot of people there. They are all there to see something. You might hear a lot of noise and see a lot of lights.

A night in the city is usually bright. The sun is gone and the stars are out. The night air is cool and the stars look very big. You can see the black sky from your window.

You can also have a night that is a little bit different. You might go to a place with many people. There are many people eating food and drinking drink. The music is loud and there are many people dancing. It can be a lot of fun.

In some places, the night is very dark. The sun is gone for a long time. The stars are very bright and you can see a lot of them. This can be a good time to go out and see what is happening.

Some nights are very cold. The air is very still and it can be very quiet. There are no cars on the road and the people are all inside their homes. It can be a good time to stay in your house and watch a movie.

Nights are also times for dreams. When you are tired, you might have a good dream. You might be flying or you might be in a far away place. Your mind is free to go where you want.
