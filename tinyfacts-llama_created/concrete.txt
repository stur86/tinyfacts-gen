To make something from hard stuff is to change it into a hard form. This is done with a hard white stuff that you make from many small pieces of rock. You break these small pieces into tiny pieces so they are all the same. You then put these tiny pieces together in a big way that lets them hold each other. After this, you put the hard form in a big box of water and let it sit for a while. When it is hard, you can use it to build houses, roads, and other big things that need to stand for a long time.
