Most animals eat food. They find it in the ground, in water, or in trees. They use their mouths to take the food into their bodies. They then break it down into tiny pieces. This makes their bodies strong and helps them stay living.

Many animals have babies that are their own. The baby is inside the mother animal. The mother animal keeps it safe inside her body until the baby is ready to come out. Then the baby comes out and starts to eat food and grow strong.
