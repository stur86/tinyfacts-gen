When we say a thing is by themselves, it usually means the thing is alone. You can say a book is alone on the table. You are not with it. You are not touching it. You are not reading it.

If something is alone, it does not need to be with other things. It does not need anyone else to be complete. It is just the thing by its own self.

Sometimes, you might say a person is alone in a room. They are not with anyone else in the room. They are not sitting with friends. They are just being alone. This can be a time of quiet and calm. It can be a time to think and feel about what you are doing.

You can also say a group of people is alone. They are not with any other group. They do not have to be with other people to feel like they are alone. They are just the people in the group.

Being alone is often a good thing. It means you do not have to worry about what other people think. You can just do what you want. You can be happy with yourself. You do not have to share your life with anyone else. But it can also be hard. If you are alone for too long, you might start to feel sad. You might miss the feeling of being with other people.
