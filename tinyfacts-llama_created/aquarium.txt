An animal that lives in water, but can also live in a big glass box with water. These boxes are like a very big room for many animals. They have small parts that hold water and air. The animals breathe in the air from outside and drink water from the water inside the box.

Some animals have hard parts that keep water inside their bodies. Other animals have soft parts that can change color to hide from people. They might turn red when they are afraid. Some animals have long hair or soft parts that they use to catch food.

The animals can be very different. Some are very small and have few parts. Others are very big and have many parts. Some have bright colors, and others have very quiet ways to move.

People keep them in these boxes to show them to others. They want to know how they live and how they act. Some people also use them to learn about how to take care of water and how to keep animals safe.
