Interesting is a word you use when you want to talk about something that makes you feel a bit of a surprise. It is a word you use to say that something is new, or that it has a strange story, or that it is very different from what you are used to.

When you say a thing is interesting, you are telling the other person that you think it is a good idea to learn more about it. You might say,  This book is so interesting that I want to read it. Or,  This food is very interesting, because it is not like the food you eat every day.

You can also use this word when you are talking about a person. If a person is interesting, it means they are not boring or like everyone else. They have a story to tell, or they do something that is different from what everyone else does. It is a way to say that the person is not just another face in the crowd.

Sometimes you just use this word to say that something is fun. If you are playing a game with your friends, you might say,  This game is really interesting. It is a game that makes you feel happy.

The word interesting is not the same as exciting. Exciting is when something is going to happen very fast and you can not wait. It is a word you use when you want to talk about something that will make you feel a rush. An interesting thing is not something that is going to happen in the next few seconds, but something that happens over a long time.
