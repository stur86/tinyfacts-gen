When you say a thing is good, you mean it is very nice for you. A doctor is a person who helps you feel better when you are sick. They use their brain to tell you what to do.

If you like a food, you might say it is good. If a friend is being nice, you might say they are a good person. We also use this word when we tell a person to do something.

When a person says you should do something, they are giving you a good word. It means you should follow the word. If someone gives you a bad word, it means the word is not good at all.

You can also use the word when you want someone to do something. You might say, I want to go to the movies. This means you want to go to the place that shows pictures and movies. You are telling the person you need them to take you there.

Sometimes, when you are not sure if something is good, you ask a person for a word. They tell you, the thing is good. This helps you know if you should try it or not.
