A line that goes around a round thing is a line. This line is not straight. It is not like the lines you draw on a piece of paper. It goes in a way that is not the same as going straight across.

Imagine you are walking around a big house. You walk straight out of the front door. You go around the front and then you go around the back of the house. You walk straight again out of the back door. This is a line. This line is going around the round house.

But if you draw a line on a piece of paper. The line goes straight across. If you draw many lines all around a round thing, they do not all go straight at once. They go in different ways and together they form a thing that goes around the round thing. This is what we mean with a line that goes around a round thing.

The word is important in many ways. If you are about to do something, you might say you are going around. You are going to do it but you are not going straight. If you are sitting in a chair, you are going around the chair. If you are walking, you are going around the world. If you are in a line at work, you are going around the lines.

In a long story, a line can mean a story that goes over many people. It can be about a person who does something and then another person does something after them. It can be about a group of people who do a thing together. It can be about a place where many people go. These are all kinds of lines.
