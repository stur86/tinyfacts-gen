When you say something is an after thing, it is not the thing you are talking about. You are not talking about a person. You are talking about a time.

Think about a school year. In the first year, there is a very new teacher. The teacher has not seen the students before. The students do not know what to expect. They are very nervous. The teacher tries to help them. This is an after thing.

There is also an after thing in a job. Sometimes a person is not the right person to do a job. They are not as good as they should be. This is an after thing. The person is not the one you want to work with.

Sometimes a thing is an after thing because it is not finished. It is still going on. It is not over yet. The thing can still happen. It can still be done.

There is also an after thing in a story. Sometimes a person dies. But the story does not stop there. The story goes on. The people left tell what happened. This is an after thing.

You can also say that something is an after thing if it is not the best. It is not the best thing to do. It is not the best way to do it. It is just a way that happened.
