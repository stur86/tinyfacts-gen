To understand something means you see how it works. You do not just hear it. You think about it too. You want to know why it happens.

Imagine you are playing a game. You see the ball go across the floor. At first, you do not know what is happening. But then you watch the ball go around the corner. You see the floor and the wall. You see the way the light falls on the ground. You understand how the ball moves.

You can also understand a word. You hear the word and it is clear. You do not need to think. You know that the word means something. You can say the word without thinking. This is easy for us because we have heard many times before. But for a child, or for a person who does not speak the same way, it is harder. They must use their mind. They must try to see what the word means.

Sometimes, you understand something only when you see it. You do not need to read a story. You can see a picture. You see the colors and the people. You understand the story then. This is why we use pictures so much.

Also, you can understand a feeling. You see a person is sad. You understand that they are not happy. You feel what they feel. This helps you be kind.

To understand is to see how things are. It is not just to remember a fact. It is to know how it all works together. It is to know why things happen.
