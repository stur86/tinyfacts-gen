To make something wider means to change its sides so it can hold more things. You can do this by moving it to the side. Imagine you are standing in front of a door. If you move your hand across the door, the door becomes wider. This makes it easier to walk through.

Things are often wider in the middle. Imagine a big table with a long round side. The side is wider than the side that is shorter. If you move the table so the shorter sides are facing out, the table becomes shorter. The side that is wider stays the same, but the table becomes smaller.

You can also make a room wider. If a room is very small, you might build a wall across it to make it wider. This keeps people from going in the wrong direction and makes the room feel bigger.
