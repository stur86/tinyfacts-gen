To say something is a class is to say it is old. It is something people have used for a very long time. A book, a car, a chair, or a phone can all be classed as a class.

We often use this word to talk about people. If you say a person is a class, you mean they are well known. You might say,  The class is the man who made the music. Or,  I am a class. I am a student.

It can also mean something that is not new. If you say a new idea is a class, you mean it is something you have seen before. The class is the idea, not the person who has it.

Sometimes, when we say something is a class, we mean it is something that must be followed. If you are a class, you must do what the teacher says. You have to be there at the right time and do your work. If you do not follow, you are out of the class.
