Several is a word that means many. When you say this, you are talking about things that are many. You can say it many times.

Imagine you have many books. You read them one by one. Now you have read all of them. You did not just read one book. You read many. You were reading several books. You can say that many books. You do not say one book.

You can also say this when you have many friends. You have many friends in your school or in your city. You do not just have one friend. You have many friends. You are friends with several people.

You can also say this if you are in a room with many people. You are in a room with several people. There are many people there. You are not the only one.
