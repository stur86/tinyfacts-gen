To have a clear idea about what you want can be very important. A person might have a clear idea of what they like to do in their free time. This is a clear way of thinking about their own life. 

Sometimes, people do not have a clear idea. They might want to eat a different food, see a different movie, or go to a different place. In this case, they do not have a clear way of thinking about it. This is because they have not thought about it very much. They are looking for something, but they do not know what they want yet.
