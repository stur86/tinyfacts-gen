A thing that pulls things toward it is a very strong kind of thing. It has a round part on the top and a long part that hangs down. This round part has a bit of power inside it.

When you move a tiny bit of paper near the round part, the paper will move toward the round part. If you put the paper near two different round parts, it pulls the two papers together. This pulls things toward each other. It pulls hair toward your head. It pulls shoes toward your feet. It pulls clothes toward your body. It even pulls hair from other people.

You can also hold a paper that feels this way. You might hold a paper in your hand and feel the pull. You can use this to pull things toward you.

If you put a paper with a bit of power inside it near a long piece of wood, the wood will pull toward the paper. This is why a paper that has power is often put near a thing that needs to be moved.

The round part of the thing is like a great hand. It is so big that it can pull a rock or a great big tree. It is strong enough to pull a car. This is why it is called a pull.

Everything has a pull. The ground has a pull because it wants to pull you toward it. The sky has a pull because it wants to pull you toward it. The sun has a pull because it wants to pull you toward it.
