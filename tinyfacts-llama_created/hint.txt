A word is a thing you use to talk. It is a way to tell other people about a thing. A word can be small and quiet. Or it can be loud and strong. Sometimes a word helps you understand what is happening. Other times a word tells you to do something.

Think about a child. If a child sees a dog, they might say,  That dog! They are giving a small word that helps them understand. They are not really telling everyone, just the person they are talking to.

A word can also help you learn. Imagine you are trying to remember a word. You might look at the word and try to figure out what it means. You might think,  It is a thing that flies through the air. Or you might think,  It is a thing that can make food grow.

In a house there are many words. Some words are hard to understand. Other words are easy to hear and understand. A word can also change. If you say,  I am happy, a few minutes later you might feel sad. This happens because your brain is getting ready to feel the other way.

Words are important. They help us talk to each other. They help us share our ideas. They help us understand each other. Without words we would not be able to tell each other what we are thinking.
