A place you go to.

When you want to go somewhere, you need a way. You might walk, drive, or fly. You pick a way and a time. You are ready to leave.

Think of a place that is far away. If you want to go to a town, you are on your way to a place. You see houses and streets and a big wall. You do not know if you will arrive at the end of the road.

There is also a place inside your mind. A place you want to go. If you have a dream, the dream is your way to a place. Your heart tells you to go there. You feel happy when you think about it.

You can also go to a place to see a movie, to play games, or to eat food you love. These places are not for people. They are for people who want to feel happy or relaxed.

When you reach the end of your way, you feel something. You are tired and you might smile. You are in a new place. You see a new city or a new kind of tree. You feel you are where you are meant to be.
