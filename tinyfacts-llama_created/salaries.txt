A person who works for a group or a big building gets paid money every week. They do a job, like taking care of people, or making things, or writing words. They give the money back to the group or building at the end of the week so they can pay their own family.

Imagine you work in a big store. You help people find what they need. You also do some work at your desk. You show people how things work. The store owner gives you money at the end of the week, so you can buy food for your family and pay your own money. You are paid because you do important work.
