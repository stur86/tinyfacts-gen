A big group of things that do not fit together are called a problem. If you have a lot of small pieces that are not in the right place, they can make a big noise or a lot of trouble. You might need to move them around so they can work together.

Sometimes a group of people do something they do not want to do. They might be angry, sad, or want to stop. This can cause a lot of trouble in the city or school.

You can also have a lot of food that is not good for you. If you eat this food for a long time, you might feel sick. This is a problem because your body needs to stay clean.

A group of animals that live in the same place can cause a problem. If they eat too many of your things, you might need to find a new home.
