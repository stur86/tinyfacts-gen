To Promise

When you promise something, you say you will do it. You say you will help someone. This makes them feel safe.

Imagine you have a friend who loves to play with a ball. If you say, I will play with the ball, you are promising. You are making a deal. You are telling your friend that you will be the one to throw the ball.

A business also uses this word. If a person says, I will make the food, they are promising to do it. This helps the people know they can trust the person.

It is also about being the first one. If you are the first one to do something, you are promising yourself that you will do it. You are telling yourself you are the best at that thing.

Sometimes you promise to be nice. You say you will be kind to a friend. This helps the friend feel safe. They know they can countering you when they need to.

To Promise is to say you are ready to do something. It is to tell others that you are the one who will do it.
