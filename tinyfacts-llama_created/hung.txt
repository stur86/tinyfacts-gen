To hang something means you put it up so it can stand on its own. You might hang a picture on a wall, or a door, or a bag. It has nothing on the ground anymore, and it is standing up.

If you hang your clothes, you put them on a line. You might use a big line that goes around a room, or a line that hangs down from a tall thing. You can even hang your bed in a room.

Sometimes, you hang a thing that is heavy. You put it on a high part of a room. This makes the room look bigger because the heavy thing is not touching the floor. It gives the room a strong feel.

You can also hang a thing for a very long time. If you hang a picture, it stays up for many years. It does not fall down. It stays standing right there, looking at the people around it.

Being hung means you are not moving. You are standing still. You might feel a little bit sad because you want to move. But then, you see the picture or the door you put up and you feel happy. You remember why you did it.
