To work with a person or group who does a job you don't do is to work with a person or group who does it. They do the work. You pay them. They tell you what they are doing and how long it will take.

This person might be a man, a woman, or a small group of people. They might work in a big house, a small house, a school, a street, or a park. They might do a job that is very hard or a job that is very easy.

A person who works with a person or group who does a job may be called a:

A. Worker. A worker works with people. A worker can do many jobs.

A. Builder. A builder works with a person or group who makes things. A builder can build a house, a school, or a road.

Bigger jobs are often done by a person or group with many people. They are like a team. They have a plan and a leader. They work together to get the job done.

Some jobs need a person to be alone. A person who does this job might be a:

A. Person who works alone. A person who works alone does the job by themselves.

Bigger jobs need a person to work with others. A person who works in this way may be a manager. A manager is a person who makes plans and pays people. They help make sure the job is done right.

In some cities, people buy a thing that does a job for them. They pay money for a few days and the thing does the job while they are away. This is called a job.
