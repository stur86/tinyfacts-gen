To pull something means you use your hands to move it from one place to another. This is often done when you are trying to get a heavy bag from a high place. You reach up and use your arms to move it down. This helps you get the bag before it falls.

You can also pull a thing that is not heavy. If you pull a dog, you use your hands to make it move. You use your arms and your legs to push it forward. This makes the dog go in the direction you want.

Sometimes, to pull means to move a person or a thing away from something. If you are angry, you might pull your hair. This is because you are trying to show that you do not like something. You are using your hands to pull on your hair.

You can also pull a thing that is stuck. If a door is not opening, a person might pull. They use their arms and their legs to pull on the door until it opens. This helps the door go across the room.
