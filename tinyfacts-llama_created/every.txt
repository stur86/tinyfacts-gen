Every is a word that means nothing is left out. It is about how many things are there. 

Think of your family. There are mom, dad, and two kids. Every person in the family is there. No one is left out. Every person has a family too. If you have a friend, you have a family. If you have a cat, you have a family too. 

Every is also about the big, wide world. There are many, many stars in the sky. Every star is there. Every rock is there in the ground. Every tree is there in a park. Every animal is there in the water or on the ground. Everything is there, so there is nothing left out.
