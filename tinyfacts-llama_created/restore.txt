To rest means to let your body and mind feel better. When you are tired or hurt, you want to stop doing things and just be still. You might lie down on a soft thing, close your eyes, and breathe deep.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You run around for a long time. Your legs are tired and you are very hot. You need to stop and sit down so you can catch your breath. You might put your feet on a chair and close your eyes to rest your head.

When you rest, your body gets better. Your heart stops beating as much and your body gets less pain. You feel less worried and more calm.

You can also rest things. If a tree is broken and you need to fix it, you can rest it for a while. You might let it sit in the sun or cover it with a soft thing. This helps the tree get ready for the next day when it will grow stronger again.

Sometimes, you rest when you are sad. When you are sad, your heart feels heavy inside. You might want to cry, but you are afraid because you are afraid of how people will think. Resting can help you feel better. It lets your heart and mind know it is okay to feel this way.

To rest a computer also means to stop it. When a computer is very busy, it uses a lot of power. If we keep it running all the time, it gets tired and might break. So, sometimes we close it and let it rest. This helps the computer stay strong for the next time we need it.
