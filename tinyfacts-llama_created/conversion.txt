To change one thing to another is a very important word. It is about how things work.

Imagine you have a bag full of money. You take that money and you want to buy something you want. But the thing you want is not money. You need to use something else. You might use a box that can hold your money to hold your things. But if you just use the box, you will not have the money anymore.

The word means you take one thing and you use it for another thing. It is a way to change something. You can change the way you walk, the way you talk, or the way you act. You can change who you are so you fit in with other people. Or you can change how you do things so you are stronger.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about how things are made. A car is made of many pieces. When we put those pieces together, we change the car into a thing that moves on the road. We take the things we need and we use them to make something new. This is a kind of change.

People also use this word when they talk about how much of something there is. If you have a lot of money, you have more money. If you have a few things, you have fewer. This is also a change.

To change is always a good thing. It helps us grow and learn. It makes us new in a good way.
