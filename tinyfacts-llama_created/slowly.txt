To slowly means you move very, very small steps. You do this for a long time. It might take a very long time to do one thing. You do not rush. You go very, very slowly.

Imagine you are on a long walk. You start walking. At first, you walk quickly. But then you start to walk very, very slowly. You are still moving. You are just going very, very slowly. You are not running. You are not jumping. You are just moving.

You can also use this word for things that are happening. If something is slowly falling down a high wall, the wall is going down very slowly. Or if a tree is slowly growing, it is growing very, very slowly. The years go by very slowly.

This word is also used with people. If a person is slowly becoming old, they are getting older. If a child slowly learns to talk, they are learning very quickly. The word does not mean the person is moving very fast. It means they are moving very slowly.

So, to slowly is about moving very, very small steps. It is about going very slowly and taking your time.
