To ride something is to move on it. If you are on a horse, you are riding. If you are on a car, you are riding it. You hold the seat and the thing that you push with your feet. You use your hands to keep it moving.

You might ride a thing that is round. You hold the round thing and use your feet to push it. You go very fast. You might go over the ground.

You can also ride something that is not moving. You might sit on a chair. You hold the chair and the people around you. You use your feet to push the chair. You go in a line. Many people ride chairs in big places with many floors.

Sometimes, you ride a thing that is not a chair. You might ride a thing that is strong. You hold the thing and use your feet to push it. You go far. You might go over the ground. You might cross a wide water.

To ride is to be on a thing and move.
