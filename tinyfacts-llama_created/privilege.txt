To have a right is easy. You just do what you want. But to have a real right is hard. You have to do nothing wrong, and everyone around you must let you be. You can not make people do things against their will.

A person with a big right is the head of a group. They tell everyone else what to do. They say, if you do this, you are safe. But if you do not follow their words, you are in trouble. They are the one with the power.

But there is also a kind of right. You do not have to follow the head of a group. You do what you think is right. You can fight if you feel wrong. You can say, you are not safe, and you do not want to follow their words. You have a right to your own body and your own mind.

Sometimes, there is a middle ground. If you have a right to do something, but another person also has a right to do it, you must decide. You can say, I do not want to do it. Or you can find a way to do it that is safe for both. This is a hard thing to do.

To have a right is to have something that is not given. You get it because you are who you are. It is a part of you. But when you have a right, you are not the one who gives it. You are the one who gets it.
