A bathroom is a room in a house where people go to clean themselves. Inside this room, you can find a round thing. This round thing is for making water go away. It is called a bathroom. You use it to get ridden of the bad stuff you have on your body.

The round thing is usually in the middle of the room. It is often tall and has a round top. You sit on it and push a tiny round thing to make the water go out. Sometimes, you have to use a strong round thing to make the water go away very fast.

You also need a round thing to put your wet clothes into. This round thing is for keeping your clothes clean. After you use it, you can go into the room with the round thing to get ready for the day.

Sometimes, you need to go to a bathroom very fast. This is when your body gets very heavy with bad stuff. You need to go very quickly before you hurt yourself or before the bathroom gets very full. This is why you can not always sit on the round thing.
