To save something is to put money away for later use. You do this when you need money later but do not want to use it now. You save your money because you think it will help you in the long run.

Imagine you have a little bit of money in your pocket. You can use it to buy a small thing you want. But one day, you do not want to buy the thing anymore. You feel better if you have the money in your pocket ready for later.

You can also save money for a big thing you want. If you have a job, you work hard and save your money so you can buy a new car, a house, or a computer when you want it. This helps you feel happy because you did not buy the thing before you needed it.

Some people save money for a very important reason. They want to buy a house for their family. They save money until they have enough to buy the house. This helps them have a safe place to live and give their children a home.

You can also save money for a short time. You might save money for a big eventually. If you do not have enough money for a trip, you can save it. When you reach your money, you can go and do what you want.
