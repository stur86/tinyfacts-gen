To take a picture is to show a moment in time. You look at a thing that is happening right now. You press a small thing that makes the picture. The picture is like a tiny story.

You can take a picture of a person. You can take a picture of a dog. You can even take a picture of a big thing in the sky.

When you take a picture, you need to be quiet. If you make a sound, the picture gets wrong. You have to be still and wait for the right time.

You can also take pictures with a computer. You can look at the pictures and see what happened. You can see the face of a person, the feet of a dog, or the bright blue sky.

Sometimes, people take pictures of things they want to remember. They might take a picture of a happy time. They might take a picture of a place they love. These pictures are like a story that you can look at later.

In some places, you must follow the right ways to take pictures. There are things you must do before you take the picture. You must ask for a sign. You must not make a sound that breaks the quiet. You must be carefully where you walk.
