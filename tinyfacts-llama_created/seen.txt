To see something means you watch it with your eyes. When you see something, you know it is there. You can see a tree in a green field. You can see a dog in a park. You can see a bright light in the sky.

You can see things that are very far away. You look at the sun in the morning. You see the world around you. You see people, cars, houses, and trees. You see what is happening in your world.

Sometimes you see things that are not there. You might see a person who is not really there. You see a wall that is not a wall. You see a thing that is only in your head.

People use the word to talk about things that are true. If you see a person, you know they are real. If you see a thing, you know it is there. If you see a story that is not real, you might feel confused.
