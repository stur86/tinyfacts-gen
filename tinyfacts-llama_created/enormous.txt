A very big word!

To be huge means you are very large. If you are a big dog, you are huge. If you are a tall tree, you are huge too. If you have a huge house in the middle of a city, people want to go there.

Something that is huge can be a person, a car, a rock, or even the whole world! If you look at the world from space, it looks huge. If you look at a huge dog, you might feel afraid. You might want to hide.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are very heavy. A huge box of books is very heavy. If you drop a huge rock, it falls down quickly. That is because the rock is very heavy.

When we say a thing is huge, we mean it is very big. We do not say it is small or tiny. We do not say it is very small. We just say it is huge!
