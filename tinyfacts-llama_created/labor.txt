To work is to do a job. You may work in a house. You might work in a school. You might work in a big building. You do this work for money. You do it because it is a job. People also work in the morning and the afternoon.

When you work, you give the world something. If you build a house, you make a place for people to live. If you make clothes, you give them to people to wear. You also give people food so they can eat.

Sometimes you work very hard. You work for a long time without a break. You may feel tired and sad. But even when you are tired, you still do the job. You want the job done. You want the world to be better.

In a big group, there are many people. Each person does a little part of the job. Everyone works together. They share their work. They help each other. This makes the job easier.

Work is a part of life. You need to work to buy food and clothes. You need to work to pay your money. You need to work to live.
