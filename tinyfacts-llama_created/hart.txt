A heart is a big thing in your body that makes you very strong and can keep going even when you are very tired. It is in your chest and it is very round and full of air.

Your heart is like a box that holds your blood. It takes the air you breathe into your body and gives the blood to all your parts. It also keeps your body warm. When you are very happy, your heart beats faster. When you are very sad, your heart beats slower.

Your heart is also very good at keeping things inside. If something is big and heavy, you can put it in your body and it will stay there. Your heart does this all the time, even when you are not thinking about it. It is always working, even when you are sleeping.

You have two hearts. One is under your chest and the other is on top of your chest. They beat together so that your body gets the air and the blood it needs. If one of them stops beating, the other one will take over. This is why you do not feel pain while you are dying. Your heart keeps going, even when you can not feel anything else.
