When something happens, it usually starts with a word. This word tells us what is happening. If a dog comes, a person might say, or a child might say, the dog has arrived. This word is important because it shows us what is happening in a place.

Sometimes, this word can also mean you are coming. If you are going to the store, you are coming. You are moving toward the store. You are not still there.

The word can also mean you have something inside you. If a baby is coming, the baby is coming. This means the baby is ready to be out. It is a big moment.

You can also use this word to talk about how something looks. If a room is coming, it is going to be a nice place. It will be very bright and warm. This room will feel good to be in.
