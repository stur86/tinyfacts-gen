To stand means you are in a place. You are not on the ground anymore. You are on a high spot.

Imagine a person. They walk in a park. They walk for a long time. They get tired. Now they stop and sit down. They are standing because they are where they are.

You can stand with your feet on the ground and your arms out in front of you. This is when you are ready to move. You stand in front of a table when you are telling a story. You stand for a job. You stand for a sign.

Sometimes you stand for a fight. You stand up for your friend. You stand up for what you believe in.

You can also stand for a job. You stand at a desk. You stand at a store. You stand at a school. When you work, you are standing. You stand when you are waiting for something to happen.

When a dog stands, it means it is ready to move. It is not afraid. It is ready to go.
