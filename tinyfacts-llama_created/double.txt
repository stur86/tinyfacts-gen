To do something or to have something means to act or be in the right place at the right time.

Imagine you are at a party with many other people. One person has a drink in their hand. If you go over to them and say, you want a drink, you will be doing something. You have already been in the place. You do not have to go there again.

If you want to have more than one drink, you can do the same thing. You go to the person who has a drink and say, here is a drink. They will give it to you. Now you have two drinks.

Sometimes, you might do something to make another person happy. If you give them a small present or a word of thanks, you are doing something. You have already been in the right place.

You can also do something to your own body. You might take a drink of water. You go to the bathroom and you have to go. You are doing something to your body. You have already been in the right place.

Sometimes, to do something means to make things bigger. Imagine you are playing a game with a ball. You throw the ball far away. Now the ball is bigger than before. It is not the same as it was before. You did something to the ball.

You can also do something to a person or to something else. If you tell a friend a funny story, you are doing something. You are not in the same place as before. You are in a new place.

To do something is to act or to be in the right place at the right time. It is about being ready for what you want to happen.
