To waving means you use your hands to move around in a way that is fun. This is how people often greet each other. When you see a friend, you might say,  How are you? Or you might just say, Hi.

Waving can also mean to act very much like a person you like. If you love a movie, you might watch it many times and try to remember every tiny thing. You might even try to act like the person in the movie. You might walk like they walk, or talk like they talk. You feel like you are really in the movie.

Sometimes, to waving is used to talk about time. If you have to be at a place at a very small time, you might say, I have to go. This means you need to leave right away. It does not mean you are angry. You are just ready to go.

Waving is a way to show you care. You might hold someone close when you are happy. This can make them feel safe. It is also how you say you are glad, even if the thing is bad.
