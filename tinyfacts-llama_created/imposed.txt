To put someone or something under a person is to make them do it.

You might put a child in a room if they are being bad. You tell them to stay there until they are quiet and happy.

You can also put a person in a job they do not want. This happens when a person does not like to work but you still want them to do it. You might say,  You are the manager of this store. You tell everyone they have to follow your orders.

Sometimes, you have to put a thing in a box. This is when you take something from one place and give it to another place. You are putting something in a place where it will not be broken.

You can also say you are putting someone in a group. This is when you put many different people together so they can learn from each other. This can be a good way to make people feel at home.

Immediately means right now. When you say something is immediately, you mean it happens now.
