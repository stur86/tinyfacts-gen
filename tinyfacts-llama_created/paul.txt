A person you follow as a leader is called a leader. This person is important because they help their group do what they need to do. They make the right moves. They work to keep everyone safe.

Sometimes, a leader is a man. These men are often very good at what they do. They help their people feel safe and happy. They tell them what to do and how to act.

Some people follow a person who is a woman. These women are also good at what they do. They help their group and make sure everyone is safe. They are important because they show that women can do many things.

People often follow a leader who is older than them. These older people have more years of life and more stories to share. They can give them good ideas and help them learn from their past.

A leader can be a man or a woman. They might be a man who is very strong and good at fighting. Or they might be a woman who is very kind and good at making people feel happy.
