To break down food is how your body makes power. You eat food from the ground, from animals, and from the green things that grow in the ground. Your body takes in this food and breaks it down into small bits that are easy for your body to use for power.

One of these small bits is a type of food that goes into your blood. This is the stuff that gives you life. Your body is like a big car. It needs power to move. The food goes into your blood and is carried to every part of your body. Your body then breaks down this power into tiny bits that give you the power you need to move, to think, to feel, and to stay warm.

Your body uses three of these tiny bits. Two of them are for making your brain and your heart work. One bit is for making your body work. Your body also uses power from the third bit to keep your skin and your hair looking good. Your brain is like a very high computer. It helps you remember, learn new things, and keep your body working. It also helps you decide what to do and how to act.

When you eat food and your body breaks it down, it takes these tiny bits. Your body then puts them together to make the power you need. If you eat food that is high in power, your body can make more power. If you eat food that is low in power, you might feel tired and your body might need more power to stay going.

Sometimes, your body gets too much power. This can happen if you eat too much food or if you drink too much water. This can make you feel sick. Your body tries to get rider of the power by using it to move your body away from the place where the power is coming from. You might feel hot and feel like you need to go to a bathroom. You can also feel very tired or feel like you want to cry.

Your body needs power to move, to breathe, to keep your head up, and to keep your body warm. It takes power from the food you eat. If you do not eat enough food, your body starts to break down its own power. This is why it is so important to eat enough food every day so your body can stay working well.
