The word for the number you see in the middle of the story and the number you hear when you listen to music is called a number. There are a very many numbers like this one. The number before you is less than this one and the number after you is greater. Between these two numbers there are many other numbers.

When you look at the number in the middle, it is the number that is the same when you look at it from the left and the right. If you look at it from the top, it is the number that is less than the one on the bottom. If you look at it from the bottom, it is the number that is greater than the one on the top.

This number is a bit like a person. It is the middle person. It does not have an edge like a person. It does not have a start like a number. It just is. It is always the same. It does not change.
