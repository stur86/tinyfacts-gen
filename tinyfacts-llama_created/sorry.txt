The word sorry is used when you do something wrong. If you make a person angry, you might say sorry. When you make a friend angry, you feel sad.

To say sorry is like saying sorry to the person. You use this word to tell them that you are sorry for what you did. If you do not say sorry, the person might stay mad at you.

If you are the one who is mad, you might feel angry for a long time. If you are the one who did the wrong thing, you might feel sad. You might want to fix the problem so the other person feels better.

Sometimes, sorry can mean you are sorry for your own trouble. If you did something that makes someone else very angry, you are sorry. You are sorry for the pain you caused.

You can also say sorry if you are sorry for what you did to yourself. You might feel sorry for a time when you were afraid to try something new. You are sorry for not being strong.

The word sorry is important. It helps us show that we care about the other person. It is a way to say that you are sorry and that you want to make it right.
