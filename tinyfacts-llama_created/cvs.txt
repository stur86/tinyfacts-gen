To buy something is to pay money for it. You go to a place with many people. The people there are buying and paying for things too. You walk in and you see many people. You find a thing you want. You pay the money the person is paying with and you take it.

This is how people buy food, clothes, and things they need. A store is a place where you buy things. The store has many things to buy. You can see the things you want, and you can pay for them.

The word can also mean to change your life. If you buy a house, you move into it. You start new life there. You have a new home. You have a new place to live.
