To make something soft

A soft thing has a soft cover. The cover is made of many small parts that push against each other. When a tree grows, it makes more of these small parts. As the tree grows, these parts push out more and more. The soft parts are very good at letting air go through. This makes the tree grow big and strong.

You can also make something soft by putting it in hot water. If you leave it for a long time, the water will make the soft things turn into a soft, running stuff. This is why you can put a soft thing in a bag of hot water to make it soft.

Sometimes, when you walk in the rain, your feet get wet. This wet stuff is the soft of your feet. It helps keep your feet dry. It is like a soft cover for your feet.

You can also use the word to talk about things that grow. When something makes more of a thing, it is growing. The tree is growing, and the soft parts are growing too.
