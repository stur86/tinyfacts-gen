To gently means you act without thinking. You do not plan. You act when you see something that makes you want to do it. You act because you feel it.

This happens when you see a person who is very happy. You want to be happy too. You start to smile and feel your heart feel good. You act by smiling. You act by looking at the person. Your body feels good because the person makes you feel good.

This happens with many things. When you see a bright light in the sky, you act to follow the light. You act to go to the light. You act because you want to see what is there.

People also act when they want to get something. They act to buy a thing they need. They act to get a job. They act to meet a friend.

Sometimes, you act to act. You act to make something happen. You act to do a job. You act to show that you care.

To gently can also mean to act when you are afraid. You act to hide. You act to stay close to someone. You act because you are afraid of the things that are going wrong.

So, to gently is to act without thinking. It is to act because you feel something. It is to act to follow your heart.
