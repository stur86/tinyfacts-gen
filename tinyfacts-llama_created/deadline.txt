A word you might use when you need to do something very fast. It tells you when you must finish.

Imagine you want to write a letter to a friend. You write it, then you need to put it in a box. If you do not put it in the box before the box is full, you are behind. You have to wait for the next day.

A person might give you a job to do. They tell you the time you have to finish. If you do not finish by that time, you are late. This can happen if you work too much or if something goes wrong.

You can also say the word to a teacher. They set a time for a big work. If the work is not done by the time, the student gets a low mark. This helps the teacher see what the student is good at and what they need to work on.

Sometimes, a word might mean more than one thing. If you say you are going to do something, you might mean you are going to do it very soon. You are not waiting to see what happens. You are ready to start right away.

You might hear this word in a movie or a story. A person might say, I have a lot to do before the night. This means they have many things to do and they must do them soon.
