A small round thing is what you use when you make a drink very hot. You put it in a cup, and then the drink gets very hot. After that, the round thing goes away. Sometimes it is filled with small white pieces. These white pieces are made from the skin of animals. Sometimes the round thing is very small and has a top that opens. This top is used to take small pieces out of the drink.

You can also use this word to talk about something that is very small. A baby is a very small thing in the world. A tiny animal is another way to say that something is very small. A tiny bit is a very small part of something.
