To why is to explain the reason behind something. When you say you are tired, you are saying, I am why I am tired. You are not just saying you are tired. You are saying why you are tired.

Imagine you are watching a movie. You are happy and you are not thinking about anything. Then you hear a loud noise. You jump up and you say, I am why I jumped up. You are not just saying you jumped up. You are saying why you jumped up. You are the thing that happened.

You can also use this word when you are explaining a reason for a thing. If you tell a child why they can not play with a ball, you are explaining the reason. It is not just saying they do not want to play. It is saying why they do not want to play.

Sometimes you need to say why when you are angry. You might say, I am angry because you did something bad. This is not just saying you are angry. It is explaining why you are angry. You are not just saying you are angry. You are telling the story of why your anger happened.
