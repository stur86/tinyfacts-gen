To be a part of something means you are near it. You can be in the same room as a friend, or you can be near a house or a city.

Think about how you feel when you see a dog. You see the dog and feel happy. You are a part of this feeling. You are not just standing there, you are part of it. Your heart is in it.

When you are a part of a group, you are a friend. You share things with them. You might talk or laugh together. You feel like you are home.

If something is a part of a story, it is part of how the story goes. It shows what happened before or what happened next. It adds to the story.

We all have things in our lives that we are a part of. Our lives are made up of many parts. Our family, our work, our school, and our friends are all parts of our life.
