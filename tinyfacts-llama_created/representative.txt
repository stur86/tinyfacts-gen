A person who stands for a group of people is called a leader. They help decide how things should be done in a place. A person can be a leader for a city, a state, a group of people, or even the world.

Imagine you are in a school. You want to decide what game to play next. You can pick yourself to be the one to pick. This is like being a leader. You are the one that everyone looks to.

Sometimes, people pick a person to stand for them. They do this when they are not in the group. The person is like a voice for the group. They can speak about the group and what the group wants.

When people pick a leader, they are letting that person know that they care about the group. They are saying, you are the one who should help make this group happy.

A person can be a leader for many reasons. They might be the one who was first. They might have done something very well. Or they might have had to work very hard. Being a leader is a big job. It is hard to stay in the same place for a long time and still get things done.
