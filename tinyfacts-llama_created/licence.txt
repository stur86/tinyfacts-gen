To have a thing is to be allowed to do it. If you want to fly, you need a thing that lets you go up into the sky. You do not just sit on the ground and let things happen. You must follow the word, the sign, the book, or the name of the thing. This is what we call a thing you have a right to.

Think of a person. If you are a doctor, you have a thing called a book that tells you what to do. You follow the words in that book and you can not do anything that makes the person very mad. You have a right to do this thing.

A thing you have a right to is not the same as a thing you can do for yourself. A thing you have a right to is something that another person allows you to do. You may not build a high wall across a street because you think it is better. You must follow the city sign, even if you think it is a good idea.

Things you have a right to can be very important. If you have a thing you have a right to, you must follow the word. If you do not follow, you can lose it. The word is there so that everyone can know what is allowed and what is not allowed.
