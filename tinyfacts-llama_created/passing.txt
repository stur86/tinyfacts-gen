To pass something means to move it along. It happens when you walk or run past people or things in a room. If you are walking, you are passing something. If you are in a line, you are also passing each person.

Think about a small thing you throw. You throw it across a room. You are passing it from one hand to another. The thing goes from your hand and goes to the hand of someone else. You are not holding it anymore. It has moved on its own.

You can also pass things that are not moving. If you pass a ball to a friend, you are giving it to them. You are not holding it anymore. They have it now. This happens many times in a game.

Sometimes, we pass things that are hard to carry. If you have a heavy bag, you must carry it. But if you have friends to help you, you can pass it to them. They can carry it for you. This is when you share the thing with others.

We also use the word to talk about the end of a story. If someone tells a story, the story ends. You have heard the story. Now the story is closed.
