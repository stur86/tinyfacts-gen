To be is to have. To know is to understand. But to be also means to be here now. It means to be in the space between the past and the present.

When you are a child, you are a being. You are not a thing. You are a living thing. Your body is here. Your mind is not. But you are not alone. There are other children, other animals, other people. You are part of a group.

As you grow older, you start to see things that are not there. You see a thing you did not see before. You hear a sound you did not hear before. You feel a place you have never been. You start to understand that the world is not just what you see and hear. There is more. There is a world inside you, inside the mind.

This is how you become a person. You start to be a person by being here now. You start to know yourself, to know what you want, to know what you need. You start to be a part of the world, but still, you are not the world. You are still a living being, a thinking being.

But being is not just about you. It is also about the world. The world is a big group of things. When you see a thing, you are a part of the world. When you hear a sound, you are a part of the world. When you feel a place, you are a part of the world.

The world is also about you. The world is a place where you can be. It is a place where you can live, where you can think, where you can feel. The world is not just a thing you see. It is also a feeling you have inside.

So, to be is to be here now. To know is to understand. But to be also means to be part of the world, to be a part of everything. It means to be a living, thinking, feeling thing, and to be a part of a big group of things.
