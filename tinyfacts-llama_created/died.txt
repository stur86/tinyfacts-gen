To die means you are gone. When you are very old, your body stops working. You need more and more things to stay safe. One day, your heart stopped beating, your breath was gone, and your body stopped moving. This is the end of your life.

Imagine you love a person very much. You want them to stay with you always. But one day, you are very sad and do not want to see them anymore. This is what it means to die. Your love and your life are gone.

You can also die from things that hurt your body. If a bad thing is put in your body, it kills you. You might feel very sick and your body might stop working. This is very sad for the person who loves you.

Sometimes, people die because of things they did. If you do something that makes others very angry, you might die. This is because your body was not safe anymore.

We also die when we are very old. As we grow older, our bodies change. We might lose our hair or our teeth. Our skin might feel dry or red. Our bodies need more and more food to stay going.

You can not die. Your life is always moving. Even when you do not feel the world around you, your heart still moves. You are still a person.
