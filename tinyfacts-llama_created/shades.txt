Shadows are the dark parts of a room after the light from a window has gone. They happen when you move around a light thing. The light hits the side of a table or a wall and goes in, but the dark part of the wall stays in the dark.

Imagine you are standing in front of a bright light. If you look straight at the light, the light goes through you and comes out the other side, but the light from the wall stays in the dark part of the room. The dark part looks like a long, dark line or a small dark spot.

When you move around, the light hits the side of the thing you are standing next to. The light goes through you, but the light from the wall stays in the dark part of the room. This is why the dark part looks different for each person. Some people see the dark part as a long line, while others see a small dark spot.

In the dark of night, the shadows are very big and cover most of the room. In a dark room, you can not see anything. The only light comes from the windows or from a small light on a phone.

Shadows can also happen when you are moving. If you walk past a small table, you will see the dark part of the table. If you walk past a tall wall, you will see the dark part of the wall. But if you are standing next to a small table or a wall, the light from your face will show you what you are really looking at, and the shadows will be small.

Sometimes, you see a thing with no light around it. This is called the black spot. The black spot is like a very bright, very dark spot. It is not really a part of the world, but it is the dark part of the room that is not lighted.

You can also see shadows when you look at a picture. If you look at a picture of a person, the person is not really there. The person is in the dark parts of the room, and the light from your eyes is showing what you see. The dark parts of the room are the parts where the person is not there.
