A small water thing that moves across the water. It has a hard bottom. You can sit inside it and hold on to a bar so you don't fall. People use it to go to far away places. They carry food and things they need.

You can also use a small water thing to go into the water. It has a small door at the front. You put your legs in and sit down in the water. The water thing goes under the water and takes you to a place you want to be.

Sometimes a small water thing is a car that moves on water. It goes very fast and can carry many people at once. It has a big bottom and a hard wall. The water inside is very quiet.

You can use a small water thing to carry things across a big water. It can carry things that are heavy. It keeps the things safe and does not let them fall.

You can also use a small water thing to go into the water and look around. It has a window on top so you can see what is under the water. It is a good thing to use when you want to see if there are things in the water.
