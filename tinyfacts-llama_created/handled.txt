The word handled means to use something that is already ready to use. You might use a book, a ball, or a cup. When you are finished with a thing that is ready, you do not start with it again. You take it and you do what you want.

Think about a child. A child can handle a ball, a small dog, or a piece of paper. The ball is ready to be thrown. The dog is ready to run. The paper is ready to be drawn. The child is ready to play. They are not starting from nothing. They are just using what is already there.

If you try to use something you do not know how to do, it can cause you trouble. If you are a driver, you should not try to drive if you are not sure. You should use the car because it is ready. You should not start pushing it. You should let someone else handle it because they know how to use it.

Sometimes, you might handle something that is very hard. You might be scared or you might feel the air is moving in your face. But when you are strong and you take control, you can do it. You can use the thing to your help. This is what being handled means.
