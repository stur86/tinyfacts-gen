To need something means you want it, but you can not have it. You feel a strong want for it. It is like a big voice in your head.

Think about money. You work hard to get money. You use it for food, a house, and things you buy. If you do not have money, you need it. You need to live and eat. You need to have a place to stay and clothes to wear.

You also need sleep. Your body needs rest to feel good. You need to close your eyes and let your body be quiet. This helps you feel better when you are tired.

Sometimes, you need a friend. You might need someone to talk to when you are sad or angry. You need a person to share your story with. This helps you feel less alone.

You also need things you like. If you love a game, you want to play it. You might need a new game or a game with the person you love. This makes you happy.

When you need something, you act. You go to the store to buy it. You pay the money. You take the thing home and use it. This is how you show you need it.

Sometimes, you need to change. You might need to leave a bad place or a job you hate. You need to find a new one that makes you happy. This is an important part of growing up.

To need something is to feel a strong want. It is a way to show you care about the things that matter to you.
