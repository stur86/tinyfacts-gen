To write something is to use your hands, your brain, and your body to make a piece of words. You start with a thought, a feeling, or a story. You use your fingers to type or write the words. You might use a computer or a phone to make the words.

Then you put the words together in a way that makes them make sense. You might put words near each other. You might put words in a line. You might put words on a paper. You might put words into a box that can hold many words.

When you are done, you have a piece of writing. You can look at it, share it with others, or keep it for yourself.

You can write many different kinds of things. You can write a story about a person. You can write about a place, a thing, or a feeling. You can write about what you think, what you feel, or what you want.

Writing helps us to tell our stories. It helps us to share our ideas with others. It helps us to keep our memories and our hopes. It helps us to talk to each other in a way that is not hard to understand.

Sometimes we write to be heard. Sometimes we write to be seen. Sometimes we write to be understood. Every time you write something, you are using your brain to tell a story.
