To give someone more power or help is to give them a big push. Imagine you are playing a game. You are very good at it, but you need a little bit more help to stay ahead. A parents help you by giving you a ball to hit. This makes you feel better and you can keep playing.

You can also give power to a group or a person. If a person is very sad, a kind friend might give them a hug. This makes them feel better. When you do something for someone, you are helping them.

Sometimes, you might give power to a thing. If you drive a car, you are giving a thing power to get you from one place to another. You are using it to move fast and make your life easier.
