To go away from a place is to leave it and go somewhere else. When you go to a place that is far away, you are going to a different city, a different town, or a different part of a land. You might go to a place where the sun is very bright during the day and very dark at night.

Going away can also mean going to other parts of your own city. You might go to a different school or a different part of a building. You might meet new people or see new things. It can be a great way to grow and learn.
