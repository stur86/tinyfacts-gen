To be an answer is to make another person happy. If you answer a question, you help them. You give them the right thing they need to know. You do not change the way they think.

But sometimes you have two answers. One answer is good. The other answer is not so good. You can tell which one is better by looking at how they make you feel. If you feel sad, the bad answer is better. If you feel happy, the good answer is better.

When things are not the same, we say they are not an answer. This means they do not help. We say this when two things are very different. One is very big. The other is very small. They do not help the person who needs to know. In this case, we say they are not an answer.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about numbers. If two numbers are the same, you can say they are an answer. This means they are the same as each other.

In life, this word is very important. When you do something and it does not change the way things are, you can say it is an answer. This helps you see that you did not change anything. You did not change the world. You just did what you had to do.
