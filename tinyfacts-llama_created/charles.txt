A person who works for a big group that makes things for the world is called a manager. This person is important because they help the group do its work.

To be a man or a woman, you need to be in the right age. A man is a boy who is not yet a grown man, and a woman is a girl who is not yet a grown woman. Most people are men. They build things, fight with other men, and work with other men.

A man can also be a father. A father is a man who has children. When a father is a man, he is the one who helps the children grow up. He gives them money, food, and a warm place to stay.

Sometimes, a man is a teacher. A teacher shows people how to do things so they can learn. This is a very important job. A teacher helps young people to become good people.

A man can also be a writer. A writer writes stories, stories about people, or stories about places. People read these stories to learn or to relax.
