To open something means you let it be. You let something that is closed happen. This is very important.

If you close a door, you make it shut. You make it safe for a little while. But when you open it, the door is let out again. You are letting the inside out. This happens with many things. You open a box to look at what is inside. You open a book to read. You open a door to go to a place you want to be.

You can open a box that is heavy. You use your hands to lift it. Or you use a thing with a long part that you turn. This part is like a hand that lets the box go. You open the box and you see what is inside.

You can also open a door. If you have a door in your house, you open it to let in the light when the sun is up. You open it to let a friend in. Or you open it to go to a place you love.

Sometimes, you open a bag. You take out the things you need. You open a bag of food to eat. You open a bag of clothes to put on. This is when you let the bag be empty again.

To open something is to let it happen. It is about being ready for what comes next.
