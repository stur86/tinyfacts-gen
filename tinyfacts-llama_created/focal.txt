To focus something means to make it clear and strong. Imagine you are looking at a picture of a cat. If you look at the picture with your eyes, it is bright and easy to see. But if you look at the picture through a glass or a small window, the picture becomes very soft and you can only see the edges.

In the world, things need to be focused in order to see them well. A light from the sun is focused on the sky so we can see the colors of the day. A ball when it is moving quickly can be focused to see where it will land. When you look at a book with a strong light, the words and the pictures become clear and easy to read.

A person who focuses something is called a person with good eyes. They can see the world well and do not have to look at things many times to understand them.
