To be ready means you are not afraid or worried. You are in a good place. You have the things you need to do.

Imagine you are about to go to a party. You have your clothes, your shoes, and your bag. You are ready to have fun. You feel happy because you know what to expect. You are ready to meet new people and laugh with them.

When you are ready, you can do your work. You can learn something new. You can talk to a friend. You can do a job. You feel safe and ready to do what you want to do.

You can also be ready for a big thing. If you have a big job, you have to be ready. You have to know what to do. You have to be ready to act. You have to be ready to face things that might happen.

Being ready is important. When you are ready, you feel calm and strong. You can do what you want to do without a lot of trouble.
