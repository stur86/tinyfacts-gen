A person who drives a car has a job. They follow many ways to drive. They must follow the right road, follow the right time, and follow the right signs. They also have to follow the way the city says is right. A person who drives a car is often called a driver.

There are two kinds of these jobs.

One kind is for a car. If a person wants to drive a car, they must buy a car. They pay money to buy the car. They also pay a small feel to the city. This feel helps pay for the road and the police. The person must also follow the car ways.

The other kind is for people who do not drive. If a person wants to work for a group that sends people to other places, they need a work paper. This paper says the person is allowed to work. They pay money to the group. They also pay a small feel to the city. This helps pay for the work paper and the city police.

People also need to pay to drive if they are going to a place that has a lot of people. If you drive to a place with a lot of people, you must pay a small feel. This is to help the city police and the people who live there.

A person can get a work paper or a car paper from the city. They must follow the ways for that paper. They must pay the money and the small feel. If they follow the ways, they get to work.
