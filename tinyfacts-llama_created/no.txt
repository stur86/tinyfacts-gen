No is a word we use to tell what something is not. We use it when we want to say that a thing does not happen. We might say,  No, you can not be here. This means you are not allowed in that place.

You can also say no when you do not want to do something. If you want to watch a movie but your mom says you must be inside, you say, No. This means you do not want to go.

Sometimes, when you say no, people feel a bit angry. It is like they are telling you that you are doing something wrong. If you do not want to do something, you can just say no. It is a way to say,  I do not want to.

In a big group, if someone asks everyone if they want to go to a party, everyone says no. This means many people do not want to go. The person who asked is left alone.

We also use no to talk about time. If you do not have time for a friend, you say you do not have time for them. This means you do not have enough time for them.

In some places, if you want to say you are tired but you do not want to go out, you say, No, I am tired. This is like saying, I do not want to do this, but I need to do it.

Sometimes, we say no to a plan because we do not feel it is right. If a movie you loved is being shown again, you might say, No, I do not want to see it again. You are saying the plan is not good for you.

No is a word that helps us tell what is happening around us. It can tell us that something is not possible. It is a word we use in many different ways in our lives.
