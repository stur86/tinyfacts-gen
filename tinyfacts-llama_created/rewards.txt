To get a thing as a thank you is to get something you want. When you do a good job, you might get a small present or a word that says you are good. This is a way to show you care.

Sometimes, people give things to each other. If you help a friend, you might get a small thing back. This is a way to help each other. 

If you do something very well, you might get a job for a long time. Or you might get money to buy things you want. This is how people show they like you.

You can also get a thing by doing well in school. If you work hard and get good marks, the teacher gives you a small thing. This is a way to show you are doing well.
