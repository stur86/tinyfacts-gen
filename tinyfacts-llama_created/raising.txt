To raise something means to make it grow or get bigger. You can raise a child, a dog, or a small thing you found in the ground. You do this by giving it food, water, and a safe place to live.

Imagine you have a small baby animal. You bring it into your home and care for it. After a few months, the animal is strong enough to go out and find its own food. You did not just sit and watch it. You helped it learn how to stay safe and find what it needs. You gave it the world it needed to grow into a strong animal.

You can also raise animals that help us. You might keep a small animal that gives us air to breathe or a big animal that pulls a car. These animals need food and a safe place to live. You care for them and give them what they need to stay strong and do their job.

Sometimes, to raise something means to make it better. If you work at a job, you are raising your pay. You work hard and do your job well. This makes your life better, and you feel happy.

In a different way, to raise something can mean to make it strong. If you put a small ball in a box, it will get bigger and stronger as it goes. You are helping it grow into a better ball. This is how you raise power inside a person or a thing that can do work.

So, to raise something is to help it grow, get stronger, or become the best it can be.
