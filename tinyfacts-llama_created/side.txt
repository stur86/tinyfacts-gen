A side is a part of something that is on one side of a line. It is the part you see on the other side. Think about a piece of paper. There is a top side and a bottom side. Each side is on the other side of the paper.

Imagine you have a box. The sides of the box are the things you see. The front side of the box is the side you see when you look at it. The back side of the box is the side you see when you turn it around. The top side of the box is the side you see when you look up. The bottom side of the box is the side you see when you look down.

The word side is also used for the number side. In a line of numbers, the side number is the number next to the number that is next to it. If you have a line of five numbers, the numbers  one, two, three, four, and five are the sides of the line.

A side can be a line or a part of a group of things. It can also be a part of a story. In a story, a side can be the person you follow or the person who is against you. In a game, a side can be the number of the number that is next to the number you are trying to reach.

When you say something is on the side, you mean it is on the other side of something else. It is not in the middle. It is on the outside.
