To be in a group means you are with many other people. This group is often called a team or a family. When you are in a group, you can do many things together. You can talk, laugh, work, or play games.

Imagine you have a big box full of balls and a small box full of soft things. When you put the big box of balls and the small box of soft things together, you get a new group. This group is made of many different things. Each person in the group is different, but they all work together.

You can also say that something is in a group when it is not alone. A person might be in a group of friends. They are all together and have fun. They talk and laugh. They share their stories and their ideas. They feel like they are part of a bigger group.

Sometimes, you might feel like you do not fit in a group. You might like to do things on your own or work alone. You might feel sad or angry when you are left out. In these cases, you might feel like you are the only one. But when you are part of a group, you feel like you are home.
