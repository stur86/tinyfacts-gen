To make something stronger is to make it bigger and more safe. Imagine a small ball. The ball can roll around, but if you put a hard cover on it, the ball becomes bigger and it is more safe.

If you are a person, you can get stronger. You can run faster and lift more heavy things. You become more able to do things that make you feel good. You might feel like you can do anything if you keep pushing yourself.

Sometimes, things get broken. A door falls down or a piece of wood breaks. But if you put strong stuff on the door or on the wood, it will be safe again. You have made it stronger.

You can also get stronger in your mind. If you are afraid, you feel smaller. But if you stand up and show the world you are not afraid, you become bigger and more sure of yourself.

To be strong is to feel good when you can do things. It is to know you are the best for a job or for a game. It is to feel like you are the one who can do it.
