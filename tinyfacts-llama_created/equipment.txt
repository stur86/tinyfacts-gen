To have something is to have it in your hands or in your space. When you have a thing, you use it. You might use a book to learn something new. Or you might use a thing to do a job.

A piece of stuff for work is called an office thing. It helps you do your job. It might have numbers on it. It might have a small part that opens and closes. This thing helps you do your work, so you can do more things.

A thing that moves is called a car. It is a thing you use to go from one place to another. It has a round thing on top. The round thing helps the car go round a big round road. It also has a long side that allows you to sit. You can get in and out of the car many times while driving.

There are many other kinds of things you need. A thing to eat is food. You eat food to give your body power. You need food to stay going. A thing to sleep is a bed. You sleep in this thing to rest your body.

When you have many things, you have a lot of power. You can do many more things. You can go to many places, learn new things, and make new friends.
