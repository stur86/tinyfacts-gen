To be a good person means you act in ways that are nice and kind. You might help a friend who is sad, or you might stay calm when you are afraid. When you do these things, people often say you are a good person.

When you wear clothes that fit you well, you feel happy. You look like you care about how you look. This is like being a good person.

You can also be a good person if you follow the right way. This way is about being safe and following what is right. It is like following a line that is clear and easy to follow.

Sometimes, being a good person is about being kind to animals. You can give them food or a home. This shows you care about all living things.
