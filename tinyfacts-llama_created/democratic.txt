A group of people who get to decide what they want is called a group of people who are in power. They are not one person who has all the power. They are many people who work together. They listen to everyone and try to make sure everyone is happy.

In a group like this, there are three people who are usually the most important. One is the leader. The leader is the one who helps the other people make the right answer. The second person is the one who helps the leader. The third person is the one who helps everyone. They work together so everyone has a say.

This kind of group is good if everyone is happy. If one person wants to say something and the others do not like it, the person who wants it can not force the others to say it. Everyone can talk and make their voice heard. This is how you get a group of people who are in power.

The word comes from the word for many. It means you are many people working together to make a group of power.
