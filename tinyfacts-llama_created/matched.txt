To match means to find something that is the same as you. You can match a person, a thing, or a place with yourself. You might look at a picture and feel like you are the person in the picture.

You can also match people. If you know someone and you talk to them many times, you can know their voice and their words. You can match a person with a place. If you like a place, you might feel happy when you are there.

If you match something, you are doing it right. You are doing it the way it should be done. It is a way of being sure that the things are the same.

Sometimes, you might match two things that are not the same. You might try to match a book to a person. This can be hard because the book is about the story and the person is not the story. You have to think about what makes the book and the person different.
