To feel a way is to allow your body to change. When you are standing, your legs are straight. But when you want to walk, you let your legs move. You turn your foot forward and your foot behind you. This makes your bottom go around. You feel a little bit of the ground under you and a little bit of the air on the outside of your legs. This is what it means to feel a way.

When you are sitting on a chair, you are not standing. You feel like you are sitting because your feet are on the floor. But you are not really sitting. You are just going around your body. Your legs are still moving, but your body is not straight. This is what it means to feel a way.

You can also feel a way when you are scared. Your heart feels a little bit hard. Your hands might shake. You might feel like you want to run away. This is your body trying to help you because it is afraid.

Sometimes you feel a way when you are happy. Your face might light up. Your body might feel warm. You might want to jump up and down. Your heart might feel full and happy. This is your body feeling good because it is glad.

To feel a way is to let go of what you are doing. You let your body go in a new direction. You might be standing and then you turn and walk. You might be sitting and then you get up and move. This is what it means to feel a way.
