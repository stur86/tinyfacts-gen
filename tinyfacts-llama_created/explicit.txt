To be clear about what is happening.

When you say something is clear, you mean it is very easy to understand. A person who is clear is not afraid to show what they think. They say what they want. They do not hide or try to keep things from you.

If you are clear, you do not surprise others. You do not make them think they are wrong. You show the truth. You say what is happening, even if it is not easy to hear.

An animal is clear too. If you see a cat, you know it is a cat. You do not need to think much. You just see the animal. If a person says something, they are clear. They are not afraid to show how they feel.

You can also be clear if you tell the truth. If you say you are not afraid, you are being clear. You are not hiding. You are telling the truth.

Sometimes, you might be clear in your words. You speak very well. You say what you mean. You do not try to change what you say. This makes you clear.

But being clear is not always easy. Sometimes you want to keep things from others. You might not want to tell them. You might want to hide. In this case, you are not clear.

When you are clear, you can trust others. You know that what they say is true. You can follow what they say. You do not have to think much. You can just believe.
