To show a strong feeling is to show that the body or mind is not going to do something. When you do not want to do something, your body or mind can be angry or very hard. This happens because your brain is not happy with what you are doing.

Imagine you are standing in front of a big, hot fire. If you reach out to touch it, your skin will burn. This is because the fire is strong and you are not ready for it. You are showing the fire that you do not want to be near it.

You might feel this kind of feeling when you are afraid. If you are afraid of a thing, your body will get very hard and your heart might beat very fast. This is not the same as being afraid of a person. Being afraid of a person is different, because you are afraid of them because of who they are.

You can also show this feeling when you do not want to do a job. If you do not want to clean the floor, you might say no. This shows that you do not want to do the work. You are not going to do it because you are angry or because you think it is not important.

Sometimes, you might feel this feeling when you are angry. You might shout or throw things. This is not the same as showing that you want to do something.
