To have something now is to be in the middle of something. When you say you have something now, you mean you already have it.

Imagine you are waiting for a friend to call you. You are waiting for a long time. You are not sure if they will answer. If you have the phone right now, you can listen to the sound and you know they are still there. You are not in the middle of nothing. You are in a place where the person is right now.

This word is used when you want to say something is happening at the moment you talk. It is like a word that tells you exactly what is going on. You can say, I have something now, or I am in the middle of this thing. It helps you know what is happening right now.
