To have a few or many things at your hands is to have a chance to do something. This is because you are not locked into a single thing. You can pick from a listened group of things or pick one from a group of many. This makes you the one in control.

Think about a person who is playing a game. They might have a few cards. But if they play with everyone, they can get more cards. If they play with only one person, they might not get any more cards. They have a few chances because there are many people.

In life, you also have many chances. You can do something today, tomorrow, next week, or next month. You can do a new job, move to a new city, or start a new business. Every day gives you another chance to act.

Even if you are in a bad place, there are still chances to be better. You can try to do better next day, next week, or next month. You can learn from your past and try a different way. You can also find a new job or a new way to live. This means you have the power to change your life.

Sometimes, things might seem very hard. You might feel afraid. But you can still have a few chances. You can try to do the right thing. You can find a good friend. You can learn. You can grow. And with these chances, you can find a way out of the hard place.
