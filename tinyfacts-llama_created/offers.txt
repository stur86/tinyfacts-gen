To offer something means to give it to someone. Imagine you are in a room with a friend. You have a book that you really like. You are not allowed to keep it. But you can give it to them. You are offering it.

You can offer a food you have. You might say, I am offering you a piece of my food. I want you to take it. You are giving it to them. You are showing you are kind.

You can also offer to help someone. If you see a friend who is sad, you can offer to sit with them. You are giving them your time. You are letting them know you care.

Sometimes, when you are talking to someone, you might say, I am offering my time. This means you are giving your time so they can talk to you. You are helping them.

You can also offer to do a job. If a person wants someone to clean their house, they might say, I am offering you the job. You are giving them the job. You are letting them know you are ready to help.
