A name can be a person, a job, or something else. It is a word that we use to talk about a person or a thing.

When you talk about a person, you might use a name. This is how you show you know a person. You can use a name to say who they are. You might say,  This is the man who is standing next to you. Or you might say,  He is the man who is on the phone.

Sometimes we use a name to remember a person. If you know a person but you can not remember their name, you can try to remember the way they look or the sound they make. You might see a picture of the person and say,  That is the man I am thinking of. Or you might hear the person say something and say,  That is who I am.

You can also use a name to show you care. If you have a friend, you might give them a name. You might say,  Hi, I am your friend. This shows you are happy to see them. Or if you are happy, you might say,  I am so happy to see you.

A name is also important when we talk about things. We need to use the right name to talk about a thing. If you say,  That is a book, you are telling the truth. You are talking about a thing. You are not talking about something else.

Sometimes people give their names to their children. This is a way to show you love them. A baby will hear their mother and father say,  My name is baby. They will learn to remember their name. They will know that the sound is the same.

In some places, you might use a name with a story. You might say,  This is the place where I grew up. This shows you know where you are. You are not just talking about a place. You are telling a story.

A name is a word that tells you who or what is there. It is a word that shows you know something.
