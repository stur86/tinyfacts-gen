To be drunk is to be very, very very very strong. This happens when you drink a lot of things that make you feel happy or make your head feel very light.

When you are drunk, you might feel like you can do anything. You might laugh a lot, talk a lot, and act like you are the best. People say this is like being in a happy place, but it is not good because it can hurt you in the long run.

Drunk people usually do not know what they are doing. They might pick up things that are not safe to touch. They might say things they do not mean. They might do things that are very wrong. They can get very angry and might hit someone or throw things.

Drunk people can also get very sick. They might have to go to the hospital. They might need to stay in a place to rest and drink water until they are better. This is why it is not good to drink a lot of things without a grown up.

If you know someone who is drunk, you should be very carefully. You should not touch them or try to get them to drink more. You should tell a grown up if you see anything wrong.
