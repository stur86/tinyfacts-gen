To know something means you understand it. It is about being clear in your mind when you learn something new. You can know things that are not in front of you right now. You have already seen them before and you know what they are. You can also know things after you learn them. You have learned them and now you can understand them.

A know leg is the one on your right foot. It is the one you walk on. It helps you move around. The other leg is the one on your left foot. It is the one you stand on. Both legs are important. They help you stay standing and move in different ways. You use your legs to run, jump, and walk.

The word know also means to have a feeling. When you know something, you can feel it. You might feel happy or sad. You might feel a bit afraid. You might feel calm. To know is like being in a place inside your mind. It is like you have a big picture of what is happening. This picture stays with you even when you are not looking at it.

Sometimes we know things that are hard to understand. We know that things change. We know that the sun moves across the sky. We know that water is wet and that air is not wet. When we know these things, we are not afraid anymore. We feel ready to face the world. We feel ready to learn more.
