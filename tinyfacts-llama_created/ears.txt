Your ears are two parts that help you hear. They are very small. You put them on your head to listen to sounds. When you hear a sound, like a dog belling or a car driving, your ears send the sound to your brain. The brain then tells your body what the sound means.

Your ears are also important for seeing. When light from the sun or a light from a window comes into your eyes, it hits the light inside your head. Your brain then uses this light to see pictures.

Sometimes, you might feel something in your ears. If a loud noise happens, it can hurt your ears. If something goes into your ear, it might feel like a tiny thing is moving inside. You can try to breathe or drink water to get the sound out.

You have two ears. One is on the left side of your head, and one is on the right side. When you move your head from one side to the other, the sound changes. This is how you can hear different people talking or music.

Your ears are very important for how you feel about the world. They help you know when it is quiet or when something is happening near you. They also help you see the world around you, even when you are not looking at anything.
