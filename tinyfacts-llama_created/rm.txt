To put things in a room means to move them into a place where they are safe. You do this because people might be afraid to stay in a room that is dark and quiet, or because there is a loud sound that makes them feel scared.

You can put many things in a room. There are books and papers on a table. You can put clothes on a bed. You can put a chair and a table in a room so you can sit and eat. Sometimes you put things in a room to sleep. You can put a soft bed under a bed with hard things on top.

You can also put things in a room to make it look nice. If you put a pretty picture on a wall, the room looks happy. If you put a green table and some green chairs, the room feels calm. You can put many things in one room. This is why people live in many rooms in a house.
