To give something to someone means you are sending it to them. Imagine you have a big box of food. You want to give it to your friend who lives far away. You find a way to get it to them. This is what we call giving or doing something for another person.

Sometimes, you give something that you own. You might give your book to your friend. You feel happy because you are helping them. Other times, you give money to a person. You give it so they can buy food or a new house.

When you say a person has been given something, it means they have been given a chance or a new thing to do. It can also mean someone has been told to do a thing. If you tell a group of people to be quiet, you are giving them a new order.

If you are asked to do something, you must do it. If you say you are going to the store, you must go there. You can not just say you are going without doing it. This is how you follow the words and the order.

To give something can make a person or a group feel happy. It can also make them feel sad if they are given a thing that hurts them.
