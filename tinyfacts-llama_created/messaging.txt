To send a word using a small box that you carry with you is called a short way. You type what you want to say on a small clear piece of paper. You can send it to anyone in the world who has a box of their own. When you send it, it goes through the air and reaches the person you want.

The person you send it to might not know you. They might not know your name. You have to use a number or a name so the person can find you. The person reads the word. They might laugh at it or tell you that you are funny. They could also write a word back, so you can talk to each other.

You can use this short way to talk to a friend you do not see every day. You can tell them you are happy or tell them you are sad. You can also send a word to a person who does not speak the same way as you. It is a way to share your ideas with other people.

This short way is very good for people who live far away. People can send words to each other in a short time. It is like a quick walk down the street, but instead of going down the street, the words go through the air.
