The word for the day before a new day is called a day number. We know this because there are only  Seven days. One day is the first, one day is the second, and so on. When we go around this number of days, we come back to the first day. The day after the first day is the second day, the day after that is the third day, and so on.

We call this day the day before the new day. This is important because it helps us keep track of how many days have passed. Imagine you have a paper with numbers from one day to the next day. If you start on the first day, you can see how many days have gone by. On the day before, you are one day short of the next day.

This word helps us move forward in our days. We have a day for work, a day for school, and a day for rest. We use this word to plan our life, to see what is coming next.
