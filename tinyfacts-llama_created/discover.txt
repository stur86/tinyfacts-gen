To find something means to look for it. When you look for a ball, you move your eyes around. You might look at the floor, then at the walls, then at the window. If you see the ball, you are the one who found it.

Sometimes, you find things that are not there. A small thing might fall out of a box. Or you might see a tiny thing in the water. When you find something, you know you were the one who did it.
