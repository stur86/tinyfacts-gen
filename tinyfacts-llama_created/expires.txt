To end something is to stop it. When you buy food or a drink, you buy it so you can eat later. When you buy a book, you buy it so you can read it later. But if you finish the book and you can't bring yourself to put it down, you have ended it.

Things that are things can also end. A thing that is in the ground can end when the ground falls down on it. A thing that is in the water can end when it gets tired and stops moving. A thing that is in the sky can end when it falls to the ground.

We also use this word for money. When something is in a box and the box is opened, the thing inside ends. You pay for it and you have it. If you do not pay, you do not get it anymore.

Sometimes, we say something ends a fight. If you and someone say you are done, it means you are stopping what is going on between you. You are not going to talk anymore, and you do not want to be near each other. The fight is over, and you can go.

Also, we say something ends a day. If you go to bed early and you have a good night, you might have an early day tomorrow. Tomorrow is a new day, and something that happened today ends and something new begins.
