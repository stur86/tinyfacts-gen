To carry something means to hold it. You can carry a small thing you use to drink water. You might carry a ball for a game. A person can also carry money. They might carry a bag with food. This word is used when you are not holding a thing, but you are still in the same place.

If you are carrying a heavy thing, you might feel a bit heavy. You might have to push the thing so it does not fall down. If a person is carrying a child, they are also carrying the child. This means they are holding the child to help the child stay safe.

You can also carry a feeling. If you are very happy, you might carry that feeling. It is like a warm light inside you. Sometimes, you might carry a feeling you do not want to show to others. This is because you are afraid. When you carry a feeling, you are keeping it inside your body.
