To make something better

When you break something, you might need to fix it. You can do this by putting pieces together. The pieces are the parts that are broken. You take the pieces and put them back in the right way. This makes the thing work again.

Think about your car. If you hit a car, the car might hurt. The police take the car to a place to fix it. They take out the part that broke and put a new one in. The car is fixed. You do not have to worry anymore.

You can also fix things that are broken at home. If a chair leg breaks, you might use a piece of wood to fix it. You put the wood under the leg and hold it tight until the wood starts to dry. After a few days, the wood is strong again and the chair leg is fixed.

People use this word in many ways. If a friend does something wrong, you might tell them they need to fix it. If a house has a big problem, the people who work there might call them to come and fix it.

Sometimes, we fix things because we are sad. If you are sad, you might want to make the world a better place. You might want to help people or make a new thing to make them happy. In this case, fixing is like giving your heart a big hug.
