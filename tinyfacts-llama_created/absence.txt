To be in an area means you are there. When you are in a room, you are in the room. When you are in a city, you are in that city. When you are in a school, you are at that school.

An empty place is a room with no people or things in it. It looks like a big box. If you go into an empty room, you are in the room. You see nothing. There is no light coming from the window.

An empty thing is something that does not have people or things in it. If you have a thing that you use every day, it is not empty. You put things inside it. It holds water or food.

If a person does not feel well, they might be in an area where they can not go. If you are in a room, you might feel sick, too. Your body is not working well.

If you are in a place with many people, it might feel empty. There are not as many people as there are rooms. This can happen when many houses are empty or when many cars are parked in one place.
