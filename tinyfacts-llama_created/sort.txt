To sort something means to put it in a way that makes it easy to see and understand. Think of it like playing a game with your friends.

Imagine you have a box with a few small rocks. You want to put them into a box with the red rocks, the blue rocks, and the green rocks. If you put them in order, you can see which rock is red, which is blue, and which is green. This makes it easy to find the rock you want.

You can also sort food or clothes. You put the food in a box that is easy to see. You put the red food at the top and the green food at the bottom. This way, you know what is red and what is green. You can use this to find the food you want to eat.

You can also sort animals. If you have many animals that are all of one kind, you can sort them by the color of their further. You put the animals with the red further at the back and the animals with the blue further at the front. This helps you keep them together.

Sometimes, you sort things that are not even things at all. You might sort papers by who wrote them. You put all the papers from one person in one box and all the papers from another person in another box. This makes it easy to find who wrote what.

A computer also sorts things. It uses a way of thinking that is like the one above. It looks at the numbers and letters in a word and puts them in a order that makes sense. This helps the computer find the word you want quickly.

So, to sort something means to put it in a way that makes it easy to see and understand. It helps us know what is there and how it fits with what else is there.
