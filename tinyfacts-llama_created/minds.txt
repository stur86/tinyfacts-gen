To have a mind is to be in your head. It means you have a brain inside your body and your brain is always working. You do not just sit and look at things. Your brain is always doing things.

When you are happy, your mind is happy too. You feel good, and you want to do things. You can think about the day that just happened. You remember how you felt when you walked down the street. Your mind is always thinking, even when you are asleep.

You can also have a bad mind. This is when you feel angry, scared, or sad. Your brain gets very strong and it wants to keep you safe. It tells you to be afraid so you will stay inside and do not go out. This is why people go to the doctor if they have a bad mind.

Your mind is also like a book. You can read what is inside the book. If you are sad, you can look at pictures of happy times. If you are angry, you can look at pictures of things that hurt you. Your mind is always reading and writing.

Some people have a very bright mind. They can learn a lot very fast. They can remember many words and do many things at once. They can also think about many things at once. They are very good at finding answers.

You can also have a dark mind. This is when you have a bad brain that wants to hurt you. You might feel like you are a bad person. You might act in ways that are not nice. This is why a doctor might give a person a kind word to help them feel better.

When we say a person has a good mind, we mean they are kind and they are not afraid to show their face. When we say a person has a bad mind, we mean they are afraid and they do not want to show their face.
