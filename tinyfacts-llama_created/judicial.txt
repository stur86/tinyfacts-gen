The word you are looking for is called a just person. A person who is just is a person who does not do wrong. They follow the same way that everyone else follows. They do not take what is not their own.

Imagine you have a fight with a friend. You both want to be right. One person says they are right and the other person says they are wrong. A just person listens to both sides. They hear what the other person is saying. Then they decide. If the person with the most points is still wrong, the just person says they are wrong.

A just person also follows the words they say. If they say they are going to do something, they must do it. They do not change their word later to say something different.

In a big group of people, there is one person who is just. They hear all the people and decide what is right and wrong. This is important because it helps keep everyone safe.

Some people are not just. They do what they want, even if they are wrong. They might take something that is not their own, even if they do not say it is wrong. They do not listen to anyone else. They are only their own.
