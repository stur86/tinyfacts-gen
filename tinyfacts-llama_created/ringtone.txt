A ring tone is a sound you hear when your phone rings. It is the sound you hear before a person answers. It is usually a short sound that starts with a high sound and ends with a low sound.

Think of it like a bell. When you ring a bell, you hear the bell and then the bell stops. A ring tone is like a quick ring of a bell. It is a sound that happens before a person answers.

Some phones have a ring tone that is set to a song. If you set your ring tone to a song, when your phone rings, it starts playing the song. This is a fun way to show who you are.

Ring tones can also be different for different people. One person might have a ring tone that is a long sound, while another person has a ring tone that is very short. You can find ring tones on the phone book, on the computer, or on other phones.
