To carry something means you move it from one place to another. You might carry a bag of food to school, or a ball to a game. When you carry something, you are holding it. You keep it in your arms.

Imagine you have a friend who wants to go to the park. They want to play with a ball. You can help them. You pick them up, put them in your arms, and walk to the park. When you are there, you give the ball to the friend. They can now play.

You can also carry money. When you go to a shop, you give the money to the person who runs the shop. They take the money and put it in a safe place so you can use it later.

You can also carry animals. Some people have a cat or a dog. They keep the cat in their home and give it food and a bed to sleep in. The cat is not a person, but it is still a living thing. It needs care and love.

Sometimes, you carry things that are big. You might carry a heavy box or a very long bag. You hold onto it until you reach your place. This is a way to move things from one place to another, even if the things are very heavy.
