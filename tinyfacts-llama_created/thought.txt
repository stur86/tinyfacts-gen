To think is to put your mind to work. When you think, you are doing your brain work. Your brain is the thing inside your head that helps you remember, learn, and stay safe. When you think, your brain is busy.

You can think about many things. You might think about a game you play. You might think about a food you love. You might think about a person who is important to you. Or you might think about something that happened in your day. Your brain is always doing this, even when you are not paying attention.

Sometimes, you just think about nothing at all. You might sit and watch the air go by. You might look at a picture in your mind. You might think about your favorite place. Your brain is always working, even when you are not paying attention.

You can also think about the world around you. You might think about the sun coming up in the morning. You might think about the way the light falls on a tree. You might think about the sound of a dog belling in the street. Your brain is always taking in new things, even when you are not thinking about them.

Thinking is how we learn. When you learn something new, you are putting your brain to work. You are using your brain to remember the words, the pictures, or the stories. Your brain is the one doing the work.
