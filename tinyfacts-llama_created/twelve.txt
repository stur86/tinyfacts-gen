Twenty is a number that is more than ten. It is a number after ten. We use it when we tell how many times a day we have a break. If you work from morning to night, you might have two or three breaks. If you have four or five of these breaks, you have twenty.

We also use it when we talk about things that are in the middle. If you have a small animal, it might be two or three. If it is much bigger, it might be twenty. This number helps us understand how many of something there are.
