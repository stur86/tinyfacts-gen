To make a comment about something

When you say something, you are making a comment. This is when you tell a story, or when you show how something is. You are not trying to tell everyone what to do, but you want to share your own ideas.

A comment might be about a person, a place, or an eventually. You might say, I love this food because it is very good. Or you might say, I am afraid of this thing because it is very loud. You are not trying to tell anyone, just sharing what you think.

A person can make a comment in many ways. They can laugh and smile, or they can look serious. They might use words they like, or they might use words that seem strange to others. They might also use body words. This means they are not just talking, but they are also moving their face, their arms, and their hands.

Comments can happen in many places. In a movie, someone might say something. In a book, the words might tell you what is happening. In a room, people might talk and make comments about the things they see.

The word can also be used for other things. You can make a comment about a game. If someone does something good, you might say, that was a good move. Or you might say, you should have done that. You are not trying to tell them what to do, but you are helping them see what is happening.

In a way, comments help us understand each other. When you hear someone, you might think, what are they doing? You might wonder if they are happy or sad. You might ask them questions to learn more. This is how we stay friends and build relationships.
