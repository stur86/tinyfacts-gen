An age is a time that passes. It is after a time and before another time.

Imagine a tree growing. A tiny baby grows into a small child. A small child grows into a boy or a girl. A boy grows into a man. A man grows into a man who has children of his own. This is an age.

A person can have many ages. You might say, someone is in their age. But they are not a baby anymore. They are a grown person with many years of life. Some people are very old. They might be more than  hundred years old.

Some ages have more meaning than others. Being old is not always good. You might feel tired and not want to do anything. You might feel sad because you have lived through a lot of times and seen many people come and go. This is a heavy age.

Some ages are happy. You might feel like you are the best person in the world. You might have many great friends and do many fun things. You might feel like you can do anything.

Sometimes, people change their age. If a child grows up to be a young man, the child becomes a young man. This is called growing up.

In some places, people are not allowed to say how old they are. They might say, they are twenty years old, but they are actually much older. This is not nice.
