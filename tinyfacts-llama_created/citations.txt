A word is made up of many tiny parts. When you write something, you take these tiny parts and put them together in a way that makes sense. You use the word to tell others about what you wrote.

One of these tiny parts is a word that you use. This word tells you what to say. You might use the word to say a name, a word, or a number. This word is the beginning of your story. Without this word, you would not have a story at all.

Another tiny part is a number. Numbers tell us how many of something there are. If you say there are five books, the word for the number is five. Numbers are like tiny numbers that you use to tell stories.

Sometimes you write a story that has many different words. You use words to tell a story. In this case, the word is the story. You are the writer. You use the words to make the story happen.

When you write something, you often need to give the story to others so they can read it. You do this by giving the story to a person. This person then reads the story and maybe writes their own story with the same words. This is how stories are shared.

In a big group of people, many of these tiny parts are put together. The words are shared with others, and the stories are passed down. This is how ideas and stories become part of a group.

Cities are also used in the world. In a group of people, a person who does not do what is right might be called a cities. This means they are not following the group. They might act like they are better than everyone else. In this way, the word shows that the person is not part of the group anymore.
