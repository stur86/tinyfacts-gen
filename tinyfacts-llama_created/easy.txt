To easy something means to make it very small or easy to do. It can also mean to make something much better than it was before.

Think about a road. If you are walking a long way, you feel tired and need to stop a lot. But if you are walking on a soft road, your feet hurt less, and you can go faster. You do not feel as much pain. This is easy because the road is not hard to walk on. You can still see things around you and hear sounds. You can move your body to one side and then the other. It is not very hard.

Sometimes, when you do something that was hard for you before, it becomes easier. Maybe you learned to play a new game. You were afraid at first, but now you feel happy and want to play more. You are not afraid anymore. You feel like you can do it. This is easy because you have learned and become better at it.

You can also use this word to talk about the world around you. If a big city is very busy, it might seem hard to find a quiet place to sit. But if you walk a little further, you find a quiet park. The sounds are much softer and the air is cleaner. The city is still big, but it is not as hard to deal with. It is easier to get what you need when you walk a bit further.

In a small group, someone might say, I am easy to talk to. This means that the person is not very strong or is not very good at being angry. They are kind and can talk to everyone. They do not get angry easily and can listen to what others have to say. They are easy to be around because they are easy to talk to.
