To finish something is to do it completely. When you start to do something, you begin. But when you are done, you finish it. This is important.

Imagine you build a house. You start to put the floor. You add the walls and the windows. After many hours of work, the house is finished. You did not stop until everything was done. You finished what you started.

If you do something and then you stop, you did not finish. You started, but you did not end what you started. To finish something means you are the end of it. You are the last part. 

To finish a story is also important. When you finish a story, you tell the end. You show what happened and why. This is important for people who like to listen to stories or watch movies.

Sometimes, you might say you are almost done with a job. This means you are very close to the end. You have done a lot of work, and you still have a little bit left. You are very close to being finished.
