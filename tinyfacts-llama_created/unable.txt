When you are not able to do something, it means you are not strong enough or you do not have the power. You might be very tired or very sick. If you try to do something but you are not ready, you can say you are not able.

Imagine you want to go to a big building, but you are not a good runner. If you run, you get a big stop. You are not able to go to the building yet. You still have to work on your running so you can go soon.

Sometimes, you might be able to do something but you are not sure if you can do it right now. You might feel a little nervous. This can happen when you are about to go to a new place or when you are about to do something important.

You can also be not able because you are not in the right place. If you want to go to a city that is very far away, you might not be able to do it because you have not enough money to buy a bus or because you do not have a car.
