An idea that is the beginning of something that is long. When you hear a song, it is one part. An idea is when you know the whole thing. You know how it starts and ends. You know how it feels. You know what happens next.

Imagine you like a book. You read one story, then you read another. Each story is like a song. But you do not just listen to one song. You read the whole book. You see the pictures and the words together. The book is the idea.

Some people make these things. They write and play music. They put the songs together so you can hear them all at once. This is why you can buy many of them. You can listen to one story and then another. It is like a long story.

You can also make them for people. When a person loves a movie, they might buy it many times. They get the story many times. The person keeps the story in their home so they can watch it again and again.

Sometimes, you can make many of them in one place. People make many stories in one building. This is why music stores are big. They have many books of music. You can listen to them all at once.

Everyone does this. People write stories, make movies, and make music. They all share the same idea. They all start with a story and end with the whole thing.
