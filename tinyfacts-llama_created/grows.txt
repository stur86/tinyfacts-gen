To grow means you are getting bigger. When you are a tiny baby, you are small. As you eat food and drink water, your body gets stronger. You get taller and your skin gets softer. Your hair might grow on your head. Your arms and legs get bigger. You are becoming a person. This happens in many ways.

Some things grow from the ground. A tree grows from a small piece of wood. It uses the wood and water from the sky to grow. It makes its own food from the green stuff around it. Other things grow on a wall. They use the wall for their food and grow big. This is how a house can look very tall.

You can also grow in your mind. When you learn new things, your brain gets stronger. Your memory gets better. You remember things better after you have seen them. Your heart might grow stronger too. When you love someone, your heart feels happy all the time.

Growing is a change. You are not always the same. You get new parts and become different. This is how you change when you grow up, when you grow older, or when you learn new things.
