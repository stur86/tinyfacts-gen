To respond means you give a word or a thing in return. When you hear a voice, you might say a word. If a friend asks you a question, you give an answer. This is a way to show you are interested.

When you are asked a question, you are the one being asked. You do not simply stand there and not answer. You use your words to tell what you think. This is a way to show you care. It is a way to show you are thinking about what is being asked.

You can also respond to a word. If someone says something, you might say something back. You can answer a question or point out a problem. This is a way to show you understand what the other person said.

Sometimes, you respond to a thing. If a friend gives you a thing, you might give back something of your own. You might take the thing and keep it. You are the one who gives the thing back.

To respond also means you give a thing when you do not have it. You might say you are busy, but you still give the thing you are holding. This is a way to show you are not the one with the thing.

In a group, people often respond to each other. If one person is talking, others might join in. They might add their own word or point out a problem. This is a way to show you are part of the group.

In a fight, you can respond. If someone is trying to hurt you, you might stand up and say, I am not going to let you do that. This is a way to show you are not afraid.

So, to respond is to give something. It is a way to show you care, to answer a question, or to show you are not the one with the thing. It is about being a part of something.
