To ask someone means you want to know something. You might want to know if they like a thing or if they have something for you. You do this by talking to them.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You want to know if your friend has a new ball. You say,  Can you show me the ball? Your friend smiles and says, Yes, I have it. You are happy because your friend told you they have the ball.

When you ask a person, you are using the word  to start. If you are asking a question, your first word should be  how,  why,  what,  when,  or  which.

If you want to know something, you say, I want to know. This is like asking a question. If you say, I need money, you are also asking for help. You need something, and you want it right now.

In a school, if you want to go to a different room, you ask the teacher. You say, Can I go to the other room? The teacher might say, No, you can't go there. This is a way of asking someone to help you.

You can also ask if something is true. If you are wondering if a book is real, you might ask, Is that book real? If the person you talk to says yes, you know you are right.

So, asking is a way to try to find the answer to a question. It is a way to show you care about what is going on.
