When you have many things to do, you need to pick out the ones you want to do. To do this, you look for the things that are important. You might look at the listening music you like, the books you read, the movies you watch, or the games you play. You decide which ones you want to do.

You do not pick one thing. You pick many. You might pick all the music you like at one time. You might pick two books you read last week. You might pick one movie you watched the night before and another one you plan to watch tomorrow.

To pick something is to decide it is the one you want. It is to say, I want to do this. It is to say, I am going to do this. This is how we do things every day.

You can also pick a person. You might pick a friend you like. You might pick a person who helps you. This is when you want to be near this person.
