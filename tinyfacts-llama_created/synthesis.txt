To make something new is to add pieces together. You take a few parts and make them work as one. This is what you do when you build a house or make music. You take different pieces and join them so they can stand alone.

Sometimes, this happens in your brain too. When you learn something new, your brain takes pieces from your past and adds them to the new pieces. This is how you remember things. Your brain is always making new things.

You can also use this word with other animals. When one animal does something, it is a kind of making. If an animal makes food, it is a kind of work. This is how all living things are made.
