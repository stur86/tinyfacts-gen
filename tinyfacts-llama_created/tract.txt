A long line of things that are the same or very close to each other. This is often used when talking about very big areas of land. You could say a long line of houses or roads or a long line of trees.

You can also use it for people. If someone has been following another person for a long time, they say they have followed them in a track. This is like a long line of people, but one is following another.

It can mean to follow many things at once. If you are trying to do many things at the same time, you are following them in a track. You do many things and they follow you.
