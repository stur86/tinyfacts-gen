Pain is a feeling inside your body when something is wrong. It is like a loud voice in your brain that says, you hurt.

You can feel pain when your skin is hurt. If you cut your finger, the skin around the cut is very red and you might feel a lot of pain. You might want to stop touching the finger to stop the pain.

You can also feel pain if you get a booked foot. When the foot hurts, your brain sends out a warning. This is because the foot is telling you, something is wrong here.

Sometimes, pain is a sign that something is very wrong. If you have a hurt leg, you might feel pain. If you do not move, the leg can become much worse.

You can not see pain. You only know it because your brain tells you. A person can not feel pain if they are very strong or very old. But a baby and a small child can feel pain very well because their brains are still growing.

To help with pain, people use many things. They use cold water to stop the pain. They use a bag of water to put the foot in. They use a small bottle with a hard part on top to press on the hurt area. They also use strong drinks to help the body get better.

Sometimes, people want to be a little bit better. They might take a drink with a lot of water. They might put a soft thing under their body to help the hurt feel less.

In a hospital, people have strong things that help with pain. They are like big bags of water. They sit on the floor next to the bed. When you need to be a little bit better, you tell the doctor. They give you the bag and help you feel a little bit better.
