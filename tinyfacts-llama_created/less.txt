When you say something is less, you mean the number is smaller. If you have a bag of food and you eat five pieces, you have less food now. You do not have as much as you had before.

Sometimes you might say something is less than something else. If your friend drives very fast, you might say, I am less safe than you. This means the person who drives fast is doing things you are afraid of.

You can also say something is less strong. If a person is less strong than another person, they do not have the same power. This is because their body and brain are not as good at doing things.

If you have money, you might say you are less happy. You do not have as much money as you want. This can make you feel sad or worried.

Sometimes, you might say something is less good. If a movie is less good than another one, it is not as good. If a person is less good at a job, they are not as good as the person who did it better.

We also use this word when we talk about time. If you are less than the time before you, you are behind. If you are less time now than you were before, you are ahead.
