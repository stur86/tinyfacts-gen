When you say a friend is your favorite person, you mean you like them more than any other person. You know their face, their words, and what makes them laugh. You trust them with your most important stories and your biggest worries. You feel safe when you are near them.

If you know someone who makes you feel safe, you can talk to them about anything. You tell them how you feel without fear. You can be your self around them. This is what it means to be a friend.

Sometimes people say, you are very close to your family. This means you are part of a group of people who love you and are always there for you. You feel a deep feeling of love and care. You know you can always go to them if you need help.

Being a friend is about being there when someone needs you. It means you are there to listen, to help, and to be a good friend.
