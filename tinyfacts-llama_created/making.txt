To make something means to put things together so that it can work. You can make a small thing with your hands. You might put pieces of wood, small pieces of paper, and a small box together. This box will hold a book or a game.

You can also make a food. You can start with a small ball of air. You add water and then a bit of sweet stuff. After you add the sweet stuff, the air turns into a soft white thing that looks like a soft ball. This soft white thing is the food. You can eat it as a drink or put it in a bag to take home.

Sometimes, you make a thing that helps people. You can build a house for a family. You take many small pieces of wood and make a strong wall. You put many of these walls together so that the house can stand strong against the wind and rain.

You can also make a thing that tells you a story. A computer can make a story for you. It takes words and makes them into pictures that you see. You can also make a thing that lets you talk to many people at once. This is like a big room where people can talk to each other.

Making is a very important job. It helps us make our lives better. It allows us to build homes, eat food, and tell stories. Without making, we would not have many of the things we use every day.
