A word used to keep a door open is called a word. It is a set of letters or numbers that only you know. You type it in when you want to enter a room that you do not want others to enter.

Imagine you have a box with a lock on it. You know the word to open the lock. If you want to get into the room, you type the word. The computer or the lock does not ask you again. Only you know the word.

You can use a word that is easy to remember, like a word you learned when you were a child, or a word that is hard to guess. You can write it down in a book, or you can type it into a computer.

Many people use this word to keep their own rooms or their cars locked. You might type it into a phone to get a lock on your phone. Some people also use this word for their computers so that only they can see what they see.

We also use words for things that are not locked, like a word for a game. You might type a word to get a new game. Or, you might use a word for a story. You tell the story, and the people listen.

You should never give your word to someone you do not know. If someone asks you a word, you should say no. You can say, I do not know the word. Or, you can say, I do not know the word now.
