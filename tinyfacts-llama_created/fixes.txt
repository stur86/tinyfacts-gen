When you feel something is wrong, you might want to fix it. To fix something means you make it right again. This can happen with things you touch, things you see, or even things that are broken inside you.

Think about a small thing on your desk. If it falls over, it might break. You can fix it. You might use your finger to press it back into place. Or you might pick it up and put it away. This makes the thing look the same again.

You can fix many different kinds of things. You might fix a car if it is broken. You might fix a dog if it is hurt. You might fix a book if it has a small problem. This helps people and animals feel better.

Sometimes, fixing something is not just about making it look the same. You might need to fix it so it works better. If you have a phone and it does not work, you might need to fix it. You might need to change the inside of the phone or fix the power. This makes the phone ready for use again.

Even when you are not fixing a thing, you are still fixing yourself. When you feel sad, you might need to fix your face. You use your face to show you are happy again. When you feel angry, you might need to fix your body. You use your body to stand up straight and show you are ready to fight.

Fixing is an important part of life. It helps us feel better when things are wrong. It helps us feel ready to move forward.
