To show something is to make it clear. Imagine you want to tell a friend where you are. You stand up and walk to a place. You open your arms and say, I am over there. This is showing. You are showing the friend where you are.

You can also show a picture. If you have a picture of a dog and you show it to a child, the child understands what the dog looks like. They see the dog and think, this is a dog. This is showing.

When you show something, you are letting everyone know that it is true. If you are afraid, you show your face. If you have a story to tell, you show it to the person who listens.

Showing is important. It helps us understand each other. It helps us know if we are right or wrong. We show our words and our bodies, and then we see how others feel.
