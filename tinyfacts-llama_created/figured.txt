To figured something usually means to think about it a lot before you know the answer. It is when you try to understand something without looking at it for a long time.

Imagine you are trying to find a small thing in the ground. You look at the ground and the ground looks like a big line. You look at the line again and again. You think about what it looks like. After a while, you see that the small thing is on the line. You figured it out.

This is the same with a story. If you are trying to figure out what is going to happen next, you might think about the past and try to see what will happen. You might ask yourself, what was before? What was after? What is happening now? You are thinking about the story until you find the answer.

Sometimes, you might figure something by looking at a picture. If you look at a picture and then you watch a movie, you might see the same thing. You figured out what the movie is about. This is a kind of figuring.

You can also use this word when you have a problem and you are trying to find the answer. If you are trying to fix a thing, you might look at it and try to figure out why it is wrong. You are trying to understand what the problem is.
