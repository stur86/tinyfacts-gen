To be in a way that is more than one other way.

You are bigger than your sister. You are taller than your brother. You have a bigger chest than your mother. You are not as small as you were when you were a baby.

Being in a way that is more than one other way is like a line. When you are one way, you are on the line. When you are more than one way, you are on the other side of the line. The line is like a road that goes through the whole world. Every person is on the line at some time.

You can also use it to talk about things. If you say something is bigger than something else, you are saying it is more. This can be true or not true. If something is bigger, it is more. If something is smaller, it is less.

Sometimes, we say we are in the same way. If you are taller than your friend, you are in the same way. You are not more or less. You are the same.

It is also about how things look. If a book is bigger than another book, you can see that. You can tell the book is bigger by looking at it.

So, being in a way that is more than one other way is about how things stand in relationships to one another.
