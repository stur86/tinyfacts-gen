A person is a human who lives in a place. They pay money to live there. They follow the same ways as everyone else.

When a group of people does something, they are all together. They act as one group. They share the same ideas and follow the same way of doing things. They are part of the same world.

A city has many people. The police are the ones who help keep everyone safe. They are like a group of people who work together to keep the area clean and safe. They help if someone is broken or if a person is in trouble.

You can also say a group of people is a group of friends. When you are with your friends, you feel happy. You laugh and talk. You are together and have fun.

A group can be big. There are many people in one city, one town, or one area. A group can also be small. One family or one person might be a group.

Sometimes, a group can change. When a new person comes to live in a place, they may not know the ways yet. They might need help from the people who already live there. This can make the group feel strange at first. But as time goes by, they learn and become a part of the group too.

A group can also be about ideas. A group of people who want the same thing can work together. They might have different ways, but they all want the same thing. They are all about the idea.

Being a person means you live in a world with other people. You follow the same ways as everyone else. You do not break any more than the world already has. You do not hurt anyone else. You act like everyone else.
