When you want something, you have an idea about it. This idea is what you mean. It tells you what the thing is and what you need it for. It is about what you want to happen.

You might want money. You might want to go to a place where you love to be. You have this idea in your head. It is what makes you want to act. When you act on this idea, you are following what you want.

If you do not follow your idea, you act on something else. You might act on a story, a game, or a joke. This is not what you wanted to do. You followed something else. You did not follow what you wanted.

People do this all the time. They have an idea about a place. Then they go there. They do not follow the idea. They follow another idea. They might want to see a city, but they go to a far away place.

Sometimes, your idea does not work out like you wanted. You might go to a place and find nothing you like. You might feel sad. You feel like you were wrong. Your idea was good, but it did not happen. This can be very hard.

But you can still try again. You can learn from what did not work. You can follow another idea. You can try to do something different. You can still follow what you want.
