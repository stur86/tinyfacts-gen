When things go wrong because of other things happening at the same time, this is called a problem. The word can also mean something that makes it hard to understand or see.

Imagine you are trying to hear a song. But there is a loud voice from another room. The loud voice is the thing that is causing a problem. The loud voice is like the thing that is doing something wrong.

If you put a small rock in the water, the water will move around and make the rock feel heavy. The water and the rock are doing something together that is making it hard to understand.

In a room, if a loud person talks and another person wants to talk, the other person may not hear the other person. The loud person is doing something wrong. The other person is doing something right.

When people speak, words can go into each other. If one person says something and the other person hears it, the words can cause a problem. It is like the words are doing something wrong.
