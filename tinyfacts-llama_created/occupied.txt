When a room or a space is filled with people, things, or animals, it means they are there. This can happen because a group of people are working there. Or, it can happen because many animals are living there. If you do not want to be in a place, you might say it is busy.

You can also say a place is busy if many cars are going by. This is because many people are on their way home or to school. The room is not empty because people are inside. They are doing work, eating food, or talking to each other. They might be laughing or talking quietly. This makes the space feel more like a group of people than a single person.

Sometimes, a place can be busy even when there is no one there. If you leave your room and the door closes, you can still hear the sound of a computer or a phone. The room is filled with sounds, and it is busy.
