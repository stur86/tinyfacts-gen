When someone is talking to you, they call you. It is like sending a short sound that lets people know where they are and who they are. They use this word to talk to friends, family, or even animals.

To call something is to send a small sound so it can find you. You might call a friend who lives far away. You might call a phone. A phone is a box that makes loud sounds and lets you talk to people. If you press a number, the phone looks for that number. When it finds it, it lets you speak.

Sometimes, you call a dog. You might call it when you want to play with it. A dog hears the sound and goes to find you. If you are in a dark place, you can call a friend or a family person. They will hear the sound and come to find you.

You can also call a place. If you are going to a new city, you might call the city to ask for directions. This is like sending a word to get help.

A phone call is like a short story. You send a word, and the word goes through the air to the other person. When the person gets the word, they listen. It is a way to talk with each other when you are far away.
