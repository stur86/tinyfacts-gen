To box is to fight with a small hard thing. People do this for fun. They wear a round hard thing on their head and on their arms and legs. The thing does not hurt you at all. It is only for showing how strong you are.

When you box, you stand facing a friend. They hit you with their hands. You try to hit back with your hands. You also jump and move your body. This makes the thing on your head go around your head many times.

There are different types of box. One is for the men who are very strong. The other is for the young people. Young people often play this game in a large room with a floor. They stand on the floor and hit each other. The person who hits the most people is the one who did well.

Boxing is a way to show you are a good person. It helps people be safe. If you are very strong and you fight, you can stop the other person from doing any more bad things.
