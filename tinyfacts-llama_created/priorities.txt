To have a top thing is to have the most important. You do this when you are very busy. You do not have time for other things. You do them one after the other. You do not think about them. You only think about one thing at a time.

Think about a person who is very old. They are tired. They want to be safe. They want to rest. They do not want to be in any more trouble. They know what they need to do. They do not worry about other things. They have their top thing.

You can also have a top thing in your life. You do not worry about money. You do not worry about your friends. You only think about the one thing that is most important to you. You are happy because you are doing what you want.

If you have a job, you must have a top thing. You must do your work. You must not be late. You must be there when you say you are. You do not think about your family while you are at work. You are only there to do your job.

When you have a top thing, you feel good. You feel safe. You feel like you are doing what is right. You do not worry about what others think. You just do what you need to do.
