To have a different way of thinking or being is to have a different way of doing things. When you say a person or thing is a different way, you mean they do not act the same as you do. You are not the same.

Think about a song. If you listen to the same song many times, you start to know it very well. You know every word and every note. But if you hear the same song once and then another time, you hear it for the first time. You see a new side of it. That is like having a different way of thinking. You are not the same person, but you hear the world in a different way.

Sometimes you see a person. You do not like them. You think they are mean. You might shout at them. They are not the same person. They act in a way that is different from how you act. You have a different way of thinking. You are not the same person, but you see the world through your eyes.

People have different ways of doing things. Some go to school. Some go to work. Some have a family. Some are alone. We are all different, and that is what makes the world so interesting.
