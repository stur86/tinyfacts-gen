Visit means to see something or someone. It is like going to a place and looking at the things inside. You might go to a store, a school, or a city to see the people, the things, and the places.

A very old kind of movie was called Visit. It was on a thing with a long line. You would stand in line for a long time. Then, you went into a room with a big box inside. The box had many short lines on it. You went through each line one after the other until you saw the movie. This kind of movie was very good for people who like to watch many stories at once.

Today, we call this kind of movie something old. Some people say it is a thing of the past. But it is still remembered by many because it shows how people did things a long time ago.
