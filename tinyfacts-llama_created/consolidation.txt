To put together means to group things together and make them one. Imagine you have many small pieces of wood. Each piece is small and alone. But now, you put them all together in a way that they are all one thing. You have one strong wall with many pieces of wood around it. This wall is stronger than each piece alone.

When things are not in order, it is often hard to see what is true. We might feel like there are many different ideas and no one way to know which is right. But when we put things together, we can see how they fit. We can see which ideas work well together and which do not.

In business, when a person or a group of people work together, they can make a plan. They put their ideas together and make a plan to do those things. This plan helps them stay on the same side and work together to get things done.

Sometimes, when people talk, they might say something is old. This means something is not new anymore. But when we put things together, we can find new ways to do the same things. We can use old things in new ways to make life easier. This is how we can change the way we live, the way we work, and the way we play.
