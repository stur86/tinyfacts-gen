To have something is to be true. When you are told something is so, you can show it is true. This happens when you look at things that already happened. You can see them. You can see if the story is real or if there are other things that are the same.

If you tell your friends that you are going to a place, and you really are, then you have done something. Your friends know that you are going. They can see you. You did not just say you were going. You showed they were right.

This is what we call a fact. A fact is a thing that has been shown to be true. This can be a person, a thing, a time, or a place. Facts are important because they help us know what is real.

Sometimes, we say someone is a fact. This means they are true. It is like saying someone is a real thing.
