To eat something means you take it into your body. You can eat food from a bag, a box, or from a table. Food gives you the power to run, to think, and to stay strong.

When you eat, you take in small bits of the food. Your body breaks them down into tiny pieces. These tiny pieces go into your stomach. Your stomach is a hard thing inside your tighter. It helps break down the food so you can get the good stuff.

After you eat, your body uses the food for different jobs. Some jobs help you move. Some jobs help you grow. Some jobs keep you from getting sick. Your body uses the food for all these jobs every day.

Sometimes, people eat more than they need. This can make their body big and heavy. It can also make their breath hot. This is because their body is making more air to help them breathe. Sometimes, eating too much can be bad for your body. It can make you feel very full and heavy, even after you have not eaten at all.

It is also important to know when to stop eating. If you eat too much before dinner, you might feel sick later. If you eat too much before you go to bed, you might feel tired the next day. It is always good to listen to your body and eat when you are not very full.
