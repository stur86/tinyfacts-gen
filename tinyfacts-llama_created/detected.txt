To see something is to know it is there. If you see a person, you know they are standing next to you. If you see a cat, you know it is sitting on the floor. To see something means you notice it.

When you use this word, you are talking about something you found. You were looking for it and you see it. This word can also mean something you heard. If you hear a sound, you have heard it. If you hear a word, you have heard the word.

Sometimes, to say something is caught is to say it is seen. If you are caught doing something you should not be doing, you have seen the act. This means you have noticed the act with your eyes.

In a group, if someone tells a story, everyone hears it. If everyone sees what happened, then the story is known. This word is important because it shows we are all watching what is happening.
