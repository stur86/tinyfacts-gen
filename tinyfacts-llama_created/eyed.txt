To see something is a very important thing. When you look at a person, you see their face. You see their eyes, their hair, and their skin. You see if they are happy or if they are sad.

When you see a cat, you see its eyes, its ears, and its body. You see how it moves and how it acts. You see if it is afraid or if it is playing.

Even a small dog can see a lot. It can see the face of a person who is walking down the street. It can see the door of a house. It can see if a person is going to say something or if they are going to run away.

But you can also see things that are not there. You can see a tiny thing on the floor. You can see a drop of water. You can see a tiny bit of air. Your eyes are like a door that opens and closes. They let in light and let out dark. They help you see the world.

Sometimes you see something that is not meant for you to see. Maybe you see a house that is not your house. Maybe you see a person who is not your friend. But even when you see something you do not want to see, your eyes can help you understand what is happening.
