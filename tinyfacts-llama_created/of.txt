The word of is used to talk about things that are important. It is about who does something or what they say.

When you say something, you are of something. This means you are a part of it. You can be of a group, a place, a time, or a thing. This helps people understand each other.

Imagine you are a child. Your mom is of you. She is your mother. She is the one who brought you into the world. You are a child of her. You are a part of her. This means you are under her and she is under you.

You can also be of a place. If you live in a city, you are of that city. You are a part of the city. You have a home and a job there. You know the streets and the people.

The word of is also about people. If someone is of a group, they are a part of that group. They like the same things, they believe the same way, and they act in the same way. They are like them.

Sometimes, of is used to talk about things that are still happening. You can say, of time, of money, or of a place. This means the time is now, money is now, and the place is now.

In a group, the word of is important. It is the person who says, what we are doing is right, and we should follow them. This is how we order the world.

So, the word of is about who, what, and when. It is about the things that are important in life.
