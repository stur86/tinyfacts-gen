To be written and then changed

When you write a story, you are the first one who sees it. You put words into a paper and put them into your head. You think about what you want to say. Then you write it down. This is the beginning of a new book.

Later, a person who knows how to read and write comes along. They look at the words and make changes. If the words are not right, the person might change them. If the story is not good, the person might change it to make it better.

Sometimes this person only changes the words. They do not change the way the story starts. They just change what is written. If you change a word, you have changed the story. You have changed how it looks.

Sometimes they change the story too. They might change the way a person acts or the way the world looks. This makes the story new and different from the one you started with.

When you change something, you are making it better. You are taking a story and turning it into something new. You are showing the story in a different way.
