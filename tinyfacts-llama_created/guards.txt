To guard something means to keep it safe. There are many kinds of guards. One kind is the police. They go from house to house and car to car. They look for people who are doing bad things. They use their arms and their eyes to watch. They want to stay safe so everyone can feel safe too.

Another kind of guard is a person who watches the door of a house. When you enter a room, you must say your name and show your money. The guard is there to make sure you are who you say you are. This helps keep everyone inside the house safe.

A guard can also be a person who stands on a high place. They stand there when bad things happen. They help the people who are fighting or the people who are being hurt. They keep everyone safe until the bad things are gone.

Sometimes, a guard is a person who works in a big building. They keep the door and watch the room where people do work. They make sure no one gets inside who should not be there. This helps keep the people and the work safe.

A guard is important because they keep people safe. They help keep our homes, schools, and places of work safe. They are like the ones who keep the world safe from bad things that want to hurt us.
