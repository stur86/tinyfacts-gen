To be inside something means to be a part of it. You do not have to be the one who does the work. You can watch and learn from others. This helps you feel safe when something bad happens.

Think of a person who works in a store. They do not have to be there every minute. They are only there when they are supposed to be. They do not have to be the one who makes the money. They are a part of the store. If something bad happens, the store can still stay open because the people are still there.

You can also be inside something for your own life. You might want to have money so you do not have to worry about how you will pay for your food or your home. You are putting money into a box. This box is like a safe. If something bad happens, the box keeps your money. You do not have to be there to keep the money. The box has you.
