To be a good house can be very important. When you live in a house, you are the one who makes it feel warm and safe. You fix the things that break and you keep everything clean.

If you have a big house, you might need to ask for help. You might ask a friend to watch the kids while you go out. Or you might ask a family friend to help you with the house. You want to be safe and have food and water.

A house can also be a place where people go to be with friends. When you have a big house, you can have many people over. You can have a big dinner or watch a movie together. It is a place where people can feel happy and relaxed.

Sometimes, a house is not just for living. It can also be for work. If you work from home, you might need a room to sit in and a desk to work at. You might have a phone in your room so you can talk to your team. You are the one who makes your house a place where you can be safe and work well.

To be a good house is to feel safe and happy. It is about having the right people around you and being able to do what you need to do.
