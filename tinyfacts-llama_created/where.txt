Where is a place. It is a word you use to tell where something is.

Imagine you are in a big city. You are standing in front of a tall building. That is your home. You are in a place where many people live.

You can also say this: I am in the store. This means you are in a building that makes things you need to live or work.

Sometimes, you might ask, where is the park? To answer this, you have to tell you which place you are looking at. If you are looking at your house, the answer is, the house is there. If you are looking at a park, the answer is, the park is there.

You can also ask, what is where? This means you want to know where something is. If you want to know where you are, you ask, where are you? If you want to know where something is, you ask, where is that?

There is also a word that starts with this. It tells where your body is. Your body is in your head, your chest, and your arms. Your body is in the places that make you feel safe and strong.

So, where is a place where you are or what you are looking for.
