To get something is to find it. You are looking for a thing, a person, or a place. When you get it, you have it. You can hold it, look at it, or use it. If you got a book, you read it. If you got money, you buy things with it. When you get something, you have it now.

You can get a thing by doing things. You can get a job by finding a job. You can get a house by moving into a new home. You can get a car by buying it. You can get a friend by talking to them. You can get a feeling by being in a place you love.

You can also get something by being the best at something. If you are the best at a game, you get a good job. If you are the best at a job, you get a higher pay. If you are the best at a thing, you get to do it every day.
