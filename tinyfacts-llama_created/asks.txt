To ask is to make a question. You might ask a friend, a teacher, or a computer. You do not give them the answer yet. You want to know if they know something.

If you ask a person about something, you are trying to find out if they have any ideas. You might ask about the day, the week, or a story from their life. If they say something, you might feel happy. If they say nothing, you can feel confused or worried.

You can ask many different things. You might ask how something works. You might ask why something happens. You can also ask for help when you need it. If you are afraid of a thing, you might ask a friend to stay with you until you feel safe.

Sometimes, you ask because you want to know more. You might ask about a book you read. You might ask about a movie you watched. You want to learn more about the things that happen in the world.

You can ask in many different ways. You can say a word like this: I ask you a question. You can use your voice and say, I need help. You can also use words like this: I am looking for an answer. This shows that you are trying to understand.

To ask is to show you are interested in what other people say. It is a way to be a good friend and to show you care.
