A position is a word that means a position. To be in a position is to be where you are. You are in this place, and you are safe.

Imagine you are in a room. You are standing in front of a table. You can see the door. You have a chair next to you and a table in front of you. This is your position. You are in a spot where you can see what is happening and where you are not in trouble.

Sometimes we need to decide where to go. We might think about where we want to go. If you want to go to the park, you are in a position to do that. You are ready to go.

You can also be in a position because you are doing something. If you are writing a paper, you are in a position to do that. You are doing your work. You are not doing anything else.

Being in a position is important. It helps us know where we are and how we are feeling. It tells us if things are going well or if things are getting bad.
