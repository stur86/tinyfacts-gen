To be a different kind is to be a different way. Think of a box. A box is one kind of thing. If you open it, you see the same things inside. You can have many boxes. Some boxes hold food. Some boxes hold clothes. Some boxes hold books. Each one is a different kind.

Now, let us talk about music. Some people like to listen to the same kind of music. They do not change it. They like the same songs and the same kind of beat. This is like being in the same box. They do not feel different. They do not like to open the box. They are not happy.

Some people like to listen to different kinds of music. They like to change the kind. They open the box and see new things. They feel happy. They like to move their body to the music. They dance. They jump. They shout. They are different. They are the same kind of person, but they like to be different.

In life, we also do this. We like to be with people who do the same thing. We like to work with the same kind of people. We like to go to the same school. We like to live in the same city. But when we grow older, we learn that being the same is not enough. We need to feel something new. We need to be different. We need to feel like we are the only one.
