To be an animal is to live. This is how every living thing is. You breathe in air. You drink water. You grow. You feel the sun on your back. You move. You are living.

But sometimes we need help. One person might stand up for another person. They do not have to be the one who did the thing that hurt. They tell the world, this is not right. The other person must know. The person who helped is the one who is an animal. They are an animal because they care.

A person who acts like this is called an animal. They are not the one who hurts. They are the one who stops it. They are the one who says, this is not how we are going to act.

Sometimes people act like this too. They try to change how things are. They tell other people that they are wrong. They try to make things better. They are like the animal. They care about what happens.

But sometimes, the person who is not the one who hurts is the one who is the animal. They do not care. They just want what they want. They act like this because they are afraid. They are afraid of what will happen if they act like the animal. They act like the animal because they do not want to be alone.
