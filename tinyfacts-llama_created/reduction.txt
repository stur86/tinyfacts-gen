To make something less strong is to do this. If you have a lot of money, you might want to save it. You can buy many things with it. But if you get a job, you work and you get more money. The money you had before is gone. You have fewer things now.

You can also do this with your own body. If you eat too much food, you feel heavy. This makes it hard to move. If you drink too much, you might feel sick. You might have to go to a doctor to feel better.

In a long story, the bad man is doing this to the people. He wants to keep them very afraid. When people feel afraid, they do not want to talk. They keep their eyes down and their faces are hard. This is not a good thing. It makes the world a harder place to be in.

To do something is to start a new way. If you walk to school, you start your day. If you work hard, you start a new job. You are starting from the beginning. You are not the same person you were before. You are stronger because you tried something new.
