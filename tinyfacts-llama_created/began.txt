To begin something is to start it. It is when you start doing something new. You might start with a word, a story, or a plan. You are moving to the next step.

Think about a story. If you are telling a story to a friend, you are beginning. You start the story. You do not finish it yet. You are just starting to tell it.

When you start a new day, you begin. You wake up and you start your morning. You go to the bathroom, you eat breakfast, you get ready, and you go out.

If you are trying to learn something new, you are beginning. You start to read a book, you watch a movie, or you study a new word. You are starting to understand.

Sometimes, to begin something is to start a fight. If you start a fight, you are the first person. You push the first word, the first step, or the first hit. You start the fight.

You can also begin a new job. You start at a new place. You learn how to do the job and you become a part of the team. You begin to do things for the new people and the new place.

In this way, to begin something is to start a new thing. It is the first step. It is the start of a new life, a new story, or a new way of doing things.
