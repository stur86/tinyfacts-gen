To write a computer is easy. A person just types what they want. But a computer that only takes what you give it, but does not ask back, is different. A computer needs to know how to work. This is done by people who are called computer people. They write things that the computer can understand. These things tell the computer what to do.

Computer people use a way of writing that is different from the way people write. They use numbers and letters. These numbers and letters tell the computer what to do. The computer reads these numbers and letters and does what the person told it.

Some computer people are very good at making things. They make things that can do many things at once. Some make things that can talk to other computers. Some make things that can show pictures and words on a picture box. Some make things that can remember what you said and do the same thing next time.

Some computer people are not so good at making things. They make things that are not very good. These things can not do many things at once. They can not talk to other computers. They can only do one thing at a time.

All of these people, computer people, and things that they make, are part of how we live today. We use them every day.
