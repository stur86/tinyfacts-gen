When you talk, you use your lips. They are the parts that go around your mouth. They help you make sounds. The front part of your lip is soft. The back part of your lip is hard. This hard part is like a little box that holds your air. When you close the box, the air comes out. When you open it, the air goes in.

Your lips also help you hold your food. When you eat something, your lips move around it. Sometimes you use your lips to make your face look nice. You can press your lips together to make a happy look. You can also use your lips to pick things. If you want to pick a tiny thing, you use your lips.

People use these words when they talk about lips. They say, a person has a good lip. They say a person has a bad lip. They say, the boy has a nice lip. They say, the girl has a mean lip.

Sometimes, you use the word to talk about things that are hard to hold. If you want to hold something for a long time, you say it is hard to hold. If something is very hard to carry, you say it is a hard thing. If something is very hard to do, you say it is a hard job.
