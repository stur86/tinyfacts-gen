To feel something means to know about it. When you touch something, your skin feels it. If you feel a small thing on your arm, it is a feeling.

You can feel things when you see a bright light. You can feel the wind on your face. You can feel the ground under your feet. You can feel the soft touch of a friend. These are all things you feel.

You can also feel things when you are sad. You might be sad because a person you love is gone. Or you might be sad because you lost a game. Your heart feels heavy. You might cry.

Sometimes, you feel things that are not in front of you. You might feel things in your mind. You might feel things in your stomach. When you are afraid, you might feel your heart beating fast. Your legs feel like they are going to move.

When you feel a thing, you want to know what it is. You want to know why it is there. You might try to touch it again. You might try to look at it. You might try to listen to it.
