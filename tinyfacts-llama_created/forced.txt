To be forced means you are made to do something. It happens when a person or a group of people are told they must do something, even if they do not want to.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You like to run fast and jump high. But then, a teacher says, you must run very fast and jump very high. You do not want to do this. You want to play another game or go home. But the teacher says you must do it because you are in a school or a place where you must follow the orders.

Sometimes, you are forced to do something because of a problem. Maybe you have a lot of money and you have to give some of it to someone who needs it more than you. Or, you may be forced to do something because you have to follow a set of hard words that say you must do it.

It can also mean something happens when someone is not wanted. If you are walking down a street and a police car comes and stops you. You might feel afraid. You are not wanted to be there. You want to go home.

But when you are forced, you are doing something against your will. You do not want to do it. You might feel bad or angry. It can be very hard to feel this way.

So, being forced is when you are made to do something by someone else. It is a time when you are not free to do what you want.
