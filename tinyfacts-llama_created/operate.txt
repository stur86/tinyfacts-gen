To work or to act is a very important word. It is about how something is done. You can work for many years and you might not even know what you do. You might do things that are not even part of your job. But when you finally get a job and you start doing the work, you start to feel like you are doing what you are meant to do.

If you are a teacher, you work with children. You show them things and help them learn. When you are a doctor, you help people feel better. You fix their bodies when they are hurt. You are the one who is working for the people.

Sometimes, you work alone. You are the only one doing the work. You might be fixing a car or making food. You do not need anyone else to tell you what to do. You are alone with your work and your mind.

But sometimes, you work with other people. You share what you have to give. You all work together to make something. You might be a team of people doing the same job. You all work together to get the job done.

To work is to act. It is to be in the world. It is to be here and to do what you need to do. It is about living and being a part of the world.
