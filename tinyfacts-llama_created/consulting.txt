To find the right person for a job

When a person has a problem, they often look at a book or talk to a friend. Sometimes they talk to a person who knows a lot about the same thing. This person has been around and can give them a good idea. This is called a meeting with a friend.

A meeting with a friend can be very good. You talk about the problem, and the friend gives you a way to fix it. You can also get ideas about things you have not thought about before. This is like getting a new idea.

Sometimes, people do not know the right answer. They may talk to many friends, go to many places, and read many books. But they still do not know what to do.

A person who works with many other people often uses this word. They talk to many friends and work with many people. They use the word when they are not sure how to do a job. They want to find the right person to help them.

In some places, you have to pay to do a job. In this job, you talk to the person who will pay you. You tell them what you did and how you did it. You must follow the words. You do not get to decide. If you do not follow the words, you can get in trouble.

People also use this word when they are not sure what to buy. They go to many stores and talk to many people. They ask if the person has the thing they need. The person who has the thing tells them if it is a good one. They do not get to pick. They have to follow the person.

In a small group, you can use this word. You are all working on the same thing. You talk to each other. You share ideas. You work together. This is a good way to find the right person for the job.
