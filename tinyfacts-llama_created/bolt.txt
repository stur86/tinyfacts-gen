A long, hard piece of stuff that you use to hold things together. It is usually made of hard white stuff from the rock that forms high up in the sky. When you need to fix something, you use this piece to join it to another thing. It is like a big push that makes the two parts fit together.

Imagine you have a small box with a small top. You need to put the top on the box so it will stay closed. If you do not use this piece, the top will come off and the box will open. The hard piece helps keep everything in place so the top stays on.

You can use this piece to fix a car. You put it in the car and it helps hold the top of the car together. It is also used to fix a table. You use it to join two long pieces of wood so the table stays put.

This piece can also be used to fix a door. You put it in the door so it will not come open. It is a very important thing to use because it helps keep things safe.
