When you look at something, you see what is right in front of you. Your eyes help you see colors and pictures. You also get some of this from the people and things around you.

When you get a piece of this, you have your own mind. You know what the world is like. You know what is right and wrong. This is called your understanding.

Sometimes, you get this from the people you talk to. They tell you things. They tell you about things that have happened. They tell you what is going to happen. This is how you learn. You learn that things are important and that some are better than others.

You also get this from the world around you. You hear about what is going on. You see pictures of what happened. You feel how people are feeling. This is how you understand the world.

People use this word a lot. If someone gives you a piece of this, you are learning. If you learn something new, you have your own mind. If you get this from others, you are hearing.
