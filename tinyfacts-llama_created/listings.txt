To listening means to pay attention to what someone is saying. You are listening when you are quiet and not doing anything else. You might listen when a friend is telling you a story, or when a teacher is explaining a new word.

You can also listening to music. If a song has many good parts, you might want to hear each one. You listen so you can understand what the music means. You might repeat the words so you know them by heart. You might even try to remember the sound of the music.

You can also listen when a person is speaking. You might listen to a phone call, a book, or a movie. You are not doing anything else. You are just listening to what is happening.

When you are listening, you do not think about what you want to say. You are only interested in what the other person is saying. This helps you understand and remember things better. It is an important part of how we learn and how we stay close to others.
