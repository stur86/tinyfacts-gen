To look at something or something else. You might look at a picture in a book. You might look at a computer. You might look at a thing you want to buy. This means you are not looking at one thing at a time. You are looking at many things at once.

Imagine you are in a store. You see many things you like. You want to buy them all. But you do not buy one thing. You look at them all. You see how they look. You see how they feel. You see how they look when you touch them. You see how they look when they are held. This is like what you do when you are looking at a computer. You look at many pictures and words on the computer. You see how they look. You see how they feel. You see how they work.
