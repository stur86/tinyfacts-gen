To decide something means you have to think about it. You might want to do one thing, but you also want to do another thing. You have to pick which one you want to do.

Imagine you are a parents. You have two kids, and you want to decide which one to bring home for dinner. You look at their faces and their clothes. You see that the baby is crying. You also see that the other child is laughing. You want to keep the baby because the baby needs you more. You decide to bring the baby home.

You did not just pick a one. You picked between two things. You decided to be the one who takes care of the baby. This is a big thing to do. It takes a lot of thinking and feeling.

Sometimes, you decide not to do something. You might say, I do not want to go to the party. This is also important. You are making a clear plan about what you want to do with your time.

You can also decide what to do when you do not know what to do. If you are in a room with many people, and you hear a loud noise, you might decide to leave the room. You are making a plan to get out of the situation.

Deciding is important because it helps you feel sure. When you decide, you are telling yourself, I am the one who is in control. This makes you feel stronger and more sure about what to do.
