To end something is to stop it. It happens when a group of people say they are done with a story. A song ends when the last note is played. A game ends when everyone has finished playing.

You can end a day, a week, or a month. You can end a day by closing your eyes and letting your body rest. You can end a week by getting ready for bed at night. You can end a month by the first day of a new month.

When you end a thing, you stop it. You do not keep going. This helps people feel safe. It tells them what to expect next.

You can also end a feeling. If you are sad, you may feel better after a while. If you are angry, you may stop being angry after you talk with a friend. If you are excited, you may feel calm after the fun ends.

Ending can be hard. You might feel sad when a story ends because you do not want it to end. You might feel angry when you are done with a game because it is over. But in the end, ending is important. It helps us understand what has happened and what comes next.
