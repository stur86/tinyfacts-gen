A long thing you find on a animal. When an animal wants to make a sound, it opens the part at the top. The air comes out very fast. This makes a loud noise that can be heard far away. This sound is important for many animals. Some animals use it to warn others about a thing that might happen. Other animals use it to say hello.

You can also make a noise like this with a thing you hold in your hand. When you press the thing together, air goes out from the top. This makes a different kind of sound that is very different from the noise a dog makes. Some people like to make this noise to be funny.
