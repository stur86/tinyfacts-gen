To go along a piece of land that is close to the water is to go on a way that starts on the land and goes around the water. This way is often on a long line that goes across the water. People who live near the water often live on the land that is close to the water. They are called people who live near the water.

When you are on this way, you go in a direction from the water. This direction is often away from the water. You can go through many small pieces of land before you reach the end of the way. At the end, you are near the open water. You can see the open water all around you. This is a nice way to go because you can see many things and feel the wind and the sun.
