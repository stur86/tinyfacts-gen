A person who leads a group of people who follow them. They help the group do things and keep them safe. They often work with a higher person who is like a leader.

There are many kinds of this. Some are the top person. They are like the head. They make big plans. Other people lead smaller groups. They help their groups do things and keep them safe.

The word comes from a story about a man who was the top person in a city. He had many people who followed him. He was very good at leading. He made sure everyone was safe and did the right things.

There is also a person who is the top person in a group of people who follow them. They lead and help them. They are like the top leader in a school or a group of people.

Becoming a person who leads this is a big job. You need to be able to work well with many people, listen to them, and make them feel safe. You need to have a good way with words so people know you are there to help them.

Sometimes these people lead people who do things that are not right. They help the people who do those things so they can do more bad things. But the leader also tries to stop them so people are safe.
