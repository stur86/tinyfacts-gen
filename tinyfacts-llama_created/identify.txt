To know a person or a thing means to understand who they are and what they do. When you know someone, you can see their face and hear their voice. You can tell if they are happy or sad.

You can also know a thing. If you look at a picture of a cat, you can see that it is a cat. You can hear the sound of a dog. You can feel the soft hair of a dog. You can see the bright colors of a bright blue sky. All these things help you know what is happening.

Sometimes, you might want to know a person so you can be their friend. You want to know their name and their life story. You might want to know how they feel about something. You want to know if they like to laugh or if they like to fight.

When you know a person, you can be kind to them. You can tell them that you like their smile. You can say nice things about what they do. You can be there when they need you. This makes the world a better place because everyone feels seen and heard.

You can also know a place. If you go to a park, you know what to look for. You know where the green stuff is. You know where the water falls. You know where to find the big tree with the white soft hair. This helps you move from one place to another without getting lost.

Finally, knowing something means to understand its truth. If you know that a thing is red, you understand that it is a color. If you know that a person is happy, you understand that they are feeling good. Understanding is a big word that helps us make sense of the world around us.
