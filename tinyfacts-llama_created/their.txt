Their is a word that is used when talking about people.

When you say a person is their, it means they are that person. You are talking about the person you know, the person you love, or the person you work with.

Imagine a little girl. She loves to play. Her mom says, she is the girl. This is because the girl is the one she loves.

You can also say, they are coming. This means the people who are going are the ones you know. You are talking about the family or friends you are going to see.

Sometimes, when you hear a voice, you can tell who it is. If you hear your mom, you know it is her. If you hear a friend, you know it is them.

They can also mean things that are not people. If you have a big house, you say it is their house. This means the house is the one you live in.

Using their helps us understand who or what we are talking about.
