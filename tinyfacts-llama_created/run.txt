To run is to move your feet very fast. You walk, you jump, and then you start to move your feet from side to side, and you go very quickly.

When you run, your legs do not stop. They keep moving and keep going. You use your arms to help you, but your legs are the ones that do the work. This is why you can go very far and very fast at the same time.

To run is to be free. You do not have to be standing still. You can go across a field, down a street, or up a big building. You can even run in the water if you are a strong person.

There are many different kinds of running. You can run with your eyes closed. You can run on a hard floor. You can run on a soft floor. You can run on a floor that is on water. You can even run while you are asleep.

When you run, your body gets very hot. Your heart works hard to get the air and the food into your body. Your arms and your legs get tired. You might want to stop and sit down, but you keep going because you want to finish.

Running is good for you. It can help you feel strong and happy. It can help you be in good mood even when things are bad. It is a way to use your body and to feel free.
