To cut wood is to break it so it can be made into many different pieces. You need a hard thing to hit with a long piece of hard stuff. This hard stuff is called a saw.

Imagine you have a big piece of wood in your room. You want to make a table. You can use a saw to cut it. You push the hard stuff through the wood, and the wood breaks. Then you take the pieces out and make a table. You can also cut wood for a car. If you cut the wood in the right way, the car can be made very strong and safe.

There are many kinds of wood. Some are very hard. These are for making things that you need to last a long time. Other kinds are softer. These are for making things you use every day.

Wood can be green. When it is green, it is still growing. But when it is old, it is dead. When it is old and dead, it becomes brown or black. You can use the brown wood for making things that will not break easily.

Wood is a very old thing. The first trees were made by animals over a hundred years ago. The first people cut wood from those trees to make homes, food, and clothes. Now we use wood from many different kinds of trees to make many different things we need.
