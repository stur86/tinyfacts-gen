To do an act is a way you act. A person might be a doctor. They work with a body to fix it when it is sick. The doctor looks at the body with a look on their face. They use a book to learn how the body works. They might ask the body a question. The body tells them what is wrong.

You can do this too. You might ask a friend a question. If you want to know why they did something, you need to look at what they said. You need to look at their words, their face, and what they did. This is how you learn. It is how you get to know a person. It is how you understand what is happening.

To do an act can also mean to look at many things at once. A person might do this when they are trying to decide. They look at many books. They look at many words. They look at many numbers. They look at many things they know. They try to find the answer.

If you are trying to decide what to do, you are doing an act. You are looking at many things. You are trying to find what is right for you.
