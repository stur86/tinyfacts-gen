To tell the story of a person is to write about them. This is something you do when you want to know more about someone who is not your family. You try to find the right words so that the story is clear. You look at what they did, who they were friends with, and what they liked. You might also look at pictures or look at what they wrote before they died.

A person can be a person you know. They are a teacher, a friend, or a family person. Or a person can be a person you do not know. You might hear about them through the stories of others, or you might read about them in books or movies.

When you write about a person, you are not just telling them how they were. You are also showing what happened after. You are showing how they changed. You are showing how they learned. You are showing how they had to work to get where they were.

Sometimes, people write about their own lives. They write about when they were a little kid. They write about when they were a big kid. They write about how they grew up. They write about what they did with their life. They might write about the good times and the hard times.

There are also stories about people you do not know. These stories are like a picture book for a world you do not know. They show you what a person looks like, what they do, and who they are. They help you understand the world better.

Everyone tells their own story. Every time you tell a story, you are writing about your own life. If you are a child, you might tell a story about what you did the day before. If you are a grown person, you might tell a story about your life.
