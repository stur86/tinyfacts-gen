To light something means to make it bright. Imagine you are in a room and there are no lights. The room is dark. You can barely see what is around you. But if you turn on a light, the room is filled with bright white light. The light makes everything look nice and clear.

You can light things that are not always on. A fire can be lit. This means to make the fire start by moving something inside the fire. When you light a fire, you make it happen. You do not put the fire on. You make it start.

Sometimes you light things that are already on. You might turn on a table light. This is when you turn a power from the wall into a light that can be seen. You use a small box with a small piece inside. The box sends power to the light, and the light comes out.

Light can also help you see in the dark. When you are walking at night and there are no lights on the ground, you need light to see. Light from a far light can help you find your way. It keeps you from walking into things that are not there.
