To make something is to give it a new form. You can do this when you paint a picture. You start with a white wall. You use big soft pieces of stuff to cover the wall. Then you use colors from a box. You put the colors together in a way that looks nice. The end is a picture you did with the wall.

You can also make something feel a new way. If you are cold, you can put a warm thing on your body. This makes your body feel warm. If you are sad, you can listen to a song. The song makes you feel better. 

Sometimes, you have to make a thing that is hard to do. You might make a book. You write many lines in a line. You keep writing until you have done all the lines. You then put the book together. When you do this, the book is new and you have done it.

If something is made, it is no longer a thing of nothing. It has a form and a way of being. It can be seen, touched, or heard.
