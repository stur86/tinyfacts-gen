To clear something means to make a space or a room empty. This happens when you move things away from a person, a thing, or a place so that nothing else can be in there anymore.

Imagine you are in a room with many chairs. You pick up a chair and put it away. Now the room looks different. You did not just move the chair. You also made the room feel less full because one chair was gone. You have cleared the room.

You can clear a room with many things too. You might get ridden a book or a game that was being played. This makes the room feel quiet and new. You are making space for new things to come in.

Sometimes, to clear something also means to get ridden it. If you have a problem in your head, you might clear it by telling yourself that the problem is gone. This is like taking a step back from the problem so you can breathe again.

If you are talking about a place, you can also clear it. If a place is very crowded, you might tell the people to leave. This makes the place feel less full and more open. You are clearing the space so that new people can come in and make it their own.

In your life, clearing can mean finding what is important to you. It is about letting go of things that do not matter and making room for new ideas or people. This can help you feel more free and ready to move forward.
