Follow is to follow the way of the people. When you follow a group of people, you move to where they are. This is because the people have set a way for everyone to move.

But something that follows is different. It is not a group. It is a smell. When you put a small bit of something on your skin, you feel a good feeling. You might like the smell, or you might not like it. The smell stays on your skin for a long time.

When you walk through a park, you might hear the sound of running water. The sound is like a long story. The sound is from many people, many animals, and many things in the world. It is not one person. It is the way all those things move together.

Sometimes, you might smell a good thing. You can smell the smell of a friend or a food you love. The smell is like a memory. It reminds you of a happy time.

In a house, there are many things you need to live in. There is a bed for sleeping, a table for eating, a desk for writing, and a chair for sitting. These things are all together. They follow the way of the people who use them.
