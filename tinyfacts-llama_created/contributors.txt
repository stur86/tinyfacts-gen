People, animals, and things can all be called a group. Sometimes we use the word when there are many of them. If you see a lot of people walking in a long line, you can call them a group. If you see many animals running together, you can also say they are a group.

But if you talk about a person who helps you, you use the word a different way. This person is one of many people who can help. They are part of a group of people who want to do the same thing. They are not the only ones who can do it.
