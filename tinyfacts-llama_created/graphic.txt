A picture is a thing that you see. You use your eyes to look at it. It does not move. It is always there.

A computer can make a picture too. It uses many numbers and tiny pieces of light. You tell the computer what you want. It makes the picture on the computer. Then you see it.

A picture can be many things. It can be a face. It can be a car or a tree. It can be a city. It can be a fun place. It can be a thing that is very cold or very hot.

You can also make a picture with words. You write words and colors. You make the words go across the paper. The paper makes the picture for you. This is how you make a picture with words.

Sometimes, you use pictures to tell a story. You show many pictures one after another. The story is in the pictures. You are not telling the story. You are just showing it to people.

A picture can also be a word. You can write many words and put them together. You can make a story with many words. The words are like the pictures. They help you say what you want.
