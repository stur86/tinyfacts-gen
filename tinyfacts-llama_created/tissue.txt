A piece of soft stuff is called a piece of paper. You can use it to clean your face when you get a bit red. You can also use it to cover a cut. It is very easy to use because it does not hurt your hands. The piece of paper is made from long lines of white stuff. This stuff is very good at keeping things together. If you cut the white stuff, it can not be made again. It stays the same even if you cut it many times.

A piece of this stuff that is very small is called a small piece of paper. It is used for writing. You write on it with a small writing thing. The small writing thing makes marks on the paper. These marks are for people to read later.

Sometimes you use a piece of this stuff to help a baby breathe. The baby needs to breathe air that goes into its body very fast. This small piece of paper goes into the baby. It helps the air go in and out of the baby very quickly.

A piece of this stuff that is very thick is called a big piece of paper. This is the kind of paper you sit at a table and write on. You can write on it with a big writing thing. The big piece of paper can hold many lines of writing. It is very good for making books and papers that people use every day.
