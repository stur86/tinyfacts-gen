To move from one thing to another

You can move things from one place to another. If you have a small box, you can put it in a car. Then the car will move and the box will stay in the car.

You can also move from one person to another. If you are very happy, you might want to share the feeling with a friend. You can say,  Let me see how you feel. Then you will tell the friend. This is a way to share a moment.

Sometimes we move from one room to another. If you want to go to the kitchen, you might say, Let me go. This means you are moving away from your bed.

You can also use this word for money. If you have some money and you want more, you can give some to someone else. This is called giving. It is a way to share what you have.

You can also move from one day to another. When the day after today starts, the day after today begins. You are moving forward to a new time. This is a big change.
