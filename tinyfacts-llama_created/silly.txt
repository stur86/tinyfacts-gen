To be funny is to be in a way that is not normal. A person who is funny does not follow the usual way people act. They do things that are not expected. They might laugh or make a face when they are trying to say something serious. People laugh when they see this kind of funny. It is like a warm feeling that comes up inside them when they see something not right.

Simply is the word we use to say something is easy to understand. When we say something is easy, we mean that it is easy to do. We do not need to think a lot about it. It is like eating a sweet food. You just take it from the box and you are done. We use this word when we talk about things that are easy to do.

Sometimes we say a person is a bit funny. This means they are in a funny way, but they are still a real person. We do not say they are a dog. We say they are funny because they make us laugh. We like to see funny people because it makes us feel happy.

You can also say something is a bit funny if you think about it for a moment. If you look at a picture and you feel a little weird, you might say it is funny. Sometimes, we do not understand at first, but then we see something and we laugh.
