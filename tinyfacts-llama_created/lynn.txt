To light something means to make it seem less heavy or more light. When you light a light, you turn it on. When you light a fire, you make the wood and air turn into small white moving parts and warm light. When you light a book, you turn the words so the words are not dark anymore.

You can also light a person. If someone is very sad, they might light. This means they try to make the feeling go away. They might tell jokes, listen to music, or talk to a friend. They want the other person to feel better.

In a word, light is often used with people. When someone lights a friend, they help them feel better. When a friend lights another friend, they give the other friend a big hug. This is a kind way to show you care.

We can also light things in our minds. If you are very angry, your mind might be heavy. You might feel like you want to cry. When you light, you calm down and let your mind go. You can breathe again, and you feel better.
