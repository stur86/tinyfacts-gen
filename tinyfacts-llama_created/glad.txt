To be glad means to feel happy. When you are glad, you feel a warm feeling in your heart. It is often because something good happened to you.

You can feel glad when you do something you love. Maybe you went to a place you have never seen before and it was beautiful. Or maybe you played a game with your friends and you all laughed a lot. This makes you feel happy inside.

Sometimes you are glad for the day. You wake up in the morning and the sun is out. You go to school or work and everything feels right. You feel glad that you have another day to do fun things.

You can also be glad when you are not alone. If a friend calls you and you talk for a long time, you feel happy. Or if your family is together, you feel glad to be near them.

Being glad is important. When you are glad, you can feel stronger. It helps you stay happy even when things are not going well. It is a good way to show you care about what is happening around you.
