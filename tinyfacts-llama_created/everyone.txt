Everyone is a big word. It means all of us, from the first person to the last person.

When you say everyone is a person, you mean every single one of them. You are not the only one. There are many more people in the world than you can see.

Think about a group of people you are friends with. You all like the same thing, or you all like the same game. You all do the same thing when you are together. In this group, everyone is the same. You are all the same person.

Also, when you say everyone has something, you mean every person has a thing. Every baby is a baby. Every child is a child. Every person has a heart and a mind. Everyone has a face and a body.

Sometimes, when we talk about everyone, we mean all of the people in a place. If you are in a big city with a lot of people, everyone is a person. If you are in a small town with only a few people, not everyone is a person.

Finally, you can also say everyone is a person in a story. If you are reading a book, every person is a person in the story. You are not the only one. Everyone has their own story and their own way.

So, everyone is a person. Everyone is a group. Everyone has something. Everyone is a part of the world.
