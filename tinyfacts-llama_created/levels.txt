A world can have many different high places. If you look at a top of a small house, the top is the highest part. If you walk up a long road and the road gets higher, the higher place is where you are. You can walk to this place faster than to the lower places.

Things in our world also go through many different places. The ground under your feet is a lower place. The ground above you is a higher place. The sky is the very top place. When you look up, you are looking at a higher place than where you stand.

In a world of people, you might have different places inside. You might be at home, or at a school, or at a friend. Each place is like a high place. You are not always at one place at one time. Sometimes you are in one place, and sometimes you are in another.
