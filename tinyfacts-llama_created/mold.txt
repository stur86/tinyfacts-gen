To grow something means to change in a way that is new and different from the way it started. A tiny tiny thing can start to grow on a piece of wood or a wet floor. This tiny thing is a very small piece of the whole. It starts to change how it looks, how it feels, and how it acts.

Imagine you have a small bit of food. This bit of food is very hard and does not change much. Over time, the tiny tiny thing that grows on it changes how it looks, how it feels, and how it acts. It turns brown and gets smaller. This is how small things grow in the world.

Sometimes things grow in the ground. The ground is hard and strong. It keeps the tiny tiny thing and the food inside it. The tiny tiny thing grows and gets bigger, and the food becomes bigger too. This is how things grow in the ground.

Sometimes things grow in the air. The air is full of tiny tiny things that fall down from the sky. When these tiny tiny things hit the ground, they start to grow. They change from tiny tiny things to something new with a different look, feel, and act. This is how things grow in the air.

Things can also grow in the water. Some tiny tiny things live in the water. They are very small and go very deep into the water. Over time, these tiny tiny things grow and grow, and they become a new kind of life. This is how the tiny tiny things that live in the water grow and change.

When something grows, it often changes how it acts. It becomes bigger or smaller. It can change color. It can change how it feels. It can change how it looks. This is why things grow.
