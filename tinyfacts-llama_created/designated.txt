When you are given a job, you usually know what you do. But if you are given a name, it can mean you do a different job. This word helps us talk about people and things.

To be named is like being given a name. A child is named at first. This name is very important. It helps everyone know who that person is. A person who is very good at a job may get a new name that is better. This name can be the name of the person or the name of the job.

Imagine you are in a big house with many people. There is a big man in a chair. He is the one who sits in the chair. He is the one who gets to say what to do. If you want to be the one who sits in the chair, you have to ask. You need to say, I want to be that man. This is how people get new names.

Sometimes, a group of people gets a new name. They have done a lot of work together. They have built a big building or run a big bus line for many years. The group gets a new name so everyone knows them. This new name is like a new job.

This word can also mean when you are given a job to do a very hard thing. You are not the one who does it. You are the person who helps the person who does it. You help them so they can do their job.

There is another way to use this word. If you are a person who likes to act, you might get a new name. This name might be the name of a movie or a kind of music. It is like a new job. You are the person who plays that name.

So, being named is like being given a job. It means someone gets a new name so everyone knows who they are.
