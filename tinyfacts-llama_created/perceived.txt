When you see a person, you do not just see them. You also see how they act, how they look, and how they feel. This is what we call what they are like. We see their body and how it moves. We see their face and how it looks. We see their eyes and how they look at us. We see how they talk and how they move their mouth. All of this together is what we call what they are like.

What you see is only part of what they are. The rest is inside. This is why we can see a person, but we can never see what is inside their mind or heart.

Think of it like a room. You can see the walls and the floor. But you can never see the air inside the room or the people who are there. The room is only what you see. It is not what you know.
