People often use the word this and that to talk about things. This word tells us which one is the first. When you say this is the book, you mean the book is the first. If you ask what book this is, the person who knows will say the book is the one I am looking at.

Another way this word works is in games. In a game, you might say you need this. This means you want to play this game. If you do not get this, you are not happy. You might feel sad or angry.

People also use this word to talk about their friends. If you are with friends, you are with this group. When you are with friends, you feel happy. You might laugh or talk with them.

This word is also used in writing. When you write a story, you might use this word to show who is doing what. This can help the reader know what is happening.
