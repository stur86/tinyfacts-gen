To be the right side of something means you are not in the middle. Imagine you are standing in a room with a table in front of you. The table is the right side. If you were standing in front of the table, you would be in the middle.

If you have a thing that is very big, like a car or a house, it is the right side of the room or the street. You are the one standing there, not the one on the floor.

If you have a friend, you can say they are the right side of you. You are the one who is not in the middle. They help you and are good for you.

The word can also mean to show how big something is. If you have a small dog, your mom might say, this dog is the right side of the bed. She is saying the dog is small.

Sometimes, we use this word when we talk about people. If a man is the right side of a woman, he is the person who is on her side. This is not always a nice thing to say. It is a way of showing how we feel.
