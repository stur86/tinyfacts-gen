To have an answer means to know how something works. An answer shows what you know about a person, a place, a thing, or a story. It can help you understand why things happen. When you say something has an answer, you know how the thing is.

You can also use this word when you talk about how a small thing can change a larger thing. If you drop a ball, it falls on the ground. This is an answer. The falling of the ball shows how the small thing can change the big thing.

Sometimes, an answer can mean to do something and see what happens. If you drink a glass of water, your body will feel better. This is an answer because you did what your body said to do.

You can also use this word to talk about how a person feels. If a person is happy, they are an answer. If a person is sad, they are an answer too. They are showing how they are feeling.

In the world, we use this word to talk about many things. We might say an answer is how a person acts. We might say an answer is how a person feels. We use it to show how things are and how people change.

To answer something is to show you know. It is about knowing how a thing works, how a person feels, or what happened.
