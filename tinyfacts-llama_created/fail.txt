To fall means you hit the ground. You might be running with your friends. You are going fast. Then, you feel a strange feeling. You might lose your way. You hit the ground again. This happens many times in life. You try to be the best, but you do not do well. You fall.

Sometimes, you fall because you are very slow. You try to run, but you are not fast enough. You hit the ground and get hurt. This is a bad thing. You need to get up and try again.

You can also fall when you are afraid. You think something bad is going to happen. You are worried. You try to stay safe. But the thing you are afraid of happens anyway. You fall into the ground because you did not stay calm.

To fall is to lose your way. It is to let go. It is to give up. If you fall, you must get up and try again. You can try to move away from the thing that fell you. You can try to find a new way. You can learn from your fall. This helps you become a better person next time.
