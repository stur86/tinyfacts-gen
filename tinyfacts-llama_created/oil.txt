To make something clear or to move through a hard place is to use a type of thing that is found in the ground. This thing is made from tiny pieces that are from the sun and from the ground. It is very strong and helps things that use it move.

You put this thing into a big round thing to make things go. It is also used to make bright lights. It is found in the ground of many places. When this thing is broken, it can be left in the ground or it can be put in a long line of things so that the power can come out of the ground to homes and cars.

The word comes from the word that means to cut. The old people used this word to talk about the hard pieces of rock that are found in the ground. Now we use it to talk about the thing that makes things move and the bright lights that use it.
