To be the one who gets something is to have been given a job. When you are the one who gets a job, you are the person who is going to do it. You are the one who has to do the work. You are not the one who is giving the job to you.

Think of a group of people. If one person does not want to do the work, the group can pick another person from them. This is how the work is shared. Each person gets a turn to do the job. If one person does not do their part, the others must pick them up and do the job for them.

When you say a job is not done, the person who was supposed to do it is the one who is left. They are the one who is the wrong person for the job. When you say someone is the one who is left, you are telling everyone else that the job is not done.

Sometimes, a job can be given to the person who is the best at it. This is when you are the one who is going to do the work. If the job is given to the person who is the best, the person who was supposed to do it is the one who is left. This is the way jobs get started.
