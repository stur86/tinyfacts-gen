An often word that people use.

When you say a thing happens a number of times, you are talking about how many times it happens. If you do this a hundred times, the word is used to show how much happened. It is like a number.

You can also use it to talk about how often something happens. If you do this one time, then you do it again next time, and next time after that, and so on, you are doing it all at once. This is called repeating something many times.
