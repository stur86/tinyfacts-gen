A word is a thing that you say. It is a group of words that tell a story. A story is when you tell many things that happen one after another. One word tells you a small thing. The next word tells you another small thing. The next word tells you another small thing again. This keeps going until you tell a story that has many many small things.

A number is a very big group of numbers. When you use numbers, you can make many different stories. You can use one number to tell a story about how the sun comes up. You can use two numbers to tell a story about how the rain falls. You can use three numbers to tell a story about how the days go by.

Sometimes you have a group of numbers. This group of numbers is called a group. You can add numbers in this group. You can add two numbers, then you add the third number, and you get another number. This number is the answer to your question about the numbers.

Sometimes, a word is a group of letters. This group of letters is called a word. You can put this word next to other words to make a story. The word is the first word. The next word is the second word. The word after that is the third word. And so on.

You can also have a long story with many many words. This is a very long story. You tell it one word at a time. Each word tells the story. You do not tell the story all at once. You tell it one word at a time, so the person listening can follow along.

A group of words is like a story. A number is like a number group. A word is like a word group.
