To be in a state that is not of the world is the most important thing. It is when you let go of the world and of yourself. You see that everything is one. Every being is the same, and every moment is the same. This is what is meant by a deep and quiet place of being.

Sometimes we think we are in a place of being, but this is not the same. The place of being is not a room in your house. It is not a chair in a corner. It is not a picture on the wall. It is a part of you. It is the quiet that you get when you stop thinking and stop moving.

This quiet is not the same as a quiet night. This quiet is not the same as a quiet room. This quiet is the quiet of your heart. It is the quiet when you are not thinking, when you are not doing anything, and when you are not afraid.

To be in a state of being is to let go of what is not. To let go of the world is to let go of your fears and your worries. To let go of yourself is to let go of your own needs. It is to be one with everything.

The idea of being is old. It comes from the time before the world was built. In those times, people did not have houses or clothes. They lived in trees and under the ground. They were one with the land. They did not need words like we do. They just lived.

We have built a world with walls and cars and computers. We have lost the way to be. We have lost the way to feel. We have lost the way to be one with the world. But it is still there. The quiet of the heart is still there. We can go back.

Going back is not going back to the old time. Going back is going back to the quiet of being. Going back is going back to the heart.
