What is a thing?

A thing is a thing you can see or touch or hear. You can find them all around you. A book is a thing you can hold in your hand. A dog is a thing you can see. You can also find things in the ground, in the water, or in the sky.

What is something that does not have a name?

A thing with no name is a thing that you do not know. You do not say its name. It is just a thing. You might see it and not know what it is. You might hear it and not know what it is.

What is a thing that you use?

A thing you use is a thing that helps you. You use a chair to sit in. You use a phone to talk to your friend. You use a box to keep your food. A car is a thing you use to go to a place far away.

What is a thing that you do not need?

You do not need a thing that makes you feel happy. You can live without a pretty thing. You can live without a book that tells stories. You can live without a thing that helps you sleep.

What is a thing that is always there?

Everything is a thing. The air is a thing. The sun is a thing. The ground is a thing. The water is a thing. The sky is a thing. You are a thing. We all are things.

What is a thing that you can change?

You can change a thing. If you do not want a thing, you can throw it away. You can change your life. You can change how you feel. You can change where you live. You can change how you act. You can change who you are.

What is a thing that you do not understand?

Sometimes you do not know what a thing is. You do not know how it works. You do not know why it is there. You might feel confused or scared. But even in those moments, the thing is still there. It is still a thing.

What is a thing that you see every day?

A thing you see every day is a thing you do not think about. You see a building, a car, a tree, a street, or a house every day. You see them and do not think about them. You do not say, but you know.
