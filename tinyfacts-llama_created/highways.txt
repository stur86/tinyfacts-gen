High ways are long roads that go across many cities and towns. They are usually very wide, with many cars and big buses going on them at the same time.

Imagine you are going to visit your friend who lives on the other side of a big city. You need to get there quickly. You can drive on a road that goes around the city, but it is very crowded. High ways are like a long road that goes around the city many times, so you can drive faster and see many other places before you get to your friend.

High ways can also go through the middle of a big area, like a big field or a big field of green. They help people get from one place to another, no matter how far they need to go.
