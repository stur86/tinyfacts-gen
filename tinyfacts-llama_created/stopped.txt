To stop means you stop doing something. You do a thing, and then you stop. You might stop running because a big thing is in front of you. You might stop talking to a friend because they are quiet. You might stop playing a game because you are tired.

When you stop a car, you use the hand that goes around the round thing that is in the middle of the car. This makes the car stop moving. You can also stop a thing that is moving by pressing a small round thing that is under it.

Stopping is important. If you do not stop, you might hit something. If you are running and you stop, you stay safe. If you are talking and you stop, you keep your voice down so you do not hurt anyone.

Sometimes, stopping can be hard. If you want to go to a place, but you are in the middle of it, you might stop. You stop looking at your phone and you stop thinking about the things you want to do later. You just stop and wait.

You can also stop something that is already happening. If a song is moving, you can stop it. You might press the stop thing on the music. If a game is running, you can stop it. You can use a game stop to pause the game.

Finally, you can stop something that is inside you. If you are feeling angry, you might stop it. You take a deep breath and you calm down. You stop your heart beating and you stop your mind. This helps you feel better.
