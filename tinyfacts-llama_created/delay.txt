A time thing is a word we use to say when things happen later than they should. We use it to talk about when you want to do something and when you actually do it.

Imagine you want to go to a movie. You put the movie in your car and drive to the movie house. But before you start the car, you have to pay money. This is like a time thing. You are using money to buy time.

When you say you will be there soon, you are saying you will be there later than you actually are. This is called a wait. You are waiting for something to happen.

Sometimes, you might have to wait for a phone call. If you are waiting, you might think about what you want to say or do next. You might feel nervous or think about how you feel.

A time thing can also mean when you are not doing what you want to do. You might be playing a game, but you are still waiting for your mom to finish the phone call. This is not a fun thing to do. You are waiting for a time when you want to do something else.

You can also use this word when you are going to a place. You might say, I am going to the store, but I will be there after I finish this work. This means you are doing one thing now and then you do another thing later.

So, a time thing is a word we use to talk about when things happen later than they should. It helps us know when to be ready and when to wait.
