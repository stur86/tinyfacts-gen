To settle means you are done with a thing. It is a word you use when you are finished with a problem or a fight.

Imagine you and a friend have been fighting for a long time. You both want the same thing, but you do not want to give it up. You are both very angry and want to yell. One night, you both go to bed. The next day, you meet again. This time, you sit down and talk. You say, you want to be friends. You find a way to share the thing you want. You both say, we are happy we can be friends. You have settled the thing. You are done with it.

You can also settle a problem in your head. If you are afraid of something, you might think about it for a long time. After you think about it a lot, you feel better. You have settled the fear. You are safe again.

Sometimes, you settle into a place. If you have been moving from one city to another for a long time, you might settle in a new town. You become a part of the town. You meet new people. You find a job. You are happy to be there. You have settled.

You can also settle a fight. If two people have a big fight, they might need to talk to each other. They might go to a place and sit down. They will listen to each other. They will say, we are sorry. They will try to understand how the other person felt. After they talk, they might decide to be friends. They have settled the fight.

To settle something is to close the door on it. It means you are done with it. You do not need to think about it anymore. You can move on to something else.
