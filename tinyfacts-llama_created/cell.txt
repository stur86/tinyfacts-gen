A cell is a very small living thing. It is the tiny part of an animal, a tree, or a rock. Inside the cell, there are many tiny bits that work together. These tiny bits give the cell its life.

Imagine you have a house. Inside the house, there are many rooms. Each room is a tiny part of the house. The rooms are all lined up in a line. Each room has its own job. The rooms work together to keep the house standing.

A cell is like that house. Each cell has many tiny rooms. Each room has many tiny bits. These tiny bits do different jobs. Some rooms are busy making food. Some rooms are busy making new rooms. Other rooms are busy making the walls. The rooms all work together.

A cell can live for many years. Some tiny living things that are too small to see can live for a very long time. They grow and change. They make new rooms or change the rooms they are already in. This is how they keep growing and stay strong.

Every living thing is made of many cells. Animals, people, trees, and rocks are all made of many cells. If you take a group of cells from one living thing, you can grow a new living thing with those cells. This is how people grow from babies to old people.

Cells can be very different. Some cells are very small. Some cells are very big. Some cells have many tiny bits that move around. Some cells have very few tiny bits. Each cell is different, but they all work together to keep things going.
