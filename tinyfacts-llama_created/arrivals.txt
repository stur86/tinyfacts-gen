Arrive is a word you use when people come to a place. When you arrive, you have finally reached your home. You are there.

Imagine you are going to a big city. You have been driving for many hours. You have stopped many times to rest and eat food. Now you are finally here. You are the first one to this place. You have been waiting for this moment for a very long time.

When you arrive, you say you have arrived. This is a way to tell people you are here. You might also want to say you are happy to be in this place. You might be excited because you are finally there.

You can also arrive at a place by foot or by a big thing that moves very fast. If you walk to a place, you have arrived. If you use a bus or a car to get there, you have also arrived. These are all ways to say you are here.

Sometimes, people say they are arriving. This means they will be there soon. They are looking forward to meeting others or doing things they enjoy.

When you arrive, you often need to find a place to stay. You might go to a room in a big building or to a home with a family. You might be in a room with other people who are also arriving. Everyone is there now.
