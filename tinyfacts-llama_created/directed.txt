Imagine you are a driver. You are the one who says, the way to the store is the left road. Your friends are the people who follow you. They follow your words and go where you say they go. This is a kind of direction. You are the one with the power.

You can also direction something that is not a person. If you want to make something happen, you might direction it. You tell the computer, the light, the water, the air, and the ground to do something. This helps the thing happen. If you direction a song, you tell the sound to go in a way that is nice and clear. You tell it to go fast or slow.

Sometimes, you have to direction something that is not a thing. You might direction a story or a movie. You tell the people who make the story what they should do. You tell them what to say and how to show it. This helps the story become a good story.

To direction a person is different. You are not telling them what to do. You are helping them understand what they have to do. You are like a friend who is there to give them the right words and the right way to do the thing.
