To show light is to make something look bright. When you look at a cold glass of water, the water looks dark. If you put a red thing in that water, the water looks red too. The red thing makes the water look red.

When you are very tired, your body starts to make light. This light is like a small bright thing that comes out of you. It is like a small sun in your head. You might feel warm when this light comes out. It is a sign that your body needs sleep.

You can also make things look bright when you put them in a bright room. This is why you can see things that were not there before. It is like the room is making the world look new.

Sometimes, when you use a small thing to make light, you can make it very strong. This is why you can use a small light in a room to show things to people. It helps everyone see what you are saying.

In a long time, people used to make fire. When you put wood in a box, it gets very hot and light comes out. This light is what makes the fire look bright. You can hold the light to your face to feel how hot it is.

We can also use this word to talk about things that are still. If something is still, it does not move or change. It stays the same. The sun is still in the sky at night. It is still there in the morning.
