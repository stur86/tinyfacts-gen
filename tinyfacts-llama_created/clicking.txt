To close something is to stop it. You can close your eyes. You close them so you are safe. You close your mouth to stop the air from going out. When you close a door, you are closing it.

Another way to close something is when you make it go quiet. If you are in a room and there are many people, the sound can be very loud. If you close the door, the sound goes away. The people in the room are quiet.

You can also close a thing that does not have a door. If you are playing a game with your friends, and you want to stop, you can close the game. This means you are stopping and you do not have to do anything else.
