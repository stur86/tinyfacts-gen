To get something is to find it. When you look, you might see a big animal. You want to see it more. You walk near it and you see it. You did not have it before.

If you want to drink water, you need to get it. You go to a house with water. You open a door and you drink. You got the water.

You can also get something you need. You go to a store and you buy food for your family. You pay with money. You got the food.

Sometimes, you need a thing to help you. You might go to a doctor. A doctor is a person who helps you feel better. They give you things that make you feel good.

When you get something, you might feel happy. You might feel like you got a big present. You did not have it before. This is what we mean by get.

We also use this word to talk about money. If you get money, you can buy things you want. If you do not have enough money, you might have to wait. But if you get money, you are safe.
