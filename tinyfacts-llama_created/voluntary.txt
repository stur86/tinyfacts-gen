To be a good friend means you help other people. You do things that make them feel better. You listen when they talk and you agree with them. You also do things on your own. You might help your brother when he is sad or you might help your mom when she is tired.

Sometimes you have to do something that you do not want to do. You might have to pick up a heavy box or go to the doctor. You do it because you want to be a good person. You want the other person to be happy.

When you say you are doing something, you usually use the word you. You can also say you are doing something for yourself. If you want to run, you say you are going to the park. This means you are doing it because you want to, not because you have to.

You can also say you are doing something for a group. You might be a leader in a class or a team. You help all the people around you. You are doing it because you want everyone to be safe and happy.

Being a good friend is important. It helps you feel happy and it helps the people around you feel happy too.
