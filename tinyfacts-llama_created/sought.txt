When you look for something, you are trying to find it. You might look in a room, in a book, or in the air. This is called a search.

If you are looking for a friend, you are looking for them. You look in your house, in the park, and in the street. You ask your family or your friends if they know where they are. If you find them, you are very happy.

Sometimes, you are looking for something that is not there. You might look in a dark room or in a box. You might ask questions to find out if the thing is still there. This is a big job.

You can also look for something that is in your head. You might feel sad or angry. You try to remember what happened. You look at pictures of happy times. You might write down your words. This helps you feel better.

Sometimes, you look for something that is not real. You might think a person is a friend, but they are actually just a person you met once. You look at them many times, but you still do not know them. This is a hard thing to do.
