A person, a thing, or a group of people that does something for another person, a thing, or a group of people. They do work for the person or the group. They must follow the person or group and do what the person or group tells them to do.

Think about a phone. The phone is a thing. When you press the little round thing, it makes the sound of a person on the other end of the phone. The phone does this job for you. It is a work thing.

You also have a job. Your job is to do work for your family. You might work in a school or a hospital. Your work makes the people who live with you safe. You do what the people tell you to do.

A computer is a work thing too. It does many jobs. It helps people write, it helps people talk to each other, and it helps the police and the doctors work. It follows the person who wants to use it. It does what the person tells it to do.
