To buy something is to get it. When you go to a place where many people buy things, you look for the people with the small paper they use to buy money. You look for the paper with numbers on it and the word that shows the time you need to pay. You tell the person how much money you have, and they tell you how much money they need. You give them the money, and they give you what you want.

A person who buys things is a buyer. They are looking for the things they need. Sometimes they buy things to give to their friends or family. They might buy food, clothes, or things that help them do their work. Other people buy things to make money. If you buy a car, you can drive it around and make money by working at a place where people pay you.

There are also people who buy things to make money for someone else. They buy things for a store or a big building. When they buy the things, they are telling the store that they want to pay later. The store will send the money later. This way, the person who bought the things does not get it right away. The store keeps the money until the person who wants to pay them does.
