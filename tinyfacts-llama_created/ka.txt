To make something smaller is to change it. You can use a small cup to drink from or a small box to keep your things. If you make a thing bigger, it becomes much larger. You can do this with a computer. If you make a computer small, it runs fast and you can play games.

People also make things bigger by putting them in a room. Imagine a small room with many tall walls. If you put a large box in the room, the room gets much bigger. You can stand in the room and feel the space inside.

When you make something smaller, you are changing how it looks and how it feels. You can make a small dog feel like a tiny animal. You can make a small house feel like a big room.
