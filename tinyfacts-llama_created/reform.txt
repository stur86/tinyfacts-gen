To change or to make something new.

Imagine you are at a place that does not want children. The people there are very angry. They want everyone to leave. They think it is not right to let children be near them. They want to make sure no one stays.

A person who is in power can do this. They tell everyone that they are doing this to keep people safe. They do this by making things look different. They say the things they want are better for everyone.

This is the same as what a teacher does. If a teacher is not happy with a student, they might change the way the student does their work. They might give the student more work or ask them to do it in a different way.

Sometimes, this is good. It helps people feel safer. But sometimes, it is not good. It can make the people who were happy feel angry. They might want to stop the changes. They might want the old way to come back.

To change is to move. It is about being different. It is about taking on a new way to see things.
