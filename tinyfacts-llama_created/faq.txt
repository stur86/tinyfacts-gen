A question and answer is a group of things that help people understand something. It is like a book for a word. A question is when someone asks a question. An answer is when someone gives a good reason for their answer.

Think about a game. In the game, there are many questions. If you want to know how to do something, you look at the book. You read the questions and answers. You can learn.

Sometimes, people write a group of questions and answers for other people. This group is like a book for everyone. It helps people when they ask the same question many times.

You can also find this kind of group on the computer. It might say things like how to make a picture or how to use a word. The group of things helps you when you need to know.

Some groups are about fun things. You can ask, and the group gives you a story. You can ask, and the group gives you a joke. You can ask, and the group gives you a way to do something.
