To act means to do or pretend to do something. You act when you make a plan. You might want to do a job. You act it by getting ready. You put on clothes. You might say words to a friend. You act like you are another person. This makes the story more interesting.

You act when you are a person or a thing. You act in a movie. You act in a play. You act in a game. You act when you act like you are a person in a story. This helps us understand what is happening.

If you act bad, you do things that hurt people. If you act good, you help people and make them happy. You act when you follow a plan. You act when you follow the words of a teacher or a leader. You act when you follow the ways of a group or a family.

To act can be hard. You might feel nervous. You might want to stop. But then you do it anyway. You act because you need to.
