To get a thing to work is often called to get something that lets you use it. This can happen with many different things. 

Imagine you want to play a game on a computer. You might need to pay a small money to the people who run the game. If you pay, you get to use the game for a few days. After that, you have to pay again each time you want to play. This is like getting a tiny key. The key is only for you. You can use it only on your computer.

A different kind of thing is a way to act. If you want to drive a car, you need to follow very many ways to stay safe. You must follow a set of things. If you do not follow those ways, you might hurt yourself or other people. This is like getting a job. You do a work, and you get paid. You can only do that work.
