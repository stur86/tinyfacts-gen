To give something to someone means you are sending them a thing they need. You might give them food, a book, or a small thing that helps them. This is different from giving them something they already have. If you give a friend a book, they already have books. If you give them food, they already eat.

Sometimes you give a thing to someone who is far away. This can happen when you send a letter or a small box with your name on it. The person gets it later. You did not give them the box or the letter in one go. You sent it over a long time.

You can also give something to someone while you are still there. If you give your friend money, you are giving them money right now. You can give them money in a store or give it to them in your pocket. When you give it, you are helping them buy things they need.

To get something is also an important word. When you get a job, you are getting money to work for a while. You do work, and then you get paid. You do not give money to the person who works for you. Instead, the person pays you. This is how people get money so they can do what they want.
