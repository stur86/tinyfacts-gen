A thing that makes it hard for people to do something.

Imagine you want to walk across a big water. There are many long pieces of wood or strong stuff across the water. These things are called a high wall or a wall. They keep you from reaching the other side.

You can not cross them. If you try to go, you will hit the wood or the strong stuff. It is hard to get over. It stops you from moving forward.

This is what a wall does. It makes it hard for people to do what they want. It is not a thing you build. It is just there, standing in the ground.

Sometimes, these things are made by people. They build walls to keep people out who do not want to be there. They build walls to keep bad people from getting to a city.

Sometimes, a wall is built by the people who live in a city. They build a wall to keep people from going into their city. It keeps them safe and helps the people who live there feel better.

In some places, a wall is also a thing that holds back the water. A long wall of rock or hard stuff keeps the water from going over. This helps keep the ground safe and does not let the water come into the town.

The word also means a thing that stops a person from doing something. If you try to take something that does not go in your bag, you might get in trouble. The thing is there to keep you from doing what you want.
