To notice something means you see it. It happens when you look at a person, a thing, or a place. You might not see it at first. Then, you see it. You feel a feeling. You are happy that you noticed it.

You can notice things that are big. You might see a tree, a car, or a house. You can also notice small things. You might hear a sound that you did not hear before. You might see a tiny animal. Every time you notice something, you remember it.

Sometimes, you notice something that is not there. You might see a dark room. You might hear a sound that is very quiet. You might feel a strange feeling. This is a kind of surprise.

When you notice something, you understand it. You understand what is happening. You do not have to think about it. You just see it and know it.

You can also notice things that are hard. You might notice how hard it is to breathe. You might notice how hard it is to walk. You might notice how hard it is to stay calm. These are strong ways to notice.

To notice is a way to see and understand. It helps us stay safe. If you notice a road that is broken, you can go around it. If you notice a dog, you can follow it. If you notice a person, you can say hello.
