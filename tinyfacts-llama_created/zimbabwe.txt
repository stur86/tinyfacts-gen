A long time ago, in a far away land, there was a very old and very important place. It was a land with many big red rocks and big green areas. This place was very cold and very far from the water.

Many years ago, the people who lived there were very happy. They had big houses and many children. But then, the people started to fight with each other over food and land. They made many fights and had a great warmed down the land.

After many years, the people decided they wanted to leave the land. They went to another place with more water and a different kind of rock. They left behind many of their old homes and many of their old ways of living.

The people of the old place were very sad. They had to leave behind many of their old friends and many of their old ways of living. They had to learn a new way of living in the new place.

The new place was very different from the old place. The air was warmer, and the water was closer to the ground. The people had to find new ways to grow food and find new ways to make things.

Even though they left behind many of their old ways, the people of the new place were very happy. They built new homes and new schools. They made new ways to work and to live together.

Today, the old place is still a very important place for many people. Many people go there to visit the old homes and the old places where they lived. They also go there to learn about their old ways of living and to try to understand what happened to them.

The old place is also a very important place for the animals. Many of the big animals that were once there still live there. Some of the animals went to the new place, and some of the animals stayed behind.

The old place is not a very big place anymore. It is very small. But it is still a very important part of the world.
