To pay something is to give money to the person who owns it. This is how people buy things they need. You buy food to eat, a ball to play with, a book to learn, or a car to go around.

When you buy something, you give the person the money you give them. The money helps the person buy the thing they want. In this way, you are helping the person have the thing they need.

Sometimes, you also pay for a thing you use. If you go to a place that makes music, you pay money so you can hear the music. You give the money to the person who makes the music.

You can also pay money to people who do work for you. If you need a new job, you give money to the person who will do the job. They use this money to buy things they need to do their job.

When you pay, you are showing that you care about the thing or person you are giving money to. It is a way to say, I think you are important, and I am paying to show that.
