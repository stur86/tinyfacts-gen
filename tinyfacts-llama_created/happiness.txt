Happier means to feel very good inside. When you are happy, you do not worry about anything. You can laugh and smile. You feel like you are in the right place.

Sometimes, things happen that make you happy. You get a new job, you meet a new friend, or you get something you have been wanting. When this happens, you feel a strong feeling of being happy.

When you are happy, your face might look a little bit brighter. You might feel a little bit warmer inside. It is like a warm feeling that goes down to your chest.

You can also get happy from things you do. If you run or jump, you might feel happy because you are moving quickly. Or if you play with a favorite game, you might feel happy because you are having fun.

But when you are happy, you do not always feel this way. Sometimes, you might feel sad or worried. If something bad happens, you might feel very sad. But even when you are sad, you can still feel happy later when you are with a good friend or when you have something good to look forward to.

It is a good feeling to be happy. It helps us feel better when things are hard.
