To break something is to stop it. You can break a hard thing. You push it with your hands. This makes it stop falling down. You can also break a thing that is moving. If you are moving fast, you have to slow down so you do not hit anything.

To break a word is to stop talking. You say something, but then you stop. You might breathe or your eyes get heavy. This is a sign that your brain has stopped thinking.

When you are driving a car, you need to break the power. If you are going too fast, your car will start to move again. You need to slow down by pressing a hard part on the floor. This makes your car stop and stay still. You can also use a word to break the power. If you are afraid, you can say, in a quiet voice, that you are afraid. This breaks the power and makes you feel safe.
