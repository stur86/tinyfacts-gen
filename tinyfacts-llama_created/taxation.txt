To pay for something you use or live on is called a kind of money. When you work or do work for a business, you give them some of this money. This money is paid to the people who run the business. This money helps pay for the roads you drive on, the schools you go to, and the police who keep you safe.

Think about it like a big group of people. Everyone in the group pays a little bit of money. This money is taken from each person and given to the group. The group uses this money to pay for many things.

In some places, the money you pay is not the same for everyone. Some people pay a little money and some pay a lot. The money you pay is often used to pay for the same things for everyone.

There are also some places where you pay money to the people who give you food or clothes. If you work hard, you might give a small piece of money to these people. They use this money to buy food and clothes for everyone.

When you pay money, you are helping the people who run your life. You are paying for the roads you walk on, the schools you go to, and the police who keep you safe.
