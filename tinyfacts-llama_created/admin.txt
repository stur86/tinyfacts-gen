An office is a place where many people work. In this place, there is a person who is in the middle. This person helps everyone and makes sure they are doing their jobs right. 

This person is not the one who makes the food. They do not decide what is eaten. They do not decide who is the head of the group. They only help the people who do the work. They are the one who answers when a question is asked. They are the one who tells the people when a job is done or when something is wrong. 

This person is like a small head. They are not the top person but they are very important. They help the top person and all the other people in the office work well together. They are the one who keeps everyone moving in the same way.
