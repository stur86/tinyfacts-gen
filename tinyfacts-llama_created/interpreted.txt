To understand something means to see how it is. When you see a word, you do not just look at it. You also think about it. You might wonder what it means. You want to know what it does.

A word can mean many things. It can mean a person or a place. It can mean an idea. It can mean a thing that is hard to do. It can mean a feeling. It can mean a story.

When you talk to someone, you often use words. You use words to share your ideas. You use words to show how you feel. You use words to tell your story.

Think about a story. A story is a way of telling what happened. You use words to tell the story. You use words to show who is doing the thing. You use words to show why. You use words to tell you how.

People often try to understand each other. They want to know what the other person is saying. They want to know what the other person means. They use words to ask questions. They use words to listen.

When you understand a word, you are not just knowing the word. You are understanding the idea behind it. You are understanding how to use the word. You are understanding how the word fits into the story.

So, to be understood means to know something. It means to see how the word works. It means to see how the word fits into the story.
