A thing that looks like a small animal is called a searcher. You can find them in the water of the big blue place. They have a hard bottom on their backs that keeps them safe. They come out of the water when the water is too cold.

Sometimes you see a large round thing on the ground. This is what people call a ball. But a ball is not a small animal. It is something that goes very high into the sky and does not come back down.

A group of these small animals is called a school. When many of them move together, they cover a lot of water. They help keep the water clean by eating small animals that live in the water.
