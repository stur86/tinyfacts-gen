To work means you do things. You do not just sit or play. You do things to get money, to make food, or to fix things that break.

Imagine you are a person who helps a big house. You do many jobs. You clean the floors, you fix the windows, and you make sure everything is in order. You do all these things every day so the house stays safe and clean for people to live in.

You also work when you do things in a school. If you are a student, you work on papers and books. You work on a job that helps teachers teachers. You work on a job that helps people learn new things.

If you are a person who makes things, you work. You sit at a desk and use a computer. You write stories, you make pictures, or you build a thing that flies in the sky. You do all these things to make more things for people to buy or to watch.

If you are a person who makes food, you work. You work in a kitchen. You take in many things and make food for people to eat. You work all day so food is ready for dinner and breakfast the next morning.

We all need to work. Without people who work, there would be no food, no money, and no homes.
