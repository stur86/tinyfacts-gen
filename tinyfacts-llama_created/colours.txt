To see colors is to feel a world of light. Every person sees the world in their own way, but all of them see the world with colors.

Some colors are very bright. They are like a bright sun. When you look at a bright blue sky, you see blue. You see green when you look at a bright green tree. You see red when you look at a bright red fire. These colors are very easy to see. You can see them in a room with a window.

Other colors are not so bright. They are like a quiet night. You may not notice them at first, but you can still see them. These colors are very important for people who do not have bright eyes. They help them see things that would be hard to see because of how bright the world is.

There are many more colors than you can see. Some are very small and very far away. Other colors are very big and very close to you. A color that is very far away is often green. It is a very good color for a green tree. A color that is very close is red. It is a good color for a fire.

Sometimes, people make colors by putting different things together. They can make white by putting many white things together. They can make red by putting many red things together. They can make blue by putting many blue things together. This is how we get many colors.
