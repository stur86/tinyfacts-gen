To reach a point is to have a clear idea of what happened. When you feel sure about something, you can stop thinking about it. This is called a moment.

Imagine you are trying to decide whether to go to a movie or to a party. You think about it for a long time. You look at the movie and the party. You think about what you like and what you do not like. After a while, you have a clear idea. You know what you want to do.

You can also reach a point when you have learned all the answers to a question. You have studied or listened. You know the truth about something. You feel complete.

Sometimes, we reach a point when something is finished. A story is over. A game is done. A person has done something important. This is also a moment.

In life, we reach many points. When you start a new job. When you meet someone you like. When you learn to do something new. When you know the answer to a hard question. These are all moments of reaching a point.

Sometimes, we reach a point that is hard to understand. We might feel confused. We might need to think about it some more. But in the end, we reach a clear point. We see what is true.
