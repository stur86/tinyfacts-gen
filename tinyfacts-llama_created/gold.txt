To be a good friend is to have the power to make others feel safe and loved. A friend is a person you trust and who trusts you. When you are a good friend, you help each other in times of need. You listen when someone is sad or angry. You help them to feel better.

Being a good friend is not only about being nice. You must also be true. You must not lie to each other. You must not do something that you know is wrong. If you do this, the other person might feel angry with you. This can hurt your relationship.

A friend can give you things that you need. They can tell you that you are doing a good job or that you are strong. They can show you that they believe in you. They can also give you things that help you feel better when you are sad.

A friend can also be someone who is there when you need them the most. If you are afraid to go to school, your friend is there to help you. If you are feeling sad, your friend listens to you without saying a word. They are there for you when you need them most.

Being a good friend is a big job. You must be kind, listen, and show that you care. You must also be there when the other person needs you.
