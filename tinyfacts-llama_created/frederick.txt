Freed means you are no longer a part of a group that is doing something against you. It is about your life being your own.

Imagine a man who is a big part of a group that is making money. He is afraid to say what he really thinks. He follows the words of the people around him. But one day, he is not afraid anymore. He says what he really thinks. This is being free.

A person can also be free if they are not afraid of the things that make them afraid. If a person is afraid of a great big animal, they might stay away from the animal. But if they find a way to stay near the animal without being afraid, they are free.

You can also be free if you do what you want. If you love a person and you do not want them to leave you, you are free to stay with them. You are your own person.

The word comes from a story about a man who was a great leader in a far away land. He did not want anyone to tell him what to do. He was very free. He could do what he wanted and he did not have to answer to anyone else. He was the one who decided.

Today, we use the word to talk about people who are very free. They can do what they want, and they are not afraid to say it. They are like the big group, but they are not part of it anymore. They are their own people, and they get to decide their own way.
