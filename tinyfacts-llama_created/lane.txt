A line is a way that things go. You can walk on a line, or you can drive on a line. If you are walking, you are following a line on the ground. You follow it until you reach your house or your school.

A line can also be a line of people. Imagine a long way with many people walking in the same direction. They are all following the same line. This line is like a team that is doing something together.

Sometimes, you see a line of cars. They are moving in a straight line. If you want to get to a place, you might follow this line to get there. You can drive on it and follow it until you reach your home or your school.

There is also a line of things. Imagine you have many books on a table. If you want to put a book away, you can place it on a line. You pick the book and put it on the line. You do this until all the books are on the line.

Sometimes, you see a line of money. This line of money is like a group of people who are trying to buy things they want. They are following the same line to get to the store or the place they need to go.

Being on a line means you are following the way things are. It is about doing what is right and following the order of things.
