An opening is a place you go through. It is where you start a new thing. A door is a kind of opening. It is usually made of wood or other hard stuff. It has a line in it that you push to open it. You can also push a small thing through the line to go through.

When you go into a room, you are entering the room. You step into the space where people live, work, or play. The room is where people can be together or work. It is also a place to feel safe or calm.

You can also enter a building. If you go into a big house, you enter the living room. If you go into a school, you enter the room where the teacher talks.

Sometimes, people enter a place because they have a problem. If you have a problem at work, you might go into a meeting room to talk to your friend or your manager. You are entering a new space to try to find a way out or to find help.

The word can also mean the first thing you see when you enter a room. If you walk into a room, you see the chairs, the table, and the floor. The door is behind you.
