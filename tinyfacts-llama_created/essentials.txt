To be an important part of something means you are very good at what you do. You need to be there so people know you are there.

Think about your bed. Your bed is an important part of your home. You sleep on it. You use it to sit. You use it to stay warm in the cold night. You need it so you can rest.

You also need food to stay strong. You eat food to give your body the power it needs to do its job. You need to drink water so your body can stay clean and keep you feeling well. A car needs food to run and move. A dog needs food to stay strong and have fun.

You need things to live. You need air to breathe. You need water to drink. You need a place to rest. You need money to buy the things you need. You need friends to talk to and share your life with.

When you say something is an important part, you are telling the world that it is very good at what it does. It is the thing you are most focused on. You are saying, this is what you care about most. This is the thing you love.

If you have an idea, you want it to be an important part of what you do. You want it to be the key to making your life better. You want it to be the thing that makes your heart feel happy.
