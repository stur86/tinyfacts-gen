The word pop comes from a very old way of making music. A long time ago, people made music by hitting things together. They used a long piece of wood with a small round part at the end. They would hit the round part and make different sounds. This made a lot of different sounds all at once.

When people wanted to make a single sound that was very bright and fast, they would hit the wood many times very quickly. This made a very loud, very bright sound. They called this sound a pop. The word comes from the sound of the hit. When the wood hits the round part, it makes a small, bright sound. This is why they call it a pop.

Today, the word pop has other uses too. You can pop a piece of food that is very hard. This happens when you open the food and it goes out in a small, bright way. You can also pop a ball when you hit it with your hand. This makes the ball go very fast.

Sometimes, people use the word pop to talk about something that is very easy to understand. If a person says something is a pop, it means they think it is very easy to do. They are not trying very hard. This can be a good thing because it makes things feel less hard.
