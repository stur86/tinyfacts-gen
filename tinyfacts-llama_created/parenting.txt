To parents is to care for children. When a child is very small, the parents do many things for them. They pick them up, put them in their bed, and give them food. The parents also teachers. They show the child how to walk, how to use a table, and how to be kind to others.

A parents also talks to the child. They listen to what the child wants and makes sure the child is safe. When the child makes a bad thing to do, the parents say, but also they help the child to feel better and learn from the story.

Parents teachers are people who help the child learn. They might take the child to school, read books, or do fun things together. They show the child how to act and how to be happy. This way, the child grows up to be a person who is kind and strong.

Some parents do not have kids. They work in a big building or a school, and they care for other people. They help them with their work and show them how to be a good person. These people are called work parents. They are very important because they show kids that life is a work that needs to be done.

In some families, there are many parents. This is called a big family. They all help each other and make sure everyone is happy. They share food, clothes, and money. They also have fun together.

In some families, there is only one parents. The parents are very strong and make sure the child is safe. They might do things for the child all the time, but they also let the child do their own things.

Everyone learns to be a parents at a time. As the child grows older, they learn to care for themselves. They start to do things by themselves and take care of their own needs. This is a big step in life, and parents are very happy to see this happen.
