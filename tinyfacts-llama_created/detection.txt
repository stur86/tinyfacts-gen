To notice something

When you see a person, a thing, or a sound, you are the first to see it. This is the beginning of a story. If you do not see it at first, you must wait and watch. You are looking for it. It is not there yet, but you are looking.

In life, you often look for things. You look at a face to see who it is. You look at a room to see if it is empty. You look at the sky to see if there are stars. You look at a book to see what is inside. You look at a word to see if it is the right one.

Sometimes you look for something hard to find. You look for a word that you know is there. You look for a person who is hiding. You look for a thing that is gone.

You can also use the word in other ways. You might say you are a good person. You are a person who does the right thing. You are a person who is kind to others. You are a person who wants to help.

You can also use the word to talk about what you see. If you see a big tree, you are saying this is a thing that is very tall. If you see a dog, you are saying this is a thing that can do a lot.

The word is important because it tells us what to look for. It helps us see what is happening. It helps us know that something is there. It is a word that helps us notice the world around us.
