When you join two things, you make them one. This happens when you put two parts together. You can join a person and a thing so that they become one person with the thing.

Imagine you have a small box. You want to join the box with a long piece of wood. You can put the wood into the box. Now the box has a long part inside it. The wood is the part that joins the box. You can also join a person with a dog. The person might walk with the dog. Now the person is a part of the dog.

You can join many things. You can join a book with another book. You can join a table with chairs. This makes the table look like a big room.

Sometimes, you join two words. When you join two words, you make them one word. This happens when you want to say a bigger word. You can join the word and the word to make a word that means a lot. You can also join the word with the word to make a word that means something that happened.
