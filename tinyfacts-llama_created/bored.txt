To be bored means your brain feels very empty. You might have done the same things many times before. You might have watched the same show, read the same book, or played the same game for a long time.

Sometimes, you just need to do nothing. You sit in a room, or you stand in a park. You look at the same wall. You listen to the same music. You do not want to talk to anyone. You just want to stay in the same place.

You feel this way because your mind is not working very well. Your heart is not happy. You might feel like you are going to fall asleep. You want to do something fun.

This feeling can be very hard. It can make you feel sad. But it is also a good thing. When you are bored, you can think about other things. You can watch a movie, look at pictures, or play a game on a computer. This can help you forget the time.

You can also get bored while you are doing something you do not like. If you are sitting in a room when you do not want to, you might start to feel bored. You might start to think about other things, like what you are going to do next. You might start to think about how you can escape from the room.
