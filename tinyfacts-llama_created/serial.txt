To be in a line means you are in a group of things that are the same. Each one has the same number. You are all the same, but you are still in a group with other ones that are the same.

When you say a story is in a line, you are talking about a book. The story has many parts. Each part is a part of the story. You can read one part and then read another part after it. You can read them one after another.

People also use this word with a group of things that are the same. You have a friend, a family, a group of animals, a group of people who do the same job. You are all part of the same group.

You can also say a person is in a line with others. If you are in a line to go to school, you are following the line that leads to your school. You follow the line to get to your school.

Sometimes you see a movie or a show that is in a line. Each person in the show is in a line with other people. They all do the same thing, but each one is doing it in their own way.
