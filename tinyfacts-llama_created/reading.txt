To read is to understand what a word says before you look at the letters. When you read, you move your eyes from one line to another and find the words. A person reads books, stories, and even stories about animals.

Imagine you have a book with many words. One day, you start to read a story. The words come from the book to your brain. Your brain says, yes, this word is real. This word is important. You keep reading until you reach the end. Then, you stop and think about what happened.

You can also read a letter. A letter is a small part of a word. When you read a letter, you look at the word it is part of. You might read one word and then another. This helps you understand what the person wrote.

Some people like to read in their own way. They might read slowly, or they might read quickly. Some people like to look at the words one by one, while others like to look at the whole story at once. Some read books with pictures, and others read only words.

To read is important because it helps you understand the world. You can learn new things, like a teacher. You can also learn about people and their lives. You can understand what they are feeling. Reading can make you feel happy, sad, or angry.

Some things you read are for fun. You might read about your favorite game. You might read about a new movie. Or you might read a book about the world or a long story about a man who did great things. You can also read about animals, like dogs or cats, or about the stars in the sky.
