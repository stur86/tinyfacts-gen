When the sky is dark and the air is full of water, the power inside gets very strong. This power wants to make a very loud sound. When it gets ready, it starts making a sound all around the world. The sound goes up and down and round, and it is very bright. It is like a huge wave of air that goes very fast.

The sound is called a loud noise. It is much louder than the sound of a dog or a car. It is like the sound of many people talking at once. Some people hear it from far away, and some people hear it right next to them.

When the sound gets close to a house or a tall building, it is much louder. The sound goes up through the walls and around the corners. It is like a very bright wave of air that is going through the house.

Sometimes, the sound is very bright and white. This is when the power inside is very strong. It is like a huge light in the sky. When this happens, people cover their eyes to stay safe.

Other times, the sound is more like a deep sound. This is when the power inside is not as strong. It is like a deep sound that comes from far away. People might feel the ground shaking a little bit.

When the sound goes away, the sky is dark again. The power inside has gone back to a smaller, safer part of the sky.
