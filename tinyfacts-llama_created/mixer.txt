A thing that makes food or drinks is called a thing that makes. It is a box or a thing with many small parts. Some have many small round parts that move in a line. These small parts go round and round. They turn the food or drink, so it becomes a different kind of food or drink.

You can make your own. You can use one of these things and some food. You put the food in a big, round thing with many small parts. You close the top. Then the thing does the work for you. After a while, the food is ready. You can eat it.

There are also big things that make food for people. These big things take many small things and make a very good food. They take some of the small parts and some of the food to make the food. They do this in a long room. After the food is made, it goes to places where people eat it.

These things are very important. They make food for many people each day. They help us eat and stay strong.
