A widest

To make something big

Imagine you are looking at a very wide room. The whole room is open in front of you. There is no wall on one side. There is also a big wall behind you, but you do not see it because you are looking straight at it.

A movie is made on a computer. The computer makes the whole picture. It makes the big wall in front of you and the big wall behind you. It makes a wide room with many sides. This is why the movie looks like a wide room.

A thing that looks like this is called a widest. It is a thing that makes a wide picture. Some people have widest on their phones. They can watch a wide room with them. This is good because they do not need to leave their home.

Sometimes, when you watch a movie, the picture might not be very big. The picture might look small. This happens if the computer does not make the picture big enough. It might look small because it is not enough room.

A widest can also be a thing that is very strong. It can hold a lot of power. A car with a big widest can go very fast. It can go very far in a short time.

There is also a type of picture that is very big. It is like the wide room, but it is not on a computer. You can see it on a big wall or on a very big piece of paper. This picture can tell you things from a far away place. It can tell you where things are and how they are moving.
