To do a head or a neck check is to see if you are safe. It helps you know if you are hurt or not. You do this with a person who is afraid. You look at their face and see if they are crying or if they are still okay.

Sometimes you do this with yourself. If you feel very bad, you might do a head or a neck check. You might touch your face, look at your mouth, and see if you are still breathing. You might also look at your eyes to see if they are closed.

You can do this with a dog too. If the dog is scared, you might check them. You look at their eyes, feel their ears, and see if they are still safe. If the dog is hurt, you might help them feel better.

When we talk about doing a head or a neck check, we are talking about being safe. We check to see if the bad thing is still happening. If you feel safe, you feel better.
