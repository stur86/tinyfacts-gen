To ask means to want to know something. You might ask a friend if you want to go to the movies tonight. You might ask a teacher if you have a problem on a paper. You are not sure what you want, but you are trying to figure it out.

You can ask a question to someone you know. You might say, I do not know why. You are not sure why something happened. Or you might say, How can you do that?

You can also ask a question to a person who is not your friend. You might say, I do not know why you said that. The person might not be sure why you said it either.

Sometimes, you ask a question to get something. You might want food, a drink, or a new game. You are showing that you need the thing. Asking is a way to show you are thinking and want to know the answer.
