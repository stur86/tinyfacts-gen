The word you are looking for is the number seven. This number is often the number of the week. There are six days in a week. The first day is the day we start on. The next day is the second day. After the second day comes the third day. Then comes the day after that, which is the day after the third day.

There are only seven days in a week. You do not have seven days in a month. You do not have seven days in a year. But you have seven days in a week!

Some people like to use this number when they talk about the number of a thing. It is a number that tells us how many times a thing happens. If you have seven books, you have found seven books. If you have seven colors, you have found seven colors.
