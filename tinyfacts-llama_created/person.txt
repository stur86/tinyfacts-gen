A person is a living thing that has a heart. They are not animals, but they live on this big world we call home. You are a person.

To be a person is to have your own mind. You can think, feel, and want things. You can say what you think and feel. You can do things with your body, like walk or run. You can also do things with your hands and feet.

Most people live in cities or towns. They work, eat, sleep, and play. They may go to school or college to learn more. Some people work with their hands, like a doctor or a teacher. Others work with their heads, like a writer or a leader.

People are different from one another. You might be very tall or very small. You might have hair, eyes, or skin that is different from others. You might like to eat food from one place or another. You might like to play games or watch movies.

Even though people are different, we all share something. We all need air to breathe, water to drink, and a place to live. We all want to be happy and to have friends. We all have hopes and fears.

People are also important because we help each other. We share our food, our clothes, and our money. We work together when we need to fix a house or when we are sick. We tell each other when we are happy or sad. We listen when someone is scared or angry.

In short, a person is a living thing that lives on this world. They are their own people, with their own minds and their own ways.
