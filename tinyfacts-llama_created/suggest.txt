To suggest something means to think about a thing and tell others about it. You do not have to tell them all at once. You just think about it and then say it in front of them.

Imagine you have a friend who loves to go to the movies. You are sitting in a room with them. You see a movie you think is very good. You might say, 

I think this movie is going to be great. I want to see it so I can tell you about it. I think it is going to be an exciting one. 

Your friend asks you what you think. You answer, 

I think it is going to be a very good movie. I am looking forward to it. I hope you like it too.

In this way, you are suggesting the movie to your friend. You are showing them what you think. You are not telling them everything. You are only telling them enough to make them want to watch it.

Sometimes, you might suggest something that is not a real thing. You might say, 

I think you should go to the park today. You look like you need some air. It is a beautiful day.

Here, you are not suggesting a real thing. You are just telling your friend what you think. You are trying to help them decide what to do.

In short, to suggest something means to think about a thing and tell others about it so they can know it.
