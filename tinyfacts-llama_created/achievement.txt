To have an idea is to have a plan. You think about what you want to do. You make a plan. You break the plan into many small steps. You do each step one after the other. This is how you get closer to your idea.

You can also have an idea in your heart. This is when you know you want to do something. You feel like it is the right thing to do. You might feel a little scared, but you are happy. You do not need to follow a plan. You just go and do it.

Sometimes you get an idea while you are doing something. You see something and you feel like it is the right thing to do. You do it and you feel very happy.

You can also have an idea when you do not have a plan. You see a problem. You think, and you get an idea. You can do something about it. You use your brain to find a way. This is a big idea.
