To shut something means to make it stop working. You can do this with a door, a box, or a computer. When you shut a door, you close it so the light from outside can't come in.

You can also shut a computer. If you are playing a game and your friend asks you to leave, you can shut the computer. This helps the computer rest and does not keep the game going.

Sometimes people shut a door or a box so they are alone. They might close the door to keep something inside or to feel safe.

In a different way, you can shut your eyes. This helps you stay quiet and do not see things that are not there. When you shut your eyes while you sleep, your body gets ready for the next day.
