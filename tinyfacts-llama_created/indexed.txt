When you look through a book, you can find many words. If you need to find the word that means a small animal, you look for it in the book. You might have to read through many other words to find it.

An ordered set is a group of numbers or words in a very clear order. Imagine you have a box with many small boxes inside. Each small box has a number on it. The numbers tell you where the small box should go. You can open all the small boxes and see what they are.

If you have a large set of many many numbers, you can order them. This order is like a line. You can go through the line one number at a time. You can find a number by looking at the first number, then the next number, and so on.

The word we use is an ordered set. It is an ordered set that is very clear. You can look at it one number at a time. You can find any number in the set. You can not find any number without looking at it first. This is why we say it is an ordered set.
