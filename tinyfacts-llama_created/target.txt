To hit a ball is to move it from one side of a hard ground to the other. If you are a player and you throw the ball to a friend, the friend must catch it. This is a game where you try to hit the ball with your hands or a stick.

When you say a thing is a hard ground, you mean it is made of very hard stuff that can not be broken. This ground has a hard bottom and sides. This ground is the place where a game is played. In this game, people hit a ball with their hands or a stick.

A person who plays this game is called a player. They run across the ground while trying to hit the ball with the stick or hands. If they hit the ball, they get to run around the ground. They also try not to hit the other people. One person might hit the ball a lot. This person is the best at this game. They can hit the ball very far and very fast.

A game is the whole thing. There is a start time and a end time. After the time is over, there is no more game. But you can watch the games later and learn about the people who played them. You can also try to play the game at home with a ball.
