To show something means to show a person or thing that is true. You can show a picture of a house to someone so they see it. You can also show a game to a child so they understand how to play it.

When you show something, you tell people you are saying it. You use your words and face to show the truth. This helps everyone understand each other.

Imagine you are trying to tell a child you love them. You can show them by giving them a big hug. You are showing them how you feel. This is a kind way to show love.

You can also show something through your body. If you walk fast, you are showing that you can do it. If you smile at a friend, you are showing that you are happy. Your body is a way to show how you feel.

Sometimes, you show something by doing it. If you say you are going to the store, you are showing that you plan to do it. You are telling people you are going. You are showing them you are ready to act.

To show can be different for different people. One person might show through their words. Another person might show through their body. Both ways help people understand each other.
