To use something means you use it for your own needs. You might use a phone to talk to your friends. You might use a car to go to school. You do not hold the thing while you use it. You use it in a way that makes life easier.

When you are using something, you are doing something with it. If you use a book, you read it. If you use a table, you sit down on it. You do not just look at it. You are taking part in what it is for.

Sometimes, you use the word to talk about how people act. If a person always does something wrong, they might use the word for how they act. If a person is very kind, they might say they are using the word for being kind.

You can also use the word when you talk about things that are not new anymore. If you say a thing is old, it means it has been around for a long time. If you say a thing is old because it is old, you are using the word to show how something stays the same over many years.

It is a word that tells us how things work and how people act.
