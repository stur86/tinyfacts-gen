A word tells is when you tell a story. You use your voice and your body to show what is happening. You might say things, move your arms, or show a picture. This helps people understand what is happening.

Imagine you are telling a story to a friend. You might say,  The sun was up in the sky. The dog was happy. The baby laughed. Your friend understands what you are saying. They know the story is about a day when a baby laughed at a dog.

A tell can be a long story. It can be about a person who went on a big trip. It can be about a fight that happened between two people. It can even be about a day when a group of friends went to a park.

Sometimes, a tell is not a story at all. You might just tell a fact. You might say,  The sun is in the sky. The stars are in the sky. The night is dark. This is telling a fact. It helps us know what is happening at night.

A tell can also be about a person who is doing something. You might say,  He is running. He is moving quickly. He is very fast. This tells us what the person is doing.
