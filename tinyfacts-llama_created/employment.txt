To work means to do a job for money. You might be a teacher, a doctor, a police man, or a person who builds things. Your job is to do things all day. You use your hands, your brain, and your body to make things that people need.

When you work, you are not home. You go to a place that is called a work place. There is a room where people sit and talk. There are many computers and tables there. You may work with other people who do the same job.

You might work in a big building or in a small house. You might work on a bus or in a car. You might work from a room in a tall building or from a small room in a home.

You get money to buy things you need. You can buy food, clothes, and things that help your body. You also get a place to live and a safe way to get home at night.

Sometimes, people do things that are not part of their job. They might work on their own. They might work at night. They might work while they are tired or while they are doing other things. But they are still working.

You do not have to be alone all day. There are other people around you. Some people are friends with you. Some people are your family. Some people are people you work with. They help you do your job and make life easier.

Being an worker is important. It keeps our world moving. It gives us food, clothes, and a place to live. It helps us build things, keep our homes safe, and make sure everyone is safe.
