To be or to do something is to show your self to the world. When you are very happy, you might shout out a word. If you are very good at a game, you might laugh and say, I am the best. 

When you are very strong, you can lift heavy things. When you have a dog, you must walk it and give it food. When you are very good at a job, you can do it many times. You are not afraid to show how well you did it.

If you do something, you are doing it. If you say you are going to a far place, you are actually going. This is a good word because it shows you are happy with what you do.
