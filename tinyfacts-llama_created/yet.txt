The word yet is a word that is used when you are waiting for something. It means you are waiting for something to happen. You are looking at the time. You are waiting for a dog to come out of the house. You are waiting for the sun to go down.

Sometimes you use this word when you do not have the thing you want. You have the thing you want, but you are still waiting. You are waiting for the day to come so you can do your work.

You can also use this word when something has not happened. You have been waiting for a long time. You have been waiting for a big moment to happen. Then something else happens. The waiting is over.
