To make sure something is done is to make sure everything is okay. This word helps us know that things are not just right, but are actually right.

Imagine you have a friend who needs to buy food for their family. You want to make sure the food is not the wrong kind. You look at the box and see if it is the food they need. If it is, you are sure everything is okay. You have made sure.

This is what we do with many things in our lives. If you are not sure if a door is closed, you use your brain to check. If you are not sure if a car is safe to drive, you check with a friend. If you are not sure if a room is quiet, you close your eyes and listen.

When you do this, you are not just waiting. You are doing something to be sure everything is right. It helps you feel calm and safe.

This word is used in many ways. You might say,  The teacher is sure that the student will do their work. The student is sure that they will do their work too. Or, You are sure that the water is not too hot.
