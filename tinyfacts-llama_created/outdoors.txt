To go outside means you leave your house. You put on your shoes and your clothes. You might walk or run. You can also go to a big green area with trees and tall things in the sky. This is a beautiful place where you can be alone. You might see animals, like a cat or a dog.

You can also go to a place where water is. This is where people go to drink water or to play. There are big bodies of water, like a big blue water that covers many places. You can see people and animals there. You can also go to a place with many tall things on top. These places are like a very high place where you can see everything.

When you go outside, you are moving away from your house. You are changing your world. You are going to a new place, and you are starting a new day.
