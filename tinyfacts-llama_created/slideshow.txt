A picture show is a way to show many pictures one after another. You can put them on a computer, a phone, or a big box with many small boxes. Each picture is a tiny piece of a story.

Imagine you have a big box with many small boxes. Each small box has one picture. You put the pictures in the right order, and then you show them one after another. People watch and enjoy the story. It can be about a person, a place, or a thing.
