To see something is to look at it with your eyes. You can look at a tree, a dog, or a book. To see means you can see it.

You can also see things that are very far away. If you are standing next to a big house, you can see it from your house. You can also see things that are very small, like a tiny thing in a box.

Sometimes, you see things that are not real. You might see a dog in your room, even though it is not really there. Or you might see a thing that is not a thing at all. This can happen with pictures or with things you see in your mind.

You can also see things that are not about you. You can see a group of people, a city, or a world. This is about everyone in the world or all the people who live on the ground.

To see something is to understand it. When you see a picture, you understand what it looks like. When you see a word, you understand what it means. Seeing helps us know about the world and about each other.
