To write something is to use your brain to make words. You can write a story, a song, a picture, or even a long story about what happened before you started writing it. When you write something, you are changing the world.

A person might write a story to help other people understand a feeling. They might write about a time when they felt sad or happy. The person is trying to show how they felt so that others can feel the same way. This is how you help people feel.

Sometimes you write words on a computer. You type into the box and the computer makes the words into a new story. You do not have to be there when the words are made. You only see what the computer has done.

People also use this word when they make a movie. They write what happens. They write the names of the people who are in the movie, the places, and the things that happen. Then they tell the story in a way that people can understand. When you watch a movie, you are following what the person wrote.

To write something is to use your brain and your hands. It is a way to tell a story or to show how you feel.
