To be able something means you can do it. It is about having the power to do a job or a thing.

Think about a book. You can read it. You do not have to wait for someone to give it to you. You can pick it up and start to read it. This is because you have the book. You are able to read it.

Something is also able if it can do things on its own. If a car is able, it can drive by its own. It does not need a person to tell it to go. The car is able.

Sometimes we say something is able to do a job. This means the person can do it. If a teacher is able to teachers, they are able to share their ideas.

In life, you are able to do many things. You can work, you can play, you can be friends. When you are able, you are happy.
