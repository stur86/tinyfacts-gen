Relationships are things that happen when two people, animals, or things are close to each other. This can be between a boy and a girl, or between two animals. It is about how the two things feel toward each other.

When you have a good relationship, you feel happy and safe. You might do things together, or talk to each other many times. You understand each other and can trust each other. This is a good feeling because it shows that you care about the other person.

You can also have a bad relationship. This happens when you and another person do not agree. They might fight or say bad words. This can cause a lot of hurt and make it hard to feel happy again.

Sometimes, a relationship can be between people. A boy might have a good relationship with a girl. They like each other and do things together. This is a happy feeling.

You can also have a relationship with an animal. If you help a dog or a cat, you are showing the animal love. You are not mean and do not hurt it. This is important because animals feel the same way.

When you have a relationship, you learn about each other. You learn what makes each person happy and what makes them sad. This helps you become a better person. It also helps you understand how to act when things are wrong.

So, relationships are about the way we feel toward each other. They are about being kind, understanding, and safe.
