To put together means to join two or more things into one. This happens when you take a small piece of wood and put it next to a bigger piece of wood. The wood from the small piece is not strong, but when it is joined to the bigger piece, it becomes one hard thing that can hold a heavy box.

You can also put many small things together. Imagine you have a lot of books and a few small pieces of paper. If you put them together, you have a book that can tell you stories. This is how you put your books into a big book so you can read them all at once.

You can also put people together. If you are very good at a game, you can help your friends. You are not one person, but many people working together. This is how you work in a group to do a job.

Sometimes, you put things together to make a new thing. If you take many small parts and put them together in a way that makes them work, you make a car or a phone. This is how you build new things that help us live our lives.

When you put something together, you make it stronger. You do not need as many pieces to hold it up. This is why you can have many friends if you all work together.
