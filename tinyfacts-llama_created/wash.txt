To clean something means to make it look and feel better. You use water and a thing that cuts off the bad stuff on your hands when you have to clean your face. The water goes down and the bad stuff comes out.

When you use this thing on your clothes, you are also cleaning them. It takes away the small bits of hair and the old colors. After you clean, your clothes look bright and new again.

You can also clean a car. A car has bad stuff on it that makes it look old. You use water and a strong thing to cut off the bad stuff. After that, the car looks like a new car.

Sometimes, to clean, you use water. You might use hot water to clean your hands after you eat. This makes your hands feel warm and your skin look clear. You also clean your room when you are done playing. You pick up the things you have played with and put them away so they do not make a new bad stuff.

To clean something is to make it look and feel better.
