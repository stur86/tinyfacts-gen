When you grow, you become bigger and stronger. You start as a tiny thing inside your body and become a big, moving person. You use the food you eat and the air you breathe to get bigger.

Imagine you are a small tree. The tree starts as a tiny green thing on the ground. Over many years, the green thing grows into a strong green thing with many big leaves. The tree gets bigger and stronger, and it becomes a tall tree that stands in a green field.

You can also grow things. If you put a small baby in a warm bed, the baby starts to grow. The baby gets bigger and stronger every day. It becomes a tiny child, then a small child, then a big child, and finally a small person.

When you grow, you become more like the person you are. Your body becomes stronger. Your hair grows. Your eyes get bigger. You can feel things in your body that you did not know before.

You can grow in many ways. You can grow taller, or you can grow hair. You can grow your brain. You can grow your heart. You can grow your mind.

Growing is a big change. It takes time. It can hurt at first. But when you grow, you become a new person. You are different from who you were before. You are growing into a new you.
