To be simply means you are not doing anything at all. You are not doing anything that is hard or important. You are just being. You are not doing something good or nice. You are not doing something that will change the world.

Think about a small rock. The rock is not doing anything. It is just sitting there. It does not move. It does not change its place. It is just there. This is how we can be simply. We do not have to do anything. We can just be.

Sometimes we want to be more than that. We want to be strong. We want to be happy. We want to do things that will change the world. But being simply is good too. It is okay to not do anything. It is okay to just be.
