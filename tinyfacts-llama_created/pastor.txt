A person who helps a group of people is a kind of leader. They help them feel safe and happy. They also help them understand what they believe.

A man who is a good friend is a kind person. They do not say bad things. They listen to what the other person needs. They help when the person is sad or angry.

In a group of people, this person is the one who says, and everyone listens. They tell the group what to do. They help make the group feel good about each other.

The word comes from a man with a long hair who went to a place where many people live. He helped them and made them feel happy. He gave them food, water, and a place to sleep. Later, his group became a group of people who follow a way of living.
