To go toward something means to move toward a point or a place. When you move toward the sun, you are moving toward the warm air that makes the day start. When you walk toward a door, you are moving toward the room inside.

Sometimes, when people talk about things coming together, they are talking about how many of them are going the same way. If a group of people and a group of animals go to the same place, they are all going toward the same place. This makes them close together.

Think of a line of many people. Each person is on the line. They are all going the same way, toward the end of the line. If you join another group of people, you are now going toward the end of the line with the new group. This is a kind of coming together.

Sometimes, we talk about a point where many things are going the same. This point is like the end of the line. When many people go toward the same point, they are all on the same side of the line. They are not all going in the same direction. They are all going toward the same place at the same time.

You can also use this word when things are getting closer. If a person is talking to a friend, they are both coming closer. The person who is speaking is moving toward the other person. They are not going away. They are getting nearer and nearer.
