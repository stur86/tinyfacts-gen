To recognize something is to know it. It is to see it as a person, a place, or an animal. You can see a face, a word, a sound, or a feeling and know it at once. You do not need to look at it again. The first time you see it, you know it is there.

We can recognize things that are real. We see a friend, a house, or a car. We do not need to look again because we know what they look like. We can also recognize things that are not real. A picture can be a person. A song can be a sound. These things are not there, but we know they are there because we have seen or heard them before.

In a big world, it is hard to recognize each person. There are many people in the same city, and many people are in the world. To know them is to remember names, faces, and stories. When we see a person, we look at their face and think, yes, that is my friend. We also remember what they did and how they feel. This is how we know each other.

We also recognize things by how they make us feel. A bright light can make us happy. A loud sound can start a game. A warm cup of coffee can make us feel good. We can see the color of a bright light, hear the sound of a loud noise, and feel the warm feeling of a cup of coffee. In this way, we recognize the things that make us feel happy.

Sometimes we recognize things that are not there. A word, a sound, or a feeling can be the same as many other words, sounds, or feels. They are not really there because they are not a thing. We see them as not there because we do not really see them. But this is not the same as not knowing them. We can still feel them and remember them. We can still say, yes, this is the word, or yes, this is the sound, or yes, this is the feeling.

In our lives, we often recognize what is right and wrong. We know that a person should act with love. We see a child who is afraid and know that the person should be kind. We also recognize what is not right. We know that a person who lies is not a person we like to be around. We can see the way they act and know that it is not good.
