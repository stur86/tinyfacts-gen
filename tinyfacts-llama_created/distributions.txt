To make something into different parts, so that everyone can use it. A way of sharing when there is more than one thing.

Think of it like food. If you have a big bag of food, you can give some to your friends. You are still in control. But if you share a book with a friend, you have to let them read it. They are not the one who made it.

This happens with many things. If you have a lot of money, you can give some to people who need it. Or if you have a computer, you can share it with your friends. Or if you have a song, you can share it with others so they can hear it.

Sometimes, when many people work together, they make things for everyone. This is how you get a new thing or a new way of doing things. The work is done by many people, and the thing becomes for everyone.

To share can be very good. It helps us feel like we are part of something bigger. It makes us feel like we are not alone. But it can also make us feel afraid if we share too much. We have to know who to share with and how much to share.
