A high place is a big area of land that is high up in the sky. It is often very far away from the ground. These places can be very cold at night. They can also be very dark during the day. There are many of these high places all over the world, and they are often found near big bodies of water. Some are very small, but others are very big. People often visit these places to see the beautiful trees and the wide open spaces. They may go by car, by bus, or by flying to get there.
