To do something once in a month means to do it only once a year. This is because a month is a group of days. There are  four of these groups in a year. One is for the first week of the year, one for the second week, one for the third week, and one for the last week.

When you do something once in a month, you are doing it only once. If you do it many times, you are doing it many times in one year. This can help you to learn more and to make sure you are not doing something too many times.

Imagine you have a box of books. You buy one book each month. After a year, you have bought many books. You have seen a lot of stories. You have learned many things. You did it all in one year.

You can also do this with money. If you save money in a box each month, you have saved money for a year. You can buy more things with this money. It is a way to save money and to plan for the next year.

In this way, doing something once in a month is like taking a small step forward. You are moving closer to your next step.
