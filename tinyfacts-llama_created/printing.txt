To make something by writing it on a piece of paper is called to be written. This is what we do with words, pictures, and stories.

You might have a book. You write something on a piece of paper. Then you put the papers together to form a book. Many people do this work. You do not see the book at first. Only when the book is on the table, people can read it.

When a person makes a picture, they write the story on a piece of paper. After that, they use a big round thing to press the paper. The paper is pressed so hard that the words are not on the paper anymore. The picture is now on the paper.

The paper can be made from many different things. Some papers are very strong. They hold a lot of words and pictures. Other papers are very soft. They are easy to hold and do not hurt the hand. People use different papers for different things.
