An a is a number that is very, very high. It is the number right after one, and after two, and after three, and so on. There is a big number one that is the beginning of all numbers. Between one and two, there is an a. Between two and three, there is also an a. This goes on for many numbers. The number after many of these a is numbers.

The word also means to beat. If you are the best, you are an a. If you beat your friends or your family, you are an a. You are also the best at a game or a hard job. You do not lose or you do not make a wrong move.

Sometimes, when you say a group of people is a group, you mean they are all a together. When a group has many a, it means they are all high, strong, or good.

We use this word to tell if someone is the best. We might say, she is an a. This means she is the best, the top, the top one. We use it when we are happy, when we want to say someone is very good.
