To stay means you are still here. You do not move to another place. You are in one spot.

Imagine you are at a school. You have to go to class. You go to the room with the desks. You sit and listen to the teacher. You learn new things. When the time is over, you go home. You are still at the school. You are not gone yet. You are staying there.

You can also stay if you are waiting for something. If you want to go to a movie, you stay at your house. You watch the movie by yourself. You do not leave. You are waiting for the time to come. When the time comes, you go. You are still at home.

You can stay with a friend. You live together. You do not go out to do anything. You just stay at the house. You are in the house with them. You are part of the group.

You can also stay in a room. You have a bed and a chair. You are in a room with a door. You can close the door so no one comes. You are safe inside.

Being stayed is also about being quiet. If you are in a quiet room, you stay there. You do not make a sound. You are not moving. You are still.
