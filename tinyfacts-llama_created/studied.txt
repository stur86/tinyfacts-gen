To studied something means you are very interested in it. When you study something, you read books or look at pictures about it. You want to know every little thing about it so you can understand it better.

You might study a book on a computer or a book about a person who lived a long time ago. You want to know how they lived and what they did. You also study about a problem that a person or a group is having. You want to find out how to fix the problem so it does not happen again.

When you study, you do not just look at the words. You think about what the words mean. You try to understand why things happen. You also try to remember things that are important.

You can study many different things. You can study a game to learn how to play it. You can study a food to learn how to eat it. You can study a city to learn about its people and its ways of living. Studying helps you to learn new things and to understand the world around you.
