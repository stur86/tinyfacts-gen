To flash means to show very quickly. When you flash a picture, it is like you are turning it around very fast and then stopping it. You do this to make people notice you.

You can flash your hands in a high way to get attention. You might do this in a school or at work. It is like you are saying, watch me! People often use this when they are playing with friends.

Sometimes you flash a small light on your phone. This is like a tiny light that moves around in the air. You can use it to show where you are or where you are going.

You can also flash when you are about to say something important. This can happen when a person is trying to talk to a person who wants to know. It is like you are saying, I am here! I am telling you the truth!

Flashing is a way to show quickly that you are there or that you are doing something. It is like a quick show of your face.
