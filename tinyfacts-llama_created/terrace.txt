A high place on the top of a wall or house is called a house top. But sometimes, people use the word to mean a long floor with many floors above it. You can walk from one floor to another many times. The ground under your feet is always changing. You can see many different kinds of houses and streets and places in one walk.

Imagine a long wall that goes around the top of a big field. On the top, there are many houses. Each house has a different room and a different kind of door. There are no windows on the top because the sun can go in and out many times. On the top, people often have a big room with a high floor. They can sit and look out at the ground far away.

This kind of place is also called a floor top. It is like a long floor with many floors above it. You can walk from one floor top to another many times. You can see many different kinds of places and houses. It is a great place to watch the world go by.
