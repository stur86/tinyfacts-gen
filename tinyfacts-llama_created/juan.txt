To cut something means to break it into many small pieces. You can also cut a piece of wood to make a table. You use a hard thing with a long part to push through the wood. This makes the wood become smaller pieces that can hold other things.

A person is a living thing. We call them a person because they are not a thing like a chair. They have a head, a body, and a face. They can think and act. They can be happy, sad, or angry.

A book is a thing that people write. It has many words inside. A person reads these words so they can understand them. They can learn new things, hear stories, or find answers to their questions. Many people keep books for their own use.

A street is a way for people to move around. You can walk along it, drive a car, or ride a horse. Some streets are very long. Other streets are very short. You might walk a few blocks to get to a place you want.

A house is a place where a person lives. It has a door, a window, and a floor. Inside, there are rooms for sleeping, a kitchen for food, and a room for playing. A person lives there with their family and friends.

To make money is to get a thing that you already have. You do this by doing a job. You might work at a store, a school, or a hospital. You do not have to be a person to make money. You can use a computer or a phone to do it.

To love someone means you care about them very much. You might feel happy when they are near you. You might also feel sad if they are gone. You might want to stay with them and be close to them.
