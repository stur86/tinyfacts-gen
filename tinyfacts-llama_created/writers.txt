A writer is a person who makes words and stories. They are the ones who put ideas into paper or a computer. They might write about a person, a place, a feeling, or a story that happened.

Some people write books that people read. They write about things that are real, like a day at school or a fight between friends. Other people write words that can be read many times. They might write about a person who is strong or a person who is sad.

Writing is also about seeing the world. A writer might look at a picture and try to find the story behind it. They might look at a person and try to know what they feel. They write what they see, what they hear, and what they think.

Sometimes a writer writes for money. They write words to be read by people who want to learn. Other times, a writer writes for fun. They write because they like to use their words. They like to build a story or to tell a story that makes them feel happy.

A writer is not the same as a person who speaks. A person who speaks is a person who talks. A writer is the one who makes words that come out of their head.
