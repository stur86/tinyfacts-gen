The word Need is a word that means you need something to be okay. To need something is to want it. If you are very tired, you need to sleep. If you are sad, you need to cry. If you are angry, you need to say something. But when you say you need something, you are not really sure if you have it.

The word Need comes from a time when people lived in small houses. In the houses, there was a small room with a bed. The people in that room needed to sleep. They would go there and say, I need to sleep. The room would close and they would rest. They did not need anything more.

Later, when people moved to cities, they had to stay in big houses with many rooms. But the word Need stayed. Now it means anything that you need to feel okay. You need money to buy food. You need a job to pay the money. You need friends to talk with. You need a bed to sleep at night.

We also use the word Need to talk about the world. We need the sun to be bright in the day so we can see. We need the water to drink. We need air to breathe. We need to eat food to stay strong. We need to be safe so we do not get hurt.

So, the word Need is about the need to feel okay and to have what you need to live a good life.
