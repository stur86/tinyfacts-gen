To lift something means to make it go up. You can lift a small thing, a book, or a heavy chair. This happens when you push it with your hands or your feet. The air around you moves up and the ground falls down.

Sometimes, you lift people. You stand behind them and use your arms to help them up. This makes them feel safe and stronger. You are helping them get into a car or a big house.

You can also lift a heavy bag. You stand under it and use your legs to push it up. When you do this, your legs get strong. This helps you run faster and move quickly.

If you lift something heavy, you might break it. You might pull something heavy across the floor or up a high wall. This can be very hard work. But when you lift something that is not heavy, you feel happy because you are helping it go up.
