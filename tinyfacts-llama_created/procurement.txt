To buy or get something is to get it. If you need a new book for school, you go to a store and pick one. You pay money for it. This is buying.

Sometimes, we buy things we do not need. This is when we buy food or clothes. We take money from our pockets. This is how people get what they want.

We also buy things for work. If you are a doctor, you buy books and things you need to work well. You pay money for them so you can learn more and do better.

Buying is about getting things you want or need. It is a way to pay for what you need to live.
