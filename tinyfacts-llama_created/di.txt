To dies means you die. When you die, your body stops moving and your brain stops working. You don't breathe anymore and you can't come back. After you die, your body goes to a place where there are no more living people. This place is often far away from your home.

Sometimes you die because of a bad heart attack. This happens when the blood goes out of your heart very fast. You feel like you are going to die before your body even starts to close your eyes.

Other times you die because of a very bad cut or a very big hurt. If you have a very deep cut, the blood might not stop coming. If you have a hurt in your chest or your head, you might die very quickly. This is because the blood is going to your brain and heart very fast.

You can also die because of a very bad time. If you are very sad or very angry, you might feel like you are dying inside. This is because your body and mind are very worried.

When someone dies, we say they are gone. We do not say they are still living. They are really gone.
