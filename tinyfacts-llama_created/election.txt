An eventually important word.

This word is about what people do when they want to decide who will live in a city or a town. Most of the time, the people who are already there pick the person they want to live in. But sometimes, the people want to change. They want someone new to live there.

To decide, people go to many places. Some of these places are only for people with money. Some are only for people who can not work. Some are open to everyone. People go to these places and talk about the person they want. They ask questions. They listen to the person and see if they are good for the job.

After many people talk, the city or town holds a big meeting. In this meeting, each person in the city gets one or more pieces of paper. Each piece of paper is for a different job. The person who got the most papers is the one who will live in the city or town for the next many years.

The big meeting is like a fight for the job. The person who has the most papers is the one who gets the job. They might say, I am the best one for this job. This is because the person who got the most papers is the one who had the most people like them say they want them to live in the city.

People also say that in this meeting, each person gets one chance to say, I want this job. If many people want the same job, the person who gets the most papers gets the job. This is why it is so important to get the right papers.

Sometimes, the big meeting is not about just one person. It is about many people who want the job. In this case, the person who gets the most papers is still the one who gets the job. The big meeting is about many people, not just one person.

This word is important because it shows how people work together to decide who will live in a city or town. It is about listening to others and following what they say.
