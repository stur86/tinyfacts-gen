To be a good person is to act like a person who is liked by everyone. This is called being a good person. When you are a good person, you do not hurt others. You help those who need help. You are kind to those who are not kind. You are a good friend.

Being a good person is also about being a good family person. You care about your family. You tell them how you feel. You listen to them when they talk. You do not yell. You do not use your hands to hit people. You act in a way that shows you love them.

When you live in a place, you become a part of the people who live there. You learn about their ways. You learn about their food. You learn about their music. You learn about their stories. You become a friend to everyone.

But being a good person is not just about how you act. It is also about the things you do. You work hard. You pay your money. You give some of what you have to the people who need it. You do not take things that do not give you a reason to be happy.

Being a good person is like being a light in the dark. You give hope to those who are afraid. You make the world a better place. You show that there is still a good side to the world.
