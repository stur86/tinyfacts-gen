To move quickly is to go through a lot of ground very fast. You might ride a horse, drive a car, or walk very fast. You might feel like you are moving, even if you are standing still. This happens when your body is very strong and your legs are very fast.

Sometimes, when you talk, you move your words quickly. You might speak so fast that you are almost shouting. This happens when you are very happy or excited. It is like your words are coming out in a rush, like a lot of water going over a rock.
