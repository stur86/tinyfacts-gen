The word entire comes from a time when people used big round things to carry heavy things. When you put one of these round things around something, it was the entire thing. You put the round thing around everything. The round thing covered everything. It was the whole thing.

Imagine you have a big box. You put the round thing around the box and it is the entire box. If you put the round thing around a small piece of paper, it is not the entire paper. It is just a part. The paper is still the paper.

You can also say something is entire if it is complete. If you have a book with many stories, and you read all the stories, you have read everything. You have finished the book. It is the entire book.

Sometimes, you might say something is the entire story. This means the whole thing happened. It is the end of the story, the whole time.

You can also say something is the entire answer to a question. If you look at a book and you find a word that makes sense, that word is the answer. It is the whole thing. It answers the question.

In life, being entire can mean you are not afraid of anything. You have nothing to worry about. You are safe. You are the whole person.

It can also mean you are the best. When you are the entire person, you are the best. You are the one everyone looks up to.

So, being entire is when you have nothing left to worry about. You are the whole thing. You are the best.
