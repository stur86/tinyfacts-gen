The word for your hand is a very good word to know. It is the part of your body next to your arm. This part is very strong. You use it to hold things. You use it to shake a drink. You use it to feel the ground. You use it to pick things. 

Your hand is not a finger. It is not the part you use to point. It is the part that holds your body together. It is the part that lets you do all the things you want to do. 

You can move your hand. You can turn it. You can pull it. You can give it a little shake. You can even make a funny sound with it. 

When you are very young, your hand is very small. As you grow older, your hand gets bigger. Your fingers grow out. You learn to use your fingers to do many different jobs.
