To be in a high place means to be in a position where you can see many people and many things. If you are high up, you can see the ground far away. You can see the wind and the sky.

A thing that is in many parts is called a thing of many parts. This is like a tree. The tree has many hard parts on the sides. On the top of the tree, there are many green parts. On the bottom of the tree, there are many hard parts that hold the tree up.

A group of many people is a group. When many people work together, they can do more things. They can build a house, fix cars, or help people who are sick. This is why a school is a group of many teachers, students, and parents.

A long story with many people and many places is a story. This is why the story of a man who went to the city and the city that went to the land is a story. It is a story about many people and many places.

A thing that is very hard to understand is a thing of many parts. This is why it can be very big. Imagine you are looking at a picture. The picture has many many parts. Each part tells you a little bit of the story. If you look at each part, you can learn more about the story.
