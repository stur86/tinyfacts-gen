To give something is to help others. When you do this, you are also helping yourself. You share your time, your money, or your things with others.

Imagine you have a book. You love stories and want to share them with others. You tell your friends about the story. You show them the pictures. This is helping others. You are giving them the story.

If you are a doctor, you help people feel better when they are sick. You give them the right things to drink or the right power to make their bodies strong again. You are also helping the people in the room. They are safe because you are there to help them.

You can also give money to help people who are in trouble. You pay their food or their money so they do not have to go on the streets. This is helping them.

Sometimes, you can give something that you do not need anymore. You might give a book you read or a thing you made. This is a way to share your life with others.

To be a good friend is to help your friends. You listen to them when they talk. You make them feel good. You are there for them. This is helping your friends feel safe and happy.

When you work, you often give the money to the people who own the place. You help them pay for the things they need to do their job. This is helping your city or town.
