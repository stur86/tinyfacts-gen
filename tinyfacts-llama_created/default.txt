When you do something, you usually follow a set of ways. But if you do not follow these ways, you are the one who does not follow. This is called the wrong way.

Think of money. If you do not pay money you already have, you are the one who does not follow. You are the one who is wrong. Everyone follows the same ways. You follow the money ways, the school ways, the car ways, or any other set of ways. When you break these ways, you are the one who does not follow.

When a person does not pay money, they have to face the right way. They might go to a place where they pay money. They might be told to pay money now or later. They might have to pay many times.

When a group of people does not follow a set of ways, the group is the one who does not follow. This is why the group might become hard to work with. If everyone follows the same ways, the group can work well. If everyone does not follow, the group can become a problem.

Sometimes, you might do something wrong because you are the new person. When you are the first person, everyone follows you. You are the one who does not follow. If you do not follow, you are the one who is wrong. But as the person you follow goes, they learn. They learn to follow. They become the ones who follow. This is how everyone learns.

A computer can do this too. When a computer does not follow a set of ways, it is the one who does not follow. It might break a way and try to do the wrong way. If the computer follows the wrong way, it can hurt people. If the computer follows the right way, it can do what it is supposed to do.
