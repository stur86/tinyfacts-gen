To be turned on means you are ready to work. If you turn a key in a box, it starts to move. If you turn a light, it makes the room bright.

Animals and things can also be turned on. A light turns on after you put in the right kind of power. A car needs to turn on before you start to drive. A phone turns on when you pick it up.

You can also turn something off when you are done with it. If you play a game, you might turn it off after you finish. You can turn off a computer so you do not have to keep it on all the time.

Sometimes, we turn something on because we are excited. We might be very happy to see a movie, a game, or a new show. When we are turned on, we feel ready to do things.
