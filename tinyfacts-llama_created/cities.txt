A city is a place where many houses, shops, and places to eat live together. It is a big area with many streets, roads, and places where people go. Some cities are very big and have many many houses. Other cities are smaller and have only a few houses.

People live in these cities for many years. They go to work, go to school, and buy food. They also go to places to see things that are fun. Some cities have many different kinds of people. There are people who live there because they were brought there as babies, and there are people who moved there for work.

Cities have many jobs. There are jobs for people who clean, jobs for people who work in stores, jobs for people who work with computers, and jobs for people who drive cars. In a city, there is always something going on. People go to places to watch a game, go to a movie, or go to a big hall to eat and talk.

Cities are also important for learning. There are many schools and places to learn how to read and write. Some cities have big schools with many students. Other cities have small schools with only a few students.

Sometimes people move to a city to start a new life. They might move because they need a job, or because they want to be near their family. They might also move because they want a better life. Cities can feel very big, but they are also very small and close together. This makes them very interesting to learn about.
