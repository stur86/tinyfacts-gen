To make something is to build it so people want to buy it. When you play a game, you build it so other people want to play with it. When you tell a story, you make it so people want to hear it. This is called making a thing.

When you make something, you need money and time. You use this money and time to build the thing. You also tell people about the thing. You show them it is real and that they need it. You tell them how they can get it.

Some things are made to help people do things. A car is made so people can go fast to work. A book is made so people can learn new things. Some things are made to make money. A movie is made so people want to watch it.

People also use this word to talk about people who make money. When a business makes a lot of money, people say it is doing well. When a person makes a lot of money, people say they are doing well too.
