To play a game with a computer is to use the computer to do a job for you. When you play a game like this, you use your computer to move a small thing around a big place. You might have to fight a bad person or find a way to get to a far away house.

Most of the time, you are a person. You are not a person, but a computer plays you. You use your words and your body to tell the computer what to do. You might say you want to run fast or you want to jump up high. The computer follows your words and moves the computer thing around the place.

Sometimes, you play a game with your friends. You all sit in front of a computer and do the same thing. You might play games where you are a person in a world. You might play games where you are a person and you have to fight people. You might play games where you are a person and you have to find a way to get to a house.
