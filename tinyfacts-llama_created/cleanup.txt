To clean something means to make it look nice again. You use things that cut off the bad bits and make the floor or the table look bright and clean.

Imagine you have a room full of old food and tiny bits of paper. If you just walk in, the floor will be covered in these bits. But if you use a big brush and some water, you can pick up the old food and make the floor look clean. This is like cleaning the room. You are making it look better for people to use.

You can also clean things that are broken. If a chair has a cut on it, you might use a soft thing to pull out the blood. Then you use a wet thing to clean the cut. This makes the chair look nice again so it can be used.

Sometimes, you clean things that are not broken. You might pick up your clothes and put them in a bag. You might wipe down the table to make it look nice for dinner. These are just things you do so things look nice.
