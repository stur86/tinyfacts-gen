A person who helps a city or a town is a kind of top leader. They make sure the city runs well and that all its people are safe.

When a city has a problem, the top leader might not know what to do. This is when a person with a strong head comes in. They listen to the people, talk to the police and the people who work for the city, and they decide what to do. They also tell the police what to do and who to trust.

The person in this position is not in school like a student. They have to know about the city and its problems and how to fix them. They often have to work with many other people, like the police and the teachers, to keep the city safe.

Sometimes, a person who is not a doctor but still helps people with a big problem can be called a top leader. They might be the one who helps a city after a big fire or after a big fight. They stay for a long time to help the people feel better.

The person who is in this job is very important. They help keep the city safe and make sure everything runs well. They are like the head of a big team of people who work together to keep everyone happy.
