A thing that grows from a tree is called a green thing. It grows from the ground of the tree and goes up to the top. On the top of the green thing, there are many small pieces that are long. These green pieces are very important. They hold the water from the ground. They hold the air. They keep the green thing warm.

When the day is cold, the green pieces close. In the cold night, the green pieces open again. This keeps the green thing warm. The green pieces are very good at keeping warm.

The green thing makes the air clean. It takes bad air and makes good air. The green thing also makes the air sweet. If you breathe in too many bad things, you might feel sick. But if you breathe in the sweet air, you feel better.

The green thing also helps keep the ground clean. The green thing takes bad things from the ground. It keeps the ground from getting very wet. The ground stays hard and strong. This keeps the ground safe for the tiny animals that live there.

When the green thing is gone, the tree looks barely. But in the fall, the green things fall down. The ground is full of red, brown, and green pieces. The wind picks them up. They go down the street and into the houses. The children like to play with them. They make a game where they try to catch all the green pieces before they go away.

In the cold months, the green thing stays on the tree. It is very strong. It keeps the tree warm. The tree stays green even when the days are very short. This helps the tree live another year.
