To get a point is to be the one with the ball. You hit a ball with your hand. If you hit it far away, the other team gets a point. You do this many times, and the team with the most points at the end is the one that gets the ball.

Sometimes, a team has many points. If they get more than the other team, they say they have a higher number. If they get the same number, they say they are tied.

A game is a long time with many points. You might hit the ball many times. After a long time, the team with the most points is the one that was first. The person with the most points is usually the one who hits the most balls.

A person can get a point in many ways. You can hit a ball, run with the ball, or throw the ball to a friend on your team. When you do one of these things, you get a point. If you are the one with the ball and you run without the ball, you get a point too. You can get one point for each time you run without the ball.

The team with the most points at the end of the game gets a small piece of paper. This paper is the most important thing. The team that gets the most points is the one that gets the paper. The person who gets the paper is often the one who was the best player.
