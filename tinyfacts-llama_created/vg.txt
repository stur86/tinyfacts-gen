The word you are looking for is a bit different from the one we use in our day.

When people say a word is big, they mean it is very large. A house is big. A car is big. A tree can be big.

But when they say a word is small, they mean it is very little. A small dog is much smaller than a big dog. A small book is not as big as a book that is very heavy.

Even when we talk about a small thing, we are not talking about something that is really tiny. We are talking about something that is not very heavy. If you have a tiny ball, it is small because it is small. But if you have a tiny tiny thing, it is not small. You need to look really close to see it.

Sometimes, we say something is small because it is easy to carry. A small bag is easy to hold. But if something is small, it does not mean it is easy to carry. It just means it is small.
