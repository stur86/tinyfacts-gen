To join is to join something or someone with another. When you join, you become a part of a group or a group of people. You are not alone anymore. You are part of a team that does things together.

Imagine you love a game. You play it every day. One day, you get a new game. You want to play it with your friends. You go to a place where you can play games. You are the first one to enter the room. You are the first one to start playing. You are the one who starts the fun.

When you join, you do not just do one thing. You do many things together. You learn from the people around you. You learn how to be good at the game. You learn how to work with others. You learn how to be strong and how to be kind.

You can also join with a place or a city. A city is many towns joined together. They work together to keep the city safe and clean. The towns share their money and their ideas. This makes the city a strong place to live.

In a big group, you may join to a team. You are a part of a team that fights for a cause. You work together with other people who share the same ideas. You are all on the same side. You are all fighting for the same thing.

To join is to become part of something. It is to join with others to make something new and strong.
