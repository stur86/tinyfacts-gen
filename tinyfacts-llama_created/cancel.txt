When you say you want to do something, but something else happens instead, it is called to be the same. You can also say you want to stop doing something because it is not right.

Imagine you really want to play with a ball. You start to run to the park. Then, you see a dog running around. You feel very angry. You want to go back home. You tell the dog to stop. This is like you want to be the same. You want the same feeling.

You can also say you want to stop doing something that hurts you. If you are afraid, you might want to stop going to a place where things are loud. You want to be safe.

Sometimes you need to stop a thing before it happens. If you see a car coming, you might want to step to the side. This helps keep you safe.

You can also say you want to stop something that is not right. If someone is being mean, you might want to stop talking to them. This helps you stay safe.

To be the same means you do not change. You stay the same person.
