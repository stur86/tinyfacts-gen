A big group of hard things that grow in the water is called a rock. These hard things are from the old world. They have been here for a very long time. When the water gets very high, the rock comes up out of the water. The top part of the rock is hard. The bottom part is soft and you can not walk on it. This is why the rock stays in one place.

The rock with hard top is like a wall. The wall keeps the water in. The rock with soft bottom is like a soft floor. The floor is very good for the small animals that live there. They can hide under the rock and feel safe.

Some of these hard rock things are very small. They are like tiny pieces of hard stuff. Some are very big and cover many hundred feet across. They are like the big top of the world.

There is also a group of hard things in the water that grow in a line. These are the things you see when you look out the window of a car. The line goes under the water and comes out again. When the water goes up, the line goes up with it. When the water goes down, the line goes down with it.

The hard things are very important. They help keep the water clean and keep the air clean. They keep the water from getting too hot or too cold. They help keep the water from getting all grey and breaking down. Without these things, we would not have clean water to drink.
