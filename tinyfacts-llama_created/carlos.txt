A person who works at a place that makes things is called a worker. One of these people might drive a big car for many hours, going from one place to another. They might drive to work, to visit a friend, or to go to a store to buy food. They use the car to get around the town. They have to be very carefully, because they can hit other people or things that are on the road. They also have to follow the same ways as the other cars on the road, so everyone can stay safe.

The car has a seat for one person, and sometimes there is a seat for a child or a dog. Some cars are very big, and they can carry many people or many things at once. Other cars are small and can only carry one person. They can go very fast and make a loud sound when they move.
