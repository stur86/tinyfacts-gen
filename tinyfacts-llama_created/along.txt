The word along is used when you are moving away from something. You are not going in one direction, but you are moving in a different direction. It is like moving from one place to another, but not in the same line as the other person.

Imagine you are walking through a park. If you keep moving straight, you will go to a far place. If you go in a different line, you might end up in a different part of the park. You are moving away from one place, but you are not going in the same way as the other person.

Sometimes, you might walk with a friend. You are moving in different directions, but you are together. You are not moving away from each other, but you are moving in different lines. This is okay because you are still friends.

You can also use this word with numbers. If you are moving a long way across the road, you might say you are walking along the road. This means you are moving in a line with the road.

In the past, people used this word to talk about moving from a house to a school. They would say they were going along the road to school. This was because they were not going straight across the street, but they were moving in a line with the road.
