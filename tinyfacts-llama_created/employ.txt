To use something means to use a thing that is for you. This can be a computer or a car or even a word.

When you work, you use your brain and your body. You think about a job and you decide what you will do. You then go to a place where you can learn how to do the job. You might start as a tiny person who does very little work. Over time, you learn how to do the job very well.

You might work for many people. One person might be in a top job and another person might be in a job that is very important. You might do the same thing for many years.

You can also use this word to talk about how people act. If someone is using you, they are using you against you. If you are being used against you, you feel like someone is doing things that are not right.

To use someone means to make them a part of your group. If you decide to use a person, you might take them on as a friend. You tell them that they are now a part of your group.

To use a word means to use it in a way that is not its own. You might use a word to talk about something. You might say, I am feeling sad because I am using this word. This word helps us talk about how we feel.
