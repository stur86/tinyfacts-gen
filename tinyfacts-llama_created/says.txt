To say something is to tell it. When you say something, you are making a word into a sound. You use your lips, your tongue, and your teeth to make the words come out. You use your voice. Your voice is like a long, soft wave that goes through your body and out to other people.

When you say a word, you are also showing that you think it. Your body is doing things that show you are happy, sad, angry, or surprised. Your eyes might be wide. Your face might be soft. Your voice might go up or down. The words you say help tell the story.

You can also say something when you do not have to. You might say, I am tired. This means you are not very strong. You might also say, I like the food. This means you like the food. Sometimes you say things when you do not want to tell the truth. This can be confusing.

To say something is important. When you say something, you are telling everyone who hears you about it. If you tell a lie, you are not saying the truth. You are lying. If you tell the truth, you are being true.
