The word with is a very important word that helps us talk about things.

When we say something is with another thing, we mean it is next to them. If you are sitting in a room with a friend, you are next to them. You are in the same space.

Sometimes, we use this word to say a person is with a group of friends. If you are with your friends at a movie, you are with them. You are part of the group.

We also use it when something is with a person. If you have a book, you have it. If you have a dog, you have the dog. You are in a group of things that are the same.
