To be a trust is to act like one. You help a person or a group make sure money or things are done right. They give you a job, and you do it. You help them make sure the money stays in the right place.

Think of it like this. If someone buys a house, they want to keep it safe. They might not know how to fix it. So, they ask a friend or a family person to watch it for them. The person watches the house and makes sure it stays in good hands.

A person can be a trust. They can be a parents trust. They help their children do good. They also can be a business person. They might act as the manager of a big group of people. They decide who does what job.

Sometimes, a trust is a group of people who act together. They decide how to do things. They work to make sure everyone is happy. This helps keep the money and the things safe.

It is also about the money. A person might put money into a trust. They help it grow. After a long time, the money can be given to someone else. The person who helped the money stay in the right place is said to be the one who acted as the trust.
