To have a number is to know how big it is. It shows you what is going on around you. If you know what number a thing is, you can think about it and see if it is bigger or smaller than other numbers.

When you say a person is in a room, you are saying they are within the room. You are not saying they are outside the room. They have a very small number of space around them. If you go to a city far away, the number of people and houses is much bigger than the number you know.

Sometimes, a number can mean you can not go any further. If you reach the top of a high building, you can not go any higher. You have gone as far as you can. Or, if you try to put a box into a very small box, you will not be able to do it. You have hit the end.

A number can also mean how much you can do. If you know how much money you have, you can buy many things. If you know how many hours you have to work, you can do your job. Knowing how many you can do is the number of your power.

We also need numbers to plan things. If you know how many hours you have to spend, you can plan your day. If you know how many steps you need to walk, you can walk a long way. Numbers help us stay safe and make sure we do not go over a line.
