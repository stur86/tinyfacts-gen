A tree is a big living thing that grows in the ground. It has a strong body that is made of wood. The wood is very hard and keeps the tree going for many years.

A tree can be very tall. Some trees are taller than a house and stand in the sky. Others are much smaller and grow near a road. They can grow very wide and cover many pieces of land. Some trees have big wide leaves that help keep the air clean.

Trees need to drink water from the ground and air from the sky. They take in the water and air and then make their own food from it. This food gives them the power to grow and stay strong. They also make their own air, which is good for animals and people.

Trees are important for many reasons. They give us food to eat. They make clothes, paper, and wood for houses. They help keep the air clean by taking out bad things that make us sick. They help keep the ground calm and soft. They give us places to sit and play. They are a big part of our world and help keep it green and bright.
