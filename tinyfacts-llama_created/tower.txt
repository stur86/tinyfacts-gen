A tall thing that stands by the road or in a city. It is built high in the air and has many floors. People live in the top floors and go up and down many times in a big round thing that turns.

Some tall things are very high. You can see the whole city from the top. They are like a great building that goes over the town. Other tall things are smaller. They are still tall and strong, but not as high as the very tall ones.

You can climb to the top of the tall thing. On the top you can see many more tall things and the whole world from far away. You can also see the bottom of the world or the big water that is under the land.

Some tall things have windows so you can see what is inside. Others have doors that you can open to go inside. You can go up to the top and then down to the floor or room inside the tall thing. It is a place where people go to live, work, or play.

The word comes from the old days when tall people were seen as strong and important. Now we use the word to talk about tall things that are very high or strong.
