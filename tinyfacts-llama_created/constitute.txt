Together means that many people are doing the same thing. When you put many people together, it is a group. It is like a big family.

You can also say that something is a group of people. This group is made of different kinds of things. If you have many friends who love the same things, you can say they are a group. You are all together because you like the same things.

Think about your school. You are a group of kids. You all go to the same place to learn and do things. You all have the same teacher and the same room. You all do the same work during the day. This is a group of people.

You can also say that a word is a group of letters. When you write a word, it is a group of letters. You put the letters together to make the word. You put the word together with other words. When you put many groups of letters together, you get a long word.
