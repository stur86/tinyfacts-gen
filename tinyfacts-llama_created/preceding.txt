To follow before something happens is to come before. When you start to do something, you have already done the first part. You are the one who starts. After you start, other people might start too. They follow you and do what you did.

Think about a story. If you go to the movies, your friends might go before you. They might go to the same movie. They are following you. You are the one who starts the fun.

You can also talk about days and weeks. If today is a day before the next day, you are the day before. The day before is not the same day, and you are not the same day. You are a different day.

To follow is like being part of something. You are part of a group or a team. You help them do something. You are not the one who did it. You are just one part of the group. If you follow someone, you do what they say and you follow their steps.

When you follow, you might feel happy. You might feel safe. You can learn from others and get help. You can also follow a game. You follow the ball. You follow the other team. You watch and you try to do what they do.
