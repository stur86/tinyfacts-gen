To be lighter means to have less heavy stuff inside you or around you. Imagine you have a big bag of things. If you fill the bag with more things, it gets heavy. But if you take some of the things out, it becomes lighter. You can walk faster or run around because you do not feel as heavy.

People also say you are lighter when you are happy or when you do something you love. When you laugh or feel good, your body feels lighter. This is because your heart is beating fast and your face is smiling, making you feel more care free.

In a room, a light thing is one that does not need to be turned on. It just sits there and lets the light from other things fall down on it. This is why you can walk through a room at night without a light. The light thing is helping you see what you are doing.

Sometimes, a heavy thing is used to make something stronger. When you put a heavy thing next to a light thing, the heavy thing makes the light thing feel heavier. This is why you may feel more tired or have more pain if you have a heavy thing on your back for a long time. It is like the heavy thing is trying to keep you down.
