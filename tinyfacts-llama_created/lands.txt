To land something means to put it on the ground. If you drive a car, you are landing it on the road. You are moving from one place to another.

Imagine you are in the sky. You are moving very fast. Then, you start to come down. You start to touch the ground. This is landing. You are putting your car on the ground.

If you are on a tall building, you can not land here. The ground is too high. But if you are on a small piece of water, you can land on it. You can drop down to the water and get out.

Sometimes, you have to land a thing. If you fly a big thing, you have to land it on a hard ground. If you walk to a place you have never been, you might have to land on the ground. You might have to stop and breathe before you can keep moving.

The word can also mean where something is. If you land on a new place, you are in a new home. If you land on a piece of land, you are in your own home.
