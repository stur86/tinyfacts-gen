Normal means things are in the right way. When something is normal, it is not too big, too small, or too heavy. You do not feel afraid or worried. You can do what you want.

Think of the air you breathe. The air is not too hot or too cold. It is just right for you to breathe. If the air was too hot, you would feel very hot. If it was too cold, you would feel very cold.

In your body, your heart is normal. It beats all the time. You do not feel like you are going to fall down. Your skin looks good. You do not feel like you are sick.

When you are normal, you feel happy and you are able to do what you want.
