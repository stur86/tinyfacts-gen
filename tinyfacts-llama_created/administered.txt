To give someone a thing means you are in a position to give it to them. You decide what to do with it and who to give it to. This is different from saying you are the one who has the thing. You are not the one who has it, but you are the one who is telling the story.

Think about it like a box. You might have a box with money inside. You take the money out and put it into the hand of another person. That person now has the money. You did not have the money anymore, but you gave it to them. You are the one who gave it to them.

Sometimes we give people a job. We say the person is the one who will do the work. They are the one who is supposed to do it, but they are not the one with the job. The person is the one who is telling you what to do.
