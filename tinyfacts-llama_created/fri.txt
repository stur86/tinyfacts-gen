The word friends means people you like. It is a word you use when you have fun together. You might hang out, play games, or watch a movie. You feel happy when you are with them.

When you are friends, you do not always say what you think. You keep quiet and listen to each other. You talk about small things. You laugh and smile. You feel safe with them.

Sometimes, people become friends when they meet by chance. Maybe you see each other at a party or school. Or you might meet a person who is very good at something. You are drawn to them because you like who they are.

Being friends is important. It helps you feel happy and less alone. It makes life easier. When you have friends, you can tell them how you feel. You can ask them to help you if you need it. This makes you feel safe and loved.

Being a friend is not just about being kind. It is about being there for each other. You can be there even when you do not want to be. You can listen when someone is sad or angry. You can help them feel better.

You can also be a friend to animals. If you have a dog, cat, or other animal friend, you are a good friend to them. You help them feel safe and happy.
