To buy something on the big boxes is called a big buy. When you buy a whole box of things, you are buying for the whole family. This can be a lot of money. Sometimes people buy big boxes to give to their friends. If you buy a big box of food for a school, you are helping the kids. 

People also buy things on the big boxes to move to another city. If you buy a car, you pay money for many years. You pay this money every month until you own the car. 

You can also buy things on the big boxes for the money you do not have. If you want to buy a book but you only have a few hundred pieces of money, you can buy one book at a time. This helps you buy more books later. 

Sometimes, you buy things on the big boxes for your family. If you buy a new set of clothes for your sister, she is happy. You show her that you care about her.
