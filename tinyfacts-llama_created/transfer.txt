To put something from one place to another is to move it. This happens when you carry a thing from one home to another. Imagine you have a bag full of books. You carry it to the school. The book is the thing you put from one place to another.

You can put the books on a table or under your arm. You do this because the books are heavy. They are not small, so you do not carry them all the time. If you carried them all the time, they would be very heavy and you would be very tired.

Sometimes, you put people from one place to another too. If you work in a big city, you go to a city that is far away. You move to this new place to find a job. You might move with your family. You carry your bags and your things with you.

You can also put money from one place to another. You use a card to pay for things. You put the money on the card and then you pay for what you want. This is a way to move money from one place to another without using your hands.

To move something, you need to move the thing across a line. You might use a car, a bus, or a train. You need to drive it to the right place at the right time. You might also use a big box with many small boxes inside. You carry the boxes to the new place. When everything is in its right place, you can stop.

Sometimes, we move things that are not heavy. We move a small box that has a picture on it. We carry it to a room in our home. We put it down on a table. Then we look at the picture. The picture is the thing we put from one place to another.
