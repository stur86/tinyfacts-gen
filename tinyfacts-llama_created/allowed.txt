The word allowed is used when you want to let something happen.

Imagine you want to eat a food that you are not sure about. You are afraid to try it because it looks strange. But your parents tell you that it is okay to eat. They say, yes, you can eat it. This means you are allowed to try it.

We also use allowed to talk about people who are not happy with something. If a person is not happy with a game they are playing, they say, the game is not allowed. They are not allowed to play anymore. This means the game is not for them anymore.

Sometimes, we use allowed to say that a thing is allowed to happen. When a doctor lets a person walk after they are hurt, the word allowed means the person is allowed to move around.

We can also use allowed to talk about things that are not allowed. If a person does something that is not allowed, they have done something wrong. If you are allowed to play with a ball, but you do not want to, you are doing something wrong.

In short, the word allowed means you are letting something happen.
