To make a deal means to find a way that works for everyone. When two people are angry, they often want the same thing. They might not like each other. But they want to leave the fight and start talking.

Sometimes, a person might say, I want something. But another person says, No. They do not want it. This is not good. To make a deal, you look for a middle ground. You try to find a way that both people are happy.

There are two kinds of deals. One is when you give up something you want. You give up a game or a time you love to watch. You give it up so that the other person gets what you want. This can be hard.

Another kind is when you give up some power. You might say, I give you the car. You take the car and I keep the house. This way, both people are happy.

Sometimes, you can have a deal without giving up anything. You find a way that both people get what they want. This can be easy. You just talk and listen to each other.

Making a deal is like sharing. When you share, you give up your own thing. But you still get to keep some of it. It is a way to find a way that works for everyone.
