To put two things together is to join them so they are one. This day is a part of the week.

Every day has the same name. The day after today is tomorrow. Before today is the day before tomorrow. In between these are the days that are in the middle.

There is one day in the middle. It is not the first day and it is not the last day. It is the day in between. This day is called a middle day.

Many weeks have  seven days. You use this to keep track of how many days have passed. Each week is a group of seven days. In one week, you have a day for every day of the week. There are six days in a week.

You can also put days together to make a week. Here is how it works:

Day one, day two, day three, day four, day five, day six, day seven

To make a week, you put the days of the week together. This helps you keep the same days every week.
