When you are coming, you are moving toward something. You are getting closer to a place or a person. This happens when you are walking, running, or driving. If you are walking, you are moving left or right. If you are moving toward a place, you are moving toward that place. If you are moving toward something you want to see, you are coming to visit it.

You can also come into a room. If a light is turned on, people are coming inside the house. Or if a car is moving down the street, people are coming to get on it. When you are coming, you are going toward something, but you are not there yet.
