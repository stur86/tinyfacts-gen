Sweetest are things you wear on your feet. You put them on after you get out of the hot water. They help keep your feet warm and dry. You can wear them in the morning and then again at night. They are usually made of long lines of white stuff. You can find them in many colors like red, blue, and green. Some have a small picture on them. You can also get them with a picture of a dog or a cat. People wear them every day, even when they are in school or at work.
