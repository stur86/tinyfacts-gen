An big moment happens when something very strong breaks. This happens when air and hard stuff inside something get very hot and move quickly.

Imagine you have a bottle of hot water. If you shake it, the hot water gets moving very fast. After a little while, the water comes out of the top of the bottle in a big, white line. This is like the end of a big moment.

The hot air and the hard stuff inside the bottle go out very fast. This makes a loud noise, like a very loud sound that goes through your head. This noise is what we call an air burst.

The hot air and the hard stuff can go up very high into the sky. This is like a big, high ball that goes up and comes back down. When this happens, it can make the sky look very bright and make the stars look like they are jumping out.

Sometimes, the air burst can happen inside a house or a car. This happens when the hot air and the hard stuff get very hot and build up inside. When the air gets too strong, it breaks out of the top of the thing. This can cause a lot of noise and make the walls and the floor shake.

In a big building, the air burst can be very loud. This is why people often move away from the windows if there is a big air burst. The loud noise can also cause people to feel a little scared, because it is so strong.
