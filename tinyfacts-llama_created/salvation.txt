To save something means to keep it safe from getting hurt. When you are happy, you feel safe. You do not feel afraid because you know that you are well.

To save money is to keep it for later. You might save money for a new game, for a big food, or for a long trip. You put the money away in a box so it does not go away.

To save your life means to stay safe in a hard time. If you are in a place that is very hot or very cold, you might stay there because you are afraid. You might stay in a small room with a window so you can breathe easily. This helps you live for a long time.

Sometimes, you save your body. You do not want to get hurt. You keep your body clean and strong so you can stay living for a long time. You do this because you are afraid of pain.

There is also a way to be saved by God. If you follow his words, he can save you from the bad things that hurt you. He can give you love and make you feel better. This is a very important thing because it can help you feel safe and happy for the rest of your life.
