To pick something means to decide which one is the best or which one you like.

You might pick a book to read. You might pick a game to play. You pick what you want to do for the time you are doing it.

You can pick from many things. If you are at a store, you can pick a book, a game, or a thing to eat. You can pick the one that looks nice or the one that feels nice in your hand.

You do not just pick one thing. Sometimes you pick many things. You might pick two friends, one to talk to and one to laugh with. You might pick many books to read in one night.

When you pick, you are taking the one that is the right one. It is the one you like the most. You are making a hard but important job easy.
