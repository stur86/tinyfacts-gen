To something means you take a drink. It can be a drink from a bottle, a drink from a cup, or even a drink that falls from the sky. When you drink from a bottle, you put the drink into your body. When you drink from a cup, you use the cup to hold the drink. When the drink falls from the sky, we call it a rain drink.

Sometimes, you can also say that a thing is sounding. If you are very quiet, your voice is saying. If you hear a sound, it is saying. A sound is when things make noise. If a dog bags, it is saying, or if a dog goes to the door, it is also saying.
