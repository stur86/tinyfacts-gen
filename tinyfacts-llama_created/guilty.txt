When someone is said to be sorry, they usually mean they feel bad for what they did. If you did something that hurt someone or something, and you can't say you were happy about it, you are sorry.

In this way, being sorry is not the same as being afraid. If you are afraid, you might feel a bad feeling. If you are sorry, you feel a bad feeling because you know what you did was wrong.

To be sorry is also to say you did something wrong. You say this so that the other person does not get angry. If someone is sorry, they want to make things right. They try to fix what they did wrong so they do not have to be sorry again later.

Being sorry can be easy to do. You just say you are sorry. You might cry. You might want to go to a place where people sit and talk and try to feel better. You might talk to a friend or a family person. You might also do something to help the person you hurt. This can help you feel better.

Being sorry can also be hard. Sometimes you might feel angry. You might feel angry because the person did something wrong. You might feel angry because you wanted to do something else. To be sorry means you are trying to understand why the other person did what they did. You are trying to see things from their point of look.

In some places, being sorry is a big thing. If you did something that hurt a lot of people, you might be asked to say sorry many times. This is like a job. You are doing your job to help the people you hurt feel better.

In some families, people are very happy to see the person who did the thing sorry. They feel like the person who did the thing is going to be okay. They might even give the person a big hug. This can help the person feel safe again.
