To give a short talk before a long story is called a meeting. A person who is in this kind of talk is called a speaker. They might talk about things that happened in a city, a big place with many tall houses. They might also talk about a person who did something important and why they did it.

A meeting is like a long conversation that is stopped by a person. This person is called the leader. They decide what the talk should be about and who should listen to it. The speaker and the people who are listening do not say anything at first. They just look at each other. Then, the leader starts to talk. After the leader talks, the other people might ask questions. The speaker listens to these questions and then they talk again.

Before a meeting, the leader usually says a few words. These words are like a plan. They tell everyone what to expect. They might say that they will talk about a person who did something very important. They might also say that there will be many people who want to ask questions.

Sometimes, a meeting is not about talking at all. A person might give a short talk and then they do something else. They might show a picture of a city. They might play a song. They might show a short movie. This is like a quick meeting.

A meeting is often like a game. The leader is the game. They decide the game. The other people are the team. The people listen to what the leader says. They might feel some things. They might think about what happened. They might write down their own ideas. After the game is over, they usually talk about it.
