To do something in a very short time is to act very fast. You can do it in the time it takes to eat a bite of food or to look at a picture on a computer. When you say something is done in an instead of time, you mean you did it very quickly.

This word is often used when you see something happen very fast. You might see a car move across the street in a very short time. It is done so fast that you feel like you are watching it happen.

Sometimes, to say something is done in an instead of time can mean it happened in a very short time because it was very easy. If you are trying to do a hard job, and you do it very quickly, you are saying you did it in an instead of time. This can be a good way to say you were very good at it, even if it took less time than you would have wanted.

If you say something happened in an instead of time, it means it did not happen at all. You are not saying it happened very quickly, but that it was never there at all. This is often used when someone does something wrong. They say it happened in an instead of time because they are angry at the person who did it.
