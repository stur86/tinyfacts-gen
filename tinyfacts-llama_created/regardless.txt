To be without a care about something is to not worry about it. To be without a care can mean to not think about it at all, to not even notice it is there. It is like being in a place where nothing is happening, like a room where no one is there.

Sometimes you feel this way when you are happy, or when you are very good at something. You do not think about the things that you are afraid of. You just let your mind go and enjoy the feeling.

This is also true when you are in a new place, a new city, a new school. You do not worry about what people think or what might happen next. You are just ready to learn and have fun.

But it can also mean to not care about the things that hurt you. If you have been hurt before, you may feel a strong feeling of not wanting to let anyone near you again. You might keep your door closed and your face turned away. You are not going to talk about it anymore.

It is very important to know when to be this way. Sometimes it helps us feel safe. It lets us know that we are not in a situation that is going to hurt us. But sometimes it can also make us feel alone. If you are always without a care, you might not notice the good things in your life. You might not be able to show your friends that you care.

You can also be without a care if you are in a place where there is no one there to see you. If you are in a dark room, or if you are alone in a big house, you might not think about the things you do. You might just move from one place to another. You are not looking for a person to talk to. You are not thinking about the person in front of you. You are just moving.
