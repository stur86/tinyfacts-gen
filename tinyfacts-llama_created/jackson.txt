A person who lives in a city is called a person from the city. This person goes to school and works so they can have money and a home. They are the people who live in the big towns and cities.

A person who lives in a small town is called a person from the town. This person goes to school and works too, but they do not live in the big city. They live in the town with their family and friends.

A person from the city is often very good at what they do. They might be a doctor, a teacher, or a police person. They use their brain and their body to help people.

A person from the town might work in a store or a house. They might help their family or make money by doing work for others. They are also part of their town and help keep it going.

People from the city and people from the town can be different. They might have different ideas about what is good or bad. Some people like big cities and live there all the time. Others like small towns and stay there for many years.
