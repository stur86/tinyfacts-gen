To change something is to make it better. When you say something is an idea, you mean it is not perfect. You want it to be better. You want it to help people.

If you are an animal, you might need to eat more food. If you eat more food, you become bigger and your body gets stronger. This is an act of changing to become a better animal.

If you have a problem at home, you might try to fix it. You might clean the room, call your family, or talk to a friend. When you work on the problem, you are changing it. You are making it better.

To change something also means you change your own mind. Imagine you really want to go to a place you have never seen. If you are afraid, you might not go. But if you decide you want to go, you change your mind. You are making a change in your head.

Sometimes you can only make small changes. You can change your clothes or your food. But if you want to change your life, it is a big change. You might start a new job, move to a new house, or meet new people. These are all acts of making something better.
