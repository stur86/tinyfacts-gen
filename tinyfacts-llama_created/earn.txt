To Ears

Your ears are on your head. They are two little pieces of skin and some hard stuff inside them. They help you hear things. When a sound is big, your ears make the sound. When a sound is small, your ears make it go away.

You can hear things that are far away. You can hear your mom or your friend when you are in a room with many people. You can also hear the sound of a dog if you are in a quiet house.

Your ears also help you feel. When something is hard on your head, you feel it. If a cold comes into your body, your ears might feel funny. This is because your body is trying to fight the bad thing.

You can also hear words. If someone says something to you, you hear the words. When you hear a story, you hear the words. When you hear music, you hear the sounds that make it.

Your ears are very important. They help you hear and feel. Without them, you would not be able to hear or feel anything.
