A person who speaks in a quiet way is called a quiet person. They do not shout like a loud person. They speak softly but they also say what they really mean.

People often use this word to talk about someone who is very calm. If a person is very quiet, they can think about what they are going to do before they do it. They might be thinking about the day or the night ahead. They might also be thinking about how to act in a situation.

Sometimes, a quiet person is a teacher. They know a lot about things. They are very good at reading and writing. They use their words to help others learn.

In the world of music, a person who plays a quiet song is a good one. They play the right notes and they do not make a lot of noise. This helps the music to sound clear.

In some stories, a person might have a baby. The father might be very quiet when he sees the baby. He is happy but he also feels a bit strange. This is because he is the one who brought the baby into the world.

So, a quiet person is someone who keeps their voice low and their words are clear. They are often good at thinking and they can help others.
