To over something means you do it again and again until it becomes easy or very well done. You might over eat food and feel full after a few bites. You might over run a race and finish it before the others. You might over talk and hear what everyone else is saying.

When you say something is over, you mean it is done. The time or the situation is finished. You can also use it for things that are very strong or very big. Like a big wave, you can say the water is over the high ground. This means the water is at the top of the land and there is nothing left but water behind it.

You can also say something is over and gone. If you fall down and hurt your knee, you are over it. You are not going to be that same person again. You are just getting better.
