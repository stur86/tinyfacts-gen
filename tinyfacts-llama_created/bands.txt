A band is a group of people who make music. They all play different kinds of music on different kinds of things. Some bands are very big with many people. Others are small and only have one person.

They often play in front of many people. This is called a show. When they are on a big street, they might play music in front of a huge crowd. When they are in a small town, they might play for a smaller group of people.

Sometimes the people in a band are from different places. They might all be from the same city. Other times they are all from different places. This is why they can make different kinds of music. They can make music that is happy and music that is sad.

People in a band often have different jobs. One person might be the one who makes the music. Another person might be the one who makes the clothes the band wears. A third person might be the one who makes the things that the band uses to make music.

When people say a band is making music, they mean they are making sound that people listen to. It is like a big group of people who all work together to make a strong sound.
