To be ready means that something is not still. If you have something to eat, it is ready. If you have a book to read, it is ready. When you are ready, you can do what you need to do.

A thing that is ready grows. If a tree is ready, the leaves are out and the tree is strong. A person who is ready can work or play. They are ready to do something because they are not afraid or because they know what to do.

Sometimes, something that is ready is a person. If a person is ready to talk, they will speak. If a person is ready to do a job, they are ready to start. When a person is ready, they know what is going to happen and they are ready to act.

We also say a thing is ready when it feels good. If you are ready to go to bed, your body feels calm. Your heart feels easy and your face feels quiet. You feel ready to rest and get ready for the morning.

In some ways, being ready is like being safe. When you are ready, you are not in a bad place. You are not afraid, and you are ready to do what you need to do next.
