To totally means you have done everything. You have done your work. You have done all your jobs. You have done all the things you need to do.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You play for many hours. You are very good at the game. You are the best player on the team. Everyone knows you are the best. You are so good that you can do everything.

You can also mean you are the only one. You are the only person who knows the answer. You are the only one who can do it. You are the only one who can fix it.

Sometimes, you might say something is totally wrong. This means everything is very bad. You are very mad. You do not like it at all. It is the worst thing that has happened.

You can also use this word with things. You can say, a car is totally broken. This means the car is not working at all. It is dead.

So, totally is a word that means you have done everything and you are the only one. It is a word that makes you feel like you are the best.
