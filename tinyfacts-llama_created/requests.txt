When you say a thing you want is a thing you need, it usually means you have a need. A need is a thing you need to live. You can't live without air, water, food, and a place to sleep. You need these things every day.

But when you say you want a thing, it is different. You want something you do not have. You might want a new book, a game, or a friend to talk to. You are not sure if you can get it, but you think it would be nice to have it.

When people give each other these things they want, we use the word to talk. If you want to go to a movie, you say you want to go. If you want to eat food, you say you want to eat. When you give these things, you are showing you care. You are telling the person you are thinking about them.

Sometimes, when you want to do something, you also say you are asking. You are not sure if you can do it, but you are asking. You are using the word to let someone know you want to do something.
