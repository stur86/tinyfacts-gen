To be late is to be behind in time. When you are late, you arrive after what should have happened. This can happen in many ways. You might be running to the door before you should start. You might be waiting in line before the line is over. Sometimes, you just wait for what you want.

Being late can be a problem. If you are late for a meeting, you might miss important parts of the talk. If you are late for school, you might miss the part you need to learn. If you are late for a party, you might not be able to join in.

Being late can also be a sign of a problem with your body. If you have a hard time falling asleep, you might sleep late. This can make you tired and grinning.

Sometimes, being late can also mean you are happy. You might be late to the party because you wanted to see your friends. You might be late to the meeting because you were talking to someone. This is a good kind of late because you get to do something you like before you have to do what you need to do.

In some places, being late is seen as a bad thing. People might yell at you if you are late. They might ask you why you were late. They might say, you should have been there earlier. This can make you feel bad and worry about what might happen next.

But, when you are late, you can also learn from it. You can learn to be more carefully. You can learn to plan ahead. You can learn to let things happen when they are ready. This is a good way to grow and change.
