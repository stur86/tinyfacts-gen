To make something out of small pieces of hard stuff is to build it. When you build a house, you use many of these small hard pieces. You take one hard piece and you make it very strong. You put many of these hard pieces around it so it does not break easily. You can also use these hard pieces to make other things like shoes, cars, and tables.

You can also use these hard pieces to keep water clean. You take a small hard piece and you press it against the water. This makes the tiny bits of the water go away and keeps the water clean. This is why you need to clean your hands many times each day.

Sometimes, people throw these hard pieces into the air. When they fall back down, the hard pieces come together to form a hard ball. This is how you get balls for games. The ball is made from many of these small hard pieces.
