Conversations often start with how many people are there. You might say, five or ten. This tells us how many are speaking. Sometimes, you might say, one or two. This shows you are talking about one person.

But sometimes, you talk about how much stuff is there. If you have a lot of books, you might say, you have ten books. This tells us that you have many books. You can also talk about how many people are in a group. If a group of friends is ten, you know you have ten friends.

You can also talk about how many people are watching a game. If everyone is watching and you have ten people, then ten people are watching. This means everyone in the room is paying attention.

A third kind of talk is about how much air there is. If you take one big breath, you have one bit of air. But if you take ten bits of air, you have ten bits of air inside you. This is how much stuff is in a room.

Sometimes, when you talk about these things, you use the word they are and they are. This means you are talking about how many are there. You are telling us how many are in a room. It is like a number.
