To drive something means to move it from one place to another. You put it in a car and start the car. The car starts moving and you go to a different city. When you get to the city, you stop the car and you are not allowed to leave until you stop. You must follow the street signs and the people who give you a number. The number tells you when to stop. This is called driving.

There is another kind of drive. If you are a parents, you drive their children. You take them to school, to the park, or to see a movie. You are the one who gives the children food to eat. You also help them when they are sick. You stay with them until they feel better.

We also drive a computer. This computer can do many things. You can write words, make pictures, or show movies. It can remember things from the past and tell us things that are happening now. You can use it to talk to people all over the world. You can also play games with it.

Sometimes, people drive things that fly. A person sits in a seat and pushes a thing that goes through the sky. This thing goes very fast and can go very high. It can carry many people at once. It is a very safe way to go far.

In a game, people drive a car around a big round thing. They do not hit the other car. They try to go around the round thing as fast as they can. It is a fun way to watch people move quickly.
