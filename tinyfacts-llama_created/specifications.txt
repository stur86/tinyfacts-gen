When you write something, you must know what is asked of you. If you are writing a book, you might know what kind of words to use, how many words you should use, and how you should write them. You might know about the pictures you should put in the book and how they should look. This helps you make sure your book is what the person who will read it wants.

When you are making something new, you need to know what you are making. You do not just make something and then say, it is done. You must know what you have made. You might say, this is a car. This car has a round body, a long body that goes on the road, and a part that goes to the ground. You know this because you saw the car and you remember how it looks.

Sometimes, people make things that are not what they are supposed to be. They might make a car that is very fast but also very big. Or they might make a book with pictures that are not like what they expected. If you do not know what to look for, you might not see the problem.

You also need to know what people expect from you. If you are a doctor, you know what you do and how you do it. You know what kind of clothes people should wear when they see you. You know what kind of words to use and what kind of face to show. If you do not know what people expect, you might cause them trouble or make them angry.

In your life, you will often have to learn what to do. You might learn how to ride a car or how to play a game. You learn by doing and by listening to others. You learn that if you do not follow what is told to you, you might get into trouble or hurt yourself.
