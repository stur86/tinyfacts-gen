A warning is a way to tell people about a problem or a thing that might happen. It is a way to say, before things go wrong, that you should be ready.

You might give a warning to a child. If a dog is going to run out of the house, you might say, in a quiet voice, that the dog is going to come out and that you should close the door. This way, the child is ready for what is coming.

You might also give a warning to a group of people. If a big fire is going to break out, you might say, in a loud voice, that the fire is coming and that everyone should leave the building quickly. This helps the people stay safe.

A warning can also be about a way to do something. If you are about to drive very fast, you might give a warning to your friends. You might say, in a quiet voice, that the road is very wide and that you should not be afraid. This helps everyone stay safe.

Sometimes, a warning is about a person or a thing that is not a person. If a bus is going to stop soon, the people who drive the bus might give a warning. They might say, in a loud voice, that the bus is going to stop and that everyone should get off.

A warning can be very important. It helps us stay safe and avoid trouble.
