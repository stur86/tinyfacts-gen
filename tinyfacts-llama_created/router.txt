A computer is a thing that can do many things. It does not have a brain like a living thing. It does not think. It just does what it is told. You tell it what to do. It follows that. 

Think of a house. In a house, there are many rooms. Each room has a door. The door opens to another room. If you go from one room to another room in a house, you walk through doors. A computer works like that too. It has many rooms. Each room is a part. The room where you give the computer words is the first room. The room where you look at the pictures is the second room. The computer then goes to the next room and does what you tell it to do.

You use the computer to talk to other computers. You send words to them. The computer takes those words and goes to the next computer. It says, here is what you want. The computer then does what you asked. You can also send pictures. The computer takes the pictures and shows them to you. You can see what you want to see.

You can also use the computer to send things. You can send money to another person. You tell the computer what to do. It goes to the other computer and takes the money. The money goes to the person who wants it. The computer is like a box that helps you send things to other people.

A computer is also used in a place where many people work together. They do many things. They write papers. They make things. They answer people. The computer does all of this for them. Without a computer, people could not do all these things. They would have to write papers by hand and make things by hand. The computer makes it all much easier.
