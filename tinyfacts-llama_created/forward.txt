When you move something, you are moving it forward. You are going in a line from where you start to where you end. If you go across a field, you are moving forward. If you walk, you are moving forward. You are on your way.

You can also move something that is not you. If a dog starts running, it is moving forward. If a ball is thrown, it is moving forward. This is how things change and move.

Imagine you have a piece of paper with a word on it. If you write more words on the paper, the paper will get bigger. This is like moving forward. You are adding more pieces of paper to what you have already.

Sometimes, we use the word forward to talk about things that are happening now. We might say, The movie is moving forward. This means the story is going on and things are happening.

We also use forward to talk about people. If a child is a year older, they are moving forward in life. They are getting more years of school, more money, and more power.

In this way, moving forward is about changing and going in a new way.
