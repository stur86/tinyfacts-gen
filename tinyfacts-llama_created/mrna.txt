When you are very, very sick, your body tries to make something new so it can fight the bad stuff. It does this by making tiny pieces of the bad stuff, but these pieces are not the same as the bad stuff. They are like a picture of the bad stuff, but without any of the bad stuff inside them.

These tiny pieces go into a place inside your body where it makes them. The body then takes the pieces and uses them to make more of the good stuff. It does this over many days and weeks until the good stuff is strong enough to fight the bad stuff.

The tiny pieces are made from pieces of the bad stuff. These pieces are cut into very small pieces, and they go into the body. The body then takes these pieces and makes them. Inside the body, the pieces are used to make new white blood cells. These new cells are the good stuff the body needs to fight off the bad stuff.

The tiny pieces that the body makes are like a very small movie. The movie shows the body what the bad stuff looks like. The body is like a person who is watching a movie. The movie is very important because it helps the body know what to do to fight the bad stuff.

Sometimes, when a person is very sick, the body tries to make these tiny pieces from other things it finds in the air. It takes pieces of skin and other parts of the body, and it makes them. These tiny pieces are like a very small movie from the body.

In a way, the tiny pieces are like a picture of the bad stuff. But they are not the bad stuff. They are the picture the body makes of the bad stuff, so it can know what to do to fight it.
