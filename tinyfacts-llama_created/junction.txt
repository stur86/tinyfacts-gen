A place where many lines meet is a kind of area. It is where roads, lines for cars and buses, and lines for trains cross.

Imagine you are on a long road with many ways to go to different places. Each way is like a line. When you want to go to a new city, you might have to use many lines. You might start on one line and then use another line to go to the city.

In this kind of area, many lines from different roads and lines from different trains meet. This is a place where all these lines can join together. When many lines meet, it is a place where people can get ready to go somewhere. It is a place where you can plan your trip.

Some places have many of these kinds of areas. These are called cities. Other places might have only a few of these areas. They are still places where people live and move around, but they might not have as many lines.

In some places, the lines are not just for cars and buses. Some of them are for people who carry heavy things. These lines are like big places where you can drop off heavy things and pick up other heavy things. They are important because they help move things around the city.

People use these kinds of areas because they help us move from one place to another. They help us go to work, to school, or to a friend. They are a kind of area where we can find many lines to help us get where we want to go.
