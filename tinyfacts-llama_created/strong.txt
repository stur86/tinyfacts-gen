The word strong can mean many different things.

First, it can mean very good at doing a job. If you are very strong, you can lift a heavy box and hold it up for a long time. You can also run fast and jump high. This is when you feel like you have power inside you.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about things that are not living. A tree is strong because it can hold up a heavy sky for many years. A rock is strong because it does not break when you push on it.

We also use this word to talk about people. If someone is very strong, they are very good at fighting or standing up for what they believe in. They can stay calm in a hard situation. A strong friend is a person who listens to you when you need to talk.

When you say something is strong, you are usually talking about something that is hard to do. Maybe a person is very strong at playing a game. Maybe a big animal is strong. Or maybe something is strong because it can stay up when the ground around it falls down.

So, the word strong is about how something is built inside you, or about how it can hold up for a long time, or about how it helps you in a hard situation.
