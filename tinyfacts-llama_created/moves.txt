When you say something moves, you mean it is changing its position. Imagine you are standing still. The ground is the same and the sky is the same. But if you start to walk, the ground is under you and the sky is above you. You are now moving from one place to another.

If you hold a cup and you bring it to your face, you are moving from one spot to another. You are on the ground and you are pointing at the wall. The wall is moving, but you are still holding the cup.

A car also moves. It goes very fast and it goes around a round road. The road is the ground under the car, and you are following it as the car goes by. You are not staying in one place. The car is moving from one spot to another.

Things that move are not always big. You can move your arms, your legs, and your face. You are moving. You are not just standing still like a rock.
