To hope means to feel that things will happen soon. It is about the things you believe will happen in your life.

Imagine you see a bright light in the sky. You hope it is a fire going away. If you hope for a good day, you might feel a little bit sad if you are afraid of something happening. But if you hope for a good day, you feel happy and you are ready to play.

You also hope for things that are not real. You hope your friend will call you soon. You hope a ball will go into the back of the house. You hope things will work out. This is because you are sure that something good will happen.

People hope to get a job or to be happy with a family. They hope to have a nice home. They hope to live a life with the people they love.

You can also hope for the best. If you hope your car will not break down, you feel safe. You hope your food will be good. You hope your friends will be there when you need them.
