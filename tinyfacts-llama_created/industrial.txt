An area where big things are made by many people using many things.

There are many places like this. Some are in cities, and some are far away from cities. Some are in the middle of the land. Some are in the water, where people build big houses and make things that go fast.

Here, people use many things that make the things move. They use strong power from lines, water, or air. They also use many small parts that are made by small people. These small parts are like the tiny pieces that make a car work.

Big places like these are very important. They help people buy things, make food, and make clothes. They make things that people use every day, like food bags, cars, and houses.

Some places are only for one kind of work. They make only one kind of thing. But many places are for many kinds of work. They make many different things and use many different kinds of power.
