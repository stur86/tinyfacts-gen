A thing you buy to get into something happens with a word. When you buy a thing, you are giving money to the person who makes it. This money is what they use to pay the other people who are working inside the thing.

Think of a movie. You buy a thing that lets you see the movie. Inside this thing, there are many stories. You pay to see the first story. You pay again to see the next story, and the next story, and so on. When you finish the last story, the thing is done. You have paid for it.

Sometimes, you buy a thing that lets you go somewhere. You pay money and the person helps you get to the place. When you arrive, you use the thing to get into the building or the car. Inside the place, you do not pay again. You are already inside.

You can also buy a thing that lets you do something. You might buy a thing that lets you play a game with a friend. You pay money, and then you can play the game. You do not have to pay money again after you finish playing.

If you have a thing and you do not use it, it might become old. The thing might need new parts. You might have to pay money to fix it. This happens with a car. If a car has a long time, the parts might get old. You have to pay money to get new parts.

A thing you buy is important. It lets you do or see something. You do not have to buy it. But you pay money to buy it.
