To flash or to flash is to move your hand quickly from side to side. You can do this while you are looking at a picture or a moving thing. This is how you make your eyes look like they are moving very fast. 

When you flash a light, it goes in and out very fast. This makes a bright, bright light that is very hard to see. If you flash a light while you are sleeping, you might wake up and feel like you saw a bright light.

You can also flash your face. If you are very happy or very excited, you might smile and then quickly flash your face. This makes your eyes look like they are smiling. This is how you show that you are happy.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about small things. If a person is telling a story, they might say,  I was trying to get a picture. They are flashing their hands to show how quickly they are moving.
