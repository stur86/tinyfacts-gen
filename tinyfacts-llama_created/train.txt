A train is a big thing with many cars. It can carry a lot of people, things, or animals from one place to another. It moves along a long line of lines that go through many places.

Imagine you have a box with a door. You put the box in a car and close the door. The car goes very fast. After a while, you open the door and take out the box. The car still keeps going, but now you are in another place. You did not use the box at all! The box was on the train.

There are different kinds of trains. Some are very small. They carry only a few people. Other ones are very big. They carry many people or even a lot of things. The cars are often round, but some are more like a long line with many rooms.
