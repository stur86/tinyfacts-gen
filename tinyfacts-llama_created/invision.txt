To imagine something is to picture it in your mind. When you look at a picture, you see what the person has made. You see a house with a big window and a green green green green tree in front. Your brain is using all the colors and the lines to show you what the picture looks like.

You can also imagine what might happen if something happens. If your friend is going to the store and you know they will buy a book, you might imagine the book on the floor and the friend walking down the street. Your mind is helping you think about what will happen next.

Sometimes you imagine things that are not real. You might imagine a dog that is not there. You might see a bright light and think the world is very bright. Your brain is doing its job, making the picture in your mind.

Sometimes you imagine things that are good for you. If you think about a happy day, you feel happy. You might imagine a time when you played with your friends and had fun. Your brain is helping you feel those good things.

You can also imagine things that are far away. You might imagine a city that is very tall and very bright. You might imagine a cold place where the air is very cold and the water is very clear. Your brain is using your eyes to see the pictures in your mind.

Imagine you are a time far in the past. You are looking at a picture of your family. You see your mom and your dad and your little sister. You might imagine what they were like and what they did. Your brain is helping you remember.
