To make something sound

When you want to share what you see or what you feel, you can use a thing that makes sound. This thing has many tiny parts that move in and out very fast. When the parts go in one way, the air goes out the other way. The air makes the sound.

You can put these parts into a box that sits in your hands. Then you can use the box to make the sound. You can press the box and the sound comes out. You can use the box to play music, talk, tell stories, or make people laugh.

Sometimes you use these boxes to talk to people. You speak into the box and it sends your words into the air. This lets the other person hear you. This is how you talk to your family and friends.

You can also use these boxes to make the world feel the way you feel. You can put music into the box and it makes you feel happy. You can put pictures into the box and it makes you see things in a new way. This is how movies work.

In your home, there are many of these boxes. Some are in the kitchen. Some are in the room where you sleep. Some are in the car. Some are on the table in the living room. You can carry them with you so you can listen to music or talk to a friend while you walk.

These boxes are very important. They let us share our ideas and stories with each other. They let us hear what the other person is saying. They let us feel the world in a new way. They make the world feel like we are all part of the same family.
