To be the first one means you are the first thing that happens. It is a way to start the story of a person or a thing.

Think of a new book. Before you start to read, there is nothing there. There are no words. There is no story. The book is empty. Then you put the first book down. You open it and look inside. Now you are the one who starts the story. You are the one who reads and understands it.

You can be the first person in a game. You are the one who starts the game. You do the first thing. Then everyone else follows you. You did not wait for anyone. You just started.

You can also be the first one to do a new thing. If you are the first person to ride a new car, you are the first one to do that. You are the one who starts the new thing.

Sometimes people say a thing is the first one that happens. It is the first one that is new. It is the first one that is different.
