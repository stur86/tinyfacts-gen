To arrive means you have reached a place. When you are on a long trip, you might start at one town and go to another town. After many hours of driving, you finally arrive. You have arrived.

Imagine you have a book that you want to read. You start to read it. You read one word, then another word, and another word. After you read for a long time, you reach the end of the book. You have read the book. You have arrived.

You can also arrive at a place where people are waiting for you. If you are a doctor, you might arrive at a hospital to help people. If you are a person who loves music, you might arrive at a place to play for people. When you arrive, you are the first person there.

Sometimes, you might arrive late. If you leave your house at  afternoon, and it is already five, you are late. You could have been at your house two hours later. Being late is not good. It is better to arrive on time so you do not cause trouble.

You can also arrive in other ways. If you walk to a place, you arrive there. If you fly to a far place, you arrive there. You might arrive by car, by bus, or by train. Each way is different.
