To hit something with a hard thing

If you are angry, you might hit your face. You use your hand to make a loud sound. You are using your body to make a strong move.

If you are talking to a friend, you might say something that makes them laugh. You are using your words to make them smile.

You can also say that something is hitting you. If you walk into a room and hit a wall, you feel a bad feeling. Your body moves in a way that you did not want.

Sometimes, when you see a bad person, you feel angry. You want to hit them. This is a strong feeling that you do not want to act on. It is a way to show you are very angry.

You can also hit things that you do not want. You might hit a rock to get it to stop moving. You are stopping it so it will not hurt you anymore.

The word is also used to talk about the sun. When the sun is very bright, it can hit the ground. This makes the ground hot and the air around you feel warm. We use this word when we talk about a bright day or a hot day.
