To break something means to make a big change. This happens when you use or push something too much. A small thing can break if you drop it. A chair can break if you push it too hard. A child can break a glass if they drop it on the floor.

Sometimes, people break a promise. If you say you will do something, but you do not follow through, you have broken it. You did not keep your word.

You can also break a word. If you use words that are not right, people might laugh at you. You might feel sad or angry.

In some games, you break a line. If the line breaks, the game is over. You must try again.

Breaking can be hard to do. Sometimes you need to let go of something that hurts you. It is an important part of life.
