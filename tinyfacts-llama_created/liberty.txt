To be free means you are not held in a place you do not want to be. You are your own person. You can move where you want, and you do not have to follow the same way as everyone else.

Being free means you can say what you think. You can say things that are good for you and things that make you angry. You can do what you want, even if it is not the same as what other people do. This is a good thing because it keeps you safe and helps you grow.
