Step is a word that tells how many small steps you take. You can step on a floor or a table. When you step, you move from one place to another. It is a way to go.

Imagine you are walking. You need to take a step. You step one foot forward. Then you step another foot forward. This is one step. If you take two steps, you have two steps. If you take three steps, you have three steps. This helps you know how many steps you have taken.

People use this word with other words. If you say a person is a step up, it means they are higher than another person. If you say a book is a step ahead, it means the story is going in a different way than the story before it.

It is also a word to show how many steps it takes to get to a place. If you have to walk one hundred steps, you will get there. It can mean you have to walk in a straight line or you can walk around things.
