To use something is to make it work for you. When you say you want to go to a place, you are using something. You are telling it where to go and why you want to go there.

Think of a dog. If you give a dog a ball to chase, the dog is using that ball. It is not thinking about why you want it anymore. It is only thinking about the ball.

When you say you are using something, you are saying you are acting on it. You are not just sitting there looking at it. You are moving and doing things with it.

You can use a word. You can use a word to tell a story. You can use a word to ask a question. You can use a word to say how you feel. All these are ways to use something.
