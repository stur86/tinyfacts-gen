A drier is a thing that keeps water inside. You put water in it, and it keeps it. You can not drink the water from it. It keeps it for a long time.

Inside the thing, there is a tiny room. This room is very good at keeping warm. The room keeps the water inside the thing very hot. When the room is warm, the water inside the thing feels hot too. This keeps the water from going cold.

You can use a drier to dry clothes. You put a piece of clothes in the thing. The thing takes the water out of the clothes. The clothes dry quickly. The room inside the thing is very good at taking out water.

Sometimes you use a drier to dry hair. You put your hair in the thing. The thing takes the water out of your hair. Your hair feels dry and feels good. This is very nice to do.

You also use a drier to clean things. You put a thing in the thing. The thing takes the bad stuff out. The room inside the thing is good at taking out bad stuff. This helps keep the thing clean.
