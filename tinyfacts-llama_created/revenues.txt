To get something is to have it. If you buy a book, you get it. If you see a movie, you see it.

When you work, you get money. This money is called your money. You use it to buy things you want or need. If you get more money than you need, you have more than you wanted.

A business is a thing that makes money. If a store has many people coming in and buying things, it is making money. It has more money than it needs. The money goes into a box and then the box goes to someone else who wants it.

If you have a job and you get money every week, you have your money. If you have money and you do not need it, you have more money than you wanted.

Money is important because it lets you buy things you like. You can buy food, clothes, and things to play. It helps you live a good life.

Sometimes, money comes from work. You work and you get paid. You keep the money for later when you need it. This helps you plan your life.

Money also helps you buy things you want right now. If you see a new car, you might buy it because you want it. If you want a new book, you might buy it because you need it.
