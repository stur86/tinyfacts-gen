The word while is used to talk about two things happening at the same time.

When you are waiting for something to happen, you might think, while waiting, I could do something else. This means you are waiting and doing another thing at the same time. You are not just waiting one thing, but both at the same time.

Imagine you are waiting to go into a room. You are sitting on a chair. You are not just sitting, you are also waiting. You are doing two things at once.

Sometimes this is easy to see. Think about a time when you are talking to a friend and at the same time you are looking at a picture. You are waiting for your friend to stop talking and you are also looking at the picture.

Other times it is harder. Think about a time when you are doing work and you are also watching a movie. You are waiting for your work to be done and you are also watching the movie.

The word while is important because it helps us talk about time. It lets us say, I am waiting, and at the same time, I am doing something else. It makes our words more complete.
