To be yourself means to stay true to who you are. This is different from pretending to be someone else. If you dress like another person, you are not being yourself. If you talk like another person, you are not being yourself either.

When you are yourself, you are happy. You do not feel like you are missing a part of yourself. You know who you are and you like it. You feel like you are the best person in the world. You do not try to change to be like someone else.

If you try to be someone you are not, it is not good. You might feel sad or angry. You might feel like you are hurting your friends. You might feel like you do not have the right words. Your heart is telling you to be yourself, but your mind is telling you to change.

Being yourself is important. It is a part of what makes you, you. You have a story to tell and a life to live. You have a life that is your own. You do not have to change to fit another person. You can stay true to yourself and still be loved and accepted by others.

To be yourself is like having a door in your body. You can open the door and let the world see who you really are. It is a beautiful thing to be yourself.
