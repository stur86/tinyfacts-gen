An old word for things that have been kept for a long time.

Think of a box where you keep things you do not want anymore. This box is not for you. It is for someone else. Sometimes, people use this word for books, papers, and other things that are important but you do not need anymore.

It is also about the past. Imagine you are looking at a picture of your family a long time ago. The picture is from a time you will never see again. You look at it and remember things your parents and other family people told you. This is like looking at an old book that keeps the story of your family.

Sometimes, people keep things from the past for many years. They store them in a room or a building so that others can find them later. This helps us learn about the world and our place in it.

You can also use it to talk about things that have happened many times before. If you are going to a place you have never been before, you might say you are going to the old city. This means you are going to a place that has been around for a long time.

The word can also mean a place where important things are kept. Imagine a city with many old houses and many old books. People go to this city to find books or papers that are important for their work. It is a place where many people look for things they need.
