The word wait is often used to mean waiting. People use it when they want something but do not know if it will happen right away. When you are waiting, you are not doing anything. You are just standing there, looking at the wall, or looking at a picture.

This word is important because it helps people understand how they feel. If you are waiting for a friend to arrive, you might feel a little bit sad. You might think, Is this friend really going to come? You might feel a bit worried.

Sometimes, you might wait for a long time. You might sit in a room for a long time, waiting for a phone call. Or you might wait for a food to come out of a hot box. Waiting can be hard because you have to be still and quiet.

You can also use this word to mean being calm. If you are waiting for something to happen, you might be very quiet. You might not say a word, but you are still there. When you are calm, you feel safe.

There are also other ways to use this word. In a game, you might wait for a turn. The person who is playing the game waits for the other person to make a move. In a story, a person might wait for a big thing to happen.

In a way, waiting is a big thing. It is about being ready and not moving. It is about being still and waiting for what is coming next. When you wait, you are taking care of yourself. You are letting things happen without making them happen yourself.
