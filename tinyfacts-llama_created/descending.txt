To go down means you are moving in a way that is going under something. Imagine you are in a high house and you want to get to the ground. If you go down a long way, you are moving away from the top. This is what you mean when you say something is going down.

Sometimes, things go up instead. If you are in a high room and you want to get to the floor, you are going up. This is like the ground, but going the other way.

You can also say something is going down in a way that is not good. If a person is feeling sad, they might go down. This means they are moving away from being happy. It can also mean that something is falling apartment by part.

When you use this word with people, you might talk about their lives. If a friend is having a bad day, they might say they are going down. This means they are feeling sad and might be moving away from being happy.
