To deal with something means you do something to it. When you deal with a person, you are talking to them. You say things to them so they know you are trying to understand them. You might laugh at a funny joke. You are not afraid of them.

To deal with a thing means you try to find a way to make it not hurt you. If you have a ball, you might throw it across the room. This is a way to deal with the ball. You are not letting the ball go. You are holding it in your hand.

You can also deal with a problem. If you have a hard time at school, you might ask for help. A teacher can give you a paper to read. You are trying to find a way to get out of the problem so you do not feel sad anymore.

To deal with something is also to let it stand. If you do not deal with a thing, it stays and you do not know what to do with it. If you do not deal with a problem, it will build up and you will get very angry.
