A long, straight piece of wood, usually made from one kind of tree, that goes up from the ground to the sky. It is often used to build tall things like houses, schools, and stores. It can be very heavy because it is made from a hard stuff that does not break easily.

Imagine a long line of people, one on the ground and one in the air. The one on the ground is holding up the line. The one in the air is holding up the line for everyone to see. If the ground person falls, the line falls with them. If the line falls, everyone falls with it.

A strong one is usually taller than a strong one. A strong one can hold a lot more stuff. A strong one is often used to build tall things that stand for a long time. A light one might hold up a sign or a picture, but it is not as strong as a strong one.

The word comes from the way the pieces fit together. The long piece has a line of hard stuff at the top that holds it up. The hard stuff is like the skin of a dog. It keeps the piece from falling down.
