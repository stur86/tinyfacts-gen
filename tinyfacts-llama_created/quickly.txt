To quickly means you do something fast. When you quickly do something, you do it before other people. This can be a good thing. It helps you get where you need to go quickly. If you quickly walk, you can run fast. If you quickly pick up a ball, it goes high into the air.

If you quickly do something, you do it before time is over. You might quickly answer a question. You might quickly find a word you need. You might quickly find a way out of a hard situation.

Being quick is also about being good at things. If you are quick at a game, you can hit a ball fast. If you are quick at a job, you do it fast. You can do many things quickly.

Sometimes, being quick is not good. If you quickly move around the room, you might trip. If you quickly do something bad, it can cause trouble. You might hurt yourself or someone else.
