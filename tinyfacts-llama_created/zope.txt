To go to a place is to move away from home. You might go to school, work, or visit friends. Many people use this word when they decide to leave their city or town to find a new place to live.

You can also go to a place to have fun. You might go to a movie. You might go to a game. You might go to a place with big lights and music. You can dance and laugh with friends. This is a good way to get away from your worries and have fun.
