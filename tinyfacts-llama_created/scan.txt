To see something with your eyes is one way to look. You look at a person, a house, a tree, or a car. Your brain tells you what the thing looks like.

You can also look at things that are far away. You look through a small window or a computer. The computer sends your brain the picture. Your brain then puts the picture into your eyes so you can see it.

Sometimes you look at things that are in front of you. You see a thing, a person, a book, or a chair. You see the front of the thing, but you do not see the back or the sides.

To do this is to look. It is a way to find out what is in front of you. You might use your eyes to find a door, a phone, or a chair. You are looking to see what is there.

You can also do this with things you do every day. You look at your watch. You look at the time. You look at a book. You look at the words on the paper. You are looking at things in your life.
