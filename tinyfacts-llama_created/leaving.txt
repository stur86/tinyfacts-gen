To leave means you are going away from a place. You might leave your home, your school, or a city. You might also leave a job. When you leave, you are not there anymore. You are not part of the group any longer.

Think about a time when you were very young. You loved a person or a dog. You would follow them around. One day, you said, I love you more than that. Now, you want to be alone with that person. You are leaving them. You are going to a new place where you can be alone.

To leave can be good or bad. If you leave a job, you can start a new one. You might be happy to work on a different job. But if you leave home, you might feel sad. You miss your family and your friends.

Sometimes, you leave because you want to see another place. Maybe you went to a different city for school. You saw new things and learned new things. You want to stay there for a long time. You might want to find a job there or make new friends.

You can also leave for other reasons. If a person is mean, you might leave them. You do not want to be near them anymore. You want to move to a different place where you are safe.

To leave is to go away. It means you are moving to a different place. It is a part of life when you go to new places.
