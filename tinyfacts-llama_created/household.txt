A house is a big building where people live. It has many rooms and floors. The rooms are for different things. There are rooms for sleeping, rooms for eating, rooms for playing, and rooms for work.

Inside the house, there is a kitchen. In this room, people make food. They use a big box that makes food and a small box that makes drinks. There are also tables and chairs where people eat together.

There is also a room for cleaning. People clean the house every day. They pick up the floor, they wipe the tables, and they make the rooms look nice.

Some houses have a room for the baby. This room is very quiet and warm. The baby sleeps there. When the baby is very small, the room is like a tiny house inside the big house.

There are also rooms for other people. One room is for men who work. Another room is for women who work. Some houses have rooms for children. These rooms are very quiet and safe for the children to play.

A house is also a place where people live together. They share a big room and a kitchen. They all help each other. They talk and laugh with each other. In the end, the house feels like home.

You can also have a house that is very tall. This is a very big building with many floors. People live in these tall houses with many rooms and many people. They have a lot of space and a lot of work to do.

Sometimes, people move to a new house. They buy a new house and move there. They like their new home because it is different from the old house. They like the new rooms and the new kitchen. They feel happy there.

In some places, houses are not just one building. They are many small houses that are joined together. These are called cities. The cities have many streets and many houses. People move from one house to another. They are all part of the same big group.

A house is a very important thing. It is where people live. It is where they make their home. It is where they feel safe.
