To say something is a thing that is round is to say it is a round thing. It is a word that we use when we talk about a small round thing that we hold in our hands. It is like the ball you use to play a game with your friends. It is round and it does not go up into the sky like a big ball.

Sometimes, this word is also used to talk about money. If you have a lot of money, you can say you are making money. This means you are getting more money than you need. You are in a good place with your money.

In the past, people also used this word to talk about people who were very kind. They said that if a person is nice and makes other people happy, they are a nice person. This word helps us understand what a person is like.

When you say something is a thing that is round, you are telling the story about the thing.
