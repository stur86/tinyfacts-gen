To go to the middle of the night is to go to the part of the day when the sky is dark and there is no light from the sun.

Imagine you are in a room with a window. If you open the window and look out, you will see the world. If you close the window and the room gets very dark, you can see things at night. That is when the middle of the night is.

This is also true with the sky. When the day is over and the night begins, the sky gets darker. The stars come out and the night sky is very quiet. It is a time to rest and think about what happened during the day.

In some places, the middle of the night is a time when it is very quiet and there are not many people around. This is when people like to stay in their homes and watch the night. They might watch the stars, read books, or listen to music. It is a time for quiet and for the night to be alone.
