When you are fast, you move very quickly. You can go very far in a short time. You can run, ride a car, or even fly. This is because your body is moving a lot and your feet hit the ground or the ground hits the air. 

You can also be fast with your mind. If you are very good at doing things, you can do them in a short time. You might finish a job quickly or learn new things fast. This is because your brain is working well and your body is able to act quickly.

If you are very fast with your heart, you might feel happy. Your body is moving fast and your face might be smiling. This is because your brain knows you are moving quickly and it feels good.

Sometimes, people use the word fast to talk about something that is not a person. If you have a lot of money, you can buy many things quickly. If you have a lot of power, you can do many things very fast. But if you are very old, you might not be as fast. Your body might not be as strong, and it might be harder to move quickly.
