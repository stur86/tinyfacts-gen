To be in a position to help is to be ready. Many times, people move into new jobs. They are not always ready. They are still learning. They need time to learn and grow.

When you are ready, you are in a good place. You know what to do. You are not afraid. You are ready to work. You are ready to help. This is what people mean when they say you are in a position to help.

In some places, people work for a group. They do not work alone. They work with many others. They all work together. They help each other. When you are in this group, you are in a position to help.

You can also be in a position to help a person. If you see someone in need, you can help. You can tell them to stay safe. You can give them food. You can tell them to go home. This is a good way to show that you care.

Sometimes, people work for a big group that is very important. They are not always the ones who do the work. They work behind the people who lead them. They help to make sure everything works well.

To be in a position to help is to be a good friend. It is to be someone who cares. It is to be ready to help when you need to.
