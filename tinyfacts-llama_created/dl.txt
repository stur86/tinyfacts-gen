To do something with a computer

A small group of computers works together to do many things. One computer does the hard work. Another computer helps the first one. They do this together. When you want to see pictures or play music, the computers work together to make it happen.

You can also use these computers to talk to people who are far away. They use words and numbers to send your voice or your words to the other person. The computers are very good at this. They can understand what you are saying even if you speak different words.

You can use computers to do many things. You can write letters or send money. You can play games with other people. You can even learn new things from the computers. They have a huge book of things that people have already learned.

Some people use computers for work. They work with numbers and words. They make money from their work. Others use computers for fun. They play games or watch movies. They can also use computers to learn about things they are interested in.

There is also a word that means to do something very well. If you do something very well, you are doing it with a computer. You are using the computer to make it happen. It is like doing something with a friend. You both work together to make something good happen.
