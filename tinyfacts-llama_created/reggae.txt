To make a sound with your hands is a way to make music. When you hit a thing with your hands, it makes a sound. You can hit the same thing many times and they will all go the same way. This is why people call it one sound.

A person who makes this kind of sound is a very good one. They use their hands to make the right sound. They can make the sound go up and down and side to side. It is very good music. People like to listen to this kind of music when they are happy or when they are feeling sad. It is also a great way to relax.
