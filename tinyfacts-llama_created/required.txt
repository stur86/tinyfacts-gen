To be needed means you must be present. If you do not have something, you are missing. This is often found in rooms or cars. You might need to get a key to open a door. You need the key because it is there to let you in. If you try to enter without the key, you will not be allowed to go in.

In life, you need many things. You need food to stay strong. You need water to drink. You need a bed to sleep. You need money so you can buy things you want. You need a place to live. Without these, you would be very sad and alone.

You also need to follow the right way. If you drive to the wrong place, you will get lost. You need to know the way to get home. If you do not follow the right way, you might not get there when you want.

Sometimes, you need to give something too. If you love someone very much, you might want to give them a big hug. This shows you care. You do not just keep the love inside. You share it with the person.

Being needed is important because it shows we are important. If we are not needed, we are not important. We are like a tree that falls in the ground. The ground stays there for many years. The tree was important because it stood there. Without the tree, the ground would be empty and cold.
