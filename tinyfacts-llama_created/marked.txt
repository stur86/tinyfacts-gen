To mark something means to put a small thing on it so people can see. A person might put a small piece of paper on a book. If they have a problem, they might write down the word that is wrong. They then put the paper in the book so everyone can see the answer.

You can also mark a place. If you go to a new city, you might write the name of the city on a piece of paper and put it on your bag. This way, when you come back to your home, you know where you are.

Sometimes, we mark things so we can remember. You might draw a picture of a cat. You put a small line around the eyes and a small line around the mouth. This helps you remember the cat at night when you are not looking.

In a game, people might put their hands on a table. This is a way of showing they have done something. It is a way to say, I am doing this, and I am ready to start.

We also mark things that are not living. If you have a problem with a computer, the person who fixes it might write down what the computer does wrong. This helps them fix the computer so it works again.

So, to mark something is to change it so we can see it more easily. It is a way to show that we have noticed and are ready to act.
