An animal is an animal. Animals are living things. They breathe air, drink water, and eat food. They move their bodies from side to side so they can reach food. They use their mouths to take in air and food.

An animal is often very small. A tiny animal might be smaller than a small rock. A big animal might be the same as a house. Animals are all living things.

Animals can do many different things. They can run across the ground, fly through the air, or walk on a floor. Some animals can even live in the water.

Animals need to eat. They have a hard part in their body that breaks down the food so they can get the power they need to move and grow. Some animals eat only one kind of food. Other animals eat many kinds of food.

Animals have a brain. The brain is the part of the body that thinks. It helps the animal know what to do. The brain also helps the animal remember where it is, what it is doing, and who is around it.

Animals are living things. They grow as they live. They give babies the power they need to live. They help make the world a better place by eating the food that grows on the ground and in the water.
