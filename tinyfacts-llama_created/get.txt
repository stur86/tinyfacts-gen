To get something is to get it from one person to another. You might want to get a drink from a tall person. You go over to them and say, please give me a drink. The tall person smiles and hands you the drink.

Sometimes, you get a job. You do things that help a big building or a city run. You might work with a group of people to fix a road or clean the streets.

You can also get a small thing that you need. If you forget your keys at home, you might get a friend to help you get them. You write down the number of the friend and give it to them.

If you want to move to a new place, you get a job there. You work hard and learn the ways of the place. You become a part of the city.

You can also get a thing that you love. If you like a book, you might get it from a friend. You give the book to them and they are happy.

To get something can mean to take it away from someone. If someone is angry and wants to get back their stuff, they might fight with the other person. They try to take back what they think is their own.
