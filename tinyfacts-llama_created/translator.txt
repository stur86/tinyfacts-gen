A person who works with words is called a writer, or a person who writes. They take two different ways of saying something and put them into one way of saying it. This makes it easier for people from different places to understand each other.

Think of a book. The person who wrote the book must have talked to many people and taken notes on what they said. They might have also talked to other people who did not speak the same way. They put their words into a single line that all people can read.

If you want to talk to a person who speaks a different kind of word, you need a person who can help. This person is like a door between the two groups. They listen to what you say and tell the person you speak to the words you said.

There are many kinds of people who do this job. Some write stories, some write books, and some work with computers. They all help people from different places understand each other. Without them, we would not be able to talk to each other or write our own stories.
