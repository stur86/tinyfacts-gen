To hold a meeting is a way to talk and plan together. It is when many people sit around a table. They talk about a big problem. They listen to each other and try to find a way.

The word comes from a place where people go to talk. In the old days, people went to a big room in a house. They would sit at tables and talk about their day. Over time, the word became about talking with many people at the same time.

Sometimes, many people from far away are brought together to talk about a thing. They might write their ideas before the meeting. Then they all sit together and listen to each other. After, they talk and decide on a plan.

A meeting can be about many things. It might be about a work problem, a school problem, a family problem, or a big plan for the world. It is a way to share ideas and work together.
