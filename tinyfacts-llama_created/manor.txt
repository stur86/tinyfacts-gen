A man is a person with a large house. Many people live in houses like this. The house is often in a green field or a far place.

A man who works here is called a man. They often live with the family they work for. He might be the one who runs the house or helps the owner with work.

A small house is not as big. A man can live in this house with his wife and children if they have many. But a big house with many rooms is for a man who works many hours. He might have a big room to sleep in, a room for food, and a room for his work.

A man can have a house with a name. The name says who owns the house. If you see a big house with a big name, you can know who owns it.

A man can also have a small house that is very close to his house. He might walk there to get food or to meet friends. This is good for a man who has a job far away.
