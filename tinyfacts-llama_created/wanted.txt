To want something means you need it very much. You might want a book to read, a ball to play with, a friend to talk to, or a place to go. When you want something, you feel a strong feeling inside your heart.

Imagine you really need a drink after a long day. You could want the drink more than anything else. This is because your body is telling you, right now, it is very dry and it needs the drink to feel okay.

You can also want something that you do not have. You might want to go to a far away land. You want to see the bright blue sky, feel the warm sun on your face, and hear the sound of the water. You want to meet people who speak the same words as you.

Sometimes, you want something because you think it will help you. If you have a bad day and you want to feel better, you might want to watch a funny movie. You want to laugh and forget your worries.

Wanting something is like having a wish. It is when you want to be the way you want to be. It is a big feeling that makes you act.
