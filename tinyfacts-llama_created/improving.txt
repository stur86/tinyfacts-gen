Sometimes you get better by learning. You might listen to someone who is good at it. You watch them do it many times. You try to do it the same way, but with a small change. This helps you learn and become better.

You can also get better by doing things you like. If you play a game you like every day, you get better at it. You might feel more happy and less tired. This is a good thing. When you are happy and strong, you can do more things and have more fun.
