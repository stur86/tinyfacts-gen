A person who keeps things they find interesting is a person who is a good person. They often keep things they like to look at later. People buy things from stores or find them in the ground. Some people keep money they think will be good in the long run.

They also keep things that people write about. If you write a story, your mom will keep it. If a doctor writes something important, the hospital might keep it. A person who is a good person keeps things they found to remember the day.

Some people keep things that are old. They might keep a book that was given to them by a friend. They might keep a picture of a family they love. These things help them remember happy times.
