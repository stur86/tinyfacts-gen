To draw something is to make it. You use a thing that makes lines. You move the thing in your hand. The lines go across the paper. The lines are like the lines in a book. They tell what the thing looks like.

You can draw a house, a dog, a tree, or a person. You can also draw a picture of your family. You might draw a sun or a bright day. You can draw things that are very small or very big.

Sometimes you draw from your memory. You remember what you saw. You draw the lines of the things you saw. This can help you remember things later.

You can also draw things that you did not see. You might draw a picture of a story or a movie. You are using your own mind to make the picture.

To draw is to use your hands. It is to use your body. It is to show what you think.
