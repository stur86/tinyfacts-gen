To make something bigger is to make it grow. Imagine a small tree. This tree is small and not very tall. One day, you give it some water and it starts to grow. Its leaves get bigger and its wood gets thicker. This makes it taller and stronger. It is getting bigger.

You can also say a thing is getting bigger. If you keep eating a big bag of food, your body gets bigger. Your stomach gets bigger. This makes you feel bigger too. Your clothes might feel tight and your arms might feel heavy.

When people talk about money, they say it is getting bigger. If you save money for a long time, it grows. You have more money and you can buy more things. This makes you feel happy.

Sometimes, when you do something that makes you happy, you feel like you have more power. You feel like you can do more. You become stronger.

Sometimes, when a person does something wrong, they feel their power is being taken away. They feel they are not as strong as they thought they were.

To be growing means you are changing. You are not the same as you were before. You are becoming more. This is a good thing. It means you are getting ready for something new.
