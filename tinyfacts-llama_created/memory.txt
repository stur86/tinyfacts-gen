A memory is a place in your brain where you keep things you have seen or done. When you see a picture, hear a song, or meet a person, your brain stores that as a memory.

You can remember things from when you were very young. You remember your first day of school. You remember what your mom said on your  age. You remember places you have been.

You also have memories from when you were very old. You remember your mother dying. You remember your father getting a new job. These memories help you know what happened to you and how you feel.

Sometimes you can remember things from your past that you did not do. You might remember a song you heard, but you did not play it. You might remember a word, but you did not use it. Your brain is trying to help you understand why these things are there.

 Memories can also change. Sometimes you remember something from one time, but later you remember it was different. Your brain is still trying to figure out what happened.

You can also have memories that are not in your brain at all. You might remember something that happened to a friend, even if you never met them. You might remember a story that happened to you many years ago, even if you did not hear it when it happened.

Memories are important because they help us learn. If you do not remember something, you might not understand why it happened. And if you forget something important, you might feel confused and sad.

Sometimes people forget things they need to remember. They might forget their name or their phone number. They might forget what they did the day before. This can make it hard to remember important things.

But you can always try to remember. You can look at old pictures. You can listen to music. You can talk to someone who remembers the same things. And if you are very tired, you can try to remember what happened before you went to sleep.
