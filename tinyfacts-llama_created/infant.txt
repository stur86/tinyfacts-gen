An baby is a young child. They are not yet grown. A baby is very small. They have very little hair and their skin is soft and warm. A baby can not walk or run like a grown child. They need to be held all the time. A baby looks at you and smiles.

Babies are very good at learning. They see and hear everything. They listen to you when you speak and they try to do what you say. They also learn how to do things by watching. A baby might see a ball and then they pick it up. The baby learns how to use their hands.

Sometimes, a baby cries. They cry to show they are sad. They might cry when they are afraid or when they are hurt. A baby also cries a lot when they are very happy. Their cries can be very loud.

A baby needs food and drink every day. They eat from a bottle or from a cup. A baby also needs to be changed often. They need to be cleaned so their skin does not get red. A mother or father helps the baby with these things.

As a baby grows, they start to walk and run. They learn to talk and say words. They make sounds and words like a grown child. A baby is a very important time in a person life. It is a time of learning and growing.
