A run is a thing that you do while you are moving. You start at the beginning of a long way and keep going until you reach the end. If you run very fast, you go many times around the end of the way before you stop.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You both go down a long road. Your friend pushes you forward, but you move your feet. You go around many times. After a while, you feel tired. You need to stop and rest. This is what a run is. It is a time you spend on the road while you are moving.
