To have a kind heart is to have a good way with people. When you act kind, you show that you care. You do not want to hurt anyone. You help people who are in trouble. You are not afraid to be nice.

When a person is very kind, they can calm a fight. They do not want to use their power to make someone else afraid. They want everyone to feel safe. They make people happy.

A kind person does not think they are better than everyone else. They do not say, I am the best. They are happy to help. They know that everyone is important.

You can feel a kind way when you see a child who is scared. You might want to give them a big hug. You want them to feel safe. You are showing them that they are not alone.

When we say a person has a kind heart, it means they love everyone, even if they do not know them. It means they want people to feel happy. It means they are a good friend and a good person.
