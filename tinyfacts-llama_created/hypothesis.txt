To make an idea about something that is not sure yet.

When you think something is true, but you are not sure, you use this word. You might do this when you are trying to understand what happens. You try to guess what will happen next.

This word is like a guess. You take a few pieces of your idea and put them together. You look at how they work and how they feel. You try to see if your idea is right.

Sometimes, your idea is wrong. This happens when you see things that are not there. But you still try to learn from your guess. You might change your idea because you see a different way.

You can also make a guess about how things will be in the morning. You might guess that everything will be bright. But you are not sure. You might be wrong.

The word is important because it shows that you are trying to learn. You are not sure, but you are still trying. You are taking a step forward.
