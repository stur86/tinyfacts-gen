To laugh is a way you feel when you are very happy. When you laugh, your body gets a big group of tiny things that go around and around very fast. They go so fast that they make you shake your face and your chest and your arms. It is like you are moving all around your body.

Sometimes, you might laugh because something funny happens. Maybe you see a funny picture in a book. Or maybe you hear a funny sound. Or you might hear someone make a funny voice.

When you laugh, your face gets red. Your eyes are wide open, and you have to smile. You might be laughing so hard that you can not breathe. You might laugh even if you are not really funny. You might laugh because you are happy for the person you love.

People laugh to be happy, to feel safe, to feel loved. They laugh when they are with friends or when they see a good thing happen. They laugh when they are surprised or when they feel a big feeling.

But laughing can also make you feel sad. If you laugh too hard, you might cry. Your body starts to shake and your chest gets very heavy. You might feel a lot of pain inside your chest. So, it is very important to laugh in a way that feels good for you.
