To give food to someone means to give them something they need to live. When you are very tired, you need to eat so you can feel better. You go to a store and buy food. You then put the food in your bag and walk to your house.

When you work, you need to eat many times. You eat food from a box or from a person who makes food. You drink water or other drinks to stay strong. Food helps you feel happy and not sad.

You can also give food to animals. You might put food in a box for a dog or a cat. The animal will come back and eat the food. This helps keep the animal happy and the people who care for them safe.

In some places, you need to give food to people who are very sick. You might go to a hospital and give them food. The food helps them feel better so they can stay strong until they get better.
