To place something is to put it in a spot where it is safe and easy to find. When you look at a world on a big top round thing, you see many places in different areas of the world. Some are very high up in the sky, some are near the ground, and some are in the water.

You put a house on a piece of ground. You put a car on the ground. You put a book on a table. You put a picture on the wall. Every house is in a place. Every car is in a place. Every book is in a place. Every picture is in a place. Every thing is in a place.

You can also put things in a place that is not always the same. You can put a piece of paper in a bag and put the bag in a room. You can put a picture on a wall and put a desk in the room. You can put a book on a table and put a chair next to the table. This is like moving a thing from one place to another.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about where we live. If you say your place is your home, you mean where you live. If you say your place is your school, you mean where you learn. If you say your place is your city, you mean where your city is.

You can also say something is in a place. If you say a friend is in a place, you mean they live there. If you say a thing is in a place, you mean it is there. When we say something is in a place, we mean it is where it is.
