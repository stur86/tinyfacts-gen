To be or to be done with something means to have finished it. You can do it. You have finished a job at work. You have finished a game. You can also do it to a person. If a person is done with a thing, they can throw it away.

When you put a thing on a line or on a wall, you are putting it on a piece of long stuff. You use a strong part of the long stuff to hold the thing. This part is like a big hair on a dog. It holds the thing in place.

If something is not done, it is still there. It is in the room. It is not gone. It is just waiting. You can wait for a friend to come home. You can wait for a food to come out of the kitchen. You can wait for the sun to go down.

Being done with something can be a good thing. It means the job is done. It means you can rest. It means you are free. It can also mean the end of a time. If you finish a book, you can stop reading. You have done it.
