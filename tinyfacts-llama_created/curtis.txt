To cut something means you make it smaller. You can cut a piece of wood to make it straight or you can cut a piece of paper to make a long line. When you cut a person, you are cutting their part of their body so you can see or get to their face or their hair.

A person who cuts is called a cutter. They might work with a power thing to cut wood. They also use a small thing to cut hair. If someone is very good at cutting, they can make a small piece of paper out of a big piece of paper.

Sometimes people cut their hair for a reason. If they have long hair and their hair falls down their back, they might cut it short. This helps them look clean and make them feel better. They might cut their hair to a short, round top or to a long top with a round bottom.
