A line that shows how things change is called a line. A line is a long piece of paper that you can hold in your hand. It goes from one place to another.

Imagine you are walking through a big city. You start at one end of the city and walk to the other end. You follow this line. As you walk, you see things you have never seen before. You see tall houses and small houses, green trees and red houses. You see streets that go in different directions.

You can also use a line to show how many things are in a group. If you have many books, you can make a line. You start at one book and walk to the next. You keep going until you reach the end. This line tells you how many books you have.

Sometimes, a line is a way to show how things are different from one place to another. If you go from a cold place to a warm place, you are going down a line. The line goes from one place with cold water to the other place with warm water.

A table is a long piece of wood with lines on it. These lines help people see how many people are in a group. You can use these lines to know how many people are in a room or how many people are in a group of animals.

A picture is a way to show how something looks. You can make a picture of a person, a dog, a house, or even a city. The lines on the picture help people see the colors, the way the light is falling, and the way the things look.

When you talk about a line of work, you mean a group of people who do the same thing. They follow the same steps and do the same things every day. They might work on computers, build houses, or drive cars. This line helps people know what to do and how to do it.
