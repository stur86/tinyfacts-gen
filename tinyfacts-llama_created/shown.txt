To show something is to let it be seen. When you show something, you make it easy to see. You might show a picture of a cat or a dog. You can show a small thing, like a ball or a book, so that others can see it.

You can show things in many ways. You can point at a thing. You can make a face and say, like, you see a cat. You can use your hands to show a thing. You can hold a thing up and say, like, I have a ball.

You can also show things to people. You can talk to a friend about something and let them know. You can show a movie to your family so they can watch it together. You can show a book to a child so they can read it.

To show something can also mean to let a person know how you feel. If you are sad, you might show your friend that you are sad. You might cry. You might say, like, I am very sad. Your words show how you are feeling.

Sometimes, you show something and no one notices. You might be in a room with many people, and you are sitting quietly. But you are still showing something. You are making the room feel quieter because you are not making a sound.

Showing is an important way to act. It helps people understand each other. It helps us share our ideas and our feeling with others.
