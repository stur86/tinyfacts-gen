A group of things that work together is what we call a group. Many people are in this group. They do the same job or play the same game. They help each other out.

Think of a box. You might have many boxes. Each box is one thing. But together they are many things. You can put them all together and you have a big box that can hold many small boxes.

We also have a group of people who work together to build a wall. Many people help. Some build the wall high. Others put the pieces together. If everyone did their job, the wall would be strong.

In a group, you can be the leader. The leader helps the other people do their work. You tell them what to do and how to do it. You also help them if they need it.

Sometimes, a group has many different kinds of things. Each person has a job. They do what they are good at. This is a good way to work together. Everyone knows their job, and everyone is important.

In a school, there are many groups of students. Some groups are for reading books. Others are for doing games. Everyone works together and learns from each other.
