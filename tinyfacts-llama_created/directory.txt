A computer or a phone has many places where things are kept. One of those places is where you look for things. You look for a word or a number, and the computer finds it. The computer then shows you the things that have that word or number. 

Think of a room. In that room, there are many boxes with books. You can look at a book if you know where the book is. The room has a way of telling you where everything is. It is like a big book. You can walk around the room and find what you are looking for. 

A computer has something like that. It keeps many places. One place is where it finds the things you ask it to. It is like a big book. It can tell you where to look for a word or a number. It can help you find things quickly. This helps you to work, play games, and talk with others.
