To be let go is a thing that happens when someone stops holding you. You have been in a place or a room for a long time. You are doing something, but you want to go back home.

Imagine you have a box with a small animal inside. You want to keep it for a long time, but you also want to see it run around your room. You can hold it and look at it, but you are not letting it go. You are holding it too tight.

One day you decide it is time to let the box go. You open the door and walk out. The box falls down the steps and flies away. You feel happy because you are finally free.

People use this word to talk about many different things. You can say a person is let go from their job because they did not do their work. You can say a person is let go from a game because they did not follow the right way. You can also say a person is let go from a movie because they did not like it.

It is also a way to talk about the end of a story. When a story is over, the people who were in it are let go. The people who lived in the story are free to go home. The story ends, and the story is closed.
