Cats are animals. They are very good at hiding. They can sleep a lot and only get up to eat. They have soft further than you would think. They like to be near people, but they also like their own space. Some cats are very small. Others are very big. They all have one thing in their heads. They want to be happy.

Some people are called cats. This is not because they are cats. These people are people who follow a way of life. They believe in one big god. They do not believe in many small gods. They also believe that a man should be the one to tell everyone else what to do. This way of life is called a cat. People follow a cat if they want to live by the same way.

A cat is also a word from a book. In this book, people use it to talk about a man who tells stories. He was the one who was not afraid of anything. He told stories of far away lands. People liked to read this book because it made them feel safe.

Today, you might see a cat in a house. You might see a cat in a car. You might see a cat in a park. Cats like to be near people. They like to see what they are doing. They like to see if you are a good person.
