To rush is to move very fast. You might run from one house to another, or you might rush to get to a meeting before it starts. This means you are moving quickly and doing many steps at once.

You can also rush a person. If a person is very angry, they might rush at a bad person. This is because they are not calm and are ready to fight.

Sometimes, when you rush, you might forget to think. You might act without stopping to see what you are doing. This can cause problems. If you rush while driving, you might not see a car coming. If you rush while walking, you might step on a rock.

Rushing can make things feel fast. It can make your heart beat very quickly. It can make your face feel hot. But it can also make you feel nervous and worried. You might feel like you are in a hurry and do not have time to think.

To relax, it is good to slow down. If you are rushing, try to stop and take a deep breath. You can close your eyes and focus on your body. This can help you feel calm and safe.
