To close something means to shut it up. You can close doors, windows, or boxes. When you close a door, you make it go away. This is a way to keep the things inside.

Think of it like a person. If a person is talking and the other person wants to stop, the person closes their mouth. They stop talking and listen to the other person. This is how they close a conversation.

You can also close things that are made to hold things together. A book has a cover. When you close the book, you shut the cover around the cover. This keeps the words inside the book safe.

In a way, closing is about letting go. When something is closed, it is not open anymore. It is in a new state. Think of a door closing. It is not going out anymore. It is going into a new room.
