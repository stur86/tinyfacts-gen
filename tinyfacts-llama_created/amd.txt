An arm is a part of the body. It is a long thing that comes out from your shoulder. You use it to move your hand. You can lift things or push a car. The arm is very good at letting you do these things.

Your arm is made of many parts. The top part is hard and strong. This part holds your hand in place. The middle part is where your hand moves. It is like a soft, round bag. When you want to pick something, your hand goes into the bag and then comes out again. This is how you hold onto a thing.

The bottom part of the arm is very small. It has lines and a round part. This part helps your hand move. You can use it to write or draw pictures.

Your arm is also good at letting you move. When you want to walk, you use your arms to push the ground. Your arms help you stay standing.

You have two arms. Most people have two. One person might have one arm and one foot. This can happen because of a very bad thing that happened when they were very young.
