To have a number as a part of a group means you are a part of many things. It is about how many parts you are in one group of things.

Think of a group of friends. Some friends like to play with the same game. Some friends like to play with different games. If you are one of those friends who like the same game, you have a fact about your friends. You know how many friends you have in this group.

A fact can also be about something that is in you. If you have a fact about your body, it means you have a fact about your heart and your brain. Your heart has a fact about how much blood it takes in each minute. Your brain has a fact about how many words you can remember.

When you look at many of these facts, you see how many parts you are in many groups. You see how many facts you have about your friends and how many facts you have about your body.

This word is important for many reasons. It helps us see how many things are in one big group. It helps us see how many different ways there are. It helps us understand how the world works.
