To trouble someone means to cause them a lot of worry or pain. It is when you do something that makes them feel very bad. You might do this because you are angry, or you might do it to make them angry. You are making them feel very bad.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You are having a good time. But then you do something that makes the other person very angry. They get very mad. You are causing them a great deal of trouble. This is what trouble means.

Sometimes, trouble can also mean something that is hard to fix. If you break something, you have trouble fixing it. You need to find a way to make it work again. Or if you do not do something, you might have trouble. You might have to go to a place where you are not supposed to be.

You can also trouble someone who is not doing a good job. If a teacher is not giving the right answers, or if a parents child is not following the house orders, this is trouble. You are making them feel a lot of worry.
