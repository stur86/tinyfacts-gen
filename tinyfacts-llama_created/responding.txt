When you hear a voice or a sound, your brain tries to understand what it means. You might feel a bit confused or think that the sound is just noise. To respond means you are the one doing the talking. You are the one who says something back to the person who made the sound.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are both laughing and having fun. If you want to tell your friend, you must say something. If you say the word, you are responding. You are answering the question the other person asked.

You can also respond to a question. If someone asks you a question, you must answer. This is like being the one who talks. You are saying what you think about the question.

Sometimes, you respond to a sound. If a dog bags, you might feel a bit surprised or excited. You are the one doing the talking because you are the one who says, well, you saw the dog!
