An animal is a living thing that grows from a tiny part of a parents body. They do not use power like a car to get from one place to another. They move around on the ground or in the water.

Animals have a body that is made of many parts. Each part is important for the animal to stay living. They have a head with eyes and a mouth. They breathe air. They can hear and see.

Many animals have hair or other cover that keeps them warm. Some animals have long hair that they use to catch food. Some animals can change color to hide from other animals.

Animals need to eat. They drink water or eat food that grows on the ground or in the water. They also need to stay away from things that might hurt them.

Animals are very different from people. They do not have hands. They use their feet, legs, and sometimes their heads to move around. Some animals can fly, while others can only move on the ground.

Some animals live in the ground. Some animals live in the air. Some animals live in water. Some animals live in the ground and in the water at the same time.

Animals are important for our world. They help us by giving us food and making the world feel like a big place. They also help us by keeping the air clean and making the ground nice to walk on.
