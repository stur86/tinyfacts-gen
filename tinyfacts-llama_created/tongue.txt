The word tongue is about a part of your body. It is long and small. Your head has many parts, and this part is very important. It is near your mouth. When you talk, you use it. You use it to make sounds. When you want to eat, you use it to pick food. You move it up and down in your mouth. This helps you understand what you are eating.

You also use your tongue to feel things. You press the ground with it. This helps you know if something is hard or soft. It can also tell you if a thing is sweet or not. It is very good at this job.

Your tongue is also important when you drink a cold drink. You put the drink in your mouth and your tongue goes around it. It helps the drink go down into your body. This is why your tongue feels warm when you drink something cold.

Sometimes, people use the word tongue in other ways. They might say it is your tongue or they are using it. This can mean they are talking about your body or using your words.
