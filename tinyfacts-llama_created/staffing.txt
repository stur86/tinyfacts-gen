To fill a job means to add people to a place where work happens. This can be a school, a store, a house, or a building. Many jobs need help because there are too many people and not enough jobs to do everything.

Think of a busy road. Cars go on the road. People need to be there too. If there are too many cars and not enough roads, the cars might not reach their places. This is what happens when you have too many people working at one job and not enough jobs.

People fill jobs by moving to the place. They get a job that they like. They pay money to work there. They work with other people who do the same job. Together, they do many different jobs to keep the place running.

If a place needs more people than it has, it might ask for help. This is called a call. The place will tell the people who are already there to move to another place. The people might move to a different part of the city, to a far away town, or even to another state. This is a way to get more people to help.
