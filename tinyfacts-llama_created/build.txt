To build something means you make it from the ground up. You start with small pieces and then you add more and more pieces until it is as tall as you want it to be. You use your hands, a strong thing that can lift heavy things, and your brain to decide what you want it to look like.

You can build a house, a car, a tree house, a house for animals, or anything else you can imagine. You use wood, rock, or other strong stuff to make the walls and the floor. You put up the walls and the floor, then you put the big pieces of the room on top of them. When everything is done, you have a house.

You can also build a road. A road goes from one place to another. You lay down many long pieces of hard stuff. When you are all done, there is a long line of hard stuff that goes from one city to another.

Sometimes, we build things that are not for living. We build cars for people to get from city to city. We build big things that go up into the sky. We build things that help us breathe and keep us safe.

When you build something, you show that you have the power to change the world. You are the one who can make a new thing, or who can build something new that will be around for a long time.
