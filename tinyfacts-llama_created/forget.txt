To forget something means you forget to remember it. This happens when you do not think about it anymore. You might not remember where you put your keys or what you did the day before.

Imagine you are in a room with a book. You were reading it before, but then you left the room. The next morning, you look at the book and you do not see the words anymore. You have forgotten the words.

You can forget a thing in many ways. You might forget a name of a friend. You might forget where you went on a trip. Or you might forget to buy something. This happens because your brain gets busy with other things.

Sometimes, you forget something important. You might remember to eat dinner, but then you forget to eat. This can make you feel very sick. Your body is not getting the food you need, and this can hurt your body.

You can also forget things that are not important. You might forget to do a job. You might forget to answer a question from someone. Or you might forget a word you learned. This does not feel as bad as forgetting something important, but it is still important to remember.

To remember something is to keep it in your mind. You can remember things from the past, things you saw, or things you heard. You can also remember things from your memory. If you remember something, you can use it later. You can use it to learn new things or to help you in your life.
