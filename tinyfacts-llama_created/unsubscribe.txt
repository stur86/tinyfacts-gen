When you sign up for something, you give the person the right to tell you when they need to know about new things. You might sign up to get a letter from a store every week. You also sign up to get a phone call. This is called an order.

But sometimes you want to stop getting those things. You might say, I want to stop getting a letter from that store. You are telling them not to send the letter anymore. This is called an understand.

If you want to stop getting a phone call, you are saying, I do not want to hear your voice anymore. You are telling the person that you are done. This is called an until.

You can also say you want to stop an order. This is when you tell the person you are done with giving them things you want. It is like you are telling them to stop sending you a thing.

This word is very good to use when you do not want to hear about things anymore. It helps you stop getting things you do not need. It is a good way to say, I am done with that.
