A fight is when two people or animals try to be the best. They might use things they have to hurt each other. A person might hit you with a stick. An animal might use its teeth.

Sometimes, people go to a place where they know each other. They fight there because they want to control that place. Sometimes, people fight because they have a lot of money and want to be the one who owns it.

In a fight, you might hear a lot of noise. You might see people or animals moving fast. You might feel a lot of fear. But you have to stay calm and do what you think is right. If you are strong and fast, you might be the one who gets the best.

Sometimes, people fight with a word. They might say things they do not mean. This is not a good way to act. It can cause a lot of pain.

There is also a word for when many people fight at the same time. This is called a big fight. It can be very loud and make it hard to hear anything else. Sometimes, the fighting can go on for many hours or even days.
