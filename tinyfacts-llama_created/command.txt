A word is something that you say or do. You can use it to tell someone what to do. You can also give someone a word that says what to do.

Think about a child. If you tell a child to be quiet, you give them a word. They listen because they want to stay safe. If you give them a word to be a good boy, they follow it because they feel happy.

A leader is a person who gives orders. They pick who should do things. They might tell some people to go to a place, or some people to buy food. The person in the middle of the group follows their word. If they say, people should work hard, everyone works.

If you give a dog a word, you tell it what to do. You might say, go out or come back inside. The dog follows because it wants to be safe.

Sometimes, you give a word to a person who is very old or very tired. You give them a word to rest. They might feel very sad because they do not want to be alone.

You can also give a word to a group of people. You tell them, let us work together and find a way. They work as one team and find a good way. The word helps them understand what they need to do.

In a big group of people, you might give a word. You might say, let us talk and listen. Everyone listens because they want to be heard. The word helps them stay calm and talk to each other.

So, a word is a thing you say or do. It helps you tell people what to do. It can be a word that tells a child to be quiet or a word that tells a leader to pick who should do things.
