To follow someone or something is to try to catch up with them. It is about being very interested in what they are doing or what they want to become.

Imagine you see a person running in a park. You want to catch up with them. You start running after them. You are following them. If you are very fast, you might even catch up to them. If you are not as fast, they might get away.

Sometimes, we follow people who are doing something we do not want to do. This can be hard because it makes us feel like we are doing something wrong. But following someone who is doing something good can also be a good thing. It shows that you care about them.

You can also follow a place or a thing that you love. You might follow a beautiful city or a long road to a far away place. You want to see it and know everything about it.

Following can be about many things. You might follow a game to watch your favorite team play. You follow a book to find out what happens next. You follow a friend to see them at a party.
