A shoe is a thing you wear on your feet. It is a thing that keeps your feet dry and safe. It has a bottom part that goes around your foot. There is also a part that goes over your foot. This part is where you put your foot into the shoe. People wear shoes for many reasons.

Some shoes are very hard. These are for people who run a lot. They are hard so they do not break easily. Other shoes are very soft. These are for people who like to walk a lot. They are soft so they do not hurt your feet.

Shoes can be made from many different things. Some are made from hard stuff like wood. Others are made from soft stuff like soft brown things. Some shoes are made from things that look like animals. These are often for kids.

You can buy shoes at stores that have clothes. You can also buy them from people who make them. Some people make shoes by hand. These shoes are very nice. You can buy shoes that are very old, too. These shoes can tell you a lot about the person who made them.

Even animals have shoes! A horse has a shoe on one of its feet. It helps the horse move around. Some dogs have shoes too. They help the dog walk and run.
