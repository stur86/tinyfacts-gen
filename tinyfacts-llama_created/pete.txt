To keep a person is to take care of them. This is how you do things when you are a father or a mother. You do not let them run around all day. You keep them safe and make sure they eat. You also do things for them when they need help.

When you keep a person, you give them love. You make them feel safe. You are there when they are sad or angry. You help them feel better. This is a good thing to do.

Sometimes, you keep a thing. You keep a book or a dog. You do not throw it away. You want to keep it so it can live a long time. You want to show it to your children when they are old.

But keeping a person or a thing is different from playing with them. When you play, you have fun. You make things happen. You are the one who moves the things. You do not keep them. You let them go. This is how you play with a ball or a computer.

So, to keep someone means to take care of them or to keep a thing. It is about being kind to others.
