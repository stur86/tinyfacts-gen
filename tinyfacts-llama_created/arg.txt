To ask a question is to show you are not sure. When you ask a question, you are looking for the right answer.

When you ask a question, you are using words. You use words that start with a different sound than the words you are using to talk. This makes your question different from your usual talk. It is like a break in your words.

Sometimes you ask a question because you want to know more. You might want to know why something happened or who did something. You are trying to find out.

You can also ask a question if you do not know. If you are not sure about a thing, you can ask it. This is a way to say you do not know.

A person can ask a question in many ways. They can ask in their head, they can shout it out, or they can write it down. They might even ask a question to someone else, so they can learn too.

When you ask a question, you are taking the first step in learning. You are showing that you care about what is going on around you.
