Soon is a word you use when you want something to happen very quickly. It means that the thing will happen before your time is over.

You can use it to talk about the day after today. Maybe you have a school day tomorrow. You might say, soon we will leave. This means you will leave tomorrow.

You can also use it to talk about time. If you have a big party tonight, you might say, soon it will be over. This means the party will end soon.

Sometimes, you might say soon because you are waiting for something. Maybe you are waiting for a friend to call you. You can say, soon you will call.

You can also use it to talk about time with a number. If you want to know when it will be, you can say, soon it will be five. This means five minutes from now.

The word soon is very important. It helps us know how far time is and when things will happen.
