An easy way to say this is: a thing that can send power to a house. Some things that do this are very big and heavy. They have many hard parts that move around a round thing inside them. When the power goes into the house, it makes the lights, the air that you breathe, and the computers work.

Some things are smaller. They might only be a few feet tall and a few feet wide. They sit on your table or in your room. They give you power so you can read, watch movies, or play music. They also help when you do things with your hands, like write or draw.

You need to be carefully put these things in your home. The power inside them can hurt you if you touch them too many times. But if you follow the steps that the people who make them give you, you can stay safe.

These things are a big part of how we live today. They make our lives easier and help us do many things we could not do without them.
