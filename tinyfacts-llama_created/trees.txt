A tree is a big living thing that grows from the ground. It can be tall and strong, or small and round. They have a wide, round top where the big parts of the tree grow. This top has many small parts that hang down from it. The parts on the top are very important for the tree. They help it stay up in the air and give it food from the sun, air, water, and ground.

Trees make their own food. They take air and water from the air and ground and turn it into a drink that gives them power. They also take tiny pieces of green from the ground and break them down. This food gives them the power to grow and stay strong. Some trees can live for many years. Others can live for just a few months.

Trees help keep the air clean. They use tiny things that go into the air to break down bad things that come from the ground. They also help keep the ground clean. They keep the ground from getting too wet by holding water in their wide tops. This keeps the ground from becoming a home for many kinds of tiny animals.

Trees give us many things. They give us wood for houses, tables, and chairs. They give us paper from their big round parts. They give us food in the form of sweet things that grow on the tree. They give us homes for many kinds of animals. They give us a calm and quiet place to sit and think. They help keep our world clean and strong.

Trees are very important. They help keep our world going. They help keep the air clean and the ground clean. They give us food and many other things. They are very good living things.
