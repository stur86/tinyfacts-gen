A tabling is a word that is used to talk about a thing. It is a word that you use when you want to show that a thing is ready to do something.

To table means to wait. You might say, you are tabling for the game. This means you are waiting for the game to start. You are ready.

You can also table for a small thing. If you have a small book you want to read, you might say you are tabling it. This means you are putting it down and not looking at it for a little while.

Sometimes a tabling is used for the things you use to write. You might put a paper down and then put a paper on top. This is like a tabling. You are putting one paper on top of another.
