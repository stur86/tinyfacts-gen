When you press something, you push on it with your hand. This makes the thing move or change. It can make a book open or a door close.

If you press a door, you are making it shut. If you press a hand, you are making your fingers hard. This is how you can make things happen in your world.

Sometimes, you press a thing that you eat. You put food on your teeth and then you take a bite. The food is inside the thing, and as you bite it, the food is pressed into your body. This is how you get the power you need to move and to stay strong.

In a car, you press the big round thing. This makes the car move. It is like a big push that makes the car go around a round road.
