To old is to say that something is old. It is like saying a person is old. You might use it when you are talking about a thing that has been around for a very long time.

Think of a small box you found in your room. It has been there for many years. You do not know how old it is. You can not tell how many years it is, but you know it is an old thing. You might say, the old box is older than me.

You can also use this word for people. A man who has lived for many years is an older man. A woman who has lived for many years is an older woman.

Sometimes, you use this word when you are talking about a house. If the house has been there for many years, it is old. You do not think it is very new anymore. It has seen many people come and go.

You can also say that something is old because it has been around a very long time. A book that has been read many times is old. It has seen many people reading it. It is not new anymore.

The word also has a funny side. When you want to say you are old, you might say you are older than them. It is like saying you are better than them. But if you say you are old, you are not better. You are just very old.
