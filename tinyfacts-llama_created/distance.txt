To see a far point is to be very far away. It is when you can not see a thing at all. If you are standing right in front of something, the thing is right in front of you. If you walk a little, the thing might be behind you. If you walk a very long way, the thing will be far away on the other side of the world.

You can also see the world around you. If you look at the sky, you see the blue of the sky and the red of the sun. If you look at the ground, you see the green of the trees and the brown of the ground. You are seeing the world. You are not seeing a far point, but you are seeing your own world.

A long time ago, people did not have a word for this. They would say they were in a place or they were in a city. They did not have a way to tell how far they were from the big round thing in the sky. Now we have a word that means this.
