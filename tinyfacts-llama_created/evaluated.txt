To be understood means to know what a word, a story, a picture, a number, or a thing is about. You must be able to understand the meaning.

Imagine you see a picture of a cat. You do not know what the cat looks like, how it acts, or why it is there. But you can tell how the cat looks and how it moves. You have understood the picture.

In a different way, you might see a person who is very sad. You do not know how they are feeling or why they are sad. You do not know their story. You have not understood them. You do not know them at all.

If a group of people say a thing is true, they are saying it is understood. If you understand what they say, you can use that to decide what to do. If you do not understand, you do not know, and you can not act.

A person may say something that is not true. They might lie. They might say a story that is not real. If you are not sure, you do not know if the person is right or wrong.

A thing can also be understood. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You do not know the game. But then you see how they play. You see that they are moving a ball and hitting a ball. You understand the game now. You can play the game with them later.

To be understood is to know what is happening and what is right. It is to have a clear idea.
