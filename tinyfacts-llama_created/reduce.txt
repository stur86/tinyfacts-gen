To begin with, a word can be a thing, a person, or a place. When you talk about things, you usually want to make them better. You might want to make them less heavy, or less hot, or less cold. You do this by taking away some of them.

Imagine you have a big bag of food. You eat a lot, and now the bag is empty. To make it better, you can take some of the food out. You can take only a few pieces, and then you feel better. This is what we call to go down. When you go down, you are not going away. You are still there, but you have less of what you started with.

In the same way, if you are angry, you might want to calm down. You can talk to a friend, or you can go for a walk. This is to go down. You are still the same person, but you are not as angry. You are still you, but you are in a better place.

Sometimes, people do this with money. If you have a lot of money, you can spend some of it. This does not make you go away. You still have the money, and you still have the same person. You just have more of it.

To end, to go down is to make something less strong. It is to take away some power. This happens when you lose a fight, or when you lose a game. You are not the same person anymore. You have less power, and you feel different.
