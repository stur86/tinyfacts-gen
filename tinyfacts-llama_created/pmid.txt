To look at something very fast when you are not looking at it. You might look at the time on your phone. You see how many minutes have passed. In a short time, you have looked at the time many times. You see how the numbers change. You might see that the minutes are going very quickly.

You can do this with papers. You write a name, then you look at the papers again. The papers might be in a big box. You look at them very quickly. You see who wrote the name and when they did it. This is a way to see the past very fast.
