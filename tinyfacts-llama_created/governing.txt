To be a good leader means you help many people and make sure they are safe and happy. You do this by following a set of ideas that everyone agrees on. It is like being the one in the middle of a group of people.

Imagine you are the one who decides what game to play at a big school. You decide which game is fun, and everyone follows you. You are the one who makes sure everyone has fun and that the game starts and ends at the right time. You are also the one who makes sure everyone follows the same set of ideas about how to play.

A leader can be a person, a group of people, or even a computer. They can be in a place where people give orders, like a high school, a city, or a big building. They can also be in a group that works on a problem, like a team of people who want to build a new road.
