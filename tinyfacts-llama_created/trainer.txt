A person who trains other people is called a teacher. They help them learn new things. They show them how to do things right. They also give them small jobs to do. This makes them better at what they do.

A person who trains animals is called a handler. They help the animal feel safe. They give it food and water. They help it move so it is not afraid of people. They also teacher the animal how to do things like sit or stay.

A person who trains things for money is called a worker. They make the things. They fix them if they are broken. They also make new things. They work with other people to make sure everything is done right.

A person who trains the mind is called a person who leads. They help other people see things. They tell them what to do. They make sure everyone follows the same way.
