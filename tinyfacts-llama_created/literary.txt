To write something in a way that people read and understand is to be a writer. When a writer writes, they use words to make a story, a picture, or a feeling. They decide how to make the words sound and how to use the words to show a story. A writer might use words that are short or words that are long. They might use words that are bright or words that are quiet.

A writer can write about anything they like. They can write about a story from their life, a story from someone else, or a story from a far away place. They might write about a person who is happy, a person who is sad, or a person who does something they do not want to do. They might write about a time when they were small and learned to play, or a time when they were very old and remembered their life.

Some people write for fun. They write stories that are not about real people. These stories might be about a world that is not our world. They might have animals, spaces, or other things that are not real. Other people write for work. They write books or papers for a job they do every day.

Some people also write for money. They write stories that people want to read. They write about places, people, and things that are not real. They write stories that are like a movie or a show on a computer. They write stories that make people laugh, cry, or feel something else.

A writer is a person who makes stories with their words. They use their brain to come up with ideas and to put those ideas into words. They work with their hands to make the words look right and to make the story feel real.
