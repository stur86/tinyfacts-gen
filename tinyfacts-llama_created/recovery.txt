To get well again is to feel better when you were sick or hurt. This happens when you are not in pain anymore and can do things again.

You might have been in a room for a very long time because you were very sick. Your body was not working as well as it should be. You might have felt very tired and had a lot of pain. One day, something changed. Your body started to work again. You were not as sick anymore. You could stand up and walk. Your hair might have been falling out if you had been in the dark for too long.

A person can get well in many ways. They might get better from a cold. They might have a big cut on their hand and get it all better. They might lose their voice and then get it back again. They might have a big fight and feel very angry, but then they calm down and feel better.

You can also get well after a hard time. You might have been afraid to leave your home for many days. You might have eaten a lot of food but not wanted to eat at all. You might have felt very sad. But then you went out to walk the streets. You saw people and felt the sun on your face. You started to feel happy again. You felt like you were going back to the way you were before.

Sometimes, you just need to wait. You might have a cold and you do not feel better. You might be feeling very sad and tired. But after a few days, you start to feel a little better. Your body starts to work again. You start to feel happy again. You are getting well.
