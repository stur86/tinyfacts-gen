To be stuck means you are holding something because it is very hard to let go. You might use a paper thing to keep your hand warm. You press it against your skin for a long time because it is cold.

Sometimes you are stuck to a chair. You are not moving because you have no other way to go. You are holding the chair because you are afraid to leave it. You might be stuck to a chair because you are afraid of the dark. You stay in the room because you are not ready to go home.

You can also be stuck to a person. If you talk to someone for a long time, you can become their friend. You feel like you know them very well. You might feel happy when you talk to them. You are not afraid to be around them anymore.

In school, you might use a piece of paper to write a word. If you write something wrong, the paper gets stuck to your hand. The paper does not want to come off. It is holding onto your hand because it does not want to fall.
