When you fill something, you put things inside it until it is full. This can happen with many things. You can fill a bottle with water, a cup with food, or a room with people. When you fill something, you make it bigger or bigger. This makes the room or the bottle or the cup have more space inside.

You can also fill something with air. When you put a small ball into a bottle, you are filling it with air. The air makes the ball go up and down. This helps the ball move through the air. If you fill the bottle with too much air, the ball might burst.

Sometimes you fill something with things you do not want. You might put a book you do not like into a bag. You put the book inside the bag to keep it safe. This keeps the bag full.

You can also fill a place with people. If a town is full, there are many more people than the town can hold. Then they go to another town. This makes the town full again.

When you fill something, you make it ready for a new time. You might fill a car with gasped air so it can go far. You fill a box with things for a child so the child can play.
