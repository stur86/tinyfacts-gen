To pause something means to stop it for a short time. If you are talking to a friend, you might pause to think about what you want to say. If a movie is ending, the person doing the talking might pause so you can think about what happens next.

When you pause a word, you are taking a moment to understand it. This can help you better understand what the person is saying. It is like taking a deep breath and waiting for the right moment to continue.

In a game, a player might pause to think if they should hit the ball. They might decide to wait until the right moment before they act.

Sometimes, we pause because we are feeling sad or angry. We might sit down and cry for a while before we feel better. We might also pause when we are trying to decide what to do. We might stop and think about our own needs before we decide what to do next.
