To put something out means you send it to someone or something else. You put it on the street, in a box, or in the air. It can be a word, a picture, a sound, or even a tiny bit of air.

Imagine you want to tell a story to a friend. You sit down and write down what you want to say. Then, you use a computer to type the words. After you finish, you type the words and send them to your friend through the air. Your friend reads the words and listens. They can even see the words if they watch you type.

When you put something out, you are giving it to others. You are not holding it in your hands anymore. You let it go into the world so others can use it. Think about how important it is when you put out a word. A word can change what people think and how they feel.
