A thing that is not allowed can be a number, a word, a place, a time, a thing you do, or a way of thinking. It is a way to stay safe.

When you say something is a thing that is not allowed, you mean that you are not allowed to do it. You might not be allowed to enter a school building after hours, or you might not be allowed to carry a gun in a room.

Think about a time when you wanted to have a fun day with your friends. You might want to play games, watch a movie, or run around. But your parents might have said, you must stay inside and do your school work. In this case, the time is a thing that is not allowed.

A thing that is not allowed can also be a way of being. Imagine you are afraid of the dark. You might say, I am not allowed to go to bed at night. This means you are afraid, so you stay in a different room.

Things that are not allowed can make life more interesting. If a game has a time to finish, you must play it until it is done. This makes you feel like you are working hard.
