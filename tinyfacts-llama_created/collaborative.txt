To work together is to share ideas and use each person to help the group. When many people work together, they can do more than one thing at a time.

Imagine a big room where many people sit and talk. They might be talking about a big problem. One person might say a word that is not right. The others might say, no, we want to say that word is wrong. This way, everyone gets to feel heard. This helps everyone understand each other.

You can also work together in a group at school. You might be doing a big job with your friends. If one person does something wrong, the others can help fix it. They work together to get the job done.

A team is a group of people who all work together. They might work in a big place, like a store or a hospital. They might work on a big plan. They share their ideas so everyone is on the same team.
