To say something is often means you are not sure if it is true. This word helps us talk about things that happen very little.

If you say a dog is often happy, it means that dog usually feels happy. This does not mean the dog is always happy. There are times when dog looks very sad or angry. But it is most of the time happy.

If you say a person is often late, it means they usually do not arrive on time. This does not mean they are always late. There are times when they arrive right on time. But it is usually their body that is late.

You can also say a thing that happens is often. This means the thing happens very often. It can be the sun coming up in the morning or a person eating a very big number of food.
