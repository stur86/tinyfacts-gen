Corners are hard places on a room or a building. They are the parts where the walls meet. When you look at a room, you can see that the walls are all around you. There are many corners, and each corner has two sides.

When you walk, you walk around these hard places. You have to be carefully so you don't hit your head. If you step on a corner, you might fall down. This is why people often walk around corners with their eyes on the ground.

On a car, there are many corners. The front of the car has many turns. The back of the car also has many turns. When you drive, you need to be very carefully so you do not hit the other cars or the things on the road.

Corners are also where people sit. In a room, you might see a group of people sitting on the floor. This is often in a room with chairs and tables. The people are leaning against each other, and they are looking at a picture or a story.

To find a corner, you look for the place where the wall and the floor meet. You might see a line on the floor. This line is where the wall and the floor touch. You can stand against the wall to find the corner.
