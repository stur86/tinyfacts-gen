To point something means to show it to others.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You throw a ball to them. They catch it and say, I did it. This is pointing. You are telling them that you did something.

You can also point at a thing in a room. You might want to say, Look at that book! You are showing them where to look.

In a game, you might use a small thing to point at a high place. You are telling the other people where to go. This helps everyone understand what you want.

In a different way, if you are walking down a street, you might point at a door. This means you are saying, I am going into that house.

Points can also mean the end of a thing. If you build a house, the top part is a point. It is the highest part of the house.

Sometimes, we point at things that are far away. If you see a far dog, you might point it out to your friend. This helps everyone see it.

When you are talking, you might point at a word. You are asking someone to remember that word. This helps you remember later.

In this world, we use points all the time. We point at computers to show we are interested. We point at things when we want them. We even use points to show who is better in a game.
