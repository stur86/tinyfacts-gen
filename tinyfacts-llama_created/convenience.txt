To be easy is to be not hard. It is when you do not have to work as much. When you do not have to walk a long way or do a lot of work, you feel less tired and you can do more.

If you have a friend who lives far away, you can talk to them at any time. You do not have to go to their house or wait for them to come back. You can do things with them while you are still in your bed. You can watch a movie, play a game, or read a book all at once. This makes life easier.

A thing that is easy is also one that is small. If you have a small bag to carry your things, you do not have to carry a big bag. You can put all your clothes and your money in one bag and you are done. This is a good thing because it saves time.

Sometimes, being easy means not to do something. If you do not feel like going to the store, you stay home. You do not have to go to a place where you do not want to be. This is a good thing because it lets you keep your own time.
