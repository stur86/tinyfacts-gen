When you glance at someone, you look at them for a very short time. It is a very quick look. You do not really see them. You only see the word on the paper and the sound of their voice. You see the eyes for a second before you look away.

Sometimes, when you glance at a picture, you see the whole picture. You see the colors and the lines. It is like looking at a story in pictures. You see the story without really listening.

When you glance at a book, you might see a word or a picture. It is not like when you read a book. You do not really know what is happening. You just look at a few words. You do not remember anything that happened before.

You can glance at a face. You look at the eyes and the nose. You see the lines on their face. You do not really see who they are. You only see how they look.

You can also glance at a thing. You look at it for a second. You see the thing you are looking at. It is like looking at a small thing on the floor. You do not really see the thing. You see only the tiny part you look at.

Glancing is easy. You can glance at many things at once. You can glance at a word, a picture, a face, a book, or a thing. It is a way to see quickly without really paying attention.
