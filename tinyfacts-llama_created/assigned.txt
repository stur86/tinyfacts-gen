When you are given a job, you have been set to do it. You are the one who does the work. This is called being on the job. It is a way to help the people around you know what you will do. It is also a way to tell everyone who is doing the work.

Sometimes you are given a job by someone else. This person says you must do it. You do the work because you were told you would do it. You do it for them. You are doing a job because you have a job.

You can also be given a job without being told by someone else. This is when you get a job by yourself. You decide to do the work. You might have a job because you like it. You might have a job because you need the money. You might have a job because you are good at it.

Being on the job is important for everyone. It helps keep the world running. When people work, they make things. They build houses. They drive cars. They work in schools. They work in hospitals. Everyone works to make sure the world is good.
