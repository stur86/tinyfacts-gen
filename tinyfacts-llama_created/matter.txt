To matter something is to be a part of it. You can think of a rock. It is part of the ground and the sky and the water. The rock is not the sky. The sky is the one above the ground. The rock is only part of the ground and the water.

You can also think of a person. You matter to a friend. Your friend cares about you. You matter to a family. They love you. You are important because you are there.

When you say something is matter, you are talking about the world around you. You are talking about the things that are there. You are talking about how everything is made.

Sometimes, when you talk about something, you might be talking about how it looks. If you say a dog is matter, you are talking about its body. If you say a tree is matter, you are talking about its green parts and its hard parts that hold it up.
