To give something to someone means to share it with them. You might give a book to a friend, or you might give money to a family. When you do this, you are helping the person.

Sometimes, when you work in a big group, you have to give parts of the job to other people. This is called a job. You might give your part of the job to one person, or you might give it to a group of people. Everyone gets a part, so the job is done.

You can also give things to people when you are in a place. If you are at a party and there is food, you might give a small bit to a friend. This is like sharing a piece of food with them.

Sometimes, you give things to people because they need them. A doctor gives a person a drink to make them feel better. A teacher gives a student a book to read.

To give something is to help someone. It is a kind thing to do.
