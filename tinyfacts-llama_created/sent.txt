To send something means you give it to someone else. You might send a piece of paper to a friend. You might send a phone call to someone you know. You can also send things through the air from one place to another. This happens when you put a small thing into a box and close the box. The box flies through the sky until it reaches the other place. The person you sent it to can then pick it up and read it or listen to it.

Sometimes, when you send something, you are sending a word or a picture. You can send a word to your friend through the computer. You can type a word on the computer and press the send key. Your word goes out into the world and reaches your friend. You did not hold it in your hand.

You can also send money to someone. If you have money, you can put it into a box with your name on it. You then give the box to the person you want to pay. When the box reaches the other person, they take the money and use it for their own needs.

Sometimes, sending something can be a bit strange. Imagine you want a food you love from a far place. You send a box with the food to someone you know. The person picks up the box and takes it home. They might not know if the food is still good or if it is something they really want. They might eat it and then ask the person who sent it if it was okay.
