To take a picture is to show what is happening at a moment. A person might do this for a job. They might put their head down and take a picture of a person. They might also take pictures of things that are beautiful or important. They might use a small box with a light inside to see what they are seeing.

We also use this word to talk about the pictures. If you see a picture in a book or on a computer, you are seeing what someone else saw at a moment. If you look at a picture in your phone, you are looking at what someone else took.

Sometimes, you can use a picture to tell a story. If you take a picture of your dog, you can show it to your friends. You can say, This is my dog. This is how he looks. If you take a picture of your friend, you can share it with others. You can say, This is my friend. This is how they look.
