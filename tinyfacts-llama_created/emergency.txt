An immediately important situation that is happening right now. When you hear a loud noise or see a fire, you act fast. You get people out of the way so they are safe. You also do not stop what you are doing until the situation is under control.

Imagine you are walking to school. Suddenly you see a big car coming. You need to get away fast. You do not stop to look at the car. This is an immediately important situation because you need to act quickly.

Some things that are not an immediately important situation are things that happen later. Maybe you have a plan to study for a long time. You do not have to act right away. You can wait until things are better.

In a big city, there are many people who are in an immediately important situation. They are trying to get to a place that is safe. If the situation is too big, there might be a bigger problem later on.
