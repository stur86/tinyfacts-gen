To be the one you need

Imagine you have a big box full of small pieces of paper. Each piece of paper has a name on it. You want to find a person who has a name on that paper. You look for the name and pick the person. You then write that person down. You keep writing the names of the people you find until you have the one person you need.

But sometimes you find a wrong person. They have a name on the paper, but it is not the one you need. You have to write them down too. You might need to write them many times because the name keeps changing.

This is what a person does who is very good at finding the right person. They look at the papers and find the one who needs them. They write the name down and keep doing it until they find the right person.

You can also say this when you want to do something. You set a word. You say you want to be the one. You want to do it. You are the one you need.
