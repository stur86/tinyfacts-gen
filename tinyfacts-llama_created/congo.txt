The word conversations is a big word. It means you talk with people from other places. You are trying to learn about the world and the ways of the people who live there.

When you talk to a person, you are using your voice. Your voice is like a sound that you make. When you talk with a different person, you need to be carefully. You must say the right words so that the other person feels that you understand them.

Think about a child. A child loves to listen to stories. They want to hear about far away lands and big animals. A grown person who loves to read stories is also a conversation. They talk with the person who wrote the story. They use the words of the story to understand what happened.

This word is important because it helps us understand how people from different places feel about each other. It helps us know when to be kind and when to be carefully.

The word also comes from a place far away. A long time ago, people went to a place where the water was very deep. They went to this place to buy food and other things. They stayed for a very long time. When they left, they told each other about what they saw. This word is about sharing stories with each other.
