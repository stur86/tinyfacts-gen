To join something is to put a part into it. You can join a book with another book. You put the two books together so that they can read together.

A person who joins people is a man who makes many friends. He brings people together. He makes them talk and laugh. This is a good job because he helps people be happy.

A group of people is a group of many friends. When you are in a group, you are with many people who share the same ideas. You can talk about what you like and what you think. You feel safe because you are with others who understand you.

A person who joins a group is a person who looks for a place where they can be part of many people. They might go to a school where many children are there. They might go to a place where many animals live. This is a good place because they can see many different kinds of people and animals.

You can also join something with your body. You might join your legs to a chair. You put your feet on the floor and your body on the chair. This helps you stay standing while you are sitting.

Sometimes, you join with your mind. You might join to a team. You work with many people to do a big job. You help each other and you all work together. You feel happy because you are part of a team.

To join something also means to become one. If you are joining a game, you are becoming part of a group that is playing. When you join a group, you start to act like that group. You follow the same way of thinking and act. You become like them.
