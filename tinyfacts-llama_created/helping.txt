To help someone is to give them something they need or make things easier for them. You can help a friend who is afraid. If your friend is nervous at a party, you can stand next to them and tell them not to worry. You are there for them.

You can also help a family when they are in trouble. If a child falls down and breaks a leg, you can help them feel better. You might bring them a drink or hold their hand so they do not get too tired.

When you help someone, you are showing that you care. You are not just doing something for your own fun. You are doing it because you want to be kind and make life better for the person you are helping.
