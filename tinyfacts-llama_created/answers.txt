An answer is when you find the right thing to say when someone asks you a question. It is when you give the right words to show you know what they are looking for.

Imagine you are talking to a friend and they ask you, Why are you sad? You might say, I am sad because my dog is gone. This is an answer because you are telling them why you are feeling that way.

When you answer a question, you are helping the person know that you understand what they are asking. It is like giving them a word to make their life easier.

You can also answer questions in your head. If you do not know the answer to a question, you can think about it and find the right word. This is how you learn and remember new things.

Sometimes, you might feel confused or you might not know how to answer a question. In these cases, you might ask someone else for help. The person who answers the question can give you the right words to help you understand.

In a game, you might get an answer to a question. If you do not know the answer to a question, the game will tell you the right word. This is like an answer to a question.

In your brain, there are answers to every question you have ever asked. If you think about a problem for a long time, your brain finds the right way to answer it. This is how your brain helps you learn and remember.

In this way, answers are a way to show you know what the other person is looking for. They are a way to help each other and to make things easier.
