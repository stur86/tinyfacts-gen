To fix something means to make it so that nothing can escape or change. Think about a place that is always the same. You walk into a room and there is a table with a chair. You do the same thing every day. You do not change the way you look or the way you act. You are stuck in the same place.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You both want to be the one who says the word first. You can not agree. You keep saying the word, and your friend keeps saying it back. You are both doing the same thing every time. You are not changing; you are just following the same order.

If you try to change the way you look or your way of acting, you will not be able to stay in the same place. You will change, and your life will change. You will not be the same person anymore. You will have to start over.

In the world, you can also fix a fight. A person might try to fight with you, but you are not going to let them. You are ready for them. You are the one who is going to stay calm and keep moving. This way, you keep your life safe and you do not have to worry about getting hurt.

Sometimes, you can fix a situation that does not want to happen. You can do something and then you are done with it. You are free from the problem. You did not have to go through the trouble of trying to change the situation.

In a way, to fix something means to take control and make it what you want it to be. You are the one who decides what happens next.
