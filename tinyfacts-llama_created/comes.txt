To comes is to start or begin a thing. When you come, the day starts. The night before, it was dark. When you come, the morning is bright.

You come from a city. You live there with your family. You go to school and learn many things. After you finish school, you look for a job. You work very hard to pay your money. When you find a job, you do it every day.

You also come to see a friend. You might have been apartments with them for a long time. You have talked many times. Now you want to be together again. You say hello and smile. You are happy to see them.

Sometimes you come from a far place. You are going to a new home. You are going to see new people and try new food. You might feel a little afraid. You are starting a new life.

To comes means you are moving forward. It means the end of one thing is the start of another.
