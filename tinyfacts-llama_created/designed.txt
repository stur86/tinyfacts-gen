To be a thing is to be a part of the world. Everything has a name and a job. A car moves. A tree grows. A book tells stories. When you say a thing is made, you mean that it was built by someone. 

A person is a being. You are you. You look like you. You act like you. You feel like you. When you say you are a person, you mean you live in this world. You breathe air. You eat food. You love people and animals. You feel happy and sad. You are you.

A thing can change. A tree grows. A car moves. A person gets older. You become older. You learn new things. You learn how to do things better. You may become angry. You may feel sad. You may want to be happy.

A thing can be broken. A glass breaks. A book falls. A car stops. You must fix the thing. You must make it whole again. You must put the pieces back together. You must be strong when you do this.

You can make a thing. You can build it. You can fix it. You can change it. You can make it better. You can make it new. When you do this, you are changing the thing. You are changing the world.
