To do with clothes is to clean them. When you are done, you put them in a big box so they do not get wet again.

You use water to make the clothes clean. You can use hot water for clothes that are very bright and need to be clean. You can also use cold water for clothes that are soft or for clothes that need to stay white. If you use hot water on clothes that are not bright, they will get very hot.

You also need to use a thing that can make the clothes look nice. This is a thing you put clothes on when they are wet. It makes them look clean and looks good.

Sometimes you have to do this many times. If you wear the same clothes all the time, they get old and become very soft. You have to clean them and then put them in a box so they stay clean.

You can also do this with other things. You can clean a table, a floor, or a car with a box of water and a thing that makes the water go round. This is how you keep your home clean.
