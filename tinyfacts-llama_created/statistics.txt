To do a group of things and find out how many are in each group is to make a group of numbers and a group of words. This is called a group of numbers and a group of words.

Imagine you want to see how many kids in a school are in a group. You have a paper with all the names and you look at each name. You write the name on a piece of paper and then you counters the number of papers you have for each name. For the name of the girl, you write down how many papers it has. For the name of the boy, you write down how many papers it has. Then you add up all the papers for each name. If the name of the girl has one paper, the name of the boy has one paper, and the name of the girl also has one paper, the number of papers for each name is the same. This is how you find out how many kids are in the group.

This work is important for many people. They do this work every day. They work with numbers and words to find out how many people are in a group. They use this work to decide how to share money, to decide how many people will be in a room, or to decide how many people will help a person who is sick.

The word is also used when we talk about how many things happen in a group. If a movie has many people and many dogs, or if a group of people is in a town, we might say that the group is big. This means there are a lot of people and many different things happening together.
