To write something is to use words. You take a set of letters and put them together. This is a way to tell stories, or to show the world what you mean. You can write a word by one letter at a time. Or you can write a group of words. This is why you can write a story in many different ways.

Think of this with numbers. You have a set of numbers. You do not change them. If you change one number, the whole set changes. You can not change the numbers while keeping the set the same. You can not write a number and then change it without changing the whole number. A number is like a person. You can not change who they are, even if you change one thing at a time.

When you write something, you use words. You use words to tell stories, to show how you feel, to show what is happening, to show who is there, and to show where something is. You write this because words are the best way to say what you want to say.

To write something is to follow a set of steps. You start with a set of letters. You follow the steps to put the letters together. You do not start with the end. You start in the middle. You go forward, one letter at a time.

You can not write something and then change the words. If you change a word, you change the story. You change who is there, who did what, and where everything is. If you change something, you change the story. You do not change the story by changing a few words. You change the story by changing the whole story.

Writing is a way to show. You use words to show how you feel, to show what is happening, to show who is there, and to show where things are. You write to share your story with others.
