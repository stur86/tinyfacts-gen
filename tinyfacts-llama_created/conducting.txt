To be a good friend is to care about someone. You must be kind. You show that you love them. This is the most important thing. When you do this, everyone feels better.

A person who works in a big building is a good person. They are part of a team. They help the people who run the building. They are very good at their job. They are like the person who runs your school. They keep everything running so everyone can live a safe life.

If you are the one who leads a team, you are the one who is in control. You decide what to do next. You are the person who makes the plans. You are the one who says, here we go!

A person who works with a computer is also a good person. They use the computer to find the answers. They use the computer to help people. They are like the person who works at your office. They help the people who use the computer.

To act is to move your body. When you act, you change the world. You can make someone laugh. You can make a room feel happy. You can make a room feel safe. You can act when you want to show you care.
