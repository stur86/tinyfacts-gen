To write about something is to tell others how you feel about it. A person who writes about things they see or think about might write about a game they watched. They might write about a person they like. They also might write about their own life.

Becoming a writer is a bit different. When you are a young person, you might write in a book. You might write about what you saw or what you felt. You might write about your hopes and dreams. Later, you might decide to write about other things. You might write about your work, your family, or your friends.

Some people do this job for money. They write about their life and share it with many people. Many of these people write about things they saw at a party. They might write about what they did or what happened. They also might write about their own story. They share their life with many people so that others can see it.

Other people do this job because they love to write. They do it for fun. They write about their own life, their family, or their friends. They also write about things they are interested in. They write to share their ideas with others.

Begins are people who start a new job. A person might start as a helper. They do not know how to do everything. They learn as they go. They learn about their job. They learn about their work. They learn about their people. They learn about their world. As they learn, they become good at their job.
