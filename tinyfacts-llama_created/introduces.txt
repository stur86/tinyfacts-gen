To Interest someone or something means to share a feeling or idea with them. When you are interested, you care about what the person or thing is. You might ask them questions or try to learn more about them.

Imagine you are in a game. You want to know why the person on the other team is doing the thing they are doing. You might ask them. If you ask and they tell you, you are now interested in them. You start to care about their story.

Sometimes, a person is very good at something. You watch them do it and you are amazed. You might want to learn how they do it too. This is an act of interest. You want to know more about how they do it.

You can also be interested in a movie or a book. If you watch a movie, you might feel happy or sad. If you read a book, you might feel like you understand the story. This is because you are learning about a new idea or feeling.

When you are interested, you do not just sit there. You might ask questions or try to understand. You want to know more. This is important because it shows that you care about what is happening.

You can also be interested in a person. If you meet a new friend, you might ask them questions. You want to know more about them. You want to see if you can stay friends. This is an act of interest, too. You care about what happens between you and the person.
