To corner something means to make it so that one side of it is blocked by something else. Think of a corner of a piece of wood or a piece of paper. If you put a small thing in the corner, it will not be able to go through the other side. This is because the two sides are together.

You can also use this word in a different way. Imagine you are at a table with many people. You want to talk to one person who is very important to you. To corner them means to sit next to them, so they are alone with you, and to talk to them while they are looking at something else. This helps you have a chance to talk with them.

Sometimes, if you corner a ball, you can stop it from running around. You can grab it and hold it so it does not move anymore. This is a way to control something that is moving.

We also use the word to talk about people. If someone is in a corner, it means they are the only ones left. They are the ones who are not being watched or noticed by anyone else. They can feel alone and a bit scared.
