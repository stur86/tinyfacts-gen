A small hard piece is used in a gun to fire a round. It is made of hard stuff that stays straight. The round has a hard part at the top and a soft part at the bottom. When a person pulls the gun, the hard piece is pulled out of the gun. It is then moving very fast and goes into the body of the person. The hard piece hits the inside of the body and makes a mark. This mark tells the body that there is a small piece of hard stuff on the inside.
