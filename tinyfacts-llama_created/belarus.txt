Believe is to trust something. You see a person or a thing and you think they are true. You believe that they will keep their word. If you believe something, you are ready to act on it.

Think about a child. A child believes in a story. They believe that a man in a box is real and that he can give them food. When the story ends, the child might feel sad. They might ask, why did the man in the box stop? If you are a friend, you would say, I believe you. This means you trust what the other person says.

The word also means to keep things safe. If you believe a thing is safe, you are sure that it will not hurt you. You might believe that a book is safe to read. If you believe something is safe, you feel calm inside.

Believe is also about being part of a group. When you believe in a group, you do not think the whole group is wrong. You might believe that the group is doing the right thing. If you are a part of a team, you believe in the team. You trust the leader and the other people.

Sometimes, you might feel you do not believe something. You might think, I am not sure. This is when you are not sure if something is true. You do not trust it. It is like not feeling the ground under your feet. You are afraid to move because you think something might fall down.
