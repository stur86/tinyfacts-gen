To be open means to let people see you. When you are open, you let the world know what you are doing or what you think. You do not hide your face, or your hair, or your clothes.

Think about a street. People walk on this street every day. They see many people, cars, and houses. If you walk down the street, people see you. They might say hello, or they might say no, or they might say something else. You are open to everyone who sees you.

You can also be open about your life. If you have a problem, you tell your friends, family, or a teacher. You might write a story, or you might talk about it. You are showing the world what is happening in your life.

Sometimes, being open is hard. You might feel afraid or worried. You do not want people to see you. You might hide your face or your voice. But even in those times, you are still open. You are still letting people know about you.

A person who is open is often a good friend. They are someone you trust. They tell you the truth, even if it hurts you. They listen to you when you talk. They show you that they care about you. Being open is important because it helps people feel safe and happy.
