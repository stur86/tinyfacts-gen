A box is a thing you build. It has sides and a top. You can put things inside it. You can also use a box to carry things from one place to another.

A person also has a box. This box is in their bag. Inside the box is a book, a phone, and maybe a small thing that helps them talk.

Sometimes, a box is a thing that gives power. You can use a box that gives power to do things. You press a part, and the power comes out. This is how you power cars, computers, and other things that go round.

A box can also be a place for people to live. You can live in a small house inside a big box. Or, you can live in a large box with many many people.

People use words to talk about boxes. They say things like, you are in a box. This means you are in a place. It is a way to tell where you are.
