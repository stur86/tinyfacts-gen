To speak is to use words to tell other people what you think. When you speak, you are sending a word to others. You are telling them something that happened, a feeling, or a idea.

Imagine you want to talk to your friend about what you did this morning. You use your voice and words to tell your friend about how you felt and what you did. Your words are like a story. When you speak, you are sharing your story with others.

People also speak when they are happy. When you smile and say, you mean you are glad, you are speaking. You are letting others know you are feeling good. You are telling them that you like the world and that everything is okay.

Some people speak in their head while they walk. They are thinking about what they need to say. They do not use words. They are listening to their own mind. Sometimes, they speak to themselves because they are afraid. They are afraid to say what they really think. This is a kind of speaking.

There are different kinds of speaking. Some people speak very fast. They say many words in a short time. This is like a song. Other people speak very slow. They take their time to think and say each word. This is like a story.

When we speak, we can show how we feel. If you are angry, you might shout. If you are sad, you might cry. Your words tell others how you are feeling. This helps people understand each other.

Sometimes, people speak without even thinking. They just say words because they must. This can happen when someone is afraid or when they are trying to hide a bad feeling. In these cases, the words do not come from the heart. They are just a way to act.

To speak is a way to share our lives with others. It is a way to tell them about what we did, what we think, and how we feel.
