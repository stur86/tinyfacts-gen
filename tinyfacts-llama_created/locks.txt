To lock something means to make it very hard to open. Think about your house. You lock the door to keep your family safe inside. You lock the box that holds your money so you don't lose it.

You can lock a car. A car has a strong lock. If you try to open it, the car will not move. You can also lock a bag with a lock. This keeps things safe when you are not around.

You can also lock a thing that is not a box. You lock a book so you do not touch it when you are not in a school. You lock a chair so a child does not push it.

There are different kinds of locks. Some locks have numbers. Others have a small round thing. Some locks are for one door, and others are for many doors. You need to know the numbers or the round thing to open the lock.

Locks are important because they keep us safe. We lock our doors at night so we do not get hurt. We lock our bags so we do not lose money. We lock our computers so we do not share our work with others.

You also lock people. In a place where people are kept, they lock them. This means they keep them inside the place until they are free.
