To Relax

When you feel safe and strong, you can relax. This means you stop thinking about what is wrong or what is happening now. You let go of your body and mind.

Imagine you are in a hot room. You look around, but the room does not move. You feel the air on your skin, but it does not change. You are not moving. You are still. You relax.

When you are in this state, you are not afraid. You do not worry about things that are not there. You do not think about the big problems that are very much there. You just feel calm.

You can relax in many ways. You can read a book. You can listen to music. You can watch a funny movie. You can even lie down and close your eyes. All of these things help your body and mind feel safe and strong.

But you can also relax in your own home. You can sit in a chair. You can look at a picture. You can listen to a voice. You can just sit and be. This is a quiet time.

When you need to relax, you might ask someone to help you. You might say, I need to relax. You might ask them to turn on the air. You might ask them to turn off the lights. You need them to be quiet so you can rest.

Relaxing is important. It helps you stay strong. It helps you feel safe. It is a way to calm your body and mind when you are scared, worried, or very much.

So, when you say someone or something is relaxing, you mean they are safe and strong. They are not thinking about the bad things that are happening. They are just being quiet and calm.
