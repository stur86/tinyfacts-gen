To throw something is to move it away from you. You can throw a ball or a rock. When you throw a ball, you hold it in your hand. You stand near a wall. You start to move your hand. The ball goes out of your hand. It is going very fast. You want to keep it so it does not hit you. If the ball hits a person or a chair, the person or the chair might fall down.

You can also throw a thing that you do not want anymore. If you have food you do not like, you can throw it. This makes the thing go away and does not hurt anyone. It is a way to get rides of things you do not want.

To throw something into the air is another way. You stand near a high place. You take a small thing, like a small ball. You throw it up into the sky. The air catches the ball and makes it go up very fast. It goes high in the sky. You watch it until it disappears from your eyes. This is a fun way to watch things move high in the sky.
