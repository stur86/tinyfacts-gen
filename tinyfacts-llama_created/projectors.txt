A thing you put light on is called a picture. This is how you see a movie, a game, or a story. You put it in a box or a box with a clear window. The light from a far away place goes through the window and comes out of the box. The window lets the light reach your eyes.

A person who works on a picture is a person who makes it. They decide what the picture should look like. They might pick a color, a way the people look, and the way things are. They also plan where the picture will go on the wall.

Another person might work on the box part. They make sure the light goes through the window without any air getting in. They also make sure the box is strong so it does not break.

Sometimes, you need a box that can go out into the street. This is a bigger picture. People carry these boxes in cars, buses, or trains. When you sit in a car and look out the window, the picture comes out. You see what is happening on the street. You might see people driving, cars moving, or animals running.

There are also things that go out into the sky. They send pictures down to your home or to your friend. These are pictures from a far away place. You can watch them when you are home.

When you use a box to put light on a picture, you are making a picture that is not there. You are using the light that is already there to make a new picture. This is why you need to put light on the thing first. If you do not put light on it, nothing can happen.
