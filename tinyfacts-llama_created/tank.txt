A big thing that holds water or air is called a box with a round top. It is often used in the water. When you fill it up, it makes a loud noise and starts to push water out. If you hit it, the water inside goes out very fast.

People also use a box with a long top to carry things. It is like a box with a door on it. You put the thing in, and then you close the door. This box goes very fast from one place to another. It goes over roads and over water. It helps move people and things to far away parts.

In a big building, there are many of these boxes. They are called tall boxes. Some of them are very tall and go very high. They carry people to places where they can go to other places. They help people move quickly and easily.

Some boxes are very small. They are used to carry things you carry with you. You might put a book, a phone, or a small bag inside. These small boxes are very fast and can go very quickly. They are good for people who need to run around quickly.
