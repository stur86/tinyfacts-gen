To ask someone something means you want to know their answer. You might say, in front of a person, a room, or a computer. You do this because you are not sure how to do it.

Imagine you are playing a game. You need to know how to stop. You ask the person who is in the game, and they tell you. If you ask a dog, it will do something because it wants to. You did not tell it what to do, but it followed because you asked.

You can ask a person many things. You might ask a friend, a family person, or a teacher. You might ask about a story, a game, or a plan. The person you ask will try to give you an answer. If they do not know, they will tell you.
