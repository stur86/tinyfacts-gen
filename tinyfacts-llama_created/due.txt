To pay a fine can be a big thing. If you do something wrong, you might have to pay money. This is called a fine. It is the money you have to pay to the person or the place that says you did something wrong.

Sometimes, you do something and you do not want to pay. This is not good. The person or the place might say you must pay because you broke the thing you were supposed to keep safe.

If you pay a fine, you are doing the right thing. You are following what you are told. It is a way to stop the bad thing you did.

You might pay a fine for driving fast. You might pay a fine for not following the way. You might pay a fine for using your phone while you are driving. Paying a fine is a way to show that you did something wrong and you are ready to do what is right next time.
