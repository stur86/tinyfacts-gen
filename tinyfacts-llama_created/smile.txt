To smile means you feel happy and your face is kind. When you smile, your eyes go up and your mouth goes wide. It makes your face look soft and warm. It is like a little light in the middle of your face. When you smile at a friend, you show you care about them. When you smile at a child, you show you are happy to see them.

Sometimes you can smile at things that are not happy. If you watch a funny movie, you might smile because you laughed. If you see a beautiful picture, you might smile because it is pretty. A smile can make people feel better when they are sad or afraid.

You can also smile at yourself. When you look at a picture of yourself, you might smile because you like the way you look. You might smile when you see a picture of your family. You are smiling because you are happy to see them. This is how you feel when you are happy.

Smiling is a way to show you are happy and care about the people around you. It is a way to show you are a good person.
