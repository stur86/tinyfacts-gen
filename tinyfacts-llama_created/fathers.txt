A father is a man who has a child. He is the one who gives them life. He is the one who tells them how to act. He is the one who helps them when they need it. A father is like a home.

Imagine a house. The house has doors and windows. The doors open to the street and the windows open to the sky. A father is like the door. He opens it to let his child in and out. He is the door that says, come in.

A father is also the one who helps his child learn. He shows them how to walk. He shows them how to eat. He shows them how to use a bed. He is the one who helps them become the person they want to be.

Sometimes, a father has a child. A child is a baby. A baby needs a father to be there. The father is the one who gives the baby food and the air they need to breathe. The father is the one who says, eat, drink, and sleep.

A father is very important. He helps his child grow into a strong person. He helps them learn how to be a good person. He helps them feel safe. He is the one who is always there for them.

Even though a father is the one who gives life, a child also has a father. A child has a mother who loved them before their father was there. The mother and the child have a life together. They have a father who loves them, even if he is not there.
