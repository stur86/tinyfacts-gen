Today is a word we use to talk about the day that is happening now. It is the day you are in right now. You can see the day on your wall. You can look out the window and see the sun or the stars.

Today is a short day. When you look at your phone, you see how many hours are left. If the time is Ask, you can see that there are only three hours left. When you are done, you can close the phone and go to bed.

Today is also the day when you do things. You might go to school, go to work, or play with your friends. You might go to the movies, eat food, or read a book. You can do many different things today.

Sometimes, people say today is the best day. This is because it is the day when you have done many good things. You can feel happy because you did something good for other people.

You can also say that today is a good day if you feel the sun on your skin. You can feel the wind in your hair or the soft ground under your feet. Today is a day to enjoy the world around you.
