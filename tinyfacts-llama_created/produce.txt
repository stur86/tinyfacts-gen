To make something new is to give life to something. If you cut a piece of wood, it will grow into a tree. If you give a person food, they will have power and can walk. You are the one who makes something new.

Imagine a small room. You put small pieces of paper in the room. Then, you put a small ball on the floor. When you close the door, the paper and the ball are all in the room, but the room is not the same room anymore. The room has a new story.

You can also make a drink. You take the parts of a tree, the parts of a thing that grows on the ground, and you put them into a box. When you open the box, the drink comes out. You did not just pick it from the box. You made it.

Making things new is important. It helps us build houses, make clothes, and grow food. It is also how we make new ideas. When you learn something new, you are making your brain new.
