A thing that says, something bad is happening, but it is not really so.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You say, you are going to jump over the wall. But then you jump over the wall. You say, you are going to do it, but you were just playing. This is like a thing that says, something bad is happening, but it is not really so.

People also use this word in business. When a big store says, we do not carry this thing, it means, this thing is bad for you. It is not really in our store. This helps people know what is really going on.

Sometimes, a thing that says, a picture is not real, is also used. A movie or a book might say, this is not real, but it is. It is just a story. You can not see it with your eyes, but you can feel it in your heart.

In the world of money, this word is used. A thing that says, something is not true, might be a thing that is not true. If you say, this money is not good, but it is, it is a lie. And if you say, you are going to do something that is not safe, but you are, you are also a lie.
