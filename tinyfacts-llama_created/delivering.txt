To give something to someone means you are sending it to them. Imagine you have a box with a small thing inside. You want to give that thing to your friend. You put the box in the hands of your friend, and then your friend is in control of the box and the thing it holds.

If you want to give money, you put the money into a bag. The bag now holds the money, and you are done. If you want to give a book, you read it for the person, and then you give it to them. The person reads the book and can keep it.

When you give a thing, you are showing that you trust the other person. You let them take care of the thing. It is like letting a child play with your things.

Sometimes, when you give money, you are giving it to someone who needs it. You are helping them to get a better life. This is a kind act that can make a big change in someone.

You can also say someone is giving you a hug. This means they are showing you love. They are letting you know they care about you.
