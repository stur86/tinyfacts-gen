When people move around, they need a way to do it. They need to follow a set of roads and signs so they know where to go. These roads are like big lines on the ground. They tell cars, buses, and other things where to go and when to go.

Imagine you are going to school. You have a plan to get there. You know which road to take, when to start, and how long it will take. But if many other people want to go to the same school at the same time, it can get very busy. The roads become full. Cars and buses start to move. There are not enough spaces to drive.

This is what happens when many people are moving around at the same time. The roads become very busy. Cars and buses have to follow the same lines as before. People are also trying to get where they need to go. This can cause a lot of stop and go. It can be very slow.

In cities, there are also other ways to get around. You can walk. Some cities have roads that are very wide for people to walk on. Or you can use a thing that goes very fast. This thing can carry many people at once and can go very quickly. It is very good for people who want to get to a place quickly.

Sometimes, there are things that can stop cars and buses. If a car does not follow the right lines, it can stop. If a bus does not follow the right lines, the people inside the bus can get out and go on foot. This helps keep everyone safe.

When people move around in a city, they need to follow the right lines. They need to go to the right places at the right times. It is very important. It helps everyone stay safe and get where they need to go.
