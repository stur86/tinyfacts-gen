The word we use when we say something is fixed is called a word. It is a thing that people use to talk about their world. We use it every day. When you hear a word, you know what it means right away. You know that the word is a name for a thing.

Sometimes we use words to talk about time. We say things like, I am going to school. Or, I am going home. The word here tells us when we are going. Another time we use words to talk about where we are. If you are in the city, you might say, I am in the city. The word here tells us where you are.

We also use words to talk about things we have. If you have a book, you say, I have a book. This word tells us you have something. And if you have money, you say, I have money. The word here tells us you have something.

But the word is not just for things we have. We also use it to tell stories. A story is a way of moving from one thing to another. We use words to make the story go. We say, the sun is up. We say, the dog is happy. The word here tells us what is happening in the story.

Sometimes we use words in a group of people. If we talk to a group of friends, we use words to show we care. If you say, I am happy to see you, you are a friend. When you use a word, you are telling the world what you think and what you mean.
