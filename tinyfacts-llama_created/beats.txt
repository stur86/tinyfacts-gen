To beat means you do something before the person next to you. When you run a race, you beat the person who is in front of you. You go faster. Your heart is beating faster than the other person.

If you play a game, you beat the person who has more points. The person with more points is the best. If you beat your friend at a game, you are the one who did better.

It also means to make a sound that is very loud and strong. You might hit a thing to make it go very fast. You can beat a big animal by hitting it. The sound is like a strong noise.

Sometimes, to beat something means to break it. If you have a small thing and you hit it many times, it will break into pieces. This happens when you use power to make something fall down or when you use a gun to make a person stop.
