To put something in a line means to put it in a line of things. Imagine you are walking down a street. You see many people. Each person is in their own line. Some people walk in one line and some people walk in another line. But you are also moving forward. You go in a line with the other people.

You can put things in a line too. You can put papers in a line. You can put books in a line. This makes a line look like a line of things. It helps you see all the things one by one.

Sometimes, you put things in a line to make them easy to see. If you have a long listened word, you can put it in a line. This makes it easy to read. You just read one word at a time.

We also use this word when we talk about computers. When a computer does work, it moves in a line. It starts with one job. Then it goes to the next job. It does each job one by one until it finishes.

You can also put things in a line when you talk about how things are. If you say a group of people are all together, you put them in a line. This helps us see how many people there are.
