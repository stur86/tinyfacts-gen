An hour is a long time in the day. It is the time from the first minute after the sun is up to the minute before the sun comes down.

When you are on a train or a car, the person who drives the thing says how many hours they have been driving. If the person says  hours, it is an hour.

You also hear about hours when you are watching a movie. The movie is a long story with many parts. Each part is an hour long. When you finish the whole movie, you have been watching for many hours.

There is also a thing you see in the sky. It is a big round thing that goes around the world. It is very high. If you watch it from the ground, it looks like it is moving. It is called a day. There are many hours in a day.

A high power is a number that tells how strong something is. If something is very strong, it has many big numbers. If something is not very strong, it has fewer big numbers. Think of a car. A car that can move fast has a high power number. A car that is slow has a low power number.

You also see numbers that show how many hours of time something will take. These numbers help us plan our day. If you know how many hours it will take to get from one place to another, you can plan your time.
