A desk is a piece of wood with a hard top. It is usually round or long and has four sides. On one side, a person sits and works at a computer. The person can type, write, or draw on a paper thing that is held between their hands.

Inside the desk, there is a small table with four legs. This table holds a chair and a box that holds papers. Some desks have a small box on top that holds a phone. People use this box to talk to friends or to a person who works in a big office.

The desk has many small things that help a person work. There are things to write with, things to draw, and things to keep papers. Some desks have a small box where the person can keep their money. This makes the desk a very important piece of a room.

People use desks for many reasons. Some sit at a desk while they work. Some sit at a desk while they play a game. Some sit at a desk while they talk to a friend. Some people do things on a desk that can not be done on the floor.
