To ask is to want to know something. You might ask a friend, a family person, or even a person you just met. When you ask, you are looking for the answer you need.

Imagine you are in a room with a big table. There are many books on the table. You want to know which one is the most important book. You would say, in your head, to your friend, or to the person sitting next to you. You are not asking them to give you the book. You are asking them to tell you where it is.

You can ask many things to ask. You might ask what you are doing, how you feel, or what you want to do next. If you are feeling sad, you might say, I am sad. I want to know why.

When you ask, you are showing that you care about what happens. You are not just looking at something. You are really thinking about the answer and what it means for you.

In a school, you might ask a teacher a question. You want to know what will happen next or why something happened. The teacher is the one who gives you the answer. The question is what you are asking.

To ask can be hard sometimes. You might feel scared or worried. You want to know. But you do not want to say it out loud because you are afraid of the answer. So you ask instead. It is a way to show you care and to ask for what you need.
