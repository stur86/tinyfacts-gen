To name someone is to give them a name. It helps people know who is standing next to them. You might have a little sister, a brother, a friend, a teacher, or a parents child. You can also name things. You might name a book, a movie, a game, or a place.

A name can be short. A person might have a short name like Tomorrows or a short name like Jumper. It is usually one word. A long name can be many words. Some people have a very long name because they were growing up very many years. You might hear a man say, I am from the city of New Hope, or a woman say, I was named after a book by a great writer.

You can also name a dog or a cat. A dog is a living thing that can walk on its legs. A cat is a living thing that can sit and move when you want it to. You can name a dog a funny name like Bigger or a sad name like Sad Dog.

Sometimes, you give a name to a person after they do something good. You might say, I am so happy you saved my cat. This name helps people remember the person.

We also give names to the things in the world. You might name a tree, a house, or a street. A name is a way to show you care about the person or thing.
