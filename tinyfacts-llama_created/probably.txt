Probably is a word you use when you are not sure about something. When you say something is probably true, you are saying it is almost sure.

Think of a game. You have a small ball and you throw it into a box with many balls. You are not sure which one will come out. You do not know if it is the red one, the blue one, or the green one. But you are almost sure it is the one that came out.

You might also say you are probably going to the movies tonight. You do not know if you will go. You are not sure, but you think you will go. You are making an easy guess.

If you say something is probably true, you are using your brain. You are not thinking hard about it. You are just trusting your eyes or your memory.

The word is like this: you probably. It helps us to build a story or a plan.
