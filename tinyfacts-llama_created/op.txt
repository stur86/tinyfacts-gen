To opened something means you are letting it go. When you are full, you need to give some of your food to a dog or a cat. If you keep all of it, it becomes too full for you. This makes you feel sick.

When you are on a bus, you might feel this way. You are moving from one place to another, and you do not need to be in one place. You want to be where the bus stops, but the bus does not stop there. You are letting go of the place you want to be.

Sometimes, you might feel this way when you do something you do not want to do. You might say, I am not ready. You are letting go of the moment. You are not afraid anymore. You are free.

You can also use this word with people. If a person does not want to talk, you say they are opened. This means they are closed or quiet. They do not want to share their ideas or their stories.
