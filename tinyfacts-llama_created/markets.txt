A place where people buy and give things is called a place of buying and giving. It is a spot where people go to buy food, clothes, and things they need. There are also places where you buy things that are not for eating. You can buy clothes, shoes, and other things to wear. You can also buy things to fix your body if you have a problem. These places are like a store, but they are not for eating.
