A small thing that makes a sound when it moves past something else. It is the tiny part of a big thing that is not part of the big thing. When a thing with many parts moves, the small parts move too. They go in different directions. Sometimes they go in the same direction. When they go in the same direction, they are the ones that make the sound. When they go in different directions, there is only one sound.

Think of a ball. The ball is a big thing. It has many parts. When you throw the ball, the parts move past each other. The parts that hit your hand and the parts that hit the ground make different sounds. The sound of your hand is the sound of the parts going in the same direction. The sound of your foot is the sound of the parts going in different directions.
