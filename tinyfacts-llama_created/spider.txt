A thing that goes around is called a fly. It flies from house to house, from street to street. It goes through windows and through open doors. It goes down the street and finds a home in a house.

There is a thing with many long hair. It goes through the ground to find food. It goes under the ground and out again at night. It does this because it does not need to eat like a fly does. It does not need to go out and find food every day.

A thing that goes under the ground is a dog. It goes out at night to find food. It goes to the ground and comes back to its home. It does this because it is a dog.
