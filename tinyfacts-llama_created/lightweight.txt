To be light is to be very small. A light thing is not heavy like a big box of books. It does not feel heavy when you carry it.

Imagine you have a small piece of paper that you throw across the room. The paper goes far and stays up in the air. This is because the paper is light. It does not pull on your hand or your arm like a big piece of paper would.

Some things can be light because they are very small. A tiny piece of paper that is only a tiny bit bigger than the paper on your desk is light. This tiny piece of paper is used to write or draw on a small piece of paper.

But light things can also be light because they are not heavy. A small dog that is not very big at all is light. This is because the dog does not have too much body and does not pull on your clothes or your hand.

The word light also means to be easy to see. If you look at a bright light, you can see it easily. This light is like a big sun in the sky.

A person can also be light. A person who is very small and light can move around easily. They do not feel heavy when they walk or run.
