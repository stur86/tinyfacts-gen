To tell means to share what you know with another person. You can tell them a story. You might say,  The sun is up today. Or you might say,  I found a book in my room.

You can also tell a story to someone who does not know. You might say,  I am going to the park. This way, you are sharing your plans with them.

Sometimes, you tell a story so that others can understand. You might say,  I am scared. Or you say,  I am happy because I have a friend.

When you tell, you are giving people a picture. You are helping them see what happened. You are sharing your own life with them.
