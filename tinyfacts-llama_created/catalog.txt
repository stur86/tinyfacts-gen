To look at something is to see it. You look at a picture. You look at a book. You look at a thing in a room. This word means you look at many of these things.

Imagine you are a person. You want to know what your friends are doing. You go to their houses and look at what they are doing. You see them eating food. You see them playing games. You see them laughing.

You also want to know what things look like. If you have a box, you can look at it. You look at the box. You see the cover. You see the side. You see the bottom. You know what the box looks like.

You can also look at things that are not in your house. You might look at a book about your town. You look at a picture of your family. You look at a story about a person who lives far away. You see how the person looks. You see what they do.

To put these things together is to make a listened word. You look at many of these things so you can remember them. You can use this word to keep track of things. You can use it to find what you are looking for.
