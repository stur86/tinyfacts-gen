A long way is a very big space. You can walk for a long time and still not reach the end. If you walk five hundred feet, you have gone one long way. If you walk one hundred feet, you are only one long way away from the end.

To tell how many long ways a thing is, you use numbers. One long way is one hundred long ways. Two long ways are two hundred long ways. Three long ways are three hundred long ways. And so on. You can also use numbers with three or four numbers. If you have three long ways, you are three hundred long ways away from the end.

Long ways are used to help us understand how big something is. Imagine you are in a room and you want to know how many feet are in it. You can counters the long ways on the floor. If you walk from one corner to the other, and the floor is one hundred long ways long, then the room has one hundred long ways long walls.
