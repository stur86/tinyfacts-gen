An idea is a thing that a person thinks about or a thing that is true. When we learn something, we make a set of ideas. If these ideas are not all the same, then something is wrong. This is what we call an idea with an idea.

Think about a story. A person might start with a good idea. Then they write the story and show how it happens. When a person writes a story, they make many ideas. If they make one idea different from the others, then they have made an idea with an idea. This is where the story gets different from what the person thought the story would be.

People also make these kinds of ideas. When you try to write a book, you make many ideas. You think about what the story should be. You think about the people, the place, and the time. You put these ideas into words. If you make one idea different from the others, then your story is not what you thought it would be.

Sometimes, something wrong happens. You write a story and it is not what you thought it would be. You might think the story was about a happy time, but it is actually about a sad time. This is an idea with an idea. The story is wrong because of the idea.

We also make these kinds of ideas in our day. You might make an idea about how to make a good breakfast. You think about what you should put in the food. You think about how to make it look nice. You put these ideas into your brain. If you follow these ideas, then you will make a good breakfast.

In every group, people have their own ideas. If people do not agree, then there is a problem. They might not like what each other says. They might make the same idea, but they put it in a different way. This is because they have an idea with an idea.
