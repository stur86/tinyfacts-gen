To pick something can mean many different things.

First, you might pick a small thing from a box. You might want a bright color. You might want a soft sound. You might pick something small and round that you like to hold. This is a very easy thing to do. You just reach out and touch it.

Another way is if you are in a room with many people. You want to be near a friend. You might go to a table and pick them. You might go to the floor and pick a person with a bright face. You are the one who decides who you want to be near.

Sometimes, you might pick a food to eat. You might pick a sweet thing to have for breakfast. You might pick a strong food to eat for lunch. This is a way to decide what you want to eat when you are not sure.

When you pick something, you are the one who decides. You are not told by anyone else. You are the one who decides what is best for you.
