Inside your body, you have many tiny things that are very important. These tiny things are like blue lines that go in many different directions. One of these lines is very long and goes all the way around your body. This line is made up of many many very small pieces that are even smaller than your hair. These small pieces are the building blocks of life.

The long line goes through every cell in your body. Every time a cell makes a new body, the long line starts over. This means that no matter how old you get, your body still has the same long line inside it. This long line tells your body what to do.

Imagine you have a long line that goes in a box. Inside the box, there are many pieces of wood that are very different from each other. But every piece of wood has the same number of sides. Every piece of wood has the same number of sides. The long line is like this box. It has the same number of sides for every cell in your body.

Inside each piece of wood, there is one set of numbers. This set of numbers tells the wood which color to be. The number of sides is the same for all the pieces of wood. So, all the pieces of wood in the box are the same. But the box is much bigger than one piece of wood. Because of this, the numbers in each piece of wood are different.

When you look at one piece of wood, you can tell what kind of wood it is. You can see the number of sides. You can see how many sides have a corner and how many sides do not. This helps you know if the wood is strong or not. If the numbers are the same, the wood is the same kind. If the numbers are different, the wood is a different kind.
