An air cleaner is a thing that makes the air clean. It helps when you breathe. It takes the bad air that is stuck in your home or car and makes it good to breathe. This makes you feel better.

You can put one in your car. It sits inside the car and makes the air clean. It does this by using tiny parts that catch the bad stuff. This keeps you safe while you are driving.

Some air clean things sit on your desk. They sit on the floor in front of your chair. You can turn them on and they make the air clean for a long time. They can clean the air that you breathe every day.

You can also buy one that sits on your windows. It sits on the windows and makes the air clean while you are inside the house. It helps keep the bad stuff from going into your room.

A good air cleaner is like a friend that keeps you safe. It helps you breathe and feels nice when you use it.
