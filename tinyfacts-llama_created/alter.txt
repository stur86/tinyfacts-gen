To change something means to make it different. If you want to change your hair, you might use a hair cut. You take the hair from your head and you make it look new. You cut the hair into different parts so it looks like a different kind of hair. This is a way to change how you look.

You can also change the way you act. If you are very angry, you might shout. If you are very sad, you might cry. You are changing your body and your mind. You are doing something different with your body and your heart.

Sometimes people change their mind. If you think something is wrong, you might decide it is not true. You might change your feeling. You might find a new way to look at a problem. This is a way to change how you see the world.

You can also change a word. If you say a word, you might change it. You might say the word with a different meaning. This is how people change their words. They learn new ways to speak.

To change something is to make it new. It is about taking something old and making it different.
