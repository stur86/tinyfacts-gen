A notice is a way to tell people that something is happening. It is a way to let everyone know about a thing that is going to happen, or a thing that has happened.

Sometimes a notice is written on a piece of paper. It might say something like, you are in trouble, or a person is in the building. Other times it is a picture or a drawing.

When you see a notice, you might feel a bit surprised or worried. You might think, oh, I should have noticed that before. It is like a word that lets you know that something is happening.

You can also find notices in books, papers, and computers. These notices tell you that there is something new. You can read them to learn about a story, a game, or a thing that happened.

In a big building, there are many notices. One might say, the food is out, or there is a problem with the water. These notices help people know what to do. They help everyone stay safe and know when to act.

Sometimes notices are about things that are not happening. You might see a notice that says, the park is closed, or there is a big eventually in the town. This kind of notice tells you to stay inside or to wait.

In your life, you see notices all the time. You see them when you drive and see signs. You see them when you go to a store and see a notice about what is on the table. You see them when you watch a movie and see a notice about what is happening next.
