To do something new is to start something. It is when you begin a job or a plan.

When you first start to do something, you are very nervous. You are not sure if you will do it well. You might feel afraid or a bit wrong. But you keep going anyway.

Your first time is a big moment. It is a time when you see how you do. You learn if you are doing the right thing. You might make a small problem. You might feel happy because you did it.

After the first time, you do it many more times. You become very good at it. You learn how to do it faster and better. You can do it without thinking. You have become good at it.

You can also do something new because of a show. Imagine you watch a movie. The movie starts and the story begins. You watch the story from the start until the end. When it ends, you feel happy. You know everything happened because of the show.

You can also do something new when you meet a person. You meet them for the first time. You do not know them yet. You do not know if they are nice or not. But as you talk, you start to know them. You become friends.

When a group of people do something new, it is called a start. They start a new job or a new plan. It is a new beginning for them.

In music, when a person starts a song, it is a start. They begin the song with a word or a sound. This word makes everyone know what to do next. They start to single the words or make the music. The start is important because it tells everyone what to do.
