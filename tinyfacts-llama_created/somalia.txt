Somewhere in the world, there is a place called a land in the middle of the water. This land is very hot and very dry. It is a long way from any other land. People who live there do not have a big city like the ones you see in the world. Instead, they live in small groups of people who are very good at moving around on foot.

The people of this land have their own way of living. They make their own food, and they grow their own food that can be eaten. They also make their own clothes and use their own kind of wood to build houses.

The people of this land are very strong and good at fighting. They have been fighting for many years because of the way the world works. Many of them do not want to be under the control of a big group of people who sit in a place far away.

There is also a big group of people who live on the other side of the land. They are also very strong and want to be free. They have fought many times against the people who live on the land. This has led to many hard fights and many deaths.

In the end, the people of this land have to fight for what they want. They want to be free and to make their own way. They want to be able to live as they want, without the big group of people telling them what to do.
