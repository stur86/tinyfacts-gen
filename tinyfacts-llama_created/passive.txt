The word is about how things work. It tells us about what happens when you are not trying to do something.

When something happens, it starts. It begins. Then it does something to you, or to a person, or to the world. If you take a drink, you are the one doing it. You are the one moving to get the drink. If a tree falls down, it is the tree that fell down.

But sometimes things happen without you being there. A house falls down. A car goes very fast and then stops. A child is playing and then they fall down. In these cases, the house, car, or child did not want to happen. They were not the ones doing the thing.

Sometimes, things happen because of other things. A dog runs because of the wind. A cat jumps because of a ball. In these cases, the dog and cat are not the ones doing the thing. They are doing it because of the other things.

The word can also tell us how we feel. If you are very happy, you might smile. If you are very sad, you might cry. This is because your body is moving in ways that show how you are feeling.

In a big group, these words help us understand how things work together. We can say that a tree fell because of the wind, or because of the way the wind was moving. We can say that a dog ran because of the ball, or because of the way the ball was moving.

So, the word is about how things happen, and how we feel about them. It is about the order of what is happening.
