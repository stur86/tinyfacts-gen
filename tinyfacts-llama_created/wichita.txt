A city is a big area where many people live. It has many streets, houses, and shops. People go there to buy food, see movies, or go to school. It is often near a big water, a big field, or a tall green area.

A town is a smaller area than a city. It usually has fewer houses and fewer shops. People in a town often know each other and live in the same area. They might have a small school, a doctor, or a police car.

A town might be near the water or the green area. It can be close to the big water so people can go there to walk or drive. It can also be in a high place, so people can see the whole area.

Sometimes, a city and a town can be the same. They might be very close together. Or sometimes, a town might be a small area with houses and shops, but it is not as big as a city.

People use these words to talk about where they live. It helps them know if they are in a big place or a small area.
