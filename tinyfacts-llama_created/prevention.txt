Present can mean two different things.

One way is when you are doing something. If you are going to eat, you might be present at the table. This means you are there. You are not doing anything else. You are only waiting for the food to be ready.

The other way is when you are not doing something. If you are present at a party, you are there. You are part of the people around you. You do not leave. You stay to have fun. You are there because you want to be there. You are not doing anything else.

Sometimes, you might be present for a reason. You might be at a doctor. You are there to get a check. You are waiting for the doctor to come and see you. You are not going to run away. You stay because you need to see the doctor.

Present is also about being safe. If you are present while a fight is happening, you are there to help. You might stay and talk to the people around you. You help them if they need it. This is important because it keeps everyone safe.

You can also be present in your own life. If you are present with your family, you are there for them. You listen to their stories and you show that you care. You are not doing anything else. You are being there for them.

In every life, there is a moment when you are present. It is a moment when you are in the right place at the right time. It is a moment when you are ready to do what you need to do.
