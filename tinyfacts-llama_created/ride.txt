To ride means you move on a thing that moves around on the ground. You are on the front of the thing, or on the back, or maybe on the floor in front of it.

When you ride a horse, you sit on its back and hold its head. The horse walks or runs and you feel the wind on your face and your hair. You might be laughing or you might be feeling a bit scared because the horse is moving fast.

You can also ride a car. You sit in the front seat and hold onto the back of the car. The car moves around a big round place. You might be going very fast or very slow. You might feel the car rocking when it turns or the wind going through your hair.

Sometimes, you ride a thing that goes up in the air. You might sit on the back of a thing that looks like a big round thing with many lines. You hold onto the lines and feel like you are flying. You might see the ground far away and feel a big rush of power.

To ride is to move from one place to another. You might go to a park, a school, or a big city. You might ride with your friends or with a family. You can also ride alone if you have a car or a thing with many lines.

Sometimes, to ride is to be strong. If you are a good rider, you can carry a heavy thing. You might carry a box, a bag, or a big bag of food. You can also ride a thing that carries people. You are the one who helps them reach their place.

Riding can be fun. You might feel like a big animal. You might feel like you are the one in control. You might smile and laugh. Or you might feel a bit scared. It is how you feel when you are on a moving thing that is not going where you want it to go.
