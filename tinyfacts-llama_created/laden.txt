To be filled with something means you are full. If you have food, you can eat it. If you have clothes, they are on your body. If you have money, you can buy things you want. If you have a house, you live there.

When something is filled, it means that it has the thing inside. If a bag is filled with books, it is full. You can not put more books in it because it is full. If you put more, it will break.

A car can be filled with power to move it around. When the power is in the car, the car can go fast. If you fill the car with water, it will be heavy. If you fill it with air, it will be light. This is why cars have a top and a bottom so they do not break when they fill with air.

You can also be filled with love. If you love someone, your heart is full. You feel happy when you are with them. You feel safe when you are with them. You feel strong when you are with them. You feel like you can do anything because you love them.

You can not be filled with only one thing. You need to have many things to feel complete. Think about the things you love, the people you care about, and the things that make you feel happy.

The word can also mean to carry something heavy. If you are carrying a heavy bag, you have to be carefully. You do not want to fall and get hurt.
