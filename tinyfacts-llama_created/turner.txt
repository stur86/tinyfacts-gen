A turn is a way to change something. You can turn a round thing around to face other sides. You use your hands to turn it. If you turn a table, you move the round part to a new place.

A person who turns things is called a turn. They might turn a car round the road. They also turn a big round thing that holds water in a house. This helps people drink water and get clean.

In a game, a turn is a moment. One person plays a word. Then the next person must play a word. This keeps going round the table until all the words are said.

A turn can also mean to change your mind. If you start to like a food, you might change your mind and not want it anymore. You turn away from the food because it does not feel good to you.

People also use this word in their lives. If a friend asks you to hang out, you might say you have to turn your day around. This means you must do other things before you can meet them.
