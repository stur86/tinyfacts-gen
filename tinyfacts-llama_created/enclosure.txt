An area that is closed is an area that people do not go into. It is a space where no animals can run or fly. It is usually made of wood, bars, and high walls. It can be in a park, a city, a school, or a home.

Imagine you are playing with a dog. If the dog is not in the room, you are safe. You do not have to worry. This is what an area like that does for people.

Some areas are closed because of a problem. You might not be allowed to enter a room where bad people are. This is a way to keep everyone safe. Other areas might be closed because of a big problem. You might not be allowed to enter a place that has a very big fire.

An area that is not closed is called an open space. This is a room where people can go, run, or walk. Many parks and fields are open spaces. They are not closed. People can go there to play, eat, or rest.

You can also say that something is in a closed area. If you close a door, you are keeping something inside. If you close a box, you are keeping things inside. This is like a way to keep what is inside safe.
