A person who is very good at changing how things work is called a leader. They want to make the world a better place. They might want to build new roads, build new homes for people who live in the same city, or make food that is good for everyone.

Sometimes people want to change the way they live their lives. They might want to go to a different town, go to a different school, or do things that are different from what their family or friends do.

These people are often called the ones who are going around. They go from town to town, talk to people, and write things. They ask questions and listen to answers. They try to find what is wrong and what is right.

The word comes from a word that means to move in a different way. When you move your body, you are changing your position. A person who changes how things work is changing the way things happen. They are using their power to make new ideas and new ways of doing things.

It is not always easy. There are many people who do not want to change. They might like the way things are as they are. They might feel that the new ideas are not good. The person who leads might have to fight and try to make the new ideas accepted by all.
