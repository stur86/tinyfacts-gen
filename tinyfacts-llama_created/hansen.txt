To handle is to do things with your hands. You use your hands to work with things. You might use a door to open or a box to close. You use your hands to pick food or to put things on a table.

Sometimes you handle things that are small. You might hold a book or a ball. You hold it so it does not move. You keep your fingers around it.

You also handle things that are big. If you are a doctor, you use your hands to touch people. You touch their hands or their faces. You use your hands to cut or to give them a little shake. This helps your body stay clean.

In some places, if someone does a job very well, they get a small thing. They can keep it for a day or a week. They may have to work hard to get it. This is a way to show you are good at your job.

People also use the word to talk about how someone is feeling. If someone is happy, they might say they are handling things well. If someone is sad, they might say they are handling things hard. They are not afraid.
