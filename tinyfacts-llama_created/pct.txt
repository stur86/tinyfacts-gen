A Packed City
A city is a big place where many people live and work. It is usually a place with many tall houses and big shops. People go there for work, school, or to buy things.

There are three different ways to talk about a city. The first way says the city is full. This means the city has many people living there. It is like a big room with a lot of people.

The second way says the city is very busy. This means the city is always moving. Cars and buses go very fast. People walk and run. There is a lot of noise and people shouting.

The third way says the city is very strong. This means the city is a place where people come to be safe. There are police and fire people. They help keep everyone safe.

Some cities are very tall. They go up high into the sky. Other cities are very wide. They go around a big water or a big field. Some cities are very old. They have been around for a long time.

Cities have many streets. Some streets are very long. Others are very short. There are streets for cars, buses, and people. There are also streets for big cars that go very fast. People walk on those streets. They go to the store, to the school, or to see a friend.

Some cities have parks. These are big green areas where people can go. They have trees, a small water, and a big field. People sit on the green. They read books, play games, or talk. It is a quiet place.

Cities also have many houses. These are the homes of people. They have rooms for sleeping, a kitchen, and a living room. Some houses have a big room for many people to sit together. Other houses are small and have only one room.

A city can have many different kinds of people. Some people are very tall. Some people are very small. Some people are old. Some people are young. Each one has their own life story.

Cities are important for people. They give us jobs. They give us food. They give us clothes. They give us a chance to learn. They give us a chance to be near our friends and family.

Sometimes cities can be very bad. They can have a lot of noise and people fighting. They can have a lot of cars and buses. They can have a lot of problems with the police. But most of the time, cities are a great place to live.
