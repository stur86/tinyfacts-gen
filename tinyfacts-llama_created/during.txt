During is a word you use when you talk about a time.

When you say this word, you are talking about the time that is in the middle. It is the time you are in right now. You are not in the past, and you are not in the far far away time.

Imagine you are sitting in a room with your friends. You are all talking and laughing. You are all together. This is during. You are right in the middle.

You can also say it when you are doing something. If you are eating dinner, you are eating during dinner. You are in the middle of the time when you do this food.

Sometimes you might say it when you are talking about a fight. If two people are fighting, they are in the middle of the fight. They are not at one end, and they are not at the other end. They are right in the middle, where the fight starts and ends.

You can also use it to talk about something that happened before and something that happened after. If you tell a story, you might say the story happened during a time when the day was light. This helps you know which time to use.

So, during is a word that tells you which time you are talking about.
