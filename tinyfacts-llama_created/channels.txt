To have a number is to have a set of numbers. Think of a line of numbers. You can move from one number to another. You can say the number after the first number is this, the number before the second is that, and so on.

Sometimes, to have a number does not mean you have a set of numbers. It can mean you have a way to send things. If you want to send a book to a friend, you use a number. You give the number to the friend, and then the friend looks at the number and says yes, this book is for them.

There are many kinds of numbers. You can use numbers to talk about time. You use numbers to tell how many minutes are left on a day. You can also use numbers to show how much money you have. If you have ten pieces of money, you have ten numbers.

To have a number can also mean you have a place. A number can be a number of rooms on a floor. You can look at a number and see where the room is.

Sometimes, to have a number is to have a thing. If you have a number of books, you have many books. If you have a number of friends, you have friends.

Numbers are important because they help us understand the world. They help us talk about time and space. They help us do our work. They help us remember things. They help us plan for the day.
