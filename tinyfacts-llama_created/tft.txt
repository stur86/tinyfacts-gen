To fill something means to put things inside a thing so it can hold more. When you put a ball inside a box, you are filling the box.

You can also fill a person. If a man is very sad, he might fill himself with anger. He does not stop feeling sad because he is angry. 

When you fill your car, you put things inside it. You might put food for the day, or things to wear, or books to read. You fill the car so it can move.

If you fill a room, you make it full. You put many things there or many people. The room is very full now.

Sometimes you fill a small space with things. You might put many books on a small table. The table is full of books.
