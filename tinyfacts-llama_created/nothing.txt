Nothing is the empty space between two things. Imagine you are sitting in a room. If you pick up your chair, there is still nothing in front of you. You did not add anything to the space. It was always there.

Nothing is also about what happens when you are waiting. If you are waiting for a friend to come, you are not doing anything with your time. You are just sitting there, and nothing is happening.

Sometimes, nothing can mean the end of a story. If a book has no more words, there is nothing left to say. The story is done.

In some places, nothing is a word that means nothing at all. It is not a word that says you feel a thing. It is just a word, and nothing is the word.

Nothing can also mean the empty air in a room. If you leave a room and go out, the air is still there. You did not take it away. It is still there when you come back.

To say something is nothing can be a way to show that something is not there. If you say a thing is nothing, you are telling everyone that the thing does not matter.

Nothing is very important because it helps us understand what we have. When we talk about nothing, we are talking about what we do not have. We are talking about the space between two things or the end of a story.
