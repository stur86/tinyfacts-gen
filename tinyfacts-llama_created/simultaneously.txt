To do something at the same time means you do it before everyone else or while others are still doing something else. This can happen with many things in one place at the same time. Sometimes, many people or animals do it at the same time.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are all running around the field. If you are running one way, your friend is running the other way. If you both do it at the same time, you both will reach the end of the field before anyone else.

You can also do something while talking to someone else. If you have a friend who is very good at playing a game, you can talk to them while they are playing. You do not stop talking because they are playing.

Sometimes, doing two things at once is hard because you have to pay attention to both at the same time. You might be driving while you are talking on the phone or listening to music.

Doing something at the same time can help you learn faster. If you watch a movie and do your work at the same time, you see the movie and do your work more quickly. This is because your brain is doing two things at once.
