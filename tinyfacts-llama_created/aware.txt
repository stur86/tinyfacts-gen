To be able means you know that happens. You understand what is going on. It is a big word for the way you see things.

Think about your family. If you know your mom is not happy, you feel something. You are not just sitting there. You know something is wrong. You are able to feel it.

You can also be able to do something. If you learn how to play a new game, you are able. You can now do it on your own. You are able to pick up the thing.

Sometimes you are able to know what is going to happen next. You see things coming. You are able to say what you think. This is important because it helps you plan your day.

But there is another way to be able. You can feel a way inside you. If you love your friend, you are able. You know that they care about you. You feel happy when you see them.

Being able is important. It helps you do what you need to do. It helps you stay safe. It makes you know what to do when things are not right.
