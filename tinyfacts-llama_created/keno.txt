To knock a door means to knock on the door. When you are waiting for someone or something, you might knock on the door. The door is hard and does not open easily. If you knock and no one answers, you might feel a bit angry. This can happen if someone is in a room and you want to talk to them.

Sometimes, you might knock on a door that is not really a door. If a window is a kind of door, you can knock on it. You use your hand to hit the glass with your finger. You might also knock on a door that is made of wood. If you are in a place with a door, you can knock on it.

You can also knock for a drink. If you see a person who is busy, you can knock on their door. This is a way of saying, please let me go. If you knock and no one answers, you might go to another room or find another way to get what you want.

A game that is very much about the door is the game of kissing. People stand in a line and try to knock a ball into a small box in the middle of the line. If you are the one who knocks in the box, you get a number. Then, you try to guess the number of the box. If you guess the number, you keep playing. If you guess the number wrong, you have to go back to the start.
