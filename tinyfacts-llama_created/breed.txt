To breathe means to take air in and out of your body. You do this every time you move your chest or your stomach. Air goes into your body through your nose and mouth. When you breathe, your body gets the water and good stuff that it needs.

When you breathe, your heart beats faster. Your chest goes up and your stomach goes down. This makes your chest cover more air. This is why you can see your face when you are excited or scared.

Some animals are very good at breathing. They can take a lot of air and still not get tired. They can do this because they are very strong and can make their bodies very light.

You can also talk about a group of animals that are very good at breathing. These are animals that live in the same place and eat the same food. They are like one big family. They all breathe in the same way and eat the same things. This helps them stay together and live well.

If you are very good at breathing, you can run very fast. You can go many blocks in one go. This is because your body is very strong and can take in and give out air quickly.
