A word for a line of things you see on a computer is called a paper. A paper is a long line of words that you can read. Most of the time, you do not see one at once. You look at it one word at a time. You move your eyes from the top down, and from left to right. This is how you read a book.

But there is a different kind of paper. When you want to share a thing with many people, you use this kind of paper. You write the words you want on it. You put the words on the paper so that everyone can read them. You can put these papers on a table, a desk, or even on a wall in a room. This way, people can see the words without having to look at them one by one.

In the world of computers, you can also use a word for a line of things. This is called a line. A line is a group of numbers or words that is held together. You can add many lines together to make a very long group of words or numbers. You can also use these lines to show how something works.

Sometimes, people use words for lines in a different way. If you look at a picture, you might see many lines. These lines help your eyes see the different parts of the picture. The lines show which parts are close to each other and which parts are far away. This is very important for making sure you see the picture right.

In the world of the computer, these lines can also be used to make things move. A line of numbers can tell a computer which number to change. This change happens very fast, so the picture or the word moves in a very short time. This is how computers make things move on the computer.
