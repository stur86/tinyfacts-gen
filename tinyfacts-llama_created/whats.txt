What is something that is important to you? When you ask someone what, they tell you what they think or what happened. Sometimes the word is for people. You might ask a friend what they did. You might ask a teacher what you did wrong on a paper.

Sometimes the word is for things. You might ask what a book is. You might ask what a ball is. You might ask what a dog is. If you do not know the answer, you could ask again. If you are very confused, you can ask a grown person for help.

You can also use this word when you are thinking about what to do next. You might say, I need to do this. I need to go home. I need to eat breakfast. If you are looking for something and you do not know where it is, you can ask, what is that thing?

This word is very important because it tells us what is going on and what we need to do. It helps us make our lives better.
