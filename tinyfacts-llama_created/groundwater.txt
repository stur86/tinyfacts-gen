Water that is under the ground is called water that goes down deep. This water does not come from the sky. It comes from the ground, from the rain, from the old ground water, and from the water that has been there for a very long time.

When you turn on the water, the water that comes out is not the same as the water you drink. The water that comes out is usually very cold. It is the water that the ground holds. This water goes down into the big spaces under the ground where the rock is hard. It goes very deep. The water that goes down deep is what makes the ground grow. The ground grows when the water goes into the ground and gets warm. The warm water turns into air, and the air goes up, making the ground grow.

When you pull water from the ground, you might not get the water you want. You might get water that is very old, water that has been sitting in the ground for many years, or water that has been broken up by the water that comes out. You might get water that is very cold, or you might get water that is very hot. The water you get is what you need for your home or your school.
