To grab something is to take it. When you want something, you look for it and then you reach out to it. Your hand goes out to the thing. Your fingers close around it and you pull it. This is how you get what you need.

Imagine you are playing a game with a ball. You want the ball, but it is hard to get. You use your hands and your arms to push it. You pull the ball and it goes into your hands. You have it now.

You can also grab things when you are in a hurry. If a friend is going away, you might grab them. You might grab a bag or a book so they do not trip. You are taking them so they are safe.

Sometimes, when you grab something, you are afraid. You are afraid of losing it, of getting hurt, or of something bad happening. You feel a rush of fear. You want to hold on tight. Your heart is beating fast.

You can also grab food. If you are very hung up, you might grab a drink. You take a big drink and it goes down your throat. You feel the cold and the warm together. This is what it means to grab something.

A person might grab a thing that is not their own. If a friend takes a thing from you, you are angry. You feel a strong feeling of hurt. You want the thing back. You might shout or cry. This is how we show our anger.
