To write something that only a computer can understand is called a computer word. When people write this word, they use numbers and letters. These numbers tell the computer what the word is. The computer then follows the numbers and makes the word happen.

A computer word can be many things. It can tell a computer to do things like:

   shows pictures of things that are not really there
   plays music or sounds
   makes a movie
   talks to other computers
   makes a game for people to play

A person who writes computer words is called a computer person. They use these words to build new things for people to use. They are like the people who build roads and schools, but instead they build computers and the things they use to talk to them.

When a computer person writes a word, they think about what the word needs to do. They might think about what to show, what to play, or what to say. They write the word with numbers and letters, and then they use the computer to make it happen.

Sometimes people write words for their own fun. They might write stories, songs, or pictures. These words are like their own way of saying things. They use numbers and letters, but the words are not for computers.

People also use computer words to talk to other people who speak different ways. If you want to send a word to someone who speaks a different way, you might use numbers and letters that the computer can understand. The computer then sends the word to the person.

In short, computer words are like a way of talking to computers. People write these words and send them to other people, so they can understand each other.
