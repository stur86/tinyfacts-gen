To waters something means to make it move. When you put water into a glass, it starts to move. This is because water is made of many tiny pieces that are free to go. When the pieces move, the glass gets filled with water.

You can also waters things by making them wet. If you touch a wet hand, the water comes off. This happens because the wet parts of your hand are using the water from the air to help them stay wet.

Water is a very good thing. It helps keep us and the ground clean. It can make the ground soft for our feet so we do not hurt our legs. It can also make the air feel nice to breathe. When the sky is blue, it is because the sun is making the water in the sky move, making it look blue.

Sometimes, water can hurt us. When the water is too strong, it can pull us under and carry us away. When the water is too cold, it can hurt our skin. You should always be carefully when you go near water.
