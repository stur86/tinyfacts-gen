A gasps are the sounds we make when we are angry or scared. They come from the inside of our body and start very fast. You can hear them before you see what has happened. They are like big, hard breaths that go in and out very quickly. People often use them to show they are not okay or to show they are very angry.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about things that are moving very fast. A car or a dog might make a lot of gasps when it moves. It is like a big, hard breath that comes from the whole animal. This helps the animal move.

We also use the word to talk about things that are very strong. A fire can make a lot of gasps. When something is very hot, it makes the air around it move very fast. This makes the air go in and out very quickly. This makes a sound that we call a gasp.

If you are feeling very angry, you might make a lot of these sounds. You might gasp for air. This can be very bad if you do not breathe fast enough. But if you are calm, you can hold your breath for a longer time and feel better.
