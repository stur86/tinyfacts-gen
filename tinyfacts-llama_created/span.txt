To cover something can mean two different things.

One way is when you put a wide piece of stuff across something. This makes the wide piece go over the thing you are talking about. If you put a wide piece across a table, it helps keep the table clean. Or, if you wear a wide shirt, the wide parts cover your body.

The other way is when people use this word to talk about time. Imagine a line with numbers on it. This line goes from one day to the next day. If you walk across the line, you are moving from the first day to the next day. You are covering the line.

It is also used when you decide to go somewhere far away. If you have a job in a city, but you want to live near the seat, you might move to a city by the water. You are going across the line to find a better life.

Sometimes you use this word to talk about a big group of people. You can say, they all are part of the group. Or, if you need to talk about something that is bigger than one person, you say they are part of the group.

In every case, the word means you are going over something, whether it is a wide piece of stuff or a line of days, or a group of people.
