To have a picture of something means you see it. You look at a thing that is far away. You use your eyes to see the light and the colors.

Imagine you are a child. You look at a picture of a cat. You see a white further than your own hair. You see the eyes and the mouth. You feel like you are right there with the cat.

If you look out the window, you can see trees and a big blue sky. This is like a picture of the world. You see what is happening in your world right now.

You can also have a picture of something that is not in front of you. You might watch a movie on a computer. The picture is like a picture of the world in your mind. You do not see the people or the cars like you see the world outside.

Sometimes we say we have a good picture of a thing. This means we understand it well. We know what it looks like and what it means.
