To be considered good is not always easy. Sometimes, people are very bad and do not want to be held in the same way as others. This is why we often talk about what people are not. 

You might not be a good person, but you are still a person. You are not bad, even if you do not act well. You are still here, and you have your own life. 

But when we say a thing is good, we mean it is nice. Good things are often the things you want or want to do. You might want to be happy or to do a job you love. Good things help you feel well and make your life better. 

Think about money. If you have a lot of money, you are good. You have the things you need to live a good life. But if you have nothing, you are bad. You do not have enough money to buy what you need. This is how we see the world: good is about how we feel and how we act, while bad is about how we act and how we do not feel.
