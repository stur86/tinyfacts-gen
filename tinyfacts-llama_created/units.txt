A number is a set of many numbers put together. Think of how you countered with your friend. You start with one, two, three, and so on. Each number is one more than the one before it. In this way, you go from one to many, from one to two, from two to three, and so on.

There are many different kinds of numbers. Some are big and some are small. Some can be broken down into many other numbers. Some numbers have many sides. You can use these to build things. If you have many of one kind, you can build a long line. If you have many of another kind, you can build a house.

We use these numbers in our lives. A doctor tells you how many times to take a drink. A teacher says you must finish every word. A game tells you when to hit the ball. A car tells you when to stop.

The word we use is also important. If you say you have one, two, or three, you mean the same thing. If you say you have ten, you are talking about ten numbers. The word is the same way for numbers like one, two, three, and four. But the word changes when we talk about big numbers. We say one hundred, two hundred, three hundred. That means the number is much bigger than one, two, or three.

People also use these words to talk about things that are not numbers. If you say you are happy, you are not just talking about a number. You are feeling good because of the things that happened to you. If you say you love someone, you are not just talking about a number. You feel something real and true.
