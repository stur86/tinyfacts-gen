To be a person who is a part of something is to have a job that you do. You might be a teacher, a police person, or a doctor. You work with others. You use your brain and your body to help others.

Sometimes people are the ones who get hurt. They might be very angry because something bad happened to them. They may cry. They might feel very sad. They might want to be safe again.

Other times, people are the ones who help others. They might drive a car to take you to a place where you are safe. They might carry a bag with food and water. They are the ones who keep you safe.

You can also be a person who is a part of a group. You might be in a school or a team. You do things with other people who share a love for a game, a book, or a movie. You work together to reach a point where everyone is happy.

Being a person who is a part of something is important. It helps us know that we are not alone. We are all part of a bigger world with many other people who do different jobs.
