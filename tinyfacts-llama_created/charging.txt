To be a good person is to act with a kind heart. It is to give and share your time. It is to listen and to understand. It is to be safe for everyone around you. 

If you feel angry, you can stop. You can say, I am mad. You can go away. This is a way to stay safe. 

To be a good friend is to be there for someone. You can be a good friend when you do not say much. You can be a good friend when you listen. You can be a good friend when you help them. 

Being a good person is not just about being nice. It is about being real. It is about being true. It is about being strong when things are hard.
