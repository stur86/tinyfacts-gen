To open or open something means to make it ready to be seen or used. Imagine you want to go to a room. If you close the door, you are closed. But if you open the door, you are ready to go in. You can open the door with a key or with your hands.

This word is used with many things. If you are open to people, you are open to new ideas or friends. If you are open to food, you are ready to eat. If a song is open to everyone, it means it can be heard by anyone who wants to listen.

Sometimes you open a box to see what is inside. You open the top and look inside. You can also open a book to read a story. When you open a book, the words come out and you can read them.

We also use this word with things that move. If you are open to the wind, the wind can push you. If you are open to the sun, the sun can warm you. If you are open to a tree, the tree can give you a soft light from its leaves.
