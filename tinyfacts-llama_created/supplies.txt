Suppose you are planning to go on a trip. You need to buy things to help you. You need food to eat, clothes to wear, and things to do things. You may buy a bag to carry all these things or a car to drive them.

A person who works in a store is called a person who works with things. They buy things for people to buy. They also buy things for their own work. They keep the things and help people.

You might also have things at home that you already have. Your home has food, clothes, books, and other things you need. You do not need to buy these things. You just have them inside your house.

Things that people give to other people are called things to give. You give money to a friend who is in a hard place. You give a book to a young person who wants to learn. You give a thing that makes you feel happy to a person who is sad.
