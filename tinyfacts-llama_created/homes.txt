A home is a place where you live. You go there every day. You sleep in the bed. You eat food from the table. You play with your friends. You feel safe. You also have things you love, like books, pictures, or a tree.

You live in a house with a door that opens and closes. Inside, there are rooms for different things. There is a room for sleeping. There is a room for eating. There is a room for playing. There is a room for work. There is a room for looking at pictures. There is a room for reading. There is a room for being with family and friends.

You also have a back room for your clothes. You keep your shoes there. You keep your books there. You keep your favorite things there. This room helps you feel calm.

A home is not just a house. It is also the people you live with. You have a family. You have a friend. You have a group of people you like. You all work together. You all help each other. This is what makes a home a home.

Sometimes, a home can be in a city. You might live in a high building with many floors. You move from one floor to another. You see new people and new cities every day. But you still feel safe and happy because you are home.

A home is a place where you can be yourself. It is a place where you can feel loved. It is a place where you can make memories with your family and friends. It is a place where you can rest and feel safe at night.
