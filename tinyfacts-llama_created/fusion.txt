To join something together is to make it one. This happens when two or more things are put close to each other. They start to feel the same way. The parts start to work together instead of being alone.

Imagine you have a bottle of red drink. If you put a bottle of green drink next to it, they will not touch. The red drink will stay red and the green drink will stay green. They are two different drinks. But if you add a bit of water to each bottle, the red drink and the green drink start to change. The red drink gets a bit green and the green drink gets a bit red. They have become one drink. The drink now has a new color and a new way of being.

In the world, when two stars join together, they make a new star. This new star is brighter than the old stars. The two old stars are gone and the new star is the new one. The old stars are not really gone, they just changed.

In a city, when people from different homes come together, they learn from each other. They share their food, their clothes, and their ideas. This is a way of joining together. They are still different, but they are now part of the same group.

The word is important because it shows how we can change and become something new. When we join together, we are changing.
