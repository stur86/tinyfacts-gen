The word is used to talk about what happens when you do something.

If you have a plan, you might write it down. This plan is important because it helps you know what to do. If you do not follow the plan, you might not get the right answer. A plan is like a set of steps you follow to get a job done.

Sometimes, someone else has to follow the plan. They might read it and then do what it says. This is how an idea becomes a thing that people live with. The plan has been put into work, and now it is part of what everyone does.

You can also talk about how a plan changes. Imagine you want to start a new business. You might plan how you will make money and how you will give to people who need help. After you follow the plan for a while, you see how it works. You might decide to change it because you see something better. This is how plans are made and changed.

When you talk about a plan, you are talking about what happens when you follow it.
