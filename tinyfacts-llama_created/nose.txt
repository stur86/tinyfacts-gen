Your nose is a thing you can feel on the top of your head. It is very important for you to breathe. When you breathe in air, it goes into your body. Then it comes out of your nose. The air goes through your mouth before it goes into your body. 

The nose is also very good at smelling things. You can smell food, or the air you breathe, or even a person who is near you. Your nose helps you know what is happening. It is like a very good computer that helps you understand the world around you. 

Sometimes you might touch your nose. You might use your fingers to feel where it is. Or you might put a cold drink on it to help your body feel better if you have a cold.
