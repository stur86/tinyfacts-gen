A big group of tiny water drops in the air that cover everything. When the ground and the water are very close, the air gets cold and the water turns to tiny drops. These drops are very small, so they are hard to see. When the air is cold, the drops stay close to each other. When the air gets warm, the drops move away and the drops go up into the sky.

The ground can look very dark when there is a lot of this. It makes everything look like it is under a heavy white cover. The ground can also look soft and green under the white cover. This happens when the ground is warm and the water drops are very small.

This is why people say the ground is under a cover. It is like the ground is under a big white thing that keeps everything soft and dark. When you walk under it, you feel like you are walking under a soft piece of white stuff.
