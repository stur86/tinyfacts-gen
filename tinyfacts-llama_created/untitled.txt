An until is a word you use when you do not know what to write on a piece of paper. It means you did not finish what you started to write. You did some work and then you stopped. Now you have to start again.

Sometimes, you might leave a piece of paper with some words on it, but you did not finish the writing. You know the words are there, but you do not know what they say. You can feel a little sad when you see this, because you had a good idea, but you did not finish it.

You can also use this word to talk about a picture. If you made a picture and then you stopped to think about it, you might say, I did not finish my picture. It is still there, but you did not finish it.
