A well is a deep room in the ground where water comes from. It is usually made by people who want to get water or drink from far away places.

Imagine a long line of tall things that go under the ground. These tall things are like very strong arms that go down deep into the ground. At the end of the line, there is a very strong cover that keeps the air inside. Under this cover is the room where the water comes out.

When you want to get water from a well, you need to go down into the room. You might use a long stick with a long end to push the air from the cover up, and then you can pull water out of the end. You can also use a small round thing to go down into the room and pick up the water.

The water in a well is not always clean. It can be very strong and make your skin hurt. Some wells have big rocks or hard stuff in them that make it hard to get water out. Other wells might have water that is very hot or very cold. You need to be very carefully carefully when you are in a well so you do not get hurt.

Wells can be found all around the world. People build them on high ground so the water can come up easily. In some places, people build them in the middle of the ground so they do not hurt the ground. They are like big rooms under the ground that help us have water and other things we need.
