To do something makes you want to do it. If you are a dog and you see your owner, you want to run to them. If you see a ball, you want to pick it up. If you are a person, you want to be happy.

You can also do something to help another person. If you see a child who is sad, you might give them a hug. If you see a friend who is angry, you might calm them down with a word. When you do this, the other person feels better.

People often use this word in their day. They might say, I want to do this, I want to be the best. When you do this, you are making yourself a better person.

You can also use it to talk about things. Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You are excited. You want to play more. This means you are glad and want to keep doing it.

The word can mean to give a little bit of something. If you give your friend a small thing, they feel happy. If you give a small book to a child, they might be very glad.

Sometimes, we use it to talk about our own lives. We might say, I want to be happy today. Or, I want to do this one thing. This means we want to feel better and do things that make us happy.
