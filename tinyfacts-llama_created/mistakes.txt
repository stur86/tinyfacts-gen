To make a wrong thing is to make a bad move. This happens when you do something you did not plan. It is often because you did not understand what you were doing.

Think about a child. A child can not do many things right. They try to build a tall thing with wood. They put the pieces in the wrong order. The thing is broken. This is a big wrong. The child did not plan to make this happen.

Another way to say it is to do something wrong because of a tiny thing. A small word or a small move can cause a big problem. If a person thinks a word is a word when it is really a word that means something else, they are making a big wrong.

Sometimes, we make wrong things by trying to do something that is not right. If you try to cut a line but you cut the paper, you have made a wrong thing. Your hand is not the same as the paper.

To make a wrong thing can be hard. It can make a person feel sad. But we can learn from these things. We can try again. We can think about what we did wrong and how we can do it better next time.
