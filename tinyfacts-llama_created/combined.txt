To add something together means to put it with other things to make a new thing. Imagine you have a few pieces of wood and a small rock. You can use them to build a house. In this case, the wood and the rock are two different things. But when you put them together, you make a strong house.

When you add many things, you can make a lot of things. If you have many books and a few bags of money, you have a lot of books and money. You can buy many things with the money.

You can also add people together. If you have many friends, you have a group of friends. You can talk with them and play games together.

Sometimes, when you add a number with a number, you get a bigger number. If you have five and you add one, you get six. This happens with numbers too.
