To answered something means you tell someone that it is true. You use this word when you hear a story or a word. You might tell a friend that a thing is happening right now.

You also use this word to tell if you know how to do something. If you know a way to do a thing, you answered it. You have found the answer.

Sometimes, you might answer a question. If you are asked what you are, you can say you are a person, or you are a thing with a body. You are answering the question.

You can also answer a problem. If you have a problem, you answer it. You find a way to make the problem go away. This helps you and your friends.

When you answered something, you show that you care. You are telling the other person that they are right. This can make the other person happy.
