To control something means you decide how it works. You do this by telling it what to do, when to do it, and how to do it.

Think of a car. The driver controls the car. They tell the car what to turn, when to go, and where to go. They decide if the car should go fast or slow. They also decide if the car should stop at a stop sign or keep going on the road.

When you talk to a computer, you are also controlling it. You tell it what to do, like what to do with a picture or what to do with words. This helps the computer do the job you want it to do.

Sometimes, people control things that are not moving. They might control a phone when someone is calling them. They tell the phone who to call and how to answer. They decide if the person should speak or not speak.

In a house, you might control the lights. You turn them on when you get up and turn them off when you go to bed. You also decide how bright the lights should be.

A person who works in a big building might control things inside the building. They tell other people what to do, like when to open doors or when to close them. They also decide if people should go inside the building or not.

In a game, you might control a thing that is moving. You tell it to go around or jump over other things. You decide how fast it should go and how high it should jump.

So, to control something is to decide how it works and what it should do.
