To light someone or something means to make them see or feel something. When you light a fire, you make the air warm and bright. You can light a table or a book so everyone can see.

You can also light a person. If a person is feeling sad, you might say they are lighted. This means the person is not happy anymore and they feel a little bit of a dark feeling inside.

You can also light a place. If you light a room, you make it bright. This helps people see what they need. It is like giving a room a new face.

Sometimes, when people talk, they might say they are lighted by something. This means they are very happy. They are like a big ball of light in the sky.

To light something can also mean to make it look good. If you light a picture, you make it look very pretty. You might use a bright light to make colors look better. This is how a movie looks when people watch it.
