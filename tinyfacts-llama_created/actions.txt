To act something means you do it. You do something and it changes a place or a person. You might make a sound, move your body, or use a word. You might say, act like you are angry, or act like you are happy.

When you act, you tell the world what you mean. If you say you are afraid, the other person might feel afraid too. They might move away or look at you. You act in front of people. You might be a teacher, a doctor, or a police person. You act in a movie or a story.

You can also act in your own life. You might act when you are afraid. You might act when you want to say something. You might act when you feel you should do something. You act when you want to be happy or when you want to be sad.

If you act, you show the world what you think. You show them that you are the person you are. You act when you are the person you want to be. You act when you want to make people happy.

People act all the time. We act when we are happy, sad, angry, or scared. We act when we want to tell a story. We act when we are trying to get a job. We act when we are trying to be with someone.
