To put a heavy thing on someone can be hard. You might want to let them go. But if you do, they feel a pull. This pull is the word we use to say they are under a lot of force. It can happen when you pull on a long thing that is made of many parts. Each part has a pull of its own. When you pull on one part, the pull from all parts together is much stronger.

You can also say this when you pull on something that is full of things. If you pull too hard, all the things get pulled out. This is what happens when you pull on a ball. The pull is like a big pull from many people.
