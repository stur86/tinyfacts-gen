A group of people who move on a large thing that takes many people from one place to another. They sit in the big room at the front of the thing. Some sit in the front, some in the back, and some in the seats in between. They often pay money to be on the thing.

Most of them go home. Some go to see people they know. Some go to see places they have never seen. Some go to work. Some go to school. Some go to go to a place where they can have fun. They bring their things with them. Some bring books, some bring music, some bring games.

The thing has many doors. The people get in at one door. They get out at another door. They change places when the doors open at different times. They see people from many different places. They talk to the people and hear stories. Some are happy. Some are sad. Some are angry. Some are afraid.
