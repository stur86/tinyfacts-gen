When you decide something, you are making a hard one. You are looking at many things and you think which one is the right one.

Sometimes you decide to go to a place. You might decide to go to a store, to eat a food, or to see a movie. You think about the places you have already been, and you decide which one looks the most nice.

You also decide about what to do. If you have to pick a book to read, you decide which book is the best. You read that book and then you decide if you like it more than another book.

Sometimes you decide to do something bad. You might decide to play a game that makes you angry, or to watch a movie that is mean. This is not good for you, and it can make you feel bad later.

But you can also decide to do something good. You might decide to help a friend who is in trouble. You decide to buy them food or to give them money to buy a drink. This is a kind thing to do.

To decide is to use your brain. You look at what is happening and you pick one thing to do. It is a way to show that you are the one who says, in this case, what is right.
