An all is a thing that is for one person only. When you have an all, you have the word or the picture or the game that is only for you. This is because everyone else does not have it. You can only read the book or watch the movie when you have the all.

Think of a game. In one game, you can only play with your friends. The other games you play with everyone. An all is like that game. It is one game and one only.

You can also have an all with a word. If you have an all with the word, you can only say it. You can not say anything else. This word is for you only.

If someone has an all on something, it means they have the right to it. It is their thing and they do not let anyone else have it. This is why they are called the owner.

An all can be with a person, a thing, or a place. You might have an all with your name. You are the only one who has that name. Or you might have an all with a food. Only you can eat that food.

Sometimes, you can have an all with a thing that is in the air. This is called a right. You have the right to breathe. Everyone else does not have that right.

When you have an all, it is like you are the only one who can do it. You get to do it without anyone else helping you. But this all can go away if someone else wants it.
