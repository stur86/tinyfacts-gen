A realer is a person who helps people buy and move into houses. They are like middle people between the people who want a house and the people who already live there. They go from one house to another, talk to the people who own the houses, and find the one that is the right one for them.

The realer looks at what the houses look like, how old they are, and how much money it will take to pay for them. They also talk to the people who live in the houses to see if they like the place and if they want to live there. This helps the realer find the right house for the right person.

Sometimes, realest also help people who do not know what they want. They will look at many houses and tell the people who want to buy which house is the best. This way, the person who wants to buy will know they are getting the right house.

Realest work with people who own houses too. If someone wants to move out of a house, they will give the realer a key so they can go in and look at other houses. The realer will then show the new person the houses they are looking at.

In some places, people call these people people who help with buying and moving houses. In other places, they are called realest. The word comes from the word for a person who buys things.
