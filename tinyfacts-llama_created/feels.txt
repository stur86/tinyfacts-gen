To feel something means you know it is there. You do not just see it. Your body tells you. Your heart tells you. Your mind tells you.

When you feel a thing, you might be scared. You might be sad. You might be angry. You might be happy. You might feel very cold. You might feel very hot. Your body does the work. Your brain is the mind.

You feel things from the world around you. You feel the sun on your skin. You feel the wind in your hair. You feel the ground under your feet. You feel the water in a deep place. You feel the air in the morning when the world is new. You feel the love of a friend. You feel the love of a family. You feel the love of a friend.

You also feel things within you. You feel your own life. You feel your own power. You feel your own heart. You feel your own mind. You feel your own body. This is what it means to feel.

Sometimes, you feel things that are not in the world. You feel a feeling in your chest. You feel a feeling in your head. You feel a feeling in your heart. You feel a feeling in your body. This is a good thing. It helps you know you are here.

To feel is important. Without it, you would not know if something is right. Without it, you would not know if something is bad. Without it, you would not know if you are happy.
