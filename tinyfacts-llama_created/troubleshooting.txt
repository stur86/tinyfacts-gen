To troubled something means to fix the problem that makes it not work. When you touch something and it hurts, you are troubled. If a computer has a problem with the way it makes money, you might trouble it. You try to find the problem and fix it so it can work again.

Sometimes you might trouble a thing because it is not what you need. You might buy a car that is much bigger than you want. You have to find out why it is so big. You might need to send it back to the store or find another car that fits better.

You can also trouble a place. If you are in a place and the food is not good, you are troubled. You might go to another place to find better food. This helps you because you are not happy when you eat something bad.

Troubling is a way to find out why something is not working. It helps you fix the problem so the thing works again.
