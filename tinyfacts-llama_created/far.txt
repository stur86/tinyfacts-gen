The word far is a way to talk about a long way from something.

When you are near a tree, the sun is high in the sky. If you walk to the tree, you are not very far. You walk for a little while and then you see the tree. You are only a small step away.

If you walk a long way, you are very far. This word is not about how many steps you have, but about how many steps it takes to get there. If you walk to the other side of the town, you are far. If you walk to the other side of the world, you are far.
