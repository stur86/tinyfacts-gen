A person or thing that gets things from others is called a listener or a catcher. When you speak, you are sending a word. The person you talk to listens to you. They might write down what you say, or they might just hear it. This helps them understand what you mean.

Think of a phone. When you call a friend, the phone is the person you are talking to. You are the one sending your words to them. They are the one you are listening to. Everyone has a phone or a computer that lets them talk to other people.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. When you throw a ball, you are sending the ball to your friend. Your friend catches the ball. They are the one who gets the ball. This is how people work together.
