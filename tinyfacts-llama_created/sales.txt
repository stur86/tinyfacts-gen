To make a lot of money by giving people something they want is to do a job that makes people happy. This is when a person or a group of people buy many of the things they want.

Imagine you have a small store where you make clothes. If many people want the same kind of clothes you make, you can fill your store very quickly. This is because there are many people who want the clothes. You have done a job well. You are making a lot of money.

Sometimes, people buy things they do not really need. They do this because they want to get something that looks nice or something that makes them feel happy. In this case, the money the person gives is not really for what they need. It is for something they want for themselves.

Also, people can buy things from each other. They might give money to a friend or a family person so the person can buy a thing they want. This can happen many times. The money is not given for the thing you want, but for the thing the other person wants.

To make money, you often have to try to get as many people to buy the same thing. You might tell them about the good things about the thing. You might show them how good the thing is. You might also try to make the thing look nice so people will want to buy it.

Some people do this job alone. They work very hard to get many of the same thing to people. This can be very hard work. They might have to go into a store many times, talk to people, and try to change their minds. Other people do this job with many other people. They are a team. They all work together to get many people to buy the same thing.

Sometimes, a business makes money by getting many people to buy the same thing. A store might be very good at making people happy. People come to the store many times. The store keeps the money it gets. This money is not for the store. It is for the person who owns the store. The store uses the money to buy more things, pay its people, and keep the store open.

The word is important. If you buy something, you are part of a group that has a job. If you buy something that is not really for you, you are not really buying for yourself.
