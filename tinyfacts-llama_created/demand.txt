To have a high or strong need is to feel that something is needed. A person may want a drink or a bed. They might feel that their body is tired. This is a strong feeling.

When you want something, you are said to have a need. This need is important. You can't live without it. If you have a need, you will act.

You can also say that someone has a strong need. This means they are very much in need of something. They might be very sad or angry. They want the thing very much.

Some people need food. They eat every day. They need to eat to stay strong. If they do not eat, they get very sick. They want to be in a place where they can eat and be safe.

You can also say that a thing needs to be done. A car needs to be fixed. A tree needs to be watered. A person needs to work on a job. To have a need means you are waiting for something to happen.

Sometimes, we have a strong need because we feel we must. We might have to do something to make our life better. We are not just talking about things we want. We are talking about things we need to stay safe and live well.
