Pack is to put things in a bag. You put food in a bag. You put clothes in a bag. You put a book in a bag. This is how you keep things safe.

When you pack, you make sure the bag is strong. You put things in it one at a time. You close the bag when you are done. This is the end of the job.

Sometimes, a thing that is not a bag is also called a bag. A car is a bag that you drive. A box is a bag. A box is a very small bag.

You can also say that something is packed. If a person is very full, they are packed with work. If a room is packed, many people are there.
