A person who has a dog is called a dog owner. They take care of the dog. They make sure it is happy and does not get sick.

A person who has a cat is also a cat owner. They care for the cat too. They give it food and clean the house so the cat can stay clean.

A person who has many animals is called a family. A family has many kids and a mom and a dad. The mom and dad take care of the kids. The kids play with the animals.

People also have animals that they keep for fun. They buy these animals and let them live in a home with a big room. The animals eat food and drink water. Some animals can fly. Some animals can run very fast. They are very good friends to the people who care for them.

To care for an animal is to give it food and water. You also have to give it a place to sleep. You should not hurt the animal because it is a living thing. You have to be kind and show love to the animal.
