To hit something hard with your hands is to use your power. You might hit a ball in a game of a big team. You use your arms and your feet to push the ball across a big ground. The ball goes very fast and you hit it many times so it goes very far.

You can also hit things that are heavy. You might hit a tree with a big stick. The stick falls down to the ground and the wood breaks. You use your body to help the stick fall.

You can also hit things that are hard to hold. You might hit a rock with your hand. The rock is very heavy and it makes a loud sound. Your hand moves fast to hit the rock.

Sometimes people hit things to make them stop. If you are being very loud, you can hit your knee to stop the sound. This stops the noise and it makes you feel quiet.

To hit something can also mean to make it move. If you hit a ball, it goes flying through the air. You can hit a piece of paper. When you throw it, it falls down to the ground.
