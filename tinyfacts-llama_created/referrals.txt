When you know someone, you might ask them to do something for you. This is called a name. If you want to get a new job, you might ask your friend to help you. If they say yes, you are the new person. You have become the person they were helping.

A friend can also help you if you have a problem. If you have a problem with a car, you can ask a friend who knows the car. They can fix the car for you. This is how people get help. They ask others to do the work for them.

We can also use this word in a different way. If you like a movie, you might tell your friends. They watch the movie and are glad you like it. This is how people share things with each other.

When you give a job to someone, you are giving them a part of your work. You are telling them to do it. This is how people work together.
