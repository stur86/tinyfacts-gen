A small box that makes your room warm. It sits on the floor and you turn it on or off with a hand. When you turn it on, hot air comes out. The hot air goes into the room and makes the walls and the floor warm. The hot air keeps the room warm for a long time.

Some rooms are very cold. If the air in the room is very cold, the box helps. It keeps the cold air out and makes the room feel warmer. You can use this box for many hours.

There are also big boxes that are in the kitchen. These boxes can make water hot. If you put water in one of these boxes, it becomes very hot. People use these boxes to make drinks and food that need to be hot. They are very important for eating and drinking.

You can also use the word to talk about a place. If you go to a warm place, the room feels nice. If you go to a cold place, you feel cold.
