To call something is to use a phone. You use the phone to reach a person. You say the name of the person you want to call. The phone looks for the person and then you hear their voice. You talk with them. You can also call a place where food is made. If you want to buy something from a store, you can call them. They will send you a small paper with your order.

You can also use a small box that you hold in your hand. This box has numbers on it. When you push the numbers, the box calls a person. The person hears the numbers and calls back. This is how people talk to each other when they are far away.
