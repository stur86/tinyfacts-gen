To part is to leave one place and go to another. It is to be a part of something bigger than you.

You might join a team in school or a game. You are a part of that group. You help them work together to do a job.

Sometimes, you might be a part of a story. You might see a picture and feel like you are in the story. You are part of the story.

You might also be a part of a group of friends. You share things with them. You talk, laugh, and play together.

When you join a fight, you are part of it. You do not fight alone. You are with many other people.

When you move to a new home, you start to part from your old life. You leave behind the people and places you love. You begin a new life in a new place.
