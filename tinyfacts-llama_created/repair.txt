To fix something means to make it better or stronger. When a thing breaks, you can not fix it like you fix a book. You have to change the piece that broke.

If a car stops moving, you can not fix the car. You have to go to a place that fixes cars. They take the car and make new parts to make it move again. If a person falls, they can not fix their body. A doctor comes to see the person. They fix the hurt or the broken body part.

You can also fix things that are broken when you are making something. If you make a bag with a small cut, you can fix it by putting a small piece of clothes over the cut. It looks nice again.

Sometimes, you need to fix things before you use them. If you have a thing you love, you may fix it so it is better for you. You can fix a thing if you want to keep it, or if you want to make it new again.
