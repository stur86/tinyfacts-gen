To be classed is to decide what things are and what they are not. Imagine you have a big box that you use to put things into. You put books in the box. You put clothes in another box. You put things you want to keep for a long time in the box that is not for you.

If you put a child in the box with the books, you are classing the child as a person who loves to read. If you put the clothes in the box with the clothes, you are classing them as people who wear clothes. This makes it clear which box the child goes into.

You can also class things that are very different. If you put a dog in the box with the clothes, it is a strange thing. Dogs are not clothes. They are living animals. But if you put the books in the box with the books, it is clear that the dog is not one of them.

Sometimes, people also class things that are not yet decided. If you do not decide what to put in the box, you can still put it in the box later. This is because you are not sure yet.

It is important to be clear about what you are classing so that everyone knows what is going on. If you do not want to say what something is, you can say it is something else. This helps to avoid trouble later.

In some places, things that are not yet decided are put in a box with the things that are already decided. This is called a place that is still in the making. It is a way to look at what is going on at once, without having to wait for something to happen.

A person can also be classed. If you are a doctor, you are a person who does a job that helps people. If you are a teacher, you are a person who helps other people learn. This makes it clear who you are and what you do.
