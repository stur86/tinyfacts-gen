To manage something means to take care of it. You do this when you are the person in the top job or when you are a teacher or a parents. You help people, animals, or things. You keep them safe and make sure they do not get into trouble.

Imagine you have a big room with many children. You must pick who gets to play with the balls, who gets to sit in the chair, and who gets to eat from the table. You decide who does what so that everyone has fun and no one feels too much power or too little.

You might also manage money. You look at how much money you have and how much you need to buy food, clothes, or a house. You decide when to spend it and when to save it. This helps your family stay safe and have enough money for the things they need.

In the office, a person might manage a team of people. They work with them to make things happen and keep them happy. If the team does not work well, the person manages to find the problem and fix it.

Sometimes, to manage something means to keep it in order. You might clean a room, a car, or a computer. This helps you feel safe and know that everything is okay.
