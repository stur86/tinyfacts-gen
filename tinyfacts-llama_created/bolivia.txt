Bodies are places where people live. They are usually near a big water, a big tree, or a high ground. Sometimes there are many of them. They can be small or very big.

In the old days, people lived in the ground. They made their homes from wood. They made a thick soft stuff from the ground so they could stay warm. Some people built houses with many rooms. Others built houses with a big room in the middle and many small rooms around it.

Today, most people live in houses made of hard stuff. They have many rooms with doors and windows. Some have big rooms with beds and chairs. Some have rooms with a fire. In the night, they all sleep in the same house.

People in this land like to move around. They have big cars that go very fast. They go to work, to school, or to buy food. They also go on trips to see other places. They like to look at the big water, the green ground, and the high ground.

There are many different kinds of bodies. Some are very big. Some are small. Some are in the middle of the water, and some are on the high ground. Each kind has its own way of living.

Many people speak the same words, but they also have their own way of saying things. They have words for animals, for the big water, and for the high ground. Some people also speak words for the green ground and for the trees.
