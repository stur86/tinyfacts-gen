To follow is to do the things you are told to do. It means you do what someone else said.

Imagine you are in a big group of people. One person says,  Follow me. You follow that person. You walk in the same way as they do. You do not stop to look at what you see. You just follow. 

If you follow a person who is afraid, you do not see if the person is really afraid. You just follow. You do what they say, even if you are afraid too. 

If you follow a dog, you go where the dog goes. You follow the dog until the dog says stop. You do not try to change the dog. You just follow. 

It is also about doing things when you are not the one in the lead. If you follow a teacher, you listen and do what the teacher tells you. You do not try to change what the teacher says. You follow the teacher.

You can follow a ball that is rolling on the ground. You want to catch the ball. You run after it. You follow the ball until you catch it. You do not try to stop the ball. You just follow it until you get to where it is.
