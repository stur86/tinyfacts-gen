To be in a bad place all the time is to be a person who is always trying to make others feel bad. This can happen in a school, a work place, or even in a small group of people you know. The person might say mean words, touch you in a way that makes you feel scared, or make you feel like you are the only one who is wrong.

It is not okay to do this because it is a way to hurt someone and make them feel alone. If a person is being this way, you might want to tell a teacher or a parents so they can help the person stop. You can also tell a friend if you feel this is happening so they can help you stay safe.

It is also important to be kind to everyone. Everyone has their own things they are happy with and things they are sad about. We should try to understand how someone is feeling and not try to make them feel bad. If someone is being this way, we might need to talk to them and try to find a way to make everything better.
