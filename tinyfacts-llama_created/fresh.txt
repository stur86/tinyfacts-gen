To be new is to have not happened before. When you eat a thing, you are eating it. But if you eat something that has been sitting in a box for many days, you are eating old food. This old food is not good. It is bad for you.

Things that are not old are still new. A baby is very new. The baby has never seen a tree before. The baby has never felt the sun. The baby has no old things to remember.

A thing that is still new is often very good. A new car can carry you to many places. A new book is a great way to learn. A new game is fun to play with friends.

Being new can also mean feeling safe. When you are new to a place, you do not know the way. But you are not afraid. You try to learn. You ask questions. You look for help. You feel happy because you are learning.

When you say a thing is new, you are saying it is not old. You are saying it has not happened before.
