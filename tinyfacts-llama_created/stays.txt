To stays something means you keep it in a place. You might keep a book on a high book case for many years. Or you might keep a small animal in a small box so it stays safe.

When you stay, you do not move from one place to another. You stay in one spot. You might stay in a school for a long time. You stay in a room with a group of friends. You might stay with a family for many years.

Sometimes, staying is good. If you stay in a safe place, you are safe. If you stay with a friend, you feel happy. Staying can be a good thing if you are not moving from one place to another.

But staying can also be hard. If you stay in one place for too many years, you might feel tired. You might miss the feeling of moving and being in different places. Staying can make it hard to learn new things.
