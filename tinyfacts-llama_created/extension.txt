To put something out is to make it go further. When you wear shoes, the ground is hard. You can only walk on it for so long. But when you put on a shoe with a long part, you can walk a little bit farther. This is the same thing with words.

Imagine you want to write a story. You start with a small word. But then you start to write more and more, and your words get bigger and bigger. You can write for a long time and still only say a few hundred words. But when you put your story down, you can look at it and see how many lines you wrote.

You can also put a word out into the world. If you write something about a problem and then people read it, they get to know about it too. You are helping them understand what is going on.

Sometimes, we put our ideas out to people. We say, here is what I think, and I hope you will listen. Or we put out a picture of a thing that we like. We want people to see it and be happy.

In this way, putting something out is about sharing your ideas or your body with others. It is a way of showing you care about what is happening in the world.
