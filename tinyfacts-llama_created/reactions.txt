When you do something, the air around you changes. This is called a change of air. The air in your room becomes different from the air in the room next door. The air can get warmer or colder.

Think about food. When you eat a hot piece of food, your body feels warm. This is a change of air. Your body is getting the warm air from the food into your body.

Animals do this too. When an animal sees a loud noise, it can get scared. This is a change of air. The animal is changing its way of being.

People also change when they do something. If you are happy, you might laugh. If you are sad, you might cry. This is a change of air inside your body.

When you do something, you are making the world around you change too. You might move a block. This change makes the world look different. You are the one who makes the change happen.
