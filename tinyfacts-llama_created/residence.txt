A place where a person lives is a home. It is a place you go every day. You use the rooms and the rooms are for sleeping. You eat there. You do other things in the room.

A person may have one home in one city. They might have another home in another city. They can move from one home to another if they want. This happens when a person is a student. They move from high school to a new high school. They find a new home.

You also have a home at work. Some people work in a big building. They have a desk and a chair. They write papers. They talk to people. They live there for many hours a day.

A person can also live in a different kind of place. If you are a teacher, you live in a school. If you are a doctor, you live in a hospital. These are places where people go to get well.

Sometimes, people live in a place that is not their home. They might live in a city. A city is many high houses and big building. They live in many rooms in those houses. They might not have a home. They live in the city for many years. They move there to find a better life.

When you are a child, you might live with your family. Your family lives in the same home. You play with your friends. You eat from the same table. You are part of the family. When you grow up, you might have your own home. You build a new home. You make it safe and nice for yourself.
