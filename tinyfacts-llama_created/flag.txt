A long piece of clothes that you hang in the sky to show which team you are for. It is usually green, but some are red, blue, or other colors. You put this on your back so your friends know you are a part of the team. Many people also wear these on their arms and legs.

When a team plays a game, they wave this piece of clothes. It is like a big sign that says, I am here. When one team beats another, the team with the clothes gets a point. The clothes are also important when there is a fight. The team with the clothes is said to be the good team.

Some people also use a short piece of clothes to show which team they are for. This one is smaller and is hung on a tree, a wall, or a car. It is like a tiny piece of the clothes that you hang in the sky.
