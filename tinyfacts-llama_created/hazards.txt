To face a problem means you are ready for it. You know what to do. But sometimes, things are not ready for you. You do not know how to do them. You might have a big problem in front of you.

Think of a house. The house is in a place where many people live. If a big tree falls in the house, it can cause a lot of hurt. The people who live in the house must be ready. They can see the tree coming. They can try to get away. But they might not have enough time.

This is a problem. It is a thing that will happen in the near past. It is something that you must be ready for.

You can also talk about things that can cause trouble. If you drive very fast, you are going to move a lot of people. If you do not follow the road, you can hit them. This is a big problem.

You can also talk about the world. There are many things in the world that are not safe. You might hear about a big fire. You might hear about a fight between people. These are things that can happen. You must be ready for them.
