A number that is the way a many number is set up is called a number. This is like a way of writing. To make the same number, you do not change one part at a time. You keep the same parts but change how many times you use them.

Think of a room with many chairs. If you have ten chairs and two tables, you have ten chairs and two tables. You do not change the number of chairs. You just change how you are using them. You are using ten chairs and ten tables. One way is ten chairs, one way is ten tables. You are using the same number.

A number can also be a number of things. If you have one book and one book, you have two books. You have the same number of books. You do not change how many books you have. You just change which books you are holding.

To make a number bigger is to add more numbers. To make a number smaller is to take away numbers. A number of things can be one, two, three, or more. When you have one thing, you have one. When you have two things, you have two. When you have three things, you have three.
