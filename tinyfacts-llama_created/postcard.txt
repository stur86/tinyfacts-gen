To send something to someone you know.

You might write something on a piece of paper. You do not want it to stay in your room. So you send it to a friend or a family person who lives far away. When you send it, you tell the person where you are.

The paper has a picture on it. You might draw a picture of a thing you see. You might write a short story about what happens in your life. You can also write a short word. The person then reads the word or sees the picture.

Later you might get a small thing in the middle of the paper. It is a tiny picture of the place where you are. You can look at it and see where you are.

Sometimes you send a card with a small thing on it. This can be a tiny picture or a small thing that looks like a thing you want. When someone opens the card, they might feel happy.
