A person has money in their pocket. They can use this money to buy things they want. This money is kept in a safe place. Many people have money in their homes. They keep it in a box in the corner.

Money that has been given to you does not go away. It stays with you. You can use it to buy food, a house, or a car. You can also use it to pay for work you do. When you give money to a store, the store keeps the money until it is time to give you a bag of things you want to buy.

Sometimes, a group of people can save money together. They put the money into a big bag. This bag can be kept in a safe place. The group does this so they can buy a big thing together at a time when everyone is ready.

People also talk about money that has been put into a box for a long time. This money is kept safe and is not used until a person needs it. Imagine a box in a safe house. The box keeps money that was given to people to help them in a hard time. The money stays in the box until the box is opened.

When money is put into a box, it is a way to keep the money safe and to give people who need it money at the right time.
