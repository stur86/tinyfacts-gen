To fall means you move down from a high place. You can fall many times and get hurt. A tree falls down when it gets too heavy. A child falls down when they run and then they hit the ground.

When you fall, you can feel a strange feeling. Your body might go out and your eyes might close. You might hear a sound that is very loud. If you are very small, you might just fall down and stop. But if you are very big, you might hit the ground many times and get very hurt.

Sometimes you fall because you want to. You might jump from a tall building or from a top of a tall car. Other times you fall because something is wrong. You might fall down a long hall because you are afraid or because you did not follow the steps.

To fall is not the same as to sit down. To sit down means you put your feet on the floor and your bottom on the chair or the floor. To fall is when you move away from the ground.
