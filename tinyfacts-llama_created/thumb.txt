Your hand is a big thing that you hold onto. It has a long part that you can push against other things. This part is very important because it helps you grip and hold onto things.

Inside your hand, there is a small part with a long finger. This finger is very good at reaching into small spaces and holding onto things you are using. It is often the finger you use when you write, draw, or use a computer.

Your hand also has a part with a small finger at the end. This finger is good at picking up small things. It is also very good at pressing the right keys on a computer or a phone.

You do not have a second hand like some other animals do. Your hand is always there, and you use it every day. You use it to write, to draw, to eat, to shake a ball, and to hold things.

Sometimes, you might use your hand to touch your face or to put your arms around a person. It is a very important part of your body.
