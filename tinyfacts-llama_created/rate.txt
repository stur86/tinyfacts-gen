A word is a thing you use to talk about things. When you say a word, it makes your voice different. This word is important because it helps us say what we mean.

To talk about a thing, you might say:

One thing is very hard to do. It takes a long time.

This word is called a number. Numbers are like a set of numbers from one to ten. When you use this number, you are talking about how many things you have.

Another word is called a word. This word is something you say. It can be short, like a word you use when you are afraid, or it can be longer, like a word that tells you what something is. When you say this word, you are telling a story or making a point.

You also use a word to talk about how you feel. If you are angry, you might use a word like this: I am very angry! This word is like a sign that says how you are feeling.

Sometimes, when you talk about how fast something is moving, you use a word. This word is like a number that tells you how fast a thing is going. When you say this word, you are talking about the thing.

In this world, we use the word to talk about people. A good word is a word that shows you care about a person. If you say a word is nice, you are showing that you like them.

To say a word is to use it in a way that tells others what you mean. When you say a word, you are helping people understand what you are talking about.
