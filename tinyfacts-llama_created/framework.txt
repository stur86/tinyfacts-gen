A thing that holds things together is a word for a kind of way. It is a line that goes through many things at once. Think of a box. The box has lines around it. These lines are the way the box stays standing.

Imagine you are building a house. The house has a floor, a wall, and a top. The floor is the ground. The wall is the wall around the house. The top is the top part of the house. All these parts work together.

A word for a way is called a word. But a word is not a thing. It is a way to say something. A line of words can be many lines. This is why a word is called a way. It does not hold things like a box holds things. It is a way of talking.

A person who makes a word is called a word maker. Word makes are the ones who tell people what to do or how to feel. They write words and put them into a book. Then people read these books to learn how to do things.

There is also a word that means a thing that holds things together. This word is used when talking about big things that are made of many parts. When you look at a car, it is a kind of way that holds all the parts. It has lines that go through the car. These lines hold the car together.
