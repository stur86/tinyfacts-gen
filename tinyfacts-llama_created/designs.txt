To make something new is to start from nothing and use your brain to find a way. You might start with a little bit of paper. You put some lines on it. You add a few colors. You change the colors to make it look nice. You make it fit into a box. You tell your friends to look at it.

When you make something new, you are always changing. You do not keep the same thing for a long time. You try different ways to make it. You ask for help if you need it. This is what people do when they make a new game. They watch other games. They try what works and what does not. They change. They try again and again until the game is just right.

You can make a new way to walk. You walk in a long line. You go one step at a time. You use your feet and your legs. You do not go back to the same step. You keep going. You change your way. You move forward.

You can also make a new kind of food. You take things from the ground. You use them to make a new kind. You do not keep the same food for a long time. You change the food. You add new things. You make it fit into a bag.

Making something new is important. It keeps things moving. It helps us find new ways to live. It makes us change and try again.
