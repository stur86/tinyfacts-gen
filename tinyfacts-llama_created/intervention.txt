An act of helping someone or something is when you step in. You might do this to stop a bad thing from happening. Sometimes you do this because a person is in trouble. Or you do it to help a person who is sad.

When a person acts in a way that is not right, the world might get angry. This can cause a lot of problems. The act of helping them can calm things down. It is like a big hand on the chest that says, I am here and I care.

You can also act like this when you help someone who is not very good. Maybe they do something that is wrong. You might tell them to stop. This is a way to stop them from doing bad things.

Sometimes people act to help a group. They work together to fix a problem. This is a good way to make everyone safe.

It is also important to act if a person is hurt. If someone gets very hurt, you might help them. You give them air if they are not breathing. You also stay with them until they are better.

To act is to show that you care. It means you are there to help.
