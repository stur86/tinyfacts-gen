The word afternoon

To afternoon is to come after a morning. It is a time when the sun is going down and the day is ending. We call this time the afternoon.

You might feel tired after a morning walk or a long day at work. It is a time to rest your body and your mind. You might watch a movie or a game. You might eat a cold drink or a warm drink. It is a time to relax.

You might also use this word for a later time at night. You are after the evening. You have seen the sun go down, and the stars are coming out. You are ready to go to bed.

Some people say you are after the night when you are very tired. Your eyes feel heavy, and you do not want to move. You just want to be alone in your room with your books and your music.

The word is also used to talk about a later time for eating. You might say, after breakfast, after lunch, after dinner. This is when you eat your food before you go to bed. It is a time to be ready for the night.

So, afternoon is a time after a morning, after a day, after a night, and after a time for eating.
