To mean something is to know it is there or to show that you understand what it is about. You can not change what you mean by yourself. You must know that the word is the thing you are talking about.

If you say you are going to the store, you mean you are going. If you say you love your family, you mean your family. You do not change what you say. The word is the thing you are talking about.

If you hear someone say they are happy, but then they start to cry, you know they were really sad. You know the word is the thing they are talking about. You understand that they were not just happy.

You can also mean something that is true. If you say your friend is great, that means your friend is very good. You do not change what you say. You know they are great.

Sometimes, you mean something else. You might say you are going to bed, but you are not really going to bed. You are going to watch a movie instead. You are not changing what you say. You know that is what you mean, even if you do not act on it.

To mean something is important because it shows you care about what is going on. It helps you understand each other.
