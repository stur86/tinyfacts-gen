The state that is the big piece of land in the middle of the great water. It is the one where the big water and the small water are joined together. This land is very big and has many big water. You can see the water from the top of the land.

The town where the big water starts is called the city of the big water. It has a big high place where people can look out over the water. There is also a place where the small water starts and a place where the two waters join. There are many big cities and many small cities in this land.

The land is very cold in the long dark time of the year. People from the city live in the tall houses that stand on the ground. They work hard and have a lot of money. They go to the high place to look at the water and have dinner. In the summer, they go to the big water and the small water, and in the cold time, they stay in the high place and drink coffee.
