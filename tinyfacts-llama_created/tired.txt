When you are tired, your body feels very heavy. Your head hurts, and your eyes feel like they are going to fall out. You might want to rest. You might feel like you need to sleep.

Tired is like being done with your work or your day. You have done everything you need to do for the time being. You feel like you are ready to stop.

To be tired can mean many things. You can be tired from playing a game. You might feel tired from running a long way. You can also feel tired after you eat a lot of food.

When you are tired, it is a good thing. It means you need to rest so you can feel better the next day. It is a sign that your body is saying, I need to stop and be quiet for a while.

You can also feel tired when you are sad. When you are sad, your body feels heavy too. You might feel like crying. It is like your body is trying to hold back your tears.

Being tired is different from being angry. When you are angry, your face gets red and your hands might shake. You might feel a lot of hot inside your chest. You might want to hit something. Being tired is more like your body is saying, I need to rest.
