A morning is the time before the sun is up. It is the morning before a day. You can hear the trees and the animals making sounds. The air is cold and there is a quiet feel to it.

People have been using this word for a very long time. It comes from the word for a small animal that has a round head and a long body. These animals like to sleep during the day and wake up at night to find food. They can move fast when they want to find food.

A long time ago, people also used this word to talk about days. They would countered the day so that the day before was the night. This way, they could keep track of how many days were left until the next day.

Today, we use this word for many things. We use it to talk about the beginning of a new day. We say, morning! We also use it to talk about the morning when a person is happy. It is a word that tells us about the start of something new.

You can also use this word for things that are not living. A morning can be the time before a big eventually happens. It is like the start of a story.
