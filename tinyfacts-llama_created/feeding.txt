To give food is to give something to eat. It is a way to keep a person or an animal going.

When you are very tired, you need to eat. You open a box with a soft top. You pull the top down and out comes food. You take a small piece and put it in your mouth. Your body gets the food and you feel better.

You also need to give food to your baby. A mother puts food into her baby. This helps the baby grow strong and stay safe.

In a house, people work. Some work in a big building with many floors. They go up and down many times. They carry heavy things on their backs. After they work, they come home and eat dinner. They drink water and maybe drink a cold drink.

Animals also need to eat. A dog goes out at night and finds food. It might walk around the street or run through the field. When it finds something to eat, it takes it to its body. It uses the food to stay strong and move around.

The word can also mean to give a drink. If you have a drink, you are drinking it. If someone gives you a drink, they are giving you the drink.
