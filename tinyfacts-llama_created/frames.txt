To make something look good is to put it in a way that makes it look nice. Think of a picture in a book. The person who makes the picture might have many pictures to work with. They cut the pictures to the right way so they look right next to each other. They use a way to hold the pictures in a line so they do not fall over.

When you talk about something in this way, you are saying it is in a good place. A room is made in this way. You put the things you want to see in the room where the people will be. You want the room to feel like a home. A room feels like a home when everything is in order and the light comes in the right way.

This is also how you talk about things that stand still. You might say a tree is in the middle of the room because it is in a straight line with the other things. If the room has many things and the tree is in the middle, it is in the middle. The word does not say the room is moving. It just says the things in the room are in order.
