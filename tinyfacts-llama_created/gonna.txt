To gonna means you are going to do something. You are planning to go somewhere or do a thing. You are not sure if you will go, but you are planning.

You can use this word with people. If a friend says, you gonna go to the movies, they mean they are going to go. If a mom says, you gonna eat your lunch, they are telling you to eat. They are not saying you have to do it. They just want you to be ready.

You can also use it with things. If you say, I gonna buy a new book, you are saying you will buy one. You are not sure if you will buy it. You are just planning.

Sometimes, you can use it for animals. If a dog says, I gonna go out, he means he is going to go. He does not know where he is going, but he is going.

It is like saying, I am going to do something or I am going to do it later. It is a word you use when you are getting ready to move.
