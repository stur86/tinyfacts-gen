To reach out means to move toward another person or thing. It is when you try to get to them. You are not in the same room, but you want to talk with them or see them.

Imagine you are in a big room and you want to talk to the person sitting at a table with a computer. You are not in the same room, but you want to know what they are doing. To reach out is the act of taking a step toward them. You can reach out to a friend, a family person, or a person you do not know.

Sometimes, you reach out to something. You might reach out to a book or a picture. You want to know more about it. You are trying to see it. You are not in the same room, but you want to know what it is about.

You can also reach out to a place or a thing that is far away. You might send a letter to someone who lives in another city. You are trying to get to them and tell them a story.

To reach out is important. It shows you care about something or someone. It shows you are thinking about them and want to know what they are doing.
