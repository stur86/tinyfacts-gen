To stand means you are standing up from the ground. When a person or an animal is standing, they are not lying down. They are standing with their feet on the ground and their body straight up.

Imagine you are a tree. You are on the ground. You stand up and reach up to the sky. You are standing. You are not falling down because you are standing up. You are standing in one place.

If you stand and you look at someone, you are watching them. You are not looking at the ground. You are looking at the person. You are standing in front of them.

You can also stand for a very big thing. Imagine a huge building. It has many floors. You can stand on the top floor and look down at the other floors. You are standing in one place and looking at many other places at the same time.

To stand is to be ready. It is to be ready to do something. If you stand up, you are ready to go. If you stand up for your friend, you are standing for them. Standing is a way to show you care.
