To be a friend to someone is to show them love and care. This can happen when you help them when they are sad. Or, if you tell a good story, you are being kind.

A person who is a friend to everyone in a city is called a good friend. You might know them, or you might meet them through a game. You have to be kind and not tell them anything they do not want to know.

Sometimes, a person who is not a friend is one who is very mean. They might shout at you, or they might make you feel bad. This is not good. A good friend is the person you want to be near when you are sad.

You can also be a friend to animals. If you have a cat, a dog, or a horse, you are a friend to them. You give them food, or you let them sleep in your house. This is important because animals need people to care about them.

A group of people who all want the same thing is a group. They are all friends because they do not want to fight. They work together and help each other. This is a good thing because it makes the world a safer place.

In a story, a person might be a friend to someone else. If a person is very nice, the story might tell about this. The person is the one who helps the other person or makes them feel better. This shows that the person cares and is a good friend.
