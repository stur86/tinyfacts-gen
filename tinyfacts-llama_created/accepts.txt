When you accept something, you decide to take it. You do not try to change it or push it away. You let it be. You may want to say yes or no, but you are not afraid of the other person or the thing you are talking about. You are sure.

You can accept a kind word from a friend. You feel happy and calm when you hear them. You might even smile. You accept a small thing too. A book you want to read. A game you have been looking forward to. You are ready to start.

You can also accept what is happening in your life. If you see a big car in the road, you do not try to stop it. You let the car go. You are ready for what is coming next. You are ready to face it.

Sometimes, you accept a problem. You see that it is not right. You want to change it. You try to find a way to fix it. You accept that the world is not always nice. You are ready to work on it.

Accepting is about being ready. It means you are ready to let go. It means you are ready to move on. When you accept, you do not fight. You do not try to change the world. You just let it be.

Think about a time when you felt this way. Maybe you had a bad day. You were sad. But then you saw a funny picture. You smiled. You felt better. You accepted the day. You let go of the bad feeling. You moved on.
