A long time ago, people made things by cutting wood. They would use a hard piece of wood and a small thing that cuts through the wood. They would put the wood into a box with the small thing. Then they would put the box in hot air so the wood gets red and the small thing cuts through it so it can come out.

When they were done, the box had a small piece of wood that was not cut. This piece of wood was like a small door. It was very small and it could only open one way. This was why they called it a small door.

A small door is like a door on a small house. It is small and you can not open it from the other side. If you try to open it, you will break it. This is why people say small doors are not good.

If you use a small door to close a box and then you want to open it, you have to use a different kind of door. This kind of door can open both ways. It is like a door that is made so you can get out of it just as easily as you can get in.

A small door is not very strong. It does not hold a lot of power. It is not for big things. If you want to open a door that is strong, you need a big door.
