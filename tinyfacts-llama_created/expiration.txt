To go out is to leave a place. It happens when you leave something that is still inside a thing. You can go to a city or to a field. You leave your home and go to a new one. You might go to a far city or to a city near your home.

When you go out, you do not stay there. You go and then you come back. Your time is gone. You have done what you wanted. You are not still there.

A thing has a time. When a thing is full, it has gone out. If you have eaten food, you have gone out. If a book is closed, it is out. When the sun goes down, the day is gone. When the night comes, the day is gone.

To live is to be here. To go out is to move from one place to another. When you go out, you leave behind things and people. You take things with you to a new place. You also take a new place into your heart.

If something is about to go out, it is ready. The air is ready to go. A house is ready to go out at night. A car is ready to go when the keys are in the door. When something is ready, it can go.
