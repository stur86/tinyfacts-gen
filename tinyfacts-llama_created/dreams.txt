To dream is to be inside your body when you are not really there. It happens when you are very tired and your brain is not stopping. Your eyes are closed, but you are still moving. Your body might feel like it is falling down, or you might feel like you are flying.

Dreams are like pictures in your mind. They are made from things you have seen before, or from things that did not happen. They can be strange and not make sense, or they can be very clear and easy to understand.

Sometimes dreams are about you and your life. You might dream about a fight with someone, or about a place you have never been. Other times, they are about things that are not real. You might dream about a tree that is not there, or about a car that is moving very fast.

Dreams can also be about the things you fear. If you are afraid of a loud noise, you might dream about the noise. If you are afraid of a dark room, you might dream about the room. Your brain is trying to tell you something, even if you do not understand it.

People often talk about their dreams in the morning. They might say something like, a great big cat came to their bed. They might laugh and say that was the most amazing thing they ever saw. They might also say that the dream was very strange. Some people do not talk about their dreams at all. They keep them inside, like a deep inside place in their brain.
