An idea is something that helps you do something. If you do something good, you get something back. This is called an interest.

Imagine you are a teacher. You want your students to learn a big number of books. You tell them that if they learn the number, you will give them a new book each week. This is an interest. They want the book because they think the teacher will give it to them.

An idea can be about money. A person might have a plan to make more money. They think, if they work hard, they will get more money than before. This is an interest. They want more money.

Sometimes, an idea is about how to do something. If you want to walk, but your legs hurt, you might find a way to make your legs feel better. You might walk quickly or run. This is an interest. You are doing it because you want to feel better.

In a business, an idea is something that will help the business. A new kind of food might be very good. A new way to make money could be the best way ever. An idea is like a light that shows us what to do.

An idea can be about how to act. If a person is afraid, they might feel a lot of worry. This is an interest. They are afraid because they want to stay safe.
