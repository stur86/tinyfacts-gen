A thing that happens in the far ahead is called a thing that is in the works. It is not yet here. It will happen later.

If you buy a thing, you are buying it for a later time. You are not buying it now. You are buying it when it is done.

A thing that is in the works is like a plan. It is a set of things that will happen next. It is not a thing that you can see with your eyes. It is something that is only for your mind to understand.

You can buy a thing that is in the works. You buy it and it happens. You did not see it happen. You did not know about it until it happened.

If you do not buy a thing, it will not happen. You are not buying it. You are not helping it to come true.

A thing that is in the works is important because it helps us know what is to come. It shows us what is going to happen next. It is like a story that we are following.
