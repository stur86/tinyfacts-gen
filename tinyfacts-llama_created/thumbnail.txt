A tiny picture is a short picture that you can show on a computer or your phone. This picture is often very small and small, but it still has enough to tell you what is happening.

Imagine you are looking at a book. You want to see a picture of a dog so you can know what the dog looks like. You can look at many pictures in the book. But if you want to see just one small picture of the dog, you can use a tiny picture from the book.

This kind of picture is very good for small places. If you are writing a story and you want to show a picture, you can use a tiny picture. It is a good way to show something quickly.

Sometimes, you can make a tiny picture from a big picture. If you look at a picture and then press the little round thing on your phone, you can make a tiny picture. This is how you take a big picture and make a small one.

People use tiny pictures for many reasons. They can show a picture in a short time. They can use them to add something to a word. They can also use them to tell a story in a short way.
