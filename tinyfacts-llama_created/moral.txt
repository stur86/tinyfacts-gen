To be a good person means you follow the right way to live. A person who follows this way is called a good person. They do not do things that are wrong. They help others and show they care.

You can also be a good person when you are kind. If you are nice to a friend or to a family person, you are being a good person. You are showing you care about them.

Some people are good at being kind. They help many people every day. They give money, food, or clothes to those who need them. They also listen when someone wants to talk and they try to understand what the other person is saying.

Some people are good at being good friends. They are friends who listen to each other. They do not try to tell each other what they think. They just listen and show they care. This is important because friends help us feel better when we are sad or angry.

A good person is not someone who always does what they want. They are someone who follows what is right and good. They are a person who wants to be a good friend to everyone and to the world.
