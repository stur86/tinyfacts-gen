To let something happen is to give up control. You can let a ball go or a dog go. You can also let a story go. The story will happen by its own power. You are not in control.

Imagine you have a bag of food. You are the one who puts the food in the bag. But if you close the bag and leave it alone, it will stay there. The bag is still there, but you are not. You gave up control. You let things happen.

Sometimes you let people be in a place they do not want to be. You let them walk into a room even though they should not. You let them see things they do not want to see. This is also letting something happen.

You can also let people be happy. You can let a child see a funny picture. You can let a friend feel a happy feeling. When you let something happen, you are giving up your power and letting the other person decide what happens next.
