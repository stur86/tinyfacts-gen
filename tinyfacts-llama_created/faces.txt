A face is the front part of your body that you show to the world. It is the part that has the most important work. You use it to tell who you are and what you mean. If you look into a person, you are looking at their face.

Your face is made of many parts. The eyes are at the top and look out. The nose is in the middle. The mouth is at the bottom and can show a wide smile or a small, tight look. Your skin is the white part you do not see. It keeps you warm and shows how you feel.

A face is also very strong. It can tell a lot about how you feel. If your face looks sad, you might be feeling sad too. If your face looks happy, you might be happy too. People often use their faces to show how they feel.

You can also change your face. You can wear a new hair cut, a new shirt, or a new pair of clothes. This is how you change who you are and how you look. You are always changing, but your face stays the same.

A face is also a part of what people remember. When you look at a face, your brain remembers it. If you see a face many times, your brain gets used to it and you do not think about it anymore. But if you never see a face before, it is new and you remember it.
