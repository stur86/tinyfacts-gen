To fly in a way that is very slow, with the wind, is to fly. When you walk, you move from one place to another. If you walk very fast, you go far in one hour. But if you walk very, very slow, you go far in many hours.

Animals that fly can do this. A cat that flies is a very good animal. It can fly very close to the ground. This helps it find food. It can also fly very high in the sky.

When you are very, very slow, and you walk on the ground, you walk all the way around the world. This is the round way you walk. If you walk one way, and then turn, you walk the same way back, but you have walked all the way around once.
