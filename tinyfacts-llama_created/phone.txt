A phone is a box that you hold in your hand. You use it to talk to people you know. You can also send words or pictures to other people. You can even use it to watch moving pictures or music. Some phones have a small computer inside. You can look at the pictures or listen to the music using this computer part.

You can carry a phone with you all day. You can talk to your friends or family while you are walking. You can also use it to call your doctor if you feel sick. You can even use it to order food or buy things from a store.

Some phones have a small part that you can hold up. This part is called a phone number. You can call someone by putting the number into the phone and speaking the number. This way you can talk to someone even if you are far away.

You can also use a phone to write. You can type words on the computer part. This is like writing with a computer. Some phones have a tiny paper inside. You can write down your name, your name with a picture, or a story.

Many phones have a small part that you can press. This part is called a key. You press the key to make the phone do something. You can press the key to make a call, to send a word, or to send a picture.

You can also use a phone to keep your money. Some phones have a small part that you can use to pay for things. You can use this part to buy food, clothes, or things you want. This is like having money in your pocket.

In short, a phone is a box that you carry with you. It helps you talk to people, look at pictures, and do many other things.
