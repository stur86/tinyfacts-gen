The word is about something that goes on and over a big area. It is the area that is far away from you. When you look at the ground, the ground starts and ends in a line. This line is called the edge of the world.

Imagine you are on a car with your friend. You are in a big city. You look out the window and see the road far away. The road goes on and over the big area between the city and the water. You can see the water and the sky. The road is the edge of the world.

When you walk or run, the ground is in front of you. Then, the ground goes over the top of the world. After that, the ground comes back down and you see the same ground again. This goes on and over the big area many times.

You can see the same ground from many different places. If you are on a small house, you can see the same ground from your window. If you are on a tall building, you can see the same ground from your floor. And if you are in a small town, you can see the same ground from the street.

The word is very important because it shows how big our world is. It helps us see the world as a whole and understand how everything is part of it.
