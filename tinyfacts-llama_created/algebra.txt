Alone means you are not with anyone. You are by yourself. You do things alone.

When you do numbers, you use a box. This box has lines and numbers in it. You write numbers and words in a line. The box shows how many times a number is bigger than another number.

Imagine you want to know how many groups of three you can make from a group of five people. You write the numbers in the box. Let one person be the head of the group. You can make five groups with this person as the head. Then you can make the other four groups with the other four people. You write this down in the box.

You can also write the same thing with words. You write the words in a line. The line shows how many times each word is bigger than another word. This is how you do the same thing with numbers. You write the numbers in the box and the words in the line.

Alone is used when you do something by yourself. If a person is alone in a room, they are not with anyone. They are in their own space.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about how things change. Imagine you are in a room with a friend. You both look at the same thing. Then one of you looks at it for a second, and the other one looks at it for a second. After that, you look at it again. The picture is different. This is how things change when you are alone.

You can also use the word alone to mean you are not with anyone. If you are alone at a party, you are not with anyone. You are by yourself.
