When you talk about things, you often say they are important. You might say a pretty book is a beautiful thing to look at. You might say a small dog is a good friend. This word helps us talk about how much something is really liked.

To say something is a thing you love, you say it is a thing. This means the thing has a very strong feeling. If you love someone very much, you are going to be very happy if they say you love them back.

We also use this word for money. If something is very good, it is a thing you want. If you have a lot of money, you can buy many things. If you have one thing you love, you might want many of those things.

Sometimes, this word can mean a person. If you love someone, you feel happy when they are near you. If you are very happy when you see a friend, you are saying you love them. This word is about how you feel when you are happy.

It is also a word for a thing that is very small but very important. A tiny thing in your body is a very important thing. If you lose it, you might feel very sad. If you find it, you are happy because it was there for you.

So, the word is about how much you like something or a person. It is a way to show you care about the thing.
