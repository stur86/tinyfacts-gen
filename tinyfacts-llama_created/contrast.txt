To do something different means you change how you act or what you do. Imagine a room. In one room, people are all happy and laughing. In another room, people are all very angry and scared. This is a big change.

You can also do this with words. If you tell someone they are wrong, you are doing something different. It makes your words different from what they said before.

Think about a cold day. On a hot day, the air feels warm. On a cold day, the air feels cold. The air is different in two different times and places. It shows how different things can be.

Sometimes, you might see two things that are very different. You might see a tree with many big leaves. You might see a tree with only one small green thing on it. These two pictures are different. The first shows how many leaves the tree has, while the second shows how small the green thing is.

You can do this with people too. If you are very good at a game, you might do it one way. If you are very good at running, you might do it another way. Your way is different from someone else.

Using this word helps us talk about how things are different. It is a word that helps us see how the world works.
