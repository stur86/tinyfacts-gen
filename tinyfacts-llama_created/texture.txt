When you touch something, you feel the way it is. A soft thing feels like a bed. A hard thing feels like a rock. A thing with many small parts can feel like a bag of food. A thing with many long pieces can feel like hair.

Think of a wet floor. The floor is wet and it feels heavy. The floor is not hard and it is not soft. It is something that is in the middle between the hard and the soft. When you walk on the floor, your feet go over it and it makes a sound.

When you look at a picture, you see the way the light looks on the paper. The picture is made of many colors. Each color is a small piece of light. The light goes through the paper and into your eye. Your brain says, this is red, this is blue, this is green. The picture is the way those colors look.

A book has words on it. Some words are short. Some words are long. Some words tell you what to do. Other words tell you what happened before. The words are like a story.

If you put your hand on a hot thing, your hand feels warm. If you put your hand on a cold thing, your hand feels cold. The thing is hot and the thing is cold.
