An order is something you do when you need to do something. It is the way you act. You do this because you want to change a thing. You might order food, or you might order money for a store. You are telling the person you are speaking to what you need.

Sometimes, when you order food, the person is not just listening. They are doing something else. They are going to the store and buying the food. They are not waiting for you to say how to do it. They are doing it because they have to. This is an order.

We also say that to do something. If you are a doctor, you are doing an order. You are telling a person what to do. You are giving them a word. You are telling them which way to go.

An order can also be a small thing. You might order a book. You want to read it. You say, I want this book. The person who has the book is doing an order. They are saying, here is the book.

An order can be about how to act. If you want to be quiet, you might order people to be quiet. If you want to be safe, you might order the police to stay away. You are telling them what to do.

You can also order something to happen at a later time. You might order a movie. You want to watch it later. You say, I want to watch this movie at seven. The person will bring it to you when it is time.

To order something means to tell the person you are speaking to what to do. It means you want them to do a thing for you. This is an important word in our lives.
