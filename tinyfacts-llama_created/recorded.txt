To be written down is to put a story or a picture into a book. When you write something, you use your hand and your brain. You put down the words one after the other. This makes a new story.

Sometimes, you have to stop and let something happen. You might see a cat, hear a dog, or feel the sun. You might write down what you saw and felt. This is called a story about what you saw.

A movie is a story that you watch on a big box. You see pictures and hear the sound of a person speaking. It is like reading a story, but you see it all at once. You do not have to read the words each time. You just see them in the pictures.

Music is also a story. You hear the sounds of a person playing a song on a box. The sounds tell a story. You can imagine what happened before the music started. You can feel the music in your heart. It is like reading a story, but with sound.

When you say a word, you are telling others that you know it. You are telling them that you want to tell them more about it. If you say a word, others can follow you and learn. This is how people share their stories with each other.

You can also say a word that is not there. You might say, I am happy. You did not tell anyone that before. They heard you say it. This shows that you are real and you are telling the truth.

A computer can also be said. You do not hear the words. You do not see the words. You only see the words on the computer. You tell the computer to do something. It follows the words and does the thing.

To be written down is to share a story with others. It helps us learn about the world. It helps us understand each other. It helps us feel things together.
