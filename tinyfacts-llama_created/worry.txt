To worry is to have a feeling of fear that you might not be safe. It is when you are thinking about what might happen and are making a listened to your heart. You might worry about money, or whether you will do the right thing, or if you are safe in a place.

Sometimes you worry about things that you can not see. You worry about people you do not know, or about the world as a whole. You might worry about your body or your mind. You might worry about how you will do tomorrow or next week.

Worry is like a heavy feeling in your chest. You might feel it in your stomach, in your face, or in your head. It is a feeling that is always there, even when you are not thinking about it. It is a feeling that you need to act.

But sometimes, worry is not a good thing. It can make you feel very sad and scared. It can make you want to hide or to run away. It can make you want to avoid what is happening. Worry can also make you feel like you are alone. You might feel like you are the only one who feels this way.

If you are worried too much, you might not be able to do things you want to do. You might feel like you are not safe or like you are in a big, hard world. But if you learn to let go of your worries, you can feel better. You can start to see that life is full of many different things to think about. You can start to feel more calm and happy.
