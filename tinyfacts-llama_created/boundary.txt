A line that starts where two things meet and ends where they do not can be a big problem. It is like a piece of paper that is not quite clear. People often try to know where this line is so they know what to do on the other side.

Imagine you are a person who lives on the edge of a small field. The field goes all the way into a bigger field. If you want to go to the other side of the bigger field, you have to cross this line. But it is not a hard line. It is a place where the two fields meet. You might walk around the line many times.

There is another kind of line. It is not on a piece of paper. It is a line that goes through the world. It is the line that shows where one place is and where another place is. If you know where this line is, you can plan your trip. You know which roads to take, which cities to visit, and when to go.

Some things are not on a line at all. They are in a place. They are not moving around. They are always there. Think about the air you breathe. The air is not a line. It is a big, round thing that goes all around you.

You can also have a line of people. If everyone in a city follows the same way, the line is clear. People know which street to take to get to work. They follow the line and know where they are going.

Sometimes, a line can be very important. It is the line that says you are not allowed to go there. It is the line that says you are not allowed to do this. It helps keep people safe and tells them what to do.
