Reached means to find something that is very important. You might go to a place you have never seen before and find a big tree. If you walk around the tree, you reach a high spot. You can see the world from there. You feel like you are in a new world.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about their lives. They might feel that something is a great find. It is the thing that makes them happy. It is the moment when everything happens for them.

In a story, a person might say they reached their dreams. It means they did what they wanted to do. They followed their heart and found what they wanted. This word is important because it shows us that we can find what we need in our lives.

You can also reached a memory from your past. You might remember a time when you were very happy. You are finding the happy moments from your life. This helps you remember the good times.

In a way, reaching something is like finding a small piece of a much bigger story. It adds more to what you know about the world and about the people around you.
