To own something means you have it. You did not ask anyone to give it to you. You found it, or you bought it from someone else. You are the one who can do whatever you want with it. You can use it, keep it, or give it to another person.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it and it goes far away. You run to catch it. You are the one who catches it. You are the one who has it. If you do not have something, it is not yourself. It is something else.

You can own a car, a dog, a house, a book, or a piece of paper. Each thing is different. You can own only one thing at a time. If you take something from another person, you have taken it away from them. You have taken it to yourself.

To own something is a good feeling. It means you have a part of yourself in that thing. It makes you happy to think about it. It makes you want to keep it safe.

You can also own your own body. You are the one who owns your own skin, your hair, your eyes, your teeth, and your hands. You can decide what you do with each part. If you hurt yourself, you are the one who must feel the pain.

Sometimes, you might own money. You do not give it to anyone. You keep it for yourself. You use it to buy things you need or to buy things you want. Money gives you power.

The word mine is used to talk about things that are hard to find. If you see a small rock, you are the one who finds it. If you find a long piece of wood, you are the one who owns it. If you find a beautiful blue sky, you are the one who sees it.
