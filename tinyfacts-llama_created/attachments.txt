To have an attention is to pay close to something. When you feel an attention, you are focused on the thing. You give your full mind to it.

You might feel this way when you listen to a song. You close your eyes and hear the words and the sounds. You do not look away. You are completely with the music.

Sometimes you are an attention to a person. You talk to them and listen to what they say. You show them that you care. You are there for them when they need you.

You can also feel this way when you are with a friend. You talk and laugh with them. You enjoy each other. You feel safe and happy.

To have an attention is to be near to a thing. It is to be in the right place at the right time. When you have an attention, you can learn and remember. You can feel happy and calm.

But having an attention can also be hard. If you are always looking at a thing, you might miss other things. You might forget to talk to people. You might feel alone. You need to find a way to share your time and be near other people so you can feel safe and happy.
