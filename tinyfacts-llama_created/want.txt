To want something means you have a strong feeling that you need it. You might want to eat a sweet drink, or you might want to see a movie. This feeling is inside your body. You might be very happy when you get what you want.

Sometimes, you want to do something. You might want to run around the park. You might want to play with your friends. This is because you want to be happy. If you do not have something you want, you might feel sad.

You can also want something that you do not have. If you want a new book, you do not have the book. This is when you feel a strong need to get the book. In this case, you are looking for what you want.

To want something is like having a brain inside your body. You keep thinking about what you need until you want it.
