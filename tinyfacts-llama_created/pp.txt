To use the word is to make a sound.

When you say a word, your brain sends a little bit of power to your mouth and throat. This power goes from your brain to your tongue and teeth. When the power reaches your tongue, it makes the air come out in a long way. This makes a sound. 

The sound can be very different. You might say a word that means a cat. The sound of a cat is very quiet. You can barely hear it. But if you say a word that means a loud thing, like a car, the sound is much louder. It makes your ears hurt! 

Sometimes, when you say the word, you do not even hear it. It is like you are just moving your mouth from one place to another. You did not really say it. Your brain is doing it for you. 

The word is also important for animals. When an animal wants to go away, it uses this word. If you want the animal to go, you must say it. If you do not say it, the animal stays where it is. This word is like a key that opens a door in the animal brain. 

So, the word is a way to talk about sounds.
