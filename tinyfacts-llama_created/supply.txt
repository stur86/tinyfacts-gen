To give something means to put it in front of someone. This is what you do when you want to share a thing.

Think about food. If you have food, you can give it to a friend. You put it in front of them. This shows that you care about them. You are helping them. This is giving.

You can also give something you do not have. Imagine you are playing a game and you run out of time. You must find a way to finish it. You decide to run. You are doing something to get the job done. This is giving.

Sometimes, you give something that is not yourself. You might give money to a person who needs it. You are helping them. This is giving.

You can also give a voice. If a person is very happy, they might shout. This is a way of giving their heart out. When you shout, you are giving your people a feeling. You are letting them know how you feel.

A store has many things. When you buy something, you are giving money. You are giving the store the money so they can make more things for you. This is giving.
