A group of people who are not the same but are still all together is called a group. A group can be made of many different kinds of things. It can be a group of kids, a group of people who work at the same place, a group of friends, or a group of animals. They all have something in mind.

Some groups are very small. One person might have a group of friends. They do things together. They also have fun. But a big group can be many hundreds or even many more than that. It is a way to join with many others who share the same idea.

In a group, there are usually some people who are in the head. They tell what to do. There are also some people who are not in the head. They help too. The people in the head set the plans. The people who help are the ones who do the work. They work together to make the plans happen.

A group can have many different ways of being together. Some groups are very quiet. Others are very loud. Some people like to talk a lot, and others like to be silent. Some groups have a leader. Some do not. Some groups are all about fun.

People join groups for many reasons. One person might join because they have a friend who is already a part of the group. Another person might join because they want to learn more about a thing. A third person might join because they want to be part of something that helps them. Every person does things that make them feel happy.

In many places, people need to pay money to be part of a group. This money is for the work they do. They have to follow the plans set by the people in the head. Being a part of a group can be a great thing. It can give you new friends, new ideas, and a feeling of being part of something bigger than you.
