To be around something means you are near it. You might be standing in front of a house, or you might be sitting on a couch. This is when you are not far away from it, but still close enough to see what is going on.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You are on the ground, and the ball is in front of you. You can see it with your eyes. This is around. You are not too close and not too far away at the same time. You are right next to it.

Sometimes you might be around other people. If you are sitting in a room with a group of friends, everyone is around you. You are not in the middle of the room, but you are near the people you are talking to.

Being around a place can also mean you are near it. If you are walking through a city street, you are around the street. You are not standing on the side of it or in the middle of it. You are between the two lines that make up the street.

If you are around something that is not there, you are also around it. Imagine you have a friend who is far away from you. You can still hear them if you listen carefully. They are around you, even though they are far away.
