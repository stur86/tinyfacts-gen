To show a small part of something can be very important. This word is used to talk about a small piece of a big thing. It is about what is left over after you have taken away the big parts.

Think about a car. The car is made of many parts: the big round thing that goes around, the long piece that goes in the middle, the parts under the seat, and the parts under the floor. If you take away the big round thing, you are left with only the long piece, the parts under the seat, and the parts under the floor. This is like a small part of the car.

Sometimes, you need to know the small parts of something before you can understand the big parts. If you see a picture of a house, you might want to know where the front door is and where the windows are. You need to know the small parts so you can understand the whole picture.

The word is also used when you want to make something look better. If you paint a picture, you might want to know where to put the small lines that help the picture look right. You are showing the person how to see the picture more easily.
