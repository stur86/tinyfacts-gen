To do something is to act on it. When you do something, you use the things you have. A baby learns to walk by stepping on the ground. A child learns to make a fire by doing the right things.

We also do this with our jobs. We work for many years to learn how to do a job. Then we use what we have learned to make money. We do this every day.

A person might do a thing to stay safe. They might follow a plan that is made for them. They follow the steps to stay safe. They do this so they do not get hurt.

When you do something, you are moving. You change where you are. You can do this with your body or with your mind. You might do something that makes you happy. You might do something that makes you feel strong. You might do something that makes you want to be happy.

You can also do something that is not safe. This is when you do something that you know is wrong. You might do something that hurts you or others. This is a big no. We try to stay safe by following the right ways.
