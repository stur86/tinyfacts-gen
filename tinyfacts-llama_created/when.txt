When is a word that shows time. It tells us what time is now, or what time comes after.

When you say you are going to a party, you are telling your friends you will go after dinner. You do not know when the party starts yet.

When you go to bed at night, the day is over. You are not thinking about what you did that day. You are not thinking about the day tomorrow. You are just resting.

You can also use when to talk about a thing that has happened before. If you look at a book, you have read before. You have not read all the words, but you know the story.

When we say we are glad, it means we are happy. We are not sad. We are happy to be together with our friends.

Sometimes, when you talk about the past, you use when. You might say, I remember when I was a little boy. I did not know how to walk at first. I was afraid.

We also use when to talk about the things that are going to happen. If you say you are going to finish a book, you are telling your friends you will not stop reading.

When is a word that tells you what to do. It is like a sign that says, go this way. Do this. Do that.

You can also use when to talk about things that are happening now. You are in the middle of a game. The ball is going to the other team. You need to watch the game and know what is happening.

When is a word that shows you are ready. You might say, I am ready to go. I am ready to speak. I am ready to act.

So, when is a word that helps us know time and the world around us. It is a word that shows us what is happening at this moment.
