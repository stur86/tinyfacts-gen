A name is a word that tells where something is. It is like a name for a person, a dog, or a house. When you write something down, you give it a name. You use this name to find it later.

A computer has many names. One is for the word you type, another is for the picture you see, and another is for the word you hear. You use these names to tell the computer what to do.

When you want to save a picture, you give it a name. You might name it with the day, the time, or a short word. This helps you find the picture later. You can also name it with the person who made it. This helps you remember who made the picture.

Sometimes, you give a name to a song. You might name it with the year it was made, the song it is about, or a word that tells how you feel. This helps you remember the song.

When you make a new word, you give it a name. You use this name to remember what the word means. This helps you use the word later.
