To press something means to make it stop moving. When you press a door, you press it so it does not open any more.

You can also press a word. If you want to write something, you press the keys on your computer. You press the keys one by one to make the words happen. This makes the paper move on the computer.

If you press a person, you are trying to make them stop a bad thing. If you press a person who is angry, you may use your words to calm them down. Or you may use your body to show you are afraid.

Sometimes, you press a thing to make it work. If you press a door to open, it opens. If you press a ball to stop it from moving, you stop it.
