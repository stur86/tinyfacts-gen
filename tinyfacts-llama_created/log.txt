A long piece of wood is called a long. It is often very large. People make these from small pieces of wood. They cut the wood into long pieces. This is why we say a long is a long piece of wood.

We also use this word for a very tall person. If a person is very tall, we say they are tall. In this case, the word is not about the wood, but about the person.

If something is made from many long pieces, we say it is made from long. If a group of people do something, they are doing it with long.
