To make something is to start it from something else. When you make a thing, you give it life. You take things you already have and you turn them into a new kind of thing. This can be easy if you use things you already have at home.

Sometimes, you need to take many small pieces and you put them into one place to make one thing. This is how you make a chair or a car. You take many small parts and you put them into a big box so the chair can stand up and move. You do this because you need to make sure everything is right.

You can also make something that is not made by people. You can use things from the ground and you add more things to them. You then let them sit for a long time, and the new things grow in them. This is how you make food that is good to eat.

When you say a thing is made, you mean it was started from a thing. You did not just take it and put it away. You were in the middle of making it. This is important because it tells us that the thing is new.
