A small piece of paper that a person gets when they do not break a thing. When you want to do something in a place where it is not allowed, you might ask a person if you can do it. If they say yes, they give you a paper. They tell you that you can do it for a short time, and you must follow the same things that the place says.

You do not need to have a big word for this. You just need a small paper. You can get one from a store, or from a man who is in a small building. He writes the words you need to follow.

If you do something wrong, the person can say, you are not allowed to do that. They give you the paper and tell you what to do instead. This helps keep everyone safe.
