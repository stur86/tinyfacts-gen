To be real is to be a part of life. It means you are a living thing that is part of the world. You are not just a thing you see. You are a person who feels and thinks and acts.

When something is real, it happens. It is not just a story or a picture. It is the real thing that happens to a person. A real friend is someone you love and talk to. A real food is something you eat and that makes you strong. A real book is something that tells you a story so you can understand what happens in a story.

To be real is to be true. It is not about how you look or how you act. It is about how you are inside. When you are real, you are not afraid to show your face. You are not afraid to be sad or to cry. You are real because you are the way you are.

We need to be real in our lives. We need to face the hard things. We need to be kind to each other. We need to be happy when we are happy and sad when we are sad. When we are real, we build a strong family and a strong world.
