A water is a thing that you drink. It is very good for you. It makes your skin feel soft and your hair nice.

But water can also be something else. It is the blue stuff in the sky at night. It is the water that falls from the trees when it rains. It is also the water inside your body. It keeps your body warm and makes it go.

A water is very, very good. It is like a friend that never leaves you. It is always there for you.

There are also big things made of water. These are the places where the water goes. They are very big and cover many places. They are like the wide side of a big green thing.

Sometimes you see things that look like water. These are things that move but do not go over the ground. They are like the things that run across the ground in the middle of the night.

A word is a short way to write words that mean the same thing. We use these words to talk to each other. We use them to say things like, when we want to say, you are happy, we use the word happy.

You can also use the word to talk about things. If you say, the water is very cold, you are telling about the cold water. If you say, the water is clear, you are telling about the clear water.
