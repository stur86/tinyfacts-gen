To focus on something means to pay attention to it. You might be watching a movie and see a person walking down the street. You are not thinking about anything else. You are completely looking at them.

If you want to be more sure, you can try to focus on your own breath. You close your eyes and take one deep breath in. You hold it for a moment. Then you let it go. You are not thinking about anything else. You are only thinking about your breath.

You can also try this with a computer. If you want to write a letter, you are to focus on the paper. You do not look at your phone. You do not look at the wall. You are only writing. You are not thinking about anything else.

Sometimes, when you are very angry, you might feel like you want to go after someone. You can focus on them. You see them and you are ready to fight. You are not thinking about anything else.

But sometimes, you might be feeling very sad. You are not looking at anything. You are just sitting there. You are not thinking about your breath or your computer. You are only feeling the pain inside you. This is a kind of hard thing to do, because it is hard to stay still when you are sad.
