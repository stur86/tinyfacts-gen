To be a dad is to be the one who does the same things you did when you were young. He is the one who loves you and makes you feel safe.

When you are little, your dad is the one who gives you a hug. He is the one who says, in his voice, that you are okay. He shows you how to be kind to your body and your hair. He tells you that it is okay to cry and that you are not alone.

As you grow up, your dad becomes the one who is always there to help you. He is the one who tells you what to do when you are afraid. He is the one who shows you how to be strong.

If your dad does not want to do something, you might feel sad. You might want to fight him or run away. But your dad is always there to help you feel better. He listens to you and makes you feel safe.

Being a dad is not just about the big moments. It is also about the little things. It is about making your kid happy. It is about being there when they need you. It is about being the one who loves them when they do not love themselves.

Sometimes, being a dad is hard. Your kid might be mean to you or act like they do not want to talk. But you still love them and you want the best for them. You are the one who wants to make them happy.

So, being a dad is to love your child and be there for them. It is to be the one who helps them grow into a strong person. It is to be the one who loves them for who they are, even when they are being a little bit of a trouble.
