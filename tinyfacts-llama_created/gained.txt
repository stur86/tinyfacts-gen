To have something is to be in a better place than you were before. If you are tired, you are often said to have had a good night. If you are happy, you have a good day. When you have something, you feel good. You do not have to worry because you have it.

If you walk around and find a small rock, you have a small thing you did not expect. You found it in a place where you did not look. This is a good thing. It makes the day better.

If you go to a place you have never been before, you are going to a new place. You will see new houses, new streets, and new people. You might see things you have never seen before. This is a good thing. You will learn and be surprised.

Sometimes, you might not have something but you feel better. You might feel like you are doing a good job. This is also a good thing. You are moving forward.
