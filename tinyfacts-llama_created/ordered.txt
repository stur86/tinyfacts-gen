When you say something is ordered, you mean it follows a clear plan. It is set in order so that people can follow it without getting confused. Think about a big room. When you walk into a room, you do not just go in any way. You follow the signs on the floor. The signs tell you where to go and what to do. This is an ordered way of doing things.

Order also means things are in the right place. If you have a big box of books, you can order them by how they look or how big they are. This helps you find what you want quickly. In a school, you can order food in a way that helps you eat quickly. You can order food from a box or from a person who brings food to your desk.

Sometimes, you might say a room is ordered. This means everything is in its place. The windows are closed, the lights are off, and the music is quiet. This is a good feeling because it makes you feel safe.
