To put something is to move it from one place to another. You can put a book on the floor, or you can put a box in a car. You put something in a place where it is safe and it does not hurt anyone.

Imagine you have a thing you love to play with. You might put it in a bag to carry with you. Later, you need to put it in a box to keep it safe while you are not using it. You are moving the thing from one place to another.

Sometimes, you put something in your mouth to drink. You put a drink in your mouth so that the cool water goes down and the sweet stuff does not stay on your face.

You can also put things in a box so you can see them when you want to. You might put a small animal in a box to keep it safe, or you might put a picture on a table so you can look at it later.

Putting things is an important part of our lives. It helps us move from one place to another, keep things safe, and keep our things ready for the next time we need them.
