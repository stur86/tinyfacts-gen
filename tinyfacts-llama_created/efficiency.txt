To be very good at something means you do it very well. This can happen in many ways. 

When you drive a car, you want to get to your home quickly. You drive fast, and you do not stop until you reach your house. This is an act of being very good. You are very good at driving a car because you know how to use the car to go fast and get where you want to go.

In the same way, a person who works can be very good at their job. They know how to do their work and do it right every time. They are good at what they do. This is an act of being very good.

Sometimes, you might see a person who is very good at making things. They work very hard and use their hands to make a beautiful thing. You see them and you are amazed. This is an act of being very good.

Being very good can mean many things. It means you do not make a lot of trouble. It means you do not take more than you need. It means you use things that are already there in a good way.

When you say a thing is not very good, you are talking about how bad it is. If something is very good, it is the best. If something is not very good, it is not the best.
