Trails are long lines of ground that people walk on. They are usually made by many people. You can follow a trail if you want to see where people were going before or after they were there.

Sometimes, trails are made by animals. If a big animal runs across the ground, it leaves behind lines. These lines go in different directions. If you follow these lines, you can see where the animal moved.

Trails can go over water. If you walk on the top of a long line of water, you might feel the water pushing on you. This makes the ground feel soft and wet under your feet. You might hear the water running behind you.

Trails can also go through the ground. They might go over rocks or through thick green stuff. The ground might be hard and cold in some parts. Or they might go under the ground and be very dark and quiet.

You can use trails for many reasons. You can walk them to see a place you have never been before. You can walk them to be near a friend who lives far away. You can walk them to go to a school or to a place you want to visit.

In the morning, people often use trails to get to work. They walk to the city or to a place where they work. Later, they might walk home after they finish work. They might walk with friends or family.

Trails are important for the world. They help keep the ground clean. They help keep animals and other things from getting hurt. They help people move from one place to another. They help people learn about the world and about how things work.
