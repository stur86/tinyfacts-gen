To own something is to have it by yourself. It is not given to you by someone else. You are the one who gets to keep it.

Think about your home. Your mom gave it to you when you were a little boy. You keep it and make it your own. You decide what to do with the land around it. You decide who can live there and who can not.

If you buy a car, you own it. You are not a kid anymore. You can drive it on your own. You pay for it and you keep it safe.

Some places are owned by many people. One person owns a big field in a far away land. Another person owns a house in the same city. They both pay money to the same group. This group keeps the money they pay to them. Everyone in the group has the right to be there.

Sometimes you buy something, but it does not always feel like you own it. If you buy a book but someone else is working it, you do not really own it. You are only paying money for it. If you pay money to a person, you are saying they own the thing.
