A team of people, usually six, plays a game with a ball. They stand on a floor with a low wall in the middle. They use their hands to hit the ball up to the top of the wall. The ball goes very fast.

The team that gets the ball first tries to hit it. If they do, the ball goes into the top of the wall. The team with the most hits at the end of the game is the one that does better.

The team with the most points, by hitting the ball, gets more points. If the points are the same, the team that did not get the most hits gets more points. The team with the most points at the end of all the games gets the most points.

The game is played in a way that no team can touch the ball. If a player touches the ball with their hand, they must walk to a line. If they cross the line, they are out. The game continues until all the teams have played. The team with the most points at the end is the one that won't have any out.

The game is fun because it is hard to hit. The ball goes very fast and can change direction very quickly. The teams must work together and use their hands and feet. They also have to move around the floor to get the ball. It is a game that is played for many hours, but it is very easy to watch and play.
