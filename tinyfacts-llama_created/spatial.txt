A space is a big area with many places. You can not see it. It is like a huge room in your head. Everything that happens in this space is happening at the same time. This means you can not hear someone while you are looking at the same thing.

Imagine you are in a room with a door. The door is the space. Everything that happens in this space is happening in the room. You are inside the room. You can see the wall, the floor, and the other people. But you can not see the things that are outside the door.

A person can not move to another space. They can only be inside one space. If you go to another space, you leave your old space behind. This is why we say that a space has only one door.
