To be scared means you feel very afraid. It makes your heart beat fast. You might not want to be near anything at all.

Sometimes you see a big animal you do not want to see. You want it to go away. Your body gets ready to run. Your face goes white. You might cry. You feel like you are in a bad place.

You can be scared of many things. You might be scared of the dark. You might be scared of a loud noise. You might be scared of a thing that looks like a cat or a dog. You might be scared of a big thing in the sky that looks like a ball.

You can also be scared of your own body. If you see blood coming out of your mouth or if you feel like you are going to die, you are afraid. You are afraid because your body is telling you it is not safe.

Sometimes, you are scared because you do not know what is happening. You might be scared to go to a place you do not know. You might be scared to go to a place with loud sounds. You might be scared to go near a thing that makes you feel bad.

It is not the same to be afraid and to be happy. Being afraid is when you are really worried about what might happen. Being happy is when you do not care at all. But being scared is a feeling that is inside your body.
