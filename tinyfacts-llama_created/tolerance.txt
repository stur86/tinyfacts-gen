Told means to let something happen. You might be very angry when you see a dog that is not your dog. You might say,  This dog is very bad. But you do not want to hurt it. You let it go. The dog goes away and the day is safe.

In the same way, a person who is very good at something might have a bad word. They might say a word that is not nice. But they still work. They do not stop. They say the word and then they keep going.

Sometimes you might feel bad when you see something that makes you want to cry. You might want to shout. But you stay calm. You let the person or the thing be. The person or thing is still there.

Told is important. If we are too angry, we might hurt our friends. But if we are calm, we stay safe.
