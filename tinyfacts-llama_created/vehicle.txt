A thing you drive is a car, a bus, a train, or a big moving thing that carries people. It moves along the ground or along water. It can go very fast and carry many people at the same time.

You can use a car to go to work, to school, or to run away quickly. It has a seat where you sit and a back for your head. You put your hands on the sides so you can drive it. Some cars are very small and move on roads. Other cars are big and carry many people.

Cars can also carry things. If a car has a bed under the floor, you can carry food, clothes, or other things when you drive a bus. A big car is like a big house on a moving road. Many people ride these cars to go to work, school, or movies.

You can also use a car to go to a place. If you want to go to a city, you might drive a car. You use the road to move from one place to another. Some cars are very fast and go very high. Other cars are slow and need to follow many roads.

Cars are very important. They help people get from one place to another. They also help us carry things that we need to buy or that we carry to our homes.
