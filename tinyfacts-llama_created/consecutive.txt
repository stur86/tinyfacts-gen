When you say something is followed by another thing, you are talking about a line. In this line, each word is next to the word before it.

Imagine you are walking and you pass a friend. Your friend says,  How are you? You say,  I am fine. The word next to your friend is  you. After your word, your friend says,  I am fine too. This is the second word. It is next to the first word.

This is what happens when you say a story. Each word builds on the word before it. If you stop at one word, you have a short thing. If you keep going, you have a story. A story is a line of words that tell you about what happened.

When you are sitting and you watch a movie, the lines of the movie are like this. Each line has another line next to it. The first line is the start of the story. Each line is the part of the story that happens next. The story goes on and on until the end.
