A group of people who work together to make things or give money to people can be called a business. They are usually very strong at making things and at making money. They might buy the things they need to make things, or they might buy the things they want to give to people.

These people often have a plan. They decide what they want to build or what they want to give to people. They make sure everyone is doing their job and that everything is made the right way. They also try to keep things safe for the people who work for them and for the people they give money to.

In this way, a business is a big group of people who work together to do important work. They are like a team that makes things and gives money.
