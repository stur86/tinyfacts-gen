An easy word to forget

An old word for a part. Every day you use it to talk about the small things you see. A table is an easy thing. A chair is a chair. A car is an easy thing to drive. But there are many more. A rock, a tree, a dog, a sun. All these are things you do not break. They are not like a ball you throw. A ball is for playing with. An easy thing is a thing you do not think about. You can see it. You can hear it. You can feel it. You do not have to think about how it is made. You just know it is there.

You can have many easy things. You have an easy walk. You have an easy run. You have an easy walk home from school. You have an easy walk home from work. You can do this every day. You do not have to think about what you do. You just go. You do not have to think about the food you eat. You just eat. You do not have to think about the water you drink. You just drink. An easy thing is a thing you do not have to think about. It is a thing you just do.
