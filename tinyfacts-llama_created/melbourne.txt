The city in the big land. It is in the part of the world where the sun is warm but not hot. People move there to live and work. Many of them move from other big cities in the world.

The city has a big water from the top. The water goes down into the land and then comes back up again. This water is very important for the city and the world.

There are many big things that help the city. There are tall houses and high streets that go up very high. There are also big rooms with many people. In these rooms, people from all over the world work on things that help the city run.

On the water, there are many small towns. People live there and work on the water. They go out to the water in the morning and come back at night. They work with the big water and the high streets.

The city also has a big green area. People go there to walk and run. They go to the green area during the day to be away from the city noises. At night, the green area is quiet and dark.

Some people do not like the city because they feel it is too big. They want to live in a small town with fewer people. But the city is very important for the world and people need it for work and life.
