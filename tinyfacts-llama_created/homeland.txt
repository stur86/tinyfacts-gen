A home is where you live. It is the place you feel safe and happy. Many people have a home. You might have a home at your house or a home in your city. You might live in a big city, a small town, or a place in the middle of the land. Your home is where you can be yourself and feel like you are part of the world.

Your home is made of many different things. You might use wood, soft stuff, or hard stuff to build it. Sometimes, you might use old things you found or things your family made for you. You might also have a bed, a table, chairs, and a window. You put your things there so you can see what is outside and know where you are.

A home is not just a place. It is also a feeling. When you are in your home, you feel safe. You do not have to worry about bad things. You know that you are the one in the house. You can be quiet, you can rest, and you can think. When you are home, you feel happy and you feel like you are part of the world.

You might have a home in your family. Your mother, father, sister, or brother might live there. You live with them and they live with you. You all share the space. You talk, you laugh, and you play together. This is a kind of home where everyone is part of each other.

Sometimes, you might have a home in a far place. You might live in a big city or a small town. You might move there from another town because you need to work there or because you like it. You might like the way the air smells or the way the light looks. You might make new friends and find new things to do. This is a different kind of home where you learn to be a person in a new world.

In the world, we all need a home. It is where we are when we are alone. It is where we feel safe and happy. It is where we can be us and be part of the world. We need a home to feel like we are home.
