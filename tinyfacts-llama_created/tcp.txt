To talk over the phone is easy. You use a phone and the person you want to talk to is on the other end. You say what you want to say and the other person hears you.

But if you are talking to the whole world, you need something that does not let people hear you at the same time. This is why we use a different kind of talk. When you talk over the phone, the sound comes out of your mouth and into the line. The line then carries the sound to the other person. The line is like a long, strong line of tiny bits that carry your words.

When you want to send a word to someone, you send the words through the line. The line carries the words from your computer to the line that the other person is on. The line then takes the words and sends them to the person.

The line has many parts. One part is the start of the line. This is where you are sending your word. Another part is the part where the person you are talking to is waiting. They send their word to the part where you are waiting. This keeps everyone talking at once.

When you are talking to many people at once, it can get very heavy. The line has to move many words at once. If one word is very big, it can slow everything down. If one word is very small, it can go very fast. The computer that runs the line decides which words to send first.

The computer also helps decide when to send a word. It looks at the words you are sending and the words the other person is sending. If the words are very close, the computer decides to send the word right away. This helps keep everything moving quickly.

The computer does not just send words. It also helps with things like the words should go. It decides if a word is a good word for a person to hear. It decides if a word is too loud or too quiet. It also helps keep everything safe. If someone tries to send a word that is not safe, the computer stops it.

The computer also helps with things like how fast the line moves. It looks at how often the line is moving and decides if the line should slow down or go faster. This keeps everyone talking at the same time.

When the computer is working, the line seems to move all by its own. It is like a very fast car that carries your words from one person to another.
