When you are angry, your face gets red and you feel a lot of strong, bad words inside. You might shout or hit your hands on the table. You might want to hit someone else. You feel the world is against you.

Sometimes, being angry helps you feel safe. It is a way to show you are mad at something that is wrong. You are telling yourself that you are right. It is also a way to tell others that you do not like what they are doing.

But when you are angry for too long, your face stays red for a very long time. Your eyes look bright, and you might have trouble breathing. Your body might shake. This can be very bad for your body and your brain.

Anger is not the same as being sad. Sad is when you feel a deep feeling of pain. Anger is when you are angry about something. If you are angry because you lost a game, you might feel sad. But if you are angry because someone took your things, you might stay angry for a very long time.
