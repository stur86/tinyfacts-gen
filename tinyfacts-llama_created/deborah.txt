Deeper: Something that is not just a small thing. It is a big idea. You can not just have a small idea. You have to be able to feel it. You have to be able to show it.

A woman is a person with a name. A name is like a word. You use it to talk about a person. She is a person too. She has a name and a face. She is a real person. She is not just a name.

A name can change. You can change your name. You can become someone new. You can become a person you did not know you were. A name tells you who you are.

When a person dies, they leave their name behind. They leave the name of the person they love. They leave the name of the family. They leave the name of the city. They are gone. They are not around anymore.

You can also leave a name behind. You can leave a name for your children. You can give them a name. You are giving them a part of you. You are passing on your love.
