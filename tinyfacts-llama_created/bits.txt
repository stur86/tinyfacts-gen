Bits are small pieces of numbers that tell us different things. You can use them to make computers do work or to show pictures.

Think of a line of numbers on a piece of paper. Each number on the line is a bit. If the number is one, it is a bit on. If the number is two, it is a bit and two, it is a bit and two. If the number is three, it is a bit, bit, and two, it is a bit, bit, and two.

These bits can be one or two at a time. If you have one bit, you know if the number is one or two. If you have two bits, you know if the number is one, two, one, two, or two, one.

Bits are the tiny parts that make computers work. They help computers store and read numbers. When you put many bits together, you get a number. You can use these numbers to make computers do many things at once.

Imagine you have a box with many bits inside. Each bit can be one or two. You put two bits together and you get a number. Then you use that number to tell a computer what to do.

Bits are also used to show pictures. When you put many bits together, you get a picture. The bits tell the computer which colors to use. It is like a very small way of telling the computer what looks like.

Bits are very important because they help make many things we use every day work. They are the tiny parts that make computers, phones, and computers inside your house work.
