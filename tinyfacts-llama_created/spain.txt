To be in a place is to be there. You are in a city, a town, a house, or a room. You are not in another world. You are in your world.

You can also be in a place because of what happens there. If a place has a high ground, you might be in a place where the ground is high. If a place has many water, you might be in a place where the water is deep.

The word is often used to talk about a place where people live and work. We say, the city is in the place. The town is a smaller place that is near the city. The house is a small building where a person lives.
