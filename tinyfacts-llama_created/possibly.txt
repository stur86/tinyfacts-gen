When you say something is possible, you are saying that it is happening or that it will happen. This means the world can be different. 

If you throw a ball, you can do this. You are doing something that is normal. The world is telling you that this is how things go. But if you try to fly through the air, something strange happens. The world is not telling you this. This means your idea is not normal. This is what you mean by something being possible.
