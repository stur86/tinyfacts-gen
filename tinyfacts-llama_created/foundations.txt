A set of strong lines and strong ground in the ground. The lines are under the ground. They are long and thick. They go all around the house or the ground. The ground under them is hard and strong. This ground is very good for a person to stand on. The lines help the ground stay up and keep the house from falling down.

Imagine a wide road. The road is not a single line. There are many lines on either side of the road. Each line is made of hard, strong stuff. When these lines are put together, they form a strong wall. The wall is the road. It keeps people and things from falling over.

In a big city, there are many lines. These lines go under the roads and the streets. They go under houses and cars. They help keep the city safe. The lines are like the strong ground under the city.

The word can also mean a set of things that help people. In a school, the walls, the floors, and the windows are all parts of the same set. They help keep the people inside safe and happy.

People also use the word to talk about things that are very strong. A big rock is a strong set of lines and strong ground. It can hold up many heavy things.
