A long way is a long way to walk. You can walk many hundred feet. You might walk to the other side of a big town. Or you might walk to a far city. It is a very big number of steps.

There is a very short way. This is the space in front of you when you stand still. It is one foot long. You can counters the steps as you walk. If you walk one hundred steps, you have gone one hundred feet.

Some things are one hundred feet. You might have a one hundred foot book. You might have a one hundred foot room. A one hundred foot room is a room that is one hundred feet across. It is a very big room.

You can also say one hundred. This means one hundred people. If there are one hundred people, all of them will be there. It is a number that helps you know how many people are in a group.
