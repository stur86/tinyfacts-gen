When you are told you can do something, you are allowed to do it. You do not have to say yes. This means you can do what you want, but you are not the only one who can do it.

Think about a room. If a person tells you you can play a game, you can do it. If a teacher says you can talk, you can. You are not the only one who can do that thing. Everyone in the room is doing it.

But if you do something that is not allowed, you might get in trouble. If you break a chair, you might get a time out. If you say a bad word, you might get in trouble with a grown up. Being allowed to do something is like being part of a group.
