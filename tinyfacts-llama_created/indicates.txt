To show a thing means to tell it. When you say, I am happy, you show that you are happy. It is a way to expressions.

Imagine you are telling a friend, you like a movie. You say, I like that movie. You are showing them that you like it.

This word is also used with people. If a person is angry, they might show it. They might shout, they might use their hands, or they might get very red. They are showing their anger.

You can also use it when you are trying to tell someone you are going somewhere. If you say, I am going to the store, you are showing them where you are going.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about time. If it is time for dinner, you are showing that it is the right time. You are not saying, I have food, but you are showing that you have food ready for your body to eat.

In the world of animals, if a cat is happy, it is showing. If a dog is happy, it might jump up and run around in a happy way. It is showing they are glad to be there.

This word helps us understand what is happening. It helps us see if someone is saying something or if someone is really there.
