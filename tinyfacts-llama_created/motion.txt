When something is moving, it is in a place but not standing still. Think of a car. It goes over a road. It does not stop. If it stops, it is not moving anymore.

An animal that moves is also called a moving thing. It is often very fast. A flying animal is a kind of animal that can fly. It has parts that help it go very high.

A thing that moves can also be something that moves through the water. A small animal that goes very fast in the water is a moving thing. Or a big thing that goes over land is a moving thing.

We also have moving things that go around a big thing. A chair is a moving thing. You sit in it and it moves with you. This is why you do not fall down when you walk.

If something is moving, it is changing where it is. It might move across the floor or move up high into the air. It might go very far or just a little bit.
