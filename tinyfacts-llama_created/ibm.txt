An computer is a thing you use to do work. You can tell it what to do, and it does it very fast. It can do many things at once. It can do things that help you with your work, things that make your life easier, and things that let you play games with other people.

A computer is made by many people. They take small bits of hard stuff and make them into a big thing that can do many things. They use these bits to tell the computer what to do. The computer does all this work very fast.

You can give the computer a word to do. If you want it to add two numbers together, you tell it to do that. The computer then looks at the numbers. It adds them together. Then it tells you the answer.

You can also give the computer a picture. It looks at the picture and makes a new picture. It can make pictures for you that you can see on a computer or on a phone.

Some computers are small enough to fit in your hand. You can carry them with you. Other computers are very big. They stand in a room and many people work on them at the same time.

The computer is a very important thing. It helps us do work faster, and it lets us talk to other people all over the world. It also lets us watch movies and play games. It is an important part of our lives.
