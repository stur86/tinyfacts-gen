The bottom is the part of a thing that is under something else. It is the very end of a line. You might see a line with a small end. That end is the bottom.

Think of a bed. The bottom of the bed is the ground. It is the very floor of the room. If you sit on the bed, your feet are on the bottom of the bed. You are at the very bottom of the room.

The bottom can also be the part of a thing that makes it heavy. If you have a big bag, the bottom is the bottom part. It is not as light as the top part. It is the part that keeps the bag from falling over.

Sometimes, the bottom is also the end of a story. If a story starts on a small thing, it often ends on the bottom. This is why the end of a story usually has a person who falls down or a thing that falls. It is a way to show how the story ends.
