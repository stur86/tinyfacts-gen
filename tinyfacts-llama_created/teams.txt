To play a team game is to work together with many other people. You are not alone. You are part of a group with other kids or people who are also playing a game. The team has a leader. This person helps the group know what to do and when.

A team has many different jobs. One person might be the one who hits a ball. Another person might be the one who throws the ball. Some people might run around the field. Some might stand in front of a big line. Everyone does their job so that the team can play well.

When the team is playing, they all work together. They shout out words to help them know what to do. They might shout when they hit the ball or when someone makes a good play. They are all happy to work together and help the team winding.

There are many different kinds of teams. You might play a game with your friends. You might play a game with your school. You might play a game with other kids in a city. Some teams are big. Some are small. Some have many people. Some have few people.
