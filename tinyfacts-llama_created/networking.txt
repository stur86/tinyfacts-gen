To put things together and make them work is what this word means. It is about sharing your things with other people so you can get help when you need it.

Imagine you have a small group of friends and you want to talk to them about something. You could write a letter to each one or send them a quick word on your phone. But if you have many friends, it would be much easier to find one person and talk to them. That is what happens when you start to use this word.

You can use it to talk to people you know in your school, your city, or your place of work. You send them a small piece of your brain that says, I am here, and I need help. Then, they send you a small piece of their brain that they are also here, and you can talk to them. This way, you are sharing your brain and your heart with many people.

It is like building a group of people who are all working together. Each person has a small part to do. One person sends you a word, another person sends you a picture, and another person sends you a story. You are all working together to make something new.

This word is used in many ways. You might use it when you are in school to talk to your teacher about a problem you have. You might use it when you are in a job to ask for help from a friend. It is a way to show that you care about other people and that you are happy to share your brain with them.
