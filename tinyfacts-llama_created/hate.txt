To hate something means to feel very angry or sad because of it. You might feel this way when you are afraid for your life or when someone does something that makes you want to fight. When you hate something, you want it to be gone.

Sometimes, you hate a person. You might feel this way because they did something wrong. Maybe they took your thing or said something mean. You might feel like they will not be nice to you again. You might want to get away from them.

You can also hate things that are big and heavy. If a house falls and hits you, you might feel this way. It is a very bad feeling.

You can hate things that are small and hard. You might hate a book if it is too hard to read. Or you might hate a dog if it does not want to play with you. These are also strong feels.

To hate something does not mean you want to kill it. You do not want to hurt it. You just want it to be gone so you can feel safe.
