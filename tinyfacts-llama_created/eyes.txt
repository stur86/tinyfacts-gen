Your eyes are the parts in your head that look around you. They help you see what is in front of you. You can not feel them, but you use them every second of the day.

Imagine you are looking at a bright light. You can not see the light, but the light helps you see the world around you. Your eyes help you see people, trees, cars, and other things that are in front of you. They can see very far away. If you look at something very tiny, like a tiny thing on the floor, your eyes see it very well.

Inside your eyes there are many tiny parts. One part sees light. Another part sees colors. There is also a part that sees things that are very small. Your eyes are the way you see the world. Without them, you would not be able to see the world around you.
