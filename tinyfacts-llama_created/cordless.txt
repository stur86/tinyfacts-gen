A long time ago, when things were very small, you could use a long piece of stuff to make your life better. This stuff would go from one part of your home to another part. You would carry it in your hand and use it to turn things on and off. You could even use it to move your chair when you sat down to eat. People liked this because they did not have to go out to a room every time they wanted something.

Today, these long pieces of stuff are not as small as they used to be. Now, we have something that does not need any of them. It is a thing that you can hold in your hand and use to do many things. You can use it to talk to a friend, to send a picture to a friend, to make a song, or to make a phone call. You do not need to carry a long piece of stuff with you.

This thing is called a phone. It is like a small computer that can do many things. You can use it to talk to people far away. You can use it to look at pictures. You can even use it to make things happen on your computer. It is very small, but it does a lot of work for you.

We call this thing a phone because it is like a long piece of stuff that only one person can use at a time. You can have many phones in your home, and each one is for one person. This is why we say it is a phone, not because it is long, but because it is only for one person at a time.
