Tracks are things that people walk on. They are lines in the ground. When you walk, your feet leave behind a mark on the ground. This mark is a track.

Tracks can be for many reasons. A long time ago, people used them to go on a trip. They followed a long line to see where they were. They walked for a day, and then they found their way to a new place. This made them feel like they were following a story.

Today, you might see tracks in a field. If a dog runs, it can leave behind tracks. A tree can also leave tracks. The water from a tree can go down the ground and come back up again, leaving a line where it went.

If you look at the ground, you can see these lines. They are lines that show where a person walked. Sometimes, people use these lines to find their way. They look at the line to figure out where they are. It can help them when they are lost.

You can also see tracks in the water. When a person walks in the water, their feet leave behind lines. These lines are like a story of where they went. They can help us see where something has gone.
