To get something is when you do a thing that was asked for. When you are not in the way of a person, you can get a thing. This is how you get a job, a house, or a car. You do not have to wait for a long time.

You can also get something from the group. When everyone wants to be the top person, the top person gets to be the top person. This is why you have a leader. The leader is the one who does the things for everyone else.

You can also get something from your family. When you are the one who does not follow the way of the family, the family still loves you. This is a good feeling.

Sometimes, you might get a thing that you did not ask for. You might get money you did not have. You might get a thing that you do not like. It is hard to understand why this happens.
