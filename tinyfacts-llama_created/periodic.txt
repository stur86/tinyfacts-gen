A thing that goes round is called a round thing. We call a round ball a ball. We call a round thing that goes round the world a round world. The sun is a round thing. The round thing that goes round a street is a car. The round thing that goes round the floor is a chair.

Things that go round are often very good at moving. They do not stop and stay in one place. The round thing that goes round the world is the biggest round thing of all. You can see it in the sky at night. It is very big and very bright.

The word comes from the word of a dog. A dog does not like to run round a big round thing. It likes to be close to the person it loves. But a round thing is always moving and never stops. It is always going somewhere.
