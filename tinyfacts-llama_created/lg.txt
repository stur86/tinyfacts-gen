An Largest is the thing that is the biggest. It is the top of a tall tree. It is the part of a house that is very high. It is the thing that makes a big sound.

Sometimes we say a house is the largest. This means the house is the biggest house in the world. If you have a big house, you are the largest person in your town.

We also say a dog is the largest. This means the dog is the biggest animal in the room. If you have many big dogs, you are the largest person in the room.

If you have a very big bag, you can say it is the largest bag. This means the bag is the biggest bag you have ever seen.

Sometimes, we say a big thing. This means something is very large. It is the biggest car, the biggest water, the biggest ball, the biggest rock, the biggest water, the biggest house, or the biggest dog.
