A money thing you keep for later.

You buy things you want. You pay money. If you save the money, you keep it in a safe place. You do this so you do not need to buy the thing now. If you have money in your pocket, you are free to do what you want.

You can also save money for a big thing. If you want a car, you might not have enough money right now. But if you save your money, you can buy it later. This is a good thing.

Sometimes, you save money for a hard day. If you have a job that is very hard, you might save your money so you can buy food or pay your money. This helps you feel safe and stronger.

You can also save your computer time. If you play games for a long time, you might not want to play anymore. You can save the game so you can pick it up later. This helps you stay on your computer.

A person who saves money is called a money person. They like to keep their money safe. They do not spend it right away.
