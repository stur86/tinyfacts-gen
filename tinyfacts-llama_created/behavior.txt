To act or to do something is to be in the world. The word is very important when you talk about a person, a dog, a group of people, or a thing. It is about what they do.

When you walk, you act. You step from one foot to the other. This is your way of moving. It is how you use your body to get from one place to another. You act because you want to go somewhere.

An animal acts when it wants to eat. It looks for food. It moves to a place where there is food. It takes a bite. This is the way it lives. An animal acts.

A person acts when they tell you something. They say, I am happy. They act on their feeling. They tell you how they are. They act in front of others.

You can act like a person. You can act like a dog. You can act like a computer. You can act like a tree. You act when you follow what you think is right.

Sometimes, you act against what you think. You might act in a way that is bad. You might act like you want to hurt someone. This can be very hard to do. But you can also act in a way that is good. You can act like you care about someone. You act when you want to help them.

To act is to do something. It is about moving, being, and doing. It is how you live your life.
