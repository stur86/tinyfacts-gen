To give an order
When you are the head of a group, you need to tell the people what to do. You must say what they have to do, how they should do it, and when they must do it. You give the order to take something. If you say to pick something from a box, the person must pick the right one. You are the one who makes sure everyone follows the plan.

You also have to be sure everyone knows who you are and who you are leading. If you are the one who should give the order, everyone should know what you say. This helps you stay in control and keep things running well.

Sometimes, you need to give a new order. You might need to change what is happening because something has gone wrong. You tell everyone what they have to do now. You change the plan to fix the problem. This is like starting a new order.

Sometimes, you need to give an order to someone who is not the head. You tell them what to do and how to do it. This is like giving a new job to someone. They must follow your order and do what you say.

You are the one who is in control. You are the head of the group. You make sure everyone is following the plan. You are the one who decides what to do and when.
