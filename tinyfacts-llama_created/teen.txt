A person who is young and still learning how to act is called a young person. They might be between the ages of ten and twenty. This age is big, but it is also a time of growing and trying to find out who they are.

When a person is still very young, they look like a baby. They might not be able to say the words they want to. They might need to be told many times. As they grow older, they start to look like a real person. They learn to walk, to talk, and to do things that other young people do. They also learn about their own body and how it works.
