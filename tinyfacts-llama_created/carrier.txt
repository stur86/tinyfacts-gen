A carrier is a person or thing that carries something. 

Imagine you have a bag with food in it. You want to give this food to your friend who lives far away. You can not carry the bag because it is too heavy. So, you ask a friend to carry it for you. This friend puts the bag on their back and walks to your house. When the friend arrives, they open the bag and give the food to your friend. They then put the bag back on their own back and goes home.

In a different way, a carrier is a person who carries people. When you go to a far city, you need a person to drive you. The person is the carrier. They drive a car, bus, or other kind of moving thing. The person follows a set of roads to reach your city. This person is the one who carries you from one place to another.

Sometimes, a carrier is a thing that carries people. Imagine a person who is very strong. They can carry a heavy box or a person for a long time. This person is the carrier. They use their power and their body to carry the box or the person.

In a school, there are people who carry books and papers for the students. They carry these things on their backs or in bags. When the students are not at school, these people carry the books and papers so the school can have them ready for the next day.

In the air, a carrier is a thing that carries people from one city to another. It is like a car, but it flies through the air. When you fly on a carrier, you may feel like you are moving very fast. You might see many cities and towns from the sky.

To carry something means to move it from one place to another. It is a way to get things from one place to another.
