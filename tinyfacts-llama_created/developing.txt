To start something is to begin. This means you start from a point and you start moving toward a point that is farther away. You do not know what will happen, but you start anyway.

When you are young and you learn how to walk, you are starting to move. You are getting closer to the place you want to go. As you get older and you learn to do more things, you are also starting to learn how to do them right.

Some things are starting. A new baby is starting. A house is being built. A person is starting a new job. When something starts, it is moving toward something else. It is going to happen or become a thing.

There is also a word for when you are not starting anything. You are waiting. You are doing nothing. You might feel a little scared or a little worried. This is because you are not sure what will happen next.

When you are starting something new, you might feel a little nervous. You might think, What if I make a wrong move? What if I do not do it right? You are trying to do something for the first time. This is a good thing. It means you are learning and growing.

To start something is to take a step. It is to move from one place to another. It is a step into the world.
