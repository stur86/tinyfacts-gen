To offer something is to give it to someone. It is when you want to help someone. You do this because you care about them. You can offer a thing you have. You can give money, a book, or a smile.

Imagine you are a friend. You feel sad because a person you like is not doing well. You want to help. You go to their house. You bring them a cup of coffee. You say, I am here for you. You are offering your help.

You can also offer to do a job. If you know how to build a table, you can offer to do it for someone. They need a table and you say, I will build it for you. You are offering your time and your work.

Sometimes, you can only offer what you have. You are not strong enough to do something. But you still try. You offer yourself to a person. You say, I am here to listen to you. You are offering your heart.

An offer is a way to show you care. It is when you want to help another person.
