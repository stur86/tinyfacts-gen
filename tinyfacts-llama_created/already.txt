The word already is a big word that people use to talk about things that are done before you start talking about them. When you say something, you are doing it. If you say you are going to the store, you are already going. You are doing it right now.

When you use this word, you do not have to wait for something to happen. You can say you are already there when you are standing in front of a house. You are there. You did not wait for the house to come before you said you were there.

You can also use this word to talk about time. If you are already at school, it means you are there. If you are already in the middle of a story, you are already there. The word is used in many different ways.

Sometimes you use it to say you are doing something but you did it before you started. If you say you are going to the park, but you are already in the house, you are already doing what you said you would do. This word is very important when you talk about what you are doing.
