To talk is to share your ideas with others while you are still in the room. You are speaking to someone in a quiet room, or you are speaking to a group of people. You use your words to show what you think, how you feel, or what you want.

When you talk, you are not alone. Many people hear you at the same time. This can happen with many people talking at once in a big room or with one person talking to many people through a computer. You can hear their words too.

Talking can be very important. It helps us to understand each other and to share our ideas. You can talk about a game you played, a movie you watched, or a book you read. People listen to these stories so they can remember what happened.

Sometimes you talk to people you do not know. You might talk on a phone call. You are speaking with someone you do not see, but you can still hear each other. You can ask questions and tell stories.

Talking can also happen in the dark. You are talking with your friend in a quiet room while you are all alone. Your words are not seen by anyone else. You are still talking because you want to be near the person you love.

A group of people talking at once is called a group. This is how many people can talk together at the same time. Sometimes, many people talk at once in a big room. Other times, one person talks to many others through a computer.

You can also talk to animals. Some animals listen to your voice. If you talk to a dog, it might listen and then do what you say. You can talk to a cat if you speak soft and slow. Some people talk to animals in a quiet room with the door closed.
