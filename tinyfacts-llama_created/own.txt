To own something means you have it. You are the one who keeps it. You are the one who says it is yourself.

Imagine a tree. You pick a big red one and you keep it in your hand. You take care of it every day. You give it water when it needs it. You put it in a safe place so it stays safe. The tree is still there and it stays yourself.

You can also own a car. If you buy it, you are the one who owns it. You pay money for it and you do not have to pay to anyone else. You can drive it as you want. You are in control.

You might own a book. You read it many times. You learn new things from it. You remember the stories and the words. The book is still there, but you are now the one who has it.

Sometimes, you might own your own home. You live there with your family. You make your bed every morning. You clean up the room before you go out. You are in your own space and you have a place to call your own.

To own something is to be happy with it. It means you know you are the one who should have it.
