A week is a group of seven days. It is a way to keep track of how many days have passed since a start point.

Think of a week like a short time when you can do many things. You might plan what to do on the first day, the second day, and the third day. On the four days after that, you do the same things you did on the first three days.

A week is like a short time. It is much shorter than a month, which is a longer time. You can do many things in one week, like work, play, and see many people.

You can also say the week is a group of seven days. You start a week on a day that is near the beginning of the week. You go through the same days again, and then you start on a new day.

One week can start on any day. You can start on a day that is very early in the week and go all the way to the end. You might start on a day that is very late in the week and go all the way to the end.

Some weeks are very short. One week is just one week. Some weeks are longer. Many weeks are a bit longer than one week. They go by very fast.

You can use a week to talk about time. If you say you have two weeks left, you mean you have two weeks that are still going. If you have one week left, you only have one week that is still going.
