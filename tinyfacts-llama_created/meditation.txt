To sit in a quiet room with your eyes closed is one way to do this. You sit on a chair or a floor with your back straight and your body relaxed. Your face is turned to the wall or to a picture. Your hands are in your pockets or on your knees. You breathe in and out slowly, feeling your body and your mind change.

Another way is to focus on a small thing you see. You look at a small rock or a line on the floor. Your eyes are closed and you are not thinking about anything else. Your body stays still and you feel calm.

Sometimes you might sit on a soft thing and hold your breath. Your eyes are closed and your body feels heavy. This is a way to feel the calm inside you.

This word comes from the word for a quiet place. A long time ago, people sat in quiet rooms to find their minds and their bodies. They wanted to know what they were doing and how they felt. They wanted to be alone with their own hearts.
