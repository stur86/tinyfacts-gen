When you say something was a long time ago, it means it happened a very long time before you were even here. This word is used for things that happened in the past.

You can see this in the word day. The day starts at am and ends at night. In the middle, there is a time in the middle, called the middle, and before that, the morning, and after that, the night. All these times are from the past.

It also works for people. If a person is a long time older than you, they are the older person. If a person is older than you, they are older than you. This is how we know who is older and who is younger.

You can also use this word for things that are gone. If something is a memory, it is a thing from the past that you remember. If a thing is gone, it is no more. You can not see it anymore.
