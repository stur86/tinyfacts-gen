A person or thing can be seen in a different way than others. This is what a person has. To be a person with a point of mind is to have a point of mind. You think things. You believe. You feel.

Sometimes, a person sees things not the way others see them. They see one way and believe that is the only way. They do not like what another person thinks. They do not want others to think the same.

This can happen because of where you live or where you go. A person from a city might have a different point of mind than a person from a town or a far away place. The city person might see the world as busy and crowded. The town person might see it as quiet and small.

You can also have a point of mind from your family or friends. You might think a friend is nice. You might think they understand you. But if you look at them through a different point of mind, you might see them as strange.

A person can also have a point of mind because of what they have learned. They might have watched a movie and thought that is how people act. They might have talked to a friend and thought that is how people talk. They do not realize they are missing out on other ways of thinking.

This is why we have different ways of thinking. People are different. We are all on our own road. And that is what makes the world interesting.
