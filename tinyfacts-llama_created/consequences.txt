To have a story is to have a past that has already happened. One story might tell how a person got a job and how they became the person they are now. Another story might tell how a small animal lived in a dark place and how it came to be the animal it is today. Stories are made by people who want to show how things are or how things happened.

When you do something, you set things in order. You open a door. You start a work. You start a game. You do these things, and your life changes. You become a person. You get new ideas. You learn new things. You make new friends. You see new places. The story of your life is the story of how you got to where you are now.

Sometimes, you do not know what will happen next. You do a thing, and then something strange happens. You might feel scared or angry or surprised. You have to decide how to act next. This is what people call a story. It is the story of what happened when you did something.

The word also means the thing that is left behind when something happens. If you pull a door, it makes a sound. If you eat food, your body gets a lot of power. After you are done, you leave behind the door and the food. This is what the word means.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about how things are. If you have a job, you have a story. If you have a friend, you have a story. If you have a house, you have a story. Every story is about a person or a thing.
