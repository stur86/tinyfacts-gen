A fact is a thing that is true. You can see it, hear it, or read it. A fact is not a guess. It is not a story you tell yourself. It is real.

Think about the number seven. You know this number. You see it every day. You know it is the number of days in a week. You know it is the number of days in a month. You know it is the number of days in a year. This number is a fact.

You can also have a fact about a person. You know their name. You know they are a man. You know they are from a far away place. This is also a fact.

Sometimes, a fact can be hard to find. You might see a picture of a tree and think it is a house. But the tree is not a house. The tree is a living thing that grows on the ground. This is a fact.

You can also have a fact about a thing. You know it is red. You know it is a ball. You know it is a thing that can be held in your hand. This is also a fact.

Because facts are real, we can trust them. We can use them to learn and grow. When we know a fact, we know that we are right.
