To stated something means to tell it in a clear way. When you say something, you are stating it. You are telling the world what you think.

Think about when you say a name. You are stating the name. You are telling everyone who the person is. You are not just saying a word, you are telling a story about who this person is.

Sometimes, you might state a fact. You might say, I am a person. I live in this city. I love to read books. When you state something, you are giving the world what you have to say.

You can also state a problem. If a friend is being mean, you might say, They are being very bad to me. You are telling the world what is happening. You are not just talking about it, you are giving a story about the situation.

If you state something that you think is wrong, you are making a point. You are telling the world why you think it is so. This is important because it helps people understand each other. When you state something, you are showing that you care.
