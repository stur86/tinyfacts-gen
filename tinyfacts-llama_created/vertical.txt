A line that goes up and down is called a line. This line is not straight. It goes up, then comes back down. It goes over one side of something and under the other side. This line is part of a bigger picture.

Imagine you are standing in a room. The floor is the ground under you. The wall is the side of the room. The line that goes from the top of your head to your feet is the line that goes up. This line helps us understand how the world works.

In a house, you can stand on the floor. You can walk to the top of a high floor. You can go from the floor down to a room with a window. This line is important for people who live in houses or for people who work in a big building.
