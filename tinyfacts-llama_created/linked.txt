To be in a group is to be a part of it. You are not the only one. There are other people, animals, and things that are also there. They are all in the same place. You can see them, hear them, and feel the same things.

When you are in a group, you have a friend. You can talk to them, laugh with them, and share a story. You feel safe because you are near them. You can also learn from them. They can tell you about the world, about life, and about how to act.

Some groups are very big. You might hear many different voices, different ideas, and different stories. Other groups are small, with only a few people. This can make it easier to talk and feel safe. You can know everyone and everyone knows you.

There are also groups that do not share the same space. You might be in a city and see animals that fly, animals that move on water, and animals that live in the ground. You can learn about each one without seeing the others. This can be very interesting.

Groups can change. People join, and others leave. They may start to like a group more than another one. This is how groups can become different.
