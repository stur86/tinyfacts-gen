To shake or to feel very small and fast can be the same thing. If you are standing in a room with many people, you might feel that a small thing is going around your head. This makes you feel a bit scared.

If you are a person, you can also feel small. You might feel like you are going to fall over. You might feel like your body is moving in a strange way. This feeling is often because you are worried.

Sometimes, when you are very happy, you might feel like you are going to jump up and down a lot. Your feet might be moving very fast. You might want to shout and laugh at the same time. This is a good feeling.

You can also shake things that are not people. If you drop a box, it will fall down on the floor. You might hear it make a loud noise. This is because the box is moving very fast. It is moving from one place to another without stopping.

We can shake our hair when we are scared. Our hair might go around in a big ring. We might want to pull it back. This helps us feel better.
