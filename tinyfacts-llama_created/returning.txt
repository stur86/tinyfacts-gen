To return something means to go back to where you started. This can happen many times in your life. If you go to a place you love, you return home. You might go to the park where you play. You might go back to your room to eat dinner. This shows you are coming back.

You can also return money. If you buy something you need, you pay the money back to the person who gave it to you. This is important because it helps your money stay in your hands.

Sometimes you return things you found. If you find a ball, you might put it away in your bag. Later, you might find the same ball again. This means you got it back.

You can also return something that is not yourself. If you try to take a thing that doesn't go in your bag, you have to go back to the store. The store says, you can not take the thing, it is not your thing. You have to leave it there.

Returning is important because it keeps things and people. It shows you care about what you have and about others.
