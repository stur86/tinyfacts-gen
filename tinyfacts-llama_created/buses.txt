A bus is a big car that many people use to get around. It is usually very large and has many seats inside. You can pay to sit in a seat or you can pay to sit near the front of the car. The bus goes in a long line, and when you want to get to a new place, you tell the driver which stop you want to go to.

Some buses are big and have many seats. They have big windows so you can see what is going on. They often have loud music or talk on the phone. Some buses go very fast. You can feel the wind when you drive on one of these cars.

Most buses run on the same line. You can get off at a stop near your home, work, or school. The driver tells you when to stop. When you get on, you sit down and hold on to the seat in front of you. The bus goes around many towns and cities until you reach your stop.

You can also buy a bus that you drive yourself. These are called cars. They are smaller than buses but you are the one who drives them. You need to know where you are going, and you follow the same roads as the buses. Some people use these cars to go to work, or to run away from a bad place.

Some buses go to places you can not walk to. They carry people who are sick or need help. Others go to places where people go to eat. Some buses go to places where you can see shows. They carry music, movies, and games for people to enjoy.

You can buy a bus card. This card lets you ride the buses in many cities for a few days. It is like a money card that you carry with you. You pay for each ride, and you can use it to go where you need to go. Many cities also give you a card with numbers so you can know when to get off at the stop.
