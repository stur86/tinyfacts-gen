To get someone to act in a way is to make them do something. When you say you want a friend to meet you for coffee, you are trying to get them to join you.

If you want someone to do something for you, you can say,  I need your help. This means you need them to act. They must follow what you say. They must do the right thing.

You can also say,  Let us play a game. This means you want them to be part of a game with you. They will do what you tell them. This is a way to get people to do something you want.

Sometimes, when you see a group of people, you might want them to join you. You might be a leader, and you say,  Come on. They come. They follow you. You are in control.

When you say,  Let someone act, you mean they get to decide what happens. They decide what to do. They can say yes or no. They can say yes if they like what you want. They can say no if they do not want to do it.

To get someone to act means to make them feel like they must do it. It means you are in control.
