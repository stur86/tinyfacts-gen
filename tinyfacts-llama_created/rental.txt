To pay for something you use is to do this. You do not own it. You pay someone else to use it for you. This is called a short stay or a short time.

Imagine you have a room in your home. You pay a person to stay in your room. During this time, you do not pay them. The person stays and you do not pay them. But at the end of the time, you pay them. The money you give is like a small part of what you paid for the room in the first place.

Sometimes, you can pay to use a car. You do not own it. You are just a person who wants to use it for a short time. You pay the driver or the place where you park the car. You do not own the car. You are just using it.

You can also pay for a home. You buy a house. At first, you do not pay any money. Later, you pay money every month. This money is like a small part of what you paid for the house in the first place. You own the house now.

You can also pay to use a phone. You pay the person who owns the phone. They let you use it for a short time. You pay them money. This is like paying for a room. You are not living in the room. You are just using it.

In all these cases, you are using something that someone else owns. You are not the owner. You are just a person who uses it for a short time.
