To cut something means you use a thing to make a line across it. You can use a thing that looks like a small round piece or a thing with a long long part to do this. You hold the thing under the thing you want to cut, and you press it down. The thing does not cut the thing right away. Instead, it makes a line that you can see. After a while, the line goes across the thing. This is important because it helps us make new things.

Sometimes you cut things to make them easier to carry. You might cut a piece of wood to make a small box or to make a long piece that you can carry in your bag. You also cut the hair off a person. This helps to clean their face so they look nice.

You can also cut something that is hard to your body. If you have a piece of wood that is very hard, you might use a thing with a long part to cut it. You press the part down on the wood and the wood lets the part cut it. This helps the wood break into smaller pieces that you can use.

Sometimes, when you cut something, you might make a cut that hurts. If you cut yourself on a hard thing, you might feel pain. This is because your body is trying to close the cut. Your skin is a very strong part of your body. It keeps you safe from the world.

We also use the word to talk about how a thing is. If something is very small, you can say it is cut up. If something is very big, you can say it is cut into many pieces. If something is broken, it might have pieces that cut across it.
