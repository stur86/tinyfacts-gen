An  is a group of things that get together and stay as one. They are usually made of tiny parts from a bigger thing. When these parts are joined, they form a new thing that is different from the parts alone.

Think of it like a small group of people. Each person has their own name, clothes, and face. They are all from the same city, but each one is a different person. An  is like this group, but it is made of tiny tiny parts. They are all the same kind, but they are all in different places.

Sometimes, when an  gets very hot, the tiny parts start to feel very strong. They want to move away from the hot part. This happens because of a thing called a pull. The pull is like a strong pull that pulls things together. The pull is why the tiny parts stay in the same place, even when the hot part is moving fast.

An  can also get very small. When the pull is very strong, the tiny parts can get very close together. This can happen when a hot thing touches a cold thing. The pull is so strong that the tiny parts start to feel like they are in the same place.
