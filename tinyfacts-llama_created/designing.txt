To make something new is to use your brain and your hands. When you are a little child, you make your own things. You cut a piece of paper into a funny way. You use colors from a box. You make a picture of a dog. You did not just sit there. You thought about what the dog looks like, what the dog is doing, and what color the dog is. You made it happen.

A grown person also makes things. They think about how people need to live. They think about what food is good for the body. They think about how to keep the air clean so people can breathe. They make plans for where to build houses, where to put roads, and how to care for animals. They do this because they care about people.

To make something new also means to change what already is. You can use a word. If you say, a dog is a good dog, then you change it. You say, a dog is a bad dog. Now people know what you really mean. You are not the same person. You have changed your mind.

There is also a word that means to make something look nice. If you put some things together, you make a room look good. You use colors, pictures, and things that make people feel happy. You make the room feel like a warm home.

You can also make something that will last a long time. You can use wood to make a chair. You use a strong stuff that stays in your body for many years. You did not just build a chair for today. You built one that will be there for many years.
