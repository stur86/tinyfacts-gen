To explain something means you tell others why it is the way it is. You might tell them about a thing, a person, or a place. You show them how it looks, what it looks like, and what it does. This helps them understand.

Think of a story. If you want to tell a friend you love them, you need to explain why you love them. You tell them how you feel in your heart. You might say you like the way they laugh, the way they smile, or the way they make you feel when you are with them. This is to explain love.

Sometimes you explain a thing by showing how it works. If a car is fast, you can tell the police how it works. You explain that the car has strong power. You show them the part that makes the power. You show them how it makes the car go very fast. This helps them understand.

You can also explain a feeling. If you are sad, you can tell others why you are sad. You might say you are sad because your friend went away. You tell them about the day you saw them. You tell them about the words you said. This shows others how to help you feel better.

To explain can be hard. Sometimes you do not know the right words. You might feel afraid or confused. But if you are kind and try, you can say things that help others feel better.
