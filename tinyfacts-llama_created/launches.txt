To start something can mean different things. If you are starting a new job, you are beginning a new day. You start working at a new place.

When something is about to go up, it is about to start. It might be a car going to the top of a big high place. Or it might be a thing in the air. It starts moving and goes up. Then it starts to go fast and higher.

Sometimes, you start a game with others. The first person to reach a far point is the first one to window. They are the ones who start the game.
