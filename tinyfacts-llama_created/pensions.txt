A person who works for many years can get a thing that helps them when they are old. This is called a job for life.

Think of a person who is very old. They have not had to work for a long time. They have money all the time. This money is like a big box with many small pieces inside. Each piece is like a small thing that helps them.

When a person works for many years, they put some of these small pieces in the box. At the end of the work, the person gets the money from the box. The money is what keeps them safe and happy when they are old.

This is why a person who works for a long time gets something that helps them when they are very old. It is like a box with many small pieces that they can carry with them for their whole life.
