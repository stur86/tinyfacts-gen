To eat is to take food from a person or thing. When you eat, you are putting things from one body into another body.

You eat things you find on the ground. You might eat a piece of a tree, a small animal, or a green thing. You also eat food made from other living things. This food is often from the ground, but it can come from animals, like a small animal you kill and then eat.

When you eat, you are making your body strong. Food gives your body the power it needs to move, to keep you standing, and to keep you going. You need to eat many times a day so that your body does not go without food for too long.

Sometimes, you eat because you are very full. You might sit down and not want to get up. You might eat a whole thing in one bite. When this happens, you are eating because you are tired and need to rest.

When you eat, you are also taking in things that are good for you. Your body uses this food to make your skin look good, to keep your hair growing, and to help your heart work well. You need to eat so that your body can stay strong and keep you going.
