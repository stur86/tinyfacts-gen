To make a thing into one whole is to take many parts and join them together. This is what we do with money.

Imagine you have many small pieces of paper that you pay for something. Each piece of paper tells you how much you paid for a small thing. If you get many of these pieces together, you get one piece. You did not keep each piece alone anymore. Instead, you keep all the pieces together in one big thing.

This makes the money easier to use. If you have many small pieces, you have to carry them around. But when you have one big piece, you can carry it easily in your hand.

We use this word when we talk about people or things that are made of many parts. We call it a group or a team. When many people work together, they make something big and strong. They can fix a car, build a house, or help in a big way.
