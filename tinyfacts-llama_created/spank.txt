To hit someone with your hand can be very hard. It is when you use your hands to make someone feel pain. This is a way that children learn to use their power.

The word comes from a story about a dog. The dog had a baby. The baby was very bad to its mother. The mother wanted to keep the baby safe, so she hit it. The dog was very angry. The dog hit the baby so hard that it made the wood crack. The baby was very hurt, but the mother was happy that she did not have to pick it up anymore.

This is why we use the word. We do not hit people with our hands because it hurts. We use it for children because they are young and need to learn how to act.
