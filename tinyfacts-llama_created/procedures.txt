A way of doing something. When you do something, you act in a way that is like a set of steps. These steps are the way you follow to get the job done.

You might use a way to eat food. The steps are:

First you sit down. Then you open the box. You look at what you have. You cut the food. You take a bite. You drink the water. This is a way of eating.

You might use a way to fix a thing. The steps are:

First you look at what is wrong. Then you get the thing you need. You put the thing in the box. You turn the box. The box works now. This is a way of fixing things.

You might use a way to do a job. The steps are:

First you decide what you want to do. Then you do the work. You might write a letter. You might make a picture. You might talk to a person. You follow the steps and the work is done.

When you follow a way, you are doing what has been planned. You follow the steps until you are finished. This helps people be sure that the work is done right.
