To have a name means you know who you are and where you come from. A name is like a word that tells everyone who you are.

The word we use for the things that give you your name is from the word for animals. It comes from the word for the same animal.

Think about a dog. A dog has a name. You can not tell a dog what name it has. It is just the name the dog has. A baby animal, like a baby dog, does not have a name yet. It is just a thing that is growing. When it is big enough, it gets a name.

The thing inside you that makes you who you are is like that. There are many of these things inside you. You have many tiny pieces of these things that you did not even know you had. You have a set of these pieces. This set is called your name. The name is what makes you, you.

When you are very old, you might get a new name. This is when a new part of your body grows. Your old parts go away and you get new parts. You might get a name that is different from your old name. It is like getting a new name when you grow taller.

The name of your parents is also important. You get their name when you are very young. This is how you learn how to say it. Your parents are like the ones who gave you your name. If you change your name, you are changing your family.

Sometimes, you can get a new name because you do something. If you do something very good, people might give you a new name. They might say, you are a very nice person now. This new name is like a new word that tells everyone who you are.
