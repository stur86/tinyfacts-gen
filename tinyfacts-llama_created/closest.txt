To be the closest means you are very near to someone. You are not far away. You are right next to them.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You both want to see a ball. You are both standing on the floor. You are not very far from each other. You can hear each other very easily. You are very close.

Sometimes, being the closest does not mean you are the best. You can be very close to someone even if they are not the best person in the world. This is because you care about them and you want them to be happy.

If you have a book and your friend has a book, you can be the closest if you both have the same book. You can sit next to each other on a bed or stand next to each other in a room. You are both very close to each other.

Being the closest can also mean being the first one to do something. If you are the closest to a door, you are the first one who opens it. If you are the closest to a computer, you are the first one who types on it.

So, to be the closest is to be very near to someone or something.
