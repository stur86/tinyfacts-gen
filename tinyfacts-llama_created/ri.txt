To run is to walk quickly. If you run a long way, you go very fast. You might feel your legs move in a strange way and your face might be wide open. When you run, you use your big, strong legs and your arms. Your arms help you push forward.

People often run in parks or on streets. If you run far, you might feel like you are flying. You might feel your heart beat fast. Running can make you feel strong and happy. It is a good way to clear your head when you are feeling very angry or sad.
