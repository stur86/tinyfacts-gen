To offer something is to show it is there for someone. It can mean many different things.

Imagine a person who has a lot of money. They can give it to you. This is giving something. They are letting you have it. They are being very kind.

You can also offer food. If you see a person who is very cold and has no money, you can offer them a cup of water. You are showing you care. This is a way of helping someone without them asking.

Sometimes, you offer a word. If you tell a friend that you like them, you are offering them your love. You are saying you want them to know you care. This is a way of showing you are happy around them.

You can also offer to help. If a person is stuck and can't move, you can offer to pull them. You are helping them get out of a bad place. This is showing you are there for them.

There is another way to offer. When you are in a place, you might offer to move to a different room. This is letting someone know you are not in the room they want. It is a way of letting them know you are not doing what they asked.

To offer something can be a little strange. If a person wants to play with you, but they do not want to be friends with you, you could offer to play with them. This is letting them know you want to be their friend. You are showing you want to be with them.

In short, to offer is to give, share, or let someone know you are there.
