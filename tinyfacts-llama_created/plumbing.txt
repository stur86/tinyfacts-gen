To make water run through something is to fix the way it is in your house. It is the work of a person who does this job.

Imagine you have a cup and a glass. You put water from the cup into the glass. If the glass is full and the cup is empty, you put the water into the glass. If the glass is full and the cup is also full, you do not need to add any more water. The water is in the glass now, and it is where it should be.

Planning is like this. A person who is good at fixing things makes a plan. They decide what they want to do and how to do it. They write down the steps they will take. They also tell everyone who is helping them where things are.

In a house, the water runs through the floor. It goes down to the street and then it goes into the water that you drink. The water does this because it is under the ground and it is pushed by the ground. The person who does this work makes sure the water comes out where you want it to go.

The word comes from the word for water. A long time ago, people did not have big things that can carry water for everyone. They had to carry water to each house one by one. They would carry heavy water up a long road. The person who did this work had to be very strong and very good at fixing things. They were like the ones who make the road for cars.

Now, the person who does this work is not as hard to find. The houses are bigger and the roads are better, so people are not as tired. But they still do the same job. They make sure the water is where it should be, and they do it every day.
