The word is used to talk about how you look. You are beautiful if you look nice and happy. A person or a thing can be very beautiful. A person might look like a picture you saw in a book. A thing that is very big can look beautiful too. 

You might feel this way when you see a beautiful picture. You might feel happy. You might want to smile. You feel good. You think the picture is very pretty. Sometimes people say a person is beautiful because they have a nice face, a nice hair, or a nice body. 

But you are not just beautiful because of how you look. You are also beautiful if you are kind to others. If you smile at a friend, they are happy. If you are nice to a child, the child feels happy too. You are beautiful because you make people happy.
