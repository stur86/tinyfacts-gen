To stuff means you put things inside something. You can stuff a bag with small things. You can also stuff a box with food or other things you do not want anymore. When you stuff, you make room inside a thing by putting more things around it.

You can stuff a food bag. You take something you like and put it inside the bag. Then you close the bag and the food stays inside. This helps keep the food inside and does not let it go out.

You can also stuff a small box. You might put a few small things inside. This makes the box look fuller. It is like putting a small number of pieces in a box to make it look like a bigger thing.

Sometimes, people use this word for a very small thing. If you have a small dog, you might say, he is stuffed. This means he is very full. He has eaten a lot of food.

But you can also stuff a person. If a person is very full of anger, they might stuff their face with food. They eat and eat until they feel happy again. This helps them feel better when they are very angry.

So, to stuff is to put things inside something. It is a way to make room and to put things you do not want away.
