To have a favorite is to really like something. You might like a person or a thing more than others. You might like a game, a book, or a food. This is because you feel happy when you think about them.

Sometimes, you have two things you like. You love one more than the other. This is okay. Many people have many favorites. They might like different things at different times. One day they love a game and the next day they love a book.

When you have a favorite, you do not mind when others do not like it. You might even laugh or smile at the other person if they say their favorite is the wrong one. You feel happy to be with them and to talk about it.

Having a favorite helps you feel complete. You know what you are good at and what makes you happy. You do not feel sad or worried when you think about your favorite. It is like having a warm feeling in your heart.

You can also have a favorite time of day. You might like to watch the sun come up or to see the stars at night. This time of day can feel very nice and calm. It is like a big hug from the world.

If you have a favorite animal, you might see it in a movie or a picture. You might imagine how it looks and what it does. You might feel a strong feeling when you see it. This is because your brain is already filled with pictures of that animal.

In short, having a favorite is when you really like something. It is a happy feeling that makes you want to be with the thing or person you love.
