A group of people who work together to make things can be called a group. Inside this group, the people who own the things make the plans.

Imagine you have a small store. You buy food to make food. You also buy things you need to make money. One day, many of your friends come together and make the plans. They decide how much money to spend, which foods to buy, and how to make the money. They all work together to make sure the store is doing well.

The people who own the store are like a group. They decide what the store should do and who should be in the store. They make sure everyone follows the same plan. They pay some people to work in the store, and they pay others to work for other groups.

A large group of people can also be called a group. This group might make things for many people. They work together to make a new car, a new phone, or a new book. They share the money they make and use it to buy more things or pay other people to work on their things.

In a big group, there are usually people who lead. These people are like the top of the group. They help make the plans and make sure everyone follows them. There are also people who work together, like the ones who buy and make things. There are people who help with things like money and people who work on the things.
