But is a word that people use to show that two things are different.

If you say, I am happy. But I am afraid, you are showing that there are two different ways to feel. You are telling them that your two things are not the same.

Sometimes you do this with people. If you say to a friend, I love you, but I am angry at you, you are showing that your two things are different. You like him, but you are mad at him. This shows that you have two different ideas about him.

But you can also use this word with things. If you say a tree is green, but a dog is brown, you are showing that the two things are different. The tree has green leaves, but the dog has brown further.

This word is important because it helps us keep things straight. When we have two different ideas, we can say, but. This lets us know that two things are different and helps us not get confused.
