To hurt someone means you cause them pain or make them feel bad. This is not a kind thing to do. It can hurt their heart or their mind.

Imagine you are playing with a ball and you hit a friend. You feel good when you hit the ball. But if you hit your friend on the head, they feel pain. They cry and might be afraid. You did not want to hurt them. You just wanted to play with the ball.

In a big group, if one person says something mean, they are hurting everyone. They are making the other people feel sad or afraid. This is not a good way to act.

Also, you can hurt animals. If you throw a rock at a cat, it hurts the cat. You are causing the cat pain. You should not do this.

You can also hurt the world. If you drive a car very fast and do not follow the road, you are hurting the people who live on the road. You are also hurting the trees and the houses. The air and the water are also getting hurt.

To help is to make things better. If you give a friend a book, you are helping them. You are making them feel happy and calm. You are not hurting them. You are making them feel good.
