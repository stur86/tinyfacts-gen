The word short is used to talk about the number of things you have.

Imagine you have a box full of books. You have many books. One day, you take out a book and put it back. Now you have one more book than you did before. This is short. You did not have so many books anymore.

A person might say, a short person is a small person. This means they are not as tall as you are.

A short story is a story that is very short. It is not as long as a story that is many lines, but it is still important. You read a short story to learn something new or to laugh.

You can also be short with your words. If you say you are short, it means you are a small person. But if you say you are short to try to make a friend, you are being small. You are not afraid.

People use this word to tell the truth. If a person is very tall, they might say they are short. This can be a way to be different.
