Night is the time after the sun goes down. The sun is gone for a long time. During this time, the light from the sun is hidden by the dark sky. The stars come out. They are very bright and look like a big group of tiny lights in the sky.

The night is the time when you are not seeing the sun. You can not see the sun in the sky, but you can see the stars and the world around you. It is a time to rest and think.

People use night for many things. They go to bed at night and sleep. They watch the stars through a window or a small window in a tall building. They look at pictures or watch a movie. They talk with friends and family.

Night is also a time for stories. Many stories take place at night. There are stories about a man who goes on a long walk at night. There are stories about a girl who loves to walk under the stars. There are stories about a group of friends who have fun together at night.

Sometimes, people say that night is a time of fear. If you are afraid, you might be scared at night. You might hear strange noises or look at the dark. But most nights are calm and quiet. They are a time to rest and let your mind be still.
