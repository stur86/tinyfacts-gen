An eventually is a word that means something happens at a time that is not before the time you are talking about. It is like a place in your mind where you think about the day, the week, or the year.

Imagine you are talking to a friend about how you feel. You might say, I am feeling sad and angry. You are not thinking about a day from the past. You are only thinking about how you are feeling right now. This is an eventually thing.

Sometimes you have a fun day with your family. You play games, eat food, and laugh together. This is an eventually time.

You also have a very important day. It is the day you get to go to school or work. This is an eventually time that you have been looking forward to for a long time.

An eventually thing can be a small thing. You might be late for a bus. This is an eventually time.

An eventually thing can also be a big thing. It is a time when your city is going to be very crowded. People are going to be walking and talking. This is an eventually time that everyone is talking about.

An eventually thing does not happen because you say it does. It happens because of the way the world is. You can try to plan your day, but it is not going to happen. This is what an eventually is.
