To cover something means to keep it safe from the outside. Think of a bed. You put a soft cover on the top part. This cover is like a big piece of white stuff that you put over the bed. This helps keep the bed warm in the cold night and keeps the floor clean when you walk across it.

You can also cover a table. If you throw a big heavy thing across the room, it might fall and break the table. You can use a big piece of stuff that covers the table. This keeps the table safe until you can find it again.

Sometimes, you cover a person. If a person is scared, you might cover them with a soft thing. This helps make them feel better. You can also cover a room with a big cover. This keeps the room warm or keeps the bad air out.

You can also cover a thing that is broken. If a car has a broken window, you might cover it with a clear piece of stuff. This helps keep the rain from getting in. You can do this with a cover or with a big cover that goes over the whole car.

The word covers can mean many things. It means to keep something safe, it means to cover a room with a cover, and it means to cover a person or a thing that is broken.
