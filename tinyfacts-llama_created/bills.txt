To pay a person is to give them money. When you have money, you can buy things you want. You can buy food, clothes, games, or anything else you need.

If you want to buy something that is not free, you need to pay. This is called paying a person. You give them money, and they give you what you want.

There is a group of money that you pay each month. This money is called a pay. When you pay, you are paying the group of money. You are helping the group pay its money so that people can have things they want.

Buses and trains also pay money. When you get on a bus or train, you pay with money. The money goes into a big box. The box holds all the money from many people. Then the box gives the money to the bus driver or the train driver so they can go to their next stop.

You also pay money when you use a phone. If you make a call, you pay. You pay the person who is on the other end of the line. You pay with money or with your phone money.

When you pay, you show that you have money to spend. It is a way to say you are ready to buy what you want.
