A big number of things that are not used at the same time is called a number of things in a lot. When you have too many of something, you have a lot of it. You do not need it. You can keep it for later.

Imagine you have a box with many books. You want to put them away. You take the box out of the house. Now you have the box, and the books are not in your house anymore. You have a lot of books that you do not need.

In this way, having too many of something is a kind of number. It means you have more than you need. You can keep it for later and be happy.
