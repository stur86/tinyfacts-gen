To build something is to use wood, rock, and other things to make it stand up in the ground. This is what people do when they want to make a house, a school, or a place to work. They use their hands and the things they find in the ground to make the walls, the floors, and the top. They put the walls together and cover them with a hard stuff that keeps rain and wind out.

People also build to show how they feel. They can build a room that looks like a big room in a city or a room with many small rooms like a school. They can build a place where people can sit and talk. They can build a place to go when you need to be quiet.

Sometimes, people build things that are not for people. They build roads, big roads that go across many places. They build big boxes that carry heavy things from one place to another. They build things that fly through the air and carry people and things to far away places.

To build something is to use your mind and your hands. You use your hands to move the things you need. You use your brain to think about how the thing should look and how it should work. You think about the space, the walls, the floors, and the top. You think about the way the light will come in and the way the rain will fall.

Sometimes, you have to work with many people. You all work together to build a big thing. You all decide how the walls should look and how the floors should be. You all help to move the heavy things and to put them up. When it is all done, you can be very happy.

The word comes from the word build. You build something, and you are building it.
