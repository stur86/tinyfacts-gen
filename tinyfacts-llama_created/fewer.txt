When you have more things, there are fewer. This is because you have taken some of them away.

If you have a big bag of food, you have a lot. But if you take a few bags and throw them away, you have fewer bags. You still have the bags you are left with.

You can also say this about people. If you have many friends, there are fewer people in your life. It means you have fewer good friends.

Sometimes, when you want to tell a story, you might say something like, there are fewer stars at night than the days. This helps you remember that there are fewer stars than the number of days in a night.

So, fewer is about taking away or moving something from a group so there is less of it left.
