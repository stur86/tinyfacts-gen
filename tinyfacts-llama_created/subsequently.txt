To follow something

Think about what you did last. You did it first. Now, you are going to do something new. This new thing happens later in the day. The new thing is after the old one. When you do the new thing, you follow the old one. You are following it.

Imagine you are in a room with your friends. You start to talk and they start to laugh. Then you tell a story and they start to smile. You follow the story. You are following the first one.

If you went to a movie and your friends went to a different movie, you were following each other. You were both following the same movie.
