To fix something is to make it work again. You might be trying to fix a broken thing. You look at it, see the parts that are wrong, and you do the thing that makes them right. After a while, the thing is not broken anymore and it works like it should.

Sometimes you fix a word that is wrong. You take a word that does not mean what it says and you make it mean what it should. This helps people to understand each other better.

You can also fix a bad feeling. If you feel very angry, you might want to make the bad feeling go away. You can do this by taking a deep breath, letting your body feel calm, and then you feel better.

To fix something means to make it right. It is about taking what is wrong and changing it into something that is good.
