To work and get money is called working. You do a job. You do things to make things. You might do a job for a person who owns a business. You might work for a school. You might work in a store. You do this for many hours. After you finish, you can buy things you want. This is a good way to get money.

Sometimes you do this work alone. You work by yourself. You might do something that only one person can do. You might fix a car. You might fix a phone. You are the only one who can do that job.

You can also work with many other people. You work with a team. You all work together. You do some of the work and your friends do the rest. This is a good way to share the work. Everyone does what they think is best.

If you work hard, you can get a better job. Maybe you will work for a bigger business. Maybe you will work for a person who is very important. You might even get to work in a nice house. You might work in a big office. You will be the one who tells people what to do.

Working is a way to get money. It is also a way to learn new things. You learn how to do things. You learn about people. You learn about how to work with others. You learn how to be a good person.
