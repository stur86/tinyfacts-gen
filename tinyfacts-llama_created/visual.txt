To see something means your brain understands what you are looking at. You can not hear a sound or feel a touch. But with your eyes, you can see a picture or a word that is not there.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. You do not know which person is who. You look at them. Your eyes see their faces and their clothes. You see the hair on their head. You see their eyes and their skin. This is what you see.

You can see a line. You can see a number. You can see a picture of your mother. You can see a picture of a dog. Your eyes take in the light and the colors.

Sometimes, you see something that is not really there. You see a thing that does not really have a front side. You see a wall or a door. You see the floor under your feet. You see the air around you. This is not seeing, but rather looking at what is in front of you.

You can also see your own body. You see your face and your arms. You see your legs. You see your chest and your head. This is your own picture of yourself.

Sometimes, you do not see at all. You are quiet and you do not move. You are in a dark room. You do not see the people around you. You do not see the light. You just are.
