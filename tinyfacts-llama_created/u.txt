The word until is used to talk about how something is done or what happens. It is like saying you are until something happens next.

Think of it this way. If you are waiting for a friend to come home, you might feel a little quiet. You are waiting. The time is passing, and you are waiting. You do not know if your friend will come or when they will come.

In the same way, if you are doing something for a long time, you are until you finish. You are not done yet. You are waiting to see the end. This is why we say you are until something happens next.

Sometimes, you use it to talk about how far you have gone. If you have been walking for a long time, you might say you have been until you reach the top of a big rock. You have done a lot of walking and you are almost at the end of your trip.

It is also used to talk about the end of a story or a long time. If you read a book for many years, you might say the book is until you finish. You have read it all and you feel happy and tired.

So, the word until is used to talk about how time works. It helps us understand when things happen and how we feel about them.
