For is a word that means before a time. It is used to show that something comes before another thing. Imagine a line. You can put a word on the left side of the line. The word is before the line. You can also put a number next to the word. The number tells you how many lines there are between the two words.

You can also use for with people. If you are going to a party, you might say, I am going for dinner. This means you are going to eat dinner before you go to the party. If you have a friend, you can say, I am going for a walk. This means you are going to walk.

For is also used when you want something. If you want a book, you say, for my friend. This means the book is for your friend. You are giving the book to them. If you want a ball, you say, for me. This means you are giving the ball to me.

Sometimes, we use for with things that are already there. If you have a bag, you can put your things in it. You are putting your things into the bag. You are holding the bag for your things.

When you use for, you are telling the world that something is important. You are saying, this is for you. You are putting it on the same line as other words.

For can also mean when something is gone. If you have a book, and you read it, then the book is gone. You can say, the book is for you now. It is not there anymore. You do not need it anymore.
