A book is a thing that people read. You can hold it in your hands. You can turn the words around so you can read each line many times.

But there are books that are not like this. They are very thinker. They have many small pieces of paper in a line. The book has many of these pieces, all put together. This makes the book very easy to carry.

You can buy these books in stores. You can pick which one you want to read. Some are very big. Some are very small. You can find them in many colors. Some have pictures. Some do not. They are good for people who like to read many times.

One kind of these books is good for young children. They can read these books many times. The stories are easy to follow. The pictures help you know what is happening. You can read them with a friend.

Another kind of these books is good for grown people. They have stories that are not easy to read. They have many words. You have to read them slowly so you do not miss any words. They are for people who like to learn about the world.

There are also books with only one piece of paper. These are for people who want to know one thing. You can read them in one go. They are good for people who want to know about one thing very fast.
