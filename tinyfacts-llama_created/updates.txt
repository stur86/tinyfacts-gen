To tell someone you have something new is to give them a story about what happened. You tell them you did it. You might say you saw something or heard a sound. You might say you went to a place and found a new thing.

When people talk about this, they are talking about what happens in a long time. They tell you about how things change. They are giving you a story about the world.

Sometimes you tell your friends that you have a new book. You might say, I got a book with a story that is not like the one I read before. Your friends might say, What happened? They want to know what happened.

You can also say that you have an idea for something. You think it will change the world. You might tell them about it. They might say, This is a great idea! They are excited for you.

To keep something up to date means to keep it the same. You do this when you write or draw. You are not changing the story. You just put new words or pictures next to the old ones. This helps the story stay the same for many people.

To be up to date also means to know what is happening in the world today. You do this when you read the papers or look at the computer. You see what happened in the past hour. You know what is going on in the world right now.

Sometimes, people use this word in a different way. When you tell a story, you are telling them you did something. When you say you are up to date, you are telling them you know what is happening in the world now.
