An hour is a big piece of time. It is a very long time that is a hundred minutes. There are    hours in a day. You can not have an hour without a day.

An hour is a part of the day. It starts at one o.k.'s. It goes until one o.k.'s. The same happens every day. You can not be in two places at the same time. Because of this, people have to be ready at a set time. This helps them know when to wake up and when to go to work.

You can also use an hour to talk about how much you have done. If you have done something for an hour, you have done it. You can not say you have done more than an hour. This is why you often say,  I am tired. I have done all my work for the day.

People also use hours to talk about how much time is left. If you have an hour and a half left, you have an hour and a half of time. This can help you plan what you want to do later.

Some people use hours to talk about time in other ways. If you want to know how much time it will take to do something, you might ask how many hours it will take. You can also ask how many hours it will be tomorrow.
