An hour is a part of a day. It is a small time that starts when the sun first comes up and ends when it goes down.

Imagine you are watching a movie on your phone. A movie usually starts when the lights go down and the story begins. The movie will last a few hours. If the movie is one hour long, it is a very short story.

An hour is also how many minutes you have before the next hour starts. If you have  hour of time left after dinner, you will have  hour of time left after you go to bed.

You can also use this word for something that happens many times in a day. Think about the time you need to go to work. If you have an hour to get ready, you have an hour to get ready for work. If you have two hours, you have two hours of time to do things.

People use this word to talk about how much time is left. If someone says, I have an hour, they mean they have an hour to do something. It is a small piece of time that is very important.
