To keep something is to save it so that it stays in good form. You can do this with many things.

If you keep a food for a long time, you are keeping it from going bad. You cover it with a cover so that light does not reach it, and you put it in a box. This keeps the food from turning green or brown and from breaking.

You can also keep a picture. If you keep a picture on your wall for many years, it stays clear and does not change. This is because the light does not touch it as much. You are keeping the picture from getting old or broken.

In your home, you keep food from the store. You keep it in a box and store it in your kitchen. This keeps the food from going bad. You do this so you can eat it later when you are ready.

Sometimes, you keep things that you do not want. You keep a thing that you are afraid of, or a thing that you do not like. This keeps it away from you. You do not see it anymore, and you do not touch it. This is a way of keeping things that you do not want.
