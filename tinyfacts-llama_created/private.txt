A thing that is not shared with everyone is called a thing that is not for everyone. You can keep something you find. You do not have to tell anyone what you have.

Think of a room. You want to be alone in the room. If you have a door and you close it, you are in your own space. No one can come into the room. The door is for you alone.

You might have a phone at home. Only you can use the phone. You can talk to your family or friends without anyone else hearing. This is a good thing because it keeps your voice safe.

Sometimes you might want to keep things you did. You might not want someone to know what you did. You can keep those things in a box or a book. You are keeping them safe for yourself.

There are also things that are not for everyone to see. If a movie is not for everyone, the movie might be locked. People who watch the movie are not allowed to see it again. They might feel like they are seeing something only they can understand.

In the end, a thing that is not shared with everyone is one you keep to yourself.
