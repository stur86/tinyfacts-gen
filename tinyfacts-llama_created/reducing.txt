To get smaller is to become smaller. You start with a big room and the walls are very high. You put things inside the room and the room gets smaller. You can see the walls from inside. The room is no longer big. Now you have a tiny room.

You can do this with your body too. If you eat food, your body gets bigger. If you drink water, your body gets bigger. But if you stop eating or drink water, you get smaller. Your body becomes less heavy and you feel lighter.

Sometimes, you might want to get smaller so you can hide. You might wear clothes that cover your body. You might put things over your face so people do not see you. But it is not good to try to be smaller than you are. It can make you feel sad and afraid.

You can also get smaller in a different way. If you are in a place with a lot of people, you might feel very crowded. This can make you feel angry. You might want to leave. But sometimes, it is okay to be small in a room full of people. The room is still big and there are many people there. You just feel like you are a small part of the room.

You can also get smaller when you lose things. If you have a thing you love, you might feel sad. You want to find it. If you look for it for a long time, you might get smaller. You feel like you have lost your heart. But then you find it again and you feel happy.

Being smaller is also about having fewer things. If you have a lot of books, you get smaller. You do not have as much room in your head. You only need one book. If you have a lot of money, you get smaller. You do not need to buy many things anymore.
