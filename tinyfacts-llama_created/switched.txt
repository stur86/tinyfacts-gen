To change something means you do it instead of the way it was before. This happens when you use a different word or a different way of doing a job.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it and it goes to a friend. Then, you throw it again. This is because you are changing the way you are doing the job. You are not still doing the same thing.

You can also change the time. If you are going to sleep at seven, you change it to ten. This is also a change because you are doing something different.

Sometimes, you might change how you act. If you are afraid of a dog, you might not go near it. You are changing your way of feeling and acting.

You can also change your plans. If you want to go to the movies, you change your plans and go to a game instead. This is a change because you are doing something different.

In the world, people also change jobs. One day, you work at a store. The next day, you work at a school. You might change your work because you want to do something else or because you want to learn something new.
