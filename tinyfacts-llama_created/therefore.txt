To be followed by a word that starts with the letter Everyone

This word means you follow the next thing with what you say after you. When you know something, you can tell others about it. You use this word when you want to make sure everyone understands.

Here is an easy way to remember it. If you want to tell someone something, you can use this word. If you want to ask another question, you can use it too. It is like a sign that you are about to give more facts.

Sometimes, if you are trying to do something, you might say, I am following the word. This is because you are following the step you did before. It is like a line of things you do one after the other.

We also use this word in other ways. If you are very happy, you might say, I am following the day. This means you are enjoying the time that is coming next. It is like you are looking forward to it.

We use this word in many other words too. If you tell someone you are going, you are following the day. If you say you are going to the store, you are following the day. You are following the step you do every day.
