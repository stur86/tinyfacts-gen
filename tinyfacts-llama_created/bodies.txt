The word bodies means things that are living. They are made of many parts. In your body you have a head, a body, arms, legs, a chest, and a bottom. Your head is the top part. Your chest is the middle part. Your arms and legs are the bottom parts.

Your body is made of many tiny parts. The tiny parts are like small pieces of wood. They are all stuck together and make your body. Your body can move. You can walk, run, jump, and fly through the air. You can also feel things. You can see, hear, smell, and feel the ground under your feet.

Your body needs food to stay strong. You eat food every day. Your body takes the food and breaks it down. This makes you grow. Your body also needs water. You drink water from a bottle, a cup, or a glass. Water is very good for you. It helps you keep your skin clear and your body warm.

Some bodies are very small. A tiny body is so small you need a glass to hold it. Other bodies are very big. A baby body is like a small house with many rooms. A dog body is like a big dog. A horse body is like a very strong dog with four long legs.

Some people have bodies that can change. A baby grows into a grown person. A person can get very old very quickly. An old body might feel very heavy. A person might feel very tired. Their body might change because of things that happen in life.

Your body is very important. It helps you live. It gives you the power to walk, run, and move. It keeps your head on your body and your bottom on the ground. It makes you able to feel and see the world around you.
