Begins with a word that means life. In this word, we talk about what makes things living. A living thing is something that can move, eat, and grow. You can find living things in the air, water, ground, and even in the very bright light in the sky.

There are many kinds of living things. Some are big and some are small. Some need the warm ground to live, and some can move through the air to find food. There are tiny things that are too small to see, and there are the huge ones that cover the whole world. Some live in the water, while others live in the ground, or fly through the air. Some live in hot places, and some live in cold places.

We also study what living things need to live. We look at how they eat, where they get the water, and how they make food from the air. We try to understand how they grow and how they change as they get older. This helps us learn more about the world and how it works.

People often use this word to talk about how things change over time. A living thing can grow bigger, change color, or change its form. Sometimes, a living thing can die and then come back to life. This is a big part of how we understand the world and how it changes.

So, the word tells us that life is what makes things work and what keeps our world going.
