To be is to be not nothing. It is a word that helps us talk about people, things, and how they act.

When you are a person, you are a thing. You are a person, you live, you breathe, you grow, you learn, you feel, you love, you cry, and you act. You are a person because you are here now, and you are not dead.

When you are not a person, you are not here. You are a thing, a thing that does not have a heart, a brain, or a body. You are a thing that is only there until you are taken away.

But the word is not just about people. It is also about how things act. If you say something is true, you mean it is real. If you say something is not true, you mean it is not real. If you say something is a thing, you mean it is a thing. If you say something is not a thing, you mean it is not a thing.

The word is also about time. When you are a person, you live in the past. You did not live in the present. You are in the moment now, and then you are gone. When you say you are old, you are in the past.

When you are a thing, you have passed. You are not in the past anymore. You are in the present. When you say you are new, you are in the present. You are not old anymore.

So the word is about change. It is about how things act and how people act. It is about time and how we see it.
