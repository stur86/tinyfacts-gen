To park means to leave a car in a place so people can use it later. It is like putting a car in a quiet area so it does not bother anyone. A car might be parked on a street or in a parking lot.

Imagine you are going to a city with many buses. You need to find a spot where you can stop the bus without hurting anyone. The bus driver looks at a sign that says where the bus is going and where it will drop you off. They might also look at the sign to see how many other buses are coming. This helps them decide where to stop.

When you want to park a car, you tell the people in the area that you need to stop. They might ask you to show your papers or to follow the signs. They also might ask if you want to park near the bus stop or if you want to stay far away from the street.
