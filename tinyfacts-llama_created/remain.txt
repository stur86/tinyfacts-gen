To remain means to stay in a place without going away. It is when you are not moving from one place to another. You can be in a room, a house, or even in the sky.

Imagine you are in a city. You are standing on a street. You look around at the tall houses and the people walking. You are happy and feeling safe. You do not want to go. You stay in that place.

If you are in a school, you stay there for many years. You learn many things and make many friends. You do not want to go home. You stay with your friends and the teacher until you are old enough to go to a different school.

You can also remain in a way that is not safe. If you have a hurt body, you may need to stay in a room for a while. Your body is not working right, and you need to rest. You stay there until the doctor says it is safe to go.

Sometimes, people remain in a place for a very long time. This can happen when a person wants to stay in a place where they feel happy. They may stay in a city they love and do not want to leave. This is a good thing, because they are happy and safe.

In some stories, a person remains in a place. They do not go away, even when they want to. They stay and fight for what they believe in. This is a strong way to show that they are not afraid to stand up for what is right.
