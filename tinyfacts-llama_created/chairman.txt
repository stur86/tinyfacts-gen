A chair is a thing you sit on. Most of the time, you sit on a hard chair in a room. You can also sit on a soft chair in a room with people you know.

When a person is the head of a group, they are often called the chair. This is a good job because they make sure everyone follows the right way. They also help decide what the group does. The chair might say, if we want to go to the park, we should go. They help keep everyone safe.

You can also be the chair of a game. This means you are the person who makes sure everyone plays well. You might pick who goes first or who does not. You help the group stay happy and have fun.

Sometimes, you might be the chair of a job. This is when you are the person who makes sure everyone does their work. You tell the people what they need to do and who is doing what job. This helps the job go well and everyone gets their work done on time.

People also use the word chair to talk about a person who is a leader. If someone is a great leader, they are like the chair. They tell everyone what to do and keep them safe.

In a school, a chair might be the person who tells everyone what to do. They might say, we must follow the teacher. They help keep everyone in line and make sure the school runs well.
