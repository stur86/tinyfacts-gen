The word understands has many different meaning. It can mean something is not there. It can mean no one is doing it. It can mean the floor or the ground is empty.

Think about a bed. If you lay down on the bed, you are on it. But if you are not on the bed, you are under it. You are not touching the top of the bed. You are under it. This is what understands means.

It can also mean something is not true. If you say you are going to the store, but you are not, you are under the table. This means you are not telling the truth. You are hiding the fact that you are not going.

Sometimes we say under the table. This means we are hiding something or we are not telling the truth. This is a word that people use to stay quiet.

We also use this word when we talk about money. If you have money, you are under it. You are holding it. But if you do not have money, you are under the table. This means you are in a hard place and you do not have what you need.
