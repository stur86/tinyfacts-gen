To own something means you have it. When you own a thing, you are the one who gets to decide how it is used. You are the one who gets to decide if the person who does not own it is allowed to be near it.

Think about a house. If you live in that house, you own it. You have the right to be there, to use the rooms, and to leave it in the same way you found it. If you let someone live in your house, that person does not own it. They can only stay until they pay the money you gave them.

You can also own animals. If you buy a dog, you are the one who owns it. You pay money to the person who made it, and they tell you how to take care of it. You are the one who decides if the dog is well or not.

Sometimes, you own a piece of land. If you buy a small piece of land, you own it. You pay money and you get to use it for a long time. You do not have to let anyone else use it.

When you own something, you are safe. You are the one who decides what happens to the thing. You can make it better or you can make it worse. You can also decide who can have it and who should not.

People also own their money. When you work and you get money, you can use it to buy things you want. If you own money, you have power over how you spend it.
