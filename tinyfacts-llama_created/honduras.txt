Hundreds is a big word that tells us how many things are there. You can counters them with your hands. If you have one thing, you have two. Three is three. Five is five. If you keep going, you have a hundred.

When you say you live in a far place, you are saying you live in many hundred houses. Maybe you live in a city. Maybe you live in a small town. Maybe you live on a big land with many roads. You could live in a place with a lot of water.

In some places, the word is used for a kind of money. It is like a very big number with many numbers in it. People use these numbers to buy things they need. You can pay with these numbers, and then the person gives you the things you want.

There is also a word that tells us how old a person is. If you are five, you are one year old. If you are ten, you are two years old. If you are twenty, you are three years old. You keep going, and you are twenty one, twenty two, twenty three, and so on.

A long time ago, a man in a far place said, he was one hundred years old. He lived for a very long time, and he saw many things. Now, we know that if you start a new year when you are one hundred years old, you are one hundred years old again. It is a very big number!
