An air or space around the world is called the world. We also call the sky in front of us the sky. The world is where you live. It is where all the houses and streets and cars are. It is also the blue big thing in the sky that looks like a big ball.

An air is the tiny stuff that you breathe in. When the air comes into your body, it goes into your chest and into your blood. It helps your body move and keeps you warm.

An air around the world is like a big round thing that keeps everything in place. It is made of many small pieces of air. These pieces are moving all the time, but they stay together in the round thing so they do not fly away.

The air also helps keep the ground and the water in place. The ground does not go up into the air, and the water does not go down into the ground. The air keeps everything in the world from falling off.

There is a big round thing in space that is like the air around the world. This thing goes very, very fast and is always turning. It keeps the stars and the other big things in space in their places.

When you look up at the sky at night, you are looking at the air. The stars are made of the same kind of air as the sky.
