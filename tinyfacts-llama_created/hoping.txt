When you hope for something, you are not sure if it will happen. You do not know if a person will do what you want or if a thing will work as you think it will. You just feel that things are going to be okay.

You might hope for a new day. You think it will be a better day than the one before. You hope that your family will have a nice time together, and that everyone will be happy. You hope that the sun will come out and that the air will be warm. You hope that the food you eat will be good and that you will feel strong and happy.

You might hope for a new friend. You think the person you want to be friends with is out there somewhere, and you hope they will reach out to you. You hope that we will talk and laugh together and share fun things. You hope that we will become close friends and that we can help each other when we need it.

You can also hope for a good thing to happen to you. Maybe you hope a big job will come your way, or maybe you hope your car will fix so you can go on a trip. You hope that the money you need will come through, so you can buy the things you want.

Hoping is a good feeling. It shows that you believe something will happen, and it can help you feel safe and glad when good things do come.

Sometimes you hope for things that are not real. You hope a girl will like you, even if you do not know her yet. You hope a game will be fun and exciting. You hope that a movie will make you laugh and feel happy.

When you hope, you are not afraid. You are not worried about what might happen. You are just looking forward to the good that is coming.
