A shift is a round part that people use to move money. It is usually made of hard stuff and has lines that go through it. You put money on the lines and then you use the round part to turn the lines and move the money to where you want it.

Imagine you are a money man. You carry a bag of money to a store. But the store does not want to give you the money. So you put the money in the round part and it turns. The round part moves the money from your bag to the store. This makes it easier to carry money to places you want to go.

A shift has a round at one end and a line at the other. The round is what people use to turn the lines. The line is what you put money on. There is also a round at the other end. When you turn the round, it pulls money out of the line and puts it into your bag.

In big houses, there are many shifts. These shifts go all around the house. They carry money from the money man in the room where the money is kept to the people who pay their money to pay their money. The shifts help people give money to the right places so that the money goes where it needs to go.

Shifts can also be used for other jobs. You might use one to carry money to people who need it. Or you might use one to carry money for a thing that is not money. It is a very important part of how money works in our world.
