To raise something means to make it bigger or stronger. You can raise a small animal or a child. You can also raise money for a person or a group who want to help.

Imagine you are a teacher. You want your students to learn more. You stand up in front of them and tell them to keep going. You are helping them to become better.

You can also raise your voice when you are angry. This means you are saying things that are stronger than your usual words. This helps you get the attention of others.

If you are the head of a team, you may raise your voice to tell the other people what you want done. You want the team to work together as a group.

Sometimes, you raise money for a good cause. You go to a room and ask people to give you money. You use the money to help people who are sick or need food. You are helping others because you want to.

We also raise our voices when we are excited. A big sound can make people laugh or feel happy. If you are happy, you might raise your voice so everyone can hear you.
