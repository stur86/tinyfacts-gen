The word you are talking about is a number. It is a number from a group of people who make numbers for the world. This group is called a time. They decide which number to use for each place in the world.

Most of these numbers are the same. They are the same when you say them. But one person might say that    morning is   am. Another person might say that  night is  am. If you want to tell someone which time you are at, you would need to know what number you are talking about.

The group also decides which time is the best time of day. They do this so that people know when to go to bed and when to wake up. They decide that   middled  is  half past  am  and that   late  is  past  am  and before   night  starts. This way, people know what to do at different times.

The numbers are used on many things. You can see them on the face of a watch. You can see them on the face of a computer. You can see them on a phone. They help us know when to do things and when to stay still.

The group also decides when to tell time. They decide when to say   am  and when to say  night  so that everyone knows what time it is. This helps keep everything in order.
