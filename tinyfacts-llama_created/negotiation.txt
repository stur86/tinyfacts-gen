To talk and reach an understanding, usually between two people, about how they want things to be.

Imagine you have a very good book that you really want. You are going to school and need the book for your class. The person who owns the book does not want to give it away. You and your friend sit down and talk. You both want to be the one with the book. You use your words to see if you can find a way that works for both of you.

One person might say, you can keep the book, but you have to do some work for me. The other person might say, you have to give me the book right away. You both try to understand each other.

Sometimes people use this word when they talk about money. If you want to buy a thing and the person who owns it does not want to let you, you are in a situation where you need to talk and reach an understanding. Maybe you want to pay more money, or maybe you want the thing for less money. You try to find a way that makes both people happy.

In other times, people use this word for other things too. If you and your family are in a fight, you might talk and try to find a way out. This is an important word because it shows that you are trying to find a way that works for everyone.
