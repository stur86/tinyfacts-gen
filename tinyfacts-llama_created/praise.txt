To give something is to say that it is good. You can give a child a big hug for being nice. You can say,  You are a great friend. You are giving me a big smile.

You can also give money to someone who does a good job. This is called a kind word. You give them money so they can buy food or a new book.

Sometimes, you give a person a word that tells they are very good at something. If you do a great job, you might say, You are doing a great job. This helps the person feel happy and want to keep doing it.

You can also give a word to a person when you like them. You might say, You are a great person. This word makes them feel happy and want to be around you.

Everyone gives something different. You might give your heart. You give your love. You give your time. You give your words. These are all ways to show you care.
