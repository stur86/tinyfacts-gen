To do something year after year means you do it every year for a very long time.

If you are happy with your life, you might do something that makes you happy every year. You might go to a place you love, spend time with your family, or work on something you are very good at.

If you are studying, you might do the same thing every year. You might read books, learn new things, and do things that help you learn. This is important because it helps you become the person you want to be.

Sometimes, people do something that is fun every year. They go to a place with a big water, go to a far away city, or have a big party with their friends. This is a great way to spend your time because you can do something you love many times.

Also, the year is the time that passes from one year to the next. It is the number of days from one year to the next. You can start a new year on a new day. Sometimes, people use the word to talk about big changes that happen every year.

Think of it like a big line. The line starts from the very first day of the year. Every day that comes after that is another day on the line. You go around the line one hundred times and you come out the other end. This is one year. You go around the line two hundred times and you come out the other end. You go around the line three hundred times, and so on.

So, the word year is about doing the same thing over and over again, and how it changes with time.
