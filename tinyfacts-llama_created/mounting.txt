To put something up can mean two different things.

First, when you put something on a wall, you are putting it up. You are making a place where a thing is so people can see it. This can be a big picture, a picture of a person you like, or a small thing that is important for your life. When you put something on a wall, you are giving it a home.

The second way is when you put a person up for something. If someone does something very good, a teacher might say, they are doing a good job. If you think a person is the best at a game, you might say they are the best. In this case, the person is being put up as a way of showing you how much you like them. It is like saying, you are the best, and you want people to know about it.

In a different way, when you put something in a bag, you are putting it away. You are taking it and putting it in a place where it is safe and quiet. This can be a bag you use to carry your things when you go to school or a bag you have at home.
