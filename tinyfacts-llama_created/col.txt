To color something means to make it look bright and warm. This happens when light hits a thing. The color is what the light looks like. When you put white light into a box, it stays white. But if you put it through the box, the white light gets broken into many small parts and each part makes a different color.

The box has three parts. The part at the top lets light in. The part in the middle lets some of the light go through, and the part at the bottom keeps the light inside. The light goes through each part and changes how much light it takes in and how much it keeps.

The middle part is like a door. It opens and closes to let some light in and keep some light out. If the door is open, the light can go all through the box and the box looks bright. If the door is closed, the light stays inside.

The part at the bottom keeps the light inside. This is why we see the world around us, even though our eyes are not looking at a box. The box only lets light out, and the room keeps everything inside.

The word comes from the old days when people did this with wood. They would put wood in a box with a door. The wood would get brighter and warmer as it went through the box. The wood got a color. This was a way to make things look good for people who did not have a light box.

Today we do this with computers and pictures. We put a picture into a box and the box makes the picture look bright and warm. This makes it look like it is coming from a real place.
