A woman who was important in the past is called an annoys. This word is used when talking about a person from a long time ago. She was a woman who did many things. She was very good at what she did. She was a kind person too.

In the old days, a person could be called an annoys for many years. If a person did something very good, they might get this name. It is like a big sign that says, you are important.

Sometimes, we use this word for a man who is very good at a job. He might be a doctor or a teacher. He does not need to say it, but we say it because he is very good at what he does.

You might hear people say, she is an annoys today. This means she is very good today, like today.
