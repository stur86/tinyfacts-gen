To buy something means you use money to get it. You can buy food, clothes, or things you want to play with. A store is a place where people buy things. They have many things to buy and you pick the one you like.

If you do not have enough money, you might not be able to buy what you want. This can be very sad. Sometimes, people have to wait for the money to come, or they have to wait until their next pay day.

There is also a word that is a bit different. When you buy something, you give the person the money. This is a way to show that you care about the thing. You say, here is my money, so you can have it. This is like a way to pay someone.

In some places, you have to pay for a thing before you start to use it. If you do not pay, you might not be allowed to use it. This is important because it keeps everyone safe and helps the city stay clean.
