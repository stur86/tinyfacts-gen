To be ever is to stay the same for a very long time. When you are living, you are changing and growing. You are becoming a person. But if you do not change, you stay the same. You do not grow older. You do not get bigger. You do not get smaller. You stay the same.

Even a tree stays the same for many years. It grows new leaves and new wood, but it does not change its form. It stays the same as it was before. This is because it is a big thing that stays one way.

Even when you die, you are not gone. You are still there. Your body is gone, but your heart, your mind, and your memories are still there.

Being ever can be hard. You have to stay the same even when things are changing around you. You might feel sad when a friend dies, even though the friend is gone. You might feel happy when you see a beautiful place, even though it is where you lived for the last time. You have to stay the same, even if everything else is changing.

You can also be ever when you love someone very much. You love them even when they do not love you. You love them even when they say they hate you. You love them even when you want to shout and scream. You love them even when they are very far away.

Being ever is a way to feel safe. When you feel safe, you are not afraid. You are not worried about what is going to happen next. You just stay there. You stay the same.
