To hope is to feel that things will be better. When you hope, you think that things are going to get better. You might hope that you will find a friend who likes you. Or you might hope that you will get a job. You hope that the sun will stay up tomorrow.

When you hope, you are not sure if it will happen. You do not know if the sun will stay up or if the rain will come. But you want it to happen. You want to feel happy and safe.

Sometimes, you hope for a good thing. You hope that you will be happy when you finish a hard job. You hope that the food you eat will make you feel full and strong. You hope that a new game will be fun to play.

If you hope for something, you are not giving up. You are still looking forward to it. You are still thinking about it. You are not afraid because you do not know if it will happen. You are just trying to imagine what it might feel like.

You can hope for many things. You hope for a better life. You hope for a friend to call you. You hope for a new home. You hope for a bright day.
