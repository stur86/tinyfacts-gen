To use a word means you are using it. You are saying it is there because of the person who told you to use it. The word is not the person. The person is the word.

Imagine you want to tell a friend you love them. You do not say the word. You say something else. You say, I love you. You are saying the word is not the thing you say. The word is only the word.

Think of a book. You read the book. The book is the thing you read. You are not the book. You are only the person who reads it. They tell you to read the book because of the story.

When you talk about a story, you use words. You might say, The book was very good. Here, you are using the word. You are not the book. You are only telling them how you feel about the book.

You can also use a word to talk about a person. You might say, She is nice. Here, you are using the word. You are not the person. You are only telling them how you feel about the person.

When you are writing a story, you might use many words. Some of those words are the same as the word you are using. You use them to tell the story. You are not the story. You are only the person who is telling the story.

This is why you need to be carefully when you use a word. You must use it to tell the story, not to say it is the word.
