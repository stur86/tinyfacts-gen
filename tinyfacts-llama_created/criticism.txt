To give a person or thing something to think about is to give them a job. You are telling them to look at what they are doing and to see if it is good or bad.

Sometimes, you give someone a word. You are telling them that their word is not right. You are saying that it is not good or that it is not true. You are not happy with what they say. You want them to change their way of thinking.

You can also give someone a thing to look at. You are telling them to watch what is happening. You are giving them a job to see if they are doing the right thing. If they see something wrong, they have to fix it.

If you are a writer, you might give your story to others to read. You are telling them to look at the story and see if it is good. If they like it, they will say nice things about it. If they do not like it, they might say bad things.

If you are a person, you can give yourself a job. You might say to yourself, I am doing this wrong. I should be doing it this way. You are trying to fix your way of doing things.

The word comes from an old time when you gave someone a job. A job was a way to pay for doing work. You would do the job and get money back. Now, we use it to talk about how others see us.
