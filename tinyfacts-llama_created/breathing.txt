To breathe means you take in air from the sky and then you push it out from your body. You do this many times every minute. You use the air to keep your body warm and to give you the power to move.

Your nose and mouth are the places where you breathe in. You put air from the sky into your body through your nose and mouth. This air goes down into your chest and then into your stomach. Your stomach is the big bag inside your body that holds many things you have eaten.

When you breathe in, the air goes into your body and gets into your chest. Your body uses the air to make things happen inside you. You use the air to make your heart beat, to make your arms and legs move, and to make your brain think.

When you breathe out, the air goes out of your body. You push the air through your mouth and out your nose. This helps keep your body from getting too full of air. If you breathe out too much, you might feel tired and your face might feel funny.

Sometimes you breathe in and out very fast. This can make you feel a bit funny. Your heart might beat quickly and your face might feel cold. This is because your body is trying to get ridden of the air that is inside you.
