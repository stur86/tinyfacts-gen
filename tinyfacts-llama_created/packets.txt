A pack is a small group of things that you send over the phone, the computer, or the world wide way. It is like a tiny box with many small pieces inside.

Imagine you want to give a book to a friend. You do not carry the whole book with you. You put the book in a box with many small pieces of paper. Each piece of paper has a few words on it. When your friend gets the box, they can read the words and get the book.

A pack is the same thing. You put many small things together in one box. When you send the box, you tell the person on the other end that you have a small group of things for them. The box is like a tiny group of things.

You can send a pack by phone, computer, or by using a box that is put in the ground. The box goes to the person who needs the things. When the box arrives, the person can open it and read the things.

Packs are used for many things. You can use one to send a small word to a friend. You can use one to send a picture. You can use one to send money. You can use one to send a book to someone who does not have a book.

Packs are like small groups of things that help us share things with other people. They are very good at moving small pieces from one place to another.
