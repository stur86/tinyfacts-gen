To marry is to join two people together. When a man and a woman love each other, they say they are going to get married. This is a very important step in their lives. It means they are going to live together and start a family.

A woman who is going to marry is called a girl. A boy who is going to marry is called a man. When a girl and a boy get married, they wear a white dress for the first day and a big white tie on the next day. This is because they are going to be married to each other for many years.

A woman can also get married to a man who is already married. This happens if the man is older than the girl and wants to make a new family. It is like starting a new life with the person he loves.

Getting married is a big change. It is a day when two people become one person. They share their life, their money, and their love with each other. It is a very happy day for everyone.
