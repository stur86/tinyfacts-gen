A tomorrows is a word that is used when you talk about days in the past. It is the word for the day that is after today.

Think about how you feel when you look at your phone. You look at it every morning. You know what day it is. You know what time it is. You know what time you go to bed. You know what day you are on.

If you want to talk about days, you say, tomorrow. Tomorrow is the day after today. It is the day before today. It is the day when you are waiting for today to end.

You can also say, tomorrow will be a good day. Tomorrow will be better than today. Because tomorrow is after today, tomorrow is a better day.
