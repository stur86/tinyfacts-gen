An accept is a thing you do when you agree with someone. If you are happy with what they say, you might say,  I accept this. You are not trying to change their mind. You just want to be on the same team.

It can also mean a thing that is in your head. If you think a thing is true, you are accepting it. This is how you know if something is right. You are not afraid to say, I accept this.

Sometimes, you might accept a problem. If you do not like the way you act, you can try to change. You can ask for help or you can try to do something different. You are not giving up. You are still trying to do the right thing.

There are also things you accept in games. If you play with another person and you are not the best, you might still play. You are still having fun. You are not afraid of losing. You are ready to try again.
