To open a place where people keep money is called a business. Many people live in this kind of place. When you want to get money or put money into a safe place, you go to this business. They have a big room with computers and a way to keep numbers. They also have people who help you.

Imagine you want money to buy a car. You tell the person at the business how much money you have. They look at the numbers and give you the money. You can then use this money to buy the car. If you have too much money, you can keep it in your room.

Bodies also keep money for the people who give them money. When you give them money, they take it and put it into their computers. This way, the money stays safe and you do not have to worry about it being gone.

Some places are only for one kind of money. If you want to put money into the computer and then take it out later, you have to go to another kind of place. These places are not open at night and are usually very strong to keep the money safe.
