When people talk, they often make their words and sounds so that others can hear them. This is called a conversation. In a story or a movie, the people talk to each other. In a book, the people talk between the lines.

This is what people do when they speak to each other. It is like a small talk. They talk about the day, what they did, or what happened before. They also talk about their hopes and fears. This helps people understand each other.

A person might say a word. The other person hears it. Then they might say something back. This is the talk. The words are like the lines in a story. They show who is saying what and why.

Sometimes, two people do not speak at all. They just look at each other. They know what the other person wants, and they act on it. This is how a person feels, without using words.
