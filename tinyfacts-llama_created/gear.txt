To put a thing in a way that it turns easily

You see a thing that makes things move around. If you pull it, it turns. If you push it, it goes in a line. When you are driving a car, you put a thing in the car that makes it move. This thing is called a car. You put it in a way that it turns easily. If you push it, it goes in a line. If you pull it, the car turns.

When you are building a thing, you put it in a way that it turns easily. You put a round thing at the top. When you turn the round thing, the thing falls down. This is how a car falls down the road. Or you put a round thing on the end of a long line. When you pull the line, the round thing turns, and the line turns too.

You can also use this word for things that make things easy to do. A door that opens easily is a thing you put in a way that it turns. It is a thing that makes it easy to get in or out. A thing that turns is something you put in a way that it turns. This is how you make things easier to do.
