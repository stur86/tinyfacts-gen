People often talk about a person or a thing. They say the name, and then they tell more about it. This is what the word least means.

To be least is to have less of something. Think about how you feel when you have no money. You can buy many things. But if you have only one small thing, you feel very sad. This is because you have less to do with.

In the old days, a man was least if he had no money. He might not be able to buy food. He would be very tired and sad. This was a hard life. People tried to help each other. They shared what they had so the man could eat too.

Today, we use this word in many ways. We say a person is least if they are tired. We say the world is least if there are too many people. We also say that something is least if it is very small. This is because the word is about having less.

So, least is about having less. It tells us about how someone feels or how the world is.
