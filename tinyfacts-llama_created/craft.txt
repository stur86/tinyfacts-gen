To make something new is to use your hands and your brain to change old things. You start with a thing you already have, like a piece of wood, a ball of paper, or a small box. You think about how you want the thing to look and feel. You look at pictures, you watch other people make things, and you try to figure out how you can make the thing you want.

You start with a few pieces and then you do the work of many small steps. You cut the wood, you cut the paper, you cut the paper into small pieces. You put the small pieces into the box. You put the box together. You add a soft cover. You add a strong cover. You make sure everything is right.

When you are finished, the thing is new. You did not use the old things anymore. You made something better. You made something that you can hold and use. You made something that can bring you a lot of fun.

You can make many things. You can make a chair. You can make a house. You can make a car. You can make a picture. You can make a story. You can make your own words. You can make your own music. You can make your own pictures. You are a person who makes things new.
