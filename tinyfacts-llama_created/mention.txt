To mention something means to tell others about it. You might say, I want to tell you about my dog. I am going to tell you about him. He is a small animal with further than a cat. He has a long hair all over his body.

You can also mention a person. If you are talking about a friend, you might say, I am going to see my friend later. You are going to talk with him.

Sometimes you mention things that are not really there. You might talk about a story that never happened. You are just making things up to sound good.

To mention a word is like giving it a name. You are telling others that the word means something else now. It helps people understand each other.

You can also mention things in a group. If you are talking to many people, you might mention the same idea. This helps everyone understand what you are talking about.
