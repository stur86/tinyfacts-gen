A person can make a big help to a group when they give them a thing they do not have. This can be a word, a picture, a story, or even a small act. When a person acts in this way, everyone is a little better because they get something that was missing. This kind of help is called a small part.

A person can also give money to a place that does not have it. This can help the person have things they need or help the place to stay open. In this way, the person is helping the people.

Sometimes, a group of people work together to build a house. Each person does something different, and together they build a strong house that is safe for everyone. They all share the work so that everyone feels part of the group. This is a big thing that happens when many people work together.
