To do something alone means you are the only person doing it. You have no other person helping you. You are doing it all by yourself.

Think about a tree. A tree does not need anyone else to grow. It just sits there with its green parts and its strong wood. It makes its own food from the sun and the air around it. It does not need anyone to help it.

This is what we mean when we say someone does something alone. You did something without another person. You were the only one there.
