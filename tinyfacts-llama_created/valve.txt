A big thing in a car works by letting water go through it so it can go where it needs to go. This thing is very important because it keeps the car from breaking down.

Imagine you are drinking a glass of water. You want to drink it slowly so you do not get too much at once. If the glass has a small opening, you can drink from it without getting the water all over your face and clothes. This opening allows the water to go out while keeping the water inside. This is what a big water thing in a car does.

There are many kinds of these things. You might find one that opens and closes. You might find one that is covered with a round thing. You might find one that is made of hard stuff. All of them work in the same way. They all let water go out and keep the water in.

These things are very important in cars because they help the car move. They keep the car running and do not break. If you did not have them, the car would stop and you would have to get another one.

Sometimes, these things are used in other things too. You might see a round thing on a water line in your home. It helps the water go where it needs to go from your kitchen to your bathroom.
