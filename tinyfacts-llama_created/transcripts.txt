A computer is a thing that does many jobs. It can help with writing, make music, and show pictures. A computer does not have a brain like a human. It does not think. It only follows a set of numbers. When you type on a computer, the numbers it follows become a word, a picture, or a song.

A group of people who are not friends can make a computer do something. They write a set of numbers that tell the computer what to do. They put these numbers into a box. The computer reads the numbers and does the job. The people then see what the computer did.

Sometimes, when a group of people wants to tell the world something, they make a box of numbers. This box of numbers is called a computer. The computer reads the numbers and sends the story to the world. The world can then read the story.

A computer is like a very fast way to keep a story. You can write many stories and put them in one place. When you want to read them later, the computer reads them all at once. It does not need to think like a person. It only follows what is in the numbers.

People use computers for many reasons. They can look at pictures that are many hundreds of times larger than what you can see with your eyes. They can play music and watch movies. They can talk to other people who are far away. They can even do work that takes a lot of time.

In some places, computers are used for things that are not safe. They can give you a bad word if you type it wrong. They can also give you a picture that is not real. These things should not be done without a grown person telling them to.
