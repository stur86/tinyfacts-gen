When we talk about others, we are talking about people who are not us.

Imagine you are in a room with many friends. Each person is different. Some are happy, some are angry, and some are calm. You might laugh with one friend, be sad with another, or feel angry with a third. If you talk to them, you are talking to each one. You are not talking to yourself.

This is what we mean when we say, I am talking to others. You are not the only one in this room. There are many people around you, and you are one of them. You are not alone.

We also talk about others when we are about something. If you are playing a game with a friend, you might say, I am doing this. But if you are talking about a book, you are also talking about others because you are reading it.

Sometimes, when we are talking, we use the word others to mean many people. We might say, We are going to see many people today. Or we might say, I have many friends. When we do this, we are not just talking about one person. We are talking about many people.

So, others is about people who are not us. It is about the many people around us, and it is about the many people we talk about.
