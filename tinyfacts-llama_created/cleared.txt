A clear is something you see without any things in it. When you look through a window, or through a piece of paper, you are looking at a clear thing. It is very bright and you can see every small thing.

If you are in a dark room, it is not clear. You have to look through your hands to see what is around you. If you cover the window, you can still see what is outside. But if you open it, the room is clear again.

You can also clear a small hard thing. You use a thing that cuts through wood. When you cut wood, the wood goes away in small pieces. This is like clearing a small hard thing. It makes the thing much smaller so you can see what is inside.

Sometimes, a clear can mean something that is very clean. You might see a clear piece of water. The water is very clear and you can see all the tiny things in it. This means the water is very clean. It is not full of bad things or tiny pieces that you can't see.

In a game with cars, a clear can mean a place where the cars can stop without hitting other cars. This is a safe place for people to drive.
