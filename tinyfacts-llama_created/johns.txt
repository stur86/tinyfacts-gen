People often talk about a group of people who do the same thing. These people are usually not big like a city. They live in a place with many houses, like a town, but they are not part of the town. They might live near the water or in the green places.

Most of the time, they do the same work every day. They might work in a big building, a park, or a house. They might make food, clean rooms, or move things from one place to another. Some of them might be doctors, teachers, or police people. They all work together to keep the place safe and make sure everyone is well.

They may be very different from each other. One might be very tall and strong. Another might be very small and fast. Some might speak a different kind of word or have a different way of talking. But they all share one thing: they are a group of people who do the same job.
