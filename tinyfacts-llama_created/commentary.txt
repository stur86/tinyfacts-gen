To comment is to talk about something that is happening. You might talk to a friend while watching a game. You could say, like, how good the player is or how the ball is flying through the air.

Sometimes people talk about a movie while they are watching it. They might say what they think is the best part or what they do not like. This is a kind of talking about something that is already happening. It can help you understand what is going on.

You can also comment on a thing that is not happening. If you are waiting for a phone to ring, you might say, the door is opening. This means the person who is calling is near. You are telling the people around you what is going on.

Sometimes you comment on a game. You might say, the ball is going to the left. This means you think the person who is playing will move the ball to the left side of the field. You are helping the people who are watching by telling them what you think is happening.

In life, you might comment on things that are happening. You might say, the sun is coming up. This is a way of talking about what is happening at the time of day. You are sharing your idea with others.

You can also comment on something that is not happening. You might say, the food is bad. This means you do not like the food you have eaten. You are telling others about what you think is wrong with it.
