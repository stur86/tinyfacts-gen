When something is allowed to go out, it can cause a change. Think about a door in a house. If you close it, the room stays small. But if you open it, the room becomes bigger and you can see more things outside.

Animals have this too. When they are happy, they breathe quickly. When they are afraid, they are quiet. Their bodies do this to keep them safe.

We can also use this word for people. When you let someone go, they leave the place where they are staying. This can happen in many ways. You can send a letter to a friend in another town. You can give money to help someone who is in a bad place. Or, you can let a child go home from school so they can rest.

Sometimes, people use the word to mean that a story is ending. The story is no longer new. The ending is the part where the story ends, and there is nothing more to say.

We also talk about letting things go into the water. If you put food into a water bottle, it will stay there. If you throw it away, the food will go out into the water and help animals and people who need it.
