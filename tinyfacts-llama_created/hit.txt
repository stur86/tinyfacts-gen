To hit something is to make a hard sound by moving your body over it. When you hit a ball, you use your hands to hit the ball so it goes to a place where it can not move. You are using your power to make the ball stop moving.

You can hit a rock, a tree, or a wall. If you hit a tree, the wood on the outside of the tree gets broken. The tree falls down and it makes a sound that you hear. This is the same kind of sound you hear when you hit a ball.

You can also hit things that are not a ball. You might hit a book. When you hit a book, you are using your hands to push it. This makes the cover of the book break. The hard part of the book falls to the floor.

Sometimes, you hit something that you do not want. If you hit your sister, she might get angry. This can cause a fight. If you hit a dog, the dog might feel bad for a long time. The dog might not want to be near you anymore.

It is also possible to hit your own body. If you hit your knee while you are running, you might feel the pain. You might see blood coming out of your knee. This can be a bad feeling.
