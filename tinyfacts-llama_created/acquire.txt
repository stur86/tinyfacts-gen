To Across means to cross a wide water or a high ground. Imagine a long line of people. Each person wants to reach the other side. They use the water to get from one place to another. They walk on the water or hold onto the top of a tall thing so they can not fall. Some people walk for many days. Others use a long car or a big thing that goes across the water.

You can also say you have something to across. You want to get to a far place. You are going to do this because you need it for work or for a new life. It is a big step. You might feel a little scared because you are moving away from your home. But you know it is the right thing to do.

Sometimes, you can across a high ground. You see the top of the world. You feel the wind and the sun on your face. You can see far into the sky. This is a great feeling.

To Across can also mean to learn something new. You watch a movie or a show. You learn about a new world, a new way of life, or a new idea. You are moving your mind and your heart. You are opening your eyes to new things.

When you say you are across a problem, you mean you are working to fix it. You try to find a way out. You think about what you need to do and you start doing it. You do not give up. You keep going until you reach a good place.
