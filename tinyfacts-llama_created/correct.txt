To be right means that you are doing what the person says or what is written. If you do something, and it is not what you wanted to do, you have made a wrong thing. This happens when you say something and it is not true.

If you see a person and you think they are not who they are, you have made a wrong thing. You did not see them right. This is a bad thing to do.

You can also be right in a different way. If you write a story and it is not true, the story is not right. You made a wrong thing by changing what happened.

In a game, if you get a number that is not the right one, you have made a wrong thing. You did not play the game right. This is also a bad thing to do.

Being right is important. It helps us know what is true and what is not. It makes us feel good when we see that something is right and bad when we see that something is wrong.
