To put something out is to send it to someone else. You can put something out in many ways. You can walk out the door and go to a far place. You can put a thing in a box so it is safe inside another box.

The word is often used when something is being let go. If you are a person and you do not want to work anymore, you might put yourself out. This means you are leaving your job and your home to start a new life with another person.

You can also put out money. If you have money in your pocket, you can put it out to someone who needs it. This is when you give money to a person who is in trouble. It helps them pay their own money when they are very sick or need food.

There is also a word that means to put something in the air. When you put out a fire, you are putting it out. You are letting it go so it does not burn any more. You are also letting people who are afraid feel better. This is a way to help when there is a big fire in a town.

Sometimes, putting out a thing means to make it not happen anymore. If you are angry at someone, you might put out your anger. You are letting it go from your heart so you can be kind again.
