A couple is a group of two people who are in love. They are together and they live with each other. They usually have a bed to sleep in together. They also have a home, a car, and money.

Sometimes, a couple is just two people who are very good friends. They like to do the same things and they like the same food. They have a lot of fun together. They might go to the movies, play games, or go to a park.

You can also have a couple of animals. A couple of dogs is when two dogs are together. They are very close and they like to play with each other. A couple of cats is when two cats are together. They like to sleep in the same spot and they like to hide behind the same thing.

In your life, you might be a couple. You and your best friend are like a couple. You do the same things and you talk a lot. You are very close and you trust each other.

A couple can also mean a group of people who are working together. They build a building, make a movie, or write a book. They all do the same job and they work together for a long time.
