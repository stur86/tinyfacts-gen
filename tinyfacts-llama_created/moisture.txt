Mothers have a soft part inside their bodies where they give life to their young ones. This soft part is filled with a clear stuff that helps them stay wet even when it is very hot outside. This clear stuff is what we call water. When a tree is very dry and does not give any water, its leaves turn brown and it does not grow. This is because the inside of the tree is also very dry. The inside of the tree needs the clear stuff that comes out of the leaves to stay wet.

Another way to think about it is that a small child who is very happy and full of life has lots of water inside them. When a child is very sad or angry, the inside of their body gets very dry. They can feel cold and their face gets red. This is because the clear stuff is being pushed out of their body.

When water falls from the sky, it is called rain. This is when the clear stuff from the sky comes down to the ground. The ground is like a very wet bed. When the rain hits the ground, it fills up the small spaces and makes the ground soft and wet. This makes it easy to walk and play on the ground.

The clear stuff in our bodies and in the water is very important. It helps keep us from getting very hot. It helps keep our skin and hair from getting dry. It also helps keep our eyes from getting too red. Without this clear stuff, we would get very sick and our bodies would not be able to work right.

Water is also important for growing things. When the ground is wet, the green parts of the things grow. When the ground is dry, the green parts die. This is why we need to water our houses and our cars so that the green parts can stay growing.
