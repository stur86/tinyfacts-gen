A land is a big piece of ground that covers a whole area. It is not water. It is not made of water like a big room in a building.

Most of the world is land. You live on land, and you walk around on it. You build your houses and your cars on it. You go to work and go to school on it. You go to the store or to the movies on it.

There are also big pieces of land in the sky. These are called high places. They are much higher than the ground you walk on. You can see the ground far away from you from the top of a high place. People go to high places to breathe air that is not made of water.

There are also small pieces of land that cover only one or two small areas. These are small pieces of land. You might find them between two big pieces of land. These small pieces are like a tiny piece of a much bigger piece of land.

People also use the word land for a piece of ground that is under the water. This is called water land. You might live on a land that is under the water. This is far away from the land you walk on.
