Once means a very long time ago. Before a long time ago, the air was not safe to breathe. There were bad things in the air that made people sick. People tried to kill these bad things, but they did not work very well.

Then, a man found out that the sun made a white thing that went up in the sky. This white thing was very good at keeping the bad things away. He wanted to catch this white thing so he could keep the air safe. He made a thing that would go around the world and catch the bad things.

The white thing was very good at catching the bad things. It caught the bad things in the air and put them in a way that would not come back down to the world. This made the air safe for people to breathe.

Today, we know that the white thing is still there. We call it the strong white thing. We do not have to worry about it being bad because it is caught. People still use the word once in a while to mean something that is very important or very good.
