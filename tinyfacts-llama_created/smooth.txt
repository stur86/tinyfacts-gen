To be soft means you are not hard. You do not feel like you do when you touch a rock or a piece of wood. You feel like you are a soft thing.

If you put your hand on a piece of paper, the paper feels hard. If you put your hand on a soft thing like a cat, the cat feels nice on your hand. You like the way the soft thing feels.

Some things are very soft. You can not stop your mouth when you eat food. You have to keep going. The food is so small and easy to take in. Your mouth feels happy.

But some things are not soft at all. A rock is very hard. You can hit it with your hand. You can not stop hitting it because it does not give.

You can also say a word is soft. If you say the word, it feels nice. You want to keep talking. You do not want to stop.

Being soft is a good thing. It means you are easy to handle. You are not going to hurt anyone. You are very calm.
