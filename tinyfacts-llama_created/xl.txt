To go through a lot is to move through a lot of things. Imagine you have a big bag to carry food and clothes. If you move through the bag many times, you are going through a lot. You are taking many things with you.

This word is used for many things. You can say, if you go through the city, you are going through many streets. If you go through a big room, you are going through many rooms. It can mean you are doing many things or you are moving through a lot of people.

Sometimes, we use it to talk about a very big number. One hundred is a big number. If you want to say how many times you have to go through something, you can use this word. If you are going through many books, you are going through one hundred books. If you are going through many days, you are going through one hundred days.

It is also used to talk about how much something is. If you buy a very big box, it is going through a lot of money. If you have a lot of money, you are going through many good things. If you go through a hard time, you are going through many hard days.
