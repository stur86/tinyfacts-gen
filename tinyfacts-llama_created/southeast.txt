The word for the direction of the sun during the day comes from how the world looks when you look at it from one side.

Think of a big line of points on the ground. If you move one point to the right, the line is longer. If you move it to the left, the line is shorter. The line is longer when the sun is in the middle of the two points.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. If you stand in front of a big window, the people behind you are to your right. If you stand in front of the window, the people behind you are to your left. This is like the long side of the line. It is the side where the sun is between you and the window.

The word for this direction is from the side of the sun. It is the side where the sun is between you and the ground. When the sun is in this side of the world, you face it all day long. When it is not, you face the side of the world where the sun is not between you and the ground.

The word also comes from the name of a big body of water that is near where the sun is in the sky. The side of the water where the sun is between you and the ground is the side you are facing.
