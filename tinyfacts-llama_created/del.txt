To do with things you have bought

When you buy something you don't need, you get it. Then you use it and it goes out of your hand. You might give it to a friend or put it in a box. After that, the thing is gone.

Sometimes you buy things that you want to keep. You might buy books or clothes. These things are your own. You can keep them for a long time and use them many times.

You can also do with money. If you spend too much money on things you do not want, you are in trouble. You have less money to buy food or a new car. This can cause problems with your family or your friends.

It is also about things that are not in your body. You might buy a new hair. You might buy a new face so you look like someone else. These things make you look different from how you were before.
