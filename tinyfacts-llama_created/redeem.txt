To reader or to reader is to read a thing. But it is also to give back when you have done something wrong.

Think of a boy who loves to read books. One day he reads a book that tells the story of a man who was a great leader. The boy learns that the man was kind and that he showed his friends he cared. The boy feels happy because he saw that the story was true.

Then the boy feels bad because he broke a glass at a party. He does not want to tell the people that he is the one who made the break. But he knows he must tell them.

He goes to see the man who wrote the story. He says, in a soft voice, that he is sorry. He tells the man that he loves to read and that he loves the story because it shows how kind a man can be.

The man smiles and says, you are a good boy. He gives the boy a book with the same name as the one he read. He says, take care of this story and remember the good man who told it.

The boy is happy. He feels like he has done something right. He reads the book many times and learns more about the man and the story. He also learns that he can find the good in people, even when they do not act well.

This is what it means to reader and to reader. You read a story to learn. You reader to show that you care.
