To flash means you make a sudden, bright light in the air. You do this when you are very excited. When a group of people are laughing, you might flash a smile. This happens when you are happy or surprised.

You can also flash a thing. If a car is going very fast, it might flash its lights. This is like a quick jump in the air. A person might flash a finger to get someone to stop. When you are doing something wrong, you might flash a word. This is a way to show that you did not do what you should do.

A flash of money is a small money thing. You might get some money in a short time. This is not the same as the money you keep in your pocket. The money you get is small and might not last a long time.

You can also flash a picture. When you take a picture with a flash, the light comes out of the box and hits the picture. This makes the picture look bright and clear. You might flash a picture of a baby before a family picture. This is a fun way to make the baby look happy.
