To be a friend to someone is a very kind thing to do. When you are a friend to someone, you are not their mom or dad. You are not their sister or brother. You are someone who cares about them.

A person who is a friend is usually one person. They do not have many other friends. They are one person who cares about one other person. If you are a friend to someone, you listen to them. You hear what they say. You do not laugh at them or tell them to stop talking. You just listen and try to understand them.

You also try not to change who they are. You do not try to be them if they are not you. You keep your own ways and your own face. You do not try to wear their clothes or their hair. You just are yourself.

Being a friend is important because it helps people feel safe. When you are a friend, you are not afraid to talk to someone. You do not worry about what they will say. You are ready to listen. This makes people feel happy and at home.

If you are a friend, you help them when they need it. If they are sad, you listen. If they are angry, you try to calm them down. You do not try to change what they are doing. You just show them you care.

Sometimes, being a friend is also about being there for the bad days. When someone is very hurt, you are the one who sits with them. You listen to them and they talk about what they are feeling. You do not try to fix everything at once. You just sit and be there, so they do not feel alone.

Being a friend is a big job. It takes a lot of time and love. But when you are a friend, you do not feel alone. You have someone who cares about you, someone who wants to help you. This makes life a little brighter, even when things get hard.
