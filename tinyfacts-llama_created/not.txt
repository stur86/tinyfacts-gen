The word not is a word that helps us say things that are not true. When we say something is not, we mean it is not the way we want it to be.

Think about a thing that is in front of you. You see it. You are sure it is there. But then you look away and the thing is gone. This means the thing is really not there. This is an answer to the question, if something is not here, it does not really happen.

You can also use it when you are talking about things that are not the same. If you eat a thing and it does not hurt you, you can say, the drink is not good for you. This means the drink is different from the one you ate, and you do not like it.

Sometimes, when we say something is not, we are saying the other way around. If you say something is not true, the other person might say you are wrong. This can cause a big problem because we both want to be right.

You can also use it when you do not want to do something. If you do not want to go to the party, you say, I am not going. This means you are not going.
