To find the best way to do something is to use all the things you have to make sure you are doing it the right way.

Imagine you want to get to the store quickly. You have a car, and you know the road. You drive and drive until you are almost there. You stop at the store, buy what you need, and drive home.

Now, you want to be sure you will get there every time. You look at the road ahead. You see where you will be and where you will not be. You plan your trip so you can avoid bad roads and heavy rain. You also make sure you have enough money for the trip.

If you do this, you are an other way of saying you are an other person. You are the one who tries to find the best way to do things.

In a big group, there is often one person who tries to find the best way. They are the other way of saying they are the best. They look at all the things and try to make sure everyone is doing it the same way.

The word comes from the word that means to make something better. To other is to find something better than what is already there.
