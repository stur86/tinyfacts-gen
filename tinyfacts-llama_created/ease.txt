To use something is to make it easy to do. It is when you do not have to work as hard. You can just take things and move them around. You do not have to think about each step.

Imagine you have a big box with many heavy things inside. You want to move the box to a far place. If you are too strong, you will lift the box many times and feel a lot of pain. But if the box is easy to carry, you can lift it one small time and feel much less pain. This is what it means to use something.

You also use this word to talk about a time when things are going well. If something is going well, you can say it is easy. A person might say, after they started the new job, everything is going very well. They have no more problems to worry about. Everything is going as they want.

It can also mean to let something happen without trying to stop it. You can let a dog go out and play when you are busy, and the dog can run and have fun. You let something happen by not doing anything.
