To use a word means to act with it. You might use a word to ask a person a question, to tell a story, or to show you are sad. A word is a short way to write down what you think.

There are many kinds of words. Some are for animals. Some are for things that are very big. Some are for people. Some words tell us about time. Some words tell us about how we feel. Some words help us talk to each other.

You also have words for things you see. If you look at the sun, you can say it is up in the sky. If you see a big tree, you can say it is tall. If you see a small dog, you can say it is tiny.

Sometimes, people give words to things they do. If you do something nice, you might say you were a good friend. If you eat food, you can say you are full. If you do something bad, you can say you are angry.

There are also words for people. If you say you love a person, you are showing them you care. If you say you are sad, you are showing you are feeling the way you feel. When we use these words, we can be kind and show everyone how we feel.
