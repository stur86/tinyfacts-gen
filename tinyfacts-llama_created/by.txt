To by something means you buy it. It is when you go to a store or the house of a person to get something you want.

If you want a book, you by it. You pay the person who owns it. They give it to you. You now have the book.

If you want to play a game, you by it. You pay the person who is in the game. They let you join. You now play the game together with them.

You can by a thing for many reasons. You might want to have a new car. You might want a friend to talk to. You might want a job. You buy it so you can have it.

The word by can also mean you give a person something. If you by a friend, you give them your money. You give them money so they can buy food or a house.

Sometimes, to by something is the same as to buy it. You buy something and then you by it, too. But buying is when you pay for it. By is when you give it to the person or when you join a group.

You can also by a feeling. If you are sad, you by it. You let the sad feeling stay in your heart.
