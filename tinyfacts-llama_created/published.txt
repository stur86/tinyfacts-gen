To be written is to have been put down on a piece of paper so that people can read them later. This makes things known to many people.

Think about your mother telling you a story. She wants to tell you so you can remember it when you are old. She writes the story down so you can read it later. This is being written.

When a person writes something, they have to think of what they want to say first. They might write a story, a letter, or a picture. They use words to make the story happen. They write words that show how they feel. They write words that tell what is happening.

After they write something, it goes to a place called a paper house. The paper house is where people keep the papers. The papers go to other houses that are far away. The papers might go to a house in a city, a house in a town, or a house in a far away land.

When many houses get the papers, they put them together. They read what is on the papers together. This helps everyone understand the story. It helps us know about other places and what they are like.

Some papers are for people to read. They are like a book. They tell us about people who lived a long time ago. They tell us about their lives, their families, and their friends. They help us learn about how the world was then.

Other papers are for people to write. They are like a letter. They tell a person something or ask them to do something. These papers help people talk to each other.

Finally, many papers are for people to see. They are like a book of names and numbers. People read these papers to find out what has happened. They read about what happened in far away places and how people lived there.

In this way, written words go from one person to many, from one house to many houses, and from many houses to many people. They help us know the world and each other.
