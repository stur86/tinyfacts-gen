To reason means you think about how things work. You look at many facts and try to find a way to say how they are all put together.

Imagine you want to know if a thing is a cat or a dog. You look at how they act and what they look like. You might think, a cat is smaller and has longer legs. This is a way of using facts to figure out what you think. You are not just saying the words, you are using what you know to build a picture in your head.

You do not just use one bit of fact to figure out the whole thing. You look at all the facts and try to find a way to say them together. You might say, a cat is a type of animal, and a dog is a type of animal, and because they both have four legs and a body, they are both animals. This is a kind of reasoning.

Reasoning is important because it helps us make good guesses and build our understanding of the world. If you are not sure about something, you might try to find out. You ask questions and look at facts. You use your brain to think about what you learn. This way, you can be sure that you are not just guessing.

There is also a kind of reasoning that is not about facts. You can use your brain to think about how to act in a situation. If you are afraid of a big thing, you can think about what will happen if you are afraid. You can try to find a way to stay safe. This kind of reasoning helps you feel better when you are scared.
