Bathrooms are rooms inside houses where people go to clean themselves. These rooms have lots of boxes and lines to fill with water and stuff that makes the water feel warm. Some rooms are very big and have lots of boxes. Other rooms are small and only have a few lines.

People go to these rooms to get clean after they use the bathroom. They put their hands in the water. They move their faces. They move their bodies. They shake. Sometimes, they put on new clothes. This helps them feel better.

Some people only go to the bathroom once a day. Some people go many times. They might go in the morning to feel good, and they might go again at night before they go to bed.

In some places, the rooms are very strong. They have hard boxes that keep the water inside. These rooms do not need water to be warm. The water stays hot for a long time.

Bathrooms have many things. They have chairs for you to sit on. They have tables with things you need to use. They have lines to put your hand. They have lines to put a thing that makes your face look nice. Some rooms have a big box with a face in it. People sit in this box for a short time.

Some people go to the bathroom to feel bad. This happens when you eat a lot or when you drink a lot. You might feel heavy and you might feel like you need to go. You might go in a hurry. You might go many times in one night.

Bathrooms are very important. You need to go sometimes. You need to feel clean. You need to feel nice. You need to feel good.
