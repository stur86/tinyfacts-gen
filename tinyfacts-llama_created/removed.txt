To be gone means a thing has left your place or body. Imagine you have a room in your house. You sleep in the bed. You eat from the table. After you sleep and eat, you get up from the bed. You walk out of the room and go to another room. You do not stay there. You leave the room. You are gone.

Sometimes, you can also be gone from a place that is very far away. If you fly in a big thing, you can go very far and then you are gone. The stars you see in the night sky are also gone when it gets very dark.

You can also be gone if you are very sad. If you are sad because you lost a friend or a cat, you might want to forget. You might want to do something to make the feeling go away. This is because your body is still here and your mind is still in your room.

You can also be gone if you are very, very strong. If you throw a heavy thing, it might go far. If you run very fast, you might get tired. Your body gets very heavy and your legs feel very hard. Your arms and hands might feel like they are heavy too. This is because your body is moving a lot.

There is also a word for when a person is gone. If a person dies, their body stops moving. Their heart stops beating. Their breath stops coming. Their eyes close. They are not here anymore. This is a very sad thing to happen.
