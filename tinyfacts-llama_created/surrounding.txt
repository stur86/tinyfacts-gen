To surround something means to put it all around you. You can surround a house with trees and green ground. You can surround a person with friends and family. You can surround a thing with lines of air and water that go around it.

Imagine you are sitting in a room. You can see the wall in front of you, but you can not see anything behind you. You are on the edge of the room. This is like being surrounded.

You can also surround yourself with things that help you feel safe. You might surround yourself with a book or a computer. These things help you relax and forget about the world outside.

Sometimes, something can surround more than one person. If a group of people are all around a town, they are all surrounded by that town. This is like a big group of people who are all around each other.
