A word is a thing you say to make other people understand. It is a way to tell how you feel or what you want. You can use it to talk to a friend, a teacher, or a family person.

When you say a word, you are sending a sign to the other person. They can see or hear the word and then understand what you mean. If you use the word wrong, the other person might not know what you are talking about.

A word can also be a part of a story. You can read a book and hear the words of the people who wrote it. You can watch a movie and hear the voice of the person speaking. These stories are made up of many small words.

Sometimes, a word can mean many different things. If you say you are happy, you mean you feel good. But if you say you are sad, you might mean you are angry. You are using the word to tell how you feel.
