Admitting something means to let it be. It is when you decide to accept what is there.

Imagine you see a big dog. If you are afraid, you might say, I am afraid of the dog. But then you decide to look at it. You are looking at it and you see it is just a dog. You are not afraid anymore. You are admitting that the dog is there.

In school, you might see a person who is not allowed to be there. The teacher says, You are not allowed to be in this room. But then the person says, I am here. They are admitting that they are in the room.

Admitting can be hard. Sometimes you might want to say, No, this is not right. But if you admit, you are showing that you accept the situation. It is a way to show you are ready to do what is needed.

You can also admit when you are wrong. If you do something that is not right and then you say, I was wrong, you are admitting that you were wrong. This shows you are sorry and you are ready to change your way.
