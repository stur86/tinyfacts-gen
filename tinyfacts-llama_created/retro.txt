A word that is fun to say and easy to use.

To go back in time is to move to a time before the one you are in now. It is like getting a second chance. We do this when we go to a place that is far away. We might go to a city that is very old. We might go to a place that has a big park. We are going back in time.

You can also go back in time in your brain. When you are very young, you have not learned many things yet. You might not know how to use a computer. You might not know what a phone is. You go back in time because you want to remember what life is like when you were very small. You want to remember the first day you ever saw a tree.

Sometimes we use this word when we talk about things that are old. We say, a movie is very old. This means the movie is very old and it is not very new anymore. It is like a book that is many years old.

In some places, people say, we have gone back in time. This means we have seen the past. We have seen what happened before we were here. It is like looking at a picture of your family. You are looking at a picture of your great great great great great great great great great great family. You are going back in time so you can see who you are and where you came from.

Sometimes we use this word when we talk about things that are old and broken. We say, this car is old. It is not as fast as the cars that are new. It is like an old book. It has many broken spots and it does not work very well.
