To drive something means to move it from one place to another using your own power.

If you are driving a car, you are doing this job. You sit inside the car, and your hands are on the big round parts that help the car go. You use your feet to press on the round parts. This makes the car move forward. You can also use your hands to turn the car. When you turn, the car turns in the same direction. This helps you stay on the road.

You might drive through a city. You follow the signs that say where to go. You drive past houses and trees. You drive past other cars. If you want to get to a different city, you might drive a long way. You might go over a high road and then cross a wide water. This can be a fun way to see the world.

Sometimes, you drive a big animal. You hold the animal and tell it to move. The animal follows you and goes where you say. This is a way to have a friend that can carry you.

You can also drive a thing that makes food. You put food into a box and close it. Then you press a round part. This makes the food come out. You can drink the food or eat it for later.

To drive is to use your power to move something. It is a way to get from one place to another.
