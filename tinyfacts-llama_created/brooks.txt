A broken thing is a thing that is broken. This can happen to many things. A dog might break a leg if he falls hard. A house might break down if it gets very old.

You can also say a broken person. This means a person who is very sad. They might cry a lot. They might be angry too. They are not happy at all.

Sometimes, we use this word in a different way. A broken thing can also mean a thing that is not working well. If a car breaks down, you can say the car is broken. It is not moving anymore.

Broken things can be very hard to fix. You might need to get a new part. You might need to fix the thing many times before it is good again. This can be very hard.

But if you break something, you can always fix it. You can try to make it work again. You can use your brain and your hands to fix the thing.
