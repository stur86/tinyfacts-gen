Normal means normal or usual. If you are normal, you do not have any big problems. If you are not normal, you have a problem. When you are in a big city, you might be used to the bright lights. You might not like the loud sounds or the long lines. If you go to a small town, you might feel safe and sound. You know the people and the way things work. You feel like you can do anything because you are normal.

Normal things often happen in the world. The sun is normal to be up in the morning. The air is normal to feel when you breathe. The water is normal to drink. When something is normal, it is okay. It does not hurt you or make you feel strange.

Normal people also live in normal houses. They have a room for sleeping. They have a room for eating. They have a room for playing games. They have a room for watching movies. They live in their own way, without fighting or hurting each other. This is what we mean by normal.

But there is also something called a normal thing that is not like the rest. A normal thing is when you are not in trouble. A normal thing is when you are safe. Sometimes, being normal can be boring. Sometimes, you want to do something different. Sometimes, you want to be in a place that is not like your home. That is also normal.
