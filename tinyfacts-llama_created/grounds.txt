To ground something means you make it safe and ready to stay. You do this when you are playing with a ball that might hit a wall. If you hit the wall many times, the ball becomes very heavy and it does not move. You have made the ball safe for you to run with.

You also ground people when you say they are very happy. They feel like they are safe and they do not have to worry. The word comes from the way a child plays with a big thing that makes the floor feel heavy. The child feels safe because the floor is not moving.

In a school, teachers ground students for doing things they should not do. This is like a time out. The teacher tells the student to stop and then they go to a room. The student stays there until they are ready to go back to their class.

You can also ground money. If you spend too much money, you have less money left. It is like you are making the money safe for you to use later.
