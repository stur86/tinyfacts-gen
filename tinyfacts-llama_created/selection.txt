To pick something means to pick a person or thing from many others. It is about finding the best one. You might pick a friend who is nice. You might pick a food you like at a store. You might even pick a thing you need for work.

When you pick something, you are not always sure why. You might pick because it looks nice. Or you might pick because it is the best. You are making your own call.

Sometimes we pick for reasons. We pick for a game. We pick a team. We pick a book to read. We pick a movie to watch. We pick food to eat. We pick clothes. We pick a place to live. This is how we make our own plans.

In a different way, you can pick something. You can pick a word from a book. You can pick a number. You can pick a color. You can pick a thing you do. When you pick, you are making a new thing.

We also say people pick. When someone is a good person, they pick good things. They pick friends. They pick a job they love. They pick a way to live. They pick to help others. They pick to be kind.
