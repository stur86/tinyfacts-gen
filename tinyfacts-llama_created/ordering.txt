To order something means you tell the person or the thing you want to do what to do. This is how you tell the person to bring you food from the kitchen or to give you a drink.

Imagine you want to watch a movie. You say to the person, the movie. They know what you mean and go to the room with the movies. You did not ask them to bring the movies to you. You asked them to show the movie to you. This is an order.

You can also order food. If you want a hot drink and a cold drink, you say, I want a hot drink and a cold drink. The person brings both drinks to you. You did not ask them to pick one drink out of a box. You asked them to bring both.

Sometimes, you order things you do not want. You say, Can you bring me my books? They hear you and go to get your books. This is an order.

To order is also to ask for something. You say, Can you help me? Or you say, Can you give me a phone? They understand what you are asking for.

You can order at many places. You can order food from a store or a kitchen. You can order a drink at a bar or a store. You can even order a job. If you want to work at a place, you say you want to work there. The person hears you and helps you find the job.

In your life, you often order yourself to do something. You order to clean your room so you can sleep well. You order to go to school so you can learn. You order to eat your food so you do not get sick. This is how you follow your own orders.
