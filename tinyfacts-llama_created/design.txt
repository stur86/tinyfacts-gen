To make something new is to use your mind and your hands. You start with a plan. You think about what you want. You look at pictures of what you want. You also look at what is already there. You use your hands to make it. You might use wood, or make paper, or build with blocks. You try to make it right.

When you make something, you are doing something new. You are not making the same thing. You are adding your own ideas. You are making it for people who do not know what it is. You are helping them know the thing.

In the world, people use this word to talk about things they make. They talk about how a car looks, how a house looks, how a person looks. They talk about how much money they spent. They talk about how good it is for them. They talk about how it makes them happy.

Sometimes, people make things to make money. They build a house for a family. They make a car for a person to drive to work. They make clothes for a person to wear. This is how they change the world. They help people live their lives better.

You can also make something for fun. You might build a thing to play with. You might make a game to play at school. You might make a thing to fly. You build it to fly. You do this because it makes you happy.

A person who does this work is called a maker. They work with their hands and their mind. They try to make something that people will like. They do not follow someone else. They are the one who decides. They are the one who makes it happen.
