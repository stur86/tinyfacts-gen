Let is a word that helps us talk about numbers and other things.

When you say, let me tell you, it means you are going to tell the person what you want. You are not going to ask them to do something right away. You are going to wait until you want to talk. It is like holding a cup of water and then you want to drink it. You do not ask the person to give it to you. You just say, let me have it, because you are ready.

You can also use this word to talk about time. If you are waiting for something to happen, you might say, let it happen. This means you trust that the thing will come. It is a way to say, I am not in a hurry, I just wait.

Sometimes, you might say, let something be. This means you want something to happen right now. You might want to eat a food you like or you might want to watch a game. You are using the word to say, I want this right now.

You can also let something go. This means you are letting go of a problem. If you have a bad feeling, you might let it go. You are not keeping it inside anymore. You are letting it go and moving on.

One more way to use this word is when you talk about how things feel. If you are feeling happy, you can say, let the good times be. This means you are letting the good times go into your heart and mind.

So, let is a word that helps us talk about waiting, trusting, letting go, and how things feel. It is very important in our lives.
