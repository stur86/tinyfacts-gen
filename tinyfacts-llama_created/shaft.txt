A long piece of hard stuff that goes through something can be a big word. It comes from how things are built.

Imagine a big animal. The hard stuff that runs from its head to its legs is like the long part that goes through your body. When you pull on the legs, the hard stuff goes through, and you move. It is like the long piece that goes through the animal.

This word is also used for things that go through the ground. It is part of how the ground works. When you pull on a rock or a piece of wood, the long piece goes through the ground. It helps things fall down or things to stay up.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about very strong hard stuff. If you cut a very hard piece of wood with a long piece, you are using the word. You are taking something very hard and making it smaller so you can use it.
