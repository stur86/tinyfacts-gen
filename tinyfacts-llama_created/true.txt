When we say something is true, we mean it really happened. It is not a lie. If a person tells you something is true, you believe them. You trust them.

Think about a story. If you tell your friend you saw a cat when you were playing, if the story is true, you felt happy. You believed what happened. You did not try to change the story to make it look better.

We also use this word in the sun. When the sun is up, it is true. The light is coming from the sky and reaching us. The sun makes our day warm. If the sun were not there, we would not be able to go outside.

Sometimes, we say something is not true. If a person lies, they are not true. They did not tell the truth. If you do something wrong and you know it, you must tell the truth, even if it is hard.

The word true is about how we see things. It is about the facts. It is about what really happens.
