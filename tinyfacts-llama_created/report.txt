A story is when many people write what happened. You can hear a story from a friend, see a story on a computer, or read a story written by another person.

A story usually has a beginning, middle, and end. The beginning tells you who the people are and what they are doing. The middle shows what happens when the people act. The end tells you what happened and why.

A story can be about a person, a place, a thing, or many things. It can also have a time and a place in it. People use stories to teachers, to tell jokes, and to show how people feel.

Sometimes you hear a story on the air or see it on a computer. This way, many people can hear or watch the story at the same time. People also use stories to remember things that happened before.

We all need stories to understand the world. Stories help us learn from the past and make sense of what is happening now.
