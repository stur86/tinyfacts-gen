To be on the other side of something is to be in a different place. Imagine you are standing on the ground near the water. The water is on one side of you, and the land is on the other side. You are in the middle, but you are on one side. If you cross the water, you move to the other side.

The word can also mean a place where the water ends. Look at the land around the water. There is a high place on the top of the land and a low place on the bottom. In the middle is the middle part, where the land is the same. This is where the water ends and the land starts.

Sometimes people use this word to talk about how they feel. If you are feeling happy, you might say you are on the good side of things. If you are feeling sad, you might say you are on the bad side. This means you are in a place where the good times are over.

You can also use it to talk about how people act. If someone is being kind, they are on the good side. If someone is being mean, they are on the bad side. If someone is in a fight, they are on the bad side.
