To have something is to be there. It means you are in a place or a thing. You can be inside a computer, inside a car, or inside a book. This is because you are working with the thing to make it right.

When you have something, you check it. You look for the wrong things. You try to fix them. If the thing is not working, you try to fix it. This makes the thing work again. You are fixing the problem.

If you are working with a computer, you might be a computer person. You use the computer to fix problems. You also use the computer to build new things. You make sure everything is okay before you let people see them.

If you are a person, you might be a computer person too. You might work with computers to fix problems. You work to make sure people are safe. You also help people to know how to use computers.

To have something can also mean to show you are doing something right. If you are walking, you are having something. You are moving. You are not falling down. You are standing on the ground.

You can also say someone has a thing. This means they have a problem. They are not working well. They need help. They need to find a way to fix the problem. If you see this, you might be worried for them.
