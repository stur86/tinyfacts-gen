A door that leads to another world. It is a place where you can go from one place to another. You can step through it and be on the other side.

Imagine you are in a room. You want to go to a room with a lot of books. But the door in your room is locked. You have to find a way to open it. A door like this is a way to go to another room.

There are many kinds of these doors. Some are in the ground. Some are in the sky. Some are in the water. Some are inside a tall thing that goes up into the air. Some are made of a very strong stuff that can not break.

People use these doors to go to other worlds. Some go to worlds with a lot of green trees and cold water. Some go to worlds with a lot of fire and rock. Some go to worlds with a lot of bright light and strange things that fly through the air.

When you go through a door, you are leaving one world behind and entering another. It can be very strange. You might meet new people. You might see things that are not there. It can be a big change.

Sometimes, people go through doors to find something they lost. They might have forgotten their name or their clothes. They go through the door and find a new world, and they remember who they are.
