The word you asked about is a word that means you are very clear about what you want. It is used when you are giving a person or a thing a job to do. This kind of job is very important because it is clear what the person must do.

Sometimes, a person says they are going to do something, but they are not sure what they are going to do. This can be very confusing. If you want to know what they plan to do, you can ask them to be more clear. You can say,  You are going to buy a new book today. This tells everyone that you know what will happen.

You can also use this word when you are making a plan. If you want to build a house, you can say you are going to build it. The word helps you keep your plan straight.

There is another kind of word. It means you are very sure about a thing. If you are sure, you do not worry about what might happen. You are sure that the thing will happen.

Think about a time when you were afraid to go to a big room. You were nervous. If you had said,  I am going to go to the room, you would not have felt so afraid. The word helped you plan your steps so you could go without being afraid.
