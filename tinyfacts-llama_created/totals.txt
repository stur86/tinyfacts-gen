To totally means to do something all at once. It is like when you have a big box full of things. If you add a lot of things together, you have a totally number of things.

Imagine you have ten boxes of food. Each box has five pieces of food in it. If you add all the food from the ten boxes together, you have a totally number of pieces of food. You did not do one thing and then another. You did all of them.

Sometimes, you want to know the whole story. If you know who did the bad thing and who did it, and who saw what happened, you can say you know the totally. It means you are telling the whole story.
