To buy something is to pay for it. If you are looking for a book, you can go to a store. You look at the book. You see that you like it. Then you go to the store and you say, I want this book. The store person takes the book and gives it to you. You are now the owner of the book.

If you are a person, you can also buy something. You work hard for many days. At the end of the days, you get money. You use the money to buy food, clothes, and other things you need. You have bought something.

Sometimes, you can buy something with money from a computer. You type a word into a box. The computer shows you the word. You see the word and you know it is what you want.

To buy something is to get it from someone else. You do not make it yourself. You get it from a store, a person, or a computer. After you buy something, you can have it in your hand. You can hold it and feel it in your hands.

If you are a person who loves to buy things, you might buy many things in a short time. You might buy clothes, shoes, and other things you want. You might buy food to eat for many days. Buying is a way to get what you want without making it yourself.
