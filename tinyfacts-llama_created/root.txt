When you look at a tree, you see big parts up in the sky. These big parts are not like the parts you eat. They do not touch the ground. Instead, they go deep into the ground. The parts you eat grow from the ground, but the big parts go down into the ground very deep.

Think of a tree like a person. The person is the top part. The top part has the head, the arms, and the hands. The person is the top. The body is the middle part. The middle part has the legs. The legs are the parts that go down to the ground.

The ground is the big parts under the tree. The ground is the deep parts. It is the long way down. The ground is where the tree gets the water and food it needs to stay growing. The ground is also where the tree keeps its own power. If the ground gets too hot, the tree might get hurt. If the ground gets too cold, the tree might not grow at all.

Sometimes, a tree grows a little round at the top. This is because the top part gets too much power. The top part wants to stay up high. The bottom part wants to stay down deep. This is why the top part turns into a round thing. This is the part that stays on the ground.
