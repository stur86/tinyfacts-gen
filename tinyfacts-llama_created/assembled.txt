To put something together is to bring it all at once so it will stand or work well. It is like when you build a house. You start with the floor. Then you put the walls on top. You put the windows and the doors next. You add the floors on top of the walls. You do this for each room in the house. This takes a lot of time.

You can also put things together in a different way. If you want to make a new thing, you start with the pieces and put them all at once. Then you use your hands to join them. This makes it stronger and more good.

Imagine you have a box. Inside the box, there are many small things. If you take all these things one at a time, it is hard to put them away. But if you put them all at once, it is much easier. The box is now full and ready to keep things inside.
