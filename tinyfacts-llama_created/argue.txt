To fight is to use your body and your mind against another person. When you fight, you try to make your point. You use words and your body to tell the other person why you are right.

Sometimes people fight because they have different ideas. Maybe one person thinks a thing should happen, and another person thinks it should not happen. They get angry because they do not agree. They might shout, push, or hit each other.

You can also fight when you play a game with another person. You might run around a big area and try to catch the other person. You use your legs and your arms to try to stop them. You try to stay in the same place as long as you can. If you are the one who is first to stop, you are the one who won't fight anymore.

If you are in a room with people you know, you might get angry. You might say something you do not mean. You might use your voice to shout at someone, or you might get very quiet and act like you are not there. This is a way to fight when you are not fighting with a body.

People also fight when they feel something is not right. If you are afraid of a thing, you might feel very angry. You might want to hit it or pull on it. This kind of fight is inside your head. You are fighting with your mind.

To fight is to try to get something you want. If you want a game, you fight. If you want to eat a food you like, you fight. If you want to see a movie you love, you fight. You fight until you are the one who gets the thing you want.
