An thing that happens right now is called an instead. It is not like a thing that takes a long time to be ready. A drink that you want is an instead of a drink that is already there. You did not order it. You did not wait for it. You just take it.

When you are very fast, an instead is the same as being right there. You do not need to wait for anything to happen. You are already doing it.

A phone call is an instead. You pick it up and talk with the person. You do not wait for the other person to answer. You are right there with them. This is why people say you are in an instead of someone.

An instead can also mean that something happened so fast that you did not even see it. You were doing other things and then you saw that something was happening. This is a very fast instead.

Sometimes, an instead is used to talk about a thing that is not real. A thing that is not real can be an instead of a real thing. It is like a picture that you see that is not really there. You are looking at it, but it is not really there.

In some places, an instead is not a bad word. When a person is very happy, they might say, I am in an instead of a big party. This means they are feeling so happy that they want to be right there with everyone.
