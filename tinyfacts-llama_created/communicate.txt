To talk to someone is to share your mind with them. You use words to say things. You use your voice and your body. You use things that let people hear you, like a phone. You talk about what you want to say. You want to tell them something or ask them to help you.

Sometimes, you do not know how to say something. You feel a little scared. You try to find the right words. You look at the person you want to talk to. You look at their face. You see how they look. You see if they are smiling or if they look sad. This helps you know what you should say.

When you talk to someone, you give them your mind. You share your ideas, your hopes, and your fears. You help them understand what you are thinking. You show them that you care about what they think.

You can talk to a friend. You talk about your day. You talk about what happened. You talk about things that make you happy. You talk about things that make you sad. You talk about your family and your friends. You talk about your school or your work. You talk about your life.

You can also talk to someone you do not know. You talk to a teacher. You talk to a doctor. You talk to a police person. You talk to a person who helps you find your way. You talk to a person who gives you money. You talk to a person who helps you when you are afraid.

When you talk, you are not alone. Everyone has a brain and a body. Everyone wants to be heard. Everyone wants to be understood. When you talk, you help each other.
