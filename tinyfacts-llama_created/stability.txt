Staying on a line means you are not moving away from it. You are in the right place and you do not go over or under it.

A tree is a big piece of wood. It is standing in one place. It does not move. It does not go up. It does not go down. It stays in one spot. This is why it is called a tree. It is strong and it stays where it is.

An animal that can live for many years is an animal that stays in one place. It does not go far. It does not move. This is why it is called an animal. It stays with its family and its friends.

You can also talk about a person. A person who is calm and quiet is someone who stays in one place. They do not go around. They do not go to a different city. They stay where they are. This is why they are called a person.

When things are in a good place, they are in a good line. They are not falling over or going under. They are staying where they are. This is what we mean by staying on a line.
