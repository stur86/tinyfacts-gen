To wait is to stay in a place for a long time because another thing is happening. You might wait before you eat a food that will make you full. You can also wait for a moment when you need to do something but you are not sure when it will happen.

Sometimes you wait for a person who is not there. You might be waiting for a phone call or a meeting. You might feel nervous or worried because you do not know what will happen next.

You can also wait for a time to pass. If you have a book that is about a long story, you might wait for the end of the story. You stay in the same room or in your chair until the end.

Waiting is often hard. It makes you feel like the time is very slow. You might look at the wall or look at your phone. You might think about what you want to do next. You might even feel sad if you are waiting for a long time and you do not know what will happen.

But waiting is also important. It helps you learn. When you wait for a time to pass, you learn about what happens when you are not busy. You learn about how people act and how things change. You learn to be calm and to wait for what is next.

Waiting can be a hard thing to do. Your body might feel heavy. Your mind might feel quiet. You might feel tired. But it is also a time to rest and to think about what is happening. It is a time to listen to your body and to wait for what is next.
