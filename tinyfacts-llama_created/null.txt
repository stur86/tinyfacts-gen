A number is something you use to tell how many things are there. Numbers go from one to many numbers. A number that is one is a small number. A number that is two is a bigger number than one. Numbers are like a line in your mind. You can write them down or you can just remember them.

There are many different kinds of numbers. You can use numbers to tell how many people are in a room, how many books you have in a book, or how many times you have to do something. You also use numbers to show how much money you have. When you pay for something, you pay with money, and the money you give is one of those numbers.

A number without a number is not a number at all. If you try to give a number to a person, they will say it is not a number. A number without a number is like a word without a word. It does not mean anything. A number with no number is like a number that tells you to do something, but you do not know what to do.

Sometimes numbers are used to show how things are different. If you have a cold, your number is one. If you do not have a cold, your number is two. If you are happy, your number is three. Numbers help us tell how many things are happening or how many things are not happening.
