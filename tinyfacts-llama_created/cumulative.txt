To go around something in a group of things can be a big job. Imagine you have a group of friends who want to go to a movie. They all walk in different directions. One person goes around a corner. Another person goes around a street. Another person goes around a big park.

If you go around all of these streets in one group, you are doing a round. But you are not going the same way. You are going in different directions. This can be very hard because you might run into each other.

To go around a thing in this way can also happen when you are building something. You start at one end. You walk along the wall and then you walk around the corner. Then you walk along the other side of the wall. You keep going around the thing until you reach the other side.

Sometimes people use this word when talking about money. If you have a lot of money, you can go around it from one place to another. This means you are not taking it all at once. You are breaking it down so you can use it for different things later.

In this story, the word is important because it tells us how many steps there are. There are two steps around the corner. There are two steps around the street. There are two steps around the park. In the end, there are ten steps in totally.
