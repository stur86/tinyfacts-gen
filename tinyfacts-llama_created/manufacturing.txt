To make something new is to take the old and turn it into something new. This happens when you use things you already have and put them together to make a new thing.

Imagine you have many tiny bits of wood. You take one bit and use it to make a chair. You take another bit and use it to make a table. Each time you use one bit, it changes what the chair or table is. The chair is not the same as the table, but both are made from the same wood.

You can also make things from the air. You have a thing that makes hot air, and you can put hot air into a box. Inside the box, tiny pieces of air go around and round. They change the air in a way that makes it warm. After a while, the air inside the box is the same as the air in your house. You have made a warm air that you can use for a fire.

Sometimes, you make things that you do not need. You might make a box to hold food, but you do not need to eat it. You keep the box because you like the way it looks or because you think it will be good for the people you will give it to.

People also make things that help them. You can make a thing that makes your hands warm so you do not get cold in a cold room. You can make a thing that makes your eyes see better. You can make a thing that helps your body grow strong. These things do not change the world, but they help you live better.

There are many kinds of this. You can make things that are for your body. You can make things that help your mind. You can make things that make people happy. You can make things that help people feel safe. You can make things that help people work or play together.
