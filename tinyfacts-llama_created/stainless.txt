Standing means to stand still. You can stand in a place on the ground, on a chair, or on a table. If you are standing in a room and someone asks you to move, you must get up and walk to the next room.

Standing can also mean to have very little to lose. If you are very sure that something is going to happen, you feel ready. You do not worry because you know what will happen.

Sometimes, people use the word to mean a place that is very safe. If you stand in front of a tall wall, you feel safe because the wall is very high and no one can climb over. You do not have to worry about falling down.

In the world of work, people often stand for long hours. They stand at a desk and work on a computer. They do not move much until the work is finished. This is a hard way to work because your body feels tired.
