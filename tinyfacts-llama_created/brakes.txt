To slow something is to make it go very fast but very slow. This happens when you press a small round thing on the floor.

When you are driving a car, you press the round thing when you want to stop. If you press it very hard, you will stop very fast. If you press it too little, you will not stop at all.

There is also a part on the floor of a car that is red. When you press this red part, you make the car go very slow. This is why you can stop very quickly on a long road.

There are also things that you can put on the floor of a car. You put these things on the floor so that they do not get on your feet. They are like a soft cover that helps you stop very quickly. You can put many of these things on the floor of a car if you need to stop many times.
