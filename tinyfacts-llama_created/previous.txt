To go before something is to be before it. Think of a line of people walking. The person who is walking first is the one who comes before the rest. They are the ones who start the line.

Imagine you are looking at a picture. You look at the person in the middle first. The person in front of them is the one who comes before. The person behind them is after them.

Something that comes before a day is a word we use. We use this word when we talk about the day before. We might say, the day before tomorrow is this day.

A person who has done something is a person who has been before they do it. If you have never done something, you are the one who is new. You are the one who comes before the person who has done it many times.

We also use this word to talk about time. A day is one of many days. Before a day starts, there are other days. Before a time, there are times before it.
