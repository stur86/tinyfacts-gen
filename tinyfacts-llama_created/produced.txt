To put something into a box is to make it small enough to hold. When you put a ball in a box, it is already small. You can not put a big book into a box because the box is too big. The ball is already small, and the box is too small to hold it.

When you make something, you put it into the world. You can make a house, a car, a tree, or a dog. You start with the tiny parts and then you put them together. A tree starts with a tiny piece that grows. A car starts with many pieces that are all together.

You can also make a feeling. If you are afraid, you feel a bad feeling. You can put a bad feeling into your heart and it stays there. You can try to get ridden it by breathing deep or by doing something to calm you down. The feeling is already there, but you can change it with your body and your mind.

A story is like a box that holds many pieces of time. You put a few words together and you get a picture of a story. You can put many stories together to make a book. You can put stories into your mind so that you remember them later.

When you make something, you put pieces of the world together. You take the pieces of a tree and make a chair. You take the pieces of a car and make a car. You put all the pieces together and you get a thing that you use every day.
