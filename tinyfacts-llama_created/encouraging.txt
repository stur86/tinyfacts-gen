To be or do something is to make a thing happen. When you say you are happy, you are being. A friend is someone who makes you feel better when you are sad. A mother is a woman who gives you the food you need to live. When you do something good, like help a friend, you are showing you care.

To be a good person means you try to be kind to everyone. You might say sorry when you do something wrong. You act like you are sorry and you try to do better next time. You also try to help others when they need help. This makes everyone feel safe and happy.

When you are a good person, people listen to you. They want to hear what you have to say because you are a friend. You show that you care about what others think.

Being a good person is important because it helps us feel good. When we see someone being kind, we feel happy. It makes the world a better place.
