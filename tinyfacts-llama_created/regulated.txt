To be controlled is to have a person or a thing in a place where they are not free. This is often done so that people do not do things that are not right.

Think of a place where animals live. If you are not allowed to enter, you can not touch the animals. You are not allowed to hurt them. This is like being under a person who says you must follow these things.

A thing that is made to follow these things is called a thing of control. It is like a person who says, you can do this and you can do that. It helps keep everything safe.

If a person is in a job where they can not leave, they are under control. They must do what the person says. This is done so that everyone is safe.
