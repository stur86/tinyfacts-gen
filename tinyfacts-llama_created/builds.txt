To build something is to make it from the ground up. You start with nothing and use your hands, your mind, and your body to turn a piece of wood, a rock, or a wall into a house, a car, a chair, or a table.

You begin by thinking about what you want. You might want to live in a house with a big room, a kitchen, and a bedroom. You might want to have a table to eat at. You start by making a plan. Then, you start to make things happen.

One person might be the builder. They use power and strong parts to move heavy things. They use long pieces of wood to build the wall. Another person might be the one who makes the floor. They lay down many small pieces of wood to make the floor strong.

There are also people who build things for work. They build roads that help people get from one place to another. They build schools so children can learn. They build big houses that have many rooms and lots of people living there.

Sometimes, people build things that are very big. They build tall houses or cars. They build big rooms in tall places where many people live together. They build things that fly through the air so people can go far away quickly.

To build is to change the world. It is to take small pieces of wood or rock and turn them into a life. It is to make the world a better place.
