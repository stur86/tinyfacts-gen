To be next to something means you are close to it. You might sit next to your friend, or you might stand next to a wall. If you are next to a big building, you are also next to a street.

Imagine you are in a city. There are many streets. If you walk down one street, the next street is next to it. They are next to each other. This makes it easier to get from one street to the other.

A person can also be next to someone. If a child sits next to their parents at a table, they are next to them. This is because the parents are sitting right next to the child.

In a story, a thing can be next to another thing. If a dog runs next to a cat, they are next to each other. This shows they are near to each other.

Sometimes, being next to something can be a problem. If you are next to a fire, you are at a higher place than the fire. This is because you are behind the wall of the fire. You are safe, but you are also far away from the fire.
