A number or a word shows how well someone or something is doing. If you watch a movie, the number shows how many people watched it. If a song is very good, you might give it a high number.

Sometimes, people give numbers to things that are not real. They say, a car is very good to drive, or a food is very good to eat. This is not true, but the people gave the numbers anyway.

We also use numbers to show how many people like a person or a thing. If many people like a game, the number is high. If only a few people like it, the number is low. This helps us decide if a game is good or not.

Sometimes, people give numbers to things that are not things at all. If a movie is very good, a writer might give it a high number for its writing. This is like giving a number to a person for their work.
