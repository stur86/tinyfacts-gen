To help someone means you give them a thing they need. You can do this in many ways. You might give them money so they can buy food. Or you might move things from one place to another so they don't have to walk.

Sometimes you help a friend who is sad. You listen to them and tell them everything is okay. You also tell them they are not alone and that there is a person who cares.

You can also help a child who is afraid. You might sit with them until they feel better. You might tell them things are going to be okay. This helps them feel safe.

Helping people is important. It makes the world a kinder place. When you help someone, you are showing you care about them.
