Afraid is a word that means you are scared. It is about how you feel when you are in a place that is not safe.

Imagine you are in a room with a dog that you do not like. You are afraid. You want to run away. You are very worried. You are afraid to go near the dog.

Sometimes you feel afraid in your home. You might be afraid of the dark or afraid of a loud noise. You might feel afraid to go to bed because you are afraid of the night.

Afraid is also about being afraid of a person. If you see a person who is mean, you might feel afraid. You might want to hide or run away.

In a place far away, there is a very old and very tall tree. Many people go there to sit and read a book. But one day, the tree starts to fall. The people are afraid. They want to leave the tree before it falls on them.

Afraid is about how you feel. It is a word we use to show that we are scared or worried.
