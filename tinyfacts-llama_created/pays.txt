To pay something means you give money to someone who needs it. It happens when you do a job and you give the money to the person who wants to buy it.

Imagine you are a street cleaner. You clean the street for many hours. You walk many blocks. You pick up a lot of small pieces of paper and big pieces of wood. You put the small pieces of paper in a big bag and the big pieces of wood in another bag.

After you do your work, you walk to the store and give the bags to the person who owns the store. The person says, thank you. You did not get paid yet, but now you have money in your pocket.

If you buy a food from a store, you pay the store money. You give them the money, and they give you the food. You are paying for what you want.

You can also pay for a car. If you want to drive a car, you must pay the driver. You give money to the driver, and then they drive you where you want to go.

In a school, you pay to learn. You pay money to the school so you can go to the school and learn how to write and how to read.

When you pay, you give the money to the person who needs it. This is how the world works.
