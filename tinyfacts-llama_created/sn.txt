To snap is to use your hand. This means to make a hard sound. It happens when you close your hand around something and then suddenly open it. This is often done quickly.

You can snap your fingers to show you are very happy. Or you might snap your hand to stop a hard thing from moving. This is important when you are trying to keep something safe.

Sometimes, to snap at something means to cut it. If you are angry, you might use your hands to snap at a person. You can also snap at a piece of paper if you think it is not good enough.

People also use the word to talk about being very close. If two people are very close, they might snap at each other. This is a way of showing how much they love and trust each other.
