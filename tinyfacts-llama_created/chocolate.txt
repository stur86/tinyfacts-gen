To make something sweet is to make it soft and easy to eat. You take some brown stuff from a tree, or some red stuff from a red thing, or some white soft stuff from a big animal. You put the brown stuff with the red stuff together in a big round thing made of hard stuff. When you do this, the brown stuff and the red stuff become a single thing that is very sweet. We call this sweet thing a drink.

Sometimes we use this drink to make our bodies strong. We drink it before we go to school or work. It gives us the power to think and to move our bodies. It is like a drink of life.

We also make sweet things that are easy to hold in our hands. We use the brown stuff, the red stuff, and sometimes other brown and red things. We fill the round thing made of hard stuff with these sweet things. Then we close the round thing and let it sit for a while. After this, the sweet things are all inside the round thing. We can take a small piece out and eat it. This is a sweet thing that you can eat on the go.

Some people like to eat these drinks. They say they make their mouths happy and their bodies feel good. They say they help them feel happy and full. But some people do not like them. They say they make their teeth hurt or make their face feel funny.
