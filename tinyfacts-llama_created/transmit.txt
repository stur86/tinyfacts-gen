To send something is to move it from one place to another. If you want to tell a friend a story, you might write it on a piece of paper. You would put the paper in a box and give it to the person. They can then read the story by opening the box.

We use this word in many different ways. You might say, she is sending money to her family. This means she is moving money from her home to her family home. Another time, you might say she is sending a picture. This means she is sending a picture to someone else.

Sometimes we use this word for things that go very fast. When you send a word quickly over the phone, you are sending it through the air. This makes it go very fast and you can hear it almost right away.

We also use it to talk about how we feel. If you are happy, you might say, I am feeling good. This means you are not sad or angry. You are just feeling well because of the things that are happening.
