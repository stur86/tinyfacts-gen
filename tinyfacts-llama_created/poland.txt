Police are the people who keep our towns and cities safe. They go out at night and during the day to look for people who have done wrong things. They also help people who are hurt and want to be fixed.

The police are usually led by a man who is very good at keeping people safe. They have to be very strong and very good at thinking. They must also know how to use a gun if things get very bad.

In some places, the police also help with food. If there is a big fight at a school or a store, they might help bring people to a safe place where they can stay until they are all safe again.

The police also work with the other people in the town. They talk to the people who run the stores and the schools, and they make sure everyone is following the right ways. This helps keep everyone safe and makes sure everyone is happy.
