To tell someone something is to say it. When you want to tell your friend that you are angry, you can shout or use your voice. You can also use your hands to make a face. This is a way to show you are very mad.

You can also tell stories. If you tell a story about what happened in your day, people listen. They might ask you questions. They want to know what you did and why.

Sometimes you tell a friend a joke. When you laugh at a joke, you are telling it. You are sharing the funny words with them so they can feel the same way.

You can also tell a story about a place. If you tell a friend where you went to stay, you are telling them the place. This helps them know where to go next time they want to go there.

When you tell a story, you show people how things happened. This helps us learn. It is a way to share what is happening in your life with others.
