To pass something means you have done it. If you want to go to school, you must pass the books. You have read them and you are ready to go. If you do not pass, you must try again next time.

When you are on a bus, you can pass other people. This means you are moving past them. If you are sitting in a car, you are also passing other cars.

You can also pass a game. If you are playing with your friends, you have to play the game. If you do not play, you have to wait for the next time.

Sometimes, you must pass something so it can go to another place. You might have a bag of food. If you do not pass it, it stays with you. Later, someone else might use it.

You can also pass a story. When you tell a story, you are passing it from person to person. Each person adds to the story, so it becomes a bigger story.
