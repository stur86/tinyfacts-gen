An easy word to understand.

When you do a job, you usually know what you have to do. But sometimes, you need to know how you did. You might see if the work is good. You do this by looking at how your work looks. You look at the things you did. You also talk to people who see the job. They tell you if the job was done well.

You also do these things for people. You might say, if a job is done well, you are happy. You might say, if a job is not done well, you are sad. This is what you do with the word.

Sometimes, people say, after you do a job, you get an idea. This is called an idea. An idea is like a feeling. It is how you think about what happened. If you do something well, you might have an idea that is good. If you do something bad, you might have an idea that is not good.

In a place where people work, there is a person who does this. This person is the one who looks at the work and the ideas of the people. They tell everyone who did a job how good it was. They help people see if they did a good job. They are like the ones who say, if you do something well, you are happy.
