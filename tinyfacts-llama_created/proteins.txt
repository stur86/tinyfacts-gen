Problems

Problems are things that you want to fix. You might see a picture that does not show the way. You might see a word that is not right. Your brain is very good at finding answers to these kinds of problems.

When you grow and you need to build your body, you need many tiny things inside you. These things are very strong and help your body work. They are called things that help your body.

There are many kinds of these things. Some are very small and can only be seen with a tiny thing. Some are very big and can only be seen with a computer. Some are made from many small things that are very good at doing one thing.

Your body uses them to make many of the things you need to live. Your hair is made of tiny things from the body. Your skin is made of tiny things that keep you warm. Your eyes are made of tiny things that help you see.

Sometimes you might have a problem with a thing that you eat. Your body needs the right kind of food to stay strong. If you eat food that has too many bad things, your body gets sick. Your body tries to get ridden of these bad things, and it uses the tiny things to fight them.

People also use these things to make things. If you make clothes, you need many tiny things that help them stay on your body. If you make food, you need tiny things that help your food grow. These tiny things are very good at doing one thing.

Problems are important to learn about. They help us understand how things work and how to fix them. They also help us make new things that help us live better lives.
