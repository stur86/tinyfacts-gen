To book something is to put it in a listen so that it is ready for later. If you want to go to a big city, you might book a room. You do this because you are not sure when the room will be ready.

Sometimes you book something because you need it for a short time. You might book a car if you need to go to a place a day later. You can also book a job. You book the job so that you are the one who does it.

To book a place is also an important word. If you want to sit near someone, you might book a seat. You do this so that the person you want to sit next to does not sit there first.

You can also book a time for a thing. You might book a movie to see later. You do this so that you do not forget.

A book is a thing with many words inside. It is like a very long story that you can read. Some books are very big and heavy, while others are small and light. Most people read books because they are a way to learn new things or to escape from their day.
