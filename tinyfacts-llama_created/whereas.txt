To do this means to do something at the same time as the other thing. You can do this with people, with things, or with ideas.

When you do this, you are showing that you care. You are saying, this is important. You are doing it for the person you love, the thing you love, or the idea you love.

You can do this with words. You can say, I am sad because I am afraid. This means you are sad because you are afraid.

You can also do this with things. You can do a new thing. You can do a new game. You can do a new book. This means you are trying something new. You are being open and taking a chance.

Sometimes you do this with people. You can do this with a friend. You can say, I am sorry. This means you are sorry for what happened. You are trying to fix the problem.

This word is very important. It helps us say how we feel and how we act. It helps us stay calm and keep our words.
