A fire wall is a thing that keeps bad things out of a computer or a phone. It is like a big door that stops people or things that are not good from getting inside.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. If someone starts a fire, it can make the air very hot and make the room very dark. You need a way to stop the fire before it gets too big. A fire wall is like that door.

It is usually made of strong stuff that can not let people or bad things through. It keeps the bad stuff from getting to the computer or phone. This helps keep you safe from things that could hurt you.

Sometimes, a fire wall is also used in places where people talk to each other. It keeps the bad words out so everyone can talk without getting angry.

It is like a big, strong hand that keeps the bad things out.
