An order is when you buy something from a store. You tell the store who you want to buy from and what you want. The store sends a paper to your home with the totally of the thing you bought. You pay the money to the store when you get the papers.

After you buy something, the store gives you a paper. This paper is like a notice. It says you have to pay for the thing. This paper is important because it makes sure you pay the right money.

Sometimes a store has a paper for something you already bought. This paper can help your money stay safe. It shows the store you already paid and does not let them take more money.

You might have a paper from a store if you buy food, clothes, or a book. You can also have a paper from a doctor if you have to go to their office to get a check up. This paper helps keep you safe and shows you did the right thing.
