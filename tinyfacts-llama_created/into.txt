To go into something means you are moving into a new place. It is a way to step into a room, a house, a car, or a box.

Imagine you are in a room. You are standing in front of the door. You want to go to another room. You open the door and step inside. You are now in the new room. You have entered the new place.

You can enter a room when you are very small. A baby goes into a room with its family. You enter a room when you enter a school. You are entering a new school day.

You can also enter a place when you are trying to find something. You might be looking for a book. You look in a book, and then you see the word you are looking for. You enter that word into your brain, and now you know it. You have found the thing you were looking for.

To enter something with your body is also a way to get into a place. You might enter a room with a heavy box. You take the box and go into the room. You close the door behind you, and the box is in the room with you.
