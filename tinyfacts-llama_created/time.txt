Time is a way to understand what happens in the world. It is a feeling that tells us how long is passing.

Imagine you have a long walk. You start when you are very small and you walk a little way, then you walk a little more, and another little more, and so on. The time goes by very quickly. One day is like a hundred walks. One week is like one hundred days. One year is like ten hundred weeks.

Time can also tell us about what is happening now. If you look at a watch, it says how many minutes or hours are left. This helps you decide what to do next. If you know the time, you can plan your day and make sure you do not forget anything important.

Time is also about what has already happened. We remember past things when we look at old pictures. We remember how we felt when we were very small. Time helps us understand how things change from one day to another.
