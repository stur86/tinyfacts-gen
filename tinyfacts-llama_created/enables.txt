To allow something is to give it power so it can work. If you are a driver, you do not need to tell your car to do what you want. The car can move on its own because you have given it the power it needs.

Imagine a child who wants to fly a small flying thing. If you give them a piece of paper, they can fly without you. They need the power to move the paper through the air. If you only say, they can fly, they do not have the power. They can only look up and see what is around them.

People also use the word to talk about how a place works. If a city is a great place to live, it means many people like to live there. This city has good roads, good schools, and a safe place. The city allows many people to move in and stay. It gives them a good life.

You can also use the word when you let something happen because it is allowed. If a person is not doing something they should do, the police might say they let them do it. This is because the person did not follow the right orders.

Sometimes, to allow something means to let it be. If you are playing a game with a friend, you might not want to play. But if you let them play, they can do what they want. You are not in the game anymore, but you do not stop them from playing.
