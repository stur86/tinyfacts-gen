When you say something is done in a way that is right, it means you did it the way it should be done. This is very important.

Think about a game. If you throw a ball into the air and you catch it, you did it right. You followed the game and you did not break the game.

Sometimes, you might do something wrong. You might hit the ball and you go to the other side of the field. You could also do it because you are afraid. You are afraid to take the ball and you hit it anyway.

If you do something wrong, you are in trouble. You might get a time out. If you stay in trouble for too long, you have to leave the game. In this case, the person who is in trouble is not the person who did the thing wrong.

But if you do something right, you feel good. You feel like you are a good person. You feel like you followed the right way. This is very important.

So, to say something is done in a way that is right is to say it was done the way it should be done. It tells you if you did the right thing.
