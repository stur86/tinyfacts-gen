A thing that joins other things is called a friend. It is the part that holds you to another person. If you see them, you feel happy. If you do something for them, they are glad. They help you. They can also help you when you are in trouble. A friend is a person you know. They are people you like.

You can also have a friend in your mind. This is a person you see in your brain. You feel good when you think of them. They are the things that make you feel happy. You can have many friends. You can have friends who are far away. You can have friends who live in your city. You can even have friends who are animals. Some animals have friends they like.
