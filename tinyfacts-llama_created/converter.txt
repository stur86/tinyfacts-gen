A thing that changes is called a changer. It can change the form of something. You can use it to change money, or you can use it to change the form of your hair.

Think of a thing that can show numbers. It has many numbers on it. The numbers change when you press a key. This key is the changer.

You also have a thing that changes the sound. When you press a key, the sound changes. This is the same thing that changes form. A computer has this kind of changer. It changes the numbers that are inside the computer into the sounds that the computer makes.

There is also a thing that can change the time. You can use it to change the time. It tells you when to be ready for a meeting. You can also use it to change the time when you want to go to sleep.

In a way, all these things are changed things. They change how we look, how we move, or how we hear.
