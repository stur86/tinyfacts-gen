To ask for something means to ask a person or a thing. When you want to buy a book, you are asking for it. You say, I want this book. This is an ask.

You can ask for many things. You might ask for food. You are looking for food. You say, I want food. You might ask for a drink. You say, I want a drink. You might ask for a friend. You say, I want my friend.

Sometimes, you ask for something you already have. You are looking for something you are already holding. You say, I want my phone. You already have a phone. You are asking for a new phone.

When you ask for something, you tell the person you want it. You show them that you need it. This helps them know what you want.
