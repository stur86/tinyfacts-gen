A book is a thing with many lines and words. It tells a story for a long time. You can read one book from start to finish. If you read one book, you know what happens before and after.

A book can be many parts. There are many books in one book. You can have a big book with many long parts. You can also have a book with only one long part. This big book is like a group of many small books.

Sometimes, we read a book with many stories. Each story is like a small book. These stories are about people and things that happen in different places. You can read these stories one by one.

You can also read books that are not stories. They are like how things work. They show you what happens when things are not the same. These books help you understand how the world works.

There is also a book that you write. This is a thing you make up. You write words that tell a story. When you are all done, you have a book. You can keep it for a long time and share it with others when you want.

In a school, there are many books. Each book is for a different kind of work. You might find a book about a story if you want to read. You might find a book about how things work if you want to learn.
