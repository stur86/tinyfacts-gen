A word is a thing you say or a picture you draw. It is a short word that helps you know what another word means. You can use a word to tell another person what you are talking about.

Think about a book. The book has many short lines. Each line is a word. If you say the word, you are talking about the book. The word is like a door. It opens to the next line.

We also use words to talk about things that are not in front of us. We talk about what happened before the story started. We use words to ask questions. If you ask, the person you ask answers. The word is a way to ask a question.

When we write a story, we use many words. Each word is like a door. Each door opens to a new idea. The doors are closed one after another, but the story is still there.

We need these words to talk. When you talk, you use words to tell other people what you think and feel. You are showing them what is happening.

Sometimes, we use a word in a different way. If we say, you are a good person, we mean you are kind and you do good things. But if we say, you are a very big person, it means you are very tall. The word is the same, but the picture is different.
