An idea is a thing you think about. It is how you feel inside. It is like a wave in the water. You might feel happy when you see a bright sun. You might feel sad when a friend says they are sick.

Inside your head, there are many of these little things. You have different ones for different times. When you are afraid, your heart is beating very fast. When you are very happy, your face might feel warm. When you are very angry, your face might feel red.

When you feel many of these things at once, it is called a feeling. It is like a big group of small things that move inside you. You do not always know why you are feeling this way. Sometimes you just know that you are feeling it.

You might feel this way because of what happened. You might feel sad if someone you love is gone. You might feel angry if someone tells a bad thing about you. You might feel happy if you see a good thing happening.

These are the things inside you when you are feeling.
