The word we use when we say something happened because of what else happened.

You might have a friend who always does the right thing. One day, you do the wrong thing. Your friend is surprised. The wrong thing you did is like a big push. This is what we call an after eventually. The friend is after the eventually, because the wrong thing you did happened because of the friend.

Think of it like a line. If you are on the left side of the line, the line goes left. If you are on the right side, the line goes right. Your friend is after the line. The wrong thing you did happens because the friend is on the line, and the line is what the wrong thing happened to you because of.

You can also use this word to talk about how things happened. If you have a plan, and you follow it, everything goes well. But if you do something different, or something happens before you did it, the plan did not happen. The wrong thing happened because of the plan, and the plan happened because of the wrong thing.

Sometimes, we use the word after to talk about how a thing is done. If you are after someone, you are doing something to them. If you are after money, you are trying to get it. If you are after a story, you are trying to understand it.

This word can be a little hard to understand at first. But it is a very important word that helps us talk about how things happen.
