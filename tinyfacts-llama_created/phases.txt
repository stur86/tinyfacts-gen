To go through a change is to pass through different times. You might start in one place and then move to another. This happens many times in life.

A big change can happen when you grow up. You might leave your home to find a job or go to a new school. This is a new time for you. You are not the same person anymore. You might feel nervous, excited, or sad.

A smaller change happens when you change your clothes. You put on a new shirt or pants. This is like a small move from one place to another. It is a quick change and you do not stay in one place for a long time.

You can also change your mind. Maybe you decide to do something you did not want to do. This is a change because you are moving away from what you thought was right. It is a new feeling and a new time for you.

When people say a thing is in its first, second, or third place, they are talking about how many times it happens. Think of a ball that goes up and comes down. This is the first time it goes up. The second time it goes up is the second time it goes up. The third time it goes up is the third time it goes up. It keeps happening until it reaches the top and then comes back down.

You can also say something is in a different place. If you are in your room now and you go to the kitchen, you are in a different place. You have moved from your home to another room.
