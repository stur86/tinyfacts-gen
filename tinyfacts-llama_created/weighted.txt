A heavy thing is something that makes you feel a lot. It is not light like a ball. If you put a heavy thing on your back while you walk, it makes your legs feel heavy too. This happens because heavy things are more than just pieces of stuff that are all over the place.

Things that are very heavy are like the ground under your feet. They are there, and you must move around them. Light things are like a ball that you can throw very far. They are not holding you down.

Sometimes, things that are very heavy are not just heavy. They are also very strong. A big heavy thing can hold a lot of water. A heavy thing that is also very strong can move a lot of ground under it. This is why heavy things are important in the world. Without them, our world would be very different.
