A word is a thing you say or a word you use to mean something. To be a good person is to use this word. To find what is right is to use it.

When you say a problem, you are not just talking about the hard part. You are also talking about how to start to fix it. You are looking for a way to be better. You are trying to find a way to say, or do, something new. When you find a way to do this, you have a new word.

Sometimes you use this word for something you are afraid of. You might say, I am afraid of a dark room. Here, the word is a way to show how you feel. It does not mean the room is really dark. It means you are afraid.

This word is also used when people agree. If two people want the same thing, they use this word. They are saying, we agree on this. They are finding a way to be both right.

The word is important because it helps us talk about what is going on. It helps us find what is right and what is not right. It helps us understand how to act when we have a problem.
