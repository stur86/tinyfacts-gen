A word is a thing you say or write. It is a small group of letters. You can use one word to talk about a thing, a person, or a feeling. You can also use words to write stories, make songs, or talk about what you think.

Words are made from sounds. When you say a word, you are making those sounds. A word can be short or long. A short word is one or two letters, like the word for a cat, which is cat. A long word is many letters and has many sounds. You can use one word to talk about a person, a house, or a thing that makes you happy. You can also use many words together to talk about a story or a feeling.

You can not think a word in your head. You have to say the word or write it down. A child learns words from the people around them. A doctor says, you have a word, you feel pain. A person might say, I am sad because I lost my friend. You learn many words from your family and friends.

Words help us talk and share ideas. When you hear a word, you can understand what it means. You can feel how the word makes you feel. You can use words to show you are happy, sad, angry, or surprised.
