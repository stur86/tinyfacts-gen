To show something is one of many ways to do it. You can show a picture in a book, watch a movie, or look at it on a computer. When you show something, you are letting everyone see it.

Sometimes, you show a person something. You might show them a book, a game, or a story. This helps them understand. You also show things that are hard to see. You might show a picture of a deep water or a bright night sky.

You can also show something in your mind. If you see a house, you show it to your brain. Your brain remembers it and you can tell others about it. This is why you remember places and things you have seen.

When you show something, you are giving people a chance to learn. You are making them see or hear so they can understand. This is the first step in making something happen.
