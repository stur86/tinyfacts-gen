A group of people who act is called a group. One person does all the work. Others help. They might act together. They are the people in the story. They are the ones with the words and the body parts.

In a story, we have many of these groups. One is the group of the people who are fighting. Another is the group of the people who want to be free. Another is the group of the people who are very old. They might have lived for many years and learned how to do things. They might have seen many things and learned how to act.

These groups are important. They show us how people act. They help us understand how people feel. They help us know how to act in a world that is full of many different kinds of people.
