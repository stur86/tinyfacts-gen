A word is a thing you use to talk or write. It helps us say things. A word is very small. You can say a word many times.

One kind of word is very big. It takes up a lot of space. You might write it in a book. This word is called a number. Numbers help us know how many things are there. They help us build things. You use numbers to build a house. You use numbers to build a car. You use numbers to build a computer.

Another kind of word is very small. It is not a number. It is a word that tells us what. A word is a word that tells us the meaning. If you say this word, you tell the other person what it means. If you say this word, the other person knows what the word is.

The word we are talking about is called a word of three. This word has three parts. The first part is the name. The second part is the number. The third part is the word that tells us the meaning. Think of a word like a house. The name is the house. The number is the number of rooms. The word is the story about the house. The story has three parts: the name, the number, and the story.

We use words all day. We use words to talk to our friends. We use words to write letters. We use words to write books. We use words to tell stories. We use words to show how we feel.
