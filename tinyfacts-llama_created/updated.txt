When something is new, it is not yet the same. It has changed. You can feel this when you see a new picture, hear a new song, or try a new food. This is what we mean by new.

When we say a thing is old, we mean it has been around for a long time. It has seen many years pass. It has had many chances to change or learn. This is what we mean by old.

But when you say a thing is up to date, you mean it is the same as it was before. It has not changed much. It is still the same way it was. This is what we mean by new.

Imagine a book. If you read it a few years ago, it was the same. It was not changed. If you go back a few years later, it is still the same book you read. It has not changed. This is what we mean by old.

Sometimes, people say a city is up to date. This means the city is the same as it was before. It has not changed much. It is still the city it was. This is what we mean by old.

But sometimes, people say a city is new. This means the city is changed a lot. New things have been built. New ways of doing things have been tried. This is what we mean by new.
