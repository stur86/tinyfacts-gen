To make an idea into a different way of writing or speaking is to make it an edging.

Think about a story. When you write it down, it is one way. But if you make it into a song, it is a different way. You are adding new words and making the story sound different. This is what an edging does.

Sometimes, people make an edging because they want to show how things are different. They might write a story about a cat and then write a story about a dog. This is a way to show that two things are not the same.

People also make edging because they want to share their ideas. If you have a new idea, you might make a picture of it so others can see it. You are putting the idea into a new way of showing it.

To make an edging is to take an idea and turn it into something new. This is how we learn and grow.
