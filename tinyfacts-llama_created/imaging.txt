To imagine something is to think about it. This does not mean you see it. You do not look at it. You do not see with your eyes. You are not looking at a picture. You are not seeing a real thing.

You imagine a house. You do not look at a picture. You are not seeing a real house. You are only thinking about what it will look like. What color it will be. What it feels like. What it looks like when the sun is going down.

Imagine you are a person who is very sad. You imagine you are a baby. You imagine the baby is crying. You imagine the baby is small and tiny. You imagine the baby is very, very small. You imagine the baby is in the room with you.

You imagine this because it feels real. It feels like something that can happen to you. It feels like a real story.
