A manager is a person who helps a group of people do their jobs. They are like the people who run a big team.

A manager is usually a person who has many things to do. They might be in a big office or a small room. They have to make sure everyone does what they say, and they have to keep the team moving.

The manager also has to decide what the team should do. They talk with the people who work there to see what they think. They might pick who does what job, or who gets to be in a new job. They use this to keep everyone working together.

A manager is like a parents but for a group. They help everyone feel safe and happy. They also help the people do their jobs so the team can do its job better.

Sometimes, a manager is the one who makes the hard calls. They might say something is not okay. They might say a person does not do their job. They tell people to change and keep moving forward.

A manager can be very important. They help keep a team going. They make sure everyone is following the right way. They keep everyone safe and make sure things stay running well.
