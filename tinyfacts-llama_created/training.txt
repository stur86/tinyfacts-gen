To train someone is to help them learn. When you train a dog, you teachers give it food and water and a place to live. You also show the dog what is right and wrong. After many days, the dog becomes able to do things you ask it to do.

When you train a person, you help them do things they already know. You might tell them to do a job or to follow a set of steps. You also give them a way to learn new things. If they ask you a question or want to try something different, you help them figure it out.

Training is important for many jobs. If you want to be a doctor, you train for many years to learn how to care for people. If you want to be a teacher, you train to understand how to help others. If you want to be a police person, you train to follow the right ways to act.

You also train your brain. When you learn something new, your brain gets stronger. This is why you remember things better after you have tried them. Your brain also gets better at understanding what is not right.

Sometimes, training is hard. You might feel sad or angry. But you know it is for your own good. You are helping the person learn and grow. At the end, you feel good because you did something that helped someone.
