A person who moves for another person is called a carrier. They carry things from one place to another and often give them to the person they are for. This is when you send a letter. You write what you want to say and put it in a box. Then you give the box to a person who carries it. The person reads what you wrote and gives it to the right person.

Another way to look at this is that a carrier is someone who moves things from one person to another. They do not carry money. They carry things like books, papers, or food. Sometimes they have to go very far. They might walk or run across a wide water or cross a big city.

There are many kinds of carrier jobs. One person carries a ball for a game. Another person carries a bag with food for a child. A carrier for a movie might carry a small box with pictures. A carrier for a computer might carry a small box with numbers and words.

When we say a person is a good carrier, we mean they are good at moving things. They are carefully kind and do not drop anything. If a carrier is good at their job, people trust them.
