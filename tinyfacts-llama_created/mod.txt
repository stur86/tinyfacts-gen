To change something is to make it different. If you want to keep a number, you keep it the same. But if you want to make it bigger or smaller, you change it. This can happen with many things in life.

A person might feel angry. They might raise their voice. This makes their face red. This is changing the way they feel. They are not still the same person they were before.

A car can go very fast. If you want to slow it down, you can turn it. This makes the car stop. This changes how the car is moving.

You can also change something inside your body. When you are very tired, you might feel tired. You might want to sleep. This is changing how you feel.

To change can be hard. Sometimes we want to keep things the same. But in life, we often need to change. This is how we grow and become better people.
