When you look at a piece of paper, the lines go from top to bottom, from left to right. This is because the paper is sitting on a table and the top line is where you sit. The bottom line is where the table stands. The lines are all the same, and they do not change.

If you take a picture of a road, the lines on the road go from left to right and from top to bottom. These lines are the same in every place. If you look at the road from different places, you will see that the lines are the same, but the road is different from one place to another.

When you walk, your feet go from left to right and top to bottom. Your legs are under you, and your body is above you. This is the way your feet and body move in the world.

Also, when you watch a movie, the lines on the wall go from left to right and from top to bottom. You might see a line of people walking, or a line of cars moving. The lines tell you where each person or thing is and where they are going.

In short, anything that is straight and goes from top to bottom is said to be on a line. Lines help us understand how the world works and how we move through it.
