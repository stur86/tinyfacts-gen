To be or to do something means you are an important part of a group. You are the one who works hard and makes things happen. People say this when they see how a job is done.

If you are a worker, you do many things. You wake up early. You go to work. You do your job. You do not play with your friends all day. You stay and do your work. Your job is important. It helps the world.

When you do something, you make new things. You build a house. You make food for people to eat. You make clothes for people to wear. You do things that help people live.

If you are not working, you are not being. You might play with your friends. You might go to the park. You might read a book. You are still a part of the group. But your work is not going on.

The word is also used when someone is very good at their job. They do many things very well. They can do it fast and do it right. They are like a very strong person who works hard every day.

Being or doing something is important. It shows you care about the world and people.
