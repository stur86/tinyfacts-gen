To have an answer means to know what is right. If you know what is right, you can do it. You can say you are the best. This is how you show you are strong.

When you play a game, you have two people. One is your friend, and the other is your friend. You both want to be the one who does not get hurt. You try to stop the other person. If you do not stop them, they might do something bad and hurt you.

In a game of running, you are an answer. You try to be the first one to cross the line. If you are the one who crosses, you are an answer. You are the one who did not get hurt.

This word is also used in school. You might have an answer to a question. If you are the one who answers, you are an answer. You have the right one.

Sometimes, you play with a friend. You are both an answer. You both try to be the one who does not get hurt. This is how you work together and stay safe.

To have an answer can also mean to try to find the right thing. If you want to know if a food is good, you try to find out. You ask your mom. If she tells you it is good, you know you have an answer.

In the game of life, you often have answers. You might get an answer to a question. You might get an answer to a problem. This is how you know what to do next.
