An about is a word that is often used when talking about something that is very big or very small, but you can not see it. You can not touch it. You can only hear about it.

When you read about a story, you do not see the pictures. You only see the words on the paper. So, you need to use your mind to imagine what happened. An about is like this.

Think of a person who is very good at playing music. You do not see the person. You can only hear how they play. You can not see their hair or their clothes. You can only hear their music. Because of this, you can understand who they are. You do not need to see them.

An about is often used when talking about people, animals, or things that are very far away. It helps us understand them without seeing them.

Sometimes, an about can be used to talk about a feeling. You might say, someone is very sad. This is an about because you are not seeing the person. You are only hearing about how they feel.
