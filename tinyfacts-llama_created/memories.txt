Memories are things you remember from your past. They are things that happen in your mind when you are thinking about them.

When you see a picture, you remember what the picture looks like. You remember the colors, the people, the places where you were. You remember the story the picture tells you. Your memories are like a book that you carry inside you. Every time you see a picture, a sound, or a word, it is like a new memory that adds to the book.

Some memories are very strong. You remember a fight with a friend or a time when you were very happy. These memories stay with you for a long time. You might remember the face of a person from a movie or a song. You might remember the smell of a place you went to many times. These memories are important because they help you remember who you are and where you came from.

But some memories are not as strong. You might remember a time when you were very sad, but you do not remember the face of the person you saw. You might remember a word, but you do not remember what it means. Your brain is always trying to make sense of everything, but sometimes it gets confused.

You can have many different kinds of memories. You might remember a time from your early life, a time from your school days, or a time from your family. You might remember a time from a movie, a book, or a song. You might remember a time from a game or a work. Every memory is like a small piece of a big story.

Sometimes, memories change over time. A memory from when you were young might seem funny now. A memory from when you were very old might seem strange. Your brain is always changing, and your memories are a part of that change.
