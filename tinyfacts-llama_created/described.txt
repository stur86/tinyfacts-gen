To be seen is to be seen. A thing is seen when someone looks at it. If you look at a tree, you see the green leaves and the brown wood. You see the way it looks and the way it feels. 

If you do not look at something, it is not seen. You are not seeing it. You are just doing other things. You are eating a food or you are playing with a ball. In these cases, the thing is not there to your eyes. 

But when you look at a picture, you are seeing it. You are looking at the lines of the picture and the colors. You are seeing the way the light falls on it. The picture is not there to your eyes. It is only for your eyes to see. 

To be seen is to be noticed. If you walk down a street, people might see you. If you stand still for a moment, you might be noticed by a friend or a family person. But if you walk very fast, people might not see you. They are too busy. 

You can also be seen in a different way. If you are a person who is not afraid to talk, you are being seen. You are being seen by people who are interested in you. They are looking at you. You feel heard. 

In some places, being seen is a right. If you are not doing anything wrong and you just walk by, the people might see you and let you go. You are not being held. You are not being watched. You are free.
