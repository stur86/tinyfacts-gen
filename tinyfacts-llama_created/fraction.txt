A number is a way to show how many parts something is into. Imagine you have a big box of food. You eat one part and then you eat another part. You eat three parts in all. You can write this as three over one. This number tells you how much of the food you ate. If you eat two parts, you have two over one.

A number is always between one and two. If you have one part, the number is one. If you have two parts, the number is two. If you have three parts, the number is three. If you have more parts than two, the number is bigger than two. If you have fewer parts than one, the number is smaller than one. Numbers help us tell how much of something we have.

A number can also show how many times one part fits into another. If you have two hundred parts and one part is one, the number is two hundred. This number tells you the totally of parts you have.

Sometimes, we use numbers to show parts of a bigger thing. If you have one hundred five pieces of money, you have one over five hundred. This number shows how many parts money are into the bigger thing.

We use numbers to write stories, to show how much is left, and to tell people how many are in a group.
