A sign is a way to leave your mark. You can use your hand, a piece of paper, or a computer. When you sign something, you are saying that you are the one who made it.

A person might sign their name when they write a letter. This is important because it helps everyone know who sent the letter. You can also sign your name when you buy something. This is how you show that you paid for it.

You might see a sign on a street that says, please be quiet. This sign says that people should not talk while they are on the street. It helps keep the city safe.

Sometimes you might sign a book or a movie. This can mean you liked what was happening. It is a way to show that you enjoyed the story.

You can also sign a work made by another person. This means you are giving them your okay to use their ideas. It is a way to say, I trust you.
