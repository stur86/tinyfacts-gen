Bathrooms are rooms where people do their business. They are usually in homes or schools. In a home, the bathroom is a room in the house where people can go to clean themselves and use the water. 

In a school, there are many of these rooms. Some are very big and have many doors. Other ones are smaller and have only one door. These rooms have lots of lights and many kinds of things for cleaning. They have cold water and warm water. Some rooms have a small table and a chair. People go in these rooms when they want to clean their hands and face after using the bathroom at night.

Some people also have a room in their home that is for their body. This room has a big box or a bag. Inside the box, you can find many things to help your body feel good. These things are like a soft cover, a soft bag, and sometimes a cold thing to put on your skin. When you put these things on, you feel better because your body feels warm and you do not feel so much pain.
