Practically and often, when we talk about a group of people, we use the word they follow. This word is easy to use. You can say, for many years, they followed a group of people. After some time, they changed. They are no longer following one person.

A group of people has a set of things they believe. These things are not one person. If you follow them, you follow these things. If you change, you follow these new things. This is what we mean by a set of ideas or a set of ways.

Sometimes, a group follows many different things. They follow many people at once. They follow the ideas of many different people. This is a very strong set of ideas. They are all around them. They are not just one person.

A set of ideas is not the same as a set of ways. A set of ways has things in a line. You follow a way from one place to another. You follow a road. You follow a line. But a set of ideas is not like this. It is a group of many different things. You can never follow all of them at once. You have to pick some and leave the rest.

To follow is to follow this set of ideas. You follow it if you believe in it. You follow it if you act on it. This is how you change. You change your life. Or you change the world.
