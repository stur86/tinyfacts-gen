A doctor is a person who helps keep you safe and well. They work with your body and help you feel better when you are sick or hurt.

Doctors are very good at being sure things are right. They look at what you tell them and do things to make sure you stay okay. They use things to cut or push inside you to find the problem. They also give you things to drink or take by mouth to help your body.

Some doctors work in a big building with many other people. Others work in a small room in a house or a school. They may see many people in one day and help a lot of people at once.

Doctors also talk to you about what is going on. They ask you questions to understand how you feel and what is wrong. They listen to what you say so they know if you are scared or if you are very happy.

There are many kinds of doctors. Some are very good at making you better with tiny things they do. Others are good at fixing things that are broken. There are even doctors who help you feel happy again after you are sad.

To become a doctor, you must go to school and learn a lot. You study about how the world works, about how the body works, and about how to keep people safe. You also learn about how to make the right answers when you hear them.

Doctors are very important because they help keep us all safe. They help us feel better when we are sick or hurt. They show us that we are not alone when we are afraid or when we are very sad.
