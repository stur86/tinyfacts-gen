To bed something means to put it in a safe place. Most of the time, we put our beds in our rooms at night. This is so we can sleep and feel safe. When you are in bed, you lie down and rest your body. Your head is on a soft, hard piece of wood. You might have a cover to keep the cold or warm air from getting into your bed.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about a group of animals that live in the water. They are the ones that sleep under the water with their eyes closed. This is very important for them because they need to rest while they are waiting for food to come.
