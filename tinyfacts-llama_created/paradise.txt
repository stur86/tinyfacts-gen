A place where everything is nice is called a place of a nice life. It is a place where you can be very happy. It is a place where you can do what you want without any fear.

Imagine you are in a big green room. All the trees are tall and green. There are many rooms with many rooms inside. In one room, you can play with soft things. In another room, you can read books. In another room, you can eat food you like. In the room right next to the food room, you can go to the bathroom and get clean.

In this room, there is no loud noise. There are no people fighting. Everyone is kind. There is no fear. You can go to the room you want and stay there as long as you want.

Some people say the world is like this place. They say that is where everyone should live. They say it is where you should be if you want to be happy.

But some people also say that the world is not like this place. They say that there are many bad people and bad things. They say that this place is not big enough for everyone. They say that if everyone went to this place, there would not be enough rooms for everyone.

So, people have different ideas about what a place of a nice life looks like.
