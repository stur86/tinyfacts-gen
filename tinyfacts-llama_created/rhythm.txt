A word is an idea you use to talk. It is a way to say something. We use these words every day. You use them to say how you feel. You might say, you feel sad. Or, you might say, I am happy.

When you say these words, you make a sound. The sound is part of the word. The sound is like a group of short and long sounds. You can put the short sounds together and the long sounds together. This group of sounds is the word.

You can also use the word to talk about how things move. If a dog runs, the word can tell you how fast it is going. If a tree moves, the word can tell you how far it has gone. The word helps us understand the world.

Sometimes, a group of sounds is just the word. If you hear a word, you know what it means. You do not need to think about it. The word gives you the answer.

You can also use the word to talk about music. When you hear music, you can feel the sound. The sound can make you want to move. The sound can make you feel happy or sad. The word is like a story for your ears.
