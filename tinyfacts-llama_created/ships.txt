A big water thing is called a shop. It is a place where people can buy things they need. You can buy food, clothes, and other things that help you live. The water goes around the world inside the big water thing. People use it to go where they want. Some shops stay on the ground, and others go out on the water.

Inside a shop, there are many rooms. One room is for the things that the shop makes. Another room is for the things you buy. There is also a room where the people who work in the shop go to rest. In some shops, there is a room where the people show you how to use the things.

People use big water things to go to many places at once. They are very important. They help us move from one place to another. Some people drive these big water things. Others sit inside and control the thing with their hands.

A shop is like a house that carries many things. It has doors that open and close. When you want to go inside, you push the door. When you want to go out, you push the door again. Inside, there are many boxes and bags. You can buy a box of food or a bag of clothes.

Shops also have a way to keep things safe. They use doors and windows that close when people are inside. They also use things to stop the water from coming in. In this way, the shop stays safe.

A shop can be very big. Some are as big as a city. Others are small and can be in a small room. Everyone needs a shop because they need to buy things every day.
