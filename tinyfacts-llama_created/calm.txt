A calm person is a person who feels very quiet and still. They do not get angry or worried easily. They can be very quiet, even when no one is around. They do not get loud or shout.

You can feel calm when you are in a quiet place like a park or a room. You might feel this way when you are not afraid of anything. Your heart might feel light and your body might feel better.

A calm person does not get nervous. They do not feel like they are going to fall down or that something bad is going to happen. They stay in the same place or move very slowly.

Sometimes, a calm person might look at a picture. They might look at a beautiful picture of a tree or a bright day. They might see the colors and the way the light falls on the picture. This makes them feel calm and happy.

A calm person can be very kind. They might listen to someone who is sad or angry. They might say, or they might just sit there and be quiet. This helps to make the person feel better.

Being calm is important. It helps us feel safe. It helps us feel like we can do anything.
