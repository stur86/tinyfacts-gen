To break something down is to take it into many small parts. These parts are the very things that make the thing whole again.

Imagine you have a box. You want to know how it works. If you look at the box, you see a top, a side, and a bottom. These are the parts of the box. If you take the top, the side, and the bottom, you have the very things that make the box work. You can put the top back on, the side on the box, and the bottom on the floor. The box is now working again.

In our world, we use these parts to make many things. We break down a car so we can see what makes it move. We break down a chair so we can see what makes it stand. We break down a computer so we can see what makes it work.

We also use this word in our money. Each piece of money has many parts. There is the paper, the box, the paper with numbers on it, the numbers that tell us how much money there is, and the picture of the money. When you put all these parts together, you have money that you can buy things with.

When we say something is made of many parts, we mean that it is made from many small pieces that are joined together. It is not one big piece. It is many pieces that work together to make it what it is.
