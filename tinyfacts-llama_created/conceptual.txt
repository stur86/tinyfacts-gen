To think is to consider. When you think, you are not doing something at the same time as the thing you think about. Your mind is doing something different while your body is doing something else. This is because your mind is working on a picture or a story in your head, even if your eyes are closed or you are not looking at anything.

When you talk about something, you are using words that mean the same thing. But when you think, you are using your own words. Your words are like a picture in your head. They are not the same as the thing they are talking about. This is why your mind can go in many different directions at once. You might be thinking about a big picture and then suddenly you hear a sound.

A person who does this is called a thinker. Think is a very important thing to do. It helps us understand the world. It helps us decide what to do. It helps us know how to feel. Think is also important when you are trying to learn something new. You can learn many things at the same time, but your mind is still working on them.

Think is different from feel. Feel is what you do when you are in your body. You might feel happy when you hear music. You might feel scared when you see a big animal. Think is what you do in your head. You can think about a lot of things at once, and you can think about different things at the same time.
