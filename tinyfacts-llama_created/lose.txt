To lose something is to take it away from you. You are holding it, and then you let it go. This can happen in many ways.

You might lose a ball in a game. You are playing with your friends, and you throw the ball. You watch it go down the street. Then, you look around, and the ball is gone. You did not know it was there anymore. You have lost it.

You can also lose money. You save your money for many years. You buy things you like. Then, one day, you run out of money. You are left with nothing. You have lost your money.

Sometimes, you lose your way. You are walking with your friends. You follow a street sign. But then, the street turns around. You are now in a different place. You did not know this would happen. You have lost your way.

Sometimes, you lose a person. You love this person very much. You want to be near them. But then, they leave. You are left alone. You are lost without them.
