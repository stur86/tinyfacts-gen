To breathe is to use your air. When you breathe, you take in the air from your mouth and your nose and put it into your body. This helps your body have the power it needs to move. Your body uses the air to give off the things you need to stay living.

When you are a baby, you breathe very fast. You take in air very quickly and give it out very fast. Your body needs a lot of air to grow. As you grow older, you breathe a little less. You still need to breathe, but you breathe more slowly.

You can also breathe when you are very cold. When your body feels the cold, you start to breathe more. You take in air and let it out very quickly. This helps get warm air into your body.

Sometimes, people do this to a dog. If a dog is very strong, it can take in a lot of air at once. It can breathe very fast to get enough air. This helps the dog stay strong and keep going.
