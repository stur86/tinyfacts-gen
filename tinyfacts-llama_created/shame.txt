Sometimes, you might shake a cat. The cat feels bad when you touch it. It tries to get away from you. The cat shakes its body to make the bad feeling go away.

People also shake with fear. If a loud noise happens, you might shake. This makes you feel like you are going to fall. You might want to hide.
