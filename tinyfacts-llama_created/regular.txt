To be a normal thing means you follow the way things usually happen. You do not change what you are doing all the time. You stay the same way most of the time.

Think about a house. The people who live there go to the same place every day. They eat the same food, go to the same school, and do the same work. They do not change what they are doing. They stay the same.

If you are a person, you might be a teacher. You tell other people what they should do. You follow the way things are done in your school. You do not change what you do. You stay the same.

It is also true for things that grow. A tree stays the same from year to year. It does not change how it looks or what color it is. It stays the same way every year.

Sometimes, things change. A child grows and becomes a young man. A dog grows from a baby to a big animal. But a tree stays the same. It does not change how it looks or what color it is. It is still the same tree.

Because of this, we use the word to talk about things that stay the same. If you say something is a normal thing, you mean it follows the way things usually happen.
