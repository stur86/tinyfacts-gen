To pay something means you give money or a thing to someone. You give them what you have, so they can do what you need them to do. This makes both you and the other person happy.

Think about a time when you did a job. You did a work for a person and they paid you. You gave them what you had so they could pay you later. This is an important thing to do.

You can pay money in many ways. You can give it in one big group of money at once. You can also give it in many small groups. Some people use this when they pay for things they buy.
