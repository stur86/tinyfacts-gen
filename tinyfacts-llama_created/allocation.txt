To give something to someone means to share a thing you have with them. If you have a lot of money, you can give some to your friend. You are not keeping it for yourself.

Sometimes you give money to a school or a hospital. This helps people who are sick or in need. It helps them buy food or a bed so they can rest.

You can also give something that is not money. You might give your time to help a friend. You might give your help to a family who is very sad. This is a good thing because it shows you care about them.

When you say you are giving your attention, you are using this word. You are not watching a movie. You are listening to a friend talk about their day. You might let them talk and then you join in. This is a nice way to spend time together.

An idea is something you think about. An idea is like a story in your head. You might have an idea for a new movie. You can also have an idea for a game. You try to figure out how it will work.

If you say you have an idea, you are not giving your brain a thing to hold. You are just thinking about it. You are not doing anything with the idea yet.
