To be the one who is doing something can be a great feeling. If you have a big dream and you work hard to make it happen, you can be the one who makes it real.

This word can also mean how people are feeling when something happens. If a thing happens that makes a person very angry, they might feel like they are the one who caused it. If a thing happens that makes a person very happy, they might feel like they are the one who made it happen.

Sometimes, we are all the same. What one person does can make another person feel the same way. This is why we use this word when we talk about how people feel.

If a person does something and it changes how other people feel, they are also the one who was changed. They are the one who had an idea and made it happen.
