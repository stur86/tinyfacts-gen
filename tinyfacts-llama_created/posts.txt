To make a thing stand up can be a strong job. A person might put a big word into a computer to tell it what to do. They write a lot of lines, each one saying a few words.

When this happens to a person, they are the ones making the thing stand up. They use their words to show the computer what they want to happen. The computer then does the work. The person does not need to be there. The computer is the one who stands up.

In the same way, a person who makes a picture stands up. They are the ones who decide what looks good. They pick colors and make sure the picture is right. They write the lines that go around the picture. This is how they make the picture stand up.

Some people build tall walls. They put many, many small pieces of wood together to make a strong wall. The wall stands up because of what they did. They made it so the wind can not push it over.

To make a thing stand up is to give it power. It is like a small push that goes up and stays there.
