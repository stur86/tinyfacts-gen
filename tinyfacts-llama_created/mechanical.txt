A thing that does work is called a thing that moves. When you pull a door, it opens. When you push a car, it goes. You use your hands to make the thing do this.

A box that does not need a hand is a thing that moves. It can go without you touching it. You just tell it to go and it goes. It is not because of the person who pulls the thing.

A thing that moves can be very small. A tiny thing that is in the air can go very fast. Or a thing that is very heavy can move in a slow way. Sometimes a thing that moves is a car, a dog, or a person.

When something is made to move, it is called a thing that moves. It is like a living thing but it does not need to eat or drink. It just needs power to go.
