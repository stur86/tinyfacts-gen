To leave someone out is to leave them behind. This happens when people do not allow others to join their group. A person may be told they are not good enough to join a game. Another person might be told they are not good enough to work in a place where they do not have a job. A child might be told they are not allowed to play with a friend because they are too old.

When you are left out, you feel very sad. You might want to join the group. You might try to say, I want to be part of this group. But the person who told you this already knows you are not allowed. They are just following a set of orders.

Sometimes, this happens because of things that are not right. Maybe there is a lot of money in the group, and the person who is told to leave is the one who has more money. Or maybe the person who is left out did something wrong. This can lead to more problems.

In a different way, to leave someone out can mean to keep a story to yourself. If you tell someone a story, but they are not there, you are still telling them. But if you do not tell them, you have left them out of your life. This can hurt because you wanted to share the story with them.

It is also about how we act when we are left behind. If you leave someone alone in a busy room, they might feel scared. But if you leave a child alone at night, they might feel very safe. This shows how we can change our ways when we need to be left alone.
