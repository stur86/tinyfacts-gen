An under student is someone who is learning a big job. They do not have a job yet. They are still learning. They go to a school that is not as big as one with many many students.

Usually, you go from high school to this kind of school. You are still a young person. You have not been in a big job for very long. You are learning to think and to act.

There are two kinds of under students. One kind goes into a big room. They sit with many other under students. The room is very big and very loud. They do not sit together. They are alone in their rooms.

The other kind goes to a small room. They sit with many people. They talk with them all day. They are not alone. There is a big room in the back where they can go if they need to be alone.

Both kinds of under students learn things. They learn how to write, how to talk, how to work with others, and how to be a good person. They also learn how to do jobs that are important for the world.

Sometimes, under students are not in school at all. They work and learn at home. They watch a teacher on their computer. They read books. They do work to help others.

Under students are very important. They help the world. They learn how to act. They learn how to be a good person. They learn how to work with others. They learn how to be a part of a team.
