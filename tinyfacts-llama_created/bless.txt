To be in a good place is a feeling we get when we are happy and safe. You can feel this when you are with people you love. You might feel like everything is okay because they are there.

We can also use this word for things that are good for us. If someone helps you, you feel happy. If you eat food that is good for your body, your body feels better. You might say, a nice dinner makes me feel better.

Sometimes, we use this word for things that happen when we are glad. When a baby is brought into the world, many people say, a new life is being added. We are glad that new life is here.

We also say that a place is in a good place. If you live in a city, you might say, this city is a good place to live. It is safe and has many things to see and do.

When a person does something kind, we say they have a good feeling. We might say, you are a good person. This means you are a person who is kind and does not hurt others.

To be in a good place can also mean you are not afraid. If you are in a big room, you might feel a little scared. But if you are in a room with friends, you are in a good place. You feel safe because you are with people you trust.
