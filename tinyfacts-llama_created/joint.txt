A join is a place where two pieces of something meet. This is where they touch. The join can be very strong or very small. It can hold things together, make them look nice, or make a way for water to go through.

Think of it like two hands. The hands are different, but they join to make a hand. The hand is the part that you use to do things. The join is the area where the two hands meet. Without a join, the hands would be very different, and you could not use them the same way.

A join can be in many things. You can join two table legs together to make a table. You can join two parts of a book so that they fit together. You can join the edges of a picture so that it looks nice.

Sometimes, a join can be very small. A tiny join can help keep a small thing shut. If a small door were left open, the air could go in and out very fast.

A join can also be about how things work. Imagine you have a car with many parts. When the parts join together, they work well. The car moves. The parts help each other so that everything works as a team.

In some places, a join can mean how things are made. A person can join a group of friends to make a team. The team does not have to be made of one person. Many people join to work together.

In some places, a join can mean how things are put together. A person might join a school to learn more. The school becomes a place where they can grow and learn together.
