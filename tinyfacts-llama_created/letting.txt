To let something is to allow it to happen.

When you are afraid, you might try to stop a thing from happening. You might close your eyes, cover your mouth, or try to run away. But if you let something happen, you are letting it go. You are giving it space to happen without trying to control it.

Think of a child. A baby might cry and let their parents know they are sad. This is letting the baby know they need something. It is saying, here, I am here, and I need you.

You can also let go of things. If you are holding a thing too tight, it feels heavy and hard inside your chest. You might feel better when you let it go. This is letting go. You are giving up the power to hold onto something and letting it become its own.

Sometimes you let go of the past. A person might hold onto a memory for too long. They might not want to let it go because it reminds them of a good time. But letting it go is letting the memory become a part of the story of your life. It is letting go of the past and moving forward into the new days ahead.

Letting can be hard. You might feel a little scared or worried. But it is also a part of life. It helps us learn how to be with others, how to be happy, and how to let go of things that hurt us.
