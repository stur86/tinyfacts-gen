Your heart is a very important thing in your body. It is a soft thing in your chest. You can feel it when you are happy, sad, or angry.

Inside your chest, there are many tiny rooms. One of these rooms is for your love. When you love someone, you feel your heart in that room. Your love makes you feel warm and makes you want to be near the person you love.

Your heart also has a room for fear. When you are afraid, your heart beats fast. This is because your body is ready to run away from the thing that makes you afraid.

Your heart is also for your life. It keeps you going even when you are tired. It helps you remember things from your past and plans for your life.

You can not see your heart. But you know it is there because of how you feel. If you feel a big jump when you see a bright light, you know your heart is working hard.
