Glass is a thing you make from many small pieces of hard stuff. These pieces are very tiny and they fit together like a very small wall. When many of these small pieces are joined, they form a big, clear box or a tall, clear room.

To make glass, you start with a bit of these tiny pieces. You put the pieces together in a hot room until they are very, very close. Then you take the hot pieces out and let them cool. When they cool, they hard and become very strong. This makes the glass clear and not break easily.

People use glass to make cups, bottles, and many other things we need every day. You can even cut glass into small pieces to make pretty things. You can also use glass to make windows for houses so that light can come in. The glass helps keep the cold out and the warm air in.

Glass can also be very hot when it is first made. If you put the hot glass in water, it will start to change and become soft. If you keep it in the water for a long time, the glass will become very soft and then break. But if you put it in cold water, it stays hard and strong. This is why you can keep the glass for a long time without it breaking.

There are also different kinds of glass. Some are very clear and some are very dark. Some are clear in the middle so you can see both sides. Some are very hard and make a nice cold sound when you hit them.

Glass is a very good thing to make because it can change color when it is hit. If you hit a glass with a hard thing, it might turn red or brown. This is a fun way to change the color of the glass.
