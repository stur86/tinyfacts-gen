To talk means to use words to tell other people what you think. You use these words to say your ideas, ask questions, or tell a story.

When you talk, you use your mouth to move words. You open your lips and move them. This makes the sound we hear. The words inside your head go out through the air and reach the people who hear them. They can hear you and understand what you are saying.

Talking helps us share our ideas. If you want to tell someone about a thing you found on the ground, you can talk about it. You can say, I saw a red thing that fell from the sky. This way, the other person can understand what you are talking about.

People also talk to each other at work. They talk about what they did. They tell their friends about the day so they can know what happened. This helps them stay in touch and work together.

Sometimes, we talk when we are sad. If someone loves us, they might want to be near us. They might say words to make us feel better. They might say, I am here for you, you are safe with me. This helps us feel less alone.

Talking can also mean to talk about a movie or a game. You watch a movie with friends. You talk about what happened on the big white thing. You might say, Wow, the dogs were fast! You are all laughing and having fun.
