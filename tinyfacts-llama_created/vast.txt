The word is about how big something is. When we talk about a thing, we want to know how many parts it has and how big each part is. If a dog has a lot of hair, we say it is big. If a tree is very tall, we say it is big too.

If you look at the sky, you see so many stars up there. This is a very big number. You would need a very large box to hold all the stars. Our world is a small part of this sky. The sun is so big it could cover all the land in our world and still have room to breathe.

The word can also mean something is not in the small space of our world. If you go very far away, the world looks different. The sky looks different, the stars look different, and the ground looks different. The world is not just one big piece. There is a lot more going on in the world that we can not see.

To be small is also good. A tiny room in your house can feel like a tiny world. But if you look out the window at the world outside, you can see a huge number of other people, cars, and trees. Being small can be nice, but being very big can be very strong too.
