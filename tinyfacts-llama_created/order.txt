An order is a way to do things. When you are a child, you do what your mother or father says. You go to the table and eat. You do not do what you want because you are afraid of getting in trouble. An order is like that. You do not want to do it, but you have to.

Sometimes, when you are working, you have to follow a plan. You have to do this and that. If you do not follow the plan, you can lose your job. An order is like a plan.

You can also order food. You tell the person at the table that you want something. If they do not get it right, you tell them again. You do not say, I want it again. You say, I want it now. This is an order.

In a big city, there are many orders. There are orders to keep the streets clean. There are orders to keep the air clean. There are orders to keep the water clean. Everyone follows the orders because they are important for the city.

You can also order people to do something. You tell them to pick up their things. You tell them to walk to the store. An order is a way to get things done.
