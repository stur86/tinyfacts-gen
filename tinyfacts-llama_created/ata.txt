To attack is to start a fight. You might use this word in a few different ways.

First, you might say you are about to attack a person. If you are angry, you might want to hurt them. You might say,  You are going to attack him. This means you are going to use your hands to hurt him.

Another way you might use this word is in a game. You are playing with your friends. You start to shout at them. You are saying,  Stop! You are attacking them. You want them to stop what they are doing and listen to you.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about how they feel. If you feel very angry, you might say you are attacking your own mind. You feel like you are going to break. You are trying to fight the hard feeling inside you.

You can also use this word to talk about the world around you. If the wind is moving very fast, it might be like the world is attacking us. This is a good way to talk about something that is changing very fast and might make you feel a bit scared.

But remember, to attack is not always good. It can mean using force to hurt someone. In this case, it is not nice. We should try to find other ways to talk about our problems or to be angry that is not about hurting other people.
