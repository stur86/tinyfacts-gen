To faced something means to see it. You are in a room with a man. He is angry. You want to get away from him. The door is closed. You look around the room. You see the man. You see his face. You have seen his face before. You know what he looks like.

You are not afraid because you know him. You are ready to face him. You are not afraid of what he might do. You are ready for the fight. You are ready to see him.

If you are faced with a big problem at school, you must face it. You must look at it. You must know what to do. You can not run away. You must be ready to act. You must be ready to try.

If you are faced with a bad thing, you must also face it. You must see it. You must know what it is. You must know what to do. You must not try to hide. You must face it head on.
