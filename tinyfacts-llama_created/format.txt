To change the way something looks so it is easier to read or understand.

Imagine a long piece of paper. You write a lot of words on it, but the words are very long and make the paper very heavy. Then you cut the paper into small pieces. Now each small piece has only a few words. You can read one piece at a time without the paper getting too heavy.

You can do this with many things. You can change how you write on a computer so that the words come out one line at a time. You can change how a picture looks so that it is easier to see. You can change how a story looks so that the words do not make the reader feel like they are trying to learn a lot.

Sometimes you need to change how a set of numbers looks so that it is easier to work with. You might want to change the numbers so they do not have so many different places. This makes it easier to do a lot of different things with the numbers in one step.

This word is important in many parts of life. You do it every day. You change how you write, how you look, and how you talk so you can do your work in a way that is easier for everyone.
