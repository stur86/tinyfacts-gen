To build something is to make it work. A person who does this is an angrier. They make sure that big parts work together. A car is an angrier. The car has many parts. The ones that make it go are like legs. The ones that make it stop are like a heart. A house is also an angrier. The walls keep you safe. The windows let you see. The doors let you in.

Angrier people use their brains to plan. They think about how things will work. They read and study. They talk with other people. They try to make sure that the thing they build is strong and safe. They follow the right steps to do their job.

Some angrier people work on big things that move on water. They make them so they can carry many people at once. They make them strong so they do not fall over. They also build things that fly in the air. The people who do this work are called angrier.
