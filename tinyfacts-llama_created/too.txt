The word you say when you are too busy to talk is too. If you are doing something and you need more time, you say it is too. You might be in a hurry to finish something or you might be doing something that is very important.

Sometimes, when you want something very much, you say you are too old. If you are very young and you want to go to a place where older people go, you say that. Or you might say you are too tired to do something because you have been working hard all day.

If you are doing something and you are already doing it very well, you might say it is too easy. You might think the game is not hard at all if you have already done it many times.

If you say something is too, you are telling others that it is not right. You are saying that the thing is too hard, too long, or too much. This word helps us tell others when something is not okay.
