To show a kind way of acting can be very nice. When a person is very angry, they might act in a way that hurts other people. They might shout, or they might hit a person. This can cause a lot of pain and fear.

A kind way is when you let people be. If you see someone who is very sad, you might not say anything at first. You just look at them. This helps the person feel safe. Later, when they are ready, you might tell them that you care.

Sometimes people give something they have. If you are very bad, a kind person might give you a book or a small thing they do not want. They are not trying to hurt you, but they still act in a way that shows they care. This is a way of showing you that you are not alone.

We also say that someone is a friend. This means you feel safe with them and you trust them. If you are scared, you might tell your friend that you are afraid. They listen and they tell you what to do. This helps you feel better when you are worried.

In a world full of bad people, having a kind person is very important. It helps us feel less alone and more ready to help others.
