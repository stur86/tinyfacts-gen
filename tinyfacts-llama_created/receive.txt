To give someone something happens when you give them a thing you own. You might give a book to a friend. You are the person who gives the thing. They are the person who gets it.

You can also give things to people. If you give money to a family in need, you help them. You are helping them by giving them money.

Sometimes, you give your own body things you need. You might give a person air to breathe. You give them the air they need to live. You are giving them life.

You can also give things to things. If you give a chair to a child, the child now has a chair. The chair will stay in the child until they want to get rider of it.

When you give something, you are making it happen. You are the one who makes it happen.
