A big house for many people is called a house. If there are too many people in one house, it can be very hard to get to the bathroom or to the kitchen. In this kind of house, the people who live there have many rooms. One room is for sleeping. One room is for eating. One room is for the kids. Some rooms are for the parents. There is also a room for the doctor. In this kind of house, there are many people working together. The people who work there are like a team. They all help each other and keep the house clean.

If many people are moving from one city to another, they might go to a big house. This is called a house. The big house is very strong and has many floors. On the floors, there are many rooms. There is a room for each person. In this big house, all the people live together. They share food and things. They work together too. This is why the big house is often called a house for many people.
