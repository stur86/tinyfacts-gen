To make something from wood is to change the hard, strong stuff inside the wood into a new kind of thing.

Imagine you are in a room with many small pieces of wood. Each piece is strong and can hold a lot of things. If you take one of these pieces and you cut it into small pieces, each small piece becomes a new thing. You can use the small pieces to build a table, a chair, or many other things.

To start, you take a piece of wood. You look at it and see if it is hard and strong. If it feels hard and does not break when you press on it, it is a good piece of wood. If it feels soft or breaks easily, you can try to change it.

You can change a piece of wood in many ways. You can cut it into long lines. You can cut it into small pieces. You can round the ends of the pieces so they fit together well. You can make the sides of the piece strong so it does not break. You can make the top of the piece soft so it can be held in your hand.

Wood is a good thing to use for many things because it is strong and can change easily. It can be made into many different kinds of things. Some are very hard and can hold many heavy things. Some are soft and can hold a small thing. Some are strong and can hold many heavy things. Some are very light and easy to carry.

Wood is also a good thing to use because it can grow. Trees grow from tiny pieces of wood. As the tree grows, it takes in more wood and makes more trees. This is how many of the trees and things you see today were made from wood many years ago.

So, to make something from wood is to take the hard stuff inside the wood and turn it into a new kind of thing. This is how wood is made and how many things are built from it.
