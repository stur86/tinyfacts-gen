To carry something or someone means you move them from one place to another. This is a very important job.

Imagine you have a lot of books you want to get to a school. You can not carry them all in your arms. You ask a friend to help. The friend carries the books for you.

When you say you are going to a party, you are also going to carry your friends. You might carry a bag with food and drinks. You are carrying them while you walk to the party hall.

Sometimes you carry money or food for people who are going far away. You drive a car, you walk, or you ride a bus. You are carrying them to a far place.

In a city, you can carry many things. You can carry a bag for your clothes, a box for your books, or a small box with food. You carry them until you reach your home.

People also carry animals. You might take a dog to a dog park. You carry the dog so it can run and play.

To carry something means you move it from one place to another. It is a way to move people, things, or animals from one place to another place.
