To be approached means you are talked about. When you are a good person, people might say you are an amazing person. They do this because you are very kind and you help others.

You can also be approached if you are a thing. A person might walk up to a table and say, I saw this. It is a nice thing. They are talking about it because they think it is very pretty or very fun.

Sometimes, if you do something nice for someone, they say you are a good person. You are doing things that make them happy. This is a good way to feel happy inside.

If you are not approached, it means you are not talked about. You might feel sad or alone. But if someone says you are a good person, you can feel happy. You know you are doing the right thing.
