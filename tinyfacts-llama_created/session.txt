To sit is a way to stay in a place. You can sit on a chair, on a bed, or even on a hard floor. Sometimes you sit to eat food. Other times you sit while you watch a movie.

When you sit, you are still. You do not move. You do not go around. You are in one place, and you are in one time. This is important because it helps us have quiet. We can sit and listen to our friends, to music, or to our own voices.

Sometimes you sit with others. You can sit with a friend, or you can sit with people you do not know. When you sit with others, you learn from them. You might hear what they say, or you might learn new words. You might also learn to be kind and to share your own ideas.

You can also sit for long times. You might sit for hours or even days. During this time, you do not move. You think about the things you want to do later. You might read a book, or you might watch a movie. You might write in a book, or you might draw pictures.
