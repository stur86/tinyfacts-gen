A time thing is a thing that helps you know how many hours or minutes you are left. It is usually a round thing with numbers and lines that go around it. You put a small round thing with numbers on it into the round thing and it tells you what time it is.

Some of these things have a short number. This number tells you how many minutes it is. If you see the number  hours and minutes, you can add the number of minutes to the hours to find out what time it is. If you see a short number, you can look at it and see how many minutes it is.

Some of these things have a line with two numbers. This line tells you if it is morning, afternoon, or night. In the morning, the line is up. In the afternoon, the line is down. If the line is in the middle, it is time for you to go to bed.

You also use these things to keep track of days. When you see the same short number many times, you know that many days have passed. You can use the short number to keep track of the days and weeks.

People also use these things to keep track of how many hours they have left before a big eventually happens. A person might use one of these things to know when it is time to go to bed to get ready for work or school the next day.

You can find these things in many places. You might see one in a house, a school, or a store. Some of them are in the kitchen. Some of them are on a wall. Some of them are on a table. Some of them are on a desk.
