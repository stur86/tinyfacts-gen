A thing you can do because someone says you can, or a way you are allowed to act.

You might get a thing you want. If a teacher says you can sit in a new chair, you can. You are allowed to do it. You did not ask for it, but the teacher said so.

You can also have a thing that is not allowed. If you are not in the right place, you might get a stop sign. The stop sign is there to keep people safe. You should not be there.

Sometimes you get to do something you were not sure about. You might try to fly a thing that goes very fast. If you are not ready, a good person will tell you it is not safe.

Sometimes you get to do a thing for a friend. If a friend wants to go to a place that is very far away, you can go with them. You are helping them.

You can also get a thing that makes you feel happy or bad. If you are afraid, you might be very quiet. If you are very happy, you might laugh a lot.
