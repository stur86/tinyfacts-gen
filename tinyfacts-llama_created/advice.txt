To give or to get something is to give a person a bit of what they need. You can get a piece of paper to write on, a book to read, or a word from a friend. This word is often used by people who want to help others.

If you give someone a piece of your mind, you are telling them what to do. It is like a long way of telling them how to act. You might say, you need to be quiet, or you need to be happy.

You can also get a bit of what you need from someone. If you are feeling sad and a friend says, you are doing okay, you feel a bit better. This is a way of telling you that you are not alone and that someone cares about you.

Sometimes, people give each other things they do not have. If a teacher gives you a new book to read, you get to read it and learn from it. This is a way of helping you grow as a person.
