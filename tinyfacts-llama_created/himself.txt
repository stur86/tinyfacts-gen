To be yourself means to not try to be someone else. This is a very important thing.

Think about a time when you were a child. You saw your family, your friends, and the world around you. You knew who you were and where you came from. You did not try to be like your mother, or your brother, or your father. You were happy with who you were.

Now, when you are an old person, you might feel like you are not sure who you are anymore. Maybe you have children, or friends, or a job that you love. But sometimes, you still feel like a little child. You still feel like you do not fit in with the world. You feel like you are not sure who you are.

This is when you say to yourself, I am myself. I am not trying to be someone else. I am happy with who I am. I am the person I am today, and I am okay with that.

This is also true when you are a person who is afraid to do what you want to do. Maybe you are afraid to leave your house and go to a new city. Or maybe you are afraid to try something new. When you say to yourself, I am myself, you are telling yourself that you are not afraid anymore. You are telling yourself that you are ready to be the person you want to be.

Being yourself is important because it makes you feel safe. When you are sure who you are, you do not have to worry about what other people think. You can be your true self, without trying to be someone else.
