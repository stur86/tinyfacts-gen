More is a word that we use to talk about how many of something we have. When we say a thing is more, we are talking about how much of it is there.

Imagine you have a bag of sweet things. You start with one bag. If you find another bag inside your house, you now have two bags. If you find more bags than the two you already have, you have more bags. You have more of the sweet things.

More can also mean another thing you have. If you have a book and your friend gives you another book, you now have two books. This is more than you had before. You have more books.

Sometimes, you might want to say you are more than someone else. If you are a tall person and your friend is short, you are more. This means your body is bigger or longer than what they have.

You can also talk about how much money you have. If you get more money, you have more. This means you now have more money in your pocket.

When you talk about how many people are in a room, more means there are more. If you have a group of people and there are ten people, then there are more than ten.

More is an important word because it helps us understand how many things we have or how many people are there.
