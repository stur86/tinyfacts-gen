To show something means to put it in a place where everyone can see it. Imagine you have a book with a picture on the cover. You can not show it to the whole world right away. But if you put the book on a table in your room, everyone who walks by will see it. This is what we do with things.

You can show a movie to many people. You can also show a picture of a person. When you do this, everyone sees the picture. It is a way to share how you feel or what happened.

Sometimes you show things to help people understand. If a person is feeling sad, you might show them a picture of something that is happy. This helps the person feel better.

You can also show something to a group of people. When you have many friends or family people, you might have a party. You can show pictures, music, or games. This is how you show love to the people you care about.

In some places, a city might have a big show at night. People walk around in the dark to see the music and lights. This is a way to show the city and its people to everyone.
