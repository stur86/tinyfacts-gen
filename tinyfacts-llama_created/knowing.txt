Knowing is a big word that means you are not just a person, you are also a thing. You are not a thing, but something that helps you know.

Imagine you are a child. You see a dog. You do not know what that dog is. But if you look at the dog again, and you look at the way it looks, and you look at the way it walks, and you look at the way it acts, you start to know it. You know what the dog is. You know what it does.

You know things because you see them, you hear them, you feel them. You learn from what other people tell you. You learn from what you find in the world. You learn from your family. You learn from your friends. You learn from your own body. You are not just a person, you are a thing that is always changing.

Knowing is about being sure. You are sure about how you feel, how you think, and how you act. You are sure that the world is this way. You are sure that the sun will come up tomorrow. You are sure that your friend will smile when you talk to them.

Knowing can be hard. You might see something that hurts you. You might see something that makes you afraid. You might see something that makes you sad. But knowing is what helps you get through those hard times. Knowing is what helps you keep going.

Knowing is also about being open. You can know a lot of things without ever really thinking about them. You can know a lot about yourself without ever really feeling it. You can know a lot about the world without ever really looking at it.
