To go different ways

When you move in a line, you move in one way. You go straight. You do not turn. You are always moving in the same direction.

Think of the numbers  a and band you can move a little bit to the right. If you go  a, you have moved a little bit. If you go band, you have moved a little bit more. The numbers are different, so you are going in different ways.

In real life, when you go to a store, you might go in one way. You walk down the street. You go to the store. You come out. You might have gone a little bit. You might have gone a little bit more. You are going in different ways.

To go different ways can also mean to be different. You are not the same as someone else. You have your own ways. You have your own stories. You have your own voices.

You can also go different ways to a place. If you go to the city, you go to the city. You might go by car. You might go by bus. You might go by train. You are going in different ways.

So, to go different ways means you move in a different way. You move in a different direction. You move in a different order. You move in a different way from others.
