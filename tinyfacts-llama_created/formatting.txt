To change the way something looks is called changing its form. This is often done in writing, drawing, and making things that are used by people. You might change the words in a story to make it more interesting. You might also change the pictures so they look different on a computer.

Imagine you have a piece of paper with a long word on it. You want to make it shorter, so you can write it down more easily. You can use different colors to make the words stand out. You can also cut the paper into smaller pieces, so you can write in many places at once.

When you change how something looks, you are changing its form. You are changing how it looks so people can understand it better.
