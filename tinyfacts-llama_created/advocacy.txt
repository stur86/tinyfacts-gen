To speak up for something is to act in the name of a person or group. It means to help them. Many people do this because they care about the well being of others. They might speak at a meeting. They might write a letter. They might tell their friends and family about what is happening.

You can also speak up for a thing that you do not like. You might go against what others think. This is important because it shows that you are not afraid to be different.

Sometimes people work with others. They all work together to find a good answer. They listen to each other. They try to see things from each other. This is how they build things that work well for everyone.

If you are an animal, you can also speak up. You might use your voice to say that you need to be free. You might say, I am tired. I want to go home. This helps people understand that you are a living being too.

Being an animal can be hard. You have to fight to stay safe. But when you speak up, you are helping others see that you are not alone.
