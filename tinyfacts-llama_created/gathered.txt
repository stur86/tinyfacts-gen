When you say someone has been looked at, they are being looked at. It is like looking in a big room. You can see many people at once. There is a feeling of being watched over.

If you are doing a job, you are doing it. You are trying to make things right. You might feel a bit nervous. Then, you are done. You can walk away and go do something else.

If you are talking to someone, you are sharing your ideas. You are trying to make them see what you think. You are being open.

If you have a bag, it is something you carry. You put things in it. You might carry money, books, or clothes. You can go to a new place or go back home.

If you have a feeling, it is a strong idea in your heart. It makes you want to do something. You might feel happy, sad, angry, or afraid. Your heart is like a fire inside you.

To have something is to have it in your body. You are holding a small thing. You are keeping it safe for a long time. You do not let anyone take it from you.

When you have a plan, you are thinking about what to do. You are making a way for what is going to happen. You are building a story. You are making sure everything is okay.
