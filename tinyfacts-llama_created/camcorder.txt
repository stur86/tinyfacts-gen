A came order is a thing that makes pictures on a small box. It has a moving part that looks at what you want to take. You point it at the thing you want to see, and it takes a picture. The pictures are then put into a long thing with many small pieces so they do not get all over your hands.

The person who makes the pictures is called a came order. They are very good at this job. They can take many pictures in one go. You do not have to walk around and watch them. You are in the middle of what they are doing.

You can use the pictures later. You can send them to other people who want to see what you saw. You can also watch them later when you are in another place. Some people like to use this thing to go to far away places and see what happens.
