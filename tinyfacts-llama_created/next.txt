To next something means you are going to do it after the time you are in now. It is like taking a step. You are not at the top yet, but you are moving forward. You are not done yet, but you are getting ready for the next thing.

Imagine you are waiting for your friend. You are sitting in the same place, but you are getting ready to go. You are waiting for the moment when you can go and play with them. That is what next means. You are waiting for something to happen after you are in the middle of it.
