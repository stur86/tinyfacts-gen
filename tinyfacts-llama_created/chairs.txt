A chair is a thing you sit on. It is a piece of wood or other stuff that you put your back against. People use them for many reasons. Some sit on them to rest when they are tired. Others sit on them to eat food. Some people even sit on them to read books.

You can find chairs in many places. You can see them in a room where people talk. You can see them in a room that has a table and a desk. You can even see them in a room with a floor that is hard so you do not hurt your legs.

Some chairs are small. They are for one person. Other chairs are big. They are for many people. They have many legs and a top. Some chairs have a back and a seat. Other chairs have only a seat and a back. They sit on the floor.

You can also make chairs by your hands. Some people make them from wood. Others make them from other stuff. They cut and put parts together. They make sure the chair is strong and safe.
