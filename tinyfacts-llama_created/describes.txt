To say something is a way of talking can be a big thing. It helps us know what is happening. If you tell me you are going to the store, I can know you are going. If you tell me you love dogs, I can know you love animals.

When you say something is a way of doing, you are talking about how things work. You are telling me what happens. You can say something is a way of eating food. If you say you eat a big breakfast, you are telling me how to think about that. You are using a word that helps me understand.

You can also say something is a way of feeling. If you say you feel happy, you are telling me how you are. If you say you feel sad, you are telling me that too. This is important because it helps us understand each other.

Sometimes, to say something is a way of doing something is the same as saying something is a way of being. If you say you are a happy person, you are also saying you are a person who feels happy. It is the same thing.
