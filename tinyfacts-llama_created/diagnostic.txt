To do a job for someone is to help them find out what is wrong with their body, brain, or head. This is important because it keeps them safe and helps them feel better.

Imagine you are a doctor. You see many people in your office every day. You look at their faces, their clothes, and their bodies. You ask them questions about how they feel. You take their blood to a far away place to see if their bodies are working very well. You look at the pictures of their eyes. You look at their brains, too. You are trying to find what is wrong with them so you can help them.

The word comes from doing a job for a dog. A dog has a body that works very well. But sometimes the dog feels sick. A person with this job is trying to figure out what is wrong with the dog. They look at the dog. They look at the dog. They try to see what is wrong. They look at the dog so the person can help the dog feel better.

A doctor also does this job for people. They look at the body. They look at the brain. They look at the heart. They look at the eyes, the ears, and the skin. They try to find what is wrong. They try to find what makes the body sick. They try to find what makes the body hurt. This helps people feel better.

Sometimes the job is hard. The person has to do it very fast. They have to look at many bodies at once. They have to ask many questions. They have to look at many pictures. They have to use their brain very much.

But the job is important. It helps keep people safe. It helps them live their lives. It helps them feel okay.
