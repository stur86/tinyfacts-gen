To write means you use your hands to put words on a piece of paper. You start with a thought. You write it down so everyone can see it.

You might write a story. You tell people what happens. You might write a letter to a friend. You write to them so they know you care. You might write a letter to a teacher. You write to them because they are helping you learn.

You also write to remember things. You write down your name, your age, your school days. You write down numbers, words, and pictures. This helps you remember what happened.

Writing is a way to share your ideas with others. It is a way to say, I am here. I am thinking. I am trying to tell you something.

Sometimes, you write things you do not want to say. You write a bad thing to a friend. You write a word that makes people angry. Writing these words can be hard. But it is also a way to learn. It is a way to understand why people act this way.

Writing is a fun thing to do. You can write a story in your head, and then you write it down. You can write a letter, and then you send it. You can write a song, and then you play it for others.
