To be a person who is not like everyone else is to be a person of high order. They are not afraid of the hard things in life. They do not want to be the one who is sad or angry all the time. They try to do good for others. They are kind and they help those who are in trouble. They do not take things that are not their own. They are carefully what they say and do. They are not afraid to stand up for what is right. They are strong and they do not give up.

Being a person like this is a good thing. It means you are a good friend, a good brother or sister, and a good person in the world. It means you are the kind of person you want to be.
