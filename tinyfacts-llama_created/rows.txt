To make a long line of things, you put them one after another. If you put a line of books on a table, the books are one line. If you put a line of clothes on a table, the clothes are another line. But if you put a line of books next to another line of clothes, they become a different line.

Imagine you are going to a big store. You walk through many doors. Each door is a line. The doors are one line. You walk in one line, and then you walk in another line, and another line. When you get to the store, you are in a different line than when you started. This is how we can make many lines from one thing.

We also use this word when we talk about how things look. If you look at a picture that has many lines, you can see how the lines go together. If one line of the picture is not straight, it can look strange. But if the lines are all straight, the picture is clear.
