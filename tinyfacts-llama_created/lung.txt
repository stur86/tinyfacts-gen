Your body has a big part in your chest. This part is like a bag inside your chest. It helps you breathe in the air you need to live. The part is very important because it takes in air from the ground, and it gives out air that goes out of your body.

The part is made of many tiny rooms that join together. Inside each room, there are many tiny air spaces. When you breathe in air, the air spaces fill with air. When you breathe out, the air spaces empty and the air goes out of your body.

The part has a wall around it. This wall keeps the air inside your body. It also has a cover on top so you can see what you are doing. Inside the cover, there are tiny lines that carry air to your other parts. Your brain tells your heart to beat so the air keeps coming into your body.

Your body also has two of these parts. One is in your chest and the other is under your arm. When one part is heavy, you might feel tired. The heavy part is where the air is moving around. When your body is very full of air, the heavy part feels heavy. This is why you might feel tired when you are very full of air.

You can not see the inside of your body. You can only see the part that is outside. But the part inside is very important because it keeps you going every day.
