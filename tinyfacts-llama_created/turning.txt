To turn is to change direction. When you walk, you are moving one way. You turn and you move in another direction. This makes things happen in different ways.

Imagine you are on a road. You are going toward a town. But then you see a green light. You turn left. Now you are going toward the green light. You are not going in the same direction as before. You are moving in a new way.

You can turn while walking, while standing, or even while sitting in a car. When you turn your head, you are turning your body. This is important because it helps you see what is happening around you.

You can also turn things around. If you are sitting on a chair and you push the chair away, you are turning it around. This makes the chair go the other way.

Turning is important in many ways. It helps us move around. It helps us find our way when we are lost. It helps us change from one place to another. It is a very good thing to do.
