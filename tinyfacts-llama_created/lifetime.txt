A life is the time that a person has lived. It starts when a person is very young and ends when they are very old.

You are living right now. You are still here. This is your life. You will live for many years. Some people live more than  hundred years. One person lived for one hundred years and a half. Another person lived for one hundred years and more. They all lived the same number of years, but each one lived in their own way.

A life is made of many years, many days, many hours, many minutes, and many seconds. It has many big moments like when you were a small baby or when you were very old. It also has many small moments like when you laughed or cried.

Each person has their own life. Some people have happy lives with many good friends and many good times. Some people have sad lives with many hard times and many hard days. Some people have long lives with many years of work and many years of family. Some people have short lives with many years of fun and many years of rest.

When you look back on your life, you can see how you grew. You saw things you never saw before. You learned from your friends and your family. You found out what you wanted to do with your life. You made your own way. This is what we mean by a life.

A person who is still young can still be very happy. They have many years of fun ahead of them. A person who is old can still feel young. They still laugh and still smile. Their lives are not over yet. They have many more days left.

You are living. You are here. You are a person with your own story.
