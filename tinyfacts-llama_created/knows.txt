To know something means you understand it. You learn about it. You get to see it. You can understand things that are not there.

Imagine you see a picture. You do not know how it was made. You do not know where it is or who made it. But then you look at it again. You see a different picture. You understand what the picture is. You know what it is.

You also know things that are always. You know the sun is up in the morning. You know the sun will go down at night. You know the day and the night are always there.

You can also know things that are not there. You can hear a sound that is not there. You can see a thing that is not there. You can know a word that is not there.

When you know something, you understand it. You do not just see it. You feel it. You feel what it is like. You feel how it makes you feel.

You can know many things in your life. You can know how to write a word. You can know how to do a thing. You can know how to stay safe. You can know many things that help you live well.
