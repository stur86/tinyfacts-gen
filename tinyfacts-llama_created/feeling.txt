To feel something is to have it inside you. It is when you are not just thinking about it, but you are really inside, in your body. You might feel happy when you see a good movie. You might feel sad when you hear that a friend is not doing well.

You can also feel things that are not there. Imagine you see a bright light. You might feel like you are in a big room with many people. You might feel like you are falling down. These are all your body telling you something is happening.

Sometimes you feel things that you did not ask for. You might feel angry when someone takes your thing. You might feel scared when you hear a loud noise. You might feel calm when you are in a quiet place.

You feel things that are not always easy to understand. You might feel a big rush when you run fast. You might feel a small smile when you see a person you like. You might feel a heavy feeling in your chest when you are sad.

To feel something is important. It helps you know what to do. If you feel fear, you should not act. You should stay still. If you feel happy, you should enjoy it. If you feel sad, you should talk to a friend.
