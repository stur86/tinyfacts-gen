To put something behind something else means to move it to the back. When you put a baby in a car, you move the car to the back. You are going to a new place.

Imagine a long line of animals. A big one is the front of the line. The other animals are behind it. When you move to a new place, you move to the back of the line. This is what happens when you put something behind something else.

You can also put something behind you. When you walk into a room, you are putting the room behind you. This helps you find your way back later. It is like putting a line on your back so you know where you were.

There are also two other ways to use the word. You can put something behind you if you are afraid. If you see a dog, you might run behind your back. This is a way to hide so you do not get hurt.

You can also put something behind someone. If a friend has a problem, you might help them. You might give them a word of hope or a drink to make them feel better. This is a way of showing you care.

So, putting something behind something else is about moving to a new place or hiding from a problem.
