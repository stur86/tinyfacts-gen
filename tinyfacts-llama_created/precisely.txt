When you say something is exactly, you mean it is very close to the way it is supposed to be. Think of numbers. If you have two numbers and you add them, you get a third number. If you add two numbers and you get three, then you know the two numbers were the same.

To say something is exactly is like saying it is the same as something else. If you say a book is the same as another book, you mean they are both books with the same words and the same pictures. You are not just saying they are the same because they look the same. You are saying they are the same because they are the same inside.

If you try to say something is exactly, but you are wrong, the word does not help. If you say a cat is the same as a dog, you have not said it is exactly. You have just said you think they are the same.

It is also important to know if you are saying something is exactly or if you are just trying to make it sound better. If you are being very carefully, you are saying it is exactly. If you are being a bit more, you are saying it is not exactly.

In every case, you are looking at what is real. What is real is the way things are. If you try to change the way things are, you are not being exactly.
