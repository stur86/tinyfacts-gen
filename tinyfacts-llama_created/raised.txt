To raise something is to make it higher. Imagine you are a parents. You want your child to be strong for the game they are going to play. You can give them a small rock to stand on, or you can pick them up and walk them up a few steps. This helps them feel more safe and more ready to do what they are doing.

You can also raise a person. If someone has a bad life, they might feel sad and angry. They might want to be better. You can help them by talking to them, giving them a job, or helping them find a new school. This makes the person feel more safe and gives them a chance to become a good person.

When you raise a thing, you are giving it more power. This is important in games and in our lives. When you feel more power, you can do more and be more sure.
