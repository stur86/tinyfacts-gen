To be a number that does not change is to be a number that is always the same. It is not like a number that goes up and down like a line on a paper. A number that is not changing is not going to change any more. It is always the same.

Think of time. You are always in the same place, in the same time, no matter what day it is. Your age is the same every year. Your body is always the same, no matter how old you get. This is what it means to be a number that is not changing.
