To share something means to give it to many people. When you share your food, you give it to your friends. You do not keep it all for yourself. You are happy because you helped others.

A business can share money. If many people work together, they can use the money to build a new building. This is sharing because they are using something that many people have.

Also, when you buy a car, you buy it for yourself. But if many people want the same car, the owner can share it with them. They give the car to a few people instead of keeping it for all.

Sometimes, a person gives money to a group. This can help people who are in trouble. It is like sharing a bit of your money so others can help too.
