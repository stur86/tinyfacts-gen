To be a good team player is to want to help your group and make them strong. A person who wants to help can be called a good team player. This person usually does things that help the group. They might bring food or clothes to keep everyone warm. They might also help decide what game to play.

Another kind of team player is one who helps others. They do not want to be alone. They want to make sure that everyone is safe and happy. They might help a friend who is afraid. They might also help a person who is sick or hurt.
