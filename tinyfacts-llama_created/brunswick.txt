To brown something means to change how it looks. A piece of wood that is very white and bright can become brown after the sun is on it for many days. This happens because the wood gets old and the green stuff inside it starts to turn into a brown color.

You can also brown your skin. When you are very hot, your skin can turn brown. This happens because your skin is making a lot of a green stuff that we call skin cells. When the green stuff gets old and starts to break down, your skin turns brown.

If you put a white thing in a hot fire, it will brown and turn into a piece of wood. This is why old clothes can look like they are brown. They have been sitting in a hot room for a long time.

Sometimes, when a animal is very old and not strong anymore, it can become brown. This can happen because the animal has not eaten for a long time, or because the inside of its body is full of old, hard stuff. The brown color can be a sign that the animal is very old.
