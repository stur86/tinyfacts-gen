To make something like another person is to make a very small one that looks like them. This is done when an animal has babies and the babies are not the same as the other babies.

Imagine you have a dog. One day a man makes a dog from this dog. He takes the dog and makes a small one that looks like it. This small one is called a baby dog. The baby dog is almost the same as the real dog. It has the same eyes and hair. It can even walk and run like the dog.

When you do this many times, you have many babies. Each one is like the other ones, but they are not the same as the one before them. They are all made from the same one small one.

This makes it easier to help people who are sick. If you have a bad heart, you can make a small one that is good. This small one can live for many years. It will grow and become a person. It will learn and think like a person too.

Sometimes this is done for animals too. If an animal is very old, a doctor might make a baby from it. This baby will grow and become a strong animal. It will be able to live for many years and help the old animal.

But sometimes people do this for fun. They make a baby that looks like them. It is like having a new friend. They can play with it and it is almost like having a real child.
