To buy air is to pay for a way to fly. You pay a money box to let a big flying car go from one place to another place. You do not use your hands. The car goes very fast through the air and comes to your place. You need to pay before you start. Sometimes you pay for the whole trip, and sometimes you pay for each hour.

When you buy air, there are a few ways to pay. You can pay with money. Or you can pay with a card. If you pay with money, you pay for each hour of flying. For every hour, you pay a little bit of money. If you fly a lot, the money adds up. You do not pay for every hour. You only pay for the hours that are used.

Sometimes, you buy air for a group of people. If many people buy air together, the money goes to all of them. This is a good thing because it is often less money than if you bought air for one person one by one. If you buy air for a group, the money goes to everyone.

Air is very important. We need it to breathe. Without air, we could not live. We also use air to power things that move around us. Cars, computers, and phones all need air to work.

There are also different kinds of air. There is the air that you breathe. The air you breathe is very good for you. It gives you the power to live. There is also the air that goes up from the ground into the sky. This air goes up and comes back down a few times. When the air goes up, it is cleaner than the air that comes down. This is why it is good to fly. You do not breathe the clean air you fly through.

Air is also very cold. When the air is very cold, it can make your hands and feet feel very hard to touch. When you fly in very cold air, you need to wear a warm jacket so you do not get hurt.
