To build something is a very important thing. You start with a plan. You think about what you want to do. You think about how to do it. Then you do it. You make it real. You show it to people.

When you build a house, you build the walls first. You cut the wood into the right pieces. You put the wood together. Then you put a floor on top. You add windows and doors. You make it look nice. When you are done, the house is real. It is not just a plan.

When you build a computer, you do the same thing. You write a plan. You work with people who can help you. You build the parts of the computer. You add the brain. You make it work. You show it to others. When you are done, the computer is real.

Sometimes, you do not have a plan. You start with a idea. You try to build it. You make it happen. You learn as you go. You change what you need. You make it better. You do this with people, with money, with time.

People build all kinds of things. They build houses for people. They build roads so cars can go. They build schools for children. They build places for animals so they can live in them. They build things that help us breathe.

To build something is to make it real. It is to show what you want to be. It is to make it happen. It is a way to start a new thing.
