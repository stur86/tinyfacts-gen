Human is a word that means people and animals. It is something that is in your world. You are a human, and so are all the people you know, the people you have met, and the people you will meet.

A long time ago, humans were the only animals. They lived in one place and made food from the land. They made a bed on the ground, built houses, and made things to keep them warm. They also made things to kill the animals they needed to eat. Over time, more and more people joined together to form groups. These groups became towns and then cities. People started to move from one place to another in big groups. This changed what life was like for everyone.

Today, humans are all over the world. We are in cities, in small houses, and in cars. We use many things we did not have before. We have computers, phones, and cars that can move fast. We also have things we do not need anymore, like food that can stay warm for many hours.

We are all different. Some are small and some are big. Some have hair that grows long and some have hair that grows short. Some have blue eyes and some have brown eyes. Some are tall and some are short. Some have hair on their faces and some do not. Some have arms and legs, and some do not. Some can fly, and some can walk very fast. Some can do many things at once, and some can only do one thing.

We also have things we do not understand. We have things that make us feel happy, things that make us feel sad, and things that make us feel angry. We have things that make us want to do things, and things that make us want to be alone.

Because of all these things, we are the ones who build our world. We build roads, schools, and places to live. We build cities and towns that can hold many people. We build houses so that people can stay in them while they go to work. We build things that help us move from one place to another.

We are also the ones who try to learn from each other. When people from different places meet, they ask questions. They try to find out what the other people need. They try to understand how the other people live. This helps us to become better people.
