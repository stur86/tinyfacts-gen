To reply means you give a word or a story back to someone. If you hear a voice, you might want to answer. If you see a friend, you will often talk to them.

You can reply to a question. If someone asks you a question, you might give a short answer. This helps people understand what you mean.

You can also reply when you are angry. If someone makes you mad, you might shout or say, so others know you are angry. This shows you are not quiet.

In a group, people often reply to each other. When you say a word, the person next to you might say something back. This helps keep everyone talking and makes the conversation go on.

In a game, you might reply to your friend. If you hit a ball, you might say, hit! If you run, you might say, go! This lets everyone know what you did.

You can reply to a joke. If someone makes a funny joke, you might laugh and say, that is nice! You are showing you enjoyed the joke.

To reply to someone is also to listen. If a friend is talking, you might listen and remember what they said. You are paying attention so you can be ready for your own words next time.
