To be a person is a big job. You have to follow the right ways and show that you care about what you do. If you lie, you are not a person. You are a person who does not want to follow the right ways. You want to have power and get what you want.

Being a person is also about being kind. You should help others when they need it. You should not be mean or hurt them. If you do this, people will trust you and want to be your friend.

Sometimes, people need to tell the truth. If you say what you think, even if it is bad, people will listen. This is important because it keeps the world in order.

You are a person if you act in a way that is good for everyone. If you always try to do what is right, you are a person. This is what makes a person feel safe and happy.
