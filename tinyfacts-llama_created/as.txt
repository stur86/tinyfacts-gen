The word as is used to talk about when something happens.

When you are in a room, you can see the people around you. You can also see the things you see on the wall. This is the past. You are in this room now.

If you go to a place far away, you will see many new things. You might see green trees or bright blue water. This is the past too.

Think about time. You can tell how many days have passed since you started. You can remember what you did each day. You can also look back on what happened before that day.

We use as when we talk about this. We say, if you are happy, you are as happy as a child. This means you are as happy as a very young person. It does not mean you are actually happy.

We also use as when we talk about things we have. If you have a book, you can say you are as full as your book. This means you have not added any more. You have read all the words.

In a story, the person might say, I am as tired as a dog. This means they are very tired. They have been running a lot.

So, the word as is a way to talk about when things happen. It helps us remember the past and understand how things are now.
