To sleep is to rest. You close your eyes and your body feels very good. Your head drops and your body relaxes. You might feel happy or tired. It is a way to let your mind and body go. It happens in many places, like your room, your bed, or a quiet place in your house.

You sleep because your body needs rest. Your body is always moving and working. When you are sleeping, your body is not working. Your body is not thinking about things. It is just resting.

Sometimes, you might sleep a lot. You might sleep for many hours in one night. Or, you might sleep a little at a time. It is a way to be safe and to feel good.

You can also sleep in a dark place. If you are afraid, a dark room can feel safe. You might be afraid of a small animal or a loud sound. Your eyes are closed and you feel calm.

Some people like to sleep in a quiet place. They might sit in a chair with their eyes closed. They feel the air moving around them. They might feel their heart beating slow.

Your body also needs to sleep. Your body grows and gets bigger when you sleep. You grow more hair, you get stronger, and you feel better. Your body is like a house that needs to rest and fix before it can do anything else.

So, sleeping is a way to be safe and to feel good. It helps your body and mind feel better.
