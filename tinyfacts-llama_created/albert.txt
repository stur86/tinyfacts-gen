An animal is an animal. Animals are very important. They eat, they move, they make sound, they have hair, and they feel pain.

Every animal is different. Some animals are very small. Some animals are very big. Some animals have many legs. Some animals have only two legs. Some animals have four legs. Some animals have no legs at all.

Some animals are good for us. You need to eat them to stay strong. You eat things from the ground and the water. You also eat things from the sky. These are called food animals. You must eat them to live.

Other animals are very bad for us. Some animals bite. They use their teeth to hurt. Some animals fly. They go around in the sky and catch tiny living things to eat.

Animals live in many different places. Some live in the ground. Some live in the water. Some live in the air. Some live on land with the ground around them. Some live in the dark under the ground.

Some animals are very good at hiding. They can become very small and hide in a small box. Other animals can run very fast. They can run so fast that the wind goes through them.

Animals are very important for our world. They help us grow food. They give us clean water. They help keep the air clean. They are all around us.
