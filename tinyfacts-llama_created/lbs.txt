To lift something

When you want to move heavy things, you use your arms and your legs. If you are very strong, you can lift a heavy box or a book. You pull the box up so it is not on the floor anymore. Your arms and legs work together to make the box go up.

Lifting can also mean to feel a strong push. If you are feeling this kind of push, you might be afraid or excited. You might want to run away or fight. Your heart is beating fast because you feel the push.

Sometimes, you lift things that are very heavy. You do this so the heavy things do not fall down. You use your arms and your legs to push them up and around. You might use a chair to lift a table.

You also lift things that are not heavy. You might lift a ball when you play a game with your friends. You use your arms to lift the ball and move it from one place to another. Your arms and legs work together to make the ball go up into the air.
