Wear is a word that means you have something on you. You might have a shirt that is old. This old shirt might have lines on it. The lines tell you how many years the shirt has been around. You might also have a book that has many words. One day, you want to read the book. You look for it on a computer. You type the word and see all the words that are there. You can read the book from home. This way, you do not have to move your body to go to a place where the book is kept.
