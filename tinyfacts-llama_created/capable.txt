To be able means you can do something. You are strong, or you are not afraid. You can do a job that is hard. You can run far or you can ride a car. You can build a house or you can pick a lot of nice things.

You are also able if you can do something that is not easy. Maybe you are not very good at a game. You try many times and you get better. You are able when you can do things you want to do.

Being able is important. If you are able, you can do things that make you happy. You can go to the movies or play with friends. You can buy a new game. This means you are ready to do what you want.
