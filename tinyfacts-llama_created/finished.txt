To finished something means you have done it all. You have put the last piece into a box, or you have closed a door on a room. You have made sure everything is in its right place.

Think of a book you read. When you are done with the book, you feel a big feeling. You are happy that you know all the stories. You can stop reading now.

You can also finish something you are making. If you build a house, you have finished the big part. You can look at the house and say, it is done. You did not have to start again the next day.

If you finish a game, you have won't have to play it again. You can stop watching the movie or playing the game.

Sometimes, you might finish something that is not for you. Maybe you have a job you hate. You finish your work so you can leave and go home. You do not have to stay there any longer.

To finished something means you have done it and it is done.
