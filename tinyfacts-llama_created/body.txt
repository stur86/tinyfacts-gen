To build something means to make it from nothing. You start with a tiny piece of wood and you add many more pieces over and over until you have a strong thing that you can use. You do this when you build a house or a car.

You can also build your own body. This means you are growing your own body. You are not getting a body from a baby. Instead, you are making one from the things you eat and the air you breathe. Your body is made of many parts: the head, the arms, the legs, the chest, the stomach, and the back. Each part is important for you to live.

A body is also the part of you that holds your heart and your brain. It is the place where you keep your blood and your food. Inside your body you have many tiny living things that move around and make you keep going. You need to keep your body clean and your body warm so that you can stay safe.

You can also say a person has a strong body. This means they are big and they can run fast. If you are very strong, you can lift heavy things. If you are very strong, you can do many things in one go.
