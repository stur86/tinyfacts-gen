A thing that goes over a body is called a strong. This can happen to a person, a dog, or a car.

When a person walks, a strong goes over their legs, arms, and hands. It helps them move from one place to another. The person might not even notice it because their feet and legs are moving.

A dog has a strong too. It runs across the ground, over the houses, and over the fields. The dog uses its legs to go fast. It also helps it stay clean so it can sleep well at night.

A car has a strong too. It goes over the road. The strong is a long part of the car that helps it move. When a car wants to go fast, the driver puts on the car and the strong is pulled tight. This makes the car go very quickly.

Sometimes a strong can break. This happens when a person falls. The person might hit their head. The strong is like a piece of wood or a strong line that holds the body. If the strong breaks, the person might fall down and hurt themselves.
