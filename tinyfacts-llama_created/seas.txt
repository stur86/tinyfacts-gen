To go over something means to move past it many times. When you are a child, you are full of questions. One day, you ask a teacher, and you get a good answer. You feel happy and want to learn more. This is like going over the question many times in your mind.

Sees is to look at something for a long time. You might watch a movie or look at a book. When you look at the word, it makes you think about the world around you. If you see a big tree, you might see how it is in the room. You can also see the people who are walking. This word is used when we talk about people and things.

You can also see something that is far away. Imagine you are in a room with a friend. You are talking, and then you look out the window. The city far away looks like a big green area. If you keep looking, the green area becomes smaller and smaller until it is just a tiny point. This is what it means to see something that is far away.
