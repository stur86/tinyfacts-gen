A person who is a leader in a group of people is a leader. This person is often very good at making sure everyone is safe and happy. They tell people what to do and how to act.

Imagine a group of people who all want to go to a big party. The person who is in a better position to make things happen is the one who is the leader. They decide who gets to go and who stays home. This person also says what music to play and what food to eat. Without the leader, the group would not know what to do, and the party might not happen.

A person who is a leader does not always get to say everything they want. They must listen to the other people and hear what they have to say. This is because they have to make sure everyone is happy and safe. It is a big job!

In a place with many people, the person who is the leader is often a person who is very good at being calm. They can stay in one place for a very long time and still not get angry. They can also hear what everyone is saying without getting angry. This helps them make sure everyone is safe.

A person who is a leader can be a man or a woman. Some are older and have done many things. Others are younger and are still learning. Everyone is different, but the most important thing is that they are the ones who are able to stay calm and help everyone.
