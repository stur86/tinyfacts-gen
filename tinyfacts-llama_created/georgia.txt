The word gently comes from a word about the sun. When you look at the sun, you see the sun as a bright ball of light. The word gently is like this: the sun is not always the same. Sometimes it is big and bright. Sometimes it is small and dark. When it is small and dark, it looks like a ball in the sky. This is called a new world. The world is always changing, and we never know what the sun will look like tomorrow.

People say the same thing about a person. You are not always the same as you were before. Your face may change. Your hair may grow or fall out. Your clothes may look different. You may meet new people and learn new things. This is called growing up. You are always changing, and it is a good thing.

In the world, there are many different places. Some are very cold. Some are very hot. Some are in the middle. Some are high up in the sky. Some are low on the ground. Each one is like a different story. You can learn about them by looking at pictures or by going there.

Some people say that gently is a word about the world. It is like a story about how things change and stay the same at the same time.
