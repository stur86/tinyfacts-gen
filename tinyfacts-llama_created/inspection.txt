An eye is a thing that looks at things. It can see light and colors. It can look at a small thing or a big thing. You use it to see if things are right.

An eye is like a window. It lets light in and lets you see the world. You use it to see the sun, the sky, and the things around you. When you look at a thing, your eyes send a picture to your brain. Your brain then tells you what you see.

An eye also has a covering on it. This covering keeps the light out and lets you see in the dark. When you go to bed, your eyes rest. They are ready to look again when you wake up.

You can have an eye. A person can be an eye. A small animal can be an eye too. An eye is a very important thing. Without eyes, you could not see. You need eyes to live.
