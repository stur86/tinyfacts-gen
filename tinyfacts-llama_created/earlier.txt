Earlier is a word that tells you what time is before a time that has already happened.

Think of a line that goes from the very start of the world to now. If you walk along that line, you are moving forward. If you look back at where you started, you are looking at the past.

Today is earlier than tomorrow. If you look at the day you are in, you are looking at the day before tomorrow. Tomorrow is later than today.
