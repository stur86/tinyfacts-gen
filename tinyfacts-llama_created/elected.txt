To be a person who is often picked by a group to be in a job is an important way to show that the group trusts them. This happens when many people want the same person to be the one who does a job.

Imagine you are the person in a high school class. You do well on many papers. Your friends and some of the people in the school like you. They think you are one of the best. They want you to be the person who does the jobs so everyone does not have to do them all alone.

A person who is often picked by the group is called an office person. They get money to do their work. They must follow the orders of the group. Sometimes, the office person might not want to do what the group says, but they have to do it anyway because they are in a job.

In some places, you can get a job if you show that you are good at doing things. You can go to a job and say, I am ready to work. You do the work. If you do a good job, you might get a job for a long time. This is a way to say, the group picked me, so I am here now.
