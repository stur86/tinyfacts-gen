When you block something, you stop it from happening. This can happen in many ways.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You both want to jump over a high wall. If you block your friend, they can not jump over the wall. You have stopped the game. You did not let your friend play.

This happens in life too. If you block something, it stops happening. Maybe you block a door. If you do this, nothing can go out. You are keeping the thing inside. You are not letting it out.

You can also block the sun. When the sun is high in the sky, you block its light. This keeps the sun from reaching your face. You are stopping the light from hitting you.

Sometimes, you block your own mind. If you are afraid of something, you may block it. This means you are keeping your mind closed. You are not thinking about it anymore.

In a game, you can also block your own body. If you block a ball, the ball goes around your hand. You have stopped it from reaching you. You are keeping the ball away from you.

In a school, you can block a person. If you do this, the person is not allowed to talk to you. You are keeping them away.

Blocking is a way to stop something from happening. It keeps the thing or the person out of your life.
