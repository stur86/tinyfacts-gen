To ask something is to want to know or to make a wish. You can ask a person, a group of people, or even a thing you see.

When you want to know something, you use the word ask. If you want to know if the sun is up, you can say, ask the person if the sun is up. If you are very happy, you might say, I am so happy.

Sometimes, you ask because you are not sure. If you are trying to decide what to eat for dinner, you might say, do you want a hot dog or a bag of food? You want to know which one you should have.

You can also ask because you want something. If you want to go to a new city, you might say, can you tell me the way there? This helps the person who knows the way to help you.

People use this word in many different ways. You can ask a teacher if you did a good job on a paper. You can ask a friend if they want to go to a movie together. You can even ask a parents to buy you a new game.

To get something you want is to ask. You are asking the person or thing to give you what you need.
