An leg is a part of a living thing. It is the part that goes down from the top of a person or a thing that has four sides. There is one leg on each foot. Most people have two legs.

On a dog, the legs are very strong. They can run very fast. The dog uses them to jump over things. The dog also uses its legs to move around. It can move very fast and very far.

A leg can also be a piece of wood. If you cut a piece of wood, you get a leg. This is how people build houses. They use wood to make the legs of the house. The legs are the parts that hold the house up.

Legs can also be parts of animals that fly. The legs of a flying thing help the thing go through the air. The legs are very important because they help the thing stay up in the sky.
