To be a good friend means to always be there for the person you care about. You do this even when things are hard. You do not leave them alone or tell them you are busy. You listen when they need to talk and you try to help them in any way you can. You follow through on your promises.

A person who is a good friend is one who is always there. They do not change who they are. They do not pretend to be someone they are not. They are themselves, all the time.

Being a good friend is important because it helps people feel safe. When a person is a good friend, they make others feel okay. They show that they care about what happens to them. They are a good person to have around.

Sometimes people have friends who are not like this. They might be mean to them or ignore them. This is not good for anyone. But sometimes, even if a person is not a good friend, you can still be a good friend to them. You can still be kind and show that you care. This does not mean you have to be like them. It just means you are being a person who is not giving up.

Being a good friend is not just about being nice. It is also about being true. You must be yourself, even if you do not always like the way things are. You must not pretend to be someone you are not. You must be the person you are in the moment.

In a way, you can be a good friend to yourself, too. You can be kind to yourself when you are sad or when you make a bad move. You can say, I am okay, and that you will try again tomorrow. This is the same as being a good friend to a real person.
