A big group of stars is a group of many stars. They all are in the same place in space. There is a big room in space with many of these groups inside it. The group is so big that you can't see all of it from one place.

The stars in the group are very far from each other. The light from one star has to go a very long way to reach the next star in the group. This makes the stars look like they are very far away. The group is also very heavy. It has so much stuff that it is hard to hold up on your own hands.

These groups are made of many smaller groups inside them. Each small group is also made of many smaller groups. This goes on and on for many hundred of these big groups.
