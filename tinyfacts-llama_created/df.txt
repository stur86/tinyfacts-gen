A letter or a group of letters is called a word. To write a word, you use these letters. You can use one letter or many. You write what you want to say.

Sometimes, a group of letters is called a word. If you are talking with a friend, you can say,  I love you. This is a word. It is a group of letters that means a person is happy.

Words are very important. You use them every day. You use words to talk to others. You use words to write stories. You use words to read books. You use words to understand what is happening. Without words, we would not be able to talk or write.

A word can also mean a person. If a person is very kind, you can call them a good friend. If a person is very small, you can say they are little. These words are important because they help us understand how people feel and how we should act.

Words can also change. If you say,  I am sad, you might later say,  I am angry. This is because words can have different meaning. They are like a door. When you open the door, you are saying something. When you close the door, you are saying something else.
