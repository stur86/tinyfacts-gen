To pay something is to give money or another kind of thing to the person who needs it. When you buy food, clothes, or other things you want, you are paying. You give the money to the store. The store uses it to buy the things you need.

You can pay with money from your pocket. Or you can pay using a card at a store. Some people pay with a small thing they use to buy things. If you are late for work, you might have to pay a teacher or a person who helps you. This is because you were not ready when you were supposed to be.

Sometimes, you pay for things you have done. If you park a car in a lot and you do not leave it where it should be, you might have to pay the city. If you play a game with a friend and you lose, you might pay your friend money. This is because you were not the best at the game.

You can also pay to leave a room or a house. If you want to go to a place that is far away, you might need to pay a money person. They take your money and send you to the place. They do not care if you are there or not. They just need the money.

To pay money to someone is to show that you need their help or their money. When you pay a doctor, you are saying, I need your help.
