Dance is when you move your body so many times that your feet and legs feel like they are flying. You might jump up high. You might do a round. You might touch the ground at the top and jump up again. People like to dance for fun, for work, or for money.

People have been doing this for very long. In the old days, only the very good people could do this. They were the ones who could make beautiful music with their feet. Now everyone can do it.

You can dance to music. You can dance to a song with words. You can even dance to a song that only sounds like a low sound. When you dance, you are moving your body to the music. Your heart is also moving. Your face might go up or down. Your hair might go around your head.

Dance is a way to show how you are feeling. You can be happy, you can be sad. You can be angry. You can be calm. You can be very, very strong.

Some people dance in groups. They all do the same dance. They move like one big body. They are called a group. Some people dance alone. They do their own dance. They do it because they want to show how good they are.

Dance can change. A group of people can do the same dance. Later, they might change. They might add new moves. They might use different music. They might wear new clothes. They might dance in a different place. Each time, they show how good they are.

Dance is a very happy thing. It makes people feel like they are flying. It makes people feel like they are part of something new.
