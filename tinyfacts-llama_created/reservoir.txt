To store water is a way we keep it safe for later. You can imagine a big bag under a tree. You fill it with rain. When the rain is gone, the water stays in the bag. This water is safe to drink when you need it.

The word comes from a place where people built big boxes. These boxes hold water from far away. When there is not enough water in the boxes, people fill them again. This is how we keep the water going.

Some places have big water from far away. They put the water into large boxes. When the boxes are full, the water goes back into the big water. This keeps the water safe for everyone.

In some places, there are big water from the ground. We build tall things on top of them. We fill them with water from far away. The water goes down into the tall things. We can drink it and use it for many things.
