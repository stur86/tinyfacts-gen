To use a word means to show how you think or feel. When you use the word, you tell others what you are thinking. It is a way to show your mind.

Think about food. You eat something that is made from animals. This food is the start of a story. If you do not use the food right away, it starts to get bad. You must use it before it is too old or it starts to grow tiny white things that grow more and more. This is what happens with the food in your body.

You also use the word to talk about a thing. If you say something is very new, it is a word for what you do not know. If you say something is old, you are telling a story about how it was made. If you say something is very small, you are talking about the tiny things inside you. If you say something is very big, you are talking about the huge things in the sky or in the water.

Sometimes we use this word for people. If a person is very strong, we say they are strong. If a person is very quiet, we say they are quiet. We also say things like this when we talk about animals. If an animal is very fast, we say it is fast. If an animal is very small, we say it is small.

In some places, we use this word to mean not clean. If something is very small and white, it is clean. But if something is very big and dark, it might not be clean. It might be something that can make you sick. We use this word to tell others if something is safe to eat.

This word is also used for things that are not done yet. When we say something is coming, we are telling people what is going to happen. If a movie is coming, people know they will watch it later. If a game is coming, people know they will play it soon.
