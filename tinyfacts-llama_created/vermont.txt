Very is a word that means very. It is like the word more. When you use this word, you are saying that something is a lot. You are not just saying it is bigger or stronger. You are saying it is much better.

Think about a very bright day. The sun is up for a long time. The air is warm and there is a lot of light. The world feels happy. This is a very day.

You can also use this word with animals. If you see a dog that is very fast, you are happy for it. If you see a cat that is very good at climbing, you are happy for that too.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a place. If you visit a place that is very green, you feel happy. If you visit a place with very tall trees, you feel like you are in a big park.

In this world, we use this word when something is very good. It is like saying something is the best.
