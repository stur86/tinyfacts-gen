To put something in a place

Imagine you are in a city with many houses. There are streets and roads that go in all directions. There are also areas where people can not be there at all. These areas are called different places.

When people build a new house or a building, they have to follow the city plan. They can not put it in the middle of a street because that would hurt other people and make the streets hard to clean. The city might say, for a long time, the house or building can not be on that street.

The city also says how many houses can be in each area. If you build too many houses, it can make the streets hard to walk on. The city might say, no more than two houses on each side of the street.

There are also areas where people can not build anything. These are the places where the water goes into the ground or where the ground is very hard. People do not build houses in those areas because it is not safe.

There are also areas where people can build houses and stores. These are the areas that are open to everyone and are safe. People can go in and out of them easily. They are like the middle ground of the city.

The places are put in this order so that everyone can live in their area and not hurt anyone else. It keeps the city safe and makes sure everyone has a place to live and work.
