Immediately means you do it right away. In the world of living things, your body has a way to stay safe from bad stuff. You do this by sending out tiny bits that fight the bad stuff. These tiny bits are like a strong body guard that keeps you from getting hurt.

The word comes from the word for the inside of a person. You can imagine it like your own body. When you are very well, your body is not afraid to fight. But if the bad stuff is too strong, your body might get hurt. This is why you need to be strong and keep fighting.

In a small group of people, they all have the same kind of body guard. If one person gets hurt, the others can all fight together. This helps keep everyone safe.

When we talk about a person, we mean their body guard. It is the part of them that keeps them safe. If a person dies, their body guard can still stay strong inside them. This helps their body remember what happened and be ready to fight again in the next life.

Immediately also means you should act fast. If something bad happens, you should try to stop it before it gets worse. You do this by getting help from others. In a fight, you need to act very fast to stay safe.
