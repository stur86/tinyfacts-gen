A question is something you ask to know the answer. You might ask a friend, a teacher, or even a computer. If you ask a person, you are trying to understand what they think or what they feel.

You can ask many kinds of questions. You might ask about the world around you. You might ask about what happened last night. You might ask about what you are doing. You might ask about what a friend said. You can also ask about things that are not real.

Questions help us learn. When you ask a question, you are showing that you are interested in what is happening. This makes the person feel happy and the person might give you the answer.

You can also ask questions about how things work. You might ask about how the sun works or how the water in a bottle moves. These questions help us understand the world and how it is put together.

Sometimes, you might not know the answer. You might say, I do not know. This can be a little strange because you are still interested in the question. You might ask again, can you tell me? This shows that you are still trying to learn.

Questions are an important part of our lives. They help us grow and learn. They make us think about the world and what is happening.
