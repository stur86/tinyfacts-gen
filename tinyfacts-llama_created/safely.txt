To be safe means you are not in trouble. You are not afraid because you are not doing anything wrong. You are not worried because you know you are not in a bad place.

Think of it like this. You are playing with a ball. You are not running, but you are still moving. If you fall down, the ball might break. But if you stay still and watch what happens, you are safe. You did not cause the trouble.

You can also be safe if you are in a house. You are not going out and you are not doing anything that makes a noise. You are not afraid of the dark. You are just waiting for morning.

If you are safe, you can go to a place you like. You can go to a store, a school, or a park. You do not have to be afraid to go there. You feel happy.

Sometimes, being safe means you are not afraid of things that are not there. You do not feel fear when you are in a quiet room. You feel calm.

To be safe is to be ready. It means you are thinking about what might happen and you are doing things to stay safe. It is like you are being a good person.
