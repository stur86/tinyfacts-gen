A person who works with water to make things. They move big things through water from one place to another. They are very good at this job because they can control how fast the water moves and how strong it is.

You might see them pushing a long, round thing with many lines. They turn the lines to make the water go fast or slow. They might also use a kind of water that is very clear to see through. This water helps the person see what they are doing.

They can work alone or with many other people. They must be very strong and have good hands. They must also know how to handle the heavy things they move.

Sometimes, they go into deep water and have to wear a big bag around their neck. This bag keeps water out of their clothes and body. They also wear a cover to keep their hair and face dry.

This job is very important. Water is all around us and helps many things grow. The person who works with water is helping keep our world clean and safe.
