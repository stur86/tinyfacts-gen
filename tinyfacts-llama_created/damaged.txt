When something is broken, it is often called a problem. If you drop your book, the floor is a problem. If you cut your hand, you are a problem. If you make a car go very fast, the car is a problem.

But if something is not working right, it is also called a problem. If a phone does not work, you are a problem. If the air in the room is bad, you are a problem. If a tree falls and hits you, you are a problem.

We also use this word with people. If a friend breaks your heart, you are a problem. If a teacher is mean to you, you are a problem. If someone is lying to you, you are a problem. When you are a problem, it means you need to find a way to fix it so that everything is back to normal.
