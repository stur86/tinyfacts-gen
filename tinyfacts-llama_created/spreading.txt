To start with, when you are very small, you are alone. Then you grow and get bigger. You are not alone anymore. You are now a part of something much bigger. You are part of a group of animals. You are part of a group of people. You are part of a group of trees. The group of people is part of the group of animals. The group of animals is part of the group of trees. And so on.

This is how things get from one place to another. If one group of animals moves to a new land, the new land becomes a new group of animals. The new land is not alone anymore. It is now part of many groups of animals.

We also talk about things that are already there. A house is already there. A car is already there. If you go into a room, you are already inside. You do not have to start over. You can just step inside the door and you are there.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about how things change. If you eat a food, it changes inside you. Your body is not the same anymore. You are now different from who you were before.

If you have a friend, you start with them. You share your life with them. You talk to them. You do things together. When you finish, you are still friends. You did not start over. You just shared your life with them.
