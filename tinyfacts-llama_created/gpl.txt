To do something with someone else is to share a thing. If you have a book, you can read it to someone. You do not keep it alone. You share it with them so more people can learn from it.

Sometimes, you might want to make a thing. You want to make it better or new. You might need help. You ask other people to help you. You work together. This is how you do things when you do not do it alone.

When you do something with someone, you are part of a group. You are not alone anymore. You are with many people who want the same thing. This makes the work easier and fun. People often do things with friends, family, or other people they trust.

You can also say that something is in the green group. This means it is safe and good for you. You can eat green food, like leaves from the ground, or drink green water from a bottle. Green is a color that many animals and growing things use to show they are strong and happy.

Sometimes, when a group of people do something, they become a group with power. They are not alone anymore. They are a group with a plan. This group can help many people at once. They can help those in need or work on big problems that need many people to fix.

The word giving can also mean to give something. If you give your friend money, you are helping them. You are not keeping the money for yourself. You are giving it to them so they can buy a thing they need or save money for a big plan.

When you give, you share your money, your time, or a thing you have. This makes the world a better place for everyone.
