A picture is something you see when you look at it. You can hold it in your hand or see it on a box. Most of these things are made by a person who takes a picture. They point a small thing with a light on it toward something. This makes a light go through the thing and into the picture.

The picture shows what happened at a moment. You can see a person, a dog, a car, or anything else. The picture can be small and look like nothing at all, or it can be very big and show a whole street or a huge city.

Some pictures are very old. They were made a long time ago. You can buy these pictures at a store. They are like old books that you can look at. Some pictures are new. They were made just for you. You can look at them and remember happy times.

You can also use a phone to take a picture. This is like a small box with a light on it. You point it toward what you want to see, and it makes a picture. These pictures can be very small and can go on a box you carry around.

All these pictures are a way to remember what happened. They help us remember and share stories with others.
