A thing that is important in a group. When you talk about something, you use this word. It tells you how important the thing is.

If you have a big party, the music is the most important thing. If you have a small party, the food is the most important thing. You might say, the music is the most important thing in this party, or the food is the most important thing.

It does not mean you have to be the most important person. It just means the thing is very important to the group.

Imagine you have a friend who always does the right thing. You might say, that friend is a very important person. You like them a lot and you want them to stay in your life.

A thing that is important is often in the middle. It is not the biggest thing, but it is very important to the group.
