A thing that has many sides is called a box. These boxes are very good at keeping things inside. You can put a book, a ball, or a picture in a box and keep it safe.

A box is also a thing that has many lines. When you look at a book, the lines on the paper help you understand what the words mean. These lines are like the sides of the box, but they are made of paper.

Sometimes, a box is a person. You might meet a nice person who is very kind. They might say nice things and help you when you are sad. People call them boxes of good things.

A box can also be a place where things are kept for many years. When we grow up, we learn to use a box to keep our things. We close the box when we are done with them. Then we open it later when we need to use them again.

In a computer, you might see a box that shows you a picture. This box is like a box from the time before. It helps you understand things that are very far away.

So, a box is something that has many sides and lines. It can be a thing that keeps things inside, a person who is very kind, or a place where things are kept.
