To build something is to make it from the ground up. People who are good at this use their hands and their minds to make new things.

Imagine you want a new house. You start by thinking about how you want the house to look. You see pictures of houses and decide what color and how many rooms you want. Then you start looking for a place where you can live. When you find a good spot, you start to make the ground around the house. You cut trees that block the way, and you fill in the ground with things that hold the ground together. After a while, the ground is ready and you can start to build the walls of the house.

You also need to make the walls strong so they do not fall down. You do this by putting many small pieces of hard stuff together. You put one piece over another, making sure each piece is on top of the other, and this keeps going all the way around the house. You also use things that keep air out, so the house does not let in cold air or hot air. This helps keep the house warm in the cold time of year and keeps it cool when it is hot.

When you build a house, you need to plan it so it is safe. You think about what will happen if the ground gives way or if strong wind comes through. You make sure the house is built on a strong spot, and you use things that help people get out if something bad happens.

People also build houses for people who live far away. These houses are much bigger than the ones you build for people who live near you. They have many rooms and are often in a different area. These houses are usually made of strong things that can not fall down.

To build a car is a bit different. You start with a box of hard stuff. You fill this box with other hard stuff, and then you add pieces of hard stuff to make the car move. You put the car together piece by piece, so each part is strong and safe before you put the next one on top. You also make sure the car is strong enough to hold the heavy parts.

Sometimes, you build things that are not for people. You build houses for animals. You build places for food so animals can eat. You build houses for things that fly so they can live in a place where people do not want them. You build houses for things that can not move, so they stay on one spot.

To build something can be hard. You have to work very hard for many hours, and you have to follow many steps. But when you do it, you have made something new that will live for many years.
