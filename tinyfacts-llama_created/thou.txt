To say a word to someone is to use it. You speak the word and the person hears it. When you say a word to a friend, you are showing you care about them. You are telling them you are there for them. You use the word to talk about what happens.

When you say to someone, you are not just saying a word. You are making a plan. You are telling them to do something. If you say to eat food, you are telling them to eat. If you say to play, you are telling them to have fun.

A person who is always saying to another person is a person who loves them. They love to be around them. They want to see them, talk to them, and be with them. They want to make them happy.

You can also say to someone you do not like. This can be very hard. You might say, I do not want to talk to you. Or you might say, I am angry with you. This shows you are not afraid.

People use this word with other words. When you say to a dog, you are telling it to do something. If you say, you must move, the dog moves. If you say, you must be quiet, the dog stays quiet.

To say to a person is not the same as to show. To show is to act. If you say to a person, you are acting. You are doing something. But saying is about how you use the word.
