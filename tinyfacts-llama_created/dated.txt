To date something means to show when it happened. When you see a picture of a young girl, you know that girl is the one you love. But if you see a picture of a girl who is old, you are not sure if she is the one you love.

You use this word to show how time works. Everything changes when time goes on. A baby is very small and the same as a tiny animal. A grown man is the same as a very old animal. An old house is older than a new house.

People also use this word to talk about time with a friend. If you go out to dinner with a friend, it is a good time to go. If you stay home and watch a movie, it is a bad time. When something is old, it is not good anymore. It is old and not nice to look at.

You can also use this word to talk about a story. A story has a start and a end. The start is the beginning. The end is the end. When you read a book, you are following the story. If you do not finish the book, you have not dated the story.
