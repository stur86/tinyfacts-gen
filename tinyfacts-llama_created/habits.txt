A thing you do many times is a thing you do. You might eat the same food every day. You might walk in the same way. You might say the same words to your friends. A thing you do is a way you live.

A bad thing you do is one you want to stop. If you drink too much, you feel sick. If you play a game too much, you are tired. These things are not things you do, they are bad for you.

Things you do that are good for you are called things you do. If you do your work, you are doing something good. If you help a friend, you are being kind. These are things you do for other people.

We all have things we do. You might like to watch a movie. You might like to play a game. You might like to read a book. These are things you do. They make your life better.
