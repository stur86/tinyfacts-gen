To ask someone to listen to you is to ask them to pay attention. If you are angry, you might shout at someone. If you are sad, you might cry. When you ask someone to listen, you are trying to make them understand what you are saying.

Sometimes, a person does not listen to you. They hear what you say, but they do not want to hear it. This can make you feel angry. You might want to shout or scream. But if you do that, the other person might get angry too.

If this happens, you might ask the person to stop. You might say, I want you to stop yelling. This helps you to calm down and be quiet. You are still saying your thing, but you are not hurting anyone else.
