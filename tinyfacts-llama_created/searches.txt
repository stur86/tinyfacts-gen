To search something means to look for it. You might look in a book or a box. You might look under a bed or in a small room. You might ask a friend if they have a thing you are looking for. If you do not find it in one place, you might go to another place or ask another person if they know where it is.

Sometimes, people search for things that are very important. Maybe a person is looking for a friend who has gone away. They look in many places, and sometimes they find the person. Other times, they do not find what they are looking for at all.

You can also search for things that are hidden. If you are looking for a thing in a dark room, you might use a light. You are looking for the light and the room at the same time. This is like a game where you try to find a small thing that is not big at all.

Some people use computers to search. They type a word and press a key. The computer looks through many lines of words and finds what the person is looking for. This is very fast and can find things in a very short time.

When you search, you are looking for something that is not there. You are looking for the thing that is not in front of you. This can be good because it helps you learn new things. But it can also be hard because you do not know where the thing is until you start looking.
