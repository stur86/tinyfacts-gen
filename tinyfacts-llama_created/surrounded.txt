To be surrounded is to have many things around you. Imagine you are in a big room with a lot of people. You are standing on the floor and you look out into the room. There are people all around you, some standing and some sitting on chairs. You can see the walls all around you and there is a bright light coming from the top of the room.

If you are sitting in a car with your family, you can be surrounded by many things. You are in a car with a group of people and a lot of things on the road. You might see a big road, a city, or a long line of cars in front of you. You are also surrounded by the sounds of your family: the voices of your mother, the sounds of your father, and the sounds of the kids laughing.

If you are standing on a high wall with a clear night sky above you, you are also surrounded. You are on the top of a tall wall and the world around you is very quiet. You can see a bright blue sky above you and you feel safe because you are alone on the top of the wall.

Even if you are not in a big room or a car, you can still feel surrounded. If you are in a dark room with a full house, you can feel the walls around you. You can hear the sound of a child playing in another room or the sound of a dog belling in a room next door. You are surrounded by the people and things that make your life happen.
