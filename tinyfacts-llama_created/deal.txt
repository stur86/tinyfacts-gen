To deal with something means to manage it. It is a way to handle a situation.

Imagine you are a manager in a big house where many people live. You have to order food, pay money, and make sure everyone is happy. You decide how many people will have what kind of food and when. You also decide how much money each person should pay. This is a deal.

You and the people in the house agree to do this. You do not fight. You both follow your plan. This way, everyone is happy and there is no trouble.

You can also deal with money. If you have a lot of money, you might want to buy things you like. You can decide what you want to buy and how much money you will give. This is a deal. You are showing that you are a good person who can do what is right.

Sometimes, you might deal with a person who is mean. You might say, I am not going to talk to you. This is a deal. You are showing that you do not want to talk to them anymore.

You can also deal with a problem that is very hard. You might talk to a doctor or a teacher. You tell them your problem. They listen and then they help you. This is a deal. You have found a way to get the help you need.

In a game, you might deal with a ball. You throw the ball to a friend. The ball goes to the friend. You did not catch it, but you did not hurt them either. You were a good player. This is a deal. You are showing that you are a good person who follows the right way.

In this world, when you do not want to do something, you might say, I will deal with it later. This means you are going to do it at a later time. You are not going to do it right away, but you will do it eventually.

The word deal is used to talk about how people work together. It is about finding a way that is good for everyone.
