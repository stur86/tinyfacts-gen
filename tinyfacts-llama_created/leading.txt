When you say something is leading, you mean it is moving in front of the other people or things. It is the first one, the one that starts the way. It is the one that shows the way to where you want to go.

Imagine you are walking down a street with your friends. If you are the one in front of them, you are leading them. You are the one who sets the direction. You tell them where to go and they follow you.

If you are a person who runs a big store, you are leading. You are the one who has the power to decide what happens next. You decide how people should buy things and how they should act.

People also use this word to talk about how things move around. If a car is leading a group of people, it is the one that drives them. The car is the one that moves the group.

Sometimes, you might say something is leading to a problem. This means that the thing happening is the one that brings the bad thing into being. It is the thing that starts the trouble.

So, leading is about being the first one and showing the way. It is about having the power to decide what happens next.
