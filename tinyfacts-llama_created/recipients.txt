When you give something to someone, you are giving it to them. You are giving it to them so they can use it.

Think about a group of people who want to help others. They are the ones who will take the money or the food. They will use it to buy food for many people. This is the act of giving to others. They are the ones who are being given to.

Imagine you give a book to your child. The book is the thing you give. Your child is the one who uses it. The book has stories and pictures. Your child can read it and learn new things.

A group of people can also be called a group of people. This is a group of many people who are not one person. They are all together because they want to do the same thing.

When you say someone is a good person, you mean they give to others. They help other people. They do things for someone without expecting anything back. They are very kind.

You can also say a thing is a good thing. If something is a good thing, it helps you. It makes you feel better. It makes your life better.

In this story, the people are the ones who are giving. They are the ones who are being given.
