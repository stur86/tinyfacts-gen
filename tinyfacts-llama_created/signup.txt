To sign something means you are taking an important step. You are making your mark on something. You are saying you are ready to do it.

Imagine you want to join a new group. You know how much fun it will be. But you do not know if you can stay. So you ask the group if you can join. This is signing.

You also sign papers that tell who you are and who you are with. This is important because it helps keep everything straight. It keeps your word and your name.

Sometimes you sign your name to say you agree with a story. You are saying you like it. You are showing you care about what is happening.

In your phone, you might see a word that says you must sign before you can use it. This is like signing. You are taking the word seriously. You are saying you are ready to use it.

You can also sign a paper when you buy something you want. You sign your name so the store knows you bought it. This is signing as a person who has made a big deal.

In a game, you might get a number that you must sign. This is like signing your name. You are making your mark on the game.
