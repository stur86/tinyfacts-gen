To be in a line means you are following one thing after another. Imagine you are moving through a room. You can be standing. Then you walk. After you walk, you sit. After you sit, you stand again. You are always following one thing after another.

If you are in a line with your friends, you all have the same thing in your hand. One person may have a book. The next person has a paper. The person after them has a computer. Each person has a different thing, but they are all following the same line.

A line can also be a number. A line is the number that comes after a number. If you have two numbers, you can put them next to each other to make a line. This line shows how many numbers there are. There are many lines in a book. Some lines tell you what the words say. Other lines show how the story goes.

Sometimes, people say a line is a way of thinking. When you have a problem, you might start with a small thing and see how it works. You go from one thing to another until you find a way. This is like following a line. It helps you understand how things are.

The word can also mean a long way. If you walk for a long time, you are on a line. This line goes across the world or across your city. You follow it to get from one place to another.
