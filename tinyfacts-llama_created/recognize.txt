To recognize something means to see it. You see a person, a place, a thing, a sound. You know who they are. You know where they live. You know what they do.

When you see a word, you are recognizing it. You are seeing the letters. You are seeing the sound it makes. If you see a word you know, you feel a strong feeling. You say, yes, I know this.

You can also recognize a face. If you see a friend, you know who they are. You feel happy and safe. You know their eyes, their nose, their hair.

You can also recognize a thing. If you see a chair, you know what it is. You know it is a chair. You know it is hard to the touch.

To recognize something is to see it and understand it. It is about feeling a strong feeling when you see a word, a face, or a thing you know.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. You are looking at the ball. You see its round body. You see its color. You see the lines on its side. You see the way it moves. You are recognizing the ball.

You are recognizing it because you know it. You know what it is. You know how to play with it.
