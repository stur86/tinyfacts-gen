A person who writes things that are good to read. They use words that are nice and make the story feel happy. They might write about people, places, or animals. They try to write in a way that is clear and fun.

A writer can be a man or a woman. They might write about their own life or a story from someone else. They might use many different words and make the story feel like it is happening right now.

Some people like to hear the words of a writer. They might read a book or listen to a story. Others like to write things themselves. They might write short stories or books that are not for kids. They might write about what they think or how they feel.

Writing is a job. People do it for many reasons. Some people do it to tell stories. Others do it to share their ideas. Some people do it to be heard. Some people do it to show how they feel.
