To waving or to waving is to move from side to side. It is a way to show you are happy or to tell others you are not going to do something. When you waving, you keep your arms straight and let them go as you walk. Your hands go up in the air and your fingers go behind your head. This helps the other person know you are moving away.

If you are talking to a friend and you want to say you are not interested, you can waving your hand. You keep your eyes on your face and look away for a moment. This shows the other person you are not paying attention. If you keep your hand up in the air, it means you are still talking.

Sometimes people say, I waving on the way home. This means they were walking and they waved at a person who was standing on the street. It is a way to say hello without saying a word.

You also use this word with animals. If you see a dog or a cat and you want to tell them to stop, you can waving your hand. The animal will see the hand and stop moving.
