When you say something is always, you mean it will never change. You can say it is always true, always green, or always your best friend. This means nothing will ever happen that is different.

Think of a line of people. You walk on this line one day and you will walk the next day, and the day after that, and so on. The line never ends and never changes. You will always stay on this line.

A tree can grow as long as it lives. If it dies, nothing new can grow. It is always the same tree.

The word always is also used to talk about time. You can say something is always happening. This means the eventually happens every day, every week, every year, and every month. It does not stop.

If you say your life is always getting worse, you mean your life is always going bad. There is never a day when it gets better. It is always getting harder.

Sometimes people use this word when they are afraid. If you are afraid, you are afraid for the whole time. You are always worried. You do not know what is going to happen next.
