To be in a state of a middle is a way to say a thing is not too good and not too bad. You are not too hot, not too cold. You are just right.

Imagine you are eating a sweet food. If it is too hot, it feels like fire. If it is too cold, it feels like ice. But if it is just right, it is nice to eat. This is what it means to be in a middle state.

Sometimes things are not right, but we can make them right. If you are angry, you might be in a state of anger. But if you calm down, you are no longer angry. You are in a middle state.

This word is used when talking about things that are not easy to decide. If you are sad, you might feel a lot of pain. If you are happy, you might feel a lot of good. But if you are just okay, you are in a middle state.

It can also mean to act in a way that is not too strong or too small. If you are trying to be nice to a friend, you are in a middle state. You are not too mean or too kind. You are just right.
