To be a good fit for someone or something.

Think of a room in a house. The room might have a window that looks out to the street. The room is a good fit because it is where you live and feel safe.

You can also say something is a good fit for a game. If you are a fast runner, you might be a good fit for a game that uses running. You feel like you are meant to be there.

Sometimes, you might find something that is a very good fit. You love a book because it tells you a story you care about. A book is a good fit because it is the thing you love.

Being a good fit means you feel right. You do not have to be like everyone else. You can be your own person.
