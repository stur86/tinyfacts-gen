To explain something means to show why it is true. When you want to tell someone why you did something, you explain. You use words to tell them what happened. You might say, I ate a food because I was tired. This is an answer. It tells the person why you were doing it.

Sometimes you need to explain a thing that you did not know. If you did not know something before, you might ask the teacher or a friend. They answer you and tell you the truth. This is also an answer.

If you want to explain a big thing that is very hard to understand, you might break it down into small parts. You tell them one part at a time. This helps them see how it works and how it can be done.

People often explain things when they want to help others. If you tell a child why you are afraid, you explain things so they know why. This makes them feel better.
