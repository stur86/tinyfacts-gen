A wall is a long thing that is built around a house or a building. It is made of many small pieces of wood or hard stuff. The pieces are put together so that the wall stays up.

You can see walls in many places. There are walls on houses and on high places where people live. There are also walls in big places with many rooms. Inside these walls, people work and live. There are walls that keep people inside a small area. There are walls that keep people out of a big area.

A wall can be very high. You can walk behind it and still not reach the top. Or it can be low and not very wide. The wall can be made of different colors. People often paint their walls in bright colors to make them look nice. They may also add pictures or words to make the wall feel like a home.

Sometimes a wall is broken. If the wall is old, it can break and fall down. People fix the wall with new pieces. If a wall is very high, people might use a strong thing to hold it up while they work.

Walls are important. They help keep us safe. They keep people from getting into houses they do not want. They help keep people from getting hurt. They also help us feel safe inside a place.
