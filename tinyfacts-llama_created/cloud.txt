A thing in the sky is called a clothes. It is made of many tiny drops of water that are all joined together. When the sun is high, the water drops become so heavy that they fall down to the ground. But when the sun is low, they go up into the sky and form the white stuff we see in the sky, the one that looks like a big round thing.

Sometimes the water drops get very big and they cover the whole sky. This is why people say the sky is covered. Other times, the water drops are so small that they do not cover the sky at all. In this case, the sky looks clear and bright.

We also use the word for a group of the white stuff that covers the sky. This group of white stuff is made of many, many tiny drops of water. When the group of white stuff gets very heavy, the water drops in it fall down to the ground. When the group of white stuff is very light, the white stuff goes up into the sky.

The white stuff can also be seen when it is moving around in the sky. If it is going very fast, it looks like a big moving thing. If it is going slowly, it looks like a big round thing. When the white stuff is falling from the sky, it looks like a long line that goes down to the ground.

Sometimes, a group of white stuff can look like many small white things. This is why we call it a big group of white stuff. When the white stuff is many small white things, it looks like a group of white things.
