A man named Lander was the one who was doing the work. He was very good at it. He was the one who was building a new house. He had a big job to do.

Later, another man came and said,  You can do the work! He was a new kind of man. He did not have to follow the same old ways. He could think his own way.

People were afraid of this new man. They did not know what to do. Some were happy. Some were angry. Many were confused.

The man with the new way was not happy. He felt he was doing the right thing. He felt he was the one who was doing it right.

There was a fight. The man who did the old way wanted to stop. The man who did the new way wanted to keep going. They did not agree on what was right.

In the end, the man who did the new way was very strong. He was able to make people follow him. Many people were happy with the way he did things. He made the old way look bad.

The man who did the old way was sad. He felt he had been wrong. He did not understand why people were doing things his way instead of his way. He did not know how to fix things.
