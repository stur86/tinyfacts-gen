To send something is to give it to someone else. You can send a letter or a small box with a note. If you send a ball to a friend, you are sending it from one place to another.

Imagine you want to tell your friend about a fun thing that happened. You might write a short story and put it in an outside box. You then give the box to your friend. When your friend opens the box, they read the story and feel happy.

Sending is also about moving things. If you want a book, you can send it to a friend who lives far away. The person gets the book and can read it later.

We can also send money to someone we trust. Think about your money in a safe place. If you send the money to a friend, they can use it to buy food or clothes.

Sometimes we send people to places where they need to be. If you are in a hospital and you need to see a doctor, you might send a person to the hospital. This helps the doctor know about your problem so they can help you feel better.

When you send something, you are giving it to someone else so they can use it or enjoy it.
