To group things together is to put them in one place. When people do this, they become a group. This can be a group of friends, a group of animals, or a group of people who share a thing. 

Imagine you and your friends are playing a game. You are all on one side of the room. The other side of the room has people who also want to play. They are all there because they want to be part of the game. This is a group because you are all doing the same thing.
