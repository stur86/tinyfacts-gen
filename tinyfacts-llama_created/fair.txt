The word can mean different things.

To play a game is to do something fun. You go to a place with many people and you do things like run, jump, or throw a ball. You are not the only one, and there are many other people. Everyone has to follow the same set of steps so you all have the same time and the same fun.

To be seen is to be noticed. If you walk down the street and you are not heard by anyone, you are not seen. If a doctor helps a baby, the baby is seen by the doctor. If a person is kind to you, you see them.

To buy something means you pay money for it. You use money to buy food, a book, or a thing you want to own. When you buy something, you are not just getting the thing. You also pay for the person who made it and the work they did to make it.

To be kind is to show you care. If you see a person who is sad, you might be kind. You could say a word that makes them feel better. This is important because it helps people feel better. We need to be kind to each other because everyone needs love and attention.

To have a ball is to play with a round thing. You throw the ball into a box and the person who catches it has fun. You also play with the ball to run and to be fast. You might play with the ball with your friends or with the people you work with.
