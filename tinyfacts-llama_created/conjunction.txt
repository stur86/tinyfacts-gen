A word is a thing you use to help your words sound right. When you want two things to happen at the same time, you use this word.

Think about a story. You could say, in this story, I did this and I did that. But this word can help you say, together I did these two things. It is like a word that says two things are happening at the same time.

Another word is an and. It is like a line that you put in between two words. If I say, I love my family, and I love my friends, the word and helps me say I love both. It is like a word that says two things are true at the same time.

There are also words that join two things together. When you say, I am going to the store, and I am going home, the word and is helping. It is saying two things are happening at the same time.

Sometimes we use these words in our own words. If you want to say, I am going to the store and I am eating food, the word and is helping. You are using it to say two things are happening at the same time.

These words are very good to use when you want to talk about two things that happen at the same time.
