To spend something is to use money for a reason other than buying things. It means you give money to someone or to a place. This money is not for yourself anymore. It is for someone who wants help or for a place that needs money to be done.

Imagine you have a big bag of food. You can buy food for many days. But if you have money to spend, you can buy many more days of food. You are spending money. You are giving it to someone else.

You can spend money on things you want. You can buy a book. You can buy a game. You can buy food. Or you can spend money on things that help people. You can give money to a doctor so they can help a person who is hurt. You can give money to a school so they can teachers and kids. You are spending money to make the world a better place.

Sometimes, people spend money because they are afraid. They might spend money to feel safe. They might buy a house if they feel they need a safe place to live. Or they might spend money to buy a car if they need a way to go to work.

Spending money is important. It helps us buy things we need. It helps us pay for things that help us. It helps us build things that make our lives better.
