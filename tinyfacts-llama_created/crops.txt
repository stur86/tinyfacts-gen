To grow things is to make them live and grow. You give them water and food, and they become strong and tall. They are the food for many animals.

If you grow food, you can eat it. If you grow clothes, you wear them. If you grow hair, you can use it to make hair for people. You grow things that people need to live.

You can grow many kinds of things. You grow green things from the ground that make food. You grow tall things with big leaves that make food for animals. You grow things with hard parts that people use to make things they need.

Sometimes, people grow things that can fly. These are very big and can carry many people or many animals at once. They go over wide spaces and bring people to far away places.

You can also grow things that can make other things. If you grow a kind of wood, you can make other kinds of wood. If you grow a kind of thing that makes the air clean, people use it to make houses that keep the air clean.

Growing things is a way to give life. It is a way to show that something is growing and becoming strong.
