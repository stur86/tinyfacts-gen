The word or is used to show that something is either one thing or the other. It is like saying if you have a ball, you either have it or you do not. If you have a ball, you are a ball person. If you do not have a ball, you are not a ball person.

The word also can mean when you are between two things. You are between a chair and a table. You are on the line where the chair and the table meet. You are not in the chair part, normal to the table part. You are in the middle, with both sides of you in between.

Sometimes, the word or is used to ask if you like something. If you want a drink, you can say, or do you like a drink? This means you are not sure, and you do not know yet.

It is also used to tell when something starts and when it ends. If you start your day at am, you are getting ready. When you reach your home, your day is over. You can say, I am going to work, or I am going home.
