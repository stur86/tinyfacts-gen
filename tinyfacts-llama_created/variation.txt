When you are talking about something, you can use words that are different. These words are called different words.

Imagine you have a ball. You throw it up in the air. As it goes up, it changes. It is not the same as when it was in the air. It is different.

You can do many things and still be the same person. You might wear the same clothes or eat the same food. But you are still you. You are the same person, even if your body looks different or you have a different name.

Another way to think about it is to look at the world around you. There are many different animals. Some are small and some are big. Some fly, and some walk on their feet. Some have long hair and some have short hair. All these different things show how many different ways there are to be.

When you are talking about this, you are talking about how many different ways something can be. You can counters this by saying, for every one person, there are ten, one hundred, one hundred and ten, or one hundred and one ways to be.
