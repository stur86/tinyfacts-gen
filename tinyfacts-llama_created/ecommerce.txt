Easier Money

To buy something you want is easy. You go to a store and pay with money. You pick the thing you want and walk away. But if you want to buy things from many places, it is hard. You have to go to each one, look at what they have, and pay money for each one.

An easier way is to buy from a place that is near you. This is called a store. You can walk there and pick the things you want. You do not have to go to many places. You can buy many things in one place.

A place that is far away is called a store. You do not have to walk there. You can send money to them. They send the things to you when you ask. This is easier than going to many stores.

People buy things from these stores using a thing they use to send money. You give them some money, they send you the things, and you pay when you want. This is called money on the computer. It is like a big box where people send and get money.

When people buy things, they often use their names and numbers. They tell the store who they are. The store says, if you want this, you can pay this. This is how the money gets from your name to the store, and then from the store to your name.

These places are like a big group of stores all in one place. They have many things to buy, and people can go to them many times. This makes life easier because you do not have to go to many places to buy things.
