To keep something happens when you hold a thing for later use. Sometimes you keep a thing that you bought. You might keep a food you buy for a day. Or you keep a word you want to tell your friend. If you keep something, you do not throw it away.

Sometimes people keep money they do not want to spend. They keep it for later. If you save money, you keep it safe. You do not spend it all at once.

You can also keep something that is not wanted by others. If a person does not want to talk to you, you keep that person. You do not have to talk to them. You keep them in your mind.

Keeping things can be good. It helps you have what you need when you need it. But keeping things for too long can be bad. If you keep a thing for too long, you might forget to use it or you might get angry with it.
