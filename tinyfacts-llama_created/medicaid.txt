To pay for a doctor is a good way for people to stay well. When you have a baby or a very young child, you need to pay a little money each week. This money is what helps you pay for what your child needs to stay safe and strong.

But sometimes, no one can pay. The people who care for you might be very tired. The doctor might give you a tiny bit of money so you can go home and rest. This is called getting help.

The money for this is made by the city or state. They take some of the money from your pay and add it to what they already pay. This money goes to many people and helps many babies and young children stay well.

Some people do not pay at all. They live in a place where the city or state does not pay for them. But they still get help because they are in a place that needs it.

You do not need to pay this money. You do not have to leave your home and go to a different place. You can stay where you are and still get help.
