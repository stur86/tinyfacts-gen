Because is a word we use when we want to explain why something is true. It is used when you want to tell someone why a thing happens or why a person did something.

Imagine you want to tell your friend why you are late. You might say,  I am late because I was waiting for my friend. This is because. It means you are telling the truth because of what happened. You are explaining why you are late.

You can also use this word to explain why things are the way they are. If you take away something, people get less of it. This is because. The word is helping you understand why things change.

Sometimes, because is also used to ask if something is true. You might ask, Is it true that you are here? If the person answers, yes, then the word is used.

We use this word in many different ways. We might write it at the beginning of a story to show how the story began. Or we might use it to talk about a person. If you say a person is good, you are saying they are because they are kind or they are doing a good job.

Because is an important word. It shows us how we understand the world and the people in it.
