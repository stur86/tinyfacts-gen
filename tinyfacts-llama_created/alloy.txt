To make something new is to change the old. You take some things you already know, and you add more of other things. This makes the new thing stronger, or lighter, or different. People use this to make things that are better for them. They make things that are stronger for walking, or things that are lighter for flying.

You can make an answer to a question by adding words. Imagine you want to know if a cat is big. You say, the cat is big. But if you add, it is very tall, you have found the answer. You were not trying to find a single word, but you found a complete picture. You have used the word to make a story.

You can also make a thing that is not for eating. People make things from wood. They take wood from one place and make it into something else. The wood comes out as a strong, hard piece of wood. It is not eaten. It is made to be held.
