To do something in a short time is to do it now. You do it right away or soon after. If you do something on a day that is in the middle of the week, it is a short time. If you do something on a day that is very far away, it is a long time.

To be a great person is to do many good things. A person might work hard and do a lot of work. They might also help their family and friends. They might also do things that make the world a better place. When you are a great person, you make people happy.

To eat something is to take a small bit of food from a big box. You close your mouth and take a small bit. You might eat a piece of food that is sweet, a piece of food that is hard, or a piece of food that is soft. When you eat, you give your body the food it needs to stay strong.

To go somewhere is to move a long way. You might drive a car or walk. You might also fly in a big thing that can go very fast. When you go somewhere, you leave your home. You go to find something new or to see a place that you have never seen before.
