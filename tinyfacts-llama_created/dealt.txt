When you deal something, you give it to someone. In a game, you give a ball to a friend so they can play with it. In a business, you give money to a person who will work for you. This is an important step before a group of people decide who will be in a group and who will not be in it.

You can deal a card from a box of cards. You pick a card and put it in your hand. Then you give the card to another person. This is like giving a friend a small paper that says you are glad they are there.

You can also deal food. You put a piece of food into a box. Then the person who gets the box gets the food. This helps people eat at a table.

Sometimes you deal a word. You take a word from a book and give it to someone who wants to read it. This is how you share stories with others.

In a game, you might deal a hand of numbers. You give each person a few numbers so everyone knows what they have. This helps everyone understand who has what and who is safe.
