To use something means to use it for something important. You use a book to read or write stories. You use a chair to sit and stay. You use a phone to talk to friends or to do work.

You also use things that help you live. A computer is a thing that helps you do work, play games, and look at pictures. You use a car to go to school, work, or to play with friends. You use a bed to rest your body when you are tired.

When you use something, you take it and it becomes part of you. You might feel happy when you play with a ball. You might feel safe when you are in your room. You use the time you have to do things that make you feel good.

Sometimes, you use a word or a name. You might say, I love you. You use it to show how you feel. You use your name when you talk to people. You use your phone when you want to talk to someone.
