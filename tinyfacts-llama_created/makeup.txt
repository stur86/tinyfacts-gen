Make up is the stuff you put on your face. People use it to look good for work or for a night out with friends. It is often made of tiny bits that make your skin look nice.

You can buy a box of make up with many different colors. You put the color on your face, and it makes it look a bit like the sky, the sun, or your hair. Some colors are bright and make your face look happy. Other colors are darker and make your face look nice and round.

You can also use make up to cover up things that are not nice. If you have a big green mark on your face from a fight, you can use make up to make it look like it is gone. Or, if you have a red spot on your cheek from a story, you can use make up to cover it up.

Some people like to use make up to feel like a movie star. They might put red on their lips, blue on their eyes, and a bright color on their nose. They feel like they are in a movie and that everyone is looking at them. It is fun to feel like a different person every day.

You can also use make up to feel safe. If you are scared of something, you might put on a small bit of make up that makes you look calm. It helps you feel better when you are nervous.

But remember, make up is only for the face. It does not change how you are inside. If you are happy with who you are, you do not need make up.
