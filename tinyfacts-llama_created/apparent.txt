When something appears, it is already there. You do not need to see it to know it is there. You can hear a sound, feel the ground, or see a light without looking at it. You might not have seen it until you look at it. It is right in front of you.

Think of a tree. You see it from your house. But if you walk very far away, it is still there. It is right in front of you. It is not something you have to look at. You just know it is there because you have seen it before.

Some things can not be seen. A big blue sky is not something you see. It is the whole world up there. You are a part of it. You are not the whole sky. The sky is much bigger than you.

Sometimes you see something that is not really there. You might see a dog that is not really there. It is just a picture your brain makes. You did not really see it. It is something you are doing to your brain.

An apparently hard thing can be very easy to handle. If you are afraid of a thing, you might try to do not look at it. You can sit quietly. You are not really looking at it. You are just sitting there.
