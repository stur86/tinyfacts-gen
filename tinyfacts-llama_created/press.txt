To press something means to put a lot of force on it. You might press a door shut by pushing on it with your hands. You press a book so you can read it. You press your hand to your chest when you are afraid or sad.

When you press something, you are making it hard for air to go in or out. This can happen with a door, a box, or a chair. If you press on a door, it will not open. You have to let it go.

You also press on your face when you are angry. This makes your skin feel tight and hot. Your eyes might feel like they are going to pop out.

Sometimes, you press a small piece of paper. You press it down with your hand to make it hold its form. This helps you keep things straight.

To press can also mean to tell someone that something is true. You might say, I press you to tell the truth. This means you are making them say the right thing.

There is also a word called press that has nothing to do with the above. In this case, a press is a place where people write words and pictures. They sit at a big round thing and push papers down on a floor covered with soft stuff. This makes the papers turn into words. The press is how people make things that tell stories.
