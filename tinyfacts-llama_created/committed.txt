When you do something, you are said to be done with it. If you are not happy with a job, you might say you are done. If you decide to leave a job, you have already been done with it. You have made your mind up and you are ready to move on.

To be done can mean you are finished with a problem. If you have been working on a big question for a long time, you might feel that you are done. You have found the right answer or figured out how to do it.

It can also mean you are ready to do something new. If you have been waiting for a date with a friend, you might feel you are done. You have waited for the right time and now you are ready to go.

In a group, if everyone is done with a game, the game is over. The people who were first and stayed at the top are the ones who were done first. There is no one left to do the work anymore.

To be done is also about being finished with a feeling. If you have been feeling sad for a long time, you might feel done with the feeling. You have taken all the time you needed to work through it and now you are ready to move on.

Think of it like a book. If you read the whole story, you are done. You have reached the end and you can stop reading now.
