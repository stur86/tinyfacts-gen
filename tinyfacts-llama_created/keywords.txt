When people talk, they often use words for different reasons. To say something is easy is to say it is easy. To say something is hard is to say it is hard. One of these words is easy. One of these words is hard.

We also have words that help us find what we want. To find a word is to use these words. A word you use to find something is a key. Think about a phone. You do not look at all the numbers. You only look at one number. That one number is the key. If you do not know the key, you will not find what you want.

We also have words that help us tell a story. To tell a story is to use these words. One word tells you what is happening. Another word tells you why. Another word tells you who is doing it. A story uses many of these words. Without many of these words, a story would not be the same.

There are also words that help us talk to each other. To talk is to use these words. A word you use to talk to someone is a key. If you say the same word as the other person, you are using the key. If you use a word you do not know, you are not using the key.
