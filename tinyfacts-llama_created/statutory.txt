A word in our world is something that is made by a group of people, usually in a place, and it is about a way to act or a set of things that must be done.

Think of a big group of people who want to keep a place clean. They want to make sure everyone does the same things, like pick up their things when you leave, and does not do anything bad. So, they make a set of ways that everyone must follow.

This is like a big book with many lines. Each line is a way to act. If you follow the lines in this book, you are following the way the group wants everyone to act. The group does not change the lines. They keep the same ways they decided on.

Some of these ways are good for everyone. They keep the place safe and clean. Others are not so good. They might cause a lot of trouble or hurt people. In this case, the group might change the ways.

A word like this can also happen with money. If a group of people want to make sure everyone does the same things when they pay money to the place where money is kept, they can make a set of ways. These ways might be how much to pay, how to pay it, or how often it is paid. Everyone must follow these ways.

The word is important because it helps keep everyone on the same side. It gives a clear way for people to follow and for everyone to know what is expected of them.
