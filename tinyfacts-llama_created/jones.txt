The word is short for a person. A person who has a job is a job holder. To be a job holder means they work and make money for people who need them.

You can also use this word for a person who is very good at doing a job. If someone is a good job holder, they are very good at their work. They do it well and they keep their job.

Sometimes, a person who does a job is called a person of the job. This means the person is part of the job. They are a part of what the job is. This is why the word is short for job holder.
