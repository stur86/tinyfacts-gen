To make a thing is to put many small parts together. When you make a book, you start with one paper. You put many other papers on top of it one after another. You write things on each one. When all the papers are together, the book has a story.

You can also make a picture. You start with a small bit of color. You add more bits of color and more bits of color. As you add more, the color gets stronger and the picture gets clearer. You keep adding until the picture is just right.

Sometimes, you take many small things and put them together into one thing. You can make food from many different foods. You can make a drink from many different drinks. You can even make a thing that is made of many different parts.

When you make something, you use your hands and your brain. You think about what you want to do. You decide where to start and where to go. Your hands follow your brain. You press the things together and the thing comes into being. It is a good way to make new things.
