To pay something is to give money for it. You might pay for a thing you need. You might pay for food or for a book. When you pay a person, you give them money so they can do what they do. This helps the person and it also helps you.

Imagine you have money in your pocket. You want to buy a thing you like. If you pay for it, you give the money to the person who has it. The person takes the money and does what they say to do with it. They might use the money to buy food, clothes, or other things you need.

You can also pay for something if you do not have enough money. You might say, you paid for the movie. This means you gave money to the person who had the movie. The person shows the movie to you. You see the pictures and hear the sound.

When you pay, you give something in return. You do not get something back. You only get to keep your money. If you do not pay, you might have to do more work. You might have to give the money to the person instead of keeping it for yourself.

There are many ways to pay. You can pay with a piece of paper, a small bag of money, or even with a thing you make from your own hands. Some people use a computer to send money to someone else. This is how people can send money far away or to someone you do not know.
