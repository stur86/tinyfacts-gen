To be a part of something means you are a small part but you are still a part. You are not the only one but you are a group of people who share the same thing.

Think of a group of friends. If you are the one who says, I am a part of the group, you are the one who is part of the group. You are the one who is not alone. You are part of a bigger group. This is how the word works.

Imagine you have a big box of food. You eat a piece of food. Now you are part of the box of food. You have become a part of something. You are not the only one who has the food. You are also a part of the box.

Sometimes we use this word with people. If you have a sister, you are a part of her family. You are part of the family. You are not the only one. You are also part of her life.

You can also say you are a part of a story. If someone says, I am a part of the story, you are a part of the story. You are not the only one. You are part of the story too.

Being a part of something can make life easier. If you are a part of a team, you can do things together. You can help each other. You can do a job that needs to be done. You are not alone. You are part of a bigger group.
