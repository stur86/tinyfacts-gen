Pasts are the things that happened before now. We are always talking about the past because it is what is already gone.

Think of a tree. It has been standing for many years. You can see it from your house. It has seen many suns and many nights. It is old. The past is like the tree. It is something that has already happened.

We use past to talk about the things that are done in the past. If you tell someone you saw a movie last night, you are talking about the past. If you remember a fight, you are also talking about the past.

The word can also mean something that happened a long time ago that you do not remember. A long time ago, you might have seen a dog that you had never seen before. The dog was very big. You do not remember its name because it was so many years ago.

When we talk about the past, we are looking back at what is already happened. We are trying to figure out what happened before we were here.
