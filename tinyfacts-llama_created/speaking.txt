To speak means you use your voice. Your voice is a sound that comes from your body. You can make this sound by moving your lips, the skin in your face, the back of your throat, and the air from your mouth.

When you speak, you tell other people what you think. You might say a story, tell a joke, or explain how something works. You use words that mean things. Each word tells a small part of what you are saying.

You can speak many different ways. You can speak very fast and loud, or you can speak very quiet and slow. This is how people hear each other.

You can also speak to animals. When you talk to them, your voice helps them understand you. This is why you might use words that mean food, water, or a place.

Sometimes, you speak to your own mind. When you think, you are speaking to yourself. Your mind is like a big computer that helps you remember things and make plans for the day.

You can also speak to the world. You might speak about things you care about, like the air we breathe, the water we drink, or the trees that grow around us. Your voice can help people understand and learn.

When you speak, you are using your voice to share your ideas and your stories. This is an important way to build a world of understanding between people.
