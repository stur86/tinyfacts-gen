A house where people live is called a home. This is a place you come back to after you are gone from the city. It has a door and windows. Inside there are many rooms. Some rooms are for sleeping. Some rooms are for eating. There is also a room for the people who take care of you when you are a baby. Some houses have a big room with a high wall. This room is for people to sit and talk.

People also use a word that means a small house. This is a small home. It might be on top of a big building. It has a small door. Inside there is only one room. Some small houses have a tiny room on the top. The people who live there do not have a door on the bottom. This makes the house feel like it is in the middle of the city.

A big house for many people is called a high building. It is much taller than a small house. Many stories are above the ground. Some high building houses have many rooms and many floors. On the floor with the most rooms, there is a room with a big door. This room is for the top people. On the floor with the least rooms, there is a room for the people who do not speak the same words as everyone else.
