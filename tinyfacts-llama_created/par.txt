The word parked is often confused with the word park. In the first, a car is moved to a place. In the second, a child makes a game where they move a small round thing to a big area. People also use it to mean a very strong person. When you are very strong, you might be called a parked person.

In the first, you move the car to a new spot. In the second, you are the one who makes the round thing move. A park is a big area where people can go to play. There are many kinds of parks. Some have big trees and many green areas. Some have many tall things and a big blue sky. Some are very quiet and have only a few people.

To park a car is to put it on the ground so it will stay there. This is important because it is a place where people can be safe. If you drive to a park and do not put the car down, the person might get hurt.
