To consider something means to think about it. You might not know the answer right away. Then you think about it some more, and you start to see it in a different way. This can be very good. It helps you learn and grow.

When you are sad or angry, you might feel that you are alone. You might feel like the world is too big for you. But if you can think about what is really going on, you can start to feel better. You are not alone. There are many people who care about you.

A computer has a way to think. It can do many things quickly and stay calm even when things are very busy. It does not get angry or sad like a human does. It is just doing what it was made to do.

A person can also consider a problem. They think about what is going on, who is hurt, and how to fix it. They try to find a way that will help everyone. This is important because it helps us understand what is happening and how to act.

To show you something means to let someone see you. You might be afraid to show your face to a big group of people. But if you trust someone, they help you. They let you see them so you can understand them better.

A game is a way to show you how to act. You play with other people who have the same idea. You try to be the best. You can use your head, your body, and your heart. When you are good at a game, you feel happy and you want to play again.
