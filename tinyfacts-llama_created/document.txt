To write something is to put words on paper. When you write, you use your mind to tell stories, make things clear, or tell what happened. A paper is a thing that holds these words. You can hold the paper in your hand, or you can put it on a table or a wall.

A word is just one tiny thing that tells something. If you want to tell a story, you write words one after another. Each word is like a small piece of the story. If you have many of these words, you have a story.

A group of words can be a story. A book is a group of many words. A person can write their own story. A doctor writes a story about how a person feels. An animal writes a story about what it sees.

When people write, they can send their story to other people. If you send a story, you are giving them something to read. This can be fun. It lets you share your ideas with others.

A person can also write about what is happening. They write about what they see, hear, or feel. This can help us understand the world better. It gives us a way to think about what is going on.

Sometimes, a group writes a story together. This is called a story. They all use their words to make a big picture. This can be a happy story, a sad story, or a story about a person who did something new.

You can write your own story too. You can use your mind and your words to tell a story. You can write about your day, your life, or a place you love. Writing is a way to show the world who you are and what you feel.
