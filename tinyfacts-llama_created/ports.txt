A place that lets people and things go in and out is called a part. You might hear about parts in the home or the city. It is where water from a big body of water goes into a small area. This area is safe for people to enter and for things to come out.

Imagine a long road that joins many big bodies of water. Along the road, there are many stops where you can leave your car and walk into the next city. Or, you can walk across the water and get to the other side. This is like a small part where people and things leave and enter.

Sometimes, parts are big and cover many cities. They are the places where you can get a train or a bus to go to a different city. They are also the places where you buy food, clothes, or other things you need.

Parts are important because they let people move and things be brought to each other. They help our world stay strong and our lives go well.
