Class is a word that tells us about a group of things that are all the same. It is used to talk about people, animals, and things that are made of the same stuff.

Think of a group of your friends. You are all the same. You all like the same games, the same food, and the same kind of car. You are all in the same group. You all feel like you are one person.

When we say someone is a class of things, we mean they are all the same kind. If you have a lot of books, you are a class of books. If you have a lot of dogs, you are a class of dogs. You are all the same kind of animal.

This word is also used to talk about groups of people who share a strong idea. Imagine a group of people who believe in a cause. They are all part of the same group. They have the same ideas and the same way of thinking. They are all part of the group.

We use this word to explain why something is the same. It helps us see that everything is the same.
