To be dead is to be very, very far from being around anymore. It means you are no longer in your body and you are not coming back.

When you are dead, you are not going to get any more air. You need to breathe, and you can't breathe anymore. Your body stops making food for you, and your body starts to break down. This is why you look like a very still thing.

Even if you are dead, you are still a person. You had a life, you had a family, you had friends, and you had hopes. You were once a living being, and you will always be remembered.

But because you are dead, there is nothing you can do. There is no more fear, no more pain, no more hope. You are just a thing that has stopped moving. It is very hard to understand.

Sometimes, people say that a dead person is like a tree that has fallen to the ground. They are not there anymore, but they are still there. They are a part of the world, even if they are not a part of it anymore.

Also, you can be dead without being gone. You can be a memory from the past. You can be a story that someone told. You can be a picture in a book that someone read. You can even be a feeling that someone felt.
