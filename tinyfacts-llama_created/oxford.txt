An old word for a big city in a far place. People use it when they talk about things they are studying. If you want to know something very well, you might go to a place like this. It is often the city where a very old school is, and the school is where many people go to learn for a long time.

Many years ago, when the world was different, this city was the place where the most important words in the world were written. The city was where people who were very good at writing and thinking went to learn. They were the ones who made the books that tell stories and help people understand the world. This city is still the place where the most important words are made today.

The word is also used to talk about books. If you read a book that is very good and important, you might feel that you have learned a lot. In this way, the city is like a book that is very important for people to know.
