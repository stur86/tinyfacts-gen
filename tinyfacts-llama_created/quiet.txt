The word quiet means a place where people do not make a lot of noise. When you are quiet, you do not talk as much as you do when you are talking. You do not make a sound that is very loud.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. If you have a lot of things to say, you will make a lot of noise. But if you sit in a quiet room, you will be very quiet. You might even fall asleep!

Sometimes quiet is important. If a child is quiet, it means they are happy and do not want to play. But if a room is very quiet, it can be a bit boring. You might hear only a few sounds, like a soft drink being opened or a soft moving thing.

You can also quiet things that are moving. If a car is very quiet, it means the people inside are not talking and the car is not moving very fast. If a dog is quiet, it means it is not browning its teeth or making a lot of noise.

We also need quiet times. If you stay in a quiet room for a long time, you can feel calm and safe. You can close your eyes and think about what you want to do next.

To quiet a person means to make them stop talking or do not make them angry. If you say sorry to someone who is quiet, they will often feel better and stop being angry.
