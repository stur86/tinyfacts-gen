To be strong and stand up against something is to show that you are not afraid. When you are very sure about something, you are not going to give up. You are ready to try it, even if it is hard or if it is not easy. This is a good thing to have inside you. It helps you to do what you want to do.

Imagine you want to move to a new city. You have never lived there before. You are very afraid. But you say to yourself, I am going to this city. I am going to learn this new town. I am going to try new food. I am going to make new friends. You are not afraid because you are sure that you can do it.

Some people get things they want by working hard and being very good at what they do. They are good at their job or their game. They do not give up. They keep trying until they get what they want.

Being strong with your head is also important. It means you do not give up when things are hard. You stay calm and you do what is right even if it is a very hard thing to do. This helps you to stay safe and to stay happy.

To be strong and stand up against something means you are a person who is ready to try and to act. You do not let fear stop you. You keep going even when it is hard.
