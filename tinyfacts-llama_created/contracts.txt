To sign something means to put your name on it. You and another person are agreeing to do something. This is when you say you will do a job, or you will give money, or you will do something for a person.

Imagine you buy a book. You do not just pay the money. You also agree to come back to the store and buy another book. This is like a small group of people who agree to do something together. One person buys a car. Another person buys a computer. They signed a paper to be sure they are doing it.

In business, you might sign a paper with a friend to buy a car. This is like a deal. You and your friend agree to pay money and to drive the car at a later date. If you do not pay, your friend can take the car away. This is like a small group of people who agree to do something together.

You can also sign a paper with a school. This is like a promise to follow the school ways. If you do not follow, you might get in trouble. You are agreeing to follow the school and its teachers.

There are many kinds of papers. Some papers are about money. Some papers are about people. Some papers are about things you own. Sometimes, you sign a paper with a person to pay them money. Sometimes, you sign a paper to help something.
