To be in a higher place than someone else is to be better. If you are the top dog in your school, you are the one who does everything. If you are the one who gets the best job, you are the one who gets paid the most money.

You can also be in a higher place than someone who is smaller. If you are taller than a little girl, you are better than her. If you are older than a younger boy, you are better than him.

To be in a higher place is to have more power. You have more money. You have more friends. You are more important. You can do more things.

Sometimes, we use this word with people who have done bad things. If someone is in a higher place than another person, it means they did more wrong things. It means they are worse than the other person.

To be in a higher place is also to be in a group with many other people. When you are in a group, you are part of a team. You are not alone. You are with other people who care about you.

Being in a higher place is a good feeling. It makes you feel like you are the best. You feel strong and happy. It makes you want to be the best at all times.
