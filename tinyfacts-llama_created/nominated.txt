To be named is when someone is picked to do a job. A person or a group of people are asked to do it. They must be good at it before they can be named.

Imagine a school. Each student has a name. Later, they are asked to write an answer for a class. Some students write the best answers. They get a name. Later, a teacher says, your name is the best. This is being named.

In a city, a person might get a name if they do something good. They might be a teacher, a doctor, or a person who helps people. They are named because they did well.

A person can also be named if they join a team. They might join a team that fights against a bad team. If they do well in the game, they get a name. This is because they were the best one on the team.

Sometimes, a person might not be named. This is because they did not do well. They might have been very bad at the job. The people in the city might not want them to be their name.

In some places, a person can not give their name at first. They must learn how to use their new name before they can use it. This is important for their life and for the life of those around them.
