To be in a place means you are there. But if you are in a far place, you may feel scared or lost. You might not know the way back home. That is when a person or a thing helps you find your way.

If you want to go to a place, you must first know how to get there. You read a book. You look at a picture. You learn about the roads and the houses. You may watch a movie with a man who is a man of the road. He shows you how to cross a wide water.

Then, you do something. You hold a small thing that shows you where you are. You look at it. It tells you which way to turn. You walk in the right direction until you see a sign that says you are near your place. You might feel a bit of a rush. You are going home!

In the water, you need to know how to use a thing that helps you find your way. You look at a small picture. You find a line. You follow the line to a big rock. You cross the rock. You see the big water on the other side. You follow the water and it takes you to your place.

This is how people and animals move through the world. It is about using the world to your way. It is about following the signs, the lines, and the pictures that help you find your way home.
