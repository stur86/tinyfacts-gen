The town where the bad guys were found is called Small. It is a small town with not many people. It is in a state with many big cities. The town has a high school, a hospital, and a police office. The people who live there are often afraid because the police and the school are very good at keeping everyone safe.

The town has a big building where people go to be heard. This is where the police and the school go when they need to tell the whole town what is going on. The people in Small are trying to stay safe because of this building. They are afraid because of what they heard from the man who was found in the water.
