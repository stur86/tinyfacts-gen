When you say something is quite, you mean it is very good. It is better than another thing you have seen.

You might say a person is quite nice. This means they are very kind. You like them because they are so good with words and with their face.

You can also say something is quite cold. This means it is very, very cold. If you wear a heavy jacket or you drink something hot, it will feel like the room is very cold.

Sometimes, when you are walking through a park, you hear a song you like. You might say the song is quite fun. This means it is very good. You want to listen to it again and again.

You can not say something is quite small. Small is a tiny thing. It is less than one foot long. If something is not quite small, it might be big or very heavy.

Being quite can mean you are very good at something. If you are quite good at drawing, you can draw a picture that is very nice. It is very nice to look at.

So, when you say something is quite, you are telling someone it is very good.
