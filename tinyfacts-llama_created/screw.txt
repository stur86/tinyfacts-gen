To put something into a place

You might use a small thing with a long part to fix something. This part is often round or pointed. You put the round part into a thing with a top, so it can hold something. You push the long part into the top until it is in the right place.

Sometimes you put a round thing with lines on it into a small box or on a thing that holds papers. This small round thing turns and fits into the box. The box then holds the papers or other things you need to keep.

You can also use this word for a thing that holds things together. If you put many things together, you can make a big thing that holds many more things. A small round thing can be part of this big thing.

In another way, to put something into a place can mean to keep it safe. If you put money in a bag, you put it into a place where it is not hurt. You keep it there so it does not go away and make trouble.
