A bright, hot thing that goes out from a big star.

When a star gets very very old, it runs out of the power to burn. This happens when the star has been burning for so long that there is nothing left to burn. The star then gets very hot and starts to shake and shake. This shaking makes a huge burst of light and light that goes out into space. This light is so bright that it can be seen from many, many light years away.

The light comes from a place very close to the star. This place is like a tiny ball of fire, but much smaller than the star. When the light from the ball of fire reaches us, it is what we see. The light is so bright that if you saw it in person, you would be very afraid.
