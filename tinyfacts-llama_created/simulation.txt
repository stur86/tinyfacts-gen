A computer game is a thing that lets you play a game where a person does not move. The game is not real. But you can play it on your computer or phone.

Imagine you are in a big room with many other people. But you are not really there. You are just watching the people and listening to their voices. You can not see the people or hear them. You can only think about what they say and what they do. This is like a game.

There is also a computer game that makes you feel like you are in a real world. You can walk through a city. You see houses, cars, and people. You can feel the sun on your face. But you are still not really there. The computer does everything.

A computer game can be about fighting a bad person. You are the person and you do things to stop them. Or it can be about a group of people who work together to save a city. You have to use your brain to figure out how to do things so the people can stay safe.

Sometimes, a computer game lets you talk to a friend who is not really there. You send a word to the computer and it says the word back. You feel like you are really talking to your friend. This can be very fun.

You can also play games with numbers and pictures. You try to get the right numbers so the picture turns out right. This is like a game of numbers.

Computers can also play games with moving pictures and sounds. The computer makes the moving pictures and the sounds. You see the moving pictures and hear the sounds. You feel like you are really moving through the pictures.

All these things are called computer games. They are fun ways to play and to learn.
