To do something is to act on the world. When you do something, you change the world in some way. You take something that was not yourself and turn it into something new.

If you write a story, you did something. You took the words and made them into a new story. You did this because you wanted to share your ideas with others.

Sometimes, you might do something that you do not want to do. You might feel bad after you do it. You might want to stop doing it, but you can't. This is when you have to live with the thing you did.

When things are made or done, they can be kept or they can be gone. When you make something new, you keep it as your own. If you do something bad, you might have to pay for it later.

In our world, we use things every day. We use a computer to learn, we use a phone to call our friends, and we use a book to learn. We keep these things as our own because we use them to live.

But sometimes, things are taken away. If you break a window, the person who owns it might take it. This happens when you are not supposed to have something that is not yourself. If you take something that is not yourself, you have to give it back.

When something is gone, it is not there anymore. You can not see it, you can not hear it, and you can not feel it. It is only in the past, and you can not change it anymore.

Sometimes, we talk about things that are gone. We say, like, when something is gone, it is gone. We do not try to bring it back. We just accept it is not there anymore.
