When you lead a person, a thing, or a group of people, you are the one who starts them. You tell them what to do. You decide where they should go. You show them the way. You are the one who says, here is what we have to do.

Think about a dog. If your dog is not sure, you say, go this way. You show him the way to the food. He follows you. He does not think for himself. He only does what you say. You are the one who leads him.

Sometimes, when you lead a team of people to a place, you are the one who makes the plan. You decide what to do. You talk to the people. You tell them what to do. You are the one who tells them what to do.

You can also lead a game. You tell the other people what to do. You give them the order. They follow you. This is how you lead a game.

In a long story, a person may lead a group of people. They might be the one who starts the fight. They might be the one who says, we should do this. They might lead the people to a safe place.

When you lead, you show them what to do. You show them the right way. You show them how to act. You show them how to be strong. You show them how to be safe.
