When we write a word, we use our own voice. It is like a story. We put down the letters one by one. A word is a thing that you say or write.

But when you want to keep your word safe, you use a set of numbers and letters. This is a way of writing. You write down the same numbers and letters over and over again. You do this so that only one person can understand the word.

Think of a phone call. When you talk to a friend, you use words. But if you want to send money to someone, you use numbers and letters. You put the numbers and letters in a way that only the person you are sending the money to can read them.

There are many kinds of these sets. Some are very easy to use. Others are very hard. You have to be very good at reading them to use them. But they keep your word safe.

You can also use these sets to write things that you do not want anyone else to know. You can write a story that is not true. You can use these sets to keep your life very quiet. They help you keep your word safe and your life safe.
