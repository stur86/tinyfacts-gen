To write something on paper is the same as to make it. When you write a name, a story, or a picture, you put words and numbers on the paper. You put your hand on the paper. The paper is the thing you use. It is like a box where words go into.

If you have a book, you can read it. The words are in the book. You can not make the words come out of your hand. The words are already there.

When you make a picture, you are using the word. You use the same word to tell the picture. You put the word in front of the picture. The picture is made from the words.

You can also make a picture with a computer. You use the computer to tell it what to do. You tell the computer the words and numbers. The computer uses those words to make the picture.

When you make a picture, you make something that is not there before. The picture is something new. It is not just the words. It is also the way you put the words together.
