A work is something you do to get things done. It is a way to move things from one place to another. Imagine you are a doctor. You take a person to the doctor. The doctor sees the person. The doctor tells the person what to do to get better. You help the person. You are the one who does the work.

A work can be very long. A long time might go by before you finish. You might feel tired and want to stop. But you keep going. This is because you are doing something important.

A work can be done by many people. Each person does a little part. They all work together. They talk to each other and show what they are doing. If one person does not do their part, the work might not get done.

Sometimes, a work is not just one person. It is many people. Many people work together. They do their parts. They all help to get the work done.

There are also things called a work and a job. A job is when you work for money. You do a job to get money so you can live. A work is what you do.
