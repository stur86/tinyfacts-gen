When you are in school, you have many classes. Most of these are from the morning to the afternoon. You go to these classes in order. You do not have to go to them every day. In the morning, you go to the first class. Then you go to the next one, and so on. After school, the day is almost over. This is when the really important parts happen.

There are four of these days. One is in the morning. The day after that is in the afternoon. The one after that is in the morning again. The one after that is in the afternoon again. This is the day you do not go to school. This is when you have your finally.

The word comes from a story. There was a man. He had a big job. He had to do many things. He had to do them all before the end of the year. The end of the year is the end of the day. So, he had his finally. This means he finished his job. He did all the things he had to do.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a game. A game is a way you play with a ball. You run with the ball. You try to get it to the other side of the field. You are the one who tries to get the ball. This is your finally. You have done everything you can do with the ball.

In school, we have a finally too. We have many classes and we take a big paper. The paper has many questions. If you answer all the questions, you get a big check. This check is your finally.
