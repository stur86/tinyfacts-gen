To suggest something is true can be a good thing. You might say something that is not really true but you think it sounds nice. This is called a guess.

If you think a movie is going to be very funny, you might say, I think this movie is going to be a lot of fun. If you do not see the movie, you still feel good. You might even laugh. This is how you try to make other people feel the way you do.

Sometimes, we want to do something but we do not know if it is right. In this case, we might say we want to go for a walk. We do not know if the walk will be good for us. We might think about it for a little while. If we are still thinking about it, we might decide to do it. This is a way of asking for help.

A person who wants to do something can also say they want to give something. They might want to give a book to a friend. They do not know if their friend will like it, but they think it is a kind thing to do. This is another way of suggesting something is true.
