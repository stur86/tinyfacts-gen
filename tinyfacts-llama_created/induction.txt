To use the word means to find out. You start with something, and then you use it to show how things work. You see how it works on your own body, then you use it to make something work for other people. This helps you build new things that do the same thing without you having to tell them how.

Imagine you want to make a fire. You do not start it. You do not put the wood in. You just leave it and let it happen. You did not tell the fire to start. The fire did not start. You only help it go from one place to another. This is what it means to use something to find out how it works.

In some places, people use this word to talk about how a person feels. If you say a person is happy, you did not tell them to feel that way. You showed them that they are happy. You let them find out for themselves.

The word can also mean when you try something. If you try to fly a big thing, you are using it to find out how it works. You do not tell it to fly. You just let it try and see what happens. You learn from what happens.

Sometimes, the word is used to talk about people who are good at making things. They do not tell you how to make something. You see how they do it and then you try to make it too. They let you learn from their work.
