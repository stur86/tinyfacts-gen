To get a thing you want can be a good thing. You might get a job you love. You might get a new car. You might even get a pretty dress. 

Sometimes, you get a thing you need. A doctor might give you a drink to make you feel better. A teacher might give you a book to read. You might get a food you like. This is when you get what you need.

You can also get a thing that is not what you wanted. If you buy a book you do not like, you have gotten something that is not good for you. If you get a job that makes you very tired, you have gotten a thing that is bad for you.
