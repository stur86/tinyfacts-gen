To make something is to take pieces and make them into a new thing. When you make something, you are doing the same thing many times over. You start with one piece and do it many times. After you have done it many times, you have a much bigger piece than you started with.

Flown is a word that means you are moving through a water or a stuff that goes down a big high place. You might walk on a floor or stand in a room. If you walk, you are moving. You are not standing still.

You can also say that something is made of small pieces. You can see these small pieces all around you. They are the tiny parts that make the thing you see. When you make something, you are putting these pieces together. You are taking the small parts and making them into a new thing.

Sometimes, when you make something, you need to use a kind of white stuff. This white stuff helps the small pieces stay together. It keeps them from falling off. This white stuff is very important for making many things.
