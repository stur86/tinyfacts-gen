To have an opening means you start something. You can start a story, a game, a plan, a song, or a new way of doing things.

An opening can also mean when the beginning of a world ends. If a world is full of green, but then the ground turns red and the air becomes hot, the world ends. This ends one kind of life and starts another.

Sometimes an opening is very small. A tiny opening is like a tiny door that you can not see. You can not see it, but you know it is there.

Big opening means a very wide place. A wide road or a wide open space is a big opening. This is where you can drive, walk, or fly to many places.

An opening can also mean when you start to learn. If you have an opening in your mind, you are ready to understand new things. This is a good thing because it means you are learning and growing.

A word can have many different starts. If you say the word, the sound changes. If you say the word again, the sound is different. This is how words change and grow.
