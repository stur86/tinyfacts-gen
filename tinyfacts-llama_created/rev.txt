To turn something around

You can turn a thing around to change its direction. If you are walking and you want to go to the left, you can turn your body. You turn your feet around. The front of your body points to the right.

You can also turn a thing around when you are moving it. If you roll a ball, you are turning it. The ball moves around in a big round way.

A car can turn around too. You use the back of the car to change direction. If you want to go to a different place, you turn the car around so the back goes to the left or the right. This helps you avoid other cars.

When you do this, you are changing the way you are facing. You are turning your body around.
