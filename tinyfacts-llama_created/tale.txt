To tell a story is to share what happened. You might tell a story to your friends or family. You use words to show what is happening. You might use pictures too. This is a way to share a story with others.

You can also tell a story to a group of people. This is called a group story. Many people listen and try to follow the story. It is a way to share ideas and make people feel happy or sad.

Sometimes, a story is about something that happened in the past. You might learn about a person, a place, or a time. You learn about how they lived and what they did. This story can help us understand the world better.

Stories can also be about what is happening now. You might hear a story about a new thing that happened. Or you might hear a story about how to do something. This is a way to learn new things.

You can tell a story by yourself. You can sit down and think about what happened. You can write down what you think. Or you can talk to a friend or family person about what happened.

Sometimes, you might not tell the whole story. You might leave out parts or add your own ideas. This is okay. People like stories because they can be different. You might hear a story, then you add your own ideas. This way, you can share what you think.
