To set a high point as a way to see if something is really good.

Imagine you are a runner. You want to be the fastest person in your town. You start to run every day. You are always the first person to finish. You feel like you are the best. But you are not the only one. There is another person who also runs. This person is very fast too. They finish before you.

One day, you see the other person and you feel a bit sorry. You think the other person is really good. You want them to be the one to beat you. So you decide to set a high point for yourself. You run every day and you get faster and faster. You start to think you are the best runner in the town.

This is a way to see if you are really the best. You are setting a high point to show everyone that you are the greatest. It is like making a listened to show that you are the best at something.

You can do this with any thing. You can set a high point for how fast you can walk. You can set a high point for how many books you can read in one day. You can even set a high point for how well you can play a game. The point is to show everyone what you can do.
