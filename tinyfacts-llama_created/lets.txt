Lets is a word we use when we want to say many things at once.

Imagine you are playing with a friend. You want to build a big thing. You start with a small thing. Then you add more and more small things. After a while, you have a big thing. You are happy because you did it.

You can also use this word with numbers. If you have five books, you say, let it be five. It means you want the number five. If you have ten books, you say, let it be ten. It means you want the number ten.

Sometimes, we use lets to say we are going to do something. If we are going to eat dinner, we say, let us eat. This means we are going to eat.

We can also use lets to say we are waiting for something. If you are waiting for your friend to come home, you say, let me wait. This means you are waiting for them.

The word lets is important because it helps us say many things at once. It is a word that makes things easier.
