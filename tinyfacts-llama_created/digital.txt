To send something through the air using a box or a small computer is called sending. But when you send something through the air, the box or computer does not hold the thing. The box or computer sends the thing to a far away place.

The thing that you send is made out of many tiny pieces. When these pieces are in the air, they can not be seen. But when they get to the far away place, they come together again. The computer puts the pieces back into one place, and the box or computer sends the thing.

The computer also sends things. You can send a picture from your phone to a friend. You can send a word, like a story. You can even send a song. The computer sends this thing over the air, and the box or computer gets it.

Because everything sent is made out of many tiny pieces, you can send many of them at the same time. This is why the world is full of many small computers and boxes that send and get things.
