To power something means to give it the power it needs to work. Think of a computer. Without power, the computer stays dark. But if you turn it on, the computer starts to work and makes the pictures and words on the computer appear.

A person who does this is called a power person. They show many pictures with words, and the pictures tell a story. They might show how to do something new. Or they might show how to fix a problem.

When a person talks like this, they use words that are very fast. They move their hands and eyes to show the pictures. They use these words to help people understand. So, the word power point is used to talk about a way to show a lot of things quickly.
