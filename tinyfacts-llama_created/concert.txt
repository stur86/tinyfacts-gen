A group of people playing music together is called a group. In this group, the people have the same sound. They play the same songs over and over. They might singled out one song and do it many times.

When this happens, many people go to watch. They stand in a big room and listen. They all hear the same music. Some people might dance. Some people might feel happy. Some people might feel sad.

In a group like this, people work together. They follow a person who is the lead. They listen to him and follow what he says. They do not play their own music. They play the same music every time.

Sometimes, a group plays for money. The lead person asks people to pay money to hear the music. This is how a lot of money can be made from one group. The people who work there have to make sure they play the music very well so everyone likes it.

A big group with many people is called a band. Some bands have many people. Some bands have only one person. They all play the same music.
