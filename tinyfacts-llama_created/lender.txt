A person who gives money to another is called a money person. They take your money and give it to you when you need it. When you say you need money to buy food, you are asking a money person. They give you the money.

You can also ask a money person to give you money so you can do something. If you want to go to the movies, you can ask your mom or dad to give you the money. The money person gives it to you and you can go.

Money people usually work with many other people. One person might give you money to buy a car, and another person might give you money to buy a house. When you all work together, you have money to buy what you want.

The word money person is important because it helps us live. Without them, we would have nothing. So we are very happy when a money person helps us.
