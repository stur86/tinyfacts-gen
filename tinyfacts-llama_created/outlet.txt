An open point in a wall is called an opening. It lets air, water, or power go out of a house, school, or shop. You can feel the air when you open a window. You can also hear the sound of running water when you turn a handle in a shower or a kitchen.

A door is a bigger opening. It opens to let people in or out. You need to push the door or pull it to open it. Some doors have a lock to keep people out. A door can also have a window to let in the light during the day.

An opening can be under the floor of a house. When the power goes out, the lights go down and the air in the room goes out too. You can hear the air moving around.

Some cars have an opening. This is a door that can open to let people in or out. It is usually on the side of the car. The people inside can get in and out of the car quickly. The door can close when the car moves fast, like when you drive on a road.
