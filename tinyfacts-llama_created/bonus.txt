A big number of money you get when you do a job well. This money is usually given by a person who works with you. The money is not the job you did, but because you did it well. It is like a small present for being good.

Imagine you work for a store and you do a job very well. The store manager is very happy with you. As a thank you, the manager gives you a few hundred more money than you usually get. This is a nice surprise.

The money can be in a few ways. You might get money at the end of a week. Or you might get money at the beginning of the month. It is a way to show you are important.

Some jobs give you a big number of these small presents. This can help you buy things you want or pay for the work you do. It can also help you feel happy when you see the money.

The word comes from a time when people gave money to the people who build roads. If you did a job well, you were allowed to keep some of the money. Over time, people started giving money for doing a job well instead. Today, everyone gets a small number of these presents when they work well.
