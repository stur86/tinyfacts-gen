To accept something means you take it. You do not fight it. You do not say you do not like it. You just say it is okay.

Imagine you are very happy. You have a new dog. You are so happy that you start to cry. You are not afraid of the dog. You do not try to stop it from being there. You just feel good because of it.

Accepting is important for you to feel safe. It is also important for you to be happy when people give you things. If you do not accept a small thing, you might feel angry. But if you accept it, you can use it later.

There are two ways to accept. One way is to say you accept it. You might say, I accept this thing. Another way is to feel it. If you feel something is right, you accept it.
