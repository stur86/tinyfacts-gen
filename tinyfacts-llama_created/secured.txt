To be in a good place or to be safe is to be in a good situation. You are not afraid or worried. You do not think about things that might go wrong.

If you buy something, you have made it safe for you. You did not have to worry about it. You are in a good place. You can do what you want without fear.

Imagine you are at a store. You buy a book. You feel safe. You are not afraid. You can leave and go home. You are in a good place. You did not have to worry.

You can also be in a good place if you are in a place that is safe. If you are in your home, you are safe. You can sleep without being afraid. You are in a good place.

Sometimes, you might feel safe in a place that is not your own. Maybe you are in a big city. You are not afraid of the people or the cars. You feel safe because you are not there. This is also in the good place.

To be in a good place can make you feel happy. You might feel safe and calm. You can do what you want. You are happy because you are not afraid.
