To pack something means to put it in a way that it will not fall down or get lost. When you are about to move to a new place, you often need to pack your things so you can leave easily.

Imagine you have many books and you want to take them to a school far away. You can put the books in a big box with soft stuff on the bottom. This helps the box stay closed and does not let the books fall. You also put the box in a car so it does not get broken. When you are done, you can open the box and find all your books inside.

You can also pack food for a trip. You make a box with a cover so the food does not get wet. Inside, you put the food in a way that it stays together. This way, you can carry the food with you without having to worry about it falling out.

When you are a person, you also pack your ideas and your heart into your mind. You take what you want and put it inside. This is how you learn and grow.

In our day, we often say someone is packed. It means they are ready for a big change. If a person is packed, they are strong and ready to face anything that comes their way.
