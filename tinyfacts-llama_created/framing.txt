To build something can be hard. You need many pieces of wood or other stuff. You use these pieces to make a wall or a box. Inside the box, you put things you want to keep. You put the wall in front of you so you can see everything inside.

Sometimes, a person is a builder. They build houses and big things for people to live in. They use their body to push the wood into the ground and then they use a power thing to make the wood go up into the sky. They put the walls around the house so it is safe and strong.

A person who works with wood is called a from. They make things from wood. They cut the wood into the right pieces. They use a power thing to make the wood go. They then put the wood together to make a box or a wall.

A building is a big thing made of many parts. It can be a house, a school, a hospital, or a big building for a city. It is the place where people live, work, and play.
