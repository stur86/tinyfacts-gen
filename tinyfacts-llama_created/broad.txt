To be wide means you are very big. You are not small like a tiny thing. You are also very open. You can show the world what you have to say. This is why a wide road is important for people to move around.

Imagine a long line with many ends. The ends are far from each other. The line goes out into the water or across the ground. This line is very wide and helps people cross far places.

You can also be wide in other ways. You can be a wide body of water. The water is big and deep. It covers much of the ground. You can see very far in the water because the water is so clear. Or, you can be a wide mouth in a land. This is where a big water goes out into the seating water.

A wide person is also someone who is very tall. They are often over six feet tall. This is because their body is very big. Some people have a wide face, too. They have a round face with lots of skin around their eyes and mouth. This makes them look very big.

Being wide can also mean you are very good at something. You might be wide at a fast game or a big play. You use your long arms and your strong legs to move the ball or run around the field.
