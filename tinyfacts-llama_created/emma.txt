To empty something is to have nothing inside it. You can do this with a cup. If you drink from it, the cup is empty. You can also do this with your body. If you stop eating, your body is empty.

A person who does this is called an empty one. They do not have any food inside them. They are very quiet and do not talk much. They may look sad or tired. They might even cry. 

Sometimes, when a person is very old, they become empty. This happens when the body stops growing and the food does not come in anymore. This is why old people often look very old.
