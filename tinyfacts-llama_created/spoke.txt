To speak means you use your voice to tell people what you think. You use words to say your ideas. You might tell a story, ask a question, or tell someone you love them.

When you speak, your body moves. Your chest goes up and down. Your face changes. You might look at the person you are talking to. You use your arms to show your words. You might shake your head, look down, or hold your hand up.

You can speak to many people at once. You can talk to a group of people or just one person. You can speak in many different ways. You can use your voice very loud or very soft. You can use your voice to be funny, to be serious, or to be calm.

Sometimes you speak when you want something. You might ask for money, a phone, or a friend. When you speak, you are using your voice to get what you want.

People also speak when they are sad. They might cry and say words that help them feel better. They speak so the other person can understand what they are feeling. They use words that are kind and help them feel less alone.

In a big group, people speak to each other. They talk about what they see, what they think, and what they want. They listen to each other and try to understand each other. This helps them work together and make good plans.

When you speak, you are sharing your own story. You are showing the world who you are and what you believe. It helps us learn about each other and understand why people act in different ways.
