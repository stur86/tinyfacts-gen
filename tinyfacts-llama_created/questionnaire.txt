A question is a thing you ask. You say to someone, or to a group of people, what they think or feel. It can be about a person, a thing, a place, or a thing that happened. A person might answer your questions to learn more about them.

Sometimes, people ask this kind of question to see how many people think a thing is the same way. This helps people understand if a thing is important or not important. It is like a big game to see how many people agree or do not agree.

There are many different kinds of questions. You can ask a person one question and then another, or you can ask many questions at once. You can ask about one thing or many things. You can ask about how something is or what something is like.

When you ask questions, you are trying to find out more about something. You want to know the truth. It is an important thing to learn.
