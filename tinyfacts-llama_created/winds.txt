Winds are moving air. When the air moves from one place to another, it can go very fast. This can happen when the sun makes the air warm and the ground gets hot. This warm air wants to go down and join the cool air higher up.

Imagine you are in a room with a window. The air that comes out of the window is moving in a way that is different from the air coming in through the window. The air that comes out of the window is usually going very fast because it is moving away from the window. This is kind of like what happens with the winds on a big water.

The wind can go in many different directions. It can go from the side, from the top, from the bottom, or it can go around in a big ring. When the wind is going in a big ring, it can be very strong. This is why you might see many moving pieces of wood on the water when you are on a big water and the wind is coming in from the top.

Winds can also make things move. If you are standing on the ground, you can feel the wind when it is going in your face. This can make your hair move and your clothes get a bit wet. If you are in a car and the wind is going over you, you might feel a bit of a pull.

There are also different kinds of winds. There is a wind that comes from far away across the sky. This kind of wind is very strong and can make the sky look like a big round thing in the air. There is also a wind that comes from the ground. This kind of wind can make the ground shake and can make things fall over. When there is a lot of this kind of wind, it can be very bad for people and animals.
