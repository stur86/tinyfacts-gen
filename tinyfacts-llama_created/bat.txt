A bathroom is a room in a home where people go to clean themselves. It has a big water thing inside that goes up and comes down. This water helps to clean the skin and hair. You might use this room after you shower or after you use the bathroom. It is also used when you have a lot of hair that you want to get clean.

In a home, there is usually one bathroom. But sometimes, there are many. These rooms are often in the back of the house. They can be very small or very big. Some bathrooms have a little window so you can see outside. Some have a big window so you can look out at the sky.

When people say they are going to bathroom, they mean they are going to get clean. It is an important part of being a person.
