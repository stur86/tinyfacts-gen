Pain is a feeling that comes when something is wrong. It happens when your body is hurt.

Imagine you touch a hot thing. The skin on your hand feels very hot. You might want to pull your hand away. This is because your skin is trying to stay away from the hot thing. This is a kind of pain.

Pain is not just about the body. Sometimes it happens in your head. If a person is afraid or they hear a very loud sound, they feel pain in their head. This is because the brain is sending a strong word to the body to get ready.

There are many kinds of pain. Some are from things you eat. If you eat something that is bad for you, it can hurt your stomach. Some people also feel pain when they are very cold. They might feel a lot of pain and they might want to move away from the cold thing.

Sometimes, pain is a sign that something is wrong. If you fall and you hit your knee, you might feel pain. This is because your knee is breaking. The pain is telling you that you need to stop moving.

You can also feel pain when you cry. Tears are like big drops that fall from your face. When you cry, your body is trying to get rides of the bad stuff inside you. It is like the body is sending a word to the brain to stop the pain.

Most of the time, we do not think about pain at first. But then we see something that hurts. After that, the pain becomes a strong word in our mind. We might want to rest, drink water, or find a way to make the hurt go away.
