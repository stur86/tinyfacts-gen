The word hours is a big word. It is about time. When you say hours, you are talking about how many minutes you have in one day. You take a look at a long line of numbers and find out how many of those numbers are there. Most of the time we use hours to talk about days. One day is a long time. Many days are many hours.

Think of a long walk. If you walk for many hours, you will get tired. Your feet are heavy, and your legs feel like they are going to fall down. If you walk for a long time, you might even fall down. This happens to people who walk a lot. They walk for many hours every day. But people do not just walk hours. They also use hours to talk about things. When a person says they have a busy hour, they mean they have a lot of work.

You can also use hours to talk about time when you are waiting. If you are waiting for a bus, a train, or a phone call, you might say you have an hour to wait. This means you will be there for one hour. You can use this word to talk about how long you have to wait.
