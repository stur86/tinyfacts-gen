Sounds are things you hear. When you make a loud noise, your voice makes a sound. You can hear your own voice, the sound of a dog, the sound of a car, or the sound of the wind.

Sound is made when things move. When you breathe in, air moves into your body. When you move your arms or legs, the air inside your body changes very fast. This change in air makes a sound. The sound goes out of your body and into the world.

You can hear sounds from far away. If you walk very far, the sound of a car or a dog gets louder. But if you are very close to the ground, the sound of a dog might not be as loud. It is like the sound of a far away door opening.

Sounds can be different kinds. A cat makes a high sound when it wants to say that it sees something. A dog makes a low sound when it wants to say that it sees something too. A tree makes a low sound when it wants to say that it is in the ground.

You can also hear sounds that are not from things. You can hear the sound of a running water. You can hear the sound of a song. You can hear the sound of a baby crying. These sounds come from the world and are not from your own body.

Sometimes, sounds can make you feel things. If you hear a loud noise, you might feel scared. If you hear a soft sound, you might feel calm. The sounds around you can make you feel like you are a part of something much bigger than you.
