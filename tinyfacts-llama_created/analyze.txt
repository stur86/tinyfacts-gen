To understand something means to see how it works. You can see how a car goes. You see the road. You see how the car turns. You see how the car stops. You have learned about the car.

You can also understand something by looking at it. You look at a picture. You see the colors. You see the words. You see the story. You learn about a person. You learn about their life.

You can also use this word when you are making something. You take a piece of wood. You see how it looks. You see how it feels under your hands. You see how the wood will change when it gets hot. You are studying the wood. You are trying to know how it will change.

Sometimes, you have to break things down. You take a big thing and you break it into many small pieces. You see each piece. You see how they are made. You see how they work. You see how they fit together. This is called breaking down.

You can also use this word when you are trying to know if a thing is right or wrong. You look at the answer. You see if it is true or not. You see if it makes sense. You see if it is right. You are trying to understand.

This word is also used in many other ways. You may say, I am going to study this. I am going to learn about it. I am going to see how it works. You are going to learn something new. You are going to understand.
