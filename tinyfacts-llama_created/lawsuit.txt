To take a problem to the right place

Imagine you are very mad because someone did something that was wrong. You want to tell them why. You might go to a person you think is in the right. This is a way to try to get what you want.

When you say this, you are saying you want the person to pay money for what happened. This is called a case. You are the person who started the problem.

The person you are mad at might also want to take you to the right place. They might want to talk to a man who can help. This person is like a very good friend who listens and helps you.

When everyone is done talking, the case goes to a higher place. This is a place where a group of people makes the right answers. They listen to both sides and decide what is true.

In this case, you have to pay money to the person who started the problem. You pay them a small money number. This is like paying a fine. The money number is like a number that says you did something wrong.

Sometimes, if one person does not want to pay, the higher place can say that they are not doing their job right. This is like a big word that means they did not follow the right steps.

Sometimes, you might go to a place where people can watch the whole thing. This is like a big room where many people watch and decide. They might see everything before they decide.

In some cases, a person might say they are not the one who did the wrong thing. They might say they did not do it. This is like saying they did not have anything to do with the problem.

Even though people are talking, they must still follow the same steps. This is to keep everyone happy. If everyone is happy, then the whole thing is done.

This is a way to try to get what you want when you are mad. It helps us find the right answers and keep everyone safe.
