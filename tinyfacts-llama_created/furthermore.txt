When you talk, you use words to say more about what you mean. This word is very good at doing this.

When you have already said something, you can add more to what you said. You can do this in many ways. You might add a story. You might explain why something happened. You might use more words to make it clear.

This word is often used with other words that mean more than one thing. If you say one thing, and then you say another thing, the first thing is not the only one. The second thing helps us understand the first one better. You are saying more about what you already said.

Think of a line in a story. You might say the first word. Then you say the next word. You are building on what you already said. If you did not use this word, you would be left with only one word.

This word is important for people who like to talk and write. When you use it, you are telling the story more completely. You are showing the reader or the listener more about the story.

You can also use this word in a way that is funny. If you say something, and then you say it again, and again, you are making the same point over and over. You are saying, in a way that is not very nice, that you are doing it again because you like it.

So, this word is about adding more to what you are saying. It helps you build a story that is more than just one thing.
