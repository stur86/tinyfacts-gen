The word anymore is used when you want to tell a story or when you want to tell a fact.

When you say anything anymore, it means you are done. You have reached the end. You have shared all the things you wanted to say. You can stop talking.

You might use this word in a group. If you talk about your day, and then you say something else, the other people will stop listening because they already know what happened. They do not want to hear more. They have heard enough.

You can also use this word when you talk about a thing. If you watch a movie and the story ends, you say the movie is over. You have seen everything. You do not want to watch it again.

Sometimes people use this word with people who are different. If you and a friend are fighting, you might say, I am not anymore. This means you are both angry, but now you are not fighting. You are both calm. You are both tired.

There is also a word that means you are not going to do something anymore. If you promise to do something, but you do not, you are not anymore. You have broken your word.

So, when you say anything anymore, you mean you are done. You have shared all your ideas or all your facts. You can stop talking.
