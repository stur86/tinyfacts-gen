To be a good thing means to be nice to others. If you do something good, you are a good person. You are not afraid of what others think. You keep your word.

A thing that is very strong can hold a lot of power. A strong man or a strong house can keep many people safe. A strong word can hurt or make you feel happy.

Money is a way to buy things we need. When you give money, you are giving the person you give it to. You are helping them.

A thing that is very clear is easy to see. If a picture is clear, you can see what is happening. If the sky is clear, you can see the stars.

To be a good friend means you care about the other person. You listen when they talk. You help them when they are sad. You are there for them when they need you.

You can also be a good friend to animals. If you help a dog or a cat, you are being kind. You are making their life better.
