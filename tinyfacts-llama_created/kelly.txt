To killed means you took someone away so they could not live. It is a word that is often used when someone did something very bad. When you kill a person, you are taking their life.

Imagine you are in a room with many people. You are angry because one person did something bad. You want to stop them. You find their phone and you look at their computer. You find something they want to keep. You take it away. You are killing them, because you took their life.

Sometimes, killing someone is not always so easy. You might feel very sad after it is done. You might wish you could not have done it. This is because the person you took away will never be the same again.

We also say that someone is killed when they die. This is a different kind of death. If you kill a person, you are taking their life. If you kill someone, you are taking their life too.

You can also kill a thing. If a tree is killed, it does not grow anymore. It dies. This happens when the thing gets hurt or when the ground around it does not give it enough water. Sometimes, killing a thing helps keep the world safe.
