Silence is the quiet of the world. It is the time when there is no sound. When you are in a room and there is no one else, you can hear nothing. You can feel the air moving around you, but there is no sound.

You can also feel silence in a big city. When you walk down a street and there are no cars, there is no noise from the cars. There is no sound from the people shouting. You can listen to the sound of your own heart beating.

Sometimes, silence can be very strong. Imagine you are in a room with a very quiet person. You feel like you could talk to them for hours, but you know you can never. That is a kind of silence.

There are also times when you feel like there is no silence at all. Like when you are in a place with a lot of people and there is a lot of noise. Your ears hurt from the sound. Or when you are in a place with a lot of animals and there is a lot of noise from the animals. Your mind is busy with the sounds.

Silence is important. It helps us to rest. It helps us to think. It helps us to remember. When we are in silence, our brains can work better. It is like our body is saying, slow down, we are not in a rush.

There are also things that make noise but are not silence. A bell makes a sound. A dog makes a sound when it wants to eat. A baby makes a sound when it wants to sleep. These sounds are all part of the world, but they are different from the quiet of the world when there is no one or anything else.
