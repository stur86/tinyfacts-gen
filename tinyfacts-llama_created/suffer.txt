To Suck

You feel pain in your chest. You try to breathe. Your heart feels heavy. You want to cry. You want to say, stop. You want to go away. You are in a lot of pain because of something that happened. You do not feel good. You want to be better.

A person might feel this way when they are in a fight. A fight is a big thing that happens between two people. They hit each other. The person who is hit feels pain. They want to stop. They want to be safe. They might cry and scream. They do not feel well.

You can also feel this way when you fall down. You hit your head. You feel a lot of pain. You might be afraid because you do not know what is happening. You might want to sit down. You want to rest.

The word is often used with a person. You might say, my friend is going through this. They are going through a hard time. They are feeling pain. They need help.

Sometimes you can feel this way when you are happy. You feel so good. You want to tell everyone. You want to share your feeling. You might laugh a lot. You feel very happy.

You can also feel this way when you are angry. You feel your heart beating fast. You feel your face getting red. You want to shout. You want to hit something. You are very angry because you are very mad.
