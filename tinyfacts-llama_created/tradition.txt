A thing we do, or a way of doing things, that has been going on for a long time. It is something that people follow because it is the way that is known.

Think about a family. In this family, there is a way of eating food that has been passed down for many years. You are not allowed to drink from the glass on the table at dinner. Instead, you drink from a cup. Your mother and father do this every day. If you want to drink from the cup, you must ask them first.

Because of this, people in that family know what to expect. If you are not sure, you might feel a little nervous. This is what we call a thing that is part of our life.

Sometimes, a thing that is part of a family can become a thing that is not part of a family anymore. You might go to a big school or a school with a very new way of doing things. At first, you might feel like you do not fit. But then you learn the new ways and you become a part of the new group.

Because of this, a thing can become a thing of the past very quickly. We have seen this happen many times. When a new city is built, the old city often has to give up some of its old ways.

A thing can also become a thing of the past because people change over time. When you grow up, you might not want to do the same things that your parents did. You might want to do something new. This is because you have seen the world and you have learned about other ways. But sometimes, a thing that is part of a group can stay in the group because it is still a way that is known. It is like a book. You can keep reading it and it will always be there for you, even if the people who read it change over time.
