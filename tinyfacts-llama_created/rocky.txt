To rock something means to make it move very fast and then stop it quickly. It can happen with a ball, a car, a tree, or a rock.

When you rock a ball, you give it a little push and it goes around in a round way. After a while, it stops in the middle and stays there for a moment. This happens because the ball wants to stop, but it is too heavy to stop on its own.

A car can rock if it is moving very fast and then it hits a small thing. This makes the car shake and righter. It is like the car is being pushed by a strong wind.

A tree can rock when the wind is strong. The tree might go up, down, left, and right. This is because the wind is pushing on the tree, making it move around.

Sometimes, a rock can go from being very heavy to very light. This happens when the rock gets hit by a small thing, like a ball. The rock becomes lighter and can move around easily.

The word rock can also mean something that is very old. A rock from a long time ago can still be found today. This rock is a piece of the past that is still here.

We use the word rock to talk about things that move in a strange way.
