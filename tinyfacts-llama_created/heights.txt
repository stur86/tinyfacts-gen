To be at a high place is to be very far above the ground. You can see for a long way in every direction. High places are usually on top of big things like tall trees, tall houses, or very tall rocks. They are often very quiet because there is not much going on around them.

Imagine you are standing in a very open field. The ground goes very high on both sides. You can see the sky and the trees in the middle of the field. The ground is very hard and does not give way easily. If you walk up a long way, you might reach a place with a low wall. This place might be a house or a small area with trees. The wall is very high and you feel safe because it looks like a big wall around you.

Sometimes, people go to high places by flying. They sit in a small box that flies through the air. The box is pushed by a person who sits on the floor. The person feels the wind as the box moves quickly. The box can go very fast and you might feel like you are in a big car. When the box touches the ground, it falls down and you might feel a bit scared.

High places can also be very hard to get to. To reach a place that is high, you might have to walk a long way over many steps. You might have to climb up a very long road or walk up a very high road that goes over many high ground. Some places have very high walls to keep people out. This can make it harder to get to the top.

People use the word to talk about things that are very large. If you do something very well, you might be said to have been on high ground. You have done it very well and you feel like you are on top of the world.
