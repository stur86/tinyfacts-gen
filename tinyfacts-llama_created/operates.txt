Things that move around can be called to work. This means they are not sitting still. They go to a place, and then they go somewhere else.

Think about a car. It starts in one place. The person inside it wants to go to another place. The car does not just sit there. It goes. It goes very fast and makes a sound. It moves through the air and turns around corners.

When a person works, they do something. They might work in a job. They might work in a school. Or they might work in a store. When they work, they are doing a job. They are using their hands and their brain to make something happen.

We can also say that a computer works. It takes a set of words and numbers and uses them to make a new picture. It also takes a set of words and numbers and makes a new word. This is how computers work.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about how people act. If someone works well, they are doing their job very well. If someone does not work well, they might be late or make a wrong move.

In this story, the car works because it moves. It goes to the city and comes back home. The person inside the car works because they do their job.
