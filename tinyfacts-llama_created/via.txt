The word is about how you go from one place to another.

If you want to go to the park, you could walk. Or you could take a bus. Or you could drive. The word we use to talk about these things is: by.

To be by something means you are near it. You are not very far away. You are right next to it. If you are by a big body of water, you are near the water. If you are by a small street, you are close to houses.

Sometimes, we use the word to talk about people. If a friend is by your side, they are with you. If a family is by your side, they are a part of your life. We can also use the word when we talk about things. If a book is by the desk, it is near the table.

The word can also mean to be near a place. If you are by the road, you are near the street. If you are by the house, you are near your home. This can help you when you are lost. You can ask for directions so you can find the place you want to be.
