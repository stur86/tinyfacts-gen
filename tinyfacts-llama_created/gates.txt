A door with bars or a high wall is a kind of opening. It lets people in or out. You might have a house with a big door, a school with a door, a store with a door, or a place where people go to work.

Some houses have a small door with a lock. This lock is like a strong word that says, this door is not to be opened by anyone else. You need to know the right words or the key to open it.

A high wall is like a big door that is not to be touched. It keeps people from entering. Some walls are very tall and have many bars on them so that no one can climb over.

You can also talk about a set of doors. If you have many doors, you can open them one by one to get to where you want to go. Each door leads to another room, another building, or a street.

In a place where people buy things, a door is the place where you come in. You walk through the door and are inside the store. If you want something, you look for the right door. If you don't know, you might have to ask a person to help you find the right door.
