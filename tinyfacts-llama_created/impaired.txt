To make someone or something worse is to make them very bad. It happens when something that was good is changed. You can make a person feel sad or angry if you tell them something bad to do. You can also make a thing that was safe go into the water and hurt you.

You can also say a person is not as good as they used to be. If you do something wrong for many years, you become bad at it. You might not remember what you did before or you do it very wrong now. This is a big change.

Sometimes, to be made worse is just because things are not the same as they were before. If you have a cold for a long time, you feel tired and your body gets very bad. You might not be able to do your work or play with your friends. It is not because you are bad, but because of the time and how your body works.

We can also say something is very bad if we think it is wrong. If a friend does something that is not right, we might say they are bad. This is a way to show we care about what happens.
