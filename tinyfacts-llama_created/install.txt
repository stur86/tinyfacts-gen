To put something in a place is to make it where it is needed. This is what you do when you put a thing in a room. You put it on the table. You put it on the floor. You put it in the corner.

You can also say this when you put a thing in a car. You open the door and put the thing in the back seat. Then the car starts to move. You are making the car work so it can go where it needs to go.

Sometimes, you need to put something on your body. You put a band on your arm. You put a ring on your finger. This makes the thing you wear stay on your body.

You can also say this when you put a thing in a box. You close the top so it does not come out. This keeps the thing safe inside.

When you say you have something to do, you usually mean you have a job to do. You are going to put your work in the right place so others can see it.
