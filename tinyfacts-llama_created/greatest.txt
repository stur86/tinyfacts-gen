To say something is the greatest means you think it is the very best. It does not say it is the second best. It says you think it is the best.

This word is often used to talk about things that are very big, very strong, or very good. A very strong dog is the greatest dog. A very tall person is the greatest person. A very beautiful girl is the greatest girl.

You might also use this word when you are playing games. If you hit the ball the farthest, you are the greatest player. If you run the fastest, you are the greatest runner.

This word can also mean something that you love more than anything else. If a child loves their mother more than anything, they say they are the greatest love.
