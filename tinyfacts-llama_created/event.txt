An eventually is a word you use to talk about a thing that happens. It is about something that will happen next.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You have been playing for a long time, and you are ready to stop. You are all excited to see who is the best player. Then, something happens. A player is very good at the game. They beat all the other kids. This is an eventually.

An eventually is not a person. It is a thing that happens. It is like a story.

Another way to look at it is like a small story. You have a plan. You do what you say you will do. Then, something happens. This is your eventually. You followed your plan, and now you see what happened.

We also use this word in our lives. When you do something, you are doing an eventually. You are doing something you planned. It is a good thing.

You can also use this word for things that are going to happen in the near time. Maybe you hear that a movie is coming out soon. You know it is happening. You are looking forward to it. This is an eventually.

Think of a big picture. An eventually is a part of the picture. It is a small thing that happens before the whole story.
