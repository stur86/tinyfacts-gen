To make something smaller

Think of a ball. It is round and big. If you put it under a table, it gets smaller. The table is bigger than the ball. It makes the ball smaller.

You can do the same thing with your hands. If you pick a small thing and put it into your hands, it is smaller. You are making it smaller.

You can also make something bigger. If you put many small things together, they become one big thing. This happens with food. If you make many small pieces of food, you have one big piece that is full of food. It is bigger.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about things that are not living. If you look at a thing that is very small, you see it is very tiny. It is not as small as a tiny piece of paper. It is much bigger than a tiny piece of paper.

You can also use it to talk about people. If someone is very small or very short, they are small. This is different from being very cold or very hot. Being small is about how small you are, not about how hot you are or how cold you are.
