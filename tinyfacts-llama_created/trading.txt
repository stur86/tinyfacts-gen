To buy something means you pay money to get it. When you buy something, you give the money to the person who has the thing. This is called a deal.

Think about a store. The store has many things. If you want a book, you can go to the store. You look at the books and find one you like. You pay the money to the person who is in the store. Now you have the book.

Sometimes people also talk about when they are not buying a thing. If you say, I am going to buy a new car, it means you are going to pay money to buy the car. You will pay the money later.

When people talk about money, they also talk about how much money they have. If someone has a lot of money, they can buy many things. If someone has little money, they might not be able to buy the things they want.

In a business world, people also use this word. They might buy and give money to each other. They might buy a thing they need to do their work. Or they might buy a thing that will make them more money.

So, to buy something is to give money to the person who has the thing. To buy a thing is to take it from the person who has it.
