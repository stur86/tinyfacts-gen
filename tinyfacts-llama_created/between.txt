When you say something is between two things, it means you are in the middle of them. Think about your home and your school. Your school is on one side of your street, and your home is on the other side. You live there, and you are in the middle.

If you are in the middle of two friends, you are between them. You are not near one of them, but you are also not very far from both of them. You can see one friend and the other, but you are not the friend you see most of the time.

Sometimes, when you are in the middle of a big group of people, you are between many of them. You are not a leader, and you are not the biggest. You are just in the middle of the group.

You can also say you are between a start and a stop. If you are on a train, you are between the start of the line and the stop. You are not at the start, but you are not at the stop either. You are in the middle where the two things meet.

Sometimes, being in the middle is a good thing. It means you have the chance to see both sides of a problem. You are not always the one who decides what happens. You can listen to both people and find a way that works for everyone.
