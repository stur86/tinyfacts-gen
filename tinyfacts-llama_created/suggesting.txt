To suggest something means you try to tell someone about it. You do not say the words, but you show them by showing them.

If you want to tell a friend that you like a movie, but you do not say the name, you can say,  You know what I mean. You are showing them that you are thinking of it.

Sometimes you can not say the word. You might think a word, but you do not say it. This is also a way of suggesting. You are trying to show them that you are thinking of something.

You can also suggest things you did not plan to do. If you are late to work and you are talking to your friend, you might say,  Let us hang out after work. This is a way of suggesting that you want to do something together.

Suggesting is a good way to talk when you do not know if the other person will agree. It is a way of letting them know that you want something.
