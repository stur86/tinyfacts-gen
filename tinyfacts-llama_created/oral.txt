Or talk means you speak from the inside. You use your voice and your words. You do not write them down. You are talking with people who can hear you.

When you talk, you are speaking to a group of people. You might talk about a thing that happened. You might talk about a story. You might talk about your ideas or your hopes. You are sharing what you think with others.

Sometimes, you talk to a person. You might talk to a friend. You might talk to a teacher. You are talking with a person who can hear you. When you talk, you are showing how you feel.

You can also talk to a group of people who are not there. You might talk on the phone. You might talk to a computer. You are still talking from the inside. You are still telling your words to the people who can hear them.
