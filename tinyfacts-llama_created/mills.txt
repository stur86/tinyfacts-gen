To build something is to make it. If you are a builder, you use many pieces of wood and a big round thing to make a tall house. You use the pieces to make the walls, the floors, and the walls around the top of the house. You use the round thing to make the round top of the house and the sides that go around it. You use power from a big box to turn the round thing, and the round thing turns around and around. When the round thing turns, the pieces go around with it, and the whole house is made.

You can also talk about how a place works. A power place is a place that makes things move. These places have big round things inside them that turn round and round. When the round thing turns, it makes the power go out of the round thing and into the world. The power then goes to far away places to light the houses and drive the cars.

There are also things that are made by turning round things. A round thing that turns round can make a round thing for a bag or a box. You can put round things with hard things inside them in the round thing and watch how the hard things get bigger inside the round thing. This helps you make things that hold a lot of stuff.

A big round place is the round thing that is in the middle of the world. This place is where the round things of the world go around each other. The round things go around many times and cover the whole sky. We call the round thing that goes around the world the round thing that turns around the world.
