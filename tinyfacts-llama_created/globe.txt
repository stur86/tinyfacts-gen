A world is a big place with many things. We use the word when we talk about the world in a group. You can say, 

I live in a world near the big water. This world is where my home is.

Or you can say, 

The world is very wide and very high. If you look at the top of the world, you can see the blue sky.

A world is also a round thing. If you look at the top of the world, you can see the round part of the world. The round part is where the sky starts.

A world can be made of many things. One world is made of water. Another world is made of land. Some worlds are made of rock. Some are made of air. Some are made of water and rock.

We use the word when we talk about a world that is far away from us. If you look at the world through a big glass thing, you can see it. If you look through a small glass thing, you can not see it. A world that is very far away is a big thing in space.

People use the word to talk about the world and the things in it. We use it to talk about places where people live, places where things grow, and places where animals live.
