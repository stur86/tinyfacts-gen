To be even means you are not better than anyone else. You are the same as everyone else. You have the same life, the same home, the same friends, and the same job. You do not get any more important than you do now.

Think about a game. If you and your friend play the same game, and you both get the same number of points, then you are even. You are not better. You are just as good as your friend.

You can also be even with your body. If two people are the same age, the same hair color, and have the same clothes, then they are even. They do not stand out more than the other person.

Being even is nice. It means you can live with yourself and feel safe. It is a way to stop fighting and start being kind.
