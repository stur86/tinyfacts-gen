An idea is when you think about something that will happen in the far away time. You might be thinking about a place where the air is very clear, or a kind of food that people eat for breakfast. When you say you are an idea, you mean your idea is not yet real.

A person who works in a big building is an idea. They plan how to make things work. They write a plan and make sure everyone knows what they have to do. They make sure the things will be made on time.

An idea can be about money. You could have an idea to start a new business. You think there is a need for that kind of work and you think you can make money from it. You write a plan and look for money to start your business.

Another way to use the word is to talk about a person who has a lot of money. They have a lot of money in their pocket or in their house. They can buy things they want or do things they do not want to do. They do not have to work very hard.

People also say someone is an idea if they have a very strong feeling about a thing. Maybe someone loves a movie and they spend a lot of time thinking about it. They tell their friends about it so everyone else knows too.

To be an idea is to have a brain that thinks about the world. It is to have a way to see what is going on and to know how to act.
