Bright is a word that means light. When something is bright, it is very bright. Light is how the world looks to us. When the sun is up in the sky, the world is bright. You can see the blue sky and the green trees and the green green things growing on the ground.

If something is bright, it does not hide. It shows everything. You can see a bright light in the dark room. This is why we need bright things in our homes and cars.

You can also use this word for people. If you are very happy, your face might look bright. If someone is very strong, their body looks bright. Bright is a word that shows how something is or feels.
