To move from one place to another. You can do it by car, by bus, by walking, or by flying. If you have a car, you drive from one city to another. If you have a bus, you get on a bus that goes to a different city. If you walk, you go on foot. If you fly, you get on a big moving thing in the sky and it takes you to your new city.

This word is about going to a new place. You might want to see the city you live in, but you want to see another city that is far away. You might want to see a big city with many people, or a small town with a nice old house. You could go to a place where you know no one, and you start to learn about the place and its people.

You can also say you are on a way to a place. This means you are going to that place before you get there. If you are on a way, you are moving toward your new home.

Sometimes, this word can be about a story. If a story has a girl who goes on a way, we call her a girl who goes on a way. If she goes to a far place, we say she goes on a way to that place. This is how we use the word to talk about moving to a new place.
