Silence is the quiet between two sounds. When you are alone in a room, there is no one to talk to. You can hear the air moving around you. The sound of a chair moving is also there, but you do not hear it because you are not talking. This is like the quiet that falls between two words.

Silence is also the quiet that comes when you stop moving. You are walking on the floor. Your feet are pushing the ground. The ground is still. You are not moving, so there is no sound. After you stop, there is a quiet. It is a quiet that is not like the quiet before or after. It is a quiet that is just there.

Sometimes, people feel this quiet in their bodies. It is a quiet that comes from inside. It is like a small still inside you. You can feel it when you are very quiet or when you are afraid. It is a quiet that does not need anything to happen.

Silence is also about the way we see the world. When you look at something that is very quiet, like a room, you do not see the colors or the bright parts. You only see the quiet. You see nothing else. This is because the quiet takes away the light and the colors.

In a way, silence is a thing that happens when time stands still. When you are waiting for something, you can feel the quiet. It is like a pause in the world.
