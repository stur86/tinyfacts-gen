To follow is to follow behind something else. If you are walking, you follow behind another person who is already walking. This means you are moving in the same direction but you are not at the same time.

Think about a story. If you tell a story, you are the one who is telling it. But then, someone else tells the story after you have finished. That person is following you. They are following the story and adding their own ideas.

In our lives, we often follow other people. If you want to learn a new way of doing things, you follow the person who is doing it. You try the same things so you can be as good as them. When you learn, you are following them.

We also follow things that are happening around us. If you see a car, you follow it because you want to see where it is going. You do not follow it because you want to be in its way.

Sometimes, following is about trying to do the same thing. If you want to run a race, you follow the person who is running. You try to be as fast as them. This is a way of being better than someone else.

In your life, you can follow your own dreams. If you want to be a writer, you follow your dreams. You work on writing your own stories and try to make them better. You are following your own ideas and your own heart.
