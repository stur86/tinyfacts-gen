To ride a thing you pull with your feet is to go through places in a straight line. This thing is often long and has many lines that cross it. You sit on a seat, and the person who pulls it moves along the lines. The ground goes under you, and you go over the lines.

People use this word to talk about trains and cars. A train is a long line of many cars that go on a straight road. They all carry people to different places. When you ride on a train, you are on the line. You do not pull the car. You sit and watch what is happening.

Cars are also on a line, but they pull themselves along the road. You sit in the back of the car and hold a long stick to control it. You tell the car to go faster or slower, and it does what you say.

The word can also mean a thing that holds people or things. You might say, a long line of people waiting to get in a room. This line is very long because many people want to go in.
