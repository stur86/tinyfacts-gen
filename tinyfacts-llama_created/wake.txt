To wake means to make someone or something start. It happens when the sun is up and the world is bright. 

Imagine you are sleeping in a dark room. You are safe and quiet. But then the sun starts to go down. The room is now light and you can see things. You open your eyes and you are wide open. You have been waking. 

Sometimes, you wake up in a strange place. You do not know where you are. You are not in your bed. You might be in a new house or a different city. You are waking up from a long sleep. 

When you wake, you get up from your bed. You go to the bathroom to use the water and to use the bed. You might be a little bit tired. But after you wake, you feel better. You can do things and you can see the world. 

To wake a person is also a way to make them feel better when they are sad or angry. If a friend is sad, you can say,  wake up. This means to help them feel better. It is like giving them a chance to start again.
