To decide something is to use your mind and your body. If you say something is done, you have decided it is true. You might say, I decided the book is done. This means you are sure that the book has been done.

You also use this word when you decide about a person. If you decide someone is not a good person, you are saying they are not a person you like or trust. You are using your mind to decide who to keep in your life and who to keep away.

Sometimes we say a thing is decided. This means someone or something has been said or done. The word is used in many different ways to mean the same thing.

You can also use this word when you talk about a person who has done something wrong. If you say, that person was decided, it means that person was said to be wrong. This word is often used in schools and in places where people decide what is right or wrong.

You can also say, the person was decided to do this. This means the person was said to have done it.
