You can be different in many ways. You might like to do different things. You might have different hair. You might be very good at different things. You might like to go to different schools. You might like to go to different movies. You might like to play with different animals.

A sister is a person who lives with you. You live in the same house. You share a room. You share a bed. You share a table. You share a book. You share a game. You share a story. You share a laugh.

A sister can be very kind. They give you hugs. They tell you to be happy. They tell you to be safe. They help you when you are sad. They listen when you talk. They understand when you want to cry. They help you feel better when you are very tired.

A sister can also be very funny. They can make you laugh. They can make you smile. They can tell you to be happy. They can make you laugh at the same joke. They can be your friend in a very good way.

Sometimes, a sister is the person who looks at you. They look at your face. They look at your hair. They look at your clothes. They look at your feet. They look at everything. They see everything about you. They know you better than anyone else.
