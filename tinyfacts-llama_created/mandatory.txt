When you are told to do something, you must do it. This word is used when a person or a group is making you do something. It means you must follow the order. If you do not follow, you might get in trouble.

You might do a thing by a school. The teacher says you have to be in the room at a very early time. If you do not come, you might not get to go to school. This is a way for people to stay safe.

You also might do a thing by a doctor. The doctor tells you to take a small box full of water. You must take this box every day. If you do not take it, the doctor might give you a drink that can hurt you.

Sometimes, a group makes everyone do something. They might have a big meeting where everyone is there. The group says, we must work together. We must talk and decide how to do the job. Everyone must follow the plan.

You can also say a thing is not allowed. This means the person is being told not to do that. If you do not follow, you can get in trouble. The teacher might say, you are not allowed to play with that game. You must stay away from it.

In some places, you must buy a book before you can use it. This is like a big order. You have to follow the order. If you do not buy the book, you might not get to use it.

The word is used to talk about these kinds of orders. It is a way to tell people what to do.
