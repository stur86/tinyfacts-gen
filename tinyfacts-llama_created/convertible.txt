A car is a thing for moving around. It has four sides. Two sides are for driving. Two sides are for parking. The side you drive on is the one that moves the car.

You can turn the side that moves the car around the other three sides. This is called a turn. When you turn this side, you are changing the direction of the car.

You can also change the way the car looks. If you turn the side that looks at the ground, you can see the world from a different way. This is why people drive cars that are like a house. They turn the side that looks at the ground so the front of the car is facing the road.

Cars can also change how much air they take in. This is why the side that looks at the ground is often covered with a thing that keeps the air out. This is important because if the air inside the car goes out, the car can become very hot and the people inside can feel the hot air.
