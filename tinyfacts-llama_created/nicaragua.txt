Nicest is a word we use to say something is very good. A long time ago, people in the world thought that the sun was the nicest thing. They said it was the bright thing in the sky that made everything warm.

In a place, the nicest part is the big green area with many trees. This is a very good place to live because it is very green and has a lot of big trees. People who live there like to walk in the green area and have a cold drink on a hot day.

The word nicest can also mean someone is very nice. If you are very nice, people are happy to talk to you or help you. A nice person is kind and makes you feel safe. They listen to what you say and try to understand your problem.

In a city, the nicest building might be the one with the big white door. It is very good because it looks very nice and makes people feel happy. The white door is like a big smile for people who enter.

Sometimes, we use the word nicest when we talk about animals. A nice animal is one that is not mean. A cat is nice. It does not want to hurt you. It just wants to be near you. A nice dog is also a good animal because it is kind and does not run away when you say hello.
