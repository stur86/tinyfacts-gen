Bright is a word that means light or clear. It is a word you use when you want to tell that something is not dark anymore. If you put a soft thing over a window, the room is now bright. You feel happy and calm when the light is bright.

The city where this word comes from is called Bright. It is a city in a big land with many tall houses. People go there to work, to eat, and to have fun. Many people live there and many go there to visit because of its bright days.

Bright is also a word for a time when the day is very clear and bright. It can be a good day when you feel happy and your face is smiling. You might see many people walking and many cars moving on the streets. The sky is blue and the ground is green and the air is still and quiet.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about a person. If a person is very happy, they might say, I am bright today. It means they are feeling great and their face is smiling.

In short, the word bright means light, clear, or bright. It can be a city, a time of the day, or a feeling of being happy.
