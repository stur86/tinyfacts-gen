When you watch a movie on your computer, you see the pictures and the sounds. But if you want to watch the whole movie, you need to get it from somewhere else. This is what you do with the word you are talking about today.

To get something you have, you send it to your computer. You type a few words and press a key. The computer then looks for that word in a big book of words. When it finds it, it sends the word to your computer. Your computer then reads the word and uses it to show you the pictures and sounds.

This is how people get books, pictures, music, and movies. They send the word to their computer. The computer does not keep it. It only uses it to show you what you want.

You can also get things you want by looking at a computer. If you want to see a picture of a cat, you can look at a computer. You type a few words and the computer shows you the picture. The computer is like a big, fast way to show you what you want.

Sometimes, you can get things you want by using your phone. You can send the word to your phone. Your phone then looks for that word. If it finds it, it shows you the pictures and sounds. You can watch a movie, look at a picture, or listen to music with your phone.

So, when we say someone has something, we mean they have the word. It is the word that tells the computer to show you what you want.
