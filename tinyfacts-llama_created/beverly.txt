A beautiful place where a big water is. It is in the middle of the world. It is a long way from the big city. It is not very big, but it is very nice.

People go there to see the water. There are many houses with big windows. Some of them are very old. You can feel the old feeling in them. There is a big green place too. You can walk around the green and feel the green all over your feet.

There is a big park in the park. You can go there to run or play. The park is also a place to sit and watch the water. You can sit on a big rock and look at the water. It is a very quiet and calm place.

Some people live there. They are from the city. They like the quiet life. They like to walk and be alone. They do not have many friends, but they have each other.

There is also a big house with a big room. The family lives there. The room is very big and has a lot of windows. You can see the whole town. They like to sit there and watch the world go by.

In the summer, the water is very hot. People go there to cool down. They run in the water or play with the water. They are very happy.
