When you say something is a team, you mean you are all working together. A team is a group of people who do the same thing. They are not alone. They have a leader who helps them.

You can have many teams. There are teams for work, for school, for games, and for cars. In a work team, everyone does a job. You might drive a car, and another person does the work inside the car. You both work together to get the car to a place and back.

In a school team, you are part of a group of kids who play a game. You learn how to work with other people. You follow a plan. You all try to get as many points as you can. You are a team.

When you have a team, you can help each other. If one person does not do their job, the others can help them. This makes the job easier. It is a good way to work together and feel happy with your group.

You can also have a team in your life. Your family might be a team. You all love each other. You do things together. You might go to a movie, eat food, or play games. Being part of a team can make you feel happy and make life more fun.
