Imagine you have a big group of people to tell stories. You need to get them all to say the same thing. If you talk to each person one after the other, it will take a long time. But if you tell them all at once, you can hear what they have to say and write it down very fast. That is an easy way to do many things at once.

In school, you might have many questions to answer. You do not have to answer each one one after the other. You can write all your answers at once and then read them again later. This way, you can remember everything you need to know.

Sometimes, you must do many things to show you are ready for a job. You might have to write papers, give a talk, or do a job that needs to be done many times. You must do each thing quickly so you do not miss any important part. If you do each thing slowly, you will not be ready for the job.
