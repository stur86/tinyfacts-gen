A small place where you end your walk. It is often in a city or a place where buses and trains go. Here, people can tell where they are going next. You can see the next stop and the time.

You might start your walk at home, and the small place is the end. You have walked a long way to get there. You can sit and think while you look at the sign. It is a nice place to rest.

Some places have many small places like this. They are all put together so that you can easily get where you need to go. This makes life easier.

You might have to leave your car at this place. You must go to the big city and find a bus. The bus will take you to the next place. You will then go to another place, and another place, until you reach your home.
