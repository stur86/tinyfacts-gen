To be able is to be strong enough. You are not afraid. You can do what you want. You are not very worried.

Think about a child. They need to drink water. If they drink too much water, they will feel sick. But if they drink just enough water, they are okay. They do not feel sick, and they can play.

You can also be able in other ways. You can be able to do a thing. You are able to write a word. You can be able to speak a word. You can be able to do a job. You can be able to do many things.
