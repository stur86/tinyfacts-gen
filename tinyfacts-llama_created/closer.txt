To be closer is to be near something. It means you are not as far away as you thought.

Imagine you are standing in front of a tall building. You can see the top but you are still many steps away. If you walk a little, you are closer. You are getting nearer. This is what being closer means.

Sometimes, being closer is about being near each other. If you are very far from a friend, you are still not really close. But if you walk a little, you are now close enough to talk. You can hear each other and it feels good. This is being closer in a way that makes you feel happy.

It can also mean you are near the end. If you are trying to learn a new thing, you are getting closer to understanding it. You are not far away anymore. You have made good steps. This is being closer to your finish.

Being closer can help you feel better. If you are afraid of a thing, being closer to it can make you feel less afraid. It is like taking a step closer to a wall that makes you nervous. You are now right next to it, and it feels less big. This is being closer to what makes you feel safe.
