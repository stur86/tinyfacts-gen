A group of people working together as one is called a group. When many people do the same work, it helps them work better together. They may have a leader. The leader helps everyone follow the same way. They might have a meeting where they talk about how to work well together.

Imagine you are in a school. Some kids like to play with a ball. Some kids like to play with a book. Some kids like to play with a chair. If one group wants to play with a chair and the other group wants to play with a ball, they might fight. They are like one group against another group.

But if the kids from both groups sit together and talk, they can figure out how to do their jobs well. They can find a way that everyone can be happy. This happens with many groups that work together, like in a big city or a group of people who work on a train.

It is also important to have a group that looks after everyone. When a group of people do not share the money, they might fight. But if they have a group that looks after everyone, like a doctor or a teacher, the group can work better together and everyone is safe and happy.
