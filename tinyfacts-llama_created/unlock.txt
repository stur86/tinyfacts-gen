To lock something means to make it not work. Imagine you have a box with a lock on it. The lock is like a door. If the door is closed, you can not open it. You can not look inside. The lock is like a strong word. If you want to open the door, you need to give the word. You must enter the right word. This is what we mean by locking.

When you lock something, you are keeping it safe. You are making sure it is not broken. But sometimes we want to let something be free. We might lock our computers to keep our papers safe. We lock our cars so people do not break them. This keeps us and our things safe.

Sometimes, to lock something means to stop it. If you lock a box, you are closing it. You are making it not open. This can be very important. If a box is locked, you do not open it. You do not look inside. This keeps the things inside safe.

You can also lock a person. If a person does something bad, they might get locked. This is like a time when they have to stay inside a room. They are not allowed to leave. The person is being watched. They are not allowed to go out. This is a way to keep them safe.
