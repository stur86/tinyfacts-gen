To decide something is to decide how to act. If you know that a person is angry, you might decide to be quiet. You are not sure if they are really angry or if they are just trying to have a conversation.

If you decide to go to a movie, you are using your brain. You are thinking about what will happen and how you feel. You are making a plan. This is different from just doing what you feel. If you go to a movie, you are following a plan that you made.

Sometimes, you decide to be kind to someone. You might say nice things to them or give them a small present. This is a good thing to do. You are showing that you care.

You can also decide to do something bad. You might want to play a game for a long time, but you decide to stop. You are changing what you want to do.

When you decide something, you are using your brain and your body. You are using your words and your face. You are making a plan for your life.
