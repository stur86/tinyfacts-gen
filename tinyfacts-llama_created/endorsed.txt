To end something means to make it happen or to add to it. It is a word you use when you want to add the end to a word or a name.

Imagine you have a friend. You are very happy for them. You want to tell everyone about them. You say, I am the best friend in the world. This is a good thing because it makes them feel happy. This is what it means to end a friend.

You can also end a song or a story. If you like a song, you might say, I like this song. This is like saying you have ended the song. You have added the end to the beginning.

Sometimes, you want to show that something is true. You might say, This food is good. You are saying this because you know it is the right thing to eat. You are ending the story by adding the fact that this food is good.

You can end a job or a game. If you are the best player, you can say, I am the best. This is a way to say you have ended the game.

You can also end something bad. If a person does something wrong, you might say, They must pay for what they did. This is like saying the thing is over.
