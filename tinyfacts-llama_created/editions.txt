An edging thing is a small piece of wood or other hard stuff that you put around the edge of a paper or a book. This helps keep the paper in place. You can also use it to make the cover of a book look nice. The person who makes these things is called an edging person.

Sometimes, a person makes many of these small pieces. They put them together to make a new book or a big book that has many more edging pieces. These new books are called edging books. Many people buy these books because they have nice pictures or words on the cover. They also like to have a book that looks very nice to show off.

You can also use the word in other ways. If you are the one who makes something, you are an edging person. If a teacher makes sure everyone follows the same things, he is an edging person too. If someone does not follow the right way, they are an edging person.
