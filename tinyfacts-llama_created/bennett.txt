To be a good friend is to be kind and help people when they need it. If you are a good friend, you will not tell bad things about someone. You will not lie to them. You will tell the truth.

Being a good friend is about being there when someone needs you. You can listen to them. You can help them. If you see someone who is sad, you might give them a hug. This shows that you care.

Sometimes, being a good friend can be hard. You might feel bad for telling someone a bad thing. But you must do it. If you do not help, the person will stay sad for a long time. They might even forget you.

You can also be a good friend to the people you work with. If you are happy and smile, it makes everyone around you feel better. You make the work room feel like a happy place.
