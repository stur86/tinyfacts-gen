My is a word that you use when you talk about your own things. It is like a name for the things you care about.

You can use it to talk about your family. If you are a child, your mom and dad are your family. If you are an older person, you might say,  My mom is a great person. This tells everyone who you are.

You can also use it to talk about your things. If you have a favorite book, you might say, I love that book. This means you like the book very much.

Sometimes you might say, My dog is very happy. This means the dog is very happy because it is a dog.

You can also use it to talk about things you have. If you buy a new shirt, you might say, My new shirt is nice. This means the shirt is very nice to wear.

When you use this word, you are telling everyone who you are or what you have. It is a way to talk about your own life.
