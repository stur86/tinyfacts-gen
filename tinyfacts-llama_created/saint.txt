A person who is a good man or a good woman is called a child of God. They are not like the bad people who do things that hurt the world and the people around them.

A person who is a child of God can do many good things. They can help others. They can be kind. They can love. They can be very nice to people who are not happy.

When people do good things for each other and for the world, the world gets a little better. The good things help people feel happy again. This makes everyone around them feel happy too.

Some people are made to be a child of God by God. They are the children of a very good man or woman. They grow up to love and do what is right. They might live their lives with a lot of love and a lot of good.

When we say a person is a child of God, we mean they are a person who loves and cares for the world and for everyone in it.
