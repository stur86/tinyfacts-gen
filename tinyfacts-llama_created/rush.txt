To rush means you run very fast. You might feel like you are going to fall down because you are moving so quickly. Your feet are moving forward, but you are still moving forward in the air. This feeling is often very strong.

Imagine you are at a big party and a person is very happy. They want to tell everyone a story. They might say things that are a little bit too fast. They are trying to keep everyone listening, but they are rushing through the words. Because of this, the story can feel a bit strange.

You can also rush when you are in a hurry to get somewhere. You might feel a bit scared because you do not know what will happen next. You want to get to your place quickly so you do not feel so alone. This feeling is often very strong because you are worried about the time.

Sometimes people rush through their day. They might be in a hurry to finish work. They rush to the kitchen to get food. They might rush to the bathroom before going to bed. This can make them feel tired later. They are trying to get as much done as possible in a short time.
