To show something is true or real is to say it is clear. When you say a thing is obviously bad, you mean it is clear you are wrong. Your word is not just for you. You can use it with anyone.

Think about a friend who is mean. You might say, the person is very mean. This is not just about you. Your word shows everyone else that the person is not a good person. If you say a bad thing, you are showing that the bad thing is clear, and people might start to believe it.

You can also use this word to talk about a thing that is very easy to see. If you look at a bright light, it is easy to see that it is a bright light. You do not need to think very hard. The light is clear and the word is clear too.

Sometimes, a word is used by someone who is not sure. They might say a thing is clear, but they are still thinking. The word is not just about the thing. The person is showing that they are trying to understand. It is like a sign that says, I am not sure, but I am trying to find out.

We use this word when we talk about how things look. If a picture is very clear, we say it is a clear picture. If a picture is not clear, we say it is a bad picture. It is a word that helps us talk about the way things are.
