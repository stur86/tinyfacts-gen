A big area of hard ground with water inside it is called a water box or a water box. You can jump in the water and play with your friends. If you hit a ball into the water, it goes in the water. If you throw the ball in the air, it goes over the top of the water. Some people use this water box to stay cool in the hot sun.

You can also use the word to talk about a group of people who share a love for a game or a story. They might all be wearing the same shirt or have the same hair cut. They often meet to play their game together.

In a school or a home, a water box is often a room with a long table and chairs for people to sit at when they are eating lunch. Some rooms have a big window so you can see outside when the sun is high in the sky.
