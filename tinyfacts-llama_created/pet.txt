To have a dog is to have a friend. Many people keep these animals because they are good for them. They take them to the park so they can run around. They give them food and water. They also play with them. Some people have dogs because they are happy to see them. They love to hear their loud noises. They love to see their further days.

You can have a cat too. Cats are different. They sleep a lot. They like to be alone. They like to watch you. They like to use the door. They will jump on your bed. They make you laugh. Some cats are very mean. They bite if you touch them. But most cats are nice. They just want to be loved.

You can also have a cat as a child. A child who has a cat is called a cat kid. They learn to care for the cat. They learn to do the same things the cat does. They learn to give the cat food and water. They learn to pick its hair.

Many people have many animals. They have dogs, cats, and sometimes other animals. They have animals at home. They have animals at school. They have animals at work. These animals are like a family. They are there for you.
