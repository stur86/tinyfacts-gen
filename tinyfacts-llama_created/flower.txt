A thing that grows from ground or from a tall tree is called a green thing. It has many parts. The green thing has a big top part that reaches up to the sky. This top part is made of green stuff. The green stuff gives the thing its color.

Inside the green thing, many small parts grow. These small parts are usually white. They are like tiny stars. These small parts make the green thing grow. As the small parts grow, they make more small parts, and the green thing gets bigger.

A green thing is usually very pretty. Many people like to watch it. They might sit in the green thing to watch the stars at night. They might pick its small parts to make pretty things. Some people use the green thing to make clothes. They cut the green parts and use them to make nice things.

In the world, there are many kinds of green things. Some are small and grow on the ground. Others grow on tall trees. Some grow very high in the sky. Some grow in the ground and stay there for many years. The green things change colors in the dark. They turn red or pink in the dark. In the dark, the green thing is ready to give a baby animal.
