To present something means you show it to people. You might be the one who tells them a story, or you show them a picture, or you show them a game. You want to show them how it feels or what it looks like.

Sometimes people present things to their friends or family. They might talk about their day or their plans for the week. This is a way to share what is happening with the people you care about.

You can also present things that are not your own. You might give money to someone who needs it. Or you might give a book or a work of music. This is a way to help other people.

It can also mean to show how you feel. If you are sad, you might present yourself as sad. You show your face and your words so the people see how you feel. This helps them understand you.

Sometimes we present things for a big eventually. You might present a plan to your business. You are showing them how you will do things in the long time to come. This is a big way to show everyone what you are doing.
