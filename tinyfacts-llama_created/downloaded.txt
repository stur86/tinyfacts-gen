To be down is to feel very sad. If you are down, you might cry. You might want to hide under a bed. You might also feel like you want to give up. But sometimes, when you are really down, you might want to do something to feel better.

If you do something to be down, it is called a problem. You might be down because you lost your favorite thing. Or you might be down because you are not happy with your life. You do this because it makes you feel less bad.

Sometimes, you can be down when you are not really. You might feel this way when you are afraid. You might feel this way when you see something you do not want. You might feel this way when you are feeling very tired. It can happen to anyone, even a young person.

Being down is a part of life. It happens to everyone.
