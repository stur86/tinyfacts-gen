To make something new is to start from nothing and turn it into something else. This happens in many ways.

Imagine you want to make a food for your family. You do not have the food in your house. So, you go to a store and buy it. This is a new thing. You take the food and you do not keep it in your house anymore. You use it to make something else. This is a new way of doing things.

Sometimes, you make things for your own use. You build a house with your hands. You make a bed for your bed. You make a chair for your chair. This is a new way of living.

Sometimes, people make things for others. You make a car for a friend. You build a house for a family. You make a book for a child. These are new ways of helping other people.

We also make things that are not for us. We make music, or paint a picture, or write a story. These are new ways of making things that are not for us. They help us feel happy or help us understand the world.

In this way, everything is made. A tree is made from the ground. A house is made of wood and other things. A car is made from many parts. A story is made from words and pictures. When we make these new things, we start a new story.
