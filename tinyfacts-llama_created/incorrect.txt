To be wrong is to do something that is not right. If you say something is true, but it is not, you are wrong. You can also be wrong in other ways. You might act in a way that is not good for someone. You might say a word that is bad. Or you might do something that you know is not good for the world.

We all make wrong moves sometimes. We might jump into a room thinking we are in a big room. Or we might try to catch a ball but end up hitting the wall. When this happens, we feel bad. It is like our heart is saying, we did something wrong.

But sometimes, we are wrong because we do not know. Maybe we try to do something and it does not work. Or maybe we are not sure. This can be because we are learning. Every day, we learn new things. We find out that things are not as clear as we thought.

It is also important to know when to say, I am wrong. If you know something is not right, it is better to say it than to keep it inside and hope someone else finds out. This shows that you are a person of truth.
