Eventually means you will get there. It is a word you use when you see something far away and you think you will get there one day. But sometimes, the person you are following might get lost or hurt before you do.

Imagine a long road that goes from the city to the big city far away. You are following the road. You walk for a long time and then you see a sign that says the city is near. You are very excited because you will soon see the city. But then, the sign says the road ends in a small town. You are not sure if you will ever get to the city. You might have to walk many more days to get there.

Eventually, you will arrive at the city. It might take many more days or weeks to get there. Or you might be there one day and leave the next. You will see a big city with tall walls and many people walking in the streets. You will hear many sounds from cars and people. You will see many houses with bright lights.

Eventually, the city becomes your home. You love the food, the music, and the way of life. You know everyone and make many friends. The city is where you live your life.

Eventually means we are getting closer to the end. But we do not know when it will happen. We wait and see what happens.
