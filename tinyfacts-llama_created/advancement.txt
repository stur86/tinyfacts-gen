To make an old thing better is to do it faster, or to do it in a new way. This is what happens when a new thing is built on top of something old.

Imagine you have a table. It has been around for many years. You use it every day. One day, a group of people say,  We want to do this but a better way. They take the old table and they make it bigger. The new table is bigger and heavier. It is not as easy to carry as the old one. But it is stronger and it stays the same color. The people have done it faster and better.

Or think about a city. A long time ago, the city was very small. Many people lived in one area. But the area got bigger. New people came to live there. They built new houses. They built new streets. They built new schools. They built new roads. The city is now bigger and more people live there. This is an idea called an area. An area is the big space where many things happen.

We also talk about an area as a time. When you look back, you see how far you have come. You are an older person now. You have lived many years. You have seen many things. You can remember when you were a child. You can remember when you first learned how to ride a horse. You can remember how you felt when you first met your first love. The past is an area.

Sometimes, we talk about an area as a problem. A problem is when something is broken. You can not fix it. It is very hard. But you can try to make it better. You can try to find a way to fix it. You can try to change it. You can try to make it new. You are trying to make the problem better. This is an idea called an area.
