To want something means you need it very much. You might feel a strong need to have it. You could want a drink, a book, or a game. This need makes you want to get it right now.

When you want something, you do not need to pay for it. You do not need to work hard for it. You just need to be there and be ready. You might want to go to a place you like, to see friends, or to do something fun.

Sometimes, you want something because it is not there. You might want a new thing you have never seen before. This is a big want because it makes your heart feel very happy and your body feel excited.

If you are very happy because you want something, you are in a good state. You feel good inside and you feel like everything is right. You might laugh a lot or smile a lot. This makes you feel very happy.

But sometimes, wanting something can be hard. You might feel angry if you want something that is not yourself. You might want to keep something that is not yourself. This can cause problems with your family or your friends.

To want something is to have a strong feeling inside. It is about how you feel about the thing. If you want something, you are ready to get it.
