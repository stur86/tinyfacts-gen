When animals are in an area, they are in a good place. They eat what they need. They sleep where they can. They stay with their family. They do not hurt other animals.

But when a group of animals moves to a new area, they might not know where to go. They might find food that is not there. They might have babies. The new area might not be as safe as the old one. It might have more bad things or less food.
