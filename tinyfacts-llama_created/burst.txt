To burst is to suddenly change and become much bigger very fast. This happens when you hold a tight ball inside your hand. You try to keep the ball in your hand, but the ball wants to come out. The ball starts to shake and then it breaks through the skin you hold on it. The ball goes flying out and the hand is left holding nothing.

The word can also mean to break or to fall in a very strong way. If you are sad and you want to tell your friends that you are okay, you might burst into tears. This happens when the feeling inside you is too strong to keep inside for a moment.

Sometimes, to burst means to become very hot and then to fall down. If you drink a hot drink, your body starts to shake and you might feel like you are going to fall. This is because the hot drink is making your body warm inside. When you burst, the warm inside of your body goes out and you feel a bit lighter.

You can also burst into a song or a story. When you feel very excited or happy, you might start to dance or speak in a funny way. This is a way to show how you are feeling.

Finally, to burst can mean to stop doing something very quickly. If you are playing a game and you are doing well, you might suddenly stop and look around. You might see your friends or a person you like. This is a way to stop and listen or to see what is happening around you.
