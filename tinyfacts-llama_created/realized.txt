To realize something means to see how it is really done. You think something will happen. But when it happens, you see that you were wrong. You realize that you were wrong because the thing is not what you thought.

Imagine you are afraid of a dog. You see a dog and you get nervous. You close your eyes and you think the dog is going to attack you. But when the dog looks at you, you see it is just a dog. You realize that you were afraid because you were wrong. You realized that the dog is not a real person that can hurt you.

Realizing something can make you feel better. It can help you stop being afraid. It can help you stop being worried. When you realize something, you are no longer worried about it. You feel calm again.

Sometimes you realize something that you did not want to see. You might learn that you were wrong about something that you thought was right. This can be hard to do. You might feel sad. You might want to keep the bad thing hidden. But realizing it is important. It helps you understand what is going on.

You can also realize something about yourself. When you learn something new, you can realize how you feel. You might realize that you are afraid of a place. You might realize that you are good at something. Realizing something about yourself can make you feel stronger.
