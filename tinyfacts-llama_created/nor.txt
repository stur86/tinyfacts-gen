Normals are people or things that live in the far land at the top of the great wide water where the cold water goes under the big blue sky. They do not live in the city where the people live in the warm air in the middle part of the big wide water. They live in the high ground where the cold wind and rain come.

They are good at building houses and finding ways to keep themselves warm. They also make food from the hard white stuff that grows from the ground and from the green things that grow in the green ground.

Many years ago, when the great wide water was still small, they moved up to the high ground. They brought their ways of living with them. Today, many small groups of normals live in different parts of the high ground. Some live in the green ground at the top and some live in the cold ground at the bottom of the high ground.

People think they are very quiet and keep to themselves, but some normals are very good at talking and have been known to speak many different words.
