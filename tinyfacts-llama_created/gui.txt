A computer user might use a computer to do many things. They can write a word, look at a picture, or play a game. When they want to do something different, they need to ask the computer to do it. The computer looks at their words and tells them what to do.

The computer has a way to show what is happening. This is called the computer window. The user sees what the computer is doing. The window is like a window to the world. The computer window shows the computer what it is doing.

The computer can also talk to other computers. If a computer does something for one computer, it can do the same thing for another computer. This helps many computers work together.

Some computers have a part that looks like a chair. This is the user. The user sits in the chair and tells the computer what to do. They use words and numbers to tell the computer what they want.

Another part is the computer. It does the hard work. The user just sits and tells the computer what to do. This is like a helper that makes things happen.

A computer has many parts. There are parts that do the hard work, parts that show what is happening, and parts that talk to other computers. When all these parts work together, the computer can do many things.
