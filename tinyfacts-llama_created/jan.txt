The word you are looking for is called a number. A number is a set of many numbers that tells how many things there are. 

Think of a house. One room has two doors. Two doors are two different rooms. If you counters the doors, you get two numbers. That is one number. 

There are many numbers that tell how many things there are. Some numbers are big. One hundred is a very big number. Other numbers are small. One is the number one, two is two, three is three, and so on. 

We use numbers to write stories, to make plans, and to keep track of how much money we have. We also use numbers to play games and to build things.
