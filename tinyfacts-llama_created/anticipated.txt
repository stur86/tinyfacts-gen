To expect something is to think that it is going to happen. You might say you were looking forward to a movie tonight. You had imagined the story, the music, and the people. You thought, I will see this. You are waiting for it.

When you expect something, you are not sure what it is, but you have a feeling. You feel that it is coming. You might feel a bit nervous or a bit calm at the same time.

Sometimes, you expect things that are not real. You might expect a phone call from a friend. You do not know if you will hear it. You are holding your breath and waiting. This is an important way to stay ready for something.

When you are expected, you have to be ready. You do not know when it is going to happen. You have to be ready to act or to say something. You might feel a bit strange or a bit excited.

You can also expect things that are already happened. You might think, I was looking for a book. But you already had it. Your mind is already thinking about it. You are not waiting anymore; you are just remembering.

Everyone does this. We all expect things every day. We expect to get to work on time. We expect to see our friends. We expect a good night. We expect a good day. These are all about being ready.
