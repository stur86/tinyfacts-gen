Another way is to pay with money. You put the money in a box and give it to the person. This is better if you do not want to write your name. Many people also use money to buy food, clothes, and other things they need.

Sometimes, you pay money for a room. If you live in a room with many people, you pay money to each person. This money helps pay for the room. The room is for many people to stay together.

When you pay money, you are helping the person. You are giving them money so they can do their job. The person can then pay people else when they need money. This is how money goes around.
