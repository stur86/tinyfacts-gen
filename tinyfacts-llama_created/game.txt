A game is a thing you do with people. It is a way to have fun and be strong together.

You can play a game with your friends, family, or a computer. Some games are about moving on a line. You move your body or a small thing around. Some games are about hitting a ball or a small thing. Some games are about thinking. You have to use your brain to do something right.

Games can be about many things. You can play a game where you are a person. You are a person who must find a way to get to a house. You must run and jump and use your brain. You have to do this before you reach the house. Or you can play a game where you are many people. You are all people playing together. You must work as a team to do this.

Games are fun because they make you laugh, feel happy, or feel strong. They help you learn too. You learn about the world and how to be safe. Some games are for children. Others are for grown people. Everyone likes games in different ways.

Some games are short. You only play for a few minutes. Other games are for a few hours. Some games are for a whole day. You can play them in a room, on a table, or on a big box. You can play them alone or with many other people.

Games are a great way to have fun. They are a way to be with others. They are a way to learn. They are a way to be strong.
