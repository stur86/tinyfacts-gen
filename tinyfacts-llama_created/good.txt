Good is a word that we use to talk about things that are nice. When you are good, you do things that make people happy. You might be kind to a friend. You might help a person who is in trouble. You also might do things that are right. You act like you are following the right way.

Good is not just about how you feel. It is also about how people see you. If you are good, other people might think you are nice. They might want to be near you. They might smile at you when you walk by. If you are good, you are like a light in a dark room. You are a help to others.

Good is important for everyone. Imagine you have a friend who does not want to help you. This makes you feel sad. But if your friend is good, they will help you. They will be there for you. This makes you feel safe.

There are different kinds of good. There is good that is big. A kind big good is a very important thing. It helps many people at once. It is like a big sun that makes the world warm. There is also good that is small. A small good is a small act of kind. It is like a small hand on a baby. It feels good when you give a small good to someone.

Good can also mean being kind to animals. If you keep a cat when it is afraid, you are being good to the animal. You are showing it that you care. You are not leaving it to live on its own. This is a good thing to do.
