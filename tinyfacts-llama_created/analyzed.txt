To be an answer is when you find something that helps people. When you do this, you are not the one who asked the question. The answer is the one who was looking for it.

Think about a story. You might want to know who did something wrong. You ask a friend. They tell you. You did not ask them. They did the work. They gave you the answer.

Also, if you have a box of food, and you eat all the food, then the box is empty. The food is gone. You did not keep it for later. You used it all. When you do this, the box is an answer.

Sometimes, you might feel sad or angry. You feel the right way. You want to say, I am mad! But you do not say the word. You act on your feeling. The feeling is the answer.

In school, you might study a word. You do not just read the word. You understand what it means. You have found out how to use it. You have been an answer to a problem.

It is also when you try to understand something that is hard. You work at it until you get it. You are the one who found out what you needed to know.

So, to be an answer means you take what is given to you and you change it into something new. You make it better. You help people.
