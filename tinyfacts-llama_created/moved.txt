To move something means you change where it is. You can move a small thing that you hold. You can also move a car, a dog, a tree, or a house.

Imagine you are in a room and you want to get to another room. You step forward. Your feet hit the ground. You push with your legs. Your body starts to move. You are moving. You are no longer in one place.

If you move something, you change its position. You do not keep it the same. If you move a ball, it goes to a different place on the floor. You did not keep the ball in the same spot.

You can move something in a few ways. You can walk. You can run. You can jump. You can also move something quickly across a big body of water. This can happen when a house or a car is pulled across the water.

To move a person can be very hard. Imagine you are a person who is very afraid. You want to get away from a place that makes you feel very bad. You may want to run. You might run fast or you might walk slowly. You keep moving until you feel safe again.

Sometimes, we move from one place to another because we need to find food. Animals move to find food. They move through the green parts of the world. They fly over big bodies of water. They walk across the ground. They move until they find a place with food.
