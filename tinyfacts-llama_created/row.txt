To go along a line of things means to move from one place to another. Imagine you are on a long road with many houses and cars. You want to get to the house next to the one where your friends live. You walk along the road. You walk a short way, and then you cross a small water. You keep walking until you see another road. You follow that road to the house your friends live in. In this way, you go along the line of houses and roads.

In a different way, a group of people can go along a line. Imagine a big group of kids who love to play. Each kid has a favorite game. Some kids like to play with a ball. Some kids like to play with a stick. Some kids like to run. If all the kids go along the line of games, they are following the same line. They move from one game to another.

To move along a line can also mean to go past things. If you are in a room and you want to go to another room, you can walk along the line of the walls. You walk past the desk, past the chairs, past the book, and you finally reach the other room. You are going along the line of the rooms.
