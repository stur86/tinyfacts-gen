To park means you put your car or other thing where it is not moving. You stop it in a place so people can not drive by. This is usually in a big lot or a street with many cars.

Imagine you are at a big store with many people. You need to get a thing that you need. If you walk to the store, many people move around and cars go by. But if you park your car, you can stay there and wait. When you are ready to leave, you get out and walk to the store. You are safe because your car is not moving.

People park cars in many ways. Some put them in a corner. Some put them in a long line. Some put them in a place that is only for cars. Some people even park cars in a way that they are all together so that you do not have to walk very far.

To park is important. It helps people who do not want to walk or who are tired. It makes the streets look nice so people can see each other. It is a way to show you are a person who wants to stay in place and not move around.
