To be a group is to have many people together. When people all work together, they can do many things that they could not do alone. They can help each other and make sure everyone is safe. 

A group can be very big. There are groups with hundreds of people. Or a small group might only have a few people. The people inside the group are often friends. They know each other very well. 

Sometimes, a group has a leader. This person tells everyone what to do and keeps the group running. Other times, everyone decides what to do together. This is called a group where everyone has power. 

In some places, you might have to follow the group. You might not be able to do what you want because the group says you must. But if the group is good and helps people, it is often a good thing.
