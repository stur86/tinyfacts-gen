To park a car means to put it in a safe place where it stops moving. This is usually done in a big area with many cars and people. It helps keep everyone safe while the car is not moving.

Imagine you are at a very busy street. The cars are going very fast and there are many people on the road. If you want to get to your home, you might need to walk to the next street. But if you have a car, you can just drive it to your home and park it right in front of your house. This is the right thing to do.

Some people park cars in front of houses and in front of shops. Other people park cars in big areas with many cars. The people who park cars usually drive slowly so they do not start a car that is moving. They also keep their windows closed and their doors locked.

You can also park cars in a place where you can not drive. This is often done in a big area with many people. People who park cars in these places go to a place where they can sit while they wait. They might eat food, read a book, or talk with a friend.

It is very important to park cars in the right way. If you drive into a street that is closed because of a problem, you might get a fine. A fine is money that you pay for doing something that is not allowed.
