To lead means to move people or things. When you lead a team of people in a group, you tell them what to do and how to act. You are the one who decides what is best and who should follow you.

Imagine you are the head of a school. You are the one who decides what to do during the day. You tell your teachers what to teachers to do. You also decide which games to play and which books to read. Without you, the school would not be in order.

If you want to move something, you can lead it. You might use a long stick to push a small ball across a floor. You are the one who makes the ball go. You are moving it from one place to another.

Sometimes, you lead a group of people who are afraid. You show them that you are strong and that you will keep them safe. This helps them feel better and more ready to face the problems they are facing.

You can also lead a group of animals. You tell them what to do and how to act. You show them what you want them to do. They follow you because they trust you.

To lead is to take control of a group or a thing. It means you are the one who makes things happen.
