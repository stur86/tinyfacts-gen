To do something after another thing is to follow it. You do one thing and then you do another thing. This happens when you move through time.

If you go to the park and you play with a ball, then you go to the home of your friend. You did the first thing. Then you do the second thing. You are doing things after you have done the first one. You are going forward.

If you have a big box of books and you give some to your sister, then you do the first thing. Your sister gets the books. Now you have fewer books. You are doing something after you have done the first thing. You are moving through time.

You can also use this word in short answers. If a teacher asks you a question, you might say you will do it later. You are doing something after you have heard the question. This helps you remember what you need to do.

So, to do something after another thing means you follow it. It is a way to move through time.
