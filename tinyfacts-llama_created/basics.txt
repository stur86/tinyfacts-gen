To have the very first things in something can be very important.

Think of a baby. When the baby is very small, they need the very first things to eat, like air and water. They need the very first things to drink to stay living. After that, they get food from their mother or father. These very first things help them grow and learn.

When you say, someone has the very first things, you mean they know what you do not know. If someone is very good at a game, they have the very first things to do. They know how to hit a ball and run fast.

In life, you need to know the very first things. You need to know how to breathe, how to stand, and how to walk. These are very important. If you do not know these things, you will not be able to do anything.

A school is a place where you learn these very first things. You go there to learn how to read, write, and do your work. You also learn how to work with other people, how to follow a teacher, and how to stay safe.

So, to have the very first things means you know the very first things and can do them. It is about being ready for what is next.
