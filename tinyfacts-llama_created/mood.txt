To have a good or bad mood is to feel how you are. Every day you are in a different place. Some days you are happy, some days you are sad, and some days you are angry. You might feel like you want to jump up and walk away, or you might feel like you want to cry.

When you are happy, your face is bright, and you are smiling. You might laugh or talk with your friends. You feel like you are doing the right thing. This is a good feeling because it makes you want to play, laugh, or be near the people you love.

When you are sad, your face is red. Your eyes might well up with tears. You might want to cry. You might feel that you will never feel better. This is a bad feeling because it makes you want to hide under a bed.

Sometimes, you might feel angry. You might shout or hit things. You might want to hit someone. You feel that the world is against you. This is a bad feeling because it makes you feel like you will never be happy again.

Sometimes, you might feel a feeling that is hard to name. You might feel like you are in the middle of a fight. You might feel like you are afraid. You might feel like you want to hide. This is a bad feeling because it makes you want to stay inside and wait for things to get better.

To know how you are is the best thing you can do. When you know how you are, you can plan your day. You can decide if you want to go to a movie or go for a walk. You can pick a color or a song. You can use this feeling to help you do what you want to do.
