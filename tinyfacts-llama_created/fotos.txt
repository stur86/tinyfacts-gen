Follow is a word that you use when you want to follow someone or something. It is a word that means you are going behind them.

If you follow a dog, you move quickly so you do not step on his feet. You are moving very fast. If you follow a person in a game, you try to catch them. You are trying to be the first one to touch them.

If you follow a story, you read the next part of the story. You do not stop until the end. You want to know what happens next. You use a book or a computer to follow a story.

You can also follow the sun. When the sun is in the sky, you can follow it as it moves across the sky. You might walk in a big line to see where it is going.
