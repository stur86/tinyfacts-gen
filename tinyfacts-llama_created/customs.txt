To do something the way you were raised is a way of life. It is the way you act and the way you think when you are with your family and your friends. Every family has its own way of doing things. They do things a little bit the same every day.

When you are a child, you learn how to do things from your mother or your father. You do not think you are the one who knows better, but you do it because it is the way. You grow up to be the one who does it. People do not change their way just because they get older.

Some ways are like a big word. You know what they mean. You use them every day. Some ways are like a song. You know the words and you know how to act. You do them over and over until you do them like a book.

Everyone has their way of doing things. It is how you act when you are with your family and your friends. Some people like to do things in a different way than others. This is okay. We are all different, and we can all learn from each other.

A way can also mean how you feel when you are angry. You might shout and you might get very mad. But you are still the same person. You are still your mom and your dad. You are still the same boy or girl who loves to play. Your way is still you.

If you do something the way you were raised, you are following your family way. If you do something different, you are following your own way. Everyone has both.
