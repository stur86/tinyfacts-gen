A book is a thing that people read. One book is like a story, with words and pictures that tell a story. When you read a book, you learn about people, places, and things that did not happen before.

Another kind of book is one that has many stories inside it. It is like a big story with many parts. You can pick a story to read. You might read about a man who flies or a girl who loves animals.

A book can also be a book of words that tell you to do things. You might read a book about how to make a cup of coffee or how to fix a car. You learn what the words mean and what you need to do.

Sometimes, people write a book that is not like a story at all. They write it for people who want to learn about a thing. They write about how that thing works. They write so that others can know about it too.

Most books are made of paper and words. You can hold them in your hands, or you can read them on a computer. Some books are very big, with many sides. Others are very small, like a book that fits in your pocket.

People like to read books because they can be the same story many times. You can pick a story and read it over and over. You learn more about the story every time you read it.

There are different kinds of books. There are books for kids, books for grown men and women, and books for people who are very old. Some books are for people who want to learn, and some are for people who want to be happy.

You can find books in a store, a school, a house, or on the computer. You can even buy books that are kept in a box and give them to your friends. When you read a book, you are opening a door to another world, or you are learning about a new thing.
