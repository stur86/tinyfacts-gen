To go by a thing you push is a way to move around. You sit on the thing and hold it with your hands. You push the thing very fast, so you can go far quickly.

People often go this way for fun. They might ride on a road or a trail with many turns. They can see the world around them. They can also go to a park and ride around the big green area. Some people also go to a city and ride their thing along the wide road.

You can also go this way with a group. Many people go together. They sit on many things at once. They all push and hold the things together. They are called a group of people who ride a thing.

When you go this way, you need to pay attention. You have to look at the road and follow the signs. You should also be ready for other people and animals on the road.

This is a good way to stay fit. You are moving your legs and your arms at the same time. Your body gets strong and your legs get faster. You can feel a happy feeling when you ride a thing for a long time.
