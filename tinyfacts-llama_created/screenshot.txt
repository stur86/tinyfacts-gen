To take a picture of a thing that is moving is to make a small picture from it. You use a thing that looks at the world and turns it into a picture. Then you can see the picture on a computer or a phone.

Imagine you are watching a movie. You look at the picture on the computer. The picture is like a real thing that you can see. But if you want to see the thing that happened before the movie started, you can take a picture of it. This is like a small picture of what you saw before the movie.

You can also take a picture of a thing that you see while you are talking. If you look at a computer or a phone, you can take a picture of what you see. This is called a picture of a moving thing.

Sometimes we take pictures of things that are very far away. The picture shows the whole thing. We can see every hair on the person or every word they are saying. This is a good way to remember things that are hard to see.
