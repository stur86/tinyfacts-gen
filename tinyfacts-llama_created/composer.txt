To make music is to put many sounds together in a way that a person can hear. A person who does this is called a music maker. They use things they make and things they buy to make sounds that people can enjoy.

Some people make music by themselves. They write down what sounds they want to hear and then they play it. Others make music with many other people. They all work together to make a new sound.

A person can also be a music maker for other people. They might write a song for a big group of people to single. They write the words and the music so everyone can follow.

Music can be happy or sad. Some people make music to make you feel good when you are sad. Others make music to make you happy when you are angry. They use different sounds to make this happen.

A person who makes music can have a very high job. They can work in a big school where music is part of every day. They can work in a place where music is made for money. They can even work in a place where music is made for people who are very old.

Sometimes, people just like to make music for fun. They play a song they wrote and enjoy the sound. They might play it at a party with their friends. They might also make music for a movie or a game.
