To cover something means to pay for it so everyone can use it. It is about how well something is taken care of.

Imagine you are in a school. The school has a big room where everyone can learn. But one day, the room starts to fall down and the walls start to break. The people in the school are very worried because they do not want anyone to get hurt. The school is like the city, and the people are like the people. They all need to be safe so everyone can stay there.

The school has a person who is in a job like the one of the city. This person is like the person who pays for the school. They pay for the building, the teachers, the computers, and the things the people need. They also pay for the things that happen in the room so everyone is safe.

If the school is not covered, the building will fall down, the teachers will not be able to teachers, and the people will not be able to learn. This means the school will not be there for them anymore. They need to find a way to pay for it so they can stay and help each other.

So, to cover something is to pay for it so everyone can use it or stay safe.
