Returns are when something comes back after you start something. Imagine you build a house. You put in your money and work very hard. After many years, the house is done. You are very happy and feel strong because you did it. Now, you need to pay for the house. When you pay, you are giving back what you already had.

This is also what happens when you buy a car. You pay money for it. Later, you drive the car and it helps you go from one place to another. When you do this many times, the car gets better. It starts to work well and you can go faster. You are giving back the money you paid with. This is a good thing because it helps you get what you need.
