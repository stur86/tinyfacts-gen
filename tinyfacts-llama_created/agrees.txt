When you say something is true, you are saying it is the way it should be. This is what you mean when you agree.

Imagine you and your friend are playing a game. You both know the right way to play the game. If you both say it is the way, you are agreeing. You are not saying the other person is wrong. You are just agreeing with what you think is true.

You can agree on many things. You might agree on what to eat for dinner. You might agree on how to do your job. You might even agree on what to watch on the computer. When you agree, you are showing you trust each other.

Sometimes, you might not agree. Maybe you think the game is not the right way. Maybe you think the food is not good. In this case, you might not agree. But it does not mean you are wrong. You are still showing that you care. You are still showing that you are paying attention.

In life, you can agree with many things. You might agree with your family about what to do. You might agree with a friend about a movie. You might even agree with a teacher about a plan. But when you do not agree, it can be a problem. You need to listen to each other and understand why the other person thinks the way they do.

So, agreeing is about finding the right words to say when you want to show you care. It is about being true to what you think is right.
