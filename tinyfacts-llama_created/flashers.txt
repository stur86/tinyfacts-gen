To flash something means to use light in a way that makes it seem very bright and fast. Imagine you are walking down a street and you see a person. Then, for a second, the person does not move. In that second, the person is making a bright light go from one side to the other. This makes the person look like a tiny bright star.

Flash people, or people who flash, often do this in a way that is not safe. They might stand on the street with their arms up and their hands held out. They might also wear clothes that have a picture or a word on them. When people see them, they might get a little scared. This is because the bright light and the moving arms can make it hard to see what is going on.

In some places, it is against the city or school to flash. If a person is caught doing this, they might have to go to a small room where they stay for a short time. They might also have to write a story about why they did it.

Some people flash for fun. They might stand in a place where they are seen by many people and hold up a sign that says, for one second, I am something else. This is often done at parties or at places where people like to be seen.

Also, there are people who flash pictures of themselves. They take a picture and then they show it to others. This is like telling a story with words, but with a picture. It can be a way to show your life or to show your love for someone.

Everyone has different ways of feeling around flash people. Some people feel happy and want to see them. Some people feel scared and want to go away. Some people just do not want to look at their hands or their faces when they see the flash.
