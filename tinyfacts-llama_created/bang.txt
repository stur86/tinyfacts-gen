To make a loud sound is to make a big noise. When you hit a thing with your hand, you make a loud sound. You might use your hand to hit a door or a box. This makes the door open or the box shut. It is a way to make people hear you.

If you drop something very fast, you might make a sound. This sound is hard to hear because it is very bright. It is like a big, loud jump. It is a big noise that happens very fast.

Sometimes, when you are talking, you might hit your words with your hand. You do this to make your words clear and easy to understand. This is how we say things quickly.
