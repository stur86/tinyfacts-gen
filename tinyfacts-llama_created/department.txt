A group of people who work together to do a job is called a group. Sometimes, a group has many jobs. In a big place, there are many groups that work together. Each group does a different job. They work together to keep things running and to make sure everyone is safe.

Think of it like a school. There are many groups in a school. One group is the ones who teachers. Another group is the ones who clean the rooms. Another group is the ones who help with the money. Each group does a job that is very important.

In the world, there are also groups of people who work together to help other people. If you are hurt, you might go to a group of people who fix things. They work together to help you feel better.

In a city, there are many groups of people who work together to keep things running. They might work in a group that helps the police, a group that helps the doctors, and a group that helps the people who need food. They all work together to keep everyone safe.
