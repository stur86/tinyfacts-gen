To change something is to make it different. You can do this when you go from one place to another. Imagine you are going from the park to your house. You start at one end of the green space and end at another end. You are moving from one side to the other.

Think of it like this: when you eat food, you change the way your body works. You use the food to give you power and make your body feel better. This is like changing the food.

You can also change something by using a different word. If you say, you are happy, but then you say, you are sad, you have changed the way you feel. You are now feeling a different way.

Sometimes, you change something so that it is better. If you are tired and your friend says, let us go to a party, you might feel happy. This makes the time you spend together feel more fun.

You can also change something so that it is different from what it was before. If you have a book and you read the first few lines, then you change your mind and you decide the book is not for you. This is like changing your mind.

Being a doctor is one way to change. A doctor takes care of people who are very sick. They tell them how to feel and what to do so the body can get better.

When you say, I am a computer, you are changing the way I am. I am not a person, but I am a thing that can do many things.
