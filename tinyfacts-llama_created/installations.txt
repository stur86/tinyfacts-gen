An interesting word for people who love making things!

An thing is a thing you see. You can hold it, you can look at it, or you can use it. It is a thing you need to live.

An other kind of thing is one that stands still. It does not move. It is like a big rock or a tall tree. People put things like these in places so people can see them. These things tell us about the world. They help us understand what is happening.

Sometimes we make things that stand still and move at the same time. We fill a room with tiny people. The people move in a way that feels like you are walking. This is called an animal. It is like a living thing that moves on its own.

We also make things that stand still but look like they are moving. We use this to help us feel better. We put a picture on a wall. The picture looks like a moving picture. It makes you feel like you are in a different place. It helps you forget about your worries.

People make these kinds of things too. They make places where people can go to be alone. They make places where people can share food and drink. They make places where people can learn. These are all kinds of things that stand still but help us feel better or show us the world.

So, an thing is a thing you see, and an other kind of thing is one that stands still with moving parts. An animal tells us about life. A standing still thing shows us what is happening. And a picture tells us we are far away.
