To arrive is to move toward a place. When you arrive, you are at the end of a long way. You are the last person who went in. The first one left is usually the person you see when you are standing in front of the place.

You can arrive in many different ways. You can come by car. If you drive a car, you arrive when you reach the place. You can also come by air. If you fly, you arrive when the car with the people and the people with the bags is stopped at the place.

Sometimes, you might not know what place you are going to. You are going to a new town. You have never been there before. You have no idea where you are or where you are going. But when you finally arrive, you feel a big feeling of being safe and happy.

Arriving can be very important. When you arrive at a work place, you might start a new job. You are now a part of the team. You are doing the work that the other people do. You are doing what you were asked to do. Arriving is also important when you arrive at a school. You are now a student. You are learning new things and making new friends.

Arriving is also important when you move to a new home. When you move, you are leaving your old home and your old life behind. You are starting a new life. You are building a new house. You are making a new place to live. You are arriving to a new place where you can be safe and happy.
