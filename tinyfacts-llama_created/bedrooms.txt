A room in a house is where a person lives. It is a space that is just for one person. Most people have one. They put their bed in it, and they keep all their clothes in the room. Inside the room, there is a floor and walls and a high top. There is a window on the top to let in light.

A room can be very small or very big. Some rooms are only a few feet wide and a few feet tall. Other rooms are huge. They have many windows and many doors. Sometimes a room has a window on the side. This can let in more light. Sometimes a room has a door on the back. This door is like a door that you open and close.

People use their bedrooms to rest. They sleep at night. They do not use the room during the day. They keep the room clean and make sure everything is in its place. If a person has many rooms, they might have many different bedrooms. One room might be for sleeping, another room might be for playing games, and another room might be for eating dinner.
