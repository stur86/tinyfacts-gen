A person who gives things to other people. They send things from their place to yourself. When you want a new thing, you ask them if they have it. If they do, you pay them money for it. This is how people buy things they need.

Think of a store. The store is the place where you buy things. The store has many things. People go to the store to buy the things they want. The store is also called a shop. It is a place where people buy things.

A person who gives things to people is not the same as a store. You can buy a thing from a store. But you can not buy a person. A person is a living thing that can not give you a thing. A person can give you a thing, but you give them a thing in return.

Sometimes, people use this word for a place where you get food. If you want to buy food, you go to a store. If you need food, you go to a place where people give you food. This is how people get what they need.
