To free a computer means to set it free from a group that is keeping it. This group is usually a big team that makes the computer do things for you. They might make your computer do things that you did not ask them to do.

When you free a computer, you give it the power to do what you want. You can open many different kinds of books, play music, watch movies, and do many other things without the group having to tell you what to do. You can also change the way your computer works.

Free computers are very good at doing many different jobs. Some people use them to work on their own. Others use them to help people who are in need. Some free computers are even made to help the police and the people who want to keep the world safe.

The word comes from the word free. It means you are not being held back anymore. With a free computer, you are free to do what you want.
