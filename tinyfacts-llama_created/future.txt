The word you are looking for is time. It is the part of life after today. It is the time before you start tomorrow. It is the time when things will happen. You do not know what will happen yet, but you know it is coming.

Time is very big. It is so big that it does not fit in our head. It is like the space between two great walls. But we can use numbers to write it down. We have four numbers that tell us how many years are between now and when something happens. We use these numbers to plan for the days that come after.

You can think about the next day, the next week, the next month. You can look ahead to see what happens in a few weeks, a few months, or many years. You can also think about the years that are going to pass before you are very old. This is called looking at the big picture of time.

Time is moving. Every moment is a new piece of the story. Each moment is a little bit different from the ones that came before it. When you look back, you see how your life has changed. When you look forward, you see what is still to come.

Life is like a long line of days, weeks, months, years. Each day is one part of the line. You start at the very beginning and walk forward. There are many days, many weeks, many months. And then you are in a new place, a new time. You have a new life.
