To act is to be in a situation and do things. When you act, you are not really there. You are not in the room. You are in your own mind. You are thinking of how to do it.

Imagine you want to go to a party. You do not just stand in front of the door. You are in the room before you go. You are thinking, I want to have fun. You are thinking, I want to meet new people. You are thinking, I want to eat food. You are doing all these things. You are acting.

You can act in many ways. You can act like a person who is afraid. You can act like a person who is very happy. You can act like a person who is trying to be strong. You can act like a person who is trying to be quiet.

When you act, you are not just doing things. You are also being. You are being the person you want to be. You are being the person you think you should be. Acting helps us live. It helps us understand how to feel and how to act in different situations.
