A group of things is a plan for how you will spend your time. You tell each thing what time it starts and what time it ends.

You might have a work day. You wake up early and go to a place where you work. You do your job for a few hours. Then you go home and watch something on your phone. You might have a break in between. You can use this time to do something you like, like play a game or read a book.

You also have a time for school. You go to a school and learn new things. You see a teacher who tells you what to do. You do your work and study. You go home at the end of the day.

Sometimes, you have a time for fun. You might go to a movie. You might go to a game with your friends. You might go to a park or a field. This is a time when you do something you enjoy.

A person might also have a time for doing things. If you are a doctor, you have a time to come to the hospital. You go to the hospital and see a person who is sick. You do a check. You give them a drink. You help them feel better.

You can also have a time for work and a time for rest. You need to rest so your body can stay strong. You might sleep at night and sleep during the day. When you rest, you are not doing anything. You are just your body.

Every day, you have a set of things. You do this set of things at the same time every day. You follow the same plan. You are doing the same thing at the same time. This is a way of living.
