To disappear means to go away. When you go to bed, you close your eyes and sleep. You are not there anymore. Then, the light from outside the window comes in. It fills your room, and you are there again. This is how things go round and round.

A person can also disappear. If you are angry, you might go away and not speak to the other person. You might do something else. You are not here anymore.

You can also disappear something. If you are working on a job, and you feel you do not want to do it anymore, you might stop. You are not doing it anymore. You are gone from the work.

To disappear a thing can also mean to let go. If you have a thing you love, but you do not want it anymore, you can disappear it. You are letting it go. This is a good thing. It makes you free to do other things.

You can also disappear a story. If a story is going well, and you do not want to hear it anymore, you can stop reading. You are not listening to it anymore. You have gone away from the story.
