To go back and then go around the same way is to make a round trip without stopping. It is about being ready to start again. 

Imagine you are playing a game. You run to the other side. You are tired. You go to the table. You eat a small thing. You play again. You go again. This keeps happening many times. You keep going back and moving in a big way. You do not stop until you reach the end of your round.
