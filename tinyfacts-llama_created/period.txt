A time is a very long way between two things. You can see this in your life. A month is a short time. You go from one month to the next, and you do the same things every month.

A big time is many months or many years. It is a long way from one thing to the next. You can read a book or watch many movies during this time. You can learn a lot and change your life.

Sometimes you use this word to talk about the sun. When the sun is in a new part of its round, it is not in the sun. Instead, it is in a different place. We say it is in the new part of the sun. This happens many times a year. It is a time of change.

People also use this word for things that happen in a story. If a story is about a long time, you call it a long story. If a story is about a few days, you call it a short story. This word can help you understand what is happening in a story.

You can also use it to talk about when things start. If you start a new thing, you are in a new time. It is a time when you begin to learn or do a new job. It is a time of change.
