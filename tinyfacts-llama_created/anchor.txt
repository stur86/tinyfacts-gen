An air in a bag or a ball that holds things up while they are not moving on the water or the ground.

Imagine you have a bag with a small piece of wood inside it. The wood is heavy. When the wind is very strong, the bag wants to go down. The small piece of wood helps hold the bag up. This small piece of wood is like a very strong hand inside the bag. It keeps everything from falling down.

The small piece of wood is also very good at holding things up. It does not let the bag go down. It holds everything that is in the bag.

This is why you need a small piece of wood in your bag or ball when you are not moving. It helps you stay safe on the water or the ground.

A car also has this. It has a round thing in the middle. This round thing is made of hard stuff. It keeps the car up on the ground. If it fell down, the car would go very fast and might fall on top of people. The round thing in the middle keeps everything safe.

A tree also has this. It has a strong part in the middle. This part holds the tree up. It is like a strong hand that keeps everything from falling down.

When something is held up, it is not falling. It is just sitting there. The small piece of wood in the bag or the ball is the one doing the holding.
