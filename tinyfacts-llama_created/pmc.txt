A phone number is a number that you use to talk to other people. You put the number on your phone and then you call that person. The person who answers the phone usually says if you are calling the right person. If the person says no, you need to try again.

A phone number has three numbers. The first number is the one you give to the phone. The second number is the one the phone calls back to. The third number is the person you are calling. It is usually the number of the person who answers the phone. The third number is why the person is called.

Most phone numbers are like this:  number  number  number. The first number is the area number of the area where the person lives. The second number is the area number of the city or town where the person lives. The third number is the phone number of the person.

You can also have a phone number for a business. This is like a phone number for a store. When you call that number, you are talking to the person who works there. They might answer and ask you what you want.

A phone number is important because it lets people know when you are there. If you call a number and the person says no, you can try again later. This is why phone numbers are like keys to other people.

Some phone numbers are very long. These numbers might have many numbers after the first three numbers. Each of those numbers might be a different person. These numbers are like a book of phone numbers. You can look up the phone number in that book to find out who it is.
