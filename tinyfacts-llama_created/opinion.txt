An idea is when someone thinks about a thing. You might think it is the best way to do something, or you might think it is the worst way.

An idea is not always the same as what others think. You might have an idea that you are the only one who feels this way. But if many people around you also think the same way, then you may change your idea. This is how we learn and grow.

You can have an idea about how things work. You might think that light goes from one end of a room to the other. Or you might think that a thing will not work if you leave it for a long time. This idea helps you understand the world.

You can also have an idea about how to act. If you think a person should be kind, you might do this. Or if you think that a person should be afraid, you might act this way.

Opens is another word for an idea. When you have an open idea, you are not afraid to let others know. You might be the first to speak up about your idea.

Sometimes, people have different ideas. This can be very confusing. You might want to do something, but your friend thinks you should do something else. If you both agree, you can work together. If you do not agree, you might have a big fight.
