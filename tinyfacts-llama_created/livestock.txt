Living means you live with animals. These animals give food for you and help you grow. You take care of them by giving them water, food, and a safe place to live.

There are many kinds of animals that are kept for this. Some are small and can walk. Others are big and need to be held in a box. You might have animals that jump around in a field, or animals that live in the water. Some are even animals that fly through the air!

The owner of the animals is usually a person who has a big land with a place for the animals to live. The person makes sure the animals have enough food, water, and space. They also make sure the animals are safe from other animals that might want to eat them.

When people keep animals, they must follow some big ideas. First, they must not hurt the animals too much. They must not keep the animals in a box for too long. They must also give the animals enough air and keep them clean.

Animals can be very important for food. The animals that eat green things make food for us to eat. Some animals give us things to drink or to use in our hands. And some animals help keep our homes clean by eating things that might make our homes sick.

In some places, people keep animals for fun. They might go to a show where animals act like people. They might even have a big show with animals that can run fast, jump high, or fly through the air!
