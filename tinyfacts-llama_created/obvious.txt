When something is obviously clear, you know it right away. This is because your brain knows it without having to think about it.

Think about a room. If you look at a chair, it is obviously there. You do not need to think about it. You just see it. If you are in a dark room and you see a light, you know it is there. This is because your brain knows to look for the light.

This happens with words too. If you say something and everyone knows what you mean, you are being obviously clear. You do not have to say it again. Your word is enough to understand what you are talking about.

It is also about how you act. If you are very nice to someone, they know that you are kind. This is obviously clear. You do not have to say, but everyone can see that you are being nice.

Sometimes, something might not be clear at first. You are trying to understand something and you are confused. But then you see or hear something that makes it clear. This is when something is obviously clear.
