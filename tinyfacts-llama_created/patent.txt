To get a new idea is one thing. To keep your idea and not let anyone else use it is another. This is what a person does when they get a new kind of thing they made, like a car or a box you buy at the store.

When a person thinks of an idea, they tell the people who work for the place where they live or work. They write this down so they can keep it safe. Then the person shows this idea to the people in the place. If many people want the same idea, the person can get a paper that says they are the first one to do it. This paper is like a sign to the world that the person has the right to make this thing.

Everyone else has to wait until the person gives them the paper. If someone wants to use the same idea but they don't have the paper, they can't. They can't play a game or make a thing without the right papers.

The person who gets the paper can keep their idea for many years. When the years go by and no one else wants to use the idea anymore, the person can say, here is my idea. The person who got the paper first keeps it.

This is how new things are made. It is a way to keep ideas safe and to say who gets to do what.
