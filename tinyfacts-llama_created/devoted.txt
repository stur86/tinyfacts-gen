To be a person is not just about what you do. It is also about who you are and what you love. You can be a person who is very happy. You might laugh a lot. You might have a big smile on your face. Or you can be a person who is very sad. You might cry a lot. You might feel very down.

You can also be a person who loves other people. You might love your family. You might love your friends. You might love animals. You love the world around you. You love how things work. You love how people act when they are kind.

Sometimes, you might be a person who does not love what you do. You might feel very angry. You might feel very scared. You might want to leave. But you are still a person. You are still you. You are still the same person who loves your family and your friends.

To be a person is also about what you give. You give love. You give your time. You give your heart. You give your body. You give your mind. You give yourself to others. You do not just take. You give. And that is what makes you a good person.
