To raise something means to make it bigger or stronger. You can raise a child, a dog, a small thing that you find on the ground, or a big heavy thing that is sitting on a table.

You can also raise your voice. If you are angry, you might raise your voice to make others hear you. This is because your words are coming from a high place and they can reach many people.

Sometimes, you raise money. If you have a lot of money in a box and you want to share it with many people, you use your money to buy things so everyone has enough. You raise money to help people or to buy food for families who are very short on money.

In some places, you can raise your hand to show you are ready to act. This is called raising your hand. It means you are not afraid and you are ready to do what you have to do.

When you raise your voice, you are not afraid. You are standing up and you are saying that you are there. You are not hiding. You are saying, I am here.
