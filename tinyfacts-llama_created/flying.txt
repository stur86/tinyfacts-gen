To fly is to move through the air. You sit in a thing with open doors and push it with your hands. The thing moves from one place to another, very fast. You can fly over the ground, or you can fly over the water. When you fly, you leave the ground behind and go up into the blue sky.

When you fly, you do not need to walk or run. You do not need to carry a bag or a big thing on your back. You just sit and hold the thing together. The thing does all the work for you.

Some things fly very high. They go very fast and can reach very high places. They fly over many big cities and over open fields. They can even fly over the wide water and land on a big rock.

There are many kinds of flying things. There are small things with soft things under them. These things fly very fast and can go very high. There are also big things that fly very low over the ground. They carry people and things to places that are far away.

Flying is a fun way to go. You can feel like you are on a big wind and you can see many things from high up. Many people like to fly because it is a great way to see the world.
