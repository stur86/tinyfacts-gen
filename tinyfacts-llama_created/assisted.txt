To be helped means you need help to do something. When you need help, someone else will do it for you. This can happen with many things. You might need to get out of a car. A helper pulls you up so you can walk.

You can also be helped by a friend. A friend is a person you know. They might give you a word to say if you are sad. They might help you do something that is hard for you. A friend can also be a person who is very good at doing a job. They can do it faster and better than you.

Sometimes, when you want to do something, you need to ask for help. You might say, I need help with this book. The person who works at a store will come and help you. They will read it for you so you can read it.

You can also be helped by a computer. If you want to look at a picture, your computer can help you. It will show you what you want to see. It does not do it for you, but it helps you find what you need.

When we are helped, it is good. It makes our lives easier.
