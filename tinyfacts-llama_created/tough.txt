The word you are looking for is strong. It means something is very hard to break. If you hit a ball with a hard ball, the ball might break. But if you hit a rock with a hard stick, the rock will not break. The rock is strong, and it can hold up to many things that try to attack it.

You can also use this word to talk about people. If a person is very strong, they are able to do many things. They might be able to lift heavy things or run very fast. This kind of person is not afraid of hard work. They are like the rock, ready to face any problem that comes up.

Sometimes, people say the word to talk about things that are not easy. If you have to work very hard, you might feel strong. You might feel like you can do anything. This word helps us understand how strong we are when we face a big job or a hard time.
