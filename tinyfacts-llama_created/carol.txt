A car is a thing you use to move around. It has many parts. One part is the front, where you sit. The side has windows so you can see outside. The back has a seat for many people.

A car also has a power part. This part makes the car move. It uses a tiny bit of power that comes from a box. The power part is inside the car.

You can drive the car on the road. You push the tiny bit of power with your feet. You can also turn the car so it goes in a different way.

There are many different kinds of cars. Some are small. Some are very big. Some have many seats. Some have only two seats. Some cars can fly!

People use cars for many things. They go to work. They go to school. They go to meet friends. Some people have many cars. They have many cars for their families.

We also use cars to go to places. We might go to the store. We might go to the park. We might go to see a movie. Cars are very good for moving people.

A car song is a song that uses words from the car. People singling these songs is fun. They make people happy.

In some places, we use cars on the day after a big day. This is called a day of rest. Everyone is not working. People go to the park or to a big place with a lot of food. They have fun.

We also use cars to go to the hospital. When someone is very sick, the car takes them to a place where they can get better. The car is safe because it has many people inside to help.

In the dark, we might use cars. A car has a light on the top. The light helps us see in the dark. Some cars also have a thing that makes the car go very fast.

Cars are very important. They help people move around. They make our lives easier.
