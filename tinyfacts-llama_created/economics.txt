To make money is to take things you need and turn them into money. This is how people make a living. The word comes from an old man who made a lot of money by moving things from one place to another. He would carry heavy things in his bag.

Many people do this with money they save. They put their money in a big box so they can use it later. Some work for a group that gives them money to buy things. They do this job so they can buy food, clothes, and houses.

When you buy things, you pay for them. You pay with money. The money goes from your pocket to the store. The store keeps the money and gives you the things you want. People also buy things from each other. When you buy a car, you give the car man some money. He gives you the car and you have the money back.

Some people work very hard and get a lot of money. They use their money to buy many things. They also use their money to help others. They give money to those who are sick or hurt. This makes the world a better place.

But there are also bad ways to make money. People might take money from someone else and not give it back. They take money from their friends and family. This can cause big problems. It can make people sad and angry. It can also make it hard for everyone to live a good life.
