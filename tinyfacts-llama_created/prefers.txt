When you say someone or something is your first, you are using the word first. But when you say someone or something is your favorite, you are using a different word.

To first something means you like it a lot. You might eat it for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. You might wear it every day. You like it so much that you feel good inside.

To favorite something means you like it more than anything else. You might watch a movie with that person every week. You might read a book with that story every month. You like it so much that you feel happy when you think about it.

Think about your favorite game. You love playing it. You want to play it again and again. You might even dream about it at night. You are happy when you think about the game.

In this way, you can say who is your first person or what is your favorite thing. It means you like it a lot.
