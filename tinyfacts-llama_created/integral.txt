An area that is under a line is a part of the world. The area under a line is the area between the line and the ground. If you draw a line on a piece of paper and put a small area under it, you have found the area under that line. Every point in space has an area under it, even if the point is very far away. If you know where everything is, you can find where everything is under the line.

This word is often used when you talk about how many things are in a group. Imagine you have many books on a table. Each book is a small area under the table. If you countered all these areas, you would get the totally of the table. You can use this word for any group of things.
