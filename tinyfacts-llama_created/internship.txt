An interest is a thing you do to help someone. When you do this, you are helping them learn. It is like being a little friend. You are not a big person, but you are very important to them.

If you work with a big group, you might be asked to do a small job. This is a small part of the work. You are not the top person. You are there to help them learn. You might have to do this for many weeks or months.

Sometimes, people do this job because they want to learn how to do something new. They want to see how it works. They might want to be sure if it is right before they move on to a bigger job.

You can do this job in many different places. You can work in a small office. You can work in a big school. You can work in a small town. Where you work is not as important as what you do.

You might not get money for this job. You might have to work for free. But if you are doing this job, you are learning something new. You are helping someone. You are a little stronger because of it.
