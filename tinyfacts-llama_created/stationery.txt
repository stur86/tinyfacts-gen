To write something is to use paper, a hard white thing you can hold in your hand. You put words on it. When you are ready, you take the paper and put it on a table. You write a name, a word, or a story. You use a thing called a desk to put the paper down. You do this many times.

People use this word to talk about things that help you write. You might say, a person is a writer. Or, you might say, she has a lot of writing. This word is about the things you use to make words happen.

You can also say a person is in a quiet place. This means they are not doing anything else. They are sitting in a room with chairs and a table. They are waiting for something to happen.

In a city, you might see a building that has many rooms. Inside those rooms, people work. They write papers. They talk to each other. They write things that help other people. This building is a place where people write.

People who are good at writing often have a bag with them. They carry the bag with them all day. Inside the bag, they put things they need to write. They might have a box with paper. They might have a small computer. They might even have a book with stories. This bag helps them stay ready to write.
