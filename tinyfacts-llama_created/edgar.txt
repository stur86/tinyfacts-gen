A person who lives alone is called a man. A man must have a name and a home where he lives with other people. He must also have a job and buy food to eat. A man can have many friends. They can go to a park or a movie together. They can also play games.

Sometimes a man is a writer. He writes stories, books, or songs that people read and listen to. He uses words to tell a story. A man can also be a teacher. He tells people how to do things. He helps them learn a new thing. A man can be a doctor or a police person. They use their brain and their body to keep people safe.

A man can also be a person who does not live with other people. He has a box in his room and a table. He sits at his desk and works on his computer. He reads books. He watches movies. He plays games. He lives alone. He does not need a family to be happy. This is a kind of life.
