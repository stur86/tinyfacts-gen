An air is very high up in the sky. When you fly, you go up into the sky. You can see for a long time and see far away. Sometimes, you might see the ground or the water. The air is all around you.

You can also fly over trees and houses. People fly in big pieces of air that go very fast. They carry many people at once. You can feel the wind and the ground when you fly. It is like you are moving very fast.

Some things that fly are not made of air. They are made of hard stuff that goes very fast. They do not go up into the air like a person or a car. They go very fast and can fly over many places at once.
