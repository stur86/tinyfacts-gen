To make a plan

A plan is when you decide what you want to do and how you will do it. It is a way to stay safe and to not spend too much money. You think about what you need to buy, how much you have to pay, and how you will pay it.

Imagine you have a big box of money. You want to buy a new book. You need to decide how much money you will give to the store. If you spend too much, you will be in trouble. You need to think about your plan so you do not break the money.

You can make a plan for many things: a big money plan, a plan for a school year, or a plan for your life. This helps you stay safe and avoid trouble.
