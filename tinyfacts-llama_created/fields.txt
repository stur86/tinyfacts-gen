To fields means to have many green places where many people live or where animals live. These are places that have lots of green and not many trees.

Imagine a big area with many short lines. These lines are like the edges of the green area. When the green area is full of green, the lines are full of green too. The green is from the green things growing on the lines. The lines are also full of small animals and other small things that live there.

People use these areas for many reasons. Some people live in them. Some live in houses made of wood near them. Some have houses made of hard stuff that they can go into. Some have big houses with a big area inside. They grow food and make clothes. Some have animals and watch them. Some have big areas where people go to play.

When the green is growing well, the lines can be very green. When the lines are brown, it might mean the lines need water. When the lines are very dry, the area can be very hard to walk. The green line can also show how much water is coming from the ground.

In some places, people use the lines to go to school. They walk on the lines to get to the school. Some people walk a long time to get there. Others have cars or buses that go there. They go to learn how to read, write, and do other things.

In another way, to fields means to let people do something. You might say, a family is going to fields to grow food. Or, a group of people is going to fields to play or to watch animals. This means they are going to be in one place for a while, doing their own work, and enjoying the green area.
