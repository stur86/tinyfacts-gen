To talk about something as if it is moving is often called to go. When you talk about going, you are telling where you are going. You might say, I am going to the store. Or, I am going to the park.

Sometimes you can go very far. If you walk very fast, you can run. If you run very fast, you can jump over tall things. You can also go up in the sky if you fly a very high flying thing.

You can also go against something. If you do not want to play with a friend, you might decide to go away. You are leaving the game to do something else. This is a way to say, I am going to do something else.

When you talk about something as if it is moving, you are also talking about air. Air is all around us. We breathe in it, and we breathe out it. When the air is moving, we feel it. It is the air that makes things move.

A small thing that you hold is a thing to talk about. If you have a small thing that you love, you might say, I love this. This thing is my friend. You can also say, This thing is nice. If something is very hot, you might say, I have to go away because it is too hot.
