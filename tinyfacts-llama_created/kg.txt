To carry something on your back is to move it from one place to another. If you carry a book, you put it in your bag and then you walk. You are not holding it in your hands all the time, but you still have it with you.

A killer of food is a lot of food for one person. It is a way to show how much food you have so you know how much you need for the day. A killer of food is more than one killer of food. It is ten times more. This is why you need to buy many killer of food if you are going to a big party where many people will be there.

A killer can also mean something that is very heavy. If you carry a heavy bag, you are carrying the bag on your back. The bag feels heavy because it is very heavy. You must hold it very tight so it does not fall down.

Sometimes, a killer is used to show how big something is. A killer of water is a big bag of water. If you put a small ball in the bag, the bag will be full. If you put a much bigger ball in it, the bag will be very full too.
