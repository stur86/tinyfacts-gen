Shaking is a word that comes from a story. A long time ago, a man from a far away land told a story about a girl. The girl was a very good dancer. She could jump high and run fast. She loved to dance.

The story said that the girl had a friend who was very strong. The friend could lift a heavy box. The girl and her friend would dance while the friend lifted the box. The friend would lift the box many times, and the girl would jump high and dance around them.

The word shaking comes from this story. When a person does something very fast, their body does a shaking. It is like they are moving their arms and legs very quickly. This is why we say someone is shaking.

Today, people use this word to mean anything that is very fast or moving around. A person shaking is a runner. A person shaking is a computer. A person shaking can also mean something that is very loud or very bright.

You might hear this word in a song. Shaking is a word used to show how fast a person is moving.
