To even out something means to make it the same again. You start with a big change. The ground shakes, the sky turns dark, and the air gets cold. The first thing you do is try to stop the change. You cover your face, put on a hard thing on your head, and try to hold on to the ground. It is hard because every second, the ground shakes more. Your head is hard because you feel the ground moving. You try to stay still and hold on to the ground. Then, something strange happens. The ground stops shaking and the sky turns light. The cold air is gone. The hard thing on your head feels strange. The ground feels different. You are still trying to stop the change. But it is over. The ground is now the same again. The sky is the same light. The air is the same warm. The world has returned to what it was before. This is what is meant by even out.
