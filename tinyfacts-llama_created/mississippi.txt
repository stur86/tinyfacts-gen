The big water from the sky that runs across the land is called the big water. It starts in a high place in the far away part of the world. From there it goes down through many wide lands, past many big wide bodies of water and many tall tall trees. It goes through a long wide part of the land where the high ground drops down very fast. At one point it meets another wide water and then it goes on down again.

As the water goes down, it gets smaller and smaller. It often gets very quiet and stays still for a long time. But then it starts moving again when the ground under it gets warm from the sun. The water starts to move very fast and it goes over rocks and through low places. It can make many small wide bodies of water behind it.

In some places the water goes over the top of tall high ground. Then it goes down the other side. This can make the ground look like it is falling down. The water can also go under the ground and come up again through other big wide bodies of water.

The big water from the sky is very important. It gives water to many wide lands. It helps grow green things and makes the ground soft so it does not fall down. It also gives food to many kinds of animals that live in the water and on the ground. Without the big water, the land would not be able to grow green and many animals would not have food to eat.
