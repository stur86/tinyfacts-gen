To be an actor is a job where you act out a story in a movie or a play. You wear a big white box on your head and put on a set of clothes. You act like you are a person who is afraid, or you act like you are a person who loves a person. You do this for many hours and days.

When you act, you are not really the person. You are the person acting. You use your words and your face to tell the story. The people watching the movie do not see you. They only see the story.

Sometimes people act in other ways. They act like a dog, a car, or a tree. This is a fun job because you get to be many things at once.

You can also act in music. A person who makes music might be a person who is very good with their hands and with their voice. They act out the words and the music. They use their body and their face to tell the story.

To be an actor is to put yourself into a story. You are not the real person, but you are the person you are acting. You are the story.
