To have someone means to know who they are and who they are with. When you say you have a friend, you mean you know this person and they know you. If you have a child, you have a little one. A child grows up to be a man or a woman. This word is very important because it shows how you feel about these people.

You might say, I love my family. This means you like them. You care about them. You feel safe with them. If you say, I love my dog, you mean you care about this animal too. You give it food and keep it safe. You like it very much.

Sometimes you might say, She is my wife. This means she is my wife. She is the person you love and want to live with. You do not want to leave her. She is the person you want to be with.

People also use this word to mean, He is the one. This means he is the person you think is the best. You like him a lot because he is kind and does what is right.
