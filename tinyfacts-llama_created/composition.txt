To make something new often means to put many things together. When you write a story, you use many words. You do not just use one word. You use many words in different ways.

Imagine you want to show the world how a house looks. You can show them from one side. You can show them from the front. You can also show them from the back. You have many different pictures. Each picture is like a small part of the whole thing. When you put all the pictures together, you have a picture that is new and different from the old ones.

You can also make music. A group of people can put their voices together. They use words, but they do not just say the same word many times. They use different words in different ways. They say some words to make a group sound like one person. They say other words to make the group sound like two people. They say some words to make the group sound like three people. They keep doing this until they have many different sounds. Then, when you listen to the music, you hear a group of sounds that are new and different from the old ones.

Making something new often helps us understand the world better. When you learn many new words, you can find more ways to use them. You can use them to talk about different things. You can use them to write stories. You can use them to draw pictures. You can use them to build a house. Or you can use them to make music.
