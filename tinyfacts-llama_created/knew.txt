To know something is to understand it. You do not just see it. You also feel it. You may be afraid of a person or a place. You know that thing is there because you saw it before.

When you are young, you often do not know things yet. But as you grow, you learn more. You find out things you did not know before. You read books or watch movies. You learn about the world and how it works.

You can know something by looking at it. You can see how a thing looks. You can hear it. You can feel it. Or you can know it by thinking about it. You can think about what it means. You can know a word. You know that it is the word for a thing.

You can also know something about a person. You might know their name. You might know how they feel. You might know what they like to do. When you know something about a person, you feel like you have a better understanding of them.

Knowing is important. It helps you make sense of your life. It helps you decide what to do. It helps you feel safe and happy.
