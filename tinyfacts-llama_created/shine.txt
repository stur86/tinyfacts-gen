To show light is to make something bright. You can do this by moving a small thing that makes light around you. This kind of light is often very bright. You might see it while you walk down a street at night. Or you might see a small thing that is very hot and makes a bright light.

A person can also show light. If you put a small piece of glass in front of a bright light, the light comes out of the glass. This is how you see things in a room. Your eyes take in the light and send it to your brain so you can see.

Sometimes, a person is so happy they might even feel they are going to burst. They might feel light inside their chest. This can happen when you love someone very much. It is a warm feeling that makes you want to jump up and shout.

In a kitchen, you can use a small thing that makes light to cut through food. You put the food in a box and press a small round thing. The round thing cuts through the food and makes it look nice. This is how you make food that looks pretty.

Light can also go around things. When you look at a bright sky at night, the light from the sun goes out of the sky and into your eyes. Your brain takes in the light and makes it look like the sky is still there. This happens when the light from the sun is very strong.

You can also see light when the sun is low in the sky. The light from the sun has to go through more air before it reaches your eyes. This is why the sky looks different before the sun goes down. The light is often very soft and past bright.
