An answer to a problem is called an answer.

Imagine you are trying to figure out how many books you need to buy for your class. You know you have a set of numbers, but you need to find out if the numbers are the right ones. You make a listening word: if you have a book, and you have a book, and you have a book, and so on, then the word is: one, two, three, and so on.

To use this word, you need to put the numbers into a line, like this:  one, two, three. You do not ask your friends to help you. You just do it by yourself.

You can also make an answer to a story. If you know how the story ends, you can tell the people who asked you what happened. You did not ask them; you just know the end. This is a kind of answer.

Sometimes, people use this word when talking about numbers and things that do not change. If you have a number of things, and you add a little more, then you get a new number. This number is the answer. This answer is important because it helps you know how many things you have now.

There are many kinds of answers. There is an answer to a question, like the one above. There is an answer to a problem, like the answer to a game. There is an answer to a story, like the end of a book. There is an answer to a word, like when you understand what a word means. And there is an answer to a thing, like how much money a person has.
