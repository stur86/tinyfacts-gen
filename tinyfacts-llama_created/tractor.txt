A big round thing that moves over the ground is called a car. It is good for many things. It can carry people, boxes, and many other things from one place to another. It does this by moving around in a slow way.

The round thing has many moving parts. Some of these parts are under the car. These parts go round and round very fast. When the car moves, the parts go round and round with it. This makes the car turn. If the car wants to go in a straight line, it must keep moving in a straight line.

There is a man who controls this round thing. He sits inside the car. He uses long lines with a small round thing on the end to tell the car what to do. He can tell the car to go fast, slow, turn left, turn right, or stop.

The car also has a big box under it. This box holds many things the car needs to stay moving. The box can hold food, water, and other things that help the car go. The car can also carry many other things if the box is full.

A round thing that moves over the ground is a very important thing. It helps people get places quickly and makes many jobs easier.
