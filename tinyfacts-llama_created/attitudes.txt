An Attacker is a Person Who Does Not Like Another Person. They Do Not Want to Talk or Play. They Do Not Want to Be Near You.

They Are Often Angry. They Are Often Mad. They Do Not Want You to Be Happy.

They Say Bad Things About You. They Say You Are Wrong. They Say You Are a Bad Person.

They Do Not Want to Be Friends. They Do Not Want to Help You. They Only Want to Hurt You.

They Might Say You Are the One Who Is the Problem. They Might Say You Are the One Who Did Something Wrong.

They Do Not Care About Your Feel. They Do Not Care About Your Life. They Only Care About How They Can Make You Feel Bad.

But You Can Change Their Mind. You Can Tell Them You Do Not Like Them. You Can Say You Are Mad.

When You Say This, They Often Get Very Angry. They Might Get Very Sad. They Might Say They Do Not Want to Talk.

Sometimes People Try to Get Away From These People. They Try to Find Other Friends. They Try to Do Other Things. They Try to Be Happy.

But Sometimes People Do Not Want to Leave. They Stay Near the Person They Hate. They Stay Near the Person They Are Mad At.

People Are Always Changing. Sometimes We Are Angry. Sometimes We Are Mad. Sometimes We Are Happy. Sometimes We Are Sad.

And Sometimes We Need to Let Go. We Need to Let the Person Go. We Need to Let the Sad Feel Go.

So Attacking Is Not Good. It Is Not Nice. It Is Not Right.
