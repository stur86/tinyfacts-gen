A bathroom is a room in a house where people go to clean their bodies. It is a place where you can get warm water and clean your skin. You might go in the morning to wake up, and you might go in the evening after you have done your work.

Inside the room, there is a big water thing. You fill the water in the water thing, and then you turn a handle. The water comes out, and it goes down the long, round thing on the floor. You sit on the floor with your back against the wall, and the water comes up to your neck. You can feel the water all over your body. This is the best part. You get clean and feel very good.

Sometimes, the room has a small water thing with a long long round thing in it. This water goes up your nose and down your throat. This is called a shower. You might use a lot of water and get very hot, but this is also very good. Your skin feels nice after.

You can also have a bathroom with a little table and a chair. You sit on the chair, and the water goes down the long round thing under the table. This is a quiet way to clean your body. You can sit for a long time and feel very calm.

Some rooms have a bathroom with a window. You can look out at the sky or at the water. This is a nice way to relax and feel safe. You can sit and watch the water go by while you clean your body.

Even though you are not in the water, you still feel clean. You feel like you are doing something important to your body. This is why people like to go to the bathroom every day.
