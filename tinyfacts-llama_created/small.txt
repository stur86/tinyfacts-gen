Small is a word that means you are very small. It is like a tiny thing. You can not see it with your eyes. You can only feel it with your hands or your ears.

Imagine you are standing on a big floor. If you are very small, you can move across that floor in one step. Your feet are so tiny that they can fit in a space the wide of two fingers.

A small dog is a dog that is not as big as a big dog. A small car is a car that is not as big as a very big car. A small book is a book that is not as big as a very big book. A small house is a house that is not as big as a very big house.

Sometimes, small is used to talk about the tiny things in the world. The air around you is small. The water in a small cup is small. The light from the sun is small.

It can also mean you are not in a big group. If you are small, you are alone. If you are small in a group, you are not the biggest person.

The word small is important because it shows us how small things are. It helps us understand how big things are too.
