Kids are young people, usually between the ages of one and twenty. They are often small and full of life. Children learn about the world around them, and they make many new friends.

Some kids are very small, like a baby. They do not have much hair yet. Other kids are much bigger, like a little person. They can run and jump very fast.

Many kids go to school. They learn to read, write, and do their work. They also learn about the world, how people live, and how to be kind to others. Some kids are very good at numbers and letters. Others are good at making things or fixing things that break.

Some kids like to play with their hands. They might build with blocks. Others like to run around with their friends. Some kids like to watch movies or play games. Many kids also like to draw or make music.

When kids are very young, they are often called babies. As they grow older, they become children. Children may have a mom and a dad, or they may have a mom and a dad or another mom and another dad. Children need love and care from their families to grow strong and happy.
