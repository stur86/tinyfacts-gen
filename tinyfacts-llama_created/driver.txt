To drive something is to move it from one place to another. A driver is the person who does this. They sit in a car or a big box with many parts. The driver is the one who tells the car how to go.

Imagine you want to go to school. You stand in front of a big box that can move. The box has a seat and many lines that help it go. You tell the box which line to follow and how fast to go. The box starts moving. You sit in the seat and hold on tight. The box is doing the work for you.

A driver does the same thing with cars, buses, and big boxes that carry many people. They must always follow the lines and the road signs. They also have to be very carefully. They watch their back to see if anyone is behind them. They also watch the road to see if there are cars coming.

Sometimes, a driver is the one who drives to a place where food is made. They drive through the city and follow the roads that lead to the food store. When they arrive, they help the person who runs the store put food in the cars. The driver does not touch the food, but they make sure the cars are ready to go at the right time.

In some places, people have to drive to work. They might drive on many different roads to get to their job. They might drive in the dark or in the rain. They must always follow the lines and the road signs.

A driver can also be the one who drives a group of children to school. This is important because the children need to be safe. The driver makes sure they are all in their seats and that they know what to do. They also help the children stay safe while they are on the road.
