Dance is when you move your body in a way that is very fast and very strong. You might jump up in the air or run around a big room. You use your arms and your legs to make the music. The music tells you what to do. You follow the music.

Dance is a way to show how you feel. You might be happy or sad. You might be angry or scared. When you dance, you let go of your body for a moment. You let the music take over.

There are many kinds of dance. There is the kind that is very fast and moves around a round room. There is the kind that is slow and moves in a straight line. There is the kind that is done to music so fast you can not hear the words. And there is the kind that is done to music so slow that you must close your eyes to see.

People like to dance for many reasons. One reason is to be happy. Another reason is to show how you feel about someone or something. Sometimes people dance to remember something from the past. Other times people dance to be free.

Dance is not just about the body. You can also use your face to show how you feel. You can smile, you can laugh, you can cry. You can use your voice to tell the music. You can use your eyes to tell the world how you feel.

In a way, dance is like a story. You tell a story with your body and your music. You show the world how you feel. You show them how you are feeling right now.
