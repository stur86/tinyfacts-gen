When we talk about many things, we often use the word these. This word is very important because it tells us which ones we are talking about.

Imagine you have many books. You might say, this book is on a table. This means that book is the one right in front of you. This word helps us know which book we are talking about.

If you talk about many people, you might use this word. You might say, these people are working in the city. This means that all these people are doing the same thing in the same place.

Sometimes, we use this word when we are talking about something that is happening to many people. If you are talking about the whole town, you can say, this is happening all over town. This helps us know that the thing is happening to many people.

This word is also used to talk about something that is very important for many people. If you say, these are the things we need for our life, you are talking about things that are very important for most people.

So, when you hear the word these, you know which ones you are talking about. It helps you understand what you are saying.
