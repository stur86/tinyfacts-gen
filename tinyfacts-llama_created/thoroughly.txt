To do something completely is to do it in a way that is very good and makes sure everything is right. This means you do it many times until you feel you have done it all the way.

When you say you have done something, you usually use the word done. But when you want to say you did it very well, you use the word completely.

Think of it this way. If you are trying to learn how to speak, you might start to say words that are not right. After a long time, you feel you know the words very well. You have learned them all so that you can say them without thinking. This is a completely done thing.

You can also use this word when you have found something you were looking for. If you are searching for a book in a big room, you might look for the book for a long time. When you finally find it, you feel very happy and you say, I have found it completely. You mean you did not look at all the other books.

You can also use it to say you have been in a place that is very hard to get to. If you have been to a city that is very far away, you might feel that you have gone completely around the world. This means you have gone through many hard parts to get there.

Using this word can help us show that something is the best. If you are trying to decide which food to eat, you can say, I have eaten everything completely. This means you have tried every kind of food and you are very happy with what you have done.
