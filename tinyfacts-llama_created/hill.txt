A high place is where you stand above the ground. You can see far away. A long time ago, people used a long stick to reach high places. They would go up a long way and climb until they reached the top.

Sometimes, a high place can be very high. You might go up a long, hard walk to get there. If you are very tall, you are a very high person. If you are very short, you might not be able to reach the top.

You can also use the word for a green area between two high places. This green area is like a big middle piece. It is not as high as the top of one place and not as low as the bottom of another. It is just the right place in between.

Sometimes, a person might say, I want to go to the top of the world. This means they want to get to the very top of the big green area between the two high places. It is a big dream.
