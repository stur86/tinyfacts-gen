A group of people who make the big, hard things that run our cities and towns work together. They decide what to do with the money that people give them and how to use the land. They make sure everyone is safe and follows the same set of words.

Imagine you are the one in a group. You are the one who gives the money. You have to decide what to spend it on. If you spend it all on food, no one will have money to buy clothes. You have to find a way to share it so everyone gets enough. This group is like a team.

People also work together to keep the land clean. They decide what to do with the water and air. They make sure there is no fire and that the land stays safe. They have people who help them with this job.

There are many kinds of groups like this. Some are very small, like a group of friends who share a house. Others are very big, like a group of people who work together to build a city. Some are made up of only men, while others are made up of women, children, and people who are not of one age group.

These groups are like a team. They work together, share, and help each other. They are important because they keep us safe and make sure we have a good life.
