When you look at something, you can see it. When you can not see something, you have no idea it is there. This is called being in sight.

Imagine you are in a dark room. You hear a loud noise. You look around and see a big, tall thing moving across the floor. You are in sight of the thing. You can see it because light from the thing is reaching you.

But if you are in a room with many people and there is a lot of light, you might not notice the people around you. You might just see the faces of the people. You have no idea they are really there until you see them.

Sometimes you see something that is not really there. You might see a tree that is not really there. The tree looks like it is there because the light from the sun and from the sky makes it look that way. But it is not really there.

You can also see things in your mind. If you have a bad day and you are very sad, you might see a picture of a happy time. This is how you keep memories in your head.

Being in sight is important for us. It helps us find our way. It helps us talk to each other. It helps us know if something is near or if something is far away.
