To fit something means you put it together so it does not break. Think about a shirt. If the shirt is too long, it will fall down. If it is too short, it might hit your knee. You need to find the right way to put it on so it feels good and does not hurt.

If a person is small, a chair is small for them. If a person is big, a chair is big for them. You see, every person is different, and what is small for one person might be too big for another.

When you buy clothes, you want something that looks good on you. If a shirt is too big, it looks like a bag of food. If a shirt is too small, it looks like a tiny piece of paper. You have to find a shirt that fits you so you feel happy and look nice.
