To After Something

When something happens, it can change something else in the same way. If you fall down and hit your head, you feel pain. The pain is after you fell. It happened because of what you did.

A thing that is after another thing is the thing after something. If you eat a thing, the thing after the thing you ate is the thing you have now. If you leave your house, the thing after you left is the time you will spend in a place else.

You can also say that something is after someone. If a person is very happy, their face is often after they smile. If you are very angry, your face is often after you get angry. This is because your face is the thing after your face.

People use this word to talk about how things work and how we feel. It is a word that helps us explain what happens when one thing happens after another.

You can also use this word to talk about time. If you are going to do something, the thing after you do it is the time you have left. If you are going to leave your house, the time you will leave is the time after you leave. This helps you plan your day and your life.
