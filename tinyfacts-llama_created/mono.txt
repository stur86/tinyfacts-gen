The word is about how things are made. You can have one or many things at the same time. If you have only one thing, it is the only one. If you have many things, they are all together.

Think of a house. You can have many rooms. Each room is a different thing. There is the kitchen, the living room, the bedroom, and so on. When you look at all the rooms, you see the house.

You can also have many animals. There are a lot of dogs, cats, and other animals. All these animals are different. Each one has its own way of moving and its own sound.

In a group of people, there is one person who is in the middle. They are the one who makes everyone else act. The person who is in the middle is like the only thing in a group.

The word is also about one sound. If you have one sound, you have it by yourself. If you have many sounds, it is a group of sounds. When you hear many sounds, it is like the many sounds are together.
