Covers is a city in the state of Others. It is in the state of the great water ways. The city is near the big water ways. The water ways run through the city. The city is on land but it is near the water. People can walk on the land and then go down into the water to work.

The city has a big park. People go there to play. There is a big round field. They play games that are not played on land. There is also a big round field with a high wall. They play games with a ball and a stick. Some people run around the field while others throw the ball into a high wall.

There are also many houses. Some are big and some are small. The houses are all different. People from different places live there. They make the city their own.

The city is not very big. It has a small number of houses and a few big ones. But it is a good place. Many people go there because they like the water ways and the green space.
