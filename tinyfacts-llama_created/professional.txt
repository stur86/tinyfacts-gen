A person who works for a job is a good thing. This job is important. They do things all day.

Some people are very good at this job. They know how to do it. They know what to do. They make money and have a good life. They are a good person.

Some people do this job by themselves. They do it all by themselves. They do not need to work with other people. They can work on a computer. They can work from a far away place.

Some people do this job with other people. They work together. They share the work. They all do the same job. They are a team. They help each other.
