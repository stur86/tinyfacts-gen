To add something

You can add new parts to a thing. Think of a house. If you have a small room, you can add a bigger room next to it. This makes the house bigger.

Sometimes, a place gets bigger and becomes part of another place. This happens when a city is bigger than a town. The town is still there, but now it is part of the city. People from the city go to the town and the town people go to the city. The two places are now one.

You can also add something to a picture. If you add a new picture with your name, it is your picture now. You are the new picture.

People use this word when they talk about new things they want. They might say, after we finish this, we will add some more stuff. This means they are going to do something new and bigger.
