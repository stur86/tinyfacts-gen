To begin something is to start it. You can start a day by getting ready. You can begin a book by reading the first few lines. You can start a new game with friends. Or you can start a new work. You may start with a plan, a thought, or a feeling.

To begin is also about moving into a new place. If you move into a new house, you start in that room. When you arrive, you are in the new place. You have to get your things ready, open the door, and find your way around.

When you start something, you are taking the first step. This step is the beginning of what is coming next. It is the start of a new story, a new idea, or a new day. When you begin, you are ready to start.

You can also start something that is already there. If you start a fire, you start a new part of it. You start the burning of wood. You start the growing of green leaves. This is when the fire starts moving.

To begin means to start the act of doing something. It is the start of the work, the act, or the time of a thing.
