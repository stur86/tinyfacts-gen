To be sick means you are not well. You might feel very tired. You might not want to eat or drink. You might feel like you want to cry. You might have a bad feeling in your stomach. You might have a cold or a run in your nose. You might even have a tiny bit of blood in your mouth.

If you are sick, it does not mean you are very sick. You can still see and hear and think. You just feel very wrong. If you are sick, you might need to stay in bed or rest. You might drink many cups of warm water. You might take a few tiny drops of something you buy from a store. You might feel a little better after a few days.

Sometimes, people get sick from things they eat. Sometimes, people get sick from things they breathe in. Sometimes, people get sick because they are not eating enough. Sometimes, people get sick because they are very cold.
