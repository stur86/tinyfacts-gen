To feel a shadow is to be under a part of the sky. The ground or the trees are often under the sky. If you walk to a far place, the sky behind you will be very dark. In this dark sky, the ground is under a big, heavy part of the sky. This heavy part of the sky is called a shadow.

When the sun is high in the sky, the area under it is bright. But when the sun moves across the sky, the bright area becomes dark. This is when the shadow comes out. The ground, trees, and houses form the shadow.

In a room, if you turn on a light, the light goes over the wall and under the floor. The wall is like the ground and the floor is like the shadow. The light from the top of the room falls on the ground and under the floor, making everything under the floor dark.

Sometimes, a long light can fall from the sky and cover the area under it. This is called a rain shadow. The rain falls from the sky, but it does not reach the ground. Instead, it falls on the ground on the other side of the area under the sky.

In some places, the ground and the sky are always under the same shadow. In these places, the ground and the sky are always the same color.
