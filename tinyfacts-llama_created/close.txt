To close something means to make it very tight and strong. It is a way to keep it safe so it does not break easily.

Imagine you are in a room with a door. If you open the door, you are letting light and air in. If you close the door, you keep everything inside and make it safe for the night.

You can close a door, a box, a bag, or even a heart. When you close a door, you are telling the world that you are not going to go out. When you close a box, you are keeping your things safe inside. When you close a bag, you are keeping your clothes and other things inside.

Sometimes, you close a door because something is very important. You might close a door to go to a different room. You close a door to keep your room quiet. You close a door to keep a bad thing out. You close a door when you are feeling sad or angry.

You can also close your eyes and your face. When you close your eyes, you are hiding your face. It helps you feel safe.

In a word game, you might close two words together. When you close two words, you are joining them together to make one word.
