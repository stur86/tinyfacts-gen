A book with many words is called a book. But a book that helps you find what word means can be a very important thing.

These books are made by many people. One person writes down words on one side. The next person writes down words on the other side. The person after them writes down words on the side next to the first person. This goes on for a very long time. Because there are so many books, it is very easy to find the word you are looking for.

You can look at the words in one book and remember them. You can look at another book and find a word. You can also look at many books. This can help you learn many new words. It can also help you write and speak better.

Sometimes, these books have pictures. If you look at a word with a picture, you can see what it looks like. This can help you remember the word better.
