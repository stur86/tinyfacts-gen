To break down into smaller parts and form other parts is a way the body works.

A small part of you is made of very many small groups. When you cut one of these groups, you are left with many of these small groups. These small groups can join with many other small groups to make more parts.

The body also makes many of these small groups. The body needs many of these small groups to stay strong. Think of the small groups like tiny building blocks that make up the body. Each block can make a different kind of block. The body can use different kinds of blocks to build different parts.

Some of these small groups are found in your food. If you eat food that has many of these small groups, your body can use them to build the parts it needs. You can also get these small groups from the air you breathe.

The small groups are very important. They help make the body strong and help you grow. If you do not have enough of these small groups, you might feel sick or get a problem with your body.
