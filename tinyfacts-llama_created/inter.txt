To interest someone means to be interested in them. It is when you want to know more about them. You might ask them questions or listen to what they say. This shows you care about what they do or think.

Think about it like this. Imagine you want to join a team of people for a game. You want to be part of it because you like the game. You are interested in the team because they play well and you want to be there. You have a feeling of being a part of something.

Sometimes, you might feel interested in a book or a movie. You want to read it or watch it because it sounds fun. You might read the words or watch the pictures. You are interested because you want to know what happens next.

In life, you often need to interest someone to build a relationship. If you are interested in a girl, you might talk to her or look at her. If you are interested in a job, you want to work there. You feel a strong feeling when you are interested in something.

To interest a person means to show you care about them. It is a way to show you are thinking about them and you want to be near them.
