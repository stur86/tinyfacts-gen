The word has many different uses.

When you have a small thing, like a small dog, you can hold it in your hand. But if you want to show how much more you have, you can say the dog is bigger. To do this, you might put a few more things next to it. You can add two more dogs, or you can add three more books. This is what we mean by the dog is bigger.

This is also how we talk about people. If you are the same person, you do not change. But if you change your name, your job, or your house, you are still the same person. You have not become a different person. You are still you. So, even if you change many things, you stay the same inside.

Sometimes, when we talk about a thing that is growing, we use this word. A tree starts as a small thing with one big green top. Over time, the top grows taller and the green top gets bigger. The green top becomes the top of many more green tops. The green top gets bigger and bigger, and the tree becomes a tall, green thing. This is what happens when something grows.

In some places, when you say a thing is bigger, you mean it is larger in space. You might say a room is bigger than another room. This means the room with the more space inside is the bigger one.

There is also a word for when you have more money. If you have one hundred pieces of money, you have a few hundred pieces of money. But if you get more money, you have many more pieces. You have a big bag full of money.

Finally, when you are very, very tired, you feel like you want to sit down on the floor. This is what we mean by you are going to sit down. You are not going to get up and do anything more. You just want to rest.
