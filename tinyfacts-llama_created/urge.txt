To have an idea or want something is to feel that is inside you. This can happen when you are thinking about something, or when you want to do something. It is like a push inside you.

Imagine you are waiting for a phone call. Your body might feel a bit heavy and your legs might feel like they are going to go out. This is because your body is saying, it wants to hear from the person on the phone. You might feel a bit nervous or you might feel a bit excited. You have an idea or want to talk to that person.

You also might have an idea while you are walking. You see a dog and you think, I want to see that dog. You might want to run after it. This is because your body is telling you, look at that dog.

Sometimes, we have an idea but we do not know what to do. We might have a strong feeling in our heart. This can be a very big idea. It might make us feel happy or sad. Our body is still telling us, pay attention to this feeling.

If you want to do something, you have an idea. Your body is telling you to move. You might want to run, jump, or move your arms and legs. Your body is the one that is acting. Your mind is the one that is thinking, but your body is the one that is doing it.

An idea can be big, small, or in the middle. It is always there inside you, even when you are not thinking about it. It is what makes us want to do something. It is what makes us feel a way.
