When you talk about a group of people, you might use the word groups. A group is when many people are together. One group can be made of friends, one group can be made of family, and one group can be made of people who work together.

But you also use the word groups to talk about things that are important. When you talk about the groups of people who make up a city, you are talking about how they work together. They help each other and work for the city. They are like a group of people who are all working together.

Sometimes you use the word to talk about people in a group who are all saying the same thing. When one person says something, and many people agree, you say they are in a group. They are all saying the same thing, so they are one group.
