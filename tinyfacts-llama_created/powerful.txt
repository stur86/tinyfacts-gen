The word strong comes from a word that has a deep meaning. It means things that can do a lot of work. They are not small. They are not light. They can move big things or make a loud sound.

Things that are strong are often made to stay this way. They are made from hard stuff or from things that can not break. This helps them stay standing and not fall down.

We also have things that are not strong. These are things that can be broken. If you drop a glass, it breaks. If you hit a ball, it falls down. These things are not very good at standing up.
