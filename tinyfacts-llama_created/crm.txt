When you talk to a computer, you are sending your words and ideas into the air. This is like sending a letter to a friend. The computer looks at what you say and does something with it. It can help you buy things, send money, or even play games.

When you send a computer a word, the computer breaks it down into tiny bits. It then puts these bits together in the right way. This is how the computer understands what you want.

Sometimes, computers need to remember things. This is like keeping a box of papers in your home. You write down your name, your phone number, and other important stuff. The computer remembers this so you can find it later.

Computers also have a kind of brain inside them. This brain knows what to do with the bits the computer breaks down. It can learn from what you do and get better over time.

There are many types of computers. Some are very small and sit on a desk. Others are huge and cover an entire room. Some can only do one thing at a time. Others can do many things at the same time.
