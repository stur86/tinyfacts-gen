When something is clear and easy to see, it can show the world what is happening. This is often because the light from inside the thing is let through to you. Think of a window. If you look through the window, you see what is going on inside the house. You can see people, animals, and other things that are not right in front of you. This is an important kind of clear.

Sometimes you want to know something that you do not want to know. You might feel a little afraid. This is because the thing might hurt you or make you feel very sad. In this case, you might not want to look at it because it makes you feel bad. But if you are very strong, you can still see it. You just might not want to look at it right away.

You can also have a clear mind. When you are happy or very calm, you might not think about any of this. You just feel good. This is because your brain is working well.

In the world, we need clear things too. Think of a police car. The car is clear of any things that might hurt people. The police car can see very far because it has a clear window. This is important for keeping everyone safe.
