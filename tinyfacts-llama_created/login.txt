When you want to use a computer or a phone, you need to be sure you are the right person. You are the person who knows the word and the number that is on the phone. This is called a computer name. You send this name to the computer or phone. The computer or phone says, okay, you are the right person.

To do this, you have to open a window. This window is where you enter your name and your number. You type your name and your number into the window. This helps the computer remember you. If you do not enter your name and number, the computer says you are not the right person.

You also need to know a word. When you enter this word into the window, it helps the computer know what you want. This word is called a word key. When you enter the word key, the computer understands what you want to do. You do not need to remember every word key.

Sometimes, you need to give the computer a reason why you are doing something. If you are going to watch a movie, you enter a word key for the movie. The computer then shows you the movie. If you try to do something that is not allowed, the computer says, no.

You also need to be sure that the computer or phone is safe. You do this by entering a word key for a good place. This helps keep your computer or phone safe from bad people. You can also enter a word key for a friend to be sure they are really there.

When you are sure you are the right person and the computer or phone is safe, you can start doing things. You can go to a new place, play a game, or talk to a friend. The computer or phone does all the work for you. You only need to give it the right word key to remember what to do.
