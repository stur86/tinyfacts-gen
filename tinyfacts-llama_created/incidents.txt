To have an eventually is to become finally. It is when something that started small or bad becomes better.

Think of a story. You start feeling angry. You say and do things that make others feel scared. But then something happens. You get a job you like. You make new friends. You learn how to be better. You are not the same person anymore. You are a different person. This is an eventually.

You can also use it to talk about what happens when you make a wrong move. If you try to walk across the street when a car is coming, you might hit it. This is an eventually bad thing. But if you stay still and wait, the car stops and says sorry. You were wrong, but you learned from it. You became better because of it.

Sometimes, an eventually is used to talk about the end of a time. You might say, something bad happened, and then it becomes better later. This is because time goes from bad to good.
