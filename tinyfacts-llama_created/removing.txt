To move something away from a place means you take it out of that place and put it somewhere else. You might move a small thing that sits on a table to a different table in your room. You are doing this because the thing was not in the right place and it was causing a problem.

Imagine you have a box with many papers inside. The papers are not in their right order. You take the box out and move the papers to a different table. Now the papers are in the right order and the box looks nice again. You have fixed the problem.

You can also move people around. If you have many friends and you want to see them, you move one friend to another table. This helps everyone see each other and make new friends. This is a way to change how people are in your life.

Sometimes, we move things from one room to another room in a house. If the kitchen is too busy, you might move the table to another room. This helps keep the kitchen quiet and the whole house feels better.

In your life, you might move from one school to another school. This is a big change. You are leaving your old friends and school behind to start in a new school. It can be a bit hard to feel like you are starting at the very beginning. But you are moving forward and you are doing something new.
