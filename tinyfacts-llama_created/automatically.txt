When you press a key on your computer, it does not ask you to do it. It just happens on its own. You might have typed a word and then closed the window. The computer has seen the word before and knows what to do next. It is like the computer is talking to you in your own way.

If you want to show a picture on a computer, you have to tell the computer what to do. You might say, this is a picture of a cat. The computer listens to what you say and then does what you ask. It is like you are giving a set of words to a friend.

But if you press a key without telling the computer, it does something different. If you press the key when a picture is on the computer, it jumps to the next picture. It does not wait for what you want. It does what it thinks is best in a moment.

Sometimes, when you press a key, you are not sure what you are doing. You might press it by chance because you are looking at something else. If you press it again, you might do something different. This happens because your brain is still on the other thing you saw.

A car can also do this. If you press the key to start the car, the car goes. It does not ask you to do it. You just press the key. It is like the car is talking to you in your own way.

The word we use is from a way of talking with animals. You might give a dog a word to call. If the dog hears the word before you say it, it knows what to do. It is like it is listening to you.

This word is also used when you are doing something very fast. If you are running very quickly, your body might do things on its own. Your feet might keep moving before you know what to do. Your heart might beat faster. It is like your body is talking to you in your own way.

So, the word is about how things work when we do not tell them what to do. It is about the way things happen by themselves when you are not looking.
