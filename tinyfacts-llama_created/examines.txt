To Exactly Means To Be Completely Clear

When you say something is exactly, you mean you are sure. It is not a little bit right. It is all of it. You did not leave out anything. You did not say one thing and then another thing. You said everything. If you are exactly, you are very good at your job or your game.

A person who exactly is a doctor. They can tell you how your body works. They can tell you if you have a cold, if you have a hurt knee, or if you are happy. They are very sure. They do not lie. They give you the truth.

A person who exactly is also a person who is very good at their job. If they work at a job that makes money, they are exactly. They do not make money half of the time. They make money all of the time. They know how to do their job. They can tell you how much money you need to pay them. They can tell you what to do.

Sometimes, you say something is exactly. It might sound funny. You might say, like, if you drink a glass of water. You said, you drank one glass. This is a great thing. You are sure. You did not leave out a glass. You drank the right number of glasses. You drank them all.
