To grow or to become larger is to add to a thing. Imagine you have a small piece of paper. This paper is as small as you can see. Then you put more paper on top. Each piece of paper adds to the old one. The paper gets bigger and bigger. You are growing.

This happens with many things. If you are sad, you become angry. This is also an answer to a problem. The feeling is now bigger and more real.

We also use this word for things that change. Imagine you start to smile. The smile grows as you feel happy. The face is changing. The body is moving in a new way. The face is becoming bigger and more full.

People also talk about the world getting bigger. When something happens, the world around you changes. You see new people, places, and things. The world is growing in a new way. This can be good or bad, but it is always happening.

To grow can also mean to change in a way that is not easy. Sometimes you want to stay the same. But as you grow older, you learn to change. Your mind becomes bigger, and you can see more things. You learn how to act and how to be with others. This is a big part of growing up.
