To get something means you take it from someone else. When you get something, you are the one who has it now. You might get a ball from a friend or a book from a school.

You can also get something by doing something else. If you are very good at a game, you might get a new game. If you do a job well, you might get a bigger job. Or, if you study hard, you might get a job that you love.

Sometimes, you get something from a place. If you eat a food that is good, you get a feeling in your body. If you see a picture of someone you like, you feel happy. You get something from the world around you.

You can also get something by leaving a place. If you run away from a school, you get away from the teachers and the other kids. You might feel free or scared. But if you run away to a far place, you might find a new home and new friends.
