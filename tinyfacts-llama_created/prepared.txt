To be ready is to be in a place where you are not afraid. It means you are not worried about what could happen next. You know the way and you are ready to go.

Imagine you are about to go into a room with a lot of people. You are not afraid because you know everyone and you have heard they are nice. You are ready to talk to them and to listen to what they have to say. You are ready for the conversation.

You can also be ready for a big day. You have been doing things for a long time and you know what to expect. You know you have to do a lot of work and you are ready. You feel strong and ready to do it.

Sometimes, to be ready is to do something before you need to do it. If you know you have to close a door, you can go and do it before you close it. You are ready to close it when it is time.

Being ready is important when you are going on a trip. If you go to a new city, you might feel a little nervous. But you are ready. You know the way and you have a plan. You are ready for what is going to happen next.

You can also be ready for a time of your life. You might feel ready when you get a new job. You know you are going to work with a new team and you are ready to learn. You are ready to start this new part of your life.
