A room is a place where people live. It is a room in a house or a building. A room has walls, a floor, and a high top. There is a bed inside the room, and sometimes there is a table and a chair.

A person who shares a room is called a room friend. They live together with another person. They do not have their own rooms. They share the same room. They have to work together so the room stays clean and looks nice.

Sometimes, a room has a person who lives there only. This person has their own bed and their own things. The room friend helps them. The room friend does not sleep in the room. They stay up late and go out. They are very busy. But they must also pay their money and follow the same things as the person who lives there.

You can have a room friend if you are very good at sharing. You are calm when someone is quiet. You do not take things that do not give you. You help when you need it. You are like a friend who lives in the same house with you.
