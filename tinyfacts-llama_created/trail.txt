A trail is a line of things that people follow. Imagine you are walking in the woods and you come across a big rock. You decide to follow that rock down the side of the tree so you do not step in the same place. You walk along the rock for a long time until you reach the bottom.

After that, you follow the top of the tree so you do not fall and hit your head. This is like making a line from one end of the world to the other. This line goes through many places where people live and work, and many people follow it to find food, water, or a new place to live.

If you follow a trail for many days, you might see many things. You might see animals, trees, and houses. You might hear the sound of running water or see a bright light in the sky. Sometimes, you follow a trail that is made for people to walk, like a line that goes along a road or a water way.

A trail can also mean a line that people follow to find their way to a place. This is often important when you are moving through a new city. If you know the trail, you can go to your home without getting lost.
