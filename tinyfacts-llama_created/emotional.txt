To feel something is to have a strong inside inside. Your heart may feel a big push when you love a person or a thing. You might feel happy, sad, or angry. Your eyes might look different when you feel this way.

This feeling is not just about the outside world. It is also about how you feel inside. If you are afraid, your legs might feel tight. If you are excited, your hands might feel warm. If you are very happy, your face might feel soft and your body might feel light.

You can also feel this way when you are talking to a friend. If you are very happy to see them, your face might light up. If you are very sad, your eyes might well up with tears.

Sometimes, this inside feeling can make you act. You might say you feel a way, even if you do not really know why. This can make people understand you better.

But sometimes, this inside feeling can be very hard to deal with. If you are very angry, you might want to hit something. If you are very sad, you might cry. If you are very scared, you might run away.

Everyone feels this way. Even a small child might feel a big push when they see a dog. A grown person might feel sad when they lose a loved one. A person who is very happy might feel a big push when they see a beautiful place.
