To be in a good place means you are not in a bad place. You have made things right. You have done what you needed to do.

If you are in a bad place, you are feeling very sad. You might be crying. You might want to run away. You might feel angry. You want to be safe. But if you stay in the bad place, you will not be able to get better.

If you are in a place that is not good, you might be in a room with a person you do not like. You might not want to be there. You might want to leave. You might want to go home.

When you say someone has been in a good place, it means they are happy and safe. They are not afraid anymore. They feel calm. They can think about their day. They can see the people around them and know they are safe.

You can also say something is being helped. If you are feeling sick, your doctor is helping you feel better. They give you food to eat. They give you a drink. They talk to you and help you understand your body. This helps you feel better.

Being in a good place is important. It helps us feel safe and happy. It helps us do things we like. It helps us see good things. It helps us know we are not alone.
