To be concerned means you care about what happens. You think about how things will go. If you are very concerned, you feel a lot of fear or worry. You might feel your heart beating fast or your hands shaking.

You might worry about a friend. If they are in a bad place, you feel bad for them. You want them to be okay. You ask them how they are doing and want to help. You care about their well being.

You can also be concerned about things that are happening in your city or town. You might hear about a problem and think about how it will make you or your family. You want to find out what is happening and how you can help.

Being concerned is like having a warm feeling in your chest. You are thinking about other people and what they need. It is a good way to show that you care.
