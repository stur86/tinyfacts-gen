A big running thing is called a race. Many people run this race. There are many different kinds of races, but one is very well known. It is an eventually held in a big city with a high street. The city has many big stores and many people who like to walk or run. They all come to watch the race.

The people who live in the city and the ones who come from far away are very strong. They run very fast to get to the finish line. They wear clothes with numbers on them. The person with the number one is the one to beat. This person is very good at running.

The race starts on a high street. The first person to cross the line is the one who finishes first. Then the people with higher numbers cross. This makes it harder for the other people to beat them. The person with the highest number is the one who has the most chances of beating the others.

There is also a thing called a cup. The person who has the cup is the one who finished first in the race. The person who carries the cup gets a good thing. It is like a big bag of money.

There are also other kinds of running things. Some are for children and some are for older people. Some are for people to run for fun and others are for people who want to be the best.

In the end, the person who has the highest number gets a good thing. They are the one who is the best at running.
