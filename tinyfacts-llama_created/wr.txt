To wrap something means to make it move. You can wrap a piece of paper or a small thing that is not living. You wrap it around to keep it safe.

A person might wrap their hands before they start a job. They make their fingers tight so they do not get hurt. This helps them feel ready to do the work.

Sometimes, to wrap something is to make a noise. You might use a small thing with long hair to make a loud sound. When you pull the hair, the hair comes out and makes a strong sound. This is a way to amaze people.

You can also wrap a thing that is heavy. If you put a heavy box in a room, it makes a loud noise. The box is making the air move around it. This noise is the wrapping sound.

People also use the word to talk about a fight. When two people are angry, they might start to wrap at each other. They use their hands to hit or pull. This is a way to show they are very mad.

So, to wrap something means to make it move, to make a loud noise, or to show you are very angry.
