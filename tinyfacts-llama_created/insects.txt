In a green room, you see many small animals. They are not big like a dog or a cat. They are very small and have many legs. Some of them are hard to see because they fly very fast and fly under the ground. These small animals go from one place to another. They do this to eat. They use their long head to pick up food.

Some of these small animals are very small. They are tiny, smaller than a paper cut. They have many tiny legs on their feet. They can fly fast. Some of them can even fly very high. They go up into the sky and down again many times. They can see in the dark because they have a small light in the front of their head. They can see what is behind them.

In a house, you might see these small animals. They go into the house at night. They come through the open doors. They go into the room where you sleep. They look for food. They eat tiny bits of food. They do not drink water like a dog does. They drink a tiny bit of water in their bodies. They go to the water in a house very often.

Many of these animals are very good at hiding. They are very small. They can go into a tiny place. They can go under a rock. They can go under a bed. They can hide in a bag. They are very good at keeping quiet. When they are afraid, they can make a loud sound with their head. This sound is very strong. You might hear it in a house.
