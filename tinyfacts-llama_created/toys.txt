To play with things is to have fun. When you are a small child, you like to make things move. You might hit a ball, run around, or throw a stick. This is playing. You do not need money to do this. You just use your body and your mind to have fun.

To buy things is to get things you want. You go to a store to buy food, clothes, or a game. You pay money with your money. This is buying. You do not have to play with them right away. You can wait until you want to play or until the day is over.

To keep a thing is to keep it safe. You might keep a small book, a piece of paper, or a small animal. You keep it so it does not get broken or lost. You keep it because it is important to you.

To play with someone is to have fun with them. You might play a game with a friend. You laugh, shout, and move around. This is a good way to be happy and have fun together.

To play is to act like a child. You might act very fast, jump around, or make a lot of noise. You act like you are a small person. This is a way to forget about your real life for a little while.

To play with a thing is to use it to have fun. You might play with a ball, a block, or a stuffed animal. You use it to move around, to build something, or to tell a story. You use it to feel happy and to be care free.

To play is also to learn. You learn how to do something when you play. You learn to throw a ball, to run fast, or to build a house. This is how you grow and become a person.
