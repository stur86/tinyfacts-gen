To take on something means you are going to do it. You are not going to let someone else do it for you. You are going to do it by yourself. This can mean you are going to work hard. You are going to do many things. Or you are going to go to a new place.

You can also take on a problem. If you have a bad day at work, you might feel sad. You might want to sit in your room. But then you decide to go to the store. You go to the store and you talk to the people. You find out what is wrong. You might buy a drink to make you feel better. You took on the problem.

Sometimes you take on a big job. This is when you do many things very fast. You are not one person. You are a group of people. You all work together and you do it very well. You take on the job and you do it for many years.

You can also take on a family job. If a father is away, a mother has to take care of the children. She works very hard. She makes sure the children are safe. She makes sure they eat food. She helps them when they are sad. She takes on this job to take care of the family.
