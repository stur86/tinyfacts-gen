To cut something is to make it into small pieces. When you hold a piece of paper and you want to make it smaller, you can cut it. You might use a box thing with a long handle. You push the handle down and the paper comes out in short pieces.

You can also cut things that are not paper. You can use a thing that looks like a line to cut a thing. This is a way to cut a line through something. If you cut a line through a wall, you make a small break. If you cut a piece of wood, you make it smaller for a later time.

Some things cut themselves. You can see this when a hair falls out or a piece of skin falls off. When you cut your hand, you are making a small break in your skin.
