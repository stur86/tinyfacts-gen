To be the one you are for a person means you do not pretend to be something else. You are the person you are. You are not trying to be the person you think you should be. You just are what you are.

Think about a friend. They are the person you are. They do not pretend to be another person. They are just them. They are the friend you are.

If you are the one who has something, you are the one who has that thing. If you are the one who is in a room, you are the one who is there. You are not another person in the room.

If you are the one who likes a book, you are the one who likes that book. You do not try to be someone else. You are happy with what you like. You are not afraid to say, I like this book.
