The word actually is used to tell the truth. When you say something is true, you usually say it because you believe it. But if you say it is actually true, it means you know it is true.

Think of a child. If a parents say their child is small, the child thinks the parents are small. Later, if the parents are actually small, the child is glad. The parents said their child is small, and the child believed it, so it was true. The word actually is like this: it tells the truth.

You can also use it when you are wrong. If you say something is true, but it is not, you are lying. You did not actually tell the truth. If you say your car is red, but it is actually blue, you are actually lying. You did not mean it was red.

The word actually is important. If you see something, you can tell the truth about it. If you say something, you must actually mean it. If you say it is true, you must be true.
