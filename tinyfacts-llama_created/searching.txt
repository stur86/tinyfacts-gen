To search means you look for something. You might look in a big room, a small room, or even a computer. You want to find what you need so you can use it later.

Imagine you are looking for your favorite book. You walk down a street. You look in a coffee shop, a park, and a store. You look under a table and behind a chair. You also look in a box under the bed. After a while, you find your book!

You are also searching for a person. You look in a school, a park, and a street. You ask other people if they know the person. You check their faces and their words. If you find them, you talk to them and learn more about them.

Sometimes, you search for something you lost. You look in a room and a box. You check under the bed, behind a chair, and in a cup. You ask your family if they saw the thing. If you find it, you are happy!

You can also search for a story. You look at books, a computer, and the world around you. You look for answers to questions. You want to know what happened before. You want to know what happens next.

To search can be hard. You might get lost in a big city. Or you might feel afraid when you look at a person you do not know. But the act of searching is important. It helps you find what you need and learn about the world.
