A word is a thing that people use to talk about other things. It is a word you pick and you use to make a story.

When you are writing a story, you might use a name of a person. You might write,  The girl is happy. This is a name of a person. You are telling the story with the name.

But what if you want to tell a story about the girl? You do not need the name. You can just write, The girl is happy. This is still the same story. You are not telling the story with the name. The name is just the word.

When you do this, you have a word. You have picked a word and you use it many times. You are using the word to talk about other things. You are telling a story with the word.

These words are like a box. You put one word in the box. The box holds the word. You can take the box out, and you can use the word again. You can change the word. You are always using the word in different ways.

Another way to think about it is with a car. You drive a car. You do not pick the car. The car is the car. You are using the car. You are not telling the story with the car. You are telling the story with the word. You are using the word to talk about the car.

In the world, we also use these words. When we talk about a book, we say, The book is good. We are not telling the story with the book. We are talking about the book. We are using the word to talk about the story.

You can use these words with any story. If you have a story about a tree, you can say, The tree is big. You are not telling the story with the tree. You are talking about the tree. You are using the word to talk about the story.

These words help us. They help us to keep the same story in our heads many times. They help us to talk about the same story without having to say the same words over and over.
