An animal has a life. It moves, it eats, it sleeps. It is safe in its own home. But then things happen.

One day the animal is walking. It sees a tree. It wants to eat the leaves. It steps onto a rock. The rock is very hard. The animal falls. It hits its head. The animal can not move anymore.

Another day the animal is in the water. It is running fast. It sees a big rock. It wants to be safe. It jumps out of the water. The water hits the animal. The animal is broken. It is very hurt.

Sometimes the animal does not want to go. It stays in a small place. It is afraid. It does not move. The world is very big and very loud. The animal feels alone.
