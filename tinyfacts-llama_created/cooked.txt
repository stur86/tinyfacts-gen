To be made ready for eating is to be done with food. Food is the stuff you eat to give you power. When you eat food, your body uses the power to keep you going. If you do not eat enough, you might feel very tired. If you eat too much, you might feel full and stop moving.

If food is made ready, it is not still. It is now a thing you can use to give yourself power. You put it into your body, and it starts to work. The food breaks down and gives your body the power it needs to move, think, and feel. It is ready to be eaten.

You can make food ready in many ways. You can put it in a box, put it in a bag, or make it on a fire. You can also make it ready in your own way. If you are very young, a grown person will make food ready for you. If you are old, you can help your body make food ready.

Sometimes, food is made ready so it is easy to eat. This can happen when you are very tired. You might eat something quickly to give your body a little power. Or, you might eat food that is already broken down, so it is easy to drink. This way, you can eat quickly without having to wait for food to go down.
