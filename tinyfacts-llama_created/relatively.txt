To say something is very close to another thing means you think one is more or less than the other. You can use this word when you want to talk about how much something is like one other thing.

If you say a dog is very close to a cat, you are not saying it is the same. Instead, you are saying the dog is more like a cat than not at all. This is because a cat is a larger animal, with stronger teeth and a different kind of hair. 

You can also use this word when you are talking about how much you want something. If you really want to go to a movie, you might say,  I am very close to going to the movie. This means you are very near, but you still have to go a little bit further to get there. 

The word can also be used when you are talking about how something is different from another thing. If you say a house is very close to a park, you are saying it is near, but it is not in the same place as the park. It might be on the other side of a big street.
