To be a good person is to act in a way that is kind to others. It means you try to make sure everyone is safe and happy. When you are a good person, you show that you care about what people think.

To be a good person can mean being kind, being hard, or being quiet. Sometimes, people are a good person because they work hard and do their jobs well. Other times, a good person might be someone who shows they care about a friend or a family person.

When you are a good person, you do not always say what you think you should say. You might not say the word, but you still act in a way that is good. You might listen to what others say or show that you understand how they feel. This way, you are showing that you care about how people feel.

Being a good person is not always easy. There are days when you feel angry, sad, or afraid. But if you stay calm and try to do what is right, you can become a good person.
