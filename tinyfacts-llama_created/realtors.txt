A realer is a person who buys and then tries to let a house or apartment to other people. They help people find a home where they can live. They talk to the owner of the house and the people who want to move in. They try to find a house that is the right one for the person they are talking to.

A realer usually has a computer that can show many pictures of houses. They can look at these pictures and see which house is the best for the person. They also talk to people in the house to see if they like it. If the person likes it, the realer will tell the owner that the house is ready to let.

A realer also knows how to talk to the people who want to buy a house. They know what they are looking for and how to ask for it. They are usually very good at their job. They help people find their dream home.

Sometimes, a realer can also help with other kinds of homes. If a house is broken or in a bad place, a realer can help fix it. They can look at the house and see if it needs some work. They can tell the owner if the house is safe to live in.

A realer is very important for people who want to buy a home. They help people find a place where they can live and build a life.
