An area where many people work together to do a job or a set of jobs. It is usually a big group with many different people doing different things. They might make things to buy or make food. They might build houses. They might also make money.

Imagine you are a person who likes to write stories. You have a big room to write in. You have many friends who help you. You write stories for a big group. They read your stories and tell people about them. You all work together to make the stories come out.

A business is a kind of area where many people work together to make money. Some people work in a big building with many floors. Others work from a small house. They all do the same job: they try to make money for the people who gave them money.

An area with many different jobs and people working together is called a place. It is a place where many different people do the same job. It is a way to make things happen.
