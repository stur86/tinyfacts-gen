To use a thing or an idea means you need it to do a job. Think of a house. You live there. You use the walls to sleep in, the table to eat, and the chair to sit in. The walls are a kind of store of power. You use the power from the walls to live and to move around. If you were to move away from the house, you would have to find another place to live.

A person who works is one of these. They use their body and their mind. They do a job that is important for the people. They might do work that makes food, works for a bus, or does work for a school. When you have a job, you have something to do. You have a reason to be there.

A thing can also be a store of power. A tree is a kind of power house. When it is green, it is strong. When it is old, it is more strong. When you touch the tree, you are touching the power inside it. You can feel the power when you stand near it. If you cut down the tree, you are taking away the power.

You can also have a thing that is ready for use. You might have a bag of food for the day. You can open it and you have food. You can use the food to keep you going. If you run out of food, you must find another way to live.

Sometimes, you might have to share a thing. If you have a book, you can read it. If you share it with a friend, you are giving the book to them. You are using the book as a store of power. When you share, you are giving something that was already there to someone else. This is how we share our time with others.
