A thing that makes water go is called a water push. It is a big box with moving parts inside. When you turn the box, the moving parts turn very fast. They push air and water out of the box and into a long piece of hard stuff. This hard stuff makes the water go into a room. The water then goes down to a place where animals drink it. The box also makes a little bit of water come out at the top.

There are many kinds of water pushes. Some push water out of the ground. Some push water from a high place. Some push water from a room where people drink. Some push water to fill a big room. Some push water to keep water moving in a place where people do not need to get up.

You can also use the word for a thing that makes air go. It is like a box that makes the air go from one room to another. It is very important for people who live in cities because they help bring air to the people and to the cars.
