To keep something is to keep it safe. You can keep a book in a box so it does not get broken. You can keep food in a big box so it stays good to eat. You can also keep money in a safe place so it stays safe for a long time.

When you keep something, you do not let it go. You do not let it fall down to the ground. You keep it high up in a place where it is safe. This helps the thing stay strong and not get hurt.

Sometimes, you keep a picture so it stays in your mind for a long time. You remember the day when you saw the picture and the person who took it. You keep the picture close to your heart because it is a memory that stays with you.

You can also keep something that is hard to change. If you try to change your mind, but you still feel the same way, you are keeping the same idea. You are keeping the truth of what you feel.

Keeping is also about keeping your body safe. If you do not move, your body stays still. You keep your body in a place where it can stay safe. This is how you stay well and do not get hurt.
