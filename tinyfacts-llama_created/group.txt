A group is a group of many people who are not alone. They are often made up of friends, family, or people who are doing the same thing. When a group of people do the same things, they are called a group.

Think of a group like a room. You are all in the same room with other people who are also doing the same thing. You might all be doing work, playing games, or talking. You are not alone because you are all there together. Your room is a group because all of you are together, doing the same thing.

Another way to look at it is like a team in a game. The game is the thing that is happening. The team is the group of people who are working together. They might have different jobs, but they all want to window the game. They are all part of the same team and help each other.

Sometimes, a group of people might want to do something together. They might decide to move to a new place or start a new business. This can be a good thing because people coming together can make a new place better. They can share their ideas and learn from each other.
