A person or thing that is not the front of a story can also be the back. Think about a picture. The back of the picture shows the person who took the picture. The front of the picture is the person in the picture. The back of a book shows what is happening before the story. It tells you what is happening before the story starts.

If you have a room with a picture on the wall, the wall is not the front of the picture. The wall is the back. It is not what is happening in the room. It is the thing that is not the front of the story.
