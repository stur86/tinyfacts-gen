To use a number is to enter a number on a computer or a phone. You type the numbers and the computer or phone sends the number to a far away place. This far away place is a computer that helps people and things talk to each other. It uses numbers to understand what you want to say.

When you use a number, you are telling the computer what you need. You might need to ask for a name, a word, a number, or a picture. If the computer does not understand what you say, it will send the number back to you. You can also use numbers to send money. When you send money, you are sending a number to another person. They will get this number and use it to pay you back.

Numbers can be very big or very small. A number like one is a very small number. A number like one hundred is a very big number. A number like iced tear is a very big number, but it is not as big as the number one hundred. Numbers help us do many things in our lives. We use them to counters, to show the time, and to tell stories.
