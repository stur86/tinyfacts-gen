To powered means you are making things move or do work. This happens when you give power to a thing so it can do its job.

Think about a car. When the car starts moving, it is powered. The power is coming from the inside of the car. The power is coming from a box inside the car that makes the car move. When you put your foot on the ground, you are giving the car power. The car is now going fast.

You can also power things that do not move. You might use a power thing to make a door open or a light turn on. The power from the power thing makes the door or light move.

Some things need power to keep going. If a thing runs out of power, it will stop moving. You need to keep giving it power so it can keep doing its job.

Some things are powered by the sun. The sun is like a power box in the sky. It gives power to the world every day. We use this power to make things move, to make food, and to make many kinds of things.

You can also power things that use the sun. You might use the power from the sun to make a thing that can carry people or food. This power lets people go where they want, even over big water.
