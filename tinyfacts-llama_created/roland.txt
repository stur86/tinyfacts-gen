A long time ago, people made a big place out of wood. They built it with long pieces of wood that went up high and short pieces that went down. This was their home. They also made a big round piece of wood for the door. They hung it on the side of the house so people could come in.

This round piece of wood was very important. If the door was broken, people would have to go around the house. They had to walk over the ground, through the streets, and round the corner. They would be very tired.

The wood was made from trees that grew in the area. The people would cut the wood and bring it back to their house. They would use the wood to build many more pieces of wood for their home. They also made a big round thing to use for food. They would put food in the round thing and it would stay full for a long time.

The round thing was very important because people needed it for food and water. They would carry it with them when they moved from one place to another. It was like a big bag that kept them going.

Over time, the wood became very old. People started to use other kinds of wood for their homes. The round piece of wood was no longer needed for that. They took it and put it in a big room. The room was filled with many old pieces of wood. People would sit in the room and talk about their lives.

Today, we do not use the round piece of wood like we did in the past. But we remember the people who made it and the important job it did. We still have old pieces of wood in our homes and in places where people remember their past.
