A place to keep things is a thing that holds many things. You can put things there so that they do not get lost. It is a way to keep your things safe.

Imagine you have a lot of books on a table. If you throw one book away, there are now many fewer books on the table. You can put the book back on the table and it will be there again.

A computer has a big box in the back. This box holds many things that the computer does not need. If the computer ever breaks, it can bring the things from the box back to the person who made it. This is like keeping a safe for your computer.

A person might keep money in a box at work. They do not show it to anyone. They only take it when they need it. They are like the person who keeps the box safe.

A doctor might keep a picture of a person they have seen before. This helps them remember what to look for when a person comes to the office. It is like keeping a picture of a friend so that they can remember them.

A place to keep things is important because it helps you find what you need when you need it.
