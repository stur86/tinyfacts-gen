The word you are looking for is called a computer. A computer is a thing that can do many different jobs. It can do things like:

   Books: You can write a story on the computer. The computer reads the words and makes them into a story that you can see.

  Games: You can play games on the computer. You use a set of words and numbers to make the game work. The computer does the work for you.

  Work: You can use the computer to do work. You type words into a box. The computer reads the words and does the work. It is like a very fast writing desk.

  Pictures: You can make pictures on the computer. You type words and numbers, and the computer makes a picture from them.

  Music: You can also use the computer to make music. You type words and numbers, and the computer makes a sound. You can hear the music as music.

The computer has a part inside that the words go through. This part is very fast. It can do a lot of work very quickly. This is why the computer can do many jobs at once.

The computer also has a part that remembers things. It keeps track of how you have done things. If you have done something before, the computer remembers it. It can then do the same thing again without you having to tell it.

The computer is a very important thing in our world. It helps us do many jobs, makes our lives easier, and gives us a lot of fun.
