When we talk about something being faster, we usually think about how fast it is moving around us. If you are walking, the ground is moving under your feet. If you are running, your feet are also moving fast. If you are driving a car, the car is moving very quickly because it is using a power that makes it go faster.

To be faster means you are moving more quickly than someone else. If you are in a room and everyone is sitting on the floor, you are going faster than them. If you are playing a game and your friend is trying to catch your ball, you are going faster than them.

In life, being faster can mean you finish a job before someone else. It can mean you get to a school before everyone else. It can mean you can do things before you are supposed to do them.

But being faster does not always mean you are better. Sometimes people are faster than them just because they have more power in their bodies. A person who can run very fast is not always the best in a game where you have to use your brain. You can be faster than someone who is not as strong, but you can still have problems.

Sometimes, we use the word faster to talk about the way things look. If a picture is moving more quickly than another, we say it is faster. This is like saying a car is moving faster than a dog. It means the moving thing is doing more work and is going very well.
