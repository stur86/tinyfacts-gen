To carry something means you put it into a bag or a box so you can take it later. You might carry a book, a phone, or a small bag of money. When you do this, you are moving the thing from one place to another.

Imagine you have a friend who loves to run. They want to go to a far place. They carry the friend with them so they can keep them safe. The person carrying the friend lets them walk while they stay behind, looking after them.

Sometimes, you carry money. You put the money in a bag or a box so you can buy things you need later. When you carry money, you are keeping it safe for later use.

You can also carry food. If you have a bag of food, you carry it with you. This helps you eat when you are not at home.
