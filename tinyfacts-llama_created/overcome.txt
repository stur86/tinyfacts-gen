To over come something means you are going past what you thought was hard. It is when you are so strong that you do not let things stop you.

Think about a time when you wanted to run a long race but you were very tired. You were going to give up because you felt too much pain. But you did not give up. You ran until you were so very far that you felt like you could not go any more. You over came what you thought was hard by being very strong.

You can also over come things that hurt you. If you have a bad dream and you are afraid, you might feel very sad. But then you wake up and you are ready to start the day again. You over came the bad dream. You learned to be more strong and you are ready for the next day.

Sometimes you over come your own fears. Maybe you are afraid of a big thing, like a tall building. You think, I am not strong enough. But then you stand up and look at the building. You see how small it is. You see how many people are standing with you. You are not alone. You over came your fear. You are more than you thought you were.

You can also over come things that make you feel sad. If you are sad because a friend is gone, you might feel like you want to cry. But then you remember something important. You are still your friend. You are still your family. You over came the feeling of being alone. You are still you.
