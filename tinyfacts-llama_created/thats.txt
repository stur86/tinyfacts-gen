When you say something, you are showing it is true. If you say you love a person, it means you really care about them. If you say a thing is old, you are telling the truth. If you say a game is easy, you are being wrong.

To say something is that means you are sure it is the right one. It is like when you know your name is right. You can look at a picture of yourself and say, I am this person. You are not just making a guess. You are sure.

You can use this word with people and things. If a child says, I am that kid, they mean they are that one. If a doctor says, You are that person, they mean you have that kind of heart. The word helps us know when something is right.

Sometimes we say, That is a great idea. This is a good idea. This is the right thing to do. It shows we like what you are saying. If you say, That is not true, you are telling the truth. You are not just saying the word. You are being sure.
