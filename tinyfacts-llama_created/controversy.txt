To have a thing you think about is a big thing. When you think about it, many people get very angry. This is because they do not see the same way.

Imagine you like a book. You feel the story is good. You might want to read it again. But then another person comes and says, the story is bad. They think the story is not good at all. This makes many people angry. They do not want to talk to each other anymore.

This happens when you do not agree with what another person thinks. You might not like what they say. You could say, I do not think this is right. This makes the other person angry. They want you to say the same thing.

Sometimes, this makes things hard to do. You might feel afraid to talk to the person. But you also learn to stay calm and think about what you want to say. This makes your heart work better.

A person who has a thing they think about is called a person with a thing. This is a way to talk about people who have ideas that are different from you.
