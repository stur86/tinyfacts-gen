A pinker is a thing that you use to hold things together. You put it in a place where a thing is broken. It is a small thing with a point on the end. The point is very small, and it is very strong.

You put the pinker in the broken part. This makes the part stay in one place without falling off. It helps the broken part keep its place. This is why you use these things to fix things that are broken.

Sometimes you use a pinker to make a small cut. You put the cut thing on the paper, and you use the pinker to hold the paper in place. This helps you keep your writing or drawing on the paper.

In a way, the word pinker is like a small hand. It helps you hold things in one place.
