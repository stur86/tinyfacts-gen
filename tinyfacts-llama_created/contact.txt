To make a phone call is to use a phone to talk to another person. You can call someone you know or someone you are not sure about. You say the word you want to say, and the other person hears it.

Sometimes you want to talk to a person who lives in another city far away. You use a computer or a phone to make a phone call. You say the phone number to the person you want to talk to. The person you want to talk to hears your words and your voice.

You can also make a phone call to ask a question. You might want to know if a book is on the best place to buy it. If the book is on the right place, you can ask the store for help. The store person answers your question.

To make a phone call is to use your hands to send your voice to another person. It is a way to talk with others when you are not near them.
