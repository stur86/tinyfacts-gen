To go by means to move from one place to another. If you want to go to school, you go by your mother. You can also go by car, bus, or train. When you go by car, you drive your car. When you go by bus, you sit inside a big box with many people. When you go by train, you go on a long line of many cars that go along the ground.

Going by also means to move quickly. If you are going very fast, you can go through many houses in a short time. You can go to a place where you want to be very quickly. You might be going to a store where you buy food. You can also go to a movie. Many people go to the movies on the night before they go home from school.

Sometimes, when you go by, you might see many people. You see children running in the street. You see people walking. You might see big buses or cars moving in a line. You might hear music or talk from people inside these moving things.

Going by is a way to move from one place to another. It is a way to be where you want to be.
