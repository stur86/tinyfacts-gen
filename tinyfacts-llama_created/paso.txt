A walk is a way to go from one place to another. You might walk when you go to school. You walk past houses, stores, and other big things. You might also walk in a park. You can walk alone or with a friend. Walking is a good way to move your body. It helps your legs and feet stay strong.

Sometimes, you walk in a line with other people. This can be very fun. You walk together, talk to each other, and laugh. You might walk to a movie. You walk through a big room with many people. You see the movie on a big box. You can also walk around a city. You see many houses, shops, and streets.

To walk a long way means you walk for a long time. If you walk for five hours, you have gone far. Walking can help you feel happy. It can make you feel like you are doing something important.
