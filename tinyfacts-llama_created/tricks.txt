A thing that can do something different is called a game. A game is when you play with a ball or a small thing that moves. You try to get the ball into a box. You might run fast or jump high. Some games you do with your hands. Some you do with your feet. You might also use your head to see what is next.

Another kind of game is one where you use your mind. You think about what will happen next. You try to guess a number or a word. You might see a picture and try to guess what it is. Or you might hear a sound and try to guess what it is.

Sometimes a game is just for fun. You do it with your friends. You laugh and you play. You might play with a ball or a piece of wood. You might also play with your body. You might jump and you might run. You do it because it makes you happy.

Sometimes a game is about how you act. You might pretend to be a person. You act like you are a good person. You say nice things. You act like you are afraid. You act like you are angry. You act like you are sad. You play like this for a long time. You keep the act going until your friends are all laughing.

A game can also be about what happens in the end. You might play a game where you try to get a ball. You try to run and jump. You try to hit the ball. In the end, you get the ball. You are the one who won't give up. You keep trying until you get the ball.
