To breathe means you take in air that goes down into your body. This air goes through your nose, under your tongue, and into your mouth. You use this air to keep your body going. When you breathe in, you take in a little air that will give you power for a while. When you breathe out, you get ridden of the air that is not needed.

Your body needs air to stay good. If you don't breathe in enough, you feel very tired and your head might hurt. You can also feel very sad when you are too tired to breathe. If you are very cold, you might breathe hard to stay warm. Your face might turn red and your chest might feel heavy.

Sometimes, when you are happy or excited, you breathe very fast. This is okay! It helps you have more power to move your body and feel the world around you. When you are very angry or scared, you might breathe more slowly or hold your breath for a little while. This is because your body is trying to stay calm.

You can also breathe when you sleep. Even when you are asleep, you are still taking in air. The air goes into your body and helps you rest. This is why you might feel your face and chest moving while you are sleeping.
