The word flies is often used to talk about how things move. You can say, if you fly through the air, you are flying. If you walk on the ground, you are on the ground. The word shows that you are moving very fast or very far.

The word flies is also used to talk about people. When a person is a good friend, they are flying around each other. They are moving close together and they like to be near each other. When a person is afraid, they might fly away. They might run in a different direction. They are not happy anymore.

The police are the ones who keep the people safe. They are like the flying police. They go around and look for bad people. They want to stop them and keep everyone from being hurt. They are very important to keep our world a safe place.

In a story, you might see a person who flies very high in the sky. They might see things that no one else can see. They might see the whole world at once. They are doing something very strong and important.
