To surprise means you do something that is not expected. It is a way to catch someone or something that is not planned.

Imagine you are sitting in a room and you hear a knock at the door. You are waiting for someone, but then you hear another knock. The person on the other side of the door is not who you think it is. You are surprised. You do not know who it is or why they are there.

You might also surprise a friend. If you say you are going to a movie, but you are really going to a party, you are surprising them. They did not expect you to leave.

Surprise is often used to show that something is new or different. It can be a good surprise, like a fun thing you did, or a bad surprise, like a fight or a hurt person.

Sometimes, a surprise can be funny. You might see a funny picture or a funny thing on the computer. You are surprised by how funny it is. Or you might surprise yourself with a new idea. You had a plan, but something new happens and you are surprised.

Surprise can also happen when you are in a new place. If you are in a city you have never seen before, you might be surprised by the sounds, the people, and the sights. You do not know what to expect, and it is a good surprise.

In the end, to surprise someone means you do something that is not expected. It is a way to catch them off guard or make them feel happy.
