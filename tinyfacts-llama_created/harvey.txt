A person who makes things. They make food, clothes, boxes, and many other things you need. They use a long hard thing to make the hard parts. They put the food inside the box. Then the person puts the box in a moving car. The car goes very fast over the road. The box goes inside the car. The box gets hot. The hot air makes the food inside the box grow. When the food is ready, the person takes the box out of the car. They open the box to show what is inside. They might put the food on a table. You can eat it.
