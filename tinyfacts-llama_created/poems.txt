A story with words that are not in a book is a kind of writing. You can write this in many different ways. Some people write short stories. Some people write long stories with many words. Some people write words that tell a story, like a picture.

This kind of writing is called a story. You can also write this kind of story in your head. This is how you think. You can tell a story to a friend. You can write a story for people to read later. You can also write this kind of story for yourself.

Sometimes, you write this kind of story when you are very happy. You write about what happened. Other times, you write it when you are sad. You write about how you feel.

Polices are usually short. They are the first part of a story. This part tells what is happening before the story starts. The story after this part is the story. You can also find many short stories in books or papers.

People like to read these kinds of stories. They like to hear about far away lands. They like to see things that are hard to see. They like to hear about the world and the people in it.
