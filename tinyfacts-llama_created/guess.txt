To guess something means you try to figure out what it is. You might see many things in front of you. You might see a small ball, a book, a dog, or even a city. You look at each one and try to think what it is.

If you look at a ball, you might think, Is it round? Or is it long? You look at it many times until you are sure. You might ask a friend if they know what it is. They might say, Is it a ball? If you think they are right, you have guessed it!

You can also guess things that are not in front of you. You might hear a sound. You look around and see many things, like cars, houses, and trees. Then you think, What is the sound? You try to remember the sound and you guess, What was the sound? Sometimes you do not guess right at first. You try again and again until you get it right.

People also guess things that are not real. If you hear a voice, you might think it is your mother. You look at her face and you are sure. You have guessed her.

Guessing can be fun. You might play games with a friend. You try to guess what they are thinking or what they will say next. It is a way to be close to someone without really being there.

Sometimes you guess something that was not there at all. You might see a small thing that you did not notice before. You look at the ground and you see a tiny rock. You did not expect to see anything at all. This is when you see that you were wrong.
