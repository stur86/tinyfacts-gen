A group of people who work together is called a team. They share the work. They all help. They are not one person.

One person is the leader. They make the plans. They decide what to do. The other people follow their plans. They do what they say.

Sometimes there is money. The leader gets some of it. The other people get some too. This money helps buy things they want. It helps make the work better.

There are also people who help with the work. They do the small parts. They fix things. They clean the floor. They make sure everything is ready.

Sometimes the group has a problem. They need to work together to fix it. They share their ideas. They try to find the right answer. They listen to each other. They are all working together to get it done.

This is how people work together. They share the work. They all help. They are not one person. They are a group.
