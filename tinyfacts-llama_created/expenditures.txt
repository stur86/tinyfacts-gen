To spend something means to give it away. This is different from saving. When you save money, you keep it for the moment when you need it. When you spend money, you use it right now.

Imagine you have a bag full of money. You can buy food, clothes, or a new book. If you only have one bag, you can only buy one thing. But if you have many bags, you can buy many things. You spend money so you can do things you want.

People use money for many reasons. They might spend money to buy food for their family. They might spend money to buy a new car for their child. Sometimes they spend money on things they love, like music or a new phone. They might even spend money on fun things, like going to a movie or a game.

When a business spends money, it means the business is buying things or doing things. A store might spend money on paper and things to make the paper look nice. A doctor might spend money on things to help a person who is sick. A school might spend money on books and teachers to teachers.

Sometimes, people spend money on things that are not good for them. They might buy a big bag of food when they already have some. They might buy a new car if they already have a car. This is a bad idea because they are using money they do not have.

To spend something also means to pay for it. You pay money when you buy something. If you are late to work, you might get a fine. If you go to a doctor, you pay money for the doctor to help you.
