To save something means you keep it for later. If you have money, you can save it. You put it away in a safe box so you can use it later when you need it.

Think about food. If you eat something, you need to give your body the right thing to stay safe. If you save food before eating, you can eat it later without feeling sick.

You can also save things for a big day. If you have a job, you might work hard so you can buy a nice thing. You save your money until the day comes. Then you can buy the thing and feel happy.

Sometimes, you might save money for a fun thing. You might put it away in a box and watch it grow. When you have a lot of money, you can buy the thing you want and enjoy it with your friends.

Saving is important because it helps you feel safe. It keeps you from losing what you have. It is a good way to be ready for what is coming next.
