A money thing comes out of a big box. This thing is part of a group of money. It is the part you pay when you buy food, drinks, or things you want.

Imagine a group of people. Each person has a number. When you pay, you give a little bit of that number to the group. This number is the same for everyone. You do not give any money to the person before you pay. You just take what they give.

A big group of people has ten numbers. Each number is the same. When you pay, you take one number from the group. You take one number each time you pay. This way, you have ten numbers.

The numbers are not the same every time. One month, a number is four. The next month, the number is five. So, when you pay, you only pay a few numbers. You do not have to remember all ten numbers. You just take the right ones.

In the money world, a half is a little bit. If you give the half, you are done. You do not have to give the full money. You only need the half to know how much you have. This is why a money thing is called a half.

You also have a small group of numbers inside your head. When you pay, you use the numbers from your group to find the right half. Your brain tells you which half you need. This is why you can pay without looking at the numbers.
