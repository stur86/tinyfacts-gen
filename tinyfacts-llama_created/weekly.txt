To do something every week is to follow the same days and days of the week over many months. You do this for many years or for a long time.

When you do this, you have more days than you have weeks. Each week has five days. There are seven days in one week. So, if you do this many times, you have seven times more days than weeks.

You might do it with school, work, or other things you do. You might work a full week, a half week, a few weeks, or many weeks. You can do it with one person or many people.

People do it for fun, for work, or for other reasons. Some people do it with friends, while others do it with family. You can do it with your job or with your school.
