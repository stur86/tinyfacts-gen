To make something fall through the air is to use a thing that you hold in your hand. This thing is filled with a soft, clear stuff that is very small. When you press it, the stuff comes out of the top and goes down to the ground.

Imagine you are trying to get a drink from a tall cup. You can not just drink it from the top. You need to shake the cup. This is like what happens when you make something fall. You press the thing in your hand, and the soft stuff comes out and goes down, like the drink is falling through the air.

You can also use this word for other things that do not have a cup. If you put a small bit of a bright color on your face and then run through the park, the color goes all over your face. You are making the bright color fall through the air. This is how you can change your look.

Sometimes, to make something fall, you need to make a sound. You can make a soft sound with your lips and then push air out. This pushes the air down to the ground. This can happen in a room with windows, or in a car, or even in a tree. The sound is like a tiny push that makes things fall.

You can also use this word with animals. If you give an animal a drink, it falls down and lands on the ground. The drink is making the animal fall. Some animals can fly, but they do not use a thing like a cup to make them fall. They just go up and then come down, because they are falling down in the same way as everything else.

So, a thing that makes something fall is a thing that makes air fall. It is a way to make things go down when you press it.
