When you suck something, you take it in your mouth. You use your teeth to hold it. Your tongue goes in, and your lips pull it around your teeth. The air goes out, and the thing you wanted comes out. You can suck air, water, or even small pieces of food.

Your face gets a bit red. Your skin feels warm. Your mouth feels wet because of the air. This is why you feel happy when you are happy or very tired. You might also suck at a drink when you want something sweet or when you drink from a bottle.

You can also suck things that are hard. Imagine you try to pull a long piece of wood. If you suck, the wood starts to move. This is because the air goes into the wood and makes it smaller. You are doing the same thing when you suck a drink. You are taking the drink in your body so it is inside you.
