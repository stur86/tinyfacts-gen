To simply means you do something without any trouble. You do it because it is easy or because you want to. You do not need to think a lot. You just do it.

When you say something is simply, you mean it is not a big word. It is a short way to say you are being very clear.

Imagine you have a hard work to do. You must go to a place, take a book from a place, and then come back home. If you say the work is easy, you mean it is not hard. But if you say it is simply, you mean it is not a big word or a long story. You just tell it is easy because it is easy.

You can also say something is simply because it is true. If a doctor tells you to drink a glass of water, you do it because the water is what you need to drink. You do not think about it. You just do it.

In our lives, we use this word all the time. We say things like this: my mom is simply nice, my friend is simply funny, or my family is simply happy.

So, to simply means you do something because it is easy. It means you do it with no trouble, and you are not afraid to tell people how you feel.
