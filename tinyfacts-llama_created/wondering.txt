To wonder is to feel a strange feeling that is hard to explain. It is like your mind is filled with questions that you do not have the words to say. You might look at something and be surprised. You might think, Why? Or, You do not understand how that works. Or, You do not know what it means.

When you wonder, you are not afraid. You are not worried. You are not trying to find the right answer. You are just happy to be thinking about it. You might feel like you are in a big room and you are looking at a single person. You see this person and you think, They are amazing. Or, You see many people and you think, They are all amazing.

Sometimes, you wonder about the world. You wonder about how things happen. You wonder about how people live. You wonder about how the world works. You might ask yourself, What is this thing? What happened before this thing happened? Or, You might wonder, Why do people do things?

You can also wonder about your own life. You might wonder, What am I doing? What should I be doing? Or, You might wonder, What will happen tomorrow?

Wondering is important. It helps you stay open. It helps you feel about things without knowing everything. It helps you be happy when things are not sure. It helps you learn. When you wonder, you are not afraid to ask questions. You are not afraid to think about things you do not know.

You can wonder at many things. You can wonder at the world. You can wonder at animals. You can wonder at the sky. You can wonder at the way things move. You can wonder at the way life works. You can wonder at your own heart.
