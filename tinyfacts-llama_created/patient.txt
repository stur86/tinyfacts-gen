To be a good person is to be a person who can wait. You can do this when you are waiting for food, for a ride, for a word from someone. You do not act like a child. You do not shout or cry. You just sit and do nothing. This is the way you show you care about the world and about the people in it.

When you are a good person, you do not act like a dog. You do not jump up and hit someone. You do not say bad words. You just act like a person. You listen to what the other person wants. You try to understand. You try to be kind.

You can also be a good person when you are a friend. You are there when the other person is sad. You listen to them. You help them. You do not say anything bad. You just say, I am here for you. You are a good friend because you care about the other person.

Being a good person is hard. There are times when you want to shout or act like a person. But if you remember that you are a person who waits, you will stay calm. You will wait for the right moment. You will wait for the right word. You will wait for the right time to act.
