To breathe out or to move air from one part of your body to another is to show that you are moving. When you breathe in, air goes into your chest and your head, and then it goes out from your mouth or nose, and your body gets the power it needs to work.

Sometimes you can see this happening. When a person talks, they are moving air through their mouth. The air comes out of their mouth and goes into their chest. Then it goes into their head, and they use the power from it to speak words. This happens so fast that your eyes do not see every single breath.

You also breathe out when you let go of things. If you are sad or angry, you might breathe out your anger. Your body becomes heavy because you have a lot of bad feeling inside. This makes your chest move in a different way, and sometimes you feel like you are going to cry. When you breathe out, you are telling your body that it is okay to let go.
