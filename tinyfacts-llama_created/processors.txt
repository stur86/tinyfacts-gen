A computer is made of many parts. The parts are like different people. Each person does a very hard job.

The person that does the most hard job is the one that is in the middle. This is the one that is very fast. It can do many things very quickly. This person is like the brain of the computer.

There are many other people too. They are in the top of the computer. These people do not do as much work as the person in the middle. They only help to make the computer do its job.

When you want to do something, you tell the person in the middle. The person in the middle reads what you want. It then does the job. If you want to show a picture, the person in the middle does many hard jobs. It shows you the picture.

The computer also has many other parts. There are the ones that hold your words. There are the ones that hold your numbers. There are the ones that make the computer talk to other computers.

The computer is like a very strong person. It can do many things very fast. It can remember a lot of numbers and words. It can also do many different jobs very quickly.

When a computer is very busy, it uses a lot of power. If you do many things at once, it can use more power. If you do one thing at a time, it uses less power.
