When you are happy or feel good, you might smile. You might laugh. You might feel very safe and happy.

But sometimes, your face gets all red and your eyes get tight. You feel a bit angry. This is called being up. It is when your body tells you something is wrong.

You might feel this way when you see a person you do not like. You might feel sad or mad. Your body tells you to stop and think. This is why you say you are up.

You can also be up when you are afraid. You might feel a bit scared. You might be thinking about the things that are not sure. You might want to hide.

Sometimes, you can be up for many reasons. Maybe you are happy to see a friend you love. Maybe you are up because you are very tired and your body is ready for sleep.
