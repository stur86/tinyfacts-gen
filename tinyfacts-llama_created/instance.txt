An act is when one person does something. This can be a small thing, like a child doing a job, or a big thing, like a person being a leader. It is a real eventually that happens.

An act is usually one time. Once a person does an act, that act is over. You can not say, in the end, that that person did something again.

But an act can have many more parts. A story has many parts. Each part is a little act. If you read a story, you go from one act to the next. You see what happened before you saw what happened next. This is a good way to understand a story.

You can also have many acts in one life. A person might do many acts in one year. Each act is like a small act. They all happen together.

In a big group, an act is when many people do the same thing. If all of them do a job at the same time, they are doing one act. This is how a group of people can work together.
