Calm water is the kind of water that is very quiet. It is often clear or white because of tiny pieces of dead stuff that are stuck to the bottom. Animals and tiny living things that live in the water use this calm water to live.

A thing with many small parts that grow together is called a rock. Some of these rocks are very old and have been there for a very long time. They are made from the stuff that came from deep inside the ground of the land.

These small living things that are in the calm water need a place to live. They have tiny parts that they use to move from place to place. When these tiny parts get too small, the tiny living thing dies. But when it still has some parts left, it can grow a new body.

When many of these tiny living things grow, they build a big wall. The wall is made of their dead parts. The wall is very strong. It is hard to break. This wall helps keep the calm water clean.

A big thing that lives in the water is the same as the rock. It has many small parts that are stuck together. It is very hard and strong, but it is not a rock. It does not come from the ground. It comes from the water.

Calm water is also where the living things go when they die. They go into the deep parts of the water. There, they become part of the rocks and the walls. This helps keep the water clean.
