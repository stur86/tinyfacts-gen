A powers buyer is a person who buys things for you. They do not buy things on their own. They buy them for you and send them to the place where you need them.

When you buy something, you pay money. The person with the power buys the money from you. They then get the things you need. When they get the things, they can keep them for you. Or, they give them to someone else to keep for you.

This word is usually used when you buy things that are very important or things that you need to live. The person with the power buys things so that you do not have to. They buy the things so that you can have them when you need them.
