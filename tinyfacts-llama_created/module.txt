A group of things made to work together is called a group. When many of these things work together, they can do more than one thing. They can do many things at once.

A group of computers is a group. These computers are made to work together and do many things. If one computer breaks, the group must also break. This can happen if one computer is very bad or if there is too much work for it to do.

Sometimes, a group of people is called a group. This can be a team of people who are all doing the same job. They are all working together to get a job done. They can share ideas and help each other.

A group can be very big. There are groups of people, groups of animals, and groups of ideas. Some groups are made by people who are very close to each other. Other groups are made by people who are very far away.
