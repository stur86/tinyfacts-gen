The word top is used to talk about the very top of a building. This is the part where people can see from far away. It is usually the highest part. The top of a building is also the top of a tree. The top of a tree is the very top where the leaves start to fall off.

Think of the top of a book. If you look up, you see the whole story. The top of a book is like the top of a story. It tells you what happened before the story begins.

Sometimes, people use the word top to talk about things that are very strong. A top can also mean the very top of a group. When there are many people in a group, the person at the very top is like the top. They are the one in the front and the one who makes the plan.
