To be a person who has no hair is to be a baby. This is because babies are all the time very soft and look like they have hair. When they are very small, they have lots of hair all over their body. As they grow, their hair starts to go away and they become a baby with no hair at all.

Sometimes, people do not lose all of their hair. They keep some of it, and it looks like the old hair is still there. This is why the word can mean both being a baby and not having hair at all. A person who is very old has almost no hair and looks like a baby. They might still have some hair, but it is very short and not very thick.

You can also use this word for other people. If a man is very old, he might not have any hair. This does not mean he is not a man. It just means he is very, very old. This is why it is not strange for an old man to say, I am old.

It is also a word for a job. If you are a doctor, you do not have hair. You wear a white shirt and a soft, round thing on your head. You are very important because you help people.
