To have something inside something means you are inside a thing. This is different from being on the outside. When you are inside a thing, you are part of it. The thing is also inside you.

Think of a house. The walls of the house are the outside. But inside the house there are many rooms. Each room has a door and walls. The rooms are inside the house. You live inside the house with your family.

If you have a bag, you have things inside. You can carry the bag with you. You do not leave the bag. It is still inside your hand. If you open the bag, the things inside go out. You are still inside the bag.

Sometimes, a person has a lot of money. Inside the money there are many different kinds of money. Some are small pieces of paper. Some are numbers that you can use to buy things. The money is inside the paper. You are inside the money.

You can also have many things you like. If you like food, you have food inside you. If you like a game, you have the game inside you. Inside the things, the things are what make them work.
