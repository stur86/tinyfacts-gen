To grow means to become bigger and stronger. You start small and then get bigger every day. This happens because of the inside of your body.

When you are small, your body is like a tiny box. It can hold only so much air and water. But as you grow, your body becomes more room for your food to come in. Your body starts to take in more food and water. This makes your body bigger and your body starts to change.

Inside your body, tiny tiny things move and go around. They are like tiny little things that eat your food and help you grow. This is why you get bigger. Your body starts to feel heavier because it is getting bigger.

You can grow in many ways. You can grow taller. You can grow hair. You can grow eyes. Or you can grow a long piece of hair on your head. Or you can grow a long long hair that falls down your back.

Growing is very important. When you are growing, you are getting ready to have babies. Your body is getting ready to give you new life. It is a big job, but it is also very nice because you are becoming something new and better every day.
