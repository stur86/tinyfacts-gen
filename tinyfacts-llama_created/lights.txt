Lights are things that make things bright. They are usually made of glass or other hard stuff that is filled with a clear stuff that makes things light. When you turn them on, they send out tiny bits of light that go out and fill the room.

You can find lights in many places. There are ones that hang from the sky. There are ones that are on the floor. There are ones that sit on tables. Some lights are small and fit in a box. Other lights are big and cover the whole room.

Some lights are for people. They are for houses. They light up the inside so you can see who is there. Some lights are for cars. They help people see in the dark. They keep them safe so they do not hit other cars.

You also have lights inside your body. Your eyes have them. They help us see when it is dark. This is why you can see a tree at night. You can see the stars. You can see the dark sky.

Lights can also be for fun. You can use them to make a big room feel happy. You can hang many lights together to make a cool picture. You can use them to make a place look like a fun place.
