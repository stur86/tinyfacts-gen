When the air is very wet, it is full of tiny drops of water. If you breathe into this air, the water drops will come out of your mouth and down your face. This makes your skin feel wet and your nose feel stuff.

The air also has tiny bits of water that are not big drops, but very small drops. These tiny drops are very light. They can go through your skin like you go through a paper bag. When you walk, the tiny drops go out of your skin and down your legs and arms.

When the air is very high, the tiny drops are not as many. This is why the air feels warm to your skin. If the air is cold, the tiny drops are many and the air feels heavy.

We need this kind of air to grow things. When the air is too dry, the tiny drops fall off the leaves of the trees and the ground. When the air is too wet, the tiny drops fall off the skin of the animals.

You can see this in the water. When the water is very high, the tiny drops stay on the top and the water looks clear. When the water is very low, the tiny drops go down and the water looks thick.
