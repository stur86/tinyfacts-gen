When you are excited or happy, your face may light up. You might jump up or shout. But if things do not go the way you thought they would, you might feel sad.

If you want something, like a ball or a game, and you do not get it, you can be very sad. You might feel angry. Your body might feel like it is heavy. You might want to cry. This is because your body is telling you that something important did not happen.

You can also feel this way when you do not get what you want from a person. If a friend does not come to play or if a teacher does not give you a paper, you might feel sad. You might feel like you were not good enough.

It is normal to feel this way. It is how your body and mind respond to things that do not go as planned. It is a sign that you are trying to understand what happened.
