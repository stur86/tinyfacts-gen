An area of land is called an area. It is a space with ground under it. People live in this area, and many grow food, animals, or build houses here.

A big area of land is called an area of land. An area can be very big or very small. Imagine a huge open field of green. A small area is a tiny bit of land.

A smaller area of land is often around a house. People might walk around this area many times every day. They might see animals that live there and hear sounds from the animals and the wind.

An area can also be around the water. You can walk around the edge of a big body of water. This area might have water on both sides, and there might be many small areas inside the water.

Some areas are far away from other areas. These are called far areas. You might drive a long time to get to one of these areas. Other areas are close to your home. You might walk or drive a short time to get to one of these areas.

People use these words to talk about areas of land. They can talk about the area of a city, the area of a park, the area of a field, or the area of water. They might want to know how many people live there, how much food is growing, or how much water is coming from the area.
