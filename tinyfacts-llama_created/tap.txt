To take something is to use your hands to get it. You can take a drink from a bottle, or you can take a small thing from a table. Sometimes people take money from a box so they can buy food or a new thing.

You can also take something that has been broken. If you see a ball on the ground and you pick it up, you are taking it. This is a good thing because the ball was once a fun thing to play with. If you take something that is not your own, it is not right. This is why you might feel angry if a friend takes something that is yourself.

You can also use the word to talk about the sound of water. When you turn a thing on, water starts moving. You can hear the water moving as it goes past your hands. This is why you hear the sound of running water.
