To give something is to add to a group of people. When you help a friend, you are a good person. You are giving them your time and your money. You are not taking anything from them.

When a person helps many other people, they are making a big group stronger. They are making everyone feel better. If one person is sick, many people can feel better too. If a person helps to build a new school, many children can go to school and learn.

We also give things when we are sad. If you lose your favorite book, you might feel sad. You give the book to a friend who loves books. This makes your friend happy.

Sometimes we give things when we are angry. If a person does something wrong, we might give them a time to think about it. This helps everyone feel calm.

You can also give money to people who need it. This helps them buy food and clothes. It is a kind way to help those who are not as well as you.

Giving is a way to show you care. When you give something, you are sharing your life with others. You are making them feel loved.
