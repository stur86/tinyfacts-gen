To said something means you tell the truth. You use words to tell others you did or did not do something.

When you said something, you are the one who did it. You must be sure what you are saying before you tell it. You must not make a wrong word, a wrong face, or a wrong look. If you are sure, you said it.

You can said something that happened in the past. You can also said something that you will do in the near time. If you are going to do something, you said it. If you are afraid, you said it.

Sometimes, when you say something, you hear it. You hear the words, the sound of your own voice, and the sound of others. You hear it so you know what you said.

Sometimes, you say something and someone does not hear it. The words stay there, but the person does not understand. The words go into their head, but the brain does not know what to do with them.

You can also said something that is not true. You might tell a lie. If you say you ate a lot of food, but you did not eat much, you said a lie. You did not mean to be a lie. You are the one who did it.
