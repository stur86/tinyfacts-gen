To start something is to begin it. A person can start a fire. A person can start a game. A person can start a story.

To use a gun is to make a loud sound and to make a thing go very fast. The person holds the gun. They point it at the ground. They pull a round out. The round goes into the ground. The round hits the ground. The ground is made to fall.

A person can also start to run. They start by moving their feet. They go far. They are very fast. They can go very high. They can go very deep.

When a team starts to play a game, the people put on their clothes. They put their hands on their heads. They stand up. They look at each other. They are ready to play.

Sometimes a person starts to cry. They feel very sad. They might cry because they are sad about a thing that happened. They might cry because they are afraid. They might cry because they feel very much.
