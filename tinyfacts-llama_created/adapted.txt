To be an animal is not always the same for everyone. Some people are big and strong. Some are small and fast. Some have a hard skin that can keep them safe, and some have a soft skin that can change color.

Animals need to eat food to stay living. If an animal eats too much food, it can grow round and heavy. If it eats too little food, it can get very sick. Animals also need air to breathe. They use their mouths to take in air and their bodies to get the air out.

Some animals are very good at hiding. They can turn their bodies into a soft cover to hide from the sun. Others are very good at moving. They can run very fast to get away from a big animal. Some are very good at flying. They can go very high into the sky to see the world far away.

Animals have also learned how to live together. Some animals are very small and live in many places. Others are very big and live in one big place. Some animals have babies that are very different from the mother. This is how all the animals are changed over time.
