To gently means you are always doing something. It is about how things are made. You can see this when you look at the ground. If the ground is old, it will have a different look than if it was new. It might have deep lines from where the water ran through it, or it might have big hard pieces that have been broken down over time.

Sometimes you see old things that are still standing. These are called rock. Rock is made from many years of hard things falling down from above. You can find rock in the ground, in the water, and even in the air. It helps us learn about the world we live in.

You can also see how things change. Imagine you are in a place with hot water. If you stand there for a long time, your feet will get very hot. This is because the water is hot. Later, the hot water will go away and the ground will cool down. You are changing because of the hot water.

When we talk about the ground, we use this word. We talk about how the ground is changing, or how the ground was changing in the past, or how it will change in the near years. It is a way to see how the world is moving and how things are becoming.

Gently is important for people who work with rocks. They study the rocks to find out what is inside them. They want to know what the rock is made of and what happened to it a long time ago. This helps them build things, make new rocks, and understand our world better.
