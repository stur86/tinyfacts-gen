Mumbled is a word we use to talk about how people make things. When a person says they want to go to the city, they often use this word.

A man said, he wanted to go to the city. He did not really know where the city was. He said he wanted to go there. The word made him think about the city. It made him think about the city and the city and the city. He felt his mouth was moving a lot.

Another person said, he did not know where the city was. He just wanted to go. He did not really think about the word. The word did not change his plan at all. He still wanted to go there. The word was just a thing he said.

The word is not used to mean a big city. A city is a group of many houses and streets. A mumbled thing is a way you talk when you do not really know where you are going. You might say you are going to the city, but you do not know where the city is. You are just hoping to get there.
