A man is a person. He lives on a far off place. He works at a job. He has a home and a family. He can do many things. He can build a house, go to a place, and work.

A man is also a person who does something. He might be a doctor, a teacher, or a police man. He helps people. He gives them the right to live. He keeps them safe.

Sometimes, people say a man is strong. This is because men are very good at doing hard work. They can lift heavy things. They can run very fast. They can fight if someone is mean.

A man can also be a person who has a dream. He might want to fly. He might want to be a great actor. He might want to make people happy. This is when a person wants to do something that is not easy.
