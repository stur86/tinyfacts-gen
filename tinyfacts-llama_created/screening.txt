To pass something through something is a way of keeping things safe. When you are very busy or you have many things to do, you often use a long box. This box goes through many things. It looks at how each thing works. If a thing is not strong enough, the box will move it away. This keeps everyone and everything safe.

Think about a school. Many things need to pass before a person can be a teacher. They must show they know how to write and they must show they know how to act around people. The teacher uses a box to check these things. If a person does not pass, they do not become a teacher.

You can also use this word for people. If you are looking for a job, you might use it to find the right person. You look at their work and their words. If they seem good at doing things and if they can act well around people, you might want to ask them to work with you. This is like using a box to keep the right person safe for your job.
