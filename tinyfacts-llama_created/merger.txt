To join something together is to put it with another thing. This can be done for many reasons.

Imagine you have many books and you want to keep them all in one place. You can move them to a box. Then the box is the one that holds them all. This is like a big group.

Another way to join is in a school. Some schools have many small groups of kids. When kids from these groups meet, they can talk and play together. They can share their ideas and learn from each other. This is a way to build a stronger group.

Sometimes, two people want to work together. They want to do the same job. They join so they can share their ideas and work as a team. This can help them do more work and make things better.

In business, a group of people can join together to make a new thing. They might start a new business or make a new kind of food. When many people join, they can use their ideas to make a better thing.

Even in your family, you can join. You might have a friend who lives far away. You can talk and send letters. When you do this, you are joining your family so you can stay in touch.

So, a group is when many things join together. They become one group with a shared idea. They can help each other, work as a team, or make a better thing together.
