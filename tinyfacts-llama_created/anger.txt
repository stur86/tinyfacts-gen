Anger is a strong feeling that people have when they are very mad. When you are angry, your face gets red and your body tight. You might shout. You might want to fight or hit someone. This is because your body says, you are very angry.

Sometimes, anger is good. When a child is very angry because they are afraid, the parents can help them feel better. They talk to the child and show them that they are safe. This helps the child calm down.

But sometimes, anger is very bad. When you are angry at someone and you say things you do not mean, you might hurt them. You might say something bad to make them feel bad. This is not good. It makes you feel bad too.

Anger is like a big feeling inside your chest. It is like a ball of hot air. When you are angry, you are very still and quiet. You might not want to do anything. But inside, the ball of hot air is growing and getting bigger. This is why you can feel so strong and loud.
