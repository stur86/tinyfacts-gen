A line of points is called a line. There is no end to it. It goes on for many sides. You can walk on it, drive on it, or fly over it. It can be straight or it can be a bit round.

A group of lines is called a line. Many lines can be joined together. This makes a larger picture. You can have many lines that go in the same direction, or you can have lines that go in different directions.

A place with many lines is called a city. Many of these lines are made by people. People often walk or drive through them to get from one place to another. Some lines go over the water, while others go under it.

Long lines of numbers are called a line of numbers. You can write many of these lines on a paper. Each line is a group of numbers. You can use these lines to show how things work.

A line can also be a way to show how things are grouped. Imagine a line of days. Each day is on a line. This line shows how many days have passed since the first day.

Sometimes, people use a word to mean many lines. They write this word in a book. This word tells you that there are many lines, like a picture with many lines.
