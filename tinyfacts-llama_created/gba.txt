To green means to grow. It is a way to show how old a tree is. You can look at the green parts of the tree. When a tree is very old, it has many green parts. These parts can grow and fill the whole tree. The tree is green because it has lots of water inside it and lots of tiny green things on the outside that make it look like it is growing.

Sometimes you see a word that means to look at something. This word is used for people who work with pictures. They use it to tell what a picture is about. They look at the green parts of the picture to see if it is a real picture or if it is a picture that someone made.

There is another word that means to be in a place. This word is used for people who go on big trips. They need to be in the right place at the right time. They go to the city, the park, or the house of a friend to see what is happening. They are not just standing there. They are moving from place to place, like a green car on a road.
