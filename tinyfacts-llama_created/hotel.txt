To stay in a place is to live there for a short time. You go there to rest your body or to be with people you know. This is because you need a bed or a room to sleep.

Many big cities have many places like this. They are not old houses, but big groups of rooms. The people who run these places help you find your room. You pay money for the night. This money is used to buy the food and the things for you.

Some places are only for people who stay for a few days. These are usually the ones where you go to be with friends or family. You might go to a big building with many rooms. You will be in a room with a window and a bed.

Other places are for people who stay for a very long time. These are the ones where you live. You might have a room with a kitchen so you can make food. There is a room with a big bed for sleeping. You might have a desk where you work. Some of these places are very quiet and very clean.

When you stay in a place, you often pay a small feet money. You pay this money each night so that the person who runs the place does not have to pay for it all the time.
