To control something is to have power over it. Think of a car. You are the one who drives it. You tell it where to go and when to stop. If you push the car too fast, it might go over a big wall. If you slow it down, it stays on the road.

Things are not always in one place. You can be in many places at once. A computer is a thing that can control many other things. It can help you write a letter or tell a story. It can also control the lights in a room so you can see at night.

When you say a word, the person who speaks it controls the sound. Think of a word that means you feel angry. If you say the word, you are telling your body to feel that way. This is how we use words to talk to each other.

A person who is good at controlling things is called a leader. They help other people follow them so they do what is right. They make sure everyone is safe and happy.

In a game, you might control a ball. You try to move it around a big ground so it goes in the right way. You use your hands and your brain to do this. You are in control of the ball.

To control something is to be the one who decides what happens next. It is an important part of how we live our lives.
