To be a part of something means you are a small piece of it. You are not the whole thing. You are only one small part.

Think of a tree. The tree is a big thing with many parts: the big green top, the long green parts that grow from the top, the small brown parts that cover the ground, and many more. Each part is a part of the tree. If you take the top part, you are taking a part of the tree. If you take the small brown parts, you are also taking a part of the tree.

When you say a thing is a part of something else, you mean it is a small piece that is not the whole thing. It is one part of many parts. It can be very small. A tiny piece of paper is a part of a big book. It is not the book, but it is a small part of it.

Sometimes, a part is something that is not very strong or good. You might have a small cut on your hand. The cut is a part of your body. It is not the whole hand, but it is still part of it.

You can also be a part of a group. If many people work together, they become a group. They are all part of the group. The group is a bigger thing than each person.
