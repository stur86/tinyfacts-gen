To do something immediately means you do it right now. It happens as soon as you say you are going to do it. This is how you act when you have a plan to get something you want.

Imagine you have a big bag full of money. You want to buy a new car. If you say you are going to buy the car, you do it right away. You walk to a store and pay for it. This is an immediately.

You can also do something immediately if you say you are going to do it. You might say, I am going to run. Right now, you start running. You do not wait. You run as fast as you can.

Sometimes, you can do something that has already started but you act very fast. You might be on your phone and a friend calls you. You answer the phone right away. You talk to them for a few minutes. Then you say, I am going to go. You go to their place without waiting much more.

You can also use this word for when you act fast because you have a good idea. If you see a car that is going very fast, you might act fast. You might stop in the middle of the road so the car does not hit you.

It is also sometimes used to show that something is happening very quickly. If you have a problem and you act right away, the problem goes away. This means you did something immediately and it helped you.

So, to do something immediately means you do it right away. You act now and the thing happens before it would have happened later.
