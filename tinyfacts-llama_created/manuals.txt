A book is a thing you buy. You can read what is inside. This is called a book. If you want to know how to do something, you can read a book. 

A man is a person. He is a living being. A man can walk, run, and talk. He also has arms and a head. People use him to do work. They pay him money for doing jobs. 

A small group of people is a team. They work together. One person is in the head. The other people help. They work as a team. This team can do many jobs. 

A box is a thing you hold. You can put things inside it. You can put food, books, or things you buy in it. You can also put money in it. When you open the box, the things come out. You can see what you have inside.
