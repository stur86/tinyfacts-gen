To keep something going is to make sure it is working well. If you are a teacher, you must keep your students learning. If you are a parents, you must keep your children safe. You do this by fixing things when they break, by giving them food and water, and by listening to what they say.

You can also keep something that is not moving. If you put a heavy box on a table, the table stays up. If a child does this to a table, the table stays safe. You are helping to keep the world a safe place.

Sometimes, you must also keep a thing that is inside you. You must keep your heart strong. This means you must not let your mind get too sad or angry. You must stay calm and think about what to do next.

If you do not keep something, it will fall apartment. If you do not keep a place, other people will move there. If you do not keep a thing, you will lose it. This is why it is very important to take care of the world and each other.
