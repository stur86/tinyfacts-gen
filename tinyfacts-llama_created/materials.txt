To make something is to use things to build it. This is how we make things from the ground. You can make a chair from wood, a book from paper, or a car from parts.

You can also make something from old things. You might take old wood from a tree and make a book cover. You might use old paper to make a new book. This is a good way to use things that have been around for a long time.

Some things are made from many different kinds of things. You can find a small part and put it into a bigger piece. This helps the piece work well. It also makes it very strong.

You can also make something new from things you do not need. Imagine a world where we only use things we already have. It would not be the same. Things made from many different kinds of things help us live in a way that is good for everyone.
