To pay for something is to give money to someone so they can take it. When you send things in the air, you need to give them money. You pay the money to a place where the money goes. This is called paying the person who runs the place.

Imagine you have a small box that you want to send to your friend. You put the box in the box so it can be carried. You give the box to a person who walks. The person walks to your friend. When the person gets to your friend, your friend gives you the box. You pay the person for carrying the box. This is like paying the person who runs the place so they can do their job.

You also pay for the box and the air it goes through. The box and air are made of hard stuff. The box must be put in the box before it goes out. The box and air are carried through the air to the far place. When the box gets to the far place, you pay the person again. The person takes the box and puts it in the box again. They carry it back to you.

People also pay for other things like money for a car or a book. If you buy a car, you pay money for the car. If you buy a book, you pay money for the book. This money goes to a place where the money goes, and then it comes back to you.

You also pay money when you go to a place that is far away. You pay for a seat so you can sit in the room where food is made. You pay for a place to sleep so you can rest. You pay money to the people who run the places so they can do their job.
