To go across means you are moving from one side of a hard ground to the other side. You can do this by running, walking, or using a thing that goes very fast. You are not in the same place as you started.

Imagine you have a book with pictures on each side. If you turn the book, the pictures go from one side to the other. You are moving across the picture. You are not stopping until you reach the other side.

You can go across a wide body of water. If you walk across the water, you are moving from one side to the other. This is how people cross a big body of water to get to a far away place. You might need to walk on the water, or you might use a small thing that goes across the water.

Some things are made to go across. A car can go across a wide road. A thing that flies can go across the sky. You can also go across a high place that you can not walk up. This is where you use a thing that goes very fast to go from one side to the other.
