To be in a position to pay money is to have money that you are going to give when you do something. When you get a job, you work for a few weeks. You do work and you get paid. You are in a position to pay money because you have money in your pocket or in your money box.

Imagine you want to buy a small thing. You have money for it. If you buy it, you have paid for it. If you do not pay, the thing is gone. You are in a position to pay money because you can give money.

You can be in a position to pay money in many ways. You can get a job and work. You can get money from a job. You can get money from a house. You can get money from a school. When you are in this position, you have money ready to give away.

In some places, you have to pay money for going to a place. This is for roads. Roads are lines that go across many places. When you drive a car on these roads, you pay money. You pay money when you go very far.

You can also be in a position to pay money for things that you buy. If you buy food, you pay money. If you buy a book, you pay money. If you buy a drink, you pay money. When you pay money, you are giving the money to the person who has the power to give it to you.
