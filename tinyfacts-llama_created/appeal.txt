To ask for something to be done can be a big thing to do. If you want to get a job, you might say,  I want to work here. This is an ask. You are asking the person you want to work with to change their mind about you. They will listen to you.

If you are sad, you might want to talk to a friend. You ask them to listen to you. They will try to help you feel better. This is also an ask.

You can also ask to be let into a room. If you want to see a movie, you might ask,  Can I go in now? The person who is in the room will decide.

Sometimes we ask for help. If you break a thing you own, you might say,  Can you fix it for me? A friend will try to fix it. They might ask you to tell them what happened.

You can also ask to be in a game with another person. This is a fun way to play games with friends. You ask them if they want to play with you. If they say yes, you will play together.

When we use this word, we ask why something is the way it is. We want to know why a person decided to do something. This helps us understand the world better.
