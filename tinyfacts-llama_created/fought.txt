To fight means to use your body to try to stop something or someone. You might use your arms, legs, and head to push or pull against them.

Imagine a dog that is very mean. It wants to eat a person. The person will not let the dog have them. The person might use a long stick. They will try to hit the dog on its back. The dog might try to bite. The person will try to stop the dog.

A person might also fight for their life. If a person is in a place where they are being hurt, they will try to fight against the people who are doing it. They might use their hands and feet to hit the people. They might also use things they have, like a rock or a stick, to try to stop them.

You can also fight for something you want. If you want to play a game with your friends, you might fight for the game. You will try to get the game and the most points so you are the best.

Sometimes, fighting can mean being very angry. If you are angry at someone, you might fight. You might shout and use your words. You might also use your body. Your face might be red and your eyes might be wide. You might want to hit the person.

To fight is not always easy. Sometimes it hurts. You might get a cut or a brush. You might feel very tired. But if you fight for what you want or for your life, you are doing something important.
