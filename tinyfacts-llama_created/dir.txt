The word directions is a word we use to talk about how people and things move. When you are walking, you use your feet to push the ground. The ground pushes back on your feet. This is called pulling. You are pulling the ground along with you.

Think of it like this. Imagine a long line of people all walking in the same way. They all pull on the ground at the same time. If one person pulls harder than the others, the line of people will go faster. If many people pull together, the line will go faster than if only one person pulled.

A long time ago, people used this word to talk about how things move around a big round thing. The big round thing pulls on everything that is inside it. The thing inside the round thing pulls on the air, and the air pulls on the ground, and the ground pulls on the air and the ground again. The whole thing is pulling on everything.

Today, we still use this word. We use it to talk about how people and things move around the world. We say, the directions are going here, or the directions are going there. It helps us know where we are and where we are going.

Sometimes we also use it to talk about things that are not moving. If you are looking at a picture, the picture is pulling on your eyes. The eyes are pulling on the picture because the picture is there. The picture is not moving, but it is pulling on the eyes.

So, to summer, directions are about how people and things move around the world. They tell us which way to go and how fast to go.
