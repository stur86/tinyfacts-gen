To use a thing is to make it work. You might use a thing that lets you open doors. You press a small round thing on it. This makes the door open.

You can also use a thing that lets you change what you wear. If you wear a shirt, the thing on your chest is the one that makes the shirt look right. If you use the thing on your chest, the shirt is changed.

A thing that lets you do this is called a thing with many parts. It has many small pieces inside it. When you press the right part, the thing makes something happen. You can use many of these things to make many different things.

In a house, people use these things to dress and to keep things that break. The thing on your chest helps you wear a shirt. On a desk, you use a thing to write with a piece of paper. On a wall, people use things to hang pictures or write words.

When you wear a thing, you are wearing a part of the person. This is why you feel something when you touch something new. You are also feeling the way the thing looks and feels.
