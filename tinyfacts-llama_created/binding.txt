To be a good thing often means to be a part of something. One thing is the start of it. Another thing is the end of it. The middle part is the middle. It is the thing that holds all of them together.

A person who is a good thing is one who can hold on. They hold their family, their friends, and their life together. They hold their job, their money, and their home. They hold their heart and their head. They keep everything safe.

A thing that is a good thing is not always easy. Sometimes it is hard to hold. Sometimes it is hard to keep. But in the end, it is always good. It is a thing that helps us live our lives.
