A group of people who help decide on a matter is called a group. They are usually made of several different people. They talk and listen to each other. They look at the facts and the ideas of everyone. They try to find the best answer.

One person might be the head of the group. They tell everyone what to do. Another person might be very good at making plans. Another person might be good at thinking about money. Everyone works together and follows the plan.

Sometimes, a group decides on a big problem like what to do with a city. They work on the plan for many weeks. They talk to city people, look at the money, and think about how to make the city better. The group might decide to build new streets, fix old roads, or make more parks. When the group decides, everyone follows the plan.

Sometimes, a group might be made of people who are all about one thing. They talk about this one thing for a long time. They learn a lot. They might find a new way to do something. They share their ideas with the world.

A group can also be made of people from different places. They talk about things that are important for everyone. They learn from each other. They help find a way that is good for everyone.

If you want to decide on a game, you might make a group of people. They watch the game and talk about it. They try to decide who was the best player. They might decide to play the game again with the same team. They want everyone to be happy.
