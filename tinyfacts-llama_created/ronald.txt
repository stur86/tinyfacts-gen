A person who works at a big place that makes things. They go to that place every day and do things for many people. They often wear a hard hated thing and a shirt with a number on it. They carry a long bag with food and things they need.

They talk to many people and answer questions. They help people buy things they want. They also help people if they are in a bad place or if they need money to buy food or a bed.

Sometimes they go on a bus or a car to get to their work. They meet other people on the way. They talk to them and learn about them. They might have a friend at work.

When the day is over, they go home. They might eat food they brought with them. They watch a movie or a game. They relax and do things to feel happy.

People need these people to make life better. They help us buy things we want, they help us feel safe, and they help us feel less alone.
