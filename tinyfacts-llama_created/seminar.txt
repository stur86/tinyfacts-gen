A small group of people who talk and learn about a big idea. They might talk for many hours or only for a short time. They often sit in a room together, and they listen to each other. Sometimes they write things down so they do not forget.

People come to a big group like this to learn more about a thing. They might be trying to understand how something works. They might want to see if other people have the same idea. They might also want to find out if they are the only ones who think this way.

One person might talk about a big problem. Another person listens and asks questions. Another person might say, what I think. This makes everyone feel they are part of a group.

The person who leads the group is like a big leader. They decide what the group should talk about and how they should act. They might also give orders if someone does not follow the group.

Sometimes the group is very small, just two or three people. Other times it is many more. They might sit in a room with many chairs. They might also sit on the floor if they are in a place with a lot of space.

When someone is part of this group, they are learning from each other. They are learning from the other person who is talking. They are also learning from the group as a whole. They are learning to understand how things work. They are learning to be better at thinking and talking.
