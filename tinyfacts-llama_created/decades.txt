To go through a long time is to move through many years. You are going through one year, then another, and another after that. A long time is a big number of years. It is many years that are in front of you.

Think about how you grow. When you are a little child, you are  two years old. Then you are three years old. You are four years old now. This is like one year. But when you are a big kid, you are ten years old. You have been going through many years. You have seen many things and learned many things.

Decides is when you pick what to do. You do not just go with what is given to you. You think about what is best for you. You look at what your friends do, what you read, and what you see. You decide which way to go. This is important because it helps you make your own way in life.

There are many years in a hundred. You could say a hundred years is one hundred years, two hundred years, three hundred years, or more. This is because a hundred is a big number of years. It is many years that are in front of you.

Years change us. When you are young, you are full of hope. You believe that things will get better soon. When you are old, you may feel tired and sad. You may remember things you want to forget. But every year brings a new chance to learn and to grow.
