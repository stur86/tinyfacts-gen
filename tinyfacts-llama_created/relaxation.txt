To relax is to let go of your worries and your body feels very calm. When you are relaxed, you do not feel like you are in a fight. Your body stops moving a lot and your mind is quiet. You might feel happy or you might feel very tired. You are not thinking about anything at all. You are just sitting or lying down, and your body feels very soft.

Imagine you have a big bag of heavy things. When you carry that bag around, your back hurts. If you let the bag go, the bag falls down and the back feels much better. Relaxing is like letting go of that heavy bag. Your body and your mind get a break.

Sometimes, you need to relax because your body is hurt. If you are very tired, you might feel a lot of pain in your head or in your body. If you have a big worry, it can make you very nervous. Relaxing helps your body and mind feel better when you are feeling very bad.
