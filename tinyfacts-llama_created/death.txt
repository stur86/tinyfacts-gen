Death is when a person or animal is gone. It is the end of life. Everything that was once living and moving and thinking is now not there anymore.

Think of it like a big stop sign. When you cross the stop sign, you stop moving. If you do not stop, you hit the stop sign and it stops you. This is what death does to a person. Their body stops moving and their heart stops beating.

Death can happen many ways. Sometimes it is very sudden. You might die from a cut that is deep and not going away. Other times, it happens slowly. You are very tired and your body gets very small. Your heart stops, and then you do not breathe anymore.

Death is a part of life. It happens to everyone. We are all going to die one day. But it does not mean that we have to be afraid. We can learn to live in the moment and enjoy the time we have with our family and friends. We can also try to learn about the world and how it works.

Some people do not like to talk about death. They might say, I do not want to think about it. But it is always there. It is always there, waiting for us all.
