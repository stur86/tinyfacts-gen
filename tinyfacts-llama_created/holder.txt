A holder is something you keep things on. You put things in a box and then you hold the box so you can see them or take them out later.

Imagine you have a cup of water. If you put it down and you go to the kitchen, you might drop it on the floor. You would feel sad because you lost your water. But if you keep the cup in your hand, you can still drink it later. You are holding the cup.

You can hold many things. You hold your phone when you are playing a game. You hold your book when you want to read it. You hold your money if you do not have it in your pocket.

A holder can be a person too. A person who holds a job is a holder. They keep people who work for them doing their jobs. A person who holds a gun is not a good person. They use it to hurt people.

We use the word to talk about these things. If you hold something, you are keeping it or you are being safe with it. If you do not hold something, it might fall and cause trouble.

So, a holder is a thing you keep on, and it helps you when you need it.
