A manager is a person who helps other people work. They make sure everything runs well. They plan how to do things and make sure people know what to do.

Imagine you have a big house with many rooms. You need to keep everything clean and safe. You are the manager. You decide who does what and when. You also listen to what people say and make sure they are happy.

A manager might be in a store. They make sure the money is good and that everything is right. They talk to people who work there and make sure they are doing their jobs well. They also help the store owner make big plans.

A manager might also be in a school. They help teachers and students learn. They plan how classes work and make sure everyone is following the right way.

In a small group, a manager might be the one who decides what to do next. They talk to everyone and make sure everyone is on the same team.

A manager is important because they help people do their jobs. They make sure things get done and people are happy. They are like the head of a team.
