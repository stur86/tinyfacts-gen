To cut something means to make a small round piece from it. You can use a round thing to cut through a paper or a hard thing that you do not want. You might use the round thing to cut a line in a book or a picture.

You can also cut something by pulling it. If you have a soft thing that you can hold, you can pull it to make it smaller. This is how you can make a small ball from a big ball that is hard to hold.

People can also cut words or lines from a story. This helps them to understand the story better. It is like taking a few words from a long story and putting them together again so you can see the rest of it.

Sometimes, we cut things that are not good. If a picture is very bad, you might cut it into smaller pieces so you can put them back together and make a new picture. This is how you can make a new picture from old ones.

We also cut food. If you eat a big piece of food, you might cut it into smaller pieces. This helps your body use the food quickly. It is like cutting a big thing into many small pieces so you can eat it fast.
