A person who does things for a person is called a friend. This person is very nice and helps the other person feel better. They do not make the other person do what they want, but they help them feel safe.

A person who does things for a city is called a person who helps keep the city clean. They clean the streets and make sure the water is clear. They also fix the roads so people can get where they need to go.
