A promise is when you say you are going to do something. It is a way to keep your word. If you promise to help someone, you must follow through. You must do what you said. It is a way to show you are a good person.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You say, I will catch the ball if you throw it to me. You do it. You never say you will not catch it. You followed your word. You are keeping your promise. You are doing what you said you would do.

You can also promise to be a good friend. You promise to listen to them when they talk. You promise to help them when they need it. You follow through on this promise.

Sometimes, you might promise to do something in the morning that you do not do. You say you will be up before the sun. You do not wake up. You feel bad because you did not follow through. You have broken your promise.

A promise is like a sign that you are a person. It shows that you are someone you can be trusted.
