To join something means to put it together with other things. When you join a piece of wood to a table, you add it to the table. The wood becomes a part of the table.

Imagine you are building a house. You start with the ground. You put the floor on the ground. Then you put the walls on top of the floor. You put the walls on top of the ground. This is like joining many parts together.

We also join people. When two friends become friends, they start to talk. They share stories about their lives. They learn about each other. This makes them better friends. They now have something in their hearts.

In a book, you join words to make a story. The story is like a long line of words. You start with the first word. Then you join word after word. When you finish, you have a story that you can remember.

In a big group, you join many people. You are all one group. You do the same things together. You work as one team. This makes you stronger. You can do more because you are all there.

When you join something, you are adding to it. You make it bigger. You help it become what it is supposed to be.
