To be a person is to be a living human being. It means you are not a thing. You are a living thing that can feel, think, and act.

A person needs air to breathe. They need to eat food to have the power to move, to see, and to think. They also need to sleep so their body can fix and feel better. They are made of tiny things that work together inside them.

People have their own ways of being. Some are very quiet and keep to themselves. Some are very loud and talk a lot. Some are very kind and help others. Some are very angry and fight. Everyone is different. You can have your own way of being too.

A person has their own life. This means they live in one place. They have a home where they sleep and a job where they do work. They have friends and family people they love. They have their own stories to tell.

Being a person is important. It is what makes us human. Without people, there would be no love, no work, no learning, and no fun. We need people to be with so we can feel happy and strong.
