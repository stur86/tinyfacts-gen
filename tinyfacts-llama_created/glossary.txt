A word is a thing that is used to talk about other things. When you learn many new words, you can use them to talk about your life or what you know. This helps people understand each other.

Sometimes, you might see a book with a big listen box. This box helps you find the word you need. You type the word and it shows you the answer. This is like a big word listen box.

There are also boxes with words on them. These boxes are for people who are not good at speaking. They give the words to people who want to learn. This helps everyone learn.
