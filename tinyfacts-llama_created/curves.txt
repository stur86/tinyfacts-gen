A line that goes around a round thing is called a round thing. It does not end. It goes on for a long time. You can follow it with your eyes, and it will follow you.

A line that goes in a way that does not follow the same line is called a round thing. It might go up and down, or it might go across the top and down the bottom. This kind of line is more interesting than the round thing line.

A line that goes in a way that is much better than the round thing line is called a straight line. It follows one line, one after another, without going around anything. You can walk on a straight line without ever looking at a street sign.

Sometimes we use the word to talk about how things look. If something is very round and very nice, we say it is a round thing. If something is very straight and very good, we say it is a straight line. If something is not round and not straight, we say it is something else.
