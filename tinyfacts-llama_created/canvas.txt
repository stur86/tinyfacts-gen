To put something on a thing means to make it look nice. You use this word to talk about what people make with a big piece of paper.

A person might put paint on the paper. They use a small thing to make lines and colors. This makes the paper look bright and pretty. They might also put words on the paper. This is for writing stories or pictures.

We also use this word to talk about things that are made of many small pieces. When you cut a thing into many pieces, you are putting them on a big piece of paper. This makes the thing look nice and new.

Sometimes, people put things on a thing for fun. They might put small things they found on a rock or a tree. They put these small things on a big piece of paper so everyone can see them.

You can also put things on a thing for a game. One person puts their name on the paper. The other person does something and the person who did it is the first one to write their name. The person who writes the name has to say they did it first. This game is fun because everyone wants to be the first one to do it.
