When you are a kid, you get things from your mother and father. These are the things you are told you should have. You do not get them because you do something. You get them because your parents told you so.

If your mother is very tall, she will always be tall. If your father is very strong, you will always be strong. This is called being a part of your family. It is like a piece of paper that says you have this. You can not get it from the paper. You can only tell the world what you are.

Sometimes, you get things because you do something. If you are very good at school, your teacher will give you a big box. This is like a big hug from the teacher. You did not get the box because you are good. You did not get it because the teacher said so.

You also get things because of where you live. If you live in a cold place, you will be very warm. If you live in a hot place, you will be very hot. This is like a way to say, you live in this place and you have this.

You can also get things that are not about you. A dog is an animal that is not a person. A tree is a thing that grows in the ground. A car is a thing that you use to go. These things are not because of you. They are because of where the land is, and what the people did with them.
