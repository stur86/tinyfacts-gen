To be in a safe place means you are not alone and there is no one who can hurt you. A place is a big area with lines that stop people from going there. You might be in a house, a school, a park, or a city. You are safe because the lines keep you inside.

When you are in a safe place, you can feel calm. You do not have to worry about being hurt. It is a good feeling to know you are safe. You can play, eat, read books, or just sit and listen to music without fear.

You might also say that someone is in a safe place. If a child is in a school, their parents are in a safe place too. They are not alone and there are other people around them who care about them. If a person is in a safe place, they feel happy and safe.
