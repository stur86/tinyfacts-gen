To really something means you believe it is true. When you say something is really true, you are sure. It is not just what you think.

When you are really angry, your face is red. Your voice is loud. You want to be the one who said it. You are not just saying it, you are sure it is true.

You can also really a thing that is hard. If a book is really hard to read, you are not just trying. You really care about the words. You feel that the words are important.

If you really love someone, you care a lot. You want to be near them. You feel happy when they are with you. You feel sad if they are not there.

Sometimes, we say a thing is really. We might say a book is really good. Or we might say a food is really good to eat. This means the thing is very good. It is not just good, it is really good.

So, to really something is to be sure it is true. It is to believe it is so.
