To form something means to make it. You can form a word, a face, a thing, or a story.

Imagine you want to build a house. You start with nothing. You make a plan. You take wood and make it into the rooms you need. You use the wood to build walls. You add floors. You add a door. You do this many times until the house is strong and has rooms inside.

You can also form a face. If you look at a picture, you see a face. This face is made of many lines and lines that go around your eyes. These lines help you know who you are. You can feel the lines under your skin. You can see the lines on a face that you were not able to see before.

Sometimes, you form a story. A story is when people talk about things that happened. You can form a story by thinking about what you know and what you want to say. You might start with a problem. You might tell how you tried to fix it. You might show what happened next. You might end with a way to fix the problem again.

To form a sound is another way to do this. When you make a sound, you use your body. You open your mouth. You push air out. You use your teeth to make a high sound. You use your hands to make a low sound. You can also form sounds by moving your arms and legs. You can make a sound by pushing air through a small opening. You can make a sound by making a soft or a loud sound with your mouth.
