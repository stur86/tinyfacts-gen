A thing that is a number is called a number. Numbers can be very big, like  ten or a hundred. You can also have many of them, like ten hundreds, ten hundreds of hundreds, or ten hundreds of hundreds of hundreds. Each number has its own meaning.

When you have a number of things, you say you have a lot of them. You might say you have ten books. You are saying that you have ten things.

A person who has many years is called an old person. An old person is very old. This happens because people live for many years. Some people live for a hundred years or more. When you say a person is old, you mean they are very, very old.

A group of people who go to one place is called a group. This is often for work, for school, or for fun. You might go to a school in a city or to a city to play games.

A thing that happens because of many people is called a crowd. When a lot of people are together, it becomes a very big group. There are many people in one spot. This is what happens when many people go to the same place at the same time.

A thing that happens because of one person is called an eventually. This means that something happens later, after you think it is going to happen now.

A very high number is called a huge number. This number is so big that you can not even write it down with a short set of numbers. It is like the number of people in the whole world.

A thing that happens because of many people is called a thing that happens often. This means that the same thing happens many times. Many times a month, many times a year, or many times a year for many years, the same thing happens.

When you say something happens often, you are telling the truth. You are not just saying it happens sometimes.
