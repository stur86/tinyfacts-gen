To put something on something else is to make it look or feel a different way. You might put on a shirt so you do not get wet when you go to the park. Or you might put on a hair color so your hair looks like the color of the sun.

When you do this, you are changing the thing you are on. The shirt is not still the same as the water on it. It is now different because of what happened inside the shirt. The hair color is also different because of the color you put on it.

Sometimes, putting something on someone can make them feel better. If you put a big hug on a person who is sad, their face might get a little bit brighter. This is because the person is feeling their arms around them, and this makes them feel safe and happy.

You can also put something on a thing to make it work better. If you put a small piece of paper on a computer, it might do its job much better. The paper helps the computer remember the numbers and words that make the computer work.

Putting something on something else is an important thing to do. It can change how we feel and how things work.
