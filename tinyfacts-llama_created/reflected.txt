When you look in a glass, you see your face. Your eyes are the big parts in the front. Your hair is the long part behind them. The glass makes your face look good. It shows you how you look.

But when you look at something, it is not you. The sun is the thing that makes things bright. The air is the thing that makes everything move. When you look at the sun, you see light from it. You see a bright white color. You see a bright blue color. You see a bright red color. The light from the sun is not your face.

You can also see what is behind you. If you turn your head, you see the floor. If you turn your head again, you see the wall. You see the things that are in front of you. The light from the world goes through you and comes out the back of you.

When things are moved, they change how they look. If you roll a ball, it looks like a round thing. If you shake it, it looks like a long thing. When you look at a ball, you see a different thing than you did before. You see the round part. You see the long part. You see the hard parts. You see the soft parts.

When you look at a person, you see their face. You see their eyes. You see their hair. You see their teeth. You see their skin. You see how they look. The person is not the same when you look at them. They look different.

Sometimes we look at things that are not really there. We look at a picture of a house. We see the walls. We see the floor. We see the window. We see the door. We see the front of the house. But the house is not really there. It is just a picture. We are still in our world.
