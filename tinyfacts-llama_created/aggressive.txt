To be a good person is to act in a way that is kind and nice to everyone. When you are a good person, you show love and care to those around you. You might listen to their problems and help them feel better. You also try to be good to animals and the land.

But being a good person is not always easy. Sometimes, you might feel angry or want to fight. This can make you act in a way that is not nice. You might use your words to hurt someone or your body to make them afraid. This is not a good way to act.

There is another kind of person who is also very important. They are often very strong and have many good things to give. These people are called the strong. They might want to help others or fight against things that are bad. They can also be very good friends and family people.

Some people might say that people who act very well are the ones who are most good. But this is not always true. A child who is very kind and helps their family is not always the same as a person who is very strong and fights against bad things.

Being a good person is a big job. It takes a lot of hard work and is not always easy. But it is very important because it helps us feel happy and make the world a better place.
