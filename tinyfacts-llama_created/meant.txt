To mean something is to show you understand what it is about. When you say you mean something, you are telling the world how you feel. You are not just talking the words.

If you say you love a person, you mean you really care about them. You like the way they make you feel. You want to be with them. You want to be close.

If you are afraid of a thing, you mean you are worried about it. You think something bad is going to happen. You feel a bit of fear. This shows you are not ready to face the thing alone.

If you say you are very happy, you mean you are very glad. You feel great. You are smiling and you want to show everyone how happy you are. You might jump up and shout.

Sometimes, you might mean something different. You might say you are going to the store. But when you get there, you find out you forgot your money. You feel a bit of anger. You do not mean you are going to buy food. You mean you are going because you need to.

It is also important to know when to use these words. If you want to say you love someone, you should use the word love. If you say you are going to do something, you should use the word going. If you say you mean something, you should use the word mean.

In short, to mean something is to show you understand what it is about.
