A rider is a person who sits on a horse or a fast moving animal. They hold the animal and tell it where to go. The rider wears a big thing on their head to stay safe. They also wear clothes to keep warm and have a bag with food and other things they need.

Riding can be fun. A person might ride a horse to a far place. They might ride a car if the road is very long. Some people ride to go to school. They might ride a car with other people.

A person who works in a big city might ride a car to go to work. They might ride a big bus with many other people. They stay in the car and talk to their friends.

Riding can also mean you are on a way. You might ride your own horse to go to a place you like. You might ride a car with your family to go to a new home.
