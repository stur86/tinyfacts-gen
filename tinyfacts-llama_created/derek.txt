Darker is a word that means something is not bright or light anymore. It is about how things look in the dark.

Imagine you are in a room with many walls. When the sun is high, the wall is bright and white. But as the sun goes down and the room gets dark, the wall becomes black and dark. This is what darker means.

You can also use it to talk about time. If you are in the dark, you do not see. It is like being in a place where you do not know where you are or what is happening. Your body feels strange, and you might not remember what day it is.

Sometimes, you might feel a little bit darker inside. This is when you are worried about something. It can make your heart feel heavy and you might want to hide. But it is also a sign that something is about to happen.

In a story, a person might have a darker side. This means they have a part of them that is not good. It is like having a dark eye. This can make them act in ways that are not nice. But it does not mean they are always bad.

Also, in a game, you might have to play in the dark. This is when you do not see the things around you. You must use your other senses to find your way. It is a great way to learn about how your body works when it is quiet.

In short, darker is about how things look when the light is gone. It can be about time, a feeling, a person, or a game.
