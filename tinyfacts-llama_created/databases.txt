Dinners and Dances

The word you are looking for is a type of computer thing. It is a big box with many small boxes inside. Each small box holds a very many numbers and words.

Imagine you have a room with many people. Each person has a thing they like to do. You do not keep every thing on your desk. You put them away in a box. The box is like the computer. You can look at the box and see what the people like.

You can add more boxes to the box. You can add more people who like the same things. This makes the box very big and helps everyone remember what they like.

You can also put many different kinds of things in one box. If you have books and papers and numbers, you can keep them all in the same box. This makes it much easier to find what you need.

People use these boxes all day. They look at them to remember things, to find answers, and to share ideas with each other.
