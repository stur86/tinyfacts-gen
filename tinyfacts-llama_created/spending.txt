To spend something means to use it for a time. You do this with money, with food, with time, or with things you own. When you spend, you are not keeping the thing for yourself. You give it to someone else.

Think about money. If you have a lot of money, you can buy many things. You can buy food for your family, clothes for yourself, or a new car. But if you do not use the money for a time, it goes away. It is gone and you have nothing left.

You can also spend time. You spend time with friends, watching a movie, or playing a game. This is when you use the time of another person. You do not own the time, but you use it for a time.

Sometimes, you spend money to buy things you want. You might buy a new book or a computer. You are using money to get a thing that will help you. This is spending.

You can also spend your own life. You spend your time doing things you love. You might play a game, read a book, or work on a job. This is when you use your life for a time.

To spend is to use. You do not keep the thing. You use it for another person or for a time.
