To carefully means you do not do things in a way that can cause trouble. You try to be sure everything is right before you do anything. You do not rush. You think about what you are doing and make sure you are doing it the right way.

Imagine you want to eat a thing. You want to eat it slowly so you do not make a big face. You do not jump up and down. You take your time. You look at the food and see if it is safe. You put it in your mouth. You drink it slowly. This is carefully eating.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are not things. If you say a word is carefully, it means you are afraid of it. You do not want to hear it. You want to hide. You might be afraid of a loud noise or a cold room.

In a big group, you might say a person did something carefully. This means the person did it with a lot of care. They did not make a big move. They did not say anything bad. They did what they thought was right.

You can also say a thing is carefully made. This means the thing was made with a lot of care. People who make things try to do it right. They look at the parts. They put them together. They make sure everything is strong. They do not want the thing to break easily. They want the thing to last a long time.
