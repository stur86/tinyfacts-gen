To start something is to begin it. You can start a new day, a new book, or a new game. When you start something, you are the first one to do it. You take the first step. This is an important thing to do because it helps the thing grow.

A person who starts a thing often wants to see it happen. They think the best way is. They think, plan, and decide. They might have a good idea. They might have a plan, or they might just start.

Sometimes, one person starts a group. They are the first person. They make the first idea. They ask others to join. This is a good start because it helps many people work together.

Sometimes, you start a thing by your own. You think, you feel, and you act. You might want to learn something new. You might want to help others. This is also an important start. It shows that you are ready to do something.

To start something is to take the first step. It is the beginning of a new thing.
