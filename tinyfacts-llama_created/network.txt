A group of computers that work together is called a computer. These computers can do many different jobs at once.

When many computers are part of a group, they can share things. This is how they share pictures, words, and music. This is also how they help each other when they are busy. If one computer needs to find a word, another computer can look it up for it.

There are many different kinds of groups like this. Some are big and have many computers. Other groups are small and only have two computers. Some groups have many people who work there. Other groups have only one person who makes the computer do the job.

When computers talk to each other, they use numbers. These numbers are like a set of letters. When two computers want to talk, they send the numbers to each other. The computers then use those numbers to understand what the other computer needs.

This kind of group helps people work together. If many doctors want to talk to each other about a person who is very sick, they can use this group. They can see the numbers and find the right person.

This kind of group is also important for people who work in the world. If many people want to send money to someone who is far away, they can use this group. The computers help the money get where it needs to go.

So, a group of computers is like a big team of many computers that work together to do one thing.
