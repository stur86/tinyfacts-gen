To ask a person a question is to ask them something. You might ask a friend, a teacher, or even your family. You want to know if they know something. You might want to see if they are good at a thing.

A group of people might do this. They are called a group. They sit in a room and talk about something. One person asks another person a question. The other person must answer. If they say something is true, the person who asked them gets a point.

Some people do this for fun. They might do it in a school. They ask teachers questions. Some people do it to see if they are good at a thing. They are trying to see if they know something.

You can also ask a group of people a question. This is when you ask many people at once. You do not ask one person one time. You ask many people at once.

Sometimes, you see a person who has been asked many questions. They look tired. They want to stop. If you ask them a question, they must answer. They must say what they think. This is how we learn.
