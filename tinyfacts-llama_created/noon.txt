No one knows the answer to this question. People see the sun come up. Some say it is morning. Others say it is afternoon. A few say it is night. But there is one thing we all agree on. Every day, the sun is in the sky at the same time.

The time before the sun is up is the morning. The time after the sun is up is the afternoon. The time just before the sun comes up and the time just after the sun goes down is the night. This is what we call the time between the day and the night. It is the time when the world is quiet.
