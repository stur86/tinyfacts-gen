When you use a bathroom, you go to the room where water goes away. This room is under the floor of the house. In this room, there are moving things that take water from all the rooms above and put it into a bigger water way that goes far away.

Inside the room, there is a long piece of hard stuff. When you go to the bathroom, water from your body goes into the room. The hard stuff catches the water and makes it go down into the bigger water way. This water then goes into the big water way and goes out of the house into the world.

There is also a piece of hard stuff under the floor. When water comes in, it goes into this piece of hard stuff. It gets heavier and heavier until it comes out of the top. This is why the top of the house is always wet. The water comes up through the floor because of the moving things in the room.

Sometimes, the hard stuff under the floor gets very heavy. This makes the ground around the house shake. This is why houses in the city can shake when heavy rain comes down. The hard stuff under the floor is like a big bag of water. When the bag gets too full, the house shakes and the water comes out. Then the water goes away, and the bag is empty again.

The room under the floor is very important. It keeps the water from coming up into the rooms above. It keeps the water from going back into the house. Without this room, there would be no water in the rooms above.
