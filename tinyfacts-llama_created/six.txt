To have six means you have six things. If you have one thing, you have five. If you have two things, you have three. If you have three things, you have four. If you have four things, you have five. When you have five things, you are almost done. If you have six things, you have all of them.

Sometimes, we counters say six and they mean the number six. We use these numbers to help us understand things. The sun is six days long. A day is six hours long, and six hours are six minutes long. It is very long.

You can also have six friends. You are six years old. You have six teeth. You have six legs. You have six arms. You have six eyes.

If you have six of something, you can have more. If you have six of a thing you do not like, you might want to do something else. If you have six of a thing you love, you feel very happy.
