To hold something is to keep it. You can hold a small thing in your hand. A table holds many things. A room holds people. A house holds many people.

A person who holds something is called a person who holds. In a home, a person holds a child. In a school, a teacher holds a book. In a big group, a person holds a place so everyone knows where they are.

You can also hold a group of people. When many friends or people come together, it is called a group. This can happen at a school, a work, or a big place in town. They all hold the same idea.

Sometimes, a person holds a job. They work for a place and do what the place needs. They might work in a store, a kitchen, or a place where people live. This is a job that keeps things moving.

In a game, a person holds a ball. They try to move it from one place to another. They use their hands and feet. The person who holds the ball is the one who gets to move it the farthest.
