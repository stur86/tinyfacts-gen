To have an end means to stop a thing. If you throw a ball, it goes across the ground. After a short time, the ball stops on the ground. You have ended the game.

When you have an end, you can do something. You might get a job, or you might go to school. You might meet a friend, or you might see a movie. After the end, you start something new.

If you have many ends, it means you have many things that stop. You have ended a long walk, and you are now sitting on the floor. You have ended a hard day at school, and you are going to bed.

We can also use this word for people. If a person has ended a fight with another person, they have ended the fight. If a person has ended a game with another person, they have ended the game.

To have a good end is to have a happy ending. If you finish a book, you have ended the story. If you do a job and it is done well, you have ended the job.

To end something also means to stop it from happening again. If you know that something will happen, but it does not happen, the thing has ended. If you see a bad movie, you have ended the movie.
