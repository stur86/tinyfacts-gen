To give something as a way to get more of a thing you already have. When you work hard, you might get a job. Later, you work for another big group. You do very well and are very good at your job. Your work is noticed by the group head. He is very happy with you. Because of you, the group head wants to give you more work or money. This is a kind of way to get more of what you already have, so you can have more things.

You can also give something to people. If a store has a big box of things, it might have many more boxes. The store tells the people who work there that they have to work more. The people who work more have to pick more boxes. This makes the store more money and more people happy.
