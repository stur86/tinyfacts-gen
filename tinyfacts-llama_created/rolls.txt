To roll something means you move it around in a round way. Imagine you are a ball. You start to move and go around in a big round. The ground is under you. You use your hands to push and pull the air with you. This makes the ball move.

A ball can roll down a long walk or a hard road. You might see many balls rolling down the road. They go very fast. They go so fast they do not look like they are going at all.

You can also roll a small thing. A small ball with a round top might roll down a table. It moves very fast and goes very high. People watch it to see how high it goes.

Sometimes, you can roll something that is not a ball. You can roll a big box. You push it across the floor. It moves in a round way and goes very fast. This is fun to watch.

To roll something also means you make it move in a way that is not the same. You can roll a hair. You rub the hair with your fingers. This makes the hair move. You can also roll a thing that is heavy. You push it and it goes round. This makes the heavy thing go in a different way than it was before.
