To be done with something means you have finished it. A person can do this in many different ways. They might read a book. They might go to a movie. They might play a game.

If you have a problem, you might try to fix it. You might look for help from a friend. You might do some work. When you do this and you feel better, you have done with the problem.

Sometimes you give someone a thing. You might give them a book you read. You might give them a food you made. You give them a small thing to show you care. This is done with love and with the hope that they will be happy.

You can also say that someone has done something. If a person does something very hard, we say they have done it. We might use this word when we see a man driving a car. He has done it. He can drive the car very well.

Being done with something is important. It helps us feel safe and calm. It is a way to say we are finished with a thing.
