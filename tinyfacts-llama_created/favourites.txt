To have a favorite is to have one thing you like more than others. It is about which thing you like better than all the rest.

You might have a favorite food. You like eating that food more than any other kind of food. You might have a favorite book, movie, or game. You like watching these things more than any other kind of show.

Sometimes, having a favorite is about the person. You like the person best. You want to talk to them, laugh with them, or feel happy when they are around. You might even feel sad if they leave or if you do not see them.

Being a favorite can make you feel very happy. You might feel like you are the best thing. It is a nice feeling to know that you are liked by many people. It is also important to remember that your favorite thing does not make you better than anyone else. Everyone has their own favorite thing.
