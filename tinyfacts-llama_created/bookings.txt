To book something means you use a place to stay while you are there. You pay a person for this room. You often use this word for places in a building, like a store or a movie house. You might book a table to eat or a chair to sit on.

You can also book a car. If you want to go to a city far away, you might book a car. You pay for the car so you can use it for the day. This way, you know you will have a car ready when you need it.

Sometimes, when you book something, you are making a promise. You say you will do it. You show that you are ready. This helps you feel safe and sure.

In some places, you can book a room for a night. You pay to be there. This is often better than staying in a house. If you are worried about money, you can book a room so you do not have to worry about where you will sleep.
