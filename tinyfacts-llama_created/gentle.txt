To be kind and to be not too strong is to be a good person. When you are kind, you show that you care about other people. You might smile at someone who looks sad. You might say a word of hope. You make them feel better.

Being a little bit strong is also important. It means you can do things that are hard. You might be the one who helps a friend who is afraid. You might be the one who helps a baby who is crying. If you are very strong, you can help a lot of people at once.

Sometimes, being too strong can hurt others. You might yell at someone. You might use a word that is bad for your body. You might make someone feel afraid. This is not a kind thing to do. It is a way to show power.

But there is another kind of strong. This kind of strong is about being calm in a hard time. You are not afraid. You stay quiet and you wait. You let the thing happen. Then you are ready to help.
