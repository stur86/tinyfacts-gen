To do something can mean two different things.

One way is when you do a thing. If you want to go to the store, you can walk. You walk to get the things you need. You follow a road or a way that is easy to use. You do not have to think about what to do. You just follow. You are doing the thing in this moment.

Another way is when you do something and then you see what happens next. If you buy a thing you like, the store owner will tell you how much it is. You think this is a good idea. Then you buy it. After that, you have the thing and you can use it. You are doing the thing and then you see what happens next.

Sometimes, we say someone is doing something. If a teacher gives a lot of work to a student, the student is doing it. This means the student is the one who is doing the work. They are the one who is doing the thing.

We also use this word with numbers. If you have a number, you can see how it is. If you have  ten, you know you have ten things. If you have a number and you add one, you get another number. This number is what is happening. You are doing something with the number.

So, to do something is to move your body or your mind. It is to act or think in a way that changes something. You can do this on your own or you can ask a friend to help you.
