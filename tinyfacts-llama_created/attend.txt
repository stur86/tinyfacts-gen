To be an eventually is to be ready to go. It is to be ready for a new day, a new time, or a new place. You might feel this way before going to a party, a school, or a place you do not know. You are not sure what will happen but you are ready.

Think about your life. You might be waiting for school to start. You are usually not excited. But then you go to school. You meet new people. You learn new things. You feel the feeling of being ready. You have been an eventually.
