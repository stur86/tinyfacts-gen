When you are very good at something, you do it in a way that is easy on your body and brain. This makes things happen faster and with less work. You have a way of doing things that is like a well made road. Every step is easy and every turn is the right one.

Think of a computer. It is very good at doing many many things at once. It can read a lot of words and pictures very quickly. It can do this because it does not have to think about each step. It just follows the numbers. This is an important thing for us because it makes our lives easier.

You can also say a person is very good at a job. They know how to do it very well and do it in a way that is easy on their body. They do not get tired as much because they do not have to think about everything every time.

Sometimes, we talk about how good something is. We might say a movie is very good. This means it is very good at showing things. It shows us in a way that is easy to see. It does not make us feel very many wrong things. It just shows us the things in a way that is clear and easy.

An even better way to say it is that something is very easy to do. You do not have to think as much. You can just follow the easy steps. This makes your life better because you do not have to worry about things.
