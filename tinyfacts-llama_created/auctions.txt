An open house is a place where people go to see a house or a building. When you are a real person and you own a house, you might let people go inside. They look at the house, the rooms, and the floors. If you like the house, you can buy it. But if you do not like it, the house stays with the owner.

An open house is like a big show. Many people come to see the house. There are no doors closed. Everyone can walk around. People ask questions. They talk to the owner. They ask if the house is clean. They ask if there are any problems.

Sometimes, people buy things at an open house. They buy a book, a game, or a food. They pay money for the things. The owner of the house says, if you want to buy this, you must pay the money now.

Another way is to buy things at an open house. There is a lot of food. People walk in and pick what they want. They pay money for the food. The person who runs the house says, you have two minutes. You pick what you want, and you pay the money. You leave. The person then takes the food away and puts it into a bag.

In a big city, there are many houses. An open house is like a big meeting. People go there to see the houses. They look at the floors. They see if the house is clean. They talk to the owner. They ask questions. They try to buy the house.

Some houses have something very important. They have a very old book. A person might buy this book. The person puts the book into a bag and leaves. The owner of the house says, the book is yourself. The person must keep it safe.

In a big store, people go to an open house. They see many things. They see how they work. They see how the things are made. They ask questions. They try to buy things. They look for what they want. They talk to the people who work there. They ask if they can take something home.

In all these ways, an open house is a place where people go to see things. People see houses, books, food, and other things. They talk to the people who own them. They ask questions. They try to buy them.
