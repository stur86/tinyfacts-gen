An open space is when you can walk in any direction. It is not a line, but a place with no edges. Think of a tree with many arms. Each arm is a part of the space. There is a part on the left, a part on the right, a part in front, and a part behind. All these parts are together.

When you walk into an open space, you get new ideas. You see things you did not see before. You hear sounds you never heard before. You feel the sun on your face or the wind in your hair. You are not in one place only. You are in many places at once. You are not one person. You are many people.

You can be in an open space with your family. You can be in an open space with a friend. You can be in an open space with a teacher. Or you can be in an open space with a computer. You can use it to find new jobs, to learn new things, or to make new friends.

An open space is also about what you do with the time you have. You can spend it playing games, reading books, or learning. You can go to a school, to a movie, or to a store. You can talk to a friend, or you can talk to a computer. You can be alone and quiet, or you can be with many people. You can be happy, or you can be sad. Your time in the open space is what matters.

Sometimes an open space is in the middle of a big city. It is a place where people from many different places live. There are many different sounds, many different smells, and many different words. It is a place where you can see many different faces. It is a place where you can learn from others.

In a world with many open spaces, people help each other. They share what they have. They listen to what each other says. They try to understand. They try to see things from other people. They try to be kind and to be open.
