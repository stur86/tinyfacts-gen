To buy something is easy. You pay money and get a thing you want. This is how you buy food, a book, or a game.

To use a word is not as easy. You do not need money. You just sit and say a word. You tell others you like it. You are saying you want to be a part of a group.

When you buy something, you are buying a right. You are buying the thing for yourself. When you use a word, you are buying a space. You are buying a place to tell others you like a thing.

Sometimes people buy things they do not want. They buy clothes they do not like because they want to fit in with a group. They buy food they do not like because they want to eat something new. They buy a game because they think it will be fun.

People also buy things they need. They buy a phone when they need to stay in touch with friends. They buy a computer when they need to work. They buy food when they have money to buy food.

You can also buy a word. When you say a word, you are saying you have a right to be there. You are saying you want to talk. You are buying a space to tell others you like a thing.

Everyone buys a little bit every day. You buy your food. You buy your clothes. You buy your phone. You buy your ideas. You buy your time.
