To plan something means to think about what you want to do. It is about making a plan so you know what to do.

You can plan a trip to a city or a far away place. You think about where you will stay, where you will eat, and what you will do. You also think about how you will get there. You can use a computer, a book, or a phone to plan your trip.

You can also plan a day. You think about what you want to do and when you will do it. You might decide to go to a movie, play a game, or work. You can plan your day so you know what to do and when.

Planning is important. It helps you feel ready for what is coming. It helps you know what you want and how to get there.

Sometimes, planning is hard. You might have many things you want to do, but you do not know how to do them. In these cases, you might need to make a new plan. You might need to break down your big plans into smaller ones, or you might need to tell yourself to start small.

Planning is a way to be safe. It helps you know what is going on and what to do. It can help you feel in control of your life.
