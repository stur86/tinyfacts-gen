A worker is a person who does things for money. They might do work in a house, a school, a store, or a big building. They might be a man, a woman, or a child. Some people work in a house and take care of a family. Others work in a store and buy things for people to buy. Some work in a big building and fix things that break. Some work on a road and carry heavy things to other places.

When a person works, they often do things by hand. They might use a big round thing that moves, or a small thing that hits a ball. They might also use their feet to push a car. They work so they have money to buy food, a bed, and other things they need.

Some people also work for a group. They do the same work every day with many other people. They work together to make things for people to buy. The people in this group help each other and make sure everyone does their job.

Sometimes, a person works for a person who owns a house or a car. They might do work while the person is away. The person might pay them money and give them food and a place to live.

A worker is important. They help make things that people need. Without them, we would not have food, clothes, or a place to live. They are the people who work behind the front of a store or a house.
