To work is to do something. You do this for money or for fun. You might work in a school, a hospital, a store, or a house. Some people work in the morning when they are young and some work in the evening when they are old.

You do not just sit around. You get up and do a job. You might fix a car, write a story, paint a picture, or build a building. You use your hands, your brain, and your feet.

Sometimes you work alone. You work on your computer. Other times you work with many other people. You all work together to get a job done.

When you work, you feel a bit of fear. You are afraid that you might make a big thing wrong or that you will not do it right. But then you feel happy when you do it well. You feel good because you are helping others.

You can also work at your own home. You work while you watch your favorite show or while you wait for your food to come. You do this because it is your job and it pays.

To work is to grow. When you work, you become stronger and you learn new things. You get used to doing the same thing every day. But you also learn to be better because you face problems and you try to find a way to fix them.
