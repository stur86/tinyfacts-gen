To have something in line means you are doing the right thing. When you are in line with someone, you are following their steps. You do not do the same thing as they do. You are on the same side of the road.

Imagine you are walking through a park. If a tree is in front of you, and there is a sign that says do not go under it, you must walk around it. This is being in line with the sign.

If you are on a team and you have to run to a place on the other side of the field. If your friend is already there and you need to reach them, you must do what they do first. You follow their steps so you can all reach the place at the same time.

Sometimes, people use this word to talk about numbers. When you have two numbers that are the same, you can say you have two of something. This means you have the same thing in two different places. It does not mean you have the same thing in both places.

If you have two boxes that can hold the same kind of thing, you can put things in one box and then put them in the other box. In this case, you are in line with both boxes. You are following what they do.
