To be safer means you are not in a place that is bad for you. This is because a safe place is one where there are not many things you can do wrong.

Imagine you want to go to a big city at night. You might feel afraid. If you are in a safe place, you are safe because there are many people watching the street. If a bad person tries to do something bad, the people around you will stop them. You will feel safe.

You can also feel safer when you are in a place that has many strong walls around it. If a strong wall keeps out bad people, you feel safe inside. You can walk through the wall without worrying.

Sometimes, you might feel safer if you are in a place with a good police force. If the police are there, you know that they will help if something is wrong.

To be safer also means you are not afraid of things that make you feel scared. If you are afraid of a dog, you are not as afraid as you would be if you saw a very big dog. You can stand next to it and still feel a little scared. But if you are in a place with dogs that are not very big, you feel more safe because you do not have to worry about them.

Finally, being safer can mean you are also doing well in your life. If you are in a job you like, you feel safe. If you have money, you feel safe. When you are safe, you can be happy and not worry about the things that are wrong.
