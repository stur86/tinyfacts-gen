To use something is to use it. It is a way to make work easier. It is a computer, a car, a phone. It makes it possible to do many things that we could not do before.

When we talk about a long time from now, we say something is in the next big thing. This means it is going to change the way we live and the way we work. It is a new way to do things.

Some things are small. They are in our pockets. Other things are big. They are in houses. They make food and power. They help us go from one place to another quickly.

We use these things every day. We use them to talk, to look at pictures, to play games, to see people, and to send money. They help us stay in touch with people all over the world.
