To cause a lot of trouble or to break everything together can be very bad.

Imagine you are playing with a ball. If you hit it very fast and very high, it goes in a very straight line. It does not go around many times. This is kind of like a calm day. The world is quiet and there is no big fight.

But if you hit the ball so fast it goes around many times, it starts to roll down a long, high side. It turns very quickly and does not stop until it hits a wall. This is like a big fight. Many people are running and trying to get away from it. The ground is shaking and the air is filled with sound.

Sometimes this kind of trouble happens in the world. It can be because many people are angry at each other. Or it can be because there are many problems and no one knows what to do. In these cases, people may fight or try to hurt each other.

A third kind of trouble is when everything goes very wrong at once. A lot of things happen at the same time. A big fight starts on one side, a big fight starts on the other side, and then many other fights start all at once. It is very hard to keep track of everything and it is very hard to stay safe.

In a small group, people might have a lot of fun. They laugh and play together. But if one person does something bad, it can make the whole group feel bad. They might start to fight or get very quiet.

In a story, a person might cause a lot of trouble. They might take something that does not really go to them. They might hurt someone they love. This is a very bad thing to do.

Also, when many people are doing the same thing, it can cause a lot of trouble. They might follow the same idea for too long. This can make them do things they do not want to do. It can also cause them to fight with each other.

Sometimes, things that are very wrong can happen for a long time. People might be afraid to talk about them. They might keep the bad things hidden inside their hearts. But it is always better to talk about the trouble when it starts, rather than letting it go and things getting worse over time.
