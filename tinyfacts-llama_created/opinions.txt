A person has an idea about something.

When you are very sure about something, you might say,  This is the way I feel. This is how I think. Everyone has their own way of thinking about things.

You might not like a color. Your friend might love it. You might like the same color but you do not like it as much.

You also have your own idea about how a thing should be done. What you think is good or bad can change from day to day. Sometimes, you change your mind because you learn more about it.

When many people have different ideas, the world is a bit more interesting. This is why there are many different ways to do things. People can learn from each other.

It is also important to listen to what others have to say. If you hear someone tell you that their way is better, you might think,  I do not want to follow this. But you can also try to understand why they are doing it.

Everyone has their own way of thinking. This is why we are all different. It is what makes the world so fun to be in.
