To fine someone means to give them money because they did something wrong. When you act very bad, you might get in trouble. A fine is a way to pay for this trouble.

Imagine you go to a school and you do not follow the teacher. You take a book that is not yourself. You might get in trouble. If the teacher says you did this, the teacher will give you a fine. This fine is money that you have to pay. It is a way to pay for the trouble you caused.

You can also fine people who play a game in a way that is not okay. If a child does not stop making noise, the game is stopped. The person who is in control of the game might give the child a fine. This is a way to show that the game is being followed and that the child did not follow it.

Sometimes, a fine is given to people who break other big things like not paying money to a store. It is a way to tell them they have to pay the money.

A fine is usually paid by the person who gave it. The person who gives the fine does not get money back. They give the money to the person who broke the thing. This helps keep everyone safe and helps people follow the right way.
