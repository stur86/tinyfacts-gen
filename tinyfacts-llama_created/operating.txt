To work something is to use it so you can do other jobs. A person who works in a room might use a computer. They type on it to get answers or pictures for a story. A person who works in a moving car might drive it. They follow a plan so they can reach a place before others do.

When you work, you are doing a job. You are using something to help you do more work. You are also working with other people. You might have a team of people who help you. Together you do work that is very important.

Some things you work on are big. You build a house for a family. You grow food in a field. You build a car or make a box. Other things you work on are small. You fix a phone, or you make a paper thing. You are doing work so that people can use things and live their lives.

To work with a computer is a very good job. You can look at numbers and words. You can make a story, or you can find a picture for a picture book. This job is very important because it helps us remember things and talk to each other.

When you work, you might feel happy. You might feel like you are doing a good thing. You are using your brain to find a way. You are helping others. You are doing a job that is very important.
