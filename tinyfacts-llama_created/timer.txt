A long time ago, people used a thing that looked like a box with numbers on it. You would put food in the box and the box would tell you when to eat. You had to remember the numbers so you could eat when it was time.

Today, you use a different kind of thing. You can use it for many things. You can use it to tell time. You can use it to do work. You can use it to keep things moving. A small computer can tell you what to do in a short time. It can show you how many seconds, minutes, or hours you have left.

You can also use a word that is like a box. When you say a word, you are telling the person you talk to that you are finished. If you finish what you are saying, you can stop talking. If you use this word with a time, you say you are done. You are finished with what you are saying.
