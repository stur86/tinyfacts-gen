To break something means to make a small cut in it. You do this to make it easier to move around. You might break a glass while you are eating food. The glass breaks into many small pieces because you hit it with a hard thing.

You can also break a person. This happens when a person feels very angry. They might punch another person. This makes the person hurt and they go away for a while.

Sometimes, to break a thing, you might use it in a different way. If you have a box, you can break it to get the parts inside. You do this to get what you need, like a paper or a small thing that you need.

To break something also means you let go. Imagine you have a bag of money. You might feel happy at first, but then you realize you do not need the money anymore. You let the money go and you feel free.
