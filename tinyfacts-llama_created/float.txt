When you drop something, it falls down to the ground. The ground pulls on the thing. The thing does not want to pull back. It keeps going down. Because the ground is heavier than the thing, the thing does not go up.

If you drop a ball, the ground pulls on it and makes it go down. The ball wants to go back up to the sky. But it can't go back up. It keeps going down until it hits the ground.

Things that are very heavy can also make things go down. A big car can make the ground go down. A heavy dog can make a tree go down.

You can also say something is in a state of being. When you are in a state of being, you are where you are. You are not moving from one place to another. You are in one place.

Imagine you have a cup of water. You put it on the table. The water is still in the cup. It is not moving. The table is the place. The water is the thing. The cup is the cup. Everything is in the same place.

If you move the cup, the water moves with it. The water is now in a different place. The water is not in the cup anymore. It is now in the table. The cup is in the water. The table is still the same place.
