To sit is to be in a place. A person can sit on a chair, on the floor, or on a table. When you sit, you are still standing. You are not moving. You are in the same spot.

You can sit in a room. You can sit next to another person. You can sit in a car. You can even sit in a tree. A tree is a big green thing in the ground.

Sometimes you sit to eat food. You take a small thing from a box. You put it in your mouth. Your body gets the food so you do not get sick. You drink a drink while you sit. You can also sit to read a book. You sit in a quiet place and look at words on a paper.

You can also sit to watch a movie. A movie is a long story with many moving pictures. You sit in a room and watch the story. You feel the story in your heart. You feel the way the people act and the way they feel.

When you sit, you are alone. You do not talk to anyone. You are just you and the thing in front of you. You are a small part of a much bigger group.
