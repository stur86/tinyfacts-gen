To wave is to move your hand back and forward. When you hold your hand in front of you, you are not doing anything. You are just sitting there. But when you move your hand, the light from the sun or a bright thing comes down and hits your hand, and you feel a little bit warm.

This is what people do when they talk to each other. If you are saying something, you are moving your hand. You are not still. You are telling your friend that you think they are right. You are using your hand to show your face.

It is also what you do with your hair. If you are feeling happy or sad, you might wave your hair. This is when you move your hair back and forward, up and down, or side to side. It is like a big, long wave.

In the water, you can wave your arms. This is when you move your arms up and down in the water. This makes the water go around you. It is like you are running in the water.

When you are playing a game with a friend, you might wave. This is when you use your hand to tell your friend that you are ready to play. You are not just sitting there. You are ready to do something fun.
