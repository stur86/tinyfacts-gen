To work on something is to be busy with it. You might be a teacher, a doctor, a writer, or a worker in an office. You have a job to do, and you use your body, your brain, and your time.

Think about a computer. You sit in front of it and type words on the keys. The computer does the work for you. You tell it what to do, and it makes it happen. You can play games, watch movies, or learn new things while you work.

In a big group, people work together. They do different jobs that help the whole group. The person who makes things in a room works on a table. The person who reads books works on a desk. Everyone is important, and all their jobs together make the group work.

In a school, you work on a paper. You write your ideas, your words, and your pictures. You do not just sit and look at things. You are busy making something new and important. When you finish, you feel happy because you did something good.

Sometimes, people work from home. They sit in their own rooms with their computers and phones. They work on their papers or their jobs. They do not leave until the job is done. This can be nice if you have a job you love, but it can also be hard to stay away from others when you are busy.

To work is to be busy and to be part of a group. You are doing something important for a time, and you are doing it with people who need you.
