The word hard is used to talk about things that are very strong.

If you say something is hard, you mean it is very hard to do. It does not go away easily. You might break your finger if you hit it very hard. You might have to work very hard to get something. A rock is hard. A piece of wood is hard too. It does not give in easily.

Sometimes, hard is used for people. If someone is very strong, they can do many things. If you are very good at a game, you are hard to beat. If you have a very hard time sleeping, you are tired. Your body feels like it has a lot of work to do.

You can also use this word for things that are not very nice. If you do something very bad, it is hard for others to feel happy for you. It is hard to be sad when you see a person you like is hurt. You feel a strong feeling of worry.

The word hard is like a word for power. It is the thing that makes things strong.
