To help someone or something is to give them a thing they need to do their job. It is to make things easier.

Imagine you have a big box full of books. You want to go to a far place to study. But you do not have a car. So you ask a friend to drive you. Your friend gives you the car, the money to pay for the car, and they drive you to the far place. This is helping you. You are doing the work, but you do not have to do it alone.

If a doctor is very sick, you can give them money so they do not have to work so hard. You are helping them because they need your money.

You can also give help to someone who is sad. You listen to them when they talk about their day. You show them you care. This is showing you you are there for them.

If a person does something very bad, you might try to help them. You might talk to them, tell them they are okay, and help them feel better. This is showing you you care about their well being.

You can also give help to people who have a big problem. You might help them buy food to eat. You might give them money to buy a new thing they need. This helps them get back on their feet.

To help someone is to show that you are not alone. You are there for them when they need you.
