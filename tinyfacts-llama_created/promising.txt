When you say something is promising, you mean it looks good. You are not sure if it will really happen, but you think it might.

Imagine you have a friend who wants to go to a big party. They say they will be there. You are not sure if they will actually go. You are still a bit worried, but you trust them. If you think they will follow through, you feel happy.

If you are promising, you are saying you are not sure but you are sure that it will happen. You are saying that you are ready to see if it works out. You are not afraid because you believe it will be okay.
