A big road with many cars on it. It goes from one city to another. People drive on it to go to work, to school, or to buy things. There are many of them in many cities.

You might see a big sign with words like big, many, and fast. These words tell you how big the road is and how fast people can drive on it. You also see signs that say stop, go slow, and be carefully.

Some roads are very long. They go through many towns and cities. Others are very short and only go between two places. People drive on them to get to the other place.

You can walk on the big road too. But you have to follow the same signs as the cars. There are areas where cars are not allowed. There are also areas where you can walk, like on a cross road.

Big roads can be very busy. There are many cars, buses, and people walking on them at the same time. You might see many people, cars, and buses. It can feel like a big crowd.
