To land something means to put it down on the ground. If you are on a moving car, you are always moving. But when you stop your car, you stop the moving part. You have stopped.

Imagine a small animal. It is going very fast. The ground is very far away. The animal sees the ground and wants to get to it. It jumps up and lands on the ground. This is what it means to land. It is like a big move.

You can also land things. If you drop a ball, it falls down to the ground. This is a kind of landing. It happens when something falls from a high place.

Sometimes we land things that are very heavy. When we drop a heavy box, it falls down to the ground. This is a big landing. It is important because it keeps us safe.

A person can also land a thing. If you are flying a small thing, you are going very high up in the air. You are moving very fast. Then, you have to start going down. You need to land your thing so you can walk on the ground.

To land something can also mean to stop a fight. If two people are fighting, they might try to run away. But if someone stops them, the fight stops. The person who stops them has landed their power.
