To love something means you care about it very much. You like it and want to keep it. You feel happy when you are around it. You might feel sad if you are away from it.

You love your family. You feel happy when your mom and dad are near you. You also love your friends. You feel happy when you play with them. You love animals too. You might pick them up and give them a big hug.

You love a person who makes you feel safe. You feel calm when they are near. They are like a friend who always listens to you.

Sometimes, you love an idea that makes you happy. You love to read about it. You feel excited when you learn something new.

Loving is about feeling happy inside. It is not just about the things you do. It is about how you feel when you are near someone or something you care about.
