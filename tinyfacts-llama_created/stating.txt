To state something is to make it clear that you think or believe it. You are telling others what you mean. You are not just talking. You are telling the whole world.

Imagine you want to tell your friend that you love them. You can say, in front of everyone, that you love them. You have not really said it in your heart. You are only telling others that you love them. You are not really feeling that way.

But if you say, in your own quiet way, that you love them, you are stating it. You are telling the world. You are not just talking to your friend. You are telling everyone you meet. You are showing them how you feel.

Sometimes, we state things that are wrong. We say, in front of everyone, that the food is bad. But in our own minds, we know it is not true. We are not really stating it. We are only trying to get others to agree with us. This can be a bad thing because we are not really telling the truth.

To state something is important. It helps us know what to do next. It helps us understand the world around us. It helps us feel safe and clear about what is happening.
