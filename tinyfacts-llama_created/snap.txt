A snap is a sound that happens when something breaks or falls. It happens when something hits another thing very fast. When you hear this sound, you know something has happened.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You all are running around, and one of you is running very fast. You are all laughing and having fun. Suddenly, one of the friends jumps up and hits another friend. The friend who was running so fast falls down very quickly. The sound that comes from this fall is a snap. It is like a loud, clear sound that says, something has happened.

Sometimes, a snap can mean something has ended. If you are in a story and the bad person is about to get what they want, the story ends. You can also hear a snap in a room when a door falls off its floor. It is a sound that tells you something is changing.

A snap can also be a sign that something is ready. When you are waiting for a friend to arrive, you might feel a little nervous. You are waiting for the moment when they will walk through the door. This is like a snap. The moment is now, and everything is ready.

In a house, a snap can mean a small break in the floor. If the floor is broken, the floor will start to fall down. This is not good. You need to fix it before the floor breaks more.

In the story of the boy who fell from the tree, the boy had a good snap. He was running very fast and then he fell from the tree. This was a bad moment for him. He was scared and sad. But then he learned something important: he was still standing after he fell. This is a good thing to remember.

In a game, a snap can mean the end of the game. If one team is ahead and the other team is behind, the game is over. The team with more points is the one that gets the snap.
