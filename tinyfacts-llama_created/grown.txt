To grow means something gets bigger. You start as a tiny thing that is small and small. You take in air and drink water every day. Your body gets stronger because it uses this food to make you bigger.

Imagine you are a baby animal. You are small and very quiet. You have no legs yet. You eat and drink from a mother animal. After a few months, you have grown a little bit. You can move on your own. You can feel the ground under your feet. Your legs are now strong enough to carry you around.

As you grow older, you become a different kind of animal. You grow hair on your body. You grow teeth. You grow eyes that can see. You grow a hard skin around your body. You grow a head that holds your brain. Your body gets more and more different as you grow.

You can grow in many ways. You can grow taller. You can grow wider. You can grow stronger. You can grow hair all over your body. You can grow teeth and grow your eyes. You can grow your skin and your hair.

When you grow, you do not just get bigger. You also change. Your body gets different ways. Your face might get bigger. Your body might get round. Your body might get a different color. You might get a new hair that looks different from the hair you have now.

Growing is a big change. It takes time and it takes work. But it is an important part of being a living thing.
