A long time ago is when things started. Before that, there was no time at all. It is before the day you were made.

Think of it like this. You are now. Your mom was here before you. Your dad was here before your mom. Before them, there were no people. The whole world was empty.

It is a way to say how many years have passed. You can say you are in the year of your life. Or you can use it to talk about something that happened a long time ago.

You can also use it to talk about the past. If you see a picture of a place that has been gone for a long time, you can say it is from ago. It is a time that is gone and never comes back.

People use ago to talk about things that happened before they were here. It is like a word that helps us remember how things started.
