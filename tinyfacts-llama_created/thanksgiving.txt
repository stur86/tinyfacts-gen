Thank you is a word that people use when they want to say they are glad for something. It means they are happy because they have something they want.

When you eat food you like, you say thank you. This is because you are eating and you want the food. You say it when you put the food in your mouth and you feel good.

You can also thank a teacher. If a teacher helps you learn something new, you say thank you. You do this because the teacher made you feel better.

Sometimes, you thank people for their help. If someone helps you move a heavy box, you say thank you. You are glad they were there to help you.

You can also say thank you when you are sad. If a friend tells you they are happy for you, you are glad for them. You might say thank you so the friend feels better.

You can thank a person who is kind to you. If a kind person lets you go home early, you say thank you. This shows that you are happy to see them.

You can also say thank you when you are very happy. If someone gives you a new thing you want, you say thank you. You feel good because you have something new.

Thank you is a word that shows you are glad. It helps us remember the good things that happen in our lives.
