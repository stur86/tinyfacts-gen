To play with someone is to have fun together. You do not follow the same way. You might say something different. You try to be the best. You might make a joke.

To break something is to cut it. You use a thing to cut the wood so you can make something from it. You cut a paper. You cut a box. You cut the power so you can use it later.

To type something is to use a small thing. You press the keys. The computer remembers what you wrote. You can write a story, a picture, or a word. You can write to a friend, to a teacher, or to a family person.

To build something is to make it from nothing. You use many pieces. You put them together. You make a house, a car, or a thing for a person. You are the one who makes it happen.

To look at something is to see it. You sit in front of a window. You see a bright sun. You see a green tree. You see a bright sky. You look at a picture. You look at a book. You look at a computer. You look at a thing that makes music.

To break someone is to hurt them. You say a bad word. You touch a person. You do something that makes them cry. You break a heart. You break a friend. You break a dog. You break a thing that you own.

To laugh is to feel happy. You hear a funny sound. You see a happy picture. You see a happy face. You laugh with a friend. You laugh at a joke. You laugh at something that makes you feel good. Laughs are important for us. They help us to feel better when we are sad.
