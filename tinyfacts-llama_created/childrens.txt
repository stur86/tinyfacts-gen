Children are young people who are very small. They are not yet grown up. They like to play, learn, and laugh.

When we talk about children, we usually think about the ones we see every day. These are the little ones who are in school, the ones who play with them, and the ones who are at home with their parents.

Children need love and attention. They want to be happy and feel safe. They learn from their parents and teachers. They make new friends and have fun with them. They grow and change every year.

Some children are very good at school. They read books, write papers, and do their work. They learn to write, draw, and make things. Some children are very good at playing games or doing things that make them laugh.

Other children are very different. They might be very tall or very small. They might like to play with blocks, cars, or animals. They might be very quiet or very loud. They might like to draw or build with their hands.

All children are the same at first. They look the same, act the same, and feel the same. But as they grow up, they become more like each other and more like us. They learn new things, make new friends, and change their way of thinking.

People always talk about children. We talk about how much we love them. We talk about how much we want them to be happy. We talk about how we want to help them grow strong and be good people.

We also talk about children when we think about how to take care of them. We think about how to give them food, water, and a safe place to live. We think about how to help them learn and grow.

So, children are young people who need love, attention, and care. We are always happy to see them and to help them grow and learn.
