An order in the sky is a way the world goes round. It is not a big round thing in the ground. It is a bit like a dog. The dog goes around a tree. It turns around the tree many times. The tree does not move. The dog does. The dog keeps doing this for many years.

When you look at the world, you see many of these orders. The sun is in one order. The stars are in another. The world is in one order. Our house is in one order. We go around the world many times each day. Every time we go, the world turns around us.

The word has nothing to do with how fast we go. It is about how the world moves around the middle part of our world. It is also about how things move around us. The world turns when the ground moves. We turn when the sun turns. The stars turn when they go through different orders.

We can see orders of the night sky. There are many of them. Some are very high up. Others are near the ground. Each one has its own story. Some have many stars. Some have only one big star. Some have many small ones. Each order is like a story with many numbers of things.
