When you say something is going to happen, you have made a guess. You think about what will happen in the days or weeks to come. You use the word to tell others. If you say a place will be hot, you are making a guess about what the day will be like.

Sometimes you say something will happen. It does happen. You did not guess. The thing was already there. You are telling others that what happened is true.

You can also use the word to talk about what might happen later. You think about what will happen in the months or years to come. You are making a guess about what will be true then.

Sometimes people use the word to talk about how they feel. If you are very happy, you say you are going to be happy. If you are afraid, you say you are going to feel afraid. You are making a guess about how you feel.

It is also used when you are talking about how things will change. If a person is going to change their job, you might say they are going to change jobs. This is because you know what is going to happen.
