When you add something to another thing, you are making more. You are taking from one thing and putting it into another. This is how you build things.

Think about a number. If you have  five, you are adding one more to that number. You are not already five and now you are seven. You are making the number bigger.

You can add people too. If you have one person and another person comes, you now have two. You have more than one person.

You can also add things that are not numbers. If you give a person a book, you are adding something new to their life. You are giving them something they do not already have.

When you add, you are making space. Space is where things are not yet there. When you add something, you are making room for it. This is important because space gives things a place in our world.

The word added is often used to talk about things that are now. If something is added, it has become new. It is not the same as it was before.
