A very important word.

When you are trying to do something, you need help. You might need a long thing to reach high places. Or you might need a small thing that can cut through wood. You use these things to do your job.

Think about what you do every day. You do not have the power to move things around. You do not have the power to make new things. But you have the power to fix things you already have. You use the power inside you to make things better.

There are many kinds of these things. Some are very small. Some are very big. Some are for making things. Some are for breaking things. Some are for making things look nice. Some are for moving things. Some are for making things stay in one place.

We need these things to do our work. We use them when we are very busy. We do not have time to think about what we are doing. We just use the power and the thing.

People also use these things when they are not working. They might go to a store to buy them. They might see them in a movie. They might see them in a picture of something they want to do.

In some places, you can not buy these things. You have to make them yourself. You learn how to use them. You learn how to fix them. This is a good thing. It shows you are strong. It shows you are good at doing things.
