When people do what you want, they are called to be your friend. This means you agree with them. You do not get angry. You do not say bad things. You just do what you should.

If you say something and it makes people happy, they are happy for you. They like you and want you to be your friend. You have found a good way to act.

To be a good friend, you have to be good at listening. You hear what the other person says and you remember it. You also have to be kind. You do not laugh at the other person if they are sad. You show you care.

If you do not do this, you might make a person angry. They might say bad words or walk away. This is not a good thing to do. Being a good friend is important.
