To block something means to stop it from happening or going. Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. One person does something that is not allowed. The other person can not play because they are blocked by the first person. The first person has a power that stops the second person from moving.

You can block a door. If you hit the door with a thing, the door will shut. This keeps people or things out. You can also block a computer. If you type something you do not want to see, the computer will block it. The computer does not show you the words.

You can block a phone call. If you do not answer, the person does not get your phone number. You are not talking to anyone.

Sometimes people block people they do not like. This is because they do not want to talk to the person. It is like hitting a wall. The wall stops the person from going any further.

You can also block your face. If you cover your face with a thing, you are not looking at the world. Your face is hidden, and you do not see the people around you.

In a group, you can block someone. If the person is being mean or bad, you can stop them from being part of the group. This is a way to keep the group safe.
