When you do something, you make it happen. It is a part of your life and your body. When you walk, you do your legs move. When you pick a book, your hands do something. When you talk, your mouth does something, and your voice comes out.

You do things to help others. If you help a friend, you do it for them. You do things to make your family happy. If you clean the house, you do it for your mother and father. You do things to keep your body and your home safe.

You do things to feel good. If you see a bright sun, you smile. If you hear a song you like, you want to dance. You do things to move your body and to make your heart feel happy.

You do things for fun. You play a game with your friends. You watch a movie with your family. You do things to relax and to forget about problems.

You do things to show you care. If you give a friend a small thing, you show you are kind. If you listen to someone, you show you are interested. When you do something, you show the world you are there.
