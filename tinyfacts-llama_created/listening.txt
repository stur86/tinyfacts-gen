To listen is to pay attention to what someone is saying. You might listen to a friend talk about their day or to a teacher explain a hard idea. When you listen, you do not interrupt. You let the words go in one after another, without trying to finish them before.

Sometimes, you listen to a song. You might hear the sound of a voice and the beat of the music. You feel the music moving through your body. You might also listen to a story, like a book or a movie. You might imagine what happened, even if you did not hear it.

When you listen, you can remember things later. You can remember what a friend said or what a teacher said. This helps you understand and feel more calm. It is a way to stay safe and make sure you know what is going on.

You can also listen to sounds around you. If a dog bags, you might listen to the sound. You might hear the sound of water in a park or the sound of a car driving away. This can help you feel safe and make sure you are in the right place.

To listen is to stay still and look at the person or thing you are listening to. It is to not move your head or body until you have heard what they say. This helps you understand and remember what is going on. It is a way to stay calm and focused.
