To hear is to know what is happening around you. It is when you can hear the sounds that people and things make. You can hear a loud voice, the sound of a car, or the sound of a small animal.

When you hear, your brain takes in the sound. It looks at how fast each sound is moving. Your brain then puts the sounds together to make what you hear. This is why you can hear your own name, or the sound of a door opening. 

You can also hear things that are very far away. If you are very far from someone, you can still hear them. This is because sound moves very quickly. It goes from one thing to another very fast. 

Sometimes, you hear things that you did not expect. You might hear a strange sound at night, or a sound that you have never heard before. This can be a good thing. It can help you understand what is happening in your body. It can also be a sign that something is wrong.

To hear a thing also means to understand what it says. If someone talks to you, you hear what they say. You can also hear when someone is trying to tell you something important. You might feel your heart beating fast when you hear a word that means you need to move.

When you hear people, you also hear how they act. You learn about their way of life. You might hear that they are happy, sad, or angry. This helps you feel more about the world around you.
