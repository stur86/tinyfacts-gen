When you buy something, you usually pay for it later. But when things move from one place to another, it is called a shift. A shift is when you send a thing from one place to another.

Imagine a box of food. You buy the box. Then you drive to a place where the food is made. You drop the box off at a place where someone else makes the food. The food comes to your house. It is like a shift.

In a big world, many people do shifts. They move from one place to another. They drive cars, walk, or fly. They carry food, clothes, or other things. They help other people when they need them.

When people send things, they use numbers. The numbers tell where to go and when. It is like a set of words that says, here is the box, and it should reach this house by this day.

You also use numbers to show how many shifts have happened. If many people have many shifts, it is a long day. If one person does a lot of shifts, it is a busy day. If many people have very few shifts, it is a quiet day.

To send something, you give the numbers to a person. They read the numbers and drive the thing. They follow the numbers so the thing reaches the right house.

When a person gets a number, they know where to go. They follow the numbers to pick up the thing. Sometimes, people have to work hard. They must be ready at a time. They can also have to deal with people who want to take the thing or try to stop them.
