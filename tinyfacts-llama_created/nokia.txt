A long time ago, people did not have phones like we do today. They would go to a shop and buy a small box that could ring and talk. Inside this box, there was a tiny computer and a way to send and get words. This computer was very fast and could do many things at once.

The box was usually very big and heavy, and it made a lot of noise. It was also very easy to break if you dropped it. But people loved them because they could talk to each other from far away, and they could send words to anyone with a phone that was like this.

Later, these boxes became smaller and lighter. They had more power and could do many more things. They could also do things that were not yet possible on computers. Some of them could show pictures and play music. Some could even send pictures to your phone without you having to ask for them.

Today, most people have these small computers in their hands. They can talk to anyone, send pictures, and do many other things that we did not even think about when we first had these boxes. They are like a small room in your hand that can do almost everything we need.

Some people still use these old boxes for work and for things they need to do by hand. But most people do not need to carry around a whole box anymore. They can just carry their phone and go.
