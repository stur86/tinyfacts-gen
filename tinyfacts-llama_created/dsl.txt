A drier is a thing that keeps things dry. It sits on a table or a floor. It has a round top and a long piece in front. When you put a wet thing inside, it fills up and makes the air around it dry. When it is full, you can open the top and let out the air. It helps keep the room or the car room dry so you do not get wet.

You can use a drier to get ridden wet clothes. You put wet clothes in the drier. The hot air inside the drier moves around them. The wet stuff gets out of the clothes and the air is pushed back in. The clothes become dry again. Some dries have many small pieces. One small piece lets air out, and the other pieces let the hot air in. They help keep clothes dry.

You also use a drier to get ridden big wet things like papers or things that are made of wet stuff. You put them in the drier and close the top. The hot air makes them dry. After a while, they feel hard again.

Sometimes, you use a drier to make the air feel better. If you have a room that is very hot, you can sit in it with a door closed. The hot air inside the room goes out through the small pieces in the drier. This makes the room feel lighter and easier to breathe.
