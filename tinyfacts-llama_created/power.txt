Power is a thing that makes things move or change. It is the power of a person, a group, or a thing to do something.

Imagine you are a dog. You want to chase a ball. You use your legs to run. You want to be the one who moves the ball. You have power because you can do this. You are not afraid of anything.

Power is also about being able to act. If you are the head of a group, you have power. You can tell people what to do. If you are the one who owns a car, you have power over the car.

Sometimes power is good. If you are a person who wants to help others, you have power. You can make people happy. But sometimes power can be bad. If someone has too much power, they might do things that hurt others.

Power can come from many things. It can come from hard work. It can come from being very fast. It can come from being very strong. It can even come from being very good at a game.
