A place in a room or a big building is often called the middle. This middle spot is not far from the front or the back. It is in the middle of all the rooms and halls.

Imagine a line that goes from one end of a long table. The middle of the table is the spot where you can stand and look at all the people around you.

If you have many bags and boxes, you can pick one bag at a time. The bag that is in the middle has the most stuff in it. This is because it is the spot where all the other bags sit.

In a car, the middle of the car is the spot where the driver sits. The person in this spot can see the road in front of them and behind them.

In a group of people, the person who is in the middle is often the one who talks. This person has a lot of words to say and can help everyone understand what is happening.

In a story, the middle can mean the part where the big story starts. It is the part where the good and the bad happen together.

All these are about a spot in a group of people, a group of things, or a big building.
