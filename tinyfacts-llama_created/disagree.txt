When you say something is right or wrong, you are agreeing with it. If you think a game is fun, you might say, I agree. This means you both think the same thing.

Sometimes, people have different ideas. They might not agree on something. This is when you have a different point of thought. You can still be friends because you both want the same thing. You just see it from different sides.

If you are talking to a friend, you might say, I think the food is bad. Your friend might say, I agree that it is bad. They are not both right. They both think the food is bad. This does not mean you are wrong. It means you are still friends.

You can also say, I do not agree with you. This means you do not think the same way. You might have a different idea about something, and this is what you are saying.
