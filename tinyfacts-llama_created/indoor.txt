An inside room is a room you go into to be safe. You stay inside this room instead of going out in the bright sun or wind. You can close the door and keep the light inside so you do not get hurt.

You might be inside a house or a building. The room is usually much smaller than the whole building. There are walls, a floor, and a high top. You can not see the outside from inside. But you can see your friends and family sitting on the floor.

You can also be inside a car. When you drive, the car moves from one place to another. You are inside the car, and you see the road and the town passing by. You can hear the sound of the car moving and see the people on the road.

Some rooms are inside big things like a building. You can go inside a building and visit many rooms. There might be a room with books, another room with computers, and a room with people from a group. You can walk from room to room and see what is happening in each room.

You can also be inside a box. A box can be a small room that is put inside a bigger box. You can not see the outside of the box, but you can see the room inside. The box might be for a child to play with or for a thing to be kept safe.

In some places, people use words to talk about inside. They say a place is inside because it is not on the ground. This word can help us understand where things are when we do not see them.
