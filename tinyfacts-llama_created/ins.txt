To inside something means to put something inside a place. You can put small things in a book, or you can put a ball inside a box. This is different from to outside something. When you put something outside, it is in a different room or a different place. When you put something inside, it is in the same room as you.

Imagine you are in a room and you find a box. Inside the box, there is a small key. You put the key inside the room so you can open the door. The key is inside the room, but the room is still the same.

Sometimes, you might put food inside a bag. You put food in the bag so the bag does not fill up with air. This keeps the food from falling out. This is like putting something inside a place so it stays there.

In a story, you might have a bad person who wants to hide. They put a thing in their bag that they do not want to show anyone. They hide it so no one sees it. The thing is inside the bag, but the person is still in the same place.
