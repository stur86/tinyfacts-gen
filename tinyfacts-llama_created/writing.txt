To write is to make things that people can not see. You sit down with a piece of paper or a box that can hold many words and letters. You use your hands and your brain to make the words come out. You use the words to tell a story, to say what you think, to say what you feel, and to tell what happened.

You can write anything you want. You can write a story, a book, a letter to a friend, a story about your day, a picture of what you see, or a picture of what you hope for. You can write about what makes you happy, what makes you sad, what you like to do, or what you want to be.

Writing is a way to share your voice with the world. When you write, you are leaving a mark on paper that says what you mean. People read these marks and try to understand. They might feel something when they see the words. They might smile, laugh, or feel sad. They might think about what they saw. They might try to do the same thing or think about what they want to do.

Writing is also about putting your heart into it. When you write, you are being true to yourself. You are not afraid to show your love, your fear, your anger, or your hope. You are being yourself. You are not hiding behind words or lines. You are being real.

Sometimes, writing is hard. Your words do not go the way you wanted. You make a small word wrong, or you write a word that you did not mean. You feel sad or angry. You want to stop. But you keep going because you want to tell your story, to show how you feel, or to share your idea. You keep writing because you want to make a mark on the world.

Writing is a way to build a story over time. A story starts with one word, one line, and one story. As you write more, the story grows. You add new words, new ideas, new pictures. You change the story so it is different from the first story. You add your own voice, your own feeling, and your own way of seeing the world.

Writing can be slow, but it is also fast. You can write a few words and then stop. You can write for a long time and then stop. You can write a whole book in one day or take years to write just a few lines. But no matter how much time it takes, writing is always changing. It is always moving.

To write is to be a living thing. You grow in the dark, and you take what you need. You use your words to reach people, to make them see, to feel, or to hope. You are not just a person. You are a story, a picture, a piece of paper, and a voice all at once.
