An idea is a thing you think about before you talk about it. It is a word or a story that helps you understand what you want to say. When you start a story, you are starting an idea.

Before you tell someone a story, you might want to know what you are going to say. You might want to make sure everyone knows the right words and that everyone is listening. An idea is like a warm up for a story. It helps you get ready and makes sure you are saying the right things.

An idea can be a feeling, a thought, a plan, or a plan of things to come. You might have an idea for a new game to play or a new way to do work. You might have an idea for a place to live or a new way to act. An idea can change as you learn more and as you see new things.

When you start a group, you might want to know what everyone thinks. An idea is like the first word of a meeting or a group of people. It sets the tone for what will happen next.
