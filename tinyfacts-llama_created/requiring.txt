To need something means you want it. If you need a drink, you want a glass of water. If you need a bed to sleep in, you need a bed.

You need things to live. You need food to eat. You need air to breathe. You need a body to move around. You need a home where you can stay.

When you need something, you can not have it. You must go to the place where the thing is. You must do what the place says. If you do not follow, you might get hurt.

Sometimes, you need to give up something you want. You might give your game to your friend. You might give your favorite book to someone who needs it. This is because you do not have it anymore.

You can also need to feel a way. If you are sad, you need to cry. If you are angry, you need to shout. You need to feel the way your body is telling you.

To need something is to want it. It is to have a strong feeling that something is missing. It is to feel a pull.
