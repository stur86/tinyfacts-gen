To be enough means you are not too much. You do not hurt anyone or anything. You are not so strong that you are afraid of anyone. You are not so small that you think you are not important.

Think about a child. If a child eats all the food they have, they are very full. They might be happy and tired. They feel safe. If a child does not eat anything and they still feel safe, they are not enough. They are full, but they are not full enough.

You can also be enough for someone else. If you love someone, you are enough. You do not need to be the best. You just need to be there. You can be the one who listens. You can be the one who is there when they need you.

When you do something, you must feel that you did it. You must feel that you are not going to do it again. If you do something and then you do it again, you are not enough. You did it once. You did it for one person. You did it for no one else.
