A plan is a way to do things. It is when you decide what you want to do and how you will do it. You might plan to go to a movie or eat food. You write down what you want to do and where you are going.

A plan is like a road for your life. It tells you where to go, what to do, and when. It helps you stay on track. It helps you feel safe and sure.

You can plan for many things: your work, your school, your home, or your life. You might plan for a party or a trip. You might plan for how to get from one place to another. You might plan for how to use money.

Sometimes a plan is bad. It might be a way to hurt people. It might be a way to keep you in a bad place. But you can also make a good plan. It can help you feel happy and safe.

It is very important to plan. It is the first step to doing something new. It helps you start and stay going.
