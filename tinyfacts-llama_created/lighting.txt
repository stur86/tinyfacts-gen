Lighting is about making things brighter. When you turn a room, the walls and floor become much lighter. This makes the room feel more open and bigger. You can see more of the room and feel less dark.

Lighting can be from many things. You might use a light from a table. This is good for reading books at night. It helps you see the words. You can also use a light from a street. This light comes from the street and goes into your house. It makes your home look nice during the day when you are not at home.

Some things need a different kind of light. A car needs a bright light to drive at night. This light is strong so the car can see the road and other cars. A phone might have a soft light. This light is not so strong that it hurts your eyes, but it helps you see the words on the phone.

Lighting is important for our lives. It helps us see when we need to. It also helps us feel better when it is dark. In a dark room, the light from the windows can make you feel safer.
