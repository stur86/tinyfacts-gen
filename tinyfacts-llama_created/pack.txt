To pack is to put things into a bag or a box so they are ready to go. You might put clothes into a bag for a long trip. Or you might put books into a bag for school.

When you are going to a place you have never been to, you usually pack things you need. You pack clothes for the day. You might pack a lunch or a drink. You pack a bag with your phone, your money, and your keys.

Sometimes you pack things for a fun time. You might pack a bag with games, a book, and some money for food. You might go to a big place with many people, like a store or a park, and you pack a bag to carry your things.

When you pack, you are making your things ready for the next day or the next trip. It is an easy way to make your life easier.
