To walk in a line is to go in the same direction, step by step. You might walk to a school or to a place you know. Sometimes you walk with your friends. You all move together and cover more ground than you would if you walked one step at a time.

You can also walk for a very long time. If you walk from the top of a big city to the bottom, you go through many places. You see the ground change from high to low. You might see other towns, parks, and houses. People often walk this way for work or for fun.

In some places, people walk to a place they need to go. They might walk to a store or to a job. If it is hot, they might walk to a cool place to rest. They follow a road that leads them to the right place.

To walk means to move your feet very fast while keeping your head up. It is a good way to get from one place to another when the roads are busy or the wind is strong.
