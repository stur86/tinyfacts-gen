A small box with many small pieces is called a case. It has a top and sides. You put small pieces of sound on the top. When you press the sides, the pieces go into your ears.

Imagine you are listening to music. A person has many of these small boxes. They put the music pieces in each one. When you put two of these together, the music starts. You can carry many of these in one bag or put them in a bag.

Some of these have a long line. You tell the person you want to listen to a song. They bring the right one. You put it in your bag. You can carry many small ones.

A long time ago, people used these to talk. You would put small pieces of words on the top. When you pressed the sides, the words went into your ears. This was a way to talk to many people at once.

Now, many people use these to listen to music. They put the music pieces on the top. When you press the sides, the music starts. You can carry many music ones in a small bag.

These are the small boxes with many small pieces. They help us listen to music and talk in a way that many people understand.
