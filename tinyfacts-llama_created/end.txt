To end something is to stop it. You can end a long story or a game. You can end a day so you can go to bed. Or you can end a fight. When you end a fight, you stop using your words and your body becomes quiet.

You can also end a time. If you finish a book, you have ended it. If you finish a job, you have ended it too. If you finish a day, you have ended it. The end of a time is a good thing.

Sometimes, you end something that is not safe. If you do not stop, you will get hurt. You might end up in a bed or a hospital. You can end a bad feeling inside your body. This is when you feel very sad or angry.

In life, you can end a time that is good for you. You might end a time you love to do. You might end a time you are happy. You can end a time that makes you feel bad.

You can also end a story that is not good. You might end a story that is mean or that makes you feel bad. You can end a story that is not true. This is when you stop watching a movie or a book.

In our day, we use the word end to talk about what happens when the day ends. We say, the sun goes down. We say, night has come. We say, it is time to go to bed.
