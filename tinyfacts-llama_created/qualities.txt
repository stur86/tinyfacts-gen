To have a good word is to have something in your head. This is very important. Think about a friend you like. You want them to stay with you. They help you when you are sad. You trust them. You like them. This is a good word.

Another word is good. A good word is not always easy to understand. It is not always right. It is about how things feel. You feel happy when you are with a friend you like. You feel safe when you are in a room with a kind person. You feel good when you do something you love. This word is important because it shows how we feel about things.
