When you are a part of something, you are not the only one. You share your time and your ideas. You use your body and your voice. You help make a group work better.

To be a part of something is to be a player. You are not the one who does the work. You are the one who comes and stays. You might be a student in a class. You might be a person who helps a team. You are a part of a story. You add your own part.

If you do not take part, you are not part of the group. You are left out. You might feel sad or angry. You do not feel like a real person. You feel like you are not a part of something important.

When you are a part, you are not alone. You are with many other people. You share a feeling. You share a plan. You share a moment. You are all working together.

Sometimes, people do not want to be a part of a group. They do not want to listen or they do not want to follow. They want to do their own thing. They think they are better than everyone else. But being a part of something is not just about doing things. It is also about being kind. It is about sharing your time and your heart. It is about being a friend.

When you are a part of something, you learn. You learn about the world. You learn about people. You learn about how to work together. You learn about how to be a good friend. You learn to trust others. You learn to listen.
