A thing that is different from others is called a thing that is different. This means it is not like any other thing.

Imagine you have many books. Each book has a different cover. Some are big. Some are small. Some have pictures on them. Each book is one of a kind. You can tell which book is which just by looking at it.

A person can also be different. They might have brown hair. They might wear a red shirt. They might speak a different way. This is because they are not like anyone else. They are a part of a group of people who are all different.

If you have many things and you pick one, you are left with the ones that are not the same as the ones you picked. This is what it means to be different.

We are all different. We have different eyes. We have different hair. We have different voices. We have different ways of walking. And we have different ways of thinking. This is what makes the world a big and interesting place.
