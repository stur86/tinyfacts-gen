To be and to have money are two different things. When you have money, you have it. It is a thing you buy things with. It is like the money you have in your pocket.

When you want to build a house, you need money. You have a plan, and you ask your friends and family to give you money. You do not take the money by forcing people to give it to you. You ask them because you need it to build your house.

You are also able to do things because you have money. If you want to go to a place with a lot of water, you can use money to buy a way to get there. You can buy a car or a bus that can take you across the water.

Sometimes, a group of people wants to do something. They ask the city or the school to give them money. The city says yes, and the group has money to pay for the work they do. This is how many big things happen in our world.

You can also be a part of a group that has money. If you are in the group that makes money for a store, you are a part of it. You help them make money, and they help you. This is how people work together.

Having money is important. You need money to buy food, a house, or a car. You need money to buy things you like. Without money, you would not be able to do anything.
