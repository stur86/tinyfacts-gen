To move is to change where you are. You can move through the ground, or through the air, or even through water.

When you move on the ground, you walk. You step and step, and your feet go over the ground. You can walk to school, to work, or to play. You might walk around a park or run around a big field.

When you move through the air, you fly. A car, a small flying thing, or a big dog can move through the sky. The wind can push you along, and you feel the air rushing past your face. You can fly over cities, across wide spaces, or over the dark night sky.

When you move through water, you go down. A person who is very strong might use a big thing that goes over them. This thing keeps them safe while they go fast through the water. A child might play in a small water box, or a person might work at a big place where they move through the water.

Sometimes, we move by moving our bodies. You can move your arms when you are talking. You can move your legs when you are walking. You can even move your body when you are dancing or when you are playing a game.

To move something is to put it from one place to another place. If you move a book, it changes from one room to another room. If you move a chair, it changes from one room to another room. This is how we change where we are.

You can also move something that is not a person. You can move a box. You can move a car. You can move a thing that you built so it goes where you want it to go.
