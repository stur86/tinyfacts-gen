A set of things you learn. When you are young, you learn a lot from your family, friends, and teachers. You hear stories, see pictures, and do things that help you know how to live. You also learn from people who are older than you.

You can learn many things in a short time. You might learn how to ride a horse, how to play a game, or how to talk. You might also learn about the world around you. You might hear about things that happened before you were even a day old.

When you grow up, you might only have one set of these things. Once you learn something, you will know it for the rest of your life. You might not always remember everything, but you know it is there inside you.

Sometimes, you learn by doing. You see how something works and you try it for yourself. This is a big way to learn. It is like eating food. If you eat something new, you get a feeling inside you. You remember the color, the smell, and the way it feels. This is learning in your body.

You can also learn from books and movies. You can read a story or watch a show. These things help you understand how people live, what happens in a place, and how to feel.

Learning is important for growing. It helps you become a person who can take care of yourself and others. It helps you find your place in the world and understand why things are the way they are.
