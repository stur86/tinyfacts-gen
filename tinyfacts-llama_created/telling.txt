To tell something means to share it with others. You might tell a friend a funny thing that happened to you. You might also tell a teacher a problem you are working on. You are telling them about what is going on in your head or what you saw.

Sometimes, you tell someone a story. You might be very excited. You want to show them how you felt. You might say,  My dog is very happy. He has a ball. He runs and jumps. Tell a story so people can imagine what you mean.

You can also tell a story in a different way. You might write it down in a book. Or you might talk about it while you walk or while you play. When you tell, you are sharing your own life with others. You are helping them understand what you feel.
