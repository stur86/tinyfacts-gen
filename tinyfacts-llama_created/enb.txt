To enjoy something means to feel very happy when you do it. It is about taking a moment and thinking about how nice it is.

Imagine you love a book. You read it many times and it still makes you feel happy. You smile and think about the story. You are in a good place when you are enjoying a book. You do not want the story to end.

You can also enjoy a day at the park. You walk around and look at the trees. The sound of the water from a small water place makes you feel calm. You are not thinking about anything else. You are just there, enjoying the time.

Sometimes you might want to feel this way for a long time. You might watch a movie for hours. You might listen to music. You might play a game. When you are enjoyer, you are not thinking about anything else. You are only thinking about how nice it is.

You can also enjoyer a person. This means you love them very much. You feel happy when you are near them. You might laugh with them. You might talk to them. You feel like you are a part of their life.
