A group of people who want to have power in a place where everyone has an idea decide together. They want to be in the top places of the land and the money. This group wants everyone to have the same chance to be in the top places.

The people who want this power are called the people with power. They are the ones who write the papers and decide who gets to stay in the top places. They also decide how money is spent.

There is also a group of people who are not in the top places. They have a different idea about how things should be done. They might think that the people in the top places should not be so strong, or they might think that the money should go to all the people, not just the ones with power.

Sometimes, the two groups fight with each other. But they also talk and try to find out what they want. This is important because it helps everyone know what they want.
