To fly means to move through the air. You can fly when you are on a small thing that goes very high. You can fly on the side of a big building or on a long piece of wood that goes around the world.

You need to use your body to lift yourself up. Your arms and legs help you go up and keep you up in the sky. You can also use your breath. When you breathe in and out, you are moving air. This air makes you go.

You can fly to places that are far away. If you fly very high, you can go to the top of the world. If you fly low over the ground, you can go to a city or a town. You can also fly to the water. This is called flying over the water.

You can also fly to a place that is not on the ground. You can fly over the water or over the trees in a big green area. When you fly, you feel like you are going fast. You might feel like you are moving in a line.

Some animals can fly. These animals have hard parts on their bodies that help them go up and stay up in the air. You can see them flying around the sky. They can fly very fast and go very high.

Flying is a fun way to move. It lets you see the world from a new place and feel the wind under your body.
