To keep something is to be carefully about it so it does not go away. This can mean keeping a thing that is old so it does not break, keeping a place where many people live so they do not go away, or keeping the air so it stays clean.

Sometimes, people work to keep things that are important for everyone. They might try to keep the water clean so people can drink it. They might put a stop on things that hurt the land so the air and water stay safe. They might also try to help the animals so they can stay in their homes.

In a way, this is like a game where we try to keep things from going wrong. We see how much we can do each day and try to do more than we can. This helps us keep the world a better place for everyone.
