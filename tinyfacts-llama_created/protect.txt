To have something is to keep it safe. If you have money, you keep it in a bag. If you have a dog, you keep it safe at your house. If you keep a baby, you keep them safe so they do not get hurt.

You can also keep something for another person. If you are happy to see your friends, you are happy to have them around you. If you keep your food so it does not go to someone who wants it, you are keeping something for them.

Sometimes, we need to keep things that are hard. If you are afraid of a big animal, you want to stay near a strong person. This keeps you safe.

You can also use this word to talk about how things work. If you have a car, you keep the car safe. You do not let it go without your help. You keep the keys in your hand.

Sometimes, to be safe means to be quiet. If you hear a strange sound, you might feel afraid. But if you keep your voice down, you can stay safe.
