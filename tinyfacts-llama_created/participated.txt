When you join a group or an eventually you are a part of it. You do not have to be there all the time. You can be there for some of the time and then you leave. You still feel a part of the group because you are there.

You can also be a part of a story. If you are in a story, you are a part of it. If you see a person, you are seeing them. If you hear a sound, you are hearing it. You are a part of what is happening.

When you are a part of something, you are not alone. There are many other people with you. They are all in the same group. You are together and you do the same thing.

If you are a part of a work, you are helping make it. You may do small jobs. You may write words. You may build things. You are doing your part.
