A large green area in the middle of a big group of houses is often called a park. It is usually very big with many trees. People go there to sit and play. They also go there to walk or run. In some places, there are big areas of hard ground where people play games that are not for kids. There is also often a big green area where children play with balls. This area can be very quiet or very busy.

A small green area in a big group of houses is often called a small park. It is usually small with a few trees. Some people like to sit and read a book. Others like to play small games with a ball. In some places, people have a small green area in their front or back. This is a nice place to sit and watch the world go by.

A small green area in a very big group of houses is called a small green. It is often very small and not very big. Some small green may have only a few trees. Other small green may be very small and in the middle of a big street.
