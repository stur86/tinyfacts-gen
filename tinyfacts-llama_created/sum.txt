To summering means to go from a hot place to a place with cold water. You know when you leave a hot room to go to a room that is very cold? You feel the water on your skin and you might feel very cold. When you go through this, you are getting your body ready for the cold.

You can also use this word for something that is made of many parts. If you have many books, you can summering them by adding all the numbers together. You add the numbers from each book to get the totally number of books. This helps you know how many books you have in all.

Sometimes, to summering a story means to tell the story again. You tell the story many times, and each time, you add a little more. You might add a name, or a word, or a story about the people who did the thing. This keeps the story going until you feel you have told it all.
