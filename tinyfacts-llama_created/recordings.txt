A group of sounds can be made. This group can be held. When the group is held, people can hear it later. It is like a story that was not written.

The group can be of a single person, of many people, or of many things at once. It can be made by a person, by animals, by a city, or by a group of people all working together.

Some people make these groups by their hands. They cut sounds from the air. Some people make them by moving things. They push, pull, and hit. They make the sounds happen.

People also make these groups by writing words. They put words on paper. The words tell a story. They make people remember the story.

These groups can be small or very large. They can be held for one moment or for many moments. They can be kept for a very long time or for only a few minutes.
