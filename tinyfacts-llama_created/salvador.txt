To save something means to keep it from going away. When you save money, you put it away in a box. You can use it later. If you have a bag of food, you can keep it in the bag. You are keeping it safe.

Sometimes, people use this word with animals. If an animal gets into a house, the people can save it. They take it away from the house and give it food to keep it safe until it can go back to the right place.

You can also say you are saving your life. If you are in a car that is going too fast, you can pull over and let the car stop. You are saving your own life.

You can also say you are doing something to help. If you see someone who is sad, you can give them a word. You are saving their day. You are helping them feel better.

In a game, you can save a high number. You keep the number low so you do not have to try very hard. You are saving your time.
