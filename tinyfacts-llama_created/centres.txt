To have a heart is to love. It means you feel something deep inside your chest. You might feel happy when a friend smiles at you or feel sad when a person you love is gone. Your heart is like a box inside your body where love stays.

Every person has one heart. If you love your family very much, you have a heart. If you love your job, you have a heart. If you love a dog or a cat, you have a heart.

Sometimes, a heart can break. This happens when you are very sad or angry. Your body feels a pain in your chest. You might cry or want to cry.

In a different way, a heart can be strong. If you are very happy, your heart might feel like it is going to burst. You might laugh and shout with a big smile on your face. This is a sign of how much you love what is happening.

A heart can also be very small. If you feel afraid, you might have a tiny heart in your chest. This heart might feel like it is hard to breathe because of the fear.

Every person has a heart inside them. It is a part of you that helps you feel love and care for others.
