An hour is a thing that helps us know how many minutes are left after a time that has already passed. It is a time number that is used to tell how long things last.

Most people use an hour to talk about things that happen in a day. If you start at  morning and go to bed at night, one hour is the time from morning until night. This is when you do things and then you go to sleep.

You can also use an hour to talk about a long time. If you are waiting for a food to come out of the kitchen, an hour can go very fast. You might feel like time is standing still.

There is a thing in a room that is one hour. When the room is one hour, the door is closed. This is a way to keep things quiet. It is like a quiet hour so that people can rest.

In a car, you might see a sign that says, one hour. This means the car will stop to let people cross the street. You should stop your car and wait for the sign to say it is safe to go again.
