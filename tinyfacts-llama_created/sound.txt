A sound is the way things make noise. When you make a high sound, it comes from the top of your head. When you make a low sound, it comes from your chest. When you make a strong sound, your voice goes very far. When you make a soft sound, your voice is very quiet.

You can hear sounds in the world. You hear the wind when the trees move. You hear the cars when they drive down the road. You hear the music of the band. You hear the sound of a dog when it runs. You can hear the sound of a bell when someone rings it.

You can also hear sounds from far away. When the sun is high in the sky, the sound of a far away animal is very loud. You can hear the sound of water moving over the ground or the sound of water falling from a high place.

Sometimes, you can hear sounds that are inside your body. When you laugh, your body makes a funny sound. When you are happy, your heart feels strong and you might make a happy sound. When you are sad, you might make a sad sound.

Sounds can help us. When you hear a bell, you know it is time to go to school. When you hear a song, it makes you want to move your body. When you hear a loud noise, it tells you to get away because it is too strong.

Sounds can also change when you move. When you walk down a street, the sound of your feet on the ground changes. When you jump up, the sound of your body makes a different sound. When you run, the sound of your legs moving very fast changes.

In the world, sounds are very important. They help us understand what is happening. They help us feel things. They help us know where things are. Without sounds, we would not be able to see or understand the world around us.
