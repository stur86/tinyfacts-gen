To start something is to begin. To end a thing is to stop it. When you start to build a house, the ground goes under you. You push hard, and the wall begins to go up. It is the beginning of the house. When the wall is full and you stop pushing, the wall falls down. It has ended.

If you are in a game, you start at the beginning. You move forward until you reach the end. If you get tired, you stop. You are no longer a player.

If a person is happy, they start to feel that way. They might laugh or smile. If they are sad, they start to feel sad too. Their face may look sad.

When a tree falls, it starts to go down. The wind pushes it and the ground pushes it. It falls until it hits the ground. The ground holds it until it stops falling.

To break something is to make it not work anymore. If you hit a ball with a stick, the ball falls down. The ball stops moving and falls to the ground. This is breaking.
