To changing or to change

This word is used for things that change a lot. It is not just for people. It is also for things that are not always the same.

When you say a thing is changing, you are saying it is moving. It might get bigger, smaller, or change how it looks. This happens because of how things are put together or because of what happens inside them.

Think of a house. You might start with a small room. Over time, the walls get bigger, the floors get higher, and more rooms are added on top. The house is changing. It is becoming bigger.

Animals also change. A baby might look very different from its mother. It might grow hair or grow a new body part. This is because it is learning and growing. The baby is not the same as its mother anymore. This is a big change.

You can also change things in your life. Maybe you are happy for a while. But then you meet someone new or you learn something new. This is a change. You are moving forward.

Sometimes, people change too. They learn new ways to think or they meet new friends. This is a big change. They leave their old way of being and start a new one. It is a good thing.
