To be a good person or to have a job that you are very good at is to be in a good position. You can think about it this way: if you want to be a doctor, you must go to school and learn about how the body works. You must also study how to make people feel better. You have to be very good at this job so that people trust you.

If you are a person, you might have many good things. You have a kind heart, you can listen to a friend, and you are always happy. In this case, you can say you are a good person. You have done a good job in your life.

To be a good thing in a world of bad things is to be in a place that is safe. You can feel safe if you are in a place with many good people who help you. If you are afraid, you might want to leave your home or go to a different place.

Sometimes, to be a good person is to help other people. You might give money to those who need it, or you might do something nice for a friend. This makes everyone feel happy and safer.
