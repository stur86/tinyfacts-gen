To wait is when you do not do something right away. You might want to do something, but you need to think or wait. This can happen many times in a day or in a week.

Think of a person who is waiting for a phone call. They are not doing anything at all. They are just standing in a room, looking at the wall. They do not do anything until the person on the phone calls back.

Sometimes, you might wait for a time. You plan to go to a movie at a later time. You go to the store and buy a drink. When the movie starts, you sit down and enjoy the story. You are waiting because you do not know when you will see the movie again.

You can also wait for things. If you want to eat dinner, you might wait until your mom says it is time. You do not do anything until she says, and then you eat the food you have in your hand.

Waiting can be hard. If you have to wait a long time, you might feel bored or angry. You might want to do something else. But if you are waiting for something good, it can be a nice time. You get to think about the person you love, or you get to relax and enjoy your own space.
