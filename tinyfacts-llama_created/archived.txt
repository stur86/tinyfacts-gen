To be in the past is to be in a time that has already happened. You are not living now. You are not in this moment. You are in a time that is gone.

Think of a story. You tell someone about what happened. You say, something happened to you many years ago. You did not live it. You did not feel it. You did not see it. You know it happened only because someone else remembered it.

You can also put things in a box. You put things that you do not want to remember in a box. You keep them there until later. Later, you can open the box and look at them. You are still in the past. You are looking back at the things that have already happened.

Sometimes, you can put things in the past for good. You might put old books on a book table. They are gone and you are safe. You do not have to worry about them anymore. You can still feel the books but you are not living them.

Being in the past is different from being in the present. The present is the time right now. The past is the time that has gone before. You can not live in both times at the same time. When you are in the past, you are not here. When you are in the present, you are here.
