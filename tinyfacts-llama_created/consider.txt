To consider something means to think about it. It is about what you want to know. You might think about a question. You might think about a picture. You might think about a friend. 

If you ask your friend if they like a movie, you are considering their answer. You want to know if they like it. You are looking at their face and their body. You are looking at what they say. 

You also consider things that are far away. Imagine you are on a far away place with your family. You might think about what you did the day before. You might think about your favorite food. You might even think about your plans for tomorrow. 

Sometimes, you consider things you have done. If you did something good, you might think about it. You might remember the day. You might think about how you felt. You might even think about how you feel now. 

When you consider something, you are looking at it. You are thinking about it. You are trying to understand it. This is an important part of life.
