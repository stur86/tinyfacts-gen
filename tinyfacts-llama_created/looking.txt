Looking is when you try to see a thing. You use your eyes and your brain to find it. You might look at a picture, or you might look at a person who is standing in front of you.

We look when we want to know what is going on. If a friend is doing something wrong, you might look at them and say, I see you. This shows that you care and you want to help them.

We also look when we want to see something new. If you have a box of books and you open it, you look inside to see what is inside. This is what you do when you are trying to understand a thing.

Sometimes looking can make us feel strange. If you see a person you do not know, you might look at them for a long time. This is because your brain is trying to figure out who they are.

You can look at a place, like a city or a park. You might walk around and look at the people and the things around you. You might look at the trees or the sky. This is how we see our world.

We can look at things that are far away, like the sun or the stars. When you look at these things, you are using your eyes to see them from a great way away.
