The word up is used to tell where something is. If you are in a room, you are down. If you go up, you move to a higher place. You can also say you are going up a high street. This means you are moving away from the bottom of a street.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are all standing in a line. If you cross the line to the other side, you are moving up. If you stay in the same line, you are still in the same place. This word helps us understand how people and things change when they move.

We also use up to talk about food. If you eat a whole bag of food, you have used it. This means you have taken the food into your body and it is now gone. You can also say you have eaten up a big drink. This means you have taken all the drink into your body.
