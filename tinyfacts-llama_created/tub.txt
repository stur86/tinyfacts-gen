A big thing you use to carry water is called a water thing. It has a bottom and sides so water can go in and out. Inside, there are many small rooms. When you put water in the top room, it goes down into the bottom room. You can open the top part to get the water. You can also put food or other things in it before you put it in the water thing. This way, the water thing gets the food for you.

You can also use this word for a long, round room with a high wall. It might have a top part that you can sit on. This room is often used for many people at once. You can sit on the top part and look around at the other people. Inside, the room might have many rooms. Each room has its own space. People might sit in one room, watch a game, and eat food. They might also talk to each other. This is a nice place for friends to be together.

Sometimes, a big round thing used for a game is called a water thing. It has a long part that goes into the water and a small part that you hold. When you hold the small part, you can throw the long part and hit a ball. You try to catch the ball when it goes over your head. This is a fun game with many people.
