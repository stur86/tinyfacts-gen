To look at something and decide what to do next.

When you have a job you love, you often feel very happy. You love what you do and feel like you are doing the right thing. In this case, you are not looking at a thing, but you are thinking about what to do next.

If you see a big, red car, you might feel a little bit afraid. But then you look at your phone and see a funny picture. This makes you smile and you feel better.

You can also look at a picture and decide whether you like it or not. If you like it, you might want to keep it. If you do not like it, you might throw it away.

In this way, looking at something is like deciding what to do next. You are looking at it, and then you decide what to do with it.
