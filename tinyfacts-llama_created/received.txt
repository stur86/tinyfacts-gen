To get something you want means you get it from someone. It is a big word.

If you are a good person, you might get things you need. A teacher gives you a book for school. A friend gives you a small thing they think is nice.

You can also get money from your parents. If you do good work at school, your parents might give you some money for the month. You can use this money to buy food or a game.

Sometimes, you might get a job. You work hard and do your work. After you do your work, you get a piece of paper that says you are a worker. This is a sign that you are doing well.

You can also get a thing that you want to play with. If you have a new game you like, you might get it from a store. You might pay money for it. Then you can play with it and have fun.

When you get something, you might feel happy. You might smile or shout. It is a good feeling.
