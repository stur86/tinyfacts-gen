The word for when you take something away from a number is called a number with a sign.

Imagine you have five pieces of food. You take one out, and now you have four. If you keep the food you took, you are left with four. If you keep the five you have, you have five. So, you are left with the same number.

The sign is important. If you take away a number, you are going to have a number with a sign. If you take away a number with a sign, you are going to have a number with a sign too.

If the number is very small, you might write it with a sign like this:   Five. This means you are going to have five.

If the number is very big, you might write it like this:    Five hundred. This means you are going to have five hundred.

Sometimes, you might see numbers written with a sign like this:   Five or   One. This means the number is the same, but the sign is different. For this, you use words to help you know which one you are looking at.
