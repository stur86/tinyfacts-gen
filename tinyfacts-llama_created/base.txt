A number is a way to show how many times a thing is used. It is the number before you start to write a story or a song. We use numbers in our day to plan things. You might write a number on a piece of paper to show how many hours you need to stay up. You might also use numbers to show how many steps you walk in one hour.

Think of a number like a story. The first number is the beginning. The next number is the second story. The one after that is the third story, and so on. Each number tells you a bit more about the story.

In games, numbers are used to keep track of how many points a player has. You might see a number that says you have three points. Each time you run, you get one of those numbers. If you get four points, you get the next number. If you get five points, you get the next number again. This keeps going until you reach ten numbers. Then you are done with the numbers.

In the number line, numbers are like lines that go into the air. Each number is a point on the line. When you add numbers, you are adding the points you have. The number is the number that comes before the number you are adding.

Numbers can also be used to show how much something is. If you have ten pieces of food and you take two, you have seven left. You have more food now than you did before. Numbers help us show how much we have and how much we are missing.
