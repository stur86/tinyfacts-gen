To lost something means you can't find it. When you lose something, you look for it. You walk around the house, the street, or a park. If you look in every room, you still can't see it.

You can lose a small thing like a paper or a book. You might drop it in a room or under a table. If you can't find it, you feel sad. It is gone and you have to find another one.

You can also lose a person or a feeling. If you have a good friend and then you get angry, you are lost. You are in a bad place. You are not the same. You feel different. Your face looks different, and your words are not the same.

Sometimes, you lose something important. Maybe you left your phone at home. You look for it all day. You search under the bed, in the couch, and in your desk. You are worried. You are afraid you will never see your phone again.

To be lost is to be alone and far from your home. It is to be in a place where you do not know how to get back. You are not sure if you will ever find your way.
