An answer that helps us understand what happened is called an answer. This is what people do when they want to know why something happened. If you ask why a tree fell, you might want to know what the wind did. The person who saw the tree might say, after thinking for a long time, that the wind was too strong. An answer helps us see what happened.

In a different way, an answer can mean to move away from something. If you are in a room and you hear a noise, you might want to get out. You are making an answer. You are moving away from the sound. This is what people do when they are afraid. They move away so they are safe.

In a business, an answer is what a person says when they are asked a question. They might say, I am here. I am working. This helps people know what they are doing.
