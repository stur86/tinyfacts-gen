To be a good listener is to wait quietly for someone to finish speaking before you talk. It is about following what someone says and not interrupting.

When you are a good listener, you do not think about what you should say next. You let the other person finish what they have to say. You pay attention to their words and their tone. This helps you understand how they feel and what they think.

Being a good listener is important in many ways. It helps people feel heard. It helps them feel understood. It is also a way to show you care about what someone is saying. It is a way to show you are interested in what the other person has to say.

In some ways, being a good listener is like being in a quiet room. The room is still there even when no one is speaking. The room is still there because of the people who are sitting there.
