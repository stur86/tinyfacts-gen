A small group of people who work together to make a big group do a job.

Imagine you are a teacher in a big school. There are many people who work together to make sure all the children learn. But sometimes one group can do all the work. This can be very good. The group with the power can make the big group do what they want.

A smaller group is a part of a bigger group. It has less people. These groups help make sure all people in the bigger group agree on what to do. They talk about the same things and make sure everyone is on the same side.

The word comes from the word of a person who works in a city. They work with many other people. One of these people is in a group that helps the city. This group makes sure the city is safe. The person in this group is the one who sits on a small group that works for the city.
