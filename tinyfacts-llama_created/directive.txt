A thing that tells what to do is called a direction. It is a word you use to tell people what to do.

Imagine you are a teacher in a school. You want your students to follow the way you tell them. You say, follow the way. This is a direction. Everyone follows your word, so you can trust that everyone does what you say.

A person can also give a direction. If you say, you should eat your food, you are telling people to eat. If you tell them to go to school, you are also giving a direction.

A business person might give a direction to their people. They might tell them which order to follow. They want everyone to do the same thing to make sure everything works well. This is important for a big group of people.

Sometimes, a direction is not a word at all. It can be a picture or a way that tells you what to do. You can see it on a sign in a store or on a computer when you want to go to another place.

To give a direction is to tell people what to do. It is a way to help everyone work together in a good way.
