To get something

When you do something well, you can get something. This can be money, food, a job, or other things you need. You did a good job so someone gives you this.

You might get a small thing you need to live. A person might give you a book to read. You can also get a small thing that makes you feel happy. You might get a small ball to play with.

You can also get a thing that you do not want. If you do not like a food, you might throw it away. If you do not like a song, you might turn it off. You can get a thing that makes you feel bad.

Sometimes, you get a thing that you did not ask for. You might get a surprise in the morning. You might get a new phone or a computer. This is a good thing because it is a surprise.
