To go up is to move toward the top. This happens when you are going fast on a car, a thing that goes over the ground. Or it happens when you are doing something that makes you feel happy. When you feel this way, your face might get a little bit bigger and your eyes might look bright. Your body might feel light too.

When the sun is high in the sky, it feels like you are moving very fast. This can be a little bit hard to stand up straight because the light is coming straight down from above. But you can still see far away. High is also a word for something that is very strong. Think about a strong wind or a bright light. These are all high.

Sometimes, you might feel a high feeling when you do something you love. If you do a thing you love, like playing a game or drawing, you might feel like you are on top of the world. You feel happy and you feel like everything is going well. This is a high feeling because you are doing what you like.

You can also say that a person has a high side. This means they feel happy or strong. It can happen when you do something you love or when you are doing something you are good at.
