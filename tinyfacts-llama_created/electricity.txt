To make things work is a big job. You need power to make a light turn on, a car move, or a phone ring. This power comes from far away. It goes through long lines and under the ground. When the power reaches your house, it turns into tiny bits that can move through your walls. These tiny bits are made of the same stuff as the air you breathe.

The tiny bits can move very fast. Imagine a long line of tiny pieces that are going very fast past you. If you were to hold a small piece of paper against the line, the paper would move very fast across your body. That is what happens with the tiny bits of power. They carry the power to your home.

When the power reaches your home, it turns into smaller bits that can make your things work. This is why you have a computer in your home. Or you have a phone that you can talk to. Or you have a warm bed that keeps you safe at night. All of these things use power from the long lines that carry the power from far away.

The power can also make things that move. A car, a train, or a flying thing need power to move. They need to go very fast so they can cover a long way. The power in the lines helps them do that.

Sometimes the power is not enough. If you have too little power, your things will not work. Or if you have power but it is too strong, it can burn things or make a lot of noise. People use power to make their lives easier. They use power to keep our cities clean. They use power to bring us food and water. They use power to keep us safe.

The power is also something that can hurt you. If you touch a hot thing without a cover, your skin can get very hurt. Or if you eat food that is past its time, you might get sick. We need to be carefully about how we use power so we do not get hurt.
