To change a number from one kind to another.

Imagine you have money. You have many small pieces of paper that show you have money. Each one is like a small piece of paper. Sometimes, when you want to buy something, the shop says you have money. You give them the pieces of paper, and they give you the thing you want.

Now, the shop has a lot of those small pieces of paper. They are all the same, but they are not all the same kind. Some are old pieces of paper that are not good for anything. Some are good pieces of paper that are still good.

The person who works with the money is called a money person. They change the old pieces of paper into new ones. They do this by taking the old ones and breaking them into many small pieces. They then put these pieces into new boxes. The new boxes have numbers on them. When you get the money, you put the numbers into the boxes, and then the boxes tell you how much money you have.

The money person does this because they make sure the money is the same. If one kind of money is broken into many small pieces, you do not know how much money you have. If two kinds of money are changed into one kind, you know how much money you have. This helps people buy things they want.

Sometimes, you can change money to make it easier to move from one place to another. You might have money that is not good for your home city. You can take it to a city that is far away and use it there. This way, you do not have to carry a lot of money with you. You can just carry the money that is good for the city you are in.
