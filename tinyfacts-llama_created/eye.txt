An eye is a very important thing in our bodies. It is what sees and tells us what is happening around us. It is the part of our head that looks at the world.

Inside the eye, there are two parts. The part in front looks at things that are very far away. The part behind looks at things that are very close up. When light from something comes into the eye, the two parts work together to form an picture.

The eye has a clear clear stuff inside it. This stuff helps us see colors and tells us if something is bright or dark. When light hits the clear stuff, it gets changed so we can see. If the light is not changed, the picture would be dark.

There is a tiny thing in the eye that makes the picture. This tiny thing changes the light so it looks like what is really there. It is like a tiny computer inside your head.

The eye also has a way to move. The part behind the eye turns around so it can look at different things at once. This is very important because it lets us see many things at once without our eyes getting tired.

Our eyes are very good. We can see things that are very small and very far away. If we close our eyes, we can still see things that are right in front of us.

You also need eyes to breathe. The air we breathe in goes into our body through the nose and mouth. The air then goes into our body, and our body uses it to make more air. This is how we keep our bodies going.

So, eyes are very important. They help us see the world, help us stay safe, and help us understand the world around us.
