An interest is a thing you buy or own. It is not a person, but a thing that helps you do your work. When you buy a car, you pay money to the person who makes the car. You pay them so you can use it to get to work, to play, or to go on a trip.

If you love a game, you might buy a new game to play with your friends. This is an interest because it makes you happy. It helps you think about the game and plan your next moves.

Sometimes, if you work very hard, you might get an interest from a job. This is different. You are the one who has the job. You work for a person or a business. You do your job every day, and your work helps the person or business make money.

A person might have an interest in a movie. They like watching movies because they tell a good story. They might see the movie many times and still enjoy it. They might even talk to friends about it or write a story about it.

You can also have an interest in a place. If you live near a big city, you might have an interest in the city. You like to walk around the city, see the sights, and talk to people who live there. You might even go to see a show or a music eventually.

An interest can be big or small. You might have a small interest, like a favorite food. Or you might have a big one, like a love for a game or a movie. Whatever it is, an interest is something you like and enjoy.
