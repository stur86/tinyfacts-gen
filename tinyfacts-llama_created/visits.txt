To visit someone means you go to their house. You go there to see them. You might go to a city or a town where they live. You might go to a place near their house, like a park or a store.

You might go with a friend. You and your friend go to see a movie. You go to a movie house and watch a story. You might also go to see a game. You watch a game with many people on a big box.

You might also visit a place that has many things. You go to a store that has food and drinks. You buy food and drinks. You buy things to eat for the next day.

Sometimes, you visit a place that is very old. You go to a house that was built a long time ago. You see old things like old pictures and old words. You learn about how people lived before.

When you visit someone, you might feel happy. You see them and you talk. You share stories with each other. You can laugh and be friends. You are together and you have fun.
