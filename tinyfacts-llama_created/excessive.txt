To be too much can be very bad. When you do something, you do it a lot. You might do it a hundred times. But if you do it so much that you are not safe, or if you are not good for anyone else, it is an over thing.

Imagine you love to play a game. You play it for many hours each day. You get very good. You can beat your friends very easily. But if you keep playing too much, you start to hurt your body. You might get pain in your arms or legs. You might even get a cold.

It is the same with money. If you spend too much money on things you do not need, you have an over thing. You need to be carefully how you spend your money so you do not go over.

Sometimes, you might feel this way with your mind. If you are very happy all the time, you might be an over thing. You might not feel sad when you do not feel happy. You might feel like everything is going well all the time.

To be an over thing is not always bad. You might be an over thing because you are very strong. You can lift many heavy things. You might be an over thing because you are very good at building things.

But if you are an over thing, you might not be able to stop. You keep doing the thing. You keep going until you are very tired. Your body might hurt, and your mind might feel strange.

To stop being an over thing is to be safe. You need to stop doing the thing that is making you feel this way. You need to rest and let your body get better.
