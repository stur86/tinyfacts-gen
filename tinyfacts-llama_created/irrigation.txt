To water something is to give it water. To put water on a green thing, a tree or a field, makes it grow. It helps the green thing to grow big and strong.

You put water on a dry place. The ground is hard and hard to walk on. The water makes the ground soft and lets you walk on it. You can walk and play on the water. When you put water on a green place, the green thing gets more water and grows more.

We use water to grow food. We put water on the ground where we grow food like corners and green things. This water gives the green things the food they need. The food makes the green things grow big and strong.

We also use water to keep our houses clean. We put water on the floor to keep it wet. This makes the floor not hard and makes it easier to walk on. We put water on the windows to keep the cold out. This keeps the cold from coming in and makes our house warm.

To water a car is another way. We put water on the floor of the car to keep it wet. This keeps the floor not hard and makes the car go. When the car is wet, it is easier to start and moves around better.

When we say a field is watered, we mean that water is coming from a big thing. This thing goes over the field to the ground. The water goes down and fills the ground. When the ground is filled, the field is watered. This keeps the ground soft and makes it grow green.

In a city, water is often put on the streets. We put water on the ground so cars can move around. We also put water on the streets to keep the water from going over the ground. This keeps the water in one place and makes it safe for people to walk.

In some places, people put water on the ground to help animals. They put water on the ground where there are animals that live in the ground. The water makes the ground soft and lets the animals move. This helps them find food and stay safe.
