The word wake can mean many different things. It can start the day. When the sun is up, people and animals get up from their beds. This makes them feel ready to go.

Sometimes, when you are very tired, you might feel like you will never wake up. It is a bad feeling. But if you open your eyes and look around, you might feel happy to be living.

It can also mean something that is moving. If you are on a moving car, you are walking. If you are in the air, you are flying. When you see something that is moving very fast, you might feel scared.

In the day, you might hear the sound of a door opening. This is when someone comes into a room. You wake up to these sounds and it is a good time.
