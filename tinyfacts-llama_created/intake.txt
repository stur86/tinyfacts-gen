An old word you might see in a kitchen is called an interrupt. It does not mean a sudden stop. You can interrupt a person while they are talking. You might ask them a question. Then they stop what they are doing. This is a way to stop a conversation.

A different word is an interrupt. This is when you stop what is happening. You might walk into a room. You do not say a word. People look at each other. You have stopped the story or the people in the room. 

You can also say an interrupt happens when you do not let another person finish. You might shout or laugh. The other person tries to stop you. They say, you can not do that. You should let the other person finish before you tell your story. This is important when you talk to your friends or family.
