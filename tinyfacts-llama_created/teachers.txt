A teacher is a person who helps others learn. They stand in front of a group of people and tell them what to do. They show them how to do things. They also help them understand why things happen.

Teachers use their words and their body. They might say, walk this way, or stop right there. They might also show a picture or a thing. They want the people they teachers are with to learn and remember.

A teacher can be a person who is very good at a job. They might be a teacher in a school. They also can be a teacher who works in a big office. They help people who need to learn. They are like a very strong hand that gives people new ideas.

Sometimes people ask why we need teachers. They say, how do we know what to do if no one tells us? They say, we need people who can help us understand the world. We need people who can tell us why things happen and how to stay safe.

Teachers also help people who are not very good at things. They show them how to do small things. They help them feel safe in a new place. They help them learn to be kind and to say sorry.

It is very important to have teachers. They help us grow and learn. They show us how to be good people. They help us understand the world and how to stay safe.
