To send something is to put it in a box and give it to someone else. If you want a book, you can send it. You write the name of the book on a piece of paper. You tell the person where to find it.

The person you send it to reads the paper. They look at the name. They know which box to take it from. They carry the box to the place where people live. The person inside the box reads the paper. They tell the person to bring it to them.

Sometimes, a person sends money to someone else. They write the number of the person they want to pay. They write the money on a piece of paper. They give the paper to a person who runs a big computer. They tell the computer to use the money to pay for something or for a book.

When you get a letter, you can open it. You read the words inside. You might hear from a friend who lives far away. You might hear from a person you know. You can learn new things, laugh, or feel happy.

We also use this word to talk about how things move. When you send something, it is moving from one place to another. It goes through the air or through water. It is going to a different place where people live.
