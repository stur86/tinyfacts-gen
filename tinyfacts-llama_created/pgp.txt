Packed

To pack something means to put many things in a small box. This is so you can carry them with you. You might fill a box with clothes, food, or books. You put the things in the box so they are all together.

You can also pack your brain. If you have too many ideas, you might feel a lot of things at once. You might feel happy, sad, angry, and scared. You need to put the ideas into a way that makes you feel less many. This helps you stay calm.

You might pack a bag for a trip. You put your shoes, clothes, and a bottle of water in the bag. When you go to a new place, you feel ready. You are not nervous because you have your things.

Sometimes, you pack your heart. This means you put your love, your hope, and your fear into a small space. You might close your eyes and remember the good times. You feel safe because you are holding your heart.

You can also pack a computer. You put the computer and its parts in a box. This box is like a safe. It keeps everything inside. When you open the box, you see the computer ready to work. It is ready to show you pictures, play games, or write words.

In a way, we all pack our lives. We pack our time with things we do. We pack our money so we have enough for food and a home. We pack our friends and our family so they are near us. We pack our memories so we can look back on happy times.

When you pack something, you are making a way for it to reach where you want it to go.
