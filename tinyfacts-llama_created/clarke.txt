To make clear something means to show what is happening or what is going on in a way that is easy to understand. This can happen when you look at a picture of a city. You see the roads, the houses, and the cars. If you tell the story to a friend, they might not understand at first, but after you speak, they feel like they know what you mean.

Sometimes you might say something is clear. This means you can see it very well. You do not need to think about it much. You can understand what you are looking at or what someone is saying. If you are in a room and you hear a voice, the voice is clear. You do not need to listen very hard to hear it very well.

You can also say something is not clear. If you do not understand what is happening, it is not clear. The words do not make sense. You need more time to think about them. The people around you might be talking but you do not get what they mean.

If you are trying to tell someone something, you can use clear words. You use short words and you say them slowly. This makes it easier for the person to understand. When you are very clear, people feel they can trust you because they know what you mean.
