To trust someone means you believe they will do the right thing. It is about letting someone you do not know be in a position where they will help you if you ask.

Think about a person who helps you with your money. If you give them the money to spend, you are trusting them with your money. If they spend it wrong or do not give you back everything, you are very sad. You wanted them to help you, but instead, they hurt you.

In a school, there are people who help with the money. They are called teachers. They trust the money of the students and parents. They use it to pay for things like books and food for the kids. This is important because it shows that they believe the money is safe with them.

Some people trust their friends. They do not worry about what they do. They know their friends will always try to help them. This is a good feeling. It means you feel safe with them.

In a big group of people, you might have a person you trust to help you. If this person is not doing their job, the group might not work well together. They might fight or not listen to each other.

A person who is a teacher or a manager might also trust other people to do their job. They know that people with good hearts and good ideas can do a job well. This helps them feel safe and free to do their work.

To trust someone means you believe in them. It is a way to let your heart and mind work together.
