To have a wall is to have something that keeps things out. A wall is a big thing that stands up from the ground and across the sky. It helps keep people, animals, and things from going into a place. You can have a wall inside your home to keep your family safe.

You can also have a wall on your body. If you are afraid of a thing, you might have a wall in your chest. This makes you feel safe. You feel like the thing is too big to get past you.

Sometimes, a person has a wall to keep them safe while they are on a road. If a car comes too fast, the person might use their hands to hold onto the car. This is like having a wall inside their body.

In a game, a team might have a wall to stop the other team from getting the ball. They try to stay in front of the other team so they can not pass the ball to the other team.

You can also have a wall in your mind. This is when you feel fear or worry. It is like a big piece of wood that you hold inside your head. If you are afraid of something, you might feel like the world is going to end.
