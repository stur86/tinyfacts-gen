To have a strong thing means you have a lot of power inside you. This power is like a fire that can move and push. You can use this fire to do things that make you feel happy.

A person might have a strong body. Their arms are very strong, and they can lift heavy things. Their legs are very strong too. This makes them good at doing work that needs a lot of power.

A person might also have a strong mind. They can think very fast and remember a lot of things. This helps them learn new things and stay safe.

Sometimes, people have a strong voice. This voice can make people listen. A strong voice is important because it helps us feel heard.

You can also have a strong heart. This means you are kind and you care about other people. When you feel happy, you might want to share your good feeling with others. Your heart is like a box inside your body that keeps you safe.

When you have a strong body, mind, or heart, you feel like you can do anything. You might feel ready to start a new job or to play a game with your friends. You feel like you are the best.
