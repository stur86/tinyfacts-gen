To my space means you are the person you are. You are not like anyone else. You like the same things and you do them in the same way. You might like music that is different from the music of others. You might like to write stories. You might like to draw pictures.

When you are in the world, you have a space. This is your own place. You do not have to worry about what others think. You are free to do what you want. You can be as happy or sad as you want. You can write about your life or tell other people about it.

But sometimes you might feel like you do not fit in with the world. You might feel like you are the only one who understands you. This is okay. You do not have to change who you are. Your space is still there.

The word my space is important. It tells us that you are the one who matters. You are the one who gets to be the person you are. You are the one who gets to make your own way.

Sometimes we feel like we are missing out. We look at other people on their spaces and feel a little sad. But the truth is, everyone has their own space. Even if you see someone else, you do not have to feel like you have to be the same. You can still be happy and be different. Your space is still there, and it is still important.
