When you eat, you take in the good parts of the food. Your body breaks down these parts into tiny bits that can live inside you. You use these tiny bits to build your own body. You also take in the bad parts of the food. These parts go into your body and make you sick. If you do not get ridden of them all, they can make you very hurt.

Different foods are good for different people. Some foods are easy to eat and give you a lot of power. Others are better for you when you have a cold. Some foods can make you feel very full and help you stay in bed. Other foods are better for you if you are very old or very young.

You also need to eat to give your body the things it needs to move and stay on the ground. When you do not eat, you feel very tired and your body does not work well. It is like your car without gasps of air. If you do not eat, you will feel very bad and might even go to the hospital.
