To go through something is to pass from one thing to another. You start at the first thing and end at the last thing. Between each thing you go, there is a part that goes in one way and a part that goes in the other way.

Think of a long way. You are walking. You are going in one direction. You are not going in the other direction. You are going from one end to another. You have walked a long way. You have reached the other end.

You can go through many things in your life. You might go through school, go to the store, or go to a far away place. You go through each thing so you can learn how to do it. You go through the hard parts so you can be strong and ready for the next thing.

Sometimes you might go through a big change. You might feel very sad. You might want to cry. You could also feel happy. You might want to jump up and down. You are moving through a big area of your life. You are going through many different things and learning how to handle them.

To go through something can also mean to learn. If you want to learn how to play a new game, you are going through it. You are going through the steps. You are going from not knowing to knowing.

A word like this can help us understand the world. It is a way to talk about going through things.
