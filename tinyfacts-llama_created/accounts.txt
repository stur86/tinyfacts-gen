An answer is how you do things. When you do something, you have a number. This number is like a piece of paper that shows how much money you have. You can use this number to decide how to spend the money you have.

An answer is like a word that helps you understand other things. If you find something, you can say what it is. If you ask a person a question, you are giving them an answer. If a person asks you a question and you do not know the answer, you are the one who is asking, not the one who is being asked.

You can also have an answer to a problem. If you have a problem that you do not know how to fix, you ask someone else for help. They might give you the right answer, or they might give you a new idea that helps you fix the problem.

Sometimes, an answer is a story. If you read a book, you might find answers to your questions in the story. Or, if a doctor tells you that a person is going to be better, that is an answer.
