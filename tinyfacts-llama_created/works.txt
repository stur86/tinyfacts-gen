To work means you do something. You are busy. You might be in a room with many other people. You are making things, or you are fixing things. You may be moving things from one place to another. You are also doing things that need to be done.

If you work on a computer, you are doing a job that the computer can do. You are writing numbers and words. You are making the computer do what you want. If you work in a school, you are learning new things. You are doing things to help your teachers and other students.

When you work, you might feel tired. You might have to stand for a long time. You might feel a little sad. But you are doing important work. You are helping people and making life better.

You can also work with a friend. You do things together. You laugh and talk. You share what you do. You help each other. This is a great way to spend time with your friends.

To work is to do. It is a way to use your body, your mind, and your time. It is a way to make things happen.
