A class is a group of people with the same job. Each person is a person in the group. A person who works as a teacher can be a teacher in many different schools. All of these people are part of the same group.

Imagine you are in a school with many kids. You are not a teacher, but you know many of them. If you are the one who reads to the kids before the teacher talks, you are the first person. You are not the teacher, but you help everyone.

A class is like a big group of people who do the same job. You can have many classes. One class is for kids who are very young. Another class is for kids who are a little bit older. Then there are classes for kids who are even older. There are also classes for people who are very good at a job.

A teacher is the person who leads the group. They help everyone learn. The teacher might give orders, but they also listen to what everyone says. They make sure everyone follows the same way.

When a group of people from different places or from different jobs all work together, they form a class. Everyone is important, and everyone helps each other.
