Killing someone does not always lead to a better life. What is important is what you do after you do the thing you are doing. When you do something bad, you have to face the bad that happens to you. It is like when you eat food. If you eat food that is bad, you get sick. You have to go to the doctor. You have to face the problem. You are not in the same place as before. You have to start again.

This is how life works. We all do things in our lives. We do good things, and we do bad things. When we die, we do not just leave our body. We leave the things we have done. If we have done good things, we might go to a place with good things. If we have done bad things, we might go to a place with bad things. This is how the world works. We can not change what we are, but we can change what we do after we are gone.
