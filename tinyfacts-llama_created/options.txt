An offer is when someone lets you know they are ready to do something. It is like a way to say, here is what I want, and I am ready to start.

Imagine you are playing a game. You do not know if the game will be easy or hard. You have two kinds of offers. One is a sure thing. If you do this, you will get a small thing. The other is a not sure thing. If you do this, you are not sure if you will get a small thing.

An offer can also be about money. You have to give some money to the person. If you give them money now, they will give you more money later. They might say, here is the money, but you have to wait a little while to get it.

Sometimes, people offer something that is not clear. You might hear a word and do not know what it means. The person who gave the word might say, this is what I mean. They are giving you a way to understand.

An offer can be about a job. A person might say, I am looking for a person to help me. They are offering the job. This means the person is letting you know they want you to be the one.

You can also offer something. If you want a friend, you can say, I need a friend. This is an offer to be friends. If the person you are talking to says yes, you have a friend.

An offer is important because it lets us know that someone is ready to do something. It is like a start to a new thing.
