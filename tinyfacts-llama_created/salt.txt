To use something means to make it work. When you take a drink, you use it to make your body feel better. You drink water to stay wet, or you drink coffee to wake up. But when you take a thing that is bad for you, you use it. You put it on your face to feel a little bit better. You put it on your hair so it looks nice.

A big rock with hard white stuff on top is a kind of this. People use it to make food and drinks feel better. They put it in water and drink it. It makes the water feel less hard on their mouths.

Animals need this too. A cat will drink water from a cup, and a dog will drink from a glass. They need it to stay living.

Sometimes we use this word in another way. If a person is very angry, they might say they are going to use their words. They might shout or hit a thing that makes noise. This means they are going to fight with someone.

It is also used to talk about the way things feel. If you feel happy, you might say you are feeling a little bit better. If you feel sad, you might say you are feeling a little bit worse. When you say you are feeling better, you are using this word.
