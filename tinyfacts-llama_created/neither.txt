To be both means to have two different states at once. You can not be one way and another way at the same time. When you are not this one thing, you are not this other thing either.

Think of a day. You can be happy or you can be sad. You are not happy and you are not sad at the same time. You are one thing, and then you are another thing. This is because you are the same person with different ways of feeling.

You can also be in two places at once. If you have a phone in your hand and a book in your bag, you are not holding the phone and you are holding the book. You are both things.
