A group of people that makes the big ideas of a land. They sit together in one house or one building. They talk about what they want. They decide how money should be spent and what the land should do.

The two houses are different. One house sits in a city and the other sits in a far away place. They have different ways of making their voices heard. The city house people usually get to speak more often.

When the houses meet, they try to find a way that works for everyone. They might talk about a problem, like a big problem with a group of people who are making things hard for the rest of the land. They might also talk about making money grow, so everyone has more money.

The people who make the big ideas of the land are called the people of the land. They are the ones who get to decide how the land works. They are the ones who write the very words that everyone follows.

Sometimes, the people of the land have to talk to the city people. The city people might say, a long time ago, we did not want the land people to have as much power. Now they say, we should let the land people decide their own way.

There are also many other ways the people of the land work together. Some cities have the power to make their own things, like cars or food. Some places have the power to decide how they want to live. Some places have the power to make their own money.

The big idea of the land is to keep everyone safe and happy. It is like a big family that looks out for each other.
