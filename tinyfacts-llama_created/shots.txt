Shots can mean two different things.

One way is when you take many small pictures very quickly. You might be playing a game with a ball. If you hit the ball many times, the picture in your hand will be full of small pieces. You can then look at all those pieces and see how many times you hit the ball. If you hit the ball many times, you had many shots.

The other way is about drinking a drink. If you drink a shot, it is a small drink. One shot is like one tiny drink. You can have many shots in one night. Some people drink shots to feel happy. They might drink one shot and then drink another. This can feel very good. But if you drink too many shots, you might get a tiring feeling. Your head might feel very heavy. You might also feel a bit sick.
