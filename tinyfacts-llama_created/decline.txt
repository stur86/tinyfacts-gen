To move away from something

When you are happy and you do many things, the world around you often gets more nice and fun. This is a good thing. But when you start to move away from what you like, you might feel a bit strange. You might start to feel sad or worried because you are leaving something you love behind.

Imagine you are standing in a big field of green. You love running across the green ground. But then, you see a big, tall tree. You feel a bit scared because the tree is standing in your way. You want to go around it, but the tree looks very strong. You start to feel afraid. This is like your heart is saying, I am not happy anymore. I want to go back to what I know.

Sometimes, people have to do this. When a business starts to lose money, the people running it might feel sad. They might want to stop, but they know they have to keep going. They might try to find a way to make things better. This is like the tree. It is standing in the way, but it is not gone. It is still there, and it will always be there.

To move away from something is a way to change. It is a way to say, I am not happy with what I have now. I want something new. This change can be good or bad. It can bring a new feeling or a new problem.
