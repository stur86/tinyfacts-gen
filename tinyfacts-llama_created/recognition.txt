To recognize something is to know it. You see it, hear it, or feel it. Your brain does this. It is like a lock and a key. The lock is the thing you see or hear. The key is the word or sound that you remember.

When you see a dog, you recognize it. You know it is a dog. Your brain has the word for dog. It is the key, and you know it is the dog. This happens with many things in your life. You can see a friend and know who they are. You can hear a song and know it is your favorite song. You can smell a food you love. Your brain is always looking for what you know.

If you do not see a dog, you do not recognize it. If you do not hear a song, you do not recognize it. If you do not smell a food, you do not recognize it. But if you see a dog, hear a song, and smell food, you know the thing is there. Your brain is always trying to put the pieces together so you know what is real.

You can also recognize yourself. You see your face in a picture. You know it is you. You know your hair, your eyes, your nose, and your face. This is a kind of memory. You are always looking at yourself so you can remember who you are.

Sometimes, you recognize something in your mind that is not there. You might see a face in a crowd, but you do not see the person. You are still in your head. You are still thinking about the thing you see. Your brain is working even when you are not looking at the thing. This is how you know things even when they are not right in front of you.
