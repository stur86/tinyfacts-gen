To get something done is a big thing. It usually happens when many people agree with a plan or a way of doing things.

Imagine you want to build a house. You have a plan. You pick the right wood, the right kind of door, and the right kind of wall. You show the plan to your friends and they say, okay, we agree. Then everyone works together to build the house. If you did not follow the plan, the house would not be built right.

A person can get something done by getting it from a group. They do not have to be the one who starts the work. The group says, it is okay. They give the person the power to do the work. Then the person works. If the person does not follow the power, they are not doing the work.

In business, a person can get a job done. They might not be the one who starts the work, but they are the one who follows the plan and does the work. If they do not follow the plan, the work is not done.

In a school, a person can get a thing done that is not right. They might have the power to change the plan, but they must follow the plan anyway. If they do not follow the plan, they can get in trouble.

To get something done is to show that you are a good person. If you do something right, people know they can trust you.
