To make something hard

To make a hard thing is to put things together that are already wet. You start with wet stuff and add more of the same stuff. This keeps happening until the stuff becomes hard to the touch.

A long time ago, people made roads by putting wet stuff on the ground. They would put wet stuff on the ground and let it dry. When it was dry, they would put more wet stuff on top. This made the ground hard and easy to walk on. This was very important for people who wanted to move from one place to another.

You can also make a hard thing by putting a lot of small pieces of wood together. You start with one piece of wood and put more and more pieces on top of it. When all the pieces are together, the wood is hard and can hold many more things on top of it.

Some things are made to stay hard for many years. If you keep the right stuff in the right place, a wood box or a strong chair can stay hard for a long time. People use these things to keep things safe.

A small hard thing that you hold in your hand is a tiny thing. Animals have these too. They use them to carry food or to hold their young.
