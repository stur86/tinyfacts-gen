To make something new again is a good thing. We use things every day. Cars, computers, and houses need power to work. But when we run out of power, we need to get more power.

There are two kinds of power. One kind is from the sun. The sun is very bright and makes a lot of power. When we use this power, we do not hurt the sun. The power is good for the world because it does not make the air hot.

The other kind is from the ground. When we use this power, the ground gets hot. This makes the air hot too. This is bad for the world. It makes the air hurt people who breathe it in.

We need to find power that is from the sun or from the ground, so we do not hurt the world. We use power from the sun to power things like homes, schools, and hospitals. We use power from the ground to power things like power lines and big computers.

We can also make power from the air. We use big things that catch the air and turn it into power. This power is good for many things.

Making power from the sun and from the ground is called a clean power. It does not hurt the world. It helps keep the air clean and the water clean. We can use this kind of power for many years.

So, power that is made from the sun or from the ground is the same thing as clean power. It is good for the world and for our homes.
