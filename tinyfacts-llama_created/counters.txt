A counter is a thing that you use to keep track of how many of something there are. It is often used with numbers.

Imagine you have a box of numbers. You start with one number. Then you add one more number to the box. The number in the box now is two. You have two numbers. This thing helps you keep the numbers straight.

Sometimes you use a counter for games. You might have three balls. You put the balls into a box. Then you keep counters on each ball. When you want to know how many balls you have, you look at the counters on each ball. This helps you know the right number.

In a different kind of game, you might use a counter to keep track of how many points you have. If you hit a ball into a box, you get points. You keep a counter for each point. When you finish playing the game, you look at the counter to see how many points you have.

A counter can also be a thing that you hold. If you have a hundred books and you want to know how many you have, you might keep a counter in your hand. When you finish reading a book, you put the book away. You have now found the right number.

There are many types of counters. Some are small and easy to carry. Others are big and heavy. Some are made of paper or wood. Some are made of strong stuff that is easy to break. They are all used to keep things straight and help us understand how many of something there are.
