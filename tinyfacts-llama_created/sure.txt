To be sure means you are very sure about something. When you are sure, you do not feel any fear or doubt. You know the thing is true.

Think about a game. You are sure you will hit a ball. You have watched the ball move before and you know it will go in the next time you throw. You do not worry that the ball might go into the air instead of going in. You are sure.

You can also be sure about a word. If you know that a word means a thing, you are sure. You do not wonder if it is right. You know it is right.

Sometimes, you might be sure about a plan. You have made it before and it works well. You know every step and you are sure the plan will end well. You do not feel afraid that something will go wrong.

If you are sure of something, you can trust it. You do not feel afraid to ask a question or to try something new. You know that the thing you want is there.

Being sure is important. It helps us feel safe. When we are sure, we do not worry that we might be wrong. We know we are right.
