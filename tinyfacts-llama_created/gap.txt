A space is when two things are very far away from each other. Imagine you are standing on a road with your friend. You are in the middle of the road. There is a big tree in front of you. The tree is much taller than you. If you look up, you can see the sky. But if you look down at the ground, you see many other houses and streets. This is a space. It is between the two houses.

A space can also be between a person and a thing. If a person is very tall, there is a big space between them and a chair. When you look up, you see how high the person is. There is also a space between the ground and the sky. You can see the blue sky and the white white things in the sky.

In a very big room, there are many spaces. Some spaces are small. Some spaces are very big. You can stand in one space and walk to another. You might feel like you are in a big world.
