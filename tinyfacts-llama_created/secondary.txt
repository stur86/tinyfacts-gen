A group is a way we work together with many people.

Imagine you are in a school. There are many children in your class. Some children are older than others. The older children help the younger children. They do not teachers things, but they help the people who are still learning.

If you have a dog, you need to take it to the house. The dog does not walk on its own. The person who gives you the dog helps it go to the house. Later, you learn how to walk the dog. You become a better dog walker.

A group can be many things. We use this word to talk about many people who work together. The group makes a plan. They work on a big job. They share what they know. They are important because they are one team.

In a school, the students in the second group learn. They are not the first group. They are not the ones who first learn. They learn after the first group. The first group is usually the ones who start the school. They show the way. The second group follows them.
