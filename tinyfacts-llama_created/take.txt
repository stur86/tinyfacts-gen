To take something is to get it. You might take a book from a friend. You might take a piece of food from a box. You might take a thing that is not yourself.

When you take something, you are taking it away from another person. You are not keeping it for yourself anymore. You are letting it go.

Sometimes you take a small thing. You might take a ball. You might take a book. You might take a dog. You can also take a moment. You can take a time to think. You can take a deep breath. You can take a step.

You can also take something that is hard. You might take a problem. You might take a hard thing to do. You might take your heart. You might take your mind.

When you take something, you are using it. You are using your body or your mind. You are using your voice. You are using your hands.

You can also take something that is not there. You can take a dream. You can take a feeling. You can take a moment that feels like a long time.

In a big way, to take is to change a person. When you take something, you are taking away what is already there. You are adding something new. You are changing the way you feel.

You can also take a break. If you are working very hard, you might need to take a break. You take a time to rest. You take a moment to think. You take a deep breath.

Taking is an important thing. It helps us to move. It helps us to grow. It helps us to learn. It helps us to change our lives.
