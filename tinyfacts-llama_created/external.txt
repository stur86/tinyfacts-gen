An outside thing is something that is not inside a person or a building. It is the part of a thing that is on the outside. This can be a thing you see or a thing you touch.

Think of a person. Inside, you are thinking. But the wall of your house is an outside thing. You do not touch the wall, but you see it every day. You hear the sound of the wind when the window is open. You feel the cold when it is very cold outside.

You can also talk about things that are far away. If you are in your room, you can hear a song from another house. This is an outside thing because it is not in your room.

Some things are both inside and outside. If you have food, it is inside your body. But you can also see the food. This is both inside and outside, because it is both within you and outside your body.

There are also things that are only on the outside. You can wear a dress or a shirt. These are not inside at all. They are only on your body.

In this way, an outside thing can be something very small, like a tiny piece of paper, or something very big, like a bright big sun in the sky.
