A mother is a person who gives life to a baby. She is the one who holds the child when they are very small and keeps them safe. A mother is very important because she helps the child grow. She gives them food to eat and keeps them warm when it is cold outside.

A mother is not just about giving life. She also does many other things. She does not like to tell bad things. She wants everyone to be kind to her and her family. She loves her child very much and wants them to be happy.

A mother is a woman. This means she is a woman and has a body. She also has a warm heart inside her. She feels love for her child all the time. A mother is the one who tells the child when they are scared. She is there to listen to them when they talk.

Some mothers are very strong. They work hard to keep their family going. They do not want anyone to feel alone. They are always ready to help. They also teachers how to be a good person. They show them how to be kind and how to share their money.
