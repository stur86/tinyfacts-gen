A way to show things is important. Think of the time you watch a movie. You see a story. The story is in many pieces. Each piece has a number on it. This number is like a key. When you see the whole story, the numbers on the pieces tell you which ones are which. This is how one person sees the story. Another person might see the story in a different way.

Things that show things are like these numbers. They are many and can be different. If you want to show how to make a cup of coffee, you might use many different ways. One way might be to put hot water in a cup. Another way might be to put the cup in the hot water. You could also say you use a coffee maker.

Sometimes you want to show how to do something. You might say, I will show you how to make a book. You show them step by step. You tell them what to do. They see the steps and follow them. This is how you learn something new.

A computer has many ways to show things. You can use pictures, words, or numbers. Some computers show pictures. Others show words. Others show numbers. You tell the computer what to do with the numbers. The computer follows your orders.

A computer is like a person. It listens to what you say and does what you tell it to do. It follows many ways to show things. You can give it many orders. The computer does what you say.
