Your teeth are hard parts in your mouth. They help you eat and drink. When you bite, they push food into your mouth. Your teeth have tiny points at the front that catch food so you can not eat it. Then, they push the food through your mouth and down to your stomach.

Your teeth are very strong. They help you break many kinds of food. They also help keep your mouth clean. If you do not clean your teeth, you can get many kinds of bad stuff in your mouth.

Every person has many teeth. Some people have many more than others. When you grow up, your teeth start coming out of your mouth. The first ones are very small. They are like tiny points. As you get older, the bigger ones come out. They get harder and stronger.

You need to clean your teeth many times a day. You use a wet thing to wipe them. You also use a thing to make your teeth strong. This helps keep your mouth clean and your teeth safe.
