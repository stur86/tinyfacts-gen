To be strong and not give up is a great thing to do. People can be very afraid, but when they are not afraid, they act. This is what we mean by being strong.

Sometimes, you might feel afraid of a big animal or a loud noise. You might want to run away. But if you are very strong, you do not run. You stand still and look at the animal. You look it in the eye, and you do not move. This is the kind of person we call a strong one.

Being strong is not just about not being afraid. It is about doing what is right, even if it is hard. When you work hard and try to do something that you do not want to do, you build your own kind of power. You become a person who is not afraid, but who is also not giving up.

You can be a strong person in many ways. You can be a person who is not afraid of the dark when you are a child. You can be a person who is not afraid to try something new when you are an old man. You can be a person who is not afraid to cry when you are hurt.

When you are a strong person, you make other people feel safe. You make them feel like they are not alone. You tell them that everything will be okay. You are a good friend, a good brother, or a good sister.

Being strong is a very important thing. It is about being able to face your fears, to try new things, and to stay in the middle of a fight. It is about being able to say, even when you are afraid, that you will do what is right.
