To break something into many parts is called a break. This happens when a big thing is cut into smaller pieces. You can see the pieces. You can see how they are all together before and how they are all together now.

Imagine you are a person in a big city. You want to go to the park. If you walk through the big street, it is one long line. But if you walk on a wide road, it is many short lines. Each short line is a piece. You can go to your home, to a friend, or to the park.

Sometimes, you break things so they are easier to carry. If you have a big box with many things in it, you can open the box and pick out what you need. You can then carry the box without having to carry all of the things inside it at once.

You can also break things to make them stronger. If you cut a piece of wood, you can make it harder. This is because the wood is not as hard for someone to break now.
