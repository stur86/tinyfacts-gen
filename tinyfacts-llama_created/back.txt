To back something means to move behind it. Think of a car. Imagine you are on a long ride with a friend. If you want to go to a place, you need to walk or run before you reach it. You might feel a bit nervous because you are moving away from your home. This is like backing. You are moving away from what is already there.

You can also back a story. If you have a very sad story, you might want to tell it again so you remember how you felt. You are backing your own story so you can remember it better.

In other words, to back something means to move away from it. It is about moving into the past, or moving away from what you are doing now.

Think of a person who is afraid of a big animal. They might not want to be near the animal. This is because they are backing away from the thing that makes them feel scared. They want to stay behind their own body so they do not get hurt.

You can also back a plan. If you think a plan is not going to work, you can back it. This means you are saying, yes, the idea might be wrong. You are backing the idea so you do not have to be the one who has to deal with it if it does not work.

So, backing is about moving away from something. It is about being ready to change direction if needed, or about keeping your back to what is already happening.
