A long piece of wood is used to sit on. You can put your feet on it. This is a place to rest. It is often in a room where people eat. There is a soft cover on the top. You can sit on it with your back to a wall. This helps you feel safe.

In a small room, people sit on the floor. They can talk or read. In a big room, people sit on the floor with their feet on the floor. In a room with chairs, you usually sit on the hard seat. You can also sit on a soft high thing with a cover. This is a good place to be if you want to be close to a person.
