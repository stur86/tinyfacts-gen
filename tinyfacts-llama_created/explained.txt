To explain something means you tell it to someone so they can understand it.

Imagine you have a big box that holds many small balls. You want to tell a friend why the boxes are important. You might say, 

These boxes help us carry things. They keep the things inside and let us move around. You see, if we did not have these boxes, we would not be able to go to school or run around the park.

When you explain something, you are not just saying it. You are also showing how it works. You might show pictures or make a short story. This helps the person see the idea and remember it later.

Sometimes, we explain things for a reason. We might say, 

This is how we do things. If you do not understand, you can ask me for help.

We also explain things when we are afraid. If you are afraid of a dog, you might say, 

The dog looks like a big animal. It has a long face and teeth. It might move quickly. This helps you feel safe. You do not need to be afraid anymore.

In every life, we explain many things. We explain why we are happy or why we are sad. We explain what happened and how it made us feel.
