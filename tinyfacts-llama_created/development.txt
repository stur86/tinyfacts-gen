To grow is to change. When a baby is small, it is new. As it gets older, it becomes bigger. The baby learns new ways to walk. The baby learns how to eat. The baby learns how to talk.

Things can also grow in a different way. An idea might start as a small thought. Over time, it becomes a plan. The plan turns into a big plan that works for many people. The plan makes a big change.

In the world, people grow too. As they get older, they learn new things. They become stronger. They learn how to work with others. They learn how to make things and how to keep things safe.

When we grow, we become more like the people around us. We learn to be kind. We learn to be strong. We learn to work together.
