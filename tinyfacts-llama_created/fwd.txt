When you are talking about moving things, you might say you are moving forward. To do this, you start at one place and go to another. You do not stop in the middle. You keep going until you reach the other side.

You can also use this word in a different way. If you are a person, you might say you are moving forward in your life. You are doing new things, learning, and growing. This means you are moving in a good way.

In the world of computers, you can also use this word. When people send things to each other, the computer moves the words from one place to another. This is how the computer works. It is always moving, never stopping.
