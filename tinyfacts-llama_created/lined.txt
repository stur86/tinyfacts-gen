To line something means to make it look nice and easy to hold. You can line a piece of paper, a table, or a box. You put a soft thing between the lines. This makes the thing look nice and makes it easy to carry around.

Imagine a line of people waiting to go into a big building. Each person is on a line. When you go inside, you step onto the line. You walk down the line and you get inside. You do not have to jump over the people on the line. The people on the line help you by standing still and letting you pass.

You can also line a picture. You take many pictures of the same thing. You put them on a long thing so they look nice together. You can also line a room with soft things. This helps people feel calm and safe.

Sometimes, you line a wall with pictures of happy times. This makes the room look happy and fun. It helps people feel happy when they look at the pictures.
