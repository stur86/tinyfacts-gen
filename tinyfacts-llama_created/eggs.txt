Eyes are things that you see with. They are on the head of a living thing. The eyes help you look at what is in front of you.

A baby animal has big round eyes at the top. As the baby grows, its eyes become smaller and rounder, and they turn into points. The eyes are very important because they let the animal see what is happening.

Animals have different kinds of eyes. Some have eyes that are very far away, like a look at a house across a street. Other animals have eyes that are very near, like a look at a tiny thing that is moving. Some animals have eyes that are both near and far, like a look at a thing that is very high up and a look at a thing that is very far away.

People can also see with their eyes. They can look at things that are in front of them, see how they look, and see if they are happy or sad. When you look at a picture, your eyes see the lines and colors and the picture comes together.
