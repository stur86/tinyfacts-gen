To look forward to a thing is to think about it. When you do this, you are making plans for the days to come. You are thinking about what might happen next.

Think of a place. You want to see a big green water that is very high in the sky. You want to see the water cover the ground. You are looking forward to this. You are thinking about what you will see.

You can also look forward to money. If you work hard and do well, you might get a lot of money. This is a good thing. You are thinking about the money you will have in the next few months.

A person might also look forward to a big eventually. This is when many people are planning to do the same thing at the same time. They are all looking forward to it. They are making plans and are ready.

Sometimes, to look forward to something is to be afraid. If something bad happens, you might feel afraid. This is because you know what is coming. You are thinking about what will happen next.
