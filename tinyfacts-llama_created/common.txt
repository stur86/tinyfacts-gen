To be the same means you do not change. Most people are the same as their friends. Their words are the same. They drink the same drinks. They like the same games. They love the same movies. This is what we mean when we say a thing is the same as something else.

Sometimes, you might say your friend is the same as you. You have the same hair, the same clothes, the same face. You are the same person. You both love the same books, the same music, the same food. You both like the same games.

But things are not always the same. A cat is not the same as a dog. A dog is a different animal. Cats are more like other cats, and dogs are more like other dogs. They have different further parts, and they do different things.

If something is the same for everyone, it is a good thing. It is like a big group of people who all do the same thing. But if something is not the same for everyone, it is a bad thing. It is like a group of people who all do different things. They are not the same.

Sometimes, you might see a person who looks like you. They are the same age, they have the same hair, they wear the same clothes. They might even talk like you. In this case, you are the same person. But if you do not know them, you are not the same. You are a different person.

So, the word is about how you change when you move from one place to another. It is about what you have inside that keeps you the same.
