A thing that happens many times in a line can be a part of a story. A person might have a long story that is made up of many years. Or, you might have a long listened music that goes over and over again. If you listen to a long song, it is like a long part of a story.

You can also talk about a set of things that happen in one place. If you are going to a new city, you might see many different things. There is a new building. There is a new food that you try. There is a new place to play. These are all parts of a place. They help you know what the place is like.

If you have a group of people who do something, you can call this a group. It is a way to talk about a set of people who are in one place at the same time.
