A group of people who are in a line of work is called a group. If you have a job in a big building, and there is a person who is the top one, and then people in the middle, and then people at the bottom, that is a group. This group can have different jobs, like one person who is the top one and another person who is in the middle, and another person at the bottom.

Imagine you have a house with many rooms. The person who is the top one in the house is the father. The father does not have a room next to him. The people who are above him have a room next to him, and then people have rooms next to them. This is a way of looking at how people are put in order.
