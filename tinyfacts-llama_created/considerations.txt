When you think about something, you often look at different things. You might think about how you feel. You might think about what you want to do tomorrow. You might think about what you need to buy.

A person who helps you might ask you these kinds of questions. They want to know if you are sure. They want to make sure you know what to do.

Consider is like standing in front of a big wall. You need to look at the wall before you can move. You look at the wall to see if it is strong. You see that it is tall. You see that it is round. You might think, is this wall safe to stand on? You are looking at the wall to decide what to do.

You can also consider a place. If you want to go to a new city, you need to think about the place. Do you like the way the light looks there? Do you like the food? Do you like the people? You are considering the city.

Sometimes, you need to consider many things at once. You are trying to decide if you should go to a movie or stay home. You need to think about how you feel. You need to think about how you will spend your time. You need to think about how this will make you feel in the long run.

Consider is also used when you are thinking about how to act. If you know you are going to say something bad, you might consider how your words will be seen. You might think, should I be quiet? Should I say nothing at all? You are considering what to do.

In a big group, people often have to consider many things at once. They have to think about how to act. They have to think about how to help each other. They have to think about how to make sure everyone is safe. They are considering the whole group.

So, to consider something is to think about it. It is to look at it and say, is it right for me?
