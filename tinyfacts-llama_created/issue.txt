To have an idea about something is to know what it is. It is when you try to figure out why something happens. An idea is like a small thing in your mind. You think about a thing and you come up with a way to do it.

If you have an idea about something, you can start to act. You might want to do something because you think it is a good idea. You might write a story about it or talk to a friend about it. This is how you move your life.

Sometimes, you might have an idea that you do not want to act on. You might feel afraid or sad. This is when you are worried about what will happen. You might think about how you will feel later. This is the start of a problem.

There are also things that happen when there is a problem. You might have an idea about what is wrong. You might talk to a doctor or a teacher. You might go to a place where people work together to fix the problem. This is when you are trying to find a way to make things better.

Every day, you have many ideas. Some of them you act on and some of them you do not. Ideas help us learn and grow.
