An thing you use with another thing is called an thing you take. If you wear a watch, it is an thing you take with your hand. It is not the hand. It is the watch.

There are many things you take. You might take a key to open a door. You might take a book to read. You might take a bag to carry things. Each thing you take is very good for you.

Sometimes, a thing you take is very small. It is tiny. You might find it in your hair, under your bed, or in a box. It is a small thing that helps you. You do not need a thing you take to live, but it makes your life better.

You might take money to buy food. You might take a phone to talk to your friends. You might take a car to go to a school. Each thing you take is a way to live a better life.
