To hope means you feel that something will happen. It is like having a tiny bit of a good thing inside you. You do not know what will happen, but you believe it will be true.

Sometimes you hope for a big thing that is very far away. You think, maybe one day you will see a beautiful place or meet a person you love. This is a big hope because it is very far away.

You also hope for things that are near. You hope that a good friend will come to your house. You hope that you will do well on a big game. You hope that you will be happy and have many good days.

When you hope, you feel a little bit of a smile. You may even feel a tiny bit of fear because you do not know what will happen. But you stay calm because you believe the good thing will come.

People also hope when they want to do something. If you really want to go to a new city, you hope that you can move there. You hope that you will have a good job and a nice place to live. You hope that you will be happy and have many good days.

To hope is to be ready for the good things to happen. It is to trust that the world will make things right. It is like having a small, quiet voice inside you that says, I believe.
