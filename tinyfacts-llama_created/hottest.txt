The word hottest is used to talk about how very hot something is. When something is hottest, it is the very top part of it. It is very close to the fire inside the sun. This is why it feels like you want to move away from it.

Everything in the world gets hotter as time goes by. The air around us gets hotter as it moves away from the ground. Even things that are made of cold stuff can get very hot if the cold stuff gets hot too.

When the sun is very hot, it makes the air around it hot too. This makes the sky look bright and white. On a hot day, you might feel the sun on your skin even when it is in the middle of the day. This is because the sun is much closer to you than on a day when the sun is higher in the sky.

Some things that are very hot can burn you. A hot cup of coffee can hurt your skin if you touch it. A hot piece of food can make your mouth feel very bad if you eat it. You should be carefully around very hot things.
