The word in is a word you use to show where something happens. When you say,  I am in this room, you can see what I mean. You are showing that you are inside the room.

You can also say,  I am in school. This means you are in the place where you learn to be a person. It tells everyone who you are and what you do.

Sometimes you might be in a big room with many people. You might be sitting in a chair or standing next to a wall. You could be in the middle of a long line of people.

If you have something you want, you might say,  I want it. If you get it, you are in a better place. It makes you feel happy.

You can also say,  I am in a bad place. This means you are not feeling well or you are afraid. You might be feeling sad, angry, or scared.
