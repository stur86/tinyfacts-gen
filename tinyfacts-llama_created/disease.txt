A thing that makes you sick is a word for what happens inside your body. When you get a thing, your body uses its own parts to fight it. It makes red stuff and white stuff to fight the bad stuff that is making you sick.

Sometimes, a thing can be very bad. It can make you very tired or make your hair fall out. It can make your skin look red and hot. It can make you feel like you want to run away from the world.

Other times, a thing is not as bad. It might just make you feel a little strange. You might feel like you do not want to do things anymore or like you are thinking about things that make you sad. This is okay. You just need to rest and let your body fix it.

Sometimes, a thing is a sign that something is wrong with your body. It tells you that your body needs more food, more water, or more rest. It can also tell you if your body needs to go to a doctor.
