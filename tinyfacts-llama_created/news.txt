To share what is happening is to send words to many people. These words tell us about what is happening in a place far away. They tell us about people who are doing strange things, or about a big eventually that is happening on a far away place.

Sometimes people send these words to their friends. When you hear a word, you might think about what it means. You might want to know more. You might want to see what happens next.

You can also share these words with your family and other people in your town. This is a way to stay in touch with others who live far away. It is a way to be part of many places at once.

The word comes from old times when people sent words over long lines using the air. These lines were not like the ones we use today. They were slow and hard to use. But the word stays the same. It means to share what is happening.

In the past, many people read these words by hand. They would sit in a room and wait for the words to come down. Then they would write down what they heard. Today, computers and the air are used. People can send words fast and in many places at once.
