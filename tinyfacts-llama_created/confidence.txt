To have a lot of trust in something is to feel that you can do it. When you trust a person, you believe they are going to act in the right way.

Imagine you are a child. You feel very afraid to cross a street. But your mother tells you it is safe. She is saying, you can do it. This is a lot of trust. You start to feel more and more sure. You start to walk across the street without looking at the cars.

You also feel this way when you are in a game you love. You know you are going to do well. You trust yourself to make good moves. You believe you can stay in the game.

Sometimes, you might feel this way when you are speaking in front of a big group. You know you are going to say the right words. You trust your words. You feel that you will not say anything bad.

We also have this when we are about to do something. We might feel a little nervous. But then we do it and we feel a lot of power. We felt that we could do it.

We also feel this way when we are not afraid. If you are afraid of the dark, you feel a lot of fear. But if you are not afraid, you feel calm and safe. You feel that you are not going to get hurt. This is a lot of trust.

To have a lot of trust is important. It helps us feel safe. It helps us try new things. It helps us build strong relationships with other people.
