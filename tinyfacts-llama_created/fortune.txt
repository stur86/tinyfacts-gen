To have a good or bad turn of things in a life is to have a good or bad time. It means you do not get what you want because the things that happen do not go the way you want them to. You might get sick or hurt and not feel better. You might lose a job or a friend.

A good turn can happen many times. You might get a job you love and work there for many years. You get money and can buy things you need. You are happy and have many good days. This can make you feel like you are living a good life.

A bad turn can be very hard. You might lose your home, your money, or your job. You feel sad and angry. You might think you will never get a good turn again. This can make you feel like you are the only one who does not have a good turn.

People say that you must have good turns so you can get good turns. This is not always true. You might have a bad day and then get a good one.
