A very small thing with a long, hard part. You use it to cut things. It has a hard edge and a point. The point is very small and is where the cut happens. The hard edge is the hard part on the back. The hard part helps keep the cut from getting too big.

You can use a small one to cut food. You hold it near your food. The food is under the hard edge. When you press the hard edge on the food, the food comes out. You can also use a small one to cut through things like glass. The hard edge cuts through the glass, and the point goes into the glass. This makes a small cut.

Some people also use a small thing to cut their skin. They hold it near their skin. The point goes into the skin, and the hard edge cuts through. This makes the cut. This helps if you have a cut that you want to stop.
