To carry something means to hold it or move it from one place to another. You carry a bag with you to school or to a store. You carry food to eat at home. You carry your phone when you want to talk to a friend.

When you carry something, you are not just holding it. You are moving it. You might walk to get there or you might ride a car. If you carry money, you are taking it from one place to another place. If you carry a heavy thing, you might need to use your arms to hold it.

The word carries has many different ways. It can mean to move quickly. It can mean to take something. It can also mean to keep something for a long time. Sometimes it means to have a lot of things inside you.
