To own something is to have it for your own. This means you do not let anyone else have it. You keep it in your home, your car, or your money. You tell people who you are and what you do with it.

You can own many things. You might own a house, a dog, a car, or even a piece of land. You keep it safe and make sure it is not broken.

Sometimes, you buy something to keep it. You might buy a book or a game to play. You buy food to eat. You buy clothes to wear. You can own many different things at once.

Sometimes, you own your body. You are your own person. You are the person who lives inside your body. You decide what you do, who you are friends with, and what you like to do.

You can also own money. When you work, you make money. You use that money to buy things you need. You can own money for years and years.

In some ways, owning is like being in a game. You are the one who decides what happens. You make the moves. You are the one who does the work. You are the one who gets the things you want.

To own something is often about feeling safe. It means you are the one who has control. It feels good to be the one who gets to decide.
