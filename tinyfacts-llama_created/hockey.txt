Horse

A horse is a big animal that is strong. It has a hard, green skin and a long neck. You can ride it and it will go very fast.

People play with these horses. They hit a small ball with a long stick. The ball goes very fast and the people try to catch it before it goes over a high wall. The team with the most points at the end of the game is the one that did the best.

In some places, people also play with horses. They are very fast and can go very high. The ones that are very fast are called the ones that fly. The ones that are very fast are very important because they carry people and goods to far away places.

The word comes from the word for a person who works with animals. A person who works with animals is called a horse.
