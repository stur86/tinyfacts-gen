A car can go very fast. Sometimes, it flies through the air. This is called a run or a jump. But if the car is very heavy, it will not go as fast as it wants. Sometimes, it will hit a hard thing on the ground. This makes the car go down very fast. The car may fall to the ground. The person inside the car may feel a lot of pain.

There is also a word for when a person falls down. If you fall, you hit the ground. You might hit your head or your face. You might feel very bad because of the pain. If the person falls from a tall place, they might hit the ground hard. They might also hit their head on things around them.

When a car breaks, it does not move. The car stops, and the people inside are afraid. If the car does not stop, it can hit other cars and people. This is very bad. It can hurt many people and make a lot of money for the people who make the car and for the people who fix it later.
