To change something is to move it from one form to another. You might change your clothes when you go out. You might change how you act when you are angry. You might change how you talk when you want to be funny.

Think of a tree. A tree is a thing that grows from the ground. It starts with tiny pieces that are small. Over time, these pieces grow and become larger. The tree gets taller and bigger. The wood inside the tree gets different colors and makes different kinds of wood. The tree can change how it looks and how it works.

When you change something, you change the way it is. You do not keep the same form. You take it and turn it into something new. This is what happens with a person, a thing, a way of thinking, and even a place.

Sometimes we change in a way that is good. When you learn a new way of doing things, you become better. You might get a job you love. You might learn to speak a new way of talking. You might become a friend to someone you might not have known before.

But sometimes change can be hard. You might feel sad. You might feel like you are losing something. You might feel like you are not the same person anymore.
