A word is a thing you use to talk. It is made of many small parts that come together. You can say many different words one after another. Each word tells a small story.

Think about a house. The front side of the house is round. The sides are not all the same. There are two sides that are taller than the others. There are also two sides that are shorter than the others. The sides are all different. This is a good thing. It makes the house look nice.

You can also have a body. This is the part that makes you stand up. Your head sits on top of your body. You use your hands and your feet to walk. Your legs are the parts that go under your body.

A line is a long, straight line. You can draw one on paper. This line goes from one point to another. It is always the same way. You do not have to change the line at every step.

A thing can also be a form. A car is a form. A car has a body, but it is not a round body. It has a line that goes around it. This form helps people know what the car looks like.
