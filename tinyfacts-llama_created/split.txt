To cut something means to break it into many pieces. You can cut a piece of wood. One piece stays whole. Another piece falls down. This breaks the wood into many parts.

If you cut a paper, it breaks into many small pieces. You can use these pieces to make a new paper. This is how you make many papers with a computer.

Sometimes, you cut a thing because it is broken. You take the pieces and put them together again. This makes the thing strong and new. You can use a broken bottle to make a new drink.

You can also cut a piece of food. You use a hard thing with a point. You press the point onto the food and cut it. This makes the food easy to eat.

If you cut a person, it is very sad. You break a person who is very important to you. You must be very kind and say sorry when you cut a person.
