To buy things you need is a big word. When you go to a store, you are buying things. You do not make them. You go there to get them.

A store is a place where people buy things. They have many things. If you need a book, you can go to a store. You look at the books and pick one. You pay money for it.

You can also buy things that are already made. A store has many of these. If you need a shirt, you can look at the shirts. When you want one, you buy it.

Many people work at stores. They help people buy things. They tell them what is right to buy and how much money it will take. This is important because people buy things every day.

If you do not buy things, you can get sick. You might feel very bad. If you go to a store, you can buy a drink or a food to help. This is why you go to a store.
