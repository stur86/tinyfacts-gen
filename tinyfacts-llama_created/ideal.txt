An idea is when you think about something that will happen later. You might have a good idea before you even know you want to do it. Your brain is thinking about how you want things to be.

Sometimes you want something to be a good way to help people. You might think, I want to help people feel better when they are sad. You want to make the world a better place.

Other times you want something to be the right way to live. You might think, I want to live with my family. I want to have a job and a home. You want to be happy and safe.

Ideas are like big pieces of paper. You write them down so you can remember them later. You can share your ideas with others. This helps us learn from each other.

The word is important because it shows you care about what is happening. When you think about a good idea, you want to make it happen.
