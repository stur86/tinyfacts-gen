To be is to have life. You are not a thing. You are not a number. You are a person. You are here. You are in this world.

Think about a tree. You stand on the ground. The ground is under you. The sky is above you. There is air all around you. The tree is here too. It is not a number. It is not a thing. It is a person with a green top and a wide arms that hold the green stuff from the ground.

You are the one who breathes and drinks and eats. You are the one who feels and thinks and acts. You are the one who is here. You are the one who lives.

Think about a baby. They are very small. They are not sure. They are learning. They are taking things from their mother and father. They are eating food from a cup. They are sleeping. They are here. They are a person with a tiny body and a tiny brain.

Being is not just about life. It is about having a heart. It is about loving. It is about being with others. It is about feeling things. It is about being a part of something bigger than yourself.

You can also be something else. You can be a friend. You can be a family. You can be a teacher. You can be a doctor. You can be a police man. You can be a person you love.

Being is a big thing. It is a word that makes us think. It makes us want to live. It makes us want to learn. It makes us want to feel things. It makes us want to be a part of the world.
