Calming is about making things feel less strong or more quiet. If you are very angry, your heart is beating fast and you are moving around a lot. You might shout and hit things. But when you are very angry, you have a hard time breathing. You feel like you want to cry.

To calm a person is to help them feel better. You can do this by talking softly. You can say kind words to them. You can use your voice to make them feel less worried or afraid. You can also use your body to show you care. You might give them a hug or hold their hand.

You can also calm a place. If you are in a big room with many people, it can feel very loud and busy. You can close your eyes and breathe slowly. This helps you feel more calm and you can hear the voices of others better.

When you are very tired, it is hard to stay up and think. You might feel a big heavy feeling in your body. To calm a person or a place is to help them feel better so they can rest and be ready for the next thing.
