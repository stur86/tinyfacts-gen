Wow is a word you use when you are very surprised. It is like saying you did not believe what happened. When you see something that makes you go, you say wow. It can mean many things.

First, when you see a great thing, you might go, wow. You might feel happy because it is big. Or you might feel very glad because it is good. You might also feel a little scared, but you say wow because you were surprised.

When you hear a very loud sound, you might say, wow. You might cover your ears so the sound does not hurt you. This shows you are surprised.

You can also use this word when you see a thing you never saw before. You might say, wow, that is a new thing. This means you did not know it was there.

Sometimes, you might say, wow, because you are surprised by what someone is doing. If a friend does something that makes you laugh, you might say, wow, you are a good friend. This means you are happy they are doing it.

You can also use this word when you are afraid. If you see a cat you do not like, you might say, wow, the cat is so big. This shows you are surprised and a little scared.
