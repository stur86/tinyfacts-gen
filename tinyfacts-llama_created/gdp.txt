The number also tells you how many jobs there are. If many people work at the same place and make the same money, it is a good number. This means the money is going out. The people get what they need. The place stays in good hands.

Sometimes, the number of money can change. If many people get more money, the number goes up. This means more money is being made. If many people get less money, the number goes down. This means less money is being made.

A high number means the place is doing well. A low number means the place is not doing well.
