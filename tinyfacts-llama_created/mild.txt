A thing that is not very strong. It is not very hot or very cold. It is not very bright or very dark. It is not very loud or very quiet. It is a kind of middle ground.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friends. You are all happy. But then, someone does something that makes you laugh. You smile. You feel a little bit of the good feeling again. This is because you are not too happy and not too sad. You are just right.

This kind of feeling is very nice. It does not make you jump up and shout. It does not make you cry. It is just enough to make you feel a little bit better.

Sometimes, this kind of feeling is like a small warm sun. It is not very bright, but it is still there. You can feel it, even if you are not looking at it. It is a small, quiet kind of power.

You can also have this kind of feeling when you are talking to a friend. You are not too happy to see them. You are not too sad to talk to them. You are just right. This is because you know they care about you, but you do not feel too much of any one thing.

In life, you may have times when you feel this kind of feeling. You may feel happy, but not too happy. You may feel sad, but not too sad. You may feel angry, but not too angry. This is a normal part of being a living thing.

So, a thing that is not very strong is a thing that is not very bright, not very loud, not very hot, not very cold, and not very happy. It is a kind of middle ground.
