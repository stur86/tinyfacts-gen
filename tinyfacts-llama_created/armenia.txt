Arms is a word that means things you use to fight. A person or a group of people can have many arms. Some have only two. One person might have arms like a dog, and another person might have arms like a big man.

When people are afraid, they often use their arms to keep themselves safe. They can hold a gun or a stick that makes a loud noise. They can also use their arms to fight with other people. This is why we say that an arms race happens when many different people are trying to be the best at the same thing.

Some places in the world are very far from each other. If you drive a car to one place, you might have to go many hours. In this case, you have arms because you need to use them while you are driving. You hold the car and you use your arms to help the car stop.

An arm is the part of the body that goes from the head to the hand. It is a long piece of hard stuff that holds your hand. It helps you make things, pick up things, and use your fingers to do many different jobs.

In some stories, a group of people are trying to get away from bad people. They might use their arms to fight. They might also use their arms to hide and stay safe. They can also use their arms to carry things or to carry people.

You can also have arms in your own body. You have arms like a person. You use them to make food, to move around, and to do many other jobs. You might have arms that are very strong so you can lift heavy things. You might have arms that are very small so you can pick things that are hard to reach.

So, arms are the things you use to fight, to keep yourself safe, and to do many different jobs.
