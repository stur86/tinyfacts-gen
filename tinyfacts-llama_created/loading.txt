To have something ready is to be ready. If you want to go to a big house, you need to put your things in a bag. You can't just walk into a house. You need to go first.

When you are ready, you feel calm. You are not worried. You can do other things. You might be thinking about something or you might be moving from one room to another.

A computer is a very fast thing. It can do many things at once. It can show you pictures, talk to other computers, and remember what you did before. It is ready for work every second.

You can also say a group is ready. When everyone is ready to go, they move together. They follow one person. They are all ready to go to a place.

Sometimes, you need to put things on a car. You put clothes, food, and other things in a bag. When you are ready, you open the bag and put everything in the car. You are ready to go.
