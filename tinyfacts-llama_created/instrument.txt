An animal has a body that can move. It has parts that help it breathe. It can also make sounds. Sometimes it uses these parts to catch food. Other animals use parts to move around. They might push a hard thing on the ground. Some animals use their parts to carry other animals. They carry the body of another animal on their back. This is how some animals move very fast over the ground.

People also use these parts. We use a thing with many small pieces to make sounds. We use this to play music. We use it to tell stories. Some people use a long piece of wood to hit the air. They hit it with a hard part. This makes a sound. This sound goes far. It can be heard from many houses. We use these parts in our work.
