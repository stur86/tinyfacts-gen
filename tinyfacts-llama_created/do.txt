To do something means you act on it. If you want to go to a place, you do it. If you want to eat food, you do it. You do not just think about it. You do it with your body and your mind.

You can do things alone or with others. You can do a work by yourself. You can do a job for a person. You can do a game with friends. You can do a thing that makes you happy.

Sometimes you do things because you are told to. Sometimes you do things because you want to. You might do a thing that makes you feel good. You might do a thing that makes you feel angry. You might do a thing that makes you feel sad.

To do something is often a part of a story. You might do something to help a friend. You might do something to find a place. You might do something to learn a new thing.

Doing is important for our lives. It keeps us moving. It helps us grow. It keeps us safe. It makes life interesting.
