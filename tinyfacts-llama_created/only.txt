Only means you are the only one. You are the one person, animal, or thing that is not shared with anyone else. It is the thing that is alone in a room or in a place.

Think about when you are the only one playing with a ball. You throw it up in the air. No one else can do it. You are the only one who can catch it. If someone else were playing, the ball would go all over the place and you would not be able to keep it.

Sometimes only means a small thing. You are the only one who has a book. You are the only one who knows how to read it. You do not share it with anyone else.

It can also mean that something is the best. If you have a book and your friend has a book that is better, you are only right. You are the only one who thinks that.

But being only can also be hard. Sometimes you feel alone even when you are with many people. You feel that no one understands you. This can make you sad.

In some places, only is used for things that must be kept quiet. If you are only allowed to speak one word, you must say it. This is because you are not allowed to say anything else. It is a way of keeping order.

Only is a word that can mean different things. It can mean being alone, being the best, or keeping things quiet.
