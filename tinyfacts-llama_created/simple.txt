A word is a thing that people use to talk about things.

When you say something is easy, you mean it is not hard. If a game is very easy, you can do it quickly. If a book is easy to read, you can finish it in a short time.

When you say something is easy, you are not saying it is not hard at all. You are just saying it is not a big problem. This means the thing does not take much time or work to understand. It is like a small step.

Sometimes, a word is very easy to use. If you see a word and you know what it means, you can use it right away. You do not have to think about it very much. You just know it is a word that means a thing.

In a group of people, a word can mean many different things. One person might say, you are very kind. Another person might say, you are very funny. Both of these words mean the same thing. They are both about how you act.

Sometimes, a word has many different ways to use it. If you say you are happy, you can mean you are very happy. You can also say you are sad. You can say you are angry. This is because the word can mean many different things.

A word can also change how we feel. If you see a word that makes you sad, you might feel sad too. If you see a word that makes you happy, you might feel happy. This is because the word tells you how you feel.

In a story, you use words to build a picture. You use words that show how a person acts, what they say, and what happens around them. When you read a story, you are using the word to show you what is happening.

So, a word is a thing that helps us talk about things. It tells us how to feel and what to think. It helps us understand each other and tell stories.
