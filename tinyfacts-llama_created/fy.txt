To fly means to move through the air. You can do this with a small box that flies in the sky or with a big animal that flies through the air. When you fly, you leave the ground and go up. You go so high that the world looks like a huge piece of paper.

Sometimes people fly in a group. They are all in the same box or animal and they fly together. They go on a trip to a far away place. They might see a big city that looks like a tiny town.

You can also fly your own body. When you are very small, you can fly. You can go up into the air and fly around your room. You can fly over your bed so you can see the night sky.
