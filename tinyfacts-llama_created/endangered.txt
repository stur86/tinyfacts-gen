When people talk about something being in trouble, they often use words like this. This word is important because it tells us about how well a group of animals, or how many things are left on the land, in the water, or in the sky is doing.

To be in trouble means a group of animals has to be very few. Maybe only a few more of them live in one place, or maybe only one animal lives. This makes them very hard to find and watch.

It also means their homes are being hurt. If a tree falls down, it can kill the animals that live in the wood. If a big piece of water moves, it can carry away small animals that live there. This can make it hard for them to find food or a place to rest.

Sometimes, we use this word when we talk about people. If a group of people is in trouble, it means many of them are very sick, or they are in a place they do not want to be, or they are afraid. It can also mean they do not have enough money to live.

To fix this, we need to help them. We can give them more money to pay for the food they need or the place to live. We can also help them stay safe from the things that hurt them. This is why we care about these animals and people.
