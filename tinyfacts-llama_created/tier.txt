A thing that is in a line can be a small thing or a tall thing. When we talk about people, we often use this word. We have a job that is higher than one job. We do more work and are paid more money. This is a big step in our lives.

Also, a thing that is in a line with other things can be a small thing or a very strong thing. Think of a line of food from a store to a house. The food is in the middle and all the people are in the line before them. The food is higher than the people.

Sometimes, we say that something is on a high or low side. If something is high, it is in a line of tall things. If it is low, it is in a line of small things. This is because the top is higher than the bottom. It is like standing on a big chair with your feet on a small floor.
