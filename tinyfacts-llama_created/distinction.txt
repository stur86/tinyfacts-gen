To make a clear point is to show that one thing is different from another. When you have a lot of books and one book is better than the rest, you can say this. You are making a clear point about which book is the best.

This happens when you are talking about things that are very different. If you are talking about food, you have food for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. If you are talking about a person, you have a mom, a dad, a friend, and a teacher. Each one is different from the others. You are making a clear point.

A person can also make a clear point by doing something. If someone is very strong, they can lift a heavy box. If someone is very good at playing music, they can play a song that no one else can play. They are making a clear point about how they are doing things.

When we make a clear point, we are telling the world who is who. We are telling them which thing is the best, which thing is the worst, which thing is very good, and which thing is very bad. We use this to talk to each other and to decide what is right and wrong.
