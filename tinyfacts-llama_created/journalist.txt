A person who writes about what is happening in the world is called a writer or a writer. They use their brain to put facts and stories together so that others can know what is going on.

Many of them work for a paper or a place that sends their stories to the world. They try to get the right words and the right pictures. They talk to people who are important. They also look at the world from many different points of sight.

They do not always tell the whole story. Sometimes they keep some things to themselves to keep the story interesting. They also sometimes write about things that are not true, but they try not to make the story sound like it is.

Sometimes these people are called hard to follow, because they might not give you every single word. They might give you a few lines and then you have to use your brain to figure out what is going on. But they try to tell you the right story, even if they do not show you every word.

Some people like them because they tell you what is really happening. They help you understand the world. Other people do not like them because they might say things that are not true. They might say bad things about someone. They might say things that are not right.
