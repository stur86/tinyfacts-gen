To meet means you are together. You are not far away. You are right in front of each other. This happens when you see a person. You can see them from your house. You can see them from your car. You can also see them from a phone call. When you meet someone, you say hello.

Imagine you are in a big room. There are many people. You want to find one person. You look around. You see them. You walk to them. You touch their arm. You say, hello. You say, nice day. You are happy to see them.

Sometimes you meet a person you know. You have a friend. You meet them at a place. You sit together. You talk about things you like. You are happy to see them.

You can also meet a new person. This is when you meet someone you have never seen before. You are surprised. You do not know them. You ask, who are you? They say, I am a teacher. You are happy to meet them.

When you meet someone, you might say, I am glad we met. This means you are happy you are together. It is a good way to show you are happy to see the person.

It is also important to meet a thing. You can meet a thing that is hard to move. You push it. You pull it. Then it moves. You meet the thing when you do this. You say, I am glad the thing is moving. This means you are happy it is doing what you want.
