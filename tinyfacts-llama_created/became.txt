To become something is to change into something else. It happens when you do something and you become a different person.

Imagine you are a small animal. You live in a green place with tall trees. One day, you are very strong and can run far. You feel happy and can climb the trees. After a while, you become very old and your body starts to break. You become smaller and your body becomes old. You can no longer climb the trees.

You have become a different animal. You have changed your body and your life. You are no longer the same animal you were before.

When you become something, you leave what you were and take what you are becoming. This is why you are no longer the same thing you were. You have changed into something new.

You can become many things. You can become a person who is very strong and can run very fast. You can become a person who can speak many different ways. You can become a person who has a lot of money. You can become a person who is very kind.

To become is to change from what you were to what you are. It is a big change. It can happen very fast or very slow. Sometimes, you might not even notice what is happening. Other times, you feel a lot of change inside you.

In our world, becoming is important for making new life. When you become a person, you start a new life. You start to learn and to grow. You learn from your past and you use your new life to make better things happen.

Becoming is also about growing. When you grow, you become stronger. You learn to do new things. You become better at what you do. This is why you can become a doctor and a teacher. You become a person who helps others.

So, to become is to change what you are into something new. It is a big change that happens when you do something and you become a different person.
