To show times means to tell people when things happen. 

Imagine you want to go to a movie. You need to know when it starts. You look at a listened thing that shows this. This listened thing is like a book for people. It tells you the time and the day when the movie is on. 

When you watch a movie, you are watching at that time. If you are late, you might feel bad. You could also be late and miss a part of the story. It is important to know the time so you do not make a big problem.

People also show times for other things. You might go to a game. The game starts at a time that is ready. If you arrive at the time, you get a seat. If you arrive too late, you might not have a seat. The time helps everyone know when to be there so they can enjoy the show.

There are also ways to show times in different places. In a big city, you might see many times on a big wall. These are very easy to see. In a small town, you might look for a small sign on a wall. The sign might say, like,  ices are open,  ices are open, and so on. This way, people know what time the ice shop is open.
