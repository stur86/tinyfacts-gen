Later is a word that means after something else. It is often used when you are talking about a time that is after another time.

Think about your day. You might wake up at  morning. Then, you do your school work. After you finish your work, you go to the park. Later, you might go to a movie with your friends. This is because later comes after morning.

You can also use it when you do something and then do it later. If you want to eat dinner, you say you will do it later. This means you do it after you finish your work.

Sometimes, later can also mean another day. If you have a party on today, you might have another one tomorrow. This is because you are going to the same place in two days.
