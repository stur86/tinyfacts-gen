To go up is to go in a way that goes up. You can use this word to talk about moving up a high place, like a house or a tall tree.

Imagine you are a dog. You want to run to a far place where your family lives. You start running on the ground. But you want to go fast. You want to go up. So you use your legs. You push and you pull. You go up and you feel happy.

You can also say this when you are in a room. If you go to a room that is higher than where you started, you are going up. You are moving away from the ground.

There is also a word that means to go in a way that goes away from you. This is what happens when you are angry and you want to leave a place. You want to go away from what is making you mad. You go up and you walk away. You are leaving the place and the thing that made you angry.
