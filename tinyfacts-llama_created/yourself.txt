To be yourself means you do not try to be something you are not. You do not try to act like someone else. You do what you think is right, even if it is different from what other people think is right.

You might feel this way when you are in a group. If you see many people wearing the same clothes or doing the same things, you might feel like you should do the same too. But you do not. You are the only one who does it your way.

Sometimes, you might feel this way because you are afraid. You might feel afraid of what people will think if you act like you think you should. You might feel afraid of being different. You might feel like you should fit in.

But being yourself is important. It means you are not afraid to be you. It means you trust yourself. It means you are happy with who you are.

If you are afraid to be yourself, you might try to change. You might wear clothes that are not your own or act like someone else. You might say things that are not your own. You might try to act like you are someone you are not.

But if you are afraid, you will only get worse. You will become more afraid. You will lose your way. You will lose your heart.

You can still be yourself even if you are afraid. You can still be different. You can still be a little bit strange. But you have to be true to yourself. You have to trust yourself. You have to be happy with who you are.

You are the only one who can decide how you act and how you feel. You are the only one who can decide if you are afraid or if you are happy. You are the only one who can decide if you are yourself.
