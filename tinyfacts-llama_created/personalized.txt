When you buy something, you usually buy it for yourself. You pick the color, the kind of thing, and the way it looks. But a word like this can make your life very different.

Imagine you are on a big bus that goes to many places. Each person on the bus has a different seat. You get a seat that is near the front. Then, someone sits next to you and says, I am going to the city where my mom lives. You are now sitting near the front of the bus because you are on a different bus.

This word helps people who work together. When you work at a big office, you might be in the same building. But then you meet new people every day. You might sit with them at lunch. You might talk with them in the morning. Later, you sit with them at dinner. Because of this word, you have a different table at every meeting.

It is also good for people who love animals. If you have a dog and your friend has a dog too, you can sit next to them at a party. You can talk about your dogs. You do not have the same dog. Your friend has a dog. You are not the same person.

This word is often used by people who make clothes. They make clothes for many people. They make a shirt for a boy and a shirt for a girl. But then they make a shirt for a girl and a shirt for a boy. Because of this word, you have a different shirt at every time you go to a store.

You can also use this word for things that are very important. You can say, I have a word that is very important for me. It is the word of my family. It makes me feel safe.

In short, this word means something is very close to you. It is the word you use when you want to feel a way.
