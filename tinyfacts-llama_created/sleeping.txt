To sleep is when your body is very tired. Your brain is in a deep rest. When you sleep, your body and mind work together to fix things that are broken. You might not remember the day before, or what you did, or even what time it is.

When you sleep, your body does several things. It makes new skin. It keeps your body warm. It also cleans your body. It even helps your heart and brain work well again when you wake up.

You can sleep in a bed, on the floor, or even on the ground. You might sleep standing up or you might sleep lying down. Some animals sleep standing up, while others sleep on the ground. Animals sleep just like people do.

People sleep at night and sometimes they also sleep during the day. If you are very tired, you might only sleep for a few hours. If you are very tired for many hours, you might sleep for a whole day.

Sometimes, you might not want to sleep. You might feel worried or scared. In this case, you might feel very hot and your body might be a lot of work to keep you warm. This is why you might drink water or eat something to make yourself feel better.

Sometimes, you might have trouble sleeping. This can happen if you are afraid, or if you have a lot of things on your mind. Or you might have a hard time falling asleep because you are too hot or because of the light in your room. It is okay to have trouble sleeping. You can try to relax, listen to music, or read a book to help you fall asleep.
