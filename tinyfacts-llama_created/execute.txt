To do something means you make it happen. When you say you will do something, you are telling the world you plan to act. You must follow your plan to finish the job.

Think of a word. You say, I will eat the food. You are telling the world that you have a plan. The world knows you are going to eat the food. You follow your plan by going to the table and eating. You are doing what you said.

In life, you do things that help others or make a life better. You might go to work. You work and help your family. Or you might go to school and learn new things. You follow your plan and finish. You are done.

You can also do something that hurts. You might say you will not fight. Then you start to fight. You follow your plan and you get hurt. You are not done, but you are working on your plan.

When you say you will do something, you are telling everyone about your plan. You might not follow the plan, but you are still saying, I am doing this. You are being a part of the world.

Do not always follow your plan. Sometimes you must change your mind. You can change your mind and try a new plan. You can find a new way to do something that is better for you. This is okay. It is a step forward.

In a game, you are the one who follows the plan. You follow what the other person says and you try to do better next time. You are acting like you have a plan.
