To spend something means you use money or other things you got from work. You use money to buy food, clothes, or other things you need. You buy these things so you can live and do what you want.

Imagine you have a big bag of money. You can buy many things from a store. If you buy a book for one hour, you use the money for that hour. After that hour, you have no money left for the next hour. You spent the money.

Sometimes, people spend money on things that bring them a feeling of happy. If you see a beautiful picture, you feel happy. You might buy the picture and keep it in your room. You use the money you got to buy the picture so you can look at it every day.

People also spend money on things that help them stay safe. If you have a car, you use money to buy parts. You buy new parts so your car can go far. You spend money on cars because they keep you safe.

When you spend money, you use it. You use it to buy things. You use it to live. You spend money every day.
