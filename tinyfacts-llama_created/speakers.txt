A speaker is a thing that makes sound. You can use it to talk to your friends or to a group of people. It stands in a room. When you talk into it, the sound comes out of it. If the room is big, the sound goes far. If the room is small, the sound only goes to the people in it.

Think about a car. When you press the key, the car starts. The car is like a big speaker. When you turn the key, the sound moves from the car to the road. You can also use a speaker to play music. You put a small round box with many tiny pieces inside. The pieces move when you press a key. This makes the music sound. You can put this box into a room and the people can hear the music.

Some things are not just for talking. You can use a speaker to show pictures. You put a small box with a tiny piece inside. The piece moves when you press a key. This makes a picture show. You can watch movies with a speaker. You see the pictures and hear the sound of a car driving or a dog belling.

You can also use a speaker for games. You put a small box into a box with many tiny pieces inside. The pieces move when you press a key. This makes the game show moving pictures or sounds. You play this game with friends. They watch the pictures and you hear the sounds.

A speaker can be small or big. Some small ones are hidden in a wall. They are like tiny boxes inside the wall. Other big ones are standing tall. They stand in a room and everyone can hear them.
