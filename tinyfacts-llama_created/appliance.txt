An apartment is a big building with many rooms. It has lots of different places for people to live and work. These places are often many floors high and have many stories. There is a room for food and a room for people to sit and talk. There might also be a room for cleaning and a room for doing things that need to be done. Some apartments have a big room for eating, like a large room with a table and chairs. You can live in an apartment if you have your own space and your own room. It is a way to live in a city without having to buy a lot of land.
