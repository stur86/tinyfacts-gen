To feel this is to be very, very small. You need to eat to live. If you do not eat, you will feel very bad. You might want to look at a picture of food. You might want to sit next to a table. You might feel sad.

You feel this when you are not full. Your body is telling you that more food is needed. This is like a little voice inside you. It says, you need to eat now, or you will get sick.

Sometimes you feel this when you are not sure. You do not know if you will have food later. You might be thinking about a food you want to eat. You might be looking at a picture. You might be feeling a little nervous. This is like your body trying to tell you that you should eat now.

People have this feeling all the time. It is a big thing inside us. It tells us when to eat. It tells us when to stop eating. It is a very important feeling.
