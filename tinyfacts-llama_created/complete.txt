To complete something means to finish it. It is when you have done everything you need to do. You have put the parts together. You have fixed the breaks. You have made sure everything is right. Now you can see the whole thing. You can say, the book is complete.

Think of a story. You start with the beginning. You have a story that starts with a word, a name, a time. As you go along, you add more words. You tell what is happening. You show you are afraid. You show you are happy. You use the words that make the story true. When you finish the story, you have completed it.

You can also complete a job. If you build a house, you have finished it. You have put all the pieces together. Now you can look at the house and say it is done.

Sometimes, to complete something is to fix a problem. If a car breaks, you need to fix it. You need to fix the part that is broken. You need to fix the part that is not working. If you fix it, the car is complete again.

You can also complete a game. If you are playing a game, you are doing well. You are beating the other people. You are getting closer to the end. When you complete a game, you have finished it.
