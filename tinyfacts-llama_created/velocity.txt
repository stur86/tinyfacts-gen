To move quickly or fast is to have a high number of steps in the same short time. When you walk, you are moving. When you run, your feet are moving in a fast way. When you drive a car, you are moving a lot of steps in a short time.

Sometimes you want to know how fast you are going so you know if you are moving fast enough. You can do this by looking at a number that shows how many steps are in a second. This number tells you how many steps you have in one second. If the number is big, you are moving fast. If the number is small, you are moving slow.

You also use this word with things that go up. When a ball is going up in the air, it is moving quickly. This is because it is moving away from the ground. If it keeps going up, it will keep going up for a long time.

You can also use this word with people. If a person is very fast, they can run quickly from one place to another. If a person is very slow, they might take a long time to get from one place to another.

In the sky, things move very fast too. A small piece of paper that is flying away from you will move fast. A big group of stars will move quickly across the sky.
