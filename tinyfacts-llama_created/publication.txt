A book is a thing that people write. It has many lines and words. These lines tell stories, give ideas, or show what happened. Sometimes, a book has pictures. It can be about a person, a place, or something that is in the world.

When you read a book, you hear the words. You see the words on a computer or paper. You can find books at a store or at a home. You can also read books by someone else. This is called reading.

Some books are very long. You can read one book from start to end. Other books are short. You can read one short story before the end. People use these to learn, feel, and be happy.

A story is a way to show how things are. You can hear a story or look at pictures. A story can make you feel something. It can make you laugh, cry, or feel happy. People tell stories to each other and to their children so they can remember and be ready for what comes next.

There are many kinds of books. You can find books on animals, people, places, and things that happen in the world. You can also find books on things that are not real. These are called books of ideas or books of dreams.

Books are important because they help people learn. They show us new ideas, make us think, and help us understand the world.
