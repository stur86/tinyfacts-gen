To wear something is to use it over and over. When you wear a shoe, you walk on the ground. The shoe goes through the ground many times. After a while, the shoe gets very old. The soft parts of the shoe start to break down. You might even see the hard parts of the shoe. When you wear a soft thing like a bed or a chair, it becomes old too.

Things wear out when they get broken or when their parts get old. A car that drives many times gets a new set of tires. A book that you read many times gets a new cover. Your hands get old and you might feel pain when you touch things.

You can also wear a face. When you are very happy, your face gets soft and you look happy. When you are very sad, your face gets red and you look sad. Your eyes look down because you are not smiling anymore.

People wear clothes to cover their bodies. A shirt keeps your chest warm. A pants set covers your legs. A dress is a kind of top that goes over your body. A jacket is a cover over your arms. You can wear a hated to keep the sun off your head.

Sometimes, you wear a thing for a job. A teacher wears a dress with a high neck. A doctor wears a white cover with a number on it. A person who works with computers wears a cover that has numbers and letters on it. This helps them remember what they did during the day.

You can also wear a thing to show your age. A person might wear a watch to show how many hours are left. A person might wear a ring to show they love another person. A person might wear a hated to show they are a friend.
