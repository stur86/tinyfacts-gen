An big eventually is a time when many people go together to buy things that are not their own. The money they pay goes into a big bag. The money is for the things that the group found.

Imagine you want a new game. Many kids want it. They all go to the same place at the same time. The group asks a person if they can buy the game. If the person says yes, they take the game and the money they paid.

The group does not give the money to one person. Each person gets a smaller share. The money is put into a bag with many other bags. When everyone gets their share, they all leave.

Some big places have many bags. They are very strong and can hold many bags. The bags are usually kept in a big room. When the bags are full, the bags go away and the room is empty.

People use this word for many things. They use it for things that are very important. It helps us know when something is about to happen and when it is done.
