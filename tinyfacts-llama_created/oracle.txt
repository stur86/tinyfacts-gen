An or who is a person or a thing that gives a thing that you want to know. They do not ask you to do anything. They just give you the answer.

You can ask a person or a thing this way: ask a tree, ask a rock, ask a dog, or ask a person. If you do not know what is wrong with you, you can ask. If you want to know if it will rain tomorrow, you can ask the sky.

Sometimes a person who is not a human is called an or who. They are like a person who knows everything. They have seen many days and many years. They do not ask for anything. They just tell you what is true.

People say this when they want to tell you something important. They are the ones who know.

You can also ask an or who if you do not know. You can go to a house and ask the person who lives there. They will tell you if the person is there. If the person is not there, you can wait some more time.

The word comes from old times when people thought that a thing could talk. They were afraid that a thing could give you a bad answer. Today, the word is used in many ways to tell you that someone is not afraid.
