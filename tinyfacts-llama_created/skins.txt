To skin something means to cut it very fine. When you are doing this, you take a small bit from the top. You cut it into small pieces. This helps people see under the skin. It also helps us know who someone is.

If you have a dog, you might have to skin the neck. You take a piece of skin from the dog. After that, the skin is very soft. You can use it to make a very thinking thing for a person. Or you can use it to make a soft cover for a box.

The word comes from the old days. A long time ago, a person would take a piece of skin and cut it very fine. They would do this because the skin was very strong. But the skin was also very hard. It was hard to work with. Now we use other ways to make things work.

Sometimes, people skin animals for food. They cut the skin and make it into food that can be eaten. This is how people get their food.
