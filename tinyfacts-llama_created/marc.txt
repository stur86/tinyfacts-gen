To mark something is to put a mark on it. This can be a word, a picture, a line, or a small piece of paper.

You can put a mark on a thing so that people know it is there. This helps us remember things. A teacher might write the name of a book on the wall so that everyone knows it is the book about a story.

You can also put a mark on something so that it stays. If you put a small mark on a piece of paper, it will stay there even when you take the paper away. This is why you use a paper cutter to cut a piece of paper.

Sometimes, a mark is a small picture. You can find these pictures in books and in pictures. They can be on a wall or on a table. These pictures tell stories and show us how things look.

A mark can also be a word. If you say, you are very happy, you are marking a moment in time. Every time you say this, we remember that you feel happy.

To mark something can be different for different things. A person might mark their face to show that they are a man. A dog might mark a spot on a tree so that no other dog comes there. A tree might mark the spot where it was cut so that people know it was cut.

So, to mark something is to leave a sign on it or to put a word on it. It helps us remember, keeps things in place, and tells stories.
