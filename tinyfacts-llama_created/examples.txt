An easy word to use, but also a word that can cause trouble.

An easy word is a word that is easy to say. You can say it quickly. It is not hard to remember. It is very quick to use.

An other kind of word is when you say something is an idea. You do not say it is a thing. You say it is what you think. You say it is the way to do things. You say it is what makes life. You say it is the way to feel.

Think of a room. You do not say a room is a thing. You say it is a place where people sit. You say it is where you are. You say it is the space between the walls. You are not talking about the walls.

Or think of a story. You are telling a story. You do not say it is a thing. You say it is what happened. You say it is the people and the places. You say it is the way the people act. You say it is the story you want to tell.

An easy word is like a word that tells you what to do. An idea word is like a word that tells you how to feel. An idea word is like a word that tells you what to think.

Remember: an easy word is easy to use. An idea word is hard to understand. An idea word is hard to remember. An easy word is quick. An idea word is slow.
