An idea is a thing you think about. It is a way to talk about what is right or wrong. When you say something is an idea, you mean it is something you believe.

A thing that gives you an idea is called an answer. If someone gives you an answer to a question, you can use it later to think about what to do next.

An answer is not always easy to know. You might see a picture and then think about what it means. You might ask another person to explain it. This is how you learn and grow.

Sometimes, people give answers to their work or their jobs. They are the ones who do the work. When these people get an answer, it is an idea about how to do the job better.

In a way, all these answers are one idea. There is only one idea that is true. The answer is just one part of the whole idea.

There are many kinds of answers. You can get an answer from a teacher, a doctor, a writer, or a computer. Each answer is different and helps us learn.
