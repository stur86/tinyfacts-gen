The word summer is for the time of year when the days are very long and the sun is very hot. In this time, the days are almost every day and the nights are very short.

The days are warm and bright, and it is hard to stay inside a house because of the sun. The air is warm and the ground is hot under your feet. This is why people often go to the park, the water, or the park with a big tree so they can run and play.

In the summer, the water in the big bodies of water is very high and the water is warm enough to make people want to jump in it. People also like to go to the places with many tall trees where they can sit and read books.

Summer is a time when children go to school less often because of the hot days. But the days are also a time of fun because there are many days off from school and people can go to the movies or go to a big building with a big hall to watch movies.

Even though summer is the warm time of the year, it is not the warm time of the year. In the cold time of the year, it is dark and the air is cold. But in the warm time, the days are so long that it feels like the sun is going down in the middle of the day.
