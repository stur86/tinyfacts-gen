To name something is to give it a name. This means you give it a word to say its name. This helps us keep things straight and easy to remember.

You can name a person, an animal, a thing in the room, or even a place. Sometimes you name a thing that is not yet here. This can help us talk about it later.

You can also name a feeling. If you are happy, you might say you feel happy. If you are afraid, you might say you feel afraid. This helps you stay in the same place when you feel these strong ways.

Naming is important because it helps us understand each other. If you name a thing, you tell others who it is. This makes it clear and easy to know.

Sometimes we name things that are very hard to see. We name the water in the ground. We name the air we breathe. We name the sun in the sky. Naming these things helps us remember that they are there and important.

You can also name a story. If you tell a story, you give it a name. This helps us remember the story when we want to hear it again.
