To directions means to show where you want to go. It is like giving a set of lines to a person or a thing. If you want to move your car to a different place, you have to follow these lines. You do not just go where you feel. You follow these lines because someone told you to do so.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friend. You want to get a ball that is very far away. You look at the ball and you see the direction it is going. Then you follow that direction to get to the ball. You do not just look at the ball and then you run away. You follow the lines that point to where the ball is.

You can also directions when you are trying to tell someone what to do. If a teacher says, you have to do this, you follow those directions. You do not just do whatever you want. You do exactly what the teacher says.

In your home, you can directions when you want to close a door. If you want the door to shut, you open it and then you close it. This is a direction. You are following a line to stop the door from moving any more.

You can also use this word when you are trying to get someone to do something. If a friend wants you to be quiet, you tell them to follow the direction. You do not just do what they want. You follow the line that says you should be quiet.

So, to directions is to follow a set of lines or a set of words. You are following a direction that shows you where to go or what to do.
