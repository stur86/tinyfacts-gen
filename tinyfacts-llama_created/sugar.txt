To make something sweet means to change it so it is good to eat.

Most things we eat have something called water inside them. You can also find sweet things inside the ground or in the water of a big body of water. We use the water from these places to make food so people can live.

When you cut an animal, the body makes tiny pieces that are small and soft. These pieces can be put into a big round thing so they can be shared with many people at once. When people eat the food with these tiny pieces, their bodies break them down and change them into a feeling of full and happy. This makes them want to want more food.

Sweet things are made from the tiny pieces the animal body makes. We take these pieces and add them to a big round thing filled with white stuff. We make the white stuff hot and then we take it out of the round thing. Inside the round thing, we have a sweet part. When we put the sweet part into food, it makes the food sweet too.

Some sweet things are made from the wood of trees. These sweet things can make a drink that makes you feel happy. Other sweet things are made from the soft parts of animals. These sweet things are often very strong and need to be eaten in small bits so you do not feel too much of the bad stuff inside.

You can also make sweet things from food that is already sweet. Some people make sweet things from the soft parts of animals that have already been cut into small pieces. They put the pieces together with other small pieces to make a sweet food that is very good to eat.
