To make something go around is to move it from one place to another. When you walk, your feet move your body around your legs. When you run, your legs go around your body many times. This is an important job for our bodies.

In the world, water is very good at doing this. It goes through big bodies of water that cover many parts of the land. This is why water can carry things that are heavy and big from one place to another. It goes round and round, often many times, before it reaches the place where it wants to be.

Sometimes, the word is used for the things that go through your body. When you breathe, air moves in and out of your body. The air goes round your body many times and then comes back out. This keeps you moving and gives you the power to do things.

In other places, the word is used for how things move around on a floor. If you are in a room with many people, the floor can feel like it is going around. Your feet might touch the floor many times as you move. This is why you can walk quickly or run fast.

In the world, the word can also mean to go around a thing. You might need to go around a street to get to another city. Or you might need to go around a group of people to talk to them.

Sometimes, the word is used for the way things go in the sky. The sun goes around the world on a big round thing in the sky. The light from the sun goes around the world many times each day. This is why we see the sun in the morning and the sun sets at night.

In all these cases, the word is about moving from one place to another or around something.
