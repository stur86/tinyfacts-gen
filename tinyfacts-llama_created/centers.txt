To have a heart is to have love. When we love someone, we care for them. We want them to be happy. We might feel sad when they are sad. We might feel happy when they are happy.

The word has many parts. A heart is a small round thing inside your chest. When you are afraid, your heart beats fast. When you feel love, your heart beats slow. It is a part of how you feel.

A group of people can also have a heart. When a group has a heart, they are working together. They want to do the same thing. They are strong and help each other. If one person is sick, the group can help them feel better.

In a city, a big group of people can have a heart. They live together and care for each other. They work together to make their city a great place. They are the ones who build roads, schools, and houses.

Sometimes, people say a building has a heart. This is because the building feels something. It might feel safe or happy. It is like the building has a tiny person inside it who is always ready to help others.

Everyone has a heart. Your heart is the part inside you that loves you. Your friends have hearts too. When you love someone, your heart feels happy.
