To deal with something means to work with it. You can use this word in many different ways.

Imagine you have a box with money inside it. You want to keep it safe. You can put it in a box on the floor. This is a deal. You are not going to touch the money anymore. You are letting it stay in one place.

If you are in a room with a person, you can deal with them. You might talk to them. You might ask them a question. You are working together. You are not fighting.

Sometimes, you deal with a person who is mean. You want to stop them. You might stand up to them. This is a deal because you are showing you do not like the way they act.

You can also deal with things you have to pay. You might pay a person for doing a job for you. You are using your money. You are paying the person so you do not have to do it by yourself.

To deal with a problem is to find a way to fix it. If you have a hard time in school, you might deal with your teacher. You talk to them. You ask why you did not do your work. You are trying to find a way out of the problem.
