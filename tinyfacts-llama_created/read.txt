To read means you look at words and letters. When you read, you move your eyes from one word to another. You do not touch the words. You only see what the words tell you.

You can read many different kinds of words. You can read books, stories, and papers. You can also read signs on a street, like big numbers, or signs on a computer. These signs tell you what to do.

Some words are short. They are one word. You can read a short word that says something. Other words are long. They are many words put together. You have to look at each word very carefully.

You can read at a table or on a computer. You can even watch a movie with words. People can watch a movie with you. They point to the words and you watch.

Reading is a very important thing. It helps us learn new things. It helps us understand what is happening. It also helps us stay safe and find what we need.
