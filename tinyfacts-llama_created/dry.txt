To dry something is to make it less wet. When you see water on a table or on a floor, it is wet. If you put the wet water on a soft thing, it will start to change. The water will go out and the thing will feel dry.

You can dry a piece of paper. If a piece of paper is wet from drinking a cup of water, you use a thing that makes the water go out. The paper feels nice and soft again. You can also dry a shirt if you put it in the sun.

Things that are dry are not wet anymore. They are hard. A dry floor is hard to walk on because you do not want to step in wet water. A dry room is quiet because there is no air moving around that makes a sound.

You can also dry a thing that is heavy. If a heavy box is wet, you have to put it in the air. The air makes the water go out of the box. Then the box feels lighter. You can put the box on the floor again.

A dry place is a place where the water is gone. It might be a room where people live. It might be a place where animals go to sleep. It is a place where everything is dry.
