To make something is to build it. When you buy a box, you do not just sit there. You go to a place with many people. They take the box from your house and put it into a big room. They put the right parts inside. Then they put the parts together with power from a big box. After many hours, the box is ready. You have the box.

You can also make things on your own. A person might make clothes, food, or small things from a box. They work with their hands and use power from a big box. They change the hard parts of a thing into a new one that is nice to look at or use.

Sometimes, people make things from other things. If you have many small pieces of wood and a power box, you can build a house. You put the wood pieces together with the power from the power box. This makes a house that is strong and can stand for many years.

Making things is important. It helps us live. We need to make food to eat. We need to make clothes so we do not get hurt. We need to build roads so we can get to where we want to go.
