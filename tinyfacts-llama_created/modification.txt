To change something means to make it different. You can change your hair color. You can change the color of your clothes. You can change the way you act. This happens when you are not the same person anymore.

Think about food. You eat a sweet thing. After some time, the sweet part is gone. You have a different body now. Your body has a new way to use the food. You feel strong and full at once. This is a change.

A computer can change too. It can do many different jobs. One day, it does only one thing. The next day, it does many different jobs. It becomes stronger and better.

Sometimes, we change things for people. We change how we act. We become kind and nice. We show love to those we care about. We change our words. We say sorry when we are wrong. This makes everyone feel better.

We also change things for our world. We build new houses to live in. We make roads so people can get from one place to another easily. We make the air clean. These changes help our world stay safe and happy.

When you say a word is changed, you are talking about how something is different now. It is not the same as it was before.
