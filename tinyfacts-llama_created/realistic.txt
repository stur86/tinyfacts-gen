To be real means to be true to who you are. It is when things are as they are. It is about being yourself.

Imagine you are at a game with your friends. You are trying to hit a ball. If you say the ball is going to fly far, you are not real. You are not there. You are just talking about it. You are not really there.

If you are real, you can see the world around you. You do not hide your face. You are not afraid to show your face. You are not afraid to let people see how you feel. You are real because you are not afraid to be yourself.

A real story is one that happens. It is not a story about someone else. You can not change the story. You can not make it better. You can only tell it the way it happened. A real picture is one that shows how things look. You do not add anything. You do not make the colors bright. You just show what is there.

Real people are real. They do not pretend to be someone they are not. They do not act like they are something they are not. They are real because they are themselves. They do not try to be something they are not. They just are.

Real is important because it tells us what is real. If things are not real, we have no idea what is real. If things are not real, we are all just going to forget what we are.

To see something real is to feel it. It is to know what it is like. It is to understand it. Real life is real. Real people are real. Real stories are real. Real pictures are real.
