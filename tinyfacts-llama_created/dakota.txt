A long time ago, people in the area where the sky meets the ground said the land was made for a tall man. They said it was made for a man with a long neck. They thought the tall man lived in the high places, and the ground was for the man with a short neck. These people were from a place in the middle of the big water. They lived in houses with many rooms and a big room in the middle. They grew food that grew in the ground and made their own clothes and shoes. They were very good at making things from the wood and animal hides they found on the water.

Later, the tall man went away. The people in the high places had children of their own. They told the children they were the tall man. They made their houses like the one the tall man had. They had big rooms on the ground and small rooms in the middle. They grew food and made clothes. They also made things from the wood. Some of them built tall houses with many rooms, and some made houses with only one room.

The people in the middle places were told they were the one with the short neck. They built houses with one room and a small room in the middle. They grew food in the ground and made clothes. They made things from the wood. They were very good at making things from the wood.

A long time ago, the people from the high places and the middle places did not talk to each other. They thought they were the only ones who knew how to make things. But the tall man went away. He left behind two great men. He said the children of the tall man would become the ones who knew how to make things.

The children of the tall man became the ones who built the tall houses. The children of the middle places became the ones who built the small houses. The tall houses stood on the high ground for a long time. The people on the ground built their houses on the ground. The people on the ground built houses with many rooms. The tall houses stood for many years, but they went down when the ground was too hot to stand on.

Today, the people from the high places and the middle places live together. They have children of their own. They tell their children that they are the ones with the short neck. They say they made things for the tall man and the tall people. They have their own houses and their own ways. They are not the ones with the tall neck anymore.
