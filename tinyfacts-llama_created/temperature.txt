When you see the sun, it is hot. When you see the cold water, it is cold. When you are in the air that is not warm, you feel cold. This is because everything has a kind of warm or cold inside.

The warm stuff around you is moving very fast. The cold stuff is moving very slow. The warm stuff gets warm and gets hot. The cold stuff gets cold and gets even colder. This is why when you move from one place to another, the warm stuff gets warm and the cold stuff gets cold.

You can use a thing to check this. It is a box with two small parts. One part is hot and the other part is cold. You put a piece of paper into the box and watch what happens. If the paper is hot, it will come out of the box. If the paper is cold, it will stay in the box. This shows how the warm and cold parts are changing.

The air in your room is also like this. When you open the window and let the warm air out, the cold air comes in. This is why the room can feel cold if you leave the window open for a long time.

The world is full of this. The sun is hot at the top and cold at the bottom. The water in the ground is hot in the warm days and cold in the cold days. Even the ground can get warm and then cool when the sun goes down.
