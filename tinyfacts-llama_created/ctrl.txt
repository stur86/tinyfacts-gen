When you want to move a thing, you need to use your hand. Your fingers are the things you use. You press a finger on the thing. This makes the thing go in a way you want.

Your computer is a very big thing with many lines of numbers. You can use your fingers on the computer to move the lines around. You press a key on the computer. This makes the lines go to a different place on the computer.

There are many keys on a computer. Each key does one thing. You press the key for the thing you want to do. If you press the wrong key, the computer might do something wrong.

You can use the computer to write. You type on the computer. You press a key for the letter. You press a key for the word. The computer reads your words and sends them to a place where other people can see them.

You can also use the computer to look at pictures. You press a key for the picture. The computer shows you the picture on the computer. This is a good way to learn about things without looking at books.
