To save something is to get it out of a bad place. You might be afraid to go near a water that is too deep. You want to stay away because it looks dark and full of trouble. But someone else is not afraid. They go into the water and pull you out. They carry you up to dry land and give you a drink. This is a big act of help.

We also talk about saving people. If a child is being hurt by a strong animal, a kind person might go into the area. They watch for the animal and stay away. Then, another person comes with a bag and a stick. They help the child to a safe place where they can be cared for.

Sometimes, we need to save our own lives. If a fire starts, we tell others to leave the area. Then, we go to a place where people are safe. We may carry a small bag with us, with food and water. We stay there until the fire goes away or until the people around us are safe.

In a big city, there are also people who save people. If a person is being chased by the police, they might hide. But if they know the police will not catch them, they go into the street. They hide in a room or under a car. They wait until the police go by and then they run out. This is another act of help.

Saving is about giving people a chance to be safe. It is about being there for those who are hurting.
