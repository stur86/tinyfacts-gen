To remember something is to keep it in your mind so you can find it later. When you write down a name, a phone number, or a word, you are making a listen. This listen is like a piece of paper in your mind.

Think of a street. If you walk down the street, you need to remember where you are and where you are going. You look at the street signs, the numbers on the houses, and the faces of the people walking. You are using your memory to keep track of where you are.

When you talk to a friend, you remember their face, their voice, and their words. You are using your memory to remember them. This is the same with writing. You write down what you said, what you wrote, and what you think. You are keeping this piece of paper in your mind so you can look at it later.

To write something is to make a mark on a piece of paper. You might use a ball to hit a ball, or you might use a key to press a small round thing. When you press the key, the paper makes a mark.

Sometimes, we write when we are sad. We might write a story about what happened, or we might write a letter to a friend. When we write this, we are using our memory to remember what happened.

You can also use this word to talk about computers. A computer has a memory that holds many pieces of work. When you type on the computer, your words go into the computer. The computer looks at the words and keeps them in its memory. This memory is like a listen that the computer uses to remember what you have said.

In the world of money, you can also use this word. When you pay for something, you write your money down. You tell the person you need to give them money. The person reads the money and takes it. This is like using your memory to remember what you have given away.

Remembering is an important thing. It helps us stay safe, it helps us remember our friends, and it helps us remember important things.
