An old word for something you make to show how you feel.

When you make something, you put your hands and your heart into it. You might use wood, paper, or paint. You might draw a picture of what you want to feel. Or you might build a thing that moves. When you do this, you have made something new.

People also make things to remember what happened. You might draw a picture of a place you were in. Or you might write words in a book so that others can remember too. This is a way to keep your story for many years.

Sometimes, people make things to show how they feel. They might make a picture of a sad face so that others can see how they are feeling. Or they might build a thing that makes a loud sound so that everyone can hear. This helps us understand how others are feeling.

You can also make things to show what you think about a story. You might write a story and then make pictures of it. This helps people see how the story makes them feel.
