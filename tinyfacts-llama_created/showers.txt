To shower is to use water from a high place to clean your body. This happens when you have not had time to use the water from the bathroom. You stand under a big water thing, and the water falls on you from the top.

The water comes from a big box that holds water. The box has many small parts that open and close, so the water comes out. The water is very hot at first. It can make you feel very warm. After a while, the water gets cold and then it feels good to be clean.

You can use the water in many ways. You can let it run down your face so that it falls on your eyes and your skin. You can put your hands under the water and rub your arms and your body. You can even use the water to clean your hair. Some people like to use a wet hair thing to get ridden of the hair and make it feel soft.

Sometimes, you do this in the morning, after you wake up and before you have breakfast. This is a good way to start the day. It helps you feel happy and ready to go. You can also do it before you go to bed at night. This is a good way to clean yourself before you sleep.

You do not always need to shower. Sometimes, you can just sit in front of a window and feel the sun on your face. You can also feel the wind in your hair. This is a good way to relax and be calm.
