An area of land is a huge piece of ground. It is bigger than a small city block. You might see this word in the world where people grow food.

An area of land is often many hundreds or even many more than that. You can break it down into smaller parts. One part is as small as a small room, another part is as big as a room, and the rest of the area is much bigger.

To find out how big an area is, you use something called a number. A hundred feet is one hundred lines on a long piece of paper. If you have ten hundred feet, you have a big area. If you have five hundred feet, you have a small area.
