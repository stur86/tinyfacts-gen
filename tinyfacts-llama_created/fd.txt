To cut something means to break it into small pieces. This happens when you use a thing with hard parts to cut a thing that is made of other hard parts. You might use a thing with a long hard part to cut a round thing.

Imagine you have a thing with long hard parts that is made of hard white stuff. If you use this thing to cut a round thing, the long hard parts will break and the round thing will be broken into many small pieces. This is what happens when you cut something.

Sometimes, you might cut a thing that has many small pieces. You can take one of these pieces and put it into your body. This can be important if you want to make a new thing or if you need to fix a thing.
