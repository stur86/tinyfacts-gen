To have a high number is to have the very best. If you have a book with  ten hundred big words and you find a book with ten hundred small words, the big book is better.

Sometimes, when you say a number is high, you mean it is very big. A high number is like a very tall tree. It stands out from other numbers. You can see it from far away.

If you have a number and you want to know if it is the best, you can look at the numbers next to it. If the next number is smaller, the number before that is bigger. This shows the number is higher.

You can also say something is at its highest. This means it is the best or the most. Imagine you are playing a game where you are the best. If you do something and you are the best, you are at the highest.

In a group of people, if one person is the best, everyone else is at the highest. This is how we know who is the best person in a group.
