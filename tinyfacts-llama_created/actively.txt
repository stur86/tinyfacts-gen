To act something means you do it. If you want to learn how to ride a horse, you act on what you have learned. You go to a place where people learn how to ride, and you watch. Then you try it. If you fall, you act on the situation. You get up and try again. You keep trying until you are very good at it.

You can act in many ways. You can act by doing something. You can act by speaking. You can act by your body. If you are afraid, you act by being quiet. If you are happy, you act by smiling. You act when you want to show how you feel.

Sometimes, you act for someone else. You act when you want to help them. You act when you want to make them happy. You act when you want to tell them something important. You act when you want to show you care.

You can also act when you act against something. If you are angry, you act by being loud. If you are angry because of a fight, you act by fighting back. You act by being ready to use your words or your body.
