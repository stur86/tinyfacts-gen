To have a great or small number of things between you and another person or place means you are far away from them. Think of a tree. If you stand right next to the tree, you are very close. If you walk far away, you are a long way from the tree.

You can also have a great number of things between you and another person. Imagine a group of friends. If they are all in the same room, it is a small number of friends. If there are many people at a party and you do not see them, the number is great. You see many people, so the number is bigger.

Sometimes, the number of things between you and another person is the same. It does not matter if you are very close or very far away. You can still have a great number of friends.

If you have a great number of things, you may feel happy or excited. You have many new people to talk to or games to play. If you have a small number of things, you may feel sad or alone. You are far away from others.

You can also use this word in your day. If you have a great number of books to read, you have a lot of work to do. If you have a small number, you might feel that you do not have enough time.
