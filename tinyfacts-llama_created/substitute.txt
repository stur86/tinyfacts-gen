To fill in a place means to take something from another person. This is a way to get what you need when you are not able to get it yourself.

Imagine you are very sad. You want to play a game with your friends, but you are not feeling well. You can not play. So, you tell your mom you need to stay home.

Your mom says, you can watch your favorite show. You can watch it while you rest. You are not alone. You are with your mom.

You can also fill in a place in a job. If someone is very good at a job, you can ask them to fill in for a time. This helps the other person because they are busy doing other things.

You can also use this word when you are in a situation that is not good. You might feel angry, scared, or sad. You might need to find a way to stop the feeling. You can say, I am feeling this way, so I will fill in for a time.

In this way, we all have to fill in the places that need to be filled.
