A gaming person is a person who plays games. They often use a small piece of paper with numbers or a round piece of food that can be held in the hand. They move this piece around on a floor or on a table. They try to get it to the other person before they do. This game is often done with many people.

The word comes from the word for a man who plays games. In the old days, people had to be very strong and have good hands to play these games. They would also have to be very fast.
