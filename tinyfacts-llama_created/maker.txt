A maker is a person who makes things. They use their hands and their brain to make new things that we need or want. They might make clothes, a car, a book, or a house.

Sometimes a maker works in a shop. They sit at a table with many things to make and use their hands to put pieces together. Other times, they work in a room where they make things from the ground. They might pick the wood or the rocks they want and cut them into the right pieces.

A maker is different from a doer. A doer does something. A maker thinks about what they want before they start. They plan how they will make the thing. They might decide what color the walls should be or what kind of music to play while they work.

Making can be hard work. A maker might have to cut, press, or use power to make a thing. They might also have to follow many steps so that the thing is the right way. But when they see their finished work, they feel a good feeling. They know they made something that is important to people.

There are also things called making. This is when you make something that looks like a thing you find. You might make a rock look like a piece of wood. This can be fun because you get to use your brain and your hands to make something new.
