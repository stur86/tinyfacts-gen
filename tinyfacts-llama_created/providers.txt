To be a good friend means you care about them and want them to be happy. A good friend is a person you talk to when you need help or a smile. They listen and you listen back. You help each other when you need it.

A good thing to do is to help others. When you help someone, you make their life better. You are there when they need you. You are kind and you are there for them when they are sad or angry.

A good thing to do is also to share what you have. If you have food, you can give it to a person who does not have enough. This makes everyone happy and it makes the world a better place.

To be a good friend means you are kind, you listen, and you help them. You are there for them when they need you.
