To use something is to make it work so you can do things. When you turn on a light, you use a thing that makes tiny bits of power move through the air. This power makes the light turn on. You are using the power.

Things that make power are often big and heavy. They have many many tiny moving parts inside them. When you use one, the power goes out of the thing and into the air. The air takes the power and does things with it. This is how houses work.

We also use things that use power to move things around. Cars have this kind of power inside them. The power makes the car go very fast. You can use these things to go to school, run away, or go to work.

Power can also be sent through lines to many places. If you need power to make a phone ring, the power has to go through the lines. The power is sent from a big building to every house and car. This makes it easy to use things in many different places.
