Either means you decide what to do or what to not do. It is like a big word that shows you have to make a hard call.

If you are in a room with many people and there is a loud noise, you might want to close your eyes and hide. This is like either. You are deciding what to do with your body.

You could say, either you want to be quiet or you want to talk. This means you are not sure which is right. You are still thinking about it.

Sometimes, you might say you are either happy or sad. This is also a big call. You are deciding what to feel.

If you have a thing you love to eat, you might say, either you like it or you do not like it. This is also about a hard call.

Sometimes, people say, either you are going to the store or you are going to the movies. This means you have to decide which one you want to do.

It is a word that can be both easy to use and hard to understand. You have to think about what you want or what you do not want to do.
