Morning is the time just before the sun comes up. It is the time when the light from the top of the sky starts to grow. The light gets brighter and the stars go away.

The morning is often a good time. The world is quiet. There are no people walking around. The air is still. You can hear the sound of animals moving around outside. The sky is a beautiful color, like the bright blue of the sky when the sun is high up.

Most people wake up during the morning. They get out of bed and start to get ready for the day. They eat a light breakfast to give them the power to get going. Then they go to work, school, or to the park to play.

In the morning, the world feels new. Everything is still and quiet. The day is just beginning, and it is full of new things.
