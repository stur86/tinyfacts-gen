Things need something to move. When you sit still, you have no power. But if you run or jump, you use your body to push against the ground. This is a kind of power. You are using your body to do work.

Inside the world, there is also power. This is the power of the sun and the stars. They give us light and warm us during the day. They give us the air to breathe at night. This power is always there, even when we are not using it.

Things also have power inside them too. A small rock can do a lot of work if you push it around. A small animal can do a lot of work if it moves fast and jumps around. We need power to do our work. We need it to move things, to keep our bodies going, and to make the world around us feel nice.

You can also have power inside your mind. When you are happy or excited, your heart is beating fast. Your body is also moving. Your brain is sending good stuff to your body. This is your power. It helps you feel good and make you want to do things.

Sometimes, we need power to do things that are hard. If you want to walk very far, you need to get your legs moving. If you want to fly through the air, you need power from the sun or the wind. If you want to make things happen, you need power from inside you.
