The word sends goes back to a time before computers. You can send a thing to someone else. Imagine you have a small ball that you want to give to your friend. You hold the ball and walk a short way to the street. Then you throw the ball to the street so your friend can catch it. This is like sending the ball. You are giving it to the street.

Now, when you send a thing, you are telling a computer where to go. You tell it to go to a place on the computer. If you want to send a picture, you tell the computer the number of the place where the picture should go. The computer reads the number and goes to the place. It is like sending a letter, but instead of using a paper and a box, you use a computer.

You can send many things at once. If you have many balls to give to many friends, you can send them all at once. You are using the word sends to mean giving things to many people at once.
