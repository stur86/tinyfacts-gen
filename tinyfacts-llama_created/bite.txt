A bite is when you take a part of a thing. This happens when you use your mouth.

If you take a piece of food, you can bite it. You open your mouth and use your teeth to break the food into smaller pieces. Then you take a big bite with your whole mouth. This helps you eat and feel happy.

You can also bite things that hurt. If you fall and hit your face, your mouth might hurt. You bite your lip to stop the pain. You bite your hand if you are very angry.

Sometimes, you bite your tongue. This happens when you are afraid or you are angry. You want to hold your tongue in your mouth so you do not say something bad.

A person can also bite a friend. If a friend does something bad, you might bite them. You use your teeth to make them feel bad. This is how people fight and keep things safe.

When you bite something, you are taking a part of it. You are not taking a piece from a tree. You are taking the part from your mouth. This is how you eat and keep your body strong.
