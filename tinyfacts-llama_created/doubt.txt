To doubt something means you have a feeling that is not sure. It is when you think about a thing and you are not sure it is true.

Imagine you see a tall tree in a park. You want to climb it, but you are afraid. If you really want to see the top of the tree, you might doubt. You are not sure if it is safe to climb it. You might feel a little scared or worried.

You can doubt things in many ways. You might doubt a teacher if they are not doing their job well. You might doubt a friend if they are not being kind. You might even doubt your own body. If you are afraid to leave your house, you might doubt yourself.

Sometimes, people doubt if something is going to happen. They might think, if it happens, it will be bad. Or they might think, if it happens, it will be good. They might not know for sure.

Doubt is an important thing to have. It helps us learn. When we doubt, we are trying to figure out if something is true. This makes us better at thinking and at being a person.

But sometimes, we doubt things that are not true. This is not good. If you doubt your life, you are not happy. If you doubt your friends, you are not kind. If you doubt your own power, you are not strong.

To let go of doubt, you need to try to be sure. You need to look at facts and listen to what others say. You need to trust your own brain. When you trust your brain, you feel better. You feel more sure.
