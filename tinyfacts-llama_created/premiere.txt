To start something new is a big thing. This word is about a very first time.

Imagine you have a small box of things you love. You put them together in a way that is new for you. This is like a start. You open the box and the things are there. This is how you begin something new.

When a group of people do this, it is called a show. They open a box of stories or music and act them out. This is the first time the story is seen. The box is full of new things. Everyone watches it together.

Sometimes, a group of people do this for a very long time. They put on many boxes of new stories and act them out. This is how they make a story that is known all over the world.

In a box of food, the first food is the one that is inside the box. This one is the start. The rest are made from it.

This word can also mean the first day of a big eventually. Think about a place where many people meet. The first day is the start of the meeting. Everyone is there. They are ready to talk and know each other. It is the first time they see each other.

So, to start something new is about taking the first step. It is about letting go of the old and moving to the new.
