To be in a group means you are part of a group of people, animals, or things. It means you are not alone. A group can be big or small. Sometimes a group has a leader. Other times, everyone works together.

When you say something is a number, you are talking about how many things you have. If you have ten books, you have ten. If you have one book and two more, you have three books. Numbers help us know how many things are around us.

Sometimes we use a number to show how much more or less something is. If you have a bag of money and you spend ten more than you had, you are in trouble. If you have ten more books than your friend, you are doing well.

There are also numbers that show how fast something moves. If a car goes one hundred feet in one minute, it is going one hundred feet every minute. A tall tree can grow a lot of numbers of feet over many years.

In a group, we use these numbers to plan and decide. We might decide how to share the money or how to work together. Numbers help us make good plans.

When you talk about something that is not a number, you are talking about a person or a thing that is real. A person might say they love to play music. A thing might be a book that tells a story. These things are important because they help us know about the world and the people in it.
