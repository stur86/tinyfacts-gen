To have a loud sound is to make a big noise that can be heard from far away. This is one way a dog might act when he is very happy.

A person can also make a loud sound with their voice. When a person is angry, they might say something very hard to understand. A mother might say,  Stop making so much noise. This is to tell the child to be quiet.

Sometimes we use this word to talk about a loud car. If a car is going very fast, the sound of the car is loud. If a car is slow, the sound is quiet. This helps us know how fast the car is going.

In a group, when everyone is talking, the sound of their voices can get very strong. This can be very much fun for people who like to listen to music.
