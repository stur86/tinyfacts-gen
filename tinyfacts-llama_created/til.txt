To go down a long way is to move your body or your car to the bottom of a place. You might walk down a road or run down a long walk. The word can also mean to move something down. If you put something in a tall box, you are going down to the bottom. This is because the box is getting smaller as it goes down.

You can also use this word to talk about how something feels. If you are very tired, you might feel like you are going down. Your body feels heavy and you want to sit down. This is because your body is tired and needs rest.

Sometimes, to go down a long way means you are moving away from a place. If you live in a city and you want to go to the next town, you are going down the road. You are leaving your home behind and moving to a new place.
