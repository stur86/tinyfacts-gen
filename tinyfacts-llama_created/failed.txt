To have a problem and not be able to fix it is called to have a problem. Sometimes, when you try to do something, you do not get the right answer. You might feel sad or angry. This is a problem.

Imagine you want to eat a piece of food, but you do not have enough money. You want to buy a drink. You try to find a money box, but it is gone. You are not able to buy the drink. This is a problem because you wanted to do something but you could not.

Sometimes, a problem is a person. A person might not follow what they say. They might act in a way that is not good. This is because they are not following the words they say. They are not the same person they were before.

A problem can also be something in your world. You might have a cold. You feel very bad. You do not want to go to school. You want to stay in bed. This is because you are not well.

To fix a problem is to change it so it is not a problem anymore. If you do not fix a problem, it can become worse. It might cause more hurt or make things even harder.
