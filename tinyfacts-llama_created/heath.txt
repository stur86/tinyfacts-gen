A heart is a place in your body. It is a small area on your chest, just under your arms. When you are very happy, you might feel your heart beating fast. This happens because your body wants to give you more blood to your brain and other parts that need it.

Your heart is made of strong, red stuff. It is like a big, round ball. It goes round and round inside your chest. As it goes, it pushes air into your body. This pushes air out to all your parts. It keeps your blood going and makes sure your brain and other parts stay strong.

Sometimes, you might feel a heavy feeling in your chest. This is because your heart is working very hard. If the heavy feeling goes away after you rest, it is okay. But if the heavy feeling stays, you should go see a doctor.
