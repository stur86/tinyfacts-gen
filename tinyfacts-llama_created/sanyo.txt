To say a word with two names is to say that the two names are the same but the word is different. This is important because it helps us not get confused. 

The word you use when you say a person is the name. You might say,  This is a man. The name is the one you use. But if you say,  This is a man, you are telling the same thing. The name is still the same, but you are telling the same story. 

Think of it like a room. You might say,  I am in this room. This is the same as,  I am in this room. They are the same thing. But if you say,  This is my room, you are telling a different story. You have changed the word.

Sometimes, the name and the word are the same. You might say,  This is a car. The car is also the word. They are the same. This is why we use them together. We tell the story with the name first, and then we tell the story with the word.
