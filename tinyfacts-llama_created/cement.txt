Calm is a word that means to be very quiet and still. When you calm a child, you make them feel better. You sit with them and say nice things. This makes them feel safe and happy. When you calm a dog, you help the dog feel better when it is scared.

Calm is about letting go of your body. It is like walking along a long road and not thinking about anything. You do not worry about what you did the day before. You just walk and enjoy the feeling of being still.

You can also calm a thing. When a house is very hot, the walls get hot too. If you cover them with a soft cover, the hot air goes away and the room gets nice and quiet. This makes people feel happy and safe inside the house.

A big thing that makes things stand up is also called a kind of calm. It is a hard white stuff that you put on the ground. When this stuff is on the ground, it makes the ground feel like a floor. It makes things stand up straight and does not make the ground shake. This is very important for people and animals who live on the ground.

So, calm is about being quiet and still. It is also about letting go of your body and making people or things feel better.
