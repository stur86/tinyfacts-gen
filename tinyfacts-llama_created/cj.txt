A car is a thing you use to go from one place to another. You put your feet inside it and hold on so you do not fall out. The car moves very fast. People sit inside and sometimes they wear a thing that goes around their head so they can hear what other cars are saying.

Cars are very good for people who do not have a house. They go where you want to go, even if you do not know the way. They carry many people at once. They also carry food and water to make sure you stay safe on your way.

Some cars are very small. They go very fast and they can carry a lot of people. Other cars are very big. They have many seats and many rooms inside. Some cars have many doors. You can get in and out many times.

You can buy a car with your money. Some people buy a car every year. They get a new car after their old one breaks down. This keeps the cars going and people can move around easily.
