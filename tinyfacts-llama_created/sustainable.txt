To be green means to keep things going in a way that does not hurt the world. When we say something is green, we are talking about the color of it. It is the color of the ground, the trees, and the water. We also use this word to mean how a thing is made. If a thing is made of many small parts, and these parts do not break easily, we say it is green. It is a way of living that is good for everyone and for the land.

You can also be green if you are doing something good for the world. If you help the animals, or if you do not use many things that can hurt the air, you are being green. This means you are the kind of person you want to be.

Sometimes, when we talk about green, we talk about money. If you buy things that are made of the ground, or if you buy things that do not hurt the air, you are being green. This is a way of living that is good for everyone. It is a way to live that does not hurt the world.

In some places, people say that a place is green because it is very beautiful. If a place has a lot of green trees, and the air is very clean, it is a place that is green. This means the place is a happy place for people and for animals.

To be green is also about how we act. If you do not throw things into the water, and you do not make too much noise when you walk, you are being green. This means you are being kind to the world and to all living things.
