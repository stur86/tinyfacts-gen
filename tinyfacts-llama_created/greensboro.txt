The city has a big high school. Many students who live there go to this high school. They learn many things like how to read, write, and do numbers. They also learn about how to make things from green things like wood or paper.

In this city, there are many people who speak different kinds of words. Some speak the word green, some speak the word blue, and some speak the word red. But they all understand that green is a strong color that can be found in many things.

People also call this city a place of change. A long time ago, many people moved from a far away place. They brought their old ways of life with them.
