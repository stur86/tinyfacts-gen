A thing that moves water is a very important part of many homes and places. It goes around and round, pushing the water through long long lines. This water then goes into big rooms where people drink and use to clean their hands. Without a thing like this, many places would not be able to stay filled with water for drinking.

Sometimes a person fills a big round thing in their hand. They press it with their fingers and let the water come out. This is how people fill water for drinking, for a car that needs to go fast, or for a baby when they want to give it to eat.

It is also used in places where the ground is high. There are big long lines that go under the ground. When you turn a handle, the water moves out of the lines and up. This water goes into the ground, where it gets ready to come back up again.

In many places, people have a big round thing that goes around and around in their kitchen. They press a round thing inside the kitchen, and the round thing moves with the water. The round thing pushes the water around the round thing that makes food. The round thing that makes food is very good at making food come out. It helps make food for dinner, for breakfast, and for many other times in between.

So, a thing that moves water is very important. It helps keep homes filled with water for people to drink, and it helps make food for many people.
