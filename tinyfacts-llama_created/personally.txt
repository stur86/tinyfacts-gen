To do something is to act. This means you use your body or your mind to make it happen. You might do this with a word, a picture, or a story.

If you do something for someone, you do it because they ask you to. You might help them. You might give them a thing they need. You are doing it because they are the person you care about.

If you do something by yourself, you do it alone. You are the only one doing it. You are not doing it for anyone else. This is a good thing. It is important to feel this way.

You can also do something that is not for anyone. You might do it because you want to. You might do it because it makes you happy. You are doing it because you enjoy it.

When you say something is your idea, you mean it is something you think about. You are doing it because you want it to happen. You are the one who decides.

Sometimes, you might do something that is not safe. You might do it without a plan. You might do it because you are afraid. But if you do something because you are afraid, you are still doing it. You are not in control. You are following what others tell you to do.
