To rest something means to stop doing it. Imagine you are playing with a ball. You run to the top of a high ground. You feel happy. You jump down. You land on the ground with a soft sound. You feel good. Now, you are not running anymore. You are just standing there. Your body is still warm from the jump.

If you have a thing that is broken, you can try to fix it. You look at it. You see that it has a small cut. You take a small bit of hard stuff and put it into the cut. Slowly, the cut starts to close. The thing becomes strong again. It is like your body, but it is a thing that was broken.

When you rest, you are not doing anything at all. You are just being. You are still a living thing. You are still a person. You are still a thing. Rest is an important part of life. It helps us feel safe and calm. It lets us be ready for what comes next.
