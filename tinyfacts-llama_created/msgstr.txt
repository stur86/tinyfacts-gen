A word is the thing that makes a sound. It is the word that you use to talk to other people.

When you are writing a letter to a friend, you need to write the word that means you. If you want to say, you are happy, you write the word happy. You do not write the word because you are happy. You write the word because you are writing.

In a long book, the person who wrote the words has many words. If you want to learn what the person means, you read the words. You do not read the word because you are learning. You read the word because you want to know.

A computer has words too. It reads and writes words. It uses a kind of word to send and get things. A computer word is like a word, but it is not a word you write with your hand. It is a kind of word that it makes by turning numbers into words.

When you put a computer word into a computer, the computer reads it. The computer looks at the words and says, yes, I know what this word means. Then, the computer uses the word to send or get a thing. It is like a person, but it does not have a face or a body. It only works with numbers and words.

Sometimes, you see a word from a book. You want to know what the word means. You look at the word and you read it. Then you know what the word means.
