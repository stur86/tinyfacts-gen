To send something through the air is a very easy thing. You can write a letter and put it in a box with a cover. Then you send the box to someone else who has a box in their home. The box goes from your home to their home, and the person reads what you wrote.

But the thing about the air is that nothing comes back. The air goes in one place and out in another. So when you send a letter, it goes out and never comes back. You can not see the box as it goes. You can only wait for it to arrive.

Most people use this kind of sending when they are far away. They write things to friends or family, and they send them through the air. It is faster than sending a letter by the street. It also saves a lot of paper.
