A person who buys things for others is called a store owner. They make money from this work. They buy things to give to people who want them.

When you buy a book, a game, or a thing you need, you are buying from a store. The store owner says, here is what you need. You pay money for it.

If you do not have money, you can ask a store owner to help you. They give you the money you need to buy the thing. This helps you get the things you want.

Some stores are very big. They have many rooms and many people working there. Others are small. They only have one room and one person. But all stores are the same. They all help people get what they need.

You can buy things from a store in many ways. You can go there and pick out what you want. You can look at things for a short time, and then you buy them. You can also ask for help and they will give you what you need.

Some stores are in houses. They are inside houses. Others are in big places with many rooms. They are all places where you can buy things.

If a store is very good, people go there many times. They buy many things. This is why the store has so many people working there.
