To form a part of something together is to join with another person so you both have a share.

Imagine you want to build a house. You need many people to help. You can't do it all by yourself. You need someone to help you make the walls, someone to help you put the floor on, and someone to help you put the top on. In this case, you and one other person form a team. You both have a share of the work and the money.

In this way, you are not alone. You have another person who is also helping. This is a good way to work together and share the work.

You can also form a team with animals. A dog and a cat might be friends. They do not have to be the best of friends. They just do work together for their owner. They help the owner by doing things like cleaning, chasing small animals, or being quiet.

Sometimes, a group of people form a team. They might be doing something for a big cause. They work together to make the world better. They share the work and the money. They all have a say so everyone feels part of the team.

You can also form a team with your friends. You are all going to a movie. You all sit together and watch the movie. You share the food and the money. Everyone is happy because they are all together doing the same thing.

In business, you can form a team with other people to make money. One person might be the one who does the work. Another person might be the one who makes the money. Together, they make the business work. They share the work and the money.

In a game, you can form a team with your friends. You all play the game together. You all try to window the ball. You share the ball so everyone can have a turn.

Forming a team is a good way to work with others. It helps when you have a lot of work to do. It helps when you need to share the work. It helps when you need to make things happen together.
