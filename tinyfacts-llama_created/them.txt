When you talk about people, you are talking about them. You might say,  He is a friend. This means you like him.

When you talk about things, you are talking about them too. You might say,  The sun is bright. This means the sun is making the light.

But you can also talk about things that are not people or not bright. You might say,  The book is old. This means it is an old thing. It does not tell you who the person was.

Sometimes, when you talk about a group of people, you might use the word them. This means all of them together. If you say,  They are going to a party, you mean everyone in the group is going.

Think about it this way. If you say,  The cat is sleeping, you are talking about the cat. If you say,  The cat is sleeping with her mother, you are talking about her family.

So, when you talk about people, things, or groups of people, you are talking about them. You are using the word to tell you about what is happening.
