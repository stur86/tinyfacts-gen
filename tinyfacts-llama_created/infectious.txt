An idea that goes from person to person is called a thing that can be passed. It can be a word, a feeling, or a tiny thing that you breathe. When a person has this idea, they might act in strange ways. They might talk very fast, or they might laugh or cry more than usual.

Think of a fight. If two people want the same thing, the fight is because of the idea. The people are both fighting because they believe that thing. The fight is not about the fight. It is about the thing.

Sometimes, the idea is small at first. But it can grow very fast. One person starts to believe it and then they tell others. The idea moves from person to person until it is in every body in a city, a school, or a work place. When everyone has the idea, it is not a fight anymore. It is a shared feeling.

The word often means a thing that makes you feel bad or makes you want to move around. When you are not feeling well, you might feel like you want to go out and play. This is because of the idea. The idea is like a wave that goes through you and makes you want to move.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about how a thing works. An idea is like a thing that can change how you feel or what you do. If you have a good idea, you might feel happy or excited. If you have a bad idea, you might feel scared or angry.
