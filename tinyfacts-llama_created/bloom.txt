To grow is to change. When a thing is small and green, it starts to change. It gets bigger, and its leaves turn into white. This is when the thing starts to look like a tree.

A person can grow too. You grow when you are very old. Your body gets taller. Your hair turns white. Your eyes get bigger. You can walk very fast now.

An animal grows too. A baby animal is very small. It is covered in soft, white stuff. As the baby grows, its soft stuff turns into a hard skin. The skin is like a cover for the body. The animal grows legs and feet so it can walk.

When a thing grows, it gets ready for something new. It gets ready to make a new thing. This is why a small thing can grow into a big thing.

You can also grow into a new person. You are small when you are a baby. You grow into a big person. You learn new things and become stronger. You can make more friends. You can do new things.

Sometimes we grow into new jobs. If you are good at a job, you can move to a different place. You get paid more money. You use your brain and your body to do a new job. Your job makes you grow as a person.

Growing is a very big thing. It happens in every living thing. It happens in people, animals, and trees.
