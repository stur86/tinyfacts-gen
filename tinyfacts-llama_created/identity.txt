To be someone is to be you. It is how you act and how you look. It is the person you are inside your heart. If you change who you are, you are no longer the same person.

Your name is part of who you are. It tells everyone who you are and what you do. Your age, your hair color, your clothes, and your voice are all part of your own story. You are the person these things tell you you are.

Your family is also a part of who you are. The people you love and the people you do not like are a big piece of your life. You are a part of many people and many places, and these are all part of your story.

You are also the way you act. If you smile and laugh, you are a happy person. If you are kind to everyone, you are a good person. People who know you and like you can tell when you are happy or when you are sad. They can tell because of the way you look and the way you move.

Sometimes, you are different from who you think you are. This is okay. You can still be the person you are, even if you have changed a little bit. You can still be the person you love, and you can still have the same heart. This is what it means to be different.

You can not change your whole life at once. It takes time to get used to who you are. It is okay to feel strange or to be scared. But if you stay true to yourself and to the people who love you, you will stay the same person inside and out.
