To Tore

To tore is to pull a thing very hard. It is often done with a thing that is very heavy. You might use it to get something out of a door or to break a thing that is very strong.

When you pull, you pull in the same way. You pull on one side and then you pull on the other side. This makes the thing go in the air and then it hits the ground.

It is also a word for a group of people. If many people are pulling together, they are to the same thing. They are all moving in the same way. They are not moving in different directions.

Sometimes you might say a thing tore. This means it went very fast and it broke into many pieces. The pieces go in different directions. It is like a very fast wind that goes in many different directions.

You can also say a thing tore the floor. This means a heavy thing broke and it made a lot of noise. The floor is broken. It is not safe anymore.
