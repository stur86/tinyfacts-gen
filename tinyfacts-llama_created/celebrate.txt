To say you are going to do something is to plan for the day or the night ahead. This is often done in front of many people. You might write down the plans. You might also tell your friends.

To say something happened is to tell others about it. If you did something amazing, you might tell your friends. If you found a beautiful thing, you might tell everyone.

You can also say that a time is here. When the sun is in the sky and the stars are out, everyone knows it is time to stop what you are doing. You might say, The time to eat dinner is now.

People often use this word with things that are important. They might say, We are going to the party. Or, She is going to her job. Or, We are going to the movies tonight.
