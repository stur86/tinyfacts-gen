A word you use to talk about days and time.

When you talk about the day, you might say,  this is a beautiful day. Or, I love playing outside on this day. Or, The sun is up. You use these words to tell what is happening in your life.

In the morning, the word you use is, today. You look at a paper and see the day that is starting. It helps you know when to go to school, when to eat breakfast, and when you should go to bed.

In the evening, you might say, I am going home. This word helps you plan your day. You know where you are going and when you will get there.

Sometimes you use this word with a number. If you have two days of work to do, you can say, I have two days. This is important because it helps you stay on top of what you need to do.

You also use this word to talk about time. In the morning, it is time to wake up. In the evening, it is time to go to bed. This word helps you know when to be up and when to be down.

So, the word you use to talk about days and time is a very important word. It helps you stay on top of what is happening in your life.
