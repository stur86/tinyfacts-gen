To make something work means to make it do what you want it to do. A long, hard piece of stuff is one way to make this happen. You can think of it like the lines that go through the city. They go from one building to another, from one street to another. Every building and every street has a line in it. The lines carry people, cars, and other things from one place to another.

In a house, there are many of these lines. They go under the floor and under the walls. They carry power from a big building in the town to every house in the city. This power makes the lights, the air, and the water work.

Another kind of line is one that goes through the ground. These lines are like big, hard arms that reach down into the ground. They carry power from the big building to far away places. This kind of line is very important for things that need power far away from the city.

Sometimes, people use these lines to send things across the water. If you have a big box of food and you want to give it to someone far away, you can use a long line to carry it across the water. The line goes under the water and from the water to the other side.

A long time ago, people used these lines to carry food and other things from the city to the fields where they grew food. This helped people grow food and had a big part to play in how we live our lives today.
