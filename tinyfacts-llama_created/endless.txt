To be in an end is to be done. It means a thing is finished or has stopped. When you finish a book, you are in an end. When you close a door, you are in an end. You can be in an end of a line, a line of people, or a line of things.

You can also be in an end of a story. Think of a movie. At the end, the good guys have won't give up. The bad guys have been stopped. They are at the end of the story.

Sometimes, an end is a time of the day. If it is very late, the day is almost over. The sun will be gone. You can say, it is almost night.

In some cases, an end is a thing that ends a thing. If you put the top off a bottle of water, you have ended the water. You can't drink the water anymore.

It is also important to be in an end of a feeling. If you feel sad, the feeling will end. When you are happy, the feeling will stay on your body. You might laugh, or you might cry. You are in an end of that feeling.
