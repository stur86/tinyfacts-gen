When something happens, it can be done. When a thing happens, the world changes. This change can be good or bad.

If you do something, it is happening. This is how we know. We see it. We hear it. We feel it. When you do something, a story starts. The story tells us about you and what you did.

Sometimes, something happens in a place. This place is a big area where many other places are near each other. A place where many people live is a place of many people. A place where many animals live is a place of many animals.

When something happens, it is not just for one person. Many people are around. They might feel the same way. Some might feel scared. Some might feel happy. The world is full of many happen.
