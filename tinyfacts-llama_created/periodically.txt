When you talk about something happening, you might say it happens a lot. This word is very good for this kind of talk.

To say something happens is like saying a thing is there. To say something happens a lot is like saying a thing happens many times. This is how you change the way you talk about something.

Imagine you have a dog. You take him out in a park many times a day. After a while, he gets tired. The dog needs rest. This is something that happens a lot. 

You can also use this word to talk about a big thing that happens in the world. If a city is building a new road, this is happening a lot. People talk about it because it will change their lives and make their city better.

You can use the word to talk about how things change. If a small thing is broken, it might break many times. This is because things break when they get old or when they are put in a place where they are not safe.

This word is important because it shows you think about how things move and change over time.
