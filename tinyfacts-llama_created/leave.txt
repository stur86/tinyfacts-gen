To leave means you go away from a place or a person. It is when you move from one place to another, often to a far away land. You might go to a city, a town, or a very big city. Sometimes, you leave your family and friends behind. You go to a new home where you live alone.

You can also leave something behind. If you are sad, you might leave your favorite book or a small thing that brings you a happy feeling. You put it in a bag or a box so it is safe while you are moving to a new place.

To leave something means you are taking it away. You might leave a chair in a room so a new person can sit in it when they come in. This helps keep the room clean and makes it ready for people who will use it later.

Sometimes, we leave things for other people. We might give money to a person who helps us. This is a way of saying we trust them. We hope they will use it to make a good life.

You can also leave the door open. If a house has a door that closes, you might leave it open so the wind can go through. This helps the air move around the house and keeps the door from getting too hot inside.
