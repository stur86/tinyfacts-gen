To be a part of something can be different for each person. Think about your school. You are one of the many people who go there. You do not have a name like the other kids. You are a part of the group.

You might feel happy when you join. You are part of your family. You have friends who know you. You do not have to tell anyone you are there. You just go.

You might be a part of a group that makes music. You play a song that people like. You are part of a group that works together. You are part of a group that shares ideas.

Sometimes, you are a part of a group that has a leader. This person tells everyone what to do. You follow them because you trust them. You do not have to know everything. You just follow the leader.

Being a part of a group can be nice. You are with people who are the same as you. You can talk and laugh with them. You feel safe because you are not alone.

But being a part of a group can also be hard. You might feel like you are not the best. You might not know how to act. You might feel like you are being told what to do instead of being able to decide.

Everyone feels this way. We all need to be part of something to feel safe and happy. It is how we learn to live with others.
