To dress means to put clothes on your body. You put on a shirt. The shirt is a top piece of clothes. It covers your chest. You put on pants. The pants are the parts of your clothes that go under your shirt. They are usually made of a hard stuff that keeps you safe when you are moving.

Sometimes you dress for a party. You put on a dress. It is a pretty piece of clothes. It goes around your body and you wear it. This is different from putting on a shirt and pants. When you dress, you want to look nice. You might wear a dress if you are a girl. You might wear clothes that are not for work. You might wear a shirt with a funny picture on it. You might wear a long dress with a high neck.

Dressing is also about feeling ready. If you are going to go to a house, you might dress. You might wear a nice shirt and pants. You might wear a dress if you are going to a party. You want to feel good when you are dressed.

You can also dress when you are afraid. If you are afraid, you might dress in a way that makes you feel safe. You might wear a shirt with many colors. You might wear pants that are hard to move. You might put on a jacket with a soft face. This helps you feel less afraid.

Dressing is a part of how we act. When you dress, you show the world who you are. You tell them you are a person with a job, a family, and a life. You show them you are a person who cares about how they look.
