To be under a hard thing means you are under its power. You can not move. You are not free.

You can be under a hard thing by being under a strong person. The person is too strong for you. They are making you do what they want. You are afraid to say no.

You can also be under a hard thing because of something you did. If you did something wrong, you are under the power of the thing. It is telling you to pay a fine or to go to a place where you have to stay.

When you are under a hard thing, you do not have the power. You can not leave. You are not in control. This is when you are in trouble.
