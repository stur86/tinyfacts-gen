To be somewhere means you are not in one place. It is like you are in many places at once.

Think of a room. You are in this room. You can hear the sound of people outside. You can see the light from the window. But you are still in the room. You are not on the floor. You are not in the air. You are in the room.

You can also be in many different places at once. You might be in a house, a park, a school, and a store all at the same time. You are not in one place for a long time. You move from one place to another.

Sometimes, you might feel like you are in many places at once. You feel the warm sun on your skin. You feel the cold wind on your face. You feel the soft ground under your feet. You feel like you are in many different places at once.

You can also be somewhere you do not want to be. You might be in a place you do not like. The food is not good. The people are very loud. You do not want to stay there. You want to leave and find a better place.

Being somewhere is a big idea. It is about where you are and where you want to be. It is about moving around and finding your way.
