Housing is where people live. People build these houses so they can stay. Some houses are very big. Others are small. Some are in the middle of a big city. Some are in a far away place. These houses are made of many different parts. They might have strong walls to keep people safe.

There are many kinds of housing. Some are made of wood. Some are made of hard stuff that makes a sound when you knock on the door. Some are made of cold stuff that keeps the air from coming in. Some are made of strong stuff that keeps the sun out.

Houses can be very different. Some have many rooms. Some have only one room. Some have a big room in the middle and small rooms around it. Some have a big room at the bottom and small rooms at the top. Some have rooms on top and rooms on the bottom. Some have rooms in the middle. Some have no rooms at all.
