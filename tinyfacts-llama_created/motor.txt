A car has many moving parts. Some are very small and turn very fast. These parts move the car from one place to another. They are like a big team of tiny animals.

The big part at the back of the car is the biggest one. It makes the whole car go. This part is called the back end. It turns around and around many times while the car is moving.

There is also a part in the front of the car. It helps the car go straight. This part is called the front end. It helps the car not to hit the things in the road.

Inside the car, there are many more parts. Some are for the driver. These parts help the driver feel safe and know where they are. There are parts to help the car stop. There are also parts to make the car go very fast.

The power for these parts comes from a box. This box has many small parts that move around. When the box moves, it makes the power. This power goes through the long parts of the car and into the tiny moving parts.

The tiny moving parts turn around and around. They push and pull on the other parts of the car. This makes the car go. The car also has parts to make the car go fast. When the car is moving very fast, these parts make the car go even faster.

All these parts work together. When the driver turns the key in the car, the power starts to go. The car begins to move, and the driver can feel the car go. The driver can also see the road ahead by looking at the car and the road.

A car can move on many different types of roads. Some roads are very wide and easy to drive on. Other roads are very small and have many turns. A car can also go very fast on a long straight road.

A car is a very important thing for people. It helps them go to work, school, and to visit friends. It can carry many people at once. It can also carry things like food, clothes, and money.
