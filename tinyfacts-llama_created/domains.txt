A computer has many different ways to talk to each other. When a computer does something, it can only talk to other computers in one way. It is like a door that says, only one computer can enter.

Think of a box. The box can only open the door to one room. If you try to open the door to another room, the box says, no, you can't. That is like a computer.

The sky is a big room that is all around you. But the sky does not say, only one room. It says, yes, you can enter many rooms.

A group of people can also have many different ways to talk to each other. The group can say, we all talk to each other like this. They are in the same room and use the same words.

A computer has a set of words that say, do this, do that. But the words are like a door. You can open the door to many different words and get different answers.

All living things have this too. A tree, a dog, and a person all have doors that say, only one door can enter at a time. They can only talk to each other through one door.

This is why computers and living things have different ways. Computers stay in one way, but living things stay in many ways.
