A person or a group that you are not a part of can be held for what they did. This is called a job. They have to pay money. This money is called money you are held to.

Imagine you are the one who lets a dog run in the town. If the dog gets hurt while running, you have to pay money to the dog. The money you pay is like a job. The person who lets the dog run is the one who has to pay.

If you do not pay, you might have to pay more money later. This is because the money you do not pay for is like a job that you have not done yet. You have to pay it before you can do other jobs.

You can also have a job because you are a part of something. If you are a part of a team, you are also a job. You work with others and do things that are important for the team. You do not have to pay money for this job.

But there is also a job that you do not do. If you do not do a job, you can be held for it. This can happen with people who are not in a job. They might do something that is not right. They might do something that makes others afraid. In this case, the person who did not do the job has to pay money.

People also use this word with computers. A computer has jobs to do. It follows orders. It does not make its own plans. It only follows what someone tells it to do. If you do not tell the computer what to do, you are the one who has to pay.
