To send something is to move it from one place to another. You might send a letter to a friend who lives far away. You write the words you want to say, and the letter goes through the air until it reaches your friend. When the letter arrives, your friend reads the words and feels happy because you are thinking of them.

You can also send things that you do not own. If you want a book, you can send it to the store. The store will pick it up from you and put it in a bag. Then they will put the bag in a car. The car drives to the store, and a person in the store takes the bag out. The bag goes to another city or town, and a person in that town takes it to your home. This is how books and other things are sent from one place to another.

Sometimes, you send things that are made by a person but are not yourself. You might give money to a person who does not need it. Or you might give a picture to a friend who likes it. When you send something, you are giving it to someone else so they can have it.
