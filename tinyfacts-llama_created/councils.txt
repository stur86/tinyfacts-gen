A group of people that help make big changes happens when many of them are asked to work together. These groups are made up of people from a whole city, town, or even a big area.

The people who make this happen are called the people of the area. They have a problem they want to fix. They ask the other people in the area to help them. The group then works together to find a good way to fix the problem. They talk, think, and try to find a way that will help everyone.

The people of the area might decide to change something like a street, a school, or a way the police act. They might also decide to help people who are in trouble. They might bring money to help people who are sick or hurt.

These groups are important because they help everyone in the area feel safe and happy. They also help people learn from each other and from their own lives.
