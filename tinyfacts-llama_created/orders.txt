An order is a way to tell someone to do something. When you give an order, you are telling them what to do.

You can order a person to do something that is very important. This is the most important kind of order. You are telling them to do it right now, or soon.

You might order a person to pick up something from a store. You tell them to go and get the thing from the store. You might say, you need to pick up a book. This means you are giving them a very clear and important job to do.

You can also order someone to do something for a bit. You might say, please move the chair. This is like a small order. It is a way to get someone to do something for a short time.

Sometimes, people order each other. They say, you have to do this. This means one person is telling another person to do something. This is a way to set a right way for everyone.

Orders are used in many ways. In a school, you might have to follow the orders of the teacher. In a work place, you might have to follow the orders of your manager. In a kitchen, the orders of the head person are very important.
