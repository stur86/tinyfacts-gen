The World Wide Team is an important group that works to keep the world safe. It is made up of many different groups from all the big cities and the big states.

When people buy things from one place to another, it is like a small problem. The city in the place where the thing is made might not be happy. They might say that the thing is not good enough. They might say it is not safe to buy. This can cause a lot of money for everyone.

The group works with the cities and the states to make sure that the things are safe and the money goes where it should. They also help when there is a big problem. They can talk and work together to make things right.

The group is run by a man who is very good at talking and working. He has many years of work in many different places. He has seen a lot of problems and has learned how to stay calm and how to find a good answer.

The group is also very good at making friends with the people who are in trouble. If a city is very sad, the group might send someone to talk to them. If a city is very angry, the group might send someone to help them.

The group is not just for the cities. It is also for the people who buy things. They help make sure that the things are made to stay safe for a long time. They help the people who buy things know if the things they buy are safe and good for them.

The group is also important for the money. When a city says that a thing is not safe, it can make it hard for people to buy that thing. The group helps to make sure that the money goes to where it is needed most.
