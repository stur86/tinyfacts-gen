When you say something will happen soon, you say it will come soon. You do not say it will take a long time. You say it will happen now. This is because you are ready. You are waiting to see what happens.

If a person is already dead, you do not say they will die. You say they died. This is because the person is gone. It is not waiting to happen anymore. The word is used for things that are ready to happen right now. It is about how you feel about a situation.
