A big area where many water ways meet is called a wide. This area is usually on a high ground, and water from many small lands joins together. As the water comes out of the ground and over the top of the ground, it becomes much wider. This wide area is part of a bigger water area.

You can also talk about a wide in a different way. In this case, a wide is a place where many roads meet. There are many roads that go around this wide area. It is a place where many people go in one direction and another group of people go in the other direction.

A wide can also be a place where the ground is very high and the water is very low. This is often seen in places where the ground is very high on top and the water is very clear and blue.
