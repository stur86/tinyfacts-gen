To arrive means you are getting to a place. When you are in a city, you are still in a town. But if you walk to a far away part of the city, you are arriving. You are getting there.

It can also mean something is happening. If you are waiting for your friend, you are waiting. You are waiting for the moment when your friend will come. This is a good thing because it means you are still in the room where you are waiting.

Sometimes you might say you are on the way to a place. This means you are going there. You are moving in a line. People often use this word when they are going to a school, a store, or a place where food is made.

It is also used when things happen. If a thing is coming up, it is going to happen soon. This can be a very good thing. We can feel happy when a good thing happens. But if something is going to cause trouble, it can also be a problem.
