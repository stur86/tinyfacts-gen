To have a voice means to make sounds that are heard by other people. When you speak or single, you have a voice. Your voice is like a long line of sounds that go up and down. Every person has their own voice, and it is different from the voices of others.

Think of a small animal that makes a high sound. This sound is like a small voice. It is strong and clear. Your voice is like that too, even though you may not use it all the time. You can hear your own voice when you talk to a friend or when you are trying to tell someone something.

People often use voices in different ways. If you are sad, your voice might sound soft and quiet. If you are happy, your voice might sound loud and clear. A child might use a high voice to talk to their mom or dad. An older person might use a deeper voice.

Your voice can change as you get older. When you are little, your voice is very high and bright. As you grow up, your voice gets lower and your sound gets softer. Even when you are very old, your voice still sounds like you, even if it is not as strong as it was when you were younger.

Sometimes, people use voices that are not their own. If someone is very angry, they might speak in a way that is very different from how they usually talk. They might use words that are not their own, or they might use words that are not the right way to say them.

Everyone has a voice in their own way. It is how you make your own sounds when you are talking, or how you make sounds when you are acting out a story. Your voice is a part of you, and it helps us understand each other.
