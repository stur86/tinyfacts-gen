Dark is a word that tells us about how bright something is not.

When the sun is up, the sky is light. You can see the stars at night. But at night, the sky can feel very dark. It is dark because there is no light. The world is in the dark.

Think about a room. If the light from the window is going out, the room is dark. You can not see your hand in front of your face. The room feels very heavy.

Sometimes, when you are alone, you feel dark inside. You might feel sad or afraid. It is like the world is still, and nothing is happening.

You can also use the word to talk about things that are not light. A dark room is a place where nothing is happening. It is not a good place to be. You might feel tired or scared.

To make something dark, you need to cover it. You can cover a dark room with a big thing that blocks the light. Or, you can cover your eyes with a soft thing so you do not see anything.

In a story, the dark can be a place of fear. A bad thing happens, and people are afraid to go out. The dark can be a place where no one is found.

The word dark is very important because it tells us about how we feel and how the world looks.
