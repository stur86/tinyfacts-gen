An although is a high sound in a group of notes. It is like the top note in a song. When you play a high note on a big wind thing that makes music, it is an although. It is the highest sound in the group.

You can also use the word for a person who is very tall. In a group of people, you might say, The although is the tallest one. This means he is the one with the most hair.

Sometimes, people use the word to talk about things that are very heavy. An although thing is something that is very hard to move. It is like a big rock that you can't carry around.
