To be ready is to have everything you need for something. You can be ready to go to a party, to a work place, or to a school. It is about being strong and able to do what you want.

Imagine you are going to a big party. You need to wear a nice dress and a nice pair of shoes. You also need to eat food. You want to drink a glass of water. If you are ready, you feel safe. You know what to do and you are not afraid.

You can also be ready for a fight. If you know what the other person is going to do, you can be ready. You can move quickly and hit your hands. You are ready to stop the fight.

To be ready is also about being ready for a change. When you move to a new home, you need to be ready to leave your old house. You need to pack your clothes and your books. You need to say good to your friends and family.

Sometimes, you just need to be ready for a moment. If you see a bad thing happening, you can be ready to run away. You are ready to keep yourself safe.
