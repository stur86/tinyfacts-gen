To make something happen is to give it power. You can use this power to move things, to make sounds, or to change the way people feel. This power is inside every thing that is living.

A thing can have power in many ways. A tree can make the ground soft so a small child can walk on it. A dog can make its owner laugh by telling funny words. A person can use power to build a big house or to drive a car very fast.

Power can also be in the mind. A person who is very strong can lift a heavy box, but a person who is very small can be very strong. This is because their mind is strong and can make their body do what they want.

Sometimes power is about change. You can use power to change the world. You can stand up to a bad person who is hurting people. You can say things that make the bad person feel bad. This is a kind of power that helps to keep the world safe.

Power can also be about how you act. You can use power to help others. You can be a doctor who helps a child who is sick. You can be a teacher who tells a story that makes children happy. When you use power in this way, you are giving life to others.

Finally, power can be about how you feel. You can use power to feel happy. You can watch a funny movie. You can read a book that makes you feel excited. When you use power to feel happy, you are giving life to your own mind.

So power is something that can make things happen, change the world, help others, or give you life in your own mind.
