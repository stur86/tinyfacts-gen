An animal is a living thing that moves around. It has a body and a head. It can breathe, eat, and drink water. An animal needs to eat to stay strong. It can also make sounds so that other animals know it is there.

Animals have different bodies. Some are big and hard like a rock. Some are small and soft like a ball. Some have many legs, so they can run very fast. Some have many arms, and they can use them to catch food.

Many animals live in the ground. They eat the green stuff growing under the ground. Some animals live in water. They move through the water and breathe air from the sky. They use their bodies to stay a long way from the ground.

Animals can also be very different. Some are very small, like a tiny thing you can hold in your hand. Other animals are huge, like a big car. Some animals can fly very high into the sky. Some animals are very good at hiding, so they can stay safe.

All animals need air to breathe. They need water to drink. They need food to eat. They need a place to live and a way to move around. Without these things, they would not be able to stay in one place for a long time.
