To listen is to pay attention to what someone is saying. You do not look at their face. You do not look at the papers they are writing. You just sit and hear what they say.

Imagine you are in a room with your friends. One of them is telling a story. You want to know how it ends. You might lean forward a bit. You might ask a question. You are listening because you want to know what happens next.

You can listen when someone talks to you. You can listen when a teacher talks to you so you understand what they are saying. You can listen when a person is talking about a problem. You can listen when a person is talking about their day. You can listen even when you are not talking.

But you can also listen in your mind. You can listen to music. You can listen to a story in your head. You can listen to your own voice. You can listen to your own heart. You can listen to your own mind.

Sometimes you listen to the sounds around you. You listen to a dog. You listen to a running water. You listen to a tree. You listen to the wind. You listen to the world.

Listening is an important thing. When you listen, you are not thinking about what else is happening. You are only listening. You are not doing anything else. You are just being. This makes you feel safe. It makes you feel like you are a part of the world.
