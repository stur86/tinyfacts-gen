To get a number is to find what number is the answer to a question. When you say something is a number, you are using this word.

Think of time. You wake up in the morning, and then you go to work. You go to work and come home. The time you get home is a number. Every moment you have, every step you take, every breath you breathe is a number.

You can also get a number from something that happens. If you run and you finish a race, you are a good runner. If you have a lot of money, you have a good life. If you love a person, you love them.

Sometimes you get a number from a game. If you hit a ball and run to first place, you are the top player. If you throw a ball and it goes far, you are a great thrower.

In school, you use numbers to show how many times you read a book. You read a book and then you write down how many words you read. You might get iced down for reading a book that is too long. You might get a number so you know you read the book. This number is your reading number.

In games, a team might have many numbers. One number is for the person who runs. Another number is for the person who throws the ball. Another number is for the person who catches the ball. The number is like a set of words that tells us who is doing what.

When you get a number, you are telling the world what happened. You are giving them a number so they can know. This number is a way to show you are the one who did it.
