To go out and get more stuff is called going out. You go out to a store and buy a book. You go out to a park and play. You go out to a place to eat. This is when you leave your home and go to a different place.

Sometimes, when you sit on the floor for a long time, you start to feel very tight. You might want to move your legs or your arms. This is because your body is getting ready to move. You need to go out and do something so you can go back inside and feel safe again.

You can also go out when you are not feeling well. If you have a cold or a pain in your head, you might need to go out and get some rest. Or, if you are afraid of a thing that is in your house, you might go out and run away. You are going out to get help or to stay safe.
