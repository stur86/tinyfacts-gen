The word exactly means very well. If you say something is exactly, it means you are sure that what you are saying is the right thing to say. You are not just saying it, you are also feeling that it is true.

Imagine you are trying to tell a friend that you have found a small rock on the ground. You might say, I found a rock. But if you say you found a rock, you are not really saying it. You are just saying you think it is true. But if you say it is exactly, you are telling the friend that you are sure you found a rock.

This is important because it helps us understand how people feel. If you are exactly sure of something, you have a good feeling inside. It is like your heart is saying, I am right.
