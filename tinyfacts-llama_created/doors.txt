A door is a thing you use to open or close a way to a room or a building. It is usually made of wood, but it can also be made of many other hard stuff or of a soft, round thing.

When you want to go into a room, you need a door. You push on the door with your hand or with your feet. This makes the door turn. The door opens and you can walk inside.

Sometimes, a door is big. It is like a tall wall with a round end. You can walk in and out of it many times. Other times, a door is small. It is like a small box with a round top. You can open it and look inside.

You can also have doors on the front of a car. These doors let people in and out. You can close them when you are not going and keep your car safe.

Some doors have numbers on them. These numbers tell people who can go in. If you know the number, you can open the door. But if you do not know the number, you should not try to open it.

Doors can also have a name. You might call a door a front door or a back door. Each name tells you which way the door opens.
