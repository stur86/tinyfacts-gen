To build something means to make it strong so it can hold up. You do this with wood or with your own body. When you put strong parts together, you make something that can hold heavy things or that can hold a child.

To be a good friend means to help others when they are sad or afraid. You can be a good friend when you listen to what they say, when you give them money, or when you stand near them when they are scared.

To walk is to move your feet from one place to another while you are standing on the ground. You walk when you go to a new place, when you go to school, or when you go to a store to buy food.

To write something means to use your hands and your mind to form words that tell stories. You write when you want to tell a story, when you write a letter to a friend, or when you write a story for your family.

To build something with your hands means to use your body to make it. You build with wood when you cut the wood into small pieces, when you use a power thing to make the wood come together, or when you use your own hands to hold the pieces together.

To help someone means to give them something they need or to make the hard time easier. If you see a friend who is crying, you can help them by giving them a drink, by saying a word of calm, or by standing near them until they feel better.

To be strong means to feel no fear or to be able to hold your breath for a very long time. If you are strong, you can run fast, you can jump over a wall, or you can lift heavy things.
