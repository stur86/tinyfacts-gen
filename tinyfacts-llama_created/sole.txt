A foot is the part of your body that touches the ground. It is a long thing with a hard bottom. You walk on it, run on it, and jump on it. Your feet are very important because they help you stay standing and move around.

Each foot has five parts. The hard bottom is at the bottom. In front of this hard bottom, you have the foot part. This part is soft and can move. The foot part is held together by strong white lines that go under your skin. These lines keep your foot parts close to each other.

On the top of your foot, you have the top part. This part is round and soft. It is for your feet to rest on when you are standing. You can feel the ground under you when you walk. Your top part also helps you to stand up straight.

Each foot has one foot part and one top part. Together, they form one foot. You have two feet, and each foot is different. Some people have feet that are very big and round, while others have feet that are very small and point up. Your feet are very good at helping you move around the world.
