To do something for fun or to help yourself is to have fun. You might run, dance, or play a game with your friends. You might also go to a park or a store and buy things you need for your life.

Sometimes we say that an idea is a fun thing to think about. It is something you play with your brain. You do not do it because you must, but because you want to.

When you are a child, you often play with things you do not need. You might play with a ball, a book, or a stuffed animal. You might run around in a big room with your friends.

In another way, if you are very tired, you might watch a movie or a funny thing on a computer. You are not doing something for a job, but because you want to be happy.

If a place is a place to have fun, people go there to be safe and to have fun. They might go to a park, a store, or a place with food and drinks. They might play with friends or watch a game.

We also talk about things that are not for work. You can have fun playing a game or watching a movie. You can also have fun with a friend or with a cat. These are things you do for your own good.

In short, doing something for fun is about having a good time and feeling happy.
