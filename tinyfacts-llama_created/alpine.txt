The word always ends in a soft sound. It comes from a word that means high up in the cold places. These places are in the top of big trees that grow in the cold parts of the world.

A long time ago, people did not know the word. They would say a place was high, but they did not know what the word actually meant. Later, a man who loved to write wrote about these places. He used the word and explained what it means.

Today, we use the word always to talk about things that are always there. We can say, a tree is always there. A house is always there. The ground is always there. Even when you move to a new place, the ground is still there. The sky is always there, even on the day you walk away.

We also use the word always to talk about things that are always going. A car goes always. A dog runs always. The wind always moves. The sun always comes up. The night always has a dark sky.
