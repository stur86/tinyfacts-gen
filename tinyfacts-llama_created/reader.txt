To read something is to understand it. You sit down and look at a book, a story, a picture, or a computer word. You do not need to be a teacher or a person who lives in a place. You can just sit there and think.

Imagine you have a book about a boy who goes on a big trip. You read the words. You see how the boy feels and what he thinks. You might feel happy or sad. You understand what the story is about. You are the one who makes the story happen.

You can read books, papers, or even short stories. Some people read to relax, to learn, or to be surprised. Others read because they want to know more about a person, a place, or a story.

When you read, you use your brain. Your eyes look at the words on the paper. Your brain says, what does this mean? Then, you think about it for a while. You might ask yourself, what is the story about? You might try to remember what happened.

You can also read to someone else. You sit down next to them and say the words. You listen to how they understand the story. This is a good way to share something with the people you care about.

So, reading is when you use your brain to understand what is happening in a story. It is about taking a thing that is not real and making it real in your mind.
