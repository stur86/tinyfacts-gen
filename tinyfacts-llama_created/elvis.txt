Else is a word that means you did something wrong. It is used by people who speak the word.

When you do something, you can either do it right or you can do it wrong. If you do something that is right, you are said to have done it. If you do something that is wrong, you are said to have done it wrong.

Imagine you are playing a game with a friend. You run to the table, but you trip and fall. You did not do it right. You did it wrong. You can say, that was an else situation.
