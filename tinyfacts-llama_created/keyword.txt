A word is a thing you say. It is the thing you use to tell the story of your day or your life. It is not a number or a picture. A word is only a short group of letters that tell a story.

When you say a word, you are telling the world about your world. You are telling them what happens. You are telling them what you think. You are telling them who you are and what you do.

A word can have many different jobs. Some words tell us things. Some words show us how to feel. Some words help us talk to each other.

In the world of computers, words are important too. When you type a word, your computer reads it and does what you ask. If you type a word that means you are sad, your computer will send a word to someone else that says how you are feeling. This helps people understand each other.

Sometimes, a word can be very strong. If you use the word that means you are angry, you might feel angry. If you use the word that means you are afraid, you might feel afraid. This is because the word is a sign that tells your brain how to feel.

We use many words every day. We use them to talk to our friends. We use them to talk to our family. We use them to tell a story. We use them to show how we feel. Without words, we would not be able to tell the story of our lives.
