To spend money is one thing. To save money is another. When you are the one who spends money, you are the one who buys things. You use money to buy food, clothes, houses, cars, and other things you want. You buy things that help you live.

When you are the one who saves money, you are the one who keeps it. You put the money you get from your job into a box and keep it safe. You do not spend it right away. You use the money later when you need it, like when you pay for a house or a car.

Some people work for a big group of people. They do the same work many times. They make money from what they do. If they spend too much money, their money goes away. If they save, they keep more money.

The word can also mean how money is used. If you spend money on a game, you are playing. If you save money, you are keeping. It is a way to use money.

Sometimes, when people talk about money, they use this word. They say, the person spent money on food. They say, the person saved money for a big eventually. They use it to help them. This word is important because it tells us what happens with money.
