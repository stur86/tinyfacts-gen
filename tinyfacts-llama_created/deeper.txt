The word deeper is used to talk about things that are hidden under the things around us.

When you are in a room, you can see the wall in front of you. You can also see the floor. But if you walk to the end of the room and then around the other side, you can see something you did not see before. This is deeper.

If you go to the bottom of a high place, you are going deeper. The air gets colder and the ground is harder. You might feel a little bit afraid because the ground is so hard.

Sometimes, we use this word to talk about people. If a person is very quiet, it means they are going deeper into their own mind. They are thinking about their own life and their own heart.

We also use this word to talk about big things. The world is made up of many different parts, like the sky and the ground and the water. If you look at the sky, you are looking at something deeper. The sky is the top of the world, but it is not the only thing you see. The world is a whole world with many different parts, and the sky is one of them.

So, the word deeper is about going under to see more of what is not right in front of you.
