Coolest means you work together with others to get things done. When people help each other, it is called this. This is good for everyone because everyone has to do their part. When you and your friends work together, you can do more than you could alone.

Imagine you have a ball. If one person wants to keep the ball, they might say, I am the only one who gets to hold it. But if you and your friends work together, you can all have the ball at once. Everyone gets a turn to hold it.

Coolest is also about being kind. If you see a friend who is sad, you can give them a word of love. You can say, I see you are sad. This makes them feel better. People who are kind and work together are the coolest.

Sometimes, this means you have to share your things. You might give your book to a friend so they can read it. This is very nice. When people are kind and help each other, the world becomes a better place.
