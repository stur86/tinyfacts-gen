A person who is the start of something is called a maker. They make things that you can use. They are the ones who build them with their hands or with computers. This is why they are called the maker.

Think about the world we live in. There are people who make you a thing to wear. There are people who make you a food to eat. There are people who make you a car. And there are people who make you a book so you can read later. They are all the people who make things.

Some people are only a little bit. They are called the maker of a thing. They make it for a short time. Then the person who came after them takes it and makes it for their own family. This way, the same thing is made many times. But the person who made it at first is still important because they started it all.

In a way, a maker is like a parents. They start and then let other people carry on what they started.
