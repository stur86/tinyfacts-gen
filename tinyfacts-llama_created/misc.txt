To missed means to go wrong. It is like you were going to do something. You started moving, but then you did something else instead. You might have gone to the wrong door in a big building. Or you might have walked past the person you were supposed to meet.

When you missed, you might have done something you did not want to do. You might have made a bad word or done something that was against what you think is right. You might have also made a lot of people sad because they did not know you were going to do that.

To missed can also mean to forget a place or a word. If you were looking for a small thing, but you forgot where you put it, you have missed. It is like your brain is not working right.

Sometimes, to missed is used to talk about a fight. If two people are fighting, and one of them says something they did not mean, they might have missed. This is because they did not mean to hurt the other person. They might have just wanted to stop the fight.

In some schools, when a student does not follow the right way, they might be said to have missed. This means they did not do what they were supposed to do. They might have walked into a room without being asked, or they might have talked while the teacher was trying to talk.

To missed is also used to talk about a thing that is not what it seems. If someone says something is true, but it is actually not, they have missed. They have not told the truth.
