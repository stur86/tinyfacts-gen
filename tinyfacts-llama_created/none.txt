None is a word that means you have nothing at all. When you are alone, you have none. You do not have any people, no books, no things. You are the only one in the room.

You can also use this word to say that something is not there. If you look around and there is no light, you are in the dark. It is like having no thing at all.

Sometimes, you might have a friend who does not want to talk to you. You do not have them in your life. You are alone again. This can be a hard thing to feel.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are not real. If you see a big blue thing in the sky, you might say, I saw a blue thing. But if there is no blue thing, you say, there is none.
