A house wife is a person who works at home. She does many things for her family. She helps with the children. She usually does the cleaning. She does the food. She makes sure everyone is safe.

She often stays in the same home all day. She might watch the children. She might help with the work. She might make clothes or do some other work. This is because she wants to keep her family happy.

Some house wives also do things for their own families. They might help with the work if their father is busy. They might help with the money. They might buy clothes or food.

A house wife is like a mother and a wife. She loves her family very much. She wants them to be happy. She does many hard things to keep them safe and happy.

Not all house wives work. Some people stay home with their children. They watch them. They play with them. They help with their work. They love their children very much.

Sometimes, people think a house wife is a woman who stays home. But she can also be a man who works at home. He does the same work as a woman. He just does it in a different way.
