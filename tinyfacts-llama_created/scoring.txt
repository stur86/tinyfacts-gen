To play a game means you have many chances to do things. One way to play is to hit a ball with a long piece of wood. You try to hit the ball before your friend does. If you hit the ball before your friend, you get a point. You keep getting points until you have as many points as your friend.

Another way is to hit a ball with a soft round thing. You do this to try to move the ball from one place to another. If you hit the ball far away and your team moves it, you get a point. You are also doing this if you hit the ball and it goes into a big round thing that is set up on the ground. This is called a shot.

When you play a game, you try to get more points than your friend. If you get more points, you are the one who played the game. This is why you say someone has done well.
