To need something means you have a problem and you want it to fix the thing. You are not just looking to see if it is there. You are really looking to fix it so you can be safe or happy again.

You might need food to eat. If you do not eat, you will feel very sick. You need to go to the kitchen and get something to eat so you can stay strong.

You also need money to do your job. If you do not have money, you will not be able to buy the things you need to work. You need to find money so you can work and pay for the things you need.

Sometimes, you need to be near someone. If you are far away from your family, you need to find your way home. You need to follow the roads and streets until you see the lights of your house.

When you need something, you usually say, I need this. The person who heard you might say, Okay, I will bring it to you. Or, I will find it for you. You are not just asking; you are really looking for help.

To need something is a way to show you really want something. It is important to say you need it so the people around you know you are not just looking.
