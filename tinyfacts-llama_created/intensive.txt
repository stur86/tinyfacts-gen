An important word you might use when talking about how strong or deep a thing is.

Think of a strong dog. If you talk to it, it might sit or stay. If you are very nice, the dog might listen. The dog is not just sitting there. It is really listening. It is paying close attention.

This word works with many things. You can use it for a feeling. If something feels very bad, you might say it is an important word. If something feels very good, you say it is an important word. You can also use it for a job. If you have to work very hard, you might say you are doing an important job.

When something is an important word, it means it is very important. It is not just a word. It is a way to show how you feel or how you think. This word can help you understand each other better.
