To be acted upon means something happens and you feel it. You might be in a room and hear a loud noise. You might see a light go on. Your heart might jump. This can happen with people, animals, and things.

When you act, you make a change. You use your brain to decide. You might decide to run away. You might decide to talk to a friend. Your body also acts. If you are afraid, your heart beats fast. Your legs feel tight. You want to hide.

When something is acted upon, it can change. It can make you feel a different way. If you act on a word, you might change your mind. If you act on a plan, you might do it. This is how we change the world and our lives.

You can also act on a story. If you read a story about a strong man, you might feel strong. You might want to be like him. You can act on the story by trying to act like that person.

The word can also mean to feel a lot of things at once. If you are in a bright room, you might feel happy and sad. You might feel calm and angry. This is when many different things happen inside you at once.
