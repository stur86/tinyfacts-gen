The word is used to talk about a group of things or a group of people. It is about the whole rather than one person or one thing.

Think about how many people are in a school. If you countering one person, you only know about one person. But if you say the school as a group, you know about the whole school. You know about the morning, the lunch, the afternoon, and the night. You know about how many students are there and how old they are.

A person can have an entire life. They grow up, they learn new things, they make friends, and they grow into a person. But you can also talk about the life of a city. The city starts with a small group of people. Over many years, they build a place with houses, roads, and schools. They make a place where people can live and work together.

When you talk about the whole of something, you are talking about the group of things. You are not just talking about one person. You are talking about the whole set of things that make it. This word is important in our lives because it helps us understand how big a group is and how it works together.
