To find something or to make a way for people can mean two different things.

When you find something, you look for it. You might be looking for a book, a chair, or a dog. If you find it, you feel happy and you can move on to do other things.

When you make a way, you show others how to get from one place to another. You might tell them how to get to a school on the other side of town. You might show them a road and tell them which way to turn. This helps people move around easily and makes life better.

Think about a long line of people. When you are in front of them, you have to wait. But when you find a spot behind them, you can walk in front. You are still following the line, but you are also moving ahead. This is how you can get to where you need to go without having to wait for everyone.

Sometimes, a way can be very hard to see. You might be looking at a big room and you are not sure where to start. But then you find a door. The door is the beginning of the way out. You use your eyes and your brain to figure out where you are and where you want to go. This is what it means to find something.

In some places, finding something is a big job. You might look for a lost child or a person who is hurt. You search the streets, ask people if they have seen them, and look for any sign of them. If you find them, you are very happy and you help them feel safe again.
