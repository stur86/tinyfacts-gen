To surround something means to put it all around you. You can surround a small thing, like a chair, with many other things. Or you can surround a person. If you surround a person, you make them feel safe. You might surround them with friends and family. You might also surround them with books or movies.

Imagine you are standing on a big road. The road goes all around you. You are not on one side of the road anymore. You are on all sides. You are surrounded by the road. You are surrounded by the world outside your house.

Sometimes, to surround something is also to keep it quiet. If you surround a fight, the people in the fight are not saying anything. They are just standing there, waiting. They are keeping their voices down to keep the fight quiet.

A person can also surround you. If a person is always there for you, they surround you. They are like a big group of friends who care about you.
