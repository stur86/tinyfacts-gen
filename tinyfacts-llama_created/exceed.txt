To go over something means you are going far past what you started with. It is when you do more than you were supposed to do.

Imagine you have a book to read. You read a few words. Then you read a few more words. Now you have read many more words than you started with. You have gone over the book. This is an easy way to say you went too far.

We can also use this word to talk about time. If you finish a job before a time you said you would finish it, you have gone over that time. This is because you did more work than you were allowed to do.

Sometimes we say something is too much. If a movie is too long, we say it goes over. This is because it is more than we want to watch.

Also, when you take a drink that is too strong, you have gone over. This means the drink is so strong it makes your head feel heavy and your body feels funny.
