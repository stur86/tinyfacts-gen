To make a listening story is to tell what happens. You use words to show what is happening. You might use a person, a place, a time, and a story about how people act.

One way to make this is to tell a story about a time when people were afraid. They were afraid of things that were not in the world. They were afraid of dark places and things that moved too fast. They were afraid of loud noises. They did not know how to be safe.

One person was a big boy. He had a loud voice and he could do a lot of work. He was the one who told them what to do. He made them follow his plan. He said they had to stay quiet and they had to move fast. He said they could not go to the dark place.

The people did not listen. They ran away. They did not follow the plan. They went to a different place where they were safe. They were happy to be safe.

Another person was small. He was very afraid. He was afraid of the loud noises. He was afraid of the dark places. He was afraid of the big boy. He did not want to go near the big boy.

The small person learned a hard way. He realized that he could not fight the big boy. He could not run. He could only hide. He stayed in his small home and watched from a safe place. He saw how the big boy and the other people acted. He learned to be safe.
