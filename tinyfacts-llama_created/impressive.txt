When you say something is very good, you usually say it is important or amazing. This word helps us know how something looks or how it works.

To say something is very good can mean it is very strong or very bright. A very strong light can cover the room. A very bright light can hurt your eyes. This word is for things that are very big or very fast.

You can also say something is very good because it is nice to look at. A picture of a beautiful place can make you feel happy. A dog that runs very fast can be very good for the people who love it.

Sometimes, when you say something is very good, you mean it is also very happy. If you give a child a big, fun party, you are very happy. The room is full of people laughing and having fun. The food is very good, the music is nice, and the people are very happy. You feel very happy too.

We use this word a lot in our lives. We say it when we see a great game. We say it when we watch a movie with a very good story. We say it when we see a beautiful work of paint. We say it when we hear a song that makes us want to dance. This word is very important because it helps us understand how we feel about something.
