To have a favorite thing means you like it more than other things. Think about your favorite food. You might love eating it for breakfast, lunch, or dinner. You might not want to eat anything else.

If you like a song, you might listen to it every day. You might feel happy or sad when you hear it. If you like a movie, you might watch it many times. You might feel like you're in the story.

You also like to play with a ball. You might kick it or throw it. If you are very good at it, you might be the best player. You might feel strong when you are doing it.

A favorite person is someone you like a lot. You might like their smile or their words. You might want to talk to them or play with them. You like being near them.

Sometimes, we have a favorite thing to do. We might like to read books or draw pictures. We like to be alone with our favorite thing. This makes us feel safe and happy.
