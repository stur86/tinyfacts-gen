A long time ago, people made pictures of how things work. These pictures were not for the eyes. Instead, they were for a computer to understand. We use these computers to make many kinds of pictures. We use them for work and for fun.

Inside these pictures, we put numbers and lines. Numbers tell us how big a thing is. Lines show how much of the thing is used. When we put these numbers and lines together, we get a picture of how things change over time.

This kind of picture is called a line. It goes straight from one point to another. We can draw a line by putting numbers at each point and then drawing a line through those numbers. Each line is like a story about how one thing grows.

We can also use lines to show how many things are used at one time. Imagine you have a box with many small things in it. If you open the box, you see how many small things are there. You can draw a line that goes from the top to the bottom of the box. This line tells you how many small things are there. It is like a story about the number of things.

We can use these pictures to show how things change over time. Think about a line that goes from a small number to a big number. This line tells us that the number is getting bigger. We can use these pictures to learn about the world and to understand how things work.

We also use these pictures to show how much money is left over after we spend it. Imagine you have a bag of money. You spend some money and then you have some left. You can draw a line that goes from the starting point to the ending point. This line tells you how much money you have left.
