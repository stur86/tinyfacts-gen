A car is an old thing that people drive. It goes very fast down the road. It has many parts that help it move.

To make a car is a work that many people do. They take old parts and put them in a new way. They build the car so it can carry people and things. They make sure the car is safe for the people inside.

Some cars are very strong and fast. They can go over many hundreds of feet in one second! They have many colors and many different kinds. Some cars are for running fast, and some are for going slow. Some cars are for cars that can go very far in the water.

You can buy a car if you want to drive one. Some people buy cars when they are very old and need to be fixed. Other people buy cars when they are new and want to keep them for many years. Some cars are only for one person.

To make a car also means to make things that people want. If you want a car, you tell a store what kind you want. The store tells the people who make the car what to do. They make the car and send it to the store. Then the store gives the car to you to buy.

Cars are very important for people. They help people go to work, go to school, and to visit friends. They also help people go to the store to buy food, clothes, and other things. Without cars, people would have to walk or use other ways to get from one place to another.
