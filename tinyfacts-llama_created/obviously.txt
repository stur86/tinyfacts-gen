Obviously is an easy word to use. You see things with your eyes. If you look at a wall and you see a picture of a tree, it is obviously there. You did not need to say anything. You just know.

It also means things that are very clear. If you say that the sky is blue, everyone knows it. It is not a hard thing to say. You just show the world what you mean.

Sometimes we use this word when we are talking about how things are really. If you say a thing is small, you are not just saying it is small. You are saying it is really small. It is not just a tiny thing. It is a tiny tiny thing. It is really small.

When people say something is obviously true, they mean the truth is right there in front of them. It is not something you need to think about. You can just see it.
