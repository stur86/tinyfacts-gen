To focus is to look at one thing at a time. It means you are not looking at many things at once. You are looking at one thing because it is important. You might be looking at a book, a picture, or a friend.

Imagine you are trying to find a small thing in a big room. You look at a wall. Then you look at a chair. Then you look at the floor. You are not looking at all the things at once. You are focusing on one thing and ignoring the rest.

Sometimes you focus on a problem. You might feel angry or sad. You are so focused on the problem that you forget about the world around you. You are only thinking about one thing.

You can also focus on a song. If you like a song, you might listen to it over and over. You are not listening to many different songs at once. You are only listening to one song.

When you focus, you are safe. You are not thinking about other things. You are only thinking about one thing. This can help you feel calm. It can help you do work.

But sometimes, focusing on one thing can make you feel alone. If you are trying to do a job, you might focus on a single thing. You do not talk to your friends. You do not ask for help. You just focus on the job. This can make you feel sad.
