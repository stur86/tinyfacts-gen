Your body is your home. It is where you live and where you grow. Your body needs food, air, and water to stay strong. It also needs rest and time to work. This is what we call your body.

Your body is made of tiny things called cells. These cells go around your body many times a day. They move and change. Some cells are very good at fixing things. Some cells are very good at making new things. When you eat food, the body uses the food to make more cells. When you get tired, your body needs more rest.

Your body also needs to stay clean. It takes water and air to get riding of the bad things that come into your home. If you do not let the body breathe and drink enough water, it can get very sick. You might feel very hot or very cold, or you might feel very tired or very sick.

Your body also has a way to fight. If something bad comes into your home, your body sends in the right things to fight it. Your brain sends out a call. Your heart beats faster. Your body gets ready to fight. This is why you feel a bit of a rush when you are scared.

Sometimes, your body can get very tired. This can happen if you are very hot or very cold, or if you are very happy or very sad. It is like your body is saying, I need to rest now.

Your body is like a big team. Every part works together to keep you going. If one part is not doing its job, the whole team might get sick.

You can keep your body strong by eating good food, staying clean, getting enough rest, and not smoking. You can also keep your body happy by being kind to yourself, by doing things you like, and by taking care of your home.
