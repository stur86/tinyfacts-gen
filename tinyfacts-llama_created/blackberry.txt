A black thing from the ground. You eat it in the warm days. It is sweet, but not white like a sweet thing. It is red, but it is not red. It is brown. It has many small bits on it. You can eat them by hand, but they fall off when you pick it.

You also make a drink from this thing. You take the wet parts, the small bits, and the brown parts. You add water. You make a drink with this drink. Some people say it is good to drink.
