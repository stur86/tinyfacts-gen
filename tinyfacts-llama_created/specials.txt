To offer something as a free thing that people are excited about is to give it away. This is when a store or a place says that you will get a free drink or a small thing if you do something.

This usually happens for one day. The store might have a big sign on the door. People see this and say, I want a free drink! They go inside and wait. When it is their turn, they get the drink. This is a very fun day.

Sometimes, you can get a free thing even when you buy something else. If you buy a book, you might get a free book with it. This is like a free drink, but you already paid for it.

People also use this word for things that are very good. If a movie is a very good one, you can see it for free. You do not have to pay a thing. You can watch it and enjoy it.

This word is often used with food. If you buy food, you might get a free drink. Or, you might get a free thing when you buy a book. It is a way to say, let us give you something nice.
