To be important is to have a big job. Most important things are the ones that help people live. A person who helps a child grow up is very important. A doctor is also important because they help keep you and your family safe.

Things that are not important are the ones that do not help people. A game is an important thing to do. It lets you play with your friends and have fun. A book can be very important. It tells you stories about far away places and makes you want to read more.

When you say something is important, you mean it is important to you. You might be very sad if a friend dies. Or you might feel happy if you see a beautiful picture. Being important is about how you make people feel.

You can also be important in your own way. If you help a friend who is sad, you are being important. If you tell a story that makes people laugh, you are important too. Being important does not just happen to people who are very big or very old. Anyone can be important if they make a big change in their life or help others.
