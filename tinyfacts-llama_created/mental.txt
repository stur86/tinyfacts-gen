You can also think about things. You can think about the day before, the day after, or the day after tomorrow. You can think about what you want to do, what you are afraid of, or what you want to be. Your mind is always busy with your brain.

Sometimes, your mind can be very loud. You might hear a lot of noise, like a lot of people talking or a loud car driving by. This is like a big group in your head. It can be very hard to listen and stay calm.

Your mind is also about your own body. You can feel when you are tired, when you are cold, or when you are happy. It is the part that lets you know if you are okay.

Even when you are sleeping, your mind is still working. It is just not as loud as when you are waking up. Your brain is still doing work, even though you are not thinking about anything in the same way.
