To read is to look at words and letters. You put these pieces into a computer, a book, or a box that holds them. When you look at the words, you are understanding what they say.

You can read books, papers, or signs on a sign. You look at one line after another. Each line has many words. You read how the words are put together. If you read a story, you try to understand what happened. If you read a letter, you try to understand what the person is saying.

To read is to use your brain. It is a way of understanding and remembering words and ideas. You use your eyes to see the words. Your brain then figures out what the words mean.

Sometimes you read to yourself. You might read a story or a book before you go to sleep. Other times, you read because a person told you they wanted to hear what they said.
