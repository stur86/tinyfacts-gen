An easy word to know.

To have an idea about something is to feel like you know it. An idea can be a thing you have seen or a thought you had. It can be about what happens next or about how things should be done. An idea is a way to see the world.

A person who gives an idea is called an admit. They talk about a thing that is happening, a problem, or a plan. They share what they think is important. An admit may ask for your help or your ideas. They use words like this to help people make good plans.

You can have an idea when you are thinking about something. You might feel a bit of a problem when you do not know how to do something. You might think about how you could fix the thing or what you should do next. This is an idea.

Some people are admits in their work places. They help other people with their jobs. They listen to what the other people want and then they tell them what the manager thinks. This way, everyone knows what they are doing. The admits are like a team.

Another kind of idea is a plan. A plan is like a road that you follow. You tell everyone what you want to do and how you will do it. This helps everyone know what to do. It can help you get the job done faster or better.

A person who is an admit might also be an admit for a game. They help the game run. They tell the people who are playing what to do next. They do this so everyone has a good time and the game stays going well.

We all need ideas to live. Ideas help us know what to do, how to act, and what to say. They help us build our world and make it a good place for everyone.
