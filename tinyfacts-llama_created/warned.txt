To warn something means to tell it before it happens. You can warn a person when they are about to do something bad. You are the one who says they should not do it.

You can also warn a place. If you know that a road is going to become very hard to cross, you tell people to stay away. This helps everyone stay safe.

When you warn something, you are telling it to change. If you warn a person, they will change. They will not do the thing that made them afraid.

Sometimes, you warn a thing that is coming. If you see a big dog coming, you tell your friends to be quiet. This helps everyone stay safe.

You can also warn a place where food is coming. If you know that food is going to be very hot, you tell people to wait. This helps them eat before they get very angry.

To warn someone is to help them. It is like being the one who says, I am here, I am there, I am going to be there.
