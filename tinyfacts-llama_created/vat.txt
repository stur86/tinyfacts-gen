A large clear box can hold many things. You might fill it with water to clean a house. The water goes down into the bottom part of the box.

Inside the box, there is a round thing that breaks the hard stuff into many small pieces. These small pieces are what make the water clear again. The round thing is called a thing that breaks the hard stuff.

The box is also used to keep food safe. You can put food inside the box and it stays warm for a long time. When you open the box, the food gets cold again. This keeps the food safe for people to eat.

You can also use a big box to make food. The round thing that breaks the hard stuff goes into the box with the food. The hot food goes through the round thing. The round thing breaks the hard stuff into small pieces. Then the hot food goes into the clear box and becomes a drink that people can drink.

There is also a big box that holds money. People put money into the box. When you want to get money out, you take it out of the box. The box helps keep the money safe until you need it.
