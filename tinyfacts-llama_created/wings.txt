Things that fly have two parts. One part is very wide at the top and one part is very wide at the bottom. The wide parts are like the wide arms of a person. When you push the wide parts up, the person can go very fast. The wide parts also help to keep the person up in the sky.

The other part is long and has a very hard cover. When the person pushes the wide parts up, the hard cover makes the person go down. This makes the person go fast as well. The hard cover is like a big bag that can hold air. When the air goes out, the bag gets heavy. The person falls down, but the hard cover keeps them safe.

The wide parts and the hard cover work together. If the wide parts push up and the hard cover pushes down, the person can go fast. If the wide parts push up and the hard cover pulls down, the person can also go fast. This is why people can fly.
