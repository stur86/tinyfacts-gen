To Interest Someone

To want something. When you want something, you are interested. You think about it. You think about how you can get it. If you want a book, you look for it. If you want to go to a city, you think about where it is.

Sometimes you want something because you need it. You might want to eat food. Food is important for your body. You can not live without it. If you do not eat, you will feel very bad.

Other times you want something because you like it. You like the color of a car. You like the sound of a dog. You might feel happy when you see those things. You are interested because you enjoy them.

You can also want something because it is good for you. If you are tired, you might want to go to bed. This is a way to keep yourself safe. If you want to be strong, you might want to run. This helps you stay well.

When you want something, you act. You do not just sit thinking about it. You do something else to get it. This is important because it helps you feel safe and happy.

To Interest Someone is like a word that means you care about them. If you want to know someone, you are interested. It is a way to show you care about them.
