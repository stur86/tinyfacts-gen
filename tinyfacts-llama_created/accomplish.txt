To complete something means to finish it. Think about when you build a small house with your friends. You start with the ground and move up. Each person does their part. By the time you reach the top, the house is ready.

When you say you have to do something, you are saying you will do it. If you say you want to go to a movie, you are doing it. You are going to the room where the movie is.

Sometimes, you do not finish. Maybe you start but then you stop. Maybe you have a lot of work and you do not have time. You still say you want to do it because it is important.

To have an idea is also to complete it. If you have a bright idea, you are doing something new. You are taking a small idea from a book or a movie and turning it into a real thing.

When you say you will do something, it is the same as saying you will complete it. You are making a plan to do it.
