To make something different is a very important thing. Imagine you are playing with a ball. You throw it far away and then you walk to a tree. The ball will come back to you. But if you are playing with a ball and you throw it very quickly, it will go very far and come back very little. This is because the ball is moving fast.

Now, let us talk about words. We use these words every day. A word is a thing you say. When you say a word, you are changing what you mean. You are telling a different story. You are not keeping the same story.

Think about this: if you say, you are happy, you are not making the same thing happen. You are changing the story. You are not keeping the same feeling. If you want to say you are very sad, you can say you are angry. This is the same thing. You are changing what you mean.

We use words to change our stories. We use words to tell different kinds of stories. We use words to make people laugh, to make them cry, or to make them feel afraid.

We also use words to change what we mean when we talk about things. If you say, a car is a thing that goes, you are changing what you mean. You are not keeping the same story. You are changing the way you think about the word.

Sometimes, we use words to change our own lives. If you feel you are a bad person, you can say you are a good person. You are changing what you mean about yourself. You are not keeping the same story. You are changing the way you think about what is happening in your life.

Making something different is an important thing. It helps us stay the same and it helps us change.
