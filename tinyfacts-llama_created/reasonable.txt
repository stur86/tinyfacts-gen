The word you are looking for is called a reason. When you say something is a reason, you are telling the truth. This word is used in many different ways.

One way to use it is when you say a friend is a good person. You might say,  This person is a good friend because they always help me when I am sad. This is a reason. You are giving a reason for why you like this person.

Another way to use it is when you are trying to do something. If you want to eat a food, you might say,  Eating that food is a good reason to do it. You are telling the world that eating that food is important for you.

You can also use this word when you have a problem. If you are afraid of a thing, you might feel afraid. To say,  This thing is a reason to be afraid, means you are telling the truth. You are saying why you feel this way.

Sometimes, a reason can be a thing that happened. If you got into a fight with your brother, you might feel angry. You are using this word to explain why you feel that way. It is a way of talking about why something happened.

In every case, the word helps us understand what is happening. It tells us why something is true.
