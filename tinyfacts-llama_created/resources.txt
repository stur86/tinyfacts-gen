To have a lot of something means you have many of it. Think of a big tree. A tree has many strong arms that reach up into the sky. It uses its arms to drink water from the ground. The tree also uses the green parts of its arms to make food. This food gives the tree power to grow and stay strong.

You can not have a lot of water if you do not drink it. You need to drink water every day so your body can work well. If you do not drink enough, your body will get sick. This is because your body is not getting the power it needs.

We need many things to live. Some of these things are things you find in the ground. Other things are things you buy with money. Some things are things you grow in your own land. All of these things are called a lot of something.

Sometimes, people take many of these things without asking. This is wrong. We must always try to share what we have. If we share, everyone can have more. We can help each other by sharing.
