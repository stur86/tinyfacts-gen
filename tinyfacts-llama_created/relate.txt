To Relationship means how things are between people or things. When you say something is a relationship, you mean how one person is to another. It is a way to show how they feel to each other.

You can have a friend. You are friends because you like to be around each other and share your time. You talk about your days. You also do fun things together. This is a good thing because you feel happy and you know you can countering each other.

You can also have a love. Your love is how you feel about someone. You want to spend your life with them. You feel happy every time you see them. You want to be the one they love. This is a strong feeling that can make you very happy.

When you have a job, you are a part of a group. People in the group work together to do work. You follow what the person before you does. You learn from them and then you do the same things. This is a way to show how people are working together.

Sometimes, you might not like someone at first. But if you keep talking and you learn more about them, you can become friends. You start to see them in a different way. You learn they are not as bad as you thought. This is a good thing because it helps you understand people better.

We all have our own relationships in life. Our family is a big group. We are all in this together. We share food, sleep, and stories. We also have our own friends. We talk about our days with them. This is a good thing because we know each other and we feel safe with each other.

In a school, you might have a teacher. You go to the same class every day. You learn from them. You follow what they say. They show you how to read and write. You also learn about other people. You learn how to be kind and how to be a good person.

So, to relationship is how things are between people or things. It is a way to show how one is to another, how one feels to another, and how two groups work together.
