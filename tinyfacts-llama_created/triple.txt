To do something three times means you do it many times. You can do this many times in one day, one week, one year, or many years.

Imagine you have a bag of things to buy. You want to buy a book, a chair, and a car. If you do this one time, you have to get each thing. But if you do this three times, you buy all three things at once.

You can also do this when you make something. You can make a thing three times. First, you make a small part. Then you make the whole thing. After you make the whole thing, you can use it to do other things. This is how you make many things at once.

Sometimes, we do this with numbers. If you have three numbers, you can add them three times. First, you add them together. Then you add them together three more times. This gives you the same number, three times.

We use this word when we talk about numbers. If you have three numbers, you can add them three times. This means you add the numbers together three times. This helps us understand how many times something happens.
