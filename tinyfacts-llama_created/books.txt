To read books is to put your mind into a story. A book is a thing that holds many words. You can hold one in your hand, or you can sit in a room and read them on a computer. Many people love books. Some like to read about far away lands. Some like to read about people who did things that are hard to do. Some like to read about animals that can fly.

Books can also have pictures. Some books have many pictures. These books can make you want to look at the pictures. You can look at a book while you are sitting in a car, or you can sit in a room and look at the pictures on the book while you are doing other things.

You can read a book many times. After you read one book, you might want to read another one. This is because the stories are still there, and you can learn more about the world and about the people in the stories.
