To approaches something is to think about it. When you buy a car, you do not just buy the car. You also think about how it will be for you and your family. You might think about how much it will make you pay each month or how much it will break if you drive it very much. You might also think about how it looks and how it feels to be inside.

An approaches a house too. If you want to buy a house, you might go to look at it with a person who helps you. They look at the house and say if it is a good place to live. They see how big the rooms are, how much light comes in, and how far it is from work. They might also look at how old the house is and if it has any problems. They do this to help you decide if the house is the right one for you.
