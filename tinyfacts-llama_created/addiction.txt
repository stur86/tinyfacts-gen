An adding is when you want to do something all the time. This is not good for you. It makes you do things that hurt your body or your mind. It can cause you to want more of the thing than you need.

Some things are adding to us because of how they make us feel. Food makes us feel happy. A drink makes us feel strong. A game makes us feel excited. But if we drink too much of these things or eat too much food, our bodies get very bad. We might feel sick, or our skin breaks down, or we have trouble breathing.

We can also be adding to us because of what we do. A man might work at a job for a long time. He feels like he is doing it because he has to. But really, he is only doing it to get money. He is not really happy. He gets a good pay, but he feels empty inside.

Sometimes, we are adding to us because of a person. A girl might love a boy very much. She talks about him all the time. She wants him to know her. This is a good thing because love is between two people. But if she is only talking about him, she might not really love him. She is only adding to him because she wants to be with him.

We need to stop being adding to things. We need to find other things to do with our time. We need to be happy. We need to feel good. This is the best way to live a happy life.
