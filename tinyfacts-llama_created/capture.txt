To catch something is to take it from someone or something. It can be done in many ways. If you are a dog, you can catch a ball. You use your teeth and your legs to hold it. Your eyes are very good at seeing what is happening around you.

You can also catch things that move. If you are running and a car comes, you can stop. You hold onto the car to stay safe. This is a good thing to do because it keeps you from getting hurt.

Sometimes you catch things that are not moving at all. This can happen when you walk into a room and catch a fly. You close your eyes and you hear a tiny sound. Your hand comes out and you catch the fly before it goes away.

If you catch something that is not yourself, it is a problem. You might feel angry or sad. You might want to throw the thing or hide it. This is why it is so important to be carefully when you try to catch things.
