To be very clear and not to leave out any small thing.

Think of a long line of numbers. If you do not say everything, there is still a small number in the middle. This small number is the same for everyone. If you change one number, everyone else also changes. The line is not broken, and the person who did not change the numbers is the one who is wrong.

If you write a word with the right letters but with one letter wrong, the word is not the same. You are not very clear. The person who looks at your word has to figure out what you were trying to say.

A person who does things very well is often very clear. They know what to do and when to do it. They do not leave out any small thing.

To be clear also means to be able to understand what another person is saying. If you do not understand, you are not clear. You must listen carefully and ask questions.

Sometimes, being very clear is hard. You have to be very good at what you do. But when you are, you are often the best.
