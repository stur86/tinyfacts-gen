To have a brain means you have a head with many hard parts inside. You use your brain to think about the world around you. You are the one who decides what you think, what you want to do, and how you act.

Imagine you are a tree. You start with a small round part on top. This part is like your brain. From this round part, many long parts grow out in different directions. These long parts are like your brain. They carry food from the ground into your body. They also carry water from your body to your leaves. They help you grow and make new leaves.

You also have many small parts on the sides of your head. These small parts are like your brain. They help you see and hear. They help you understand what people say and what things are happening around you.

Your brain is very strong. It can remember many things from years ago. It can also remember things from years ago that you never even knew about. Your brain is like a computer. It uses many tiny parts to work very fast.

You do not just have one brain. Some people have two brains. One brain is in your head, and the other brain is inside your chest. Sometimes, people have a brain in their chest. This is not a small part of a head. It is a whole body that is inside your chest.

You can not feel a brain. A brain is just a thing you have inside your body. You do not feel it. But a person with a brain feels things. They feel happy, sad, angry, and afraid. They feel the world around them. They feel the sun on their skin, the wind in their hair, and the sound of their own voice.

You can not kill a brain. A brain is a thing you have. You can not take a brain from another person. You can not make a brain. Only you can be the one with the brain.
