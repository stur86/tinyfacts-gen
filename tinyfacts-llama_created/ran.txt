To run is to walk quickly. When you run, you go very fast. You might run down a street or around a big field. You usually start at one end and end at the other.

Imagine you are a dog. You want to get to a ball. You start running. You go very fast. You can go many steps in one second. This is how fast a person runs.

You can also run when you are very happy. If you love a game, you might run around with your friends. You might laugh and shout with your friends. This is a good feeling because it makes you want to keep going.

Sometimes, running is hard. You might feel tired. Your legs feel like they are heavy. You want to stop. But you do not stop. You keep going because you want to reach the end. This is a good thing because it shows you are strong.

If a person is very fast, they are said to be running well. They might also be running for a long time, like many hours, to go to a far place.
