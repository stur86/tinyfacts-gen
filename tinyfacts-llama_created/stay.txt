To stay means you do not go away. You can stay in one place for a very long time. You might go to a different city or a different town. You might stay in a big house or a small house in the middle of a green field.

You can also stay in your own home. If you have a small house, you can stay there. You might stay in the same room for many days. You are not going to move. You are staying in the same place.

Sometimes you stay because you are happy. Maybe you love a place. You like the way the sun looks on the water. You like the sound of the wind. You might stay there for many years.

You can also stay when you are afraid. If you are afraid of a big animal, you stay close to your family. You want them to be safe. You stay in a room with a soft bed. You stay in a quiet place where no one is shouting.

To stay is to be in one place for a very long time. It is to be near people you like, or to have a home where you can feel safe.
