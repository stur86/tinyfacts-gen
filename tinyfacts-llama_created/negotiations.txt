When people talk, they often want to be friends. They might want to buy a thing or a place. To do this, they use words. One person says how they feel. The other person says how they feel too. They listen to each other. This is what people do during a time when they try to find a way.

Imagine you are playing a game with your friend. You both want the same thing. You talk to each other. You find a way to share. This is like a long talk. You do not fight. You find a way that both of you are happy.

A long time ago, people also did this. A man might say to a woman, he likes her, but he is afraid. She says she likes him too. They talk for a long time. They see if they can live with each other. This is how two people can become friends.

In a big world, people often talk to each other. They talk about what they want. They talk about how they feel. This is how they try to find a way that works for everyone. It is like a long talk.
