To be meaner is to know how big you are. When a person is tall, they are being meaner. It means they have a number that tells how much they stand. A grown man might be five feet tall. A tiny baby is only a few feet tall. The number tells us how much he is.

It also means to know how much of something you have. If you have a box of food, you are meaner. You know how many pieces of food you have. If you have a lot of food, you are meaner. If you have none, you are not meaner.

Sometimes, when we talk about numbers, we use this word. We say, she is meaner. This means she has a number. This is a way to talk about people who are big or small, or who have a lot of money or not a lot.
