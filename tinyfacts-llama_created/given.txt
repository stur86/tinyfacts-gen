To give something is to share it. You can give a friend a book you read. You might say, I gave you that book. This means you are letting the friend have it.

You can also give money. If you have a lot of money and you want to share it with a friend, you give it to them. This is how you help your friend.

Sometimes you give something to a person who does not have it. You might give a child a ball they want. This makes them happy because they have something new.

When you give, you must be ready to give. You must be ready to share your things with another person. Giving is a kind thing to do because it helps others.
