The word nice means something that is good. When you are nice, you are kind. You say please and thank you. You are not angry. You do not yell at people. You are very calm and happy.

When you are nice, people feel happy around you. They want to be near you. You make them feel safe. You help them when they need it. You are a friend.

If something is nice, it is also soft and not hard. It may be a piece of wood that is easy to break. It may be a book that is easy to read. You can hold it in your hand and it will not hurt you.

Sometimes, we use the word nice to talk about people. If a person is very nice, they are kind. They do not laugh at you if you are sad. They listen to what you say. They are a good friend.

You can also use the word nice to talk about things. If a place is nice, it is a good place to be. It has a big room with a tall window. There are many trees outside. The air is clean. You can stay there for a long time.

In the end, the word nice is a word that means something is good and makes people happy.
