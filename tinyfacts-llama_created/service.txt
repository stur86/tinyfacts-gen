To do a job for someone.

You might work for a school to help with the books. You are the one who finds the books and puts them back on the tables. You are also the one who helps the teacher if a student does not understand something.

You can also work for a business. If you work for a store, you are the one who helps people find what they need. You are the one who answers the phone and talks to people. You are the one who helps them get what they want.

Sometimes people say you do a job for someone. You give them what they need or help them in some way. You do not get paid in the way you pay your friends or your family. You just do what is asked of you.
