To do something in a very small way is to make a tiny part of something. You can do this by looking at it one tiny time. You might notice a small thing you did not see before. You might see that the room is not as big as you thought.

To be more about something is to know more about it. You can read about a book and learn about it. You can watch a movie and understand how things work. You can also talk to a friend and understand each other.

When you say a word is very small, you mean it is very small in number. If you have a hundred friends, you are not very small. But if you have one friend, you are very small. You are the tiny part of the group.

You can also use this word to talk about things that are hard. If you have to do a very hard job, it is a very small thing. You feel big because you are doing something you are not used to.
