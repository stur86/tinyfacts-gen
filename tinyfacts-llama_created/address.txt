To speak to someone is to talk to them. When you say this, you are speaking to their ears. You are not looking at them. You are not doing anything else.

Think about when you want to talk to a friend. You might stand near them and say, I need to talk to you. This is an order. You are not asking them to listen. You are telling them to pay attention.

You can also use this word when you write a letter. You write to someone you want to know. You tell them things that are important. You are giving them a piece of paper to read. This is the same as speaking to them.

If you want to talk to a person who is not there, you might write a letter. You can tell them about your day. You can tell them about what you did and how you feel.

It can also mean you are speaking to someone in a place. If you are in a store, you can speak to the person who runs it. You are telling them what you want. You are not asking them to listen. You are telling them to follow your words.

Sometimes you use this word to say you are going to another place. You might say, I am going to the movie tonight. You are not telling them where you are going. You are just telling them that you are going. You are not asking them to follow you. You are just telling them what is happening.

So, to speak to someone is to tell them what is happening or what you want. It is a way of sharing your ideas or your own life with the other person.
