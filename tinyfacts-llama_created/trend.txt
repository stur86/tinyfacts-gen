To move in a way that people follow is called a move. A move can be a step or a walk. It can also mean a change in what you do or what you think.

When people talk about what is happening, they often say, things are going in a way. This is like saying, a move is happening. A move can be good, bad, or something in between.

We use this word to talk about how things are changing. If you are always late, a move is happening. If you start to like a new food, a move is happening. If you change your job, a move is happening.

You can also talk about a move in your life. Maybe you move to a new home. This is a move. You might feel happy or scared. You might meet new people or learn new things. This is a move.

In a way, a move is like a story. If you follow the story, you are doing a move. If you do not follow the story, you are not doing a move.
