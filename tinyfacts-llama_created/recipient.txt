To give something is to share it with another person. This happens when you give a small thing you have, like a book or a food, to someone else. This person gets the thing and can use it later. When you give something, you have made the other person happy.

Imagine you have a small ball. You play with it and make it fly through the air. You give the ball to a friend. Now your friend has the ball and can play with it when you are not around. You have shared the ball with them.

Sometimes, when you share something, you give it up. You can give your words to a person, or you can give your time. When you give something up, you are giving a part of yourself. This is a kind way to help others.

You can also give something to a place. If you are happy in a place, you might give your heart to that place. This means you are giving your love and your feeling to the people who live there. This is a beautiful way to build a new home.

When you give something, you show that you care about another person. You are showing them that you trust them. Giving is a way to show love and to be part of a group.
