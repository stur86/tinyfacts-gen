To start with, a group of things that people buy and keep for years is called a group. This can be a book, a picture, a small thing that flies through the air, or many other things that people do not buy today. We keep them because they are nice to look at or because they are important to us.

If you look at a group of things, you might see a ball, a tree, a dog, and a car. You do not buy all of these things. You pick three or two to keep. This is a way to hold something you love.

You can also hold things that are not for eating. A group of small pieces of wood is a group. You keep them for many years because they are nice to look at. A group of old books is another kind of group. You keep them because you love reading.

Many people keep things from their lives. A person might keep a small thing from a movie. Or a person might keep a picture from a visit. They might keep it as a memory. This keeps the story of what happened going around.

Sometimes, you buy things that are old. You might buy a book that was written a long time ago. Or you buy a small thing that was made by a person who lived a long time ago. When you buy these things, you keep them and tell others about them. This can help people learn more about what happened in the past.

In some places, people buy things that are not very good at first. They might buy a small thing that is not very strong. Then, over many years, the thing gets better. The person keeps it because they like it. This is a way to show you care.
