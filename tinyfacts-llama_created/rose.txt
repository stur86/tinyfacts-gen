A rose is a thing that grows from the ground. It is a long, green thing with many small parts. The green parts are like big green boxes. The green boxes hold water and air. Inside the green box, there is a sweet brown thing. This sweet brown thing is what we eat to make food.

A rose has many parts. One part is the green top. This top is what we see. It is like a big green cover. When the top is wet, it falls off and goes to the ground. The top does not need water to live. It gets its water from the green boxes inside the green box.

Another part is the long, red parts. These red parts grow out of the green box. They are strong and cover the rest of the green box. The red parts are like a big red cover. They keep the green box warm. They also hold the sweet brown thing inside.

A rose has many small parts too. There are parts with many small pieces on them. There are parts with only one small piece on them. These small pieces are like tiny little hands. They hold water and air. The tiny hands help keep the green box and red parts strong.

A rose has many colors. Some roses are red. Some are white. Some are pink. Some are two colors at once. Some are green and white. Some are red and white. The colors are like a big bright picture. The colors help the rose look nice.

We use roses to show love. We give them to our friends. We put them in our rooms. We put them in the kitchen. We eat the sweet brown thing. We drink the sweet brown thing from the cup. This sweet brown thing makes us happy.

Roses also grow in cold places. They grow in places where it is very cold and very dark. They need the cold to grow. When it is too warm, the green boxes start to break. The sweet brown thing starts to fall off. The red parts start to dry out.

We need to water roses when they need it. We need to make the ground soft. This helps the green boxes get water. The soft ground also helps the red parts stay green. We need to water them often enough so they do not get too much water at once.

Roses can live for many years. Some can live over a hundred years! They are strong and keep growing. They stay beautiful for a long time. This is why people like to have roses in their homes.
