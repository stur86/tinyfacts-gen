An area where people and animals live together. The ground, water, air, and living things in a place are all part of the same world.

Think of a city. The building where people work is part of the city. The streets are part of the city. The green space where people go to sit or play is part of the city. The water from the big body of water that surrounds it is part of the city. Everything in this area is important for each other.

A person might live in a small house on a big piece of land. The land has trees that give food to the person. The water from the big body of water is used for drinking and cleaning. The air is good to breathe. The small house is where the person lives.

A place like this is called an area. There are many areas in the world. Some are very small, like a tiny corner of a town. Others are very big, like a whole city or a huge area of land that goes across many states.

When people live in the same area, they learn about each other. They share food, clothes, and ideas. They build things together. They work together to keep the area safe and clean.

The area can also have problems. A place where people live and work together can be very busy. There might be a lot of cars, a lot of people, and a lot of noise. There might also be problems with water, air, or food. But the people in that area work together to fix these problems.

In short, an area is a place where people and animals live together. It is a world with its own way of living, its own ways of getting food and water, and its own ways of keeping each other safe.
