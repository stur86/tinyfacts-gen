To eat something is to take a big bite out of it. When you eat food, you put it into your mouth. Then you use your teeth to break it into small pieces. Your tongue moves around the pieces, and your brain tells your body to take that food in. Your body uses the food to give you power and make your body work.

Some things you eat are good for you. They give you the power to run, to jump, or to stay warm in the cold. You might eat food that is red, green, blue, or many colors. You can eat many different kinds of food every day. Sometimes you eat food that is sweet. Sweet things make your face smile.

Other things you eat are not good for you. They can make you feel sick. If you eat too much, you might feel heavy or your tiring. You might have a big yeah or a red face. These things happen because food is not broken down enough in your body.

You can also eat things that are not food at all. You might drink water or drink something that is clear. Drinking water helps you stay wet so you can walk or run. Drinking something that is clear can make your eyes feel wet, like after you cry. This is okay because your body needs to keep its water inside you.

When you eat, your body takes in the good parts and sends the bad parts away. This is how your body stays clean and stays strong.
