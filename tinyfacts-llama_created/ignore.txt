To ignore something means to not pay attention to it. You might see a friend or a person who is talking, but you do not listen. You do not look at their face. You just keep looking at the ground or at your own face. You do not want to hear what they say.

Sometimes, you ignore things that are good for you. You might feel sad because your mom is crying. You do not want to see her. You close your eyes. You do not want to hear her words. You just want to be alone.

You can also ignore things that are bad. If you see a car that is going very fast, you might ignore it. You do not look at it. You do not think about it. You just keep moving.

If you ignore a problem, it will stay there for a long time. It will not go away. You might feel a lot of pain later. You might feel angry or sad. You might want to say something, but you do not.

To ignore something is to stop paying attention to it. It is like you are saying, I do not want to see this anymore. It is like you are saying, I am not interested.
