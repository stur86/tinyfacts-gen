A thing that has a name is a word. A word is the way we use to tell other people about things.

A thing that has a name is not a word. It is a real thing. It is the thing you see or hear.

A thing that has a name is often a group of people. When many people are in this group, they call it a group. When they all say the same name, they say they are one.

People use these groups to show who they are. They carry the name of the group on a thing that goes around. This thing is a big sign that says who the group is.

You can also carry a small sign. This is a word that tells you to look at a person or a thing. When you see this sign, you do what it says.

Some groups have many signs. One group might have one sign. This one sign tells everyone in the world who the group is.

There are many kinds of signs. Some are short. Some are long. Some have colors. Some have words that tell a story. Some are very big and show many pictures.

When people carry signs, they show who they are. They tell the world who they are and what they want. They are like a way to say, right here, I am here.
