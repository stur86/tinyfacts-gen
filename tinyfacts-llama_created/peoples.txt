The word people is something that is many. It is about the people you see around you every day. It is about the families and the groups of people who live in a city or a town.

When you say you are a person, you are the person. You are the one who lives in your house. You have your own bed to sleep in and your own chair to sit on. You have a name and you have a face. You are the one who does what you want to do.

But people are also about groups of people. A group is a set of people who are very close to each other. They do things together. They laugh and talk. They share their food and their money. They help each other if one person is sick or if one person loses their job.

There are many kinds of people. Some people love to run. Some people like to eat food from a box. Some people like to read books. Some people like to play games. Some people like to go to the movies. Some people like to play with their kids. Some people like to go to a music place and listen to music.

Everyone is a person, no matter how old they are. Everyone has their own story. Everyone has their own way of thinking. Everyone has their own voice. We can all be friends if we try to understand each other.

You are a person too. You have a heart and you feel things. You have a brain and you think. You can make things happen. You can help others or you can hurt them. You can be very kind or you can be very mean.

So, when you say you are a person, you are the person. You are the one who lives in your world.
