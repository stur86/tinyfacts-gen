When you say something is lower, you mean it is not as high as it was. Imagine you are standing in front of a tall wall. If you walk down a little, you are now standing in front of the wall. You are no longer in front of it. You are behind it.

Things can be lower in several ways. You can lower a thing. This is when you pull it down so it is closer to the ground. You can also lower a person. This is when they go down so they are not as high as they were before.

Sometimes, when you talk about a place, you say it is lower. This means it is not as high as another place. A high place is one where the ground is very low. A lower place is one where the ground is still high enough to walk on.

You can also say something is lower than another thing. This means the first thing is not as high as the second thing. It is still much better than the second thing. Think about a tall tree. The tree is very high. If you stand next to it, you are lower. But you are still much better than where you start.

Lower is also about time. Imagine you are waiting for a movie to start. The movie starts soon. You are waiting for a long time. If you wait longer, you are lower in the story. This means you are closer to the end of what happens.
