When you look at someone, you use your eyes. Your eyes are the parts inside your head that help you see. They are like two big windows that let light in. You can see the world around you. You can see colors, numbers, and words.

When you look at a person, you are thinking about them. You might think they are nice, or they might look funny. You might want to know more about them. This is why you look at them. This is how we show we care.

You also look at things. If you look at a book, you read it. If you look at a tree, you see its big, green parts. You look at a picture, and you see the people or animals in it.

Sometimes, you look at yourself. You look at your face. You look at your hair. You look at your clothes. You see how you look. This helps you feel good about how you look.

When you look at a person, you are not just seeing them. You are also thinking about them. You are thinking about what they say, or what they do. You might remember something they said before. This helps you remember them.

A look is like a word that tells us how someone is feeling. If a person looks sad, we might know that they are sad. If a person looks happy, we might know that they are happy. We use looks to talk about people.

You can also look at things. If you look at the sun, you see its bright light. If you look at the ground, you see the ground around you. The look of the sun or the ground can tell us something. It can tell us how hot it is, or how high it is in the sky.
