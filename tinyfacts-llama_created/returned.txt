When you return something to someone, it means you bring it back to them. This happens when you come home after you have been away for a long time. You might bring a book you read or a small thing you bought while you were away.

Imagine you have a friend and you promise to meet them at a place. Then you go to the place and they say they are not there. You go back to your house and look out the window. You see them standing in front of the house. You call out their name and they come to you. They have been looking for you and are very happy to see you.

You can also return something to a place. If you buy a thing you want but do not need it, you can bring it back to the store. The store will give you a small money back as a thank you for the thing.

Sometimes, if someone does something wrong, they have to return what they did. If a person takes something that does not really go to them, they have to give it back. This is a way to show they are sorry for what they did.

Returning things helps us show that we care about the people around us.
